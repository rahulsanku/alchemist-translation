# Chapter 705: Life Lost in Yellow Springs, Soul Severed at Black Cliff

"Gah!"

"Gah!"

The piercing cries of seabirds drifted over the ocean as several birds circled in formation through the air.

From time to time, they plunged downward, rushing straight at the sea surface. Their sharp claws hooked lightly, their pointed beaks stabbed down, and when they flew up again, a large fish had already fallen into their mouths.

There were also those already full who seized large fish, pecked them to death, and then did not eat them, instead tossing them back into the sea.

Blood spread in a blur, luring over some bloodthirsty sea beasts.

These first-tier seabirds were playing with endless delight.

Until a gale swept past, scattering their formation into disorder.

One seabird after another stared blankly ahead, where a vague kite-bird silhouette seemed to have raced past.

...

"Senior Han, this time I truly have to thank you."

"No matter. It was only an early-stage Sage puppet. If it is abandoned, then abandoned it is. Besides, did you not promise to give me Feiyunzi's flesh body?"

On the wooden kite, Luo Chen stood with his hands clasped behind his back.

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] sat cross-legged on it with a deathly pale face. Even with the mana shield Luo Chen had arranged for him, under such high-speed flight he still felt his breathing grow heavy.

At this moment, he was doing his utmost to circulate spiritual power and dispel that discomfort.

Luo Chen glanced at him, then continued speaking with Han Zhan inside the soul-nourishing plaque.

On Purple Spirit Island [TERM?], the early-stage Sage puppet that had resolutely self-detonated was precisely the spirit wood puppet Han Zhan had previously inhabited.

The price that could make Han Zhan willing to give up such a treasure was clearly Feiyunzi's flesh body.

Back then, on Bright Radiance Heaven's [TERM?] cloud-sea plaza, Feiyunzi had shed his body like a cicada escaping its shell, abandoning his flesh body and fleeing with his Nascent Soul.

Luo Chen had crushed his Nascent Soul in the end, but the flesh body had been preserved.

This flesh body was clearly prepared for Han Zhan to "possess."

Unlike a puppet, a Sage cultivator's flesh body was an existence that had formed a Purple Mansion [TERM?].

Even if its spirit root attributes, meridians, and apertures did not match Han Zhan's, so long as the Purple Mansion [TERM?] existed, it was still the best vessel, far surpassing any puppet.

Afterward, as long as he spent a hundred years or so, he could always improve it bit by bit, allowing Han Zhan to once more step onto the great road of cultivation.

"What a pity. Although you left behind a drop of essence blood to lure Moon Wanderer onto the island, in the end you did not cause her any real trouble."

Han Zhan sighed as he spoke.

Luo Chen was also quite regretful. Borrowing the power of the formation and a Sage puppet to detonate Purple Spirit Island [TERM?] would have left an ordinary Sage half dead even if it failed to kill them.

But according to what a soul-fragment puppet Han Zhan had left beneath the sea saw, Moon Wanderer had actually been unharmed.

"The ability of a Great Cultivator truly exceeds imagination!"

Luo Chen sighed, growing even more wary of Moon Wanderer.

An existence like this was fine if one did not provoke her, but once provoked, she was like a thorn at one's back, filling his heart with unease.

Moreover!

"I am afraid I cannot go to the Xuanyan Sea Region now."

This was what troubled Luo Chen most.

His original plan had been to take a detour through the outer sea and head to the Xuanyan Sea Region where he had once hunted demon crabs.

Because there was a Xuanyan Island there!

Although that island had sunk, the foundation of its fourth-tier spirit vein still remained.

Luo Chen was now at Adept Stage Eight, only a step away from Adept Stage Nine, and the day of forming his Nascent Soul could be counted on one's fingers.

In addition, everything needed for the Nascent Soul Pill was already fully prepared.

For Nascent Soul formation, the only condition he lacked was a fourth-tier spirit vein.

Xuanyan Island was the most suitable target.

There was the foundation of a fourth-tier spirit vein there. With a little nurturing, it could reappear in glory. There were no powerful demon beasts nearby either, so Luo Chen could cultivate in peace and quietly attempt to break through.

But now an accident had occurred.

Moon Wanderer had not chased him, but she had sent a demon beast comparable to a late-stage Sage to hunt him down.

The urgent task was to shake off that strange demon beast.

"I have to go deep into the Demon Sea!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, already having made his decision.

When he had first entered the Northern Sea, he had been incomparably awed by the Demon Sea beyond human territory.

But now, he was not so afraid.

First, his strength had undergone a qualitative leap. If he faced a Sage demon cultivator head-on, he had the power to fight.

Second, Han Zhan was protecting him at his side. The other man not only possessed Sage ability, but under full effort could also drive that white tiger puppet and temporarily resist a late-stage Sage demon cultivator.

Finally, Luo Chen had a clear understanding of the current Northern Sea situation.

Most of the top demon emperors were sealed inside Arctic Yemo Heaven, so their lairs had instead become the emptiest places.

As long as he was a little careful, going deep into the Demon Sea should not be too dangerous.

"Luo Chen, go faster. That demon beast's speed is unimaginably fast. If this continues, you will be caught."

Han Zhan's voice sounded again.

Luo Chen frowned and did his utmost to mobilize the mana in his body, further urging on the wooden kite beneath his feet.

A streak of flowing light crossed the sky like a meteor.

...

Several days later.

A muffled groan came from the wooden kite.

Luo Chen asked nervously, "How is it?"

Han Zhan said with some exhaustion, "The late-stage Adept puppet I left along the route was torn apart."

Luo Chen was startled and uncertain. "Is its speed truly that fast?"

Han Zhan said in a low voice, "Our speed being slow is also one factor. Unless you abandon this Initiate junior Sang Jinghe [TERM?], then use your Heavenly Peng true body [TERM?] and fully unleash Ninety Thousand Li [TERM?], perhaps there will be a slim chance of survival."

Luo Chen glanced at Sang Jinghe [TERM?] and shook his head.

It had not yet reached that point.

Moreover, if he flew at full strength, could he truly shake off that strange demon beast?

His face looked slightly dark.

"Something is wrong. I clearly used the Dragon-Slaying Art to clear away the aura behind me. Why can that demon beast still accurately chase us?"

No matter how fast its speed was, it needed an accurate direction.

Otherwise, it would only go south while the cart pointed north.

Therefore, resolving this issue was the most important thing. Otherwise, even if he temporarily shook off that strange demon beast, it would eventually find him again.

But what method was the other party relying on?

Luo Chen sank into deep thought.

Han Zhan did not interrupt him. Instead, he took another Adept-stage puppet from his storage bag on his own, placed several restrictions on it, and sank that puppet into the sea.

...

Whoosh!

In the sky, a gigantic moth flapped its wings and crossed the sea.

Suddenly.

A water pillar erupted violently below.

The moth opened its mouth and spat, and the water pillar immediately vanished.

The puppet within, which had been about to launch an attack, was also shattered inch by inch under that gray light.

The moth circled around the area suspiciously, then suddenly shook its head and continued chasing firmly in one direction.

...

"It is my essence blood!"

On the wooden kite, Luo Chen looked at a drop of crimson blood on his fingertip, his face dark as water.

He had wanted to give Moon Wanderer a surprise, but had not expected to harm himself instead.

That strange demon beast the other party summoned could actually absorb his essence blood from the aftershock of Purple Spirit Island's [TERM?] explosion, thereby locking onto his position.

This kind of method could not be concealed even if he fully used concealment spells.

Moreover, there was another reason this situation had arisen.

That was the disappearance of the Concealment Veil Formation!

Inside Bright Radiance Heaven's [TERM?] Pill Palace, Luo Chen had completed his physical nirvana, reshaping his meridians and qi sea and improving his fleshly aptitude.

But during that physical nirvana, the Concealment Veil Formation he had once engraved on his body had been forcibly erased.

Luo Chen had not paid attention to this aspect at all before.

But now it had become a huge hidden danger.

How could concealment spells compare to a concealment formation that covered him at all times?

After understanding all this, Luo Chen continued analyzing.

The other party had locked onto the aura of his essence blood.

Unlike the essence blood of ordinary cultivators, Luo Chen's essence blood not only contained Adept-stage mana, but was also filled with powerful origin power!

This power was far too special, to the point that it stood out in heaven and earth like a crane among chickens.

That was why the strange demon beast could chase him without the slightest deviation in direction.

"In a short time, I have no way to engrave the Concealment Veil Formation again to protect myself. I need to think of another method."

As the thought flashed through his mind, Luo Chen asked:

"Senior, how many puppets do you have left?"

"Four remain. But the white tiger puppet cannot be used lightly. That puppet consumes far too much mana, and for my Nascent Soul to drive it is extremely difficult."

"Mm. Leave me one Adept-stage puppet."

"What are you going to do?"

"Deceive the enemy!"

Han Zhan understood instantly.

"Also, help me pay attention to whether there are any places nearby occupied by powerful demon beasts," Luo Chen said again.

Han Zhan asked in surprise, "You want to redirect disaster eastward?"

"Exactly!"

...

Three days later.

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] looked at Luo Chen sitting cross-legged on the wooden kite's back, curiosity flickering in his eyes.

Another person was also sitting cross-legged before him.

However, that person had no life in him at all, clearly a lifeless puppet.

Luo Chen paid no attention to Sang Jinghe [TERM?]. For now, he would bring him along. If things truly reached the point of absolute necessity, he was not some pedantic fool.

At present, he needed to complete the final step.

Blood light thickened around Luo Chen as strands of origin power flowed out.

Abruptly, Luo Chen gave a low shout and pressed a finger against his bare chest.

"Come out!"

In an instant, a drop of blood containing terrifying power seeped out from his heart.

Seeing this, Sang Jinghe's [TERM?] expression jumped wildly.

Heart blood!

Essence blood second only to soul blood, and still above blood from the tip of the tongue.

Ordinary cultivators would not dare easily stimulate it for use, or they would suffer serious damage to their vitality.

Stimulating heart blood did not carry such severe consequences for Luo Chen. With his powerful heart, he could afford to waste one drop of heart blood.

An unnatural flush flashed across his face as Luo Chen watched the crimson blood seep bit by bit into the puppet's heart.

Gradually, the puppet began emitting an aura identical to Luo Chen's.

"Is it enough?"

He murmured.

Sang Jinghe [TERM?] could not answer. Moreover, he knew the other man was not asking him, but another powerful existence.

Sure enough, Han Zhan's voice drifted over.

"For an ordinary cultivator, this method of yours would be enough to pass the false off as true. However, that strange moth..."

Luo Chen's face dimmed. "This is all I can do. If it truly fails, then when the time comes, we will fight it with all our strength. In any case, Moon Wanderer has not chased us. With you and I joining hands, against one wicked beast, there should always be a sliver of life."

Han Zhan was not too optimistic about this.

That moth demon beast was an existence that dared bare its teeth and roar at Moon Wanderer. No matter what, it had to be a troublesome late-stage Sage creature.

He thought for a moment, then suddenly said, "Add one more layer!"

"Mm?" Luo Chen was puzzled. Could he still make it better?

Han Zhan's words drifted faintly into his ears.

"Do you still remember Locked Pearl Curtain and the Mao-Opening Art [TERM?]?"

...

"Here will do!"

"By this old man's perception, along the path we traveled, there were occasionally one or two powerful fourth-tier demon beast auras, but the place that makes this old man's soul tremble most is the sea region before us."

The pale-faced Luo Chen paused, and the wooden kite stopped on a barren little island.

He lifted his eyes and looked into the distance. His pupils could not help shrinking.

What entered his sight was a stretch of murky yellow, as if he were in the middle of a desert.

Yet when a breeze blew past, what rose was clearly wave after wave of turbid yellow seawater.

"What sea is this?"

"I do not know, but according to this old man's observation, the danger contained in this sea is no less than Fallen Sea before the Fallen Demon Land [TERM?] opened. If you lure that strange moth into it, perhaps it can buy you enough time to escape. What do you think? Have you considered it?"

The pale Luo Chen gritted his teeth and finally nodded fiercely.

"There is no time. Let it be here!"

A moment later.

The wooden kite started again, and Luo Chen drove it into the yellow sea.

Not long after he left, roughly half a day later, a figure swept in with wind and towering waves.

It was a gigantic moth.

It did not pause at all, directly chasing into the yellow sea.

Its speed was extremely fast, and excited roars came from its mouth.

It seemed the prey was about to appear.

Sure enough.

Not far from a black ridge, it saw the prey's back.

That prey was driving the wooden kite, his figure staggering, his speed becoming incomparably slow.

The Star-Chasing Demon Beast [TERM?] let out a strange cry and rushed straight over.

Luo Chen's thoughts were muddled and dazed as he turned around in a stupor. He saw the moth open its mouth toward him and spit out a gray light.

When the gray light reached his body, his muddled thoughts became clear for an instant.

He did not look at the moth. Instead, he turned back to gaze at that black ridge facing the yellow sea, and a trace of memory seemed to surface in his mind.

"This place is Break..."

Before the murmured sound could fully emerge, a low and rhythmic sound came from the ocean below.

Afterward, endless yellow waves swept in from every direction.

Facing this scene, the Star-Chasing Demon Beast [TERM?] instinctively gave up on killing Luo Chen and instead beat its wings, wanting to shoot into the sky.

However, under the scouring of the yellow waves, the thoughts of all things seemed to become sluggish.

The gigantic moth was swept into the depths of the sea by a huge wave, and Luo Chen, close at hand, was swept in with it.

Very soon, sounds of chewing came from the sea below.

Suddenly, an exasperated voice rang out.

"Damn it, how did he run here?"

A ghostly light emerged from the deep sea, stumbling as it tried to escape the yellow sea.

But as the waves rolled, that ghostly light ultimately sank into the deep sea as well.

From then on, the sky was clear, the air was pure, and wind and waves were calm.

Only the unmoving murky yellow sea remained, along with that black ridge crouched between heaven and earth as if it had been carved by a blade.

...

"Hmph..."

A muffled groan came from the mouth of a woman above the nine heavens.

Rubbing her brow, shock flashed through her eyes.

The Star-Chasing Demon Beast [TERM?] was dead!

The soul fragment she had split off had also dispersed.

Even in the final blurry memory sent back by that soul fragment, Devil Lord Azure Yang had also been buried in the endless yellow sea.

Her frightened gaze slowly shifted downward. That calm, murky yellow ocean was like a man-eating maw opening invisibly.

"This is Yellow Springs Sea [TERM?], and that mountain... Soulbreak Cliff [TERM?]!"

Moon Wanderer's body trembled slightly, and all her calculations came to nothing at this moment.

With her intellect, how could she not anticipate Luo Chen's methods? Since he could leave a surprise on Purple Spirit Island [TERM?], he surely had other monitoring measures.

Moreover, compared to the opportunity in the rumors for Devil Lord Heaven-Refining to advance to the Body Integration stage [TERM?], the Myriad Immortals Assembly truly was not worth mentioning.

Therefore, even after receiving the Myriad Immortals Assembly's emergency message, she had not given up chasing Luo Chen.

Of course, an open pursuit would put too much pressure on him.

Thus, on the surface, she had only released the Star-Chasing Demon Beast [TERM?] to hunt Luo Chen, making him relax his guard, while in truth, she had secretly circled around, hidden her tracks, and pursued step by step.

Until she came to this murky yellow sea region.

She had watched helplessly as her soul fragment and the Star-Chasing Demon Beast [TERM?] summoned from the Myriad Beast Diagram [TERM?] perished inside.

And Luo Chen, whom she had been hunting, had also entered a land of certain death in his reckless flight.

"If I enter now, is there any chance of finding that wisp of gray light?"

The instant this thought rose, Moon Wanderer strangled it to death.

Soulbreak Cliff [TERM?] was one of the foremost forbidden lands of the Northern Sea!

There were very few people who could walk out alive, even among Sage Sages, or even Ascendant mighty ones!

According to rumors, only the Primordial Demon Sect's Soul-Refining Ancestor [TERM?] had explored it once and returned alive, reaping a great harvest. But only once. Since that time, the Soul-Refining Ancestor [TERM?] had never taken the initiative to explore Soulbreak Cliff [TERM?] again.

With her present ability, if she rashly entered, she would only share Devil Lord Azure Yang's fate.

Taking a deep breath, Moon Wanderer looked deeply at that calm sea region, then finally broke through the air and left.

When she reached Ascendant!

When she completely controlled the Myriad Beast Diagram [TERM?]!

At that time, she would certainly enter Yellow Springs Sea [TERM?] in person and step onto Soulbreak Cliff [TERM?]!
