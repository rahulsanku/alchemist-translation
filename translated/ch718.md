# Chapter 718: Yellow Springs Vanishes, Green Parasol Still Remains

"You and I are master and servant. It is not that I do not trust you, but that I must guard against accidents."

"I have already removed the source-blood restriction, since it would affect your normal cultivation. But the slave seal is something I must plant, to prevent you from being controlled by someone at a critical moment and turning your blade against me."

"Hand over your soul blood."

On a carriage whose spiritual light had been concealed, Luo Chen spoke slowly to Black King, who was curled beneath his seat.

His words were not without cause.

Han Zhan's lesson was right before his eyes.

Black King's innate demonic art, apart from its natural swallowing and digestion functions, shared a similar wondrous effect with the Primordial Unity Cauldron's power to seize Nascent Souls. If he launched it in a sneak attack from behind, even Luo Chen would suffer badly.

Yet ancient beasts, creatures who controlled mighty and peerless strength, were low in intelligence, weak in divine soul, and extremely easy for soul-path experts to control.

Black King was especially disadvantaged in this regard.

Although his intelligence was not low, his cultivation time was ultimately too short. Even if his realm had soared, he could not temper his soul foundation through the grinding of years.

Unlike Luo Chen. Though Luo Chen had cultivated for only a short time, his soul foundation had always been deep, and he had long since made up for this weakness.

Luo Chen had not raised this matter before because he had been busy cultivating bitterly, and Black King had only recently broken through then. Extracting his soul blood at that time would have hindered his cultivation.

Now that they had left Dragon Abyss Island and were about to face the storms of the cultivation world again, Luo Chen still had to prepare for the unexpected.

Betrayal by those at his side absolutely could not happen again!

He looked at the hesitant Black King and did not urge him.

The other party's loyalty was beyond doubt. The reason for his hesitation was clearly that once he surrendered his soul blood, life and death would be decided by Luo Chen's single thought. That was something very difficult for any living being to accept voluntarily.

In front, Sang Jinghe drove the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage, his eyes on his nose and nose on his mouth, as if he had not noticed this side at all. But if one looked carefully, the body beneath his Daoist robe was tense as well.

Heavenjade stood nearby, looking at Black King with anxiety and worry.

She wanted to speak, but under Luo Chen's indifferent aura, she could not open her mouth at all.

Beside the carriage, fierce wind blew and white clouds scattered.

After a long while, Black King said in a muffled voice, "I am willing to be Master's spirit pet beneath your seat once more!"

A drop of crimson essence blood slowly floated out from the crown of his head. Vaguely, within it was a lifelike black-scaled flood dragon, long whiskers and four legs churning as it leapt.

This was Black King's soul blood.

Luo Chen put it into his sea of consciousness with satisfaction, then placed his own Spirit-Enslaving Seal on Black King.

From this day onward, with a single thought, Black King's life and death would rest in his hands.

Seeing Black King droop his head listlessly, Luo Chen smiled and rubbed his cool head.

"Do not worry. If one day I ascend, I will naturally grant you freedom."

"In addition, I can make you one promise. When you are preparing to break through to the fifth tier, I will return your soul blood and grant you a complete soul body!"

After receiving these two promises, Black King's wilted mood recovered somewhat.

Because of Luo Chen's smile, the atmosphere inside the carriage also loosened a little, and Heavenjade could finally speak.

She first glanced at Black King, then said to Luo Chen, "Master, what about me? My slave seal was also removed in the Bright Radiance Heaven Pill Hall, so should I..."

"Forget it for you. With your current strength, even if someone controlled you, you would not threaten me. Just work hard at cultivation and do your duties properly for me."

Luo Chen spoke with a cheerful smile.

Heavenjade gave an "oh," instead feeling somewhat regretful.

Her strength was lacking, so she no longer even had the qualifications to be Master's spirit pet.

As for Sang Jinghe, after hearing that Luo Chen did not want Heavenjade's soul blood, he completely relaxed.

Inwardly, he even mocked himself: 'What was I thinking? With the Sage's breadth of mind, he can tolerate a little Golden Core cultivator like me.'

Just then, an order reached his ears.

"Change direction."

He asked in puzzlement, "Sage, are we not going north?"

"First go southeast. I have something to do," Luo Chen said lightly.

Sang Jinghe nodded and drove the carriage away from its original direction.

Luo Chen held the jade slip recording the Northern Sea's geography in his hand, his divine sense falling on a cluster of islands shaped like flying swallows.

That place was very small and very remote, but it had been his first foothold when he arrived in the Northern Sea.

In his eyes was a contradictory emotion, both expectant and unwilling to expect.

...

The carriage went straight southeast, without passing through human territory.

Yet along the way, they did not see many powerful demon beasts blocking the road.

Part of this was because Luo Chen had applied a spell to conceal the carriage's aura, but the decrease in demon beasts in the Demon Sea was also a fact.

Facing this abnormal scene, a faint oppressive feeling, like the calm before a storm, shrouded everyone's heart.

On this day, the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage stopped above a certain sea region.

Suddenly, a black shadow flew out from it and plunged directly below.

That enormous body and strangely familiar aura stirred fear in the hearts of many demons at that instant.

Panicked cries rang out from island beaches one after another, but under that heaven-shaking dragon roar, all clamor fell silent.

Very soon, the black shadow returned.

Thump!

A third-tier Demon King forced to shrink his body knelt trembling on the carriage deck.

Salty seawater streamed from his shell, and his expression was filled with terror.

"Whatever my master asks, you answer. Do you hear?"

The Red Rock Crab shuddered and instinctively raised his head to glance at the man sitting on the soft couch.

With just one glance, he felt as if he had fallen into a dream.

"I remember that in the original Xuanyan Sea Region, there were five great royal clans of demon crabs. Why, under my divine sense investigation, are there only your Red Rock Crabs, while the other demon crabs are few and scattered?"

The Red Rock Crab Demon King murmured, "A hundred years ago, in the battle of Xuanyan, the five great royal demon crab clans suffered grievous losses. Later, the Overlord Crab clan tried to unify Xuanyan, but Zhu Xiang, the clan chief of the Demon Spider Crab clan, colluded with outer-sea demons to counterattack. In an internal war, the Overlord Crab clan was destroyed. The new clan chief, Great Demon King Golden Armor, also died in that battle. As for Great Demon King Golden Claw, who had lost one arm, he fought his way out and fled into the vast Demon Sea."

"Then what about the remaining Azure Emperor Crabs, Demon Spider Crabs, Nine-Clawed Poison King Crabs, and your Red Rock Crabs?"

The Red Rock Crab Demon King continued answering, "They were summoned by the Demon Emperors and joined the Northern Sea Demon Alliance. They are currently attacking the human hinterlands on a large scale. My strength is low, so I was left behind..."

This Red Rock Crab truly was not strong, looking like he had only just advanced to Demon King.

Luo Chen frowned. "Demon Alliance?"

"It is the Northern Sea demon race allied army, led by Great Sage Beili and Lord Sea Emperor Whale. It is said to be formed from dozens of large clans and more than a thousand small clans, with exceedingly great strength, possessing ninety percent of the might of the army that attacked the Primordial Demon Sect back then!"

Luo Chen drew in a deep breath, shock flickering ceaselessly in his eyes.

He had naturally heard of Great Sage Beili and Sea Emperor Whale.

They were two famous Ascendant-stage ancient demons of the Northern Sea!

Back then, it was also under their raised banners that they led an army of billions of demons into the Arctic Yemo Heaven.

Logically speaking, they should have been trapped inside Arctic Yemo Heaven, so how had they now formed a Demon Alliance?

He wanted to ask for more intelligence.

For example, when had they broken the seal and escaped, and what was the current state of the war?

But this minor Demon King lived in an isolated corner. How could he know such detailed inner information?

In the end, Luo Chen could only wave a hand and throw this muddleheaded Demon King back into the sea.

The Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage started moving again. The one controlling this flying magic treasure now was Heavenjade.

She and Sang Jinghe alternated along the road, both conserving strength and maintaining escape speed day and night without stopping.

On the carriage, Black King's puzzled voice came over.

"Master, are we not slaughtering them? Back then, these fellows chased us without end."

"That was because we first hunted their clansmen. Why are you blaming them instead?"

"Uh... In any case, looking at these pincer-waving fellows annoys me. Their shells are so hard they scrape my stomach."

"Forget it. Since we have no need for resources, why create more killing karma? Speaking of which, you are all demons. Why is your killing intent toward your own kind so heavy?"

"Who is the same kind as them? Demon is just what immortal cultivators call all non-human beasts. In truth, we are not the same family at all. As for Old Black now, what race should I count as? Master, do you know?"

"Heh, that truly stumps me."

...

What race Black King now belonged to was indeed hard for Luo Chen to determine.

His original root had been a black-scaled giant python rolling in a mud pool.

Later, someone used a secret method to forcefully accelerate his growth into a black-scaled flood dragon. But at that time, Black King could not actually be considered a flood dragon. At most, he was a flood-python.

Even after he devoured the Cliff Snake's corpse, he did not transform completely.

After that, he absorbed the blood of several million Dragon Earthworms, Luo Chen's essence blood, and was also influenced by the remnant soul of the ancient beast Stone Dragon.

Throughout this process, Imperial Moonlight Nectar, the demon race sacred medicine that could assist demon beasts in digging out deeper bloodlines, had also been constantly taking effect.

By now, if one truly had to speak of Black King's race, whether bloodline or soul, none could summarize it completely. Only from his appearance could one see traces of the Yinglong recorded in some classics.

Yet he had no wings!

He would still be roughly described as a flood dragon!

Luo Chen was too lazy to care about these trivial matters and hurriedly passed over the Xuanyan Sea Region.

This sea region, which he had once placed great hopes on and nearly used as an alternative place to advance to the Nascent Soul stage, looked spiritually barren from his current perspective.

Especially Xuanyan Island. It had not been properly managed, and after a hundred years, its grade still had not recovered to the fourth tier. It remained at the third tier.

This sea would probably be unable to produce a Demon Emperor for eight hundred or a thousand years.

...

The carriage continued forward, and wherever it passed, Luo Chen's divine sense quietly swept over.

He discovered that in general, it had not changed from his memories of that year, but some islands were missing.

For example, there had once been a large island where humans gathered, with a Golden Core sect entrenched on it. Now both people and island had vanished without a trace.

This was very strange.

Even if destroyed in battle, an old site should have been left.

Moreover, Luo Chen remembered that the immortal island had been arranged with an island-linking formation method passed down from the Cang Hai Alliance. Ordinary demon beasts could not have killed their way onto it.

However, this strange matter seemed insignificant beside another matter.

That was the disappearance of Soulbreak Cliff!

Traveling east from Dragon Abyss Island, passing over more than ten million li along the way, Luo Chen carefully approached the Yellow Springs Sea Region where Soulbreak Cliff had been in his memory.

But there was nothing!

Yes, the sky there was clear, the air bright, and the seawater blue. There was none of the terrifying scene of turbid Yellow Springs breaking souls and destroying spirits.

Even while flying along the outer edge of the Demon Sea, he did not discover the slightest trace of Soulbreak Cliff or the Yellow Springs Sea.

"Did it run away?"

"Could that sea be a living thing?"

This guess once surfaced in his mind.

Luo Chen remembered that the puppet in which he had divided his divine soul using the Thatch-Opening Art Han Zhan had taught him had vaguely sent back an image before it perished.

Endless Yellow Springs washed across the void.

The great sea surged, and a chewing sound entered his mind.

If he wanted to explain the strange disappearance of the Yellow Springs Sea, perhaps the only answer was that that sea was a living thing.

...

One month later.

Albatrosses circled, and sala trees swayed.

Luo Chen quietly descended from the carriage.

"You all go to the Swallow Islands and wait for me. If nothing unexpected happens, I will arrive shortly."

Heavenjade was somewhat puzzled. This sea region with albatrosses circling again?

She still remembered that when they returned from the Xuanyan Sea Region that year, Master seemed to have stopped here as well, and had even sent her ahead to the Swallow Islands to investigate the situation.

At that time, she had thought Master was guarding against an ambush in the Swallow Islands.

But now, Master was already a Nascent Soul Sage. Why did he still need to stop here?

However, once Master had given an order, there was naturally no room to question it.

"Yes, Master!"

Watching the splendid carriage depart, Luo Chen took a deep breath and cast his gaze toward the few islands in the Xingtian Sea.

He did not release his divine sense, instead relying only on memory to search for his target.

Very soon, he saw a familiar little mountain.

"Back then, I passed this mountain without entering. This time, I must face it after all."

Luo Chen flicked his sleeve and slowly flew over.

At the foot of the mountain, he continued on foot.

Wherever he passed, flourishing vines seemed conscious and actively moved aside.

Very soon, Luo Chen stood with great apprehension before a cave entrance covered densely by natural vegetation.

"Go in!"

Luo Chen lifted the vines and entered the darkness.

...

Before an ancient formation, Luo Chen pressed his lips tightly together.

His hand brushed over one corner of the formation patterns, trembling faintly.

It had been repaired!

This formation had been repaired!

He remembered with perfect clarity that after he had come to the Northern Sea through Green Parasol Mountain's teleportation formation and awakened, he had cut away one corner of this formation to prevent demon cultivators from Green Parasol Mountain pursuing him.

At this moment, the formation was ancient, yet its lines were so new that not even dust stained them.

Clearly, this formation had not been repaired long ago, at most twenty or thirty years.

Who was it?

Demon Emperor Qingshuang, or Deputy Hall Master Youquan of the Duzhen Hall?

Or perhaps, that one...

A certain name churned in his mind, yet seemed like a taboo.

The reason pushing Luo Chen to come here included both homesickness for his Eastern Wilderness homeland and the grudges between Demon Lord Lian Tian and Origin Monarch Qixia in the Demon-Fall Land.

And he carried Qixia's cultivation art while also having obtained the Lian Tian inheritance.

Therefore, he wanted to see what condition this formation leading directly to Green Parasol Mountain was in. If Green Parasol Mountain had been destroyed under the attack of the Ascendant great expert of the Abyssal Deep Sect, then naturally it would have no spare power to repair this teleportation formation. When he returned to the Eastern Wilderness, he would also have fewer hidden dangers. If it had been repaired, then he would have no choice but to face the risk that his past alchemy work for the demon race might be exposed by them.

The moment he saw that it had been repaired, the conflicting emotions in Luo Chen's heart vanished completely.

It was as though he had accepted his fate.

The formation being repaired meant Green Parasol Mountain still existed, and even had the spare capacity to come tens of thousands of li to the Northern Sea to repair this teleportation formation.

They had not even bothered to move the teleportation formation, and clearly did not fear him, this insider.

"Some matters cannot be solved by blindly avoiding them. They must be faced in the end."

Luo Chen's eyes flickered, and the surging emotions in his heart gradually calmed.

"Han Zhan is dead. Apart from those demon beasts of Green Parasol Mountain, no one knows what I did there. All the human experts died under the spread of that Nirvana Sacred Flame."

"Now that I have already formed my Nascent Soul, as long as I deny those things to the end, apart from Ascendant sacred lands, no one should question me."

"If the Abyssal Deep Sect pursues the matter, I also possess alchemy capable of refining fifth-tier divine pills, and can try bargaining."

"I must return to the Eastern Wilderness!"

"Only, I cannot ride this teleportation formation."

Luo Chen shook his head and turned to leave.

He would not ride this teleportation formation because, first, he did not dare, and second, without the Azure Luan Feather protecting him, he could not actively activate this formation.

Just then, Luo Chen's heart suddenly stirred.

He subconsciously touched his lower abdomen.

Within his Purple Mansion, the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit was eager to move.

Luo Chen was surprised. "You can activate this formation?"

Immediately, he realized the key.

The Withering-Bloom True Flame had once devoured a bit of Nirvana Sacred Flame. Even if it was only a little, it gave it a slight embryonic form of sacred flame.

Demon Emperor Qingshuang might have relied on spatial law to construct a super-large teleportation formation, but she absolutely did not have that wealth, nor that need. Her main purpose in arranging this formation should have been for Origin Monarch Qixia to use.

Origin Monarch Qixia controlled the Nirvana Sacred Flame, and perhaps that was also one of the keys to activating the teleportation formation.

That was why his Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit had that sense of resonance.

"Forget it. I do not need this formation. I can return to the Eastern Wilderness by another method."

Luo Chen shook his head and walked out of the cave.

He did not destroy it again. Just as always, Luo Chen left the Xingtian Sea.

The path by which he came did not have to be walked again.

Since he had already achieved Nascent Soul, he naturally had the confidence to carve out a road to the heavens!

*(End of Chapter)*
