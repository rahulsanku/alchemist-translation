# Chapter 1037: Penned by the Ancestor, Qi Sea Floating Heaven

"The way of Qi Refinement lies in meditation. In the midst of the profound, you visualize the world's qi, then refine it for your own use. This is what is called Qi Refinement."

"Brother, I understand the principles, but how do I actually sense the existence of heaven and earth's spiritual energy?"

Faced with his younger sister Zhou Qingyu's question, Zhou Fuyou was momentarily stunned.

For him, this was the thing that required the least consideration.

Because in his perception, water-attributed spiritual energy was everywhere. Absorbing and refining it was as simple as instinctive breathing.

Yet for Zhou Qingyu, it was as if a heavenly moat lay across the gate.

"Could it be that the three pills left by the Alchemy Sect elder are useless?"

A month ago, he had already administered the Five Organs Foundation Pill, the Snow Spirit Meridian Powder, and most importantly, the Spirit Root Pill—all bestowed by the Ancestor—to his sister.

According to the descriptions, with these three pills working in tandem, a kind of "Pill Spirit Root" would be generated in a mortal, helping them absorb heaven and earth's spiritual energy.

Furthermore, this medicinal power could last for thirty years without fading!

Within thirty years, if one could break through to the Initiate stage, relying on a cultivator's externalized spiritual sense and expanded qi sea, one could already sense, capture, and refine external spiritual energy on one's own.

But Zhou Qingyu's current physical aptitude was unexpectedly poor.

Even with the Pill Spirit Root in her body, she still couldn't sense external spiritual energy.

Clearly, she and her brother were twins. He shouldn't have such uniquely terrifying talent while his sister was so lacking.

In Zhou Fuyou's view, as she grew up, his sister also showed great intelligence. Many of the knowledge from the classics, he only needed to explain once, and she could understand it.

"If only I could give half my talent to my sister."

Sighing inwardly, Zhou Fuyou's face showed no disappointment.

After a moment of contemplation, he suddenly thought of a method recorded in an ancient classic. It was something high-level cultivators used on low-level cultivators to help them gain insight into the next realm ahead of time.

Although that method was very energy-consuming and had a low success rate, under the current circumstances, Zhou Fuyou couldn't afford to care.

"Maomao, close your eyes, relax, and don't resist me!"

Zhou Qingyu didn't understand why, but her trust in her brother was absolute. She obediently closed her eyes.

Zhou Fuyou took a deep breath, slowly reached out, and placed his hand on the girl's head.

Then, strands of pure spiritual power surged from his palm, entering the girl's body through various acupoints like Baihui, Fengchi, and Taiyang.

In an instant, the girl let out an involuntary, pained groan.

Seeing this, Zhou Fuyou said in a low voice:

"Don't resist. Follow my movements and sense the outside world!"

Enduring immense pain, the girl tried her best to keep her mind clear.

Gradually, she seemed to see some tiny specks of light outside her body.

Was that spiritual energy?

She didn't know how long passed. The girl slowly opened her eyes, and across from her, Zhou Fuyou was staring at her with utmost concern.

Zhou Qingyu opened her mouth, speaking weakly but with surprise: "I sensed the existence of spiritual energy!"

Zhou Fuyou was relieved, letting out a long sigh.

Then came the same surprise.

He had thought this guidance technique would need to be performed several times, but unexpectedly, it succeeded on the first try.

His sister's body seemed to have very low resistance to his spiritual power?

The reason it had seemed so painful at first was simply that Zhou Qingyu still had a mortal body, unable to bear the powerful spiritual energy.

"Logically, everyone is an individual. So why does my sister hardly reject my spiritual power?"

This thought flashed by, and then Zhou Fuyou asked about more detailed matters.

"Did you sense the corresponding attribute of the spiritual energy?"

"All five colors are present, mixed and chaotic."

"So the Pill Spirit Root inside you has all five elements. Is there any attribute that feels more familiar, easier to absorb?"

"No, they all feel about the same."

"Tsk tsk, I don't know how to evaluate the Spirit Root Pill refined by the Alchemy Sect elder. It gives people hope, yet makes them so desperate. A five-spirit root—can it truly lead straight to the Great Dao?"

After a moment of reflection, Zhou Fuyou was not discouraged.

If the Alchemy Sect elder could do it, and Tiannan Dao Palace had specifically taken away Zhou Ping'an, who also had a five-spirit root, wanting to achieve the same, then why couldn't he try to cultivate his sister as well?

All it required was vast resources!

Fortunately, he now held a noble position in the Zhou family, second only to the Ancestor, with access to any resource he desired. Moreover, his own talent was so terrifying that ordinary resources were useless to him, so he could give them all to his sister!

"Rest well. I'll go to the family's Scripture Pavilion to find a suitable cultivation technique for you."

With a simple instruction, Zhou Fuyou stood up and left the Green Pavilion by the Water.

...

The Zhou family's Scripture Pavilion was quite far from the Green Pavilion by the Water, requiring some time to reach.

Zhou Fuyou used the Water Concealment Art he had just learned, treading on the waves, and quickly made his way to the Scripture Pavilion.

Along the way, he encountered many Zhou family members.

But everyone, upon seeing the handsome youth in red coral and white robes, respectfully made way.

Even the Initiate cultivator guarding the Scripture Pavilion, after seeing the Ancestor's token, did not stop him.

Entering the Scripture Pavilion, Zhou Fuyou began browsing with practiced ease.

From the first floor to the fourth, he only needed a glance at many techniques to understand their general effects.

But the more he searched, the more disappointed Zhou Fuyou became.

Techniques compatible with a five-spirit root were far too few.

And if they were just ordinary single-attribute techniques, he looked down on them.

Gradually, Zhou Fuyou stopped, a thoughtful expression on his face.

"Perhaps I'm being too picky?"

"For my sister, the Pill Spirit Root only gives thirty years. The technique she needs shouldn't pursue power or high limits, only speed of cultivation."

"In that case, isn't the *Dew Cicada Art* I practice myself the most suitable technique?"

The *Dew Cicada Art* had no threshold, meaning it was compatible with all five elements. Its only drawback was that the magic power cultivated would be very, very little, making it completely unsuitable for combat.

But Zhou Fuyou believed that under his protection, his sister wouldn't need to fight.

He only wanted his sister to live well, peacefully, for a few more hundred years.

Moreover, having his sister practice the *Dew Cicada Art* had another advantage: he could pass on his own cultivation insights to her, helping her avoid many detours.

Having understood all this, Zhou Fuyou, now on the fourth floor, stopped searching for techniques. His fingertip, which had been touching a white coral jade slip, slowly released it.

But just as he let go, he touched the jade slip again.

"A new spell? And it bears the Ancestor's mark. Could it be the Ancestor's latest work?"

Zhou Fuyou became interested. He took down the white coral jade slip and probed it with his spiritual sense. Four large characters entered his mind.

*Qi Sea Floating Heaven*

Then came a brief introduction left by the Ancestor.

This art is the culmination of this old man's lifelong insights, condensed from all his Dao attainments. It has no specific grade; its power can expand as the cultivator's realm increases. It is ever-changing and highly inclusive. As long as the practitioner has reached the Initiate stage and opened their qi sea, they can practice this art... If someone with exceptional talent and cleverness cultivates this art to great completion, they may one day make it comparable to the power of a divine ability!

Zhou Fuyou was stunned.

He had never imagined that this spell had such a profound origin, being the life's work of the Ancestor.

Moreover, the Ancestor had high hopes for this spell, even hoping that later generations could cultivate it to the level of a divine ability.

"But why leave it to later generations, instead of the Ancestor himself pushing it to the extreme and creating a true divine ability?"

This question arose for only a moment.

In an instant, Zhou Fuyou himself found the answer. He thought somberly:

Rumor had it that the Ancestor had little lifespan left, his end approaching. He probably didn't have enough time.

Perhaps this was also why he created this spell and left it in the Scripture Pavilion!

"Then this grandson will inherit the Ancestor's ambition and let this spell flourish!"

Zhou Fuyou's spirits lifted. He placed the original copy of *Qi Sea Floating Heaven* into his storage bag.

This trip hadn't been in vain!

He had thought of his sister's future main cultivation technique and also gained a spell with growth potential.

Truly wonderful!

...

Time flew like an arrow, urging the old to age; years galloped like a colt, chasing the young.

In the blink of an eye, eight years had passed.

The younger generation of the Zhou family had long since left the Transmission Hall, growing into genuine Qi Refinement cultivators.

On this day, a group of young people was summoned, heading towards Egret Marsh.

Along the way, many were discussing the reason for the family's assembly of all cultivators.

"It must be related to the development of Egret Marsh!"

"This plan was conceived over a decade ago, but it was shelved because a group of Initiate cultivators fell in the clan back then."

"Yes, Egret Marsh is vast, spanning three thousand li. But our Zhou family only occupies a third. The other large area is occupied by poisonous miasma and a group of black-beaked egret demon beasts. As our Zhou family population grows, a mere third of the territory can no longer meet our needs, so the plan to develop Egret Marsh has been restarted."

"I heard it's being led by the Sixth Elder?"

"Zhou Yuqi's father? After his promotion to the Adept stage, his status in the clan has become even more noble. First, he presided over the Spirit Testing Ceremony, and now he's leading the Egret Marsh development plan. Perhaps he'll take over the Third Elder's responsibilities for external affairs in the future."

"That's normal. After all, the Sixth Elder is the youngest! And he has a wide network of connections outside. I heard he even pulled strings to get Zhou Yuqi into Tiannan Dao Palace as an auditor."

"Tsk tsk, I envy him. Having such a good father."

"If you don't have a good father, having a good brother works too!"

Amidst the chatter, some people secretly cast glances at a beautiful woman at the back of the crowd.

She was only twenty years old, but she had already blossomed into a graceful young woman. Her brows, lips, and nose were like nature's own creation, perfectly matched.

If not for a stubborn and unyielding temperament, she could truly be called a beauty capable of toppling cities!

This woman was Zhou Qingyu!

Logically, because of Zhou Fuyou's status and her own stunning beauty, she should have been fervently pursued within the family.

But in reality, that was not the case!

Zhou Qingyu, who had once been tested and found to have no spirit root, had suddenly been able to cultivate one day!

And although her cultivation speed wasn't fast, it was by no means slow. In eight years, she had reached the mid-stage of Qi Refinement, even faster than some with three-spirit roots.

This inevitably led many to speculate.

Had her supremely honored brother abused his power, giving Zhou Qingyu a massive amount of resources, forcibly piling a useless person to such a level?

For this reason alone, many clan members with poor talent and scarce resources were envious to the point of red-eyed jealousy and burning hatred.

Of course, there were also some sycophantic Zhou family members who tried to befriend Zhou Qingyu. But the sudden change in attitude of those so-called "friends" at the Spirit Testing Ceremony years ago had made Zhou Qingyu never trust such easily given friendship, warmth, or affection again.

Except for her brother, she showed no favor to anyone.

Every day, she devoted herself to arduous cultivation, only to catch up to her brother's footsteps.

Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh...

The wind howled as the group of young people, riding their flying artifacts, quickly arrived at the edge of the Egret Marsh's foul swamp.

They did not venture deep but waited quietly.

Soon, several powerful beams of light flew over from the clan.

Leading them were the family head, Zhou Junxiao, and the Sixth Elder, Zhou Haiye.

The two looked out over the vast Egret Marsh, their faces clearly showing joy.

"Indeed, in the past few years, the miasma filling the foul swamp has been visibly decreasing. This way, those beasts hiding in the swamp have finally lost their advantage!"

Zhou Junxiao said excitedly.

The miasma of Egret Marsh was not as simple as it seemed.

In the most concentrated areas, even Adept cultivators dared not inhale too much.

And those black-beaked egret demon beasts not only had hard bodies and flew extremely fast but could also control the poisonous miasma, making them extremely troublesome.

With the Ancestor not intervening, the entire Zhou family was helpless against this treasure land.

Fortunately, in recent years, the miasma in the Egret Marsh's foul swamp had mysteriously decreased significantly.

After consulting the Ancestor, Zhou Junxiao received a clear order: begin clearing out the black-beaked egret demon beasts and develop Egret Marsh.

"Haiye, I grant you fifty Initiate clan members. Go deep into the foul swamp and slay the demon beasts. If you encounter an egret demon king, prioritize subduing it. If you cannot subdue it, slay it!"

Zhou Haiye smiled faintly, then led a group of Initiate cultivators, each holding a Poison-Resisting Pill painstakingly refined by the Fifth Elder, Zhou Chongxiao, in their mouths, and flew into the miasma-filled foul swamp.

Zhou Junxiao then looked at the hundreds of Qi Refinement cultivators below.

Among them were not only older Qi Refinement cultivators but also the newly grown younger generation.

He said sternly, "The Sixth Elder will handle the threats inside. If any first-tier egret demon beasts escape, you are responsible for capturing and killing them. For many of you, this is also an opportunity for training. Alright, from now on, form teams of three, spread out, and surround the Egret Marsh!"

Hundreds of voices answered in unison, then they dispersed.

Seeing this scene of disciplined obedience, Zhou Junxiao couldn't help but curl his lips slightly.

Under his leadership, the family was finally recovering some of its vitality.

But when he saw a solitary woman in the southeast corner, his brow furrowed.

"Why is no one teaming up with Zhou Qingyu?"

At this moment, Zhou Qingyu held a small, exquisite flying sword in her hand, standing on a hilltop, gazing into the distance at the Egret Marsh.

She seemed oblivious to the surrounding discussions, mockery, and sneers.

"My brother said dragons don't dwell with snakes, and tigers don't accompany dogs. To achieve anything, you must endure loneliness and hardship. As for friends, it's fine to have them, fine not to. There's no need to force it!"

She gripped her flying sword tightly, a hint of anticipation in her eyes.

She looked forward to possibly encountering an egret demon beast later and engaging in a real battle.

She also looked forward to the Egret Marsh being cleared out, so her brother might return.

Yes, only she knew that three years ago, when her brother disappeared, he had actually gone deep into Egret Marsh to cultivate.

She had heard he was practicing a special spell?

She wondered how his cultivation was progressing now...

...

At the same time.

Deep within Egret Marsh, in a swamp thick with miasma.

Several beams of light flew past, and one of them seemed to sense something and descended.

It was a beautiful young woman, holding a map in her hand, following its path.

"According to the map from the Yin family, that precious small-scale mineral vein should be around here."

"Haiye has to manage the overall situation, so I'll collect it."

"Speed is of the essence. Try not to let the other clan members discover me!"

The young woman held a Poison-Resisting Pill in her mouth and advanced quickly.

As she went deeper, the surrounding miasma grew thicker, giving her a bad premonition.

This wasn't the deepest part. Why was the miasma so dense?

Suddenly!

The thick miasma abruptly dispersed, revealing the scene within.

A handsome man, dressed in a white Dao robe embroidered with red coral, opened his eyes and looked at her indifferently.

"Seventh Aunt, why are you disturbing my cultivation?"

The young woman, addressed as Seventh Aunt, saw the young man and her expression changed slightly.

"Zhou Fuyou, why are you here?"

"That's what I should be asking you. And..." Zhou Fuyou slowly rose to his feet, a strange light shining in his eyes. "Incidentally, I also want to ask about that matter from back then!"

(End of chapter)
