# Chapter 625: Xuanchen Armor Emerges, Blood Wanderer Returns

Black King set out.

On the third day after meeting Luo Chen, he quietly left with the map Luo Chen had drawn for him.

His destination was Purple Spirit Island, deep in the Demon Sea.

Because he had to comb through the formation and nourish the magic treasure, Luo Chen could not free himself in the short term. But he also did not want this matter delayed, so he could only have someone make this trip in his stead.

Logically speaking, Heavenjade was more suitable.

Human body, avian type, and after practicing the Nine Myriad Li spell, her escape speed was even faster.

However, in the boundless sea, powerful demon beasts were countless, and danger could be found everywhere. In comparison, escape speed was less reliable than absolute strength.

Black King was now infinitely close to the third-tier late period. His innate demon art, combined with the two middle-grade magic treasure flying knives Luo Chen had bestowed, gave him extremely powerful battle strength.

He was qualified to walk the depths of the Demon Sea!

Moreover, Heavenjade had handled miscellaneous affairs for Luo Chen over these years, often coordinating with the Zhang family and the Myriad Immortals Assembly. If she suddenly left, whenever something happened Luo Chen would have to personally act, which would be especially troublesome.

After brief consideration, Luo Chen made the decision to have Black King go to Purple Spirit Island and ascertain the Purple Monkey Flower for him.

Purple Monkey Flower, one of the three great fourth-tier main ingredients of the Nascent Soul Pill!

In today's cultivation world, its reputation was not prominent.

But in ancient times, it had been a rather famous poison flower.

The floral fragrance released by the place where it grew could disturb and confuse the souls of living beings, and it could even feed on living beings!

And what this poison flower most liked confusing were ape-type demon beasts with half-opened intelligence. It could "raise" such demon beasts as food while also having them protect it, preventing other powerful demon beasts from plucking and devouring it.

After human cultivators suffered no small losses from this spirit medicine, they gradually discovered its uses.

Using this flower in medicine, incense refining, and even the practice of poison arts all brought tremendous benefits.

Unfortunately, after the ancient war between humans and demons, traces of Purple Monkey Flower became increasingly scarce.

Later generations would not connect it with the main ingredient of the Nascent Soul Pill at all.

That Nascent Soul Pill formula from Green Pill Valley had come from Han Zhan.

As for Han Zhan's experience obtaining that pill formula, it had originated from the sitting-death cave residence of an ancient cultivator.

After taking the soul-nourishing wood for Han Zhan on Cold Light Island, Luo Chen obtained the complete Nascent Soul Pill formula from Han Zhan.

In the past, he had still thought about using large amounts of trial and error, along with the system's help, to deduce the complete pill formula. He had never expected the final result to be so convenient.

After obtaining the pill formula, Luo Chen naturally could not leave it idle.

Even though he had been rather busy over these years, he would always squeeze out a little time to study the pill formula.

In his view, this Nascent Soul Pill formula truly was in the ancient style.

The many auxiliary materials relied on quantity to win.

The three great main ingredients, Divine Iris protecting the body, Five Elements Lotus Platform guiding qi, and Purple Monkey Flower drawing the spirit, perfectly matched the essence Luo Chen had comprehended in the method of Sage formation.

Essence, qi, and spirit, the three treasures united as one.

Judging by this simple and direct pill formula style, it was domineering and straightforward, placing very high requirements on the taker's foundation, yet Luo Chen was very satisfied.

His own foundation was extraordinary in the first place, even terrifying.

If it were another Nascent Soul Pill, he might still have misgivings, but with this pill formula, he decided without hesitation that he would refine it.

All cultivators in the world took seeking the Dao, ascending, and long life as their ultimate goal.

To reach this goal, there were thousands upon thousands of roads.

In fact, from the very beginning of Qi Refinement, the paths constantly branched.

All sorts of spirit roots, all sorts of physiques, many attributes, many races.

Foundation Establishment spirit medicines were countless, Core Formation methods were divided into many categories, and Sage formation methods were still strange and varied, differing from person to person.

And the Nascent Soul Pill refined from this pill formula was undoubtedly the one most suited to Luo Chen. He also did not need to waste effort seeking other auxiliary resources for Sage formation.

Over these years, Luo Chen had already intermittently collected all the many auxiliary materials needed for the Nascent Soul Pill. Now, he lacked only the three great main ingredients.

"I have one Divine Iris. Though the quantity is insufficient, through the Demon Hunter Office intelligence system, I found that Divine Iris is often traded in the Canghai Alliance. After all, the land occupied by the Canghai Alliance was once the essence of the Northern Sea apart from Arctic Yemo Heaven, and it also has the greatest number of Sage cultivators."

"After my cultivation is accomplished, I must make a trip to the Canghai Alliance, whether I go myself or send someone else."

"Now I have also obtained news of Purple Monkey Flower..."

Hope appeared in Luo Chen's eyes.

According to the records in the miscellaneous book, the entire island of Purple Spirit Island was filled with purple qi poison miasma. With such a scale, a large number of Purple Monkey Flowers must be growing there.

If he could control this island, then Luo Chen would no longer need to worry about his share of Purple Monkey Flowers.

"I hope Black King can bring me back good news!"

Luo Chen murmured, though he did not have much confidence in his heart.

So many years had passed, and so many major events had happened in the Northern Sea in recent years. Among them was even the heaven-shaking battle of the demon race allied army attacking the Primordial Demon Sect. Who knew whether Purple Spirit Island still existed?

Having Black King make the trip could both explore the path in advance for him and avoid some potential "dangers."

"Unfortunately, only the Five Elements Lotus Platform has had no related news after so many years. Even in classics from before ancient times, the recorded contents are few and far between."

"The occasional mentions are only the phrase 'of the five qi, the essence of the five elements.'"

Within his hopeful eyes, Luo Chen could not help showing some gloom.

The current him, though said to possess the cultivation speed of heavenly spirit root aptitude, was ultimately not a true heavenly spirit root.

Rumor said that when heavenly spirit root cultivators advanced to Sage, there was almost no obstruction. They only needed to consider the matter of undergoing tribulation.

He could not do that.

Therefore, he had to consider auxiliary external objects.

His search for medicines over these years was precisely to supplement external assistance.

As for undergoing tribulation?

Luo Chen's gaze fell on the gray cauldron before him, and his pair of spiritual eyes seemed to penetrate the cauldron body and look at the existence within.

"The Primordial Unity Cauldron and Xuanchen Armor are the preparations I have made for undergoing tribulation!"

The former was beyond doubt. As his lifebound magic treasure, it would have to stand alone in the great Sage tribulation.

And this Xuanchen Armor was the name Luo Chen had given the defensive magic treasure inside the cauldron.

The source of the name came from taking one character each from its former owner, Xuanyan Demon Emperor, and Luo Chen's own name.

Simple and clear.

Although Luo Chen prided himself on his powerful physique, he still did not dare use his body to directly resist tribulation lightning.

At that time, the Primordial Unity Cauldron would hold the front, and Xuanchen Armor would have to protect his body.

With this two-pronged approach, perhaps he could pass the Sage lightning tribulation.

"Perhaps..."

Luo Chen sighed and once again mobilized divine sense and mana, striking restriction after restriction toward Xuanchen Armor inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

...

It had to be said that this artifact-forging had truly had too many twists and turns.

If one used giving birth as a comparison, then obtaining the Demon Emperor shed husk before was obtaining a spouse, while later frantically practicing artifact-forging arts was equivalent to preparing for conception. The special effect born from the Primordial Unity Cauldron had practically let Luo Chen hit the mark in one shot.

Yet after finally conceiving the treasure embryo, at the final step, it began a difficult birth!

What Luo Chen was doing now was like finding that natural delivery would not work, and forcefully performing a caesarean section.

The basic restrictions he now struck out from time to time were almost no different from a sharp surgical knife.

Fortunately, through practicing formations these years, Luo Chen had gradually made up for his previous weakness in restrictions.

From initially understanding only obscuring restrictions, to later becoming proficient in obscuring restrictions, then turning to set foot in other basic restrictions.

Mountain, water, wind, cloud, obscurity, brightness, emptiness...

Unknowingly, he had already grasped the basic restrictions required for formations solidly and thoroughly.

Even the space restrictions most ordinary formation masters found hardest to grasp had been groped out slightly by Luo Chen through peeking at the spatial restrictions Azure Frost had arranged inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Luo Chen was even confident that if he were given the relevant materials for storage bags, he could attempt to refine low-level storage bags.

...

While Luo Chen waited for the magic treasure to emerge, Black King spent more than half a year and finally reached the destination.

In a deathly still sea region, waves did not rise and sea wind did not blow strongly.

A single horn slowly surfaced from beneath the water, finally revealing a pair of huge pupils.

He raised his head and looked at the island in the sea region, covered in purple qi poison miasma. Wariness appeared in his eyes.

"Is this Purple Spirit Island?"

"It cost this demon so much time, and several fights, before I finally arrived."

"Looking at it, apart from being a bit mysterious, there is nothing special about it!"

Black King muttered, but his next movements were not so reckless.

His throat stirred, and an object was vomited back out from his stomach pouch.

Looking through those faintly visible sharp teeth, it was clearly a red pill.

He bit the pill to pieces in one mouthful.

Immediately, a spicy taste spread down his intestines and surged through his entire body.

"I already have a body invulnerable to a hundred poisons, and I also have the detoxification pill Master personally refined protecting me. This purple qi poison miasma should pose no threat to me."

With a chuckle, Black King shrank his body and swam toward the island covered in poison miasma.

Three days later.

An angry dragon roar rang out from Purple Spirit Island.

The poison miasma accumulated over many years was stirred by the aftershock of that dragon roar, rolling like wind and clouds in all directions.

At the same time, an enormous figure shot into the air from Purple Spirit Island, while black and white colors circled beside it, occasionally slashing out huge blade lights dozens of zhang long behind him.

The jiaolong seemed somewhat dispirited. Staggering, he plunged headfirst into the depths of the sea and fled into the distance without hesitation.

Behind him came thunderous running sounds.

Not long after, more than a dozen purple figures rushed out from the poison miasma in the forest, each one wreathed in vast purple demon qi.

They roared angrily and pursued.

But when they reached the seashore, they could only stop and vent helpless fury at the great sea.

...

Inside the Pill-Artifact Hall.

The white-robed, red-faced Daoist seemed somewhat thinner, but his eyes were incomparably bright.

Looking at the gray cauldron before him, he drew in a deep breath.

"Since arriving at the Myriad Immortals Assembly, eighteen years have passed. Even if you were an immortal artifact, it would be time for you to emerge!"

He brought his palms together, then slowly pushed them out.

Enormous mana surged wildly, and the gray cauldron began trembling violently.

In the next instant.

A streak of spiritual light slowly floated out from the gray cauldron.

After the spiritual light scattered, a suit of armor-clothing appeared before the Daoist's eyes.

Without hesitation, Luo Chen grabbed Xuanchen Armor in his hand, and his divine sense swept across it.

The armor presented a deep, profound black color, its radiance restrained to the extreme.

Layer upon layer of armor scales seemed distributed like a turtle shell, and also like turtle cracks in earth that had suffered drought too long.

Yet when divine sense swept across it, those turtle cracks echoed each other from afar, flickering with spiritual light. The surrounding spiritual qi surged toward it involuntarily.

"The formation and embryo have fused, and qi is born from the armor."

"The Two Modes Six Armors Array has perfectly fused with the treasure embryo, and its impurities are nearly nonexistent."

"Xuanchen Armor has emerged, and its grade is... upper!"

After carefully counting the complete formation patterns upon it, Luo Chen finally concluded that Xuanchen Armor ranked as upper-grade.

This result was somewhat unexpected, yet also within reason.

What was unexpected was that with Luo Chen's artifact-forging skill, he had refined an upper-grade defensive magic treasure.

What was reasonable was that it should have been so!

A Demon Emperor shed husk as the raw material, supplemented by large quantities of precious auxiliary materials, plus the Penglai Immortal Sect's refining method, and finally the nurturing merit of the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

If the final result had been a lower-grade defensive magic treasure, Luo Chen would not even have had a place to cry.

His lowest psychological expectation had originally been a middle-grade magic treasure, but clearly, upper-grade better matched his wishes.

However, this was only a hurried glance for now. Its specific power still required testing.

Without thinking, Luo Chen beckoned toward Xuanchen Armor.

In an instant, spiritual light transformed and attached itself to his body.

Inside the Pill-Artifact Hall, mana surged.

When one looked again, Luo Chen's figure had vanished.

Above Peng Lake, a streak of escape light shot into the sky, heading straight toward the nine heavens.

Upon reaching the horizon.

Luo Chen formed a spirit seal, and his body immediately began swelling.

One zhang, two zhang...

Whoosh!

A pair of wings spread out from behind him.

When his body finally reached nine zhang in size, Luo Chen stopped transforming.

This body size was somewhat smaller compared to before, but his power was more restrained and even stronger!

After years of body tempering, plus improving his cultivation method, Luo Chen's body cultivation path had long since become worlds apart from when it had first begun.

Luo Chen had no time to pay attention to his current body cultivation progress. Instead, his attention fell on the armor over his body.

Xuanchen Armor fit him extremely snugly, without the slightest discomfort.

This was precisely what it meant to be custom-made!

Not only that, Luo Chen did not even feel any sense of weight.

When combined, the Two Modes Six Armors Array naturally possessed the effects of each formation.

Clearly, this sense of lightness came from the Spirit-Channeling Cloud Armor Array.

Luo Chen's mana surged and poured into Xuanchen Armor. In the next moment, a five-colored light appeared and enveloped him.

"Qi armor, as expected, has formed!"

Joy appeared on Luo Chen's face. His heart suddenly moved, and he descended from the nine heavens to the earth.

The sounds of wind and thunder rose loudly, frightening Peng Lake's cultivators into panic.

The instant both feet stood on the earth, a sense of endless flow came to him.

Even if Luo Chen no longer continued pouring in mana, the qi armor remained radiant and tough.

"This..."

Luo Chen had previously thought it was an accident. Now it seemed this upper-grade Xuanchen Armor truly did possess this wonderful effect.

So long as his feet touched the earth, it could automatically absorb nearby earth-attribute spiritual qi and maintain the qi armor undying and indestructible.

"I do not seem to have arranged a similar spirit-gathering formation, though?"

As Luo Chen was puzzled, his fingers touched the physical inner armor.

After thinking briefly, he roughly guessed the origin of this wonderful effect.

Xuanyan Demon Emperor had begun as a Ring-Headed Turtle, purified his bloodline step by step, cultivated to the Sage stage, and returned to his ancestry to become a black turtle body.

And the black turtle was one of the representatives among earth-attribute archaic beasts.

Having this characteristic was not strange.

Luo Chen stood there motionlessly, sensing the difference between being in the sky and being on the earth.

Although after leaving the earth, it could still automatically absorb nearby drifting earth-attribute spiritual qi, the effect was almost reduced by ninety percent.

From this, he reached a conclusion.

What it absorbed might not merely be spiritual qi, but earth qi!

The most direct evidence was the land beneath his feet. Originally, green grass had been flourishing and the black soil fertile.

But the longer he stood there, the more the black soil beneath the green grass turned gray-white. Even the depths of the earth vein trembled slightly, as if unable to bear the burden.

The earth found it difficult to bear its weight!

"This small island only has fragmented first-tier spirit veins spreading through it. It cannot withstand my extraction."

"But if I fought in a better spirit land, I could almost stand invincible."

Excitement appeared in Luo Chen's eyes.

This was only the qi armor.

Next, he should test its true defensive power.

Luo Chen raised his head and looked around. In the distance, escape lights were flying toward this side.

With his excellent eyesight, he naturally discovered that the leader was Heavenjade, while the others were Zhang family cultivators.

"This is one of my trump cards. It is not suitable to reveal before others."

Luo Chen shrank his body and returned to normal, but did not remove Xuanchen Armor.

Instead, with a thought, he changed its clothing color and patterns.

In the blink of an eye, a set of moon-white Daoist robes, the kind he most often wore, was on his body.

"It truly is entirely as one wishes."

Luo Chen chuckled softly, sent Heavenjade a divine sense voice transmission, and returned to Star Dou Hall.

Not long after.

Heavenjade entered Star Dou Hall.

Seeing Luo Chen smiling pleasantly and no longer trapped inside the Pill-Artifact Hall, she instantly realized something.

"Congratulations, Master! Joyous congratulations, Master!"

Luo Chen was in an extremely comfortable mood. He waved his hand at the woman bowing to salute.

"Dispense with ceremony!"

"Tell me, during this recent period of my seclusion, were there any major events in the Myriad Immortals Assembly? Also, how are things on Black King's side?"

Clearly, the latter was what Luo Chen cared about most!

Heavenjade's expression grew solemn, and she immediately replied, "Blood Wanderer has returned to the Myriad Immortals Assembly!"

Blood Wanderer?

Luo Chen could not help being startled.

*(End of Chapter)*
