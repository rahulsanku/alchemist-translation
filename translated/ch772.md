# Chapter 772: Red Sky Qi, Rosycloud Beast, Nether Origin Cultivator, Thunder Daozi

"Black King, watch over them for me!"

A month later, Luo Chen spoke thus.

With a flick of his left arm, a miniature dragon fell to the ground.

Rubbing his arm, Luo Chen said with slight helplessness, "Don't cling to my hand next time."

Black King felt a little wronged.

Somehow, ever since he had switched to the path of cultivating as a wild beast and advanced to the fourth rank, he had grown increasingly affectionate towards Luo Chen.

He didn't even want to stay in the Primordial Unity Cauldron, let alone the spirit beast bag. He only wanted to shrink his size and stick close to Luo Chen.

It was as if merely breathing in the aura emanating from Luo Chen's body was beneficial to his cultivation.

Now, he looked at Luo Chen curiously.

"Master, where are you going?"

"Up there!"

Luo Chen pointed at the canopy of the carriage, but he didn't set off immediately. Instead, he began carving restrictions onto the carriage using his mana.

One restriction after another fell, crisscrossing and interlinking to form a complete fourth-rank formation.

This formation was a variant of the Nine Heavens Ten Earths Grand Transposition Formation. It couldn't teleport directly, but it could lock onto a location.

Luo Chen wasn't sure how long he would be gone once he went up there.

But the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage couldn't stop; it needed to keep moving. To prevent losing his target later, he needed to leave behind such a "coordinate."

After the final restriction fell, Luo Chen reached out and wiped his hand across it, causing the radiant light to instantly fade.

"Protect them well. I'm off!"

With those words, Luo Chen quietly slipped out of the carriage without attracting anyone's attention.

Before leaving, he glanced back at the scene inside the carriage.

Zhang Jingqing stood at the front of the flying carriage, the breeze lifting the tips of his hair, his expression somewhat forlorn.

Heavenjade was sitting cross-legged, meditating to recover her mana.

Sang Jinghe was channeling mana into the formation's central hub within the carriage, supplementing it with the surrounding spirit stones to propel the Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage forward at great speed. However, in his other hand, he held a brand new book, occasionally reading a passage. It was one of the many classics related to spirit plants he had copied while in Central Continent.

"Quite studious!"

Luo Chen chuckled softly. His Nascent Soul resonated with heaven and earth, controlling a stream of free-floating spiritual energy to drag him rapidly upwards.

The place he was heading to was called the Nine Heavens!

The Nine Heavens represented the extreme limit of the sky!

Simply put, it was the highest point of this realm, also known as the Azure Firmament, the Empyrean, and so on.

Rumour had it that few people travelled there, and few living beings existed.

But that didn't mean the Nine Heavens were completely empty. Some special beings still existed there, and some unexpected treasures could be found suspended in the void.

Luo Chen was going to the Nine Heavens to collect the spiritual essences he needed to cultivate the *Thousand Heavens Myriad Transformations Sword Scripture*.

Swish!

A figure shot up like an arrow from a bowstring.

Piercing through clouds and crossing mists, wind howling and rain urgent.

Over the vast sea, anyone with eyesight comparable to Luo Chen's might have seen a meteor streaking upwards against the flow.

But in the blink of an eye, the figure vanished completely, disappearing into the azure firmament.

At Luo Chen's current speed, a distance of ten thousand li could be covered in an instant.

Yet, for some reason, as Luo Chen flew to over ten thousand li above the ground, his speed involuntarily slowed down.

"Has the controllable free-floating spiritual energy become scarce?"

Luo Chen slowed his pace slightly, pausing briefly to observe his surroundings.

He roughly came to this conclusion.

But that wasn't all. He could also feel his breathing becoming somewhat more difficult.

However, this had no effect on him.

Luo Chen opened his mouth wide, his chest and abdomen contracting.

"Huff..."

A long breath caused Luo Chen's chest and abdomen to swell.

Suddenly, he closed his mouth, and his chest returned to normal.

But a quantity of air sufficient to sustain Luo Chen's breathing for at least three years had been stored within his abdomen.

"The Qi-Nourishing Method I learned in my youth is still so useful!"

A faint smile curled at the corner of Luo Chen's mouth as he gazed into the sky above.

Ten thousand li was by no means the limit of this realm.

The Nine Heavens were even higher!

Luo Chen released his mana, transforming it into a cloud of fire that lifted him up, shooting him towards the heights like an arrow from a bowstring.

After forming a Nascent Soul, cultivators rarely used cloud-riding for their daily travel.

Because cloud-riding often consumed one's own mana. For Nascent Soul cultivators who could manipulate free-floating spiritual energy of the same attribute, following the principle of saving every bit they could, they often used the slightly slower method of being lifted by spiritual energy.

Therefore, in a sense, flying without clouds, which didn't consume mana, was just as much a hallmark of a Nascent Soul cultivator as their Sage domain.

With the aid of the mana cloud lifting him, Luo Chen's ascent speed increased once more.

Ten thousand li, twenty thousand li, thirty thousand li...

The higher he went, the greater the pressure Luo Chen felt.

That pressure came not only from the sky above but also from the distant, unfathomable ground below.

"Gravitational pull?"

When he reached sixty thousand li, Luo Chen found that the pressure had reached the limit for an ordinary cultivator.

If a Core Formation cultivator reached this point, they would inevitably exhaust their strength and fall back to earth. If they weren't careful during the descent and failed to react in time, they might be smashed into a meat patty.

Even for Nascent Soul cultivators like them, they had to muster their full mana to continue forward.

"No wonder resources from the Nine Heavens are so rare and precious. Just getting up here stumps countless people. Even a fellow Nascent Soul would have to expend a great deal of mana to make the trip. But this can't stop me!"

Luo Chen chuckled softly and actually dispelled the mana cloud!

Not only that, but he also removed the mana shield that was protecting him from the outside.

Then...

Swish!

A pair of red wings suddenly unfurled behind his back!

It was the Broken Moon Wings, which had lain dormant for many years since his Nascent Soul formation!

This magic treasure, custom-made for Luo Chen by the master craftsman of Tianfan City, was originally only of lower grade.

But after a century of nurturing, and having undergone the baptism of the thunder tribulation alongside Luo Chen during his Nascent Soul formation,

Although it couldn't ultimately break through to the true artifact rank due to the limitations of its own materials, it had reached the upper grade.

It could be considered an excellent flying magic treasure, no less effective than some ordinary true artifacts.

However, an upper-grade magic treasure might still be useful for an average Nascent Soul cultivator, but for Luo Chen, who possessed many treasures, it was no longer of much benefit.

Especially since his own speed had long since far surpassed that of the Broken Moon Wings.

Therefore, after forming his Nascent Soul, this treasure had rarely appeared in his many battles.

But now, it was finally time for it to shine.

Compared to a mana cloud, a magic treasure consumed far less mana.

With a single flap of his wings, Luo Chen shot straight towards the Nine Heavens!

The immense pressure bore down on him, but it was like a gentle breeze brushing his face, unable to compress his physical body in the slightest.

Threads of blood light swirled, deflecting the external pressure.

Luo Chen had used his formidable physical body to forcefully break through the final thirty thousand li.

The moment he stepped beyond the ninety thousand li mark, Luo Chen felt his body lighten.

He instinctively looked up and saw a pitch-black, boundless universe, with only stars twinkling, emitting faint light.

Compared to this infinite universe, he seemed so insignificant!

A feeling of powerlessness welled up in his heart.

What was a Nascent Soul? What was an Ascendant?

In this vast universe, they were ultimately nothing more than a speck of dust.

Rumour had it that even this boundless universe would eventually face its end in a great return to nothingness. Was the immortality these cultivators sought truly real immortality?

If true immortality was unattainable, then what meaning was there in the countless hardships and tribulations they endured now, and the myriad calamities they would face in the future?

The man in white stood frozen in the azure firmament, his eyes filled with confusion.

But the next moment!

"Open!"

A soft cry, like a great bell or a huge drum, resounded through his spiritual sea.

His confused soul was instantly jolted awake.

"Hoo... hoo... hoo..."

Luo Chen gasped for breath, large beads of cold sweat streaming down his forehead.

"What just happened? I actually lost my mind and couldn't extricate myself!"

The awakened Luo Chen couldn't help but swallow his saliva in fear.

At the moment he saw the vast universe, he had actually doubted the ideal he had clung to for nearly three hundred years—the ambition for immortality!

If not for the two divided souls in his spiritual sea, especially one that had reached the early Nascent Soul stage, which had promptly used the Aperture-Opening Art, he might have damaged his Dao heart. Future cultivation would have been plagued by inner demons born from doubting the path he had persisted in.

Within his spiritual sea, the divided soul sank back into silence.

Luo Chen slowly came to his senses.

"Han Zhan, you've saved me again!"

With a soft sigh, Luo Chen gradually disappeared into the azure firmament.

...

Three days later.

In the dead, silent void, a streak of rosy light suddenly appeared.

Just as the rosy light was about to dissipate,

Suddenly!

A strange force abruptly spread out, completely enveloping the rosy light.

From the void came a soft cry.

"Collect!"

Then, the streak of rosy light was seen being pulled by the strange force, eventually disappearing into a small cauldron.

Luo Chen, sitting cross-legged in the void, lowered his head to look at the wisp of bright red gas inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron, a relieved expression on his face.

"So this is Red Sky Qi?"

The Nine Heavens were the extreme limit of the sky, but the reason the extreme sky was called the Nine Heavens was because this empty void often gave birth to nine special types of gas.

They were Red Sky Qi, Green Sky Qi, Azure Sky Qi, and so on.

These special gases were natural products formed by the sinking of turbid qi and the rising of clear qi, where a certain attribute of spiritual energy gathered in the azure firmament. They were the most essential substances between heaven and earth!

Cultivators would collect these sky qi, condense them into spells and divine abilities, nurture two or three breaths of them in their chest and abdomen, and when encountering an enemy, they would spit them out, possessing the power to shatter metal and stone, and topple mountains and overturn seas.

According to the *Thousand Heavens Myriad Transformations Sword Scripture*, if Luo Chen wanted to maximize the power of his sword qi, he needed to collect the Red Sky Qi, Crimson Sky Qi, and Purple Sky Qi from the Nine Heavens that matched his attribute.

These three types of sky qi were all fire-attributed spiritual essences, or spiritual essences leaning towards the fire attribute.

However, collecting sky qi was also extremely difficult for Nascent Soul cultivators.

There were three main difficulties:

First, they were hard to come by! Sky qi appeared unpredictably and vanished without a trace, appearing and disappearing within the boundless billions of miles of void. For a Nascent Soul cultivator whose divine sense could only cover a few thousand li, encountering one was very much a matter of luck.

The second difficulty was that sky qi was violent and unruly. Ordinary magic treasures were hard-pressed to subdue it; one needed to use great mana to trap and wear it down bit by bit. Sometimes, after wearing down a surge of sky qi, only a single wisp or thread would remain.

Luo Chen couldn't solve the first difficulty; he could only rely on luck.

As for the second difficulty, which deterred countless Nascent Soul cultivators, it wasn't a problem for Luo Chen.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron had a strong suppressive effect on such existences!

He only needed to cover the suddenly appearing sky qi, suppress it, and he could quickly subdue it.

As for the third difficulty?

Luo Chen glanced at the distant points of light and immediately left the spot without hesitation.

In a short while, those points of light split into two groups. One group stopped at the place where the Red Sky Qi had appeared, and the other surrounded the direction Luo Chen had been heading earlier.

Both groups were utterly confused.

After the time it takes to drink a cup of tea.

Luo Chen appeared in a dead, silent void and breathed a sigh of relief.

"I never expected that Rosycloud Beasts actually exist in the azure firmament!"

Rosycloud Beast was the name of a beast, but it was actually an insect.

It used to have another name: Sky Insect!

According to ancient records, Rosycloud Beasts were born in the sea, inhabited the hazy void, and fed on various types of aurora.

Legend had it that a large number of Rosycloud Beasts gathered within the Primordial Magnetic Divine Light that shrouded the Arctic Yemo Heaven.

This beast was extremely powerful. At the same rank, it was in no way inferior to human cultivators wielding various magic treasures and spells.

Luo Chen knew this partly because he had read many books, and partly because of the third-rank great circle Hidden Dust Sand he had encountered in the Northern Sea back then, which had once pretended to be a Rosycloud Beast in an attempt to intimidate Luo Chen.

But later, after reading more ancient texts, Luo Chen gained a more comprehensive understanding of Rosycloud Beasts.

Their fondness for aurora was one thing, but what they truly loved were the various types of sky qi in the azure firmament.

When sky qi appeared, it often took the form of rosy light. Once it appeared, it would attract a large number of sky insects. As soon as they swallowed the rosy light, their bodies would instantly swell and expand, making them look like giant beasts.

Furthermore, because the rosy light would spill out, over time, the name "sky insect" fell out of use, and the name "Rosycloud Beast" became popular instead.

Luo Chen didn't know how powerful the Rosycloud Beasts surrounding the Arctic Yemo Heaven were, but those that could move freely and fly around in the azure firmament were definitely troublesome, and they often appeared in swarms!

With his current strength, even if he wasn't afraid of them, he wouldn't provoke them lightly.

Turning his head to look back at the path he had come from, a sudden thought struck Luo Chen.

If he could enslave these Rosycloud Beasts, wouldn't he be able to find various types of sky qi more easily?

Could it even solve all three difficulties at once?

But this thought vanished in an instant.

If he could think of it, others could too.

But no one had succeeded in all these years, which showed how difficult it was to enslave Rosycloud Beasts.

"I'll just have to keep trying my luck!"

Luo Chen murmured, found a place not illuminated by starlight, released his divine sense, and waited patiently for an opportunity.

...

It wasn't easy to come up here, and Luo Chen didn't want to go down easily.

So, amidst this kind of idle waiting, time passed bit by bit.

One day, two days... one month, two months...

Luo Chen's luck was fairly good. In just under half a year, he had managed to collect seven wisps of Red Sky Qi.

According to the sword scripture, if he could refine ten wisps of sky qi into his sword qi, it could be considered minor success, enough to dominate the Core Formation realm.

A hundred wisps of sky qi could be considered reaching the hall, a formidable technique even within the Nascent Soul realm.

If he could reach the state recorded in the sword scripture, where qi surges to the stars and a thousand streams flow together, then this sword scripture would be considered great success. If he were to release it all at once, even an Ascendant great power might suffer a loss if caught off guard.

"It's a pity that I don't primarily cultivate the sword path and cannot comprehend the sword intent. Otherwise, combining sword intent with Red Sky Sword Qi, a thousand streams flowing together, sword transforming into myriad calamities—that would be a true fifth-rank sword scripture."

Luo Chen sighed with emotion and stood up leisurely.

He wasn't going to stay any longer.

Seven wisps of Red Sky Qi were enough for him to get started on the *Thousand Heavens Myriad Transformations Sword Scripture*.

If he wanted to continue cultivating this method in the future, he could use the baleful qi recorded in the sword scripture to replace the sky qi. The quality might be slightly inferior, but as long as the quantity made up for it, the power would still be extraordinary!

The real problem was that collecting sky qi took too much time and energy.

But before leaving, he subconsciously glanced at the starry sky once more.

This time, he didn't lose his mind, but his thoughts were myriad, his imagination running wild.

"Beyond the Mountains and Seas Realm lies the boundless universe. Where is the so-called Immortal Realm?"

"What exactly is this thing called 'ascension'?"

"Back when the Alchemy Saint left, she abandoned a realm's spiritual mechanism to lift her, taking the Star Gate path. Did she go to the Immortal Realm, or to another world?"

"If I were to forcibly break free from this realm's shackles and step into the universe right now, what would happen?"

When this last thought arose, Luo Chen felt a stirring impulse.

But in an instant, he suppressed this improper notion.

According to ancient tradition, only an Ascendant could ascend. This was definitely not an unfounded rumor.

He was merely at the third layer of the Nascent Soul stage. If he truly stepped into the endless starry sky, he would only be seeking his own death!

"Forget it. Since I can't go out, I'll just take a look around inside and head towards the Eastern Wilderness at the same time."

Luo Chen cut off his train of thought, flapped his wings in the azure firmament, and strode forward along the direction of the Eastern Wilderness.

This kind of travel wouldn't consume a large amount of mana. Apart from the Broken Moon Wings, Luo Chen's powerful physical body could also provide explosive speed.

The Sixfold Imperial Flying Cloud Carriage had been moving forward for almost half a year. If Luo Chen wanted to catch up, he would have to travel this fast.

Traveling at full speed in the azure firmament without worrying about others was also a convenience.

...

Crack!

Crackle!

Boom!

In the azure firmament, in a certain void, a large amount of thunder and lightning had inexplicably gathered, forming what seemed like a thunder domain.

A broad-shouldered, thick-waisted figure with long arms and long legs lay flat within it, allowing wisps of thunder and lightning to strike down, passing through a large white net and pouring onto his body.

With every bolt of lightning that touched his body, the figure would tremble slightly, a trace of pain appearing on his handsome and majestic face.

A pitch-black great sword stood beside him, also undergoing the baptism of thunder and lightning.

Suddenly!

The great sword hummed!

The figure slowly rose, standing upright in the void, his eyes snapping open.

Crackle...

Two arcs of electricity bloomed from his eyes.

"Fellow Daoist, you have been watching from the side for a long time. What is your intention?"

From outside the thunder domain, a clear voice came.

"My apologies. I was just passing by. I have no intention of disturbing your cultivation, sir."

The voice grew fainter and fainter, showing how quickly the observer was leaving.

The man standing in the thunder domain frowned.

To be able to fly so fast in this azure firmament, he was certainly no ordinary person!

"I am Thunder Daozi of the Nether Origin Sect. May I ask your esteemed name, sir?"

"A mere wanderer, not worth mentioning."

"Hmph!"

Thunder Daozi snorted lightly, seemingly displeased with the man's secretive and evasive manner.

Several thousand li away, Luo Chen, who had slowed down slightly, put away the restless Primordial Unity Cauldron and breathed a sigh of relief.

Throughout his journey, he had kept the Primordial Unity Cauldron outside, carrying it with him for convenience, ready to collect any sky qi he might encounter along the way.

When he was ten thousand li away from that thunder domain, the Primordial Unity Cauldron had reacted.

Luo Chen had thought the treasure had sensed new Red Sky Qi, so he had specifically gone to investigate.

Instead, he had encountered a powerful cultivator cultivating in the azure firmament of the Nine Heavens.

A late-stage Nascent Soul grand cultivator!

Thunder Daozi!

Zhang Che!

Legend had it that he was the most exceptional genius of the Nether Origin Sect since ancient times. He achieved Initiation in ten years, formed his core in a hundred, and formed his Nascent Soul at the age of two hundred and fifty. After that, his progress was incredibly fast. Not only did he enter the late Nascent Soul stage at the age of five hundred, but he was also rumored to have comprehended the laws of power ahead of time.

His combat prowess, displayed in the few times he had acted against the demon beasts charging out of the Eastern Wilderness's Myriad Mountains, was fully and vividly demonstrated.

He even used the name Thunder Daozi to cross two continents, overshadowing the two famous Ascendant seeds of the Central Continent Dao Sect, Gu Daoyin and Tan Linxian of the Tianyuan Dao Sect!

Along with the Chess Saint, Ke Lianshan, he was hailed as one of the two people closest to the Ascendant realm in the current Mountains and Seas Realm!

"I didn't expect to encounter a Nether Origin Sect cultivator before even arriving at the Eastern Wilderness."

"Is he cultivating in the azure firmament to learn the Nether Origin Sect's great divine ability, the Nine Heavens Divine Thunder Art?"

Luo Chen pursed his lips, and his figure plummeted sharply.

Like a meteor, he left the azure firmament of the Nine Heavens and fell straight down to the mortal world.
