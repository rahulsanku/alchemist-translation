# Chapter 684: Spirit-Root Pill Formula, Hidden Malice Within

Was this the assessment of the True Lord's inheritance?

After Luo Chen finished reading the question left in the jade slip, no, it should be called Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's conjecture, stormy waves rose in his heart.

For "mature" immortal cultivators like them, it was already unshakable common sense that those without spirit roots could not cultivate, and only those with spirit roots, even the poorest five-spirit-root cultivators, could step onto the great Dao of cultivation.

Yet now, someone claimed that by borrowing the power of pills, one could open the divide between immortal and mortal and help those without spirit roots absorb heaven and earth spiritual qi. How could Luo Chen not be shocked by something that violated common sense so thoroughly?

"This is far too unimaginable."

Shaking his head, Luo Chen was about to put down the jade slip.

At this moment, a profound and mysterious feeling surfaced in his mind.

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly. "If I cannot refine this pill, I cannot pass the first assessment?"

This was the information transmitted from the jade slip.

For a time, Luo Chen's outstretched hand froze in midair.

He had to complete this conjecture and refine the corresponding pill before he could pass the assessment.

And this was only the first trial?

Luo Chen slowly set down the jade slip. His gaze swept across the study, across the shelves filled with bamboo slips, and across the books that recorded countless detailed medicinal-material knowledge. These were the auxiliary teaching materials Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had left for those being tested.

Together with the Herb Room and Pill Room beside it, all of this could be considered the examination ground.

It was certainly prepared thoroughly.

But could that kind of pill truly be refined?

He slowly withdrew from the study and arrived outside the stone house. Raising his eyes, he gazed into the distance. Green waters and verdant mountains still filled his sight.

Then he closed his eyes and focused completely.

The next moment, his spiritual sense suddenly burst forth!

It no longer radiated in a circle with him as the center, but gathered into a beam, released outward in the most energy-saving way while reaching the greatest possible distance.

Luo Chen's current realm was Golden Core Stage Six. His soul foundation was quite strong, no weaker than any Golden Core Stage Eight cultivator.

Even compared to Golden Core Stage Nine, he was only one step away.

Therefore, the distance his spiritual sense could reach far exceeded ordinary Golden Core cultivators.

Now that he condensed his mind into a beam, its outward reach arrived at the absolute limit.

Nine hundred li!

It circled around green mountains, crossed great lakes, and moved all the way north until it reached the very limit.

Vaguely, an invisible blocking force stood in the way of Luo Chen going any further.

Change direction!

...

After several attempts, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes. His fair, jade-like face shifted between light and dark.

Before, he had not reached the limit of this world, so he had not known there was a boundary.

Now, prompted by the jade slip, he suddenly discovered that this world was like a huge prison, firmly locking him inside.

"Do I truly have to try..."

"Forget it. Since I have come, I might as well settle here!"

In only an instant, Luo Chen adjusted his mindset.

Or perhaps, after seeing the jade slip, he had already prepared himself in this regard.

However, another problem now lay before him.

Where was the pill formula?

The jade slip had only given a conjecture.

It had no specific pill formula at all!

Although Luo Chen's alchemy attainment had reached the point of great mastery, along his path he had mostly followed maps to find horses, refining medicine according to existing formulas.

Even when he occasionally created pill formulas himself, it had been built upon incomplete formulas or improvements to ready-made ones.

Without a pill formula, he did not even have the chance to forcibly enter the door with the system.

For a time, Luo Chen fell into confusion.

His tall figure slowly paced in the courtyard.

Only after a long while did Luo Chen laugh softly.

"Yes. I have always called myself an alchemy grandmaster, but what sort of grandmaster only picks up what others have chewed?"

What was a grandmaster?

Broad learning, extensive knowledge, accepting the strengths of a hundred schools, bringing forth the new from the old, and establishing one's own door.

One might even create theory from nothing and possess something of one's own influence.

From then on, opening a sect and establishing a school, only then could one be called a grandmaster of a generation!

Luo Chen asked himself honestly. In alchemy, he could barely reach the first four points.

But in creating theory, and even in independently developing pill formulas, he had made no achievements at all.

Forget high-level pill formulas. He had never even created a low-level first- or second-tier formula.

In that case, even if his alchemist level reached fourth tier, he could only be called a master-level figure, far from a true grandmaster.

Clearly, this first trial was not only extremely difficult, it also required one to create a pill formula from nothing.

"I wonder whether I can withstand it?"

Luo Chen laughed at himself, then walked freely toward the study.

The answer should lie in those bamboo slips that recorded countless medicinal materials.

The first row of bookshelves, first layer, third scroll.

"Withered Chen Knows White: also known as Yellow-Head Crow..."

The bamboo slip entered his hand. Luo Chen, who had already emptied his thoughts, sank all his mind into it.

Within the Pill Realm, the four seasons cycled, and the sun rose while the moon floated.

At early night, pale moonlight came through the window and fell upon the silent figure before the bookshelves.

In the quiet study, only the crisp sound of bamboo slips being turned could be heard.

...

Inside the Pill Realm study, Luo Chen had become completely immersed in the ocean of medicinal-material knowledge.

He liked reading books.

This habit had not merely been formed when his realm was low.

Even before transmigrating, he had liked reading.

When young, he read all kinds of extracurricular books. After growing up, he became absorbed in classics and famous works. Even later, after becoming a working stiff, he never stopped reading various novels.

It could even be said that some simple, refreshing web novels had become a harbor where he escaped the frustrations of life.

After transmigrating, this habit remained. Even during the busiest periods of cultivation, he would always set aside some time to read miscellaneous records of the cultivation world.

As his realm rose, especially after successfully establishing his foundation, the cleansing of marrow and improvement of soul made his reading habit advance even further.

He had nearly gained the ability to read ten lines at a glance and remember what he saw.

Especially with the enhancement of spiritual sense, his memory became much better. Many books only needed to be read once or twice before he could deeply imprint them in his mind and apply them flexibly.

One had to admit,

This habit had helped him many times.

Whether in daily life and cultivation or while traveling and fighting outside, the knowledge in his mind would become assistance in another form.

Just like this exploration of Demon-Fall Land. His experience and knowledge had completely surpassed Moya Cave Master, who was older than him.

If Tai Sui had not been born and grown in this place, perhaps Tai Sui's performance would have been inferior to Luo Chen's as well.

At this moment,

In this study, the vast amount of knowledge on herbs and medicines surged into Luo Chen's mind like a tide.

Not only did he not feel dizzy or swollen-headed, he found it sweet as syrup.

Sometimes, after finishing a bamboo slip, he even felt unsatisfied and took it out to read one or two more times.

At times he marveled at the heaven-defying medicinal nature of some unfamiliar material. At times he sighed over the exquisite method of transplanting a certain spirit herb. At times he had sudden fancies, wondering whether substituting certain medicinal materials into the pill formulas he usually refined might produce better effects.

Time slowly flowed.

The sun rose and the moon set, and the years became unknown.

Ten bookshelves, over ten thousand bamboo slips, and hundreds of thousands, perhaps even over a million medicinal materials took Luo Chen more than half a year to finish reading.

When he had thoroughly finished, Luo Chen still felt somewhat unsatisfied.

But he remembered that he had not come here only to read.

Under the impact of countless medicinal-material characteristics in his mind, inspiration had erupted several times over the past half year.

"This one, and this one. These few also work..."

Luo Chen wandered among the bookshelves and took out seven bamboo slips as though counting his own treasures.

He brought them to the desk and studied them again.

One day later, Luo Chen put four slips back.

This action looked somewhat strange.

But Luo Chen knew what he was doing.

How did a mature pill formula come about?

First was the question of why this pill needed to be refined. This question had already been solved. Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had proposed a conjecture, to break the divide between immortal and mortal.

Second was what should be used to refine this pill. Luo Chen had already summarized this question. He needed to extract the main material.

When refining pills, there had to be main and secondary materials. The main material, in particular, determined the direction of the pill's medicinal power and could not be treated carelessly.

Only after that came the auxiliary materials.

As for a more mature pill formula?

That was not the business of the originator.

The evolution of time, the changes of environment, and later people's needs for the pill would all cause the original formula to be continuously optimized and reformed.

Right now, Luo Chen was stuck at the second step.

Choosing the main material.

"Above is Yellow Court, below is Guanyuan. Behind lies the Nether Gate, before lies the Gate of Life. Breath enters the dantian from between the jaws, and clear water from the Jade Pool irrigates the spirit root. If one can cultivate it repeatedly, one may long endure!"

"The so-called spirit root is the root of the tongue!"

"The upper spirit root lies at the root of the tongue. Apart from this, the middle spirit root lies in the dantian, which is why those born with petrified dantians cannot cultivate. The lower spirit root lies in the essence chamber or womb. This is also why, before Foundation Establishment, a cultivator's primordial yin or primordial yang must not be spilled, all to preserve the flourishing spirit root."

These theories originated from when Luo Chen established his foundation.

After he established his foundation, he felt them even more deeply.

Especially in the early years when Luo Tian Sect recruited disciples, it also inspected their spirit roots.

Luo Chen had faintly sensed those formations before. Most guided heaven and earth spiritual qi of various attributes to stimulate the three positions on a cultivator's body: the root of the tongue, the dantian, and the essence chamber or womb.

Therefore, the vague and untraceable spirit root was actually traceable.

After understanding the essence of spirit roots, making someone produce spirit roots from nothing also became something traceable.

Strengthen those three places, allowing them to become more compatible with heaven and earth spiritual qi.

Not only that, the most important thing was to provide mortals without spirit roots with an object that could absorb heaven and earth spiritual qi.

The Spirit-Root Pill was precisely this.

The pill Luo Chen intended to refine was named Spirit-Root Pill, plain and easy to understand.

In his view, not having something meant not having it. Even after taking a pill, it was impossible to produce something from nothing.

Therefore, this Spirit-Root Pill should not be refined after consumption. Instead, it should be entrusted long-term within a mortal's body and soul, helping them continuously sense heaven and earth spiritual qi.

The Spirit-Root Pill was even more a pill spirit root.

In this way, the selection of the main material had theoretical support.

It needed to have the medicinal property of automatically sensing heaven and earth spiritual qi.

"Demon beast inner cores, Ancestor Origin Grass, and Heaven-Earth Root. All three possess this ability."

Looking at the bamboo slips recording these three materials, Luo Chen fell into hesitation.

Suddenly,

"Hm? The material of this bamboo slip?"

Luo Chen picked up the bamboo slip recording demon beast inner cores in surprise. His magic power surged, and true fire burned across it.

Withered yellow light flashed upon it, and finally it turned a pale scorched yellow, faintly seeming to have sea qi lingering around it.

Seeing this scene, Luo Chen's expression changed slightly.

"This bamboo slip is not the same kind as those on the shelves!"

He had originally felt that demon beast inner cores were suitable to serve as the main material for the Spirit-Root Pill.

This inspiration came from those Foundation Establishment cultivators who failed to form cores and used demon beast inner cores to condense false cores. Such people were called false-core cultivators.

Moreover, demon beast inner cores could be used as medicine. It was only natural for such information to be recorded on a bamboo slip.

Although there were no demon beasts inside the Pill Realm, Luo Chen had no small number of demon cores in his storage bag.

If he truly needed to refine the Spirit-Root Pill with them, he had enough.

But now, it seemed this bamboo slip had clearly been deliberately left in the study by someone else to mislead later arrivals.

It was only because Luo Chen had previously experienced the Hundred Craft Mountain Lord incident and had become more guarded against others forging letters and bamboo slips that he noticed the abnormality of this slip.

Otherwise, he truly might not have discovered anything strange about this bamboo slip.

"Who the hell is this lacking in virtue?"

Luo Chen's face darkened as he cursed, without the slightest bearing to speak of.

No wonder he was so angry. If he truly followed the direction of using demon cores as the main material to study the Spirit-Root Pill formula, leaving aside whether that path could work, it absolutely would not be within Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's assessment.

Even worse, after taking the wrong road, who knew how much time he would waste?

"Was it that predecessor?"

Luo Chen murmured. Azure flame flickered in his hand and burned the slip to ash.

He hated these forgers the most!

Afterward, he adjusted his mood and looked at the other two bamboo slips.

Ancestor Origin Grass and Heaven-Earth Root.

The latter did not need mentioning. Luo Chen was quite familiar with it and considered it very suitable as the main material for the Spirit-Root Pill.

As for Ancestor Origin Grass, it was a medicinal material Luo Chen had not known before.

It grew in places where spiritual qi was chaotic, and there were even rumors that it could absorb heaven and earth origin qi!

Luo Chen had once occasionally heard Han Zhan discuss the difference between spiritual qi and origin qi. It was said that after Ascendant powers touched the power of laws, they could use that law power to absorb origin qi from the void for cultivation.

It was hard to say which of the two was mightier, but spiritual qi was not always present, while origin qi was everywhere.

Therefore, the problem before Ascendant where cultivator battles were often affected by the advantage of time and place would be smoothed over after advancing to Ascendant, unless the battlefield environment reached an extreme.

"This Ancestor Origin Grass is a bit too high-end."

Luo Chen murmured.

In his view, the Spirit-Root Pill was meant for mortals without any Dao cultivation to use, so its rank certainly could not be too high.

Ancestor Origin Grass exceeded the standard!

Heaven-Earth Root was more suitable.

This medicinal material, Heaven-Earth Root, could help Qi Refinement cultivators establish foundation at low age, and at high age could assist Golden Core cultivators in breaking through to the Sage realm.

It could be considered a medicinal material without a fixed grade, yet with extremely strong growth potential and perhaps even no upper limit.

In the end, after screening, Luo Chen confirmed the main material of the Spirit-Root Pill.

Heaven-Earth Root.

"In the unseen, is there perhaps heaven's will?"

As Luo Chen murmured, memories surfaced.

He had heard of Heaven-Earth Root during Qi Refinement, obtained Heaven-Earth Root after killing the Duan family ancestor, and even after coming to the Pill Realm, the first spirit herb he personally gathered had been Heaven-Earth Root.

Now, the main material confirmed for the Spirit-Root Pill was also this thing.

Curling his lip, Luo Chen took brush, ink, paper, and inkstone from his storage ring.

He bent over the desk and began writing.

"Heaven-Earth Root possesses pure medicinal nature, but its attributes are unbearably mixed. For mortals without spirit roots, this means opening wide the gate of myriad wonders... This pill will remain long-term in the body. Therefore, the auxiliary materials should primarily weaken the medicinal force of Heaven-Earth Root while also drawing out its characteristic of absorbing heaven and earth spiritual qi. Nightwing Flower and Purple Lord Leaf should be first choices. At the same time, Wood Brocade Cotton, which has the effect of guiding qi, may serve as a backup..."

This bout of writing took Luo Chen a full seven days.

The pill formula he wrote was complex and huge, and the dosage was vague. Strictly speaking, it could not be considered qualified.

But at least it had a prototype.

Seven days later, Luo Chen left the study.

This was also the first time he had stepped out of the room in more than half a year.

Blinding sunlight fell. Luo Chen, dressed in black, softly exhaled a turbid breath. Then he carried a bamboo basket on his back, took up a medicinal hoe, and flew into the three mountains and five lakes.

Next would be gathering herbs, processing medicinal materials, and then trying to refine the Spirit-Root Pill.

In his view, the progress was fairly quick.

In only half a year, he had gone from nothing to producing the prototype of a Spirit-Root Pill formula.

After this, by experimenting and comparing bit by bit, screening and refining, he would always approach success.

...

At the same time, in another place within the Pill Realm.

Inside a stone house of the same style, an old woman wearing a large flower-green dress, her hair coiled into a high Immortal-Watching bun, slowly raised her head from before the desk.

Her face was very ordinary, but her eyes were very unusual.

Her brows and eyes were wide, the pupils long and narrow, and her eyes formed five-pointed stars.

At this moment, those aged five-star eyes showed slight surprise.

"The bamboo slip I left was destroyed. Has someone else also chosen this path?"

After the surprise, the old woman's expression continuously changed.

Someone able to see through the bamboo slip she left behind could not have small ability.

Such an existence, even if only at the first trial now, would sooner or later catch up.

"Perhaps I must speed up. I cannot continue studying steadily like this."

*(End of Chapter)*
