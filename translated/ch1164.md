# Chapter 1164: God-Devil Formless, Chi Mo Falls

In the quiet study.

Luo Chen bent over his desk, writing furiously, sheets of paper covered in text piled up like a small mountain.

Occasionally glancing at the words whose ink had already dried, Luo Chen's eyes were frighteningly bright.

He had already traveled far on the path of body refinement.

At the very beginning, it was the orthodox human body refinement method taught by Wang Yuan.

Due to accidentally entering the Ascendant cave dwelling in the Accumulated Thunder Nine Mountains, he had no choice but to draw upon the technique *Heavenly Roc Transformation*, modifying and creating the *Myriad Path Convergence* that suited him.

Although when he first created this technique, his ambition was immense, with the intention of encompassing various schools and traditions.

At its root, however, *Myriad Path Convergence* was a method of body refinement for archaic beasts, leaning more toward the demon race's side.

Therefore, the realm he later cultivated was the fifth rank of the Archaic stage.

Now that he had arrived in the Great Destruction Realm and come into contact with the complete martial system, he had cultivated the Leakless Golden Body through *Divine Astral Fighting Qi*.

After some sorting, Luo Chen's body refinement methods became very clear and distinct.

They were divided into two parts!

The first part was the human body refinement method, which focused on the transformation of blood and qi. Although there were also Buddhist body refinement divine abilities like *Three Heads Six Arms* and heterodox body refinement arts like *Hundred Poisons Body Refinement Art*, these could still be considered human body refinement methods.

The second part was the archaic beast body refinement method, which focused on pure physical flesh. The endpoint of this path could be imagined. According to legend, those powerful true spirits in the Xuan Realm that rivaled Great Vehicle stage cultivators were the future. Luo Chen had indeed once condensed a drop of true spirit origin blood, but unfortunately, it had come to an abrupt halt.

And now, the *Demon Form Art* that Luo Chen had placed great hopes in was a genuine demon race body refinement method!

If he could truly modify it to a degree that suited him, then Luo Chen might become an extremely rare special existence that simultaneously cultivated the three lineages of "Human, Archaic, Demon"!

He just didn't know what changes would occur at that time.

Luo Chen did not want uncontrollable changes to happen, so after sorting and summarizing, he had to grasp the variables of the future as much as possible.

The deduced technique must also advance along a growth path beneficial to him.

This workload would be enormous.

It not only required Luo Chen to expend a tremendous amount of energy but also needed enough high-level technique scriptures as reference.

Only then could he learn from others' strengths to compensate for his weaknesses.

The initial research was still manageable, but as time passed day by day, Luo Chen increasingly felt the strain.

His accumulated foundation in martial arts over the past was undeniably very deep.

But when it came to the Leakless Realm, the subsequent Human Immortal Realm, and even the Destruction Realm, it seemed quite insufficient.

Under these circumstances, the proportion of *Demon Form Art* became very high.

If it were the Luo Chen of the past, when faced with a set of high-level techniques, even if he was cautious, he would still try to cultivate them after attempting.

Or rather, most people in the world would do the same.

But ever since experiencing the matter of *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* and Rosycloud, Luo Chen's wariness had reached its maximum!

Who knew whether *Demon Form Art* was also a "bait" released by some great existence within the demon race?

What if he ate his fill, only to be hooked by someone in the end?

If so, wouldn't that be repeating the same mistake!

With this consideration, Luo Chen's process of deducing the technique became increasingly meticulous.

Thus, three years passed in the blink of an eye!

During these three years, Luo Chen never left his house, like an old scholar studying literature, immersed day and night in the mountain of books and sea of techniques.

Even when he occasionally cultivated, it was mainly to consolidate the Leakless realm.

This situation caused Xiangfei, who attended to Luo Chen's daily needs, to marvel.

She had learned about Luo Chen's experiences in the Remnant Immortal City.

From the down-and-out cultivator when he first appeared to the powerful Leakless realm martial artist, the time spent was less than a hundred years.

Such a cultivation speed could be fully described as advancing by leaps and bounds.

But Luo Chen, who was aggressively forging ahead, had instead actively slowed down his cultivation speed after breaking through to the Leakless realm.

How could this be explained?

Could it be, as Luo Chen occasionally said when chatting with her, that because he couldn't trust others' techniques, he chose to create his own?

But this was too cautious and too time-consuming.

In this world, there were geniuses.

Not many in number, but martial arts geniuses would always appear from time to time.

People like them who could cultivate the Leakless Golden Body could all be considered such geniuses.

But martial arts geniuses who could create their own techniques were extremely rare!

Perhaps some could cultivate a thousand li in a day, but if they stopped in their tracks to delve into creating their own martial arts, they might stop for a lifetime.

Even if they truly created a powerful martial art that was unfathomable, they might not have time to cultivate it due to lifespan factors, ultimately failing to break through to the next realm and dying with regret.

Such examples had always existed!

Luo Chen's behavior was overly confident, even arrogant.

Three years, and no results.

Was he going to drag it on like this until he entered the Nine-Tiered Tower more than ten years later, still without a technique suitable for him?

Xiangfei saw this and was anxious in her heart.

But what she didn't know was that this kind of worry simply didn't exist for Luo Chen!

...

In the quiet chamber.

Luo Chen sat in a peculiar posture.

His legs were crossed, like a Buddhist monk sitting in lotus position.

His left and right hands were clasped together, resting flat on his hip bones, with the two thumbs touching each other—this was the Buddhist Samadhi Concentration Seal!

Another pair of hands extended from his ribs, one pointing to the sky, the other to the earth, full of the overbearing charm of "I alone am honored"!

And in the middle, there was a pair of hands forming a special hand seal.

It was called special because the hand seal formed by the middle pair of hands was constantly changing, sometimes closing, sometimes separating, with profound mysteries flowing between the fingertips.

After dozens of breaths, one could faintly discern that the hand seal formed by the middle pair of hands actually cycled through a total of nine seals.

And if some Taoist cultivators well-versed in Taoist true words saw those nine hand seals, they would inevitably feel a sense of deja vu.

Those were clearly the hand seal forms of the Taoist Nine True Words "Proceed, Soldier, Fight, Person, All, Array, Rank, Forward, March"!

But when placed on Luo Chen, there were additional changes.

The impact brought by these changes was very significant!

Inside Luo Chen's body, the blood and qi furnace rumbled and spun, streams of blood-refined divine astral qi continuously scoured in a great cycle, and in those tightly closed acupoints, there were clusters of astral qi moving and rippling with vitality.

Especially the last point, which far exceeded the imagination of ordinary martial artists!

The astral qi in the acupoints was merely stored to control the opening and closing of the acupoints; how could it be described as lively?

But unexpectedly, in one or two of Luo Chen's seven hundred and twenty acupoints, they truly moved freely like living beings.

And if observed carefully, one could clearly see a faint golden light blooming within the blood-colored astral qi.

After a long time.

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

His gaze was filled with exhaustion, but it couldn't hide a trace of surprise.

"It's done!"

"This method works!"

"After three years, exhausting my foundation, and investing more than thirty achievement points, I have finally deduced the most perfect and most suitable Leakless realm cultivation method for myself!"

Yes!

Luo Chen's foundation was ultimately insufficient; he couldn't bite off more than he could chew and deduce a high-level technique that directly reached the sixth or seventh realm.

But he chose a compromise.

He first deduced a stage-by-stage technique for breaking through from the fourth realm Leakless to the fifth realm Human Immortal.

Using *Divine Astral Fighting Qi* to temper blood and qi as the main focus, and the divine-intent transformation method of *Demon Form Art* as a supplement, he performed the work of bestowing spirit upon the astral qi beyond absorption and transformation.

Once this method was completed, it not only solved Luo Chen's current cultivation needs but, most importantly, resolved his predicament of having a vast divine sense that he couldn't use.

When operating this technique, Luo Chen could split his soul bit by bit and transfer it into his acupoints.

Then, it would gradually merge with the long-lasting astral qi within.

In this way, the massive soul could be broken into parts, using astral qi as a carrier to move freely. It would also not attract the attack of the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound due to the overall strength.

This clever idea was inspired by an accidental encounter with Chi Mo three years ago.

Chi Mo used top-grade spirit stones to cultivate. Although he tried his best not to let his divine sense leave his body, he was ultimately using his divine sense.

Logically, in this situation, he would attract the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound in the Guixu Extreme Heaven.

But Chi Mo did not experience this situation!

This was completely different from Luo Chen's experience in Red Leaf Valley, where he released a wisp of divine sense to probe into his Purple Mansion, being extremely careful yet still attracting the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound.

After returning, Luo Chen had a guess.

Perhaps it was the difference in the "size" of the soul essence between the two that led to different situations.

With this guess, Luo Chen thought about how to achieve a similar effect.

And *Demon Form Art* itself had a method of divine-intent transformation.

Drawing on this method, he operated *Soul-Splitting Art*, cutting off a small portion of his soul and transferring it into the astral qi of his acupoints.

In this way, he could also achieve the effect of breaking the whole into parts.

As he expected, it never once triggered the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound!

And the benefit of doing this was not only avoiding the potential danger brought by a massive soul but also bestowing spirit upon the originally rigid astral qi!

If he ultimately cultivated to the great completion stage, he might even be able to nurture astral qi full of spirituality within his acupoints.

At that time, it could perhaps be called "Martial Dao Spirits"!

As long as the number of these Martial Dao Spirits was sufficient, he could break through to the Human Immortal realm!

As for whether this technique was feasible?

The fact that Luo Chen could cultivate normally was proof enough.

Moreover, on the attribute panel, a new entry had replaced the original *Divine Astral Fighting Qi*, emitting a faint glow.

With a thought, Luo Chen named it.

"Let's call it God-Devil Formless!"

When *Demon Form Art* was cultivated to a high level, one could condense a demon form outside the body, which could attack, defend, and even have the divine ability to substitute for death and survive tribulation.

But Luo Chen did not believe that that "demon form" was truly his own dharma form!

What if it was a trigger to attract some great existence of the demon race? That would be dangerous.

Therefore, he would rather have God-Devil Formless!

If there was to be a "dharma form," it must be a dharma form that belonged solely to him!

And the change began with laying down his own imprint on the most basic astral qi!

Amidst his joy, Luo Chen glanced at the attribute panel.

The number of achievement points on it was not much, only [165] points remaining.

Originally, there were a total of two hundred points, accumulated from 170 to 200 achievement points during his time in the Remnant Immortal City, practicing techniques and various martial skills, relying on breaking through proficiency levels.

But in these years of deducing *God-Devil Formless*, he had consumed thirty-five points in one go.

And this was only the consumption for deducing a technique within the current realm.

If he wanted to deduce techniques that crossed realms, the consumption would inevitably be higher!

"To deduce better techniques in the future, I need to find a way to accumulate more achievement points."

Luo Chen murmured to himself.

Then, he dispersed his six-armed true body and left the cultivation chamber in his normal human form.

...

After lunch, Luo Chen took the initiative to propose going out for a walk and strolling around Worryfree City.

Xiangfei was momentarily surprised.

This was inconsistent with Luo Chen's reclusive behavior in recent years.

But in an instant, she realized what Luo Chen's change was due to.

"Senior, you succeeded?"

Luo Chen nodded, then shook his head.

Xiangfei was puzzled.

Luo Chen smiled and said, "A stage-by-stage success, but as for the great completion, there is still a long way to go."

Although he said this, Xiangfei couldn't help but feel happy for Luo Chen.

"Then let's go out and relax!"

The two then left Yuelong Lane and walked toward the bustling streets of Worryfree City.

As the foremost of the seventy-two cities, Worryfree City naturally had its own customs and atmosphere.

Unlike the strong commercial atmosphere of Remnant Immortal City and the bitter cold and ruggedness of Mofeng City, Worryfree City gathered the essence of the seventy-two cities. Various resources were collected in various shops, visible everywhere.

An ordinary person would be dazzled after a short stroll.

Even Luo Chen, who had seen much and knew much, discovered many peculiar materials.

Special minerals, weapons that could accommodate blood and qi, medicinal herbs with extreme properties, and... useless magic treasures.

In a Treasure Gathering Pavilion, Luo Chen even saw an existence suspected to be a spirit treasure.

However, its artifact spirit had long dissipated, leaving only the spirit treasure's main body.

Such things could only make Luo Chen feel regret.

Of course, it also made him vigilant not to use the Yuantu Sword carelessly; otherwise, if the artifact spirit was damaged, the powerful Yuantu Sword would only become a relatively sharp true artifact.

Just as Luo Chen was strolling around, his expression suddenly moved, and he looked up into the distance.

That was the direction of Pingyang Lane!

"Senior, what's wrong?"

Luo Chen sighed, "Let's go take a look!"

Xiangfei didn't understand, but she followed behind Luo Chen toward Pingyang Lane. As they gradually approached, she faintly felt a pressure.

The surrounding air became stagnant, and an omnipresent gaze seemed to be watching her.

And in the sky, streaks of dark clouds were gathering.

"This is?"

"After all, it's an obsession!"

Luo Chen said this cryptically, then stopped his steps outside a quiet, secluded small building.

He didn't go in directly but looked up at the sky.

When the momentum inside the small building climbed to its peak, the dark clouds in the sky suddenly exploded.

Boom!

A bright flash of lightning illuminated the daylight.

Under the deafening thunder, that momentum vanished in an instant.

Because of this thunderclap, most of Worryfree City was alarmed, and many people looked this way.

Even soldiers of Worryfree City came in a group.

When they arrived, they saw that the door of the small building had already been pushed open.

In the main hall, a large number of crystals were scattered in disarray. A young Daoist, his face filled with terror, collapsed on the ground, dead with his eyes open.

And a man and a woman stood inside the hall, silently inspecting.

"It's you!"

Hearing a familiar voice, Luo Chen turned his head and nodded in greeting, "Brother Fang, long time no see."

"You've already broken through to the Leakless realm? When did you come to Worryfree City?" Fang Zhibai was somewhat stunned. His gaze swept around, finally landing on the young man who had lost his breath. "This is... he wasn't delusionally trying to break through a realm, was he?"

Luo Chen sighed and nodded gently.

The young man who had died tragically before him was Chi Mo, whom he had met once three years ago.

Having reached the Great Perfection of the Adept realm, he ultimately couldn't let go of his obsession with breaking through and took the risk.

And from the large number of top-grade spirit stones on the ground and the special traces of the formation, the opportunity Chi Mo had obtained back then was indeed very great.

If it had been placed in another place with spiritual qi, using this to break through to the Sage realm would have been a natural progression.

But this was the Great Destruction Realm, the Guixu!

A single Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound, directly striking the soul, was a blow that no Adept realm cultivator could withstand.

Fang Zhibai frowned, stepped forward, and observed the situation.

"Every so often, there are always cultivators who refuse to give up, trying to cultivate and break through, and the results are always like this. Brother Luo, does this person have any connection with you?"

Luo Chen replied calmly, "No."

Fang Zhibai nodded, then waved his hand outward. The accompanying soldiers rushed in and began to clean up the mess.

After doing all this, Fang Zhibai revealed a smile.

"Brother Luo, since you're here, why not let me play host and welcome you?"

"There's no need for a welcome banquet. I've been in Worryfree City for three years now." Luo Chen waved his hand, then changed the subject and asked directly, "I wonder if Brother Fang can lead the way and take me to see the legendary Nine-Tiered Tower?"

The Nine-Tiered Tower, the location of the Destruction Nine Rings!
