# Chapter 937: A Palm That Sweeps All, Buddha and Demon as One

The clouds were dark, the wind howled, angrily rolling waves of pine through the mountain valley.

The Pine Wave Mountain Range stretched for three thousand li in unbroken undulations.

It was the ancestral land of the Feng clan, a Sage family.

Countless towering pine trees stood tall within it. When a great wind swept through, the pines would surge like ocean waves.

On this day.

Amidst the myriad pine forests, another wave of sound arose.

Listening closely, it was not the wind striking the pines, but the rise and fall of countless streaks of escape light, soaring up from within the mountain woods.

A massive cloud boat hovered in the void, waiting silently.

A moment later, three figures appeared together.

Without hesitation, tens of thousands of cultivators bowed to the leader among the three.

"Greetings, Supreme Elder!"

Feng Buyan stroked his beard, looking upon this scene with satisfaction.

"Fellow Daoist Ning, Fellow Daoist Yang, how do you find my Feng clan's children?"

An old man with a goatee praised, "High morale, full of confidence, bearing a natural pride. Brother Feng, you have indeed managed the Feng clan quite well!"

Feng Buyan laughed heartily. "The Great Dao is arduous, difficult to advance even an inch. This Feng can only put his mind into nurturing the younger generation."

As he spoke, he glanced at another man with a shaved head.

Dressed in a white monk's robe, his figure was lean, but his features were exceptionally handsome.

If not for the scars on his scalp, he would surely be considered a rare beauty in the mortal world.

At this moment, the other man's eyelids were lowered, paying no heed to the spectacular sight of tens of thousands of cultivators riding clouds and mists, as if it could not arouse the slightest interest in him.

Feng Buyan frowned slightly, but did not seek his praise. Instead, he waved his hand.

"Children, set out!"

"Target, Wind Thunder Gorge!"

With his command, the thousands of Feng clan disciples answered in unison.

"Yes!"

The vast sound actually suppressed the pine forests covering the mountains and plains.

One cloud boat after another started up quickly, streaks of escaping light echoing each other, rapidly approaching the not-too-distant Wind Thunder Gorge.

On the largest cloud boat at the center, Feng Buyan was introducing the general situation of Wind Thunder Gorge to the two helpers he had invited.

"This place was originally my Feng clan's territory. However, due to its dangerous terrain and lack of special resources, my clan did not bother to manage it much."

"About several hundred years ago, some desperadoes hid within, and gradually grew into a force."

"That brat Ming Lei is one of them."

"His cultivation is not high, merely a newly advanced Sage. At that time, the three of us will strike together and capture him in an instant!"

Yang Daoren pinched his goatee, puzzled. "According to what Brother Feng says, he is merely an early-stage Sage. Why do we need the three of us to strike together?"

Feng Buyan smiled. "The Ming Lei Cave Master is not worth fearing, but Wind Thunder Gorge is extremely treacherous. Moreover, its special terrain subtly resonates with the Ming Lei Cave Master. If we fight him inside, even I, at the fourth layer of the Sage realm, could easily encounter accidents. Furthermore..."

Speaking here, he revealed a cautious expression.

"After he formed his Nascent Soul, he went out on a journey. According to reports, he befriended many experts. He even participated in a gathering of experts from the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance. I fear he might have invited some troublesome characters, adding variables to this trip."

Upon hearing the name "Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance," Yang Daoren couldn't help but draw a sharp breath.

"Is that the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance that opposes the Life and Death Gate, constantly provoking them these years, and has gradually swallowed many of the Life and Death Gate's external territories?"

Feng Buyan nodded, his expression grave.

"Correct! It seems Brother Yang also has some understanding of this force that has mysteriously risen in the past three hundred years!"

Yang Daoren's expression shifted slightly. "How could I not understand? Rumors say there are countless experts within, with as many as two hands' worth of Sages like us. Especially those people, with their strange temperaments. If they don't strike, fine; but when they do, they go all out to kill. Truly difficult to deal with."

Southern Frontier cultivators were already known for their strange temperaments.

For a native like Yang Daoren to give such an evaluation showed just how twisted the experts of the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance truly were.

The white-robed monk, who had been silent all along, now showed some interest instead.

"This humble monk has been in the Southern Frontier for some time, but has not heard of this Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance. How powerful have they become? The Life and Death Gate is the only Ascendant sacred ground in the Southern Frontier. How dare the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance constantly provoke them? What gives them the confidence?"

Feng Buyan and Yang Daoren exchanged a glance, then spoke in turn.

"As for their confidence, we do not know either."

"But their actions have long exceeded the imagination of ordinary forces. You should know of the Southern Ocean, also called the Gu Ocean, the cradle of many rare Gu insects. And now, of the three safe entrances to the Southern Ocean, one is occupied by them. You must know that the three entrances to the Southern Ocean have always been controlled by the Life and Death Gate."

Upon hearing that the Southern Ocean entrance was controlled by the Fierce Mountain Corpse Alliance, the white-robed monk's face showed a hint of surprise.

As the three chatted, the Feng clan's main force drew closer and closer to Wind Thunder Gorge.

A distance of ten thousand li was extremely long for low-level cultivators.

But with the operation of many cloud boats, they arrived at the broad plain outside Wind Thunder Gorge in just half a day.

Boom!

With a rumbling sound, cloud boat after cloud boat landed on the plain, splashing up large amounts of dust.

Large drums were brought out, and as bare-chested strong men beat them, they emitted a sound full of fighting spirit.

Thump! Thump! Thump!

The war drums rumbled, penetrating into the treacherous Wind Thunder Gorge.

Even the most reclusive wandering cultivators were now awakened.

One figure after another silently walked out of their cave dwellings. After exchanging glances, they took out their own magical treasures and guarded various dangerous strongholds in Wind Thunder Gorge.

Regarding the Feng clan's coveting of Wind Thunder Gorge, the Ming Lei Cave Master had told them long ago.

"If you want to continue being poor wandering cultivators drifting everywhere with no place to call home, you are free to leave."

"But if you still want to continue cultivating here in Wind Thunder Gorge, free and unrestrained, not controlled by anyone, then you must do something for it."

"When a great enemy comes, if you still cower in your caves, regardless of the outcome, this Cave Master will not tolerate such people after the battle!"

This was a warning not to say it wasn't foretold.

Either contribute together, or get out.

Those who tried to sit back and enjoy the fruits would face a reckoning after the battle!

With the divine sense of a Sage, he could naturally sense the movements within Wind Thunder Gorge.

Feng Buyan shook his head. "They still don't want to come out and fight. They intend to defend Wind Thunder Gorge to the death."

The war drums still rumbled, but no one responded.

To take Wind Thunder Gorge, it seemed they would have to forcefully break in.

Feng Buyan glanced at his descendants, who, though full of fighting spirit, still had a hint of awe in their eyes. His heart was conflicted.

Forcing their way in would cost the lives of who knows how many Feng clan children!

Yang Daoren was also a lord of his own territory and saw his concern. He smiled and suggested, "Why don't the three of us strike together and force that Ming Lei Cave Master out? He is the common master of the thirty-six caves of Wind Thunder Gorge. If he is defeated, the remaining wandering cultivators are just a rabble. They probably won't put up a stubborn resistance. Your Feng clan's descendants will also suffer fewer losses."

Feng Buyan's eyes lit up.

"Excellent!"

Then, without waiting for the other white-robed monk to agree, he stepped forward.

"Let this old man strike first!"

After speaking, under the gaze of tens of thousands, he took out an extremely ornate feather fan, infused it with mana, and then swung it violently towards Wind Thunder Gorge.

Whoosh...

A huge tornado appeared out of thin air, charging into Wind Thunder Gorge with irresistible force.

Just then!

Crack!

A thunderbolt as thick as a water bucket descended abruptly, striking the tornado directly and shattering it in an instant.

Feng Buyan's divine sense spread out, instantly locking onto the one who had struck.

"Ming Lei Cave Master, you are also a formidable expert. Hiding your head and showing your tail like this damages the dignity of a Sage, does it not?"

From within Wind Thunder Gorge came the sound of rumbling thunder.

"Yes, you have dignity. You've come all the way to someone else's doorstep."

Feng Buyan laughed heartily. "Wind Thunder Gorge has been my Feng clan's territory since ancient times. You are merely magpies occupying a magpie's nest. Previously, considering that low-level wandering cultivators had a hard time, this old man tolerated it. But now that you, Fellow Daoist, have achieved the Sage realm, you should understand the principle that no one can sleep peacefully beside their bed. Leave voluntarily, and you and I can still maintain some face."

The Ming Lei Cave Master sneered. "Fine talk of 'since ancient times.' If you want Wind Thunder Gorge, then come in and take it!"

Feng Buyan's expression turned stern.

"Do you really think this old man dares not enter?"

As he spoke, his figure suddenly disappeared.

When he reappeared, he was already floating within Wind Thunder Gorge.

The feather fan swayed gently, and the bone-cutting, flesh-tearing blade-like winds scattered, forming a massive tornado that struck a certain place.

The Ming Lei Cave Master suddenly appeared, his chest and abdomen slightly swelling as he exhaled and spoke.

"Scatter!"

Thunder roared again, directly shattering the tornado.

Not only that, but streaks of lightning spread through the void, forming beams of light that rained down on Feng Buyan.

Feng Buyan snorted, his figure suddenly disappearing from where he stood. Those thunderbolts, like headless flies, landed on empty space.

"Too slow!"

But the next moment, a figure appeared behind him, a flying sword stabbing straight at his back.

Feng Buyan's expression changed slightly, and he quickly shifted his form again.

But the flying sword followed like a shadow, and not only that, the Ming Lei Cave Master's figure also closely pursued him.

With each chase and change, a large number of thunder and lightning flashed without ceasing.

"No, how can you keep up with this old man's escape speed?"

Between shock and anger, Feng Buyan found it hard to believe.

What he was most confident in was his escape technique.

The reason he dared to set his sights on Wind Thunder Gorge was also based on his confidence in being able to advance and retreat at will.

But if even his escape technique was seen through, then this Wind Thunder Gorge had completely become the other party's home ground.

The Ming Lei Cave Master said nothing, only closely followed him, continuously launching attacks and drawing down the thunderbolts of the sky.

From the outside, Wind Thunder Gorge now looked as if a heavenly tribulation had descended.

Although the attack power was not great, the frequency was astonishing.

Occasionally, when one hit landed, a large amount of silver-white electric light would spread over Feng Buyan's body.

Even if he quickly removed it with his mana, the numbing sensation gradually spreading through his body made his mana flow less and less smoothly.

If this continued, let alone being defeated, even whether he could preserve his life and retreat was uncertain.

Suddenly!

"Two Fellow Daoists, if you don't strike now, when will you?"

A sigh came from the void.

"A wandering cultivator, yet able to reach such a level, truly stunning. What a pity, he doesn't know the times. Today, he will fall here. What a pity, what a pity."

Yang Daoren appeared with emotion, pointing a wooden staff towards the Ming Lei Cave Master.

The staff flickered with faint green light, clearly extraordinary.

As it pointed out, it also carried a penetrating and fierce power.

The Ming Lei Cave Master's expression changed slightly, but he did not use his lightning escape technique to forcefully dodge. Instead, he watched helplessly as the wooden staff pointed towards him.

Seeing this, Yang Daoren couldn't help but feel a strange sensation in his heart.

"So bold, could he still have a backup?"

The next moment!

A sudden change occurred!

A cyan great hand, wreathed in wind and thunder, suddenly appeared in the void, blocking above the Ming Lei Cave Master's head.

Then, with an irresistible force, it grabbed directly at the sharp wooden staff.

It made as if to grasp!

"A helper?" Yang Daoren's doubts were answered, but this was not unexpected, as Feng Buyan had mentioned this possibility before coming.

He only revealed a cold smile.

His precious staff, after centuries of nurturing, had long been elevated to the true artifact level.

If countered with a magical treasure, it might be fine.

But to try to receive it with just a palm? That was suicide!

In his sight, the wooden staff and that great hand finally made contact.

Dong!

A strange sound was emitted, and ripples spread out.

Yang Daoren's heart trembled. "No!"

He tried to withdraw his precious staff, but an unparalleled force made it impossible for him to recall his life-bound treasure, no matter how he urged his mana.

Thinking of a certain possibility, he couldn't help but glare with rage.

"No!"

"Hmph!"

A cold snort came from the void.

The next moment.

The cyan great hand suddenly closed.

Crack!

"Return it to you!"

Two streaks of light flew back.

Yang Daoren hurriedly dodged. After finally steadying himself, he could no longer control the excruciating pain in his soul.

Opening his mouth, he spat.

"Pfft!"

A mouthful of blood, rich with vigorous mana, was expelled.

Only then did the two streaks of light reveal their original forms, lying forlornly.

They were his life-bound staff!

With just one contact, his treasure had been forcibly snapped in two?

In terror, Yang Daoren looked towards the newcomer.

A man clad in black armor, with a yellow crown and golden plume on his head, stood in the void. The wind and thunder raged around him but could not approach. A pair of black eyes looked at him, as if ignoring him, or perhaps despising him.

"Who are you?" Yang Daoren asked in shock and anger, almost subconsciously.

The black-armored man loosened his fist and said indifferently, "This seat does not change his name, nor alter his surname. I am the Overlord Cultivator, Qi Tian!"

Out in the world, one's identity was whatever one gave oneself.

If it could be matched with the corresponding performance, then its persuasiveness was beyond doubt.

Even the Ming Lei Cave Master, who had had contact with Luo Chen, couldn't help but have his eyelids twitch wildly at this sight.

He knew Yang Daoren, a seasoned Sage of many years.

Yet in just one exchange, his life-bound treasure had been lost to the other party.

Just what level had this Qi Tian's body refinement reached?

But for now, he couldn't bother to speculate on that. Instead, he said loudly, "Feng Buyan, this is the helper I invited. How does he compare to the one you invited?"

Feng Buyan's lips were dry as he looked at Yang Daoren, who had retreated to his side.

The other party shook his head in terror.

"This man is fierce and brave. Do not fight him head-on!"

"No!" Feng Buyan said sharply. "We still have one more!"

Just then.

A vast saber qi, from outside the gorge, slashed through the air!

Luo Chen spun around abruptly, without hesitation, he brought his hands together.

In the void, two great hands appeared simultaneously, clamping the saber qi tightly between their palms.

But this saber qi was quite strange. Within it, two extreme forces were constantly bursting forth, continuously eroding the great hands formed by Luo Chen's source power.

Luo Chen's brow furrowed. "Demon qi? Buddha qi?"

Then, his expression turned ruthless.

"I don't care what qi it is!"

The source power within his body surged out violently.

A pair of fleshy palms continuously expanded, merging with the source power great hands.

Like a millstone grinding, he twisted his palms.

Boom!

The terrifying saber qi instantly collapsed.

Not only that, but Luo Chen, in his fury, slapped a palm towards the outside of the gorge.

A heaven-shaking giant palm broke through the air for a hundred zhang, like a world-destroying hand, slapping down at the ants on the ground.

A bell sound rang out.

Dong...

A distant, prolonged bell sound echoed without ceasing.

When the giant palm dissipated and the dust was brushed away, a handsome white figure came into view.

Luo Chen slowly flew out of Wind Thunder Gorge, staring at the white-robed monk.

"Tell me your name!"
