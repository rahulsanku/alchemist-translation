# Chapter 680: Hooking the Soul and Nailing the Spirit, Tai Sui's Main Body

Luo Chen knew that Tai Sui had never been completely honest.

From the moment they entered Five Elements Heaven, Tai Sui had hidden things.

He had hidden old acquaintances in Hundred Crow Ghost Forest.

He had tested the existence of Han Zhan in the Demon-Devil Heart Tree cave.

He had also clearly thought about using the situation in Yue Canglan to stir up trouble.

So before they continued through the water region, Luo Chen gave him a warning.

Tai Sui listened with a smile and behaved himself afterward.

The rest of the journey through Yue Canglan was surprisingly smooth.

They avoided the core region occupied by Fallen Abyss Dragon Sect and his Dragon Earthworms. Although they still encountered many lesser monsters along the way, none dared attack openly after sensing Luo Chen's aura.

Black King walked at the front most of the time.

Whenever the mud ahead moved strangely, it would stop and bare its teeth.

Each time, Luo Chen would change route without hesitation.

He had no intention of testing how many Dragon Earthworms were hidden beneath the swamp.

Moya Cave Master looked back more than once.

"There were dozens of third-tier Dragon Earthworms and at least several thousand lower-tier ones," he said. "How did he bring so many clansmen into Demon-Fall Land?"

Luo Chen did not answer.

He was thinking about Han Zhan's earlier reminder.

Fallen Abyss Dragon Sect had brought an entire race into Yue Canglan not merely for protection.

Dragon Earthworms were best at changing earth and water.

By making them alter Yue Canglan bit by bit, Fallen Abyss Dragon Sect could transform the water region into an environment that fit his own path.

But he could not overdo it.

If the land became completely alien, it would no longer be Yue Canglan, and he might lose the original water-law accumulation of Five Elements Heaven.

The balance between transformation and preservation was the true difficulty.

That confirmed Han Zhan's judgment.

The Demon Emperor was indeed using Yue Canglan to sense the water law.

After leaving the water region, the scenery changed sharply.

Ahead of them was a barren plain.

The transition was abrupt.

One moment, damp wind and muddy water still clung to their robes.

The next, even the moisture in the air seemed to have been stripped away.

Luo Chen had originally thought that the center where five kinds of qi converged would be a place of extreme abundance.

But this plain was desolate.

There were no spirit herbs, no ancient trees, no water monsters, and no visible mineral veins.

Only wind.

And at the very center of the plain, a vast spiritual qi tornado connected heaven and earth.

Five-colored qi rotated within it, sometimes separating, sometimes merging. The sight was magnificent, but the ground beneath it was cracked and lifeless, as though all vitality had been drawn upward into that vortex.

Tai Sui stopped.

He closed his eyes.

After a long while, he opened them and said softly, "This is the place."

Then he walked forward.

Luo Chen followed behind him.

In his sea of consciousness, Han Zhan said, "This place is restricting him."

"I can feel it."

Tai Sui's steps were steady, but his aura had become tense.

It was like watching a prisoner approach the edge of his permitted range.

Nothing visible blocked him.

Yet every step seemed to press on an invisible chain.

This barren plain was different from the previous five regions.

Hundred Crow Ghost Forest, Wood Heaven Plain, Yue Canglan, Blazing Refinement Prison, and the other heavens all belonged to the structure of Five Elements Heaven. Tai Sui, as a guardian spirit bound to Wood Heaven Plain, could move through them by relying on his identity.

But this plain seemed to be outside Five Elements Heaven proper.

It was a final transition region.

For Tai Sui, entering here was like stepping beyond the boundaries assigned to him.

As they advanced, traces of battle gradually appeared.

Broken blades half-buried in dust.

Cracked shields.

Skeletons whose robes had long rotted away.

There were also pits blasted into the ground by old spells, and deep sword marks that had not been erased even after countless years.

Some corpses still carried storage bags, but most had already been opened.

The remaining magic treasures were cracked or dim.

Time had stripped away value from many things, leaving only the story of how fierce the battles had once been.

Tai Sui said, "This was the final area before the inheritance. It was suitable for cultivators of many attributes to fight. In the past, every time people entered, many battles happened here."

Moya Cave Master looked at the corpses and sighed. "Arriving late has its advantages. At least the worst fights are already over."

Luo Chen looked toward the spiritual qi tornado. "Where is the inheritance?"

Tai Sui pointed forward. "There."

They walked for ten more days.

At last, they reached the edge of a cliff.

Above their heads, the spiritual qi tornado roared.

Below the cliff was a dark abyss.

Standing there, Luo Chen could feel the two spaces pressing against one another.

Behind them was Five Elements Heaven.

Ahead, hidden beyond the abyss and the storm, was Bright Radiance Heaven.

This abyss was not merely a crack in the earth.

It was more like a wound between two sealed heavens.

The abyss seemed bottomless. Even Luo Chen's spiritual sense sank into it and was swallowed after only a short distance. A cold, heavy aura rose from below, carrying both decay and suppression.

Tai Sui stood at the edge and said, "My main body is below."

Moya Cave Master's eyes brightened. "And the inheritance?"

"Also below."

Moya Cave Master immediately became excited and was about to step forward, but Luo Chen raised a hand to stop him.

Luo Chen looked at Tai Sui. "First tell us how to enter the inheritance."

Tai Sui smiled. "Fellow Daoist Luo, our agreement was to save my main body first."

Luo Chen stared at him.

Tai Sui did not retreat.

After a moment, Luo Chen lowered his hand.

Tai Sui sat cross-legged at the cliff edge.

His hands formed seals.

Green light spread from beneath him. Lingzhi and moss sprouted from the cliff face, growing downward in a winding path. The moss was thick and soft, but each patch carried faint formation light, supporting the path like steps.

The growth was rapid, but not wild.

Each patch appeared at exactly the right distance from the next.

It was less like plants growing and more like a staircase made of life being unfolded.

"Follow me."

Tai Sui stepped onto the moss path and descended into the abyss.

Luo Chen and Moya Cave Master followed.

This place was Falling Demon Abyss.

According to Tai Sui, it was the last barrier between Five Elements Heaven and Bright Radiance Heaven.

The descent lasted half a day.

At last, Tai Sui stopped before a crack in the cliff wall.

The crack was narrow at first, only wide enough for one person to pass. But after they entered, it gradually opened into a huge underground cave.

The moment Luo Chen saw what was inside, his pupils contracted slightly.

At the center of the cave lay a giant white flesh ball.

It was translucent, like congealed jelly, and as tall as a person.

Within it, red meridian-like threads could be seen faintly pulsing. Around the flesh ball grew clusters of lingzhi, moss, and strange white fungi, all rooted in its scattered vitality.

The sight was unsettling.

There was no face, no limbs, and no clear human shape.

Yet everyone present could feel that it was alive.

Every pulse of the red threads released a wave of vitality so dense that the surrounding plants grew and withered in tiny cycles.

Tai Sui's voice trembled. "That is my main body."

The white flesh ball shook as though it had sensed him.

In the next instant, formation lines appeared throughout the cave.

Hook-shaped marks pierced into the air around the flesh ball, as if invisible hooks had caught it from every direction.

Above it hovered seven rusty golden nails.

The nails were illusory, but the pressure they emitted was terrifying. Each one seemed to pin not flesh, but spirit.

Tai Sui suddenly crouched down, clutching his head.

Pain twisted his face.

"Hooking the soul, nailing the spirit!"

His voice was hoarse with hatred.

"True Lord, you were truly cruel!"

Moya Cave Master was startled. "What is this formation?"

Tai Sui took several breaths before he could stand again.

His eyes were bloodshot.

"Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Grand Formation [TERM?]. A hidden arrangement left by the True Lord."

He looked at the white flesh ball, and his voice became low.

"In the past, I was one of the three spirits of this place. My Tai Sui body carries endless vitality and the wood law of ceaseless life. The True Lord used me to cultivate spirit herbs, test spiritual beings, and even extend the lives of some flawed enlightened beings."

Moya Cave Master's expression changed.

Only now did he understand why Tai Sui's body was so valuable.

This was not simply a spirit with strong vitality.

It was a living foundation that could sustain an entire sealed heaven's spirit-planting system.

Tai Sui continued, "After the True Lord died and Yuan Demon Sect entered this place again and again, I feared that sooner or later I would become their slave, their backyard medicine field. So I tried to leave through Bright Radiance Heaven."

Luo Chen looked at him silently.

He did not fully believe that Tai Sui had no thoughts about the inheritance then.

Tai Sui noticed his gaze and laughed bitterly. "Fine, perhaps I was also greedy. But who in this world is not greedy when facing such an inheritance?"

No one answered.

Tai Sui pointed at the formation around his main body.

"The Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Grand Formation uses my own power as its source. Unless I die completely, the formation will not stop. The hooks catch the soul, and the nails pin the spirit. My main body cannot move, and my main soul cannot leave."

That was the cruelest part.

The stronger Tai Sui's vitality was, the longer the formation could continue.

The very power that should have allowed him to survive had become the chain that trapped him.

Moya Cave Master frowned. "Then how did your remnant thought escape?"

"By cutting flesh."

Tai Sui's voice was cold.

"A Yuan Demon Sect Sage once gave me inspiration. If I continuously split and cut away flesh from my main body, the formation's power will weaken little by little. Then I can move my main soul into another body. Once the main soul leaves, the formation will lose its target and collapse. After that, I can recombine with the remaining main body."

The method sounded simple when spoken aloud.

In truth, it was a gamble made over countless years.

Cut too little, and the formation would not weaken.

Cut too much, and the main body might be permanently damaged before the soul transfer began.

If the new body could not bear the main soul, everything would collapse at the final step.

Luo Chen understood.

This was why Tai Sui needed him and Moya Cave Master.

The process required formations, protection, and precise timing.

One mistake, and Tai Sui might truly die here.

The next half year passed in this underground cave.

Luo Chen did not rush.

Neither did Tai Sui.

No one mentioned Bright Radiance Heaven during that period.

No one mentioned the inheritance.

Before the flesh ball in the cave, all those future gains were distant.

The immediate matter was survival.

They arranged formation flags and formation plates layer by layer. Supreme-grade spirit stones were embedded into key nodes, forming a massive auxiliary array around the white flesh ball.

Moya Cave Master contributed his own formation experience.

Although his realm and combat strength were inferior to Luo Chen's, his years of studying tombs, caves, and restrictions made him useful in maintaining the complex formation network.

Luo Chen also laid down a fourth-tier Spirit-Blocking Formation [TERM?] based on a diagram Tai Sui provided.

Its purpose was simple.

Once Tai Sui's soul-transfer began, it would prevent Demon Lord Heaven-Refining's hidden formation from tracing the connection between Tai Sui's main body and the Ding Yi body.

The work was tedious and dangerous.

Some days, they did nothing but inspect a single formation line.

Some nights, the seven rusty golden nails would tremble, forcing Tai Sui to endure another wave of pain while Luo Chen stabilized the outer restrictions.

Moya Cave Master complained at first.

Later, even he stopped speaking carelessly.

The more he studied the Soul-Hooking Spirit-Nailing Grand Formation, the more frightened he became.

This was not a formation meant to kill.

It was a formation meant to make something unable to die and unable to leave.

By the end, even Luo Chen had memorized much of Moya Cave Master's formation method.

At last, everything was ready.

Luo Chen sat before Tai Sui's main body.

Tai Sui, occupying Ding Yi's body, sat opposite him.

Moya Cave Master guarded the cave entrance.

The altar beneath them glowed with layered formation light.

Tai Sui opened his eyes and looked at Luo Chen.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, are you ready?"

Luo Chen nodded.

Tai Sui was silent for a moment, then said, "Thank you."

After saying that, he closed his eyes.

The formation activated.

Light rose from the altar like a tide.

The entire cave began to shake.

Moss and lingzhi growing along the walls released green light, supporting the stone so that it would not collapse.

At the center of the cave, the giant white flesh ball trembled violently.

The seven rusty golden nails above it shone one after another.

The soul-hooking marks around it tightened.

Tai Sui's body stiffened.

The rescue had begun.
