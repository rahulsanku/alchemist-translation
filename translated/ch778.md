# Chapter 778: Everyone’s Schemes, Hearts and Ghosts

“Is it appropriate to let Qin’er fight? Her identity…”

“Appropriate! How could it not be? She is Qu Lingjun’s Dao partner, and Qu Lingjun was once the sole heir of the Luo Tian Sect’s Supreme Elder. There is no more legitimate identity than this.”

“You know that’s not what I mean. Qin’er is an outstanding disciple of this generation from our Fenghua Palace. If she fights, it means our Fenghua Palace is personally interfering in the Luo Tian internal strife. When the time comes, the Five Elements Divine Sect will have grounds to make an issue of it.”

At nightfall, a gentle breeze drifted through the exquisite palaces.

As it swirled in the corners, it seemed to sob.

In one palace, amidst the doubts of her fellow sect members, Wind Moon Fairy fell silent.

But only for a moment, then she laughed.

“Senior Sister, do you think that by not sending anyone to interfere in the Luo Tian internal strife, we can prevent the Five Elements Divine Sect from finding a reason?”

With light lotus steps, Wind Moon Fairy paced slowly in the hall.

“The Luo Tian Sect is under the protection of our Fenghua Palace—everyone knows this. Now, the Five Elements Divine Sect and Motian Cliff are instigating Sima Wenjie to stir up internal strife in Luo Tian. Using the pretext that the Luo Tian Sect once received favors from the Divine Sect, they have agreed that after ten duels, the Luo Tian Sect will be annexed under the Five Elements Divine Sect’s banner.”

“Such actions are practically a slap in the face to our Fenghua Palace!”

“If they truly succeed, how will the world view our Fenghua Palace?”

“If we sit idly by, after Luo Tian, they will go on to provoke our other vassals. Over time, will this land be called Fenghua, or Five Elements?”

Behind her, the gentle, richly dressed young matron nodded thoughtfully.

The reasoning was indeed sound.

But her concerns were different.

“Even a broken boat has three nails. The Five Elements Divine Sect’s strength is still relatively intact, especially with three Sages at the Nascent Soul level! If we add the one from Motian Cliff, that’s an even more formidable force. Our Fenghua Palace has only two Nascent Soul Sages; we truly cannot afford a head-on collision.”

Wind Moon Fairy whirled around. “What are you afraid of? With the Nether Origin Sect applying pressure at this time, do you think they would dare start an all-out war? At worst, they’ll use petty tricks like provoking vassals to slowly nibble away at the Fenghua Domain. What I need to do now is cut off the tentacles they’ve extended. Extend one, cut one off!”

Having said this, she felt a pang of frustration.

“Senior Sister Hua, I told you long ago that we should have absorbed living forces when the other domains were collapsing. You refused. Now that they’ve collapsed all the way to us, our Fenghua Palace is short-handed. It’s bad enough that we were pushed forward by the old established sects, but now we have to endure these provocations from those fellows on the front lines.”

Senior Sister Hua sighed. “Indeed, I did not consider this well.”

Wind Moon Fairy waved her hand. She knew the temperament of her sect leader senior sister well. If she herself hadn’t been obsessed with alchemy in her early years, she would have been the one to take the position of sect leader.

“Fortunately, I acted early, bringing in a genius like Qu Lingjun and marrying Qin’er to him, completely securing his loyalty.”

Mentioning Qu Lingjun, Senior Sister Hua couldn’t help but smile.

That Qu Lingjun was indeed a fine one. At the mid-Initiate stage, he could already refine top-grade Jade Dew Pills, and even Netherpassage Pills, those divine medicines for soul refinement! It was precisely with his help that Fenghua Palace had been able to continuously produce rising stars like Meng Qin’er over the past century.

The only pity was that this child’s aptitude was too poor. Even with Fenghua Palace investing all kinds of resources, he would probably never have a chance to form his core in this lifetime.

“I heard that this time, Qu Lingjun requested our sect’s assistance, and the price was to contribute the second-order Netherpassage Pill formula?” Senior Sister Hua suddenly asked.

Wind Moon Fairy admitted it generously.

“Perhaps it’s a bit underhanded, but I indeed made the Netherpassage Pill formula and its refining method the condition for letting Qin’er help.”

“Don’t underestimate this formula just because it’s only second-order. It is a posthumous work of Dan Chenzi. If taken continuously, it has an extremely strong enhancing effect on Initiate cultivators advancing to the Adept stage, making it easier to break through to the Adept realm. The Luo Tian Sect, after a hundred years of hardship, has not weakened but instead continuously produced Adept cultivators—this pill must have played a role.”

“If our Fenghua Palace obtains the formula, we can refine it freely and have our talented disciples take it in large quantities. We will quickly gain a batch of Adept-level experts!”

When she mentioned the name “Dan Chenzi,” Wind Moon Fairy felt an inexplicable melancholy.

He was an extraordinary figure, rare among the common folk!

With his own strength, he had led the Luo Tian Society, which was only at the Qi Refinement level, step by step to the level of a major Adept sect.

He seized the opportunity of the Expansion War, killed demons and beasts, moved mountains to establish the sect, and aimed to lay a foundation for five hundred years.

If that Expansion War had gone without incident, how could the Luo Tian Sect have been displaced?

With a stable mountain gate and the leadership of a brilliant leader like Dan Chenzi, they would probably be a leading Adept sect in the Eastern Wilderness by now!

If Dan Chenzi had been given a little more opportunity, even the threshold of the Nascent Soul stage might have been within his reach.

Unfortunately, there were no ifs, no might-have-beens, no maybes!

Everything had turned to bubbles and shadows after that female demon, like a ghost or god, made her move back then.

…

“Luo Tian’s Supreme Elder Dan Chenzi is not dead!”

Outside a pavilion in Ling Tian Pass.

Divine Fire Sage said this to his grand-disciple Ran Feng.

“According to Sima Wenjie, Dan Chenzi and his Dao partner, Sima Huiniang, once planted a Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu in each other, allowing them to sense each other’s life and death. Sima Wenjie learned from his sister that Dan Chenzi is not dead. This is confirmed intelligence.”

Ran Feng was puzzled. “But over a hundred years have passed. Couldn’t something have happened to him?”

Divine Fire Sage chuckled lightly. “A dragon or loach that has crawled and scrambled out of the mud like him is the most cunning and shrewd. How could he die so easily? He survived the Jade Cauldron civil war. He survived the Expansion War, plundering and building the sect’s blessed land. Even in the rumored battle at Green Parasol Mountain, he survived. Are there any dangers greater than those that he couldn’t have weathered?”

Ran Feng suddenly understood, becoming even more interested in the legendary figure.

“I heard that the Supreme Elder had his eye on him?”

Divine Fire Sage twitched the corner of his mouth. That senior brother of his was becoming less and less discreet.

Shaking his head, Divine Fire Sage said in a low voice, “Indeed. This man is key to our sect’s Supreme Elder advancing to the Ascendant stage. That’s why we repeatedly tried to recruit him. Even if he disappeared, we had to control his weakness and wait for him to come to us willingly.”

The word “recruit” was not just talk.

Back when Luo Chen had advanced to the Adept stage, the Five Elements Divine Sect had already invited him across domains, but he had directly refused.

Later, after the demon calamity, the Five Elements Divine Sect repeatedly protected the Luo Tian Sect, also waiting for Luo Chen to come willingly.

But so many years had passed, and Luo Chen still hadn’t appeared. Instead, the Luo Tian Sect wanted to use Fenghua Palace’s hand to break free from the Divine Sect’s control. That was something the Ancestor could no longer tolerate.

Of course, the Divine Sect’s public justification was that the Luo Tian Sect was ungrateful, hence the forced annexation to recover losses.

They had a just cause, and they had people!

Motian Cliff had long been in cahoots with Sima Wenjie. Now, wanting to take the opportunity to get a foothold in the Fenghua Domain, they cooperated with them to test Fenghua Palace’s limits.

The two sides joined forces, pushed Sima Wenjie forward as a puppet, and thus caused this internal strife in the Luo Tian Sect.

Divine Fire Sage was about to say more when his expression shifted.

“Let’s go in. Everyone’s here.”

Divine Fire Sage flicked his wide sleeve and strode from the balcony into the pavilion.

Footsteps came from the corridor.

Soon, a gray-robed old man led Sima Wenjie inside.

“Old Ghost Motian, how have you been lately?”

“Divine Fire, you’re still as hypocritical as ever.” Old Ghost Motian sneered, then demanded, “You messed up today.”

Divine Fire Sage was unperturbed. “Emei lost, but it’s no big deal. The Jade Cauldron sword cultivator I brought won, didn’t he?”

“We should have won both matches!”

Sima Wenjie suddenly interrupted, his face full of indignation.

“If we had followed my arrangement and let Fellow Daoist Qu go up, there was a great chance of taking down Baleful Dragonzi!”

Two Sages were talking, and a mere Adept junior dared to interrupt?

Yet Divine Fire Sage only glanced at him without reprimanding him.

“Have you decided on the candidate for tomorrow’s battle?”

Old Ghost Motian replied, “I came to discuss exactly that. Tomorrow, there’s a high chance Wang Yuan will fight. For that battle, I suggest sending your sect’s Yin Sixiang.”

As soon as these words were spoken, Ran Feng, standing behind, immediately changed expression.

Divine Fire Sage waved his hand without even thinking.

“Impossible!”

“Junior Disciple Sixiang is at the Great Perfection of the Adept stage, at a critical moment in his push to break through to the Nascent Soul stage. How can he be sent to fight?”

Old Ghost Motian sneered, “Do you not know the strength of War Demon Wang Yuan? His body refinement has reached the fourth realm, the Leakless Golden Body. Unless a Nascent Soul Sage acts, ordinary Adepts have no way to deal with him. Only your Five Elements Divine Sect’s Yin Sixiang can fight him. Or do you not want to take down Wang Yuan directly tomorrow?”

“That’s not impossible either!” Divine Fire Sage replied naturally. “Using our top horse against their top horse would be a mutual loss. Why not concede this match and focus on the remaining three?”

“No!”

Sima Wenjie interrupted again, directly vetoing Divine Fire Sage’s suggestion.

He said confidently, “Wang Yuan is the banner of the Luo Tian Sect, the faith of everyone. As long as he loses, it will be a huge blow to the Luo Tian Sect! The next three matches can be won without a fight.”

Having his face slapped twice in a row by Sima Wenjie, Divine Fire Sage’s expression turned unpleasant.

He stared at Sima Wenjie for a moment, then suddenly smiled. “Are you afraid that if the battle drags on, you’ll have to go up and face Sima Huiniang yourself?”

Sima Wenjie’s face immediately turned a mix of green, red, and white, a truly spectacular sight.

Old Ghost Motian spoke up at the right moment to ease his embarrassment.

“I also think we should take down Wang Yuan directly tomorrow. If we win, we’ll have five victories in hand. Not only will it deal a huge blow to the Luo Tian Sect disciples’ morale, but we will also be in an invincible position, forcing Fenghua Palace to show their face and expose their outwardly strong but inwardly weak nature to everyone in the Fenghua Domain.”

“You don’t need to worry about Yin Sixiang’s safety. I have a true artifact here that counters body cultivators, ensuring he can secure a decisive victory over Wang Yuan. Not only will we achieve our goal, but it will also help your sect’s strongman make a name for himself. Why not?”

“Oh?” Divine Fire Sage raised an eyebrow. “Tell me more.”

…

“Wenjie, after this is done, don’t forget what you promised me.”

After Divine Fire Sage had left, Old Ghost Motian kept Sima Wenjie behind and said this.

Sima Wenjie immediately understood what he was referring to.

“This junior will never forget!”

“As long as I become the Luo Tian Sect’s sect leader, I can legitimately demand the original version of the *Fine Dust Primordial Art* from Wang Yuan. I hear that secret core-formation method is different from the simplified version we practice. It is of the ultimate method level, personally created by Luo… Luo Chen.”

“With this method’s help, Motian Cliff will surely see a resurgence of Adept-level talents!”

A resurgence of Adepts?

Old Ghost Motian sighed inwardly. That was all he sought.

In that Expansion War back then, his most promising junior, Wei Wuya, had fallen in the Yu clan’s sacred ground, Green Parasol Mountain.

This left Motian Cliff without a successor.

If it had stopped there, it might have been bearable. But in the subsequent century-long war, not only did Motian Cliff’s Adept cultivators fall one after another, but they also faced a situation of having no successors.

Under these circumstances, the Luo Tian Sect’s unusual string of successful core formations piqued his curiosity.

Back when the Luo Tian Sect was still living under someone else’s roof in the Five Elements Domain, he had begun to lure Sima Wenjie, trying to uncover the secret of the Luo Tian Sect’s continuous production of Adepts.

Unfortunately, the demon race’s invasion had come too quickly.

The Five Elements Domain hadn’t been able to stop the demon advance, and the Luo Tian Sect had defected to Fenghua Palace.

This time, letting Sima Wenjie step forward to fight for control of the Luo Tian Sect was ostensibly to test Fenghua Palace’s limits and secure a better spirit vein blessed land for Motian Cliff. But in reality, he was coveting that ultimate Core Formation secret method called the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*, hoping to revive Motian Cliff!

Everyone, every faction, had their own demands.

Including Sima Wenjie.

With the help of the two major sects, his ambitions had swelled to an unprecedented degree, and the resentment in his heart had magnified to the limit.

In his view, there was nothing wrong with what he was doing.

The Luo Tian Sect was rightfully his Sima family’s!

From its founding, his second sister had managed the Luo Tian Sect, handling all kinds of miscellaneous affairs for over a hundred years without ever slacking off.

For the Luo Tian Sect, his elder brother had worked himself to the bone until death.

Since Luo Chen had disappeared, the sect should naturally belong to his Sima family.

But of all things, the sect had poured all its training resources into juniors like Baleful Dragonzi, Mei Ruoqi, and Yuan Yijun.

And even the position of second-generation sect leader had been given to Yao Mingyue!

What had he, Sima Wenjie, gotten in return?

He had repeatedly questioned his second sister.

Her answer had left him speechless.

“When my husband was alive, he established the core philosophy of sect inheritance. I naturally follow it.”

To hell with inheritance!

When he had risked his life for the Luo Tian Sect, why wasn’t he considered part of that inheritance?

Even the Nether Origin Pill that his second sister no longer needed after her unexpected core formation hadn’t been given to him.

Instead, it had been bestowed upon that old man Min Longyu.

And he, Sima Wenjie, one of the earliest members of the Luo Tian Society, who had once managed the sect’s important affairs, the sect leader’s own younger brother, the Supreme Elder’s brother-in-law—had gotten nothing. When he wanted to form his core, there wasn’t even a single resource to spare.

When everyone had left and the place was empty, Sima Wenjie stood alone above, his face dark and gloomy, his heart burning with rage.

“All of this is too unfair!”

“I’m just taking back what rightfully belongs to me!”

“You can’t blame me!”
