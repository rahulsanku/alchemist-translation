# Chapter 1179: First Meeting with Wuyou, the Secret of Guixu

"Fellow Daoist, you've been so immersed in cultivation that you've likely forgotten what time of year it is."

Faced with Luo Chen's curiosity, Fang Qingxue covered her mouth with a laugh.

Luo Chen was startled, then suddenly understood.

Unbeknownst to him, a very long time had passed since he entered the Nine Rings of Annihilation.

Ten years in the Lower Three Rings, thirty years in the Sixth Ring, sixty years in the Fourth Ring.

Added together, nearly a hundred years had passed!

And every hundred years, the Ninefold Immortal Tower would open, both to allow the martial artists of the Nine Rings of Annihilation to go out and rest and recuperate, and also as the period for a new batch of newly promoted Leakless Realm martial artists to enter.

"I've been somewhat oblivious to the world," Luo Chen sighed, shaking his head, then said, "Judging by the three of you, are you planning to go out together?"

Mu Bugui nodded, then said in a deep voice, "We are preparing to go out, but we may never return."

Luo Chen was puzzled.

Just then, the four of them raised their heads in unison.

Amidst the vast chaos, an ethereal figure gradually descended from above.

Fang Qingxue bowed in greeting: "Ancestor!"

Gu Xun and Mu Bugui also saluted respectfully: "Greetings, Wuyou Domain Lord!"

Luo Chen could not be exempt either. Although he had not expected to meet Wuyou Domain Lord on this occasion, he had long been prepared for this day.

He clasped his hands in salute, his tone sincere.

"Luo Chen, greetings, Senior!"

His words carried heartfelt respect.

Leaving aside the pioneering feat of dividing the Nine Rings of Annihilation's cultivation environment to allow a large number of martial artists to train, the mere act of sending Fang Zhibai to deliver the Immortal Origin Stones back then was enough to fill him with gratitude.

Wuyou Domain Lord slowly descended before the four. He wore a moon-white cloud-brocade Daoist robe, a green jade belt around his waist, and a pair of plain-faced cloud shoes on his feet.

His face was that of a middle-aged man, not exceptionally striking, yet the hint of a smile at the corners of his mouth was like ancient jade that had been washed and refined by spiritual energy for years—not a trace of worry or entanglement to be seen.

He exuded no deliberately released pressure, yet he possessed a calm, imposing demeanor like a mountain or an abyss. It seemed that wherever he stood was an invisible pivot point of heaven and earth, causing even the surging chaotic energy to slow its pace.

In response to their greetings, he merely waved his hand casually.

Then his gaze fell upon Luo Chen. After a moment's appraisal, the smile at the corners of his mouth deepened.

"Good! Although you and I have met once before, now that we truly face each other, I find your bearing and spirit even more remarkable!"

Luo Chen said modestly, "Senior flatters me."

Wuyou Domain Lord waved his hand again. "Everyone here is either trapped in Guixu or has unfortunately drifted here. We are all fellow sufferers in this desolate land. Why speak of 'senior'? If you are willing, you may call me 'Fellow Daoist,' or you may learn from them and call me Wuyou Domain Lord."

Luo Chen hesitated for a moment, then still addressed him as Wuyou Domain Lord.

Whether in the cultivation world or the Great Annihilation Realm, precedence was given to those who had attained mastery.

Facing a strong practitioner whose realm and strength were both a level above his own, calling him 'Fellow Daoist' would make Luo Chen uneasy.

Wuyou Domain Lord was, after all, a respectful title.

After their initial acquaintance, Luo Chen could not help but ask the question that had been pressing on his mind.

"Fellow Daoist Mu, what do you mean by 'never return'?"

Mu Bugui hesitated and looked toward Wuyou Domain Lord.

Wuyou Domain Lord strolled to the front of the Wind-Wave Pavilion, gazed at the heavy snow falling outside, and first offered a word of praise.

"Little Qingxue, your martial will has grown even more refined. It can now influence the external world."

Martial will was born from a martial artist's will infecting their natal astral qi—a top-down process.

But after reaching the Human Immortal realm, the progress of martial will was from the inside out. Through the diffusion of astral qi, it could alter the surrounding celestial phenomena, initially influencing the external environment. When it reached its limit, this will would in turn nourish the self, transforming into the will of an inner world at a higher realm.

Fang Qingxue said modestly, "Ancestor, you flatter me."

But Wuyou Domain Lord sighed. "When the cycle of the era turns three hundred years from now and the Annihilation Mist sweeps in, I imagine this Great Annihilation Realm, this Guixu, will be just like this heaven and earth—a vast expanse of white, utterly clean, won't it?"

At these words, even though Luo Chen possessed excellent composure, a cultivator's Dao heart that could not be disturbed by anything, and a martial artist's will tempered a thousand times, his expression could not help but change drastically.

"Three hundred years from now!"

Even Gu Xun, who had been informed of some inside information, could not help but murmur to herself.

"Is it really that close, that near?"

Suppressing the shock in his heart, Luo Chen asked unwillingly, "Wuyou Domain Lord, is this information reliable?"

Wuyou Domain Lord's expression was calm. "More or less. I calculated it based on the three-thousand-year operational pattern of the Annihilation Gate. Additionally, that one from the Myriad Phenomena Star Sect in the Sea of Stars recently observed the celestial phenomena and reached a similar conclusion."

Luo Chen did not know the situation in the Sea of Stars, but he believed that an existence who could stand alongside Wuyou Domain Lord would never speak without basis.

If two people had reached similar conclusions, even if the timing was not yet precise, it would at most be a few hundred years off.

He said with difficulty, "So that's why Fellow Daoist Mu said that going out this time means not coming back?"

Wuyou Domain Lord nodded. "He will follow me to seek a way out."

Luo Chen subconsciously looked up toward the heights. "Isn't that spatial vortex called the Annihilation Gate also said to be a possible passage to the outside?"

"It is indeed a passage, but not one that you or I can traverse." Wuyou Domain Lord did not avoid the topic, then asked in return, "Luo Chen, do you know what kind of place Guixu really is?"

Luo Chen was silent.

Wuyou Domain Lord pursed his lips and said with emotion, "The so-called Guixu originally means that all rivers return to it, yet it never fills; the tail-end leaks it out, yet it never empties. This is the endpoint where all things end and time and space perish."

Luo Chen was bewildered.

Wuyou Domain Lord continued, "It exists in every corner of the endless universe, yet it does not exist in any single place. The concepts of time and space do not exist here. That you and I can barely survive is only because Guixu has not yet awakened."

Luo Chen seemed to understand.

Wuyou Domain Lord finally said, "To use an analogy, you can think of the endless universe as a complete person, and Guixu as its internal organs, its digestive system. Whether it is energy, matter, or even the souls of living beings, everything in the universe will eventually gather here."

Luo Chen frowned slightly, recalling the Broken Appearance Green Lamp he had used in his experiments in the Southern Frontier of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

When his storage pouch shattered, the green lamp had disappeared, only to later appear in a shop in a small town in the Great Annihilation Realm.

Similar scenes had actually occurred many times in his centuries of cultivation.

When enemies fought and their storage treasures shattered, some items inside would always vanish strangely.

Had they all come to Guixu?

Thinking of the strange, foreign resources in the shops of Wuyou City, Luo Chen confirmed Wuyou Domain Lord's statement.

Uncertainly, Luo Chen asked, "If that's the case, why don't we take advantage of the time when Guixu digests and expels things to leave here?"

Wuyou Domain Lord cast an appreciative glance at Luo Chen.

"In fact, that is indeed a very correct idea. Predecessors have made similar attempts, though whether they succeeded is unknown. However..."

The word "however" told Luo Chen that what followed would greatly disappoint him.

Wuyou Domain Lord said in a heavy tone, "However, as the digestive organ of the universe, Guixu has, at some unknown time, become weak and aged, nearly unable to move. It only continuously devours and digests, but does not give anything back to the endless universe. It only takes in, never expels. How can we take the opportunity to escape?"

Luo Chen's mouth fell open, and his face showed surprise.

If the universe had not yet perished, how could its digestive organ, Guixu, have become weak and aged?

Suddenly!

Luo Chen thought of what had happened before he drifted here.

Rosycloud had once said a sentence: The immortal path has been severed, the Immortal Realm has collapsed!

If the endpoint of this universe was Guixu, then its highest point might be the so-called Immortal Realm.

After the Immortal Realm collapsed, even if the countless stars that formed the universe's trunk and blood still remained, its true internal organs would lose their driving force.

Suddenly, Luo Chen noticed a discrepancy.

He asked without thinking, "If, as the Domain Lord says, Guixu has lost the power to expel outward, then how did predecessors enter the Annihilation Gate, attempting to escape outward?"

Wuyou Domain Lord once again looked at Luo Chen with eyes full of appreciation.

"Fellow Daoist, your quick wit and rapid divine sense conversion are truly rare. If I had met you a thousand or eight hundred years earlier, I might have been able to study a method to leave Guixu with you."

His praise carried a note of regret and sorrow.

Then, Wuyou Domain Lord explained the origin of the predecessors' ideas to Luo Chen.

"Although Guixu has aged, it is not truly dead. Just as after a human's consciousness fades, external stimulation of their body's joints can still trigger some physical reactions, so too with Guixu. Legend has it that when certain large stars in the universe collapse, Guixu subconsciously absorbs the power of stellar destruction, and it instinctively writhes. That is one of the opportunities to leave!"

"However!"

"The power of stellar destruction is so vast that under natural conditions, Ascendant-level cultivators and above can barely survive. But when this power is concentrated at a single point in Guixu, it condenses billions of times. Not to mention you and me, even if an Immortal were present, they could not withstand such terrifying force."

Luo Chen could understand the first part.

But he did not believe the latter part.

He had once created a divine ability called Starfire Burning the World.

Although that move could not truly destroy a world, that was only limited by Luo Chen's cultivation realm.

If one day he could fully master the Fire Origin and push his comprehension progress to one hundred percent, he had absolute confidence in destroying a world with a single move.

And that realm, in the cultivation world, was the Great Vehicle stage.

True Immortals were still above the Great Vehicle stage. How could they not withstand the power of a single star world's destruction?

This meeting today was their first, and might also be their last. He no longer held back.

Luo Chen said bluntly, "Isn't that an exaggeration?"

Wuyou Domain Lord said freely, "Not an exaggeration at all. I have guarded the Annihilation Gate for thousands of years, and I have not been without encountering similar opportunities. But in the end, I never succeeded. Every time, I could only gaze at the ocean and sigh, retreating in defeat!"

Faced with absolute facts and evidence, Luo Chen had to believe.

After all, Wuyou Domain Lord's realm and strength were far above his current self.

But he still refused to give up. "I wonder if the Domain Lord could share the appearance of such an opportunity when it occurs?"

"You want to try?" Wuyou Domain Lord said with a smile. As he spoke, he glanced meaningfully at his descendant, Fang Qingxue.

Luo Chen said frankly, "Just consider it a backup plan!"

Wuyou Domain Lord chuckled, not hiding anything, and said openly, "It's hard to describe the specific situation. When you see the Annihilation Gate stir, you will naturally sense it. And... such an opportunity is bound to appear soon!"

Luo Chen was stunned.

Then he realized what opportunity Wuyou Domain Lord was referring to.

"Three hundred years from now?"

"Yes, three hundred years from now, the Annihilation Mist will sweep everything away. All places in this Guixu will perish. Isn't that also a manifestation of a world's destruction? But this time, I will not sit and wait before the Annihilation Gate."

Luo Chen nodded and cupped his hands. "Thank you, Domain Lord, for informing me of these inside details."

Behind him, the expressions of Gu Xun and the other two were complex.

Clearly, they had long known of these inside details.

That was why Luo Chen had been the one asking questions throughout this meeting, while Wuyou did the explaining.

Wuyou Domain Lord waved his hand dismissively. "It's not particularly important information. Just things one naturally summarizes after seeing enough."

After speaking, his gaze swept over the four of them, and he pondered for a long moment.

Finally, he slowly asked:

"This time I leave, I am not without purpose. My target is a place called the Ancient God Dao Ground, a tomb. There may be a sliver of a chance to escape Guixu. Fellow Daoist Mu has already decided to go with me. What about the three of you?"

Mu Bugui also looked at the three of them, a hint of hope in his eyes.

Luo Chen chewed on the name "Ancient God Dao Ground," not understanding its meaning.

Fang Qingxue smiled. "Ancestor, you should have known my decision long ago."

Wuyou Domain Lord was helpless. Being burdened by family was a situation he had seen many times in his life, and he had long since passed that stage. But he chose to respect his most outstanding descendant's choice and did not force her.

Gu Xun hesitated. "Is that the Ancient God Dao Ground, rumored to have caused countless brilliant martial artists to fall with regret?"

"Yes, that place has existed for tens of thousands of years, having weathered several ten-thousand-year Annihilation Mists. Although I don't know if it can withstand the Great Annihilation Mist under the cycle of the era, it is ultimately an opportunity." Wuyou Domain Lord told the truth.

Gu Xun's expression was conflicted, but in the end, she said "no."

Wuyou Domain Lord looked at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen thought for a moment, then said, "I have only just broken through to the Human Immortal realm. Not only do I need to stabilize my realm, but I also have a martial divine ability that is not yet complete and requires a great deal of time to comprehend. So, regrettably, I cannot travel with the Domain Lord."

Wuyou Domain Lord nodded.

"Very well. Living beings all have their own paths. If you have the idea later, you can come find me with this map. The Ancient God Dao Ground will not open for some time yet. Perhaps we will meet again."

A map flew out from Wuyou Domain Lord's hand and landed on Fang Qingxue's path.

Luo Chen and Gu Xun both subconsciously looked at it.

If the Annihilation Gate truly could not be passed, they might also follow Wuyou Domain Lord's path, taking that unknown road.

When the topic ended, Wuyou Domain Lord was quite emotional.

"In a scene like this, if there were fine wine to accompany it, one could surely forget their worries and find release."

Fang Qingxue smiled slightly. "Ancestor, are you missing the Thousand Mountains Twilight Snow?"

Wuyou Domain Lord shook his head. "The Thousand Mountains Twilight Snow brewed by the clan now is ultimately not even one percent of what I brought in back then."

Mu Bugui hesitated for a moment, then still took out a small gourd. It was the Amber Flowing Light from his homeland, but only a very small amount remained.

If the five of them shared it, it would not be enough.

Luo Chen said generously, "Why not try some of my hometown's spiritual wine!"

As he spoke, he waved his hand. Light flickered from the ring on his fingertip, and a large wine jar landed in the Wind-Wave Pavilion.

When the stopper was opened, a rich aroma of wine emerged, along with thick spiritual energy that began to dissipate rapidly.

"What is this wine called?" Wuyou Domain Lord's eyes lit up.

"Golden Wind and Jade Dew!" Luo Chen replied. This wine came from the Dieyun Sect in the Eastern Wilderness of the Mountains and Seas Realm. The original batch had long been drunk by him. This batch was a congratulatory gift sent by the Dieyun Sect when he summoned the various sects before his ascension.

Wuyou Domain Lord could not help but praise, "When golden wind meets jade dew, it surpasses countless scenes in the mortal world! Fine wine, fine name!"

The five of them immediately divided the wine and poured it, enjoying themselves immensely.

At this moment of impending farewell, they were granted a rare moment of warmth.

As the wine warmed their spirits, Wuyou Domain Lord's gaze fell upon the storage ring Luo Chen had used to retrieve the wine.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, are you also proficient in the arts of artifact forging and formation arrays?"

(End of this chapter)
