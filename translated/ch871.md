# Chapter 871: Nine Spirits Battles Peng Xiang, Luo Chen Faces Divine Origin

Defeated?

The Old Ghost of Motian had been defeated?

A dignified Nascent Soul Sage, actually defeated by a body cultivator of a lower realm?

Such a scene shocked everyone speechless, some even beginning to doubt their own existence.

Throughout the ages, fighting across realms had always been the most talked-about topic among cultivators.

But often, this so-called "crossing realms" was limited to minor sub-levels within the same major realm.

Those who could leap an entire major realm and still emerge victorious were exceedingly rare!

And this was the Nascent Soul realm, where qualitative transformations occurred!

After Luo Chen declared the first battle a victory for the Luo Tian Sect, absolute silence was followed by a violent uproar of noise.

Everyone was voicing their opinions, expressing the shock in their hearts.

Even the assembled Nascent Soul Sages couldn't help but look askance.

Lin Qingxuan's gaze followed the still bitterly resentful Old Ghost of Motian. "He's weaker than I imagined!"

Fenghua Fairy looked at Wang Yuan, who was flying back slowly, her eyes sparkling. "No wonder he was a junior favored by the former Heavenly Feather City Lord. To possess such strength... if he truly breaks through to the fourth realm of body cultivation, I fear he would be a match even for mid-stage Nascent Soul cultivators like us."

Elder Fu shook his head and sighed. It was enough that the Luo Tian Sect had Luo Chen, but now they also had Wang Yuan. How fortunate!

Unlike his Divine Talisman Sect, which had painstakingly cultivated a Nascent Soul cultivator only to lose him in an instant.

Xianhe Sage, however, had a different focus. His mind kept replaying the two weapons Wang Yuan had used during the battle.

They were true artifacts, yet completely different from those used by ordinary cultivators.

Wang Yuan's astral qi flowed through them, greatly amplifying their power.

In all his years, he had never heard of Wang Yuan possessing these two treasures.

In comparison, the Old Ghost of Motian, lacking a suitable true artifact, had suffered a great disadvantage.

Only Deng Taiyue, who frequently traveled through the Million Great Mountains and was quite sensitive to spiritual qi, kept his gaze fixed on Taihu Domain. After a long while, he turned thoughtfully to look at Luo Chen.

"He chose the chaotic spiritual qi of Taihu Domain as his battlefield. This greatly weakened the domain power that Nascent Soul Sages use most effectively to suppress lower-realm cultivators. Wang Yuan, being a body cultivator, was even less affected by magical power suppression. Add to that two powerful treasures, and the Old Ghost of Motian's defeat was not unjust!"

On Luo Chen's side, seeing Wang Yuan return safely, his expression remained calm, but inwardly he breathed a genuine sigh of relief.

"Are you alright?"

Wang Yuan casually draped a robe over himself, revealing a rare smile.

"My thoughts are clear, with no more obstacles!"

Luo Chen paused, then smiled as well.

This battle meant a great deal to Wang Yuan. It wasn't just about defeating a stronger opponent; it was also about removing the humiliation he had suffered in the battle arena all those years ago.

Cultivators refined qi and spirit; body cultivators refined the body and the mind.

Wang Yuan was already at the peak of the Furnace Realm. Now that his thoughts were clear, his chances of breaking through to the fourth realm of body cultivation, the Leakless Golden Body, were much greater.

"Excellent!"

Luo Chen praised him, then looked at Nine Spirits Primogenitor.

"Fellow Daoist Nine Spirits, I'll trouble you next."

Nine Spirits Primogenitor smiled confidently, stepped onto the azure clouds, and entered Taihu Domain in a blaze of light.

On the other side.

The Old Ghost of Motian's nascent soul was still chattering incessantly. "They melted down my Motian Cliff and caught me off guard to suppress me. Unfair, unfair!"

Divine Origin Sage glanced at him and said flatly, "When has there ever been fairness in this world? When you interfered in the internal strife of the Luo Tian Sect back then, did you ever consider whether it was fair to those low-level cultivators?"

The Old Ghost of Motian reacted as if stung, glaring at Divine Origin. "Back then, you also—"

"Hmph!"

Divine Origin Sage snorted coldly, and the absolute suppression from his soul made the Old Ghost of Motian instantly fall silent.

Only then did he realize that his nascent soul was still under the other's magical power suppression, his life and death in another's hands. How dare he bare his fangs?

Yet he didn't dare ask Divine Origin to withdraw his power, for within his nascent soul, several strands of blood-colored energy had somehow been infiltrated by Wang Yuan. Without great magical power to suppress them, Wang Yuan could detonate those strands at any moment.

"Take good care of yourself!"

"We'll handle the rest."

Divine Origin Sage said calmly, his gaze falling on the eager Peng Xiang Daoist.

"Fellow Daoist Peng, do you have confidence?"

Peng Xiang Daoist said freely, "Nine Spirits Primogenitor has a fearsome reputation, said to be comparable to a late-stage Nascent Soul Grand Cultivator. Although I share the same realm as him, I have long resided in White Deer Cave and rarely spar with others. I'm somewhat unsure of my own capabilities in the outside world."

Hearing this, Divine Origin Sage frowned. But thoughts flickered through his mind, and in an instant, his brow relaxed.

"It's fine. Do your best. Victory or defeat is not your fault."

As he said this, both of them unconsciously glanced at the Old Ghost of Motian, who was closing his eyes to expel the blood-colored energy.

A dignified Nascent Soul Sage, defeated by a third-realm body cultivator, and in the most important battle between two factions.

Whether they won or lost truly had nothing to do with helpers like Peng Xiang.

But since matters had progressed to this point, he had to make a move and give everyone an explanation.

With a light laugh, Peng Xiang drifted into Taihu Domain.

Facing Nine Spirits Primogenitor's burning gaze, he said loudly, "After my junior brother, Iron View, returned, he said that you defeated the Thunder Prison Roc and withstood a punch from Azure Frost, earning the reputation of being the strongest below Grand Cultivator. It is truly my fortune to cross hands with you today!"

These words were quite polite, implying a desire for a friendly spar without harming relations.

Nine Spirits Primogenitor raised an eyebrow, understanding the subtext.

After all, the other party was just a helper. There was no need to fight to the death like the Old Ghost of Motian, whose physical body had been blown apart.

However...

He shook his head. "If this were ten years ago, I would have accepted the title of strongest below Grand Cultivator without hesitation. But with the Pill Master ahead of me, I don't have the face to claim it."

The onlookers unanimously turned to look at Luo Chen, standing at the bow of the ship.

The feat of defeating the Seven Rhinoceros Great Demon Emperor with a single finger, even if several years had passed, still circulated throughout the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world.

And Luo Chen's realm was merely the fourth layer of Nascent Soul!

Enough idle talk. The focus now was still on the two figures in the empty Taihu Domain.

One in a black Daoist robe, with a solemn bearing.

One in nine-colored luxurious garments, majestic and grand.

Holding their breath, the two moved.

Chanting incantations and forming hand seals, magical power surged. The first move was a collision of their signature spells.

A massive black deer ran out from before Peng Xiang Daoist, traversing the sky with mysterious steps, charging toward Nine Spirits Primogenitor.

As the black deer galloped, the originally chaotic spiritual qi of Taihu Domain seemed to be controlled, forming surging waves that rolled forward.

Such a scene drew exclamations from the watching Sages.

Even the Old Ghost of Motian opened his eyes, revealing a look of surprise and uncertainty.

The spiritual qi of Taihu Domain was so chaotic—how could Peng Xiang Daoist mobilize it, and even without regard for attribute?

On the Heavenly River Lingxu Ship, Luo Chen was equally surprised.

Perhaps seeing his confusion, Elder Fu, who was old and experienced, quietly explained, "This technique is the famous signature spell of White Deer Cave—the Deer-Chasing Art. It uses the deer as a metaphor for qi, chasing and driving it. It is said to be the high-level spell closest to a Divine Transformation divine ability, and its power is extremely formidable."

After hearing this, Luo Chen's heart tightened.

He realized he had still underestimated the heroes of the world.

Peng Xiang Daoist rarely walked the world, yet he had unknowingly mastered such a powerful spell.

Could Nine Spirits Primogenitor withstand it?

Under countless gazes, Nine Spirits Primogenitor took a deep breath, raised his hands, and slowly pushed forward.

The next moment, the earth trembled. Immeasurable amounts of stone and gravel shot up into the sky, rushing toward the black deer.

Upon contact, the black deer charged through as if entering an empty land, its speed not decreasing in the slightest.

Nine Spirits Primogenitor's expression changed slightly. He switched his hand seals, opened his mouth, and spat out a streak of blue light.

As the blue light emerged, it was like a vast river, merging into the endless stone and gravel, instantly forming a muddy swamp. The black deer's speed finally slowed.

The two stalemated for a long time, and the spiritual qi-condensed black deer gradually dissipated.

Splash!

A great mass of yellow sand fell, raising billowing smoke.

Two figures were hidden within, their true forms unseen.

Peng Xiang Daoist waved his sleeve, blowing away the smoke, revealing their figures.

He wore an expression of admiration. "Is this the Nine-Bend Yellow River Ten-Thousand-Mile Sand of the Nine Spirits Sect? Excellent technique, Daoist!"

Nine Spirits Primogenitor patted the sand and mud on his body and said solemnly, "In terms of spells, you are unfathomable. But in terms of magical power, I am slightly superior."

Only then did the outside cultivators belatedly realize what had happened.

The first exchange had already ended.

As for the result?

"In terms of appearance, Nine Spirits Primogenitor was at a disadvantage. But the actual result was a draw. Nine Spirits' magical power is simply too profound, forcibly compensating for the deficiency in spell confrontation."

Luo Chen's spiritual eyes had seen every detail of the battle clearly, and he made the most accurate assessment.

It was also at this moment that he remembered that before Nine Spirits Primogenitor had cultivated that great technique, what he was most proud of was his incomparably abundant magical power.

And the best way to use that magical power was naturally various spells.

In a sense, Nine Spirits Primogenitor and he were somewhat similar, except that he was more distracted and had mastered more other skills.

The first battle was a draw.

What next?

Without making people wait long, the two moved again.

A horsetail whisk transformed into thousands of white threads, covering the sky and earth.

A black urn emitted billowing black smoke, pouncing fiercely at the whisk threads.

The two hovered in the void, constantly transforming, like pythons and snakes, like flood dragons and true dragons.

The white threads appeared soft but were actually extremely sharp. Wherever the black smoke swept, they were pierced through.

The black smoke was omnipresent, always rolling away wisps of whisk spiritual light just as it was shattered.

This treasure battle lasted for hundreds of rounds. Finally, with a somewhat unwilling expression, Nine Spirits Primogenitor recalled the black urn.

Peng Xiang Daoist draped his whisk over his arm. It showed signs of curling and scorching, and even had lost over a hundred whisk threads.

That black urn was so domineering!

He pondered, "Is this true artifact the legendary means by which your sect collects the Nine Heavens Xiao Qi?"

Nine Spirits Primogenitor avoided the question and only said, "This time, I have lost slightly."

Then he changed his tone, a fierce look appearing on his face.

"Next, Daoist, you must be careful!"

Boom!

A loud noise. A nine-colored light shot up into the sky.

A vast nine-colored river swept across the universe, rolling forward.

"Here it comes!"

Peng Xiang Daoist's expression suddenly became extremely grave. This was the legendary Great Nine Heavens Xiao River!

The great technique that had ground down the physical body of the Thunder Prison Roc, forcing Azure Frost Demon Emperor to intervene to save him.

Not a divine ability, yet possessing the power of one!

He took a deep breath, held his whisk, and stepped into the nine-colored river of light.

The previous spell battle and treasure battle were, after all, just friendly sparring.

Who was higher and who was lower didn't matter.

Only this third exchange was the most important deciding factor of this battle.

As soon as Peng Xiang Daoist entered, the sound of crackling collisions rang out continuously.

Nine Spirits Primogenitor gradually disappeared into the river of light, his true form unseen.

The surging collision of magical power scattered the spiritual qi that had condensed some Water Mirror spells, and many cultivators suddenly lost their observation points, causing an uproar.

But Luo Chen was unaffected. His eyes only grew more golden, staring fixedly at the battlefield.

After repeated observations, he had to admit that Nine Spirits Primogenitor's technique was becoming more and more proficient, more and more powerful.

Merging Xiao Qi into magical power, its power was truly domineering.

He himself had become quite adept at controlling the Yuantu Sword over the years, but had instead neglected the refinement of the Red Xiao Sword Qi, still stagnating at around a dozen strands, long unable to step into the mastery level.

It wasn't that Luo Chen had neglected the cultivation of the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*.

After all, from the beginning, his purpose in cultivating this technique was to better control the Yuantu Sword.

As for the Red Xiao Sword Qi, powerful as it was, it was a one-time use. Releasing it in battle was somewhat wasteful.

Now it seemed he might have underestimated the upper limit of the Red Xiao Sword Qi.

After some deliberation, Luo Chen had already formed the idea of properly cultivating this sword canon once things settled down.

Time slowly passed.

This battle finally reached its end.

The nine-colored light suddenly withdrew, revealing the clear sky and bright sun.

Nine Spirits Primogenitor stood in the void, a smile in his eyes and on his brows. He made a bowing salute: "Daoist, you yield!"

Peng Xiang Daoist stood in place, his hair bun slightly disheveled, his expression somewhat dazed.

After a long while, he nodded regretfully. "I have lost. Thank you, Primogenitor, for holding back."

Then he calmly flew back to Divine Origin Sage's side, speaking in low tones.

And Nine Spirits Primogenitor also flew back, greeted by thunderous cheers.

Two battles, both victories. From now on, Motian Domain belonged to the Luo Tian Sect!

They, the low-level cultivators, wouldn't have to participate in real, bloody warfare.

It could be said that the two moves by War Demon King Wang Yuan and Nine Spirits Primogenitor had reduced losses for everyone.

So next...

With the Old Ghost of Motian unwilling, it was announced that the cultivators of Motian Cliff would withdraw from Motian Domain and choose another domain for their conquest war.

Just as the hundred thousand cultivators were about to leave, Luo Chen suddenly spoke.

"Fellow Daoist Divine Origin, since we have a three-battle agreement, ending it so anticlimactically is truly disappointing. What do you think?"

At these words, everyone fell silent, all looking at Luo Chen with disbelief.

Two wins out of three, they had already won. Why fight anymore?

Was he deliberately provoking a Grand Cultivator?

On the treasure ship, the assembled Nascent Soul Sages spoke out anxiously.

"Pill Master!"

"Brother Luo, why are you doing this?"

"Divine Origin is a senior, having attained the Dao before you."

"Yes, he is no wounded Seven Rhinoceros Commander. You are courting disaster against him!"

Even some cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect couldn't help but speak up, trying to dissuade him.

Wang Yuan was puzzled. Luo Chen had never been so reckless. Why insist on this?

Sima Huiniang and Gu Caiyi exchanged glances, revealing deep worry.

Li Yingzhang was even ready to rush up and stop his own Supreme Elder.

If they were like this, how could the other sect cultivators and scattered cultivator forces participating in the conquest war not be curious?

Was Dan Master Luo Chen so fond of battle?

In a corner not far from the Luo Tian Sect cultivators, several dozen people from the Qingdan Valley gathered together, discussing in low voices.

"Is that still Dan Chenzi?"

"The Luo Chen I know has always been polite and rarely actively antagonizes others."

"Divine Origin Sage is, after all, a senior. He should be given some respect. Why is the Pill Master being so unreasonable, insisting on fighting even after winning?"

"And it's a battle across realms!"

Hearing these discussions, Qingdan Valley's Sect Master, Tao Wan Shangren, pursed her lips, a hint of recollection in her eyes.

Earlier, she had felt that Luo Chen had changed too much, become unfamiliar.

But now, this scene overlapped with the memory of Luo Chen leading his forces to attack the Flame Alliance back then.

"He hasn't changed. He's always been like this!"

"Towards those stronger than him in strength and influence, he holds reverence in his heart, but never fear."

"As long as someone has a grudge against him, he never hesitates to retaliate."

"So, what deep hatred does Divine Origin Sage have with him?"

Tao Wan raised her slender neck, gazing into the distance at that back.

That back took two more steps forward, raising a single finger high.

"One move!"

"Divine Origin, how about we each use one move?"

"Or are you afraid to accept?"

After a moment of silence came endless uproar.

Amidst the clamor, Divine Origin Sage stopped in his tracks and turned to look at the man whose face held a smile, but whose eyes were like a cold, deep abyss.

Beside him, the Old Ghost of Motian kept urging: "Fight him, fight him! Kill him! This Motian Domain was originally your sect's base camp for opening up the Five Elements Domain. Now that the Luo Tian Sect has taken it, your sect will have to pay a great price to gain a foothold later. Divine Origin, are you willing to just hand it over?"

Peng Xiang Daoist of White Deer Cave advised, "There's no need for this. The Pill Master is now a talent specifically requested by the Nether Origin Holy Land. Unless you want to kill him in front of everyone and offend the Holy Land, this battle is meaningless."

Divine Fire Sage, who had been silent all along, muttered, "Senior Brother, our sect is about to return. There's no need to stir up more trouble, is there?"

Facing the words around him, facing the countless questioning gazes due to his delayed response, the corner of Divine Origin Sage's mouth, hidden beneath his black robe, slowly curled upward.

"I grant you."
