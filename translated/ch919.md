# Chapter 919: Far Escape to the Nether Abyss, Awakening an Old Friend

Within the azure void, a dark, lusterless flying shuttle silently wove its way through.

Its speed was extreme, yet because of the lightless beast hide covering its surface, its aura remained concealed.

Even if an Infant Soul Sage were in the same area, without releasing their divine sense to observe carefully, it would be difficult to detect the existence of the Frost Eagle Shuttle.

Inside the shuttle, after the excitement of Luo Chen's safe return had passed, things gradually grew chaotic.

"Why did we suddenly leave the Luotian Domain, which we've managed for eighteen years?"

"So sudden! One moment we were listening to Senior Uncle Qu's lecture, the next the Supreme Elder appeared and brought us onto the shuttle."

"I still have a rare high-grade spirit herb planted in my cave dwelling!"

"Junior Brother, that's nothing. I had commissioned a cultivation-assisting magic tool at Lingtian Pass. I already paid for the materials and the deposit. Now that we're leaving, it's all lost."

"Will Xiao Wan wait for me?"

"The Panshi Gang and Tianlang Hui are fighting fiercely lately. They spent a fortune to hire me as a mediator. Now that I've left without saying goodbye, I'm afraid they..."

"So, where exactly are we going?"

When this last question was thrown out, it instantly captured everyone's attention.

For a moment, heated discussions broke out.

Unlike the cabins where the disciples were staying, Luo Chen's area was relatively quiet.

Only Qu Lingjun was by his side, listening to Luo Chen explain some precautions for the upcoming Adept-stage cultivation.

These were things a master should do for his disciple, and Luo Chen was no exception to that.

At this moment, Qu Lingjun had not only achieved the Adept realm but had also changed into the robe Luo Chen had given him. Wearing it made him look radiant and spirited, finally shedding that weary, exhausted feeling from years past, adding a touch of unrestrained elegance.

"Indeed, this robe suits you better than it suits me."

The Snowfall Frost Robe was a water-attribute true artifact, specially refined for alchemists to guard against the fire poison that could invade during long days and months of alchemy.

But Luo Chen was already a master of fire techniques; what need did he have for such a thing?

In fact, wearing it for extended periods made him feel vaguely uncomfortable.

Qu Lingjun smiled, then hesitated as he glanced outside.

"Master, shouldn't you do something about them?"

Luo Chen waved his hand and said calmly, "There's nothing to manage. Wasn't it the same way when we left Dahe Market back then?"

Qu Lingjun nodded in understanding, a look of emotion in his eyes.

Back then, he had been just a child. But having lost his parents at a young age, he had matured early.

He knew the story of the Luo Tian Sect leaving their homeland like the back of his hand.

At that time, the Luo Tian Society had few members, even less cohesion, and not even many experts.

When they left, many people couldn't bear to part with their homeland.

After they left, they encountered a demon king blocking their path, were looked down upon by the natives of Taishan Market, and even suffered heavy losses from an ambush midway.

In the end, didn't they settle down in Tianlan Immortal City?

And by moving to a new place, the Luo Tian Society gained new development, which led to the Luo Tian Sect of today.

He believed that the far-sighted decisions made by his master were always the most beneficial for the sect's current development.

However, just because Luo Chen didn't bother with what the people below thought didn't mean no one else cared.

Li Yingzhang appeared before the group of disciples at just the right time.

"Sect Master!"

"Sect Master!"

Facing a pair of curious, doubtful eyes, Li Yingzhang paused briefly, then explained the reason for their departure and the destination of this journey.

"The demon race has amassed troops beyond the six eastern domains. You all must know this."

"The Tianyuan Dao Sect has landed in the Eastern Wilderness. This matter you might not be clear about yet."

"Yes, that behemoth from the Central Continent, even more formidable than the Nether Origin Sect!"

"Next, in the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, whether the demon race returns in force or the Dao Sect seeks to establish a foothold, a bloody storm is inevitable."

"My Luo Tian Sect has suffered much hardship. Even after eighteen years of rest and recovery, we haven't fully regained our vitality. We can't compare to those old-line Infant Soul Sage Sects. If we were to be dragged into the vortex of another great war between the two races again, you can all imagine what our fate would be."

"You don't want to experience those days of not knowing if you'll live to see tomorrow again, do you?"

"Therefore, the Supreme Elder, planning for the sect's future, risked his life to cross the Nether Abyss and found a blessed land for the Luo Tian Sect that is in no way inferior to the Luotian Domain."

"When the time comes, our Luo Tian Sect will simply hide in this blessed land and rule ourselves, ignoring the changing seasons of the outside world!"

A disciple asked curiously, "Where are we going?"

Li Yingzhang answered steadily, "The old site of the Nether Abyss, the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land!"

Hearing this answer, cries of surprise arose within the shuttle.

They might not understand the significance of the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, but who didn't know the weight of those words "old site of the Nether Abyss"?

That was once the cultivation ground of the Nether Origin Holy Land, the overlord of the Eastern Wilderness!

"But, haven't we heard that the Nether Origin Sect was flooded by the Boundless Netherwater? How are we supposed to get in? And, are we going to hide forever?"

Regarding these questions, which he himself had once asked, Li Yingzhang was now confident.

Because Luo Chen had already told him the answers.

"As for how to cross the Nether Abyss, the Supreme Elder has his own means. You need not worry."

"As for when we will open the mountain gates wide and re-establish contact with the outside world?"

Li Yingzhang paused, his hopeful gaze falling upon the crowd.

"When you all have achieved success in your cultivation and can withstand any storm the cultivation world throws at you, that will be the day our Luo Tian Sect opens its mountain gates wide, and its disciples walk the Mountains and Seas Realm."

"Everyone, strive diligently in your cultivation!"

...

Li Yingzhang's words to the group of Initiate and Qi Refinement disciples were not shielded by any soundproofing.

Many Adept-stage cultivators, and even the high-ranking members of the Luo Tian Sect, released their divine senses and listened in.

Thus, Luo Chen was spared the trouble of explaining everything one by one.

After they finished listening, they understood the Supreme Elder's good intentions.

Although the Luo Tian Sect had a considerable number of experts, when it came to the sect's foundation, it truly couldn't compare to the old-line Infant Soul Sage Sects.

If they were to experience another great war between humans and demons, their fate would likely be even worse than before.

Finding a secluded place to cultivate diligently—didn't this coincide with the secular philosophy of "store up grain, build high walls, and delay claiming kingship"?

In fact, this was also related to the Luo Tian Sect's current small number of disciples.

Any slightly larger Infant Soul Sage Sect would never be able to do this so easily.

One could only say that a small boat is easy to turn around!

Inside the room, Luo Chen withdrew his divine sense and looked at Qu Lingjun.

"At ease now?"

Qu Lingjun smiled. "With Master here, I am at ease in everything. It's just that I had promised Qin'er that after achieving the Adept realm, I would go with her to visit the Fenghua Palace. I'm afraid that trip won't happen now."

"There will be opportunities in the future."

Luo Chen sighed, stood up, and went alone to the window.

The boundless, trackless azure void, dotted with starlight, met his eyes, bringing with it a certain inexplicable loneliness.

Fortunately, he had his disciple, his family, his friends, and hundreds of disciples and descendants who respected and admired him. He wasn't as lonely as he imagined.

"But in the future, after breaking through this azure void and ascending to the Immortal Realm, I'm afraid I'll be left all alone again."

Thinking of the future, Luo Chen couldn't help but smile.

That was still too far away!

...

A few months later, an unobserved flying boat "fearlessly" charged into the Nether Abyss death ground.

At that time, heaven's thunder met earth's fire, and spatial rifts appeared layer upon layer, causing the hearts of all Luo Tian Sect cultivators to tremble with fear.

It was under these circumstances that Luo Chen left the flying shuttle alone.

"Come out!"

His divine sense spread, and the Xuan Turtle that had been lurking in this area slowly revealed its form beneath the water's surface.

Seeing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

Without this Xuan Turtle to carry them, it would have taken immense effort for him to bring the Luo Tian Sect cultivators inside alone.

Now, it was much more convenient.

The Nirvana Sacred Flame spread out, enveloping the Frost Eagle Shuttle as it dove into the Nether Abyss.

Then, it landed on the Xuan Turtle's back.

At the same time, Luo Chen put away the shuttle, allowing all the Luo Tian Sect disciples to see the Xuan Turtle.

Gasps of admiration and exclamation immediately arose.

The Xuan Turtle seemed somewhat displeased with so many people standing on its back, snorting hot air from its nostrils.

"Remember their auras. They are all my disciples."

Luo Chen said calmly, then waved his hand.

"Let's go. To the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land!"

The Xuan Turtle bellowed, black light radiating from its body to envelop everyone. Its massive body dove down, heading deep into the Nether Abyss.

The Luo Tian Sect, which had once been renowned, vanished with the splashing waves, completely disappearing from the world's sight.

Some said they were destroyed by the enemies of Alchemy Master Luo Chen, with not a single soul left alive.

Some said that experts from the Tianyuan Dao Sect had arrived and taken away the Luo Tian Sect cultivators, because someone had indeed seen Dao Sect experts appearing on Motian Cliff.

Others said that Alchemy Master Luo Chen hadn't died but had survived. Fearing that the Tianyuan Dao Sect would pursue the matter of him switching allegiances, he had taken the entire sect to hide and cultivate in some spirit land deep within the Million Great Mountains.

Various speculations abounded.

But few could guess that the most dangerous place was the safest. Luo Chen had actually led his disciples into the Nether Abyss, now considered a death ground of this realm.

...

This crossing of the Nether Abyss was much easier than the previous time.

Luo Chen knew the general route and direction, and with the Xuan Turtle, which had grown up here, carrying them, and his divine sense spread to guard the surroundings, it took only about two months to reach the entrance to the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

Under the curious gazes of the cultivators, the Alchemy Saint Hall, which had been eroded beyond recognition by the Netherwater, suddenly lit up.

A massive dragon head poked out from inside.

"Master, you're back!"

"Mm."

Luo Chen nodded and waved for everyone to enter.

Sima Huiniang was the first to rush to the front, eager to see her son Luo Lingxi.

Luo Chen called Li Yingzhang over, and the two walked at the very back.

"Supreme Elder?" Li Yingzhang looked at Luo Chen questioningly.

The other party took out a thick piece of beast hide.

"This records the detailed situation of the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land: the grades of the various spirit veins, the general distribution of medicinal herbs and mineral deposits, and some dangerous areas I have already explored. I'm giving you this map. The rest of the sect's construction will be your responsibility."

Li Yingzhang respectfully took the map of the blessed land. With just one glance, his eyes widened.

No wonder the Supreme Elder had said earlier that the new sect's headquarters would be better than the Luotian Domain, not worse.

With just that one glance, he saw the five most conspicuous landmarks.

All were fourth-grade spirit veins!

Thinking back, the entire Luotian Domain only had a single fourth-grade spirit vein. The cave dwellings that could be opened for Infant Soul Sages to cultivate were few and far between.

Unlike this blessed land space, which had a full five!

Any one of them was no less than Motian Cliff. A few cave dwellings could easily be opened to allow a dozen or even twenty Infant Soul Sages to cultivate together.

His shock naturally fell into Luo Chen's eyes.

Hadn't he been the same at first?

Thinking about it later, he felt it was only natural.

As a spirit land highly valued by the three great ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect, the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land was not only a refuge in times of sect disaster. The very fact that it could give birth to a fifth-grade spirit mountain like Yanfu Mountain was enough to show its profound foundation.

Later, it was sealed by Alchemy Saint Chu Yan for several hundred years, making the spiritual energy inside even more abundant.

In the future, there was even hope for it to be promoted to a fifth-grade spirit vein!

Such a special place was more than enough for the mere few hundred people of the Luo Tian Sect to cultivate.

After handing over all the sect's trivial matters to Li Yingzhang, Luo Chen also entered the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

The first thing he saw was Luo Lingxi, standing with his hands open, being hugged tightly by Sima Huiniang. He looked at a loss, his face full of embarrassment.

Others were also curiously watching this scene.

They thought that after eight or nine years, the Supreme Elder's son had become even more handsome and extraordinary, especially those eyes, which were almost exactly like the Supreme Elder's.

However, the onlookers were immediately called away by Li Yingzhang.

They didn't mind. After all, he was just a "waste" who couldn't cultivate.

At eighteen years old, with no spiritual power showing on his body—if that wasn't a "waste," what was?

At the entrance, only Luo Chen's family remained.

Gu Caiyi stood beside Luo Chen, smiling. "Huiniang, let go of your big son. He's this old now. Give him some face in front of others!"

"Mother?"

Luo Lingxi called out awkwardly. Sima Huiniang seemed to wake from a dream and finally loosened her tight grip.

"My son, you don't know how worried I've been about you all these years. Especially when I heard about the Nether Origin Sect..."

Luo Chen walked over and said calmly, "He has grown up and matured. Don't treat him like a child anymore."

Sima Huiniang stroked Luo Lingxi's face, which was gradually showing a more rugged outline. He was no longer the thin, weak child of yesteryear.

Matured?

In her eyes, Luo Lingxi was still far from grown up!

"Father!" Luo Lingxi called out respectfully to Luo Chen.

"Mm. Spend some good time with your mother these days. She misses you terribly."

"I understand, son."

Luo Chen nodded and walked past him.

But in the moment they passed each other, Luo Chen's heart stirred slightly. He turned back and carefully examined Luo Lingxi.

The young man couldn't be called burly, but having practiced some martial arts, he had indeed shed his former frail appearance.

But why had he just felt a sense of unease?

His divine sense swept over Luo Lingxi, who was as transparent as glass before him.

There was nothing unusual about his entire body, except for the most familiar Devil Heart Gu.

"Father, is there anything else?"

Luo Lingxi seemed a bit nervous in front of Luo Chen.

Luo Chen frowned, saying there was nothing.

But as he left, he called Black King over.

One man, one dragon, flew into the vast, empty sky.

"During the time I was away, did anything happen in the blessed land?"

"Reporting to Master, nothing major happened. There was just one wave of low-level ghost creatures that attacked. But their target wasn't the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land. After the formation of the Alchemy Saint Hall was eroded, a large amount of pill fragrance leaked out, attracting them to scramble for it."

"And Lingxi?"

"With me here, the young master is even less likely to have any problems!"

"That's good, then!"

Luo Chen suppressed the unease in his heart, chalking it up to not having seen Luo Lingxi for a while. The father-son relationship, which had barely begun to thaw, had become awkward again.

Black King asked curiously, "Master, where are we going now?"

Luo Chen's gaze sharpened, landing on a mountain peak where figures were bustling about busily.

"An old friend has been sleeping for too long. It's time to wake her."

The giant dragon landed on a third-grade spirit mountain rich with pure spiritual energy, drawing the gazes of a group of female cultivators.

Jueqing Fairy walked out from among them and bowed respectfully.

"Greetings, Supreme Elder. Greetings, Lord Black King."

Luo Chen waved his hand. "No need for formalities. Take me to see Fellow Daoist Canglong!"

Jueqing Fairy's eyes lit up, and she quickly led the way.
