# Chapter 959: Total Suppression, Wild Beast Source Blood

Within the rolling yellow wind.

A figure drifted, elusive and hard to track.

But his gaze was fixed firmly on the battlefield in the distance.

"Among the seven, the three great cultivators are the most troublesome. The remaining four—whether White Profound Lord, Purple Robed Marquis, or the Hidden Sword Pavilion Master—are all experts of long-standing fame."

"Only this man has a strange face and unknown origins."

"From his series of actions upon entering the Yin-Yang Realm, this person excels in body refinement, and when opening the imitation Life and Death Gate, he expended too much strength."

"A body refiner, expended too much strength..."

The Second Ancestor of the Golden Lion muttered these things to himself, gradually growing confident.

Taking this man as the first target should be the easiest.

Later, he could concentrate his strength and awaken more Golden Dragon Lions to deal with the others.

Yet, as he watched, the situation within the Yellow Wind Formation gradually became somewhat off.

"This person seems a bit too strong!"

...

The Life-Seizing Golden Plume specialized in piercing.

But at this moment, in Luo Chen's hand as he swung it, it was like a long whip, incomparably sharp.

With cracking sounds, it moved like a swimming dragon, rustling loudly.

With every lash, cracks appeared on a Golden Dragon Lion. Then, as the golden plume was withdrawn, that Golden Dragon Lion shattered inch by inch like porcelain.

Two phantom figures danced across the earth. No matter how many Golden Dragon Lions there were, none could harm them in the slightest.

As Luo Chen swung the Life-Seizing Golden Plume, he kept his attention fully focused, constantly watching the changes around him.

His powerful divine sense spread out quietly.

Capturing any moment of opportunity.

"Hiding that well, are you?"

Luo Chen's heart stirred. Seeing the number of Golden Dragon Lions increasing, he understood the intention of the enemy hidden in the shadows.

These dragon-lion statues, once awakened, were not actually as weak as they appeared.

Their hundred-zhang size said it all!

Among the demon race, aside from special species, body size could often simply measure one's realm.

A hundred zhang was already the standard for the fourth rank.

These dragon-lions had little intelligence, no demon arts or magical treasures to rely on, purely relying on their physical strength. Thus, their threat was not great.

But even if they couldn't compare to fourth-rank demon emperors, the strength they displayed was still around the late third rank.

In the short time it took to burn an incense stick, the number of Golden Dragon Lions had already exceeded a hundred, as if inexhaustible.

If it were any other Nascent Soul cultivator, under such relentless consumption, they would have lost most of their mana.

If it were a body refiner, lacking means, fighting head-on would likely result in even greater losses.

The hidden enemy had calculated well.

Trying to exhaust Luo Chen's strength this way.

But he had miscalculated one thing!

That was Luo Chen's strength far surpassed these Golden Dragon Lions!

Things that could be destroyed with a casual strike, how could they block Luo Chen's path?

However!

Luo Chen suddenly withdrew the golden plume in his hand and slowly flew backward.

As if his strength was failing.

When the number of dragon-lions gathered around him reached a dozen, instead of retreating, he advanced and struck suddenly.

His hands formed seals, and the Nascent Soul mana that had been sealed for many days poured out.

A lifelike phoenix flew out from behind him, letting out a clear cry.

Its wings trembled, scattering large sparks.

Then...

"Go!"

With fingers together like a sword, he released it.

The fire phoenix circled once above Luo Chen's head, then swooped down toward the dozen or so dragon-lions on the ground.

Wherever it passed, the previously majestic Golden Dragon Lions instantly turned to ash.

It didn't end there.

The fire phoenix, carrying boundless momentum, began sweeping wildly around Luo Chen with an unquestionable attitude.

Even the rolling yellow wind could not suppress the fire phoenix's movements.

Ten li, twenty li, thirty li...

By the time it reached a hundred li in radius, the fire phoenix finally dissipated.

But that was enough!

"Little mouse, have you hidden enough?"

In the void, a section of golden plume stabbed out abruptly.

Clang!

The Second Ancestor of the Golden Lion stumbled out, revealing his true form.

His face was filled with terror, as if he had never imagined the other party had such means.

"A spell? A divine ability?"

That fire phoenix technique was too powerful. Even the Yellow Wind Formation set up by Nether Departure Divine Lord could not suppress it.

Even more, while he was shocked, it forced him out.

What shocked him even more was that someone capable of such heaven-defying means could not be a simple body refiner!

Dual cultivation of Dharma and body!

As this thought emerged, the Second Ancestor of the Golden Lion already understood that the man before him was by no means a pure body refiner.

His troublesome nature was likely even above the other three mid-Nascent Soul cultivators!

"Retreat!"

Without thinking, he was about to flee into the Yellow Wind Formation.

But the distant man only sneered coldly.

"Thinking of running now? Too late!"

Then, his hands formed seals as he struck out restrictions one after another toward the void.

Weng! Weng! Weng!

Strange sounds came from the void, as if something had been frozen, or as if something had been cut out.

Suddenly!

"Ten Directions Sealed, Hundred Li Air Forbidden!"

The rolling yellow wind that had been flowing endlessly suddenly dissipated.

Not only that, but the Second Ancestor of the Golden Lion, who had been about to flee into the Yellow Wind Formation, found himself crashing into a wall.

His body, which had already merged most of the way in, was forcibly pushed out.

The Second Ancestor of the Golden Lion's expression was shocked as he blurted out.

"Spatial Restriction Art!"

"Good eyesight."

Luo Chen chuckled lightly, his hands brushing over the Flat Mountain Crown on his head, and the two Life-Seizing Golden Plumes returned to their original positions.

What he had just performed was indeed a spatial restriction.

He had not studied much of the foundation of spatial formations before, or rather, most ordinary formation masters were not proficient in it.

Even back then, when Heaven Smelter refined his life-bound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he had specially invited Azure Frost to make up for the shortcomings in spatial restrictions.

That Luo Chen could now so calmly use such means was the result of his careful observation and study of a large number of natural spatial restrictions during his time at Mi Jie Mountain.

And who the hypothetical enemy this technique was aimed at was self-evident.

As for whether it would be effective when he actually faced that person, Luo Chen was not yet sure.

But for now, at least this ancestral elder of the Golden Lion Empire had no way to counter it.

His demeanor was calm as he walked step by step toward the other party, his eyes emitting a strange light.

"Name!"

The Second Ancestor of the Golden Lion swallowed his saliva and subconsciously replied, "Kuang Nanzi, Second Ancestor of the Golden Lion Empire, from the Life and Death Gate."

"Since you are a cultivator of the Life and Death Gate, why are you hiding in this realm?"

Kuang Nanzi replied, "The ancestral elder of our sect had an accident in his cultivation. The Blessing Gu went out of control, causing the origin qi ocean to sink, turning fortune into disaster."

"What secret does this realm hold?"

Kuang Nanzi answered without thinking, "This is where Ancestral Master Yin-Yangzi fell. It contains a large number of his research achievements in the dao of gu insects during his lifetime, and countless powerful beings have undergone mutations. The establishment of the Yang Realm was precisely to suppress the powerful existences in the Yin Realm."

"Why did you attack me?"

"Divide and conquer, pick them off one by one..."

Halfway through his words, Kuang Nanzi's body suddenly trembled, and then a look of terror appeared on his face.

"Illusion Art!"

Luo Chen, who had already walked within ten zhang, could not help but grunt, a trace of unnatural flush flashing across his face.

He lightly exhaled a mouthful of turbid qi and praised, "As expected of a disciple of the Holy Land. In such a short time, you broke free from my Mirror Flower Water Moon."

Seeing this, Kuang Nanzi's heart grew even more fearful.

This man was not only a dual cultivator of Dharma and body but also possessed unimaginable soul foundation.

He had actually used an illusion art on him while he was shocked earlier, making him fall for it without realizing.

The words "as expected of a disciple of the Holy Land" were both praise for him and, in a sense, praise for himself.

Even a disciple of the Holy Land had fallen for it without knowing. It showed that the other party not only had a powerful soul foundation but also had reached the pinnacle in the dao of illusion arts.

Especially!

When he broke through the illusion's suppression, the other party had clearly suffered a great backlash, yet he suppressed it easily.

This showed just a corner of the other party's immense strength.

Such an existence was probably not only above the three—Purple Robed Marquis, White Profound Lord, and Hidden Sword Pavilion Master—but even comparable to those three, Li Tianshi and the others.

No!

Even those three would not have made him so embarrassed.

Facing Luo Chen, who was getting closer and closer, Kuang Nanzi could not suppress the fear rising in his heart. He subconsciously stepped back.

"Misunderstanding!"

"It's all a misunderstanding!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly, his gaze sweeping over the surroundings, pausing briefly on the red spots on the ground, then withdrawing his gaze and asking in return, "Gathering so many Golden Dragon Lions to surround and kill me, you call this a misunderstanding?"

Kuang Nanzi quickly waved his hand, "If you, sir, can let bygones be bygones, then these are all misunderstandings."

"Reason. Give me a reason to let bygones be bygones." Luo Chen still maintained his steady pace forward.

Kuang Nanzi, suppressed by the air restriction, had nowhere left to retreat.

He swallowed his saliva and said with a bluster that belied his inner fear, "If we fight to the death, even if I die, you won't fare well either."

As he spoke, he raised something like a crystal ball.

"This is the core crystal of the ancient wild beast Golden Dragon Lion, controlling the Golden Dragon Lion statues within ten thousand li. If you push me too far, I will destroy it directly. Then, thousands upon thousands of Golden Dragon Lions will come roaring, and under the suppression of the Yellow Wind Formation, you will definitely not be able to retreat unscathed!"

Luo Chen stopped.

He stroked his chin thoughtfully and said, "What you say does have some merit... relying on these trash?"

Suddenly, Luo Chen moved in an instant.

His body was like thunder, flashing past.

Appearing behind the other party, he struck out with a palm.

Hong!

The afterimage dissipated, and Kuang Nanzi appeared on the other side.

He was both shocked and angry. "Despicable!"

Then, he could not help but spit out a mouthful of blood.

Under that palm wind, even though he had dodged it, he was still grazed by a trace.

Just that trace had broken through his protective qi shield and damaged his physical body.

Once a qi cultivator was closed in on by a body refiner, the consequences were this tragic.

Luo Chen slowly raised his right hand and touched his ear.

Swoosh!

A stone rod suddenly appeared in his hand, gripped tightly.

"When you used the dragon-lions to surround and kill me earlier, hiding in the shadows, did you ever consider the word 'despicable'?"

Kuang Nanzi was both ashamed and annoyed, but under the threat of life and death, he had to beg for mercy. "Spare my life, and I can tell you the locations of some treasure stores in the Yang Realm of the Yin-Yang Realm. Don't you want to go to the Yin Realm through the Death Gate? I also know some opportunities in the Yin Realm."

Faced with condition after condition, Luo Chen remained unmoved, casually twirling the rod in a flower.

Somewhat rusty staff techniques gradually became familiar under his movements.

Seeing him unmoved, Kuang Nanzi grew anxious.

"Spare my life, and I will acknowledge you as master and be your slave!"

"Oh?"

Luo Chen's eyes lit up, and the long rod in his hand paused. His lips parted slightly.

Seeing this, Kuang Nanzi's heart rejoiced.

But the two words the other party spat out made his mind tremble with terror.

"Too late!"

The next moment!

The void boomed, and Luo Chen suddenly appeared above his head, a stone rod smashing down!

Kuang Nanzi had long been on guard. An inkstone appeared before him, blocking the rod.

Not only that, his robe swelled like a ball, and a mana qi shield spread out.

But facing triple defenses, Luo Chen only needed one rod.

Heaven-shaking monstrous force erupted in an instant!

"Pfft!"

Kuang Nanzi was struck as if by lightning. A mouthful of blood sprayed out uncontrollably, and his entire body fell toward the earth like a stone.

Luo Chen pursued relentlessly, stepping with Moon Step, appearing behind him, and struck again with the rod.

Hong!

One rod after another!

Hong!

A flying sword suddenly appeared, but Luo Chen ignored it, letting it stab at him.

The flying sword broke through the qi armor of the Mystic Dust Armor, but upon touching the inner armor, it was bounced away.

A defensive magic treasure at the true artifact level—how could it be broken so easily?

Especially since the other party was already powerless to attack.

The flying sword's attack was ineffective. Kuang Nanzi, while fully defending, still struggled desperately. With blood at the corners of his mouth, he spat out a stream of light.

But before that stream of light could approach Luo Chen's body, it was burned to ashes by the suddenly appearing flames.

Hong!

Hong!

The Heaven-Equalizing Rod continued to strike Kuang Nanzi. Under the relentless bombardment, the latter's aura grew increasingly listless.

When that inkstone was smashed, and his entire dharma robe was tattered beyond recognition, Kuang Nanzi's eyes showed despair, and then madness!

"If you won't let me live, then you won't have it easy either!"

His originally listless aura suddenly swelled.

He wanted to self-destruct his Nascent Soul!

Luo Chen suddenly stopped, standing in the void.

"Daoist, there's no need for that."

Kuang Nanzi was stunned. Was the other party afraid of a desperate man?

But the next moment, from the corner of his eye, he saw five streams of light suddenly appear from various angles, piercing through his protective qi shield and entering his body.

In an instant, he tried to mobilize his mana, but he couldn't at all.

"This is?"

"Five-Pronged Suppression Cones!"

Luo Chen answered the last question of his life, and then the stone rod came down, smashing him into a pulp of flesh.

Even his Nascent Soul had no time to escape, shattering abruptly.

A large amount of spiritual energy was released, forming a storm of spiritual energy.

Luo Chen stood within it, beckoning to collect a few items.

This Kuang Nanzi was not the Nascent Soul cultivator he had killed most easily.

But he was the one he had suppressed from start to finish, a Nascent Soul cultivator who had no power to fight back.

"All this cultivation to the mid-Nascent Soul stage, wasted."

Luo Chen shook his head, the Heaven-Equalizing Rod in his hand shrinking into a white needle that disappeared into his ear.

Then he walked out of the spiritual energy storm with ease.

He did not leave immediately but casually summoned pools of red liquid on the ground.

The amount was not large. Each Golden Dragon Lion statue left only a palm-sized pool.

After removing the sand and mud, Luo Chen fell into thought.

After a long while, he muttered uncertainly, "Could these be diluted wild beast source blood?"

In his memory, a certain matter surfaced.

He had obtained a Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree from the Demon-Fall Land. This tree was of extremely high grade and very difficult to cultivate. After over a hundred years, it had not yet bloomed or borne fruit.

After extensive research, Sang Jinghe had once said that it needed the essence blood of ancient great demons as nourishment to continue cultivation.

The blood left by these Golden Dragon Lions, though extremely weak in effect, was actually not low in grade. It had merely been diluted and then made into individual dragon-lion statues using special techniques from the Southern Frontier.

If he collected enough source blood from the Golden Dragon Lions, could he use it to cultivate the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree?

"It seems the time to reunite with Li Tianshi and the others will have to be delayed."

With a slight smile, Luo Chen's divine sense swept out, instantly destroying the divine sense imprint left on Kuang Nanzi's storage ring.

Then, the dragon-lion core crystal appeared before him.

(Chapter End)
