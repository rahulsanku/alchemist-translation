# Chapter 1134: Subduing the Enemy in an Instant

"It's the Meteor Bandits!"

"Run!"

"Run where? Gather inward, hold the convoy!"

As the bandits galloped closer and closer, even the mortals in the convoy could now recognize that it wasn't an avalanche at all, but a thundering horde of horse bandits.

Especially when some of the more knowledgeable cultivators in the convoy identified the bandits' origin, everyone became even more vigilant.

Fortunately, the City Lord's Mansion merchant convoy was experienced. Under the orders of various experts, they quickly stabilized their formation.

Steward Yan stood atop a carriage, surveying the surroundings, taking in the entire situation.

A dozen or so men ahead, ten on each flank.

None in the rear. But that wasn't good news.

Because the City Lord's Mansion convoy was rather cumbersome—the rear was packed with a large number of goods and pack animals, making it impossible to turn around easily.

Most importantly, although the incoming force was small, barely a tenth of the convoy's size, every single one of those men standing astride their white horses was a body-tempering cultivator. Each face was filled with murderous intent, clearly seasoned veterans who had seen blood.

In an instant, Steward Yan's heart sank to the bottom.

"What are the Meteor Bandits?"

A low voice came from behind him. Though quiet, it carried an immense sense of security.

Steward Yan's spirits lifted. He ducked into the carriage and quickly began explaining to Luo Chen.

"The Meteor Bandits are a group of horse bandits that rose up in the past ten years. Because they come and go like the wind, moving with great speed, and their chieftain is skilled with a pair of meteor hammers, they're called the Meteor Bandits. They lurk in the deep mountains and forests, active along the main roads of Immortal Relic City. Every member is at least a cultivator of the first stage of body tempering. Ordinary merchant convoys are no match for them. In the past ten years, the Meteor Bandits have become a major thorn in the side of Immortal Relic City!"

After finishing in one breath, Steward Yan paused to catch his breath, then said with feigned calmness, "But don't worry, Senior. They've only ever dared to rob small and medium-sized convoys. We're the City Lord's Mansion's convoy. Once I go out and reveal our identity, they'll surely disperse."

With that, Steward Yan exited the carriage.

In those few brief words, the bandits had already charged to the front of the convoy, loosely but strategically surrounding the large convoy.

Horses snorted hot breath as they paced restlessly around the convoy.

Inside the carriage, Luo Chen remained calm, holding a teacup and gently blowing on the steam. Jing Lan sat beside him, fidgeting nervously.

"Brother Luo Chen..."

"No need to panic."

Just three simple words, yet they seemed to contain immense power. The woman gradually calmed down.

Outside the carriage, Steward Yan's aged but vigorous voice rang out.

"May I ask which chieftain of the Meteor Bandits graces us with their presence? I am Yan Song, steward of the Immortal Relic City Lord's Mansion. Greetings!"

His voice, amplified by his inner strength, echoed slowly through the low snowy mountains. The sounds of horses neighing and men shouting were momentarily suppressed.

But there was no response!

Steward Yan took a deep breath, his gaze sweeping over the bandits circling like vultures, finally settling on the eight-foot-tall, blade-carrying strongman at the front.

"Brother, have you not seen the banner at the front of our convoy? We are the City Lord's Mansion's convoy. Do you dare to provoke the City Lord's Mansion?"

This time, there was a response—but it only made his heart sink.

"Immortal Relic City doesn't just have the City Lord's Mansion." The eight-foot-tall strongman spoke slowly as he drew his blade, raising it high toward the sky, then slashing it downward. "Attack!"

The moment the words fell, the surrounding bandits clamped their legs, spurring their horses along the convoy, and struck without hesitation.

"Kill!"

"Kill!"

In an instant, chaos erupted. Cries of agony rang out, and blood stained the white snow.

Steward Yan was furious. Though they had many men, most were mortals, with very few martial artists. Facing these bandits who moved like the wind and killed without blinking, they were quickly overwhelmed.

"Form a defensive formation! Cut down the horses and kill the enemy!"

Steward Yan roared, then fixed his gaze on the eight-foot-tall strongman at the head.

Capture the chieftain to capture the bandits!

If he could kill this man, these seemingly elite bandits would surely scatter like birds and beasts.

With a surge of inner strength, he leaped out.

His feet stepped rapidly across the tops of carriages and horses, like a fierce tiger descending a mountain, pouncing on the enemy. His surging blood energy coalesced outside his body into a phantom tiger head, roaring fiercely.

Facing Steward Yan's assault, the eight-foot-tall strongman grinned, a cruel light flashing in his eyes. He neither dodged nor evaded, gripping his sword hilt with both hands before slashing downward.

*Boom!*

A crimson blade aura, domineering and sharp, tore through the phantom tiger like ripping cloth, cleaving it in two.

The clash lasted only an instant.

The next moment, a figure flew backward.

He crashed into the convoy, sending people and horses tumbling.

"Dan Jin! Blood River Realm peak!"

Gasps of shock rose from the crowd, their voices thick with fear.

The eight-foot-tall strongman swept a contemptuous gaze over the few martial artists in the convoy. Though they were also in the Blood River Realm, these second-stage martial artists who hadn't condensed Dan Jin were utterly beneath him.

Even Steward Yan, a late-stage Blood River Realm expert with defensive Hua Jin covering his entire body, couldn't withstand a single explosive strike from his blade.

Dan Jin and Hua Jin—a single realm's difference was like a chasm!

Just then, a desperate cry came from where Steward Yan had fallen.

"Senior, please lend us your aid!"

The eight-foot-tall strongman frowned and instinctively looked toward the most ornate carriage at the center of the convoy.

He only heard two words: "As you wish."

The next moment, dozens of streaks of blood light shot out abruptly.

*Pfft! Pfft! Pfft!*

Each streak of blood light, as if guided by intelligence, struck precisely into the head of every bandit on the battlefield.

Whether they faced forward or had their backs turned, a finger-sized hole appeared in the center of each forehead.

It all happened too fast for anyone to react.

By the time they did, some of the convoy's fighters were still hacking at enemies who were already dead and defenseless.

Seeing this, the eight-foot-tall strongman's eyes nearly split with rage. He let out a furious roar.

"Die!"

He suddenly exerted force. The tall horse beneath him let out a mournful whinny and collapsed, kicking up thick snow.

Using that rebound force, the eight-foot-tall strongman shot into the air, gripping his greatsword. All his inner strength condensed into a single point. In one step, he crossed several dozen zhang and slashed down at the ornate carriage.

This was an unstoppable momentum!

The eight-foot-tall strongman had thought it through clearly. There must be a top-tier expert inside the carriage, but definitely not at the third stage of body tempering—because the technique the other had just used was nothing more than blatant Ming Jin.

That meant, even if the other was a Dan Jin expert like himself, he would have depleted his strength by killing dozens in a single blow.

As long as he seized this window of recovery, he could subdue the enemy in one strike.

If he killed this man, even if today's plunder failed, he could still retreat calmly, summon a second wave of Meteor Bandits, and slaughter this entire convoy.

In an instant, the eight-foot-tall strongman had formulated the perfect plan.

His speed was extreme. His momentum was overwhelming.

To outsiders, only a blur was visible as the eight-foot-tall strongman charged into the carriage. Then came a crisp *clang*.

After that, there was no further sound.

Everyone was stunned!

Steward Yan struggled to his feet with the help of his subordinates, looking at the carriage in shock and uncertainty.

Then he saw Jing Lan, her face slightly pale, lift the curtain and step out. She said to him in a trembling voice, "Steward Yan, Brother Luo said we can continue moving forward."

Steward Yan swallowed hard, already realizing what had happened.

The chieftain of the Meteor Bandits, who had defeated him in a single blow, had been subdued without any chance to fight back in Luo Chen's hands.

With such strength... could that senior be a Furnace Realm expert?

He took a deep breath, suppressing the fear in his heart.

"Clean up! Move forward!"
