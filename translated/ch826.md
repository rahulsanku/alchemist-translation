# Chapter 826: Where to Seek the True Meaning of Laws, Even in Death the Heart for the Dao Is as Iron (Asking for Monthly Votes)

"True Lord Taiyue, would you sell this item to me?"

Luo Chen's sudden words caused the lively atmosphere in the hall, which had been buzzing with discussion about the Enlightenment Pill, to fall silent.

Deng Taiyue was momentarily startled, then a look of delight appeared on his face.

"If the Pill Sovereign wishes to buy, naturally I can sell it!"

"I was here first!"

Medicine Master's voice came through, laced with suppressed anger.

Deng Taiyue's brow furrowed slightly. "Medicine Master, but you haven't brought out anything that satisfies me. Surely you don't expect to exchange the treasures I painstakingly extracted from the Misty Swamp for another one of your empty promises?"

Medicine Master's face flushed red.

Everyone knew he was trying to get something for nothing—that was fine to understand tacitly, but did he have to say it so bluntly?

Humiliating him in front of everyone like this—this Deng Taiyue had truly gone too far.

Yet he didn't realize that Deng Taiyue had grown thoroughly weary of him.

Medicine Master clenched his fists and said resentfully, "Luo Chen hasn't proposed any conditions either. You haven't even heard his price, yet you're willing to sell to him?"

Deng Taiyue hesitated slightly, his gaze involuntarily turning toward the handsome young man.

Luo Chen smiled faintly, a jade bottle appearing in his hand.

"Inside here are three Purple Qi Poison-Resisting Pills, of the fourth tier. They have a decent effect against toxins that harm the soul and spirit. Though their value isn't particularly high, they should barely satisfy True Lord Taiyue's earlier requirements, wouldn't you say?"

Then, with a flick of his hand, a storage pouch appeared.

"Of course, this sect is not one to take advantage of others. There are also some spirit stones here, as a small supplement."

"True Lord Taiyue, what do you think?"

This display was genuinely sincere and impeccable.

Not only did it barely meet the requirements he had stated earlier, but he was also willing to add a batch of spirit stones as compensation.

Compared to Medicine Master, the difference was clear!

Especially that phrase about "taking advantage of others"—when he said it, many people couldn't help but sneak glances at Medicine Master.

Such a low-level trick of insinuation—who couldn't understand the subtext?

The old man was so angry his beard and mustache bristled!

But this time he said nothing more, because anything he said would only make him look worse. He simply fixed his murky eyes on Deng Taiyue.

Deng Taiyue's expression showed some hesitation.

Three fourth-tier pills that could defend against soul toxins—it sounded acceptable.

But it was still somewhat different from what he truly wanted.

Yet, meeting Luo Chen's sincere gaze, he nodded heavily.

"Fine, let's do it that way!"

"But forget the spirit stones."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "Oh?"

Deng Taiyue asked tentatively, "I'm not short on spirit stones for now, but I was wondering if I might have the honor of speaking privately with the Pill Sovereign?"

So that was it!

Luo Chen smiled.

Over the years, though he had been in seclusion, his reputation had only grown.

And because he was in seclusion and refused to see outsiders, many people found it extremely difficult to meet him, let alone ask him for favors.

This Deng Taiyue wanted to use this transaction as an opportunity to build a connection with him!

He nodded lightly and uttered a single word: "Agreed!"

*Smack!*

The sound of a chair being struck rang out in the hall.

Under everyone's astonished gazes, Medicine Master rose to his feet, glared fiercely at Luo Chen and Deng Taiyue, then left Shouyang Mountain without another word.

No one spoke about this extremely undignified display.

But on every face, a meaningful expression appeared.

In the end, neither alchemist had fully satisfied Deng Taiyue's conditions, yet Deng Taiyue had abandoned his long-time associate Medicine Master and chosen the Pill Sovereign, Luo Chen.

Everyone understood perfectly what this meant.

In Deng Taiyue's heart, Luo Chen's importance far exceeded Medicine Master's!

And for the moment, their alchemy skills had also been ranked.

As one of the parties involved, Deng Taiyue sighed softly but made no move to stop Medicine Master.

A hundred years!

Medicine Master had disappointed him time and again. Coupled with the man's advanced age and his focus on his sect's legacy, he had become increasingly indifferent to Deng Taiyue's matter.

He would not continue hanging himself from this withered tree.

As for the Pill Sovereign—this thriving, flourishing tree with its lush branches—perhaps that was where he should place his trust.

...

The trading segment in the main hall of Shouyang Mountain did not stop because of Medicine Master's angry departure.

Under Elder Lu's facilitation, it continued in an orderly manner.

Luo Chen also made occasional purchases, using spirit stones to buy some materials he needed.

Thanks to the corpse of the Frost Eagle Demon Emperor, which Luo Tian Bao Ge had processed and sold, Luo Chen had obtained a substantial amount of spirit stones.

This had replenished his previously dry coffers.

However, using spirit stones to buy materials at this barter-based gathering was somewhat inappropriate.

After all, these long-established True Lords were mostly not short of spirit stones. Especially in the current situation, they preferred to exchange resources that could increase their own strength.

But with Luo Chen's reputation as the "Pill Sovereign," many were happy to do him small favors.

Time passed slowly.

With so many True Lords present, each with considerable wealth and many resources useless to themselves, the resource exchange segment lasted a full day and night.

By the second day at dawn, when disciples of Dongyang Sect served fine wine and pastries, the lively trading segment gradually came to an end.

Through this segment, the relationships between most people had grown somewhat closer.

Elder Lu seized the opportunity to announce the beginning of the segment for exchanging cultivation experiences and insights.

With so many people, discussions could easily become chaotic, so they took turns—one question per person, going around in order.

And the first opportunity to ask a question, Elder Lu directly gave to Luo Chen.

No one questioned this!

Luo Chen did not stand on ceremony. After a moment of contemplation, he spoke unhurriedly.

"This sect has heard that the Great Dao we seek lies in the true meaning of the laws."

"However, the true meaning is difficult to grasp; only great cultivators can capture it. For those at the Nascent Soul stage, unless they are exceptional prodigies, few can comprehend it at their current realm."

"I do not seek to comprehend it. I am merely curious about one thing: if one obtains the true meaning of a law, how should one continue to cultivate it?"

The atmosphere in the hall fell instantly silent.

When Luo Chen mentioned "the true meaning of the laws," many had perked up with interest.

They had thought Luo Chen would ask how to comprehend the true meaning of the laws—something many wanted to know.

But Luo Chen suddenly changed direction, skipping that step entirely, and directly asked how to continue cultivating after comprehending the true meaning.

This question...

Everyone looked at each other, momentarily at a loss for an answer.

Seeing the awkward silence, Nine Spirits Primogenitor, who had a good relationship with Luo Chen, sighed and said, "Such methods are the secret arts of cultivation. Perhaps only sacred lands like the Nether Origin Sect, which have produced Ascendant-level great cultivators, would have records of them!"

Elder Fu also shook his head. "Pill Sovereign, your question leaves us with no way to answer."

Asking the blind for directions?

Luo Chen's gaze swept across the hall, and a look of disappointment appeared on his face.

But when he saw the hesitant expression in Elder Lu's eyes beside him, his heart stirred.

Dongyang Sect had a very deep connection with the Nether Origin Sect. There were even rumors that Dongyang Sect's Supreme Elder was cultivating in seclusion within the Nether Origin Sacred Land. Perhaps they knew something?

Luo Chen's gaze lingered, and others followed his line of sight to Elder Lu.

Elder Lu smiled bitterly.

If he, who had set up this stage, still held back, wouldn't that be shooting himself in the foot?

Sighing, Elder Lu spoke softly: "Day after day, year after year, hold fast to your own Dao, and in the end, you will attain perfection."

Luo Chen's eyes lit up, and he pressed further: "Can this be applied universally to all laws and countless true meanings?"

Elder Lu spread his hands helplessly. "I don't know! I only heard the Supreme Elder mention it once. And he said that while the laws are equal, the true meanings have distinctions of high and low. That sentence I just spoke applies only to the most basic and fundamental true meanings. For more profound true meanings, the cultivation methods might be different."

The brightness in Luo Chen's eyes gradually dimmed, and he sank into deep thought.

The laws are equal, with no distinction of superior or inferior—this corresponds to the core idea that heaven and earth are impartial, treating all things as straw dogs. This includes the Great Dao and its laws.

But for cultivators, these things are merely means to pursue immortality. So naturally, they distinguish which means are more useful.

The true meanings having distinctions of high and low—that was exactly this.

If his Withering-Bloom True Meaning counted as a Wood-type law,

Then the Wood True Meaning that Fu Qinglan had comprehended also counted as a Wood-type law.

Which was higher and which was lower?

The Wood True Meaning should be lower, because it was the most basic true meaning of the law.

So for Fu Qinglan to cultivate the Wood True Meaning, she only needed to persist day after day, year after year, and she would have a chance to perfect it.

But would this method work for the Withering-Bloom True Meaning?

The exchange and discussion within the hall continued.

Luo Chen divided his attention, thinking while listening to the questions being raised.

Occasionally, he would join in and offer his own views.

"Below the true immortals, there are nine realms. The lower three realms are Qi Refinement, the middle three are Spirit Refinement, and the upper three are Law Refinement. Fellow Daoists, we are at the starting point of the middle three realms. The path of Spirit Refinement is fraught with danger, often leading one to fall into the mire of heart demons. If one becomes obsessed and fixated, how should one deal with it?"

"One should seek unhindered thoughts, not be obstructed by external things—only then can one's spirit be refreshed!"

"Magical power is the foundation of our techniques and abilities. When merged with external objects, it should possess unpredictable might. But when I try to merge my magical power with external objects, there is always a sense of disharmony. Please enlighten me!"

"Primogenitor, perhaps you could elaborate?"

"The path to Ascension lies in achieving the Primordial Spirit. The so-called Primordial Spirit is the fusion of the Nascent Soul and the soul. I am very curious about one thing: after achieving the Primordial Spirit, should it reside in the Purple Mansion or sink into the Sea of Consciousness?"

"I have heard something about this matter, Heavenly Master. Even a cunning rabbit has three burrows; the Primordial Spirit should also be elusive. Thus, both the Purple Mansion and the Sea of Consciousness are its homes. When facing an enemy, the opponent cannot determine the vital point, and only then can one pursue immortality without dying."

"I've heard that within the demon army, there is an evil creature that feeds on our Nascent Souls. Yellow Sand True Lord of the Five Elements Divine Sect, Sinking Fish Fairy of Taihu Palace—both died at the hands of that evil creature. Even the sect master of Overlord Saber Sect seems to have fallen in the same way. I want to ask, what exactly is that evil creature?"

...

One question after another emerged from the mouths of the True Lords.

The scope covered was miscellaneous—some about confusion regarding the future, some about their own shortcomings, and some about the current situation...

When the circle was completed, it was already deep into the night.

Many people's questions had not received definitive answers.

But through the mutual exchange, many seemed to vaguely arrive at answers that were neither right nor wrong.

The subsequent exchange was no longer limited to one question per person. Everyone asked freely as they wished, or specifically sought out someone to ask.

Luo Chen found a moment of leisure and was invited to a side hall for a meeting.

It was Deng Taiyue, who had arranged it earlier!

"Pill Sovereign!"

"True Lord Taiyue!"

After exchanging greetings, their previously distant relationship seemed to grow closer.

Finding a chair to sit down, Luo Chen smiled and asked, "Fellow Daoist was so eager to see me—could it be about the Enlightenment Pill?"

Deng Taiyue's spirits lifted. "Exactly that! The Pill Sovereign also knows of the Enlightenment Pill—have you studied it as well?"

Under the other's expectant gaze, Luo Chen shook his head.

"Apologies, but today was the first time I heard of it. Elder Lu introduced it to me."

Deng Taiyue found it hard to believe. "But you are the renowned Pill Sovereign! How could you not know..."

Luo Chen said slowly, "Let me inform you, Fellow Daoist: before I formed my Nascent Soul, I had been traveling in the Northern Sea. I truly have no knowledge of this pseudo-fifth-tier divine pill of the Eastern Wilderness."

Deng Taiyue opened his mouth, and a look of disappointment uncontrollably appeared on his face.

Luo Chen asked curiously, "I see that Fellow Daoist's cultivation is not shallow—fully at the sixth layer of the Nascent Soul stage! Instead of thinking about breaking through to the seventh layer and achieving the Great Cultivator realm, why are you so fixated on the Enlightenment Pill?"

Deng Taiyue sighed and revealed his family's secret.

"To be honest with you, I was exceptionally talented from a young age. I possessed an Earth-type Heavenly Spirit Root and was favored by the elders of Mount Tai Sect, so my cultivation speed was extremely fast. Until the Nascent Soul stage, I never encountered a bottleneck."

"But after advancing to Nascent Soul, my cultivation speed slowed down completely."

Luo Chen nodded slightly. "It's because the Purple Mansion barrier is too thin, isn't it?"

"Yes." Deng Taiyue nodded as well. "The drawback of the Heavenly Spirit Root begins to manifest at the Nascent Soul stage. The Purple Mansion barrier is too thin, making it easy for the Nascent Soul to be assimilated by heaven and earth. So we cultivators with Heavenly Spirit Roots cannot cultivate as quickly as before."

"But what does this have to do with the Enlightenment Pill?" Luo Chen asked curiously.

Deng Taiyue hesitated for a moment, then still told Luo Chen the answer.

"After becoming the sect master of Mount Tai Sect, I discovered in the sect's ancient texts that if one can comprehend the true meaning of the laws in advance, it might be possible to speed up cultivation."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, surprised. "There's such a claim?"

Deng Taiyue said seriously, "There is indeed such a saying. The ancient texts record that the most basic true meaning of the laws connects to the origin qi of heaven and earth. If one can comprehend even a little, our qi-refining speed can naturally receive the infusion of heaven and earth's origin qi, thereby greatly increasing."

Speaking up to here, he smiled.

"The method of cultivating the true meaning that Elder Lu mentioned earlier, when put into practice, exactly matches the description in my sect's scriptures. Of course, he held something back. Using the true meaning to communicate with heaven and earth's origin qi is a technique of Ascendant-level great cultivators. The risks involved are far greater than he made them sound."

What the risks were, Deng Taiyue didn't say—perhaps he didn't know himself.

But Luo Chen had come to understand. No wonder those Tianyuan Dao Sect prodigies who had just entered the Nascent Soul stage had fought so desperately in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, and their families had put in so much effort.

Once one comprehends the basic true meaning of the laws, it can speed up cultivation speed. For Nascent Soul cultivators, this was undoubtedly the greatest temptation!

"Since stepping into the mid-Nascent Soul stage, my cultivation speed has slowed down again. I simply couldn't bear it, so I began searching for shortcuts."

"Comprehending the true meaning of the laws became my dream!"

"It's not that I'm boasting, but my comprehension is not bad. Once, I took the risk of releasing my Nascent Soul to cultivate in a spiritual land, and I actually found a bit of that feeling. But unfortunately..."

"After that, I never found that feeling again. That's why I set my sights on the Enlightenment Pill."

Beside him, Deng Taiyue continued to speak in detail.

Luo Chen suddenly interrupted him. "Then why didn't you continue seeking Medicine Master's help? He was once as famous as the Pill King of the Medicine King Sect—his alchemy skills shouldn't be bad."

At this, Deng Taiyue's face showed indignation.

"That man is too perfunctory. In a hundred years, his research has made no progress. Instead, he drove me deep into the Million Great Mountains to search for various spiritual herbs for him, all under the pretext of experimenting with the Enlightenment Pill. But in my view, he was mostly using those rare flowers and plants to enrich the foundation of Seven Valleys Mountain."

For some reason, these words made Luo Chen's ears burn.

He seemed to have done something similar?

Clearing his throat lightly, Luo Chen asked again, "Then why come to me?"

Deng Taiyue's expression turned solemn. "Before coming, I also inquired about your life story. The title of Pill Sovereign represents your alchemy skill, while your conduct has the air of a gentleman."

"I believe Medicine Master is not trustworthy. The Pill Sovereign, who has the conduct of a gentleman, is more worthy of trust!"

Since the conversation had reached this point, what more was there to say?

Luo Chen waved his hand modestly. "I'm not as good as you say. My alchemy skills are only passable. But if you truly trust me, you can leave the incomplete prescriptions with me for study. As for when there will be results, I can't guarantee that."

Having received a promise, even though the other party had left plenty of room, Deng Taiyue finally breathed a sigh of relief.

He solemnly took out a large pile of things from his storage pouch.

Animal hides, ancient books, bamboo slips, jade slips, stone tablets.

As Luo Chen looked on in confusion, he introduced them: "These are various incomplete prescriptions of the Enlightenment Pill that have been scattered across the Eastern Wilderness. I spent a great deal of effort collecting them. According to Medicine Master, when combined, they only amount to fifty or sixty percent completeness."

"Additionally, this jade slip records the names of some medicinal herbs that Medicine Master had me collect over the years for his research on the Enlightenment Pill. I'm not sure if they're useful, but the Pill Sovereign can take them for reference."

Luo Chen nodded and carefully began to examine these incomplete prescriptions.

As he examined them, he casually asked:

"Although these are all copied versions, they look quite old and could be considered original fragments of the prescriptions. Didn't you give them to Medicine Master back then?"

"What I gave him were my own handwritten copies. I kept the originals and didn't give them to him."

"Oh, then why are you willing to give them to me this time?"

"If this attempt also fails, then in the vast Eastern Wilderness, I will have no one left to turn to. In that case, what meaning is there in keeping these things? At that time, I will no longer seek external shortcuts. I will cast aside all illusions, enter the forbidden grounds of Mount Tai Sect, and enter a death-seclusion!"

Luo Chen raised his head, and what met his eyes was a face with a resolute expression and eyes as hard as iron.

Was this a seeker of the Dao?

Luo Chen pursed his lips, carefully packed away all the items after examining them, and put them away.

"I will do my utmost."
