# Chapter 960: Nether-Departing Divine Lord, Why Not Show Yourself?

A bronze gate stood quietly between heaven and earth.

Its surface was covered with lifelike reliefs—ferocious skulls brandishing sharp claws, as if trying to break free from the door, yet held back by some invisible force.

Beneath the gate, five figures stood in a row.

"Five days have passed. Why haven't Celestial Master Li and Qi Tian emerged from the formation yet?" Meng Shentong paced in place, his face full of dissatisfaction.

Purple-Robed Marquis shook his head beside him. "Perhaps some accident has occurred."

"Hmph!"

Meng Shentong snorted lightly. He didn't believe Celestial Master Li would encounter any accident.

That Yellow Wind Formation was certainly strange, but its main function was to divide space and suppress divine sense—as if specially arranged to trap certain creatures.

For Sage-level cultivators like them, crossing it shouldn't be too difficult.

Especially since they had the Hundred Fragrance Sachets that Celestial Master Li had specially refined beforehand to guide them, preventing them from losing their way too badly.

Glancing at the old woman busy before the gate, Meng Shentong stopped pacing and couldn't help looking up at this bronze gate that seemed to reach the heavens.

"Is this the Life and Death Gate?"

Red Leaf Matron replied without turning her head. "This isn't the complete Life and Death Gate, only one half of it—the Death Gate, said to lead to the Netherworld."

Meng Shentong raised an eyebrow. "It looks exactly like the gate we entered the Yin-Yang Realm through."

"That one is merely a replica of this gate, with less than one percent of its power."

Meng Shentong was somewhat surprised. "That exaggerated?"

Red Leaf Matron straightened up, turned around with a worried expression.

"How could it not be exaggerated? The complete Life and Death Gate is an existence at the spirit treasure level!"

Spirit treasure!

Meng Shentong's eyes suddenly blazed with scorching light. No wonder the Southern Frontier Holy Land's defenses were so tight—they were actually using a spirit treasure as their defensive barrier!

He asked tentatively, "Can you find its weakness and open this gate?"

Red Leaf Matron's face showed difficulty.

Just then!

The yellow wind behind them suddenly dissipated.

A figure walked out from within.

With a casual toss, a corpse crashed to the ground.

Everyone looked over and saw that it was Celestial Master Li, his face showing exhaustion.

He clapped his hands, walked to the gate, and muttered to himself, "A replica, Red Leaf might be able to crack it a little, but this genuine Death Gate is not something she can break."

Celestial Master Li!

Purple-Robed Marquis withdrew his gaze from the corpse on the ground and said in shock, "You killed Lion Prime?"

Celestial Master Li didn't turn around. "He insisted on seeking his own death, so don't blame me for being ruthless. But this fellow was quite troublesome—he entangled me for quite a while."

The scene fell into silence.

Lion Prime's abilities were well-known in the outside world.

Not to mention his status as a Holy Land cultivator, just his cultivation at the late Adept stage was enough to intimidate an entire region.

Such a powerful figure had actually died at Celestial Master Li's hands?

This renowned Gu-refining grandmaster, who had enjoyed fame for many years, clearly had a temperament far less gentle than he appeared, and his strength was by no means as weak as ordinary Gu refiners.

Meng Shentong's pupils contracted slightly. His gaze met Bai Xuanjun's beside him, who was also clearly shocked.

Taking a deep breath, Meng Shentong asked directly, "According to our agreement, once we help you find the place where the Great Power Yin-Yangzi passed away, it's over. Now that the Great Power's place of passing is behind this gate, can we leave this realm?"

Celestial Master Li reached out and stroked the bronze gate, chuckling lightly. "Fellow Daoist Meng, along the way, you haven't even lifted a finger, let alone gotten your hands dirty. Is my favor really that cheap?"

Meng Shentong's face stiffened.

Celestial Master Li smiled and said, "How vast are the Netherworld's landscapes? Legend has it that Yin-Yangzi, unwilling to die suddenly, spent his last few decades before his great limit using the spirit treasure Death Gate as a foundation to construct the Netherworld realm, frantically researching various Gu insects in an attempt to escape the constraints of the Life-and-Death Sharing Gu. As a result, the Netherworld is filled with ghosts and demons everywhere, making it difficult for ordinary cultivators to take even a single step. I still need you all to protect me and ensure my safe arrival at that Yin-Yang Grave."

Meng Shentong had a subconscious fear of the space behind this gate.

"You also said Red Leaf can't open this gate, so what are we waiting here for?"

"Can't break it, but we can push it open normally!" Celestial Master Li shrugged and said matter-of-factly, "Just wait for someone strong enough to arrive."

Among those who entered the Yin-Yang Realm together, who was the strongest?

There was no need to think about that question.

A name naturally surfaced in everyone's minds.

"Qi Tian!"

"Why hasn't he arrived yet?"

"Lion Prime attacked Celestial Master Li in anger—could it be that Qi Tian was also attacked?"

"There are five Golden Lion Empire ancestors cultivating in seclusion in the Yin-Yang Realm. The third and fifth went outside, the fourth died at my hands and the Scripture Pavilion Master's. Could it be the Golden Lion Second Ancestor?"

"Qi Tian doesn't understand immortal arts and is suppressed by the formation—he hasn't met with an accident, has he?"

Amidst the discussion, even Celestial Master Li's face showed a trace of worry.

But after slightly sensing a certain Gu insect on his body, he relaxed.

"Qi Tian is fine. Let's wait a little longer."

Half-believing, the others had no choice but to be patient and wait quietly in place.

...

In the endless wind and sand, golden gravel lay everywhere.

A black figure stood amidst it. With a casual wave, streaks of blood light converged toward him, eventually entering a green-tinged gourd.

Gulp!

Shaking the gourd, Luo Chen revealed a satisfied smile.

This should be the last batch of Golden Dragon Lion statues in the vicinity.

After using the Dragon Lion Crystal Core to awaken a large number of them and kill them one by one, he had collected this one gourd of desolate beast source blood.

"I wonder if it's enough for cultivating the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree."

Muttering to himself, Luo Chen hung the gourd at his waist, tidied his robes slightly, and then quickly flew off in a certain direction.

In a moment, Luo Chen stepped out of the Yellow Wind Formation, and the scene before him suddenly changed.

A bronze gate connecting heaven and earth burst into his pupils, so majestic.

"Spirit treasure!"

With just one glance, Luo Chen's artifact-refining expertise allowed him to discern the gate's extraordinary nature.

But he immediately reacted and blurted out:

"The spirit treasure is incomplete—it's missing its artifact spirit!"

Beneath the gate, Celestial Master Li and the others had long been waiting.

Seeing Luo Chen appear, they all breathed a sigh of relief.

Hearing his excited words, Celestial Master Li's eyes lit up. "Fellow Daoist Qi has excellent eyesight, seeing through this gate's details at a single glance."

Luo Chen smiled without further explanation.

He had encountered spirit treasures before.

They were powerful artifacts that had given birth to self-awareness, even above true artifacts.

Each one possessed earth-shattering power!

In particular, the Yuantu Sword in his hand was infinitely close to the spirit treasure grade. After refining it day and night, he was so familiar with this type of treasure.

Suddenly!

He came back to his senses and asked, "Could it be that what Celestial Master Li wants me to do is push open this gate?"

Celestial Master Li nodded without hesitation. "Exactly!"

Luo Chen frowned. "This gate looks the same as the previous one, but the grade difference is too great. Can I do it?"

"You have to do it even if you can't!" Celestial Master Li sighed emotionally, then carefully introduced the specific situation of this Death Gate to Luo Chen.

"The Life and Death Gate is divided into the Life Gate and the Death Gate. Originally one body, they guarded the Holy Land together."

"Two thousand years ago, the Great Power Yin-Yangzi, unwilling to die suddenly, took the Death Gate and left the Holy Land. He constructed the Netherworld realm in what is now the Golden Lion Empire, shielding it from heavenly-human resonance. Then, in the Netherworld, he extensively researched the Gu Dao, attempting to escape the collective punishment of the Life-and-Death Sharing Gu."

"But he still had a bottom line, leaving the artifact spirit in the Life Gate so that the Holy Land wouldn't lose its gate's protection."

"And this is our only chance!"

A spirit treasure without an artifact spirit is not a true spirit treasure.

This was also the Death Gate's only weakness!

It could only defend passively, unable to counterattack.

Luo Chen was puzzled. "Why does it have to be me? Can't others do it?"

Fleshly brute force was power, but wasn't a cultivator's divine power also power?

However, Celestial Master Li shook his head decisively. "That's different. Whether we use spells to attack, magic artifacts to push, or even Gu Dao divine abilities, once they enter the Death Gate, they're like mud oxen entering the sea—gone without a trace. Only the purest fleshly power can push the door open."

As he spoke, he signaled Purple-Robed Marquis with his eyes.

The latter didn't hesitate. He came before the gate.

In front of everyone, his mana surged, and purple light thickened.

After a palm strike, the bronze gate remained motionless, as if nothing had happened.

Such a scene made everyone marvel.

Although that palm strike from Purple-Robed Marquis wasn't his full power, it was by no means something an ordinary Sage cultivator could easily withstand.

But to this gate, it was like a spring breeze brushing past, not even causing any damage to the skulls on the reliefs.

"If you want, you can also give it a try!" Celestial Master Li's gaze swept over everyone.

Meng Shentong waved his hand, directly refusing.

Bai Xuanjun seemed a bit eager to try, but under his companion's signal, he didn't do this useless work.

Red Leaf Matron had been trying all along, but no matter what formation she used, it had no effect on this spirit treasure, leaving her disheartened.

Celestial Master Li finally explained, "Originally, the Life and Death Gates were one body, with life and death circulating. They could both regenerate endlessly and dissolve all attacks, making them the strongest defense in the world. But after the Death Gate separated, life and death qi could no longer circulate, leaving a single weakness. And this weakness can only be grasped by body refiners."

As he spoke, he stared fixedly at Luo Chen.

"As long as you open this gate for me, the favor of the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art* will be settled between us!"

"Really?" Luo Chen asked.

Celestial Master Li nodded. "For the rest of the Netherworld journey, I won't need you to exert any more effort. You can even leave the Yin-Yang Realm directly after opening the Death Gate."

Luo Chen blinked and fell silent.

After a long while, he said in a deep voice, "I've expended a lot on the way here. Allow me to rest quietly and recover a bit."

Everyone exchanged glances, thinking of how Celestial Master Li had been ambushed by Lion Prime earlier. Presumably, Luo Chen had encountered a similar situation.

Tacitly, Celestial Master Li led the others away, giving Luo Chen some quiet space.

After everyone left, Luo Chen casually tossed out a formation disk, shrouding his figure.

From a distance, Red Leaf Matron inadvertently glanced over, then her expression froze.

"What's wrong?" Celestial Master Li asked.

Red Leaf Matron hesitated. "This formation... is quite exquisite."

Celestial Master Li looked at it, nodded slightly, then said with some depth, "It should be the formation style from the Central Continent and Eastern Wilderness. I've heard that Qi Tian traveled abroad in his early years, so it's normal for him to have such collections."

Inside the formation, Luo Chen was not recovering his origin qi as he had claimed.

His eyes swept around, and the image of Red Eyes Golden Pupils reappeared.

His spiritual eyes stared fixedly at the bronze gate before him, trying to discern something.

Gradually, his mind sank into it.

Space was broken, covered with spiderweb cracks, and drops of pus oozed from the fissures.

A red moon hung high, casting cold, lonely moonlight.

There were ghost messengers three zhang tall with no skin, wandering the earth.

There were those who had hanged themselves, air leaking from their throats, emitting hoarse cries.

And countless strangely shaped skeletons swimming freely through the sky and earth.

It was unclear whether it was the scene behind the gate or the Death Gate's own scene.

Shocked by what he saw, Luo Chen slowly closed his eyes.

"This Death Gate is not simple!"

That was a redundant statement—how could a gate that could become a spirit treasure be simple?

But Luo Chen intended to give it a try!

Not only to repay the favor of the *Hundred Poisons Body-Tempering Art*, but also to test his own body refinement realm.

However, there was no rush for now!

Luo Chen closed his eyes and began to regulate his physical condition.

The first thing to do was to control the Hundred Poisons King Gu to retract the toxins spread across his body, so that the omnipresent pain wouldn't affect his performance when he made his move.

Then, he circulated source power throughout his body, gradually raising his physical state to its peak.

Time passed bit by bit.

One day, two days...

When three days had passed and everyone was getting impatient, the formation hiding Luo Chen's figure suddenly dissipated.

Celestial Master Li's eyes lit up, and he quickly flew over.

"Are you ready?"

Luo Chen stood before the bronze gate and nodded slightly.

Then, in front of everyone, he exhaled, opened his voice, and slowly pushed out a palm.

This was how he had pushed open that replica Life and Death Gate earlier.

Everyone was quite expectant!

However, when his palm touched the gate, the bronze gate remained motionless.

"Could it be that Qi Tian can't do it?" Celestial Master Li frowned.

Luo Chen wasn't surprised by this. Without hesitation, he extended another hand.

Pushing with both hands, the gate gradually began to tremble.

At that moment, the skull reliefs that had remained unmoved no matter how everyone attacked suddenly came to life, gnawing at Luo Chen's arms.

Luo Chen's gaze fixed. He didn't dodge at all, letting the skull reliefs devour a large amount of his vitality.

This was within his expectations after observing.

Without the Life Gate providing vitality, this Death Gate would devour the vitality of those trying to break through, using it to compensate itself.

How much vitality could an ordinary immortal cultivator's body have?

They would probably be sucked dry into skeletons in one breath!

This was probably why there were so many skull reliefs on the Death Gate—each one was a failure who had overestimated themselves and tried to push the door open, only to become an offering.

Only a body refiner of extreme strength could withstand the terrifying vitality consumption when opening the door.

And this was actually the main reason why only body refiners could use brute force to forcibly push this gate open.

"Not enough!"

"Far from enough!"

Thoughts flickered through Luo Chen's mind, and then he roared, "Everyone, stand back!"

The others didn't understand, but they followed suit.

The next moment, a horrifying scene appeared.

Luo Chen's robes billowed, and his muscles swelled.

His entire body, as if inflated, rapidly expanded.

One zhang, two zhang...

Ten zhang, twenty...

Until a giant stood towering between heaven and earth, so tall that one couldn't see its head, appeared before them, everyone realized what was happening.

"Demon-like transformation?"

"Buddhist Vajra wrathful form, or Daoist giant spirit transformation?"

In a daze, a colossal body nearly a thousand zhang tall stood upon the earth.

Its legs were rooted deep in the earth's veins, white clouds swirling around its chest, and a fierce Yaksha face roared toward the sky.

Both hands pushed horizontally, stirring wind and clouds, landing on that bronze gate.

"Open... for me!"

In the roar, immeasurable brute force surged forth in torrents.

No matter how the reliefs on the Death Gate devoured the giant's vitality, they couldn't affect it in the slightest.

Under that pure, unadulterated brute force, the bronze gate emitted a terrifying rumble.

The rumble was extremely harsh, grating on the ears and disgusting.

But Celestial Master Li and the others were overjoyed, because the Death Gate was slowly opening!

From behind the Death Gate, roars came one after another.

But they were all completely drowned out beneath the giant's thunderous roar.

At the same time!

On a mountain peak shaped like a demon beast's horn, two figures watched the distant sky in ecstasy.

Clouds screamed, wind howled, and space trembled.

Strange ripples, like ripples, spread in all directions.

Zhan Bingtao exclaimed in amazement, "You really broke through the Life and Death Gate?"

The Songyang Alliance Leader was also somewhat surprised. He had already moved fast enough, taking the Golden Lion Empire and Unicorn Mountain by storm with lightning speed. He hadn't expected Celestial Master Li's side to be even faster.

But with an ally present, he didn't show too many thoughts.

The Songyang Alliance Leader said with an air of having expected this, "Our mission is complete. The rest depends on your sect."

Zhan Bingtao laughed heartily. "Since the Life and Death Gate has a weakness, the rest isn't difficult. This Southern Frontier Holy Land clearly possesses a spirit treasure but doesn't use it to suppress an entire continent's spiritual energy, only caring about protecting its mountain gate. Now that the spirit treasure is broken, all we need to do is give it a push."

As he spoke, he took out a jade token and said with a somewhat respectful expression, "Disciple Zhan Bingtao respectfully invites Ancestor Liancheng to make a move!"

The next moment, a faint voice came from the jade token.

"Good!"

The voice was quite young.

However, after that one "good," there was no further movement.

The Songyang Alliance Leader was puzzled. That was it?

But a strange sense of horror arose from deep within his soul.

He suddenly looked up at the sky.

In the sky, a vortex appeared.

The vortex spun rapidly, gathering large amounts of airflow, and finally, a giant hand slowly extended out.

The hand was lifelike, every detail visible, so jade-white and delicate, like a woman's hand.

But this hand, at this moment, possessed power that seemed capable of destroying heaven and earth.

Heaven and earth moved in response.

Suddenly!

The giant hand trembled and slapped toward the Life and Death Gate ten thousand li away.

Boom!

Void trembled, and a illusory gate was forced to appear.

Endless life force circulated without cease, but at a critical juncture, it couldn't maintain balance, as if a link was missing.

The young voice from the jade token appeared again.

"Nether-Departing Divine Lord, why not show yourself?"

(The chapter ends)
