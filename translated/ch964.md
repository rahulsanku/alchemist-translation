# Chapter 964: Black Coffin Suspended Over the Grave, Flame Dragon Descends

A bright moon hung high in the void.

Jade-white and flawless, it reflected myriad scenes.

The black-armored cultivator's footsteps halted outside the Yin-Yang Grave.

He stared intently at that bright moon, a sharp light flickering in his eyes.

The underworld had a crimson moon hanging high, which he had initially taken as the result of a special formation. Now, it seemed, it was actually the work of this magic treasure.

Indeed, under the gaze of his Crimson-Eyed Golden Pupils, that bright moon was clearly a wheel-shaped treasure.

The reason a red moon appeared elsewhere was merely due to light refraction.

Now that he was close, he could perceive its subtlety.

A bronze wheel-rim, a hundred zhang in diameter, was densely inscribed with bowl-sized script sutras, and faint sounds of Buddhist chanting permeated the air.

The mirror surface reflected all the scenes of the underworld, and he could even see Bai Xuanjun's hesitant, lingering figure at the Death Gate. The other seemed to waver, but after looking back at the underworld several times, he finally hardened his heart, fled through the Death Gate, and vanished without a trace.

Under the protection of this treasure, the originally gloomy grave had become somewhat more peaceful.

But only somewhat.

Luo Chen looked down, and the massive grave, covering a full ten li in circumference, instantly came into view.

Ferocious beast claws stood around it like screens.

Gleaming tiles were arranged in neat rows.

Two high-hanging lanterns seemed like guiding lights pointing the way to the Yellow Springs.

And an open entrance gaped like the maw of an ancient giant beast, waiting for food to enter of its own accord.

"Are they inside?"

Luo Chen no longer concealed his abilities. His vast divine sense spread out like a tidal wave, searching for all traces in this place.

Soon, he captured several residual auras.

Hongye's, Meng Shentong's, and Li Tianshi's!

"They're all inside!"

Having confirmed the number of people, Luo Chen prepared to enter.

But as his divine sense swept through, he made a new discovery.

"And there's another strange aura?"

Luo Chen frowned, pacing at the entrance for a moment, but finally stepped into the Yin-Yang Grave with a resolute expression.

Upon entering the Yin-Yang Grave, it was initially very narrow, as if strolling through a cramped passageway.

But as Luo Chen quickened his pace and went deeper, the passage grew wider and wider.

By the end, it was even a hundred zhang wide from left to right, hardly resembling a passage designed for people at all.

Not only that...

*Chi!*

A sword qi burst from Luo Chen's fingertip, slashing across the walls on both sides of the passage, yet it only left behind faint white marks.

Such a scene was utterly baffling.

The sword qi he had just unleashed was the sword pellet qi from the Great Five Elements Sword Formation, its sharpness inheriting the properties of a true artifact sword pellet.

Under such an attack, most true artifacts and magic treasures in the world would be hard-pressed to withstand it.

Yet, just the walls on both sides of this passage could actually block it to some extent.

"Why is this?"

Frowning, Luo Chen touched one of the walls.

But it wasn't hard to the touch; in fact, it had a certain pliability.

"If I attack with the sword pellet itself, can I cut through it?"

"What about the Yuantu Sword?"

Just as Luo Chen was growing eager to try, a shrill, piercing scream suddenly came from deep within the grave.

"It's Hongye!"

Luo Chen's brow twitched. Abandoning the study of the side passages, he shot forward at extreme speed.

A few breaths later, his vision suddenly brightened.

A spacious underground plaza instantly came into view.

The most conspicuous thing was a black coffin, flanked by dragon heads!

That, presumably, was the burial place of the great cultivator Yin-Yangzi.

Next were the battle conditions around the plaza.

Elder Hongye was pierced through from behind by a sharp sword. The flesh and blood on her body was uncontrollably rotting, emitting hissing hot air, with white bones clearly visible.

It was Corpse Rot Poison!

With the toxicological knowledge Luo Chen had absorbed in a short time, he instantly identified the cause of Elder Hongye's physical changes through their external manifestations.

This poison was very common in the cultivation world.

One only needed to extract the corresponding toxin from a corpse.

But the more common a poison, the more terrifying it could be—just like those common techniques, which were often abridged versions of profound arts.

This Corpse Rot Poison, if extracted from a corpse that was extremely powerful in life, would have its potency multiplied.

Especially, it had an unimaginable restraining effect on body cultivators.

It would surge with the blood qi, merging into the eight extraordinary meridians and the bones of the limbs.

Seeing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but feel a chill.

"It seems you recognize it. Unfortunately, this poison was originally prepared for you."

A faint, regretful voice sounded from the center of the plaza.

Luo Chen followed the sound. Li Tianshi was standing before that black coffin, gazing at him from afar.

At his feet lay a prostrate, winged monster, with three blood shackles firmly suppressing its neck, waist, and ankles respectively.

A Flying Heavenly Yaksha!

Luo Chen's pupils contracted. This monster was the very Flying Heavenly Yaksha he had startled into retreating earlier. It was a pinnacle existence among flying corpses, comparable to a late-stage Sage cultivator.

Moreover, due to its powerful physical body and immunity to myriad spells, even ordinary great cultivators were reluctant to provoke it.

Of course, Luo Chen was not afraid of such an existence.

What put him on alert was that only a short time had passed since the Yin-Yang Grave opened. Even if there had been a conflict, it shouldn't have been resolved so quickly.

Yet, from the moment he entered, he saw Hongye severely wounded, the Yaksha suppressed, and only Li Tianshi completely unharmed.

This showed his capabilities.

Taking a deep breath, Luo Chen's eyes blazed with sharp light as he surveyed his surroundings, finally stopping at a certain spot.

"Fellow Daoist Meng, why not show yourself?"

"Eh?"

"Eh!"

Two exclamations of surprise sounded, one from Li Tianshi and the other from the spot Luo Chen was staring at.

Ripples undulated, and a slightly disheveled figure slowly emerged.

It was Meng Shentong!

However, he was quite disheveled at the moment, with faint traces of blood on him.

Li Tianshi looked at Luo Chen, the surprise in his eyes growing stronger. "Unexpectedly, even the Luo Sect's Void Concealment Art, which I cannot see through, was instantly seen through by you. Luo Chen, the surprises you give me are truly too many."

Luo Chen paid him no heed, instead staring at Meng Shentong.

The other's face was gloomy as he spoke, "Fellow Daoist, be careful. Li Tianshi's strength and methods are beyond imagination."

"I can see that." Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth.

Being able to force back three peers of the same rank in a short time, whether by ambush or direct combat, Li Tianshi's strength ranking was rapidly rising in Luo Chen's mind.

His gaze fell on Li Tianshi, and he suddenly said, "Hand over the Devil Heart Gu, and I will immediately leave the Yin-Yang Realm."

Meng Shentong was stunned for a moment, not understanding the connection between the Devil Heart Gu and Li Tianshi.

Li Tianshi smiled slightly, still appearing kind and benevolent.

"Why do you suspect me? The Devil Heart Gu Emperor is notorious, universally reviled in the Southern Frontier. I, however, am highly respected. There is no connection between the two, is there?"

Luo Chen said calmly, "Intuition!"

Li Tianshi was speechless, then retorted, "Talk of the heart's nature is too illusory. The Southern Frontier is vast; why must it be me?"

Luo Chen pursed his lips. "According to your own method of elimination, the Southern Frontier is only so big. There are only a few who meet the conditions for the Devil Heart Gu Emperor. Songyang is not, the Lion Lord from the Life and Death Gate is not, and Fairy Xuanji's gender doesn't match. Could it be my companion, Meng Shentong?"

Meng Shentong quickly waved his hand. "I have seen the older generation's Devil Heart Gu Emperor, but I am not him!"

Li Tianshi chuckled. "Wasn't the suspect I gave you back then also Elder Zangsheng?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "Elder Zangsheng's methods are harsh, but not cruel. He doesn't fit the Devil Heart Gu Emperor's modus operandi. As for you, Elder, it is rumored that to refine gu, you do not hesitate to use living people and demon beasts as experimental materials. Though praised by many as a spirit of exploration and knowledge, it is perhaps excessively cruel."

"Not enough!" Li Tianshi still smiled.

Luo Chen continued, "It is said that back then, the new generation of Devil Heart Gu Emperor killed the old Gu Emperor, the superior, and usurped his position. When I first heard this rumor, I couldn't imagine how, given the gap in cultivation and the suppression of the natal gu's rank, one could achieve such a feat. Later, I gradually thought of a method: it required an extremely profound understanding of gu insects to rebel against the Devil Heart Gu and devour the superior. And if we're talking about understanding gu insects, in the vast Southern Frontier, no one is your equal!"

Li Tianshi raised an eyebrow. "Still not enough!"

Luo Chen fell silent. During this time, the sound of painful cries still echoed across the plaza.

It was Elder Hongye's wailing.

Under the Corpse Rot Poison, her physical body was constantly disintegrating.

And in this state, her Nascent Soul couldn't escape at all.

If the Nascent Soul were contaminated by even a trace of the Corpse Rot Poison, the situation would only worsen.

She could only continuously deploy techniques, trying to peel away the poison from her body.

Luo Chen's gaze swept over her, and he suddenly spoke, "Then how do you explain the hand you laid on me?"

Li Tianshi was startled, then said regretfully, "You've already discovered it?"

Luo Chen stroked his heart and said calmly, "The principle of the Devil Heart Gu is to follow the path of refining essence into qi. The stronger the physical body's blood essence, the higher the refining efficiency. The body cultivation path in the Southern Frontier was once flourishing, aided by gu insects. The reason it declined was that during the Devil Heart Calamity back then, body cultivators became the primary targets."

"Since I set foot in the Southern Frontier cultivation world, I deliberately revealed my powerful physical body, aiming to attract the attention of the Devil Heart Gu Emperor."

"I originally planned to use myself as bait to lure the Devil Heart Gu Emperor into casting a gu on me. I never expected your methods to be more circuitous, casting the Mother-Child Linked Gu instead."

Li Tianshi sighed. "It's not that I couldn't cast it, but people are too wary of the Devil Heart Gu. Any trace of it, and I would be exposed."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "So, you admit it?"

"So you were tricking me!" Li Tianshi suddenly realized.

Luo Chen waved his hand. "It's not really a trick, just a reasonable deduction within a reasonable scope. Even if I were wrong, it wouldn't matter much."

Just as Li Tianshi had once investigated the Devil Heart Gu Emperor for Luo Chen, the Southern Frontier was vast, but in the eyes of Sage cultivators, it was only this big.

As long as the Devil Heart Gu Emperor was still alive and still pursuing the Great Dao, he couldn't possibly stop cultivating.

And a cultivator who initially chose to use the Devil Heart Gu, how could they stop chasing the Great Dao?

Therefore, the suspects were only those few people.

It was just that Li Tianshi was clever, having removed himself from suspicion from the very beginning.

If Luo Chen hadn't discovered the hand he had laid on him while cultivating the *Hundred Poisons Body Tempering Art*, it would have been very difficult to place the suspicion on him.

Li Tianshi exhaled a long, turbid breath and said frankly, "That's right. The current Devil Heart Gu Emperor is none other than me."

As soon as these words were spoken, Meng Shentong's expression changed drastically, and his gaze towards Li Tianshi became even more wary.

But Li Tianshi paid no heed to the others' reactions, speaking as if to himself, "Back then, I was an insignificant junior of the Moon Worship Cult in the Golden Lion Empire. It was only by relying on the Devil Heart Gu that I managed to advance to the Adept stage. During the Devil Heart Calamity, I hid everywhere, and by chance, I entered this Yin-Yang Remnant Realm."

At that time, the underworld had not yet declined to this extent. Various ghosts, monsters, and corpse puppets were also in a state of impending change. By relying on Yin-Yangzi's research on various gu insects left behind, I gradually found a path as a gu refiner.

"It was also by relying on this gu-refining ability that I changed the properties of the Devil Heart Gu, allowing me to kill the old master, devour him, inherit his powerful cultivation, and ascend to the Sage stage in one fell swoop when he found me."

"But I knew that cultivating with the Devil Heart Gu would eventually be discovered. Therefore, I turned to the orthodox path, gradually making a name for myself in the Southern Frontier as a gu refiner."

"Step by step, I eventually reached my current position and attained my current cultivation."

"You, Luo Chen, are the only person in five hundred years who has moved my heart!"

Luo Chen listened to the past events, both amazed and somewhat emotional.

Only at the last sentence did he frown in displeasure.

Li Tianshi smiled freely. "Such a powerful physical body, if offered to the Devil Heart Gu to devour and absorb, who knows how much dharma power it could convert for me! Compared to the Purple-Robed Marquis I deliberately cultivated, you are a hundred, a thousand times better!"

Luo Chen said coldly, "You may not know this, but I hate nothing more than those who covet my physical body! Everyone who has done so has ended up with their bones and souls utterly destroyed!"

Li Tianshi shrugged with a smile. "Since you've already discovered the Mother-Child Linked Gu, you've probably resolved its consequences. That being the case, I can't do anything to you. How about we cooperate a little?"

"Cooperate?" Luo Chen asked back.

Li Tianshi nodded. "Yes, I'll give you the Devil Heart Gu, and you kill Meng Shentong for me. What do you say?"

As soon as these words were spoken, Meng Shentong's expression changed drastically, and he instinctively distanced himself from Luo Chen by over ten zhang.

Luo Chen calmly glanced at Meng Shentong, then withdrew his gaze. "Since you could severely wound Hongye and suppress the Flying Heavenly Yaksha, I imagine Meng Shentong is no match for you either. Why do you need my help?"

Li Tianshi sighed. "The Luo Sect is, after all, an ancient and long-established great sect in the Southern Frontier. The Vacuum Gu combines combat, cultivation, and concealment into one. I might be able to defeat him, but killing Brother Meng is far too difficult. Just now, when you arrived, your eyes saw through Meng's concealed position, showing that you have a way to counter the Vacuum Gu. As for your strength..."

Li Tianshi glanced outside.

Three mid-stage Sage cultivators couldn't stop Luo Chen. Coupled with the various deeds under the reputation of the Alchemy Sect, he believed that a man's reputation was well-earned, and Luo Chen was more than enough to deal with Meng Shentong.

Faced with these words, the wariness on Meng Shentong's face grew thicker.

And his figure began to grow hazy, until it vanished.

But no matter how he concealed himself in the void, Luo Chen's gaze followed him relentlessly.

This also made him understand that those eyes could truly see through the void and perceive everything!

"Fellow Daoist Qi... Luo, you must not listen to this old scoundrel's divisive scheme! Inside that black coffin is a fifth-rank gu insect, the Life-and-Death Shared Gu, the origin of the Devil Heart Gu. If Li Tianshi obtains this item, his advancement to Ascendant will be a smooth process. By then, even if your cultivation is heaven-defying, you will not end well!"

Meng Shentong's words were somewhat urgent.

Clearly, he also understood the weight of the reputation of Dan Zong Luo Chen.

In the oppressive atmosphere, Luo Chen blocked the entrance to the Yin-Yang Grave, his robes rustling.

*Kacha, kacha, kacha...*

The sound of seals breaking could be heard.

It was the three blood shackles suppressing the Flying Heavenly Yaksha, beginning to loosen under its immense strength.

Li Tianshi frowned, his patience wearing thin.

"Luo Chen, have you made your decision?"

*Shua!*

Luo Chen lifted his eyelids, his eyes pointing directly at Li Tianshi.

"I would rather take it directly than seek it by crooked means. That Devil Heart Gu, I will take it myself!"

Before his words had even faded, the world within the Yin-Yang Grave suddenly changed.

Crimson clouds spread everywhere, and blazing flames consumed the world.

A Flame Dragon broke through the clouds above Netherworld Mountain, blasted through layers of rock, and plunged straight towards Li Tianshi.
