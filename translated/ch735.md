# Chapter 735: The Chanting Monk, Ice Pole's Grand Elder. A Desolate Beast Roars, a Great Power Acts

"What are you trying to do!"

A furious shout suddenly rang out.

Almost the moment the Sages aboard the Sword-Halberd discovered the great battle in the Blue Ice Sea Region, the demon-beast force that had originally been trailing behind rapidly rushed up from the flank and blocked their path straight ahead.

One powerful demon after another watched the several thousand cultivators aboard the Sword-Halberd with predatory eyes, looking very much as if they would strike the moment a single word displeased them.

Five transformation-stage demon cultivators stood before the demon horde, coldly watching the Sword-Halberd.

Hearing the human Sage's rebuke, a gaunt elder among the five demon cultivators, looking like a bald monk, stepped out from the crowd.

In his hand hung a string of prayer beads made from human skulls, and with a compassionate expression, he pressed his palms together toward everyone.

"Everyone, this is as close as you may come to watching the battle. Ahead is a war of my race. Please do not interfere. Otherwise... Amitabha, this little monk does not wish to create slaughter karma recklessly."

As he spoke, a vast and heavy aura rose from his scrawny body and covered the Sword-Halberd.

A late-stage Sage demon cultivator!

On the Sword-Halberd, everyone's expressions changed greatly.

Fu Chaosheng's face sank. "Chanting Monk, you are Fellow Daoist Kongchan [TERM?], are you not?"

Kongchan looked at Fu Chaosheng in surprise. "I did not expect there would be someone here who knows this little monk. That is well. Since you know this little monk's name, you should also know this little monk's style of conduct, so it would be best not to act rashly."

Fu Chaosheng said coldly, "If we are unwilling to detour and insist on advancing, do you truly dare act?"

Kongchan froze, his expression stiff.

"Enough! We will not interfere in the war over the Blue Ice Sea Region, but this old man hopes that after this matter, all of you withdraw swiftly and stop following us from behind." Fu Chaosheng waved a hand and stared at the gaunt, bald elder. "Kongchan, what do you think?"

Kongchan's stiff expression softened. His wrinkled old face looked left and right, and then he nodded slightly.

"This place is already not far from Sun-Moon Island. Our mission can also be declared finished early. This little monk does not wish to forcibly convert all of you either."

After each side showed toughness, each side gave way.

The Sword-Halberd and this demon-beast force led by a late-stage Sage demon cultivator temporarily fell into a stalemate outside the Blue Ice Sea Region.

Both sides paid attention to the Ice Pole Sect battle while privately exchanging within their own ranks.

"The demon monk Kongchan's true body is a Chanting Monk [TERM?], a race extremely rare in the Northern Sea. They are born bald and long-bearded, with six legs crouched on the ground. Before transforming, their speech sounds very much like scripture recitation and Buddhist chanting, so they are called Chanting Monks."

"The demon monk Kongchan is an outstanding figure among the Chanting Monk race. He made his name during the ancient war between humans and demons. Now, three thousand years have passed, and not only has he not died of old age, he has instead cultivated to the late-stage Sage level. He is extremely troublesome."

"Unless necessary, it would be best for us not to clash with him."

No one had expected that the leading demon beast sent by the Demon Alliance to monitor Divine Origin City would be a powerful demon cultivator who had become famous in antiquity.

With him blocking the road, the Sword-Halberd had no way to interfere in the Ice Pole Sect battle at all.

On the demon-beast side, Kongchan toyed with the bizarre human-head prayer beads in his hand, his expression peaceful.

Behind him, four transformation-stage demon cultivators with extraordinary auras looked at him in confusion.

"With Your Excellency's strength, you alone would be enough to suppress all the cultivators on the Sword-Halberd. Why be so polite?"

Kongchan said in a low voice, "These people are not worth fearing, but the flag hanging on their ship belongs to the Tianyuan Dao Sect. Tell me, is this little monk stronger, or is the Tianyuan Dao Sect stronger?"

Everyone froze.

One who was indignant spoke up. "So what if it is the Dao Sect? The Demon Sect was still destroyed by us two hundred years ago. Now we are separated only by the sea. Once our demon race takes back the Northern Sea's old lands, we can recreate the glory of the ancient demon race. At that time, why would we need to fear the Tianyuan Dao Sect?"

Once these words came out, they immediately drew a chorus of agreement.

Kongchan, however, smiled and shook his head, the prayer beads in his hand moving faster and faster.

"You yourselves said 'at that time.' Has that time arrived now? Moreover, none of you experienced the ancient war between humans and demons. You do not know the fierce name of a holy land, and it is not so easily provoked."

The demons looked at one another.

They felt this was somewhat absurd.

Holy lands were indeed very powerful. When they attacked the Primordial Demon Sect back then, they had suffered heavy losses.

Even after recuperating for two hundred years while sealed in Arctic Yemo Heaven, they had not recovered even half their peak strength, which was why their present recovery of old lands could only proceed gradually, step by step.

But in the end, the Primordial Demon Sect had been taken down by them!

How much stronger could the Tianyuan Dao Sect, which shared its name, be?

Yet when they thought of the demon monk Kongchan's reverence, and Fu Chaosheng, who was only an early-stage Sage yet dared rely on a holy land's name to make bold declarations before them, the attitudes of both sides seemed to explain everything.

The Dao Sect's mighty name made even late-stage Sage Kongchan dare not act rashly. He could only warn them in words.

"Alas, I hope the Tianyuan Dao Sect obeys the agreements within their human race and does not interfere in outside-continent affairs, giving us Northern Sea demons enough time."

Kongchan lowered his brows and turned the beads, his expression compassionate.

The demons suddenly understood. No wonder they had been ordered not to attack Divine Origin City; no wonder the two ancient demons above had told them not to easily provoke disciples of the Tianyuan Dao Sect. The true purpose was here!

They would not provoke the Dao Sect, avoiding its dispatch of troops to the Northern Sea.

This would give the Northern Sea demons more time to recover their old lands and establish a true demon-race holy land!

Among the five great demon cultivators, a handsome man with extraordinary bearing was now glaring hatefully at a figure atop one of the Sword-Halberd's towers.

"Count yourself lucky!"

...

Luo Chen rested his hand on the railing, Black King lying on his shoulder.

"Master, Shark Emperor Liujun is ahead as well. He seems to have sensed my existence."

Luo Chen's expression was calm. "He did more than sense your existence. He recognized me too."

Black King was startled. With Master's ability, could he be recognized so easily?

Luo Chen did not explain.

Inside the Withering-Bloom True Flame, that wisp of deep-blue flame from Shark Emperor Liujun had not yet been erased.

It was not that he could not erase it, but that he did not want to.

Luo Chen kept that flame because he wanted to study why it could target desolate beast bodies.

If he could research it successfully, he could complete one of his own latent weaknesses.

Now that they were so close, with Black King, who had revealed his true body in the battle at Blazing Refinement Prison, also present, if Shark Emperor Liujun still could not recognize him, then he would be unworthy of being a Demon Emperor of his generation.

"No need to pay attention to him. They do not dare attack the Sword-Halberd. Besides, if we truly fight, so long as I do not face that demon monk, whether Shark Emperor and I live or die is still unknown."

Though his mouth said it was still unknown, Luo Chen's face showed no fear.

When he had been at the Adept stage, he had not feared Shark Emperor Liujun.

Now that he had become a Sage and his abilities surpassed the past, even in the vast ocean, he had the confidence to fight and defeat him!

At this moment, his gaze was deep and his divine sense spread out.

Divine sense and spiritual eyes worked together as he carefully observed the tragic battle in the Blue Ice Sea Region.

This was also his first time witnessing a clash between a top Sage upper sect and the Northern Sea Demon Alliance.

Clearly, this battle had already gone on for a very long time, at least ten days to half a month.

Across the sea covered in solid ice, human cultivator and demon beast corpses could be seen everywhere.

That Ice Soul Spirit Mountain [TERM?], built from countless pieces of ten-thousand-year ice, was dyed red with blood, and its mountain-protection grand formation tottered on the brink of collapse.

In Luo Chen's perception, the Sage-level battle strength participating numbered no fewer than both hands.

The two sides had originally been evenly matched, until the appearance of Ghost Immortal Tower.

"It is him again!"

Luo Chen murmured, and waves immediately rose in his heart.

Bone-white solid ice stretched ten thousand li with the Ice Pole Sect's spirit mountain as its center.

Beneath the solid ice, seawater flowed in murmuring currents, deep blue throughout.

The two interwove and reflected one another, thereby forming the so-called Blue Ice Sea Region.

Even the previous war between humans and demons had not caused this Blue Ice Sea Region to collapse.

But with the appearance of that monster like a red tower, everything changed.

Wherever Ghost Immortal Tower's two feet passed, the ice layers shattered and the ocean stirred into waves.

One Great King Squid tentacle after another draped behind him like hair.

As he ran, one corpse after another on the battlefield was rolled up by the squid tentacles and disappeared into his mouth. Even streams of invisible ill fortune gathered toward him, forming layer after layer of ghostly phantasms.

"He seems to have become stronger than when he was in the Fallen Sea?"

Enlightenment rose in Luo Chen's heart.

He did not know whether it was because his own realm was higher, allowing him to better recognize Ghost Immortal Tower's power, or because the other party truly had grown stronger.

At this moment, in his perception, Ghost Immortal Tower vaguely had a feeling of merging with heaven and earth.

Solid ice melted for him, the vast ocean cheered and surged for him, and even the sky darkened because of his appearance.

An Ice Pole Sect Sage acted, attempting to block him from attacking the spirit mountain.

But faced with the flying sword stabbing toward him, Ghost Immortal Tower did not dodge or evade and charged straight through.

Whoosh!

The flying sword struck empty.

This scene left everyone astonished.

Luo Chen looked thoughtful. "Those ghostly phantasms surrounding Ghost Immortal Tower can even affect a Sage's divine sense, thereby causing attacks to miss?"

The Ice Pole Sect's Sage was no fool. Very quickly, he realized this point and no longer attacked with a single flying sword, instead releasing large-area attacks.

But Ghost Immortal Tower directly ignored attacks of this level and allowed them to bombard his body.

All the attacks were like clay oxen entering the sea. They did not cause him the slightest injury.

A desolate beast body that could absorb damage?

Luo Chen gained another layer of understanding.

Afterward, he realized this was not his misconception. Ghost Immortal Tower truly had grown stronger.

He seemed to have drawn infinitely close to the fifth tier, which was why he could draw a response from heaven and earth.

"If no one acts to stop him and this continues, Ghost Immortal Tower is bound to reach the Ice Pole Sect's mountain gate."

As this thought appeared, an angry shout came from that ten-thousand-year ice mountain.

"Beast, withdraw!"

A powerful divine sense suddenly erupted.

Within Luo Chen's sight, a small black dot flew up from the ice mountain. He crooked a finger and pointed.

It looked like an ordinary finger, yet it burst out with an unimaginable aurora that landed on Ghost Immortal Tower in an instant.

Hiss!

A hole the size of a millstone pierced Ghost Immortal Tower's enormous head.

With a single strike, he had killed the desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower!

"Is this the Ice Pole Sect's grand elder, Daoist Extreme Cold [TERM?]?" Luo Chen's heart trembled. Beneath that ordinary-looking finger, cold spread across his whole body.

Clearly, with his present physique comparable to a middle-stage desolate beast, he absolutely could not withstand that finger.

A late-stage Sage great cultivator was terrifying indeed!

However, Luo Chen could also see that after using this move, Daoist Extreme Cold's aura clearly declined.

"That move is also a great burden for him!"

Luo Chen thought this, but in the next moment, he realized something.

"No!"

Beneath the ice mountain, Ghost Immortal Tower, whose head had been pierced through, still stood upright in the sea. His nearly thousand-zhang-tall body had not fallen.

Abruptly!

His body moved again, rushing into the sky with unstoppable force and roaring toward the place where Daoist Extreme Cold was located.

The rumbling sound waves shook cultivators' divine souls into unbearable turmoil.

This was what Luo Chen sensed from such a great distance.

On the battlefield near the Ice Pole Sect, within a thousand-li range, all who heard the sound bled from their seven orifices and died, and even demon beasts were no exception.

Only a few Sage-stage demon cultivators had prepared in advance and were not affected.

As for the rest, including the Ice Pole Sect's Sages, after hearing that roar, their expressions turned vacant, and their bodies fell toward the earth beyond their control.

Daoist Extreme Cold was at the center of Ghost Immortal Tower's roar and suffered the greatest influence.

Although he still remained in the air and had not fallen, he was blank and wooden, who knew what heavy injury his divine soul had suffered.

He even turned a blind eye to the giant palm Ghost Immortal Tower stretched out.

Crack!

Crack!

One light screen after another moved in response, blocking Ghost Immortal Tower's erupting attack, but wherever the giant hand passed, the formation light screens all shattered.

"Urgh..."

When Daoist Extreme Cold woke, what he saw was the slowly retracting great hand and a slightly opened bloody maw.

His expression was terrified, as if he had never imagined there would be such a turn.

Clusters of spiritual light erupted from his body, and several powerful true artifacts spat out, cutting at that giant hand.

Ghost Immortal Tower ignored this entirely. He simply opened his mouth and swallowed Daoist Extreme Cold in one bite.

After chewing several times, screams came from within his mouth.

This scene was extremely horrifying.

A dignified late-stage Sage great cultivator had actually ended in such miserable fashion?

Boom!

Ghost Immortal Tower's damaged head exploded into a cloud of blood mist, and a Nascent Soul fled out in panic.

Seeing this, Luo Chen subconsciously released a breath.

At least he still had the power to resist.

However, Ghost Immortal Tower's divine-soul core did not seem to be inside his head?

Even though his brain was continuously damaged, his movements were not affected in the slightest.

Seeing the Nascent Soul flee, he again reached out to grab without thinking.

Daoist Extreme Cold's face was full of resentment. Barehanded, he crushed a golden talisman.

"Li Canghai, save me!"

The words fell, and heaven and earth stilled.

In the sky, an enormous vortex appeared, and a figure slowly stepped out from within it.

It was a figure with indistinct features, hidden among countless wind patterns and cloud patterns.

He lowered his head and glanced below, then beckoned. Daoist Extreme Cold's Nascent Soul fell into his hand.

At this time, Ghost Immortal Tower trembled all over and forcibly broke free from the restraint of heaven and earth.

He raised his head and roared madly at that person.

The same explosive roar as before appeared once more.

However, this time, it was like a clear breeze brushing the face and had no effect on that person whatsoever.

A demon cultivator present realized something and hurriedly shouted, "Senior Li, you have an agreement with our race's great one. You may not interfere in battles below the Ascendant level."

The blurry-faced man was unmoved, and no action could be seen from him. He only released a small seal painted with indistinct patterns of mountains, rivers, lakes, and seas.

The moment the small seal appeared, heaven and earth fell silent.

The next moment, an enormous rumbling sounded from the center of the battlefield.

Under everyone's stunned gazes, the Ice Pole Sect's Ice Soul Spirit Mountain, which had stood for countless years, broke out of the sea, rushed straight into the sky, and disappeared into the small seal.

Because this mountain had merged in, the indistinct patterns on the small seal seemed to become somewhat clearer.

"The Blue Ice Sea Region is yours."

One simple sentence, and the only sentence.

After saying this, the person turned back and vanished inside the vortex.

From beginning to end, aside from Ghost Immortal Tower drawing his attention, no other person or matter entered his discerning eye.

He had come here once, saved one person, and collected one mountain. That was all.

Only at this moment did that terrifying pressure covering a ten-thousand-li radius completely vanish.

Over the Blue Ice Sea Region, only Ghost Immortal Tower's furious roars continued sounding.

Outside the sea region, the demon monk Kongchan lowered his head, his body trembling nonstop.

Only after a long while did he raise his head, a forced smile on his face.

"Congratulations to our race on taking another land."

Everyone opened their mouths, not knowing what to say.

With Ghost Immortal Tower taking the lead, taking down the Blue Ice Sea Region was not unexpected.

But the Ice Pole Sect's Ice Soul Spirit Mountain had been taken away, which meant the main vein of the large fourth-tier spirit vein in this place had been carried off.

What meaning was there in taking such territory?

On the Sword-Halberd, the cultivators looked at one another, seemingly never having expected the final result to be like this.

The survival or destruction of the Ice Pole Sect had already been decided the moment Ghost Immortal Tower appeared.

After all, in the present Northern Sea, the desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower was known as the existence closest to the Ascendant realm. Although Daoist Extreme Cold's all-out strike had heavily injured him, it did not help matters.

But the appearance of Great Power Li Canghai brought some uncertainty to the result.

What disappointed everyone was that he did not act against the demon beasts here. He merely saved Daoist Extreme Cold's Nascent Soul and took away the Ice Pole Sect's spirit mountain.

Was it because of that so-called Ascendant agreement?

No one knew.

Only Fu Chaosheng looked toward that side with a complicated expression, then withdrew his gaze.

"Let us go!"

The Sword-Halberd set sail again.

This time, no demon beasts followed.

Inside the sealed chamber on Kunxiao Palace's fifth floor, Luo Chen recalled the earlier scene of Ice Soul Spirit Mountain being taken away, his expression uncertain and startled.

"Was that the island-linking formation method?"

*(End of Chapter)*
