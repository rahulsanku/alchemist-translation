# Chapter 902: Fire Marrow Reappears, Two Modes Form the Pill

"Master Xuanyan, why have you come?"

Chen Mozi, who had been sitting in stillness moments before, rose solemnly the moment he saw the old man.

Master Xuanyan?

Luo Chen looked in surprise at the old man who had appeared so calmly.

Dressed in simple dark robes, plain yet dignified and imposing, his face was ruddy and healthy, showing none of the frail, aged appearance rumored about him.

Hadn't Ye Lingtian said he had no interest in the Alchemy Saint's inheritance?

Why had he come today?

Master Xuanyan smiled and said, "Staying on the mountain for too long inevitably grows cold and lonely. I heard you had entered the Alchemy Saint Hall before the new year, so I thought I'd take a walk and have a look. Am I not welcome?"

"No, no, no—how could we not welcome you, Elder?"

Chen Mozi quickly waved his hand. Though his manner was not obsequious, it was filled with respect.

Master Xuanyan had once had many Daoist titles, but in the end, people called him "Master" for a reason.

In his early years, this man placed little importance on sects, clans, or matters of inheritance and bloodline.

Even when his sect declined, he did nothing to turn the tide, focusing solely on the path of alchemy.

Yet he was never stingy with imparting his alchemical insights, often guiding those younger who thirsted for knowledge.

Some sought to become his disciples, but he would usually refuse, unwilling to be burdened by such mundane affairs. Even joining the Nether Origin Sect was merely for convenience—to find a backing so he could devote himself to studying alchemy in peace. To this day, over a thousand years later, he had only one direct disciple and one medicine apprentice who had once tended his fires and selected his herbs—the Medicine Emperor.

Younger alchemists of the Nether Origin Sect, such as Chu Yan, Chu Yu, and Lu Chun, had all received his guidance.

Unable to address him as master or teacher, they respectfully called him "Master."

Chen Mozi was the same. Even passing the second trial this time, aside from his own ability, was inseparable from Master Xuanyan's guidance many years ago.

As for Luo Chen, he was quite shocked.

He caught a word from the other's words—"before the new year"!

In other words, from entering the hall to passing two trials, it had taken him less than a year in total?

Even if the other had experienced the trials before, the Alchemy Saint Hall's tests were constantly changing.

To pass the trials so quickly showed just how deep his foundation was and how high his alchemical attainment!

While he was still in shock, Master Xuanyan's curious gaze also fell upon him.

"Alchemy Grandmaster?"

Luo Chen composed himself, made a bow, and performed a Daoist salute.

"Before you, Master, I dare not claim the title of Alchemy Grandmaster. Please call me Luo Chen."

"Haha, how modest!"

Master Xuanyan laughed heartily, stroking his tiger-like whiskers as if recalling past events.

"When I was as young as you, I never knew what modesty was."

Then, he changed the subject.

"To participate in Chu Yan's trial for the first time and easily pass two levels—the title of Alchemy Grandmaster is well deserved!"

Luo Chen humbly replied, "You flatter me, Master."

But Chen Mozi, unable to wait any longer, quickly asked, "Master, how should this third trial be passed?"

Master Xuanyan waved his hand. "How else? Simply select suitable materials from what's available to form a pill formula, then refine it. For us, it's just a routine matter."

Chen Mozi opened his mouth, speechless and somewhat helpless.

"Master, it's not that simple!"

Master Xuanyan's smile faded, and he said solemnly, "Was reaching the Grandmaster realm simple? We Grandmasters must ignore difficulties and obstacles, charging forward courageously. Even with only one chance, we must have enough confidence that we will surely succeed! That is the pride of an Alchemy Grandmaster!"

Chen Mozi said bitterly, "But if I fail..."

Master Xuanyan said no more. He went to find a geothermal fire vent on his own and released his own pill furnace.

Then, one by one, he cast the materials he had reverse-refined from the second trial into the furnace. Black flames sprang from his fingertips, enveloping the cauldron.

It seemed he had already decided what pill to refine before even reaching the third trial.

Luo Chen did not watch the other's movements any longer.

The three consecutive trials were interlinked, and the final results would inevitably differ.

From identifying pills by scent, to tracing back to their origins, to finally refining a pill in one go—each person's chosen pills, materials, and ultimately self-developed formulas would be different.

He turned his attention back to his own situation.

What pill should he refine using the raw materials he had reverse-refined?

His gaze fell upon the hundred or so types of pure medicinal materials, each name and property familiar to him.

White Angelica, Qi Lotus, Drifting Duckweed, Ghost Li Seed, Heavenly Thunder Vine...

Dried Ginger, Fleshy Dogwood, Six Yang Stone, Flame Cloud Smoke...

As these names surfaced in his mind, Luo Chen suddenly realized something.

In the first trial of identifying pills by scent, he had subconsciously chosen the water and fire types of pills he was most familiar with, so the raw materials reverse-refined in the second trial were mostly of these two types.

This was related to his alchemical experience.

In the beginning, Jade Marrow Pills, Netherpassage Pills, Jade Dew Pills, and the like were mostly of cold, yin attributes, while Blood Balefulness and Dustfall Pills were fire-attributed.

But back then, he had mixed them up, not excluding multi-attributed pills like Starfield Pills.

It wasn't until he went to the Northern Sea that he gradually abandoned such mixed-attribution pills and specialized in water and fire.

The alchemical style in the Northern Sea mostly drew from water-attributed herbs and demon beasts, so he often refined water-attributed pills there.

Yet because he primarily cultivated a fire-attributed technique, he needed to refine top-grade fire-attributed pills like True Flame Pills from time to time for his cultivation.

Ultimately, this led to Luo Chen having a unique preference for these two types of medicinal materials.

So now, what main material should he use, and what attribute of pill should he refine?

Luo Chen's gaze swept over the materials, and he suddenly felt at a loss.

Among the hundred or so raw materials, there were over a dozen fourth-grade and even fifth-grade main ingredients, truly dazzling his eyes.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's eyes fixed on a mass of red, flame-like liquid.

This was Flameflow Marrow!

Luo Chen was no stranger to this material, as Fu Chaosheng had given him a portion back in the Central Continent.

His origin true flame had been able to advance to the fifth grade precisely because it had absorbed that Flameflow Marrow, greatly increasing its spirituality, which later led to the opportunity for advancement.

But Luo Chen was more accustomed to calling Flameflow Marrow by the name the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had used—Withered-Source Fire Marrow!

Because this name was more vivid and easier to understand.

Withered-Source Fire Marrow was born in the withered source land left behind after a powerful strange fire departed, and it had many wonderful uses.

Especially in the alchemical inheritance left by the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, there was a fourth-grade pill formula called the "Two Modes Pill."

Using Withered-Source Fire Marrow and a special spiritual liquid from the demon realm as main ingredients, refining it into a pill, and taking it over a long period could adjust a cultivator's own true fire, gradually evolving it, eventually cultivating a powerful divine fire called "Yin-Yang Extreme Dao Flame"!

When Luo Chen first obtained that portion of Withered-Source Fire Marrow from Fu Chaosheng, his first thought was to refine the Two Modes Pill. But unfortunately, he didn't have the right materials on hand, so he had to let his Withering-Bloom True Fire benefit from it instead.

And now...

Luo Chen couldn't help but look at the other main materials, finally stopping on a mass of blue, watery liquid.

That was Nether Cold Spring!

It was sourced from the bottom of the Nether Abyss that had guarded the Nether Origin Sect for three thousand years.

In terms of properties, it was no less than that special spiritual liquid from the demon realm.

"If I use these two as main ingredients, supplemented by other precious materials, can I reconstruct the Two Modes Pill formula?"

For a moment, Luo Chen's heart raced with excitement.

...

In the geothermal fire hall, waves of heat rolled.

Three figures stood in their respective corners, each with different actions.

Master Xuanyan refined his pill with leisurely ease, Luo Chen scribbled furiously, sometimes frowning and sometimes smiling, while Chen Mozi still hesitated.

There seemed to be a sense of competition between them, yet the overall atmosphere was surprisingly harmonious.

Time passed slowly. In just over a month, a peculiar fragrance wafted through the hall.

Luo Chen, who had been deep in thought, suddenly woke up. Together with Chen Mozi, they both turned their gazes to another spot.

Master Xuanyan smiled, striking a series of hand seals toward the furnace lid.

With a soft cry.

"Open!"

The lid lifted, and a fist-sized black pill slowly rose.

Luo Chen and Chen Mozi exchanged glances. Had he succeeded?

Master Xuanyan reached out, and the black pill fell into his hand. He glanced at it and frowned slightly.

"The quality is mediocre, the medicinal power not condensed enough. But this Exhaustion Extreme Pill is at least finished. Passable!"

Then, his gaze swept over the two of them, finally stopping on Chen Mozi.

As the latter watched in surprise, the black pill was lightly tossed into his arms.

"This?" Chen Mozi was stunned.

Master Xuanyan smiled faintly. "This is a fourth-grade pill I created in my later years, called Exhaustion Extreme. I originally hoped to use it to break through the Sage limit and advance to Ascendant, but in the end, it lacked something, so it could only remain at the fourth grade, unable to reach the fifth."

"But although it can't help break through the great realm of Ascendant, it still has some use. It can allow a Sage cultivator to break through a minor realm."

"Of course, for us, realms are important, but not that important."

"The reason I give you this pill is that I hope you will be like its name—when pushed to exhaustion, seek change. Don't spend your whole life following old rules, only walking the paths laid by predecessors."

"Otherwise, even if you become a Grandmaster, you will only be a great synthesizer, not a founder of a new school!"

With that, without waiting for Chen Mozi to refuse, he left the geothermal fire hall freely.

No matter how dissatisfied he was with this refining result, he had still succeeded.

The fourth gate had already opened for him.

A wisp of cloud and smoke swept in, wrapping around Master Xuanyan, and disappeared from the geothermal fire hall.

Chen Mozi held the fist-sized black pill, falling into silence.

On the other side, Luo Chen's eyes were wide.

Self-researching a pill formula to break through to the Ascendant realm?

What bold ambition this Master Xuanyan had!

Though this world had Initiation Pills, Condensation Pills, and even Nascent Soul Pills for breaking through to the Sage realm, pills for breaking through the bottleneck to the Ascendant realm had never been heard of.

The other was trying to walk a path no one had walked before, relying on pills to become a god.

But unfortunately, he seemed to have failed, unable to refine a fifth-grade Exhaustion Extreme Pill.

Even so, a fourth-grade Exhaustion Extreme Pill that could forcibly break through a minor realm for a Sage cultivator was already heaven-defying enough.

Which of the nine layers of the Sage realm didn't require immense effort for a Sage cultivator to accumulate bit by bit?

One pill could replace decades or even centuries of bitter cultivation...

"Tsk, why didn't he give it to me?"

Luo Chen muttered inwardly, but he wasn't too envious.

He knew very well that he had no connection with Master Xuanyan. No matter how much the other liked to guide juniors, such benefits wouldn't fall to him.

Glancing at the dazed Chen Mozi, Luo Chen continued studying the Two Modes Pill formula.

Because the main materials were different, it was inevitably different from the formula left by the Devil Lord. He had to incorporate his own understanding on this basis to create a new formula—no easy task.

After all, the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining was the pinnacle of alchemy, a figure beyond description by terms like Alchemy Grandmaster or Alchemy Saint.

The formulas he left behind, aside from those obtained from others, must have been deduced to their utmost limit.

To modify and evolve them—how difficult was that?

But Luo Chen didn't want to give up!

Not because Chu Yan's inheritance was within reach—after all, Master Xuanyan had already gone to the final trial. Given his strong performance in passing the trials, the inheritance would most likely belong to him. There was no need for Luo Chen to join the fray.

He simply couldn't bear to waste these precious raw materials he had reverse-refined.

If he could truly use these materials to experiment with a new pill formula, the benefit to him would be enormous.

He had no interest in the so-called Yin-Yang Extreme Dao Flame, but within his Purple Mansion existed a Nirvana Sacred Flame that had only recently been born!

The Two Modes Pill had the effect of promoting the evolution and transformation of true fire.

If he could refine one or two, it might help the Nirvana Sacred Flame cross its most fragile infancy.

"Fortunately, I passed the second trial!"

Luo Chen felt three parts fortunate.

Improving the Devil Lord's pill formula was too difficult.

In the past, he certainly wouldn't have had the ability.

But after passing the second trial and understanding the essence of medicinal materials, his comprehension of various herbs' properties had deepened.

Hence, he had this confidence.

Yet even with such a vast foundation and deeper understanding of material essence, Luo Chen's progress in improving the formula remained slow.

The geothermal fire hall was still silent.

Days passed one by one—a month, two months, half a year, a year...

When a year had passed, Chen Mozi moved.

He seemed to have made up his mind, taking out a square copper cauldron and carefully placing the materials he had reverse-refined into it.

As for Luo Chen, he seemed to have succeeded.

Because the attribute panel was giving a prompt.

[Two Modes Pill, complete fourth-grade pill formula, can be learned]

[Learn?]

The answer was naturally yes. It was only fourth-grade, costing merely ten achievement points.

With a thought, a new entry appeared on the panel.

[Two Modes Pill Beginner 1/100]

Now, it was time to truly start refining.

But at this moment, hesitation arose in Luo Chen's heart.

He only had one set of raw materials. What if he failed?

This question was answered in an instant!

Because Chen Mozi had asked Master Xuanyan the same thing before.

The other hadn't answered, and Luo Chen hadn't thought about it at the time.

Why think about it?

Which of them, as alchemists, hadn't come through countless failures?

One must dare to try, dare to act, and have the resolve to accept failure.

An alchemist must have confidence!

Even if there was only one chance!

With a carefree smile, Luo Chen waved his hand and summoned the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Clang!

The cauldron, about ten feet in size, landed on the geothermal fire vent, emitting a dull sound.

Luo Chen activated the formation, drawing the geothermal fire to warm the cauldron first.

Because of this dull sound, Chen Mozi across from him looked over.

Perhaps because Master Xuanyan had already gone ahead, and the old man's displayed strength was truly too great, both seemed to have tacitly accepted the destination of the Alchemy Saint's inheritance.

At this moment, their eyes met. There was no fiery competition between rivals, only peace.

"Luo Chen... I wish you success." Chen Mozi said calmly, his words impossible to tell if sincere or insincere.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "And I wish you, Fellow Daoist, a swift success!"

Whatever petty thoughts each had harbored before, at this moment, they dissipated like smoke.

Fellow travelers on the same path, even if they couldn't cherish each other, at least without absolute conflict of interest, there was no need for drawn swords.

Boom!

The flames rose.
