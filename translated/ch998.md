# Chapter 998: The True Spirit Path, the Spring Eye Is About to Emerge

The sky hung low as two figures sped across it.

The figure in front moved with incredible speed and agility, like a fluttering butterfly, treading a mysterious footwork as he advanced continuously.

Deng Taiyue followed far behind, his eyes filled with astonishment.

"This Luo Chen's footwork is so profound. If used in battle, I fear even ordinary Sages would be unable to lock onto him with their divine sense."

"Especially—every time he inexplicably changes direction, it's as if he's dodging something."

"Space cracks?"

While he was curious, a warning suddenly came from ahead.

"Careful!"

Deng Taiyue snapped awake and hastily retreated.

At some point, a barely visible black thread was slowly drifting toward him on his path forward.

It was a newly formed space crack!

If not for Luo Chen's warning, and he had collided head-on with it, at best his physical body would have been severed in two; at worst, he would have been sucked in and perished on the spot.

For a moment, Deng Taiyue was overcome with lingering fear.

Ahead, Luo Chen had already stopped, frowning as he looked back.

Deng Taiyue's face alternated between pale and green. Guiltily, he said, "Sorry, I'm holding you back."

Luo Chen waved his hand. "It's fine. Just follow me closely from now on!"

As he spoke, he released a strand of mana, forming a mana chain that connected himself and Deng Taiyue. Then he set off again, flying at top speed under the gloomy sky.

Deng Taiyue followed closely behind, not resisting Luo Chen's mana chain. Instead, he grew even more admiring of his methods.

The alchemist Luo Chen could actually detect the appearance of space cracks in advance!

Such ability was truly astonishing.

No wonder the rumors said he could fight to the death with Azure Frost, who was known as the number one under the Ascendants.

The Demon Emperor Azure Frost was most renowned for two things: her indestructible physical body and her elusive Void-Dodging Divine Art.

If Luo Chen could perceive spatial fluctuations, then against the Demon Emperor Azure Frost, he would indeed have three-tenths more chance of victory.

"The rumors must be true!"

With this thought, Deng Taiyue's attitude toward Luo Chen grew increasingly admiring and even reverent.

Luo Chen was already extremely famous, and now his progress changed with every encounter. As a fellow mid-stage Sage, Deng Taiyue didn't consider himself Luo Chen's opponent at all.

I fear that in the entire Eastern Wilderness these days, there are very few who can match Luo Chen!

"If I rely on the Initiation Pill to comprehend the origin of earth and advance to late-stage Sage, could I defeat him?"

The news from the Western Desert hadn't yet arrived. If Deng Taiyue knew that Luo Chen had once single-handedly resisted the Arhat Grand Formation formed by thirty-six Sages, he probably wouldn't have this thought.

Ahead, Luo Chen's eyes blazed with golden light, already revealing the appearance of Crimson Eyes, Golden Pupils.

With these spiritual eyes containing the power to break illusions and break space, he could avoid obvious space cracks in advance.

Not only that!

He could even keenly detect and quickly dodge some tiny cracks that had just been born.

This was something he couldn't do before.

As he traveled, Luo Chen experimented with surprise. After multiple attempts, he confirmed it wasn't a coincidence.

"Why is my perception of spatial fluctuations so sharp now?"

A surge in soul foundation?

Luo Chen dismissed this answer immediately.

These naturally generated space cracks had no life force. No matter how vast one's divine sense was, they couldn't be detected.

It was precisely because of these space cracks that could appear at any moment that Ascendant powerhouses were blocked outside. This showed how much this place suppressed divine sense.

Was it the enhancement of these eyes?

Luo Chen touched the corner of his eye and soon rejected this possibility as well.

He had indeed not stopped using the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid, but the improvement of his eyes was by no means an overnight achievement. How could they have evolved to such a degree?

"Could it be Azure Frost?"

Luo Chen pursed his lips, thinking of his current relationship with Azure Frost.

The Great Dao oath, the restrictions within his body, and... the Life-and-Death Companion Gu!

Especially that Life-and-Death Companion Gu, which was said to allow both parties to enjoy each other's cultivation achievements. The lower one's realm, the better the enhancement effect.

Even though the Life-and-Death Companion Gu he had taken out back then was only fourth-rank, there was still a faint effect in this regard.

If this new ability to keenly perceive spatial fluctuations was brought to him by Azure Frost, then what had Azure Frost gained from him?

This thought flashed through Luo Chen's mind, then he suppressed it and focused on traveling.

...

On Green Parasol Mountain.

In the Daytime Void Hall!

Within an empty white world that contained nothing else, a woman in green sat cross-legged.

Her eyes were tightly shut, her mind concentrated, as she mobilized her mana containing spatial origin to feed the small white flower within her body, drop by drop.

Accumulated Space Flower!

It was said to be a divine flower that could both bloom into a world and open a second Purple Mansion.

Unfortunately, the one Azure Frost had obtained was only fourth-rank, still far from fifth-rank.

What she told Luo Chen was that she needed to refine it, but in reality, she needed to nurture it.

It was precisely because she needed to use her mana to nurture this flower at all times that her perfected combat system had developed flaws.

Not only had she been captured alive by the Ascendant powerhouse Chunyuan, but she had also revealed a weakness in her battle with Luo Chen.

This problem had actually been pointed out to her long ago by Rosycloud. Before she succeeded completely, it was best not to fight. But back then, to contain the Tianyuan Dao Sect cultivators in the Eastern Wilderness and allow her sister to attack the Central Continent without worries, she had to act frequently.

Now that she had free time, she naturally began nurturing again.

As long as the Accumulated Space Flower reached fifth-rank and opened a small world within her body, she could personally experience the process of space generation during that opening, thereby accelerating her understanding of spatial origin.

At that time, advancing to Ascendant would be a natural progression.

Azure Frost, immersed in her cultivation, was completely focused.

Suddenly!

Her mind stirred, and her back itched.

Shua!

A pair of wings unfurled involuntarily.

Then, in an instant, a massive Azure Luan true body manifested in the Daytime Void world.

Although Azure Frost was somewhat surprised, she instinctively felt this was better, so she didn't transform back into human form and continued to nurture the Accumulated Space Flower in this state.

And in this process, she discovered that as the Accumulated Space Flower grew, while absorbing her mana, it also refluxed some mysterious power that acted upon her body, making her massive physical form gain a bit more of an ancient, primordial aura.

Sacred and noble, ancient and distant.

Just as Azure Frost unfolded her true body, a sleeping woman in red within the Profound Mystery Hall slowly opened her eyes.

"The True Spirit Path?"

"Why has she embarked on this path of returning to antiquity?"

"Should I stop her?"

A gleam of thought appeared in her weary eyes, but soon the woman in red dismissed the idea of stopping her.

"This is good as well. Her bloodline power was originally weak. Her rapid progress carries the hidden danger of over-forcing growth. If she can succeed in returning to antiquity through the True Spirit Path, there might still be a glimmer of hope."

Murmuring to herself, the extremely weary woman in red closed her eyes again and sank into deep slumber.

...

Gurgle, gurgle.

Bubbles rose and fell one after another, undercurrents constantly surging.

Within the pitch-black Nether Spring, only a few specks of light flickered continuously.

Zooming in, those were faint green flames revealed from within a majestic and steady mountain.

Each cluster of green flame contained a powerful aura.

Counting carefully, there were as many as seven of them, completely surrounding a massive several-hundred-zhang Xuan Turtle.

The Xuan Turtle emitted a hazy water vapor, forming a powerful barrier that blocked the attacks launched by the seven clusters of green flame.

But under the continuous assault, the water vapor barrier grew increasingly unstable.

Suddenly!

Xiu!

A fiercely powerful blood arrow shot out from the water vapor barrier, heading straight for one cluster of green flame.

This blood arrow came so abruptly that its owner was caught off guard and was breached in the blink of an eye.

A muffled groan indicated that the owner of that green flame had suffered considerable injury.

Someone nearby sneered, "Yan Qianji, aren't you supposed to have the strongest defense among the forty-nine Ghost Emperors of the Netherworld? How can you not even block the sneak attack of a mere body cultivator?"

Yan Qianji, his face full of wrinkles like a giant softshell turtle, swept his hand across his chest. With thick ghost qi swirling, the large hole in his chest was smoothed over in an instant.

He hated it!

If it weren't for that suddenly appearing figure back then, who had stamped a palm on him, and the strange flames containing immense life force that constantly eroded his ghost qi, how could he have fallen to such a state?

Not only did his supposedly unbreakable defense have a flaw, but even his cultivation had been forced to stop.

Within this intense hatred, there was also a hint of delight.

"It doesn't matter. That person has already left. These should be his disciples and subordinates. If I can devour this Xuan Turtle and use its form to supplement mine, not only will my injuries fully recover, but my realm can also be greatly enhanced. At that time, even that Netherwater Spring Eye, I can compete for it!"

As he thought, his gaze unconsciously looked up toward the top of Yanfu Mountain.

There, dense ghost fire spread continuously, almost completely covering a hall at the mountain's peak.

Its momentum was a hundred times greater than that of the seven Ghost Emperors here!

Yan Qianji snorted coldly and rebuked, "Mind your own business. Don't let those two inside escape."

Laughter came from beside him.

"How could we let them escape? That man's rich blood and qi is simply the best blood food. I'm not worthy of competing for the Netherwater Spring Eye, but with this blood food to supplement me, this lord might be able to refine a clone, turn from yin to yang, and cultivate the Great Dao again!"

After this laughter came the echoing responses of the others.

"That white python female ghost is mine. This old man still lacks a favored concubine. Her appearance is quite good!"

"I want that Soul-Refining Banner! Ordinary treasures cannot withstand the filth of the Netherwater. That Soul-Refining Banner is mine."

...

The conversation of the seven Ghost Emperors was not particularly concealed.

Instead, they used their vast yin soul power to wantonly impact the two figures protected by the Xuan Turtle, time and again.

Ghost sounds shook the spirit, words disturbed the mind.

If it were an ordinary person, under such circumstances, they would have already felt fear.

But Wang Yuan, standing on the Xuan Turtle's back, remained expressionless, as steady as ever.

He slowly withdrew his finger, revealing a thoughtful expression.

Body cultivators feared ghost cultivators the most, because ghost cultivators had no physical form, making it difficult for their fists and feet to hit them.

But body cultivators also feared ghost cultivators the least, because their surging blood and qi was the best restraint in the world, comparable to divine thunder and true fire!

Through multiple battles, he had gradually figured out methods to attack these powerful Ghost Emperors.

The Motian Hundred Creations Mountain was useless, the Thunderclap Gloves were unsatisfactory.

But the Divine Astral Fighting Qi nurtured by his Leakless Golden Body was extremely powerful.

If he infused it with his own martial blood essence and struck with a special technique, he might have a chance to kill one when they were caught off guard.

"This move..."

Just as Wang Yuan was thinking, a voice full of guilt came from behind.

"Brother Wang Yuan, it's my fault for holding you back. If I hadn't insisted on going up the mountain, we wouldn't be trapped here."

Wang Yuan replied without turning his head, "Don't blame yourself. There has always been a rumor in this Nether Spring that whoever obtains the Netherwater Spring Eye controls the Nether Spring. If they really gain control of the Nether Spring, where would our Luo Tian Sect go? Therefore, even if you hadn't gone up the mountain, I would have personally come to fight for it."

Bai Meiling sat cross-legged on the Xuan Turtle's back, looking at Wang Yuan's tall figure. Even though he comforted her, she still blamed herself.

Ever since Yanfu Mountain fell back then, it had been sitting here.

Countless ghost creatures coveted this place!

But because of the hidden fifth-rank Ghost Emperors in the Nether Spring, powerful high-rank ghost creatures didn't dare to act rashly.

Who knew that a few years ago, Rosycloud would revisit the Nether Spring, use her great divine ability to unfold the spirit treasure Myriad Beast Diagram, and forcibly take away a group of fifth-rank Ghost Emperors.

Thus, without the suppression of high-ranking beings, a hundred ghosts emerged together, and the Nether Spring was in turmoil!

The most top-tier Ghost Emperors all headed toward Yanfu Mountain.

Especially since the Netherwater Spring Eye was rumored to be about to condense. Whoever obtained this object could master the vast ocean and become the master of the Nether Spring. There were even rumors that the Netherwater Spring Eye contained the power of creation and could create Ascendant powerhouses.

The ghost creatures grew even more frenzied!

Back then, she had received her master's orders to always keep an eye on the situation at Yanfu Mountain.

Seeing one powerful Ghost Emperor after another enter Yanfu Mountain, how could she sit idly by?

Thinking she had the Xuan Turtle to help her, she could also fight for it. But she didn't expect these old ghosts who had lived for thousands of years to be extremely cunning, far beyond her imagination.

Not only was she severely injured, but Brother Wang Yuan, who had come to her aid, was also surrounded.

While she was blaming herself, Wang Yuan suddenly said, "I'm going to launch a sudden attack, focusing on that old softshell turtle Yan Qianji. I have seventy percent confidence in killing him, or at worst, scattering his soul body. You can take this opportunity to break out!"

"But..."

"No buts. Only after you leave can I go all out. Besides, the Xuan Turtle is here; it can still hold out for a while. Then you go back to Luo Tian Sect and ask Changfengzi and Canglong Fairy to act together. They can always save my life."

Bai Meiling opened her mouth but didn't refuse again.

This was the best way to resolve the current predicament!

After making the decision, Wang Yuan said no more. He calmed his mind and gathered his strength, ready to strike.

At his right fingertip, countless Divine Astral Fighting Qi surged, continuously compressing until it finally condensed into a qi blade the size of a wheat awn.

"If this move succeeds, it will become another great killing technique of mine," Wang Yuan thought.

When the seven Ghost Emperors attacked together again, and the Xuan Turtle was on the verge of collapse, Wang Yuan suddenly struck.

"Go!"

The qi blade shot out, with the white python following closely behind.

The big softshell turtle Ghost Emperor Yan Qianji was the first to bear the brunt. Seeing this, instead of being afraid, he was angered.

"Do you really think this lord is a pushover!"

He opened his mouth and spat out a black water column, heading straight for Bai Meiling.

But the water column split into two sections halfway through.

Only then did Yan Qianji discover the hidden qi energy.

"What is that..."

Pu!

The qi blade instantly pierced his protective light shield and entered his body.

Then...

Xiu! Xiu! Xiu!

Thousands of qi forces erupted simultaneously, turning his soul body into a sieve in an instant.

Countless ghost qi shattered, trying to regroup, but were constantly being shredded.

From within came only Yan Qianji's angry roar.

"Are you all just going to stand there? When will you act!"

In response to this angry roar, there was immediate reaction from the surroundings.

Several attacks flew over, heading for Bai Meiling.

Wang Yuan, on the Xuan Turtle's back, saw this and knew it was bad. The other side had been prepared all along.

Had his repeated probing attacks made them wary?

Even the Ghost Emperor who had earlier spoken of capturing Bai Meiling alive now struck with ruthless ferocity, with not a trace of the tenderness he had shown before.

In an instant, Bai Meiling was caught in a deadly situation!

Just then!

A sudden change occurred!

A square earthen-yellow seal descended from the sky, enveloping Bai Meiling within it.

All the attacks that landed on it vanished like mud entering the sea, unable to harm Bai Meiling in the slightest.

Following closely behind were seven terrifyingly powerful cyan handprints, slapping toward the seven without any pause for breath.

(Chapter End)
