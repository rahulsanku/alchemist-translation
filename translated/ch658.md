# Chapter 658: Five-Colored Bewilderment, Starry Sky Illusion, Leaving Forgetting Heaven

"Luo Chen, are you willing to enter my Pill Hall?"

The warm recruitment entered his ears, and the gentle, spring-breeze smile entered his eyes. Luo Chen only felt chaos filling his mind.

A violent aura arose without cause.

He had cultivated for a hundred years, once stood above tens of thousands, once retreated safely from the Feather race Holy Land, and even when facing a Sage, he felt he had strength to contend.

Now, a mere Foundation Establishment True Cultivator dared speak of subduing him.

"Kill!"

"Kill!"

"Kill!"

One sword raised should be able to sever purity and quiet.

Just as Luo Chen's eyes grew increasingly red, countless voices seemed to sound beside his ear.

"Little Luo, this moment is just like that moment!" It was Qin Liangchen's voice.

But when Luo Chen looked at the man standing beside the wealthy elder, the other party had not opened his mouth.

Was this moment the same as that moment?

Luo Chen asked his own heart.

"You are a clever person. Let this matter end here." It was Miao Wen's voice.

He had always been clever and decisive. Even if later it seemed that some choices were wrong, at the time, they had absolutely been correct.

How could cleverness now become his own undoing?

"Chairman, I will wait in Danxia for you to return."

"Fellow Daoist Luo, I will trouble you with the later matters of my Li clan."

"Luo Chen..."

"Devil Lord Azure Yang..."

Voice after voice, like a tide, was faint at first. Later, the sound became like thunder, shaking Luo Chen's soul endlessly.

At this moment, Mi Shuhua's voice entered his ear again.

"Luo Chen, are you willing to enter my Pill Hall?"

Facing that sinister gaze, Luo Chen slowly raised his head, took a deep breath, then bent slightly at the waist.

The sinister color scattered, and the wealthy old man laughed heartily.

"A wise man submits to circumstances. Luo Chen, you will not regret today's choice!"

Amid this rumbling laughter, his figure began continuously scattering.

Luo Chen quietly watched this scene, but his body trembled faintly.

Yes, he did not regret it.

If he regretted every moment, he could not have reached this step.

Regarding everything he had done in the past, even if it was ugly and filthy, even if he had submitted to others and humbled himself, he had never regretted it.

No matter how profound his realm now was, no matter how surrounded by blooming splendor, it could not hide that the path he had walked in the past was once muddy.

Only by facing the past directly and accepting the ugliness within his heart could he continue forward.

After Luo Chen woke from the urge to change the past and sink into pain, he slowly blinked.

The scene before him was no small city under the bright moon, nor Danxia night scenery.

It was only boundless starry sky, and he alone, walking through it.

Floating in the starry sky, Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat.

A black chess piece landed in his palm.

Without wind or sound, the black chess piece turned into a handful of powder.

The Rotten Ke Black Chess, which had protected him along the way, had reached the end of its lifespan.

Luo Chen was not surprised this day had come.

During the earlier foul-qi attack from the desolate beast Ghost Immortal Tower, the Rotten Ke Black Chess had already been damaged.

And after he stepped into this starry sky, when an illusion so terrifying it left one without any reaction and without the power to resist descended, the Rotten Ke Black Chess quietly shattered.

Before shattering, because it had been refined for many years, it sent Luo Chen one final warning.

And this warning reached its maximum in the memory of Miao Wen interrogating Luo Chen's heart in Eastern Wasteland's Magnetic Valley, stirring up the chaos within Luo Chen's heart.

Thanks to this, Luo Chen made the correct choice at that final barrier.

Respect history and firmly believe that it was the correct route to his success.

Instead of regretting and trying to change everything in the illusion.

Besides, change?

How should it be changed?

Reject Mi Shuhua's recruitment, hot-blooded as a youth, unyielding in pride? Then he would have died under the other's vicious methods back then.

Just as if he killed Mi Li, Miao Wen would fear him. How would his later rise by taking advantage of the situation come about?

Was rejecting Sima Huiniang the correct choice? Not necessarily.

When he spared Yan Leizi, the other party would certainly seek revenge, so killing him by force back then despite the pressure of his Golden Core father had been correct.

Just like on Moon Invitation Island, fiercely killing Yan Nantian instead of keeping him by his side and raising a tiger to invite disaster.

"My heart and my actions are clear as a mirror. Everything I have done is correct."

Luo Chen smiled and casually raised his hand.

The black powder in his hand drifted into the starry sky.

Only now did he have spare strength to observe this "starry sky."

An endless black space, with only several thousand stars hanging high above.

Starlight scattered onto him. It had been extremely dim earlier, but now it became dazzling.

Looking at those stars and the starlight on his body, Luo Chen was thoughtful.

"The illusion must be born from this."

"And this illusion is completely different from those I have seen before. It does not seek beauty or light, nor show temptations of wealth, sex, or power. Instead, it pierces through the ugliness in a person's heart and makes them face it. If one dares not face one's past and tries to change history, nine times out of ten one will sink into it and never awaken."

"Truly exquisite, inconceivable."

After sighing in admiration, Luo Chen looked at the wooden plaque on his chest.

It was soul-nourishing wood, where Han Zhan resided.

At this moment, the other party was silent. Luo Chen did not know whether he had hidden himself or been forced into an illusion.

Looking left and right, he did not see Ding Yi or Cave Master Moyun.

They should have been forced apart unknowingly when they left the skeletal corridor.

"I wonder if we will emerge in the same place after leaving?"

Luo Chen thought for a moment and moved.

As his steps moved, figures gradually emerged in the dark starry sky.

Seductive demonesses in revealing clothes, holy fairies wearing white gauze, all appeared on both sides of his path, striking alluring poses. Some panted softly, some moaned quietly, some caressed their peaks...

"Again?"

Luo Chen shook his head and strode forward without stopping.

This kind of illusion might affect cultivators with weaker wills.

But Luo Chen had experienced countless illusions in his life.

Whether the dream illusion from taking Netherpassage Pills, the heart-questioning illusion of the Ghost God Heart-Questioning Mirror, or the foul-qi illusion of the Ghost Immortal Tower, none had been easy.

After long training, he had long possessed extremely strong resistance.

At this moment, even without the Rotten Ke Black Chess protecting him, his firm Dao heart was not affected at all. He simply rushed onward.

During his advance, the illusion also continuously changed.

From initial beauties entering the eyes, to delicacies later making the mouth water, fragrance surging at the nose, and even demonic sounds and immortal music rolling within the ears.

Luo Chen remained unmoved, but he was increasingly shocked inwardly.

The environment arranged in this starry sky was far too wondrous.

Eyes, ears, mouth, nose, and heart were all targeted.

One moment of carelessness and one would sink inside.

Only those with firm Dao hearts and the will to advance without turning back could remain unaffected.

Even someone as strong as him, who was likewise proficient in eye-based illusions, had almost been confused and sunk at the heart barrier on first contact.

Moreover, Luo Chen could see that the might of these illusions matched his own realm and did not exceed it by too much. It was just right.

"The master who arranged this starry sky illusion truly had extraordinary methods. It inspires awe."

Luo Chen sighed, his pace growing faster and faster. The starlight ahead gradually formed a road.

Raising his eyes, he saw an exit emitting light appear faintly at the end of the star road.

Luo Chen's spirit rose. Ignoring the bewildering sights and sounds and hidden fragrance behind him, he leapt into that exit.

"This is..."

Inside a quiet palace, Luo Chen turned back and looked at the exit.

Three words were written above it.

Leaving Forgetting Heaven.

"That starry sky illusion is called Leaving Forgetting Heaven?"

Luo Chen was thoughtful. He withdrew his gaze and looked around this palace, which was neither large nor small.

Rather than a palace, it was more like a giant stone house.

Formation patterns spread beneath his feet. Streams of spiritual qi poured in from outside, then followed those formation patterns and disappeared beneath the stone house.

Clearly, this was the origin point of a vortex passage.

It passed through the starry sky illusion, Leaving Forgetting Heaven, the Black Tortoise skeletal corridor, and finally pierced through the Sunken Sea to appear in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Large amounts of heaven and earth spiritual qi from outside were also absorbed in.

At this moment, the spiritual qi in this stone house was terrifyingly dense. From what Luo Chen saw, it was at least fourth-tier or higher.

If not for being transported back and forth, making it too violent, this would absolutely be an extremely high-quality cultivation place.

But violent was exactly right.

Satisfaction appeared in Luo Chen's eyes. With his current physique, aided by formations, he might be able to borrow the spiritual qi here to recover somewhat.

During that previous month in the Sunken Sea, even though he constantly took supreme-grade True Flame Pills, it had still consumed a great deal of his mana.

Even the second Origin Pill was now dim.

However, before regulating his breathing, Luo Chen first did two things.

He checked the soul-nourishing wood.

"Senior?"

Inside, a voice came as usual.

"I am here."

It seemed Han Zhan was unharmed.

Luo Chen looked at the soul-nourishing wood's spiritual light, which had dimmed by nearly half, and narrowed his eyes slightly.

He did not ask what had happened to the other party. That illusion was too strange and best at stirring the ugliness in people's hearts.

Asking too much would be impolite.

Afterward, Luo Chen sent his divine sense into two spirit beast pouches.

"How are you?"

"Master, how are we what?"

"Master, do you need help?"

Black King and Heavenjade's voices came one after another, as if they had encountered nothing.

Luo Chen frowned. "Did you not encounter an illusion?"

Both demons answered no.

Luo Chen looked again toward Leaving Forgetting Heaven.

Why were the two demon beasts unaffected?

Was it a difference of race, or because they were enslaved and had lost independence, causing that starry sky illusion not to target them?

No one answered Luo Chen.

Nor did Luo Chen have the thought to pursue it to the end.

He took out the Sealing God Compass and arranged the Billowing Cloud Dense Mist Array in a corner, then sat cross-legged inside and quietly meditated.

The surrounding violent spiritual qi washed over him.

Luo Chen grunted and forcibly began absorbing and refining it.

In the past, he would absolutely not have dared do this.

But now, his Ancient Wilderness Fourth Tier physique made him feel he could withstand this violent heaven and earth spiritual qi.

And the result was indeed so.

Large amounts of spiritual qi entered his body, first filtered by the blood membrane condensed from origin power, then continuously refined as it circulated through his meridians.

By the time it entered his qi sea, it had become gentle and calm.

Even if its quality was inferior to spiritual qi normally refined outside, this portion of mana did not enter the lifebound Golden Core, but recovered the second Origin Pill instead.

"I will wait a little longer."

"If they still do not come out, I will leave on my own."

This wait lasted half a day.

Half a day later, Cave Master Moyun stepped out from the exit.

Luo Chen dispersed the formation and stepped out refreshed.

"Cave Master, are you well?"

Cave Master Moyun's face was somewhat pale, but his eyes were extremely clear. He nodded to Luo Chen.

"I am fine."

Afterward, he walked to the side alone and took out a shell-shaped magic treasure from his storage bag.

With mana injected, the shell continuously enlarged.

"This old man intends to regulate his breath and recover mana. I must trouble Fellow Daoist to watch over me."

Luo Chen nodded, then watched the other party step into the shell.

After the shell closed, the surrounding heaven and earth spiritual qi washed over it, and visible wisps of spiritual qi seeped inside.

"An interesting magic treasure."

Luo Chen muttered and looked again at the starlit exit.

Ding Yi had delayed somewhat long.

He curled his lip, opened his qi armor, and walked around the stone hall.

After walking for a while, Luo Chen stopped beside a stone pillar.

"This architectural style seems unlike anything I have ever seen."

His fingers rubbed the relief sculpture on the stone pillar, and Luo Chen frowned slightly.

On it was carved a demon wolf with three heads. Its color could not be seen, but its expression was incomparably ferocious.

Luo Chen had never seen records of such a demon wolf in the classics of Eastern Wasteland or the Northern Sea.

"Senior, do you know this thing?"

Han Zhan's voice sounded faintly. "I do not know it, nor have I seen it. It does not seem like a demon of the Mountains and Seas Realm."

"What about the others?"

Luo Chen's gaze swept across the other stone pillars in the hall, which also had many relief carvings.

Many strange beasts, each with a different style.

Han Zhan was silent for a long while. Finally, he said, "I do not know them either."

For even the well-informed Han Zhan to find them this unfamiliar, Luo Chen became even more cautious about the following actions.

"Void Refining True Lord, Demon-Fall Land. The owner of this place may not have been an existence of the Mountains and Seas Realm in life."

Luo Chen murmured.

At this moment, light flickered at the exit.

Luo Chen abruptly turned his head. A pair of cunning and cruel eyes suddenly met his.

But after only one glance, that gaze scattered and turned blank.

"Fellow Daoist Ding?" Luo Chen took one step back and asked in a deep voice.

Ding Yi looked blankly around, then suddenly shook his head, held it, and made a pained sound.

Luo Chen became even more guarded. From the sword case on his back, the faint sound of sword cries rang out.

Just as he was about to act, Ding Yi's trembling body slowly calmed.

"I am fine. I was only injured in the soul by the illusion and the pain is hard to bear."

Ding Yi stretched out a hand to signal Luo Chen not to attack, then found a corner with slightly weaker spiritual qi and sat cross-legged.

Luo Chen frowned as he watched. In his mind, the cunning, cruel gaze the other party had revealed when first entering the hall kept replaying.

That was not Ding Yi's gaze.

Guarded inwardly, Luo Chen's body had unknowingly walked to the great hall's door.

Now that everyone was gathered, they should explore this Demon-Fall Land and search for the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

He looked back at the two, then resolutely pulled open the door.

Whoosh!

A gust of wild wind blew in, carrying rusted iron qi and endless sharpness.

With only one glance, Luo Chen was stunned by the scene before him.

Mountain peaks towering into the clouds like inverted long swords stretched forward without end.

Luo Chen murmured,

"Is this still the lifeless Sunken Sea?"

*(End of Chapter)*
