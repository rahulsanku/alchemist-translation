# Chapter 1106: Split Heaven Stream Lord, Honored Master Luo Chen

**Crane Head Isle!**

The foremost island of the Swallow Islands.

Here stood the largest dock, also the place with the greatest flow of people coming and going.

But today, Crane Head Isle was eerily silent.

Not a single person was in sight, not a single living creature could be seen.

Boats of various sizes, large and small, had lowered their sails and were moored in the harbor.

Only a single figure stood quietly on the dock, surveying his surroundings with a complex expression.

This had once been the environment where he had grown up from childhood. Even though centuries had passed and many things had changed, it still felt familiar.

Back then, he had not been valued by his clan. To improve his alchemy skills, he would occasionally come to the dock to take on part-time jobs, earning spirit stones.

Perhaps if he had continued like that for the rest of his life, he could have become a skilled first-grade alchemist, at most second-grade.

As for his cultivation realm, it would have stopped at Qi Refinement.

But everything had changed because of his master's appearance.

Back then, his master had needed a large number of assistants.

Whether low-grade alchemists or fire-attendant medicine apprentices, the more the better.

Various clans had catered to this preference, selecting a large batch of talents to send over. And because he was familiar with medicinal herbs, he had been called over as well.

Thus, the true opportunity belonging to him, Xu Muxian, had truly begun.

He became a nominal disciple of Azure Yang Devil Lord, learned his master's alchemy techniques, was valued by his clan, and arranged a marriage with the Xu family...

But what touched Xu Muxian the most was a conversation he had with Luo Chen back then on Moon Inviting Island.

At that time, he was merely at the seventh layer of Qi Refinement. Feeling that his own low-level life was comfortable while his lofty master's cultivation was bitterly hard, he had unconsciously grown confused. Therefore, he had specially sought guidance from his master.

The beginning of his inquiry was met with stern reprimand, a scolding for his overreaching ambitions.

But by the end, his master seemed to have thought it through and did not add further harsh criticism.

It was as if he tacitly acknowledged that people have different aspirations, which should not be forced.

In the end, his master only added one sentence: "The result is not important. The journey of seeking immortality holds more beautiful scenery and a broader world."

That single sentence had profoundly moved him.

All his confusion was perhaps hidden within that vast world.

After his master left the Swallow Islands, he relied on his own bitter cultivation and the Foundation Establishment Pill his master had left behind to successfully establish his foundation. When his Dao companion's lifespan ended and she passed away, Xu Muxian bid farewell to his master's wife, Cheng Haixin, and followed in his master's footsteps, leaving the Swallow Islands to venture alone into the Northern Sea.

In the blink of an eye, over three hundred years had passed.

Three hundred years of time, over a hundred thousand days and nights.

Different moonlight had shone upon him; violent or gentle sea breezes had blown across him.

He had witnessed the treachery of human hearts, the cunning of demon beasts, the conflicts between major powers, and the rise and fall of Northern Sea overlords, each taking their turn on the stage.

Among them, he had also seen the struggles of the weak to survive and the helpless sighs of those nearing the end of their lifespans.

Step by step, through wandering and adventure, coupled with occasional fortuitous encounters, he had ultimately achieved the Sage realm.

Along the way, he had occasionally heard the name "Azure Yang Devil Lord," but whenever he pursued the trail, it mostly led to a dead end.

Even the Myriad Immortals Assembly had been scattered by wind and rain.

Later, he stopped trying to seek out his master.

After all, he was merely a nominal disciple. If fate did not bring them together, then so be it.

Returning to his homeland today to protect his master's wife during her Nascent Soul formation could be considered fulfilling the master-disciple bond they had shared back then!

Suddenly!

The wind did not stir, yet the sails moved.

Xu Muxian's heart stirred. Amidst the rustling of countless sails unfurling, he looked up into the distance.

A streak of cyan light arrived in an instant.

Where it passed, a clearly visible scar was left across the azure sky, as if the very heavens had been torn apart by the newcomer.

A powerful aura pressed closer and closer.

Xu Muxian's heart began to pound violently. The longsword in his hand unconsciously let out a trembling hum.

His Sage domain abruptly expanded!

*Boom!*

Split Heaven Stream Lord suddenly halted, his narrow eyes squinting as he looked at the green-robed man.

"Who are you? Why are you blocking me?"

Xu Muxian's expression was calm. "I am Xu Muxian. I ask you, Fellow Daoist, to stop here and not disturb the Nascent Soul formation process of the person on the island."

"Xu Muxian?" Split Heaven Stream Lord frowned. It seemed he had heard that name somewhere. He had roamed the Northern Sea for a long time and knew most Sage cultivators, but this man was unfamiliar. After a moment's thought, his eyes suddenly lit up. "Could you be the wandering cultivator White Alchemy Master who refined the Thousand Gold Pill for Old Monster Fengzhang?"

Xu Muxian nodded. "That is indeed me!"

Split Heaven Stream Lord laughed heartily. "That Thousand Gold Pill is a fourth-grade pill, extremely precious. It was precisely by relying on the Thousand Gold Pill that Old Monster Fengzhang was able to advance to the late Sage stage. He even put out word that anyone who dares to trouble you is opposing him."

Xu Muxian breathed a slight sigh of relief.

In the current Northern Sea, after years of war and chaos, even though high-level cultivators had extremely strong life-preservation abilities, their numbers had dwindled significantly.

Under these circumstances, the demonic path was gradually gaining power while the righteous path declined.

But within the righteous path, there were also top-tier experts who rose to the challenge.

Old Monster Fengzhang was one of them. After advancing to Grand Cultivator, he protected a region of humanity.

Xu Muxian had a good relationship with him. Borrowing the other's reputation now might be enough to scare off Split Heaven Stream Lord.

However, before he could be happy, Split Heaven Stream Lord's expression suddenly changed.

"Others might sell Old Monster Fengzhang some face, but I have long-standing grievances with him. If you step aside today, fine. If not, then I don't mind tearing you apart!"

Xu Muxian's expression darkened. "Split Heaven Stream Lord, do you truly insist on this irreconcilable conflict? The person forming the Nascent Soul behind me is my master's wife. Her Dao companion is also a formidable expert. Think carefully—once you make a move, you will offend not only me, but also Fellow Daoist Fengzhang, and my master as well!"

These words carried considerable weight.

Old Monster Fengzhang was a Grand Cultivator at the height of his power. White Alchemy Master Xu Muxian was also beginning to make a name for himself in the Northern Sea. With his fourth-grade alchemist skills, he had built up a wide network of connections. If, as he said, his master was also a Sage expert, then Split Heaven Stream Lord really would have to weigh his options today.

Split Heaven Stream Lord blinked, a curious look in his eyes. "And what might your master's esteemed name be?"

The moment he asked this question, before Xu Muxian could even answer, the sky over the Swallow Islands suddenly changed.

Wind and clouds converged. Lightning flashed.

The heavenly tribulation had appeared!

This scene explained everything.

Moon Inviting Fairy, Cheng Haixin, had already crossed all the difficult steps before forming the Nascent Soul. She only needed to survive the thunder tribulation to become a true Sage!

Seeing this, Split Heaven Stream Lord was overjoyed. He no longer bothered to wait for Xu Muxian's answer.

A newly condensed Nascent Soul like this was the most delicious morsel. If he could devour both the infant and the soul, he would have a chance to take a great step forward and completely enter the late Sage stage.

Not only that, he might even be able to condense the legendary primordial spirit ahead of others!

"Get out of my way!"

He roared, transforming into a streak of cyan light as he shot through the air.

Xu Muxian did not dodge or evade. He swung his sword.

But the gap in realm between them was like a chasm, and the difference in strength was even more apparent.

Split Heaven Stream Lord merely waved his hand casually, scattering the sword light. He reached out and grabbed towards Xu Muxian.

His signature technique, the Split Heaven Divine Claw!

Facing this move, Xu Muxian's expression turned grim. A nest-like treasure artifact appeared beside him.

From within the nest, streaks of dark light flew out. One fire phoenix after another emerged, firmly blocking the Split Heaven Divine Claw from advancing.

"The Perch Phoenix Nest! Old Monster Fengzhang is truly generous, even willing to lend you a defensive true artifact like this."

His ultimate move was ineffective, but Split Heaven Stream Lord was not flustered at all.

"This treasure can protect you, but it cannot stop me!"

He let out a sharp screech, instantly bypassing Xu Muxian, and shot towards the very center of the Swallow Islands at an extremely fast speed.

Seeing this, Xu Muxian's eyes nearly split with rage!

Had he still failed?

Even though he had prepared to sacrifice himself to buy time, he couldn't hold the other back for even a single step?

Was he truly going to let his master's wife fail in her breakthrough and perish before his very eyes?

In his field of vision, everything seemed to slow down.

Split Heaven Stream Lord tore through the sky. From a great distance, he reached out with a claw towards the location of Ten Mile Pinghu.

Just then.

"Fellow Daoist, please stop!"

A grand voice, imbued with Buddhist resonance, rang out, enveloping Split Heaven Stream Lord.

One after another, tentacles extended eerily from the void, tightly binding the Split Heaven Divine Claw, preventing it from advancing even an inch!

"Who is it?"

Split Heaven Stream Lord was both shocked and enraged.

His signature technique had failed twice in a row.

Failing against the Perch Phoenix Nest was understandable, as it was Old Monster Fengzhang's defensive true artifact.

But this time, someone had used a proper spell to forcefully receive it.

He looked up. A gaunt but kind-faced bald monk was slowly descending from the air.

Around his neck hung a skull rosary. His hands formed a Buddhist mudra.

Compassion and solemnity, malice and evil intent—two completely different auras were fused together in this old man, giving him an eerie feeling.

With just one glance, Split Heaven Stream Lord recognized the newcomer.

"The demon monk, Kongchan!"

Kongchan smiled gently. "This humble monk offers his greetings."

Split Heaven Stream Lord took a deep breath. "Why are you blocking me? This is merely a human junior forming a Nascent Soul. You are a Great Demon Emperor of the demon race. Do you have such leisure to meddle in others' affairs? Or have you taken a liking to her?"

Kongchan quickly waved his hand, not even bothering to maintain his mudra.

"Fellow Daoist, what nonsense are you speaking? The one forming the Nascent Soul here is the Dao companion of my master."

Master?

Split Heaven Stream Lord was stunned!

A Great Demon Emperor, the demon monk Kongchan who had shaken the Northern Sea for centuries—when had he ever had a master? What kind of existence could make him willingly acknowledge a master?

One thought arose irresistibly.

An Ascendant great power!

And he said that Moon Inviting Fairy, who was forming the Nascent Soul here, was his master's Dao companion?

Combined with what White Alchemy Master Xu Muxian had said earlier...

Split Heaven Stream Lord asked with difficulty, "May I ask your master's honored name?"

Kongchan pressed his palms together, his face filled with reverence. "Let it be known to you, Fellow Daoist. My master's name is Luo Chen."

No additional Dao title or honored name was attached. Just the simple two words "Luo Chen" caused Split Heaven Stream Lord's expression to change drastically.

*Whoosh!*

Without a second thought, Split Heaven Stream Lord transformed into a streak of cyan light and fled into the distance.

Seeing this, Kongchan smiled helplessly.

"I already said, 'Fellow Daoist, please stop.' The word 'stop' means you can neither advance nor retreat. Just stay put obediently!"

He pointed a finger. Black mist rose all around, with tentacles appearing and disappearing within it.

Furthermore, the two true artifacts, Dawn Bell and Dusk Drum, stood erect within the mist.

Under the beating of the tentacles, they emitted vast Buddhist sounds that shook the mind and spirit.

From the very first move, he went all out, showing how hard Kongchan was trying.

Split Heaven Stream Lord's heart and gall trembled violently, but his mouth remained stubborn.

"Old bald donkey, do you really think I'm afraid of you?"

A shocking battle unfolded in the sky above the Swallow Islands.

Yet, for some reason, the battle that should have ravaged this entire area was constrained by some inexplicable force. The aftershocks could not spread, and the effects of the fight were limited to a radius of a few li.

Such a strange situation naturally aroused wonder.

As a Sage, Xu Muxian felt the terror even more deeply.

An Ascendant existence had intervened!

He swallowed his saliva, looking towards the place where the demon monk Kongchan had appeared. The words the other had spoken earlier echoed in his mind.

"Master's Dao companion!"

"Could it be that Master has returned?"

"But he called him Luo Chen, not Luo Hai... an alias?"

While he hesitated, a divine sense transmission arrived.

"Come here."

Xu Muxian's mind was in chaos. He avoided the battlefield where the demon and the devil were fighting and followed the guidance of the divine sense, flying to a cliff overlooking the sea.

It was Sea-Watching Cliff!

A figure stood with his hands behind his back. His aura was somewhat different, but his face was still familiar. With just one glance, Xu Muxian fell to his knees with a thud.

"Disciple Xu Muxian pays respects to Master!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly, raising his hand slightly to help Xu Muxian up.

He looked him up and down, then could not help but reveal an expression of admiration.

"The Sage realm. Not bad, not bad!"

Indeed, not bad!

In his life, Luo Chen had taken few disciples.

His true disciple was actually only Qu Lingjun.

But his eldest disciple, due to his four-spirit-root aptitude, was still trapped in the Adept realm, and it was unknown when he would reach the Sage stage.

Yet the two nominal disciples he had casually instructed—the demon cultivator Huamei from Green Parasol Mountain and Xu Muxian from the Northern Sea—had both successively achieved the Sage realm.

Their different karmic encounters were truly something to marvel at.

Xu Muxian's eyes grew hot. Receiving praise from Luo Chen filled him with indescribable excitement.

His voice was stammering, as green as a child rather than a Sage. His face was full of guilt. "Master flatters me. I still have many shortcomings. If Master had not appeared today, I fear I would have been powerless to protect Master's Wife during her tribulation."

Luo Chen chuckled lightly. "This is not your fault. That Split Heaven fellow has always been formidable. Back when he was at the early Sage third layer, three Sage cultivators from the Cang Hai Alliance joined forces and still couldn't keep him down, let alone now that he is at the Sage sixth layer. For you to block one of his moves at your current realm is already quite good."

Xu Muxian hummed in acknowledgment. As a wandering cultivator who had ventured alone, self-awareness was the most important trait.

Otherwise, he wouldn't even know where he had died.

He looked at Luo Chen expectantly. "Master, your real name is Luo Chen. Could you be that legendary Eastern Wilderness Pill Sage who defied and slew a great power?"

"As you suspect." Luo Chen nodded, then asked with some confusion, "The Northern Sea is chaotic, and news is blocked, so that's understandable. But didn't Haixin tell you about my true situation?"

"Master's Wife did not say," Xu Muxian answered honestly.

Luo Chen let out an "oh," understanding Cheng Haixin's intention.

As early as back then, Luo Chen had already told Cheng Haixin the general situation about himself.

The reason she hadn't told Xu Muxian was probably to avoid causing unnecessary trouble!

Xu Muxian was still immersed in the shocking truth that his master was Luo Chen. Especially the method by which Luo Chen had effortlessly restricted the battle between two powerful experts without revealing his own strength—it was even more shocking.

This represented that Luo Chen's realm had completely surpassed theirs.

His master was no longer the Adept cultivator of yesteryear, but an Ascendant great power!

While he was still in shock.

Luo Chen spoke softly. "It's beginning!"

The final step had begun!

Above the Swallow Islands, dark clouds gathered. Lightning flashed.

The power of the heavenly tribulation had converged to its peak.

And a tall, slender figure had somehow appeared in the void, ready to face the might of heaven!

(End of this chapter)
