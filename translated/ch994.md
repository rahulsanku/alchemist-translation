# Chapter 994: Buddhist Forbidden Mantra, Lord of Truth Crossing Hall

Truth Crossing Hall.

Luo Chen looked up at the lofty plaque hanging above the hall, a complex expression on his face.

He had come to Green Parasol Mountain three times in total, and each time his stopping point had been here. There was a certain fate in that, as if guided by an unseen hand.

Ahead, Nether Spring was leading the way. After passing through the main hall, they wound past numerous palace buildings. The direction they were heading was also quite familiar to Luo Chen.

It was precisely the same cave dwelling he had stayed in during his last visit.

Sure enough, not long after, he once again arrived at that familiar cave dwelling.

"Stay here for now and await the Dowager's instructions." Nether Spring stared at Luo Chen with a complicated expression, her eyes seeming to want to see through him completely.

Luo Chen knew that leaving Green Parasol Mountain would not be so simple. Therefore, even though his heart was set on returning home, he did not object.

He only made one reluctant request just as Nether Spring was about to leave.

"Intelligence on the Mountains and Seas Realm?" Nether Spring gave a light snort. "Wait!"

Luo Chen was helpless. He didn't know if she would actually deliver it to him.

He wasn't insisting, merely hoping not to go out completely blind.

After Nether Spring left, Luo Chen turned and entered the spiritually abundant cave dwelling.

Once inside, the familiar furnishings and decorations came into view, almost unchanged from when he had left last time.

This cave dwelling, comparable to that of Vice Lord Nether Spring and second only to the legendary residence of the main Lord of Truth Crossing Hall, seemed to have been sitting empty all along.

"Quite convenient!"

Luo Chen chuckled softly and settled down.

The battle at the Pure Crystal Pure Land had seen him display demonic might and achieve great results, but it was by no means as easy as it appeared on the surface.

The depletion of his dharma power and source power was no lie.

Having this high-grade spirit vein as a place of recuperation was like finding a pillow just when he was feeling sleepy.

For the next several days, Luo Chen did not leave his dwelling, focusing entirely on recovering his dharma power within the cave.

During this time, Nether Spring delivered a jade slip with a rather poor attitude.

It contained a general overview of the current situation in the Mountains and Seas Realm, focusing mainly on the Eastern Wilderness and Central Continent, but also recording major events in other regions.

From this, one could see the intelligence-gathering capability of Green Parasol Mountain!

After reading through it all, Luo Chen had a rough understanding of the current state of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Once his condition had recovered to about seventy or eighty percent, Luo Chen began another task.

"The Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Mantra."

Gazing at the jade slip in his hand, Luo Chen murmured to himself.

This was one of the rewards given to him by Master Xuan Du back then.

According to Xuan Du's description, this mantra had the wondrous effect of forcibly condensing the primordial spirit. It was a rare method from the Hanging Temple that offered a glimpse into the threshold of the Ascendant stage.

In particular, due to the danger of forcibly condensing the primordial spirit, even though its grade was not high, it had once been classified as a Buddhist forbidden mantra, not to be cultivated by those without a powerful soul.

Given the depth of Luo Chen's soul foundation, he could naturally ignore this threshold.

"If the condensed primordial spirit is solid and reliable, perhaps I won't need to risk going to the Tianyuan Dao Sect to retrieve my destiny soul. I could directly charge into the Ascendant stage in the future."

The Tianyuan Dao Sect was a dragon's pool and tiger's den.

In the past few thousand years, only Rosycloud had dared to storm their mountain gate.

If it wasn't necessary, Luo Chen didn't want to take that risk.

The *Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Mantra* was one of his contingency plans.

Late at night, after careful study, Luo Chen began attempting to enter this technique.

*All sentient beings have afflictions, and afflictions are all suffering.*

*Afflictions neither arise nor cease, are neither pure nor impure, neither increase nor decrease.*

*That which has form is born from the formless. Nothingness can give birth to existence, and existence returns to nothingness.*

*The spirit is born from the mind. If the mind is clear, the spirit is naturally settled. Afflictions do not attach to the body, do not hinder the mind. When the formless transforms into the formed, this is the mystery of the illusory soul condensing into a solid primordial spirit.*

"Namo, ratna-trayāya... bodhisattvāya, mahāsattvāya..."

The initial practice was halting and difficult.

Luo Chen's understanding of Buddhist terminology was not deep, so coordinating the mantra to mobilize his soul power felt extremely arduous.

But as he persisted in chanting, the mantra became smoother and smoother, and his state grew better and better.

Even his restless heart gradually began to settle.

Outside the cave dwelling, it was the dead of night.

A crescent moon scattered a vast swath of light across the mountains. The cave dwelling where Luo Chen was located appeared especially hazy, as if it were a dreamscape.

A pair of cold eyes, separated by a hundred li, stared at Truth Crossing Hall, devoid of any emotion.

...

Half a month later.

Luo Chen looked at the brand-new entry on his attribute panel, a satisfied expression in his eyes.

The *Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Mantra* had been initiated!

Although it was not yet enough to condense a primordial spirit, it was sufficient for now.

From now on, as long as he persisted in cultivation, when the opportunity came, he would always be able to take that step.

Moreover, during these days of practice, he had discovered that this mantra also had the effect of tempering the soul.

On Soul Severance Cliff, he had absorbed too many soul stones, causing his soul to surge explosively.

Although those soul stones were extremely pure, without any impurities, the sudden explosive growth of his soul foundation inevitably carried some restless, fiery energy.

This mantra happened to be able to clear the mind, settle the spirit, and remove that restless energy.

For this alone, the time Luo Chen had deliberately taken to cultivate it was not wasted.

"Perhaps in the future, I could try fusing this mantra into the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*?" Luo Chen suddenly had an idea.

As his realm grew higher and higher, the *Fine Dust Primordial Art* he had created back then had completely failed to keep pace with his progress.

Even though it had incorporated many techniques for cutting away distracting thoughts, such as *Settling Wind and Waves*, its grade was ultimately too low.

On the other hand, the soul-tempering methods within the *Soul-Splitting Art* and the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* were too high in grade and incompatible with the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*.

This Heart-Cleansing Mantra, relatively speaking, was of a lower grade and would not be too difficult to integrate.

At least, based on Luo Chen's insight, long-term cultivation of this mantra would greatly reduce the occurrence of heart tribulations and demonic obstacles.

For Luo Chen, who had always suffered from the harm of heart tribulations and demonic obstacles, this was undoubtedly a significant improvement.

After some careful thought, Luo Chen let out a silent laugh.

"No rush. I should at least wait until I've cultivated this technique to the Master or even Great Perfection level before experimenting. For now..."

Luo Chen exhaled a long breath of turbid qi, his gaze leveling as if penetrating the thick walls of the cave dwelling to look outside, at the two unhurried figures approaching.

Now that his condition had recovered to its peak, it was time to leave Green Parasol Mountain.

But the question was—would Rosycloud let him go?

The answer lay in the two people about to arrive.

...

On the long corridor, two figures advanced unhurriedly.

Nether Spring walked in front, her face puffed up with obvious dissatisfaction.

Behind her was a beautiful woman with a graceful figure and a unique temperament. She held a tray in both hands, her head lowered, her expression hidden.

Nether Spring suddenly stopped and turned to look at the beautiful woman.

"Xiaoyue, aren't you disappointed? The Dowager clearly promised it to you, but in the end, it fell into the hands of that stinking man."

The beautiful woman kept her head lowered and replied calmly, "Everything is decided by the Dowager. This maidservant dares not question it."

"Hmph!"

Nether Spring gave a light snort. "That's what you say, but you handed over the spirit treasure Myriad Beast Diagram and ended up with nothing in return. I don't believe you're not resentful inside."

The beautiful woman still kept her head lowered, neither admitting nor denying it.

It seemed that although she didn't say it aloud, she was indeed quite unwilling that Rosycloud had given the promised item to someone else.

Nether Spring curled her lips, too lazy to provoke this woman further.

If anyone had the right to complain, it was her.

After serving as Vice Lord of Truth Crossing Hall for so many years, in the end, an outsider had swooped in and taken the prize.

She resumed walking and soon arrived outside a cave dwelling.

However, before she could send a voice transmission inside, the formation concealing the cave dwelling naturally dispersed, as if it had already known of their arrival in advance.

This sight made Nether Spring raise an eyebrow.

"How does this guy keep getting stronger every time? Not only did he instantly defeat Thunder Prison, but his soul is also becoming sharper. He can even sense my presence in advance."

Muttering to herself, Nether Spring didn't stop and walked straight into the cave dwelling.

The white-robed man was already waiting.

The moment they met, Nether Spring waved her hand.

"Take it!"

The woman behind her stepped forward and presented the tray.

Luo Chen did not immediately look at the items on it. Instead, he first glanced at the woman with lowered eyes.

Moon Wanderer!

Only then did he turn his attention to the tray.

There were quite a few things on it. The most dazzling item was clearly a feather fan as red as glass. Then there was a white feathered robe, a large seal, a bamboo slip, and a scroll.

Luo Chen's gaze bypassed the feather fan and specifically looked at the large seal, which contained a complex formation aura, before raising his head to look at Nether Spring.

"What are these?"

Nether Spring gave a light snort. "From now on, you are the Lord of Truth Crossing Hall of our Green Parasol Mountain. You are to receive the Truth Crossing Seal, the Hundred Feathers Robe, and take charge of the Demon Cultivator Registry."

Lord of Truth Crossing Hall!

For a moment, Luo Chen almost thought he had misheard.

But after his gaze swept over the large seal, the feathered robe, and the bamboo slip on the tray, he knew she wasn't joking.

He steadied his mind and tested the waters. "Is this the Dowager's intention?"

"What, you don't want to accept it?" Nether Spring countered.

Luo Chen smiled bitterly. "But I am a human cultivator. How can I hold a position in the sacred land of the feathered race?"

Nether Spring curled her lips. "Now you're making a clear distinction between humans and demons? Where was that awareness when you were cultivating the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*?"

Before Luo Chen could explain, she clasped her sleeves and began pacing slowly in the hall.

"Let me tell you, the benefits of becoming the Lord of Truth Crossing Hall are enormous."

"The three halls of Green Parasol Mountain have a reputation that spreads far and wide among the demon race. Any lord of any hall who goes to demon settlements anywhere in the Mountains and Seas Realm will receive courtesy. Under normal circumstances, no one will make trouble for you."

"By taking charge of the Demon Cultivator Registry, you can now mobilize all the demon clan experts in the Eastern Wilderness's million mountains who have submitted to Green Parasol Mountain."

"Finally, how is the spiritual qi of this cave dwelling?"

Luo Chen was still digesting the implications of the first two benefits. Hearing this question, he instinctively answered, "Excellent! It is the finest fourth-grade cave dwelling I have ever seen in my life!"

Nether Spring stopped and pointed at the large seal.

"Excellent, but not good enough. If you become the Lord of Truth Crossing Hall, you can use this seal to open a fifth-grade cave dwelling. In the future, if you want to advance to the Ascendant stage, you can directly enjoy a cave dwelling built on a fifth-grade spirit vein."

*Crack!*

The sound of spirit wood being crushed rang out.

Luo Chen followed the sound and saw Moon Wanderer lowering her head again.

And in that moment when she lowered her head, her gaze was fixed on the large seal.

Nether Spring sneered from the side. "Lest you be unaware, the position of main Lord of Truth Crossing Hall has been vacant for a thousand years. Countless demon clan experts have coveted it but failed to obtain it. Originally, the one with the most hope was Moon Wanderer here before you. But now, the Dowager has granted it to you."

"Why..."

The word "me" did not come out.

Luo Chen closed his mouth. The answer was actually very obvious.

He had become the main Lord of Truth Crossing Hall, likely due to Azure Frost's influence.

Now, he and Azure Frost shared life and death, one prospering and one suffering together.

Since Azure Frost was the Lord of Daytime Void Hall, it was not strange that Rosycloud, extending her favor to him, would make him the Lord of Truth Crossing Hall.

As for whether there was any consideration of his strength and realm, Luo Chen could not know.

Perhaps there was, but not much.

After all, no matter how formidable Luo Chen appeared, compared to a great power like Rosycloud, who could destroy sacred lands at will, he was but a drop in the ocean.

Taking a deep breath, Luo Chen turned his gaze to the remaining item—the glass fire fan emanating a powerful aura.

"And what is this?"

Nether Spring shrugged. "The Dowager was afraid that your strength was insufficient and that you would fall outside, tarnishing the reputation of Green Parasol Mountain. Therefore, she specially bestowed this fire-attribute pseudo-spirit treasure. Its name is the Blood Jade Three-Flame Fan. It is made primarily from fifth-grade blood jade coral, forcibly infused with a portion of fire origin power, and can accommodate three different types of flames. Each type of flame fanned out can amplify its power. If all three flames are unleashed together, its power is not inferior to a normal Ascendant's strike! Moreover, the stronger the flames it accommodates, the stronger its power will be!"

Hearing this description, even Luo Chen's calm and placid state of mind could not help but stir with a few ripples.

If this was true, then if he infused his Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, which could already threaten Ascendant-level experts, and the Nirvana Sacred Flame, which had a fifth-grade origin, into it, to what level would its power be amplified?

Rosycloud had bestowed this treasure upon him, nominally for self-defense, but in reality...

"She's also afraid I'll die outside and drag Azure Frost down with me."

Luo Chen murmured in his heart.

Seeing his contemplative expression, Nether Spring showed displeasure.

"Are you still hesitating? Do you think the position of Lord of Truth Crossing Hall is beneath you?"

Luo Chen smiled helplessly. How could he call it beneath him? How could he refuse?

At this point, he had to accept it whether he wanted to or not.

If he didn't accept the position of Lord of Truth Crossing Hall, Rosycloud probably wouldn't let him leave Green Parasol Mountain.

He turned his gaze to the final scroll.

"And what is this?"

Nether Spring waved her hand, and the scroll slowly unfurled, landing before Luo Chen.

"See for yourself!"

Luo Chen looked up. The four large characters at the top of the scroll immediately caught his eye.

**[Ceasefire Treaty]**

Luo Chen's gaze suddenly froze.

...

Nether Spring and Moon Wanderer had already left.

In the quiet cave dwelling, only Luo Chen remained, sitting alone on a meditation cushion. Before him lay the tray with one corner crushed.

The five treasures on it emitted a brilliant radiance.

In silence, Luo Chen waved his hand.

The Hundred Feathers Robe, woven from the feathers of a hundred types of spirit birds, draped over his body. The Truth Crossing Seal and the Demon Cultivator Registry also fell into his hands.

This signified that from now on, Luo Chen was one of the three hall lords of Green Parasol Mountain!

After a brief examination, drawing on his knowledge of artifact refinement, Luo Chen quickly grasped the general information about the two treasures.

The Hundred Feathers Robe had no defensive capabilities. Aside from simple cleaning and dust-repelling functions, it only had the special effect of increasing movement speed.

As for the Truth Crossing Seal, it contained numerous prohibitions and formations within. It should be the central hub for controlling all the formations of Truth Crossing Hall, and it also had little combat utility.

Although both were of true artifact grade, their significance as tokens of authority was greater.

After a brief inspection, Luo Chen stopped paying attention to them and focused on the Demon Cultivator Registry.

He infused his dharma power into it. The jade slip trembled, and a light curtain immediately appeared before him.

Luo Chen looked up. Over twenty dazzling names came into view.

The first one was Nether Spring, which was expected.

As for the rest...

**[Ox Ghost, Bi Zheng, Kuang Kuang]**

Luo Chen smiled.
