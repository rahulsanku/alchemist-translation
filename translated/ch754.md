# Chapter 754: A Hundred Years in the Cave, One Year in the World, Luo Chen Takes a Silent Loss

"Still not enough?"

Luo Chen very rarely revealed a look of impatience.

Fu Qinglan was full of apology. "I am sorry. This little sister's aptitude is dull. Even two illusion cores could not let me go one step further."

Dull aptitude?

Luo Chen shook his head. How could someone who formed her Nascent Soul at three hundred years of age be called dull in aptitude?

In the end, the amount of spiritual impulse contained within the illusion cores was too small, insufficient to let a cultivator persist until comprehending the true intent of laws.

What he was truly dissatisfied with was "time"!

With a beckoning hand, under Fu Qinglan's puzzled gaze, Luo Chen said, "Let me examine your illusion chess piece."

Fu Qinglan did not understand, but she did not resist either, handing over her illusion chess piece.

Luo Chen lowered his head and looked. On the white illusion chess piece, black light had stained it, occupying about two or three percent.

Mirage Dragon power would erode the black chess pieces of those who entered the grotto-heaven, and the deeper the erosion, the shorter their remaining time inside.

And now!

"Two illusion cores and one year of time only eroded this little. In other words, if she cannot comprehend law true intent, will we have to stay inside this grotto-heaven for decades, or even a century?"

This was the main reason Luo Chen truly could not accept it!

It was impossible for him to spend nearly a hundred years of his time on another person's cultivation trial.

Moreover, this was greatly different from what Fu Chaosheng had said before, that the Tianyuan Grand Competition would at most consume one or two years.

Where had the problem occurred?

While doubts filled him, he saw the hesitant expression on the woman's face across from him.

Luo Chen probed, "After absorbing two illusion cores in succession, did you gain anything?"

Fu Qinglan hesitated for a moment, then slowly said, "I did gain some things, but I am not entirely sure whether they are true or false. If Dao Brother wishes to listen, this little sister can speak of them."

...

It was still the plain surrounded by three mountains.

It was still the same formation and bait as before.

Only this time, Luo Chen did not let Fu Qinglan come with him, instead having her rest and recover in the cave abode while he came alone to wait by the stump for the rabbit.

Standing on a mountain peak, Luo Chen's gaze did not linger on the Heavenly Secret Pill bait in the plain. In any case, that Heavenly Secret Pill was protected by the formation and his life-bound magic treasure, so no accident would occur.

Right now, he could not help constantly gazing around, as if searching for something.

But no matter how he looked, he could not detect any clue that differed from the outside world.

The words Fu Qinglan had spoken a few days ago were still battering his mind even now.

"Illusion beasts should be products of the Mirage Dragon's lingering consciousness. They instinctively attempt to reignite from dead ashes and recreate the Mirage Dragon's spirit within its body. Opening Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven once every five hundred years is both an opportunity for us and a way to clear out high-level illusion beasts, preventing the Mirage Dragon's revival. When we cultivate with illusion cores, we must remove the influence of that spiritless Mirage Dragon consciousness and use the spiritual impulse within..."

"From the illusion-beast consciousness in the illusion core, I discovered one great difference between this grotto-heaven and the outside world. That is the difference in the flow of time. The precise ratio is roughly..."

One hundred to one!

Yes, it was this number that had left Luo Chen unable to sit or stand at ease for several days in a row.

He had never imagined that he would enter such a secret realm that existed only in legend!

In those countless immortal myths and tales, naturally there was no lack of descriptions of fated people entering secret realms.

These secret realms also differed greatly. Some held countless opportunities, some were abnormally dangerous, and still others had all sorts of restrictions and rules.

Luo Chen had not encountered many secret realms in his life, and had entered even fewer.

But he had still gained no small understanding of them.

For example, the secret realm left behind after an Ascendant great power died was not suitable for most Sages to enter, because the law true intent left by the Ascendant great power would influence the later entrant's great path. Only those whose great paths were similar could enter.

This kind belonged to restrictions upon the Great Dao.

As for the Demon-Fall Land, at first glance it was fraught with danger, but in reality it did not have overly strict entry requirements.

Let alone Sages, even Adept cultivators could enter it.

Even some Initiate true cultivators could completely try their luck if they were bold enough.

If one truly had to speak of restrictions, it was probably that Ascendant great powers who had already comprehended laws could not enter. Otherwise, they might very likely die violently inside, like the Blood Sea Ancestor.

This Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven secret realm was unexpectedly one of those legendary secret realms with time restrictions, where "one day in the cave is a thousand years in the world."

Only, its flow of time was reversed.

A hundred years inside the cave, yet only one year in the world!

The ratio between the flow of time in Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven and the Mountains and Seas Realm reached one hundred to one.

Moreover, if cultivators were inside the grotto-heaven, their lifespan flowed normally, and even after leaving, it would remain consistent.

There would be no situation where someone entered at five hundred years old, spent one year outside and a hundred years inside, then came out with a lifespan of only five hundred and one. Instead, when the great limit came, they would indeed be one hundred years shorter than cultivators of the same realm who had not experienced the grotto-heaven.

Why it was this way, Luo Chen did not know. It might have something to do with the laws and Great Dao mastered by a desolate beast like the Mirage Dragon.

What Luo Chen finally knew was why the Tianyuan Dao Sect reserved this chance only for newly advanced Sages!

A Sage cultivator had a total lifespan of no more than a thousand years.

After deducting the several hundred years of cultivation before the Sage realm, only a few hundred years remained in total. Who would be willing to consume a hundred years for no reason?

Only newly advanced Sages, especially cultivators with excellent aptitude who broke through to the Sage realm at a very young age, possessed ample lifespan and could withstand Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven's consumption.

This was the greatest condition and restriction of the five-hundred-year grand competition!

As for the other factors Luo Chen had speculated about, such as the state of having just entered the Sage realm and instinctive closeness to heaven and earth, those were merely a small portion of the considerations.

"No wonder, in legendary tales, when great sects encounter unknown secret realms, they often let disciples and sect members probe them first. If one carelessly encountered this sort of secret realm with an uncertain time flow, then a high-level cultivator whose lifespan was already low might instantly die of old age after coming out. That would be too terrifying."

"Only, in this case, Fu Chaosheng has truly gone too far!"

In Luo Chen's heart, anger was rising because Fu Chaosheng had not informed him of the grotto-heaven's inside story in advance.

Or rather, when Fu Qinglan had spoken of discovering the differing time flow, he had already lost composure in a rare display. The terrifying aura that erupted suddenly had left the other party silent as if chilled. Her not coming with him to hunt illusion beasts this time was also partly because lingering fear remained in her heart.

One hundred years!

How many one-hundred-year spans did a life even have?

In Luo Chen's entire life, he had cultivated for only a little over two hundred years even counting everything. If he had known that he would expend one hundred years inside Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, no matter how good the conditions Fu Chaosheng offered were, he could not possibly have agreed to this cooperation.

"I wonder whether the other Dao protectors know this information, or whether they knowingly choose to go toward the mountain despite knowing there are tigers there."

"But as far as I am concerned, looking at it now, all the benefits Fu Chaosheng gave me before were actually secretly marking the price long ago. That price was this one hundred years of lifespan!"

"Through my own carelessness, I have swallowed a colossal silent loss!"

Luo Chen drew a deep breath and found a rock to sit down on.

First, he circulated the Fine Dust Primordial Art to calm himself.

Afterward, he began thinking carefully.

The first matter was the possible loss of a hundred years of lifespan. Anger was anger, but Luo Chen could still bear this price.

His lifespan already far exceeded that of his peers. When he advanced to the Sage realm, it had reached one thousand five hundred years in one breath!

Although it was no longer like the previous realms, where the lifespan limit increased along with each minor-realm rise, and was fixed firmly at one thousand five hundred years.

Still, one thousand five hundred years far exceeded the ordinary Sage's thousand-year lifespan limit.

A mere hundred years or so, he could bear.

Moreover, he would not necessarily lose that much. As long as he captured enough illusion cores and helped Fu Qinglan comprehend laws earlier, he could leave earlier...

As he thought and thought, Luo Chen's calm mood once again gave rise to violence!

Fu Chaosheng had failed to inform him first, so why should he still put all his strength into Fu Qinglan's matter?

This anger was not only because he was furious at Fu Chaosheng's concealment, but also because he was angry at his own "arrogance."

Ever since he set foot on the path of cultivation.

Toward sect cultivators, he had originally felt awe: awe toward the forces behind them, and awe toward their powerful strength.

But as his own strength rose, he no longer feared those things.

Instead, he had begun to look down on these sect cultivators raised in greenhouses.

Especially the poor temperaments of these younger-generation disciples of the Tianyuan Dao Sect, which had fed his contempt.

But now, reality had punched him heavily.

The younger generation might not have deep schemes, but the older-generation sect cultivators were all shrewd old foxes. How could he possibly take great advantage from their hands?

"In the future, when dealing with these old fellows again, I must think thrice before acting and weigh the pros and cons clearly."

After calming down, Luo Chen analyzed what he had paid against what Fu Chaosheng had paid, and discovered that the other party had indeed calculated things to the utmost.

If he worked hard enough, helped Fu Qinglan comprehend laws earlier, and left Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, he would at most lose a few decades of lifespan.

As for the large amount of soul essence, Withered-Source Fire Marrow, pills, Heaven-Earth Peak external affairs guest elder status, and mobilizing the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance's manpower and resources to collect massive resources for him... when all these benefits were added together in broad terms, they barely reached the level of hiring a Sage to run about for several decades.

Even if Luo Chen himself did those things, collecting those resources would require roughly this much time, and would even cost additional energy and encounter additional troubles.

"Forget it. After I leave, I will have a talk with that old thing!"

Luo Chen exhaled a breath of turbid air and continued his earlier thinking.

Since he was here, he had to make peace with it!

It was impossible for him to allow several decades of good years to flow away for no reason.

This world had scattered spiritual qi and was not especially suited for qi-refining cultivation.

And because he had to guard Fu Qinglan, he could not release his Nascent Soul true body as she did, merge his spirit with heaven and earth without the slightest guard, and attempt to comprehend laws.

Thinking that he had to guard that foolish yet clear-hearted woman, Luo Chen again felt a kind of disgust, as if he had eaten a fly but had no choice except to swallow it.

He abruptly shook his head, forcefully suppressing the disgust in his heart.

"It will be difficult to raise my realm by a large margin, but minor adjustments should be no problem. The second level of Sage is within reach."

"For the remaining time, I must spend more thought on myself."

"The proficiency of the two fourth-tier spells, Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain and Flame Dragon Descends, can be raised."

Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven was incomparably vast, and cultivating here was extremely convenient.

Not only spells!

There was also the transformation of the Withering-Bloom True Flame. After obtaining the Heavenly Secret Pill, Luo Chen could completely take the final step at any time, so long as he wished.

In addition, there was the refinement of that White Tiger Puppet, the restriction refinement of the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion...

At the end, Luo Chen even set his thoughts on the Soul-Splitting Art!

His main soul had already reached the current realm's limit and was completely able to divide another divided soul.

Back then, Han Zhan had begun cultivating the Soul-Splitting Art in the Initiate stage, strengthened his divine soul foundation in the Adept stage, and after forming his Nascent Soul, possessed nine great divided souls in the short span of one hundred years.

With wondrous pills like the Great Soul Pill assisting him, it was not necessarily impossible for Luo Chen to reach that step as well!

"One step at a time!"

Helplessly, Luo Chen set down his plan for what came next.

Life was like this. One would always suffer some losses when not paying attention, but life still had to be lived.

Not only did it have to be lived, it had to be lived better and better, making those who had made him swallow losses look at him anew, regret it endlessly, and even pay the price!

...

"Here. This is the third illusion core."

A fourth-tier illusion core was tossed before Fu Qinglan, and she hurriedly caught it.

She wanted to say something, but the other party had the appearance of keeping strangers at a distance, and raised a protective formation.

Blocked outside the formation, Fu Qinglan opened her mouth, her face full of a bitter smile.

Sure enough, after she told him the greatest secret, that the flow of time was different, she should have expected this result.

But what could she do?

The Fu family had declined for a long time and simply could not cultivate a loyal servant of a suitable realm. As for hiring a Sage from outside, leaving aside whether the conditions fit, in strength alone they would be completely inferior to Luo Chen.

Her grandfather had concealed things from the other party and tricked him into coming in because there had been no other way.

Now, the fact that the other party was still willing to hunt illusion cores for her was already the act of an incomparably upright gentleman.

It could only be said that Grandfather truly had not misjudged him.

Only, the way she and the other party would get along from now on had turned distant again after they had finally become somewhat familiar before.

"Forget it. I can only comprehend the true intent of laws as soon as possible, so that he may leave this cage sooner."

Fu Qinglan smiled bitterly and tightened her grip on the illusion core, then entered the simple cave abode.

*(End of Chapter)*
