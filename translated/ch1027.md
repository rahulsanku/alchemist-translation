# Chapter 1027: Refining the Ghost God, Nurturing the Blood Sword, Azure Firmament Enters the Dao

"Gone?"

Zhang Bairen's gaze fell upon the churning waves of the vast, empty sea, and he cried out in shock.

As one of the four hall masters of the Tinnan Dao Palace, he knew perfectly well what was sealed within the Burial Spirit Valley.

He had considered the ghost god reviving, and he had considered the seal being broken. What he had never imagined was that it would simply vanish without a trace, leaving behind barely any residual aura.

The other four were in the same state.

This time, the Tinnan Dao Palace had not mobilized its full strength to resolve the hidden danger of Burial Spirit Valley, but most of its top-tier combat power had come.

The temple master, Zhan Bingtao, plus the four hall masters.

Hui Yuanhong of the Starry Hall snorted coldly. "In such a short time, I don't believe it could have fled far!"

As he spoke, his vast divine sense spread out, expanding continuously across thousands of li.

However, as he probed, his expression gradually shifted to one of doubt and uncertainty.

"Hall Master Hui, don't waste your effort. They vanished without a trace long ago."

Zhan Bingtao stepped forward, standing on the broken high cliff, overlooking the chaotic ruins all around.

His eyes narrowed slightly, and a grave expression slowly crept onto his face.

"They?"

Among the five, Long Ting, the hall master of Longevity Hall, was most skilled in formations. His expression was also one of shock as he blurted out.

"The Kui Ox Ghost God did not break free on its own. Someone damaged the seal here, allowing it to escape the suppression left behind by Sword Lord!"

At these words, everyone's expression changed.

Zhan Bingtao nodded slightly. "Junior Brother Long Ting is correct. The Kui Ox Ghost God did receive external assistance."

"Then who could it be?" A melodious, captivating voice came from the only woman among the five, her eyes also full of confusion.

Zhan Bingtao waved his hand. "This person acted too quickly. When he didn't move, nothing happened, but the moment he did, he broke all nine seals completely. He didn't give us enough time to react. It can be inferred that someone capable of such methods is certainly no easy opponent. Chasing is out of the question, but we can investigate the traces he left behind here and see if we can deduce who did it."

Everyone agreed and flew off separately, searching for residual traces in the vast Burial Spirit Valley.

Unfortunately, the commotion when the Kui Ox broke free was too great. The waves it raised had plowed through almost the entire Burial Spirit Valley, and more than half of it was now submerged in the vast Eastern Sea.

However, Hui Yuanhong, also a great cultivator, used his secret arts to find a trace of yin-soft magical power, which seemed out of place here.

But that yin-soft magical power was clearly identifiable as belonging to the category of yin souls and ghostly beings.

After they gathered, he reported this discovery.

Zhang Bairen couldn't help but speculate, "Could it be that ghost cultivators from elsewhere came here to rescue the Kui Ox Ghost God?"

Long Ting was puzzled. "That shouldn't be the case. If they rescued it, why would they just leave directly? With the power of that Kui Ox Ghost God, it's enough to threaten the entire Tinnan Continent. If our ancestor doesn't act, we simply have no way to deal with a fifth-stage ghost god that has broken free."

Zhan Bingtao looked at the green-robed woman, who seemed somewhat dazed.

"Junior Sister Qinglan, have you found anything?"

Fu Qinglan subconsciously shook her head. "Like Senior Brother Zhang and Senior Brother Long, I haven't found anything."

Zhan Bingtao hummed in acknowledgment and began discussing the trace of yin-soft magical power that Hui Yuanhong had discovered with the others.

It was indeed of the ghost cultivator category.

But the essence of this magical power wasn't particularly strong. Logically, its owner shouldn't be very powerful either, lacking the strength to destroy all nine seals simultaneously.

Moreover, it was very strange that the Kui Ox Ghost God hadn't vented its anger on the Tinnan Continent after breaking the seal.

After a round of discussion, they were still clueless.

However, Long Ting, who was skilled in formations, had become much more relaxed after his initial shock.

"Actually, this is a good thing. The seal on this ghost god wasn't very secure, and Ancestor Liancheng couldn't spare the effort to completely annihilate it. We could only rely on periodically reinforcing the seal. But it was ultimately a fifth-stage ghost god, comparable to an Ascendant-level great power. As long as it remained, it was a sword hanging over the entire Tinnan Continent. Although its departure now is strange, at least it removes a hidden worry for us."

Zhan Bingtao nodded. "Junior Brother is right, but..."

His tone suddenly shifted, and a fierce expression appeared on his face.

"But this is the Tinnan Continent, the territory of our Dao Sect!"

"Was there ever a time when cultivators from all over the world did not look up to us in reverence and fear?"

"When did it become a place where experts from other continents could come and go as they pleased?"

"The Northern Li Continent is being invaded by sea demons, and now ghostly cultivators are sneaking into Tinnan. In the future, will any demonic heretics dare to come to our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to burn, kill, and plunder?"

Accompanying his harsh words was the release of his overwhelming aura, which directly suppressed the surrounding sea, preventing any further waves.

After saying this, Zhan Bingtao's anger seemed to dissipate somewhat.

He let out a turbid breath. "Junior Brother Long Ting, take some people and conduct another thorough search nearby. Then set up a formation to prevent any low-level ghost cultivators from reviving here."

"Yes!" Long Ting agreed without hesitation, a hint of indignation on his face.

"Junior Brother Zhang, go to the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range in the Dongyuan Continent and report the details of this matter to Ancestor Liancheng. Also, regarding the Fallen Dragon Abyss that our Zhan family is guarding, ask the ancestor for instructions on how to handle it."

Zhang Bairen nodded and immediately set off, his escape light unfolding.

"Hall Master Hui, return to the Dao Palace. You are authorized to use the personnel of the four courts of the Seven Stars Court to conduct a thorough investigation of the entire Tinnan Continent. Make sure to catch any troublemakers trying to fish in troubled waters."

This was a tough job, and Hui Yuanhong instinctively wanted to refuse.

But under Zhan Bingtao's powerful aura, he ultimately dared not meet his eyes and accepted the task.

"Senior Brother, what about me?" Fu Qinglan asked proactively.

Zhan Bingtao's fierce expression softened slightly, and he said kindly, "Junior Sister, just return to the Dao Palace with me! You have already comprehended the true meaning of the laws and are a future seed for Ascension. The Dao Palace has a fifth-stage spirit vein that can effectively aid your cultivation. How can we let these trivial matters delay your practice?"

Long Ting and Hui Yuanhong immediately cast envious and jealous glances.

The former was envious, the latter jealous.

Fu Qinglan seemed oblivious, gently nodding her head.

Before leaving the Burial Spirit Valley ruins, she subconsciously looked back at the scenery. Under the cover of her sleeve, her slender hand was holding a broken formation flag.

Although the flag was damaged, its refining technique was exquisitely subtle.

Especially some of the refining methods on it gave her a familiar feeling.

Back in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, someone had used a similar formation flag to set up a grand formation to protect her.

"Was it you who came here?"

Fu Qinglan murmured to herself, a hint of confusion and doubt in her eyes.

...

"Congratulations, Master, for subduing a fifth-stage ghost god!"

Inside the flying shuttle, Xiaobai looked at Luo Chen with eyes full of admiration.

But Luo Chen shook his head. "I've only captured it. Subduing it will require a lot more effort."

Xiaobai was curious. "Master captured it so easily, wouldn't subduing it be simpler?"

"Easily?"

Luo Chen's lips curled slightly, but he didn't explain much.

To outsiders, it seemed he had captured the Kui Ox Ghost God with almost no effort, but the methods he had used were by no means few.

Ever since he detected the Kui Ox Ghost God's intention to break the seal, he had begun his preparations.

Every time he killed a high-level ghost creature containing a sliver of the Kui Ox's weak soul, he secretly planted a restriction within it.

This included the top-grade soul stones he had used as bait, which also contained his soul imprint.

After breaking the seal, he used Bai Meiling as a decoy on the surface, while behind the scenes, Luo Chen had condensed his primordial spirit high in the heavens, expending a massive amount of magical power to drive the Primordial Unity Cauldron to its limit.

Finally, he used the Dustbound Immortal Realm and his Crimson-Golden Eyes to execute the Mirror Flower Water Moon technique.

Combined with those forbidden restrictions and soul marks, working from both inside and out, he completely confused the mind of the Kui Ox, which had just broken its seal.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron itself also possessed the marvelous effect of capturing a cultivator's golden core or nascent soul.

With all these factors combined, he succeeded in one fell swoop!

Of course, with such painstaking effort, Luo Chen's gains were also enormous.

With a fifth-stage ghost god in hand, once it was refined into the Soul-Refining Banner, the Million Soul Banner would truly live up to its name.

Such a heavy soul-type treasure was a great weapon comparable to a spirit treasure.

If used well, it could cause considerable trouble even for Ascendant-level great powers.

Xiaobai didn't know what Luo Chen was thinking. She used spirit stones to control the Frost Eagle Shuttle, its direction somewhat aimless.

"Master, where are we going now?"

Luo Chen looked up at the direction outside.

Earlier, he had used the Fire Escape Art, taking Bai Meiling far away from the Tinnan Continent. The Frost Eagle Shuttle was also extremely fast, and before they knew it, they had traveled from south to north, approaching the former heartland of the Central Continent.

"Past here is... Heaven-Earth Peak."

Luo Chen pursed his lips, a hint of nostalgia in his eyes.

"You worked hard earlier. I'll take over controlling the shuttle from now on!"

Luo Chen took over control of the shuttle and steered the flying boat towards the former heartland of the Central Continent.

He wasn't too worried about accidents.

It was said that ever since the Star Gate was taken by Rosycloud and Heaven-Earth Peak was broken in two, the place had become a land where living beings could not enter.

Even the only remaining Ascendant ancestor of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was now cultivating in the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range of the Dongyuan Continent.

He was just going to revisit an old haunt; it should be fine.

But three days later, the Frost Eagle Shuttle stopped far away, ten thousand li from that broken continent.

As far as the eye could see, there was no longer the majestic peak connecting heaven and earth. There was only a flat mountainside that resembled a landmass, and a broken heavenly pillar.

The Passing River, which once surged eastward into the sea without return, was also shrouded in countless desolate, thick mists.

But what truly made Luo Chen stop were those mists.

White, vast, dreamlike.

Luo Chen was very familiar with this mist.

It wasn't a product of Heaven-Earth Peak, but rather the clouds and mists from the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

"Could it be that the top-level Great Desolate's death-transformed grotto-heaven has been opened by someone?"

"Was it Ancestor Liancheng?"

Luo Chen hesitated for a moment, but ultimately chose to leave quietly.

Now was not the time to confront that person.

Shortly after he left, a figure emerged from the thick mist.

Clad in a black-yellow great robe, wearing a tall crown, with a face that was extremely young, yet his entire aura exuded the majesty and grandeur of a superior.

"Using the sect's secret arts, I finally opened a gap in the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven and sent the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard inside. By utilizing the different flow of time within the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, I can speed up the artifact spirit's recovery progress."

"Next..."

The majestic young man raised his head, looking at the broken heavenly pillar.

"Using the power of the Earth Origin, repair Heaven-Earth Peak!"

...

"Master, you arrived at Heaven-Earth Peak but didn't go in. Where are we going next?"

"Why do you keep asking that question?"

"Because Xiaobai hasn't been out much. Unlike Master, who has traveled across the five continents and four seas, leaving footprints all over the world. Naturally, I have to ask Master about everything."

"Haha, then let's go to a place that is absolutely safe!"

Xiaobai was extremely curious. Where was absolutely safe?

Luo Chen smiled without speaking.

The Frost Eagle Shuttle suddenly changed direction, heading all the way west.

After a long time, the shuttle stopped over a sea area filled with reefs and turbid waves.

If any Dao Sect cultivator were here, they would immediately recognize this territory.

It was the Western Continent that had split off from the Central Continent!

However, with the Yellow Spring Sea sweeping through back then, there was no longer any vast land here, only scattered broken reefs and continuous murky seawater.

The shuttle circled in place for a moment before disappearing, replaced by two figures.

Those two figures didn't stay long either. They shot straight up into the sky and disappeared into the nine heavens.

Crossing ten thousand li of layered heavens, breaking through the biting astral wind!

All the way up, magical power supported them. In just a few blinks, they saw stars twinkling, emitting faint light as if right before their eyes.

Xiaobai looked at the pitch-black, boundless cosmic void, instantly feeling a sense of insignificance.

She subconsciously grabbed Luo Chen's sleeve.

"Master, where is this?"

"The Azure Firmament!"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly. With a wave of his hand, the Primordial Unity Cauldron appeared before him.

As soon as the cauldron appeared, a dull crashing sound came from within, as if a trapped beast was angrily ramming against it, trying to break free.

Luo Chen paid it little heed. He simply opened his mouth and spat.

*Shua!*

A wisp of golden flame drifted out, completely enveloping the entire Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Instantly, a miserable, agonizing sound came from within the cauldron.

Using the Nirvana Sacred Flame to refine a fifth-stage ghost god was precisely the right method.

"Xiaobai, keep an eye on the Primordial Unity Cauldron for me. If the fire weakens, let me know. Take these soul stones and refine them to strengthen your soul foundation."

Xiaobai's eyes lit up, and she eagerly took the handful of crystalline gravel that Luo Chen handed her.

Although they couldn't compare to the top-grade soul stone from before, these gravel-like small soul stones were easier to refine and more suitable for her.

"Xiaobai will take good care of the Primordial Unity Cauldron!"

Luo Chen smiled, then took a deep breath and sat down cross-legged.

After concentrating for a while in the void, he took out the blood sword Yuantu, which he had been nurturing in the acupoint of his left arm.

This time, he seemed to hesitate no longer. He directly spat out a mouthful of blood and smeared it on the Yuantu sword.

Immediately, the sword hummed sharply, as if responding!

"If you want to become a spirit treasure, then submit to me completely!"

Murmuring, Luo Chen opened his mouth and inhaled. The Yuantu sword shrank from large to small, and was directly swallowed into his mouth, entering his Purple Mansion.

The Purple Mansion was the most suitable place for a cultivator to nurture their magical treasures and true artifacts!

It was closest to the nascent soul, possessing the purest magical power, allowing for constant nurturing.

In his early years, Luo Chen had been worried that the Yuantu sword would harm his nascent soul, so he didn't dare to nurture it directly. Instead, he took the shortcut of finding a resting place for the sword in his arm.

But this time, he no longer had any scruples.

As the sword entered the Purple Mansion, an extremely bloody aura immediately arose.

Before the sword spirit could run rampant, a small nascent soul figure casually reached out and grabbed it.

Its other hand summoned a wisp of cyan flame, enveloping the Yuantu sword.

"Behave yourself!"

The small nascent soul figure spoke true words, and the Yuantu sword immediately quieted down.

Seeing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but feel relieved. With his current strength, he could indeed suppress this sword.

This freed him up to continue refining the Nine Heavens Qi.

"There are still quite a few of the two hundred strands left, enough for me to further advance the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*."

"But this is still not enough!"

"This Azure Firmament is an inexhaustible treasure trove of Nine Heavens Qi."

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen's hands formed sword seals. Wisps of the fierce and powerful Nine Heavens Qi flew out from his storage ring, dancing around his entire body.

The Daoist sat cross-legged in the Azure Firmament, as if entering a state of nirvana.

The Nine Heavens Qi sometimes entered his mouth, sometimes was exhaled from his nostrils. The acupoints all over his body opened up like stars, faintly resonating with this cosmic starry sky.

(End of this chapter)
