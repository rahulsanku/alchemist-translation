# Chapter 1057: Blue Fish Fire Spirit, Rosycloud's Scheme

**Sovereign Shark Flame!**

Luo Chen was no stranger to this azure flame.

As early as when he first entered the Demon-Fall Land and clashed with Shark Emperor Liujun, he had faced this flame head-on.

It was quite peculiar, specifically designed to target the physical bodies of living beings.

Especially desolate beasts!

The richer the blood and qi, the greater the power of the Sovereign Shark Flame. The blood and qi of an enemy's physical body seemed to act as fuel; once touched by the Sovereign Shark Flame, it clung like a stubborn worm, refusing to stop until it had burned everything clean.

Back then, Luo Chen had accidentally contaminated a single wisp, forcing him to immediately sever his own arm to barely escape being burned to ashes.

Later, Luo Chen consulted relevant texts and roughly formed a conjecture.

The sharkfolk race had a long heritage in the Northern Sea cultivation world, but they were never a major power.

In ancient times, the sharkfolk were food and prey for many desolate beasts.

By the ancient era and the present day, the sharkfolk, due to their beautiful appearance and the fact that their entire bodies were treasures, had been heavily hunted by human cultivators.

Shark Emperor Liujun's active participation in the war to destroy the Primordial Demon Sect was also an effort by a clan emperor to change the race's predicament.

And this Sovereign Shark Flame was a countermeasure developed by the sharkfolk's top experts in ancient times to escape desolate beast predation.

They used the oil from sharkfolk bodies to nurture a kind of source-less fire, gradually cultivating it to the fourth rank, specifically designed to counter desolate beasts with powerful physical bodies.

Over thousands of years, this oil lamp had absorbed the oil, blood, and essence of countless powerful sharkfolk!

Logically, after such a long period of cultivation by generations of experts, the Sovereign Shark Flame should have long since advanced to the fifth rank. But in Shark Emperor Liujun's hands, its performance was not particularly impressive.

At least, from Luo Chen's current perspective, Shark Emperor Liujun had not fully unleashed the power of this flame.

With a thought, the oil lamp flew spinning before Luo Chen.

He stared at the blue flame, his divine sense probing deeper.

After a moment, Luo Chen suddenly understood.

"No wonder!"

Xiaobai asked curiously, "Master, no wonder what?"

"No wonder Shark Emperor Liujun couldn't unleash the full power of this flame." Luo Chen chuckled lightly, pointing at the blue flame. "After thousands of years of nurturing, contaminated by the oil, blood, and essence of countless sharkfolk experts, this flame has absorbed too many lingering wills, suppressing the birth of the source-less fire's own fire spirit! And given Shark Emperor Liujun's personal cultivation level, he couldn't suppress the lingering thoughts of his predecessors, so naturally, he could only unleash a portion of its power."

Xiaobai let out an "oh," but didn't feel deeply moved. As a yin soul, she didn't like these powerful flames.

But the demon monk Kongchan beside her thought differently.

If the Sovereign Shark Flame truly contained so many lingering thoughts that even its inheritor, Liujun, couldn't fully control it, yet Luo Chen could easily suppress it and even turn it against its master, it showed just how powerful Luo Chen's soul foundation was!

For a moment, his gaze towards Luo Chen grew even more reverent.

If Luo Chen knew what Kongchan was thinking, he would probably agree partially!

Partially, because Luo Chen's method of suppressing the Sovereign Shark Flame wasn't just about his powerful soul foundation.

There were many factors involved.

First, Luo Chen's main cultivation art, the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, was itself the strongest fire-type technique in the world. No one could match his fire control ability!

Second, Luo Chen possessed two fifth-rank divine flames, Withering-Bloom and Nirvana. Even leaking just a trace of their aura was enough to suppress the sub-fifth-rank Sovereign Shark Flame.

Finally, it was related to Luo Chen's comprehension of the true essence of laws—the origin of fire!

Before the origin of fire, no matter how unique these source-less fires were, their essence was still fire. How dare they show off before him?

After briefly explaining to Xiaobai, Luo Chen held the oil lamp in one hand and stroked his chin with the other, falling into deep thought.

Xiaobai and Kongchan dared not disturb him, standing quietly to the side.

In the howling north wind, the azure flame gently flickered, emitting a hazy radiance.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's left hand formed a sword finger and slowly began to draw outward from the starlike flame.

Kongchan exclaimed softly, "Soul-Drawing Art!"

Luo Chen glanced at him casually. "What do you mean by Soul-Drawing Art?"

Kongchan was stunned. "Isn't what you're performing right now the high-level soul art, Soul-Drawing Art?"

Luo Chen's dark brows twitched. "This is just the low-level spell, Spirit-Intimidating Art."

Spirit-Intimidating Art?

Kongchan swallowed his saliva, feeling even more the unfathomable depth of Luo Chen's soul cultivation.

He was using the lowest-level Spirit-Intimidating Art to achieve the effect of the high-level soul art, Soul-Drawing Art.

These were two soul arts that seemed similar but had vastly different power.

Both could extract souls, but the former could only guide and extract when the enemy had no physical body. However, Soul-Drawing Art could ignore the shackles of the physical body and forcibly draw out a living being's soul.

After Kongchan explained, Luo Chen became interested.

"It seems you understand this high-level soul art. Do you have the original manual with you?"

"Little monk does know this art. I collected it by chance after the destruction of the Primordial Demon Sect, but it's only a copy."

"A copy will do!"

Kongchan respectfully took out a piece of yellow skin and presented it before Luo Chen.

"Human skin paper?"

Luo Chen recognized the material's essence at a glance. He calmly unfolded the skin paper, and the text immediately entered his sight.

After observing for a while, Luo Chen's fingertip movements changed slightly.

His index and middle fingers, held like a sword, bent slightly, forming a hook like a crescent moon. He silently chanted the incantation, and his soul no longer focused on intimidation and pressure but transformed into allure and seduction.

As his fingertips trembled slightly, illusory figures began to emerge from the azure flame.

Each figure had an excellent figure, either tall and handsome or elegant and slender. Their blurred faces still showed the peerless beauty they had in life.

These were the top experts of the sharkfolk race from the past!

Kongchan stood beside him, eyes wide with disbelief.

Luo Chen had just obtained the *Soul-Drawing Art* cultivation manual, yet he had already mastered it immediately.

Just how deep was his soul cultivation?

As Luo Chen continued to cast the spell, a large number of residual lingering thoughts were drawn out of the Sovereign Shark Flame.

Gradually, the originally azure flame became deep and pure.

And faintly, the shadow of a small fish could be seen swimming and wandering inside.

"Come out!"

Luo Chen shouted softly, and his primordial spirit suddenly unified.

A pressure like heavenly might descended upon this area, and strands of power even stronger than divine sense shuttled back and forth.

That was divine thought!

As Luo Chen's divine thought surged, he forcibly condensed the small fish into form.

The next moment, the fish leaped out of the fire, and waves of flame rippled outward like ripples.

Xiaobai cheered, "Congratulations, Master, for acquiring another powerful flame!"

Luo Chen smiled and shook his head. "For now, I've only removed the impurities and forcibly helped it condense a fire spirit. Its rank is still at the fourth level, far from being called powerful."

He opened his mouth and inhaled, and the blue fish was swallowed, eventually entering his Purple Mansion.

Once inside, the blue fish deeply felt two auras more powerful than itself.

One was like a tree, its branches spreading.

The other was like a bird, soaring and circling.

It dared not disturb the two "seniors" and obediently landed in the Purple Mansion, spreading layers of flame waves to form a sea of blue fire, disappearing within.

The Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit ignored it.

The Nirvana Fire Spirit, however, seemed eager to try, pecking with its sharp beak and circling above the sea of fire.

But with a gaze cast over, the Nirvana Fire Spirit was forced to fly away, not daring to covet the blue fish.

Thus, the blue fish finally breathed a sigh of relief and felt a trace of intimacy towards the owner of that gaze.

On the glacial wasteland.

Luo Chen weighed the oil lamp in his hand, then handed it to Xiaobai.

"Take it. This is a very strong true artifact. Although I've taken the Sovereign Shark Flame, the remaining sharkfolk oil on it can still ignite the Sovereign Shark Flame multiple times."

Xiaobai took it joyfully. "Thank you, Master, for bestowing this treasure!"

Shark Emperor Liujun's legacy was not just an oil lamp and a flame, but also a storage pouch.

But Luo Chen wasn't in a hurry to inventory the spoils.

To him now, the legacy of a Sage-level demon cultivator was just so-so.

Compared to the storage pouch, Luo Chen was more interested in the "spirit pet" he had easily subdued.

"Demon Monk Kongchan, I've heard your true form is a Chanting Monk. Show me your original form!"

Faced with Luo Chen's somewhat flippant, even contemptuous words, Demon Monk Kongchan showed no dissatisfaction.

The moment Luo Chen's divine thought burst forth earlier, he knew why Luo Chen was so strong, so strong that he couldn't even muster a thought of resistance.

This was an existence who had condensed a primordial spirit ahead of time!

He had even comprehended the true essence of laws!

Coupled with the Azure Yang Great Handprint that had destroyed Tai Sui earlier, absorbing the origin qi of heaven and earth along the way.

Kongchan swallowed his saliva.

Primordial spirit, true essence of laws, control over the origin qi of heaven and earth—these characteristics were all exclusive to Ascendant-level great powers.

Mastering one made one a peerless genius.

Mastering all three—what difference was there between this young man before him and an Ascendant great power?

Boom!

A massive creature sat cross-legged on the glacial wasteland.

Luo Chen looked up and saw a strange creature that resembled both an octopus and a jellyfish appear before him.

It had a shiny, round, huge head, human-like facial features, black spots like monastic precept scars covering its body, and eighteen tentacles covered in cracks.

"Master, this is my true form."

As the head spoke, the cracks on the tentacles also opened simultaneously, making the same sound.

Nineteen mouths speaking at once, the buzzing sound was irritating, like a nagging monk constantly chanting scriptures.

"So this is the origin of the name 'Chanting Monk'!"

Luo Chen smiled and waved his hand.

"Better to transform back into human form. Your true form is rather ugly."

As demonic aura spread, the massive Chanting Monk dissipated, transforming back into Kongchan's gaunt body.

He stood respectfully with his hands down before Luo Chen, showing no trace of the Great Demon King who had once roamed the Northern Sea.

Luo Chen stepped forward, golden light blazing in his eyes, walking towards a direction of the Primordial Demon Sect.

As he walked, he casually asked Kongchan about the demon race, especially the specific details of the Primordial Demon Sect's destruction back then.

"That battle was fought by Great Sage Beili and Sea Emperor Whale Ancestor against Soul-Refining Ancestor. They shattered his physical body, and even his primordial spirit was dispersed by Sea Emperor Whale Ancestor. Before dying, Soul-Refining Ancestor scattered his primordial spirit and set up a grand formation, trapping us in the Arctic Yemo Heaven for over a hundred years."

"As for Primordial Demon Ancestor of the Primordial Demon Sect, the battlefield wasn't on our side at the time, so I don't know who was fighting him."

"I only remember that a wisp of golden flame once leaked from that battlefield, taking away millions of experts from both the human and demon races."

Luo Chen's footsteps paused.

"Golden flame?"

Kongchan nodded, then, as if remembering something, a look of shock and uncertainty appeared in his eyes.

Earlier, in the blood lake, Luo Chen's method of killing the corpse giant seemed to be a kind of golden flame.

Could it be...

Luo Chen's gaze flickered. Certain conjectures from years past were now being confirmed.

The mighty Primordial Demon Sect, which had suppressed them for three thousand years, was destroyed by demon beasts. The inside story was indeed not simple.

Rosycloud had personally taken action!

Destroying Primordial Demon, burying Nether Origin, leaving the two continents of Northern Sea and Eastern Wilderness weakened, inviting the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's invasion and occupation.

Her ultimate goal was actually the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

And later, Rosycloud did indeed personally kill her way into Central Continent.

But why didn't Rosycloud attack the Western Desert and Southern Frontier?

No!

Perhaps she did attack.

The Western Desert Buddhist Pure Land had sensed the danger and closed the Hanging Temple in advance, only opening its mountain gates after Central Continent collapsed.

And in those years, the demon race of the Western Desert had also mobilized, leading to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's military expedition.

As for the Southern Frontier?

With Netherspring Divine Lord stationed there, even someone as strong as Rosycloud couldn't rashly attack the mountain gates.

However, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect did later send people to the Southern Frontier, leaving Central Continent's forces depleted, allowing Rosycloud to take advantage.

"Rosycloud schemed so meticulously, creating perfect conditions for her attack on Central Continent. What was her goal?"

"According to intelligence, the final battlefield of the Central Continent war was Heaven-Earth Peak, and on Heaven-Earth Peak sat the Star Gate, one of the Dao Sect's Three Treasures."

"After Heaven-Earth Peak collapsed, the Star Gate organization disintegrated, and it's said that even the spirit treasure Star Gate went missing."

"Could Rosycloud's ultimate goal be to seize the Star Gate?"

Luo Chen pondered deeply, his steps slowing considerably.

Hearing no sound, he prompted, "Continue. Tell me about the recent attacks by the Northern Sea demon race on the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's three continents."

Kongchan dared not be negligent and recounted everything he knew.

"I'm not too clear about this matter either. I only know it was an order from the two ancestors."

"Some speculate it's revenge for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's interference in the Northern Sea war over the past century, hindering the demon race's unification of the Northern Sea cultivation world."

"But in this little monk's view, the intensity of the retaliation isn't high. At least, Great Demon King-level experts haven't participated."

Luo Chen blinked and stopped before a large mountain that was mostly intact.

An order from the two ancestors of the Northern Sea?

This might be related to Li Canghai.

He stopped pondering further. At least for now, Li Canghai was his ally.

An ally's ally might not be an ally, but as long as there was no real conflict of interest, they weren't enemies either.

"You may return!"

"Continue to guard Tide Overwhelming City. Don't let anyone enter the Arctic Yemo Heaven and disturb my cultivation."

Kongchan nodded, but hesitated before leaving.

"Master, the fifth-rank spirit vein of the Arctic Yemo Heaven was shattered in the battle back then. It's not suitable for breaking through to the Ascendant realm."

Luo Chen smiled. He wasn't planning to break through to the Ascendant realm here.

It was just that he had collected a large amount of Nine Heavens Firmament Qi and could proceed to the next stage of refinement.

As a fifth-rank sword dao technique, the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* at the master level could already counter an Ascendant's strike in his hands, but Luo Chen wasn't satisfied.

Only at great perfection could it truly threaten an Ascendant great power!

Luo Chen didn't explain much. He waved his hand, signaling Kongchan to leave.

After the area was completely quiet, Luo Chen walked along the broken mountain body, step by step towards the waist-level platform that had been severed by a single sword strike.

Along the way, he occasionally cast out array flags, personally intervening to regulate the flow of earth veins.

Three days later.

When Luo Chen's footsteps reached the flat mountain waist.

The fourth-rank spirit vein deep within the mountain had been mostly regulated, providing Luo Chen with considerable spiritual energy.

He casually tossed something.

Thump!

The Nine Jars Urn stood firmly on the ground.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged, formed a sword seal with his hand, and opened his mouth to inhale.

Instantly, a wisp of purple-tinged firmament qi flew out of the black urn, was drawn into his body, and began to be rapidly refined as his dharma power circulated.

As if responding, the condensed Thousand Skies Sword Seed also began to spin rapidly.
