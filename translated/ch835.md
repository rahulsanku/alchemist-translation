# Chapter 835: Wolf Clan Sovereign, Fenghua Palace Destroyed

At these words, everyone's expression changed dramatically.

Even Fenghua Fairy and Pavilion Master Li, who had anticipated something like this, couldn't help but cry out in shock.

"Those beastly demons, how can they be so despicable?"

"No wonder the sect keeps sending urgent distress messages!"

"My Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion is in danger!"

"Lord Lingtian City Master, I beg you to dispatch troops immediately to reinforce the rear!"

Voices of disturbance rose and fell one after another.

Amidst the clamor, Lingtian City Master let out a low shout.

"Silence!"

The mana fluctuations of a Grand Cultivator swept outward, and his Sage domain enveloped the great hall, pressing down on everyone until they trembled.

Seeing that all had quieted down, Lingtian City Master slowly withdrew his mana fluctuations.

"Beasts may be savage, but they are not without intelligence. Especially after advancing to the fourth rank, they are no different from us. There is no need for the word 'despicable.' After all, we once launched a surprise attack on the Absolute Spirit Domain, and they are merely repaying us in kind."

"Dispatching troops to reinforce the rear is impossible. Don't even think about it!"

"Lingtian Pass is the most important. Once this barrier is lost, the demon race's billion-strong army will surge down, and all sects will be exposed before the flood of demon beasts. Who can stop them? Tell me, which of your sects can stand alone against them?"

"You nine are already the strongest combat force in the city besides myself, Tian Duzi, and Divine Origin Sage. Mobilizing you to reinforce the rear, coordinating with the strong ones there, is the only way to possibly drive away those three demon emperor routes."

Lingtian City Master rapidly and clearly laid out his decision and judgment. It was obvious he had thought it through thoroughly before coming.

As his words settled, those who had been agitated earlier also calmed down.

None of them were fools. They naturally understood the principle that when the lips are gone, the teeth feel cold. The earlier commotion was merely concern clouding their judgment.

Seeing everyone calm, Lingtian City Master began issuing orders.

"The exact number of demon emperors under those three commanders is unclear, but judging by the number of demon emperors they previously commanded, there should be around twenty in total."

"This is an extremely powerful force. Under the leadership of three commanders comparable to Grand Cultivators, aside from top-tier superior sects like Dongyang Sect, Tiandu Sect, and Void Emperor Sect, almost no sect can withstand them."

"One can imagine that wherever they pass, nothing but ruins will remain!"

"Next, you nine will split into three groups and pursue them separately. I have already used the Nether Origin Sect's intelligence channels to notify the major sects in the rear, ordering their top experts to deploy in full force."

"Nine Spirit Primogenitor, Iron View Daoist, Pavilion Master Li, you head toward the Ten Thousand Swords Domain!"

"Shouyang Mountain Hermit, Celestial Master Hua, Taiyue Sage, you head toward the White Crane Domain! On the way, pass through the Hundred Beasts Domain and check the situation there. The demon beasts of Hundred Beasts Sect will likely be swayed by demon emperor-level existences, harming the lives of an entire domain."

"Fenghua Fairy, Jin Ling... Demon-Slaying Sage!"

When he called out Demon-Slaying Sage, Lingtian City Master looked at Luo Chen with an expression of approval.

Among the nine present, only Luo Chen appeared on the surface to be at the third layer of the Sage stage.

But battle after battle had proven his strength, earning recognition from everyone. He was in no way inferior to mid-Sage stage experts.

That was why he had been selected today.

As for others like Ling Fengzi, Divine Fire, and Medicine Master, they were not even considered by Lingtian City Master. Even Talisman Elder of the Divine Talisman Sect, a long-established Sage, was deemed inferior to Luo Chen in his eyes.

"You head toward the Fenghua Domain. If Fenghua Palace has already been destroyed, remember not to linger. Continue pursuing along the route and catch up to those demon emperors!"

Hearing this, Fenghua Fairy's face instantly turned pale.

She was clearly imagining the cruelty behind that scene.

At this moment, Luo Chen was also concerned about the small number of Luo Tian Sect disciples temporarily residing at Bai Li Qingchuan, so he did not object to Lingtian City Master's words.

But he still asked a question.

"With so many experts being drawn away from the city at once, how will those left behind face the billion-strong demon beast army led by the four commanders outside Lingtian Pass?"

Tian Duzi's projection, who had remained silent all along, now took the initiative to respond.

"No need to worry. I will act!"

Such a promise from a Grandmaster of the Dao of Formations could stand against a thousand armies and ten thousand horses!

Luo Chen immediately felt reassured.

But Tian Duzi was clearly not at ease. He looked at Luo Chen and the other two, speaking earnestly: "From Fenghua Domain heading west, passing through Dieyun, Qitong, and Tianji Domains, you will reach my Tiandu Sect's mountain gate. Three of my junior brothers are stationed there. Even if a Great Demon Emperor comes in person, relying on the mountain gate's grand formation, they can hold out for a long time. If you arrive in time, I hope you can lend Tiandu a hand!"

Luo Chen responded without hesitation, "That goes without saying, Fellow Daoist. No need to worry."

Fenghua Fairy nodded, but she was clearly distracted. In truth, if that situation were to occur, Fenghua Palace would be destroyed first.

Jin Ling said nothing. This matter had little impact on his Five Elements Divine Sect.

In the ten years of the Lingtian Pass war, sects like the Five Elements Divine Sect and the Misty Sect—homeless strays without roots—had most of their disciples conscripted into Lingtian Pass.

The number of disciples left behind in the Fenghua Domain was not large, unlikely to attract the attention of demon emperors.

The same was true for Luo Tian Sect. Over the past ten years, including Luo Chen and Wang Yuan, more than two hundred disciples had gradually entered Lingtian Pass to fight.

Only about a hundred disciples remained at Bai Li Qingchuan.

If the three demon commanders' goal was to destroy the rear, they would not waste time on such small forces.

"Everyone, set out!"

Lingtian City Master said.

Tian Duzi suddenly interjected, "Lingtian City Master, in front of so many fellow Daoists, can you give me a straight answer?"

Lingtian City Master frowned. "What answer?"

"Simple. Aside from the Cangwu Mountain faction, eight of the nine demon commanders have already appeared. The severity of the situation goes without saying. Under these circumstances, will the Nether Origin Holy Land still stand idly by?"

At this, Lingtian City Master's brow relaxed, and he let out a carefree laugh.

"Rest assured. I have already notified the sect. In this battle, the Nether Origin Sect will also dispatch many experts."

Then, his gaze swept over those about to depart.

"This battle is meant to drive away the demon emperors destroying the rear. But if there is an opportunity to keep them here forever, that would be even better!"

Keep them here forever?

Luo Chen and the others exchanged glances, saying nothing, merely nodding.

...

Nine beams of escaping light shot up from the City Lord's Mansion.

Hovering in the air, Shouyang Mountain Hermit's voice rang out.

"Demon emperors are cunning, and their momentum is fierce. This battle will not be an easy task. Though the mission is paramount, do not forget to preserve yourselves. I hope that when we return, we can still raise our cups and enjoy ourselves on Shouyang Mountain!"

The group fell silent for a moment before someone responded.

"The Hermit speaks truly. Everyone should act within their means!"

Luo Chen echoed the sentiment, his gaze falling on Nine Spirit Primogenitor.

The other party nodded slightly. "Take care!"

Luo Chen smiled gently and made a bowing salute. "Take care!"

Not only Nine Spirit Primogenitor, but Pavilion Master Li and Deng Taiyue also sent Luo Chen voice transmissions, telling him to be safe.

These were all connections Luo Chen had built up over the years.

After exchanging farewells, the group hesitated no longer and sped off in three directions.

...

On a towering mountain imprinted with five enormous finger marks, a green-robed old man stood with his hands behind his back, gazing into the distance.

Suddenly, a streak of light flew over and landed before him.

Kneeling on the ground, the figure spoke: "Master!"

The green-robed old man glanced at him. "Why are you in such a sorry state?"

Boundless Demon Emperor's expression was one of shame. "Reporting to Master, I encountered Demon-Slaying Sage."

"Demon-Slaying?" The green-robed old man's lips curled slightly, his gaze falling on the lifelike five enormous finger marks on the mountain wall at his feet. "You mean Luo Chen of the Alchemist Sect?"

"Yes!"

"You are no match for him? That is somewhat unexpected."

Boundless Demon Emperor's face flushed red, and he gritted his teeth. "It was my carelessness. Moreover, this person is different from the rumors. He also masters a sword formation. Though I can tell he has not mastered it for long, its power is immense. Furthermore..."

"Sword formation?" The green-robed old man keenly caught the key word.

Boundless Demon Emperor took a deep breath. "Yes. That sword formation condenses the power of the five elements, driven by a sword pellet. It is extremely similar to the Two Modes Five Elements Sword Formation rumored among the wolf clan. I suspect Luo Chen is connected to Han Zhan, who once besieged and killed Aoxiao."

The green-robed old man narrowed his eyes, seemingly deep in thought.

Boundless Demon Emperor dared not disturb him and continued kneeling respectfully on the ground, showing none of the bearing of a demon emperor.

If a human cultivator unfamiliar with the situation saw this, they would find it extremely incongruous.

Even if the green-robed old man was of a higher realm, they were ultimately existences of the same great realm. There was no need for such groveling.

But the nearby wolf clan experts looked on as if this were perfectly normal.

This was because, unlike ordinary demon emperors, Boundless Demon Emperor's original form was actually the green-robed old man's companion beast, having grown up with him since childhood.

Later, when the old man embarked on the Great Dao, Boundless Demon Emperor transformed and separated from him.

There were also rumors that the reason the old man had become the Wolf Clan Sovereign was because Boundless Demon Emperor had contributed greatly to his growth.

After a long silence, the green-robed old man muttered to himself, "Back then, I went up Cangwu Mountain, trying to demand Han Zhan and the others from Azure Frost to avenge Aoxiao, but was refused. I even fought Azure Frost, which only enhanced her great reputation among the demon race."

"Failing to obtain the enemy, I unexpectedly learned from the birds of Cangwu Mountain that a human cultivator was refining pills for them."

"I heard he was also surnamed Luo!"

These words carried a pointed meaning.

Hearing this secret for the first time, Boundless Demon Emperor was momentarily stunned, then realized something.

"Could it be that he obtained Han Zhan's inheritance on Cangwu Mountain..."

The green-robed old man slightly raised his hand, cutting off the other's speculation, because ahead, demonic winds were gathering again.

The rolling demonic aura suddenly converged, revealing a woman with snow-white hair.

As she landed, she bowed respectfully to the green-robed old man.

"Commander Aokuang, there has been a change at Lingtian Pass. Three sages from the western city have broken through the encirclement and are heading toward the rear Fenghua Domain."

The green-robed old man, none other than the Eastern Wilderness Wolf Clan Sovereign Aokuang, let out a light laugh upon hearing this news.

"Quite a fast reaction. That figures. These human cultivators are skilled in the hundred arts of immortality, and their communication methods are endlessly varied. It is only natural that they received news of Bizheng and the others' raid so quickly."

The white-haired woman asked tentatively, "Should we send people to intercept them?"

"Intercept?"

Aokuang tugged at the corner of his mouth, his gaze drifting lazily over the flood of demon beasts surging toward Lingtian Pass.

"This battle has dragged on far too long!"

"Instead of continuing this stalemate, it is better to settle it in one decisive engagement!"

"On the surface, we are attacking Lingtian Pass to buy time and space for Bizheng and Kuangkuang to destroy the human rear. In truth, they are the bait, creating conditions for us to conquer Lingtian Pass."

The white-haired woman was puzzled. "Are we launching a general offensive?"

Aokuang inquired, "What were the cultivation levels of those three who went to the western city?"

"Approximately mid-Sage stage, with no mean skill," the white-haired woman replied, vaguely understanding but still uncertain. She hesitated, "But only three left?"

"Three?" Aokuang waved his hand grandly. "That is enough!"

If three went in one direction, the other two directions would likely not have fewer.

Lingtian Pass had fewer than thirty Sages in total. This move took away thirty percent, and the most formidable part at that.

Of the remaining top experts, aside from those three Grand Cultivators, there was nothing left to fear.

And on their side, it was not just the four commanders.

"Notify the troops. All fourth-rank demon emperors under my command are to stand by, awaiting my orders to attack Lingtian Pass!"

"As commanded!"

"As commanded!"

Boundless Demon Emperor and the white-haired woman thundered their assent.

Not stopping there, Aokuang took out a rhinoceros horn. Under the infusion of mana, the horn slowly began to glow.

"Seven Rhinoceros, several years have passed. How is your recovery?"

Hearing this, Boundless Demon Emperor, who had been cowed by Luo Chen earlier, felt his spirits rise.

Aokuang, Huangman, Niugui, Baizu, and even the recuperating Seven Rhinoceros Commander would all act.

Five commanders, plus twenty demon emperors, and billions of low-rank demon beasts!

With such overwhelming momentum, how could mere Lingtian Pass withstand it?

He could already see the satisfying scene of countless human cultivators wailing and begging for mercy under the drooling jaws of demon beasts after the city fell.

...

Lingtian Pass bordered the Fenghua Domain. In fact, part of Lingtian Pass's territory originally belonged to the Fenghua Domain.

After Luo Chen and the other two broke out of the city, they did not stop for a moment, rushing straight toward Fenghua Palace.

Under full-speed flight, the three Sages moved extremely fast, covering the distance of one domain in a single day.

Back in the day, Pang Renxiong, a Adept, had needed about a month to travel from Jade Cauldron Sword Sect across one domain to reach Dahe Market.

Although the distances differed, a rough comparison showed the enormous gap in flight speed between Sages and Adepts.

Passing by a basin, Jin Ling Sage paused for a moment.

After a sweep of his divine sense, he said calmly, "Continue to Fenghua Palace!"

Luo Chen also released his divine sense to scan the area. Seeing the result, he felt a measure of relief.

The remaining disciples of the Five Elements Divine Sect were stationed in this basin. His divine sense sweep showed they were unharmed.

Luo Chen was not relieved because the Five Elements Divine Sect cultivators had survived, but because if those demon emperors had not bothered to attack these low-rank cultivators, they naturally would not target Luo Tian Sect either.

As for Fenghua Fairy, the urgency on her face could no longer be concealed.

If even the remnants of these superior Sage sects were beneath their notice, then the demon emperors' target was clearly unified—they were going after the established superior Sage sects in the rear!

Fenghua Palace would bear the brunt!

Luo Chen followed behind the other two using the Ninety Thousand Li technique, occasionally catching glimpses of Fenghua Fairy's anxious expression.

But he could not offer words of comfort.

News traveled with a delay. Given the mobility of a large force of demon emperors, by the time they received the news, many sects must have already suffered.

Comforting others at this point would be nothing but self-deception.

"No wonder the number of low-rank demon beasts attacking Lingtian Pass has been increasing lately. I thought it was just the Million Great Mountains accumulating too many demon beasts. Now it seems that billion-strong army is clearly all the strength of the Million Great Mountains. It's just that the three commanders handed over command of these low-rank demon beasts to the four commanders led by Aokuang and Huangman."

"A feint at the front, destruction at the rear, to shake the confidence of Lingtian Pass's defending cultivators. Once news of the destruction of numerous rear sects spreads, no matter how high the walls and deep the moats of Lingtian Pass, it will be hard to avoid collapsing from within."

"No matter how hard the reef, it cannot withstand the scouring of the flood. This move by the demon race appears to be a sneak attack, but it is actually an open and aboveboard scheme... No, if we leave, will those four commanders sit idly by, unmoved by the empty Lingtian Pass?"

Luo Chen's mind raced with thoughts, and his calm expression grew grim.

He had a bad feeling.

If the demon race's upper echelons learned of their departure and attacked Lingtian Pass without regard for consequences, could the remaining cultivators hold?

Whether in terms of low-rank cultivator numbers or high-end expert comparisons, Lingtian Pass was at an absolute disadvantage.

The only thing they could rely on was perhaps Tian Duzi's complete mastery of the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation.

Just as Luo Chen was lost in thought, time flew by.

Night fell, and starlight scattered across the earth.

Fenghua Palace had arrived!

Before they even reached it, the sound of sobbing reached their ears.

Listening carefully, it was not the wind sobbing, but the crying of many women.

Boom!

Fenghua Fairy, who had been flying at the very front, erupted with mana and crashed like a meteor into a palace complex built among towering mountains and ridges.

This was Luo Chen's first time at Fenghua Palace.

Even though he had spent a full three years in the Fenghua Domain, this was indeed his first visit.

What met his eyes were collapsed mountains, broken walls, and ruined foundations.

Palaces no longer stood in clusters, jade steps lay disconnected.

Wailing filled the wilderness, devastation as far as the eye could see!

Suddenly, a most piercing shriek tore through the night sky, echoing endlessly.

That was Fenghua Fairy's rage!

Standing in midair, Luo Chen's expression was one of pity as he pressed his lips together and tightened his face.

Jin Ling Sage, who had always appeared indifferent, was also moved at this moment.

"Alas..."

A soft sigh, feeling the pain as if it were his own, as if the time when the Five Elements Divine Sect's mountain gate was destroyed had been just as tragic.

A streak of light flew up unsteadily.

Luo Chen sent out a stream of mana to guide it over.

Feng Yue was an old acquaintance.

The woman's face was pale, her hair disheveled. She cupped her hands in salute. "Two Sages, my master invites you below."

Jin Ling Sage nodded and flew straight down.

Luo Chen glanced at Feng Yue, took out two jade bottles, and pushed them over with mana.

"Some healing pills I refined myself. They should be suitable for your current injuries."

Having said that, Luo Chen also flew down.

Feng Yue clutched the two jade bottles, her voice as faint as a mosquito as she murmured thanks. Her gaze swept over the once-prosperous Fenghua Palace, now reduced to ruins, and she fell into a daze.

Swish! Swish!

Luo Chen and Jin Ling, one after the other, entered a half-collapsed palace.

Looking closely, Fenghua Fairy was holding a blood-soaked corpse, her mana fluctuations uncontrollably spilling outward, revealing the anger in her heart.

With just one glance, Luo Chen recognized the owner of the corpse.

It was the Second Palace Master of Fenghua Palace. Back when he was stationed in the western city district, their relationship had been quite good. Later, because the First Palace Master Fenghua Fairy had gone to Lingtian Pass, she returned to the relatively safe rear to guard the sect.

But now, she had perished, her death a miserable sight.

In her chest and abdomen was a large hole, even the covering clothes had collapsed inward.

"First Palace Master, please accept my condolences!" Jin Ling Sage sighed in consolation.

"First Palace Master..." Luo Chen also wanted to offer words of comfort, but as soon as he spoke, he realized something was wrong: "Where is the Sage? Where is the Second Palace Master's Sage?"
