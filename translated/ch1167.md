# Chapter 1167: One Path, the Seventh Realm

First came the Blood Dragon Ginseng, then the Meridian-Repairing Pill.

But the reactions they provoked were as different as heaven and earth.

This was because nourishment and repair were two entirely different concepts!

None among those present were weaklings, and their knowledge was considerable. The moment they saw this pill, they instantly understood what it was.

A spirit pill!

An immortal spirit pill!

The storage bags of those cultivators who had fallen into the Great Destruction Realm in the past contained similar pills.

Although most were useless to martial artists, there were also some pills that repaired the body, which had unimaginable effects on martial artists.

Almost the moment Luo Chen announced the name "Meridian-Repairing Pill," many people could no longer hold back and began to speak out.

"I am willing to pay a million gold pearls to purchase this pill!"

"How can such a divine medicine be measured in gold and silver? This lady is willing to offer three pieces of Heavenly Star Stone!"

"Luo Chen, is it? Tell me what resources you wish to exchange for. I, Dugu Cheng, former leader of the Solitary Vulture Merchant Alliance, as long as you name it, if there are resources to be found in the seventy-two cities of the Great Destruction Realm, I am willing to seek them out for you!"

The moment Dugu Cheng spoke, the previously boisterous atmosphere gradually quieted down.

Clearly, this man truly had great influence, and the words he spoke were naturally not false.

Then the ownership of this pill would most likely fall into his hands.

Luo Chen looked at the commotion caused by his single pill within the venue, his expression very calm.

He had already anticipated this situation.

In the Guixu, he did not know how things were elsewhere. But here in the Great Destruction Realm, the development of alchemy had always been very poor.

This was not surprising.

Alchemy was often dependent on materials.

The more abundant the alchemy materials, the better the development of alchemy would naturally be.

The Great Destruction Realm had no thousand-year spiritual herbs or ten-thousand-year spiritual grasses, and demonic beast inner cores were even rarer.

Often, the source of high-grade pills came from the pockets of some cultivators who had drifted here.

That was why Wuyou City had so many establishments specifically serving cultivators, with the aim of emptying those cultivators' pockets.

And as a fifth-grade alchemist, the large number of pills Luo Chen had carried before his ascension were all top-quality items.

Take this Meridian-Repairing Pill, for example!

It was originally merely a third-grade pill specialty of the Medicine King Sect.

After Luo Chen obtained the pill formula later, through improvement and grade elevation, as well as large-scale refinement, he had refined a batch of fourth-grade upper-grade and top-grade Meridian-Repairing Pills.

Considering that his body might experience discomfort after ascension, he had specially brought along a batch of carefully selected Meridian-Repairing Pills.

And for martial artists who spent their days circulating qi and blood, the value of these things was by no means inferior to those fifth-grade divine pills!

Therefore, his asking price was unceremonious.

"Void Bud Crystal, I only want this item!"

As he spoke, he took out the stone box made of Void Bud Crystal.

This was to show a sample, in case anyone did not know what Void Bud Crystal was.

Fang Zhibai had been paying attention to Luo Chen's actions, and when he took out the stone box, he was not surprised.

After all, Luo Chen had inquired about this matter earlier.

He just hadn't expected Luo Chen to have a high-grade pill like the "Meridian-Repairing Pill" in his hands, and he felt a little pained inside.

The stone box his Fang family had given away along with the immortal essence stone was so large; exchanging it for this one pill would probably be more than enough.

But Luo Chen would most likely be disappointed.

The Fang family didn't have much Void Bud Crystal. Although there were many martial artists present, those who possessed it were probably very few.

Sure enough.

After Luo Chen specified the exchange for Void Bud Crystal, the old man from the Solitary Vulture Merchant Alliance subconsciously furrowed his brow.

"Nothing else will do?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "Only exchange for Void Bud Crystal!"

Dugu Cheng sat back down regretfully.

A buzzing sound arose again in the venue, everyone asking who had Void Bud Crystal.

Especially those martial artists who cultivated in the Nine Rings of Destruction and had hidden meridian injuries, they were willing to pay a high price to exchange Void Bud Crystal from others, and then trade with Luo Chen for the Meridian-Repairing Pill.

Suddenly!

A gloomy voice sounded.

"I have a piece of Void Bud Crystal. I wonder if it can be exchanged for this pill?"

Luo Chen looked towards the source. A masked martial artist had taken out a crystal two fingers wide and long—it was indeed Void Bud Crystal!

Luo Chen was first delighted, but after beckoning it over, he couldn't help but frown again.

"It's a bit small."

Even as he said this, he waved his hand, sending the Meridian-Repairing Pill over, and condensed his voice into a line, instructing the other party on some key points regarding how to take and refine it.

Finally.

He added, "Friend, if you have more Void Bud Crystal in the future, feel free to come and trade with me."

The man nodded, and ignoring the envious eyes of others, he directly got up and left the venue.

Clearly, for martial artists, the value of the Meridian-Repairing Pill was too great!

Staying here too long, even with a mask, would make him an easy target.

In fact, some people had already followed him out.

Luo Chen took all this in, and then unexpectedly spoke up again in a clear voice.

"I still have some Meridian-Repairing Pills left. If anyone can find Void Bud Crystal, you may come to me to trade. I, Luo Chen, am temporarily staying on Yuelong Lane."

After leaving these words, ignoring the burning gazes of others, Luo Chen sat back down.

As soon as he sat down, Gu Xun beside him took the initiative to strike up a conversation.

"I didn't expect Fellow Daoist Luo to be an alchemy grandmaster!"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly. "It's normal for high-level cultivators to carry some pills. What makes you think I am a grandmaster-level alchemist?"

Gu Xun waved his hand. "That's different! Although I am not a cultivator, I know some of your habits. For pills of the same grade, only you alchemists yourselves would have upper-grade or even top-grade treasures, because these things are occasionally obtained from dozens of furnaces. Ordinary cultivators might have some of the same grade, but they are often only lower-grade. Am I right?"

Luo Chen laughed heartily. Although he did not admit it, he did not deny it either.

That was indeed an unwritten rule in the alchemy world.

But it didn't quite apply to him, Luo Chen.

Because once his proficiency was high enough, he could control the grade of every furnace!

Even top-grade pills, he could mass-produce!

Gu Xun then asked, "Fellow Daoist Luo, besides preserving items and opening small spaces, what other wonderful uses does Void Bud Crystal have that make you search for it so diligently?"

Faced with this question, Luo Chen was about to answer when he suddenly froze.

The characteristic of preserving items, he knew.

Although it was different from traditional storage spaces, unable to enlarge or shrink, storage tools made from Void Bud Crystal had excellent sealing properties.

But what did "opening small spaces" mean?

He pondered for a moment, and without hiding his confusion, he directly asked.

Gu Xun was stunned for a moment, not expecting Luo Chen himself to be unaware of this point. But since the words were out, he explained in detail.

If he hadn't explained, it would have been fine. But after hearing it, Luo Chen was startled.

It turned out that to reach the seventh realm in body refinement, one had to open a small space within the body, forming one's own physical universe.

Therefore, the sixth realm was called the Destruction Realm, meaning to break down and then establish.

That process was a matter of life and death, and ordinary martial artists simply could not cross it.

But if assisted by a large amount of Void Bud Crystal, it could increase the chances by ten to twenty percent!

And Luo Chen thought further ahead, or rather, remembered something from his memories.

Azure Frost had once opened an internal space, with the help of a Space-Accumulating Flower.

Of course, it wasn't entirely the credit of the Space-Accumulating Flower; it was also due to Azure Frost's own comprehension of spatial laws.

But being able to open an internal space at the Ascendant stage showed Azure Frost's astonishing talent in the path of body refinement.

A divine beast, the Azure Luan, was truly blessed by heaven!

"Additionally, there is another saying about opening small spaces." At this point, a look of bewilderment appeared on Gu Xun's face. "When all things return to ruin, hiding in a small space opened by Void Bud Crystal might allow one to survive."

Luo Chen was shocked. "Where does this saying come from?"

Gu Xun sighed. "Lord Wuyou mentioned it casually, but later he denied this possibility. In his view, Void Bud Crystal is ultimately a dead thing. The small space it constructs is not stable enough, and the amount of Void Bud Crystal required to accommodate a martial artist's physical body is simply too large, completely unfeasible. However, in the view of me and two other fellow daoists, there is a certain degree of feasibility."

Constructing a small space with Void Bud Crystal to shelter a martial artist's physical body, escaping the return of all things to ruin during the cycle of eras?

Was this also a path that Lord Wuyou had envisioned?

But since he himself had denied it, it seemed this path indeed had flaws.

And if one truly had that much Void Bud Crystal, why not use it to attempt a breakthrough to the legendary seventh realm!

Lost in thought, Luo Chen recalled what Xiangfei had said earlier about some rumors regarding this martial dao immortal before him, and he gradually reacted.

"I heard that Fellow Daoist Gu, unlike the other two martial dao immortals, often appears in the lower six rings. Could it be that you are searching for Void Bud Crystal?"

Gu Xun did not avoid the question. "You must be amused. Even though Lord Wuyou said this path is unfeasible, I still want to give it a try. The other two fellow daoists have better aptitudes than me and want to rely on breaking through to a higher realm to break out of this place, so naturally they don't need to delve into these dead ends."

Luo Chen was helpless. "That's a pity. I was hoping to exchange some top-grade Meridian-Repairing Pills with you for some Void Bud Crystal."

"You really have top-grade Meridian-Repairing Pills!" Gu Xun was stunned, then said with a fervent expression, "If it's just a small amount, it's not impossible. Moreover, I have two other ores with properties similar to Void Bud Crystal: Spatial Prism Aperture and top-grade Profound Heaven Crystal!"

"Uh..."

This time, it was Luo Chen's turn to be a bit dumbfounded.

But he quickly realized that Gu Xun himself was not very confident in the path he was pursuing, which was why he was willing to take out a small amount of Void Bud Crystal for exchange.

Speechless!

Luo Chen took out a top-grade Meridian-Repairing Pill.

Gu Xun did not hesitate and took out a piece of Void Bud Crystal slightly larger than that of the masked martial artist earlier, about the length and thickness of an index finger. Additionally, there were several pieces of Spatial Prism Aperture and Profound Heaven Crystal.

Considering the inequality of this transaction, Gu Xun also added a book.

A book of notes on the precautions needed for cultivation in the Nine Rings of Destruction.

After the transaction, Gu Xun hadn't forgotten the question he had asked earlier.

Luo Chen did not hide anything and directly answered, "I have a gu insect that needs to be fed Void Bud Crystal to grow."

"Raising gu, I don't understand. But since it takes Void Bud Crystal as food, you might also try feeding it Spatial Prism Aperture and Profound Heaven Crystal, which have similar properties. After all, Void Bud Crystal is too rare. I have searched for many years and only gained a little."

Gu Xun casually gave a suggestion.

Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

...

"Fellow Daoist Luo, I look forward to seeing you in the upper three rings one day."

The trading meeting ended, and Gu Xun bid farewell to Luo Chen.

Seeing this, Fang Zhibai, who had come to find Luo Chen, couldn't help but click his tongue in admiration.

"It seems Brother Luo is indeed a piece of gold, shining brightly wherever he goes."

Luo Chen smiled, not caring about such flattery.

He knew that the other party's flattery meant he must have a request.

Fang Zhibai rubbed his hands together. "I also plan to enter the Ninefold Immortal Tower this time to cultivate. To be safe, I need to prepare more healing items. I wonder if Brother Luo could spare one of your Meridian-Repairing Pills. I don't have Void Bud Crystal, but other things..."

"Take it!"

Three Meridian-Repairing Pills were handed to Fang Zhibai.

This left him at a loss for a moment.

What he wanted was only one, and he didn't have a corresponding item to trade. He had only hoped to use his face to exchange for it with other things.

"The stone box you gave me last time is enough to offset three Meridian-Repairing Pills. I, Luo Chen, am not one to forget kindness."

Fang Zhibai was overjoyed. "Thank you, Brother Luo! If there is ever anything you need, I will do my utmost to help."

Luo Chen seized the opportunity. "Earlier, I saw that your family's teleportation formation was arranged with a large amount of Spatial Prism Aperture. I imagine your clan has a surplus of this item. If you could trade some with me, I would be extremely grateful. Of course, I won't ask for it for nothing. I can exchange it for some pill formulas suitable for low-level martial artists."

Fang Zhibai's eyes lit up.

"Before entering the tower, I will make sure Brother Luo is satisfied!"

After a brief exchange, Fang Zhibai hurriedly left.

And behind him, there were still many people who wanted to come up and talk to Luo Chen.

Clearly, it was for the Meridian-Repairing Pill.

But Luo Chen only cupped his hands and declined their kindness.

Without Void Bud Crystal, there was nothing more to say.

Bringing Xiangfei, Luo Chen returned to the courtyard on Yuelong Lane.

After returning home, he took out the items he had traded for and also retrieved the Vacuum Gu.

He had indeed taken Gu Xun's suggestion to heart.

First, the top-grade Profound Heaven Crystal!

This item was not unfamiliar. Back in the Mountains and Seas Realm, whenever he refined magical treasures, Luo Chen would mostly add this material.

Because it was said that Profound Heaven Crystal was debris formed when the world barrier was created at the beginning of heaven and earth.

Thus, it had strong compatibility with most materials.

But unfortunately, the Vacuum Gu merely sniffed this piece of top-grade Profound Heaven Crystal and then did nothing.

Next was the Spatial Prism Aperture.

This mineral material, Luo Chen had also handled many times.

It was a necessary ore for arranging teleportation formations, said to mostly come from deep within the earth's crust.

Faced with the Spatial Prism Aperture, the Vacuum Gu did show some reaction. Its toothless mouth secreted a little liquid, beginning to corrode and devour the Spatial Prism Aperture.

But after just one or two pieces, the Vacuum Gu became impatient. Its body wriggled, moving away from the Spatial Prism Aperture.

This reaction made Luo Chen both surprised and delighted.

Delighted that the Vacuum Gu would indeed eat Spatial Prism Aperture, surprised that it was somewhat picky.

"Have you eaten too much before?"

Luo Chen could roughly guess some things.

In the Southern Frontier of the Mountains and Seas Realm, the Luo Sect, lacking the special material Void Bud Crystal and unwilling to let the Vacuum Gu remain stuck at the fourth grade, had also experimented with feeding it other materials with similar properties.

Spatial Prism Aperture was a relatively easy food to find.

But having eaten too much, the Vacuum Gu had started to get annoyed.

"I'll alternate. I don't have much Void Bud Crystal on hand, and I can't let this little guy starve."

Luo Chen smiled, put all the Void Bud Crystal and Spatial Prism Aperture into a cloth bag, and threw the Vacuum Gu in as well.

Once it finished the Void Bud Crystal, when it got hungry, it would eventually eat the Spatial Prism Aperture.

In the following days, although Wuyou City still held trading meetings from time to time, Luo Chen basically stopped attending.

His reputation had already been spread, and everyone knew his address.

Anyone with Void Bud Crystal would naturally come to him.

That way, he could also avoid competing with others in a bidding frenzy.

Unfortunately, although people occasionally tried to visit him, until half a year later, when it was time to enter the Ninefold Immortal Tower, not a single piece of Void Bud Crystal had been delivered.

This gave Luo Chen a certain sense of frustration.

Holding divine pills and miraculous medicines, yet unable to exchange for what he wanted—this was rare in his several-hundred-year career as an alchemist.

...

In front of the teleportation formation, Luo Chen completed a transaction with Fang Zhibai.

What he gave were a few low-grade pill formulas.

They were simple combinations he had concocted when researching the local specialty medicinal herbs in the Lost Immortal City.

The panel showed they were feasible, and the feedback from low-level martial artists who took the formulas sold by Renjitang was also good.

What Fang Zhibai gave in return was a large amount of Spatial Prism Aperture, enough for the Vacuum Gu to eat for a while.

After handing the pill formulas to his clansmen to take back, Fang Zhibai breathed a sigh of relief.

"Brother Luo, please!"

"Together!"

Luo Chen, along with Xiangfei, stepped onto the teleportation formation, accompanied by other martial artists.

After exiting the formation, ignoring the others who were still adjusting their breathing,

Luo Chen had already walked out of the stone house, his gaze falling upon that towering, cloud-piercing, majestic pagoda.

Thirteen years after creating the *Divine-Demonic Formless*, he had completed the division of his main soul.

Now, in every single acupoint, there resided a relatively weak divided soul.

A full seven hundred and twenty divided souls!

Even if Han Zhan, who had cultivated the *Soul-Splitting Art*, were to be reborn and learn of Luo Chen's actions, he would call it madness.

But Luo Chen was still not satisfied.

The external energy was insufficient, and dietary supplementation was too slow. He needed a more abundant source of energy to fill his acupoints and generate the martial dao gods!

The Ninefold Immortal Tower, the Nine Rings of Destruction.

Luo Chen had been looking forward to this for a long time!
