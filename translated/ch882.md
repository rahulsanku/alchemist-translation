# Chapter 882: The Number One Beneath Ascension

A giant of five hundred zhang stood astride the world, nearly touching heaven and earth with its towering form.

In comparison, that woman was as insignificant as an ant.

Yet, under this extreme contrast in size, it was Luo Chen who retreated.

A man's reputation was like a tree's shadow—no wonder Luo Chen showed weakness.

Although Luo Chen had briefly achieved the feat of fighting three great Demon Emperors at once today, he had ultimately broken them one by one, and even the Bull-Demon had been taken over by his good friend Nine Spirits Lord.

But Azure Frost was different. Back in her day, she had genuinely and single-handedly defeated three great human Grand Cultivators.

Even to this day, when Divine Origin Sage saw her, he would flee without a second thought.

Luo Chen's gaze swept across the empty sky, across the Bull-Demon who had fled thousands of li away, across Nine Spirits Lord who was struggling to rise and staggering backward, and finally landed back on Azure Frost.

"Did this woman descend today for the flower, or for me?"

Luo Chen did not know the answer. He could only wait in silence.

Azure Frost quietly appraised him, a rare hint of appreciation flickering in her eyes.

"You've grown stronger again."

Luo Chen was startled. To receive such praise from the mouth of the number one beneath Ascension?

Indeed!

When he first met Azure Frost, he was merely an early-stage Adept, someone she could crush with a flick of her finger.

The second time he saw Azure Frost was in the Overlord Blade Domain. Gathering the strength of many, Luo Chen had relied on the sharpness of Yuantu to even wound Azure Frost while she was rescuing Thunder Roc.

And this time, right before her eyes, Luo Chen had forcibly burned to death the son of the Ancient Demon Emperor—Huang Man!

To say he had grown stronger was not an exaggeration.

But behind this appreciation lay Luo Chen's terror.

It meant there was a high chance she had come for him!

Luo Chen's voice was hoarse and dry, rumbling like thunder. "Daoist, you have already obtained the rare flower. Is there anything else you wish to instruct?"

Azure Frost calmly stepped forward, walking toward Luo Chen.

"I was already planning to find you. Since we've met today, come with me to Green Parasol Mountain."

Luo Chen's eyes flew wide open. Without a second thought, he threw a punch.

Azure Frost seemed to have anticipated this reflexive reaction from Luo Chen and punched back as well.

Her small, delicate white fist was utterly incomparable to the giant's heaven-pillar-like hand.

Yet, under that single punch, Luo Chen's massive body flew backward like a willow catkin.

In terms of body refinement, Luo Chen was no match for Azure Frost!

But he had never intended to contend with her for supremacy!

Midair, Luo Chen's body rapidly shrank as he borrowed the momentum to fly backward.

Not only that, but he formed a hand seal. In an instant, blazing fire erupted in the air, and between flickering and vanishing, his figure disappeared.

When he reappeared, he was already several li away, followed by another flash of fire.

Azure Frost watched this scene, her calm expression shifting as she raised her slender brows slightly.

She took a single step, and ripples spread through the void like water. When she reappeared, she was already at the spot Luo Chen had just occupied.

Two figures—one flickering with fire, the other crossing the void—vanished from the Medicine King Domain in the blink of an eye.

*Crack!*

Nine Spirits Lord struggled to stand upright, a sense of relief washing over him after surviving a calamity.

He gazed at the direction where Luo Chen and Azure Frost had disappeared, his expression incredibly complex.

*Whoosh...*

A fierce wind stirred.

Lingfengzi appeared abruptly, staring in shock and uncertainty at this battlefield that resembled hell itself.

Mountains of white bones, bottomless pits, a thousand li of scorched earth—utterly devoid of life.

The air still carried a pungent, scorching heat, and in his memory, the scene of wild thunder descending from the heavens was still vivid before his eyes.

In the end, only Nine Spirits Lord remained alone.

He swallowed his saliva. "Where is my senior brother?"

Nine Spirits Lord sighed. "I did my best."

He truly had done his best.

He had been trapped by the formation for two or three days, his mana severely depleted.

To prevent Luo Chen from falling into a deadly siege of three demons, he had forcibly activated a secret technique to take over the Bull-Demon.

If the Bull-Demon hadn't been scared off by Azure Frost, and if Azure Frost's eyes had been fixed only on the Accumulated Void Flower and Luo Chen, he would likely be a corpse by now.

But Lingfengzi didn't care about any of that. He only asked one thing.

"Where is Luo Chen?"

...

The six eastern domains were not arranged in a straight line.

They were distributed in a staggered pattern, each with its own borders.

If the Jade Cauldron Domain was taken as a reference point, the Divine Talisman Domain was the farthest, while the nearest neighboring domain was the Joyous Union Domain.

The Medicine King Domain was located in the central area, and it was precisely this unique geographical advantage that had built the wealthiest Medicine King Sect among the six domains back in the day.

Luo Chen fled from the Medicine King Sect, using his self-created Fire Escape Art to travel ten thousand li in a single burst.

Crossing the vast Joyous Union Domain took him only half a day.

If this escape speed were made known, ordinary Sages would likely drop their jaws in shock.

But Luo Chen still felt it wasn't enough.

*Chi...*

A flash of fire appeared, and Luo Chen stumbled as he fell from the air.

But in an instant, he stabilized himself with his powerful physique.

"Woo woo..."

Eerie, mournful cries came from all around.

As far as the eye could see, the earth was dark and gloomy, like the Nine Netherworld.

"Have I reached the Jade Cauldron Domain?"

The Jade Cauldron Domain of the past—the smallest in area, the weakest in strength, and the first to be attacked by the demon army.

Now, it had long since become a land of ghosts!

Especially after more than a hundred years of development, this place was completely unsuitable for human cultivation.

The moment the aura of a living person appeared, a large number of low-level ghosts, condensed from yin energy, rushed over.

But before they could even get close to Luo Chen, they were crushed into the most primitive ghost qi by the turbulent mana around his body.

Luo Chen paid no attention to these ghostly creatures. Instead, he turned his head with a ferocious expression.

The void was utterly still, as if nothing was there.

But he knew that demoness was still chasing him!

"Invite me to Green Parasol Mountain?"

"I will never go to that place again!"

Luo Chen's heart hardened. He took out another jar of Golden Wind and Jade Dew and began to replenish his mana in large quantities.

He stepped forward, shooting through the air like an arrow.

When his mana had recovered to a certain level, he recklessly activated the Fire Escape Art again, trying to widen the distance between himself and Azure Frost.

However, the gap in escape speed between the two was as unimaginable as that between Luo Chen and an ordinary Sage.

Just as Luo Chen was about to change direction toward Tianfan City, his expression suddenly changed, and he slashed to the left with his sword.

*Clang!*

A pure white fist suddenly appeared in the void, flashing and then vanishing.

Yuantu was unharmed, but Luo Chen's right arm could not withstand the tremendous force. Bones cracked and popped, and flesh exploded.

*Chi...*

A flash of fire appeared again, and Luo Chen vanished from the spot.

Azure Frost emerged from the void, her figure slowly materializing.

She looked at her hand, where a faint white sword mark remained.

"That sword..."

She murmured to herself, then chased after Luo Chen, who had changed direction.

The two pursued and fled, crossing hundreds of thousands of li.

When the second night of the third day arrived, Luo Chen landed on a patch of wasteland, his face deathly pale.

His face was pale from the unrestrained refinement of Golden Wind and Jade Dew.

The surrounding environment was white—the reflection of moonlight on a snowy ground.

Snowy ground?

Luo Chen looked around, a look of surprise appearing on his face.

This was the Great Snowy Mountains!

Without realizing it, he had been chased to this familiar place.

Or rather, it wasn't that he had fled in panic, but that Azure Frost had been controlling his escape route all along, deliberately forcing him toward Green Parasol Mountain.

"I can't run anymore!"

Luo Chen dared not use the Fire Escape Art again. This escape technique, which he had recently created, was not yet mastered.

Activating it consumed mana at a terrifying rate.

If he used it again, his Sage core would likely collapse after just a few more times.

Facing the imminent arrival of Azure Frost, Luo Chen had already formed a plan in his heart.

"I still have the strength to fight!"

He sat cross-legged on the ground, placing Yuantu across his knees. He took out another jar of Golden Wind and Jade Dew wine and, suppressing his instinctive revulsion, gulped down more than half of it.

A vast amount of spiritual energy instantly burst forth, nearly causing Luo Chen to faint.

Even with his powerful physique, after so many instances of unrestrained refinement of spiritual wine, his body had begun to automatically reject it.

But he couldn't care about that anymore!

This batch of spiritual energy would be the final fuel for him to activate the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame once more.

And how was he to withstand Azure Frost's frontal assault?

Luo Chen took a deep breath, and the water-drop pattern between his brows grew increasingly clear.

*Hum...*

Ripples spread through the void.

A woman in green robes stepped out calmly, her gaze falling on the ragged man bathed in moonlight on the snowy ground.

Finally stopped running?

...

Black water surrounded the continent, flowing so calmly that one could barely feel the movement of the liquid.

A terrifying abyss lay at the edge of the land, forming an insurmountable chasm for both humans and demons.

Within this Nether Water Abyss lay the strongest sect in the Eastern Wilderness—the Nether Origin Sect!

At this moment, in an ancient hall, three figures sat in a triangular formation.

In the center was a pool of black water, like liquid mercury.

Within the pool, a green light continuously surged, only to be constantly suppressed.

Among the three figures, the youngest spoke with the most world-weary tone.

"The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect controls the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, which is why their talents emerge in abundance and their Ascendants never cease. If we can refine this Green Grain Immortal and merge her into the Nether Origin Sacred Land, it will surely become one of the sect's greatest foundations. Then, our sect can emerge eastward, dominate the Eastern Wilderness, and contend with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

"Senior Brother Yuming speaks truly. Although the Green Grain Immortal is not as powerful as the ancient Great Desolate Mirage Dragon, she is still alive and in her complete original form. Refining her will not only benefit the sect immensely but also allow us to break through to the late Ascendant stage, leave this realm, and seek the Great Dao of longevity."

Rumors in the world said that cultivators who reached Ascension could ascend immediately.

However, these rumors were largely untrue.

The Ascendant stage could indeed achieve ascension with a radiant cloud, but it was ultimately not stable enough. Only at the late Ascendant stage could one receive the greatest support from the world's spiritual mechanism.

But in the Mountains and Seas Realm, the best spiritual veins were only of the fifth rank.

For Ascendant cultivators to use them for daily cultivation was fine, but to advance further was exceedingly difficult.

Yet cultivators were incredibly resourceful. Over time, they had devised many methods.

Humans were not the favored children of this world. The Mountains and Seas Realm's greatest affection was for those ignorant and unenlightened desolate beasts.

Thus, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had suppressed the corpse of the Great Desolate Mirage Dragon as their sect's foundation to enhance their spiritual veins. They had also built the Heaven-Earth Peak, absorbing a large number of elderly Sage cultivators, using their breakthroughs or deaths to draw in the world's spiritual mechanism.

In the present age, Great Desolate beasts were hard to find, most having retreated into the barbaric wilderness.

The barbaric lands were dangerous even for great powers to enter lightly, so the Ascendant sacred grounds like the Nether Origin Sect found it difficult to imitate the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's methods.

But they had found another way, also devising a method to enhance the foundation of their spiritual land.

If they couldn't use Great Desolate beasts, what about sentient beings?

Desolate beasts were the darlings of heaven and earth, and weren't the spirits born from plants, wood, metal, and stone the same?

This Green Grain Immortal was an extremely rare plant-based sentient being in the Mountains and Seas Realm, one that had even advanced to the fifth rank.

If she were refined into the Nether Origin spiritual land, she would continuously absorb vast amounts of the world's spiritual mechanism, greatly benefiting Ascendant cultivators in comprehending the true meaning of laws.

Therefore, after dealing with the Ancient Demon Emperor, the three Nether Origin Sect Ancestors had stopped caring about the human-demon war and returned to the Nether Origin Sect with most of their disciples, focusing all their efforts on refining the Green Grain Immortal.

At this moment, the three Nether Origin Sect Ancestors were refining the Green Grain Immortal while chatting cheerfully, full of hope for the future.

Suddenly!

Black Marsh Ancestor frowned, and his refining movements slowed for a moment.

Yuming Ancestor, who was presiding over the grand formation, immediately sensed the anomaly.

"Junior Brother, what's wrong?"

Black Marsh Ancestor formed a hand seal with a look of surprise, and the result confirmed his speculation.

"The protective divine ability I left on Luo Chen has been activated."

At these words, the other two found it strange.

Yuming Ancestor asked, "You mean he has encountered an enemy he cannot resist and was forced to use an Ascendant-level method?"

Heaven Abyss Ancestor was curious. "When I briefly observed that Alchemy Lord at Azure Bixiao Mountain, he had deep mana and a solid foundation. Few of his peers could match him. Now that he has the Nether Origin Sect's reputation to protect him, who would dare to make a move against him?"

Black Marsh Ancestor shook his head, his expression somewhat grave.

"Regardless of who it is, Luo Chen is the seedling I have chosen for the next Alchemy Saint. Only he has the potential to refine a fifth-rank divine pill to aid our cultivation."

"The Alchemy Saint was once lured away from the Nether Origin Sect by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

"Luo Chen absolutely cannot have any accidents!"

As he spoke, he was about to stop his actions.

Yuming Ancestor frowned. "Refining the Green Grain Immortal is the top priority. It has been less than eighteen years—how can we easily withdraw?"

Black Marsh Ancestor was troubled. "But..."

Heaven Abyss Ancestor smiled. "No matter. Let my incarnation make a trip. Junior Brother, lend me Luo Chen's aura!"

The three Nether Origin Sect Ancestors each had their own specialties.

Heaven Abyss Ancestor was most skilled in the path of external incarnations. When they had captured the Green Grain Immortal before, it hadn't relied solely on Thunder Daozi and the others.

Black Marsh Ancestor breathed a sigh of relief. "With Senior Brother's incarnation making a move, there should be no problem."

He flicked his finger and retrieved the trace of Luo Chen's aura he had secretly captured back in the Heavenly Water Hall. With his divine ability as a guide, finding Luo Chen's location was not a major issue.

Heaven Abyss Ancestor received Luo Chen's aura and, with a thought, cast it out of the hall.

The quietly flowing Nether Water Abyss stirred up waves.

The Nether Origin Sect disciples responsible for guarding the abyss were stunned, not understanding why the sect's barrier, which had remained unchanged for thousands of years, had suddenly moved.

The next moment, they saw a figure slowly emerge from the abyss.

He inhaled toward the direction of "Heavenly Nether Palace," as if swallowing something, then, with a splash, shot up into the sky and left the Nether Origin Sacred Land.

Only then did the Nether Origin Sect disciples realize that this was the incarnation their Ancestor had painstakingly cultivated.

But the external war was gradually subsiding. What could be worth the Ancestor's incarnation making a trip?

...

*Bang!*

After a thunderous impact, Azure Frost stood still, unmoving.

She slowly withdrew her fist, looking at the man enveloped in a blue water shield before her, a hint of disgust appearing on her face.

"It's the technique of that person who invaded Green Parasol Mountain back then. Is this your trump card?"

Luo Chen did not answer. Even with the protective divine ability of Heavenly One Water, every punch and kick from the other party contained terrifying force.

Under that immense power, his most prideful body refinement realm could only barely hold on.

If not for the years of persistently drinking the thousand-year Five Temple Immortal Brew to temper his body, even with the protection of a divine ability personally bestowed by an Ascendant great power, the backlash force would have been more than his internal organs could bear.

Inside his body, waves of force were still vibrating, trying to dismantle his flesh.

He couldn't suppress them!

A fierce look flashed across Luo Chen's face. Instead of retreating, he advanced.

He did not transform into a several-hundred-zhang true body, nor did he even assume his usual nine-zhang form.

Before Azure Frost, any advantage brought by size simply did not exist. It would only make him a living target.

In comparison, agility was far more important.

Luo Chen threw a punch, and Azure Frost, without dodging or avoiding, punched back as well.

It seemed like a head-on collision, but halfway through his punch, Luo Chen twisted his waist and spun, kicking out like a storm.

Azure Frost's lips curled slightly as she kicked out her long leg as well.

*Bang! Bang! Bang!*

Amidst the successive collisions, a furious roar erupted as Black King lunged in for a bite.

Azure Frost reached out, grabbed Black King, and pulled with both hands.

*Rip...*

Black King was torn in two, falling lifelessly to the ground.

Luo Chen couldn't spare a thought for Black King's condition. Stepping with Moon Step, he circled behind Azure Frost and struck out with his palm.

An illusion shattered. Luo Chen's palm had missed.

Before he could react, the void behind him trembled, and a finger gently tapped his back.

*Thump!*

Just one finger, and Luo Chen staggered.

The source power circulating within him still couldn't dispel the layers of surging force.

Luo Chen's expression turned ugly. He struck back with his elbow, but missed again.

His pale golden eyes swept across the void, but he could not find Azure Frost's trace anywhere.

Suddenly, a sound of breaking air came from above his head.

Luo Chen raised his sword and thrust upward.

*Clang!*

Under the crushing force, Luo Chen plummeted to the ground.

Azure Frost stood in the void, supported by neither mana nor white clouds or gentle breeze. She simply stood there, utterly ordinary.

Looking down at Luo Chen, she shook her head.

"Although you have grown a lot, you can't hit me, and you can't move me. Resistance is ultimately futile."

On the ground, Luo Chen stood up in a sorry state.

In his entire life, he had never felt so powerless.

Even back then, when Azure Frost had captured him with her Sleeve of Heaven and Earth without him even knowing, his feelings hadn't been this deep.

Only now, when his cultivation was gradually catching up to Azure Frost's, did he realize just how vast the gap between them truly was.

No wonder even someone as strong as Divine Origin Sage had to run at the sight of Azure Frost.

No wonder Ling Tian Pass had to rely on a grand formation to deter Azure Frost from attacking.

No wonder the Nether Origin Sect had to personally dispatch an Ascendant Ancestor before daring to set foot on Green Parasol Mountain.

"So this is the number one beneath Ascension?"

Luo Chen wiped the blood from the corner of his mouth and looked up. Azure Frost had vanished again.

Concealing herself in the void, appearing and disappearing unpredictably.

Even a cultivator's divine sense could not lock onto her.

Relying on nothing but a pair of bare fists, she was invincible.

Luo Chen wished his eyes could not only break illusions but also see through space, allowing him to perceive spatial fluctuations.

At least then he wouldn't be so passive.

But the battle left him no time to think.

*Bang!*

A fist suddenly appeared from the left.

The blue light shield on Luo Chen's body began to visibly collapse inward.

Azure Frost appeared again, but this time it wasn't just a single punch. Both her hands gripped the edges of the light shield, radiating white light.

She was going to tear apart the protective divine ability left by that person back then!

She had the confidence to do so!

She also didn't want to waste any more time with Luo Chen!

But at this moment, she saw the madness in Luo Chen's eyes.

*Whoosh!!!*

A cyan-white flame suddenly erupted, surging outward from Luo Chen as the center.

Azure Frost was caught off guard, and a wisp of the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame touched her.

But in an instant, she retreated. Luo Chen's sword slash missed again.

Staggering, Luo Chen fell to one knee. His mana was completely empty, and his source power could no longer circulate.

Ripples undulated, and Azure Frost appeared once more.

She looked at her scorched black left hand, a hint of admiration on her face, but also a trace of regret.

"It's gone, isn't it!"
