# Chapter 856: Boundless Grace, Hidden Amidst Layered Clouds

"Master's wife, you summoned me?"

In a newly tidied cave dwelling, Qu Lingjun bowed respectfully to Sima Huiniang.

Sima Huiniang looked at Qu Lingjun, whose aged features made him appear even older than herself, and nodded slightly.

"Foundation Establishment eighth layer, just one step away from the ninth. It seems you haven't let your master's expectations down. You haven't slacked off all these years."

Qu Lingjun smiled sheepishly. "It's all thanks to the resources bestowed by my master that I've been able to advance so quickly."

Sima Huiniang hummed in acknowledgment and asked, "Have you settled on a cave dwelling?"

"Yes, it's already decided. Qin'er is very familiar with Qingping Mountain and specially selected a natural weak third-order spirit vein for me—most suitable for my current cultivation."

"And the cultivation of the *Fine Dust Primordial Art*?"

"With the cultivation insights personally written by my master, I've already achieved minor success in this art. I have hope of reaching intermediate success before I reach Foundation Establishment Great Perfection."

"Intermediate success is already considered entering the hall!" Sima Huiniang praised. Back when she was at the Foundation Establishment stage, she had only barely achieved minor success. Let alone great success—even intermediate success had been beyond her reach.

In truth, this had been the norm before Luo Chen's return.

The *Fine Dust Primordial Art* he had personally created during his Adept stage had not been refined enough. The version he left behind had an extremely high cultivation threshold, and few in the Luo Tian Sect could even enter its gates.

It was only after Wang Yuan remembered Luo Chen's instructions before his departure and continuously revised and simplified the *Fine Dust Primordial Art* that it gradually became suitable for Foundation Establishment cultivators to learn.

Even so, being able to cultivate this simplified version of the *Fine Dust Primordial Art* to minor success was of tremendous help in breaking through to the Adept stage.

This was also why the Luo Tian Sect had been able to produce Adepts in such numbers.

Of course!

All these problems were instantly resolved after Luo Chen returned to the Eastern Wilderness.

In his hundred years in the Northern Sea, he had already cultivated the *Fine Dust Primordial Art* to the Great Perfection level. From a higher realm, he had completely revised the art, making it far more suitable for Foundation Establishment and Adept cultivators to learn.

Coupled with his personal cultivation insights, those who studied the art could now avoid many detours and find it much easier to enter its gates.

Now, within the Luo Tian Sect, including Qu Lingjun, Yao Mingyue, and others, almost everyone was cultivating the new art Luo Chen had brought back.

Even someone with unremarkable aptitude like Qu Lingjun dared to claim he could cultivate this art to intermediate success. Those with better talent would surely ascend even higher.

This was probably the meaning of "the predecessors plant trees, and the later generations enjoy the shade"!

Sima Huiniang suddenly asked, "Then, before your lifespan runs out, do you have confidence in breaking through to the Adept stage?"

Faced with this question, Qu Lingjun's previously decent mood visibly sank.

He stammered without much confidence, "I should be able to... even if not, I will attempt to break through once."

For someone with a four-spirit-root aptitude, the difficulty of cultivation was something outsiders could never truly understand.

Even with the vast resources Luo Chen had provided, in the face of his dwindling lifespan and ever-weakening qi and blood, Qu Lingjun could not make a solemn guarantee.

Sima Huiniang looked pityingly at this old man who lacked confidence.

"It was those years in the Luo Tian Sect that held you back."

Qu Lingjun jerked his head up. "Master's wife, how can you say that? Without the Luo Tian Sect, without my master, let alone the Adept stage—even the Foundation Establishment stage would have been a luxury for me. How can you say the sect held me back!"

It was as if he had been triggered.

Sima Huiniang smiled wryly, then pulled out a small wine flask.

"Take this. Your master specifically instructed me to give it to you before he left."

Qu Lingjun was curious. "What is this?"

"Longevity Spring Water. The name explains its effects. Your body, with its constantly weakening qi and blood, cannot withstand great tonics. After you take it, remember to drink little and often."

As an alchemist, Qu Lingjun naturally understood the meaning of "drink little and often"—roughly the same as eating small meals frequently.

But such a heaven-defying treasure, his master had specially left it for him?

He said in panic, "How can such a precious treasure be wasted on a disciple of lowly aptitude? It should be enjoyed by my master and master's wife."

"You do have filial piety. But have you forgotten your master's heaven-granted talent? He achieved Sagehood at just over two hundred years old. Where would he need such lifespan-extending items?"

Sima Huiniang scolded him with a laugh and stuffed the flask into the flustered Qu Lingjun's arms.

"Take it! Even your master isn't sure exactly how much lifespan this flask can extend. You're an alchemist too—consider it helping your master test the medicine."

Qu Lingjun opened his mouth, but no words came out. He silently held the flask in both hands, the corners of his eyes even growing moist.

That man who had been cold and distant to him since his return, who had barely met with him a few times—yet the things he sent were better each time.

He was but a worthless disciple. What had he done to deserve such indulgence?

Qu Lingjun asked in a choked voice, "Where is my master? I want to see him before I go into seclusion."

The smile on Sima Huiniang's face gradually faded.

"Cultivate well. When you form your core, he will naturally see you."

Qu Lingjun clenched his fists. The lack of confidence from before was now replaced by a lone courage.

He *must* succeed in forming his core!

If only to stand before his master and tell him that his disciple had not let him down!

After Qu Lingjun left, a sigh echoed through the cave dwelling.

Sima Huiniang's expression was lost. Where Luo Chen was now, she actually did not know.

But she knew that Luo Chen's deliberate departure was not an abandonment of the Luo Tian Sect—it was to protect it.

Not telling her his whereabouts was also to protect her.

"Is it because of Emperor Slaughter?"

---

On the Great Azure Mountain.

Emperor Slaughter sat in the Luo Tian Grand Hall, lonely and cold. There was no trace of the liveliness that had filled the hall not long ago.

A hint of eagerness flickered in his eyes.

Luo Chen had told him to guard the Great Azure Mountain but had taken the Luo Tian Sect cultivators away.

Wasn't this giving him a chance to escape?

As long as he returned to his father's side, with his father's abilities, he could completely remove those slave seal restrictions while protecting his main soul.

At that time, he would definitely spend a fortune to invite several commanders to come secretly to Fenghua Domain and grind Luo Chen's bones to dust!

No!

How could grinding his bones to dust quench his hatred?

He would also collect his soul blood, place a slave seal, take Luo Chen as a slave, and torment him day by day!

Just as his expression was gradually turning ferocious, a faint voice suddenly entered his ears.

"Do you want to run?"

It was Luo Chen!

In an instant, Emperor Slaughter's ferocious expression vanished, replaced by one of fearful deference.

"Master, this lowly one would never dare!"

"Heh heh..."

A light laugh, then silence returned.

Emperor Slaughter looked around, spreading his divine sense, but could never find Luo Chen's trace.

Not even a shadow, not even a wisp of aura.

But in the depths of his being, he could sense the other party watching him.

For a moment, Emperor Slaughter was nearly driven mad.

He could tell that Luo Chen was wary of his ancient demon father.

But if he wouldn't let him go, wouldn't kill him, and wouldn't let him see anyone—just making him guard this broken-down Great Azure Mountain—what was the point?

And why wasn't he going to cultivate, instead hiding here and wasting time with him?

---

Deep within the earth veins of Hundred Li Qingchuan.

A figure shrouded in a black robe sat cross-legged.

The figure was somewhat thin, nothing like the tall and upright Luo Chen.

But if Emperor Slaughter saw this person, he would definitely mistake him for Luo Chen.

Only because this person's flesh and blood were Luo Chen's, and his soul was Luo Chen's soul.

The flesh was Luo Chen, the soul was Luo Chen—so who could say this person was not Luo Chen?

But in truth, he was merely a puppet personally refined by Luo Chen—Jin San!

And the soul contained within was only Luo Chen's third soul.

The puppet slowly withdrew its gaze. With him watching here, Emperor Slaughter would not dare to escape.

Even if he tried, he could be killed with a single thought.

For someone as afraid of death as Emperor Slaughter, this was an action he would never dare to attempt.

The puppet shook its head and turned its attention to absorbing spiritual energy to nourish its body.

The fourth-order spirit acupoint that had been painstakingly gathered in Hundred Li Qingchuan had been shattered by the great battle a few days ago.

But that didn't mean the puppet couldn't absorb spiritual energy!

The fall of two demon emperors had released the vast dharma power they had accumulated through a lifetime of cultivation.

After this power leaked out, while it had disrupted the spiritual energy of Fenghua Domain, it had also added a great deal of spiritual energy to Fenghua Domain.

The puppet was not like a living person—it wasn't so refined. It could just absorb in great gulps!

Before closing its eyes, the puppet thought to itself: "Finally arrived at Dieyun Domain!"

---

Dieyun Domain!

Once the cultivation grounds of the great sect Dieyun Sect.

At its peak, it had three Sage cultivators sitting within, maintained good relations with the Nether Origin Sect externally, and with its spirit wine sold far and wide, Dieyun Domain had been one of the most prosperous places in the Eastern Wilderness.

But a single great battle had caused Dieyun Domain to decline instantly.

It wasn't to the point of ten thousand miles of scorched earth—after all, the demon emperor led by Splendid Immortal had struck directly at Dieyun Sect's mountain gate.

But saying that nine out of ten homes were empty was not an exaggeration.

A hazy shadow drifted through the high sky.

Looking down at the sparsely populated scene below, it was clear this was the consequence of Lingtian Pass forcibly conscripting the remaining forces of Dieyun Domain.

"Good as well. Without strong cultivators sitting in, it's more suitable for me to hide."

The shadow muttered to itself and accelerated its flight.

Not long after, it stopped outside the mountain gate of Dieyun Sect, which was now guarded only by a few Foundation Establishment cultivators and some Qi Refinement cultivators.

It looked down at the giant pit outside Dieyun Sect's mountain gate. Somehow, it had turned a snowy white.

He remembered it had been a pitch-black pit back then?

Luo Chen focused his gaze and immediately understood the reason for the change.

So much time had passed. The giant pit, several li in circumference, had been filled with rainwater, forming a lake.

And since the season had reached the depths of winter, the extreme cold had frozen it solid, hence the snowy white.

From this, one could also see to what extent Dieyun Sect had declined. They couldn't even maintain the formation that regulated the climate and temperature to cover the area before their mountain gate.

Luo Chen sighed with emotion. Without alerting any of Dieyun Sect's mountain-guarding cultivators, he quietly entered the mountain gate.

Following the memory route from when he had investigated the battlefield situation, Luo Chen moved stealthily all the way.

Finally, he stopped before a great mountain with an extremely rich concentration of spiritual energy.

Flowing Cloud Mountain!

The former cultivation grounds of Dieyun Sect's supreme elder, Sage Flowing Cloud.

And also the best-preserved fourth-order spirit vein location in Dieyun Sect!

Luo Chen's divine sense swept over Flowing Cloud Mountain in an instant. He only found a few low-level cultivators doing sweeping and snow-clearing work on the periphery, along with an elderly Adept cultivator sitting in a third-order cave dwelling.

"The Nether Origin Sect didn't go too far. They left an Adept cultivator to guard the mountain gate for Dieyun Sect."

Muttering to himself, Luo Chen went straight into Flowing Cloud Mountain.

Wherever he passed, there was no obstruction, but strangely, no one discovered him.

When he passed by the cave dwelling of that elderly Adept, he found the man sleeping soundly, his face flushed red, reeking of alcohol.

With a light laugh, Luo Chen finally entered the cave dwelling that had once belonged to Sage Flowing Cloud.

Those maze-like illusion formations were like nothing before his illusion-breaking eyes.

Contrary to the golden splendor or immortal aura he had imagined, this cave dwelling seemed somewhat shabby in Luo Chen's eyes.

There were only a few spacious rooms haphazardly carved out, without even isolation formations arranged.

But when he opened the cultivation chamber, the surging spiritual energy proved that this was indeed a genuine Sage's cultivation grounds!

"I've heard that Sage Flowing Cloud had a carefree personality and was slovenly in his habits. Looking at his dwelling now, it fits perfectly."

Luo Chen muttered as he inspected the spirit-gathering formation here.

After a while, he nodded in satisfaction.

That surprise attack that had killed Dieyun Sect's three Sages and collapsed their mountain gate had not completely destroyed Dieyun Sect's mountain gate spirit veins.

In fact, in his view, the degree of damage was not as severe as at Fenghua Palace.

This was probably related to Dieyun Sect's mountain-protecting grand formation.

When Luo Chen had investigated the battlefield situation back then, he had already made a very accurate judgment.

The mountain-protecting grand formation Dieyun Sect had arranged did not rely excessively on earth veins like other sects. Instead, it incorporated a large number of cloud restrictions.

Therefore, when the grand formation was broken, the endless cloud layers that had shrouded Dieyun Sect dissipated completely, but the mountain gate's spirit grounds were preserved.

Of course, this was not without consequences.

That was, after the large number of clouds that had been drawn in by the formation collapsed, Dieyun Domain experienced several months of torrential rain. The ice lake at the mountain gate was an example.

"I can only say that this benefits me now."

Luo Chen took out formation-arranging treasures and began to meticulously arrange them in the cave dwelling, outside the cultivation chamber.

He did not touch the original illusion formations that protected the cave dwelling, only arranging them within the dwelling.

Thus, outsiders would neither know that this place had gained a new master, nor would Luo Chen be unaware if someone entered the cave dwelling.

After everything was ready, Luo Chen stepped into the cultivation chamber.

Only then did he shed the heavy disguises he had worn in front of others, revealing a rare look of weakness.

Looking inward at his Purple Mansion, his Sage infant was listless, its chubby little face drooping.

Any Sage cultivator would immediately recognize this condition.

Dharma power exhaustion!

If it went further, he would have to consume the foundational dharma power that condensed his Sage infant.

Once that most fundamental dharma power was gone, his Sage infant might even collapse, and his entire cultivation realm would plummet!

The cause of such consequences was naturally the great battle at Hundred Li Qingchuan a few days ago.

Simultaneously activating his lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, the Greater Five Elements Sword Formation, and arranging the Triple Prison. Driving the Yuantu Blood Sword to slay enemies and destroy demons. Fourth-order spells and illusion techniques emerging in endless succession, exerted with full force... Each and every one of these was a great means that consumed dharma power.

Especially the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, which had instantly drained his remaining dharma power!

Others saw him looking as usual, his bearing unchanged, but it was actually his powerful physical body that concealed his weakness.

The truth was that when he had struck out with the first Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, his dharma power had already been exhausted.

If the Great Demon Emperor Seven Rhinoceros had not been frightened away and had attacked again, Luo Chen's weakness would have been exposed without a doubt.

In these past few days, Luo Chen had frequently shown his face without ever having a chance to recover his dharma power. Only now did he have the opportunity.

"All kinds of preparations before the battle, yet I forgot that I too could have a day when my dharma power runs out."

Luo Chen smiled bitterly, a faint trace of lingering fear in his heart.

In truth, even during battle, he had methods to replenish his dharma power when it was exhausted.

And that method originated from the very Dieyun Sect where he now was.

Back then, Black King had found a large batch of spirit wine, including both the thousand-year Five Temple Immortal Brew that Luo Chen valued most, and a type of spirit wine called Golden Wind and Jade Dew.

A single sip of that stuff could temporarily replenish a large amount of dharma power.

But Luo Chen had always prided himself on his deep dharma power. After countless battles, he had rarely experienced dharma power exhaustion, so he had subconsciously overlooked it.

After executing the first Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, Luo Chen had suddenly awakened to the fact that his dharma power could also run out.

But with the Great Demon Emperor Seven Rhinoceros right before him, how could he disgracefully take out Golden Wind and Jade Dew to replenish his dharma power?

Wouldn't that be showing weakness?

So he had bluffed, forming hand seals and pinching his fingers, pretending to strike out with a second Withering-Bloom Dao Finger.

If Seven Rhinoceros had really charged up, that second Withering-Bloom Dao Finger would definitely not have been released. Luo Chen would have wisely chosen to retreat in panic, drink spirit wine to replenish his dharma power, and then fight to the death.

Perhaps he could have driven back Seven Rhinoceros, but the scene would not have been as impressive.

However, the bluff was not without its benefits!

Luo Chen raised his right hand and once again formed hand seals and pinched his fingers, drawing on the surrounding spiritual energy.

In an instant, the already abundant spiritual energy in the cultivation chamber became violent.

Luo Chen narrowed his eyes, staring intently at his fingertip.

Tiny vortices began to form.

"Executing the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger in a state of dharma power exhaustion can actually manipulate the origin qi of heaven and earth?"

(End of chapter)
