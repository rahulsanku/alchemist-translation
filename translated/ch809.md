# Chapter 809: Re-encountering the Thunder Prison Roc, the Eastern Wilderness Demon Emperor Knocks at the Gate

In the azure void.

Seven figures stood shoulder to shoulder.

Surging demonic aura swirled and flowed around them.

Occasionally, sky insects flew nearby. These creatures, usually reckless and unafraid, saw the imposing aura of the group and their eyes also cleared with caution, all of them retreating.

The leader had a rhinoceros horn growing from his head, his figure tall and imposing.

He gazed down at the vast sea of clouds below, as if he could see through the layers to that ancient city crouching upon the earth, a city that had stood across the divide between humans and demons for millennia.

"Humans are skilled in formations, linking with the great momentum of heaven and earth, wielding infinite power."

"If we attack head-on, even with the strength of us seven demons, it may not be enough to break through."

"Therefore, we will need everyone to strike together, drawing away the power of the surrounding formations, and help me break through in one blow!"

The Seven Rhinoceros Commander's gaze swept over the six demons behind him. Four were temporarily under his command, while the other two he had invited through personal connections.

"Cheng Guan, Lu Hao, you attack the North City and draw their attention for me!"

"Yes, Commander!"

"Wu Guang, You Ying, you are skilled in combined attack techniques. The South City is yours."

"Rest assured. We heard that only a single Carefree Crane Sage guards the South City. The two of us can break it easily."

Then, the Seven Rhinoceros Commander turned his gaze to the remaining two demons.

"The East City Gate is personally guarded by Qin Baixiang of the Myriad Beast Sect. His cultivation level is average, but he has a fourth-rank spirit beast protecting him. I have arranged for Dark Cry to ambush him, but that is still not enough. Fellow Daoist Shacha Mo, I must trouble you to lend a hand there."

The demon emperor named Shacha Mo smiled and nodded, seemingly not too worried.

However, he had the confidence to be so arrogant.

By human cultivation standards, his fourth-rank mid-stage level was comparable to a mid-stage Nascent Soul Sage. Dealing with an enemy of the same level, especially in a two-on-one fight, was hardly a problem.

As for that so-called fourth-rank spirit beast, he didn't even consider it worth a glance.

Beasts tamed by humans had long lost their wild nature and were nothing to fear!

In his ears came the polite words of the Seven Rhinoceros Commander, but Shacha Mo's gaze was wary as it fell upon the last figure.

Just standing there, he was a full two or three *zhang* tall!

A black robe, originally quite loose, was wrapped around his body but was stretched taut by his muscles, clinging like a tight-fitting shirt.

His cheeks were long and narrow, his eyes pure white, with only a single golden speck in the pupils.

Looking closely, that whiteness was not skin color at all, but countless flickering arcs of electricity.

Thunder Prison!

His cultivation was at the fourth-rank mid-stage, and he possessed the bloodline of the desolate beast, the Thunder Roc.

These things were not unusual. In this realm, any top-tier demon emperor had some kind of background or origin.

But it was rumored that he had once fought for hundreds of rounds against a late-stage Nascent Soul human grand cultivator.

Though severely wounded, he had ultimately managed to survive.

After that battle, the name of Thunder Prison was known to every demon and unknown to none among the top powerhouses of the Million Great Mountains.

Some demon emperors even believed that Thunder Prison fully possessed the strength of a Nine Great Commander level!

Unexpectedly, the Seven Rhinoceros Commander had managed to invite even this one.

"Thunder Prison, there's no need for pleasantries between us. Of the four main gates of Lingtian Pass, the West Gate, located at the rear, has the weakest defenses and is relatively the easiest to breach. I leave this place to you alone!"

Thunder Prison looked down at the crowd from his great height, his narrow cheeks and slightly protruding mouth forming a cruel grin.

He cracked his knuckles, the joints popping and crackling.

"I've been nursing my wounds for a hundred years and haven't made a move. Today, it's time to stretch my limbs."

The Seven Rhinoceros Commander thought for a moment, then still cautioned him: "The identity of the guarding Sage at that location is unclear, but his divine sense strength is roughly at the mid-stage Nascent Soul level. Human powerhouses are adept at borrowing external forces and have an endless array of tricks. You must also be careful."

Hearing the words "human powerhouses are adept at borrowing external forces and have an endless array of tricks," a fierce look suddenly appeared on Thunder Prison's face.

"You don't need to remind me. I know better than anyone!"

In that battle a hundred years ago, countless demon beasts only thought it was glorious enough that he had survived under a human grand cultivator.

Only he knew just how thoroughly he had been suppressed in that fight.

Moreover, the sheer number of techniques that old man possessed had exceeded anything he had ever seen in his life.

The phrase "endless array of tricks" was truly the perfect description.

*Crack!*

A sound of thunder came from the clouds below.

The Seven Rhinoceros Commander took a deep breath.

"It's time!"

...

At the East City Gate.

Qin Baixiang stood upon the city wall, letting the wild wind whip his robes noisily.

Ever since he had received the news brought back by Luo Chen of the Alchemy Sect, he would personally come up to check from time to time, to prevent any unexpected incidents.

Especially today, he felt a constant unease in his heart.

Suddenly, his gaze sharpened.

In the distance, dust rose abruptly.

Several beams of light landed at the foot of the city wall.

Before they could even enter the city, Qin Baixiang sent a voice transmission over.

"What happened over there?"

Below was a patrol squad that had just returned. They looked up and saw Qin Baixiang's figure, and their panicked hearts calmed down a little.

"Reporting to the Sage, the Fire Ridge has been breached. Some fellow daoists managed to escape. However, a large number of demon beasts are still pursuing them. Our patrol squad alone cannot wipe them out, so we have specifically returned to the city to request reinforcements."

The Fire Ridge has been breached?

Qin Baixiang frowned, and his divine sense surged outward.

Soon, among the crowd a hundred *li* away, he detected a faint, elusive aura.

"Fellow Daoist Gaoling is still alive, but his condition seems not good!"

The owner of that aura also felt Qin Baixiang's unrestrained divine sense and quickly spoke: "Is that Fellow Daoist Qin of the Myriad Beast Sect? Please come out of the city and help me. My sect has only these elites left. They absolutely cannot be lost again. I will surely repay you handsomely afterwards!"

Qin Baixiang lifted his foot, but immediately pulled it back.

"It's only a hundred *li*, I can be there in an instant. Fellow Daoist, please hold on a little longer."

"You!"

Gaoling Sage shouted angrily, but did not insist.

He slashed out several sword lights in succession, slaughtering a large group of pursuing demon beasts, buying enough time for his disciples.

*Drip!*

Huge raindrops began to fall.

The rolling dust seemed to be somewhat quenched.

From afar, one could see a group of disheveled figures, supporting each other, arriving beneath Lingtian Pass.

But at this moment, the city gate was not open!

A woman with disheveled hair, still retaining some charm, flew over with a long sword in hand.

She looked angrily at Qin Baixiang, who stood motionless on the city wall. "Fellow Daoist, are you not opening the gate yet?"

Qin Baixiang's gaze swept over the dozens of cultivators below the city. The Fire Ridge, once renowned for its abundant underground resources and boasting a mighty force of a hundred thousand disciples, was now reduced to this?

"The city gate will open shortly. But before entering the city, you must undergo the Demon-Reflecting Mirror inspection. Fellow Daoist, please calm down."

Below the East City Gate tower, a bright mirror hung high, shining brilliantly.

But the woman could not wait any longer. She shot up into the air.

"What time is it now? Do you truly intend to watch my Fire Ridge be completely annihilated without lifting a finger?"

Seeing her shrewish behavior, Qin Baixiang showed a look of displeasure.

"Fellow Daoist, you know the rules of Lingtian Pa—... No!"

The area ten *li* from the city was a no-fly zone!

Below the Nascent Soul stage, without a token, one was not allowed to fly recklessly.

Even a Nascent Soul level powerhouse, without an identity token blessed by the no-fly formation, would be greatly suppressed.

For her to disregard this so recklessly...

The irritable young woman flew to mid-air, a sly look suddenly appearing in her eyes.

A dark golden light shot out from her crown, hurtling towards Qin Baixiang with lightning speed.

This sudden change came so abruptly that it gave no one any time to react.

Qin Baixiang's eyes widened, and the true form of that dark golden light appeared in his pupils.

It was actually a Six-Winged Golden Cicada!

However, this Six-Winged Golden Cicada only entered the ten-*zhang* radius around him before it could advance no further.

A surging sonic wave rolled out.

"Demon spawn, begone!"

A yellow light flew out from Qin Baixiang's body, transforming into an agile monkey that angrily beat its own chest.

A roar like thunder and drums erupted from its mouth, turning into waves of sound that swept outside the city.

Those bewildered remaining disciples of the Fire Ridge exploded into clouds of blood mist under the sonic assault.

"Dusk Monkey!"

The Six-Winged Golden Cicada screeched, emitting a sound that no human could hear, but also responded with surging sonic waves.

"Fellow Daoist, make your move now, while there's still time!"

In the next instant, amidst the flashing lightning and rolling thunder, a figure descended from the nine heavens, charging straight at Qin Baixiang.

Qin Baixiang was not flustered at all. He left behind the words, "Activate the grand formation immediately!" and shot up into the sky.

*Boom!*

Without any further words, the two began to fight.

The battle at the East City had come the fastest.

But it also gave enough reaction time to the other locations.

Within Lingtian Pass, the light of various formations flickered and flashed continuously.

At the North City.

Xiao Shiniang, the Mistress of the Fireworks Ancient Isle, looked at the two demon emperors who were charging in while resisting the no-fly formation. She chuckled lightly and parted her lips slightly.

Puffs of smoke billowed out, and her figure instantly vanished. At the same time, the two demon emperors were also enveloped within.

"Guests have arrived. Fellow Daoists, why be so rash?"

At the South City.

Carefree Crane Sage's expression was grave. He flicked his horsetail whisk, which transformed into three thousand strands of white light, striking into the curtain of rain.

Two figures were slowly emerging from within, their brows filled with murderous intent, their bodies radiating killing aura.

...

The West City District was located at the rear of Lingtian Pass, connecting to the Five Regions of Lingtian. It was the safest area.

On the city wall, Zeng Yilong was conversing with Yang Pingdu.

"Has the Grand Elder gone out of the city to experiment with spells again?"

"Mm. The Supreme Elder has been going out of the city quite frequently lately."

"Even though the Grand Elder is already so powerful, he still tirelessly studies spells. Truly worthy of our emulation! Mm, I can't slack off either. During my next break, I must master the body cultivation technique that my master passed down."

Looking at Zeng Yilong's face full of drive, Yang Pingdu felt a pang of envy in his heart.

The other party was both a personal disciple of Elder Wang Yuan and the son of an old friend of the Supreme Elder. He was doted upon in the Luo Tian Sect.

From childhood to adulthood, he had never lacked resources.

As soon as the Supreme Elder returned, he gave Zeng Yilong his own famous early-life dharma artifact, the Broken Moon Wings, and also bestowed a heavy halberd, directly compensating for many of his shortcomings.

Here at Lingtian Pass, relying on the fame of the Supreme Elder as the Alchemy Sect's master, he had been generously winning people over.

At this rate, in the future, Zeng Yilong would surely become the most famous prodigy of the Eastern Wilderness.

But envy was merely envy.

He did not have much jealousy in his heart. The Luo Tian Sect had treated him well too.

The fact that the Old Sect Leader had given him the Dark Origin Pill instead of to his own younger brother showed how much he valued him.

Later, he was appointed as the Law Enforcement Elder, overseeing the entire sect, a position of great power and authority.

He was merely an outsider, yet he received such importance. What was there to be jealous of regarding the treatment of the direct lineage?

From the corner of his eye, he caught a glimpse of a white figure.

"The Supreme Elder has returned."

Zeng Yilong also saw him and said excitedly, "I'll go open the city gate for him!"

At that very moment, Yang Pingdu suddenly turned around, staring into the distant reaches of Lingtian Pass.

Several streaks of light descended from the sky, and the formations at various locations were instantly activated.

Combined with the Supreme Elder's warnings from a month ago, he let out a sharp whistle: "Enemy attack! Full alert!"

However, this sharp whistle could not be heard by many.

Because a sudden thunderclap drowned out the entire West City District.

A great demon had descended from the nine heavens!

Below the city.

A white figure shot up into the sky without any hesitation.

As he charged, a palm imprint materialized, growing larger and larger.

The surrounding heaven and earth spiritual energy crazily converged towards that palm imprint.

The white figure pushed forward with a single palm, and the massive cyan palm imprint roared forward until it collided with a bolt of lightning.

Heavenly thunder met earthly fire, resulting in an immeasurable explosion!

*Boom!*

A wave of destruction, centered on the point of impact, surged outward in all directions.

The curtain of rain was thrown back, and the wild wind howled.

Even the ten-thousand-*li* dark clouds covering Lingtian Pass were torn open by this impact, revealing a huge gap.

Heavenly light shone straight down.

Luo Chen stood in the center of the heavenly light, hovering in mid-air. His sharp eyes stared fixedly at the figure slowly descending amidst the aftermath of the explosion.

In his ears came the other's cruel laughter.

"Such keen divine sense, such abundant dharma power. No wonder that Seven Rhinoceros fellow told me to be careful... Eh?"

The nearly three-*zhang* tall giant looked at the white-clothed man in the distance, a hesitant expression appearing on his face.

"Intruder, state your name!"

Seeing the giant's face, Luo Chen's pupils involuntarily contracted.

Without a second thought, his Mystic Armor covered his body, and his hands hung suspended in front of him. He said coldly, "You are the intruder!"

The giant let out an "oh," and was about to launch a thunderous attack, but the inexplicable sense of familiarity Luo Chen gave him made him hesitate.

"My name is Thunder Prison, under the command of the Rosycloud Lord of Green Parasol Mountain!"

So it was him!

The great demon of Green Parasol Mountain, who had once fought head-on against the ancestor of the Five Elements Divine Sect, Sage Divine Origin.

He had thought this one had already died at the hands of that old thief Divine Origin, but unexpectedly, he was still alive.

The Thunder Prison Roc!

Luo Chen took a deep breath, his hands slowly rising.

A fire phoenix soared out from behind him, its blazing flames seeming to burn away everything in the world.

Looking at the fire phoenix, Thunder Prison's white eyes narrowed slightly.

"Unwilling to state your name and origin?"

"Never mind. Once I capture you, I will naturally know where that familiar feeling comes from!"

He strode forward, running faster and faster. Arcs of electricity burst forth from his body, transforming him into a giant of thunder.

Facing the charging fire phoenix, he showed no fear at all.

Not only that, but with a flick of his hand, a black hammer suddenly appeared.

The fire phoenix arrived in an instant!

*Screech!*

In its sharp cry, it actually passed through an afterimage.

The fire phoenix was stunned for a moment, then suddenly turned its head and flew back towards Luo Chen's direction.

*Crackle!*

Electric arcs flashed, and Thunder Prison reappeared, holding the black hammer, smashing it down towards Luo Chen.

With one hammer blow, Luo Chen was turned to dust.

An afterimage?

Thunder Prison was momentarily stunned, but quickly reacted. "Such speed!"

He suddenly turned around, facing the fire phoenix that was chasing after him, and smashed out with his hammer.

"The phoenix is a divine bird born of heaven and earth. How dare you use such a mimicry spell?"

*Bang!*

Electric light trembled, and the fire phoenix was heavily struck. Its dharma power-condensed body shattered inch by inch.

Luo Chen, who had narrowly avoided that hammer blow by using the Moon Step, appeared at another location.

Looking at the imposing figure of the Thunder Prison Roc as he scattered the fire phoenix with a single hammer blow, Luo Chen's face was full of wariness.

"This demon is the strongest enemy I have ever faced in my life. Relying on spells alone, it will be difficult to defeat him."

"I could use the power of Lingtian Pass's formation to block him, or wait for fellow daoists to come to my aid."

"But, I absolutely cannot hold back!"

Luo Chen's right arm trembled, and a red light, like flowing blood, swirled, about to materialize.

At that very moment.

He suddenly looked towards the center of the city.

In the deep, dark clouds, a seven-colored giant, like a five-hundred-*zhang* mountain, stepped on the surging curtain of rain and charged down wildly.

The raging spiritual energy of heaven and earth crazily converged towards him.

It was a rhinoceros, with seven colors swirling around its body, like an auspicious divine beast from myth and legend.

As it descended, its vast demonic aura pressed down on the city-protecting grand formation that enveloped Lingtian Pass, causing layers of ripples.

From the rhinoceros's mouth came a roar like an earthquake.

"Under the command of Lord Pingshan, Seven Rhinoceros has come to knock at the gate!"

(End of this chapter)
