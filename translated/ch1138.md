# Chapter 1138: Deep Waters Run Still

Granny Yu said cautiously, "Will there be an edict? In the past, most of the city lords summoned to Worryfree City by Lord Worryfree rarely returned, and edicts were even rarer."

Xiao Feng sighed. "Hard to say. That father of mine is a martial arts fanatic. Rumor has it that Worryfree City holds an opportunity to break through to the fifth realm of body refinement. I'm afraid he'll go and never come back, forgetting all about Bequeathed Immortal City."

Granny Yu frowned. "If that's really the case, then Second Young Master will surely start making his move."

"That's precisely why I want to prepare for the martial duel to determine the master in advance! With all of you helping me, and with Uncle Iron holding the fort, even if Xiao Yun has outside riffraff assisting him, I doubt he'll amount to much."

...

Luo Chen sat in a corner, quietly listening to the group discuss matters, gradually piecing together the general picture.

City Lord Xiao had left ten years ago, and then never returned.

This bizarre situation was actually taken in stride by everyone in the hall, as if similar occurrences were common within this Great Cataclysm Domain.

Some feared it, while others flocked to it—all because of the rumored opportunity within Worryfree City to break through to the fifth realm of body refinement.

Of course, those were matters for fourth-realm martial artists to consider. For third-realm martial artists like Xiao Feng, power, status, resources, and strength were what they focused on.

For ten years, the city lord's seat had remained vacant. Even though Xiao Feng had been acting as the lord in the name of the young master, it was ultimately only temporary.

To become the true master of Bequeathed Immortal City, one either needed the previous city lord's edict, confirmed by the Worryfree Envoy, or had to settle it through the purest form of martial combat.

For a large family like the Xiao clan, it was naturally impossible to let the candidates for city lord fight personally, lest it harm the so-called brotherly harmony. Hence, the rule of determining the master through martial combat had been established.

What Xiao Feng and the others were discussing was precisely preparations for this matter.

Martial combat was typically divided into five matches, requiring five participants. To ensure a winning rate, at least Furnace Realm experts were needed.

After listening to the gist of it, Luo Chen found it uninteresting and took the initiative to take his leave and rest.

"Third Aunt, please see the senior off for me!" Xiao Feng said.

The palace-robed beauty nodded and smiled as she rose from her seat. "Senior Luo, this way please. Your lodging has already been arranged."

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment, stood up, and followed Xiao Sanniang out of the main hall.

Behind him, a trail of gazes followed, one of them carrying a strong sense of wariness.

"This man is truly rude. Does he think he can still put on airs from the outside world here in the Great Cataclysm Domain?" After Luo Chen left, Xiao Zirui was the first to offer his sharp critique.

Granny Yu smiled. "Brother Xiao, you are still young. You don't understand the influence that comes with the accumulation of time. People like them, cultivators who live for hundreds or thousands of years, are naturally accustomed to lording it over others outside."

Xiao Zirui snorted coldly. "If not for the peculiarities of this realm, our lifespans as Furnace Realm cultivators could easily exceed three hundred years, matching those Core Formation fellows."

Granny Yu countered, "And how do you know what realm Luo Chen was in before he entered here? What if he was at the level of a Divine Transformation grandmaster, comparable to the 'See the Spirit Without Breaking the Void' human immortal realm?"

"Divine Transformation grandmaster? Him?" Xiao Zirui's face showed disbelief.

"Enough, let's first discuss the martial combat after the Worryfree Envoy arrives in spring. My second brother has been recruiting quite a few good hands in recent years. We need to prepare early." Xiao Feng interrupted their discussion, bringing the topic back.

Only then did the group stop discussing Luo Chen and turn to sharing the intelligence each of them had gathered.

After the banquet ended, Xiao Feng watched the crowd disperse, with only the sinister-looking middle-aged man remaining by his side.

Xiao Feng suddenly asked, "Uncle Iron, before we entered the hall, you told me to treat Luo Chen with courtesy. Why was that?"

The sinister man, who had not spoken a word from beginning to end, finally exhaled a long-suppressed breath of stale air and spoke in a hoarse voice.

"That man is not to be trifled with!"

Xiao Feng frowned slightly, a look of confusion on his face.

"Even if he had the power of a Divine Transformation cultivator before, now that he's fallen here, his strength is less than one ten-thousandth of what it was. Is he really that fearsome?"

Uncle Iron nodded solemnly. "Ordinary cultivators are one thing. Even if they disperse all their mana and convert it into blood qi, they would at most stop at the peak of the Furnace Realm. But Luo Chen is different. He clearly appears to be only at the Blood River Realm, yet the sense of threat he gives me under my qi-mechanism perception is no less than that of a Leakless Realm existence!"

"How is that possible?"

Xiao Feng sucked in a sharp breath, his eyes filled with disbelief.

Before his father left, his realm was the fourth stage of body refinement—a powerful existence who had cultivated the Leakless Golden Body!

Was Luo Chen truly hiding his strength so deeply?

But Xiao Feng would not doubt Uncle Iron's judgment, because Uncle Iron was the only Leakless Realm expert in Bequeathed Immortal City, even if he had only broken through in recent years.

Moreover, the qi-mechanism perception technique he mastered was said to be in no way inferior to the legendary divine sense detection methods of cultivators.

Therefore, it could not be false!

Uncle Iron hummed in agreement, his expression still grave. "I cannot judge whether this is his normal state of strength or if he possesses some kind of burst technique like other cultivators, but in any case, this man should not be provoked lightly."

The concept of a cultivator's burst technique was not unfamiliar to Xiao Feng.

Within the Great Cataclysm Domain, a cultivator's mana, divine sense, and even magical treasures and divine abilities were all naturally suppressed. But that did not mean that cultivators who fell into this place were lambs for the slaughter.

There were always those resolute individuals who, at the critical moment between life and death, would throw caution to the wind and unleash a Daoist attack that even high-level martial artists could hardly withstand.

Xiao Feng's expression turned somewhat uncertain, as if he was calculating something.

Uncle Iron seemed to guess his thoughts and immediately asked, "You want him to fight for you?"

Xiao Feng countered, "What do you think, Uncle Iron?"

Uncle Iron hesitated for a moment. "Let's observe for now. If it's just a momentary burst technique, these cultivators certainly won't use it lightly. If it's his normal state of strength, then if you want him to make a move, I'm afraid you'll have to pay a much greater price."

Hearing this, Xiao Feng nodded subconsciously. "That's what I was thinking. There's still time anyway. Let's first recruit him as a guest elder and keep him under my command. Third Aunt... she shouldn't disappoint me."

...

"Senior, this is the residence the young master has arranged for you. Are you satisfied?"

Deep within the city lord's mansion, a quiet and secluded courtyard stood silently under the night sky.

The lights inside were already lit, and figures could be faintly seen moving about.

Luo Chen cast a casual glance and nodded noncommittally.

Seeing this, Xiao Sanniang revealed a smile. "Then, Senior, do you have any other requirements regarding the young master's recruitment of you as a guest elder?"

Xiao Feng had shown great respect for Luo Chen, but in terms of the guest elder arrangement, the standards were still roughly the same as for others.

An independent courtyard residence, matching servants and maids, resources needed for martial arts training, monthly guest elder stipends, and so on.

For ordinary people, these were already generous enough—things many could only dream of. But in Luo Chen's eyes, they were still nothing special, and he showed no sign of excitement.

He looked at the palace-robed beauty before him and asked bluntly, "Add one more condition: I may freely access the city lord's library at any time."

Xiao Sanniang was taken aback. "This... I'm afraid I cannot make that decision."

"Then discuss it with your young master first before telling me."

With those words, Luo Chen turned and entered the quiet courtyard.

The palace-robed beauty stood outside the gate, her expression hesitant. Because the previous city lord had been a martial arts fanatic, the city lord's mansion had collected many texts and records. Many guest elders were willing to serve under the young master precisely because they coveted these texts.

The young master was naturally not stingy, but he would only lend out portions based on the guest elder's contributions and length of service.

For Luo Chen to demand access to everything as soon as he arrived was somewhat excessive.

"Oh well, I've done what I needed to do. Let Xiao Feng worry about this himself!"

(End of chapter)
