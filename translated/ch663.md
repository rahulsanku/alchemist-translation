# Chapter 663: Soul, Return! A Dragon Appears in the Clouds

Within the dim yellow that filled the sky, sand flew and stones rolled. The eye could not see beyond ten li.

Crack, crack, crack.

The sounds kept ringing out.

Thump!

A pile of broken stones fell from the mesh of a net and clattered across the ground.

Moya Cave Master crouched and rummaged among the stones for a long while. At last, he drew out an earth-yellow rhombus crystal.

"A spirit stone?"

"A supreme-grade earth-attribute spirit stone?"

"No, that is not right. The spiritual qi contained within is clearly only at the middle-grade level, so why is its attribute so singular?"

Moya Cave Master could not make sense of it no matter how he thought.

Just now, they had encountered a stone monster.

It had looked tall and mighty, two or three zhang high, but its strength was extremely poor. Luo Chen had not needed to act. Moya Cave Master had easily dealt with it himself.

Combined with the example from the Thousand-Ren Mountains, where killing metal-attribute strange beasts had yielded many metal ores, Moya Cave Master had searched carefully afterward.

After all, he was an artifact-forger. In ordinary times, he handled many materials. Whether they were metal attribute or earth attribute, collecting more was never a bad thing.

Yet this result disappointed him greatly.

A strange earth-attribute crystal.

It resembled a middle-grade spirit stone, but was also different.

While he was puzzled, Luo Chen's calm voice came from beside his ear.

"Let me have a look."

Moya Cave Master naturally had no objection and tossed the crystal to Luo Chen.

Luo Chen lowered his head and examined it for a long while. His expression did not change. Afterward, as if it were only natural, he put it into his ink-green storage ring.

Seeing this, Moya Cave Master opened his mouth.

He wanted to say that this was his battle spoil.

In the end, he still suppressed the displeasure in his heart.

Earlier, even that supreme-grade Nine Yang Geng Metal had been monopolized by Luo Chen, much less this small middle-grade spirit stone that looked special but was not actually much.

Give it to him, then. Give it to him!

Moya Cave Master turned his eyes away. What the eye did not see, the heart would not grieve over.

But in the next moment, hearing movement beside him, he instinctively turned around.

"Devil Lord, what are you..."

A blue bird was in the sky. A black jiao was on the ground. They guarded Luo Chen from above and below.

Luo Chen nodded at Moya Cave Master.

"Nothing. I only feel this place is somewhat dangerous, so I plan to have my spirit pets scout the road."

"Scout the road?"

Moya Cave Master immediately recalled what had happened in the Thousand-Ren Mountains.

Clearly, at that time, Luo Chen had released his two spirit pets in advance. Only because of that had he been able to set an ambush against him and Ding Yi.

He had not expected Devil Lord Azure Yang to have such deep thoughts.

Moya Cave Master looked deeply at Luo Chen, then raised his hand and shook it. Two streaks of golden light appeared.

"Since we are scouting, I imagine my Tiyuan Birds [TERM?] will be more suitable."

One streak of golden light circled beside him, while the other landed on Moya Cave Master's shoulder.

Luo Chen looked at the two golden little birds in surprise.

Tiyuan and Tiyang [TERM?], twin-born demon beasts, were extremely skilled at seeking treasure. Their minds were connected, and even across a very great distance, they could transmit information to one another.

He had not expected Moya Cave Master to have tamed such spirit pets!

However, for the other party to release them now was probably because of the previous lesson. He wanted to guard against Luo Chen somewhat.

Luo Chen smiled.

"In that case, let them go together."

With a wave of his hand, Heavenjade and Black King immediately set out, each in a different direction.

Seeing this, Moya Cave Master froze for a moment. He had already produced demon beasts more suitable for scouting, yet the other side still insisted?

Of course, matters had reached this point, and there was no way to take the words back.

The Tiyuan Bird [TERM?] spread its wings and flew out.

Tai Sui sat nearby on the yellow-skinned gourd, watching coldly the whole time.

Yet he did not expect Luo Chen to keep flying while striking up a conversation with him again.

"Fellow Daoist Tai Sui, you said before that Devil Lord Heaven-Refining was proficient in the five-element laws and even enlightened five-element spirits with them. Was that so?"

"Yes. What of it?"

"As for spirits, Luo has actually seen them before. Though their abilities were weak, their spiritual intelligence was no less than that of normal humans, and they were born with souls. Why, then, were the spirits we saw earlier incomplete in soul body, shallow in intelligence, and almost entirely reliant on instinct to act? Beings like that can hardly be called spirits, can they?"

Tai Sui's withered cheeks wrinkled. His brows folded together until he looked like two pieces of skin pasted left and right over protruding brow bones.

Moya Cave Master was also curious.

"That is true. Not to mention low-rank spirits, even that fourth-tier Nine Yang Golden Eagle, in my view, had spiritual intelligence inferior to an ordinary third-tier demon king."

Facing the two men's questions, Tai Sui fell silent for a long while. In the end, he could only give the answer, "I am only a remnant soul split from the main body. My knowledge is limited."

Luo Chen was somewhat dissatisfied with this, but he did not keep pressing.

Instead, he shifted the topic toward the vast yellow sand ahead, toward a battlefield there.

At that place, Black King was easily swinging his great tail and smashing a stone monster apart.

"In this boundless Flowing Sand Sea, are these stone monsters also spirits enlightened by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining?"

Tai Sui hesitated.

"Are they, or are they not?" Luo Chen stared at Tai Sui, wanting an accurate answer.

Tai Sui smiled bitterly.

"They should be, I suppose, but I am not too certain. Five Elements Heaven has existed for thousands of years. Not every living being inside was enlightened by True Lord. For example, both I and the Five Elements Lotus Platform evolved and grew on our own. Through such long years, how could even I know every change within?"

Luo Chen frowned. Instinctively, he felt the other party was lying.

But when he thought of how Tai Sui said his main body was trapped by formation restrictions, it seemed only reasonable that he did not know the changes outside.

Even so, Luo Chen asked one more question.

"What was Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's purpose in arranging Five Elements Heaven?"

Tai Sui was startled.

"Purpose? This realm was only one of his traveling palaces. You can simply regard Five Elements Heaven as his rear garden. What other purpose could there be?"

"Would a rear garden need to be arranged so meticulously?"

"Devil Lord Azure Yang, do not forget that True Lord himself was also an extremely formidable artifact-forger. Five Elements Heaven is full of many resources. Ordinarily, they look pleasant, but when artifact-forging was involved, they were all good materials that could be taken at hand."

Luo Chen nodded slightly. This explanation did make sense.

But because of this, he became even more curious.

"The Thousand-Ren Mountains mainly contained various mineral deposits. What of this Flowing Sand Sea? We have already traveled for one day. Aside from some stone monsters, we seem not to have seen any unusual resources."

After hearing this question, Tai Sui laughed despite himself.

"After circling around for so long, it was for this!"

Luo Chen's face remained calm. This truly was what he wanted to ask.

Only by knowing how this place had been arranged and what use it had could they respond to whatever danger might be hidden here.

"There is no harm in telling you. In this boundless Flowing Sand Sea, there is only one thing and no other resource. However, it is hidden extremely deep. Ordinary people cannot find it at all."

"What is it?"

"Have you heard of Breathing Soil [TERM?]?"

In an instant, Tai Sui felt two heavy breaths.

Luo Chen and Moya Cave Master were both moved.

Breathing Soil!

Among the many heavenly materials and earthly treasures, it was one of the most famous earth treasures. It was equally renowned with weak water, yet rarely seen in the world!

Legend said it was a thing of the immortal realm. How could the lower realm seek it?

Such a treasure was something every artifact-forger dreamed of.

Especially Luo Chen. His lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, had been nurtured for many years, and its impurities had grown fewer and fewer. It could already accept more materials to increase its power.

If he could obtain Breathing Soil...

"Stop dreaming. I only mentioned it in passing."

Tai Sui tugged the corner of his mouth and pointed at the boundless sand sea beneath their feet.

"Such a treasure is rarely seen in the world. Even True Lord could not seek it out."

Luo Chen was speechless.

"Then why mention it? Are you teasing us?"

Tai Sui chuckled.

"Though True Lord could not seek it, he wished to refine Breathing Soil with his own hands."

"Refine Breathing Soil?!"

Two voices sounded at the same time, both full of disbelief.

Tai Sui nodded.

"Indeed. True Lord collected large quantities of earth-attribute resources, such as Reversion Mud, Derivative Earth [TERM?], Xuanhuang Sand [TERM?], Clear Billow Dust [TERM?], Demon-Transforming Mud [TERM?], and so on. He gathered them here, then supplemented them with earth law power, intending to reverse their origin and recreate the legendary Breathing Soil."

As they listened to those names one after another, Luo Chen and Moya Cave Master both held their breath.

Some were familiar to them, such as Reversion Mud and Derivative Earth.

Others, they had never even heard of.

So many precious earth-attribute materials, gathered here and refined together to reshape Breathing Soil. What kind of grand method was that?

"Did he succeed?"

"I do not know." Tai Sui shook his head, and his face carried a trace of uncertainty. "My main body is a wood-attribute spirit plant, and it greatly restrains earth-path things. I vaguely remember that fifteen hundred years ago, a unique aura came from this sand sea. That aura made me very uncomfortable. It gave me a feeling of heaven and earth reversed. Later, out of curiosity, I asked the child attendant under True Lord's seat. He said..."

"Said what?"

"'One step away, a difference between heaven and loam.'" Tai Sui recalled the past, his eyes somewhat dazed. "That child attendant said the Flowing Sand Sea had a master from then on. Its name was Heavenloam [TERM?]. In the future, more materials would be gathered for it so it could advance further. Unfortunately, afterward True Lord perished..."

Luo Chen and Moya Cave Master looked at one another, both murmuring the name "Heavenloam."

At the same time, in the boundless Flowing Sand Sea, light wind coiled in little whirlwinds and drifted everywhere, carrying countless grains of sand as they rolled.

Beneath the sand sea, however, there was another world.

Crude caves were spread everywhere. Within them seemed to be laughter and chatter, as if people lived there.

But when one looked carefully, where were any traces of people? Only strangely shaped mud figures lay inside the caves.

In a daze, a palm-sized mud figure stood up. Its ears twitched, as if it had heard something.

"Was someone calling my name?"

When it listened carefully again, there was no echo in the wind.

The little mud figure wrinkled its nose and no longer bothered with the matter. Instead, it blew a whistle.

"Whew..."

The whistle was long and carried through more than a hundred nearby caves.

Before long, one small mud figure after another walked out from the caves. Only, these mud figures were different from it.

Their limbs were incomplete. Their facial features were blurred. They looked as if they had been casually made, and also as if they had experienced some great battle.

These incomplete little mud figures gathered in groups before it.

At first glance, they looked like an army.

Seeing this, the little mud figure revealed a smile that seemed like crying yet was not crying.

"Ever since that fellow fled here three hundred years ago, it has refused to leave."

"Not only that, it even split stone monsters from itself and hunted me everywhere."

"This time, however, its doom has arrived!"

"Children, prepare yourselves. Take it down for me. So long as I eat it, I can advance another step and take all of you away from here!"

Below, there was silence.

But after it gave its orders, the incomplete little mud figures began walking outside one by one.

...

Hoo...

Wind blew past. A streak of golden light circled slowly through the sky.

Looking carefully, it was a small golden bird.

Its speed was slow, unlike something traveling.

At this moment, it had lowered its head and was looking down at the scene below.

A huge jiao-python was using tremendous force to strangle a hard stone monster to death. After the stone monster died, it pulled a yellow spirit stone from the creature's shattered body.

The black jiao-python raised its head and stared at the small golden bird.

"Get lost!"

"Keep watching, and I will eat you!"

The small golden bird's wings trembled, and it hurriedly flew farther away. Yet it still maintained a certain distance from the black jiao-python, monitoring it from afar.

Black King spat out a mouthful of sand, his face gloomy.

"Master too. It is one thing to assign me a scouting task, but he insists I hunt these stone monsters too."

"They are not useful. Why kill them?"

As he spoke, he swallowed the yellow spirit stone.

In his view, this thing was at most at the level of an upper-grade spirit stone. What was the use of spending so much effort collecting it?

One hundred li behind Black King, Moya Cave Master's mind stirred. He looked toward the Tiyang Bird [TERM?] on his shoulder, which was chirping.

According to the news sent by the other male Tiyuan Bird, Black King, the spirit pet Devil Lord Azure Yang had sent out, was actually exerting himself to kill stone monsters and collect those special spirit stones.

What did this mean?

At that moment, he turned his head and saw Luo Chen looking at him with a faint smile that was not quite a smile.

"Cave Master, I wonder whether your Tiyuan Bird has found any movement?"

Moya Cave Master's expression became slightly forced.

Inwardly, he thought, 'Can Black King transmit sound over a hundred li? In Five Elements Heaven, divine sense is suppressed to the limit. Even people like us with powerful soul foundations cannot easily transmit across long distances.'

'Could Black King have transmitted the information that the Tiyuan Bird was monitoring him to Devil Lord Azure Yang?'

Of course, on the surface, he still maintained calm.

"There is no movement."

"Oh..."

Luo Chen pursed his lips slightly, drawing out his tone.

"You noticed nothing, but I have noticed a little movement on my side."

Moya Cave Master froze, and even Tai Sui looked at Luo Chen curiously.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. Mana trembled through his body, and his hands traced a circle in the air.

In an instant, a rippling water mirror appeared before him.

This one move made Moya Cave Master instinctively hold his breath.

The Flowing Sand Sea was a place where earth power gathered. The spiritual qi was basically all earth-attribute spiritual qi, yet Devil Lord Azure Yang could condense a Water Mirror Art from empty air here!

Especially when the other party was clearly a fire-path cultivator who cultivated a single attribute.

This display alone showed the depth of his spell attainments.

Of course, now was not the time to marvel over such minor details. He and Tai Sui looked curiously at the water mirror.

Within the mirror, three figures appeared.

Three male cultivators, all at the late Golden Core realm, each with an extraordinary appearance.

The three sat in a triangular formation on a stretch of gobi.

It was a rare area of solid ground within the Flowing Sand Sea. Hard rocks filled the surroundings.

On that gobi, corpses of stone monsters lay scattered and split apart. The earth was also covered in scars, clearly showing that a fierce battle had taken place there earlier.

At the center of the three men stood a large white long banner.

The leader wore a Flying Cloud Topknot [TERM?] and a brown Daoist robe. He held a bell in his hand and was shaking it violently.

The Daoist muttered words under his breath.

Because they were separated by a great distance, the image was blurry. They could not clearly see what the Daoist's mouth was chanting.

But within the image, wind and clouds gathered, the earth shook, and in the sky, dark calamity clouds gathered in a heavy mass.

This scene was clearly extraordinary at a glance, as though some great matter were about to happen.

Moya Cave Master suppressed the agitation in his heart and asked curiously, "What are they doing?"

Luo Chen's expression was grave.

"I do not know. But once we get there, we will know."

As he spoke, his palm closed around the yellow spirit stone he had obtained from Moya Cave Master. A faint tremor came from within it.

...

On the desolate and boundless gobi, black clouds pressed down, and wild wind swept across the land.

A handsome blue bird hid within the storm, its gaze cold and sharp, watching the scene below like a water mirror.

The bell rang urgently, shaking the blue bird's soul until it became unsteady.

More high cries and stern shouts kept ringing from the mouth of the leading Daoist.

"Men of thousand-ren height, only the soul is sought.

Ten suns rise in turn, melting metal and stone.

They have all learned this place; when the soul goes, it must be released.

Return! You cannot entrust yourself here.

Soul, return! A thousand li of flowing sand!

Soul, return! A ten-thousand-zhang thunder abyss!

Soul, return! This realm cannot hold you!"

...

By the end, only one heaven-reaching shout after another remained.

"Soul, return!"

Amid those cries, the black clouds slowly pressed lower.

An enormous dragon head slowly emerged from within.

*(End of Chapter)*
