# Chapter 954: Accumulated Strength, Suppressing a Realm

Within the cave dwelling, blood light reflected and blood qi permeated the air.

Yet this blood qi was not rank or foul; instead, it emitted a strange fragrance that made one's mouth water.

Black King cowered in the corner, swallowing a mouthful of saliva, a hint of relief in his eyes.

"Good thing I didn't drink that Poisoned Grain Millet back then. Such terrifying corrosiveness—even master's fleshly body couldn't withstand it, let alone me."

The poison of Poisoned Grain Millet was rare in the world.

A small amount, Luo Chen could still resist.

But amplified by the Hundred Poison King Gu, its corrosiveness had reached an utterly terrifying state.

Yet seeing this, Luo Chen was not particularly alarmed.

His gaze was fixed on the pool of blood, a thoughtful expression on his face.

Within the blood, a white jade Gu worm the size of a knuckle opened its tiny eyes.

It was the Hundred Poison King Gu!

It looked around, finally meeting the eyes of Luo Chen's nascent soul.

With a thought, the white jade Gu worm slightly opened its mouth and inhaled toward the outside.

Instantly!

Threads of poisonous qi were sucked out of the blood and into its mouth.

And the white jade color became even more pure and transparent.

Able to release, and also able to take back!

This was precisely the characteristic of evolving to the fourth rank!

It meant that from now on, the degree of toxicity it devoured and fed back to Luo Chen could be perfectly controlled, and the Hundred Poison Body Tempering Art could also embark on a relatively benign cycle.

With the corrosive poison qi of the Poisoned Grain Millet gone, the pool of blood suddenly came alive.

Bubbles gurgled and popped within it, constantly appearing and vanishing.

Watching this, Luo Chen's nascent soul figure slowly curled its fingers, forming a hand seal, and uttered a clear command.

"Nirvana!"

As if an invisible giant hand swept across, the blood on the ground began to boil violently, then rolled upward, forming a blood-colored surging spring.

A hazy phantom gradually took shape within the blood spring.

Moments later, when the last drop of blood seeped into skin as white and pure as jade, Luo Chen's fleshly body, sitting cross-legged, reappeared in the world!

Black King, watching from the side, had a hint of fear in his eyes.

Even with his undying body talent as a desolate beast, it would be difficult to achieve such a feat in an instant.

Just what level had master's body tempering reached?

But he did not know that this was no longer merely a matter of body tempering realm.

In this act, Luo Chen had used his desolate beast body as a foundation, the Hundred Poison King Gu as the main agent, the poisonous wine Poisoned Grain Millet as the catalyst, and finally employed the fleshly body nirvana method from the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* to conclude.

Thus, he had helped the Hundred Poison King Gu advance to the fourth rank, while his own fleshly body had also gained immense benefits.

"If not now, when?"

The nascent soul figure flickered and vanished into the crown of the fleshly body, disappearing into the Purple Mansion.

Soft, jet-black eyelashes trembled gently, and slowly, Luo Chen opened his eyes.

The moment they opened, a breath seemingly from the ancient primordial wilderness spread uncontrollably outward with him at its center.

Crack!

The formation shrouding the cave dwelling shattered like tofu under the impact of this terrifying aura.

Boom!

The majestic aura shot straight into the sky, suppressing an entire realm!

On Mi Jie Mountain, the Songyang Alliance Leader, who had been discussing war matters in detail with Zhan Bingtao, changed expression drastically.

Zhan Bingtao's eyes blazed with light, and he blurted out, "Your alliance still has such a hidden expert?"

The Songyang Alliance Leader said nothing, merely spreading his divine sense instantly, chasing toward the source of that aura.

Inside a palace.

Li Tian Shi looked with satisfaction at the miniature pavilion he held in his hand, within which tiny Gu worms crawled back and forth, each exuding an extraordinary aura.

If released to the outside world, each one would attract eager pursuit and bidding.

But here, they were merely part of the structure forming the small pavilion.

"This Immortal Gu House I built according to the extraterrestrial Gu recipe has finally taken shape. If I could have a fifth-rank divine Gu to suppress it, its power would be no less than a spirit treasure."

"Even this Wind-Wave Pavilion as it is now is enough to crush ninety percent of true artifacts in the world!"

"For this trip to the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm, only the Qi Tian link remains."

Just as he was feeling complacent, the majestic aura spread across the wilderness.

Li Tian Shi was startled, then a grin involuntarily spread across his face.

"Good! Good! Good!"

"The great deed can be accomplished!"

The moment that terrifying aura appeared, figures stepped out of their cave dwellings one after another, strands of divine sense crisscrossing, tracing back to the source of the aura.

There were no weaklings among those who could stay on Mi Jie Mountain.

Precisely because each was a strong cultivator, they could feel the depth of this aura all the more.

It was not the pressure brought by dharma power, divine sense, or nascent soul domain, but the most essential suppression at the level of life itself.

Ordinary sages could not contend with it; perhaps only grand cultivators could fight it!

The terrifying aura came suddenly and vanished just as quickly.

After a momentary burst, it receded like a tidal wave, finally disappearing into a cave dwelling halfway up the mountain.

Strands of divine sense lingered outside, wishing to see the master of the cave dwelling.

The Purple Robed Marquis's divine sense was among them. When he traced it back to Luo Chen's cave dwelling, the shock in his heart was beyond measure.

Sure enough!

The next moment, under the gaze of all, the cave dwelling gate slowly opened.

A tall figure calmly walked out.

He merely swept his gaze across the void, and all the divine senses involuntarily retreated.

"It's the Overlord Cultivator Qi Tian!"

"Could it be that his body tempering realm just broke through, causing the aura to leak?"

"The path of body tempering actually has such power?"

In the void, strands of divine sense communicated with each other, a clamor of discussion.

Finally, with a bitter yet envious congratulatory voice from the Purple Robed Marquis, all doubt and confusion turned into congratulations.

"Congratulations, Fellow Daoist Qi, on your great divine art's completion!"

Luo Chen grinned, clasped his hands toward the mountain outside, and said, "Thanks!"

Then, with a sweep of his sleeve, he turned and entered the cave dwelling.

When the gate closed again, the divine senses withdrew, curious and puzzled.

Leaving aside the subsequent discussions among the strong cultivators of Mi Jie Mountain, the returning Luo Chen first smiled self-deprecatingly.

Divine art?

Poison art, more like!

He waved his hand toward the empty air.

This palm was unremarkable, but a faint fragrance of musk lingered, faint yet perceptible.

A palm print appeared on the cave dwelling wall, followed by large patches of stone wall melting away.

It must be known that these cave dwellings on Mi Jie Mountain were all built with various precious materials.

High-level cultivators might be able to destroy them, but to do so as effortlessly as Luo Chen, melting them away, was absolutely impossible.

"Who would have thought that the *Hundred Poison Body Tempering Art*, besides tempering the physique, would have such an unexpected gain when reaching the master level and cooperating with the fourth-rank Hundred Poison King Gu!"

Luo Chen looked at his hand, shrouded in black light, where the fragrance of musk still lingered.

After the fleshly body had been reconstituted, Luo Chen suddenly discovered that his source power had somehow undergone a mutation.

It contained a highly toxic attribute within!

This toxicity was not that of Poisoned Grain Millet, nor was it the likes of corpse poison, feather poison, or convulsion poison.

It was more like a mixed toxin, combining the outstanding advantages of most of the toxins Luo Chen had cultivated since practicing the *Hundred Poison Body Tempering Art*.

As for its true power?

Luo Chen's gaze unconsciously fell on Black King in the corner.

In that instant, Black King's entire body went stiff.

He looked at Luo Chen in terror, "Master, you wouldn't..."

Luo Chen smiled slightly, "Don't worry, you have an undying body. I'll control it a bit; it won't hurt you."

Black King wanted to cry but had no tears, and could only sacrifice himself for Luo Chen's experiment.

...

Half a year later.

Mi Jie opened, war began!

When Mi Jie Mountain opened wide and one by one the Southern Frontier sages no longer concealed their identities, walking the world in the name of the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance, it signified that this human civil war in the Southern Frontier had fully begun.

Songshan Sect, Red Leaf Sect, Moon Worship Sect, Burial Life Valley, White Cloud Temple, Purple Blood Sect, Hidden Sword Tower...

Only when the cultivators of these top Southern Frontier sects formed their great armies did people realize that the Southern Frontier was truly about to change.

Not only that, but when the cultivator army from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect merged with them, the world learned that the Southern Frontier civil war also had external reinforcements.

The numbers were incalculable, their strength difficult to gauge.

The cultivator armies formed by the various sects, like a war machine, once started, could not be stopped.

The first to be attacked was the Sword Control Villa, an early vassal of the Life and Death Gate.

In a single night, the Sword Control Villa, which boasted eighty thousand sword cultivators, was destroyed under the assault of the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance.

The army did not pause for a moment, splitting into two streams, pouncing on the Golden Toad Sect and the Xuanhua Sect respectively.

Swift in speed, fierce in momentum.

Like the waters of a great river, surging forward, determined to drown the Life and Death Gate.

And in this rolling tide, only a few brilliant waves were missing.

"Where are Songyang and Li Tian Shi?"

"He has some trivial matters to attend to; he will catch up to us at the Life and Death Gate battlefield. Don't worry, Fellow Daoist Zhan."

"Then, are Red Leaf and the others also going with him?"

"Exactly!"

"What about Qi Tian? I wanted to see his methods when we attack Unicorn Mountain."

Unicorn Mountain, a top Southern Frontier sect guarding the holy land of the Life and Death Gate.

Its terrain was treacherous, its formations outstanding.

Without a sharp weapon to breach defenses, it would be difficult to take it down as quickly as the Sword Control Villa.

In Zhan Bingtao's heart, the Overlord Cultivator Qi Tian was the best blade to break Unicorn Mountain.

Hearing this, the Songyang Alliance Leader smiled and shook his head.

"Unicorn Mountain is not worth worrying about. I will personally take action when the time comes. As for Qi Tian..."

The Songyang Alliance Leader asked back, "Do you think Unicorn Mountain is more important, or the Life and Death Gate?"

Zhan Bingtao raised an eyebrow. The "Life and Death Gate" in the other's words did not refer to the holy land, but the great gate protecting that holy land!

"Can Qi Tian do it? You must know that even our Dao Sect's ancestor had little certainty of forcibly opening the Life and Death Gate."

"Wait and see!" the Songyang Alliance Leader said somewhat mysteriously.

...

Without the holy land's summons, the many vassals of the holy land were like scattered sand, unable to organize at all.

On the contrary, with the Corpse Alliance army and the Dao Sect army having long planned, once they raised their troops, they broke through like splitting bamboo, successively destroying several forces.

The army surged forward, using a pincer formation to continuously prune the holy land's wings, heading straight for Unicorn Mountain.

Once past Unicorn Mountain, they could face the Life and Death Gate directly.

In another place, a streak of light left Mi Jie Mountain, heading southeast.

The streak of light was like a boat, parting clouds and mist.

Within it, several figures stood.

The most eye-catching was a tall man in black armor, a full seven feet in stature.

His crown was like a mountain peak, his plumes like heavenly pillars.

Jade-white skin, eyes like cold pools and deep abysses, capable of devouring hearts.

It was Luo Chen!

The Purple Robed Marquis sighed with emotion, "In just one year, Brother Qi's body tempering path has advanced to such a state—truly a miracle!"

Beside him, a middle-aged Confucian scholar carrying a sword case nodded in agreement, but a hint of wariness flashed in his eyes.

That aura emitted by the Overlord Cultivator Qi Tian back then had shaken all the sages of the Southern Frontier, and that was merely the tip of the iceberg of his strength.

This also made many cultivators who had looked down on body tempering practitioners put away their contempt.

Luo Chen remained unmoved, knowing his own affairs.

What others saw was the miracle of his transformation in a single year.

What they did not know was how many years he had been stuck at the fourth-rank mid-stage.

At the age of one hundred and sixty, he had broken through to the archaic fourth rank.

And by the age of two hundred, he had already reached the archaic fourth-rank mid-stage.

From then until now, a full hundred years had passed!

In his entire life, he had never been stuck at any minor realm for so long.

Coming to the Southern Frontier, obtaining a suitable technique, having a large amount of resources to match, coupled with a deep foundation—it was only natural that the water would flow and the accumulated strength would burst forth!

Now, his body tempering realm had reached the archaic fourth-rank late stage, comparable to a late-stage Yuan grand cultivator!

Luo Chen did not stand on ceremony, directly asking Li Tian Shi, who was chatting with Granny Red Leaf.

"Now that we have set out, Li Tian Shi, you should tell us the specific itinerary from here on!"

Interrupted from their small talk, Granny Red Leaf couldn't help but frown.

If it were before, she would have already scolded him.

But now, the strength Luo Chen displayed was enough for her to maintain a measure of respect for him.

Li Tian Shi smiled, "It is time to speak, but let's wait until everyone is gathered to say it together, to avoid repeating ourselves."

Yes, there were still helpers!

Li Tian Shi had mentioned before that there was one more.

Who exactly, it was not known.

But now, it was time for them to show themselves.

Li Tian Shi seemed to sense something, raising his eyes to look into the distance.

"He... they are coming!"

There was a hint of dissatisfaction in his tone.

The flying boat slowed slightly, and moments later, two streaks of light landed on the deck.

One was an old man with a face full of wrinkles and hands like chicken claws.

The other was dressed in white with white hair, his brows and eyes arrogant.

The leader was the old man. When he saw the strong cultivators on the flying boat, he laughed heartily, "Li Tian Shi, a hundred years unseen, how is your health?"

"Elder Meng, why are you concerned about my health?" Li Tian Shi smiled gently, exchanging a bow with the other, then looked with some displeasure at the white-haired man. "Wasn't it agreed that only you would come? Why did you bring another fellow daoist?"

Elder Meng smiled slightly, "White Profound Lord is no stranger. Thinking that Li Tian Shi was short of help, I conveniently called him along. What, not welcome?"

Li Tian Shi's expression stiffened.

Then, he took a deep breath.

"Welcome, how could I not welcome him? The esteemed leader of the Whitebone Sect is a great help. I must thank Elder Meng for thinking of me."

Then, he turned to the crowd, especially Luo Chen, and introduced the two.

"The Supreme Elder of the Luo Sect, Meng Shentong!"

"The Leader of the Whitebone Sect, White Profound Lord!"

At the first sentence, Luo Chen's curiosity was piqued.

His spiritual eyes unconsciously fell on the somewhat short old man.

This was the Supreme Elder of the Luo Sect?

Could he be the Devil Heart King Gu?

And the other party also unconsciously met Luo Chen's eyes.

"No need to introduce the others; I know them all. But this one fellow daoist is somewhat unfamiliar! Li Tian Shi, won't you introduce him?"
