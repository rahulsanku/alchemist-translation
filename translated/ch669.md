# Chapter 669: The Demon Race Reappears, Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit

"Do you think he can succeed?"

Above a vast, rolling magma marsh, Moya Cave Master asked in a low voice.

Tai Sui flew while looking back at the fire cloud stretching a thousand li, his expression somewhat complicated.

Faced with Moya Cave Master's question, he did not answer directly. Instead, he asked in return.

"Do you hope he succeeds?"

"I..."

Moya Cave Master's lips moved. Ever since stepping into Demon-Fall Land, much of his former ease and bearing had vanished. At this moment, he was even more at a loss.

After a long while, he said in a low voice, "I do not know. However, although Devil Lord Azure Yang is somewhat domineering, at least he still has a bottom line. When there is no conflict of interest with him, he does not deliberately target me. So... I still hope he succeeds."

In Moya Cave Master's view, if Luo Chen succeeded in subduing that Burning Heaven Fire [TERM?], which looked extremely powerful at a glance, his strength would surely increase greatly.

In that case, during the rest of their journey, their small team would also be safer.

After hearing this, Tai Sui sneered but gave no evaluation.

Moya Cave Master was curious.

"And you?"

"Whether he succeeds or fails has nothing to do with me. But overall, I hope he fails to subdue the Burning Heaven Fire," Tai Sui said casually.

Moya Cave Master did not understand.

"Why? Are you afraid he will threaten your main body?"

"How could a mere Golden Core cultivator threaten my main body, no matter how powerful he becomes?"

Tai Sui glanced at Moya Cave Master, seeming to imply something. Then he changed the topic.

"I am merely afraid he will fail to grasp the proper measure and affect your later work breaking my restrictions."

So that was it.

Moya Cave Master suddenly understood.

The reason Tai Sui had specially chosen him and Luo Chen was not their strength, but the high formation attainments each of them possessed!

High-tier formation masters were rare.

Even in the Northern Sea cultivation world, famous for its many wandering cultivators, powerful formation masters were usually controlled by great sects and great clans.

He and Luo Chen were outwardly artifact-forgers famous for refining instruments and treasures, but in truth, both had no shallow attainments in formations.

If Tai Sui wanted to break the restrictions and free his main body, he had to rely on help from himself and Luo Chen.

Whether Luo Chen's strength increased had little to do with him.

However...

"What does failing to grasp the proper measure mean?"

Just as Moya Cave Master was puzzled, a low shout suddenly entered his ear.

"Careful. There are people on Vermilion Bird Mountain!"

Moya Cave Master froze and looked up. On Vermilion Bird Mountain [TERM?], that lifelike mountain which seemed about to spread wings and fly, there was now a figure sitting cross-legged.

He sat there silently. A sharp long saber was planted beside him.

His hands maintained a seal, and he was silent.

"It is Dao Lan!"

With one glance, Moya Cave Master recognized the man's identity. His eyes carried disbelief.

"Did he not die under the Demon Emperor's hand?"

Tai Sui did not answer his confusion. Taking Dao Lan as the center, his divine sense spread madly.

"Not only one. There are two more!"

"On the left and right peaks."

"No, they are not humans. They are two late third-tier great demon kings!"

Tai Sui's tone was urgent, and he quickly spoke the locations of the one man and two demons.

Moya Cave Master's face changed greatly.

"It is one thing if Dao Lan is still alive, but why is he mixed together with two great demon kings? And what are they doing now?"

Tai Sui's expression was likewise gloomy.

His eyes looked deeply at that great mountain.

On the main peak, Dao Lan alone held the position.

On the two side peaks, which were like wings, there stood a huge squid and a golden toad.

The three figures had almost no concealment at all.

Or rather, they had no time to conceal themselves.

"They seem to be joining hands to operate a formation!"

After watching for a moment, Moya Cave Master analyzed what the three were doing.

Tai Sui nodded slightly. He also saw it this way.

Though Vermilion Bird Mountain showed no change on the surface, faintly, an aura completely different from this place was flowing through the mountain.

Moya Cave Master asked carefully, "Then what do we do?"

"Wait!"

Tai Sui said in a low voice, "Wait until Devil Lord Azure Yang finishes handling his aftermath. Then we will decide."

Moya Cave Master nodded repeatedly. This decision aligned perfectly with his own wishes.

In his view, none of that one man and two demons looked easy to deal with.

Dao Lan was the fourth eight-star demon hunter of the Myriad Immortals Assembly, with extremely powerful combat strength. Even when Ding Yi was still alive, he had been very wary of Dao Lan, and when dealing with him, could only tempt him with profit.

As for the other two demon beasts, that huge squid was clearly the legendary Sea King Squid.

He could not recognize the origin of the golden toad, but since it could stand alongside Dao Lan and the Sea King Squid, its abilities were surely not poor.

Without Devil Lord Azure Yang present, relying only on him and Tai Sui, who could not completely use Ding Yi's physical strength, they absolutely could not defeat those three.

Therefore, they could only wait!

...

On Vermilion Bird Mountain.

Dao Lan sat cross-legged as his mana poured outward like flowing water.

His aura was extremely weak, so much so that his face was very pale.

Even so, he could not stop the movements of his hands. He could only pour mana into the great mountain beneath him without pause.

"I never expected that I, a generation's Saber King, would fall to this point. It is one thing to become another person's puppet, but now I must ignore my foundation, greatly consume my mana, and arrange a formation for another."

Dao Lan also had a Dao title.

Because he had long been stationed at the Flowing Ocean Defense Line [TERM?] and had slaughtered demon beasts on a large scale, the many wandering cultivators of the Myriad Immortals Assembly had praised him and given him the title Demon-Slaughtering Saber King [TERM?].

He did not often use this Dao title.

But compared with his current situation, it was truly bitter.

A bitter smile hung on Dao Lan's face. His gaze occasionally wandered through this world filled with firelight.

"Recently, volcanoes in Blazing Refinement Prison have erupted repeatedly, and the earth has been shaking continuously. With such strange events, another powerful figure must have entered."

"I only hope they do not come to Vermilion Bird Mountain, otherwise they will not return."

Dao Lan shook his head and looked several hundred li away, toward that red cloud.

Within it, winds rolled through the clouds, and killing intent rang like metal.

He remembered that there was a fourth-tier fire spirit there, formed after a sourceless flame advanced to the fourth tier.

That fire spirit was exceptionally powerful, and it had the advantage of terrain. Even the three of them did not want to provoke it lightly.

An existence able to fight that fire spirit to a standstill had to be an extremely powerful one as well!

Such an existence would probably not ignore Vermilion Bird Mountain, this treasure land.

Once the other party came, a great battle would be inevitable.

"If he can kill Shark Emperor Liujun, perhaps my situation of enslavement can be resolved."

"But if the other party comes here, the first people he must deal with will be the three of us. If he is too strong, I fear I will die first."

Contradictory emotions constantly flowed through Dao Lan.

The pain in his eyes tangled without stop.

At that moment, a stern shout came from the nearby peak.

"Dao Lan, do not stop your mana. If the grand formation is interrupted, the Shark Emperor will hold you accountable!"

It was that Sea King Squid.

Anger flashed through Dao Lan's eyes.

If not because you occupied the terrain of the Fallen Sea back then, and if not because the Demon Emperor cut off my retreat, how could I have fallen prisoner?

Do you truly take yourself for something important?

However, even though he was angry, his body honestly increased the output of mana.

Within Vermilion Bird Mountain, a blue light suddenly strengthened and gathered toward one figure.

If Luo Chen were here and saw that figure, he would certainly recognize the person's identity.

Shark Emperor Liujun!

The fourth-tier Demon Emperor who had escaped from Arctic Yemo Heaven!

Threads of blue light gathered around him, and his aura became increasingly profound and mysterious. His eyes also became brighter and brighter.

Suddenly, his figure stopped inside a volcano crater that burned without end.

"Found it!"

"After spending more than a year and setting foot throughout Five Elements Heaven, I finally found you!"

Below that volcano crater, endless magma converged into a vast lake.

A lotus was quietly blooming in the magma lake, spreading five petals of different colors.

As the petals stretched, the light on the lotus continuously changed. At times it shone bright gold, at times green, and when firelight flowed, it suddenly became crimson.

Seeing this scene, wild joy appeared in Shark Emperor Liujun's eyes.

Five elements interlock and coil; nature clear, life more firm. A golden lotus planted in fire, transformed into spirit and immortal!

This legendary Five Elements Lotus Platform was of even higher quality than he had imagined!

"This trip was worth it after all!"

As if he had suppressed himself for too long, the extremely handsome Liujun threw aside all bearing at this moment and laughed loudly.

With his laughter, the magma lake immediately boiled.

That five-colored lotus platform began slowly sinking.

"It has actually gained intelligence. How can I let you escape!"

Shark Emperor Liujun's expression changed, and the blue light in his hand immediately surged toward the magma lake.

Not only that, with a thought, his Sage domain suddenly unfolded!

He isolated the fire-attribute spiritual qi here, intending to separate that five-colored lotus platform from its environment.

...

Boom!

Within the thousand-li red cloud, two enormous figures suddenly collided, stirring endless aftermath.

Looking carefully, both had the same appearance.

Even the colors on their bodies were identical.

Both were crimson great pengs!

"Its spirituality is quite sufficient. It can actually borrow cloud to take shape and withstand my attacks."

Luo Chen, who had transformed into the true body of a celestial peng, was delighted rather than alarmed.

This Burning Heaven Fire had already gained spirit. Its strength was absolutely no less than that Nine Yang Golden Eagle, and because of the advantage of home ground, it was even three parts more powerful.

Unfortunately, its home ground was also Luo Chen's home ground!

Within this thousand-li fire cloud, the power of Luo Chen's All-Encompassing Fire Prison reached its maximum. It was no longer limited to a hundred zhang and could spread to a thousand zhang, even five thousand zhang.

No matter where the Burning Heaven Fire's fire spirit fled, Luo Chen could follow like a shadow.

Speed was the absolute difference between them!

Later, the Burning Heaven Fire Spirit also realized it could not escape and began clashing head-on with Luo Chen.

However, Luo Chen, who had directly activated the second form, was possessed by Withering-Bloom True Fire and did not fear the Burning Heaven Fire's burning at all.

After hundreds of exchanges, the other side's decline had appeared.

"Trying to run again?"

Seeing the Burning Heaven Fire Spirit, now transformed into the same appearance as his celestial peng true body, spread its wings and try to leave the thousand-li red burning cloud, Luo Chen could not help smiling.

"Unfortunately, it is too late!"

The All-Encompassing Fire Prison was driven harder, covering several thousand zhang.

Not only that.

The clattering of chains sounded in the void.

One chain after another flew out from Luo Chen's body and, with the speed of thunder before ears could be covered, bound the Burning Heaven Fire Spirit.

When the chains touched its body, the fire spirit immediately panicked.

Its shape began changing continuously.

From fire peng to fine horse, then to a human appearance, then into flood dragon and giant python. It truly embodied the meaning of clouds changing a thousand times.

Unfortunately...

Under the Primordial Unity Cauldron's spirit-locking power, no matter how it changed its true body, it could not escape.

The All-Encompassing Fire Prison began continuously contracting, and the nine huge chains also tightened more and more.

Finally, the Burning Heaven Fire Spirit revealed its original form!

A flame like red cloud burned there while struggling.

Luo Chen looked at this scene with satisfaction. The Primordial Unity Cauldron's power was even greater than before.

Especially against these spirit and soul things without physical bodies, it had even stronger targeted effects. He had not even taken out the cauldron's main body, yet he had easily suppressed it like this.

Then next...

Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat.

A strand of azure flame drifted out and turned into the shadow of a one-zhang small tree in the void.

The small tree stood in the air, its roots directly piercing into the red cloud flame.

"To refine a fourth-tier sourceless flame will probably take quite some time."

Luo Chen murmured. He immediately sat cross-legged in the void without hurry and circulated his lifebound cultivation method.

But when he once again began driving his origin true fire to devour sourceless flame, he suddenly discovered that the refining speed seemed much, much faster than before.

"Hm?"

"Why is it so easy? It seems even easier than refining a third-tier sourceless flame earlier?"

Luo Chen was extremely surprised.

While refining the sourceless flame, he searched for the reason behind this change.

"Is it because my Withering-Bloom True Fire's rank is higher and higher?"

"Or because I am more familiar with this process... Or could it be assistance from my lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron?"

Perhaps all these factors existed.

But in the end, Luo Chen found the main reason the devouring speed had grown faster.

On the system panel, one entry was flashing with brilliant light.

Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture, Grandmaster 501/1000.

Without knowing when, the proficiency of his lifebound cultivation method had quietly advanced to the Grandmaster level!

After sensing this change, Luo Chen became incomparably excited and nearly interrupted the process of devouring the fourth-tier Burning Heaven Fire.

Fortunately, he reacted quickly and immediately restrained his excitement.

Even so, Luo Chen was still extremely delighted.

The Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture was the most profound divine art he had ever learned.

He had obtained it more than forty years ago!

If it were any other cultivation method or spell he had learned in the past, it would already have reached Great Perfection long ago.

Yet the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture's progress had been so slow that it was outrageous.

Even though he circulated it day and night without rest, and his realm had risen to Golden Core Stage Six, with only a small portion of the progress bar left before Golden Core Stage Seven, the proficiency of his lifebound cultivation method had still been stuck at the Perfect level.

It looked only a few dozen proficiency points away from Grandmaster, yet it crawled like a snail.

One could imagine it would only become harder later. Who knew how many years it would take to reach Great Perfection?

Unexpectedly, after stepping into Blazing Refinement Prison and repeatedly activating his lifebound cultivation method to have his origin true fire devour sourceless flames, the proficiency began soaring.

In only a few months, it crossed the Perfect level in one push and reached Grandmaster.

And once it reached this level, the efficiency of devouring sourceless flames suddenly increased by more than tenfold.

"As expected, the ability to devour sourceless flames comes from this scripture!"

"Or rather, this miraculous function should only be revealed when the cultivation method is practiced to a profound level, meaning the Grandmaster level."

The Grandmaster level on the system panel could compare to what ordinary people called great accomplishment!

And Luo Chen had discovered this wondrous use one step earlier, tested and practiced it, and driven this function ahead of time. That was why the proficiency had soared in such a short time.

"This is a double blessing!"

Luo Chen smiled freely. His mana intensified, and the devouring process sped up another three parts.

Several days later.

Luo Chen slowly drifted down from the thousand-li red burning cloud.

The aura on his body had become even more domineering and blazing.

As he flew through the void, he had a feeling of being like a fish in water.

It was as though Blazing Refinement Prison was his home!

When Luo Chen once again stopped before a low hill, surprise appearing in his eyes, a low shout came beside his ear.

"Luo Chen, too much is as bad as too little!"

Luo Chen's face was full of displeasure.

"What now?"

"You cannot keep doing this," Han Zhan's voice came again.

Luo Chen snorted lightly.

"Why can I not? I can feel that if this continues, my origin true fire will undergo a qualitative change. Perhaps it can cross the fourth tier in one push and reach the fifth!"

As he spoke, in his qi sea, a small tree covered in azure flames gently swayed its branches, as if echoing him.

Han Zhan fell silent.

Seeing Luo Chen step forward again toward the low hill that might hide a sourceless flame, Han Zhan finally spoke once more.

"At that time, should I call you Luo Chen, or should I call you Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit?"

Boom!

Azure flame spread across his whole body, burning the void.

Luo Chen's steps froze in midair as if struck by lightning, and his expression became extremely ugly.

*(End of Chapter)*
