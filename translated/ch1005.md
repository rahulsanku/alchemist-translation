# Chapter 1005: Subduing the Nether Water Spring Eye, Welcoming the Grand Supreme’s Return

*Boom!*

A dull, muffled sound exploded in the void.

It represented the most extreme resistance from the Nether Water Spring Eye.

The Nether Water pouring in from all directions grew more and more abundant, flickering with a hazy, dim light, transforming into a force of ten-thousand jun, pulling and straining the Primordial Unity Cauldron until it trembled incessantly.

Matters had already reached this point; how could Luo Chen possibly sit idly by and watch the treasure fly away?

His dharma power poured out like a great river or vast ocean, flooding into the Primordial Unity Cauldron, giving rise to more and more crimson chains.

These chains were different from before, extending out from the void, wrapping layer upon layer around the Nether Water Spring Eye.

This method of extending through the void was not Luo Chen using the Primordial Unity Cauldron after his realm had increased. It contained experiments born from his recent heightened sensitivity to void fluctuations. The reason Phantom Nether Princess had been suddenly locked down earlier was precisely because she had been caught off guard by this trick.

Luo Chen speculated that perhaps, due to the Life-and-Death Gu, he had managed to steal a sliver of spatial talent from Azure Frost.

Of course, at such a critical juncture, relying solely on such minor arts was not enough to secure victory.

Luo Chen no longer held back. He unleashed all his methods at once!

"All of you, come out!"

With a thought, streams of cyan-white flame erupted from his body, spreading wildly outward.

In an instant, a towering cyan fire tree, propping up heaven and earth, materialized above the White Jade Square, completely sealing off this realm.

In that moment, the surging Nether Water rolling in from all directions was blocked outside.

Not only that!

With a mad glint in Luo Chen's eyes, even the fire spirit of the Nirvana Sacred Flame flew out, transforming into a great bird flapping its wings, circling outside the cyan fire tree.

Wherever the Nether Water breached a gap, it would pounce directly, swiftly incinerating the water.

Currently, compared to the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, the Nirvana Sacred Flame was still relatively weak due to its short nurturing time, so it could only do this work of patching leaks and filling gaps.

But for some reason, after the Nirvana fire spirit flew out, it seemed even more at ease than the fire tree formed by the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

Luo Chen's eyes were filled with wonder. Through the Nine Nether, he looked toward the sea surface.

The orthodox Nirvana Sacred Flame left behind by Rosycloud faintly seemed to resonate.

A flicker of doubt passed through his mind, but Luo Chen quickly set aside his stray thoughts.

The most pressing matter was to subdue the Nether Water Spring Eye!

This object seemed to possess a faint intelligence. It vaguely sensed what being subdued would mean, and its struggles grew even more violent.

But with two fifth-rank divine fires blocking the outside world, the Nether Water Spring Eye had finally become water without a source, unable to replenish itself.

The original tug-of-war began to tip the scales.

The Nether Water Spring Eye, resembling an eye, was slowly being pulled toward Luo Chen.

Throughout this process, Luo Chen multitasked, doing his utmost to mobilize the power of the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

As his natal treasure, nurtured for over two hundred years since his Adept formation, it might not match the sharpness of the Yuantu Sword or the ferocity of the Blood Jade Three-Flame Fan in a single attribute, but its inherent properties were extremely comprehensive.

Binding, suppression, capture, nurturing, refinement… it had many wondrous uses, with no apparent weaknesses.

Even its most basic offensive and defensive capabilities were not inferior to most true artifacts.

Perhaps it was precisely for this reason that the transformation and ascension of the Primordial Unity Cauldron were so difficult.

But precisely because of its comprehensiveness and profound foundation, it had become the best tool for Luo Chen to subdue the Nether Water Spring Eye at this moment!

"Relying solely on the crimson chains for capture and binding is not enough!"

"I must also supplement it with a method of restraint!"

With a thought, Luo Chen slapped his hand onto the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

*Clang!*

A dull echo rang out. Luo Chen casually waved his hand.

In an instant, a never-before-seen earthy yellow light flew out from the Primordial Unity Cauldron, scattering onto the Nether Water Spring Eye.

This seemingly ordinary earthy yellow light, upon appearing, revealed an extraordinary effect.

The Nether Water Spring Eye visibly wilted.

But with a flicker of dim light, the spring eye regained a trace of vitality.

Seeing this, Luo Chen was not discouraged. Instead, his eyes lit up.

"The Subdue-Flood Divine Light, produced after refining the Xirang, is truly effective!"

Back then, he had personally gone to Divine Origin Peak and slain Divine Fire Sage of the Five Elements Divine Sect. Afterwards, he extracted a small piece of Xirang from the shattered natal treasure, the Five Elements Pagoda.

Legend had it that Xirang was the ultimate grade of earth-attributed earthly treasure.

It possessed the heaven-defying effect of calming seas and blocking floods.

Although the Five-Colored Treasure Pagoda of Divine Fire Sage contained only a very small amount of Xirang, after Luo Chen extracted and refined it into his own natal treasure, it had, more or less, brought a noticeable improvement to the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

This Subdue-Flood Divine Light was one of the derived techniques.

Its power was by no means inferior to the innate crimson chains.

Luo Chen continuously patted the Primordial Unity Cauldron, scattering one Subdue-Flood Light after another.

Earth element restrained water element. Even though the Nether Water Spring Eye clearly contained a vast power of water origin, it had just been condensed and was cut off from replenishment by the same-source Nether Water due to the two divine fires. Thus, under this repeated suppression, its arrogance could no longer be sustained.

"Get in here!"

Luo Chen let out a low roar. His four arms closed together, lifting the Primordial Unity Cauldron and turning its opening forward.

A suction force, even greater than before, erupted abruptly.

*Whoosh!*

Caught off guard, the Nether Water Spring Eye was sucked directly inside.

This instantaneous burst of power drained Luo Chen's dharma power in a flash.

Without dharma power to replenish them, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire and the Nirvana Sacred Flame both retreated sharply into the depths of Luo Chen's Purple Mansion.

The great cauldron in his arms trembled violently, indicating that the Nether Water Spring Eye inside was still resisting.

Luo Chen snorted coldly. His thousand-zhang body rapidly shrank, and the cauldron in his arms also shrank sharply.

Biting the tip of his tongue, Luo Chen spat out a mouthful of essence blood, spraying it onto the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

This temporarily made up for the lack of dharma power.

After weathering this crisis, the Sage within his body suddenly opened its eyes, spitting out even more pure dharma power.

Luo Chen breathed a sigh of relief. He cast one restriction after another, sinking them into the Primordial Unity Cauldron, beginning to suppress the Nether Water Spring Eye with all his might.

Yes, what he wanted was suppression, not refinement.

With the precedent of the Ghost Emperor Wang, Luo Chen dared not easily attempt to refine the Nether Water Spring Eye.

If his dao conduct was insufficient and he was instead assimilated, the loss would outweigh the gain.

First, suppress and store it away. Later, he would make long-term plans.

For now, with this treasure in hand, even if he could not yet control the Nether Abyss, he could at least prevent others from taking advantage of the situation.

For a time, dharma power continuously spewed forth, restrictions were constantly added, and the situation gradually fell under Luo Chen's control.

...

The sky above the Nether Abyss was always dark and gloomy.

Dark clouds seemed never to disperse, and thunderbolts and lightning flashed from time to time.

Especially the spatial rifts that appeared unpredictably, blocking the approach of all living beings.

On the sea surface, the Nirvana Sacred Flame left behind by Rosycloud was also gradually thinning, no longer showing the spectacular sight of a golden flame spreading across the sea from years past.

Of course, relying solely on natural forces to completely extinguish this man-made great divine ability would still take an unknown amount of time.

On this day.

A vast amount of Nether Water poured toward one place, forming a huge whirlpool.

But half an hour later, the whirlpool suddenly collapsed, and a large amount of Nether Water scattered in all directions.

Everything between heaven and earth returned to its previous state.

As if nothing had happened at all.

Had nothing really happened?

The terrified and anxious members of the Luo Tian Sect, led by Deng Taiyue, once again appeared on the ruined White Jade Square.

This once solemn place, where the Nether Origin Ancestor had received the worship of billions of disciples, was now battered and uneven.

A large number of dragon-carved pillars had collapsed, and countless pits were everywhere.

One of the three Yama Floating Sky Palaces, the Heavenly Nether Palace, had been reduced to a pile of rubble, its majestic sight no longer visible.

Everyone was worried. They scattered their divine senses, frantically searching for Luo Chen's trace.

Finally, beneath a pile of rubble, they found the young man sitting cross-legged.

His robes were tattered, his aura fluctuated unsteadily, and the exposed limbs and torso bore obvious wounds that, although healing quickly, were still evident.

At this moment, the young man, with a look of intense curiosity, stared quietly at the small cauldron in his hand, as if pondering something.

"See? I told you Brother Luo wouldn't be in trouble."

Deng Taiyue said this calmly, but he was clearly relieved.

It seemed that even he hadn't been as confident in his heart as his words suggested.

The Nether-Crossing Boat pushed aside the surrounding Nether Water, approaching quickly.

As the boat drew near, before Luo Chen could board, a figure threw itself into his arms.

It was Gu Caiyi.

Luo Chen was momentarily taken aback, then smiled. Ignoring the gazes of others, he patted the woman's fragrant shoulder.

"Why so childish?"

The woman in his arms seemed to be sobbing softly.

Gu Caiyi hugged Luo Chen's solid body tightly for a moment, as if to gain a sense of security, before releasing him.

"I was afraid something would happen to you."

No matter how restrained she had been when they met earlier, after the extreme conflict and brutal battle, Gu Caiyi had ultimately been unable to suppress the emotions in her heart, losing her composure in front of everyone.

Luo Chen shook his head. Taking the woman's hand, he stepped onto the Nether-Crossing Boat.

As soon as he boarded, he was met with various forms of address and gazes full of concern.

Luo Chen smiled. "I've made you all worry."

Deng Taiyue looked around curiously. "Is it all settled?"

Luo Chen nodded. "More or less!"

At these words, everyone was overjoyed. The looks they gave Luo Chen shifted from initial worry to full admiration and reverence.

The Nether Abyss ghost cultivators, the Five Ghost Emperors!

Some had died, some had been subdued. Not a single one had escaped.

This was their Grand Supreme Elder!

Bai Meiling couldn't help but ask, "Master, what about the Nether Water Spring Eye? Legend has it that this object can control the Nether Water and master the Nether Abyss. Have you already refined it?"

At this, Luo Chen's expression remained strange.

He waved his hand. "I have my own plans for this matter."

Bai Meiling understood and immediately stopped asking.

Ling Fengzi probed, "Senior Brother, what now?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Let's go back first!"

The Nether-Crossing Boat immediately turned and headed toward the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

As for the former Yama Floating Mountain?

One of the three palaces that propped up the restrictions had been beaten into ruins, leaving a gap in the formation restrictions here.

With a large amount of Nether Water pouring in, it was almost no different from the outside world.

Only a few cave mansion restrictions and the other two palaces remained, barely surviving under the filth of the Nether Water.

Luo Chen had no time to deal with these remnants of the Nether Origin Sect for now. He could only go back first and let the sect make various plans later.

...

Within the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

Streams of light flew out from various mountain ranges, all gathering toward the entrance.

Some late-arriving disciples were shocked to find that the Sect Master, Li Yingzhang, had long been waiting here.

Not only that, but even the sect-protecting desolate beast, the Mystic Turtle, was lying nearby.

Everyone seemed to be waiting for something!

"The Grand Supreme Elder is back!"

There was no need to inquire; this news quickly spread to everyone's ears.

Instantly, everyone was overjoyed.

According to what the three disciples sent by the Divine Talisman Sect had said, the Grand Supreme Elder had stepped into the Nether Abyss, personally gone to the Five Elements Divine Sect, and not only slain the great cultivator Divine Fire Sage but also annihilated the entire Five Elements Divine Sect, which had once harbored ill intentions toward them.

With such a great deed, how could they not be excited?

Yao Mingyue was among them, naturally overjoyed. But compared to others, her face held an even more expectant look.

Luo Chen was not only the Grand Supreme Elder but also her uncle-master. She knew best the purpose of his departure from the Luo Tian Sect.

This return must have yielded some gains.

Yao Mingyue couldn't help herself. She quietly left the group and flew off in a certain direction.

In no time, she flew to a lonely, desolate peak. Circling for a moment, she landed.

Before she even entered the cave mansion, an extreme, bone-chilling coldness spread outward.

Yao Mingyue frowned and entered the cave.

"Master, uncle-master is back!"

A beautiful young woman sitting on an ice bed heard this. Her eyelashes trembled, and then she suddenly opened her eyes.

Her eyes were full of joy.

But quickly, that joy was suppressed.

Sima Huiniang said coldly, "So he finally remembers to come back!"

Yao Mingyue shook her head and sighed. "I know you've been imprisoned for ten years, Master, and you're resentful. But he is, after all, your husband and the Grand Supreme Elder of the Luo Tian Sect. Many things cannot be handled with personal favoritism. Back then, the matter of Lingxi, at the smallest, was about the lives of a few disciples; at the largest, it was enough to shake the foundation of the sect..."

"Shut up!" the woman snapped coldly, retorting, "Since he's back, where is he?"

Yao Mingyue had no choice but to relay the current situation.

"The Grand Supreme Elder has run into some trouble. It's said to be related to the Nether Water Spring Eye."

Trouble?

What kind of opponent could cause trouble for Luo Chen?

The woman, who had been cold-faced moments before, suddenly showed a hint of panic. The coldness on her face instantly turned to worry.

But when she looked back at another room, filled with a chilling atmosphere, she couldn't help but bite her lip.

"Lingxi is frozen. I need to constantly supply him with blood qi to maintain his vitality, so I can't leave for now. You keep an eye on him for me. As soon as there's news, come and inform me immediately."

Yao Mingyue hummed in acknowledgment. She also glanced at that room, then took her leave.

After leaving Sima Huiniang's cave mansion, she couldn't help but mutter a few words to herself.

"This family of my master's is truly awkward. It used to be fine. I hope that after Lingxi gets better, master and uncle-master can reconcile as before!"

Muttering like this, she also resolved never to seek a dao partner in her life.

Trouble, and a hindrance to cultivation!

...

Soon, Yao Mingyue returned to the entrance of the Blessed Land.

Li Yingzhang noticed her, called her over, asked a few questions in a low voice, and then waved his hand.

"The Grand Supreme's family matters, you and I should not interfere too much. Just wait patiently!"

Yao Mingyue nodded and obediently waited nearby.

Time passed slowly. Everyone grew more and more anxious.

Especially knowing that the Grand Supreme Elder was being delayed outside because of the Nether Water Spring Eye, they became even more worried.

What if...

There was no what if. When the Mystic Turtle lying on the ground suddenly raised its head, everyone sensed something.

At the entrance, the water curtain flowed, and spiritual light transformed.

Several figures materialized simultaneously.

At the forefront was a man in feathered robes and a high crown, handsome and elegant, like an immortal descending to the mortal world.

All the disciples of the Luo Tian Sect bowed in unison.

"Welcome, Grand Supreme, on your return!"
