# Chapter 1178: The Mark of No-Self, Human Immortal Luo Chen

The Nine Rings of Ruin.

A single chaotic mass.

Time and space seemed to lose all meaning here.

Only a single enormous blood cocoon stood silently in the void, radiating an aura both eerie and brimming with divinity.

The chaotic energy around it surged violently, but whenever it reached the blood cocoon, it parted to either side like water meeting a reef.

Days passed. Years passed.

The blood cocoon remained unmoving through the ages. No one came to disturb him within the Fourth Ring.

There was, however, the martial grandmaster Mu Yun, who had been promoted to fill the vacancy left by the fallen Turbid World Martial Ancestor. He came specifically to observe for a long time.

This was the man Luo Chen had first challenged, defeated, and even kicked down to the Fifth Ring upon entering the Fourth Ring.

During those years in the Fifth Ring, he had felt nothing but humiliation at every moment. There was not a single instant when he did not wish to take revenge on Luo Chen.

But when he truly saw the blood cocoon, all the resentment and hatred in his heart dissipated like smoke.

"We are all seekers of the Dao. Why torment one another?"

Murmuring to himself, Martial Grandmaster Mu Yun took the initiative to withdraw.

---

Luo Chen was completely unaware of the outside world.

The appearance of the blood cocoon did not surprise him.

This was, after all, a passive defense mechanism he had established when creating his own cultivation technique. Once an unexpected situation occurred and he lost consciousness in an instant, his Blood Refining Divine Astral would burst outward, forming a defense like the Blood Cloth Garment.

The only difference was that the Blood Cloth Garment only had one layer without affecting Luo Chen's normal movements, while this blood cocoon, resembling a demon's egg, consisted of countless densely packed layers of Blood Cloth Garments.

Its defensive power was such that even the most top-tier martial grandmasters would need to attack with full force countless times before barely breaking through.

By that time, it would be enough for Luo Chen to regain consciousness.

Of course!

Luo Chen had not set up this protection mechanism solely to deal with enemies. Subconsciously, he had the idea of using this method to resist the Return to Nothingness of All Things.

At this moment, Luo Chen had lost all sense of time. All his attention was focused on the changes within his own astral energy.

A will, faint yet tenacious, was born from within the astral energy.

This will was filled with spirituality, like a newborn infant, curious about everything in the world.

But toward Luo Chen himself, there was a barrier.

As if they were not on the same frequency!

How could one's own martial divine intent be on a different frequency from the martial artist's will?

The answer was not complicated. It was simply because Luo Chen had not yet grasped its essence, so a membrane existed between the two.

Luo Chen was not flustered. The divided soul hidden within his apertures awakened from its deathly stillness and devoted itself entirely to sensing that martial divine intent.

Gradually, a feeling of seeming and yet not-seeming arose.

Beneath endless layers of clouds, as if falling into a fog.

At the edge of a vast abyss, standing on the brink without entering.

Within a ten-thousand-year glacier, a single crystal of ice blossomed brilliantly—this was the insight he had gained from Ice Heavenly Master.

A blazing sun hung high in the extreme heavens, angrily pouring scorching heat upon the earth—this was the embryonic form of Extreme Yang Martial Ancestor's divine intent.

A sharp sword cut through the darkness. A ferocious wolf howled proudly through the ages.

A terrifying giant bear smashed everything, wanting to crush all who stood in its way.

A soft force, deflecting a thousand jun, like a bowstring, like a spring.

One by one, completely different martial divine intents were all contained within that will born from the astral energy.

Each one seemed to exist.

But none of them fit perfectly.

Even as Luo Chen's thoughts wandered, he saw more things.

Immortals and gods dwelling high in the extreme heavens, demons and monsters entrenched in the Nine Nether, true spirits galloping across the great wilderness, and even barbaric giants resembling the ancestors of mankind running barefoot across the ancient continent.

Too many wills, causing Luo Chen to feel lost.

It seemed that as long as he was willing, he could become any one of them.

If he wanted to become a god, he would ascend.

If he wished to enter the demonic path, he would sink.

However!

"Gods and demons are originally without form. If I cling to a single form, then am I myself, or am I someone else?"

With this thought, Luo Chen seemed to return to the simplest original intention when he created the Divine Demon Formless cultivation technique.

He wanted to create a cultivation method that was most suitable for himself, not seeking shortcuts, not wanting to become a fish in the pond of some great power.

Since that was the case, any martial true intent he saw now was merely picking up what others had discarded.

Therefore, his Luo Chen's martial divine intent did not possess a single fundamental form, yet it could change into any false form at any time.

His martial divine intent was one of change, without a fixed individual mode of existence or form.

That is—the Mark of No-Self!

As he pondered, three blurry phantom figures rose and fell within his consciousness.

Like gods, like demons, and there was also a three-headed, six-armed great Buddha sitting in a lotus position with seals formed.

The Mark of No-Self is precisely the Mark of Only-Self!

No matter how all things in the world change, only the self remains unchanged.

If I wish it, then I can transform into all things in the world.

The moment this thought emerged, the three blurry phantom figures of consciousness that had been rising and falling, wavering unsteadily, finally merged into one.

And at that very moment!

The membrane that had always existed instantly dissolved. Luo Chen's will and the will of the Blood Refining Divine Astral drew close together.

A martial divine intent unique to Luo Chen began to slowly spread outward, centered on his physical body.

In an instant, the other eight martial grandmasters in the Fourth Ring all shuddered as if struck, turning their gazes toward Luo Chen.

It wasn't just the Fourth Ring.

Luo Chen's peculiar martial divine intent of No-Self and No-Other seemed to penetrate everywhere, descending along the chaotic energy that poured down through the central platform.

The Fifth Ring—he saw Xiao Lin, who was studying martial arts.

The Sixth Ring—he saw the Purple Haze City Lord, who had been promoted to fill a vacancy.

Then the Seventh Ring, the Eighth Ring, and even the Ninth Ring.

When the tendrils extended to this point, trying to go further became extremely difficult. It seemed another barrier blocked the spread of his martial divine intent.

That should be the Ninefold Immortal Tower itself.

Luo Chen began to withdraw his martial divine intent. When it finally concentrated back on himself,

He suddenly sensed something.

Unbeknownst to him, a will had been watching him all along.

"Greetings!"

"Greetings, Fellow Daoist!"

They exchanged simple pleasantries, and then that will drifted away.

Although they had not exchanged names, Luo Chen already knew who was watching him.

"Netherspring Realm Lord..."

As he murmured, Luo Chen suddenly felt a sense of hunger arise.

Looking at himself now, he found that the amount of Blood Refining Divine Astral within his physical body had decreased by more than half.

But the remaining portion had become extremely pure and refined.

"While comprehending martial divine intent, I can also polish my own astral energy?"

Luo Chen was thoughtful.

But he was not alarmed. It was just a loss of some astral energy.

Within these Nine Rings of Ruin, chaotic energy was inexhaustible.

With the Primordial Unity Cauldron assisting in the conversion, he had an endless supply, able to convert it at will.

As he activated the *Divine Demon Formless*, the qi and blood furnace within his body began to rumble and spin. Above it, the Primordial Unity Cauldron radiated brilliance, emitting a tremendous suction force.

And outside.

The blood cocoon had not yet disappeared, but it no longer blocked the outside world.

Vast chaotic energy, as if attracted, surged toward Luo Chen.

A massive amount of chaotic energy was absorbed, then converted into basic qi and blood power, and then gradually transformed into Blood Refining Divine Astral.

The process, repeated thousands of times, was as usual.

The only difference was that within Luo Chen's Celestial Court acupoint, a small figure was rapidly taking shape.

This small figure had the same appearance as Luo Chen, the same temperament, and even within its transparent shell, there were meridians and apertures, as well as a miniature qi and blood furnace.

A martial divine spirit was taking shape.

Once it succeeded, it would be the Human Immortal realm!

---

The moment Luo Chen released his martial divine intent, the entire Fourth Ring was shaken.

This was not something comparable to a seed of divine intent or an embryonic form of divine intent.

It was a genuine, extremely powerful will, comparable to the three individuals in the Upper Three Rings.

Everyone understood that at this moment, Luo Chen had truly gained the qualification to contend for the position of Martial Human Immortal.

Next, he only needed to accumulate qi and blood power and smoothly step into the next realm.

Calculating carefully, Luo Chen had only been in the Nine Rings of Ruin for ninety years, and in the Fourth Ring for a mere fifty years at most.

His speed of progress was far beyond everyone's imagination.

In this way, he truly lived up to his reputation as a Human Immortal Seed.

Ten years later.

That is, sixty years after Luo Chen entered the Fourth Ring.

A full cycle of sixty years, flowers blooming and withering.

The blood cocoon hung withered, coiled as before.

On this day.

*Boom!*

A terrifying tremor sound spread throughout the universe. All the martial artists in the Fourth Ring were shaken simultaneously.

Feeling the chaotic storm converging toward the center, they vaguely realized what was happening.

Without hesitation, the eight martial grandmasters moved together, converging toward the center.

Before long, they had all gathered.

Without exchanging words, they focused all their attention on the blood cocoon, which was now wrapped and scoured by countless chaotic storms.

In each of their eyes, there was fervor.

When a martial artist breaks through a realm, they often need a massive amount of energy as a supplement.

This scene was precisely such a sign.

And Luo Chen had already stepped into the late stage of the Leakless Realm. What realm he was about to break into went without saying.

No one wanted to miss this moment.

They knew they were witnessing the birth of a Martial Human Immortal!

Time passed bit by bit. The enveloping chaotic storm grew more and more intense, forcing the eight martial grandmasters to retreat repeatedly.

Nine days later.

Just as everyone was growing impatient, even beginning to suspect that Luo Chen had failed, a clear voice came from within.

"All marks are non-marks, the surplus yet without surplus.
Forgetting words, a moment of clarity; speaking of dreams, a double emptiness.
Empty flowers seek no fruit; sun mirages find no fish.
Gods and demons are not me; ten thousand marks return to nothingness."

---

Amidst the clear chanting, a figure in white walked out from within the chaotic storm.

As he lightly flicked his sleeve, the surrounding storm instantly dissipated.

In an instant, the sky was clear, the universe as before.

Luo Chen looked at the crowd, at their gazes filled with indescribable meaning. He smiled and cupped his hands in greeting.

"Thank you all for lending me a hand earlier. Luo is deeply grateful!"

Then, with a casual wave of his hand, jade bottles drifted down and landed before each of them.

"A small token of thanks, not worthy of mention."

"Everyone, we shall meet again if fate allows!"

With these words, he stepped lightly onto the nearby central platform, and his carefree figure vanished in an instant.

The fervor in everyone's eyes gradually faded, eventually turning into a look of envy.

None of the martial artists who could reside long-term in the Fourth Ring were young. Most were advanced in age, even nearing the end of their lifespans.

What they all sought was to break through to the Human Immortal realm and enjoy a more abundant lifespan.

But in the end, it was Luo Chen, the Human Immortal Seed, who had come from behind.

How could they not be envious?

The Barbarian Martial Grandmaster grabbed the jade bottle, twisted off the lid, and his eyes lit up.

"It's actually top-grade Meridian-Replenishing Pills!"

Upon hearing this, the others quickly checked their own jade bottles.

Inside each lay three pills.

All were Meridian-Replenishing Pills, all top-grade!

Holding the jade bottles, everyone smiled wryly. The resentment they had felt when Luo Chen had come to challenge them had also dissipated like smoke.

---

"So this is the Third Ring?"

Stepping out from the central platform, Luo Chen gazed into the distance while instinctively spreading his Blood Refining Divine Astral.

What met his eyes was a vast expanse of misty, chaotic energy.

The Nine Rings of Ruin, based on the concentration of chaotic energy within the Ninefold Immortal Tower, were divided into three major ring-shaped cultivation zones: upper, middle, and lower.

If the Lower Three Rings were layers of qi waves, and the Middle Three Rings were surging storms, then the Upper Three Rings were like an ocean!

Chaotic energy, viscous to the extreme, filled the surroundings, omnipresent and penetrating everything.

If an ordinary martial artist were here, as long as they dared to open a single aperture, they would be instantly drilled into by the chaotic energy and exploded from within.

Only a Human Immortal who had cultivated martial divine intent, whose own astral energy was full of spirituality, could autonomously repel power that did not belong to them.

This was not just a difference in will, but also a gap in the fundamental nature of power.

Within the chaotic ocean, even with the enhancement of his Crimson Eye Golden Pupils, Luo Chen could not see very far.

Fortunately, his Blood Refining Divine Astral now possessed divine intent. When spread out, it was like his own limbs and torso, allowing him to sense more things.

Although not as convenient as divine sense, it was more detailed and specific.

Just as Luo Chen released his will, three wills actively reached out to greet him.

"Luo Chen!"

"Daoist friend!"

"This way, please!"

Luo Chen did not hesitate. He followed the direction indicated by the three wills and walked leisurely.

Before long, a small island resembling a snowfield glacier appeared before him.

Luo Chen descended upon it. Three figures stood there smiling, as if they had been waiting for a long time.

Gu Xun extended a hand and took the initiative to introduce them.

"This is Fang Qingxue, from the Fang family of Wuyou City. She is also the blood descendant of Netherspring Realm Lord."

Luo Chen looked at the woman. She surprisingly had the appearance of a young girl!

This was indeed rare within the Great Destruction Realm, where most people had older features.

Even martial artists like Xiangfei and the Purple Haze City Lord, who were skilled at preserving their appearance, could at most maintain the graceful bearing of a young matron.

To have such snow-like skin and jade-like complexion, with a face so delicate it seemed it might break at a touch, in the form of a young girl—it was hard to associate her with a Martial Human Immortal.

"Luo Chen greets Fellow Daoist Fang."

Fang Qingxue seemed to notice Luo Chen's momentary surprise. She explained with a light laugh, "My actual age is already seven hundred. The reason I look young is that when I was a child, I was favored by my grandfather, who bestowed upon me an immortal beauty-preserving pill, allowing me to maintain a youthful appearance forever."

Luo Chen understood. He had guessed before that Netherspring Realm Lord was a high-level cultivator. When he had fallen into the Great Destruction Realm, he might have also carried immortal pills with him, just like Luo Chen.

A beauty-preserving pill was not surprising.

Luo Chen smiled. "In that case, I am slightly older than Fellow Daoist Fang."

"Slightly older?" The elderly Daoist beside them suddenly became curious. "May I ask how old you truly are, Fellow Daoist?"

Luo Chen looked at the elderly Daoist. He had black hair but white temples, and a face like withered wood.

Especially his eyes, which were filled with the vicissitudes of having experienced many worldly affairs.

Compared to Gu Xun and Fang Qingxue, this man was indeed much older.

"And this is?"

Before Gu Xun could introduce him, the elderly Daoist spoke up on his own. "This old man is Mu Bugui, Daoist name Wangchuan."

Wangchuan Bugui...

Luo Chen savored this person's Daoist name, then answered honestly, "Counting carefully, I am nearly eight hundred years old. Saying I am slightly older than Fellow Daoist Fang Qingxue should not be an exaggeration."

Mu Bugui asked again, "Fellow Daoist Luo should be like me, a former cultivator. What realm were you in before falling here?"

"Nascent Soul."

"Six hundred years old and already Nascent Soul?!"

Mu Bugui was extremely surprised.

Gu Xun exclaimed in admiration from the side, "Fellow Daoist Luo is truly a dragon among men. Although I do not know how remarkable it is to become Nascent Soul at six hundred, it is truly a miracle that he achieved the Martial Human Immortal realm in merely two hundred years after arriving in the Great Destruction Realm!"

Luo Chen smiled but did not speak. There might be some misunderstanding here.

When he had become Nascent Soul, he was actually just over five hundred years old.

But a little more or less didn't matter anymore.

What was important was that it had been a long time since he had heard anyone marvel at his achievements in the immortal path. This stirred a bit of melancholy within him.

Letting out a turbid breath, Luo Chen looked curiously at the three of them.

"Did you all gather here specifically to wait for me?"
