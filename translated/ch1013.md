# Chapter 1013: Power of Destruction, Ultimate Nirvana

The aura of destruction!

After arranging for Luo Lingxi, Li Yingzhang hurried toward Pill Mountain.

Along the way, he couldn't help but repeatedly glance at the increasingly violent spiritual energy tornado, his heart and liver trembling with fear.

This tornado had torn through the void itself, as if ripping apart the Sage Pill Blessed Land.

If that were all, it might have been bearable. But it had also dragged in a portion of the Netherwater and Golden Flame from the outside world.

As the spiritual energy tornado grew larger and larger, these things spread uncontrollably, and it was only a matter of time before they affected everyone in the Luo Tian Sect!

"Could it be that the Supreme Elder has encountered some deviation in his cultivation?"

Li Yingzhang believed that Luo Chen had deep feelings for the Luo Tian Sect. Otherwise, he wouldn't have taken such care of the power he had personally founded.

From the weakest Luo Tian Society, to the gradual emergence of Initiate cultivators, to the establishment of a proper sect, step by step it had grown into the renowned Luo Tian Superior Sect of the Eastern Wilderness!

If anything, the Luo Tian Sect was more like Luo Chen's true child than Luo Lingxi—if a sect could be considered a concrete "person" rather than an abstract entity.

But now, Luo Chen's actions seemed bent on destroying the entire sect!

Extremely anxious, Li Yingzhang flew on his Chiwen Sword, pushing his speed to the absolute limit.

Before he could even get close to Pill Mountain, however, he was stopped.

"Return, Sect Master Li!"

"Elder Wang Yuan?"

"All cultivators below the Sage stage must not approach Pill Mountain. This sudden change is beyond what you can contend with. Sect Master Li, quickly organize the entire sect and prepare to evacuate the Sage Pill Blessed Land!"

"Is it really that serious?"

This time, Wang Yuan offered no explanation.

Li Yingzhang cast one unwilling glance toward Pill Mountain, then immediately turned back to gather his people and prepare for evacuation.

A hundred li from Pill Mountain, Wang Yuan, Lingfengzi, and the others watched the tornado with grave expressions.

"What in the world is that thing?"

The water-fire tornado, containing vast origin qi, connected heaven and earth. Everything it touched turned to dust.

Pill Mountain itself had already been reduced to nothingness the moment the water-fire tornado appeared.

Only a blurry figure could be seen within the tornado, rising and falling, tossed about endlessly.

A large gray cauldron floated beneath him, supporting him, while a hazy gray light enveloped him.

The massive water-fire tornado continuously scoured that figure, endlessly pouring into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

It was precisely because of this that the water-fire tornado had not yet fully spread.

Lingfengzi swallowed hard and said with certainty, "He must have encountered problems in his cultivation. Otherwise, why would he put himself in such a dangerous situation?"

Canglong Fairy's eyes held a hint of worry. "The clash of water and fire, the scouring of origin qi—if this continues endlessly, Luo Chen won't be able to hold out."

Lingfengzi's face showed fear as he suggested, "Perhaps we should retreat for now! Senior Brother Luo Chen has exceptional skills, and heaven helps the virtuous. He might resolve this on his own."

At this suggestion, Canglong Fairy glared at him fiercely.

"Lingfengzi, think carefully about what you're saying!"

Lingfengzi smiled bitterly. "But what if he fails? If such vast heavenly-earthly might spreads, who among us could survive?"

Just then, Wang Yuan stepped forward.

His tall figure radiated strong confidence.

"If he succeeds, all worries are gone. If he fails, I will definitely rescue him!"

Lingfengzi was not optimistic. "How will you rescue him?"

Without hesitation, Wang Yuan replied, "With everyone working together, there is always a glimmer of hope. At that time, Canglong will activate the White Tiger Puppet, expend all its energy to blast the water-fire tornado, and try to create an opening. You will use your Wind Cave technique to suck Luo Chen outward. I will assess the situation and charge in to forcibly bring him out."

He paused, then added without turning his head, "If I don't bring him out, you can run then. Jadewater Dragon, prepare the retreat."

Canglong Fairy was shocked. "Elder Wang..."

Wang Yuan raised his hand to cut her off. "Which of us four has not received favors from Luo Chen? When great disaster looms, we must act with a clear conscience. On the other hand, if anyone tries to flee directly, don't blame me for being merciless."

Canglong Fairy took a deep breath and slowly nodded.

Lingfengzi's lips moved a few times, but in the end, he could only sigh helplessly.

Jadewater Dragon had no objections, but her eyes were filled with fear as she looked toward Luo Chen's direction.

At this moment, she finally understood the gap between herself and Luo Chen. She also understood why Luo Chen, as a human, could hold the position of Truth Crossing Hall Master at Green Parasol Mountain.

Just the water-fire tornado summoned by his cultivation could destroy an entire realm.

With such power, even if she cultivated to the late Sage stage, what could she do?

She probably wouldn't even be a match for him in a single exchange!

...

Destruction!

Why did she feel this way?

Luo Chen suddenly woke from his daze.

After three years, he had finally comprehended the Water Origin that had been within his reach.

No accidents had occurred during this process.

None of the contingency plans he had prepared were needed—it had been incredibly smooth.

But the moment he fully comprehended the Water Origin, a change occurred!

An inexplicable power, born from the interaction of Water and Fire Origins, suddenly manifested, triggering a heavenly-earthly anomaly.

This power was above even the true meaning of Water and Fire laws!

As soon as it appeared, the entire Sage Pill Blessed Land trembled.

It even crossed space, pulling at the origin qi ocean of the outside world, causing vast heavenly-earthly origin qi to descend.

This should have been a good thing.

With such immense origin qi pouring into him, once fully digested, Luo Chen's realm would inevitably skyrocket.

Not only would he cross the threshold from mid-stage to late-stage, but he might even charge straight through to the eighth or even ninth layer of the Sage stage!

But along with the vast origin qi entering the Sage Pill Blessed Land came incalculable amounts of Netherwater and the Nirvana Sacred Flame left behind by Rosycloud back then.

One defiled spiritual power, the other incinerated everything!

How could a mere Sage Pill Blessed Land bear such a burden?

Even Luo Chen himself might not be able to withstand such violent scouring.

"I have to stop this!"

A thought arose spontaneously.

Immediately, Luo Chen began to try various methods.

His first approach was to seek out that inexplicable power that had appeared earlier—after all, the one who tied the bell must untie it.

Since this anomaly had been caused by it, it should also be resolved by it.

But that power had only appeared for an instant before vanishing again.

Luo Chen could not sense its presence. In his shock and anger, there was also a hint of loss, as if he had missed a great opportunity.

"What was that?"

One thought flickered, then another arose. Luo Chen quickly stopped dwelling on it and instead employed his second method.

He unfolded his lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, to absorb as much Netherwater and Nirvana Sacred Flame as possible, lest they harm the other disciples in the blessed land.

The Netherwater was manageable—he had completely refined the Netherwater Spring Eye over the past three years, so he could absorb it very smoothly.

It was as if no matter how much came, he could take it all.

But the Nirvana Sacred Flame was far too troublesome.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron could absorb it, but not in large quantities, or it would in turn destroy his lifebound treasure.

"If I release the Nirvana Fire Spirit I cultivated myself, can I absorb Rosycloud's Nirvana Sacred Flame?"

Luo Chen's heart stirred. In his Purple Mansion, the Nirvana Fire Spirit had already begun to spread its wings, eager to soar.

But his gaze flickered, and he forcibly suppressed this impulse.

Intuition!

He had a feeling that if he used his Nirvana Fire Spirit to absorb someone else's Nirvana Sacred Flame, it would definitely harm him more than help.

"Then I'll absorb it another way!"

Luo Chen's hand moved, and a fan appeared in his palm.

The handle was like jade, the ribs like colored glaze, and when fully opened, it resembled a blood-red coral.

This was the Blood Jade Three-Flame Fan, a gift from Green Parasol Mountain after he became the Truth Crossing Hall Master.

A pseudo-spirit treasure, containing the Fire Origin, capable of holding three different types of flames.

The stronger the flames it held, the greater its power.

If it held only ordinary fourth-order flames, when released and fused together, it would be nearly invincible within the Sage stage.

If it held a fifth-order divine flame, its power could even threaten Ascendant-level experts!

Luo Chen had never tested it, but now there was no time to hesitate.

Injecting his spiritual power, which contained the Fire Origin, the Blood Jade Three-Flame Fan immediately unfolded and began to devour large amounts of the Nirvana Sacred Flame descending from the sky.

It was working!

A look of joy appeared on Luo Chen's face.

But soon, that joy disappeared.

There was too much!

Even though decades had passed and much of the Nirvana Sacred Flame in the Nether Abyss had dissipated, the remaining amount was still too much for the Blood Jade Three-Flame Fan.

In just a moment, he felt a sense of fullness in the treasure in his hand, as if it would burst at any moment.

"No, I need another way!"

Luo Chen murmured, looking up at the sky.

"This world cannot possibly bestow endless heavenly-earthly origin qi upon me. Rather than passively resist, I should actively receive it."

"The Netherwater and Golden Flame were only dragged in by the flow. If I can resolve the massive origin qi infusion, I can resolve the current crisis."

Without further hesitation, Luo Chen began to silently recite the *Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Incantation*.

In an instant, his primordial spirit manifested!

Like a magnificent canopy, the primordial spirit hovered above his tiny body, facing the heavenly might.

Endless origin qi poured into the primordial spirit, making it increasingly solid.

This time, Luo Chen held on for the time it takes to drink a cup of tea.

With a muffled groan, the primordial spirit, now carrying a massive amount of origin qi, was forced back into his body, splitting in two.

His Sage embryo returned to the Purple Mansion, and his soul returned to the sea of consciousness.

His body, which had always felt as light as a swallow and completely under his control, now gave Luo Chen a feeling of heaviness and fullness.

He looked at the sky in despair.

Although the origin qi tornado had shrunk by more than half under his successive efforts, it was still continuing!

This was terrifying!

When comprehending the true meaning of a law for the first time, one would receive a gift from heaven, drawing a small mass of origin qi from the origin qi ocean to refine into one's own spiritual power.

But just a small mass!

How could it be like this—vast, boundless, seemingly endless?

This time, he had comprehended the Water Origin, which didn't even align well with his Fire Spirit Body. It absolutely should not have exceeded the heavenly-earthly origin qi brought by his previous comprehension of the Fire Origin.

"It must have been caused by that power that appeared earlier."

Luo Chen vaguely guessed that the power born from the clash of water and fire might be of a higher rank than basic law true meanings like the Water Origin.

He had missed the opportunity to comprehend and grasp it, so it had flashed and vanished.

But the "blessing" it had summoned had now become his doom.

If this origin qi infusion continued, he would be burst apart alive, and the entire Sage Pill Blessed Land would suffer a devastating blow.

"Is there any other way?"

Luo Chen's thoughts raced as he frantically considered other options.

This heavenly-earthly origin qi infusion was aimed at him.

As long as he was alive, it would not stop.

And for a cultivator, the basis for existence was only one thing—the soul!

"Yes!"

"If I can conceal my soul's aura, I might be able to deceive this world and end this infusion. Even if some of the origin qi tornado remains, I can rely on my physical body to withstand it for a moment."

"Concealing the soul..."

Luo Chen "exerted all his strength" to mobilize a trace of spiritual power and performed a concealment technique.

But the effect was minimal.

The origin qi tornado shrank only slightly before expanding again.

In the face of such overwhelming heavenly-earthly might, this kind of concealment was truly crude. The effect achieved was less due to Luo Chen's own concealment technique and more to the inherent shielding properties of the Sage Pill Blessed Land itself.

In fact, as long as one was within this world, no concealment technique could escape the gaze of the Heavenly Dao's will.

"But... it did have some effect!"

Amidst his despair, a glimmer of hope was born in Luo Chen.

He had another method!

One that could completely hide—no, directly erase his soul. Even if only for an instant, an instant would be enough.

It all depended on whether he was willing to gamble.

If he failed, everything would be over.

If he succeeded, he would not only escape this great calamity but also reap endless benefits in the future.

In the end, Luo Chen made up his mind.

"I took a shortcut to avoid that fate back then, but now I have no choice but to face it."

"So be it!"

"I'll take the gamble. I've long had no way back anyway."

With a soft sigh, Luo Chen sat lightly in the Primordial Unity Cauldron. His left thumb pressed against his ring finger, forming the Seal of Cessation. His right hand formed a palm, with thumb, little finger, and ring finger all bent, middle finger half-curled, and only his index finger raised, gently touching his brow.

To place oneself in death and seek life—that is nirvana!

To be far from upside-down dreams, with a pure heart and few desires—that is also nirvana!

To merge with the mystery of things, to return to the limitless—that is ultimate nirvana!

The methods he had once comprehended now surged into his mind like a spring. In the next moment, a golden stream of light rushed out from Luo Chen's Purple Mansion, instantly transforming into a phoenix that enveloped his entire body.

Beneath the burning golden flames, Luo Chen's eyes suddenly widened, then closed forever.

In his sea of consciousness, the eight divided souls shattered in an instant, transforming into the purest soul power that merged into the main soul.

And the main soul, once the most solid and powerful, also began to shatter at this moment.

Bits and pieces of soul fragments rippled across the golden waves of the sea of consciousness, gradually fading to nothing.

At that very moment.

Heaven and earth trembled. The wind ceased, and the clouds scattered.

The tornado that had connected heaven and earth, as if having lost its target, collapsed with a crash, slamming heavily into the Sage Pill Blessed Land.

Netherwater gurgled forth, forming a vast black lake in the sunken ruins of Pill Mountain.

Golden flames drifted like rootless duckweed, not knowing where to go. But soon, as if drawn by some attraction, they instinctively headed toward the large gray cauldron.

Wang Yuan and the others stared in shock at this scene, completely unable to react.

Just moments ago, Luo Chen had still been making various movements. But in an instant, his aura had vanished.

Vanished... aura!

Everyone's expression changed dramatically!

(Chapter End)
