# Chapter 618: Perfect Compatibility, the Power of Blood, Learning by Analogy, a New Body-Tempering Art

On Peng Island.

One year had already passed since Luo Chen announced his seclusion.

This year, countless immortal cultivators seeking Dao-protecting treasures had tried to visit, but all were refused at the door.

The disturbances of the outside world seemed no longer connected to Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake.

But clearly, how could the outside world ignore a master-level existence capable of refining defensive magic treasures?

Even though Luo Chen claimed to be in seclusion, countless people were still paying attention to his movements, paying attention to whether he could refine a second defensive magic treasure.

If he truly refined one, it would prove he truly had that ability, and that the first had not been an accidental gain through luck.

Facing the outside world's expectations and doubts, Luo Chen seemed unaware.

Inside the alchemy-and-forging hall.

Luo Chen watched the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which was heavily surrounded by blazing Withering-Bloom True Flame. His eyes were incomparably bright, the flames reflected in them like golden fire pupils.

"This time, nurturing the magic treasure embryo has already taken a full year, and the preparations are even greater than when refining the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor."

"Even an ugly daughter-in-law must meet her parents-in-law eventually. It is time."

After calculating the time in his heart, Luo Chen raised a hand and struck out a stream of mana.

Immediately, the azure flame dispersed, and a yellowish light flew out of the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Looking closely, it was a yellow suit of armor a full seven chi tall.

Rich earth-attribute spiritual qi accumulated upon it. With a single glance, it gave people a feeling of solidity and reliability.

On the armor, there were no patterns and no cracks, as if it were a seamless heavenly garment.

But Luo Chen knew clearly that this was only the embryo. Whether it could succeed depended on the formation engraving that came next.

With a thought, the yellow armor floated before him.

To his left, a basin of specially made black liquid had long since been prepared.

Formation engraving during the refining of magic treasures was very different from ordinary formation arrangement.

When arranging formations normally, one borrowed spirit stones, formation flags, even the advantages of mountains and rivers. In short, anything that could be used would be used as much as possible to increase the formation's power.

But refining magic treasures was different. A magic treasure's body was only so large and could not contain too many external objects.

Therefore, if one wanted to engrave formations on it, one needed something similar to ink.

This black liquid, called formation ink liquid, was the most commonly used formation-arranging ink in the cultivation world.

Speaking of which, back when Min Longyu had made him study calligraphy before learning formations, it now seemed to have a similar spirit.

Luo Chen smiled faintly. His right hand trembled and turned slightly crimson. As Luo Chen's mana surged, his right hand grew redder and redder, as if congested with blood.

On the attribute panel, the Iron-Refining Hand, already cultivated to Great Perfection, had activated.

"When conventional formation masters assist artifact-forgers in forging magic instruments, they use talisman brushes and ink awls to engrave formations. From the Hundred Craft Mountain classics, however, I learned this Iron-Refining Hand method. It can be used to smelt metal ores, and it can also be used to lay formations on magic treasure embryos, even achieving twice the result with half the effort compared to conventional formation masters."

"That Hundred Craft Mountain could stand on Cold Light Island for many years without falling truly means it had some substance."

"Unfortunately, Cold Light Island was destroyed. As for those forces that once surrounded and tried to kill me, I do not know how many survived. Otherwise, after my realm advanced, I would have wanted to visit them personally."

As thoughts turned in his mind, the red color on his right hand reached its limit, as if it were a red-hot branding iron.

The Fine Dust Primordial Art revolved naturally, cutting away stray thoughts.

With complete focus, Luo Chen extended his right hand into the iron bucket. Immediately, hissing sounds rang out.

When he raised his right hand, a thick layer of formation ink liquid had already stained it.

Luo Chen's mind held no distractions. His mana and divine sense gathered entirely in one place, and his right hand slowly landed on the armor.

As his fingers turned, black formation lines landed on the soft armor one after another.

In fact, because he applied more force, the black formation lines had already pierced through the surface and begun seeping into the interior of this Bright Yellow Boulder Armor.

What he was engraving now was a first-tier formation called the Solid Gold Array, also the most basic formation needed by many defensive magic treasures, capable of reinforcing the material stability of the magic treasure's body.

Apart from this, two other first-tier formations still needed to be engraved: the Spirit-Locking Array and the Qi-Gathering Array.

After the first-tier formations came the second-tier Feather-Scale Array and Mysterious Gate Array.

Finally, there was the most core third-tier Bright Yellow Boulder Armor Array.

Three first-tier formations, two second-tier formations, one third-tier formation. Layer upon layer, they covered every part of the magic treasure, finally forming an unbreakable pure defensive magic treasure.

In this process, not the slightest failure could be allowed.

With the slightest error, all previous work would be wasted. In severe cases, it could even cause the magic treasure embryo to self-destruct.

If the magic treasure embryo's own material were good, perhaps its quality could barely preserve one or two parts of its power, becoming one of those so-called supreme-grade magic instruments.

But clearly, the materials Luo Chen used for practice could not be that good.

Although the cost was not high, if it were destroyed, Luo Chen would still feel terrible.

The spirit stone cost was not high, but the time cost was.

Other people refined directly when they had suitable materials.

He, however, had used the Primordial Unity Cauldron to nurture this magic treasure embryo for a full year.

He did not feel pain over spirit stones. He felt pain over that year.

Fortunately, mere first-tier and second-tier formations basically had no possibility of failure under his Great Perfection-level formation proficiency.

Unlike alchemy proficiency, formation proficiency leaned more toward spells. If one knew it, one knew it. If one did not, one did not. It did not have the remaining failure rate that still existed after alchemy reached perfection.

Spending two days, Luo Chen finally successfully engraved three sets of first-tier formations and two sets of second-tier formations onto the Bright Yellow Boulder Armor embryo.

Next came the most important and most core Bright Yellow Boulder Armor Array.

Luo Chen did not rest for even a moment. He merely adjusted his mindset, then activated the Iron-Refining Hand again, stained it with formation ink liquid, and began drawing new formation lines on the armor.

This time, unlike the previous silence, things were different.

The arrangement of a third-tier formation had overly harsh requirements.

Even a powerful formation master, arranging it in the outside world, would need corresponding formation flags, formation plates, and even cooperation from the terrain.

To engrave it on a mere weapon required even harsher conditions.

Not only did it test the formation master's formation attainment, it also required considering the material's own strength and even demanded attention to the compatibility between the formation and magic treasure.

"This embryo's material is average. I do not know whether it can withstand the complete Bright Yellow Boulder Armor Array."

Under Luo Chen's somewhat worried mood, formation lines gradually landed on the armor from his hand.

As strands of formation lines were engraved, Luo Chen became increasingly astonished.

"It is actually this compatible."

"Are these earth-attribute mineral materials resonating with the Bright Yellow Boulder Armor Array? Or is the embryo I refined too perfect?"

"No. This feeling seemed to appear when I refined the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor last time too. Back then, I only thought it was inspiration arriving by fortune, but now that the scene has appeared again, clearly it is not an isolated case."

The compatibility between magic treasure embryo and formation was so perfect that Luo Chen almost lost focus.

But in an instant, he adjusted and continued the movements in his hand.

It took one day.

The originally plain earth-yellow armor, with no patterns or decorations at all, began to reveal profound and extraordinary markings after Luo Chen arranged the formation.

Those were the external manifestations of the formation.

After the Bright Yellow Boulder Armor Array was engraved upon it with exceptional smoothness, Luo Chen finally let out a breath.

"Next is the final nurturing, allowing the formation and the material itself to fuse into one. During this process, rejection reactions often appear. Either the material itself cannot adapt to the powerful formation, or under activation, innate flaws appear."

"This cannot be ignored."

Luo Chen carefully looked over the armor and once again placed it into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

The Withering-Bloom True Flame, which had been extinguished before, burned again.

Luo Chen took a pill, sat cross-legged before the Primordial Unity Cauldron, and meditated.

While regulating his breathing and restoring mana, he watched the Primordial Unity Cauldron and savored the feeling from before.

A magic treasure embryo nurtured long-term by the Primordial Unity Cauldron had perfect compatibility with formations. This sort of strange thing was almost unheard of in the cultivation world.

Why had he alone experienced it twice?

"Is it because of my natal magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron?"

"But I have never heard that the Primordial Unity Cauldrons refined by Green Pill Valley in the past had a similar effect. At most, they could only increase pill completion rates slightly."

"Could it be an effect my Primordial Unity Cauldron gained after being nurtured by the Dragon-Nurturing Basin?"

As he thought bitterly, a sudden flash of inspiration crossed Luo Chen's mind.

In his memory, a certain scene leapt up.

Years ago, inside Cangwu Mountain's dungeon, Heaven Smelter had refined the Primordial Unity Cauldron for him.

In the hands of such a famous artifact-forging master, refining a mere magic treasure should have been easy.

Yet an accident had occurred.

That accident caused all the mineral materials Luo Chen had collected over many years to be consumed, and they were even somewhat insufficient. Heaven Smelter had also asked Azure Frost to act, using the power of spatial law to stabilize the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

And the cause of that accident was strange blood that had stained a fire spirit diamond.

Luo Chen had once been curious, but after thinking bitterly, he had found no result.

Especially after Heaven Smelter later broke through the restriction, Luo Chen had once thought that Heaven Smelter, this artifact-forging grandmaster, had deliberately done it and guided that accident in order to make Azure Frost Demon Emperor act and thereby probe Azure Frost Demon Emperor's spatial restriction.

But looking at it now, that had not been Heaven Smelter's plan. It had been a genuine accident.

"When that blood merged into my Primordial Unity Cauldron, it madly fused with all sorts of materials, refusing nothing that came."

"If it were an ordinary magic treasure, with so many unprocessed materials fused together, it would have self-destructed long ago due to rejection reactions."

"Yet my Primordial Unity Cauldron was completely unharmed. When the cauldron formed, it was already upper-grade. After years of nurturing, it has even grown stronger and stronger. Now, even I cannot bring out one hundred percent of its power."

"Clearly, that drop of strange blood has a unique influence on all kinds of mineral materials."

"So even the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which fused with it, gained a similar effect. Magic treasure embryos nurtured by it can be called incomparably perfect, so even engraved formations can be matched with wonderful compatibility."

His memories kept turning, and Luo Chen's thoughts grew clearer and clearer.

Although the Dragon-Nurturing Basin could nurture magic treasures, raise grade, and increase power, it did so on the original foundation of the magic treasure being nurtured and could not create something from nothing.

The special effect of his Primordial Unity Cauldron clearly did not come from the Dragon-Nurturing Basin's credit, but from that drop of strange blood.

After sorting all this out, Luo Chen suddenly understood, but his heart was also filled with questions.

What living being's blood was that?

Just one drop actually had such strange power.

If this blood had a master, what realm would that master be, or what race?

This time, even after Luo Chen used the knowledge he had accumulated over many years to speculate for a long while, he could not search out any related information.

Regarding this, he could only sigh.

"Knowing that it is so, but not knowing why, truly scratches at the heart."

"Still, for me, this is ultimately a good thing."

It was indeed a good thing.

If this Bright Yellow Boulder Armor could be refined successfully, it meant his success could be replicated.

In future refinement of the Demon Emperor shed husk, and even the future refinement of other magic treasures, it would be like adding wings to a tiger, with no more possibility of failure.

If such a thing spread, the entire cultivation world would shake.

For a time, Luo Chen's attention was not even on the Bright Yellow Boulder Armor currently fusing with the formation, but on this natal magic treasure of his.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron, an object he cultivated with both life and fate, carried infinite possibilities.

...

Half a month later.

Luo Chen opened the qi barrier sealing the Primordial Unity Cauldron and took out the flawless Bright Yellow Boulder Armor from within.

Using a newly engraved Ruyi Array, he shrank it into a yellow armor plate, as if it were a clod of earth.

"The qi mechanism is restrained inward. The formation and material are perfectly fused, without any rejection reaction."

"Inner armor and qi armor arise naturally, matching the description of success in that Penglai Immortal Sect refining method."

"It is done."

These final two calm words could express the joy in Luo Chen's heart.

But clearly, his current joy was no longer only because he had successfully refined a magic treasure.

It was because of the Primordial Unity Cauldron's miraculous effect.

For a moment, he had a great impulse to directly refine the Demon Emperor shed husk.

But in the end, he restrained himself.

"I still have not explored where the upper limit of the Primordial Unity Cauldron's wondrous effect on artifact-forging lies. What if it only works on ordinary materials and lower-grade magic treasures?"

"The previous plan cannot change. I should proceed step by step."

"No rush."

With no urgency in his heart, Luo Chen became increasingly composed in other matters.

Qi refining, body tempering, formation practice, artifact-forging. Every day's time was arranged fully.

When the third item, the Yimu Light Armor Array, was successfully refined in the second year, Luo Chen even gained something in body tempering by analogy.

Fusing all sorts of originally ordinary materials together, removing the dross, taking the essence, combining them with formations, and achieving a magic treasure of boundless power. This was the meaning of artifact-forging.

If the human body were also regarded as a magic treasure?

Could one then use artifact-forging methods to refine the physique?

To refine the body into a powerful magic treasure?

This insight did not come abruptly. It arose naturally after long accumulation.

At the same time, there was also a scene in his mind that would not fade: Heaven Smelter, unable to activate Nascent Soul power, using his fleshly body to withstand the mid fourth-tier Thunder Prison Divine Peng.

One had to know, body refiners in the current cultivation world had long since declined.

Yet Heaven Smelter, at the Nascent Soul realm, still had quite impressive body-tempering cultivation. He must also have comprehended a corresponding body-tempering method.

If Luo Chen knew that Heaven Smelter had even used his physique to receive a palm strike from Azure Frost Demon Emperor years ago, his evaluation of Heaven Smelter's body-tempering cultivation would probably be three parts higher.

Of course, even without knowing this, Luo Chen began making suitable adjustments to his body-tempering cultivation method in his spare time.

Myriad Path Convergence was originally self-created by him with the help of the system.

Even the name had been changed later, taking the meaning of drawing on the strengths of hundreds of schools and returning ten thousand methods to one source.

Although he had already cultivated this method to Great Perfection many years ago, in Luo Chen's view, this method still had room for improvement.

He began trying to merge the Hundred-Tempering Method, Iron-Refining Hand, Gold-Cutting Art, Snow-Blowing Method, and other artifact-forging arts into it.

At the same time, borrowing the fourth-tier Black Emperor Ointment, he experimentally tempered his physique.

In fact, if he did not lack suitable formations, he would even have wanted to engrave some formations on his body.

This sort of thing was not something he had never done before. Even now, his body still had a Concealment Array that hid mana fluctuations.

And truly, under his constant adjustments, although a new Myriad Path Convergence had not yet been born, his body-tempering progress had indeed improved significantly.

Originally, he had already reached Archaic Tier Three 78/100. Now, at even greater speed, he advanced toward the eighty mark on the progress bar.

One could imagine that he was not far from Archaic Tier Four.

...

As Luo Chen focused on artifact-forging and cultivation, the outside world's attention toward him rose another level.

Because the Bright Yellow Boulder Armor had been placed on Azure Yang Hall's shelves.

It was called the shop-suppressing treasure, and it would not be sold for less than five hundred thousand spirit stones.

Because of this absurdly high price, and because Master Daoist Azure Yang had made a second move, it drew countless attention within the Myriad Immortals Assembly for a time.

*(End of Chapter)*
