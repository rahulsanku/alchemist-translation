# Chapter 791: Rest and Recuperation, Sima's Calamity

"Cloud-like hair, flower-like face, golden steps that sway; behind the lotus-fragrance bed-curtain, a spring night is spent. The spring night is too short, the sun rises too high; from then on, the sovereign holds no more morning court."

On the wide, soft bed, the man murmured two lines of a little poem from his homeland, the corners of his mouth lifting slightly as if savoring the memory.

He glanced left and right at the two jade-like faces, bearing traces of weariness and satisfaction. Luo Chen smiled faintly, gently pushed aside the smooth arm draped across his chest, lifted the plump thigh pressing down on him, and slowly sat up.

Taking care not to disturb the two women as much as possible, Luo Chen covered them with a thin blanket before getting off the bed.

Casually draping a silk robe over himself, letting his hair hang loose down his back, he walked barefoot out of this warm nest.

As he walked, the cold floor tiles gradually sobered Luo Chen.

Reaching the mountain peak, thin mist and thick clouds pushed forward with the morning mountain wind, then continuously dispersed.

Luo Chen released his divine sense, silently covering a hundred li of green hills.

It was still early.

But within the Luo Tian Sect, many cultivators had already begun their activities.

Some were in the logistics department preparing meals, some were stretching their limbs doing physical training, and another group of cultivators had taken the initiative to clean up the mess left from yesterday's grand ceremony.

Looking at it all, one word stood out: *relaxed*!

No longer was there that urgent, pressing feeling that had hung over everyone's head at any time before.

And all of this was brought about by Luo Chen.

Although the hundred-li green hills were small, and the Luo Tian Sect was not large, under this relaxed rhythm, it gradually presented a scene of thriving vitality and burgeoning life.

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his divine sense, a satisfied smile on his face.

This was exactly what he wanted to see.

The Luo Tian Sect of today truly could not afford any more turmoil.

However, some matters still needed to be delegated.

He waited at the summit of Daqing Mountain for a moment, then saw a figure riding the clouds upward.

Tall hat, broad sash, a Chi Dragon sword hanging at his waist.

It was none other than the current Luo Tian Sect Sect Leader, Li Yingzhang!

"Yingzhang pays respects to the Supreme Elder!"

Li Yingzhang bowed respectfully in greeting, his manner flawless.

Luo Chen naturally paid no mind to such formalities. He smiled and said, "I called you here for nothing major, just a few things to instruct you on."

Li Yingzhang's expression turned serious, listening attentively.

Luo Chen paced back and forth, speaking slowly.

"The Luo Tian Sect must now rest and recuperate. The Golden Core cultivators need to consolidate their realms and regulate their bodies. The hidden injuries from years of battle should also be treated. The Foundation Establishment cultivators should solidify their foundations and practice their techniques, preparing for their future push to the Golden Core stage. They need not worry about Core Formation resources; I can fully support them here. As for those Qi Refinement juniors, pick and choose—select those with some talent and good character. The sect will support their Foundation Establishment."

"Fenghua Palace has already agreed to let Qu Lingjun return to the Luo Tian Sect, and our two sects have grown closer. Maintain contact with them in the future."

"As for the White Crane Sect, we have also formed an alliance with them, advancing and retreating together from now on. Pay attention and arrange for someone to take delivery of the spirit cranes sent by the White Crane Sect, and prepare the corresponding spirit stones."

"The Divine Talisman Sect is now residing in the Hundred Beasts Domain, and they are the ones performing patrol duties at Lingtian Pass, correct? Their Supreme Elder has promised me ten thousand talisman skins, but they must be delivered in batches. Keep an eye on that."

"In the future, many people will likely come to visit me, mostly those of high cultivation. Instruct the mountain-guarding disciples to be careful not to offend these individuals."

"As for the patrol duties at Lingtian Pass, there's no rush for now. The Lingtian City Lord has granted an extension. I will personally lead the team when the time comes."

"Within the sect..."

Item by item, matter by matter, they flowed from Luo Chen's mouth into Li Yingzhang's ears.

Though quite complicated, Li Yingzhang committed them all firmly to memory.

He couldn't help but marvel inwardly at Luo Chen's capability. In such a short time since his return, he had completely reversed the precarious situation of the Luo Tian Sect.

Not only had they gained a foothold in the Fenghua Domain, but they had also secured the friendship of old-established sects like the White Crane Sect and the Divine Talisman Sect.

This was something they could never have achieved, no matter how hard they managed or fought!

Of course, while listening, as the current Sect Leader, some potential issues still had to be raised.

"Currently, the Alchemy Hall is managed by Qi Yi. When Qu Lingjun returns, how should he be arranged?"

Luo Chen said calmly, "He is not to enter the Alchemy Hall. Let him choose a place for quiet cultivation on his own."

Li Yingzhang was stunned. Although Qi Yi's cultivation had later surpassed Qu Lingjun's, in the Dao of alchemy, Qu Lingjun, who had received Luo Chen's true teachings, was still far more skilled than Qi Yi. Logically, upon his return, he should resume his position as head of the Alchemy Hall.

Especially since, over the years, Qu Lingjun's loyalty to the Luo Tian Sect had been proven.

"Supreme Elder, isn't this a bit..."

"Just do as I say!"

"Yes!"

Li Yingzhang dared not argue further and immediately changed the subject.

"The spirit cranes of the White Crane Sect are renowned far and wide, not only for their swift flight but also for their great intelligence. It's naturally good that you could get them to sell to us, but as for spirit stones, our sect is currently a bit strapped."

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

After a detailed inquiry, he learned of the Luo Tian Sect's current financial predicament.

Nine Golden Core cultivators, two third-tier Body Refiners, and dozens of Foundation Establishment True Cultivators had almost completely drained the Luo Tian Sect's savings.

All the hard-won spoils from years of fighting had also been poured into them.

Otherwise, yesterday's Nascent Soul Ceremony for Luo Chen wouldn't have been arranged so... *modestly*.

Now, to take out a lump sum of spirit stones to purchase fifty spirit cranes at once was truly a difficult task for even the most skilled housewife without rice.

This problem wouldn't have been an issue if Luo Chen hadn't left the Northern Sea back then.

At that time, his personal wealth was in the hundreds of millions; he never took spirit stones seriously.

But after several transactions, converting spirit stones into large batches of cultivation resources, he was now also broke.

He did have a few top-grade spirit stones, but using top-grade spirit stones to buy spirit cranes would be a waste of the family fortune just to talk about.

Moreover, the lack of spirit stones was clearly just a surface problem.

The deeper issue was that the Luo Tian Sect now had no stable source of income!

In times of chaos, everyone was just trying to survive. How many forces could continue to generate wealth like the Divine Talisman Sect?

After some thought, Luo Chen came up with a solution.

"Accept the spirit cranes from the White Crane Sect for now. As for the spirit stones... put it on credit!"

"Get through this period. The Luo Tian Sect having fewer people also has its advantages—at least everyone's hearts are united. Everyone can tighten their belts, and we'll improve things bit by bit later."

Li Yingzhang hadn't expected Luo Chen's solution to be like this.

But since he said so, he accepted it. At worst, he would just have to be thick-skinned when keeping the debt accounts.

Before he left, Luo Chen added another instruction.

"Have all Foundation Establishment cultivators, as well as the outstanding disciples among the late-stage Qi Refinement, learn the most basic methods of beast taming!"

...

After finishing these matters, the sun was already high in the sky.

The Fenghua Domain was on the colder side, and the hundred-li green hills hadn't been well managed, nor had any formations to regulate temperature been set up. When Luo Chen returned to the simply constructed palace, he could still feel the chill in the air.

Fortunately, the rising sun brought some warmth to this cold late autumn.

Inside the hall, there were rustling sounds.

Luo Chen walked in, and two pairs of eyes immediately looked at him, then shyly looked away.

Sharing a bed and sleeping three together was, after all, somewhat absurd.

Especially since the two women usually didn't have a very harmonious relationship on the surface.

Luo Chen smiled faintly and spoke directly: "Caiyi!"

Gu Caiyi looked up at Luo Chen.

A jade slip flew in front of her.

"You don't need to concern yourself with sect affairs. Go into seclusion and cultivate quietly, consolidate your realm. The jade slip contains my reorganized *Fine Dust Primordial Art*. This technique is no longer limited to forming a Golden Core; it will also greatly benefit your cultivation."

Gu Caiyi took the jade slip with delight.

"Also, do you have any thoughts on a lifebound magic treasure?"

Gu Caiyi quickly nodded. Among the Luo Tian Sect's Golden Core cultivators, Fu Jiusheng and Chu Kui had formed their cores early and had long since prepared their lifebound treasures.

Li Yingzhang's lifebound treasure was the family heirloom Chi Dragon Sword.

Even Yang Pingwei, who had joined the Luo Tian Sect later, had suitable treasures left from the Myriad Manifestations Sect.

Only she lacked a suitable magic treasure.

"Husband, I want a treasure that is both offensive and defensive. A flying red silk sleeve would be best, followed by a brocade handkerchief or a long whip. A flying sword is the last choice."

After saying this, she sighed.

"Unfortunately, such treasures are quite niche. Even when occasionally obtained, they often retain a large number of imprints from their original owners and are not suitable for my mana."

Luo Chen chuckled, "No matter. In a while, I will personally refine one for you."

Gu Caiyi's eyes widened. She didn't quite understand this statement.

Could her husband also refine artifacts?

Luo Chen then looked at Sima Huiniang. "Huiniang, you should move around the sect more in the coming days and assist Li Yingzhang. He has only recently taken over as Sect Leader, and even if he is capable, he is not yet familiar with the relevant affairs. Yao Mingyue is going into seclusion to cultivate and attempt a breakthrough to the Golden Core realm, so I can only rely on you."

Sima Huiniang hummed in acknowledgment, accepting the task.

From beginning to end, Luo Chen never mentioned her cultivation.

Until the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu inside her body was resolved, Sima Huiniang could not cultivate easily, or else something might go wrong.

"Next, I will be refining a batch of Nascent Soul Pills for the Divine Talisman Sect!"

Both women's expressions shifted, and they looked at Luo Chen with curiosity.

Luo Chen smiled confidently. "The difficulty of refining this pill is a trivial matter to me. But if I can succeed, it will be a major event—for me, for the Divine Talisman Sect, for the Luo Tian Sect, and even for the entire Eastern Wilderness cultivation world! Therefore, it cannot be delayed."

Sima Huiniang seemed to understand. "Husband, you want to use this opportunity to solidify your status as a Grandmaster of the Alchemy Dao, making all the Eastern Wilderness powerhouses take you seriously, right?"

"Knowing me, Huiniang!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily, reaching out and making a grasping motion towards the ground.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron, which he had secretly buried beneath Daqing Mountain as the formation's central hub to suppress the Nascent Soul Ceremony, rapidly shrank and flew towards him with a whirring sound.

"No matter how eloquently I speak, it's just empty talk. Only by actually refining a fourth-tier pill will those Nascent Soul Sages believe in my ability!"

"When that time comes, with my peerless alchemy skills, I will build a vast network of connections, and I will have friends all over the Eastern Wilderness."

"Even the Nether Origin Sect... won't be able to do anything to me!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, his eyes full of confidence.

This was his method for dealing with the Nether Origin Sect's impending inquiry.

By establishing a great reputation and using his alchemy skills as capital, even if the matter of his past service to the Green Parasol Mountain demon clan were to be exposed, the Nether Origin Sect would not easily kill him.

After all, a Golden Core stage Alchemy Grandmaster and an Nascent Soul stage Alchemy Grandmaster were two different concepts.

Especially since the Nether Origin Sect had lost a figure like the Alchemy Saint!

This was nothing more than a repeat of an old trick for Luo Chen—simply making good use of the weapon of "reputation."

After giving his instructions on all matters, Luo Chen did not linger in the warmth of the boudoir. Instead, he went to begin preparations for refining the Nascent Soul Pill.

As he left, Sima Huiniang seemed to want to say something but stopped, ultimately saying nothing.

Gu Caiyi, who had finished tidying up, saw this scene.

"Why didn't you mention that wretched matter?"

Sima Huiniang lowered her head. "Husband is very busy now. I don't want to distract him."

Gu Caiyi said coldly, "So, have you decided? To kill or to release?"

"Releasing is certainly not an option. But killing him..." Sima Huiniang bit her lip, pain showing in her eyes. "He is, after all, my own younger brother!"

Gu Caiyi frowned deeply. "But he betrayed the Luo Tian Sect."

Sima Huiniang whispered, "I wronged him first."

"You weren't so soft-hearted before!"

"And I am no longer the Sect Leader of the Luo Tian Sect now!"

Gu Caiyi shook her head. Such matters were truly difficult to judge.

Before leaving, she hesitated for a moment, then said, "Do as you wish. Husband brought Sima Wenjie back but has ignored him, clearly leaving everything for you to handle. No matter what, he won't blame you."

Sima Huiniang opened her mouth, then lowered her head dejectedly.

...

Three days later.

Three figures entered the underground prison of Daqing Mountain together.

Sima Wenjie saw the three people.

Li Yingzhang, Min Longyu, and his own elder sister.

He knew that his judgment had finally arrived.

Strangely, the fear that had been hanging over his heart calmed down at this moment.

Under the light of the night pearl, Sima Huiniang's restored, rosy face made his heart even more settled.

Because of this face, because of the grand ceremony at the Luo Tian Sect before, he understood that his sister's persistence was right, and his own wavering was wrong.

Since that was the case, everything before was his own fault.

Sima Wenjie did not beg for mercy. He just smiled and said:

"Second Sister, he cured you. You're even more beautiful than before."

Sima Huiniang bit her lip, her body trembling slightly. Finally, she nodded to Min Longyu.

"I'll trouble you."

Min Longyu twitched the corner of his mouth. "Leave it to me. It will just take a few days."

Sima Huiniang took one last look at her brother, then walked out of the dungeon with Li Yingzhang.

After they came out, a suppressed, low, painful roar came from inside.

Hearing that sound, the woman covered her mouth.

Li Yingzhang sighed. "Having once reached the Golden Core realm, crippling him is crueler than killing him! If it were me, I would rather give him a quick death."

Sima Huiniang lowered her head, her expression unreadable.

"I am no longer the Sect Leader now."

Not being the leader of a sect meant she couldn't act with swift, decisive measures?

Li Yingzhang didn't understand. He only looked at the woman's desolate back, vaguely realizing that her current identity was the Dao companion of the Supreme Elder, the elder sister of the man inside enduring the Core-Dissolving Formation, and no longer the Sect Leader who could place righteousness above family loyalty and only look at the sect's interests.

He thought for a moment and said, "I will arrange a position for him within the sect. Living out his life in obscurity and dying of old age won't be a problem. What do you think of a job sweeping and cleaning the Scripture Library?"

Sima Huiniang was stunned for a moment, then slowly nodded.

"Thank you!"

The Scripture Library was where Wang Yuan was stationed.

It was the place with the fewest people, the quietest, where no one would point fingers at him. And with Wang Yuan watching over him, the crippled Sima Wenjie couldn't cause any more trouble.

This was already the best outcome for him.

Li Yingzhang accepted the thanks calmly. He wouldn't do something like a new official burning his first three fires, but selling a favor did no harm.

Besides, wasn't this matter perhaps tacitly approved by Luo Chen?

Walking through the dark, damp tunnel, just as the light of day became visible, Li Yingzhang suddenly said:

"Tomorrow, Qu Lingjun should be returning, right!"

...

Yes, everything was returning.

But unexpectedly, Qu Lingjun did not return alone.

His Dao companion, Meng Qiner, accompanied him all the way.
