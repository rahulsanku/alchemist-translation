# Chapter 867: On the Eve of Expansion, Tao Wan Comes to Seek Refuge

"Senior Brother, I've received the expansion decree!"

Accompanied by an excited voice, Divine Fire Sage hurried in from outside.

In his hand, he held a black scroll, his face full of joy.

"Senior Brother, we can return to the Five Elements Domain!"

Yet this surge of excitement inexplicably dissipated when it met the grim formation before him.

Divine Origin Senior Brother was still in seclusion, refusing to see anyone.

Divine Fire Sage felt a hint of displeasure in his heart. Ever since that trip to the Million Mountains years ago, Senior Brother had been like this.

As if nothing in the world mattered to him anymore.

He stood by with folded arms when they were forced to leave their homeland, talked but never acted after Golden Feather Senior Brother's remains fell into Luo Chen's hands, and now, even when it came to returning to their native soil, he remained so indifferent.

Did he still have the Five Elements Divine Sect in his heart at all?

Just as Divine Fire was brooding over past grievances, a hoarse voice came from within the room.

"Go ahead and do it. When the time comes, I will act."

Divine Fire was startled, then overjoyed.

With those words, that was enough!

Unlike other empty domains, the easternmost six domains, including the Five Elements Divine Sect's territory, were still infested with a large number of demon beasts.

Relying on him alone, along with the current Five Elements Divine Sect, it would be very difficult to forcibly reclaim them.

But if Senior Brother was willing to act, then everything would be fine!

After all, Senior Brother was a late-stage Sage!

Divine Fire Sage bowed respectfully, took the decree, and walked out.

Outside, his grand-disciple Ran Feng was already waiting to greet him.

"Grandmaster, has the Supreme Elder responded?"

Divine Fire Sage laughed heartily. "Of course he responded. After all, he is the pillar of my Five Elements Divine Sect."

After laughing, Divine Fire Sage exhaled a turbid breath.

"But we can't just sit idle either. Expansion wars are never easy; they require a great deal of preliminary preparation."

Ran Feng nodded. "I know that. Repairing magical artifacts, organizing the disciples, and it's best to widely invite allies to join the campaign together!"

"You know what to do. I'll draw up a list, and you can visit them one by one." Divine Fire Sage's mind raced, and several sects that had a decent relationship with the Five Elements Divine Sect came to mind.

The first, naturally, was Motian Cliff!

Although that old ghost Motian's strength was average, his life-preserving methods were truly remarkable. Without a natal true artifact, he had managed to survive several major battles.

If he could get his help, it would save a lot of effort.

Especially since, before the Biyou Mountain peace talks, the demon race had deliberately vacated the Motian Domain, where his sect's old site was located, as a buffer zone.

There were very few demon beasts there, so reclaiming the old territory should be easy.

Old Ghost Motian probably wouldn't refuse the Five Elements Divine Sect's invitation.

In addition, Tiandu Sect, Hundred Beasts Sect, Seven Mound Mountain, White Deer Cave, Jade Cauldron Sword Sect, and Divine Talisman Sect could also be invited. Mutual benefit!

Oh no, the Divine Talisman Sect should be excluded first. That old fellow Fu Lao was far too close to Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen.

Beyond that, Divine Fire Sage's thoughts raced, and he made another decision.

"Issue a recruitment order, inviting all forces, large and small, below the Sage-level sects to join the Five Elements Divine Sect in attacking the Five Elements Domain."

"After the Five Elements Domain is reclaimed, spiritual lands will be distributed according to merit!"

Ran Feng was not surprised. This was also a standard procedure for preparing an expansion war.

Relying on the strength of a single sect to wipe out the demon infestation would result in far too many casualties.

If they could mobilize those medium and small forces, they could save many losses.

For the current Five Elements Divine Sect, which had suffered a severe decline in strength, this was the best way to leverage external help.

After Divine Fire Sage gathered the sect's disciples, he quickly assigned tasks.

He ultimately decided to personally handle the visits to the Sage-level cultivators.

The task of liaising with the medium and small forces was left to Ran Feng, Qi Emei, and a group of Adept cultivators.

After making all the arrangements, Divine Fire Sage hurried out of the encampment to visit the various Sage-level cultivators.

His first stop was the nearest, Motian Cliff.

After half a day of negotiations, a series of cooperation conditions were reached. Only then did Divine Fire Sage leave with satisfaction, heading to the next destination.

About half a month later, he returned to the sect's encampment, looking somewhat weary.

Walking along the narrow mountain path, Divine Fire Sage recalled the experiences of the past half month's visits, feeling quite emotional.

Some of the potential allies on the list he had personally drawn up agreed to his invitation, such as Motian Cliff, Seven Mound Mountain, Hundred Beasts Sect, and White Deer Cave.

But some refused.

"The Tiandu Sect has suffered heavy losses and has no heart for further battle. That's understandable."

"But that Lin Qingxuan of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect, how dare he be so arrogant as to refuse to see me directly?"

"Could it be because he's wary of Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen?"

Divine Fire Sage was quite puzzled.

In his view, the relationship between Lin Qingxuan and Luo Chen wasn't that deep!

Furthermore, the Jade Cauldron Domain, the former territory of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect, had turned into a ghostly realm. With the pitifully few members of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect, it was impossible for them to reclaim the Jade Cauldron Domain on their own.

Didn't Lin Qingxuan want to borrow external force?

This doubt remained unanswered for now.

But when he saw Ran Feng, the other matter he worriedly brought up made Divine Fire Sage both shocked and angry.

"Only five Adept-level major sects are willing to follow us in reclaiming the Five Elements Domain?"

Ran Feng nodded, and couldn't help adding, "Of those five, four were originally our sect's vassals. After the great war, they only have a few hundred cultivators left. They won't be able to provide much help."

"How can this be? When did my sect's appeal fall to such a level?" Divine Fire Sage found it hard to believe.

The female cultivator Qi Emei hesitated for a moment, then softly said, "Perhaps it's because of the Luo Tian Sect?"

"The Luo Tian Sect?" Divine Fire Sage was stunned. "Isn't that Luo Chen going to the Nether Origin Sacred Land?"

Many people had witnessed Luo Chen's movements.

Personally invited by a Nether Origin Sect Ascendant, with the potential to enter the Alchemy Saint Hall in the future, leaping to become the number one figure under the Three Great Ancestors.

Everyone envied him.

As for the Luo Tian Sect he had personally established, no one paid much attention to it now.

They all thought that this small force of only about two hundred people would follow Luo Chen into the Nether Origin Sect.

But now, his disciple was telling him that the Luo Tian Sect was also participating in the expansion war?

Qi Emei relayed the intelligence she had gathered to Divine Fire Sage.

"The Alchemy Sect will enter the Nether Origin Sect, but the Luo Tian Sect will not go with them. According to reliable information, the Alchemy Sect wants to open up a domain as the Luo Tian Sect's mountain gate, leaving behind his own legacy."

"Now, the Luo Tian Sect, using the Alchemy Sect's name, has issued a recruitment order, and many medium and small forces have gone to pledge their allegiance."

"After all, those small forces also understand that the Luo Tian Sect has few cultivators and won't occupy much spiritual land. As long as they conquer a domain, they can also get a larger share of the territory."

"As for our Five Elements Divine Sect, we still have a large number of cultivators, so we can't leave too many scraps for others."

Divine Fire Sage opened his mouth, not expecting this twist.

The strength of the Five Elements Divine Sect had, in fact, affected the willingness of medium and small forces to join.

Moreover, the Alchemy Sect's appeal had truly surpassed that of Divine Origin Sage.

After some thought, Divine Fire Sage gritted his teeth.

"Forget it!"

"With the help of four Sage-level major sects like Motian Cliff, reclaiming the Five Elements Domain is enough."

"Those small forces only care about the future, but they don't realize that with the Luo Tian Sect's meager strength, when the expansion war actually begins, the ones who die and get hurt will be themselves. Truly foolish!"

Qi Emei and Ran Feng couldn't help but nod in agreement.

The situation was clear now. The small forces wanted to fish in troubled waters, but it wouldn't be that simple.

Of the fifteen lost domains, the Overlord Blade Domain had long become a spirit-dead land. Another four domains had been affected by the Ascendants' battle and were unsuitable for cultivation in the short term. Of the remaining ten, the Jade Cauldron Domain had become a ghostly realm filled with ghostly creatures.

In total, only nine domains were available for choice.

All of these nine domains were infested with a large number of demon beasts. It would not be an easy task for an ordinary Sage-level sect to conquer them.

...

Fenghua Palace.

At the foot of Qingping Mountain, it had been quite lively in recent days.

Led by Sect Master Li Yingzhang, all cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect above the Initiate stage were busy receiving leaders of various forces, large and small.

Sometimes, when manpower was insufficient, the Fenghua Palace would send disciples to help.

In a simple palace hall, Li Yingzhang was chatting warmly with an old man.

"Seventy years ago, my Rock Solid Gang fought alongside your esteemed sect at Ghost Head Mountain. I never thought we'd have another chance now."

"Hahaha, doesn't that just prove our two families are fated? Don't worry, once we conquer a domain, we can be neighbors again."

"Then I'll take your kind words, Sect Master Li."

After a brief conversation, Li Yingzhang agreed to let the Rock Solid Gang join the Luo Tian Sect's expansion war.

He personally escorted the old man to the hall entrance, watched him leave, and couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

Another helping hand!

The Rock Solid Gang was once a well-known Adept-level major sect in the Tianfan Domain. At its peak, it had as many as seven Adept cultivators and thirty to fifty thousand disciples.

Even now, they still had three surviving Adept cultivators and about four to five thousand disciples.

With their help, clearing out those first to third-rank demon beasts would be much easier.

"Senior Brother Li, judging by your smile, did the negotiations go well?"

A crisp female voice came. Li Yingzhang turned sideways and saw Yao Mingyue walking over with a smile.

Li Yingzhang laughed heartily. "Naturally! How about your side?"

Yao Mingyue smiled. "The Heavenly Wolf Society has also agreed! They don't have many people, but their disciples are all skilled at taming demon wolves. They'll be a great help as well."

Hearing this, Li Yingzhang's spirits lifted.

In just half a month, no fewer than ten Adept-level forces had come to pledge allegiance to the Luo Tian Sect.

The total number of cultivators they could muster was several tens of thousands.

There was still a year until the Luo Tian Sect's deadline for the expansion war.

And the Supreme Elder hadn't sent word yet. With his appeal, the number of Sage-level sects he could invite would surely be considerable.

It was easy to imagine that when the actual expansion began, the Luo Tian Sect would surely have cultivators gathering like clouds, and success would come naturally!

"Junior Sister Yao Mingyue, you've worked hard. After stepping down as sect master, you finally broke through to the Adept stage. Before you could even stabilize your realm, you had to come out and toil."

Yao Mingyue waved her hand. "My foundation is solid enough; my realm is already stable. Besides, I'm very familiar with these matters from my earlier years. It's not really toil."

She paused, a curious look appearing on her face.

"There's one thing everyone is very puzzled about. Senior Brother, as sect master, do you happen to know?"

"What is it?"

"Which domain is our Luo Tian Sect going to expand into?"

Faced with this question, Li Yingzhang couldn't help but show a wry smile.

He was also curious, but after asking the Supreme Elder, he had never received a clear answer.

"Junior Sister, this matter..."

Just as Li Yingzhang was formulating his words, an Initiate disciple hurriedly flew over.

"Sect Master, Uncle-Master Yao, an Adept-level force has come to pledge allegiance!"

Li Yingzhang nodded. "First, invite them to the Ten Thousand Pines Hall!"

Yao Mingyue thought for a moment and called back the disciple who was about to leave. "Do you know which sect it is? If it's not too important, I can go and deal with them later."

With the Alchemy Sect's call, medium and small forces were responding in droves.

But that didn't mean the Luo Tian Sect would accept everyone!

If a force was too weak, with too few disciples to command, the Luo Tian Sect wouldn't be interested.

Otherwise, when they conquered a domain and divided the spiritual lands, if there were too many monks and not enough porridge, it would be an ugly sight.

The disciple thought for a moment and respectfully replied, "It's the Green Dan Valley. The visitor is Sect Master Tao Wan."

Yao Mingyue's eyes showed a hint of confusion. "That name sounds familiar?"

"Junior Sister, I'll receive this one!" Li Yingzhang suddenly spoke up.

Yao Mingyue was taken aback. An ordinary Adept-level force wasn't worth the Luo Tian Sect's sect master personally receiving them. It would have to be at the level of the Rock Solid Gang.

But just by hearing the name, Senior Brother Li had volunteered.

As if seeing the doubt in her eyes, Li Yingzhang lowered his voice. "This sect is from the Jade Cauldron Domain. That Superior Tao Wan has an old connection with the Supreme Elder."

Instantly, Yao Mingyue understood!

No wonder the name felt familiar. It was one of the former Seven Sects of the Jade Cauldron Domain!

There had been no news of them for so many years; she thought they had long since perished.

Unexpectedly, the sect still existed, and Superior Tao Wan was even still alive.

Having an old connection with the Supreme Elder, it was indeed worth Senior Brother Li making a personal trip.

...

On Qingping Mountain.

In a stone hall with a rather high temperature, the sound of clanging and banging could be heard.

After a while, the sounds stopped.

A bronze mirror with cracks floating in mid-air.

Luo Chen looked at it, a hint of regret on his face.

In the end, it could only be repaired to this extent?

This mirror came from Lu Chang'an, the third of the Lu brothers, and its grade should be at the true artifact level. It had the abilities of manifestation, spatial fixation, and suppression, making it a very good treasure.

However, after two major battles with him, the degree of damage was extremely high.

He had spent some time and filled in many materials, but he could only restore thirty percent of its power.

Such a treasure was now a chicken rib for Luo Chen.

"Forget it, I'll bestow it to the people below!"

A damaged true artifact also had its benefits: the consumption of magical power wasn't that high.

As long as an Adept cultivator refined it properly, they could also exert some of its effects.

Luo Chen waved his hand, retrieved the green bronze mirror, and casually placed it on a shelf beside him.

On that shelf, the magical artifacts and true artifacts on display could be described as a dazzling array!

The Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle, a perfectly intact true artifact, came from the Frost Eagle Demon Emperor who was the first to die at Luo Chen's hands after his return to the Eastern Wilderness.

The Shadowless Needles, a full thousand of them, were top-grade magical artifacts, only one step away from being true artifacts. They came from the Gloomless Demon Emperor. Back then, Luo Chen had sold the Gloomless Demon Emperor's remains, but kept this set of flying needle magical artifacts.

Their killing power might not be able to break through Luo Chen's defenses, but if used to attack cultivators, the effect would be excellent.

After all, these flying needles had a very good qi-breaking property. Whether it was a spell shield or a defensive magical artifact, they all relied on the enhancement of magical power.

A compass plate and a banner flag were severely damaged, their power less than one-tenth of the original. They came from the other two of the Lu brothers.

Then there were dozens of dazzling magical artifacts, all containing blood essence restrictions.

These came from the Hundred Legs Great Demon Emperor!

And the vast majority were magical artifacts refined by human cultivators, so there was no problem of incompatibility.

In addition, there was a pair of silver-white gloves, four golden ropes, five black awls, and a single golden flying needle placed separately.

"Thunderclap Gloves, Binding Golden Ropes, Law-Suppressing Five Element Awls, Soul-Weaving Needle!"

Luo Chen called out their names one by one, a look of extreme satisfaction in his eyes.

Although the battle with the Hundred Legs Great Demon Emperor was quite dangerous, the gains far exceeded expectations.

Dozens of magical artifacts, four true artifacts, and the remains of a Great Demon Emperor!

This didn't even include the residual materials of that mountain and sword that Luo Chen had conveniently retrieved. That mountain and sword had been able to clash head-on with Luo Chen's Primordial Unity Cauldron and Yuantu Blood Sword for many rounds, so they were certainly not ordinary items.

Even if they were just residual materials, if used well, they might be able to be reforged into one or two decent magical artifacts.

Luo Chen reached out, put away the Law-Suppressing Five Element Awls and the Soul-Weaving Needle, planning to refine them into his own weapons later.

As for the rest?

Luo Chen sighed. These would all be the foundation he left for the Luo Tian Sect!

This foundation was not insignificant. For an ordinary Sage-level sect to have one or two true artifacts was already a blessing, let alone the ones Luo Chen was leaving behind, which were more than just one or two.

The Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle, the Thunderclap Gloves, the Binding Golden Ropes!

In addition, there was the Soul-Nourishing Banner he had given to Bai Meiling, and Heavenjade's Ten Thousand Fierce Souls Banner, both at the true artifact level.

There was also the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion that Luo Chen had seized from the Penglai Immortal Sect disciple. Once it was no longer useful in the future, he would also leave it to the Luo Tian Sect.

As long as he could also secure a mountain gate spiritual land for the Luo Tian Sect to inhabit, then Luo Chen would have done his utmost.

When the time came, he would go to the Nether Origin Sect. If everything went smoothly and he achieved Ascension, that would be best.

If things went wrong, then Luo Chen would naturally no longer be tied down by worldly affairs and people!

Thinking of this, a fierce glint flashed in Luo Chen's eyes.

Suddenly, he retracted his expression and spoke calmly towards the door.

"Junior Brother, come in!"

Ling Fengzi responded and entered, a hint of joy on his face.

"Senior Brother, everything is settled!"

"Oh?" Luo Chen became interested. "How many are willing to help?"

Ling Fengzi chuckled. "All of them!"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. It seemed he had been quite successful in his dealings!

The words "all of them" didn't just refer to one or two sects.

The Divine Talisman Sect, the Nine Spirits Sect, the Tai Shan Sect, the Fenghua Palace, the White Crane Sect, and the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect—a full six sects.

Adding the Luo Tian Sect, that made seven Sage-level major sects joining forces. Conquering a single domain would be more than enough.

When the one-year deadline was up, and Li Yingzhang had contacted enough Adept-level sects, this expansion war would surely be decided in a matter of days.

"Did the other sects have any conditions?"

Ling Fengzi nodded. "They're not really conditions, just talk about mutual aid and helping each other. The Nine Spirits Sect's old territory is destroyed; they must open up another domain. The Divine Talisman Domain is occupied; the Divine Talisman Sect wants to go back, but relying on Old Man Fu alone, a single Sage, is not enough. They all hope that at a critical moment, Senior Brother, you can lend a hand!"

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment. These were all to be expected, and he wouldn't refuse.

"What about the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect?" Luo Chen was curious. Had Lin Qingxuan not put forward any conditions?

After all, everyone understood the situation in the Jade Cauldron Domain.

Speaking of this, Ling Fengzi said with some confusion, "I don't know what method he used, but it seems he reached an agreement with the Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion. The two major sects are merging, and the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect will be incorporated into the Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion. That way, he won't have to open up a domain anymore."

Hearing this, Luo Chen fell into thought.

Both Sage-level cultivators of the Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion had fallen, and their strength had plummeted.

Even if there was a certain time limit for them to develop, whether they could produce another Sage was still uncertain.

Bringing in Lin Qingxuan, a sword dao expert, could help maintain the sect's standing.

Moreover, the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect and the Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion had very deep roots. Others couldn't really criticize the collusion between the two.

But in the future, it was hard to say which of the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect and the Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion would be the dominant one.

Ling Fengzi suddenly asked, "By the way, Senior Brother, they are all very curious about which domain you will choose."

Luo Chen smiled. "Are you curious too?"

Ling Fengzi nodded repeatedly. "Yes, I was even thinking of recommending the Misty Domain, where my Misty Sect used to be."

"The Misty Domain?" Luo Chen shook his head. "That place is too small. Its future development potential is limited."

As he spoke, Luo Chen took a black scroll from his storage ring.

It was the expansion decree given to him by the City Lord of Lingtian!

"Take a look!"

Ling Fengzi curiously unrolled the scroll, his gaze stopping at the blank space, and he couldn't help but be stunned.

"There's no specific address?"

Luo Chen's magical power surged from his fingertips, slowly falling onto the blank space of the scroll, while he spoke unhurriedly.

"Without a specific address, it means any place can be my Luo Tian Sect's mountain gate. Whether it's the Dieyun or Mu Mansion domains, which have no Sage in charge, or the once-occupied Divine Talisman or Jade Cauldron domains, or even Motian or Five Elements, I can take them if I want!"

A flash of red light.

The scroll had already been inscribed.

(End of this chapter)
