# Chapter 666: The Culprit, Blood Sea Ancestor

"Haa... hiss..."

A comfortable groan escaped the red-robed man's mouth unconsciously, causing the two people nearby to glance at him.

But they did not find it strange that Luo Chen had lost composure like this.

It was because, after stepping into Blazing Refinement Prison [TERM?], the surrounding spiritual qi environment changed abruptly.

It was no longer the Thousand-Ren Mountains' sharpness, cold as blades against the body, nor the Flowing Sand Sea's heaviness, like shackles of earth binding the flesh. Instead, it became scorching, restless, surging, and lively.

It was as if even each breath carried sparks.

Such an extreme spiritual qi environment was naturally unpleasant for Tai Sui, whose main cultivation leaned toward wood.

But to Luo Chen, who cultivated only fire-attribute methods, it was like a fish entering water, wonderfully free and comfortable.

Not only him. Even Moya Cave Master could not help stretching his body and instinctively taking a few more breaths. He also cultivated fire-attribute methods.

Amid that comfort, Moya Cave Master's mind relaxed, and resentment rose toward Luo Chen's rash action earlier.

"Why insist on provoking that monster? We were nearly left inside."

"How I act should not be something Cave Master is qualified to critique, should it?"

Luo Chen glanced sideways at him, and Moya Cave Master's expression stiffened.

In his heart, an anger suppressed for a long time seemed about to erupt.

Ever since allying with Luo Chen, nothing had gone smoothly!

First, Luo Chen had delayed the time for entering the Fallen Sea, then provoked Ghost Immortal Tower [TERM?], nearly getting them killed.

After finally entering Demon-Fall Land, this man had even craftily set an ambush and placed him beneath the threat of the fourth-tier strange beast, the Nine Yang Golden Eagle [TERM?].

Moya Cave Master had been an artifact-forging grandmaster admired by countless people for many years. When had he ever suffered such anger?

Now that they were in Blazing Refinement Prison, this was also his home ground. It was just the time to properly argue things out!

A white true flame appeared around him.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly.

At that moment, a hand landed on Moya Cave Master's shoulder.

"Your mind is disturbed."

"I..."

Moya Cave Master looked back at Tai Sui and suddenly woke up.

When he looked again at Luo Chen, that killing intent the other party had deliberately released poured over him like cold water.

Yes. How could he dare provoke Devil Lord Azure Yang!

That was someone who had escaped safely from the siege of the Flowing Sand Sea's master.

Moreover, in these years, rumors within the Myriad Immortals Assembly about Devil Lord Azure Yang's battle strength had grown more and more intense. How could he, a Golden Core Stage Seven cultivator obsessed with artifact-forging, compare?

"Hmph."

Luo Chen snorted lightly and ignored Moya Cave Master's petty thoughts. Instead, he looked toward Tai Sui.

"Why did Heavenloam pursue the dragon soul that Moluo Stream's three great Golden Cores wanted to capture so relentlessly?"

Tai Sui glanced at Luo Chen's finger. Two rings were worn there.

One was ink-green, the other pitch-black.

The Soul-Summoning Banner containing the dragon soul was inside that dark ring.

Tai Sui thought for a while.

"I also pondered this for a long while on the road and reached a rough guess. Whether it is correct or not, I cannot say. I can only mention it for your reference."

"We awakened beings, though ranked at the fifth tier and comparable to Ascendants, do not actually possess an Ascendant's might. It is because our bodies contain the laws of heaven and earth, but we cannot use them skillfully. In fact, because of the limitations of our bodies, the law power we grasp is also incomplete."

Luo Chen frowned. In his mind, he subconsciously thought of the fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine on Withered Wood Ridge [TERM?].

Now, as Tai Sui explained slowly, it did indeed seem to be the case.

Beings of grass, wood, metal, and stone found it extremely difficult to birth intelligence.

Often, only after reaching an extremely high tier would they possibly develop intelligence.

If compared with humans, it was roughly equivalent to a person possessing an Ascendant body, while their intelligence was only that of a newborn, like an infant.

Improper control, unmanifested power, sparse methods... all were descriptions of awakened spirits.

Most of the methods this kind was best at involved controlling the land where they had grown from childhood to destroy enemies.

Like the Withered Blood Vine spreading across Withered Wood Ridge, or Heavenloam stirring the Flowing Sand Sea.

"Heavenloam's innate shackles are even greater!"

"It was artificially created by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining. Though its foundation is vast, it is extremely difficult for it to display its own abilities. Therefore, these years, it has always been splitting its own body, attempting to master its strength bit by bit, and finally merge it back into one."

"In addition, its earth-path law is also incomplete."

"From what I observed, that unconscious and incomplete dragon soul should also have comprehended some kind of earth-element law while alive, either as a spirit or as an archaic beast. That is why Heavenloam wanted to devour it and complete itself."

Tai Sui paused, then continued in a guessing tone.

"As far as I know, that gobi did not exist in the Flowing Sand Sea before. Clearly, it was formed by the dragon soul."

Luo Chen frowned. As matters involved Ascendant law, his understanding was extremely limited.

Hearing this now felt like falling into fog. He caught only strands of true meaning.

Thus, he pointed straight at the core of the question.

"Where did this soul come from?"

"Not from us. It is an outsider," Tai Sui said decisively.

"An outsider?" Luo Chen was surprised.

Moya Cave Master, who had been listening to this exchange, said thoughtfully, "I heard Heavenloam shout at it and call it Stone Dragon. This name happens to match a legend within our Myriad Immortals Assembly."

Stone Dragon?

In an instant, Luo Chen remembered a story a Golden Core cultivator from Quyang Island had once told him.

The Myriad Immortals Assembly's headquarters, the Fulong Mountain Range, was said to have evolved from the body of an archaic beast stone dragon that died in remote ancient times.

Later, after the ancient human-demon war, humanity established its rule over the Five Continents, and the Yuan Demon Sect forcefully occupied the Northern Sea.

During that process, a Yuan Demon Sect Ascendant ancestor passed through the Fulong Mountain Range, extracted the mountain soul, and took it to strengthen the foundation of the sect's mountain gate, Arctic Yemo Heaven.

That mountain soul, according to rumor, was the remnant soul of the archaic beast Stone Dragon!

Stone Dragon was an archaic beast, but most people said it was a spirit born from ore and enlightened from spirit stone.

If that were true, it seemed to explain why Heavenloam was determined to obtain the stone dragon remnant soul.

As a man-made thing, what it lacked most was natural meaning.

Luo Chen's thoughts turned, and he said thoughtfully, "It is said that several hundred years ago, the Ascendant ancestor of the Yuan Demon Sect's Blood Sea lineage personally entered the Fallen Sea and never returned. Some even say he perished inside this secret realm. Could it be that he brought the stone dragon remnant soul in?"

"You mean that old man!" Tai Sui suddenly laughed aloud.

This laugh drew Luo Chen and Moya Cave Master's eyes.

Tai Sui shrugged.

"Although I am only a remnant thought and do not know many things, I truly remember that person. In fact, it was because of him that I had the chance to split a divine thought and escape Demon-Fall Land."

"Three hundred years ago, he did indeed come to Demon-Fall Land, and he brought a group of juniors skilled in formations. That group of people... I do not remember them, but I remember that old man clearly. He entered the inheritance land and then died inside."

Luo Chen and Moya Cave Master cried out in shock, "Died inside?"

Even though they had long expected it, hearing someone personally tell them the answer still made them unable to control themselves.

After all, that had been one of the top experts of the present age, a mighty Ascendant!

"He deserved to die!"

Tai Sui curled his lip, looking as if it were only natural.

Seeing Luo Chen and Moya Cave Master puzzled, he patiently explained, "The so-called inheritance land, as its name suggests, is the place where Devil Lord Heaven-Refining passed on his Dao Lineage. Those who can enter are either people of outstanding talent or people whose future path has not yet been fixed. An Ascendant existence has long since walked out his own path. How could he become True Lord's successor?"

"He clearly already had his own path, yet still wanted to walk another person's path."

"Tell me, did Blood Sea Ancestor not seek death through his own Dao?"

Luo Chen and Moya Cave Master looked at one another. For a moment, neither knew how to answer.

Could they say an Ascendant mighty figure deserved to die?

But just as Tai Sui had said, when one's own future path was already clear, to still greedily covet another person's Great Dao was indeed seeking death.

Tai Sui curled his lip.

"Later, after I possessed Ding Yi's body, I learned that the Yuan Demon Sect had been destroyed. This tragedy was carried out by the Northern Sea demon race alliance, but now, in my view, the chief culprit should still be that old man."

"How so?"

"The sect's top battle power died inside the secret realm, causing the demon race to stir restlessly. He also took away one of the sect's foundations, the stone dragon remnant soul, causing Arctic Yemo Heaven, a dangerous land under heaven, to gain a flaw and allowing the demon race alliance to charge in violently. Do you think that old man should bear the guilt?"

Fine.

After this speech, Luo Chen and the others had nothing to say.

The Northern Sea demon race was powerful, but its strength was scattered.

With the Yuan Demon Sect's three great Ascendants alive, they were enough to suppress everything.

Blood Sea Ancestor's death had created a once-in-a-thousand-years opportunity for the Northern Sea demon race.

From their current perspective, Blood Sea Ancestor truly had to carry this blame.

Luo Chen sighed and swept his heavy gaze over this world wrapped in endless firelight.

As far as the eye could see, volcanoes were smoking black clouds and erupting from time to time.

Large amounts of magma flowed, leaving almost no place for a person to stand.

This was a land where vitality was extinct!

The occasional trees that did exist were brilliant and crystalline fire-type plants.

This was Blazing Refinement Prison, the fire-element land of Five Elements Heaven!

"The Thousand-Ren Mountains, the Flowing Sand Sea, Blazing Refinement Prison, and Wood Heaven Plain [TERM?], which is rumored to contain countless precious spirit herbs... Even if the so-called inheritance land is not mentioned, Five Elements Heaven alone is already a top-tier resource secret realm!"

"If a sect force thoroughly controlled it, it could bless the sect for thousands of generations!"

"Thinking of it this way, it is no wonder Blood Sea Ancestor paid such a huge price to enter Demon-Fall Land personally."

From the perspective of a sect's upper echelon, Luo Chen sighed in this way.

Moya Cave Master nodded, feeling much the same. He barely counted as the master of a small force himself.

Tai Sui, however, felt nothing.

Looking at this prison of blazing fire and rolling smoke, he suggested, "Shall we go look for the Five Elements Lotus Platform now?"

"Wait a bit."

Luo Chen shook his head. His spiritual eyes looked against the heat-twisted void and swept continuously.

"Hm?" Tai Sui did not understand.

"First find a place with abundant spiritual qi. I need to recover some mana."

Tai Sui was stunned. Moya Cave Master was also extremely surprised.

In their eyes, the mana fluctuation around Luo Chen's body remained as before, and his aura did not look withered in the slightest. Why would he need to recover mana?

Luo Chen did not explain. His gaze stopped beneath a small mountain that had not erupted magma.

"That place is quite good!"

After saying this, he flew straight over.

Once nearby, he patted the storage bag at his waist, and Heavenjade immediately flew out.

"Guard me for a while."

"Yes, Master!"

He did not summon Black King, because Black King was proficient in water attributes and would be most restrained in Blazing Refinement Prison.

Heavenjade was slightly better. Though she also cultivated some water methods, her main cultivation was wind, so the influence on her was not as great.

Afterward, Luo Chen controlled the Soul Heaven Sword Case [TERM?]. A Seven Baleful Sword [TERM?] flew out and drilled into the depths of the mountain like a drill.

In only a short while, a crude cave residence was formed.

Luo Chen entered, took out formation plates, arranged formations, and then sat cross-legged.

While swallowing supreme-grade True Flame Pills, he circulated the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture to absorb the fire-attribute spiritual qi that could be found everywhere outside.

As surging spiritual qi entered his body, the slightly dim second Origin Pill inside him immediately became full and bright.

His lifebound Golden Core truly had not consumed much mana.

But that charged palm earlier, together with escaping Heavenloam's pursuit, had truly consumed most of his second Origin Pill's mana.

With his lifebound Golden Core serving as the core for the killing move All-Encompassing Fire Prison [TERM?], it could not be used lightly. Under such circumstances, the second Origin Pill was his source of strength.

Only when spirit, essence, and qi were full could he have enough confidence in the following actions.

When his qi sea grew full and the Origin Pill flashed, a faint restless feeling rose in Luo Chen's heart.

He circulated the Fine Dust Primordial Art [TERM?] and cut away that feeling.

"Strange?"

Luo Chen rested briefly and looked inward.

He saw that within his qi sea, the origin true fire formed from Withering-Bloom Fire [TERM?] was leaping lively.

"This place seems very suitable for the existence of sourceless flames like this?"

Luo Chen was greatly surprised.

Immediately, he thought of Moya Cave Master's sudden anger earlier, as well as the white flame that had appeared on him.

"So that is how it is!"

Luo Chen murmured to himself, then closed his eyes and continued meditating to regulate his breath.

Outside, Moya Cave Master, who had found another low hill, had already involuntarily released the origin true fire he had refined.

It was called White Cloud Vast Flame [TERM?], and it came from the same origin line as the Burning Heaven Fire [TERM?] of High Man Xingyun [TERM?] of Azure Pill Valley [TERM?].

Feeling the joyous leaping of the White Cloud Vast Flame, Moya Cave Master's eyes shone.

"If I could occupy this place alone, then perhaps in less than a hundred years, I could cultivate to Golden Core Great Perfection. I could even borrow this place to establish a true great artifact-forging sect, far surpassing Moya Mountain!"

He could sense the richness of the fire-attribute spiritual qi here. The closer one went to the large volcanoes, the thicker the spiritual qi became.

This low hill already had the effect of a third-tier spirit land.

One could imagine that if they truly reached the most majestic place in Blazing Refinement Prison, it might be fourth tier or even fifth tier!

...

In the deepest part of Demon-Fall Land.

Its name was Falling Demon Abyss [TERM?]!

Of the heaven-reaching spiritual qi absorbed from the outside world, a small portion poured into Leaving Forgetting Heaven [TERM?] and Five Elements Heaven.

Most of it gathered within Falling Demon Abyss.

At the bottom of Falling Demon Abyss, on an ancient stone platform, three figures stood.

All around was darkness.

Only a dozen or so star points flickered with faint light.

The three looked at those star points, seemingly hesitant.

Suddenly, the leading person's expression changed slightly.

Old Daoist Jiyuan [TERM?], who had been watching him the whole time, asked curiously, "Fellow Daoist Blood Nightmare, what is wrong?"

Blood Nightmare Moluo frowned.

"The three people I left outside to work for me are dead!"

"Dead?" Old Daoist Jiyuan was surprised. "Wu Qi and the other two were not weak among their peers, and they had the protective methods you bestowed. Who could kill them?"

Before Blood Nightmare Moluo could speak, Fairy Feileng [TERM?] beside them sneered.

"You attempted to use three Golden Core juniors to subdue the remnant soul of the archaic beast Stone Dragon. You should have expected they might be killed in return."

Blood Nightmare Moluo shook his head.

"That is not quite right. That remnant soul's will had long been extinguished. Only instinct remained. I also bestowed a true-artifact-level Soul-Summoning Banner. Subduing the remnant soul should have been more than enough. They... should have been killed by someone else. Unfortunately, now that we are in Falling Demon Abyss, we cannot calculate who killed them and ruined my great matter."

Old Daoist Jiyuan nodded and said casually, "It is indeed a great matter. That Stone Dragon was a fifth-tier spirit vein that swallowed the corpse of an archaic earth dragon within it and gained intelligence. It had both the body of an archaic beast and the spirit of an awakened being, truly a created beast of remote antiquity. Even after tens of millions of years, with only one remnant soul remaining, if used well, it could completely be cultivated into a fifth-tier spirit vein. Now that this matter has been ruined, Fellow Daoist's future Ascendant path will probably become even rougher."

Blood Nightmare Moluo's expression became even heavier.

Perfected Being Feileng [TERM?], however, could not be bothered with them. She moved with light lotus steps to a star point that radiated light.

"I am going in first."

Blood Nightmare Moluo frowned.

"Will you not wait a little longer? There are more than ten roads, and it is said each one leads to Bright Radiance Heaven [TERM?]. Yet among the Yuan Demon Sect's disciples through the generations, very few truly entered Bright Radiance Heaven. Will you not consider carefully?"

"There is no need. My target is not Bright Radiance Heaven."

Before Fairy Feileng finished speaking, she had already stepped into the starlight. Before leaving, she left behind only one sentence.

"Blood Sea Ancestor entered the road with the dimmest starlight. If you want his inheritance, go there."

Blood Nightmare Moluo was half-believing and half-doubting. His gaze fell on the dimmest of the dozen or so star points.

After thinking for a long while, he finally took a deep breath and walked inside.

After they left, the Daoist with immortal bearing, Jiyuan, fell silent.

At that moment, the ancient stone platform trembled slightly, and light shone brightly.

A graceful figure slowly appeared atop it.

Before the light dispersed, a pair of beautiful eyes had already fallen on Old Daoist Jiyuan.

"Third Senior Brother, you have truly made junior sister search hard."

A voice as cold as wind from the Nine Nether sounded faintly.

Hearing this voice, Old Daoist Jiyuan's expression changed greatly.

Without the slightest hesitation, he stepped into one of the star points.

After he left, the light on the stone platform shrank, and a woman walked out from within.

Her skin was like congealed fat, her eyes like stars. Her exquisite face highlighted a cold and distant air.

Farther down, peaks gathered and waves surged, while long legs faintly visible beneath her garments showed a gorgeous, unrestrained allure.

Such contradictory temperaments gathered on one person.

In the current Northern Sea, there was only one such person.

Moon Wanderer of the Myriad Immortals Assembly!

Jiyuan's escape made her snort coldly.

But then her gaze fell on the dozen or so star points, and her whole person fell silent.

*(End of Chapter)*
