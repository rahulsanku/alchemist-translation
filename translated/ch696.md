# Chapter 696: Luo Chen's Fall

For cultivators, what method could be regarded as the most powerful single move?

A bombardment of spells?

The sharpness of magic treasures?

Or perhaps a divine soul attack impossible to guard against?

No!

Strictly speaking, none of these counted as the strongest move.

The strongest and most terrifying move was one that even cultivators themselves could not control, one sufficient to affect dozens of li and even shock a hundred li: self-detonation!

Yes, self-detonation!

Especially for Golden Core cultivators who had condensed a Golden Core, once they actively shattered it, the attack unleashed would be a terrifying, indiscriminate explosion.

Under such an attack, even enemies of a higher realm would have to retreat far away.

The reason was that a "Golden Core" was the ultimate condensation of several hundred years of a cultivator's Dao.

It represented the essence gathered through bitter cultivation day and night.

When the essence condensed over countless days and nights bloomed forth in a single instant, its might was extremely terrifying. Unless forced into a desperate, dead-end situation, no Golden Core cultivator would willingly take this step.

It could even be said that many Golden Core cultivators, right up until they were killed, never once had the thought of self-detonating their Golden Core.

Once a Golden Core self-detonated, there was ten deaths and no life!

It was a certain defeat, a certain death.

The subconscious resistance to it meant that even at the moment of death, few people would move this thought.

Over all these years, Luo Chen had only heard once of a cultivator self-detonating his Golden Core, and only personally witnessed one attempt.

The former came from the Eastern Wilderness during the Falling Cloud Sect's opening war, when a supreme elder of the Myriad Manifestations Sect, facing the siege of Demon Kings, resolutely self-detonated to cover the retreat of his disciples and sect members.

The latter occurred after he came to the Northern Sea, at the docks of Feileng City, when a cultivator of the Wu clan named Wu Di attempted to kill him by self-detonating his Golden Core, only for Luo Chen to have prepared in advance and escaped the blast range.

Luo Chen had never wished to directly face the might of a self-detonation from an expert of the same tier, nor had he ever imagined that one day he himself would truly be forced to that step.

But he had never imagined that he would actually be driven to this point.

Although it was not his lifebound Golden Core, this second Origin Pill refined from third-tier Perfected Completion Hidden Dust Sand was in no way inferior in quality to the Golden Core of a late-stage Great Cultivator!

If the power it contained were slowly released, that would still have been normal.

But if it were suddenly detonated...

At this moment, an unimaginable mana storm was wreaking havoc inside Luo Chen's body.

Had it been anyone else, the instant the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire detonated the second Origin Pill, they would already have died, their Dao dissipated, their body transformed into a cloud of blood mist.

But he forcibly held on.

Everything depended entirely on this powerful Archaic Tier Four physique!

The scene was like a Great Cultivator comparable to a late Golden Core self-detonating inside the body of a small archaic beast.

And precisely because the body was so "small," the self-detonation's power became incomparably concentrated.

In an instant, Luo Chen's body began to fail, crumbling inch by inch.

Pff! Pff! Pff!

Inside the great hall, clouds of blood mist appeared one after another.

Beads of blood shot out from openings torn across Luo Chen's body.

Visible to the naked eye, his condition worsened and worsened, almost to the point where he could no longer hold on.

Not only that, the intense pain of his collapsing qi sea surged through Luo Chen's sea of consciousness and divine soul, causing him to faint instantly.

Then, because of that same agony, he abruptly awakened again.

This was a terrifying pain even Luo Chen, famed for his unyielding will, could hardly endure.

Between constant fainting and awakening.

"Ah..."

A low growl like that of a wild beast rasped from Luo Chen's mouth.

On his twisted face, veins stood out starkly.

"I do not accept this!"

"I will never accept this ending!"

Within his chaotic divine soul, a soundless roar bellowed.

He, Luo Chen, had never been lazy. He had exhausted his mind and strength, persisting in cultivation for a hundred years as if it were a single day, and only then had he reached his current realm.

He had trodden on thin ice through countless life-and-death crises, and only then harvested innumerable opportunities.

Especially after braving such hardship, he had finally obtained the True Lord's inheritance and gained the Five-Spirit Dao Body that could lead straight to Sage and even Ascendant. How could it collapse here in a single morning?

He did not accept it!

Streams of source power were mobilized from every part of his body, barely stabilizing his precarious flesh.

Not enough!

Still not enough!

Gritting his teeth, Luo Chen ignored the chaos of his thoughts and extracted source vitality from the depths of his flesh, forming source power to forcibly suppress the mana storm within his body.

Crimson mana and desolate source power used Luo Chen's body as a battlefield, forming a situation of explosion and suppression.

All of this had to be blamed on the Nirvana Sacred Fire and the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire.

The former borrowed his body to oppose the latter.

The latter, knowing it could not win, simply wanted to destroy the Nirvana Sacred Fire's "home ground."

Yet Luo Chen was the one who had to bear this bitter fruit.

Gradually, Luo Chen only felt that his source power, already not abundant to begin with, was growing scarcer and scarcer.

It was almost unable to suppress the riot of mana from the second Origin Pill.

This situation was not because source power was weaker than mana, but because there was too little of it!

In the end, Luo Chen had achieved Archaic Tier Four for far too short a time.

Almost immediately after advancing, he had come to the Demon-Fall Land with Ding Yi and the Moya Cave Master, and had simply not had much time to extract and accumulate source power.

Moreover, in his battle with Shark Emperor Liujun and while refining the Law-Derivation Pill in the Pill Realm, he had expended some of that source power.

Thus this awkward situation arose, where his source power could not keep up.

"Right!"

"Replenish source power!"

In a haze, Luo Chen gathered his focus with difficulty and took out an object from his storage ring.

It was precisely Tai Sui's main body!

Previously, for the sake of alchemy, he had eaten roughly one-tenth of it. At present, ninety percent of the translucent white flesh-jelly still remained.

This time, under desperate circumstances, Luo Chen intended to swallow it in one gulp.

His left hand formed a seal.

Rumble...

Luo Chen's head suddenly swelled, as if a monstrous giant head had abruptly sprouted from a human body.

The great maw opened wide and swallowed the one-zhang-sized Tai Sui main body whole.

Tai Sui entered his mouth, and sharp teeth chewed wildly.

As juices flowed everywhere, a vitality so dense it could hardly be described fell into Luo Chen's flesh.

Before that vitality could be tainted by the mana, source power began to seize and refine it.

As a fifth-tier spirit plant, and one that had once gained intelligence and comprehended part of the wood-element laws, the majestic vitality contained within Tai Sui's main body was first-rate even among countless spirit medicines.

Even Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had once relied on the terrifying vitality contained in Tai Sui's main body to cultivate and save the hybrid spirit plants of his experiments.

At this moment, obtaining this complete Tai Sui vitality, Luo Chen was caught off guard and actually let out a full burp.

"Urp..."

Source power suddenly increased!

In an instant, Luo Chen recovered somewhat.

At this moment, he finally had the presence of mind to attend to his own flesh.

Yet when he looked inward, all he saw was devastation.

His qi sea was completely destroyed.

His meridians were reduced to less than one in ten.

The hundreds of apertures he had painstakingly opened, once shining inside his body like stars, were now either destroyed or blocked by chaotic mana.

His five viscera and six bowels were even more riddled with wounds, too miserable to examine closely.

Were it not for the majestic vitality provided by Tai Sui, Luo Chen would now be a dying man wrapped in a sheet of human skin, hollow and left with only one breath.

"My Five-Spirit Dao Body!"

...

"My main body!"

Somewhere in Bright Radiance Heaven.

The fat cultivator struggling forward suddenly clutched his head and let out a miserable shriek.

Just now, a heart-tearing, lung-splitting agony had suddenly transmitted through his divine soul.

After the pain, he discovered in terror that his connection with his main body was gone!

Gone!

That was the shell he had painstakingly cultivated for ten thousand years, the one that had only birthed intelligence three thousand years ago!

Without that shell, forget transforming and cultivating to glimpse the Great Dao; even advancing to Ascendant would no longer hold the slightest possibility.

"How could this be..."

"What exactly did Luo Chen do?"

Between rage and despair, the fat cultivator no longer advanced carefully step by step. He began to rampage through this world.

He had to get out!

He had to find Luo Chen!

He had to retrieve his shell!

...

"No!"

"The meridians may perhaps be remade, the five viscera can return to place, and even the apertures can be born anew. But with the qi sea gone, my Dao path in this life is cut off."

"Body refinement is strong, but the road ahead is unclear."

"Especially my Five-Spirit Dao Body!"

Luo Chen's body trembled ceaselessly, unable to accept the scene he now saw.

It was at this moment.

Within his inward vision, he saw a blocked aperture where a trace of golden light flickered.

In an instant, Luo Chen recognized that thing.

The accursed chief culprit: the Nirvana Sacred Fire!

If it had not drilled into his body, how could it have drawn the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire, and how could he have fallen to this state?

"You hid yourself well!"

Luo Chen gritted his teeth and was about to force it out of his body.

But just as he was about to act, Luo Chen suddenly stopped.

"Nirvana Sacred Fire... Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture... nirvana..."

As if blessed by sudden insight, Luo Chen forcibly adjusted his body and assumed the cross-legged posture of five hearts facing heaven.

Then he began circulating the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

At this moment, his qi sea had collapsed, his meridians had snapped, and his apertures were either destroyed or sealed. He simply could not circulate a technique smoothly.

But following his memory, Luo Chen forcibly mobilized his mana, intending to circulate the art through the void within his body.

After the second Origin Pill was detonated, mana still raged wildly, causing this action to fail again and again.

"Come out!"

With a low shout, a crimson Golden Core leapt into his body.

It was precisely the lifebound Golden Core hidden inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

After the lifebound Golden Core appeared, it suppressed the inner cosmos like a great sun. The rampaging mana began to gather around the Golden Core as its center, like a violent flood finally given a channel.

Luo Chen finally had the chance to construct the circulation route of the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture through the void.

Time slowly flowed.

Who knew how long passed before Luo Chen, relying on instinctive memory, finally managed to barely construct the former circulation route of the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

Then!

"Reverse!"

Madness shone in Luo Chen's eyes as he resolutely reversed his lifebound cultivation method.

If any other immortal cultivator learned of such a mad act, they would only curse him as insane.

Who would dare reverse-circulate their lifebound method?

That was no different from suicide!

It was just like self-detonating a Golden Core.

But for Luo Chen, this might be his only chance to seek life from death.

No!

It was not seeking life from death, but rebirth through nirvana!

The Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture was the cultivation method Rosycloud practiced, and its true meaning came from the phoenix race's method of rebirth through nirvana.

And to truly reach that realm of rebirth, one needed to pass through three stages.

Strictly speaking, it was called the Nine Tribulations and Three Nirvanas!

The Golden Core fragment Luo Chen obtained, although it did not mention the Nine Tribulations in detail, recorded the methods of the Three Nirvanas.

Luo Chen clearly knew that he had once completed one nirvana.

That was the nirvana of mana.

It covered three major aspects: spirit root, qi sea, and Golden Core.

They were also called the spirit root tribulation, qi sea tribulation, and Golden Core tribulation.

He had crossed those three tribulations, thereby transforming his five spirit roots into a fire spirit root comparable to a single heavenly spirit root, remaking his qi sea into a sea of fire, and even changing his five-colored Golden Core into a crimson Golden Core. All his mana underwent complete nirvana and became domineering Heavenly Phoenix mana.

After three tribulations came nirvana.

From then on, Luo Chen entered the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

This was the first nirvana!

Pass three tribulations, achieve one nirvana!

And the second nirvana Luo Chen knew of corresponded precisely to the nirvana of the flesh!

So long as he completed the nirvana of the body, his originally ordinary flesh could be remade into a fire-spirit body, better suited for cultivating the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

All along, Luo Chen had never thought about this matter.

To him, fleshly nirvana was an extremely dangerous act of being placed in death before finding life.

Especially because the stronger his flesh became, the greater the danger he would encounter during nirvana.

Therefore, he had never dared think of it.

Especially!

After soaking in that strange spirit spring inside Bright Radiance Heaven's Pill Palace, he had obtained the Five-Spirit Dao Body.

This physique was even more excellent than a single fire-spirit body.

It could allow his future Sage Great Dao to proceed smoothly without again being restricted by cultivation methods.

If he transformed into a fire-spirit body through fleshly nirvana, the Five-Spirit Dao Body would become moonlight in water and flowers in a mirror. Even if it could make him like a tiger with wings on the fire-path, he would ultimately lose far more.

Therefore, he had buried the so-called Nine Tribulations and Three Nirvanas even deeper in his heart.

But now!

"I have already reached a dead end, and the Five-Spirit Dao Body has become yesterday's flower and a tower in the air. What future is there to speak of? What Great Dao is there to discuss?"

"Only by borrowing this chance for nirvana rebirth can there be a thread of life!"

"Whether this broken, ruined body can recover again depends on this desperate act."

Luo Chen had never lacked resolve to stake his life!

His mind settled, and there was no further hesitation.

Luo Chen abruptly reversed the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture!

From within his lifebound Golden Core, heaven-flooding mana poured out, carrying with it the violent mana left by the shattered second Origin Pill, beginning to scour every corner of Luo Chen's body.

As it washed over him again and again.

His bones began to crack inch by inch.

His muscles began to fall away chunk by chunk.

All at once.

Boom!

Only a tremendous sound was heard inside the great hall as Luo Chen's fleshly body suddenly exploded!

Amid the blood mist filling the sky, countless beads of blood floated in the void as though time had stopped.

Even his head had not a fraction left.

Only one great spine bone flickering with slight lightning stood in the void like a dragon.

Miserable cries rang out at the same time.

"Master!"

Wild wind swept through as Heavenjade suddenly arrived.

Behind her, a rumbling crawl constantly sounded, as if some enormous creature were charging wildly through the palace.

Suddenly.

A huge one-horned dragon head pushed into the great hall. Looking at this scene, its eyes nearly split.

"Master!"

"Heavenjade, what happened to Master?"

Heavenjade's eyes were filled with disbelief. Trembling, she reached out, wanting to touch that complete thunder bone.

That was the great spine bone Luo Chen had condensed during his Initiate years in White Night's cave dwelling in the Accumulated Thunder Nine Mountains, borrowing heavenly lightning to temper his body.

Over all these years, although it had never revealed any wonders, it had remained rooted in Luo Chen's body.

And at this moment, of Luo Chen's entire body, only this one bone had been preserved intact...

Crack!

Heavenjade's hand stopped.

In her tear-bright eyes, a crack appeared in that complete thunder bone.

Afterward, the cracks spread rapidly.

Crack, crack, crack, the sounds rang out without end.

Until it completely broke apart into small pieces of bone.

At that moment, a dazed flicker of release seemed to pass through the minds of the two demons.

That was the slave restriction Luo Chen had set on them in earlier years.

And what the lifting of that restriction meant, the two demons understood perfectly well.

Within the silent great hall, there was only a woman holding a complete black cuirass, murmuring in despair:

"Master has fallen."

*(End of Chapter)*
