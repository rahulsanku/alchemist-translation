# Chapter 1150: The Tides of Power Crush, The Battle at Guanchao Pavilion

On the long street.

A man strolled leisurely.

He wore a white robe, his long hair tied behind him with only a simple wooden hairpin. He walked with the ease of a casual stroll, free and unconstrained, elegant and unrestrained.

Such bearing was utterly different from that of martial artists, instead reflecting a certain air of otherworldly detachment.

The white-robed man's steps finally halted before a majestic and solemn mansion.

The man looked up, and the two characters 【Xiao Mansion】 entered his sight.

This was the Xiao Mansion, also commonly understood as the City Lord's Mansion in the Immortal Ruins City, although the City Lord did not handle official business here.

The Mysterious Armor soldiers guarding the gate saw the white-robed man pause and had the intention to step forward and drive him away.

Yet, for some reason, when they met his eyes, which were like bottomless abysses, they could not even open their mouths, deeply afraid of disturbing this person.

The white-robed man, seemingly oblivious, lifted his foot and stepped into the mansion.

As he passed the guarding soldiers, he simply said, "Inform Xiao Feng to come see me."

Those soldiers stared blankly as Luo Chen entered the mansion casually, as if returning to his own home, without any obstruction.

Only after his figure had disappeared did they look at each other, their faces full of shock and fear.

"Who is he?"

"How dare he address the City Lord by his given name so directly?"

"It seems like Luo Chen!"

"It's him! During the Martial Combat to determine the master, I was maintaining order at the arena. He is Luo Chen!"

"Quickly go and report to the City Lord!"

...

The name "Great Destruction Realm" sounded chaotic, but in reality, over the millennia, even without forming a nation or dynasty, order was very stable.

With Wuyou City as the leader, it governed seventy-two cities.

Each city had its corresponding administrative body and armed forces.

For example, the Immortal Ruins City, besides the Mysterious Armor Army, also had a complete civil official system.

From village heads and town chiefs up to county magistrates and city governors, everything was complete.

Hundreds of towns and villages, under the management of these basic officials, survived by relying on the core Immortal Ruins City.

And all power over life and death was actually in the hands of the master of the Immortal Ruins City.

The current master of the Immortal Ruins City was Xiao Feng. The place where many officials reported their duties was in the government office.

The Mysterious Armor Army Commander, Chu Zhaonan, stopped the soldier who came to report to the government office, because the meeting the City Lord was holding today was quite important, concerning the future development plan of the Immortal Ruins City, and could not be easily disturbed.

But after the soldier mentioned Luo Chen, Chu Zhaonan couldn't help but become serious.

That one could not be kept waiting!

After hesitating for a moment, Chu Zhaonan still chose to barge into the meeting.

Facing a pair of dissatisfied eyes, he braced himself and said to Xiao Feng, who was sitting at the head of the table with the first signs of authority, "City Lord, the guest elder Luo Chen is in the mansion, requesting an audience."

Xiao Feng had frowned at Chu Zhaonan for interrupting the meeting, but upon hearing the name "Luo Chen," his expression changed slightly.

An official below coughed and said, "City Lord, regarding the matter of reopening the trade route to Mocheng City, you must make a decision!"

Xiao Feng withdrew his gaze and had already made a choice.

"Open it! Although Mocheng City is located in a remote wilderness with scarce resources, the ores produced there are most suitable for forging swords and sabers. They lack the technology to refine the ores. We only need to open the trade route, import the raw ore, refine it slightly, and then resell it at three to five times the price. This kind of business must be done. However, considering the previous low prices led to a falling out with Mocheng City, we need to negotiate well before reopening the trade route this time."

Chu Zhaonan was still in the hall, his expression extremely awkward.

"City Lord?"

Xiao Feng took a deep breath and replied, "Since it is Luo Chen requesting an audience with this City Lord, let him wait for now!"

Chu Zhaonan recalled the scene from the arena that day in his mind, his heart full of scruples, and said uneasily, "But, that is Luo Chen!"

"So what if it's Luo Chen? In the end, he is just a guest elder of this City Lord." Xiao Feng said decisively, a hint of ruthlessness in his eyes.

If Luo Chen wanted to see him, and he went running over obsequiously, then what authority would he, the City Lord, have? In the eyes of others, who was the true master of this Immortal Ruins City?

Especially today, with so many important officials watching!

But after saying this, Xiao Feng couldn't help but feel a bit uneasy in his heart.

Chu Zhaonan did not dare to force Xiao Feng and could only obediently withdraw.

But as he left the meeting hall, a sharp voice transmitted into his ear.

"Go and inform Tie Xiong, Xiao Zirui, the recuperating Iron Vajra, Silver Hand Yan Luo, Helian Shanque, and the others. They are to return to the mansion with me tonight."

It was the technique of gathering sound into a thread, transmitting sound into the ear.

The person sending the message was Xiao Feng!

Chu Zhaonan's steps paused for a moment.

Was such a grand display really necessary just to meet Luo Chen?

Or was it that Xiao Feng was afraid?

Chu Zhaonan's expression shifted subtly, but he said nothing, only hurriedly leaving the government office.

...

Evening time.

The setting sun of early summer cast its golden rays upon the ancient buildings of the Immortal Ruins City, reflecting a patch of gold.

Xiao Feng rode in a carriage, traveling from the government office towards his own mansion.

Along the way, people continuously joined the back of the convoy.

Among them were his loyal old subordinate, Tie Xiong.

There was his clansman, Xiao Zirui, who had disappeared before but later came to pledge allegiance, and whom Xiao Feng had generously forgiven.

There were also those who were once enemies but now relied on him: Iron Vajra, Silver Hand Yan Luo, Helian Shanque, and others.

The Mysterious Armor Army Commander, Chu Zhaonan, was even personally driving the carriage for him.

With such a formidable lineup, it could be said that, after all the Leakless Realm experts had gone to Wuyou City, they were enough to sweep through any major city in the world.

The only pity was the absence of Granny Yu, Old Monster Redflame, and a few others, as they had all been sent by Xiao Feng to various places to hold them down.

But it was enough!

The unease in Xiao Feng's heart gradually dissipated with the gathering of so many experts, replaced by lofty aspirations and high spirits!

Even if Luo Chen's strength was the best in the Immortal Ruins City, so what?

He was just one man!

In today's meeting, Xiao Feng would use overwhelming power to crush him, making him understand whose name this Immortal Ruins City bore!

The carriage stopped at the main entrance of the Xiao Mansion.

As soon as Xiao Feng got down, he saw the palace-robed beautiful woman who had been waiting at the door for a long time.

"Third Lady, where is Luo Chen now?"

Xiao Sanniang said softly, "At the Ten Thousand Carp Lake."

Xiao Feng frowned. "Why wasn't he arranged to wait in the flower hall, but instead allowed to go to the Ten Thousand Carp Lake, which Father liked best?"

After saying this, Xiao Feng subconsciously shook his head.

Given Third Lady's temperament, how could she possibly arrange for such a powerful expert? Wasn't he himself also bringing a team of people to see Luo Chen with such a fanfare?

Exhaling a turbid breath, Xiao Feng asked again, "Was he at the Ten Thousand Carp Lake all afternoon?"

Xiao Sanniang lowered her voice. "Not exactly. After Luo Chen arrived, he went straight to the Scripture Pavilion and didn't come out until an hour ago."

"Went to the Scripture Pavilion again?" Xiao Feng said.

Xiao Sanniang nodded. "Yes, I checked the Scripture Pavilion records afterwards. Luo Chen spent a long time on the fourth and fifth floors, reading a large number of high-level techniques and martial skills. Not only that, but he also took the original copies of high-level martial skills like *Flower-Folding Finger* and *Heaven-Earth Reversal*, and even the original copy of *Thousand Astral Hundred Turns*, personally written by your father, was forcibly taken out of the Scripture Pavilion by him."

"Original copies?"

Upon hearing this, Xiao Feng's expression changed drastically.

Previously, Luo Chen borrowing books was one thing; at most, he borrowed transcribed copies. But now, he dared to take the original copies of secret manuals out of the Scripture Pavilion without permission.

This Luo Chen was truly becoming more and more arrogant, more and more insolent.

But then, he forcibly suppressed the anger in his heart.

Today, he would make him understand the difference in status.

If the other party did not comply, then from now on, there would be no such person as "Luo Chen" in the Immortal Ruins City.

Xiao Feng's gaze swept over the crowd behind him, and he waved his hand.

"Follow me!"

Xiao Feng took the lead, with Tie Xiong and Xiao Sanniang following closely on his left and right. Behind them were the five Furnace Realm experts.

The group headed straight for the Ten Thousand Carp Lake.

The Xiao Mansion occupied an extremely large area, almost like the imperial palaces recorded in ancient texts.

Especially the Ten Thousand Carp Lake, with its misty, vast waters and schools of colorful carp, was one of the most famous scenic spots in the Immortal Ruins City.

The group of experts moved very quickly, and it didn't take long for them to reach the Ten Thousand Carp Lake.

With just one glance, everyone saw the white-robed man standing alone in the Guanchao Pavilion, one hand behind his back, the other suspended in the air.

"Luo..."

Before Xiao Feng's voice could fully form, his attention was drawn to Luo Chen's suspended right hand.

A broad palm, long fingers, extending strands of pure white threads, like fishing lines, hanging over the golden lake surface bathed in the setting sun.

In the lake, plump carps leaped and competed, trying to bite the hookless fishing line.

But with the insight of these experts, how could they mistake those white threads for fishing lines?

They were clearly an extremely high-level technique of transforming Gang Qi into threads!

The move that Granny Yu had previously relied on as her trump card was precisely this: transforming Gang Qi into threads, sealing the heart and locking the meridians.

That move was something even late-stage Furnace Realm martial artists dared not underestimate.

As strong as Wood Wolf Divine Lord, if he hadn't received intelligence beforehand, he would have suffered a great loss under that move.

This Luo Chen had only broken through to the Furnace Realm a day ago; how could he have mastered the transformation of Gang Qi into threads, a technique that many long-established Furnace Realm martial artists could not comprehend?

Xiao Feng's mouth went dry, and the name he hadn't finished saying came to an abrupt halt.

And before the Guanchao Pavilion, Luo Chen slowly turned around, his gaze calmly sweeping over the crowd.

"City Lord Xiao, you have kept this seat waiting for an entire afternoon!"

Xiao Feng swallowed his saliva, forcing himself to stay calm. "You also know I am the City Lord, governing tens of thousands of people. Naturally, I must attend to a myriad of affairs daily. Coming to see you is already giving you plenty of face."

Luo Chen chuckled lightly and flicked his hand, causing the Gang Qi to dissipate. The carps in the Ten Thousand Carp Lake behind him seemed to sense something and scattered.

"Whether you attend to a myriad of affairs daily or not, this seat does not care. This seat only knows that I do not like waiting for people."

Originally, their side held the numerical advantage, but for some reason, under Luo Chen's lighthearted and casual demeanor, Xiao Feng only felt the pressure like a mountain.

Every word and sentence seemed to knock on his very soul.

He could only brace himself and say, "You waited for an afternoon, in the end. So what?"

"Alas..."

A soft sigh, and Luo Chen's gaze swept over the people behind him.

"Forget it. I originally intended to speak kindly and have you all work for me. But seeing this display now, you ultimately do not understand the gap between yourselves and this seat, hence your unwillingness."

Amidst the sigh, Luo Chen raised his right hand, his five fingers spread wide, a hazy white light appearing on them.

Then, he flicked his finger through the air.

A sharp whistling sound suddenly exploded.

The moment he made his move, Xiao Feng's side seemed to have been prepared, each making their own move.

Xiao Sanniang pulled Xiao Feng, stomped her foot, and retreated backward.

Tie Xiong, disregarding everything, gathered black light in his palm and pounced fiercely.

Behind him, Iron Vajra, Silver Hand Yan Luo, Helian Shanque, Xiao Zirui, and Chu Zhaonan, five great experts, attacked simultaneously!

Powerful Gang Qi surged out violently.

In an instant, the originally quiet and leisurely Guanchao Pavilion turned into a boiling battlefield.

Faced with this scene, Luo Chen's expression remained normal. The five finger forces he had sent out, incredibly agile, bypassed the leading Tie Xiong and shot straight towards the five people behind him.

This was precisely the *Immortal Returning to Penglai* from the Three Immortal-Slaying Fingers created by Wang Yuan!

It could flexibly control fierce and rigid energy.

After Luo Chen had cultivated it to Great Perfection, this move had not only become more flexible but could also unleash multiple finger forces simultaneously.

Even the more violent and rigid Gang Qi could be controlled with ease.

The sharp whistling sound quickly approached the five Furnace Realm experts. The danger contained within made their hearts tighten, and they all stopped to defend.

Each person gathered their own Gang Qi, forming a thick Gang Qi protective shield in front of them.

However, what terrified the five experts was that these five finger forces were not straightforward, nor were they simply winding back and forth. At the moment of contact, they even felt a frantic spiral rotation.

Under this spiral rotation, the originally thick Gang Qi protective shield became as fragile as cloth.

Puff! Puff! Puff! Puff! Puff!

A continuous series of sounds rang out. The five finger forces penetrated their bodies, passed through the Gang Qi protective shields, and following the gaps in their Qi mechanisms, entered the bodies of the five.

Tie Xiong had no time to check on his companions' situation and could only more ferociously activate his own Gang Qi. The black light on his hand became deeper and deeper.

This was the ultimate technique he had mastered after cultivating the Leakless Golden Body—the *Dark Crow Divine Palm*!

Opposite him, Luo Chen, who was close at hand, narrowed his eyes slightly.

"The Leakless Realm?"

Luo Chen's empty left hand formed a fist and slowly punched out.

To outsiders, this punch seemed incredibly slow, but only Tie Xiong, who was facing it, knew how fast it was.

In an instant, fist and palm met.

Bang!

A sound like muffled thunder rang out.

Tie Xiong suffered a terrifying, immense force. Even though his entire body was Leakless and covered in Gang Qi, he still could not withstand the impact of this force.

Only one thought remained in his mind—*Is this the power that my junior brother Iron Vajra endured that day?*

The next moment, the Gang Qi around his body was forcibly torn apart.

His entire right hand, like glass, shattered inch by inch, broke joint by joint.

Especially as the residual force was not exhausted, Tie Xiong was sent flying backward by the punch, his body crashing directly into a hall, smashing beams, collapsing pillars, and sending bricks and tiles flying everywhere.

And inside the Guanchao Pavilion, Luo Chen had not retreated an inch, still standing there.

He casually flicked his hand.

The dark black light enveloping his fist fell into the Ten Thousand Carp Lake.

Immediately after, behind him, a thousand-zhang wave suddenly erupted.

Such a scene showed the power of Tie Xiong's palm strike.

Yet it still could not shake Luo Chen half a step!

"Once, this seat also killed an existence like you!"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen raised his right hand, which had previously unleashed the *Immortal Returning to Penglai*.

Unbeknownst to them, strands of white threads were suspended from it.

These threads extended all the way to the hearts of the five Furnace Realm experts.

Luo Chen looked at the distant, still-shaken Xiao Feng with a faint, ambiguous smile.

"City Lord Xiao, what now?"

Xiao Feng opened his mouth, his face full of disbelief.

He had imagined Luo Chen would be very strong, but he never imagined he would be this strong.

Even if Luo Chen had previously defeated Iron Vajra with a single move, he had at most regarded him as being on the same level as a Leakless Realm expert.

In this battle, with Tie Xiong making a move, plus the assistance of five great experts, even a Leakless Realm expert could be fought head-on.

Yet the defeat came so quickly, so unacceptable.

Faced with Xiao Feng's dazed silence, Luo Chen's brow furrowed slightly.

"Hmm?"

He flicked his finger.

The next moment, the thread connecting to Xiao Zirui suddenly snapped.

And Xiao Zirui's eyes instantly lost their luster, and he collapsed limply to the ground.

He was dead.

(End of Chapter)
