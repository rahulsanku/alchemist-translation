# Chapter 538: Fellow Daoist, Let Us Talk This Out!

Only five years had passed since the sky over the Swallow Islands had changed.

The islands' upper ranks lamented that the people were exhausted and their strength depleted, but for the true common folk at the bottom, things were not like that at all.

Because of Luo Chen's terrifying demand for medicinal materials, the cultivators of the various great clans thought of every possible way to spend money and effort helping gather them, but the number of cultivators was limited.

Therefore, many work tasks were handed down to mortals.

For mortals, this was opportunity!

It was a chance to enter the eyes of the immortal clans and change their own fate.

Because of this, the ecology at the bottom instead appeared bustling and enthusiastic.

At several key docks, the number of mortal martial artists far exceeded what it had been five years ago.

It was precisely because of this

that when the tsunami, stretching for several hundred li along the coastline, came sweeping in, many busy mortal martial artists discovered it.

"The weather is perfectly fine. How could a tsunami form?"

"It is only a small tsunami a few hundred li wide. Do not worry."

A tsunami several hundred li wide truly did not count for much compared to those violent tsunamis that casually spanned several thousand li, or even affected ten thousand li.

The mortal martial artists on the docks were not too panicked. They packed up their own goods and nimbly sprinted backward.

But after only a few steps, an accident occurred.

"Help!"

"Help!"

Cries for help rose one after another, making the mortal martial artists running at the front subconsciously turn back.

Only then did they discover that the tsunami was not large, but its speed was incomparably fast!

It was as if some enormous beast were stirring within the sea, striking waves across the vast ocean.

Enormous beast?

"Could sea demons be inside, raising wind and waves?"

"Hurry and report to the cultivator lords!"

"No need to report. Look at the sky."

In the sky, at some unknown time, over a dozen figures had already appeared, scattered here and there above the Swallow Islands.

Their expressions were all solemn, and there was even a trace of fear in their eyes.

"Core Formation Senior!"

"Definitely an expert of the water path."

"An existence capable of manipulating the ocean for his own use and raising water mountains in the sea with a wave of his hand... as far as I know, among Core Formation cultivators, there are only a few. With this kind of display, could it be the Wu clan ancestor of Moluo Stream personally arriving?"

"Whether it is him or not, Fellow Daoist Cheng, quickly make a decision!"

In the end, the spiritual-sense voice transmissions all gathered on one young man.

Cheng Dou, who had already reached Foundation Establishment Perfected Completion, now wore an expression of alarm and uncertainty, along with a few traces of anger.

He had finally reached Foundation Establishment Perfected Completion, and had exhausted several hundred years of the clan's reserves before exchanging for a Core Formation secret art from Demon Lord Qingyang.

Recently, he had been hastening to cultivate the Qingyang Alchemy Canon, yet now he encountered such a matter. It was truly a calamity fallen from the sky.

How good would it have been if he could quietly wait until he cultivated the Qingyang Alchemy Canon, then form his core steadily and peacefully!

But at this moment, it was no longer the time to cling to luck.

"Such a fierce momentum. He is absolutely not a friendly guest."

"The newcomer comes with ill intent!"

"Activate the Sea-Suppressing Grand Formation[TERM?]!"

With one command, the order was immediately passed down at speed.

Across the thirty-six Swallow Islands, pillars of light began to rise one after another.

Faintly, a top-level grand formation was about to take shape.

Seeing this scene, Cheng Dou could not help letting out a breath of relief. So long as the formation took shape, even if a Core Formation cultivator personally arrived, they could gather the strength of the thirty-six islands and resist him outside.

Someone beside him asked quietly, "How should we handle the mortals at the docks?"

Cheng Dou frowned and lowered his head to glance down. "No time to bother with them. Let them live or die on their own!"

In the eyes of cultivators, mortals were nothing but tools!

No, sometimes they could not even be counted as tools. They were merely consumables.

Perhaps only those mortal martial artists who reached the level of Xiantian grandmaster[TERM?] could make them spare one extra glance, but only that one glance.

Just as they were conversing, a rumbling sound suddenly rang out.

"So fast?"

Cheng Dou turned his head in astonishment.

As far as his eyes could see, the small island at the very front had already been swept into the tsunami.

The pillar of light that had hastily erupted was extinguished inside it in an instant.

He swallowed and abruptly realized something.

The island-linking formation method passed down by the Cang Hai Righteous Dao Alliance was meant to deal with large-scale sea demon hordes whose movements were slow. Thus, it had a long preparation time to draw on spirit veins and earth qi.

And the enemy they faced now was clearly a powerful human expert!

Especially with the other party's speed so fast and offensive so fierce, there was no way he would give them that much time.

A look of despair had already appeared.

Suddenly,

Cheng Dou turned and cried out shrilly, "We ask Demon Lord to act!"

His hoarse, exhausted voice spread through heaven and earth, rushing straight toward the Invitation Moon island group.

Beneath the clear sky,

an indifferent voice drifted over.

"Granted!"

Before the word had fully fallen, a vast black mist surged into the sky from Invitation Moon Island.

Within it, ghosts wailed and wolves howled, as if it were a ghost domain.

The next moment, over a hundred black shadows exuding powerful auras, clad in armor and holding sabers, spears, swords, and halberds, charged out from the black mist.

This scene was clearly a hundred ghosts marching by day, blotting out clouds and covering the sun!

Over a hundred Ghost Generals formed a mysterious formation. They leaped across several islands and charged straight toward the oceanic tsunami that had already destroyed four islands in succession, rushing forward fearlessly.

Beneath the clear sky, the vast water mountain and the black-mist hundred ghosts collided in an instant.

Explosive rumbling boomed out.

...

Invitation Moon Island, inside the pill chamber.

Luo Chen divided his attention between two tasks.

On one side, he controlled the final refinement work of the True Flame Pill. On the other, he watched the water mirror condensed before him.

When he saw the surging wave crests inside that tsunami, rising one after another to a hundred zhang high, his expression changed slightly.

A hundred-zhang water mountain was already not inferior to the height of those large tsunamis in nature.

Human strength could actually rival the might of nature?

He was very clear that Core Formation cultivators were formidable, formidable enough to split a river with one sword and shatter a mountain with one fist.

But in the end, these were pure destructive power.

Destruction was always simpler than construction.

But to achieve the legendary carrying mountains and driving seas, chasing stars and pursuing the moon[TERM?], that was absolutely impossible.

During the opening war, although Luo Chen had once carried out a mountain-carrying plan[TERM?], he had gathered ten Core Formation cultivators in one breath, along with many low-level cultivators and the power of a Scarlet Flame Dragon Boat, before barely succeeding.

If one truly wanted to carry mountains and drive seas without pressure, then it had to be a Sage Perfected Being who could do it easily.

Very clearly, if there truly were a Sage Perfected Being dealing with him, Luo Chen, there would be no need for such methods at all.

"The newcomer is very strong. Even among Core Formation cultivators, he is absolutely a highly skilled expert with powerful methods."

As he muttered to himself, Luo Chen's attention returned to that side hall on Invitation Moon Island.

"Han Zhan, how much ability do you still have left now?"

At this moment, Luo Chen was not unable to move freely.

At worst, he would abandon this furnace of pills.

The reason he did not personally act was also because he wanted to test exactly what Han Zhan could still do.

A wisp of remnant soul from a Sage Perfected Being, with no fleshly body, no Nascent Soul, no lifebound magic treasure, only a wisp of remnant soul and an incomplete Soul-Refining Banner that had lost ten thousand-plus souls.

Under his expectant gaze,

Han Zhan acted.

A hundred ghosts marched by day, blotting out clouds and covering the sun.

With this move alone, Luo Chen's eyes lit up.

These hundred-plus Foundation Establishment Ghost Generals had clearly formed a formation under Han Zhan's control.

Its subtlety was still above the Myriad Ghost Grand Formation[TERM?] Yan Nantian had displayed earlier.

Even if Luo Chen personally acted, he would have to expend quite some effort.

"But if it is only like this, perhaps it can stop that tsunami, but it cannot stop the enemy hidden inside!" Luo Chen muttered.

Just as he said,

the tsunami charging toward Invitation Moon Island slowed somewhat under the hundred ghosts' impact.

After being obstructed, the wave crests in the tsunami continuously stacked and rose layer by layer.

In just a few breaths, they formed a "water mountain"!

Atop the water mountain, white waves churned, and a figure stepped out, treading lotus and trailing waves.

At the same time,

a low roar came from Invitation Moon Island.

Immediately afterward, a black shadow suddenly shot out.

Atop the water mountain!

"A Ghost King?" Wu Qi looked at the black shadow that rushed out, a strange look appearing in his eyes, and spat out one sentence. "Good timing!"

Afterward, he flipped his wrist, and a small flag landed in his hand.

Holding the flagpole, he unfurled it forward.

In an instant, three jiaolongs split out from within the tsunami and charged toward that Ghost King.

Three jiaolongs and one ghost collided in an instant.

That Ghost King roared ferociously, its two palm-leaf-fan-sized hands grabbing straight out and directly seizing one water jiaolong by the neck.

The other two wanted to attack, but chains rattled out from the Ghost King's body, binding them.

"Break!"

Following a low roar,

boom!

In an instant, the three jiaolongs turned into water spray filling the sky and blasted in all directions.

The water pillars, full of impact force, actually blasted the mortal-inhabited island below into pits and hollows, revealing countless huge depressions.

Seeing this, the strange look in Wu Qi's eyes grew even stronger.

But very clearly, his methods did not stop there.

The small flag in his hand waved continuously.

The great sea behind him issued rumbling roars. More and more seawater gathered toward him. The water mountain under his feet still maintained its original height, but more and more water jiaolongs flew out.

One, two, three... in just a few breaths, seven more jiaolongs flew out, shaking their heads and swaying their tails.

"My lifebound magic treasure, the Sea King Flag[TERM?], is considered outstanding even among middle-grade magic treasures. It has also been refined by me for several hundred years, so I control it like my own heart, and its might is like the sea!"

"If I am within the ocean, I can battle a late Core Formation great cultivator!"

"Even though I have landed now, with the sea at my back, I am still in an undefeatable position."

"Let me see exactly what ability this mysterious cultivator, who easily erased Yan Nantian, has, and whether he is a remnant evil of the Primordial Demon Sect!"

Wu Qi was very cautious.

So cautious that as soon as he acted, he directly used his lifebound magic treasure, the Sea King Flag, and even came bearing the momentum of an oceanic tsunami.

To be honest, the fact that the Hundred Ghosts Grand Formation could block his tsunami at the first moment had already shocked him greatly.

And this third-tier Ghost King, filled with battle wisdom, shocked him even more.

Even with seven jiaolongs all emerging, they were actually unable to do anything to it in a short time.

After several exchanges, although the third-tier Ghost King's aura grew weaker and weaker, it also made Wu Qi faintly confirm one matter in his heart.

"This Demon Lord Qingyang is ninety percent likely to be a remnant evil of the Primordial Demon Sect, and very likely an expert of the soul-refining lineage[TERM?]."

"Ordinary Primordial Demon Sect members cannot freely control resentful souls and fierce ghosts like this, or drive an ordinary Myriad Souls Banner to such a degree."

Although he said this,

Wu Qi was very clearly not satisfied with the result stopping here.

If he were blocked outside the Swallow Islands by a single soul banner, once this spread, how would he, Wu Qi, continue mixing in the Northern Sea cultivation world?

He thrust his right hand down, inserting that small flag into the waves.

His two hands continuously formed seals, and majestic mana surged out without cease.

Thousand Jiaolongs Devour the Dragon[TERM?]!

The next moment, streaks of spiritual light formed within the tsunami he had brought with him.

Blue jiaolongs, slightly smaller than before, flew out one after another, roaring as they charged toward that third-tier Ghost King.

Their numbers were many, their momentum vast. At a glance, they seemed boundless.

Even though chains emerged one after another from the third-tier Ghost King's body, under the gnawing of more and more water jiaolongs, they also became limp and powerless.

Not only that, after breaking through the black mist defense, those water jiaolongs all bit onto the Ghost King's enormous body.

"Roar... roar..."

Furious roars continuously rang out.

Anyone could clearly hear the anger and terror within.

...

On Invitation Moon Island, Luo Chen's brows furrowed slightly.

"Senior?"

Inside the side hall, Han Zhan smiled bitterly. "Apologies. For the time being, this is all I can do. It is not that I am not trying my best, but a clever woman cannot cook without rice! This Myriad Souls Banner has lost the assistance of its myriad souls. It truly does not live up to its name. Without myriad-soul ghost qi replenishing it, that Ghost King was matchlessly strong when it first appeared, but it could only shine for one brilliant moment."

What he said was the truth.

This Myriad Souls Banner had already been overused when Yan Nantian previously fought Luo Chen.

First, it had been consumed by Blazing Fire Miasma. Later, it had been bombarded by Mountain Collapse and the Nine Suns detonating together.

The low-level souls forming its foundation had been completely exhausted.

For Han Zhan to drive it to this degree, and hold that Core Formation Stage Six enemy outside the Swallow Islands, was already doing his utmost.

But was this Han Zhan's full strength now?

Luo Chen was somewhat dissatisfied, but also very satisfied!

"Fine. Senior, go rest. Leave the rest to me."

Han Zhan was startled. "You finished refining the pills?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly and beckoned with his hand. Ten streaks of flowing light flew out of the pill cauldron.

As far as the eye could see, every pill was crimson as agate, and the pill fragrance was like rare delicacies.

Supreme-grade True Flame Pill had now reached Perfected Completion!

Inside the side hall, seeing this, Han Zhan's eyes revealed shock.

Luo Chen put away the supreme-grade True Flame Pills, then withdrew the formation restriction and drifted into the air above Invitation Moon Island.

A pair of spiritual eyes looked from a thousand li away at the scene of a thousand jiaolongs devouring the ghost.

"It was not easy to obtain a Myriad Souls Banner. Even if incomplete, I cannot let it be wasted like this!"

After muttering to himself, he leaped.

Raging mana began to burn.

His entire body transformed into a meteor, flying toward the outer edge of the Swallow Islands.

Where he passed, firelight surged into the sky.

And at some unknown time, a wisp of azure flame had already coiled around his palm.

A thousand li were crossed in an instant.

When Wu Qi abruptly raised his head, Luo Chen had already lifted his right palm.

"Fellow Daoist's methods are extraordinary. Receive one move from me as well!"

Before his words fell, his right palm pressed down with a boom.

Weng...

As his right palm pressed down, azure flame blazed high and mana surged wildly.

In the void, a gigantic handprint a hundred zhang long and wide immediately appeared, every fingerprint upon it clearly visible.

The coiling azure flame seemed capable of burning everything to ash.

It was Luo Chen's proud method, Qingyang Great Handprint!

Wu Qi's expression changed greatly.

There was no time for words. The small flag before him grew in the wind, a drop of deep-blue light flew out, and his two hands suddenly lifted toward the sky.

The next moment, a shocking wave transformed into a giant wall and surged into the sky from the great sea behind him.

The deep-blue light merged into it, making this giant wall appear even heavier.

The azure great palm ignored everything and pressed down with a single palm.

When the two made contact, only an explosive boom could be heard between heaven and earth.

Boom!

Terrifying might transformed into a wild gale, sweeping through the eight wilds in all directions. It crushed the plants and trees on the islands, forcing all the cultivators to lower their heads and bend their waists.

Violently hot water vapor dripped onto the earth like lava, and all those it touched screamed miserably.

As for the two parties at the center of the clash, neither had an easy time.

Under that terrifying rebound force, Luo Chen involuntarily retreated several hundred steps, his figure staggering in the air.

Wu Qi himself, under the backwash of the waves, was forced to retreat into the great sea.

"So this is a Core Formation Stage Six cultivator?"

"As expected, even within mid Core Formation, those at Stage Four and Stage Six are as different as heaven and earth!"

Once Luo Chen steadied his steps, a trace of excitement flashed in his eyes.

Shua!

The Mystic Flame Sword landed in his right hand, the sword tip pointing diagonally.

His left hand formed seals one-handed, and sparks suddenly appeared one after another.

In only a few breaths, the sparks swelled, and nine blazing suns floated behind him.

It was also at this moment

that a figure rose and fell within the great sea, emerging while treading lotus and trailing waves.

Seeing Luo Chen's formation, he could not help swallowing.

"Fellow Daoist, let us talk this out!"

(End of chapter)
