# Chapter 1035: The Name of a Prodigy, the Boundary Between Mortal and Immortal

The Spirit Testing Grand Ceremony had concluded perfectly.

The shock it had brought became the talk of the Zhou, Lin, and Yin clans, and even countless cultivators within ten thousand li, for days to come.

One could even imagine that news of a peerless genius possessing a heavenly spirit root and an innate Dao body would spread across the entire Tiannan Continent!

Why was this so?

The reason was actually very simple!

In this world, those with heavenly spirit roots, though rare, would always appear given the vast population. At most, they could be called a once-in-a-century genius, and their upper limit was also locked.

Those with special constitutions were even rarer, but they too would appear from time to time.

However!

Those who possessed both a heavenly spirit root *and* a special constitution were exceedingly rare.

Moreover, unlike many geniuses whose special constitutions did not perfectly match their spirit roots, Zhou Fuyou's spirit root and constitution were of the most compatible, homologous attributes.

The superposition of the two provided an unimaginable boost to both cultivation efficiency and combat strength!

The last person to possess a similar "configuration" had long been famous throughout the Central Continent, and was once treasured by the Heavenly Origin Five Elders as a precious gem.

That person was called Shi Ju!

He came from the Shi Great House, one of the Twelve Great Houses, possessing a fire-attribute heavenly spirit root and an Innate Nine Yang Dao Body.

He had advanced to the Sage stage in just two hundred years.

Not only that, but under the cultivation of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, he had also nurtured a lightning-attribute clone.

He was hailed as invincible among his peers, with extremely strong combat power!

This could be seen from Shi Ju's participation in the Eastern Wilderness war, where he once crossed ranks to contend with a Great Demon Emperor.

The only stain on Shi Ju's record was probably his defeat at the hands of Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen in the five-hundred-year Grotto-Heaven Grand Competition, where even his lightning clone was once broken by the Wilderness Wanderer.

But even so, Shi Ju, who had comprehended the true meaning of the Fire Origin Law, was still a pivotal figure in the current Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

And now, Zhou Fuyou had also revealed similar potential.

His name would eventually spread across the entire Tiannan Continent, and even the entire Central Continent!

The prerequisite was that he would not die young!

"Dean, are we really not taking Zhou Fuyou with us?"

On a magnificent flying boat, a cultivator from the Jade Balance Court looked worriedly at Mo Wanting.

Mo Wanting shook her head. "He insisted, and I have no choice. This is the Zhou clan's territory, after all."

"But what if someone harbors malicious intentions and tries to harm him before he grows up? What then?"

To this, Mo Wanting simply smiled.

"With the Zhou clan's ancestor guarding him, there won't be any problems."

"Is the Zhou clan's ancestor up to it? Rumor has it that his lifespan is nearly over, and his cultivation is only at the mid-Sage stage..."

Faced with her subordinate's doubt, Mo Wanting did not explain. She recalled the terrifying pressure she had felt when she personally met the Zhou clan's ancestor not long ago. It was a pressure that none of the current Mo clan's Sage cultivators could bring her. Only the three late-Sage elders from the Mo clan's peak period had given her a similar feeling.

In other words, the Zhou clan's ancestor, Zhou Yunshen, had already stepped into the late-Sage stage without many people knowing.

With his time running out, he would surely protect Zhou Fuyou with his life.

With such a grand cultivator protecting him, Zhou Fuyou would be even safer than inside the Tiannan Dao Palace!

On the deck of the flying boat, a dozen or so youths were standing in small groups.

Among them were Zhou Yuwei, Zhou Yuhao, and Zhou Ping'an, who were from the Zhou clan.

On this trip, they would be accepted into the most authoritative Dao Palace on the Tiannan Continent, becoming the proud sons of heaven that everyone envied.

However, Mo Wanting, who had been somewhat enthusiastic towards them before, now paid them little attention. All she talked about was Zhou Fuyou, who had not come with them.

This made many of them feel indignant.

Mo Wanting ignored them. After her subordinate finished his inspection, she waved her hand.

"Return to the Dao Palace!"

The magnificent flying boat circled twice in the air before disappearing into the depths of the sky.

On the ground.

A group of Zhou clan cultivators looked up, only withdrawing their attention with a sense of loss after the boat was out of sight.

The clan leader, Zhou Junxiao, breathed a sigh of relief. He then looked at the twenty youths standing at the very front of the crowd, his eyes full of satisfaction.

Out of a hundred people tested, twenty-three had been found to possess spirit roots, including those with dual spirit roots, who were of excellent aptitude. The Zhou clan had reaped a great harvest this time.

However, the subsequent training could not be neglected.

"From now on, you will go to the clan's Scripture Transmission Hall every morning to learn the basics of cultivation."

"After a month, once you have a certain understanding of cultivation theory, you may formally begin cultivation and enter the Scripture Pavilion to choose three basic spells."

"Remember, do not slack off!"

"I know that some of you have received guidance at home, but those are your parents' personal understandings, which may be biased. The elders in the Scripture Transmission Hall, however, have inherited knowledge from our ancestors. It is more comprehensive, more correct, and will greatly help you in advancing to higher realms in the future."

Zhou Fuyou stood at the very front of the crowd, quietly listening to these words.

He knew that the clan leader's arrangement had other intentions besides what he had said.

Apart from the reasons he had given, it should also be to take care of the mortal descendants who had come from Coral Town. After all, these mortal descendants had not been able to be exposed to relevant theoretical guidance from a young age. This one month was a buffer period for them.

Zhou Fuyou didn't care about these things. He only cared about one thing.

"Clan Leader!"

Zhou Junxiao's gaze fell on him, and he said kindly, "Fuyou, do you have a question?"

Zhou Fuyou asked a very simple question: "Where will those who were not found to have spirit roots go?"

Zhou Junxiao smiled. "They will be sent to Coral Town to multiply, providing the clan with basic resources and bloodline descendants."

"Can't they stay within the clan?"

"If their parents or elders are in the clan, they can stay temporarily, but they will still be sent away after they turn sixteen."

The youth frowned.

Zhou Junxiao already understood his thoughts. After all, he knew why the other party was so insistent on staying in the clan.

He sighed and said, "Actually, sending them away is the best choice for these people without spirit roots. Everyone in the clan is a cultivator. Whether it's daily life or cultivation and sparring, a slight carelessness can leak mana, which can easily injure mortals by accident. There is also another point: the pain of being unable to cultivate, when faced with cultivators day and night, will grow heavier and heavier, causing psychological problems. Can you understand what I'm saying?"

Zhou Fuyou was stunned.

He murmured, "Without spirit roots, is it truly impossible to cultivate?"

...

"Without spirit roots, one can still cultivate!"

"As expected of the generation's Alchemy Master, renowned throughout the Eastern Wilderness!"

"Such an ingenious pill... how was it refined?"

In a side hall behind the Coral Hall, an old man with a face full of despair stared fanatically at the pill in front of him. Behind him was a blasted-open pill furnace, and the floor was covered in residue.

It was clear that he had failed many times in his experiments.

Suddenly.

A vicissitudinous voice sounded in the hall.

"Chongxiao, it's time."

Zhou Chongxiao was a member of the Zhou clan's second generation and one of the current six Adept cultivators.

He was one of those who, with the guidance of the clan ancestor, had advanced to the Adept stage alongside Zhou Xiaocheng.

Even during the advancement process, he had received the help of pills gifted by Luo Chen.

The reason Zhou Yunshen had specially selected Zhou Chongxiao was that he was the only remaining alchemy master in the Zhou clan, but his time was running out.

For the sake of the clan, Zhou Yunshen had placed resources on him, forcibly allowing him to advance to the Adept stage and extend his lifespan.

Hearing the vicissitudinous voice now, Zhou Chongxiao knelt on the ground and said anxiously, "Ancestor, give me a little more time. I will definitely be able to reverse-engineer the formulas for these three pills."

"A little more time... how much time?" Zhou Yunshen asked.

Zhou Chongxiao hesitated. "Three years... oh no, ten years. No matter what, as long as I can reverse-engineer these three formulas, allowing mortals to cultivate, then our Zhou clan will surely rise in Tiannan, and even become the largest cultivation clan in the entire Mountains and Seas Realm! For this, Chongxiao is willing to dedicate all the time he has left!"

"Three years? Ten years? All the time you have left?" Zhou Yunshen's tone was clearly disappointed. He sighed. "Forget it. How could his methods be something you can see through? The countless failures over these past few years have proven everything. Continuing to study them will yield no results."

"Ancestor!"

"That child has grown up and needs these three pills. Moreover, the younger generation of our Zhou clan cannot always rely on external pills to assist their cultivation. You should also leave this place and contribute your strength to the clan."

A gentle breeze swept by, and the three pills in front of Zhou Chongxiao vanished in an instant.

The old man's outstretched hand hung in the air, his face full of loss and bewilderment.

He only hated that he could not meet the Alchemy Master in person and consult him on the vast Dao of alchemy!

...

At the entrance of the Coral Hall.

Zhou Fuyou stood outside nervously, wondering why the clan ancestor had suddenly summoned him.

Was it just to see him?

If so, would his hidden strength be discovered?

If it was discovered, how would he explain it?

As the youth was feeling anxious, the door opened without any warning.

Zhou Fuyou took a deep breath, remained calm, and walked in.

Contrary to the estrangement he had imagined, as soon as he entered, he felt a sense of closeness.

It originated from within his body!

Was it bloodline?

Zhou Fuyou didn't quite understand where that feeling of close kinship came from. He simply bowed respectfully to the figure above.

"Fuyou pays respects to the Ancestor!"

"Raise your head!"

Zhou Fuyou raised his head and looked up at the old man above.

He was wearing the most iconic attire of the Zhou clan: a wide-sleeved robe of plain white. One could imagine that on his back was embroidered a brilliantly blooming red coral.

This was the highest symbol of the Zhou clan.

Below that, in order, were the golden coral white robes that only the clan leader could wear, then the blue coral robes of the elders, and the white coral robes of the ordinary clan members.

This was also a kind of commemoration for the Zhou clan, remembering their time in the Northern Sea.

But at this moment, the only thing Zhou Fuyou felt was the gaze of the vicissitudinous old man looking down at him.

It was a gaze of scrutiny, of pity and affection, yet also tinged with a hint of indifference.

How complex!

Zhou Fuyou didn't understand why he was looking at him like this.

Finally, Zhou Yunshen spoke.

His first words sent a chill down Zhou Fuyou's spine.

"So fast. You're about to build your foundation."

Zhou Fuyou swallowed his saliva and said fearfully, "The Ancestor has keen eyes. Fuyou is indeed about to build his foundation."

Zhou Yunshen smiled gently. "It's not that I have keen eyes, but because you have been cultivating right under my nose, and due to certain special reasons, I can perceive your exact realm. If it were any other Sage, they might not be able to see through your true situation."

Zhou Fuyou was extremely surprised.

He had indeed cultivated a concealment spell, which he believed could fool Initiate cultivators, and even Adept cultivators, but he had never thought it could fool a Sage.

Yet he never expected the Ancestor to have such a high opinion of him!

Was it because of his constitution?

"When can you break through?"

The old man's question came leisurely.

Zhou Fuyou answered truthfully: "At any time. The reason I haven't taken that step is only that I fear my skills are not yet refined and something might go wrong."

"And what accident could you possibly have?"

The old man murmured softly, his voice so low that Zhou Fuyou probably didn't hear it.

Zhou Fuyou hesitated for a moment, then took the initiative to ask, "May I ask what instructions the Ancestor has for summoning Fuyou here?"

"Don't be nervous. There are some things, but for you, they are all good things."

As the words fell, the old man waved his hand, and three jade plates flew in front of Zhou Fuyou.

On the first jade plate was a storage pouch painted with black and white chess pieces.

On the second jade plate was a neatly folded white robe and a token.

On the third jade plate were three small bottles.

"What are these?" Zhou Fuyou asked, somewhat bewildered.

The old man smiled. "That storage pouch was left for you by the Jade Balance Court Dean, Mo Wanting, before she left. I checked it. Inside are the three most orthodox water-attribute techniques of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, each of which can lead to the Sage stage. There are also a large number of water-attribute spells, cultivation insights, and auxiliary cultivation herbs. She is showing you goodwill by giving you such a precious gift."

Zhou Fuyou understood. "Is it because of my aptitude?"

"Yes! The Mo clan that Mo Wanting belongs to was the clan of the former Heavenly Origin Dao Sect leader. At its peak, it was the largest clan in the Central Continent, even the entire Mountains and Seas Realm. But after the Central Continent war, the Mo clan was knocked to the bottom, with barely a handful of Sage cultivators left. So they were pushed out to the Tiannan Continent. They gave up the upper-level power of the Tiannan Dao Palace and actively sought the position of the Seven Star Courts, with the aim of netting a large number of outstanding talents, hoping to use external forces to recover their strength. Your aptitude is unparalleled, and she even risked violating sect rules to secretly gift you core techniques. However, in my opinion, you won't need these three techniques."

Zhou Fuyou opened his mouth, somewhat shocked. It was clear that his clan ancestor had long known that he was cultivating the *Dew Cicada Art*, a technique focused solely on rapidly increasing one's realm.

He hesitated. "I can change my cultivation technique."

Unexpectedly, Zhou Yunshen waved his hand. "No need to change. With your aptitude, any technique in your hands will be a first-rate technique. Besides, the *Dew Cicada Art* itself is a top-tier technique from the Primordial Demon Sect of the Northern Sea holy land, not inferior to those of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. All you need to do is go to the Tiannan Dao Palace later to obtain the higher-level later chapters."

Zhou Fuyou's heart relaxed. It seemed he had chosen the right main cultivation technique.

He smiled. "Even so, I should still remember the Mo clan's kindness."

However, Zhou Yunshen denied his idea again.

"There's no need to remember this favor. In fact, I advise you not to have too close dealings with them."

"Ah? Why?"

"No reason. You will understand one day." Zhou Yunshen changed the subject, pointing to another jade plate. "You should understand what that robe represents. Only I can wear the red coral white robe. From now on, your status within the clan will be equal to mine! The meaning of that token is the same. From now on, you may take any clan resources you wish, the Scripture Pavilion will be completely open to you, and you may even freely mobilize clan members after informing the clan leader, Zhou Junxiao."

This time, Zhou Fuyou was completely stunned.

But before he could ponder why the clan ancestor was treating him so generously, his mind was completely occupied by the next words.

"On the last jade plate are three pills, gifted to me by my old friend, the Wilderness Wanderer, in years past. He has another title, the Alchemy Master, and possesses unfathomable skill in the Dao of alchemy. Since the Alchemy Saint ascended, the art of alchemy in this realm has been second to none! And these three pills are the result of his painstaking refinement, capable of breaking the boundary between mortal and immortal, allowing mortals to cultivate as well."

Breaking the boundary between mortal and immortal, allowing mortals to cultivate!

The youth's breathing suddenly became rapid.

He knelt on the ground and shouted, "Your child thanks the Ancestor for his grace!"

Zhou Yunshen turned sideways, as if avoiding the gesture, and merely said indifferently, "You may go!"

Zhou Fuyou stood up excitedly, holding the three jade plates, and slowly backed out of the Coral Hall.

When he reached the outside, facing the blazing summer sun, he felt his heart soar with joy.

After revealing his aptitude, everything had turned out for the best!

He had escaped his previous difficulties and leaped to become the highest-status person in the clan.

Any resource he wanted was his for the taking.

Most importantly, his sister could also cultivate!

"With this, I can also go into seclusion with no worries and break through to the Initiate stage."
