# Chapter 1113: Ascendant Battle, Smashing Through the Wilderness

Mountains and Seas Realm.

Since the ancient war, humanity had completely occupied the most essential lands of this realm, dividing the five continents and obtaining the fortune of heaven and earth.

The demon race had retreated in defeat, settling in a corner—whether the million great mountains, the endless demon sea, or the vast deserts where some barely clung to existence.

But the true masters of this realm were in an even more miserable state.

They had been forced to flee from the bountiful and prosperous five continents to the most barren and desolate wilderness.

These beings were the desolate beasts!

They cultivated no origin arts and refined no qi. To put it nicely, they were in harmony with the natural Dao; to put it harshly, they acted purely on instinct.

These creatures cultivated at an extremely slow pace, focusing on tempering their own bodies. If they lived long enough, they could gradually grow to a level ordinary people could scarcely imagine.

A level that could break through the world's shackles, reaching a realm that neither human nor demon cultivators could attain!

The Void Refinement stage!

The Diao Soul Wilderness that Luo Chen had seen before, and the Mirage Dragon who was the original owner of the inner pill he now held, both were at least at the sixth rank.

This was the limit of this world!

It was very difficult to achieve through acquired cultivation alone. Only desolate beasts, as the darlings of this world who were in harmony with the natural Dao, could barely reach it.

Luo Chen was not originally afraid that there would be sixth-rank desolate beasts in the wilderness.

Such darlings of heaven and earth could not be too numerous in the world. Having one Diao Soul Wilderness was already the limit.

Moreover, according to historical records, the desolate beasts that had retreated to the wilderness back then were merely supporting roles in the ancient human-demon war. Only three or four thousand years had passed since then; they could not have grown to the sixth rank.

But to be safe, he still brought along the four guardian spirit beasts!

Not only that, he also passed down a fifth-rank formation obtained from the *Immortal Secrets of the Stone Chamber*.

That formation was called the *Four Saints Grand Formation*, based on the idea of the four holy beasts joining forces, but it was incomplete.

Luo Chen himself deleted and modified it, learning from each other's strengths to compensate for weaknesses, and changed it into a *Four Spirits Grand Formation*, which happened to suit Black King and the others.

Luo Chen's goal for this trip was simple.

Hunt one or two fifth-rank desolate beasts and use their heart's essence blood to cultivate the Demon-Devil Heart-Linking Tree.

With the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, combined with the time flow of the secret realm before it completely faded, he might be able to produce a fruit before his ascension.

At that time, with his Ascendant cultivation and his Archaic fifth-rank physical body, his ascension would be even more assured.

...

One year later.

Within the Kun Mang Ancient Forest.

Under the scorching sun, heat steamed, and even space seemed distorted.

The angry roars of wild beasts continuously echoed from within.

Opposing them were four other roars.

On the ground, the Mysterious Turtle's four pillars held up the sky, and filthy Netherwater rolled out.

Black King bared his fangs and claws, charging again and again at the colossal creature in the center.

Heavenjade soared in the sky, occasionally raining down thousands of arrows.

Kongchan had now revealed his terrifying eight-hundred-zhang true body, with countless tentacles covered in mouths blotting out the sky, tightly binding the colossal creature.

Not only that, as the main array master of the Four Spirits Grand Formation, Kongchan also used himself as the center to draw upon the power of the other three beasts, forming a barrier.

The Four Spirits Barrier, sealing the heavens and locking the earth!

Their target was only one.

It was the colossal creature trapped in the center!

This creature was very peculiar, shaped like a pig but with a barking sound like a dog. Although its body was large, its overall form was slightly slender.

Especially between its four limbs, it occasionally swept, and the sharp wind it raised could cut through even Kongchan's tentacles at a touch.

Even with the abilities of the four guardian spirit beasts, they could not suppress this beast.

"According to the classics, this beast should be a Cabinet Fox, considered a relatively weak desolate beast, but with extremely strong survival abilities, especially skilled in escape techniques. Its food is shell-type demon beasts, and it likes to devour shells to enhance its own defensive abilities. If not for being attracted by the Mysterious Turtle this time, it wouldn't have been so easy to fall into the trap."

In the void, Luo Chen stood on the flying shuttle, looking at this scene with surprise in his eyes.

Gu Caiyi stood beside him, puzzled. "If it's weak, how could the Cabinet Fox grow to the fifth rank?"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly. "Weakness is not an obstacle to survival; ignorance is. Which one of us didn't grow up step by step from the weakest Qi Refinement stage? Without self-awareness, we would have long since died recklessly under the battering winds and rains of the cultivation world. This principle applies to desolate beasts as well."

Hearing this, Gu Caiyi nodded in agreement.

As strong as the top-tier Great Desolate Mirage Dragon, because it was too ostentatious, it instead fell on the land of Central Continent, its inner pill even being made into a grotto-heaven secret realm for disciples to train in by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

On the other hand, creatures like the Cabinet Fox, which were weak but skilled in self-preservation, would retreat immediately when they saw things going wrong. Naturally cautious and careful, they gradually endured through the long years to reach the fifth rank.

Another example was the Diao Soul Wilderness of the Broken Soul Cliff, which relied on its elusive nature to become a top-tier Void Refinement great wilderness capable of threatening Rosycloud.

"But they have lived in the bitter wilderness for a long time and haven't dealt with cunning humans for a while. They are no longer so vigilant." Luo Chen said leisurely, his eyes full of determination.

Gu Caiyi was a bit worried. "This beast is ultimately fifth rank. Can Black King and the others take it down?"

A fifth-rank desolate beast, even if it didn't cultivate origin arts or comprehend the Dao, because it was in harmony with nature, it would naturally grasp some true meaning of the laws.

Such beings were almost indistinguishable from Ascendant great powers!

The Four Spirits Grand Formation alone could not trap the Cabinet Fox!

What the Cabinet Fox was truly wary of was Luo Chen, standing in the void.

In its eyes, the powerful threat came from that human, who was like an ant.

"Roar!"

With a single roar, the Cabinet Fox swept its long tail, shaking the entire Kun Mang Ancient Forest.

Large swaths of thousand-year-old ancient trees fell like dominoes.

The Mysterious Turtle bore the brunt, its entire body flipped over.

Black King was cunning, using earth escape to burrow into the ground, avoiding this strike.

Kongchan, as the main array master, had all his tentacles broken inch by inch, looking miserable.

The Cabinet Fox seized the opportunity, charging into the sky and pouncing on Heavenjade.

The weakest point of this formation was precisely Heavenjade, who had the lowest cultivation.

However, Heavenjade was also fierce. Its true body was a Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull, naturally the most combative, only having suppressed its nature over the years under Luo Chen's command.

Now, carrying the residual power of the formation, it flapped its wings and dove straight down.

Rip!

With a sharp, piercing sound, Heavenjade was heavily struck, half of its wing torn off.

Amidst the rain of blood, the Cabinet Fox's slanted, long eyes burned with raging flames as it pounced on the flying shuttle.

Seeing this, Luo Chen, standing on the flying shuttle, stepped forward, shielding Gu Caiyi behind him, and then flicked his finger.

Unbeknownst to anyone, he had already prepared his divine ability.

Withering-Bloom Dao Finger!

Swish!

With a single finger pointed out, the lush green all turned to grayish-white.

The colossal Cabinet Fox rapidly shrank, instantly turning into a zhang-sized small beast, screeching as it flew backward into the distance.

Gu Caiyi stood behind Luo Chen, her eyes full of admiration.

This was her man, strong enough to ignore everything.

But Luo Chen raised an eyebrow at this moment and muttered to himself, "This Cabinet Fox is even more cunning than recorded in the classics!"

Gu Caiyi was puzzled. "Didn't it take a divine ability from you, husband, and even its original form was beaten so that it couldn't maintain it?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "That was just a feint. It's using the force to retreat and escape now."

Gu Caiyi looked over, and sure enough, that zhang-long small beast, relying on Luo Chen's finger divine ability, had flown backward a hundred li away.

Then, green light enveloped it, turning into a long rainbow fleeing into the distance.

"This..." Gu Caiyi felt a bit pained. They had spent such a great cost, yet let it escape.

"Don't worry, it can't escape!"

Luo Chen chuckled lightly and clenched his hand in the air!

In the distance, the Cabinet Fox, soaring over the forest sea like a green rainbow, suddenly stopped.

Unbeknownst to it, a grayish light had already enveloped it.

The Cabinet Fox screeched and looked up.

A great cauldron appeared faintly in the void, casting down thousands of rays of light, already covering the entire Kun Mang Ancient Forest.

Unwilling, the Cabinet Fox's entire body plummeted, wanting to burrow into the earth.

But as soon as it landed.

Clang!

The sound of metal striking metal rang out, and the Cabinet Fox was bounced into the air.

It looked at the ground in disbelief, only to find that the originally soft earth had turned the color of metal.

Only now did it truly have no way to heaven and no door to earth.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron gave it no chance to catch its breath. The entire cauldron rumbled down, Yuan Magnetic Divine Light continuously scattering.

Bit by bit, drop by drop, inch by inch, step by step, it forcibly pressed the Cabinet Fox until it couldn't move.

Even though it screeched and revealed its true body again, it still couldn't stop this terrifying suppression.

It felt as if a great mountain was pressing down on it.

Whoosh...

A gentle breeze passed, and Luo Chen drifted over.

Looking at the desolate beast below, whose eyes showed a plea for mercy, his attention fell on the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

"After initially refining the three most supreme spirit mountains of the current world, the Primordial Unity Cauldron has indeed reached my requirements."

"Heavy as a mountain, agile as the void."

"I wonder if it will have the same suppressive power when truly facing the primordial spirit of an Ascendant great power?"

As he pondered, Luo Chen finally looked at the Cabinet Fox.

Such a creature, if it could be taken as a guardian spirit beast, would be enough to protect the Luo Tian Sect for thousands or tens of thousands of years.

However, this idea only existed in fantasy.

If Luo Chen were still in the Mountains and Seas Realm, he might be able to suppress such a being.

But once he ascended, without an Ascendant great power to sit in the sect, the Luo Tian Sect would instead suffer from it.

At that time, it would be like inviting a wolf into the house.

Just like the Life and Death Gate in the Southern Frontier, didn't the Nether Abyss Divine Lord watch helplessly as the fifth-rank Nether Earth Dragon escaped, without any thought of keeping it?

A weapon that is too powerful, for those who cannot control it, will be an extremely dangerous double-edged sword.

So...

"Sorry!"

Luo Chen was as calm as still water, slowly extending his right hand.

A sword light shot from his fingertip, and in the Cabinet Fox's terrified eyes, it went straight into its chest and abdomen, reaching in to take its heart!

A heart the size of a millstone beat vigorously before Luo Chen.

A person cannot live without a heart, but a beast without a heart becomes even more frenzied.

The Cabinet Fox's entire body trembled wildly, but under the suppression of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, any resistance was futile.

A desolate beast's entire body is a treasure. Luo Chen would not waste it, especially that vast vitality!

"Come out!"

With a murmur, a faint, weak bluish-white flame drifted out from his mouth.

It was the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire!

Since the great battle of that year, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire had consumed all the vast vitality stored within to protect Luo Chen, saving his life.

More than eighty years had passed since then.

During that time, Luo Chen had healed his injuries, become an Ascendant, traveled abroad, and occasionally nourished it with his dharma power, but the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire still recovered slowly.

Luo Chen knew that to restore the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire to its original state, he had to replenish it with a vast amount of vitality!

And in this realm, only the flesh of fifth-rank desolate beasts possessed the most abundant and purest vitality.

After the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire appeared, the fire spirit sensed the bound Cabinet Fox beast before it, and a feeling of hunger flowed through its thoughts.

"Go!"

Luo Chen waved his hand, and the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire rushed out.

Instantly, screams echoed throughout the entire Kun Mang Ancient Forest.

The sharpness of the sound startled the few people resting behind.

"Your husband is handling matters, you don't need to worry. How are you all?" Gu Caiyi asked with concern.

Kongchan had already transformed into human form. "Thank you for your concern, Mistress. Although we look miserable, they are just superficial wounds."

Gu Caiyi looked at Heavenjade, whose aura was weak and dispirited. Earlier, when it had clashed head-on with the Cabinet Fox, its wing had been torn off in one move. That was no superficial wound.

Without hesitation, she took out bottles and jars from her storage ring.

"These are healing pills personally refined by your husband. Take them quickly, and don't leave any hidden problems that might affect your future cultivation."

"Thank you!"

"Thank you, Mistress!"

Heavenjade, Kongchan, and even the Mysterious Turtle all gratefully accepted the pills.

Black King also came up with eager eyes, begging for some. Gu Caiyi had no choice but to give him a pill as well, even though he had no injuries.

After that, everyone rested briefly before flying toward Luo Chen.

When they arrived, the sight before them was horrifying.

The ground was a patch of scorched black, with only a pale greenish-white fire tree comfortably stretching its branches, as if it had had a full meal.

As the fire tree swayed, streams of light were spat out.

There were sharp claws, large sinews, and demon pills, fur, and hide, among other things.

Luo Chen waved his hand casually, and these items flew in an orderly manner to everyone.

Heavenjade stared blankly at the bloodstained sharp claws before him. These were the terrifying blades that had torn its wings apart with a single casual strike earlier.

"You all contributed a lot; I won't treat you unfairly." Luo Chen smiled and gestured for everyone to accept these things. "A fifth-rank desolate beast's entire body is a treasure. With a little refinement, it will have the power of a true artifact. If you invite a master refiner to refine it properly, its future achievements will be immeasurable. Xuanming, pay attention. The Cabinet Fox's inner pill is a great tonic for you, but don't absorb it all at once. Take it slowly."

Everyone bowed in thanks.

Only Black King looked at Luo Chen with eager eyes.

"Master, what about me?"

Luo Chen looked at him speechlessly. The fact that the Four Spirits Grand Formation had been broken so easily earlier had a lot to do with this guy not putting in much effort.

"You?"

Luo Chen snorted coldly.

"In the battles to come, you will fight alongside me. Let them rest."

Black King shrank his head, cast an envious glance at the treasures the others had obtained, and then reluctantly burrowed into Luo Chen's arm.

"Let's go!"

Luo Chen gazed into the distance.

"According to the map drawn by Rosycloud, the Kun Mang Ancient Forest is just an insignificant part of the wilderness. The place to watch out for next is the Chaotic Stone Sky Domain."

...

The second year.

Within the Chaotic Stone Sky Domain, ravines crisscrossed and cliffs stood tall.

Earth-shaking sounds continuously came from within.

Two giants, each a thousand zhang tall, were fighting for their lives.

One was like a giant ape, its entire body grayish-white, the ancient great wilderness Chaotic Stone Sky Monkey recorded in the classics.

The other had three heads and six arms, holding a sharp blood sword. Every sword strike destroyed mountain peaks. It was Luo Chen.

And on Luo Chen's body, a huge black dragon coiled, occasionally extending its dragon head and claws to bite at the Chaotic Stone Sky Monkey.

This battle lasted a full three days and three nights.

When night fell, the great battle finally came to an end.

Under the faint green firelight, Luo Chen held the stone monkey's heart in his hands, his expression filled with emotion.

"These desolate beasts truly have tenacious vitality. They are extremely hard to kill."

Gu Caiyi stood beside him worriedly. "How about we stop here?"

Luo Chen shook his head, his expression firm. "No, we must continue. This trip is not just about hunting demon beasts and seizing resources. I also need to use them to familiarize myself with Ascendant-level combat. This is my only chance."

Luo Chen's gaze was distant, his thoughts even further.

In the past thousand years, several Ascendant-level battles had occurred in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Most were related to Rosycloud, and most ended with Rosycloud's victory.

Were the other Ascendant great powers too incompetent?

But how could those who had cultivated to that realm be weak?

It was simply that after advancing to the Ascendant stage, they had been at peace for too long, rarely fighting with peers of the same level, thus lacking combat experience at this realm.

And when facing Rosycloud, who was extremely experienced in combat at both the Ascendant and even the Void Refinement level, they naturally fell behind at every turn.

Luo Chen did not know what the Immortal Realm was like—whether it was peaceful and tranquil, or like the current cultivation world, full of conflict.

But at least, before ascending, he had to master the combat mode corresponding to his realm.

These desolate beasts, with relatively lower intelligence but sufficient power and the purest combat instincts, were the best whetstone for him.

In battle after battle, he would gradually learn how to control the origin qi of heaven and earth, how to unleash great divine abilities, and how to control his strength.

What Li Canghai had said back then was being verified.

Luo Chen, newly entered into the Ascendant stage, had not yet been deeply bound by the shackles of heaven and earth.

If he stayed in the Mountains and Seas Realm for longer at this realm, he might not be able to act so freely.

Therefore, this path through the wilderness had to continue!

(End of this chapter)
