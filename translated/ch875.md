# Chapter 875: Three Domains Open Together, Luo Chen Has an Heir

*"Humans are the spirits of all things; gu are the true essence of heaven and earth."*

*"In ancient times, there was a divine gu named 'Life-and-Death Together,' whose effects were incomparably marvelous. An Ascendant great power decreed that this gu grasped the true meaning of the laws of life and death, thus ignoring the disparity in cultivation realms, allowing mortals and great powers to share life and death."*

*"This gu was extremely rare, as if only one existed, and it ultimately vanished into the river of time."*

*"However, the gu cultivators of the Southern Frontier, desperately craving the legendary marvels of this gu, continuously researched and cultivated several similar types of gu insects. The Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu, the Devil Heart Gu, the Qi-Eating Gu..."*

*"Though they appear numerous in type, they ultimately converge to the same end, all striving toward 'Life-and-Death Together.' Therefore, different gu insects, upon advancing, will gradually assimilate."*

*"The Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu was originally a good gu, allowing its two hosts to share a spiritual connection. But this gu has a flaw: once it reaches the third rank, it transforms into a Devil Heart Gu. The Devil Heart Gu itself is not a problem; however, a Devil Heart Gu Emperor still exists in this world. Once a Devil Heart Gu is planted, one will be controlled by the Gu Emperor!"*

Atop the Cloud-Scraping Cave, a warm red jade box opened, and a wax-sealed pill fell to the ground.

Luo Chen held a letter, carefully reading the correspondence that Sage Heavenly Mountain had sent back through Gu Zuokai.

Sage Heavenly Mountain was the final sect leader of the Heavenly Mountain Cult of the Southern Frontier, and he himself was well-versed in the dao of gu insects.

Regarding the calamity of the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu on Sima Huiniang, he was the most authoritative among those Luo Chen knew.

The result did not disappoint him. The other party indeed had a thorough understanding of the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu's origins, development, cultivation methods, and even the hidden dangers unknown to ordinary people.

He also verified Luo Chen's earlier speculation: after the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu reached the third rank, it would transform into a Devil Heart Gu.

However, upon seeing that a Devil Heart Gu Emperor existed, Luo Chen's exceptionally fair hands couldn't help but tremble slightly.

Unlike the low-rank Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu, he had long understood the function of the Devil Heart Gu.

Once a cultivator was implanted with this gu, they became what was called a "vessel," "nutrient," or even "food"!

As long as the superior willed it, they could recall the Devil Heart Gu at any time, simultaneously stealing all the essence of the implanted cultivator.

Legend had it that the emergence of the Devil Heart Gu once stirred a bloody storm in the Southern Frontier, and later, Southern Frontier cultivators joined forces to exterminate all demonic cultivators who made and used this gu.

Yet unexpectedly, according to Sage Heavenly Mountain, although the Devil Heart Gu no longer circulated in the world, a Devil Heart Gu Emperor still existed.

Suppressing the turmoil in his heart, Luo Chen continued reading.

In the latter half of the letter, Sage Heavenly Mountain provided solutions.

*"The Devil Heart Gu is not without a solution. During the period when the demonic cultivators using this gu were being eradicated, the ancestors of the Southern Frontier had already devised several methods."*

*"The first method is to overcome the superior from below—refine the Gu Emperor and bring its power under one's own control. The current Devil Heart Gu Emperor survived precisely through this method and thereby achieved the Sage realm."*

Luo Chen pursed his lips. This method was truly too difficult.

He didn't know the current strength of the legendary Devil Heart Gu Emperor, but he understood that such a great demon would be universally hunted in the cultivation world. How could it show its face?

Just finding the Devil Heart Gu Emperor was extremely difficult.

Moreover, the other party was hidden deep within the Southern Frontier—where would he even search?

The second method soon came into Luo Chen's view.

After reading this method, Luo Chen couldn't help but reveal a bitter smile.

If a cultivator implanted with the Devil Heart Gu could reach the Sage realm through their own ability, they could transform their qi sea into a Purple Mansion and give birth to a Nascent Soul. At that point, their soul would merge into the Nascent Soul, and relying on the Purple Mansion barrier, they would no longer be affected by the Devil Heart Gu—even severing this gu would be no problem.

However, this method had a prerequisite: the cultivator must not have become too deeply contaminated by the Devil Heart Gu. It could only be limited to flesh, blood, and mana; the soul must never be bound together with the Devil Heart Gu.

Otherwise, even if they achieved the Sage realm, when their soul escaped into the Nascent Soul, it would also carry the Devil Heart Gu's consciousness within it. How could they then shield themselves from each other and remain unaffected?

Now, Huiniang's situation was already bound together with the Devil Heart Gu, including her soul!

It was precisely because of this that Luo Chen dared not let Huiniang continue cultivating, lest they become even more deeply entangled, leaving no remedy in the future.

Therefore, this method was also unfeasible.

With a bitter smile, Luo Chen looked at the third solution proposed by Sage Heavenly Mountain, which the other party had emphasized and described in great detail.

*"The Devil Heart Gu binds itself to a cultivator's entire cultivation base, including mana, soul, flesh, and blood. However, from the very beginning, this gu requires qi and blood nourishment to gradually grow and strengthen, eventually infecting mana and soul... If we reverse this process and use a bloodline method to expel it from the body, this is the most feasible approach and, for female cultivators, the optimal solution."*

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly as he murmured to himself.

"Use a bloodline method to expel the Devil Heart Gu?"

He frowned, squinting his eyes, and read the third method once more.

Gradually, a clear direction formed in his heart.

...

At the summit of Cloud-Scraping Cave.

Luo Chen's cultivation site.

This was also the most essential location in the entire Luo Tian Domain, where the heaven and earth spiritual qi was exceptionally pure and clear, extremely suitable for absorption.

Its grade might only be at the threshold of the fourth-rank mid-stage, and the spiritual qi was not particularly dense, but due to its pure and clear nature, cultivators would find refining mana here twice as effective with half the effort!

When Luo Chen first came here to avoid the aftermath of the Ascendant battle, he only then inadvertently discovered the marvels of this place.

He had once been very curious as to why the Cloud-Scraping Old Ghost, who occupied this place, had such poor progress in his realm.

And this was also one of the reasons Luo Chen had deliberately chosen the Cloud-Scraping Domain as the target for the Luo Tian Sect's expansion.

With such a special spiritual land, it could ensure the robust growth of the Luo Tian Sect's future Sage cultivators!

Of course, since Luo Chen had not yet left, this place naturally belonged to him for now.

On this day, at dawn.

Luo Chen satisfactorily concluded a cultivation session.

As he had thought, cultivating here greatly increased his efficiency in refining heaven and earth spiritual qi and converting it into mana.

He had taken another solid step toward the fifth layer of the Sage realm.

Two figures came up the mountain together.

With a wave of his hand, Luo Chen placed a pot of spiritual wine and three cups on the table.

"Two fellow Daoists, please!"

Elder Fu and Nine Spirit Primogenitor exchanged a glance and sat down calmly.

"Fellow Daoist Dan seems to have known we would come today?" Elder Fu asked.

Luo Chen smiled. "I'm not a cultivator of divination; how could I predict things like a god? But I knew that the expansion was imminent. If not today, then in a few days, so I've been waiting for you two."

Nine Spirit Primogenitor nodded and said loudly, "Indeed, we've been resting in the Luo Tian Domain for two months. It's time to set out."

Elder Fu sighed emotionally, "The Five Elements Divine Sect has already fought back into the Five Elements Domain and begun reclaiming lost territory. Divine Origin Sage personally took action, with the help of Sage Peng Xiang, Divine Fire Sage, and others, engaging in several fierce battles with the Great Demon Emperor Bi Zheng, who was reluctant to leave. Naturally, we can't delay any longer; otherwise, the pressure will only increase when we conduct our expansion wars later."

Luo Chen smiled and acknowledged this reasoning.

From their vantage point, the current situation was clear at a glance.

Among the three ancient demon emperors of the demon race, one was dead and one captured, leaving only the one at Green Parasol Mountain still holding back.

On the human side, the great power of the Nether Origin Sect had already returned to the holy land. Nether Origin experts were no longer dispatching in large numbers; instead, they sent Sage cultivators like Lin Yonghua and Gu Hong out of the mountains, silently occupying one excellent cultivation site after another in the Eastern Wilderness.

The intensity of the war had immediately dropped.

A small number of demon emperors were reluctant to give up the territories they had once conquered and felt that the Ascendant great powers would not act again easily, so they were still testing the waters.

If the human experts were truly determined to reclaim the six eastern domains, they could simply flee back to the Million Mountains.

In this situation, a single sect conducting an expansion war alone would face tremendous pressure.

But what if two sects, or even three, did it together?

With three domains opening simultaneously, the remaining demon race would have to bear pressure from three sides at once, naturally reducing the difficulty of the expansion war.

"How are your preparations?" Luo Chen asked with concern.

Nine Spirit Primogenitor said confidently, "I've already sent people to scout the situation in the Medicine King Domain. Although there are many demon beasts there now, only three formidable demon emperors remain. With my current strength, I can handle them!"

Elder Fu coughed lightly. "The Divine Talisman Domain is more troublesome. Five demon emperors are still there. However, I have already invited Fellow Daoist Deng, Splendid Cloud Fairy, and Lin Qingxuan to come along. It shouldn't be a problem."

Luo Chen asked curiously, "Lin Qingxuan actually agreed to help?"

Elder Fu said in a low voice, "He has now taken over the Myriad Sword Pavilion. Although he is the only sword-cultivating Sage, there is still considerable resistance within the Myriad Sword Pavilion. He can't easily use thunderous methods to suppress them, so he has no choice but to befriend us, using external pressure to force the Myriad Sword Pavilion cultivators to submit to him."

Luo Chen suddenly understood. Although Elder Fu put it simply, the details of the transaction between them must not have been so straightforward.

After thinking for a moment, Luo Chen said, "My Luo Tian Sect is currently rebuilding from ruins, and our manpower is stretched thin. I can't help you too much. How about this: I'll send Lingfengzi to your side, Nine Spirit."

Nine Spirit Primogenitor nodded happily. Although Lingfengzi's strength was average, he highly recognized his ability to scout and gather intelligence.

He needed such auxiliary talent!

"Elder Fu, I'll send Wang Yuan to your side. Is that acceptable?"

Elder Fu was overjoyed. "Having young friend Wang Yuan's assistance is something I could only wish for."

Two months ago, in the battle at Taihu Domain, the strength Wang Yuan displayed was in no way inferior to that of an ordinary Sage cultivator.

Moreover, this man had a fierce fighting spirit. With his help, it would greatly boost the confidence of the lower-rank cultivators below.

"Additionally, if you have any need for pills, feel free to tell me directly. This sect will not be stingy."

"If you encounter a troublesome enemy, this sect can also descend the mountain and lend you our full support!"

These latter two promises were even more reassuring.

Nine Spirit Primogenitor was slightly curious. "Now that the Luo Tian Sect's mountain gate is established, Fellow Daoist won't go directly to the Nether Origin Sect?"

To this, Luo Chen smiled faintly.

"Although the mountain gate is established, there are still many major matters that require my decision—such as nurturing the spiritual land, the sect's future development, organizing the inheritance, and so on. How can I just leave?"

That made sense.

The two did not press further. After drinking heartily with Luo Chen, they took their leave.

Three days later, Luo Chen personally went to the border to see off the two sects' cultivators.

In the sky, flying boats circled, and countless beams of light surged, advancing majestically toward the six eastern domains.

Their numbers were not necessarily fewer than when Luo Chen had expanded into the Cloud-Scraping Domain.

Nine Spirit Primogenitor and the others also had their own networks. During the preparation phase of the past year, they had invited many large and small forces to help.

Even Qin Baixiang of the Myriad Beast Sect, who had previously rejected the Five Elements Divine Sect's invitation, had joined Nine Spirit Sect's side.

After they left, Luo Chen returned alone to Cloud-Scraping Cave.

In the following days, Luo Chen did not immerse himself entirely in cultivation.

As he had said before, he frequently received Li Yingzhang and other Luo Tian Sect Adept cultivators atop Cloud-Scraping Cave, planning the Luo Tian Sect's future development together.

Black King was also released, sleeping deep within the core three-thousand-mile spirit vein, breathing in heaven and earth spiritual qi.

Even the batch of dragon earthworms he had subdued back in the Northern Sea were all released, diving into the mountain gate's earth veins to cultivate high-grade spiritual soil.

Bai Meiling wished to stay by Luo Chen's side, but Luo Chen's yang qi was now too heavy. Being too close greatly affected her cultivation as a Ghost Emperor. Therefore, a special Ghost Emperor Peak was designated for her cultivation.

Not only that, but in his spare time, Luo Chen began to take out the various cultivation techniques he had obtained over the years, large and small.

While categorizing them, he also supplemented and corrected them.

From first-rank to fourth-rank techniques, including the fifth-rank *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*, he organized them all.

Especially the series of techniques and secret arts he personally cultivated, as well as his body-tempering methods—he wrote copies of all of them.

In the future, these techniques would become the Luo Tian Sect's inheritance foundation, available for countless low-rank disciples to cultivate.

Time flowed slowly.

Reports of the war situation in the six eastern domains came back one after another.

Here at Luo Chen's side, time seemed to pass peacefully, as if even its flow had slowed.

It was under these circumstances that Sima Huiniang, supported by her disciple Yao Mingyue, walked into a newly built hall on Cloud-Scraping Cave.

Looking at the white-robed man buried in a mountain of books, Huiniang's face shone with maternal radiance as she spoke:

"Husband, I am with child!"

Luo Chen lifted his head from the thickly piled jade slips. He was stunned for a moment, then looked at her still-flat abdomen with a complex expression.

"You've already conceived?"

As he spoke, he rose and walked toward Sima Huiniang, a smile spreading across his face.

Sima Huiniang withdrew her hand from Yao Mingyue's support and let Luo Chen embrace her voluptuous waist.

"I felt something a few days ago. Today, I examined myself and had the sect's physician take my pulse. It's confirmed—I am indeed pregnant."

Luo Chen reached out and touched her lower abdomen, able to feel a life force within. Though weak, it was so vigorous and thriving.

"Since you're pregnant, you need to be careful. Don't touch any sect affairs from now on; let Mingyue handle them for you. Make sure to rest and don't overwork yourself..."

"Husband, you worry too much. I am, after all, an Adept cultivator. How could I have problems so easily?"

"This cannot be taken lightly. If you're not careful during childbirth, it will greatly deplete your origin energy. I have some methods for nurturing the fetus that I can put to use now."

For a moment, the hall was filled with warmth and tenderness.

Yao Mingyue, understanding the situation, retreated to a corner, watching this scene with great joy.

However, there was still some confusion in her mind.

When she first heard the news, the Supreme Elder didn't seem surprised at all.

Moreover, the look he gave her master's belly—her master had been looking down and stroking her stomach, so she hadn't seen it, but Yao Mingyue had seen it clearly.

That look was so complex it was hard to describe.

It was as if he had long expected it, yet also as if he were looking at some object—there was even a hint of icy coldness within it. In short, it was not at all the surprise of a father learning he was to have a child.

Because of these doubts, Yao Mingyue's joy was somewhat diluted. Even the tenderness Luo Chen was now showing seemed somewhat false to her.

She looked again carefully and subconsciously shook her head.

The gentleness toward her master was not fake; it was indeed from the heart.

But why...

Just then, the white-robed man turned his head and glanced at her.

A cold, fleeting look.

Yao Mingyue's whole body trembled, and she stood as silent as a cicada in winter.

(Chapter End)
