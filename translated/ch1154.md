# Chapter 1154: Nine-Tribulation True Astral Wind, Unforgiving Heavenly Astral Wind

Wind is a thing of pure clarity and ethereal grace.

It dwells above the nine heavens and travels across the four wilds.

Sometimes wild and explosive, sometimes light and gentle, it is the most common yet most elusive existence in the world.

Within the Mountains and Seas Realm, the ultimate development of such an existence has another name—Immaculate!

What Luo Chen sought to gather was the most essential part of the heavenly wind—the Unforgiving Heavenly Wind!

According to legend, this heavenly wind was the most perilous.

Wherever it passed, neither man nor demon could survive, neither livestock nor beast could endure. No matter how powerful one's blood and qi, there was no pardon beneath this astral wind.

Even those mighty experts within the Great Cataclysm Realm who had cultivated the Leakless Golden Body dared not touch it too much.

And Luo Chen's confidence lay in his true spirit body, comparable to the Great Desolate!

After ten years of recuperation, coupled with his study of the dao of blood and qi, his once precarious physical body had stabilized. Ordinary astral winds could no longer cause him the slightest harm.

After all, at his peak, Luo Chen's physical body could already destroy true artifacts and even withstand a blow or two from spirit treasures.

Under Chu Zhaonan's tense gaze, Luo Chen's expression remained calm as he leaped into the Windless Mountain Range.

Along the way, he let the ordinary heavenly winds batter against him, standing firm like a pine or cypress, unmoved.

Gradually, Luo Chen's figure grew increasingly blurry until it vanished completely.

"Let's go back!"

Chu Zhaonan suppressed the shock in his heart and waved to his subordinates.

Luo Chen's deep cultivation within the Windless Mountain Range would not be a matter of moments, nor a few days' effort. It would likely take a very long time.

He had already informed Chu Zhaonan beforehand not to wait here. Once his cultivation was complete, Luo Chen would return to Relic Immortal City.

A line of riders galloped away.

And on the return journey, in places Chu Zhaonan could not notice, several pairs of eyes swept over them, eventually following the path they had come and landing on the Returning Wind Mountain Range.

"That man has already entered the Returning Wind Mountain Range to cultivate. Truly reckless."

"After all, he is the cultivator Xiao Feng spoke of, and there is a great possibility he is a peerless existence who once cultivated a primordial spirit. Within the cultivation system, he could well be called an Ascendant grandmaster."

"An Ascendant grandmaster possesses the qualifications to ascend and become an immortal, no different from the end point of our body refinement—the Human Immortal realm."

"Such existences are rare even within the Great Cataclysm Realm."

"But throughout history, those who achieved something, who could leave the Great Cataclysm Realm and travel the Return to Nothingness, were all such beings. Like Xiao Lan, the Relic Immortal ancestor of the Xiao family from days past, or the current Lord of Carefree Realm."

"As long as we can divide his primordial spirit and nourish our physical bodies, even if we cannot step into the Human Immortal realm, we can at least repair our physical injuries."

"Should we make a move?"

One by one, transmissions echoed among the seven.

When it came to the actual act of making a move, a rare silence fell.

In the silence, a hoarse, light laugh suddenly sounded.

"Everyone, since we have all come here without prior arrangement, hasn't the answer already been decided?"

At these words, the unspeakable silence instantly broke open.

"Yes!"

"Let's make our move!"

"So what if he is an Ascendant grandmaster? This is the Great Cataclysm Realm. There is no heaven and earth spiritual energy for him to draw upon. His divine sense dares not be released. Even his most powerful life-staking techniques will be reduced by seventy or eighty percent. With the strength of the seven of us, we can take him down!"

"Legend has it that before the era, countless Return to Nothingness experts once surrounded and attacked a passing Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerable. We are merely dealing with an Ascendant grandmaster who has fled here in a sorry state. What is there to fear?"

"Following in the footsteps of our predecessors' grand feats—how delightful, how delightful!"

"But we cannot act recklessly. There is the Unforgiving Heavenly Wind within the Returning Wind Mountain Range, which is quite dangerous even for us. We must plan carefully."

The group spoke one after another, quickly reaching a consensus.

Only one of them, the most weathered old man, remained silent, appearing out of place.

"Lord Mofeng City Lord, you are the oldest among us, the strongest, and even once a Nine-Ring martial artist who broke into the Cataclysm Ring. Why do you say nothing?"

Faced with the crowd's doubts, Lord Mofeng City Lord spoke in a weathered tone: "I will not participate in this battle."

The others frowned, not expecting him to back out at the last moment.

"Give us an explanation!"

"You once reached the mid-stage Leakless realm, broke into the Cataclysm Nine Rings, and when you returned, both your injuries and lifespan were far more dangerous than ours. Now that an opportunity lies before us, how can you retreat?"

Lord Mofeng City Lord shook his head and only asked: "You were all persuaded by Xiao Feng, the son of Xiao Lin, were you not? But why does he not make a move himself?"

Someone retorted, "He is but a junior, lacking in strength. He merely wants to use others as knives to secure his position as Lord of Relic Immortal City."

Lord Mofeng City Lord sighed. "But the family servant protecting him has at least cultivated the Leakless Golden Body, one of our own kind!"

"Hmph, a cripple with a missing arm—can he be compared to us?"

Lord Mofeng City Lord's words caught in his throat. His gaze swept over the crowd, and he saw uncontrollable excitement and madness on every face.

Clearly, this was not something he could dispel with a few words.

He could only insist on his own opinion, firmly deciding not to interfere in this matter.

Seeing they could not force Lord Mofeng City Lord, the remaining six no longer insisted, only instructing him not to leak the news.

Otherwise, once they succeeded, Lord Mofeng City Lord would find it difficult to live out his remaining days in peace.

Lord Mofeng City Lord made his promise, then watched as the six headed toward the Returning Wind Mountain Range.

As they left, a hint of regret rose in Lord Mofeng City Lord's heart.

If that one truly had the Ascendant realm, and they could actually kill him and divide his primordial spirit, using its powerful origin qi, they might indeed recover their injuries and origin, perhaps even take another step forward!

But whenever Lord Mofeng City Lord thought of yesterday, of those red eyes and golden pupils that had looked over from afar, he could not help but shudder.

The rising regret vanished in an instant.

"I'm afraid it's not that simple!"

...

Within the Returning Wind Mountain Range.

A figure, riding astral qi, flew through the howling, passing astral winds.

Martial artists of the Furnace Realm could naturally fly!

Martial artists rarely did so in daily life, firstly to conserve astral qi, and secondly because this heaven and earth was unusual—the closer to the sky, the more dangerous.

Whether it was the Extreme Heavenly Astral Wind or the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound, both were things martial artists did not wish to provoke.

Luo Chen was actually quite curious: if one cultivated the physical body to the fifth-rank Human Immortal realm, said to be able to see spirits without decay and shatter the void, could one truly shatter this void and escape this cage?

But for now, that was not something he needed to consider.

Luo Chen gazed into the distance.

Mountain ranges stretched on endlessly, winding and twisting.

The wind howled, deafening.

Under the erosion of the year-round astral wind, the various mountain peaks were bare, with flying sand and shifting stones.

"Ordinary environments cannot produce Immaculate Wind. The Unforgiving Heavenly Wind is a top-grade among Immaculate Winds, difficult even for powerful cultivators to control. Therefore, a place with Unforgiving Heavenly Wind must be extraordinary!"

"Listening Wind once said that divine wind originates from the extreme heavens. To remain on the earth for long, it must rely on special geographical features like wind caves. Even his unique secret technique back then was an imitation of this natural principle, opening a wind cave in his own hand."

"Let me see if there is a wind cave within this vast Returning Wind Mountain Range."

Luo Chen took a deep breath, infusing his blood and qi into his eyes.

As his eyes opened and closed, the red eyes and golden pupils reappeared.

Luo Chen stood atop a solitary, towering peak, continuously scanning.

Suddenly!

His expression shifted, and his gaze fell upon a huge valley in the very center of the Returning Wind Mountain Range, shaped like a wooden basin.

"If there is a wind cave, it must be here!"

Luo Chen leaped, his entire body transforming like a roc bird, speeding toward that place.

A distance of several hundred li was covered in an instant at his speed.

As he drew closer, the features of this place became even more apparent.

Mountains surrounded it on all sides, giving birth to a single valley with not the slightest gap.

Peak after peak stretched out like dragon heads probing forward, covering the valley from afar.

As his gaze plunged into it, joy could not help but appear in Luo Chen's eyes.

Within this valley, shaped like a wooden basin, strands of pale golden breeze drifted steadily and peacefully.

Wind is formless and colorless. To appear in a pale gold hue showed its exceptional quality.

"Is this the Unforgiving Heavenly Wind?"

The joy on Luo Chen's face grew even stronger.

This quality of Immaculate Wind far surpassed the Green Duckweed Nether Wind, the Nine Nether Immaculate Wind, and even the Void Burning Wind specifically targeting the soul!

It was probably not inferior to the Buzhou Wind or the Changhe Wind recorded in the classics.

Perhaps only the supreme winds containing the power of laws from myths and legends—Chaos Wind, Karmic Fire Burning Wind, and Space-Time Rending Wind—could overpower it.

Perfect!

Those supreme winds containing the power of laws were not something Luo Chen could refine. This batch of Unforgiving Heavenly Wind was most suitable for him now.

With his mind made up, Luo Chen hesitated no longer. His entire body slowly descended into the basin valley.

The basin valley, which looked small from a distance, was as vast as a continent once he truly landed inside.

Deep within it, the barren environment actually gave one a feeling of loneliness and desolation.

Seeming to sense the arrival of an outsider, or perhaps by instinct, strands of golden wind howled toward Luo Chen in an instant.

Luo Chen did not immediately begin refining. Instead, he first extended his right hand, unprotected by any astral qi, to feel it.

As the golden wind struck, his physical body, powerful and comparable to a spirit treasure, actually felt pain.

There was even an illusion that his skin and flesh were about to be torn apart.

For a moment, Luo Chen felt a sudden clarity.

"If the Void Burning Wind of the Fallen Sea specifically targets a cultivator's soul, then this Unforgiving Heavenly Wind is specifically designed to counter all beings with flesh and blood!"

If I can refine it into my astral qi, it will form a perfect counter against body refiners who specialize in blood and qi.

"Wonderful! Wonderful! Wonderful!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily and immediately sat down cross-legged.

The surrounding golden wind rushed toward him, but Luo Chen stood unmoved. Instead, he released a strand of Nine-Tribulation True Astral Wind, drawing a wisp of golden wind into his body.

The blood and qi furnace began to operate, refining this flesh-rending golden wind bit by bit into his astral qi.

Once he entered a state of cultivation, Luo Chen's perception of the passage of time in the outside world became rather weak.

Thus, several days later, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

He spread his hands flat, extending one finger from each.

Qi spurted from his fingertips, one black and one gold.

The former was the Nine-Tribulation True Astral Wind refined from Nine-Tribulation Earth Balefulness, while the latter was the Unforgiving Heavenly Astral Wind he had refined over these past few days.

As the famous Nine-Tribulation Earth Balefulness of the Great Cataclysm Realm, it focused on dissolving qi and corrupting blood.

As for the Unforgiving Heavenly Wind, its characteristic was scraping bone and eroding flesh.

The power of the two was roughly equivalent, and the special astral qi refined from each was also on par.

Only, one was heavy and thick, while the other was light and ethereal.

Luo Chen first compared their general power, then turned his attention to the basin valley.

"There is a considerable amount of Unforgiving Heavenly Wind here. It seems it will take a long time to refine it all."

Luo Chen estimated the time. He felt no annoyance; instead, he grew happier.

No matter how long it took, he had plenty of time!

If he could take the opportunity to break through to the late stage of the Furnace Realm, that would be even better.

Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat out golden astral qi, replacing the black astral qi, forming a thin protective shield around his body.

In terms of defensive power, it was naturally inferior to the Nine-Tribulation True Astral Qi Shield, but within this basin valley surrounded by golden wind, it had a unique effect.

The two shared the same origin.

At the very least, in the days to come, he would not be battered and scraped by the Unforgiving Heavenly Wind all day long.

...

The sun rose and the moon shifted; cold and heat came and went.

Unbeknownst to all, a long time had passed since Luo Chen entered the basin valley.

The long years had no effect on this desolate land, but for those waiting bitterly, it was pure torture.

A figure in a purple-gold robe passed through a long mountain path and entered a cave within a mountain belly.

When he entered the cave, five gazes shot toward him simultaneously.

"Lord Zijin City Lord, has that Luo Chen still not come out?"

Originally full of noble bearing, Lord Zijin City Lord now had a face full of numbness. He merely nodded.

Faced with this familiar scene, the others fell silent.

After a long while, Lord Xilong City Lord murmured in a low voice: "Ten years, right? Originally, we thought he would enter the Returning Wind Basin Valley for a few days at most, or a few months at the longest. Who would have thought he could persist inside for ten whole years? Is he still human?"

The Unforgiving Heavenly Wind was something that made countless martial artists change color at the mere mention of it.

It was truly "changing color at the mere mention of the wind"!

An ordinary person would die upon contact.

Martial artists of the Furnace Realm dared not touch it.

Even mighty experts like them, at the Leakless realm, although they could endure for a long time relying on their protective astral qi, could not remain under the blowing of the heavenly wind for extended periods.

That Luo Chen, after all, was merely a Furnace Realm martial artist, yet he had stayed for ten whole years in an environment filled with heavenly wind!

Lord Sanxiang City Lord, the only female martial artist among the six, also known as Xiangfei, was now far less spirited than before.

After all, she had also attempted a breakthrough in Carefree City and suffered severe injuries, forcing her to return to her homeland.

Now, Xiangfei spoke in a low tone: "If that Luo Chen was truly an Ascendant grandmaster before, he might have used the legendary wind-avoidance formation, which is why he could stay for so many years."

At the mention of the word "formation" from Xiangfei's mouth, the group's morale sank even lower.

Lord Xilong City Lord said unwillingly: "Are we going to continue waiting here in vain? He, Luo Chen, can stay put, but my body is about to give out!"

Among the seven who had returned, Lord Mofeng City Lord, who had left, had the most severe injuries because he had broken into the Cataclysm Nine Rings.

But Lord Xilong City Lord's injuries could be called second, reflected in his lifespan—his days were numbered.

Originally, letting Luo Chen collect the Unforgiving Heavenly Wind inside was a good thing, after all, collecting such a dangerous substance would surely require great effort.

The six of them could wait at their leisure!

At the very least, Luo Chen might be an Ascendant grandmaster. To ambush such a person, one always had to gain as much advantage as possible.

But after ten years, they had only grown more dispirited.

Lord Xilong City Lord's face hardened, and he stood up.

"Enough! Even if the Unforgiving Heavenly Wind in the basin valley is not conducive to our battle, if we want to gain something, how can we not take risks? I have decided—tomorrow, I will kill my way into the Returning Wind Basin Valley, slay the grandmaster, and seize the primordial spirit. What say you all?"

The others hesitated.

Just then, a long roar like a dragon's cry resounded across the four wilds, even piercing through mountains and stones to arrive.

Everyone's expression changed!
