# Chapter 1066: Shouyang Discourse on the Dao, Four Emperors Greet Guests. Sages Gather Like Clouds, Dao Sect Arrives

Within Shouyang Mountain.

On the green stone path through the mountains, two figures walked side by side.

The man was tall and handsome, the woman radiant and beautiful. Walking together, they seemed like a pair of celestial lovers.

"Caiyi, you've lost weight!" The man held the woman's hand, his eyes full of pity.

Gu Caiyi used her other hand to tuck a strand of hair that had fallen before her eyes behind her ear, revealing her small, oval face. She smiled gently. "Compared to the countless dangers and hardships you've faced outside, husband, I've merely been in seclusion cultivating hard for a while. What does losing a bit of weight matter? As long as I can help you, even if it means more hardship, I'm willing!"

Luo Chen shook his head and sighed softly.

Back when his realm was still low and his strength weak, his views on people and matters had been rather utilitarian.

Those he befriended were mostly useful people.

The friends around him all had to shine and contribute as much as possible for him.

To put it bluntly, his union with Sima Huiniang back then, aside from being half-pushed and half-acquiesced, was not without the intention of using a closer relationship to bind Sima Huiniang, the manager of the Luo Tian Sect.

In those days, Luo Chen desperately needed various cultivation resources, so he worked hard to squeeze every bit of strength from the friends around him.

But after Luo Chen cultivated to the Sage realm and encountered chaotic times, the Luo Tian Sect had actually become a bit of a "burden" to him.

During this period, Luo Chen was no longer so utilitarian.

Or rather, towards his bedmate, his friends, and the Luo Tian Sect, he no longer primarily drove and used them. Instead, he had become a towering tree, sheltering them from wind and rain.

Especially after Luo Chen's strength advanced by leaps and bounds, to the point where he didn't even take ordinary Sages seriously, the Luo Tian Sect's assistance to him was nearly non-existent.

Therefore, even though Luo Chen was about to face a behemoth like the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, he subconsciously never thought that the Luo Tian Sect cultivators could help him in any way.

He didn't even want the Luo Tian Sect cultivators to get involved. If he himself perished and his Dao vanished, at least these relatives and friends could still live on well.

But what Luo Chen didn't expect was that the weaklings he had once sheltered had unknowingly grown up and stood firmly behind him.

Even Gu Caiyi had broken through to the Sage realm!

This was somewhat unexpected.

But thinking about it carefully, it was also quite reasonable.

Gu Caiyi's aptitude wasn't considered good. She had even failed at Foundation Establishment once, and Luo Chen had spent great effort to save her back then.

However, it was precisely that failure in Foundation Establishment that made Gu Caiyi determined to lay a solid foundation, steadily climbing the peak step by step.

Over so many years, she didn't covet high positions in the sect, gave up wealth and power, and even restrained the jealousy and rivalry she often engaged in with Sima Huiniang, focusing all her mind on cultivation.

With Luo Chen's cultivation experience, insights, and guidance as the general direction, with Ling Fengzi, an old, seasoned Sage, giving constant pointers, and with the Luo Tian Sect's abundant resources fully supplied to her, she finally broke through to the Sage realm.

That year, Gu Caiyi was over four hundred years old, and with the Golden Core's five-hundred-year lifespan, her great limit was nearly upon her.

Fortunately, Heaven does not disappoint those who persevere.

She succeeded, not only advancing her realm but also extending her life by five hundred years!

Luo Chen gently squeezed her soft, boneless hand and sighed emotionally, "Who would have thought that we, who once squeezed together in that small courtyard house in Dahe Market, could reach such a point today?"

Gu Caiyi was equally filled with emotion. That small Dahe Market had not only produced two Sages, one fourth-level body-refining powerhouse, but also several Golden Core cultivators.

And all of this was a miracle that had appeared by following Luo Chen.

She had indeed chosen the right person back then!

Gu Caiyi looked at the man beside her, at his strong, chiseled profile. While filled with reverence, her eyes also held a hint of worry.

"Husband, do you really have to deal with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

Luo Chen said calmly, "Not just the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. There's a high chance we'll have to face an Ascendant powerhouse as well."

Gu Caiyi's expression shook, and a look of fear instinctively appeared on her face.

She tightly grasped Luo Chen's hand. "Husband, why must it be this way? With the number of top experts our Luo Tian Sect now has, with your astonishing abilities, and with the Eastern Wilderness having no true master, we could live well in the Eastern Wilderness. The Luo Tian Sect could become the number one sect in the Eastern Wilderness, and you, husband, would still be the revered number one human expert of the Eastern Wilderness. Given time, even if you ascend and attain the Dao, it wouldn't be impossible!"

Hearing this, a bitter smile rarely appeared on Luo Chen's face.

If only it were really like that.

He shook his head. "None of that has any meaning."

Gu Caiyi didn't understand. "What is meaning? What deep, irreconcilable hatred does the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect have with you, husband, that you must resort to arms?"

Luo Chen said softly, "Breaking through to Ascendant and attaining eternal life and freedom—that is meaning. And to take that solid step on the path to immortality, I must fight my way into the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and seize a treasure."

Gu Caiyi was still confused.

Over the past hundred-plus years, regarding Luo Chen's situation, they had only seen the surface and didn't know the specifics.

Only those closest to him, like Wang Yuan and Gu Caiyi, had vaguely sensed Luo Chen's urgency.

It was an urgency that was willing to risk death and destruction, just to forcibly increase his strength!

And the real trigger that made the Luo Tian Sect aware that Luo Chen would deal with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect came from an old man who crossed the sea to visit the Luo Tian Sect.

Zhou Yunshen!

After sending his grandson Zhou Fuyou to the Tiannan Dao Palace, he didn't directly return to his clan.

Instead, taking advantage of his great limit not yet having arrived, he quietly came to the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world and, presenting Luo Chen's token, visited the Luo Tian Sect.

It was also at that time that he, seemingly unintentionally yet deliberately, spoke of this matter.

Only then did the Luo Tian Sect's current actions of emptying their nest to fully support Luo Chen come about.

But the reason why Luo Chen was determined to deal with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect remained a secret.

Facing his bedmate of hundreds of years, Luo Chen gazed at her for a moment, then chose not to hide anything, explaining the specifics one by one.

When she heard about the "missing life soul," the woman instinctively covered her mouth, her face full of shock.

Then all her doubts were resolved, leaving only anger and hatred towards the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

"I originally had two plans. On one hand, I was increasing my strength to prepare for unforeseen events. On the other hand, I cultivated a clone, trying to retrieve my soul through a roundabout method."

"That clone has a portion of my split soul, but it doesn't contain the main soul. As long as it breaks through to the Sage realm, when the Rotten Axe-Handle Chessboard seizes its life soul, it can silently swap with my captured portion of life soul. At that time, the clone could find a chance to leave the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and bring back my life soul. Then I wouldn't have to be an enemy of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

"After all, it's a behemoth that suppresses an entire realm. Who would truly want to face it?"

"However, time waits for no one!"

Luo Chen sighed faintly, released Gu Caiyi's hand, walked to the edge of the cliff, and gazed at the bustling city of Lingtian Pass outside Shouyang Mountain.

Because of Rosycloud, the Dao Sect had suffered calamity, and the Central Continent had suffered disaster.

The two sides had entered a tacit period of recuperation.

But the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect didn't want to sit and wait for death. They were trying to reunite the Central Continent and rebuild the Heaven-Earth Peak.

If they truly succeeded, the already precarious situation would be instantly shattered.

The God-Descending Formation!

What kind of "deity" would descend this time?

It would inevitably be a powerful existence capable of dealing with Rosycloud!

At that time, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would no longer fear Rosycloud and could forcefully kill their way into the Eastern Wilderness.

Under such circumstances, even if I could later retrieve my life soul, before such power that suppresses an entire realm, I would be nothing more than an ant.

Luo Chen believed that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, located in the Mystic Realm, had this strength!

After all, the *Mystic Realm Secret Record* that the Southern Frontier's Youli Divine Lord left him before ascending roughly recorded the situation of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, claiming they had over a hundred great experts, several True Lords, and even a Body Integration Great Sovereign!

Before such existences, not to mention without a life soul, even if he retrieved his life soul and barely advanced to Ascendant, Luo Chen would not be a match, let alone the Luo Tian Sect under his protection.

The clone Zhou Fuyou had been cultivating for less than a hundred years. Even if he was a genius, breaking through to the Sage realm ultimately lacked time.

Placing hope in him was too illusory.

In the end, he had to rely on himself!

The mountain wind howled, beating against the man's black Daoist robe, making it rustle. A cold, fierce killing intent arose.

The woman stood behind him, looking at his back. The fear and worry on her face gradually disappeared, replaced by unwavering determination.

"Caiyi will walk with her husband to the end, no matter who the enemy is!"

...

Shouyang Mountain was originally just an ordinary spiritual mountain within Lingtian Pass.

Its only special feature might have been that it contained a fourth-grade spiritual vein, forcibly moved from elsewhere when all the sects of the Eastern Wilderness jointly funded the construction of Lingtian Pass to resist the demon race.

The reason it gradually became special, becoming one of the most famous places within Lingtian Pass, originated from the most intense period of the human-demon war.

At that time, many Sage powerhouses gathered at Lingtian Pass, constantly resisting the invasion of the demon army.

In their spare time, the Sages often held small gatherings to exchange cultivation insights and trade cultivation resources.

It was during that time that Luo Chen's reputation as the Alchemy Sage spread.

And there were not a few existences with similar ideas to his.

A Sage surnamed Lu from the Dongyang Sect had no special talents himself, but relying on his personal connections, he often invited Sages to gatherings.

These were not private small gatherings within a circle of friends. Each time, the number was quite large, and the people he invited were not limited to his own network; he constantly expanded it.

The Sages who participated in the gatherings came from various sects and had various specialties. Even powerhouses from the Nether Origin Sect at that time were occasionally invited.

Even some Sages who had once been enemies would temporarily set aside their differences during these gatherings and get along on the surface.

Not only that!

That Elder Lu would also regularly hold some Dao-lecturing assemblies for the Golden Core and Foundation Establishment cultivators below, answering their questions and resolving their doubts.

And all of this took place on Shouyang Mountain.

Therefore, these Sage gatherings and the Dao-lecturing assemblies for Golden Core cultivators were collectively called the [Shouyang Discourse on the Dao Assembly].

That Elder Lu even once gained the name Shouyang Mountain Man.

Unfortunately, after one great battle, Elder Lu was severely injured, his physical body destroyed, and his Sage embryo barely escaped. He never appeared on the human-demon battlefield again, and the name Shouyang Mountain Man faded away.

The Shouyang Discourse on the Dao Assembly he had organized also gradually declined.

Of course, after the war ended, many Sages still missed the Shouyang Discourse on the Dao Assembly.

It was the worst of times, because of the constant threat of demon beasts.

It was also the best of times, with a large number of peers gathered together, discussing with each other, exchanging resources, and cultivating with an efficiency far surpassing working behind closed doors.

After the war ended, although the Eastern Wilderness was depleted of talent, the survivors' realms advanced by leaps and bounds, and this was not without its contribution.

And this time, an existence whose reputation far surpassed that of Shouyang Mountain Man had restarted the Discourse on the Dao Assembly on Shouyang Mountain!

Three days had arrived; the Alchemy Sage invited!

One after another, powerful beams of light, under the reverent and worshipful gazes of countless low-level cultivators within Lingtian Pass City, flew into that not-so-tall and majestic mountain.

The mountain need not be high; with immortals, it becomes spiritual.

The Shouyang Mountain of today was the most spiritual immortal mountain in the entire Eastern Wilderness!

Banners fluttered, warp threads hung high.

Dragon-coiling pillars stood tall, white jade stones emitted mist with every step.

One after another, beams of light descended from the sky onto the white jade square. Then, their hair standing on end, they involuntarily restrained their own proud auras.

Because on the square, four enormous demon beasts, like stone carvings, had revealed their true forms, coldly staring at all the guests.

The least among them was a white gull. It was incredibly majestic, its feathers like sharp swords. Even without spreading its wings, it gave a sharp, fierce feeling.

A fourth-level transformed great demon, Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull!

Opposite it was a giant turtle, lying prostrate on the ground like a small island. Its eyes were half-open, half-closed, as if asleep, but the black water flowing around it made people dare not approach.

A fourth-level desolate beast, Netherworld Mystic Turtle!

Further up, a black dragon coiled around a pillar, overlooking all living beings, as if it would open its mouth and refine them at the slightest disagreement.

A golden tiger crouched, its eyes tightly shut, its aura restrained to almost nothing. But just by sitting there, it made everyone dare not ignore it.

Nine Spirit Lord, leading his junior sister Changxi and junior brother Meng Ye, was the first to arrive at Shouyang Mountain.

Yet, strong as he was, the moment he saw that golden tiger, his expression changed drastically, and his dharma power leaked out.

Such a great demon was actually sitting here, guarding the mountain gate, like a docile spirit beast?

The current Golden Tiger Emperor Tu, with Luo Chen's help, had fully absorbed the legacy left by the ancient demon Emperor Ditian. Not only had he stepped into the Great Demon Emperor realm in one go, but he had also comprehended the power of the Gold origin.

Especially the remnants of the ancient demon Emperor Ditian's aura—that was the aura of an Ascendant ancient demon, enough to intimidate all existences below Ascendant!

Changxi and Meng Ye looked at each other, only now realizing the depth of the Luo Tian Sect's foundation.

Four Demon Emperors!

With just this, the Luo Tian Sect was enough to stand at the forefront of the various sects in the Eastern Wilderness, not to mention the rumored group of Sages.

"Senior Brother?"

Nine Spirit Lord took a deep breath and squeezed out a smile.

"Let's go!"

He led the two of them towards the solemnly renovated grand hall above. As he climbed the steps, he couldn't help but look back at that golden tiger.

Such an existence had also been subdued by Luo Chen?

Before they even reached the entrance of the grand hall, figures filed out from inside.

Nine Spirit Lord looked up and saw that most of the people inside were familiar to him.

Wang Yuan, Ling Fengzi, Ghost Emperor Xiao Bai, and an old man and a young woman who should be the Hao Ranzi and Jadewater Dragon he had heard of.

Ling Fengzi took the initiative to welcome them, smiling, "Fellow Daoist Nine Spirit, long time no see. Please leave your name here!"

A scroll had already been unrolled.

Nine Spirit Lord smiled reluctantly and casually left his Daoist name on it.

Then, nodding to the others, under the guidance of Ghost Emperor Xiao Bai, he entered the Shouyang Hall, which was like an ocean abyss.

Changxi and Meng Ye did the same, leaving their names on the scroll.

Ling Fengzi looked back and said to Wang Yuan, "Not bad. It seems Senior Brother Luo Chen's appeal is quite good. Three Sages have arrived early in the morning."

Wang Yuan's gaze was calm as he looked outside the mountain. One after another, powerful beams of light were heading in this direction.

"There are far more than three. We're going to be busy. Ling Fengzi, you're more familiar with the various sects of the Eastern Wilderness. You handle the reception! Leave the name-recording to me."

Ling Fengzi stroked his beard and smiled, cheerfully accepting this task.

Then, with Wang Yuan holding the scroll and Ling Fengzi receiving the guests, one by one, the names that shook the Eastern Wilderness were continuously announced in the chanting voices of Hao Ranzi and Jadewater Dragon.

The grand, resonant chanting of names even reached outside the mountain, and even throughout all of Lingtian Pass, through the formation.

"Fenghua Palace, Fenghua Fairy, Fengyue Fairy!"

"White Crane Sect, Xianhe Sage, Crane Emperor, Bai Yunfei!"

"Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion, Lin Qingxuan!"

"Myriad Beasts Sect, Qin Baixiang, Qilian Sage!"

"Tiandu Sect, Tianduzi..."

...

"White Deer Cave, Pengxiang Sage, Kuxiang Sage, Tie Guan Daoist, Nanping Sage, Huiming Layman, Kongtongzi!"

"Dongyang Sect, Grand Elder Dongyang Sage!"

"Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, Shi Chuanting, Lian Buwei!"

When the last two arrived with Dongyang Grand Elder, the smile on Ling Fengzi's face disappeared, and Wang Yuan abruptly raised his head, staring fixedly at the three people before him.

The originally warm atmosphere suddenly cooled down.

Lian Buwei, with his delicate mind, immediately sensed this. He raised an eyebrow. "What, are we not welcome?"

Wang Yuan grinned, the smile even looking somewhat ferocious. He extended his hand behind him in a gesture of invitation.

"Honored guests have arrived. How could we not welcome you? Please, come in!"

(End of Chapter)
