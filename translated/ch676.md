# Chapter 676: Echoes of Demon and Devil, Unceasing for a Thousand Years

Wood Heaven Plain was not a plain in the ordinary sense.

Mountains rose and fell across it. Ravines cut through the land. Streams wound between forests, and in the distance there were even snow-covered ridges shining under the dim sky.

If Hundred Crow Ghost Forest was a strange spirit-wood forest that seemed to have gathered every contradiction in the world, then Wood Heaven Plain was more like an enormous spirit herb garden.

Every region had its own terrain.

There were damp valleys filled with mist, dry stone slopes exposed to blazing light, cold fields sealed in frost, and fertile black earth where green light rose like smoke. Formation patterns were hidden beneath each stretch of land, separating the spiritual qi and climate needed by different spirit herbs.

Even the sky was restricted.

They could fly, but only at a low altitude. Any attempt to rise too high would be pressed back down by an invisible force.

Luo Chen followed Tai Sui through the region, his gaze sweeping across the surrounding spirit fields from time to time.

Strictly speaking, he no longer cared much about the inheritance of Demon Lord Heaven-Refining.

That inheritance was in Bright Radiance Heaven. It was what Moya Cave Master wanted, and it was also the place Tai Sui needed to reach in order to free his main body. Luo Chen's goal was only to fulfill his promise to Tai Sui, then wait until the reverse passage opened and leave Demon-Fall Land.

But he was still an alchemist.

Seeing so many high-grade spirit herbs and not feeling moved would be false.

What moved him most were not ordinary Sage-formation resources, but the demon-world body-tempering herbs Tai Sui had mentioned.

His body cultivation had long reached Archaic Tier Four.

Yet after that, progress had become extremely slow.

Was it because the human body had a natural limit? Was it because the Myriad Path Convergence he had forced upon himself was too broad and too mixed? Or was it because the Blazing Cloud Peng source blood he had originally obtained was too shallow, so the source power that supported his body cultivation was insufficient?

Luo Chen could not be sure.

But one thing was certain. External medicine could help.

If there were spirit herbs here that had once been prepared for demon and devil body tempering, they might allow him to feel out the road ahead.

They traveled for several days.

On this day, the group stopped before an enormous pit.

The pit was hundreds of zhang wide and dozens of zhang deep. The soil inside had been dug away cleanly, leaving only exposed formation patterns and torn spiritual roots. It looked as though someone had carved an entire piece of the earth out with a knife.

Moya Cave Master clicked his tongue. "This is ruthless."

Luo Chen crouched by the edge and picked up a crumb of green soil that had been left behind. He rubbed it between his fingers. "Green Pouch Soil [TERM?]."

Tai Sui glanced at him. "You recognize it?"

"A very good spirit-planting soil. It can hold spiritual qi, lock medicinal power, and adapt to many kinds of spirit herbs. If enough of it is refined together, it may even form Green Immortal Plain Pouch Soil [TERM?], which is one of the best soils for transplanting high-tier spirit plants."

Tai Sui's expression was ugly. "This place was originally a Dragon Saliva Grass garden. Those outsiders not only plucked the grass, they even dug away the soil."

Moya Cave Master grinned. "If it can be dug away, of course it should be dug away. Otherwise, leave it here for others?"

Tai Sui snorted coldly. "Northern Sea cultivators truly have no manners. When Yuan Demon Sect controlled this place in the past, they still knew how to leave roots and maintain the gardens. These people came only once and treated everything as a one-time harvest."

Luo Chen understood the difference.

Yuan Demon Sect had once regarded Demon-Fall Land as a long-term resource ground, so they naturally had to preserve it.

The Northern Sea cultivators who entered now were different. The traveling palace opened only once every three hundred years. Who knew whether they would live to see the next opening? Of course they would take whatever they could.

Tai Sui added, "Most of the high-tier herb gardens are protected by strong formations. The Sages who entered would rather chase the inheritance. Golden Core cultivators can break some of the outer gardens if they spend time and take risks, but they cannot touch the deepest places."

Luo Chen looked at the torn formation lines below and said nothing.

At the same time, in another part of Wood Heaven Plain, seven white-robed cultivators were standing outside a spirit herb garden.

The leader was a handsome man with a feather fan and tall crown. His bearing was refined, but the light in his eyes was sharp.

His name was Yu Gao [TERM?], a true disciple of Penglai Immortal Sect [TERM?].

Before them lay a small valley shrouded in pale light. Within the valley grew seven or eight purple-blue flowers, each one resembling an open eye.

"Divine Iris," one disciple said in delight. "And fourth-tier ones!"

Yu Gao smiled faintly. "Do not only look at the flowers. This entire spirit land is more valuable."

Another disciple immediately understood. "Senior Brother wants to move the whole garden into Penglai Octagonal Pavilion?"

"Naturally."

Yu Gao raised the feather fan in his hand. "Divine Iris can stabilize the body during Sage formation. Though there are only seven or eight mature flowers now, if we move the spirit land back and cultivate it carefully, the sect will have a steady supply in the future."

One disciple frowned. "The outer formation is already complicated, but I sense another killing formation hidden behind it. It does not seem to be the original arrangement."

Yu Gao nodded. "It should be a later addition from Yuan Demon Sect."

The disciples' expressions became solemn.

The war outside had already reached a fierce stage.

Penglai Immortal Sect had joined hands with Moluo Stream to oppose Cang Hai Alliance [TERM?]. Many sect elites had been sent to the battlefield, and even the people who entered Demon-Fall Land this time were fewer than originally planned.

Yu Gao looked at the Divine Iris flowers in the valley, his eyes bright.

"As long as we can bring back enough foundation from Demon-Fall Land, Penglai will sooner or later become the true master of the Northern Sea. As for Moluo Stream..."

He smiled and did not finish the sentence.

Several days later, Luo Chen and the others arrived before a hidden cave.

The cave entrance was covered by dense green light. Even from outside, one could feel surging vitality, as though a heart were beating deep within the mountain.

Tai Sui's expression carried a rare hint of eagerness. "This is the place."

Luo Chen looked at the green light. "What spirit herb is inside?"

"Demon-Devil Heart Tree [TERM?]."

The name alone made Luo Chen's brows rise.

Tai Sui explained, "It is a unique species cultivated by the True Lord himself. It sprouts at fourth tier. Once mature, it can reach fifth tier."

"A fifth-tier spirit tree should have intelligence, no?"

"Ordinary spirit trees might. This one will not."

Tai Sui shook his head. "It is not a natural spirit tree. The True Lord used the hearts of a fifth-tier ancient demon and a fifth-tier ancient devil as the foundation, then grafted them onto a True Scale Tree [TERM?]. The final product is neither demon, nor devil, nor tree. It violates heavenly harmony. Unless it survives a three-nine thunder tribulation, gaining spirit will be extremely difficult."

Luo Chen pondered. "The Mountains and Seas Realm is full of spiritual mechanism. Grass, trees, birds, beasts, mountains, and rivers can all gain spirit. Why would it be more difficult for such a high-tier thing?"

Tai Sui smiled. "Because the higher the rank and the deeper the foundation, the harder it is to open true spirit. Heaven and earth are balanced. Lowly grass may awaken spirit by chance, but it also dies by the roadside after a single frost. A fifth-tier ancient demon's heart contains too much. To make such a thing produce its own spirit is to ask heaven and earth for too much."

Luo Chen understood somewhat.

The stronger the vessel, the heavier the price of awakening.

He looked toward the cave. "Does the fruit of this tree help body tempering?"

Tai Sui hesitated. "I am not certain. But the physiques of ancient demons and ancient devils were both powerful. A tree grown from their heart blood should at least be useful for strengthening the organs."

That was enough.

Luo Chen and Moya Cave Master immediately began working on the outer formations.

The formations outside the cave were extremely old, but they were not weak. Some had been laid by Demon Lord Heaven-Refining himself, and some had been repaired by Yuan Demon Sect later. Even with Tai Sui guiding them from the side, breaking them was slow.

One month passed in a flash.

At first, Moya Cave Master was still the main force.

Luo Chen's formation skill was broader than it was deep. When he first faced these ancient restrictions, many of his methods seemed crude.

But once he became familiar with their style, his progress was frightening.

Tai Sui watched from the side and secretly nodded.

This was exactly why he needed Luo Chen.

Moya Cave Master had experience, but his ceiling was limited. Luo Chen, however, had a mind that could learn quickly and an energy that did not fear exhaustion. The later arrangements for rescuing Tai Sui's main body would need such a person.

At last, they reached the innermost green-light grand formation.

Moya Cave Master stared at it for a long while, then gave up. "I cannot find the flaw."

Luo Chen also shook his head. "It is complete. If we force it, we may damage what is inside."

Tai Sui stepped forward. "Then use my method."

He raised his hand and cut a piece of flesh from his own arm.

The flesh wriggled in the air, then transformed into a streak of blood light under Tai Sui's refining. The blood light slowly touched the green formation.

What happened next surprised both Luo Chen and Moya Cave Master.

The grand formation did not resist.

Instead, it softened like ice meeting warm water, opening a narrow doorway.

Tai Sui explained, "The True Lord used my vitality to cultivate this tree. The formation guards against outsiders, but it does not guard against me."

Luo Chen nodded and stepped into the cave first.

The moment he crossed the doorway, a roar exploded soundlessly in his sea of consciousness.

It was not a real sound.

It was a remnant echo of will.

In an instant, Luo Chen seemed to see two towering shadows.

One was a demon, its body vast and ferocious, its blood qi surging like a red sea.

The other was a devil, black-armored and horned, its eyes full of bottomless cruelty.

They roared at each other, and their roars overlapped, crashing into Luo Chen's soul like a mountain collapse.

His body swayed.

Behind him, Moya Cave Master was about to follow, but Tai Sui suddenly reached out and blocked him.

"Wait."

Moya Cave Master frowned. "What happened?"

Tai Sui stared into the cave. His expression was calm, but deep in his eyes, light flickered.

"The demon-devil echoes remain unceasing for a thousand years. A non-Sage soul cannot resist them."

He wanted to see.

After Hundred Crow Ghost Forest, Old Ghost had said there were three helpers.

Who was the third person hiding beside Devil Lord Azure Yang?

*(End of Chapter)*
