# Chapter 1096: Ascension

Vast, boundless.

Unknown how many tens of thousands of li, its edges unknowable.

This was what Luo Chen sensed after breaking free from the shackles of his physical body, using his pure primordial spirit to perceive the world's fundamental source power—the Ocean of Origin Qi!

Though named an ocean, for billions of years it had been without waves or ripples, neither water nor qi, only pure, stagnant, almost solidified chaotic energy.

It was chaotic energy composed of various attribute spiritual qi. Anyone with a spirit root, regardless of type, could find their corresponding attribute within it.

In that moment, Luo Chen finally understood why Ascendant cultivators, as long as their own mana was not exhausted, possessed boundless and inexhaustible might!

The Ascension realm controlled the origin qi of heaven and earth.

And since origin qi was omnipresent, one's own power was likewise immeasurable.

Luo Chen had previously stumbled into the Ocean of Origin Qi by chance, but never had he felt it so profoundly.

Back then, his soul was not yet strong enough; merely surviving within it was a challenge, let alone observing anything else.

One could say he knew *that* it was so, but not *why* it was so.

But this time, after consuming the Soul-Scouring Pill refined from Black Lotus Seed Pods and soul stones, his vast primordial spirit had become extremely solid, finally allowing him to maintain a third of his clarity.

Wandering within the Ocean of Origin Qi, the viscous chaotic energy, though without raging waves, was far more vast and heavy than any violent sea. Every faint push of Luo Chen's divine sense felt like stirring amber that had solidified for millions of years—slow and laborious.

Of course, if he could absorb and refine this power, even just a little, it would transform into Luo Chen's purest mana.

But Luo Chen did not rashly absorb this power.

For him, after completing his life soul, Ascension was merely a step across the threshold, a matter of natural progression.

Even if the origin qi of heaven and earth pressed in madly, his perfectly complete life soul would not show any flaw.

What he sought now was another opportunity.

Comprehending the true intent of the laws!

And a new one, completely different from the several laws he already grasped!

The Law-Derivation Pill had already been refined. The spiritual trigger within it, together with the spiritual trigger drawn by the Ascension process, one internal and one external, guided him. His primordial spirit unconsciously walked through the Ocean of Origin Qi, seeking that elusive true intent of the laws.

Suddenly, his primordial spirit felt a slight heat.

"Has the Nirvana Fire Spirit returned?"

As this thought surfaced, a wisp of flame suddenly appeared beneath his feet.

It was a tiny golden flame.

Like the Nirvana Sacred Flame, yet not the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

It burned and grew wildly, and at its peak, it resembled a true sun.

But in the blink of an eye, it extinguished.

Just like the universe of heaven and earth, all things have an end.

Suddenly!

Within the dead silence, new life was born.

A spark of light appeared again, burgeoning and growing.

"Rising from the ashes, nirvana rebirth—this is the true intent of Nirvana!"

Luo Chen's primordial spirit stirred slightly, suddenly understanding all of this.

Because of the Nirvana Sacred Flame, he naturally felt a closeness to the true intent of Nirvana. As long as he was willing, he could approach, comprehend, and even master it.

This was no empty claim.

He had done something similar back then, just as he had comprehended the true intent of Withering-Bloom with the help of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

Moreover, compared to the vague understanding of those days, he now also grasped the Fire Origin, and the true intent of Nirvana was a higher-order fire-attribute law true intent, making comprehension relatively easier.

As long as he was willing...

Gazing at the flame that had begun to burn again, Luo Chen's steps paused slightly, sinking into deep thought.

In silence, he set off again, stepping past the true intent of Nirvana.

He chose to give up comprehending this high-order law true intent!

If this decision were to spread, he would surely be cursed for wasting a heavenly opportunity.

As one of the highest-order true intents within the fire-attribute laws, the true intent of Nirvana possessed the most magnificent power and the most miraculous effects, ranking among the true intents closest to the essence of the Great Dao.

Even compared to Azure Frost's comprehension of the Space Origin, it was not inferior.

Moreover, with the precedent of Rosycloud, who could be reborn even after a hundred deaths, Luo Chen should not have given it up, theoretically.

As he left that place, Luo Chen found many reasons for himself.

He had already comprehended the Fire Origin; he could always rely on himself to comprehend the true intent of Nirvana in the future. There was no need to rush.

Comprehending law true intents through external objects was taking a shortcut, somewhat improper. It might be fine at ordinary times, but why waste the opportunity now?

Many reasons, too numerous to list.

But if he had to pick one, Luo Chen would attribute it to intuition.

Intuition told him, he could not stop here!

Since he had already given up, there was no turning back. Luo Chen continued to advance with difficulty.

As the origin of the world, the Ocean of Origin Qi contained the embodiment of countless Great Dao laws.

If he could touch upon one or two here and get his foot in the door, after his primordial spirit returned to his body, he could try to cultivate them in the future.

Luo Chen walked within it, like a speck of dust between heaven and earth, so small and insignificant.

When he had taken countless steps, or perhaps after traversing millions of li, the steps of his divine sense stopped once again.

A great mountain blocked his path.

The mountain was desolate and ancient, as if it had existed for billions of years, since the beginning of chaos.

It had always sat there, neither rejecting all things nor accepting Luo Chen, merely displaying the most fundamental true intent that constituted its own being in the most objective manner.

With a flash of spiritual insight, Luo Chen understood what this was.

The Earth Origin!

Should he choose it?

Luo Chen's steps stopped again.

This was the most basic of the Five Elements law true intents. People like Deng Taiyue and Lian Cheng had comprehended this power.

It wasn't particularly rare.

Surely, there would be better ones later.

At least in Luo Chen's perception, guided by the spiritual trigger, more powerful law true intents awaited him further ahead.

But this time, after a moment's thought, Luo Chen actively released his divine sense.

The most basic did not mean the worst.

Especially for him!

"I have already comprehended Wood's Withering-Bloom, and the two origins of Water and Fire. If I can also comprehend the true intents of the Metal and Earth laws, perhaps I can reawaken the Five-Spirit Dao Body that has lain dormant for many years!"

"This is it!"

Like a monkey entering a cornfield, too many temptations make one easy to pick one and discard another.

The true intent of Nirvana ahead, and the faintly visible existences behind, were all good ears of corn.

But ten birds in the bush are not worth one in the hand.

At this moment, the Earth Origin was the ear of corn most suitable for Luo Chen!

It was neither too big nor too small; it would neither burst Luo Chen nor starve him. It was enough to fill his stomach, enough to lay the most solid foundation for his body, leading to a more brilliant future.

Again, it was intuition. This time, Luo Chen chose to meet it head-on.

The moment his divine sense touched that great mountain, countless obscure and blurry information fragments surged in.

In the boundless darkness, a seed quietly germinated beneath the hard soil. Ores buried for billions of years silently crystallized. Hot magma gathered strength in absolute silence, and the power it nurtured gave birth to the myriad things and spirits.

There were high mountains collapsing on its shoulders, earthfire scorching its bones, mountain torrents tearing at its body, grass and tree roots penetrating deep into its marrow... The great earth was silent, bearing it all, and amidst pain and endurance, it ultimately transformed into even greater depth.

An intangible, formless pulling force guided all things in the world. Dust gathered into mounds, mounds piled into hills, hills stretched into mountain ranges... In the movement of the boundless universe, even stars attracted and orbited each other. This was the gravity of the earth.

...

An excessively complex torrent of information madly scoured Luo Chen's divine sense.

Each piece seemed to contain a great fortune, each possessed great power, and mastering each seemed to grant control over half of heaven and earth.

Luo Chen's divine sense was far too insignificant compared to it, but he tightly guarded his mind, comprehending the purest power within.

It was a power that could withstand all calamities, a power of abundant virtue carrying all things.

It was a power that returned to chaos, a power that guarded true suchness.

It was—the Earth Origin!

The great mountain remained majestic, but Luo Chen's divine sense had vanished.

Like a swift retreat, guided by the remaining spiritual trigger, his primordial spirit frantically withdrew, returning to Green Parasol Mountain.

If he had been any later, he would have been lost in the deepest layers of the Ocean of Origin Qi.

Fortunately, there was the spiritual trigger drawn by the Ascension process, and fortunately, there was the pre-prepared Law-Derivation Pill.

The moment Luo Chen returned.

His vast primordial spirit suddenly vanished, leaving only a normal physical body sitting cross-legged in the void.

Compared to before, this physical body now seemed like a speck of dust.

But sitting there, it was like a great mountain.

Ancient, weathered, rugged, heavy!

Standing there, it exuded an unshakable, infinitely heavy aura of law!

Luo Chen opened his eyes.

Beside him, the Nirvana Fire Spirit spread its wings, dancing joyfully.

At his feet, the myriad demons bowed their heads, trembling.

On the cliff wall in the distance, a gaze contained infinite unwillingness and immense terror.

Luo Chen looked at all this calmly, unmoved. He merely opened his mouth slightly.

The next moment.

The spiritual qi tide that had been expanding outward suddenly reversed, pouring entirely into his body, centered on him.

Luo Chen was already accustomed to this scene—merely the gift of heaven and earth after a major realm breakthrough.

His figure slowly descended, finally standing before the Truth Crossing Hall.

Looking at the heaven and earth before him, feeling as if there was a stifled breath in his chest, and also a sense of fullness, Luo Chen no longer suppressed himself. He opened his mouth and let out a long roar.

The vast roar, like a dragon's cry, spread throughout Green Parasol Mountain, falling into the ears of every living being.

In that instant, these beings knew that a god had been born in this world!

Truly:

The dragon's cry in the sheath seeks to break the sky, a single Primordial Unity Cauldron brews the great pill.
Sleeves roll up the thousand-qing moon of the Northern Sea, feet tread the mist of ten thousand ravines on Green Parasol Mountain.
Great Luo clouds shatter, stars make a ladder; nine kalpas turn to ash, mortals become immortals.
Bending down to pick up a single speck of dust in the human world, the vastness of a million eons lies within the wide sleeve!

...

"Hall Master, all the rebellious demon cultivators have been subdued. They kneel at the foot of the mountain, begging for forgiveness. Please instruct us on how to deal with them."

Inside the Truth Crossing Hall, Pure Crane respectfully reported the situation.

She dared not raise her head. The one sitting above her now had an aura as vast as the sun, giving her a feeling even more terrifying than when Azure Frost had achieved Ascension.

Just sitting there, he was like a great mountain, lofty and ancient, and also like a blazing sun, impossible to look at directly.

A faint voice came from above.

"Extract their souls, imprint them with seals. They shall be slaves of Truth Crossing forever."

"Yes!"

Pure Crane's heart was filled with elation. The frustration of being suppressed and beaten earlier had completely dissipated in this moment.

Traitors deserved the fate of traitors!

A hurried landing sound came from outside the hall.

Heavenjade walked in and bowed respectfully to the one above.

"Master, Moon Wanderer is dead. No bones remain; she was completely melted to ashes by the Nirvana Sacred Flame."

"That was too easy for her."

Though he said this, Luo Chen's tone did not carry much regret.

To him now, all those below Ascension were mere ants.

In fact, even before his Ascension, he had already disregarded these peers.

"How is Aokuang?"

Luo Chen was only concerned about this one person.

To break through to the Ascension realm, Luo Chen's preparations were not numerous, but each one was terrifyingly extraordinary.

Whether it was the Nirvana Sacred Flame, the Yuantu Sword, or even the final Burial Bone Umbrella, all were sufficient to handle everything.

Even the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation had been re-refined by him and was by no means something an ordinary Sage could break through.

But Aokuang had forcefully broken through, and even manifested his primordial spirit, attempting to charge at him.

He truly lived up to his reputation as an ancient existence of the Million Great Mountains.

Heavenjade said respectfully, "His physical body is being watched by Black King, while his primordial spirit is pinned to the cliff wall of Green Parasol Mountain by my master's precious sword. He wails without ceasing, in a state worse than death. Also, this is his strange treasure. Please take a look, Master."

A golden, radiant claw floated down and landed before Luo Chen.

The Heavenly Wolf Claw!

A treasure refined by a former Wolf Clan Ascendant great power using his own claw.

Luo Chen swept his gaze over it and couldn't help but raise an eyebrow.

This magic treasure had very few restrictions and formation patterns on it. It relied purely on the quality of its material.

Yet, with just that, it possessed the power of a spirit treasure.

What it lacked was merely spirituality.

If it were further refined, its upper limit might reach the level of a spirit treasure.

Luo Chen put away the claw, stood up, and walked towards the hall.

"Very well. I also want to see what kind of person dares to face me directly."

Behind him, Pure Crane and Heavenjade followed closely.

The expressions of the two women differed. Heavenjade's face was full of excitement and joy, while Pure Crane carried a hint of vigilance and worry.

Pure Crane's vigilance and worry were not directed at Luo Chen—she wouldn't dare. She was worried about Heavenjade!

This woman was also a feathered demon cultivator and had followed Luo Chen for a long time.

Being brought to Green Parasol Mountain this time, could it be that Luo Chen intended to let this woman take charge of Green Parasol Mountain?

She had worked diligently all these years; she must not end up with nothing in the end.

Luo Chen paid no attention to these petty thoughts. His figure stopped between the cliff walls.

He stood in the void, relying on no external force, only supported by heaven and earth.

Opposite him, Aokuang's primordial spirit was firmly pinned by the blood sword.

Suddenly!

"Luo Chen, give me a quick death!"

Luo Chen was speechless.

To be in such a state and still refuse to call him 'Senior'?

With a wry smile, Luo Chen waved his hand.

The Yuantu Sword instantly returned.

Aokuang's form relaxed, and he instinctively wanted to flee, but he felt that this heaven and earth were as heavy as a great mountain, as slow as a quagmire.

Even the most instinctive primordial spirit teleportation technique was unusable.

Controlling origin qi, sealing heaven and earth!

This was the most standard Ascendant technique!

The Heavenly Wolf primordial spirit revealed a human-like bitter smile. "You really won't let me go."

"Before you made your move, you should have understood that everything has its price." Luo Chen clasped his hands behind his back and said leisurely, "I will not make things difficult for you. Enter this Heavenly Wolf Claw as a spirit for a thousand years. After a thousand years, I will permit you to dissolve your physical form and enter reincarnation."

Aokuang was silent for a long time, then finally bowed his head.

"Thank you, Senior, for your great kindness!"

In an instant, Luo Chen was filled with pride and satisfaction.

The once lofty Wolf Emperor, now had to be grateful even for being enslaved.

But a moment later, Luo Chen felt a sense of listlessness.

These people were no longer worthy of his attention.

Standing between heaven and earth, he actually felt a sense of loss and loneliness.

He looked up at the void, his expression slightly dejected.

Heavenjade and Pure Crane were utterly confused. Having achieved the status of a great power, why would their master/hall master show such an expression?

They did not know that Luo Chen, looking at this heaven and earth, felt a sense of shackles and confinement.

"No wonder Rosycloud and Nether Spring spent such a great price to forcibly ascend. This heaven and earth is too narrow for the likes of us."

(End of chapter)
