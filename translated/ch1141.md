# Chapter 1141: I Will Act

For Luo Chen, life in Relic Immortal City was uneventful. Aside from the occasional excitement of glimpsing corners of this world through ancient texts while browsing the scriptures, most of it could even be called tedious.

But for the natives living within it, every moment was filled with fierce conflict.

Especially regarding the position of "City Lord," the two current candidates of Relic Immortal City were locked in intense open and covert struggles.

The earlier robbery of the merchant caravan by the Meteor Bandits was merely the tip of the iceberg.

Once inside the city, the two Xiao brothers fought with increasing ferocity.

Promoting trusted confidants, eliminating dissidents, suppressing the opposition—it had even escalated to blatantly weakening the other side's strength through bribery, assassination, and other means.

Among all this, Xiao Zirui, who had originally been designated as one of the five candidates for the "martial contest to decide the lord," was quite special. Not only was he a Furnace Realm martial arts expert, but he was also a member of the Xiao clan.

Gaining his support would undoubtedly be a massive step ahead in the competition for the city lord position.

But unexpectedly, Xiao Zirui, who had always supported Xiao Feng, had disappeared a few days ago.

This had truly caught Xiao Feng off guard.

"What happened?" Luo Chen asked.

Xiao Feng smiled bitterly. "The problem is that nothing happened, and Xiao Zirui just vanished. But I'm certain he isn't dead. This attitude just means he doesn't want to help me anymore."

Luo Chen pondered for a moment, then nodded lightly. "It seems that grievances have been building up for a long time."

Xiao Feng sighed. That was the only reason he could think of.

Although he felt he had treated Xiao Zirui well enough, in the other's eyes, perhaps what he had given was far from sufficient.

Hence, at the critical moment, the other party chose to pull this disappearing act.

Luo Chen was somewhat curious. "Xiao Zirui's strength isn't that impressive. Since he's disappeared, can't you just send another Furnace Realm martial artist?"

Xiao Feng waved his hand. "Furnace Realm martial artists aren't that common, Senior. You're thinking too lightly of it."

Luo Chen chuckled softly.

He had gained a general understanding of the number of experts in this realm through that book, *Travel Notes of the Seventy-Two Cities*.

Even though the Great Cataclysm Realm preserved a complete orthodox body-refining path, the lack of spiritual qi, scarcity of resources, and even the bizarre changes in lifespan limits meant that the number of high-level experts was far less than imagined.

Vajra Realm and Blood River Realm cultivators were everywhere. As long as one had some talent and was willing to invest time and effort in cultivation, they could always achieve something.

But Furnace Realm martial artists, who had thoroughly comprehended the transformations of qi and blood, were extremely rare. No matter where, they were uncommon talents.

As for Leakless Realm martial artists, who boasted a perfect, flawless golden body, they were even rarer. Placed among the seventy-two cities, they were unequivocally city lord-level existences!

Some large cities didn't even have Leakless Realm experts guarding them.

From this perspective, the fact that Uncle Iron was willing to serve under Xiao Feng was truly incredible.

Xiao Feng took a deep breath and said solemnly, "Moreover, how can ordinary Furnace Realm martial artists compare to you, Senior? If you fight, you will surely secure a victory. Combined with the other four experts I've prepared, I only need to win two more matches, and the city lord position will be firmly in my grasp."

Luo Chen asked curiously, "Will that Uncle Iron of yours, who has cultivated a Leakless Golden Body, also make a move?"

Xiao Feng was startled, but then nodded with a complicated expression.

Indeed, this outsider who had made even Uncle Iron feel troubled was not to be underestimated. He had actually seen through Uncle Iron's true realm.

The news of Uncle Iron's breakthrough to the Leakless Realm had been kept hidden by Xiao Feng—it was his greatest trump card!

After his father left, Xiao Feng had poured a massive amount of resources into Uncle Iron, even using a forbidden secret technique to help him break through in secret a few years ago.

He hadn't expected all of this to be so clearly visible to Luo Chen.

Luo Chen clicked his tongue twice. "Actually, why go through all this trouble? You could just have him make a move in secret and eliminate Xiao Yun, couldn't you?"

Eliminate Xiao Yun?

Xiao Feng's mouth went dry. He found that this senior seemed to act without scruples.

"Xiao Yun is, after all, my second brother!"

Just as Xiao Zirui, even though he resented Xiao Feng, ultimately chose to disappear and wash his hands of the matter rather than turn against him, the internal strife within the Xiao clan still adhered to the bottom line of blood ties.

Luo Chen made no comment.

This Great Cataclysm Realm, where the laws were broken and the Dao did not exist, as the Xiao ancestors had said—yet these descendants still confined their struggles within the rules of the game. What were they even fighting for?

"Senior?" Xiao Feng called out tentatively.

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Just arrange for me to be included. But it's best to put me last. If the first three matches are won, I'll be spared the trouble. If the first three are lost, I'll also be free. But if it truly comes down to a decisive battle, I will act."

Xiao Feng opened his mouth, but ultimately cupped his hands and bowed.

"Then I thank you in advance, Senior. I promise that whether you act or not, after this matter is concluded, your monthly tribute will be doubled. Even the special Mistress Smokecloud supplied to Worryfree City can be provided to you with one crate every year."

Having said this, Xiao Feng hurriedly left.

Luo Chen thought for a moment, then let out a faint, derisive laugh.

To him, whether the city lord of Relic Immortal City was Xiao Feng or Xiao Yun didn't really matter. All he sought was a quiet place to cultivate.

...

Boom...

Suddenly, a thunderclap exploded, followed by a continuous roll of thunder.

Like a tidal wave, it came rolling in.

Within the quiet chamber, Luo Chen was abruptly jolted awake.

Compared to ordinary martial artists, he was more sensitive to this thunderous sound, even feeling a hint of fear.

In his prime, this would almost never have happened.

Whether it was the thunder essence he had refined into his natal magic treasure or the lightning tribulation he had endured when breaking through to the Sage stage, both could greatly enhance the soul's resistance to heavenly thunder.

But this Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound of the Great Cataclysm Realm was uniquely uncomfortable!

Luo Chen decided to stop condensing pill force and ceased his cultivation.

Another two months had passed, and he had already condensed a second batch of pill force. His current number of pill seeds had reached a full seventy-two!

If this were to be made known, it would undoubtedly shock everyone.

Even Wang Yuan, who had created the *Divine Astral Fighting Qi* and proposed the perfect method for breaking through realms, would be dumbfounded.

Thirty-six pill seeds, matching the number of the heavenly cycle, was already the limit Wang Yuan had envisioned. But Luo Chen had condensed a full seventy-two.

Each one could independently form a core and become astral force, transforming the body into a furnace!

The reason he had broken through Wang Yuan's limit was that Luo Chen had combined his past cultivation knowledge with the paths of formations and alchemy to blaze a new trail, constructing a second minor heavenly cycle.

In Luo Chen's calculations, he could still construct another minor heavenly cycle. At that point, he would break through to the Furnace Realm with one hundred and eight pill seeds!

And even that would not be enough to fully refine all the source blood in his body.

There was no helping it—the foundation of this physical body was simply too terrifying.

Luo Chen didn't force it. The remaining half of his source blood would not go to waste; it would have other uses later.

Donning a thin outer robe, Luo Chen came to the window of his study.

Gazing at the dim, hazy sky where white snakes darted about in the early morning, he murmured to himself:

"Spring thunder sounds, all things are born. The special envoy from Worryfree City should be arriving soon!"

(Chapter End)
