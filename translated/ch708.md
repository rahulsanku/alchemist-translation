# Chapter 708: The Stone Dragon's Wondrous Use, Flesh Body Runs Wild

On this journey to the Demon-Fall Land, Luo Chen had gained a great deal!

Any single item taken out could cause countless people in the cultivation world to fight over it.

For example, the piece of Nine Yang Geng Metal in Luo Chen's hand was of such fine quality that it could be called the finest of the finest. If it were used as the main material to refine a flying sword or divine saber, or added into some magic treasure, its killing power would certainly multiply!

Especially for magic treasures of the metal and fire attributes, it was incomparably compatible.

And this was only the most outstanding ore material. In Thousand-Blade Mountain, Luo Chen had also obtained many middle and high-tier ores.

"All this time, although I have had many magic treasures, I have always been somewhat lacking in attack-type magic treasures."

"With this batch of materials, after I form my Nascent Soul, perhaps I can personally refine a handy magic treasure according to the artifact-forging blueprints I have collected..."

Looking at those materials, Luo Chen slowly calculated in his heart.

Only, as he thought, other ideas arose.

Good attack-type magic treasures were not common, but as long as one searched carefully, this vast cultivation world always had them.

But for him, an advantage in another area had always been suppressed and could not be fully displayed.

That was "origin power"!

After his physique reached Archaic Tier Four, beyond boundless brute force, a special power had been born, and Luo Chen had named it origin power.

This power possessed a special nature and was very difficult to make compatible with magic treasures tailor-made for mana.

Therefore, when Luo Chen used origin power against enemies, he often could only rely on his flesh body. For this reason, he had even drawn from a hundred schools and fused them to create the physical technique Devil Lord's Seven Scattering Hands.

Then was it possible to specially forge a weapon that could output origin power, or even amplify its might?

Luo Chen thought about this idea for a while, then finally shook his head.

His theoretical knowledge in this area was far too lacking. He still needed to accumulate more, lest he waste these fine materials.

Luo Chen beckoned, and two streaks of flowing light flew before him.

One was a white banner, and the other an octagonal pavilion.

Even though the two treasures had been sealed, the spiritual light contained within them was still frightening.

They were precisely the Soul-Summoning Banner obtained from Wu Qi and the others of Moluo Stream, and the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion seized from the disciples of Penglai Immortal Sect.

Both of these were true artifacts!

The latter in particular was known as a portable spirit medicine garden!

After Han Zhan helped refine it and obtained partial formation authority, Luo Chen could barely activate the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion, and so had also obtained some treasured medicinal materials inside it.

The Dragon Saliva Grass traces he had seen back in Wood Heaven Plain were caused by this Penglai Octagonal Pavilion.

Dragon Saliva Grass was good stuff. For long-worm-type demon beasts, it possessed the wondrous effect of promoting growth.

Apart from that, many precious spirit medicines such as Divine Iris, Iron Calamus, and Turtle Spirit Flower were collected within.

Unfortunately, it did not contain the spirit medicines of Penglai Immortal Sect itself, greatly reducing Luo Chen's harvest.

"However, having the Octagonal Pavilion itself, this true artifact, already means I have profited greatly!"

With a thought from Luo Chen, two hazy phantoms appeared and disappeared atop the Octagonal Pavilion, incomparably enticing.

They were precisely the Five Elements Lotus Platform that had almost reached fifth tier, and the Demon-Beast Heartlink Tree whose effects were unknown!

The former was something Luo Chen needed to form his Nascent Soul, and there was naturally no need to say more.

As for the latter, according to Tai Sui, it was a hybrid variety Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had personally cultivated by taking the hearts of a fifth-tier ancient demon and a fifth-tier ancient devil, burying them together, and grafting them onto a True Scale Tree!

On the day it survived, it was already fourth tier.

When it matured, it should reach fifth tier.

Even Devil Lord Heaven-Refining did not know its upper limit.

According to Tai Sui, the fruit it produced had a great chance of possessing miraculous effects for body cultivation.

"My body cultivation path has now reached a bottleneck, and the road ahead is unclear. If this tree truly can produce immortal fruit, perhaps it can let me open a new path."

After murmuring softly, Luo Chen carefully put away the two precious spirit medicines.

For now, the Demon-Beast Heartlink Tree could not be used.

Let alone producing fruit, he still had no clue how to continue cultivating it.

Although he had also once asked Sang Jinghe to study it for a while, the man's ability was insufficient and far inferior to Sang Jiugong.

This spirit tree still had to await fate in silence.

For Luo Chen, an alchemist, the true artifact Penglai Octagonal Pavilion undoubtedly brought enormous benefit.

Ordinarily, he could carry it with him. Once he settled somewhere for a short time, he could place it atop the spirit vein of a cave dwelling to absorb the spirit qi of heaven and earth, allowing the medicinal materials inside to grow normally.

For any alchemist, aside from pill-refining tools, this kind of treasure was probably what they dreamed of most.

However, the other true artifact Luo Chen obtained was not bad either!

The Soul-Summoning Banner!

Obtained from Wu Qi and the others, it was likely bestowed by Blood Nightmare Moluo and specifically used to gather in that dragon soul without intelligence.

Luo Chen's mind was clear as he carefully probed his divine sense into the soul banner.

Different from the Soul-Nourishing Banner, which emphasized nourishing, and the Soul-Refining Banner, which emphasized refining, this famous Soul-Summoning Banner of the Primordial Demon Sect's soul-refining lineage had the function of summoning souls.

Even damaged souls that had died many years ago, as long as there was a corresponding object to draw them, could be summoned back from between heaven and earth.

At this moment, under Luo Chen's investigation, that vast earth-yellow dragon soul inside the Soul-Summoning Banner was wandering blankly.

Its body was ancient and plain, seeming to have scales, yet in truth those were strata-like patterns of earth.

At key places, there were many breaks.

It could be seen that this soul was not complete, and even possessed no consciousness of its own.

According to the guesses back then, this earth-yellow dragon soul was very likely the remnant soul transformed from the archaic beast Stone Dragon of ancient legend.

It had originally been subdued by the Primordial Demon Sect's ancestor and served as the foundation of an Ascendant Holy Land.

Yet it had been brought into the Demon-Fall Land by Blood Sea Ancestor.

In the end, Blood Sea Ancestor fell, and the Stone Dragon remnant soul also transformed into a stretch of Gobi desert, taking root in the Quicksand Sea of Five Elements Heaven.

"Back then, I took a huge risk and snatched this remnant soul from the hands of Heaven Soil, master of the Quicksand Sea. Although it was to ruin Blood Nightmare Moluo's plans, what use does this soul actually have?"

Luo Chen was extremely curious.

He formed a spirit art for the Soul-Capturing Art, and with great effort, summoned out a thread of the Stone Dragon remnant soul.

Only one thread!

He did not dare draw out more, even if the Stone Dragon remnant soul had no consciousness.

The instant this pure wisp of soul appeared, before Luo Chen could examine it closely, he suddenly sensed something strange around him.

Within the abyss, spirit qi was incomparably abundant.

At this moment, large amounts of spirit qi involuntarily swept toward Luo Chen.

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly, and he quickly returned this thread of Stone Dragon soul to the Soul-Summoning Banner.

Only then did that violent spirit qi seem to lose its target, retreating blankly.

Looking at this scene, shock appeared on Luo Chen's face.

"It automatically draws spirit qi!"

"This..."

"In legend, the true intelligence of the archaic beast Stone Dragon was formed from a fifth-tier spirit vein gaining sentience, then occupying the flesh body of an earth dragon that had died of old age within the spirit vein. Only then did the so-called archaic beast Stone Dragon come to be."

"In other words, this remnant soul is essentially the consciousness of an ancient fifth-tier spirit vein!"

"That is why the strange phenomenon just now occurred. Just one wisp of remnant soul automatically drew spirit qi."

Because of this scene, Luo Chen thought of the strange spectacle in the Quicksand Sea.

Why had Heaven Soil both hated the Stone Dragon remnant soul and pursued it relentlessly?

When the Stone Dragon remnant soul fell into the Quicksand Sea, it stole a day's worth of spirit qi, greatly obstructing Heaven Soil's cultivation.

But if Heaven Soil devoured this soul, did that mean it could also possess a similar wondrous talent?

"If I use a special formation to confine this soul within some third-tier, no, fourth-tier spirit vein, over long years, is it possible to borrow its innate divine ability to absorb heaven and earth spirit qi and promote a spirit vein to fifth tier?"

Luo Chen's breathing became rapid.

It was as if he had seen a future in which he no longer had to rush around everywhere for spirit vein cave dwellings.

This fancy did not come from nowhere.

The scene just now was enough to prove it!

Moreover, why would Blood Nightmare Moluo go to such lengths to send people to summon back the Stone Dragon remnant soul? As a Sage Sage and nominally the master of Moluo Stream, he did not lack fourth-tier spirit veins.

In other words, it was very likely that Blood Nightmare Moluo had also had this plan.

Even because the Stone Dragon remnant soul had once been controlled by the Primordial Demon Sect, Blood Nightmare Moluo certainly already possessed the corresponding method to use it.

"In the end, it has benefited me!"

Luo Chen laughed aloud.

He carefully put away the Soul-Summoning Banner.

It was not yet time.

With his current realm and corresponding knowledge, it was not yet time to use the Stone Dragon remnant soul.

He would keep it for another day!

Someone else might be depressed that this true artifact could not be used, that this medicinal material required time, and that after finally discovering the way to use a treasure, they were still limited by realm.

But Luo Chen did not feel this way.

He deeply understood that ordinary Adept cultivators had no possibility of obtaining so many benefits in the Demon-Fall Land.

He had only relied on his fourth-tier archaic beast flesh body, Han Zhan's protection, Tai Sui's guidance as a local, and finally a bit of intelligence and effort to gain these things.

In essence, these gains were not things he should possess at the Adept realm.

It was very normal that, for the moment, he could only look at them but not eat them.

In reality, not all his gains could only be shelved.

For example, those Adept cultivators he had killed, Adept Red Corpse's group, the Adept cultivator who had crashed into his hands in Blazing Purgatory, and Ghost Spirit Child had all left behind quite a few inheritances.

The storage bags hanging in the air of the thatched hut were the spoils Luo Chen had casually collected.

These were gains that matched his realm!

There was no need to trouble Han Zhan to help erase the restrictions for him. In the process of cultivating from Adept Stage Nine to Perfected Completion, there would be plenty of time.

At that time, he could erase the restrictions bit by bit and inventory the spoils within. Perhaps there would be unexpected gains.

Moreover!

The true gains were far more than these!

The reshaped Fire Spirit Body!

The Withering-Bloom True Flame that had given birth to true spirit and already taken form!

And the lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which had devoured the nameless gray light...

Thinking of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, Luo Chen's joyful mood gradually cooled, and his expression became uncertain.

Even now, he still could not activate his own lifebound magic treasure!

It had to be said, this was always a thorn, leaving Luo Chen feeling as if something were stuck in his throat.

His right hand subconsciously pressed against his abdomen, and in the unseen qi sea, the little gray cauldron was silent and still.

...

In the days that followed, Luo Chen still focused mainly on tempering his mana and consolidating his realm.

But with his realm having reached Adept Stage Nine, the relevant cultivation essentials could not be ignored after all.

The final layer of every major realm was often completely different from the earlier minor realms.

During Qi Refinement, the ninth layer mainly focused on purifying spiritual power, so that after Foundation Establishment, mana liquefaction could be achieved.

During Foundation Establishment, the ninth layer involved trying to link spiritual power and divine sense, thereby deriving mana and advancing to the Adept stage. The Golden Core that now appeared solid was, in essence, formed by condensed drops of liquefied pure mana.

At Adept Stage Nine, another new change appeared.

That was the so-called "unity of the three treasures of essence, qi, and spirit"!

It allowed essence blood, mana, and even divine soul to freely merge together.

At this time, the Golden Core remained the main body. The fusion of the three was measured, yet their cooperation was seamless.

If examined carefully, according to Luo Chen's feeling, this was actually finding a second dwelling place for the divine soul aside from the sea of consciousness.

For cultivators, refining the spirit was the most important matter!

According to rumor, after cultivators reached an extremely high realm, even if their flesh body shattered, they could still endure between heaven and earth, shedding the mortal body and roaming the vast universe.

This kind of description was absolutely not comparable to Han Zhan's realm of surviving in the world by relying on his Nascent Soul.

It was a deeper and more profound state, one that did not rely on the mortal husk.

After indulging in these thoughts briefly, Luo Chen did not think further and instead focused on the present.

He tried once to unite the three treasures.

The experience was not very good.

In that instant, his entire person fell into chaos.

His flesh body in particular went berserk unconsciously in the abyss, punching through a stretch of mountain wall.

Only then did Luo Chen realize how difficult it was to pursue balance in the unity of the three treasures.

"No wonder Angler once bitterly sought body cultivation methods in his early years."

Essence, qi, and spirit were best balanced in proper measure. None of the three treasures should be too weak, otherwise imbalance would appear.

Cultivators were strong in qi cultivation and weak in body refinement, so their mana was vast while their essence blood was weak, causing their essence blood to be small in quantity.

Once the three treasures were united, signs of unstable mana would appear.

Han Zhan had previously reminded Luo Chen not to waste essence blood because of this taboo, fearing that Luo Chen would consume too much essence blood.

Later, when he stopped saying much, it should have been because he realized Luo Chen's physique was powerful and his essence blood abundant.

But Han Zhan forgot, or perhaps Luo Chen himself had not noticed, that his physique was far too powerful!

He had not shown the unstable mana of an ordinary late-stage Adept cultivator. Instead, his flesh body had taken control and once gone berserk unconsciously under instinct.

After understanding this point, Luo Chen relaxed instead.

If his mana was insufficient, then he would strengthen his mana!

He would purify its quality and bring it to its limit.

At that time, he would try uniting the three treasures again.

This was also in the same line as the main purpose of his recent mana tempering.

With this, Luo Chen's cultivation became much easier than expected.

Every day, he only needed to sit in meditation and refine qi, occasionally mobilizing the Withering-Bloom True Flame to temper his Golden Core.

He even gave up using pills.

His cultivation speed, already three times higher than before, no longer needed pills to accelerate cultivation in the Adept realm. Instead, they would easily leave pill poison behind.

As for the extra time, Luo Chen placed it on digesting Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's alchemy inheritance.

Those seventy-two volumes of pill books were undoubtedly an enormous treasure vault for an alchemist.

And the thirty-six pill formulas, whether for demons or devils aside, the human series alone could completely become a key for cultivators to open higher realms.

Strictly speaking, this set of inheritance was one of Luo Chen's greatest current gains.

The Spirit Root Pill formula was divided into one of the three pill formulas of the Qi Refinement realm, supplemented by the first-tier Qi-Nourishing Pill and Body-Cleansing Pill.

For the Foundation Establishment realm, there were respectively Foundation Establishment Pill, Vast Origin Pill, and Meridian-Expanding Pill.

For the Adept realm, there were respectively Condensed Liquid Pill...

Even for the Sage realm, there were three corresponding pill formulas, respectively covering a realm-breaking Nascent Soul formation formula, a formula to increase mana, and a formula that could strengthen the divine soul's origin!

As Luo Chen browsed quickly, his heart could not help surging.

This complete set of pill formulas from Qi Refinement to Sage almost covered the entire human cultivation system, all-encompassing!

If fully mastered, it would certainly be able to cultivate countless human powerhouses.

At the very least, the three kinds of realm-breaking pill formulas Luo Chen had once searched for fruitlessly were all fully recorded upon it.

If the three sealed pill formulas, Stabilizing Spirit, Void Surveying, and Dao Union, were counted, it meant even Void Refinement stage pills were not lacking.

The only thing missing was probably the Ascendant stage...

"No, the Ascendant stage is not actually missing either!"

Luo Chen recalled the final trial of the Pill Realm. He still remembered the formula for the Derivation Pill perfectly clearly.

One could even say that he had a ready-made, complete Derivation Pill in his possession!

Only, after he successfully refined that Derivation Pill and passed out, it had been taken into the internal space of the Primordial Unity Cauldron. Now that he could not use the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he could not retrieve that pill either.

The deeper he studied the pill books and formulas obtained from the Pill Palace, the more Luo Chen admired that Devil Lord Heaven-Refining whom he had never met.

This was an existence who had walked the alchemy path to its extreme!

He had connected the alchemy daos of the human, demon, and devil races, leaving behind a complete pill system for each.

Although Luo Chen had previously refined a fifth-tier Derivation Pill, in the system's evaluation, he was still a fourth-tier alchemist.

This was an extremely rare situation.

It was probably related to the fact that when refining the Derivation Pill, he had fully consumed one hundred Achievement Points.

But Luo Chen was confident that as long as he could fully digest those seventy-two pill books, relying on his theoretical knowledge and solid foundation, he would certainly be able to reach the fifth-tier alchemist level.

The Devil Lord's inheritance benefited him without end!

...

Time slowly passed.

In the blink of an eye, seven years slipped by.

During these seven years, Luo Chen opened the storage magic treasures he had obtained one by one and harvested many resources.

To say nothing else, in spirit stones alone, he obtained no fewer than thirty thousand upper-grade spirit stones!

Thirty thousand might not sound like much.

But if converted to lower-grade spirit stones, that was three hundred million!

Three hundred million spirit stones!

This was an enormous sum Luo Chen had never possessed before!

Wandering Sage cultivators might not necessarily have this much current savings. Only those who dominated a region, opened a mountain, and established a sect might compare.

Apart from this, Luo Chen also took time to refine three additional magic-treasure-grade alchemy tools.

One cauldron, one furnace, and one small kettle.

This was preparation for refining Nascent Soul Pills later.

The Purple Monkey Flowers were about to mature, there were also enough Divine Iris, and the other supplementary materials had long been collected in full.

The date to open the furnace and refine pills could be counted on one's fingers.

But since his lifebound magic treasure could not be used, Luo Chen naturally had to find replacement methods ahead of time.

As for that ancient Nascent Soul Pill formula, Luo Chen knew it by heart, and had even learned several new alchemy arts from the pill books left by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, just in case.

Under these silent preparations, the years were peaceful, and everything seemed to be developing in a good direction.

Until Heavenjade stepped into the abyss.

Standing bowed outside the thatched hut, she said anxiously:

"Master, something strange has happened to the blood pool!"

There was no response. Though Heavenjade was anxious, she could only wait patiently.

After a long while!

The mana shrouding the inside and outside of the thatched hut slowly withdrew.

Along with it came Luo Chen's voice.

"How is Black King?"

Heavenjade let out a breath, then said in a dry voice, "He is waking, but at the same time, his divine soul aura is vanishing."

Inside the thatched hut, Luo Chen, seated upright upon a golden cushion, slowly opened his eyes.

Had this day finally arrived?

He had already done everything he should. Whether Black King, whose Cliff Serpent sleeping method had ended, could make it through would depend on his own fortune.

Whoosh...

A clear breeze blew. When Heavenjade lifted her eyes, Luo Chen, dressed in white with fluttering robes and wide sleeves hanging to his waist, was already beside her.

"Let's go!"

*(End of Chapter)*
