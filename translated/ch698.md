# Chapter 698: The Storm Descends, Luo Chen Reappears

Above the cloud-sea plaza.

The battle of the two Bloods that had stretched across a dozen years gradually entered a white-hot stage.

Blood Nightmare Moluo and Old Daoist Jiyuan joined hands to fight Blood Wanderer. They were originally no match for him, yet with the assistance of that blood sword that was nearly a spirit treasure, they actually began to pull back from disadvantage.

Clang!

Sword light cleaved downward, transforming into countless illusions, and amid layer upon layer of figures, one sword struck the true body.

Blood Wanderer's body was directly split into two halves.

But in an instant, the two halves joined into one again.

Seeing this, Blood Nightmare Moluo's face sank slightly.

"Blood Sea Demon Body!"

"I never imagined that you, an outsider to the sect, not only refined Sage-stage Blood God Children, but even cultivated the undying, indestructible Blood Sea Demon Body!"

Blood Wanderer's face was somewhat pale, and he looked with extreme dread at the blood sword in the other man's hand.

With his peak abilities, protected by four great Blood God Children, even when facing a late-stage Great Cultivator, he had no fear.

But this sword!

Taking a deep breath, he shook his long whip, making a sharp crack in midair.

Blood Nightmare Moluo glanced at it and sneered. "A Blood-Kiss Demon Whip refined with my sect's methods. Though it has the trait of drinking blood and transforming qi, its main material is ultimately too poor, and it was sacrificed and refined for too short a time. It is nothing but an inferior treasure. How could it be a match for this God-Slaying Blood Sword?"

If Angler, Luo Chen, and the others knew his evaluation, they would probably never be able to accept it.

This demon whip had been forcibly refined from three living beings as its main materials.

They were, respectively, the main body of a fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine, the inner core of a fourth-tier Soul-Seizing Ghost Crow, and an archaic beast chiwen containing a faint trace of true dragon bloodline.

It covered three aspects: an intelligent spirit monster, a demon beast, and an archaic beast.

Moreover, it had consumed the Myriad Immortals Assembly's countless manpower and resources, barely being refined into existence.

Yet in Blood Nightmare Moluo's mouth, it was so unbearable.

Blood Wanderer did not refute this.

The other two main materials were not poor in quality, but the archaic beast chiwen contributed by Angler was far too weak.

It was said to have been seized from a false-core cultivator who wanted to join the Myriad Immortals Assembly. How strong could an archaic beast controlled by a false-core cultivator be?

Therefore, during refinement, the three main materials were unable to form a stable structure, thereby creating a gap between its actual might and what he had imagined.

But!

"Even if it is an inferior treasure, defeating you is more than enough!"

Blood Wanderer reached backward and clenched his palm.

In the next moment, the Blood God Child that was fighting Fairy Feileng endured one of her attacks without regard for injury and forcibly flew toward Blood Wanderer.

Seeing this, Blood Nightmare Moluo's expression changed slightly.

"Stop him! He is going to perform God-Devil Fusion!"

The Blood Sea lineage of the Primordial Demon Sect had many powerful methods.

The most famous among them was for a strong expert who had cultivated the Blood Sea Demon Body to fuse with a Blood God Child sacrificed and refined from his own mind and spirit. With the two stacked together, his might would multiply!

In the past, the reason Blood Wanderer could roam the Northern Sea without scruple, and even face late-stage Great Cultivators without fear despite being only a mid-stage Sage, was that at his peak he had fully four Sage-stage Blood God Children protecting him.

One fusion doubled his might. If four Blood God Children fused with him at the same time, the change in his strength would be heaven-shaking!

Now, although Blood Wanderer had lost three Blood God Children, the three people on Blood Nightmare Moluo's side were themselves lower in realm than him, and had gained only a slight advantage by relying on numbers and the pseudo-spirit treasure God-Slaying Blood Sword.

If he were truly allowed to perform God-Devil Fusion...

"It is already too late!"

With a low shout, Blood Wanderer's entire body shook, and rich blood light erupted.

As his hair and beard flew, his savage eyes stared at the enemies closing in from three directions.

The old Daoist of Penglai, the furnace-cauldrons matron, and the remnant spawn of the Primordial Demon Sect!

In that hairbreadth instant.

He smiled savagely and swept out one whip!

Pa!

The void trembled. At this moment, there seemed to be crow cries and the hoarse roars of a dragon's evil offspring.

The blood sword was empty-sharp, and when it cut down, it abruptly severed the Blood-Kiss Demon Whip, but it could not cut apart that strange cry.

In an instant.

Blood Nightmare Moluo suffered a heavy blow, falling onto the plaza with a dull echo.

Boom! Boom!

Blood Wanderer struck two palms in succession, tyrannically blasting away Old Daoist Jiyuan and Fairy Feileng, then lowered his head to look at Blood Nightmare Moluo lying on the plaza.

From the other man's body, many resentful souls and fierce ghosts strangely emerged and began gnawing at him.

Although a great amount of blood light counterattacked, wanting to suppress these resentful souls and fierce ghosts drawn out by the Blood-Kiss Demon Whip, it was clearly so weak.

Seeing this, Blood Wanderer, who had been about to pursue, froze.

"Resentful soul backlash, devil qi reversing..."

He seemed to think of something and burst into loud laughter.

"Haha! I thought the Holy Land's rules were as strict as heavenly law, yet it actually produced such a traitor who tried to unite three lineages and defy the ancestral teachings."

After laughing, his face turned cold.

"I will accept the Blood Sea Ancestor's power!"

Then he swooped down, one palm landing on Blood Nightmare Moluo's head.

A powerful force was forcibly extracted from Blood Nightmare Moluo while he was unable to control it, gathering toward Blood Wanderer's body.

Nearby, Old Daoist Jiyuan and Fairy Feileng looked at this scene with reverence and fear, too timid to advance.

Not far away, the gathered experts watched silently, not daring to disturb him in the slightest.

Suddenly!

Blood Wanderer lifted his head toward the sky.

Among Bright Radiance Heaven's fourteen palaces, a light suddenly dimmed.

Creak...

Amid a muffled sound, the gate of one palace slowly opened.

Afterward, a graceful figure stepped out.

That figure stood high above, like a fairy from the moon palace, teasingly watching the myriad beings below.

First came the old acquaintance Blood Wanderer, then her gaze fixed on Fairy Feileng.

The latter trembled all over and subconsciously lowered her head.

"So my dear little sister came as well..."

Moon Wanderer's teasing voice gradually lowered. Her brows furrowed slightly, and she subconsciously looked toward the Pill Palace closest to the Servant Palace.

There, a power that made one's heart tremble for no clear reason was actually piercing through the suppression of the great hall and slowly spreading outward.

No one else could perceive this power.

Only she, who had likewise accepted Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's complete slave-path inheritance, could sense a little of it.

"Could it be that treasure cauldron the artifact spirit mentioned, stronger than the Myriad Beast Diagram?"

...

Inside the Pill Palace.

Time seemed to have stopped.

Beads of blood floated in the void.

Chunks of broken flesh were evenly scattered.

Pieces of fractured bone lay divided in all directions.

As time flowed, Heavenjade, whose heart had been filled with deathly silence, gradually sensed something wrong.

"Master... seems not to be dead?"

Black King, who had been immersed in grief and release, froze.

"How could that be? The slave mark on my divine soul clearly released itself already."

For cultivators and spirit pets, unless they signed the kind of contract where both lived and died together, when one party fell, the contract restriction on the other would automatically lift.

It was precisely because of this that Heavenjade had earlier concluded Luo Chen had already fallen.

But now!

Heavenjade hesitated, memories surfacing in her mind of the days and nights she had sparred with Luo Chen.

Just now, she had merely lost her sense of her master, but his aura remained long and unbroken, as if that man with the incomparably powerful physique were still breathing beside her ear.

No!

It was not breathing!

It was a heartbeat!

Thump! Thump! Thump!

Amid countless pieces of flesh, a fresh red heart was beating with vigorous strength.

In that scalp-numbing heartbeat, a strange phenomenon abruptly appeared.

Blood beads began to gather, broken flesh was budding, and pieces of bone writhed as they tried to close together.

"Could it be?" Black King's eyes jumped. From his inherited memories, he faintly thought of something.

It was a legend of ancient archaic beasts, undying and indestructible, reborn from a single drop of blood.

Whoosh!

The huge plantain fan spread horizontally from Heavenjade's hand, blocking Black King.

Seeing this, Black King was furious. "Heavenjade, what are you doing?"

Heavenjade turned back coldly. "It is not what I am doing. It is what you were thinking!"

"I... I..."

Black King's voice broke off in fragments, and on his fierce jiaolong face there appeared a human-like expression of shame.

Heavenjade looked at him coldly, her tone growing firmer.

"Master has treated me well, and has treated you even more generously. Imperial Moonlight Nectar, demon beast bones and flesh and inner cores, magic treasures and pills, even helping you awaken your inheritance and rewarding you with the corpse of that Cliff Serpent that failed to advance to Sage."

"Even if the slave marks between us and him have now disappeared, he is still our master!"

"Those petty thoughts of yours, I advise you not to entertain them again."

Black King was both angry and ashamed.

He was angry that his companion looked down on him, ashamed that he truly had, for one instant, entertained the thought of betraying his master.

Just as he was about to explain, his pupils shrank slightly.

Heavenjade also sensed the change.

Behind them, within the great hall, the flesh, blood, and bones were trying to close and reassemble.

But!

It was progressing with extreme difficulty.

Suddenly.

A surging power began to gush forth from the shattered flesh and bones.

It was a blood-colored power.

Although it was a strange blood-red, it contained no evil or arrogant aura, as though it came from the source of life itself.

Because this blood-colored power spread out and gradually enveloped all the flesh and blood.

In an instant, the speed of reassembly abruptly increased!

Not only that.

During this process, a frail golden flame instinctively bloomed, slowly burning every newly born part of the body.

Because of this burning, those newly born chunks of flesh not only did not turn charred, but instead faintly became light and transcendent.

However, the golden flame was ultimately too weak.

As it burned bit by bit, the golden flame grew weaker and weaker, finally revealing a trembling azure fire tree.

When the golden flame was about to be completely extinguished, the azure fire tree moved slightly. Countless branches covered one golden point of light and directly swallowed it.

"Nirvana!"

A familiar voice sounded in the hearts of the two demons, and even boomed through the great hall.

With this command, countless pieces of flesh, blood, and bone abruptly closed into one.

As blood light filled the air, a strong, naked figure gradually appeared within the great hall.

At the same time.

The nearby great cauldron seemed to sense something and began to tremble.

Luo Chen, who had completed the nirvana of the flesh, still sat cross-legged on the ground with his eyes closed, slow to open them.

An extremely suppressed, violent power was stirring restlessly in his body.

Suddenly!

That great cauldron floated up from the ground and shot straight toward the vault above!

Its appearance was as if it wanted to flee far away from Luo Chen, who was currently emitting such a powerful aura.

It was the lingering influence of the battle between Devil Lord Heaven-Refining and Rosycloud!

Luo Chen had used Rosycloud's cultivation method to complete fleshly nirvana before the Heaven-Refining Cauldron. How could the remnant artifact spirit consciousness tolerate this?

Even disregarding the Devil Lord's inheritance, it could not possibly allow Luo Chen to obtain itself.

Only, once this happened, the power sealing that gray light was scattered.

With a rumbling sound.

The Heaven-Refining Cauldron broke out of the palace and rushed toward the sky.

Outside.

Moon Wanderer was the first to move. A white jade disc appeared in her hand and covered the black cauldron.

On the cloud-sea plaza, Feiyunzi's face lit with joy.

"A spirit treasure!"

Without hesitation, he flew toward the Heaven-Refining Cauldron while summoning a net-shaped magic treasure, attempting to seize the black cauldron.

The others, apart from Blood Wanderer who was still devouring Blood Nightmare Moluo's power, also began to move.

However!

"Not right!"

Moon Wanderer's expression changed slightly. In her mind, a warning came from the artifact spirit of the spirit treasure Myriad Beast Diagram.

She subconsciously retreated.

She even abandoned the white jade disc.

The others reacted a little slower.

By then, there was no room left to avoid it.

Only a heaven-shaking explosion was heard!

Boom!

Under everyone's eyes, above Bright Radiance Heaven's fourteen palaces.

The ruined black cauldron exploded with a crash!

The Heaven-Refining Cauldron had once been a heaven-reaching spirit treasure. Even though it was now ruined beyond repair and driven by no one, when it shattered, it still released incomparable might.

The powerful explosion formed an unmatched Yuan qi storm that swept across Bright Radiance Heaven!

"I cannot protect you after all." The black-robed Sage murmured, retreating frantically and no longer paying any attention to the Moya Cave Master.

He let the latter be annihilated into powder within the storm.

Cape Heaven Matron's expression panicked. Not only did she summon a great turtle-shell shield, she even manifested her true body, forcibly resisting that terrifying storm.

Feiyunzi had rushed the fastest and suffered the greatest impact. His net magic treasure not only shattered instantly, but also carried countless fragments flying toward him.

His eyes were incomparably wild as he opened his mouth and spat out a round pearl to envelop himself.

But that pearl also broke inch by inch within the storm.

"This is a treasure I obtained with great difficulty from the Artifact Realm, comparable to a spirit treasure!"

Amid endless heartache, he constantly released treasures he had accumulated over the years, trying to resist this terrifying storm.

On the plaza.

Blood Wanderer frowned and had no choice but to forcibly interrupt his action.

Beneath him, Blood Nightmare Moluo, who had gained a moment to breathe, gripped the God-Slaying Blood Sword in one hand.

"Get lost!"

Afterward, he released all the remaining Blood Sea Ancestor power.

The other two also each acted.

Fairy Feileng unfurled a painting, an image depicting a woman's profile.

Old Daoist Jiyuan summoned the heavy treasure he had taken from his sect for this journey to defend himself.

In short, before the terrifying storm formed by the spirit treasure's explosion, everyone exhausted all their strength to preserve themselves.

And at the very center of the explosion, in the Pill Palace beneath the black cauldron.

Grand formations activated on their own, layer after layer of spiritual light covering the palace.

But under the sweep of the storm, these palace-protecting formations shattered one layer after another.

Amid the endless tide, Heavenjade exhausted all her strength to wave the Nine Winds Plantain Fan, wanting to create a safe space for Luo Chen.

But beneath that vast might, such an action seemed futile.

It was at this moment.

A black shadow moved behind her.

"Black King, what are you doing!" Heavenjade's eyes nearly split as she watched Black King rush toward Luo Chen.

Ignoring her obstruction, the other party opened his bloody maw and swallowed Luo Chen's hard-reassembled body in one gulp, then swept Heavenjade into the corner by a palace beam with a swing of his tail.

Afterward, Black King glared furiously up at the sky and released an angry roar.

Only, the dragon roar that once could shake heaven and earth was rendered soundless beneath that rumbling storm.

After a long time.

The storm gradually died out.

Of Bright Radiance Heaven's fourteen palaces, only thirteen remained hanging high in the void like stars.

The Pill Palace that had previously emitted bright radiance was now nothing but broken walls and ruins. Tall purple jade beams had collapsed, and precious tiles floated like fine sand above the boundless cloud sea, falling with a rustle whenever the breeze blew.

Crunch!

Footsteps sounded.

In a corner of the broken palace, Heavenjade, in her true form, lay there. Her disheveled azure feathers were covered in terrifying fresh blood.

Incomparably weak, she forced her eyes open, and a figure was reflected in her pupils.

"Mas... master?"

"Mm."

With a soft response, Luo Chen took the Xuanyan Armor from her hand and put it on, then turned to look at the pitiful black jiaolong curled on the ground.

The enormous body that had once been nearly a hundred zhang long was now reduced to less than one-tenth.

A demon core within the faintly visible holes of its flesh emitted a dim glow.

"You have worked hard."

Luo Chen formed a spiritual seal and stored the heavily injured Heavenjade and the Black King of uncertain life and death into spirit beast bags.

Afterward, his calm eyes looked toward the sky.

There was a gray light quietly wandering within the clouds.

There was also a staggering figure charging upstream toward that gray light.

"Hmph!"

With a cold snort, Luo Chen stomped on the palace floor.

In an instant, an explosive sound rang out!

The Pill Palace, already on the verge of collapse, crumbled completely under this stomp.

But Luo Chen's figure arrived after yet before the other, crossing several hundred zhang to stand before the gray light sealed by the Heaven-Refining Cauldron's artifact spirit.

He turned and punched!

Mountain-Breaking Form!

With a single punch, the newcomer flew back at an even greater speed.

Luo Chen's eyes were cold and sharp as he quietly stared at the gray light before him.

"This belongs to me!"

*(End of Chapter)*
