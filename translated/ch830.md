# Chapter 830: Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror, Grand Five Elements Sword Formation

A green true-artifact sword pellet and three flawless sword pellets floated in midair.

Their surfaces bore intricate patterns, like countless silk threads wrapped into spheres.

Luo Chen swept his gaze across them one by one, then infused his mana into the fire-attributed flawless sword pellet.

Instantly!

*Chi chi chi...*

The sword pellet spun rapidly, darting freely through the great hall like a shooting star.

With a thought, the flying star halted. Like a lotus blooming, it spat out strands of sword-thread.

He tried controlling it using the sword-pellet manipulation methods from the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* and was very satisfied.

Indeed, these were flawless sword pellets that had never been used before!

At this level, they were likely extremely rare even within the Jade Cauldron Sword Sect and the Ten Thousand Swords Pavilion—only those seeds with hope of advancing to Sage would be granted such treasures.

And he had received three at once, plus one at the true-artifact level!

Fondling the four sword pellets in his hand, the corners of Luo Chen's mouth curled up slightly.

"As I thought, my feeling at the Shouyang Dao Discussion was correct."

"The prices I used to charge for refining pills seemed high to me, but to Sages with generous backgrounds, they were actually nothing."

"Especially now that I've built up my reputation as an Alchemy Saint over many years—that's more than enough to support me asking even higher prices!"

This lion's opening of his mouth during today's bargaining was a verification of the gold content of his reputation.

The final result was satisfying!

As for why he targeted sword pellets specifically instead of other things?

First, because these two great sword sects definitely wouldn't lack them—he had aimed with purpose.

Second... Luo Chen's gaze fell on the blood-red small sword tattoo-like mark on his right arm.

The Yuantu Sword, after absorbing a massive amount of demon blood and a fourth-rank Demon Emperor's Sage during the Spirit Severance Battle, had revealed its "demon sword" essence!

Beneath its unparalleled sharpness lay an irrepressible bloodthirsty instinct!

Although the sword spirit's recognition of him was growing deeper, making it easier to control, the sword spirit's own autonomy had also greatly increased.

Thinking back to how the Yuantu Sword had stubbornly devoured the Demon Emperor's Sage, and how it had proactively emitted a trace of sharpness when his sword qi couldn't prevail in their contest, Luo Chen's joyful expression darkened.

If it wasn't completely controlled, then it wasn't controlled at all!

And an uncontrolled magic treasure, no matter how powerful, was not a cultivator's most satisfactory weapon.

It was with this consideration that Luo Chen had conceived the idea of rebuilding a sword formation.

The improved Grand Five Elements Sword Formation left by Han Zhan, according to its description, could be called supremely powerful.

Its only requirement was five true-artifact-level sword pellets!

This was very difficult to gather, but now Luo Chen had two true-artifact sword pellets plus three flawless ones—perhaps he could try to barely set up the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation.

"No rush, no rush!"

"First, use the methods from the sword canon to properly refine these sword pellets, then consider the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation."

"For now, the urgent task is still to refine the Heart-Inquiring Mirror. I can't delay Canglong any longer."

Luo Chen put away the sword pellets, ordered people to repair the exposed ceiling of the great hall, then went up the mountain alone.

...

The Red Dog Puppet, which held Luo Chen's divided soul, had figured out the refining principles of the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror after studying and dismantling a large number of illusion-core magic treasures.

Moreover, it had attempted to conceive a new refining blueprint.

Using Seven-Poison Jade as the base, illusion cores as the main material, True Essence Liquid as the mediating substance to activate and regulate, supplemented by a large number of precious materials and various illusion formations...

After system verification, this blueprint was feasible!

After years of preparation, Luo Chen had finally reached the last step.

Before the red-hot Primordial Unity Cauldron, he carefully added each separately processed material one by one, his movements so gentle they were rarely seen.

It wasn't that he was overly cautious.

It was that the materials used in refining this Heart-Inquiring Mirror surpassed any previous refining he had done.

The illusion core of a fourth-rank mid-stage illusion beast alone—neither the Mystic Dust Armor nor the Nether Python Whip could compare.

If he failed carelessly, he would have nowhere to cry.

"I don't ask for a high-grade treasure—I only pray not to fail."

Luo Chen muttered to himself, pouring all his focus into the refining process.

His refining arts had been acquired from rather mixed sources.

Duan Feng had laid the foundation with the Duan family's refining arts, which actually originated from Tianfan City.

Later, he collected a large number of refining manuals, exploring and experimenting as he went.

The refining arts of Beihai Baizao Mountain had been his first breakthrough in the dao of refining.

Later, after obtaining Feiyunzi's legacy, the large number of Feiyun Ravine refining methods left behind had given Luo Chen an even higher perspective on the dao of refining.

The Nether Python Whip he had refined for Gu Caiyi earlier was one experiment.

That experiment should have yielded better results, but unfortunately, he was distracted at a critical moment, causing the effort to fall short and the treasure to be of mid-grade.

Now, in refining the Heart-Inquiring Mirror, Luo Chen absolutely would not allow himself to be distracted.

The Black Ape Puppet, holding a formation plate, had completely sealed off Danluo Mountain. Unless absolutely necessary, no outsider would be allowed to disturb Luo Chen.

Time passed bit by bit, and various materials began to gradually fuse, producing subtle reactions between each other.

One day, two days...

One month, two months...

A year later, Luo Chen, his eyes bloodshot, let out a low shout.

"Come out!"

A ball of white hazy light slowly flew out of the Primordial Unity Cauldron. Luo Chen's amber-golden eyes, through the protective treasure light, examined it carefully.

"It has indeed taken shape!"

"Now to inscribe the formations."

He took a deep breath, took out the prepared Ink Formation Liquid, and his right hand, under the urging of his mana, instantly turned bright red.

For inscribing formations on magic treasure embryos, Luo Chen had his own unique system.

That was the Iron-Refining Hand of Baizao Mountain!

Using this to dip into Ink Formation Liquid and draw formations was far more precise than common tools like formation brushes or ink awls.

After all, those were external objects—how could they compare to one's own hand for freedom and intuitive feel?

The only drawback of the Iron-Refining Hand was probably the extreme pain during its use!

That was why few cultivators from Baizao Mountain practiced this refining art.

Pain would cause distraction, and once distracted at a critical moment in refining, the consequences...

But this mere pain wasn't enough to make Luo Chen even frown or grit his teeth.

"First, draw nine basic illusion formations, then six second-rank illusion formations. For the third-rank illusion formation, I'll use my specialty—Mirror Flower Water Moon. This is also the formation most compatible with the Heart-Inquiring Mirror!"

"For the fourth-rank formation... I'll use Dream Bubble!"

When he made this decision, Luo Chen's emotions were complex—part worry, part anticipation, and subtly, more of a gamble.

This illusion formation wasn't one he had before, but one recorded in the formation manual *Immortal Secrets of the Stone Chamber* that Fu Chaosheng had given him.

Though complete, it wasn't complete!

The reason it was incomplete was that this illusion formation was originally of a higher rank, and likely originated from the Buddhist sect of the Western Desert. For some unknown reason, most of it had been destroyed.

But that senior from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, after obtaining this incomplete formation, had supplemented it based on his own understanding.

In the end, although its rank had dropped significantly to only fourth-rank, the formation had become completely intact.

By constructing this formation on the Heart-Inquiring Mirror, Luo Chen undoubtedly held high expectations for this magic treasure.

"I hope it won't disappoint me!"

After completing the perfect final stroke, a dreamlike, colorful light wrapped around the formed oval stone mirror as it slowly flew back into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

The mana lid covered it again.

The faint Withering-Bloom True Flame surged once more.

Luo Chen let out a long, relieved breath.

"I'll leave the rest to you."

He glanced at the Red Dog Puppet, didn't say much, and left on his own.

For a divided soul, fire-control arts were also engraved in the soul. Even when controlling through a puppet, there wouldn't be deviation.

Moreover, now that the Withering-Bloom True Flame had given birth to a fire spirit that understood human nature and knew Luo Chen's intentions.

Thus, Luo Chen didn't need to keep vigil for three years like when he refined the Mystic Dust Armor.

Returning to his room, Luo Chen slept a wonderful, deep sleep.

After waking up refreshed, he immediately removed the formation and summoned Gu Caiyi, Wang Yuan, and the Heartless Fairy.

...

In ordinary times, a year was nothing to cultivators.

But now the situation was different—changing with each passing moment. Who knew what changes had occurred in the outside world over the past year?

Therefore, Luo Chen urgently needed these cultivators who were active outside to report the situation to him.

"Due to the formation, outsiders knew the Alchemy Saint had no intention of receiving guests, so few came to disturb you last year. Only two invitations from Shouyang Hermit arrived." The Heartless Fairy said this.

Luo Chen asked for the invitations, glanced over them briefly, and set them aside.

It was still an invitation to the Shouyang Dao Discussion, but this time the scale was limited to fewer than ten people.

Luo Chen gave a derisive laugh. Though the scale was small, it was too frequent—wasn't this cheapening the prestige of the Shouyang Dao Discussion?

If he were to organize it, during wartime he would count in years, and during peacetime in decades or centuries—only then could high prestige be maintained.

Shouyang Hermit was being too eager for quick success!

As he mused inwardly, Gu Caiyi also reported her situation.

"Thanks to my husband's reputation, the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion has developed well over the years, with a large number of repeat customers."

"The sect can stably produce pills and talismans for external sale..."

"But these are only the smallest portion of our income. The main source still comes from the cooperation with the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance on the demon beast corpse purchasing business. After each large batch transaction, we can collect considerable commissions."

"The current problem is that the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance is no longer satisfied with cooperation limited to demon beast resources. They want to expand the scope and sell some Central Continent specialties through the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion. Lord Li didn't dare decide and left it for my husband to determine."

Central Continent specialties?

What specialties could there be, other than various resources needed by cultivators?

Perhaps their quality was finer, but these businesses were all handled by the Rear Sixteen Domains and the Nether Origin Sect. How would Luo Chen dare to touch them?

After a moment of deliberation, Luo Chen had already made a decision.

"We can broaden the scope of cooperation, but only limited to the pill category."

Gu Caiyi nodded and noted this down.

Luo Chen thought it through clearly. With his Alchemy Saint reputation backing him, even pills from Central Continent would have no one questioning their origin. Other forces wouldn't bother to contend with him, an Alchemy Saint at the peak of his power.

But if it were other resources, it would inevitably invite great trouble.

Luo Chen didn't want trouble now!

Finally, it was Wang Yuan's turn.

He was in charge of the war!

Under everyone's gaze, Wang Yuan slowly spoke.

"The war is now intensifying. The four great commanders are eyeing the Lingtian Pass, harassing it daily without rest."

Another one had joined?

Frowning, Luo Chen asked, "Which four commanders?"

"Huang Man, Baizu, Niugui, and Aokuang!" Wang Yuan enunciated each name that would make ordinary people shudder.

Even though he had expected it, Luo Chen couldn't help swallowing.

"Aokuang came this quickly?"

This great Demon Emperor was no unknown figure.

He was the wolf clan's supreme ruler in the Million Mountains. The former Aoxiao Demon Emperor of the Xiaoyue Mountain Range was merely his junior.

It was said that this man had advanced to the Demon Emperor realm over a thousand years ago, his age comparable to some newly advanced human Ascendants.

His combat prowess was also extremely terrifying.

He had once contended with the current demon clan's supreme ruler, Ancient Demon Emperor Ditian, and though defeated, had preserved his life.

The Azure Frost Demon Emperor, most feared by human cultivators, had also fought Aokuang once—the two battled for hundreds of rounds without determining a victor.

With these two battle records alone, no one in the world dared underestimate him.

And now, he had come so quickly?

Wang Yuan's expression was grave. "It's precisely because of this great Demon Emperor's arrival that the demon clan has gained great confidence and begun frequently harassing the Lingtian Pass. They don't forcefully attack the pass, but gradually encroach on the outer buffer zones, attempting to form a pincer. In successive battles, heavy casualties have occurred. I've heard that even among the Sages, there have been losses."

Luo Chen immediately asked, "Do you know which specific Sages?"

Wang Yuan thought for a moment and said, "Most are rumors, their truth unknown. But the matter of Old Ghost Motian having half his body severed by a wolf clan Demon Emperor happened before everyone's eyes—that can't be faked!"

Old Ghost Motian?

Luo Chen was startled at first, then revealed a delighted smile.

Bound by the human side's rules, the Five Elements Divine Sect didn't dare make a move against him, and he didn't dare strike at old ghosts like Motian.

Now, borrowing the demon clan's blade to severely wound Old Ghost Motian—how could he not feel joy?

"It must be related to my forcibly destroying his true artifact. Without that powerful method, he was severely injured."

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted.

Wang Yuan reminded him, "Now that you've emerged from seclusion, even with your Alchemy Saint reputation, you can't continue to stand aloof. Perhaps the next battle between Demon Emperors and Sages will fall to you."

Luo Chen's expression turned serious, and he nodded slightly.

"Don't worry about me. You all should also be careful, especially you, Wang Yuan."

The war on the Luo Tian Sect's side was all Wang Yuan's responsibility. Once fighting started, he was the one who charged into battle, with danger following him like a shadow.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen's mind stirred. He opened his mouth and spat out.

A wisp of a green-white small tree floated out. Amidst the confusion of the few people, a green branch snapped off, causing the small tree to sway as if very displeased.

Luo Chen waved his hand, and the green branch flew before Wang Yuan.

"This is my natal true flame—green represents flourishing, pale white represents withering. With this green branch, at times of danger, it can greatly unlock your physical potential and unleash greater power."

"But remember, this method must not be used lightly. After using it, you must return to a safe place. Otherwise, once you fall into weakness, you will be completely powerless."

He spoke gravely, but everyone knew this was a life-saving trump card.

Wang Yuan didn't refuse. Carefully wrapping the green branch in his astral qi, he swallowed it into his abdomen.

After the three left, Luo Chen fell into deep thought.

Given the current situation, he could no longer stand aloof. Encountering battle was inevitable.

He had already tested the power of the Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi on Lin Qingxuan.

At the minor achievement level, it was only at the Adept level.

Even with the Yuantu Sword's enhancement surpassing Adept-level power, it couldn't truly threaten Sage-level cultivators.

Moreover, this sword qi was consumable—using it reduced it, requiring constant refinement to replenish. For this reason alone, it couldn't be used as a conventional combat method.

The Yuantu Sword itself could be activated, but the sword spirit had its own ideas...

Therefore, he needed relatively conventional combat methods.

"It's time to cultivate the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation!"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen waved his hand and scattered four streams of light.

Then, a wisp of white-gold light rose up.

Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth—the five elements.

Two true artifacts and three flawless—though still not meeting the basic threshold for setting up the sword formation, with the system, perhaps he could try to forcibly enter the gate.

Luo Chen didn't seek the powerful might Han Zhan had envisioned, using this sword formation to threaten great cultivators. He only sought that once the sword formation was set up, it could briefly contend with ordinary Sage-level experts.

"It shouldn't be too difficult."

Luo Chen muttered, and immediately his mana surged out, completely enveloping the five sword pellets.
