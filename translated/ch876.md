# Chapter 876: Ten Months in the Womb, Born to Bear Destiny (Thanks to the Alliance Leader of Qijian Fengkuang Dian for the Alliance Leader Reward!)

The Alchemy Sect has an heir!

The news that Luo Chen, the strongest being of the Luo Tian Sect and its Supreme Elder, had a successor, had somehow spread throughout the entire sect.

For a time, disciples rushed to tell one another, filled with joy and celebration. Even some nearby Adept-level powers got wind of it and specially sent precious gifts.

In truth, this kind of reaction was strange within the cultivation world. Cultivators often valued living on wind and dew, cultivating in quietude and non-action. They would sever mortal ties whenever possible. A mere blood descendant was hardly worth such a grand commotion.

But upon deeper examination, it made perfect sense.

Sects and cultivation clans were both organizational frameworks formed by people, not much different from the dynasties and empires of the mortal world—only with higher martial power.

For mortal dynasties, no matter how vigorous and capable the ruler, their dependents would eagerly hope for an heir.

Cultivation sects were relatively less urgent about this.

But the Luo Tian Sect was different!

This newly established Sage-level supreme sect had been influenced far too heavily by Luo Chen. It could be said that without Luo Chen, there would be no Luo Tian Sect.

In particular, many knew that Luo Chen would leave the Luo Tian Sect to join the Nether Origin Sect and take up residence in the Pill Saint Hall.

Even though he had left behind a host of strong experts, a vast territory, and a deep and profound heritage, he would still leave in the end.

And once he left, would Luo Chen ever return?

Perhaps yes, perhaps no.

Moreover, once he left, how would the power structure of the Luo Tian Sect be redistributed?

Would it be under the command of his two wives? Or under Sect Leader Li Yingzhang? Or perhaps under the Sage-level elder Lingfengzi and Wang Yuan, who was comparable to a Sage?

No one dared to ask Luo Chen these questions, and Luo Chen had never voluntarily discussed them with anyone.

But now, with Sima Huiniang's pregnancy, all these questions seemed to have a single answer!

The Luo Tian Sect's Supreme Elder had an heir!

His father was a top-tier expert renowned throughout the Eastern Wilderness—forming his core at a hundred, forming his Nascent Soul at two hundred, with exceptional talent.

His mother, though not of high cultivation, was the founding sect leader of the Luo Tian Sect, who had led the sect from weakness to strength. Whether in intelligence or administrative skill, she had once earned the trust of everyone in the Luo Tian Sect.

The offspring of such a union, whether male or female, would undoubtedly be outstanding!

Even if, worst case, the child inherited only a fraction of either parent's strengths, with the father's support, their future achievements would certainly not be low.

As long as this bloodline of the Alchemy Sect remained in the Luo Tian Sect, Luo Chen could not possibly ignore the sect after joining the Nether Origin Sect.

The internal power structure of the Luo Tian Sect would also have its anchor.

Whether it was the First Wife or the Second Wife, whether it was Sect Leader Li or the Sage-level elder, they would all merely be transitional figures.

Ultimate power would be passed to that child of destiny.

The future of the Luo Tian Sect would no longer be chaotic and uncertain, but would have a clear direction.

Thus, the Luo Tian Sect cultivators who learned of this news were overjoyed.

They even felt that this child, appearing just as the Luo Tian Sect had established its mountain gate at Motian Cliff, must have been born to bear destiny—a chosen one of heaven!

The only pity was that their attempts to visit Sima Huiniang were refused, as she needed to rest quietly in the Motian Cliff to nurture the pregnancy.

But regardless, whenever Luo Tian cultivators passed by Motian Cliff, their eyes were filled with confidence when they looked toward it.

The Supreme Elder has an heir; the Luo Tian Sect has hope!

But at this moment, atop Motian Cliff, there was no such celebration. Instead, the atmosphere was grim and chilling.

Yao Mingyue knelt on the ground, head bowed, not daring to breathe loudly.

Before her, the young man stood with his hands clasped behind his back, silently gazing into the distance.

A bird flew toward the cliff's peak, but before it could approach, it fell from the sky without reason.

Luo Chen watched the falling bird and spoke calmly.

"Was it you who spread the news of your master's pregnancy?"

Faced with this calm inquiry, Yao Mingyue instinctively wanted to lie.

She could say that the physician who had taken Sima Huiniang's pulse had carelessly let it slip.

She could say that Sima Huiniang had revealed it herself.

She could even claim complete ignorance.

But in Luo Chen's calm tone, she could not bring herself to deny it.

"It was me."

"Without my permission, where did you get the audacity?"

"Disciple felt... that the fellow disciples had been restless lately, as if they had lost their anchor because of your impending departure. So I used this news to lift their spirits."

"Oh?!"

At the cold snort, Yao Mingyue's heart trembled, as if a thunderous wrath was about to descend upon her.

She pressed her head directly into the dirt and said fearfully, "Disciple was worried that you..."

"Enough!"

Luo Chen cut her off, because once certain words were spoken, they would tear the veil of civility. He could not remain unmoved.

Keeping them bottled up inside, once the dust settled, they would become irrelevant.

Yao Mingyue had, after all, rendered meritorious service to the sect.

Luo Chen looked at a figure rushing up the mountain in haste and said flatly, "Though your foundation is solid, you have only formed your core for a few years, and your realm is not yet stable. A hundred li from here, there is a secluded little mountain with abundant spiritual energy. Go there and go into seclusion for ten years to stabilize your Adept realm!"

With that, Luo Chen turned and descended the mountain.

As he passed Yao Mingyue, he shook his head.

"Sometimes, good intentions can lead to bad outcomes."

With those words, he flicked his sleeve and left.

Yao Mingyue knelt there respectfully. Only after a long time did she slowly raise her head.

Her face was pale, sweat beading at her temples.

She stood up, watching Luo Chen's receding figure, biting her lip, her expression complicated.

She understood that the Supreme Elder had raised the matter high and set it down lightly, letting her off.

She also knew that her words carried little weight and that she should not meddle in the Supreme Elder's family affairs. But her master's kindness to her was as heavy as a mountain—how could she stand by and watch her master suffer in the future?

"Mingyue has done her best. I only pray that the greater good of the sect will make the Supreme Elder change his mind!"

Yao Mingyue sighed and walked dejectedly toward that secluded, solitary place a hundred li away.

...

"She really got pregnant?"

Gu Caiyi, who had rushed back in a hurry, asked urgently of Luo Chen, who had just arrived at the bedchamber.

"Yes, two months along," Luo Chen replied with a smile.

Gu Caiyi found it somewhat unbelievable.

She knew that Sima Huiniang had always wanted a child.

She understood too. After all, the other woman had stopped cultivating in recent years, handed over the sect's major affairs to Li Yingzhang and others, and since her realm hadn't reached Sage, she couldn't help Luo Chen much. So Sima Huiniang had grown restless.

If she had a child, it would divert her attention.

But Luo Chen was a Sage, and Sima Huiniang was at the Adept stage. In the eyes of ordinary people, both were considered high-level cultivators.

How could she simply get pregnant just like that?

As if sensing her confusion, Luo Chen smiled. "Are you underestimating your husband? Don't forget, in the Qi Refinement stage, what did your husband rely on to rise?"

Gu Caiyi was stunned, instinctively recalling the past.

[All-Delight Pill]

It had the effect of consolidating essence and locking yang, preventing the leakage of vital energy, and was specially used by low-level cultivators without grand ambitions for indulgence and pleasure.

Her husband was a Grandmaster of the Alchemy Dao. Since he understood this principle, by reversing its application, how difficult could it be to conceive a child and nurture a blood descendant?

Moreover, judging by her experience from the old days at the Celestial Fragrance Pavilion, her husband's abilities in that regard were the best of the best. She alone often found it hard to cope, which was why she occasionally agreed to his requests for a threesome.

The reason she and Sima Huiniang hadn't conceived before was that her husband had deliberately held back, to avoid having one or two encumbrances during turbulent times.

Now that the situation had stabilized, and her husband's cultivation was profound and his strength great, he was more than capable of protecting everything.

Having offspring seemed only natural.

Even after understanding all this, Gu Caiyi still felt it was sudden.

"But..."

"But what? Do you think your husband is being partial? If you want a child, I certainly won't be stingy."

Gu Caiyi's cheeks flushed slightly, and she shot Luo Chen a glare.

"Adept cultivators conceiving offspring damages their cultivation. I still want to form my Nascent Soul in the future. I won't talk to you anymore—I'm going to see her!"

Gu Caiyi waved her hand and headed toward Sima Huiniang's bedchamber.

At the door, she hesitated briefly before entering.

After a while, she came out with a puzzled expression.

"Didn't you say she's only two months along? Why is she so tired and sleepy? Does pregnancy have such a big effect on an Adept cultivator?"

Luo Chen stood at the door and explained with a smile, "For a normal cultivator, pregnancy wouldn't have such a big effect. But you know Huiniang's condition from before. So, to avoid leaving any hidden problems after childbirth, I made some special arrangements to nourish the pregnancy and calm the spirit."

Gu Caiyi suddenly understood. She had forgotten about that.

She had witnessed Sima Huiniang's state in the early years—she had nearly died.

Now that she was pregnant, it was necessary to take good care of her.

"Forget it, she's already asleep. I'll come see her when she wakes up! There are still some matters in the sect that Li Yingzhang can't handle alone. I need to go help, so they don't say the Supreme Elder's family is aloof and doesn't manage affairs."

Gu Caiyi came in a hurry and left in a hurry.

She left quite freely, but there was still some jealousy in her eyes.

She had come first, yet every step afterward, Sima Huiniang had walked ahead of her.

She was the first to help Luo Chen manage the Luo Tian Sect, the first to reach Initiate, the first to form her core, the first to confirm her relationship with Luo Chen, and now even the first to have a child.

It was as if one step slow meant every step was slow.

But Gu Caiyi also understood her own advantage—she could cultivate!

If love is to last, why must it be day and night? As long as she could achieve the Sage realm and gain a surge in lifespan, she could naturally be with her husband forever.

Gu Caiyi left.

Luo Chen did not see her off but stepped into Sima Huiniang's bedchamber.

He walked step by step to the bedside, looking at the woman sleeping soundly with a smile on her face, his own eyes filled with warmth.

"As long as you get through the next half year or so, you can cultivate normally again."

"As for this child..."

The smile in Luo Chen's eyes gradually faded, and he formed a hand seal.

With Sima Huiniang at the center, streaks of formation light gradually lit up in the bedchamber, eventually converging on her still-flat lower abdomen.

Strands of eerie patterns gradually took shape, like a flower about to bloom.

The Pregnancy Spirit Flower Formation!

This was the solution that Sage Heavenly Mountain had taught him.

Using the method of bloodline guidance, the Devil Heart Gu would be directed into the fetal embryo, step by step severing the connection between the gu insect and Sima Huiniang's soul.

A third-tier Devil Heart Gu had already developed instinctive consciousness. Logically, it would resist the severing of this connection.

But blood is thicker than water, and mother and child share one heart. The resistance would not be so great.

With the guidance of the Pregnancy Spirit Flower Formation, the Devil Heart Gu would eventually fully parasitize the child.

When the spirit flower bloomed and the fruit ripened, Sima Huiniang would be completely free.

As for the child parasitized by the Devil Heart Gu?

Luo Chen shook his head, waved his hand to disperse the formation, took out a third-tier soul-nourishing pill, had Sima Huiniang take it, and then went outside.

He did not go far from the bedchamber but continued his previous work in the main hall.

He had mostly finished organizing the Luo Tian Sect's inherited techniques.

What he was doing now was sorting out miscellaneous books on the knowledge of the cultivation world and various cultivation arts.

The future disciples of the Luo Tian Sect would always need these things.

Luo Chen even planned to write a travelogue about the Northern Sea and Central Continent based on his own experiences.

He took out a stack of books from his storage ring.

Most of them he had read before and knew well.

But when his fingers touched a yellowed beast-skin book, he paused.

"The Netherpassage Collection?"

Luo Chen was startled. This was a chess manual, a gift from Fu Qinglan when he left Central Continent.

In all these years, it seemed he had never opened it?

Compared to the "Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon" and the formation book "Immortal Secrets of the Stone Chamber" gifted by the Fu family, he truly had no interest in a chess manual.

"I'll be staying here to watch over Huiniang for a while. Since I have free time, I might as well take a look!"

Luo Chen smiled and opened the first page of the "Netherpassage Collection."

[The grades of Go are nine. First: Entering Spirit; Second: Sitting in Illumination; Third: Concrete Form; Fourth: Netherpassage; Fifth: Using Wisdom; Sixth: Small Skill; Seventh: Striving for Strength; Eighth: Like a Fool; Ninth: Guarding Simplicity...]

Time flowed slowly.

The days of waiting always seemed the hardest to endure.

But Luo Chen was not flustered. He lived alone on Motian Cliff, unmoved by the outside world.

Even when the Nether Origin Sect sent the City Lord of Lingtian in person to ask Luo Chen when he would depart, he told the truth, saying that he still needed some time before he could go to the Nether Origin Sacred Land.

The City Lord of Lingtian inquired in detail and did not make things difficult.

The situation was clear. The Luo Tian Sect was recovering from a period of ruin and truly needed Luo Chen to stay for a while.

Moreover, his Dao partner was pregnant. There was no reason to force Luo Chen, a soon-to-be father, to travel far.

To express congratulations, the City Lord of Lingtian even sent a valuable gift.

This was an unexpected bonus.

Three months later.

Sima Huiniang's belly had fully swelled.

Though she grew increasingly drowsy, she would occasionally get up and walk around.

When she encountered Luo Chen sitting in the hall studying the chess manual, she would chat with him.

"Husband, why have you been so obsessed with this chess manual lately?"

"Just passing the time."

"These nine dead-end games—can you solve them?"

"I want to, but I haven't even entered the door yet. It's not the time."

"I heard the Nine Spirits Sect's expansion war is going poorly?"

"Did Caiyi tell you that? It is indeed going poorly. No one expected the two Demon Emperors under the Ox-Ghost Commander to return. Nine Spirits Primogenitor is quite troubled lately."

"Has anything major happened in the sect lately? I've seen quite a few streaks of light coming and going."

"Li Yingzhang plans to recruit a batch of youths with clean backgrounds to carry on the Luo Tian Sect."

"Recruiting disciples? That's good. In the future, our child will have a group of senior brothers and sisters."

"Huiniang thinks quite far ahead. Go rest now—you've been tired enough today."

"Husband, have you thought about what our child should be named?"

...

Ten months later.

On Motian Cliff, formation light rose from the ground.
