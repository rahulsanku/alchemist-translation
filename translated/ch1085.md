# Chapter 1085: Between Leaving and Staying, Mayflies Meet

A year later.

Within the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

A golden-robed man knelt on the ground, his expression incomparably reverent.

His ornate crown lay upon the floor, as if his pride, too, had been placed upon the ground.

The great hall was utterly silent, so quiet that one could hear a pin drop.

After a long while, a crisp sound of footsteps came.

The golden-robed man did not raise his head, did not speak. He simply knelt there in stillness, until a light cough came from above.

"Emperor Slaughter, have you thought it through?"

Emperor Slaughter replied without hesitation, "Master, I have thought it through!"

"Cough, cough. Perhaps you do not understand the gravity of the matter. Although your cultivation level has greatly increased now, the true meaning of the laws you have grasped was ultimately not comprehended by yourself, but bestowed upon you through initiation by your father before his fall. This kind of true meaning of the law will not only fail to help you break through to the Ascendant stage, but will also greatly restrict you. If you continue to follow me, after I become an Ascendant, I may be able to devise a way to resolve this hidden danger for you."

These words came from the heart, with no hint of deception.

This had always been the consensus of the cultivation world!

For a Sage to comprehend the true meaning of laws in advance brought immense benefits to increasing strength and breaking through to the Ascendant stage.

But the prerequisite was that the true meaning of the law was comprehended by oneself, not obtained from an outsider.

Therefore, when facing secret realms left by Ascendant great powers, Sages were instead somewhat hesitant to venture deep inside, afraid that the true meaning of laws left by the great power would affect them.

On the other hand, secret realms left by Void Refining True Lords of a higher realm did not affect Sages too much, because Void Refining True Lords could already restrain their own power very well.

However, this consensus was often broken by some.

Such as Thunder Daozi back then, and Emperor Slaughter now.

The reason they could break it was that Thunder Daozi was himself a descendant of the great power Bai Ye's lineage, practicing identical techniques. Emperor Slaughter himself was a blood descendant of the ancient demon emperor Di Tian, and under the initiation, there were no obstacles.

Therefore, whether it was Thunder Daozi or Emperor Slaughter, after accepting the true meaning of the law, their strength skyrocketed.

But even so, their future attempts to break through to the Ascendant stage would be far, far more difficult than others—one could even say they could see the end at a glance.

Emperor Slaughter naturally understood the good and bad, and thus fell into hesitation.

On the wide seat above, Luo Chen, dressed in a white robe, looked weary. He did not urge him, letting him think.

After a long time, Emperor Slaughter heavily pressed his head against the floor.

"I beg the master to grant me freedom!"

So resolute, with no room for turning back.

Luo Chen sighed. "Forget it. Since you insist, and you have indeed completed the tasks I assigned, granting you freedom should be a matter of course. However..."

The white-robed Daoist changed his tone, causing Emperor Slaughter's heart to tighten.

Could the Pill Sect be going back on their word?

"Before granting you freedom, you must agree to one condition!"

Emperor Slaughter was slightly nervous, quietly waiting for Luo Chen's condition.

"I am aware that among the countless demon tribes in the Eastern Wilderness's Million Mountains, the Tiger, Wolf, and Feather tribes are the most powerful. The Feather tribe represented by Green Parasol Mountain has already reached a ceasefire negotiation with our Eastern Wilderness human race, and the next thousand years will be peaceful. On the Wolf tribe's side, as long as Aoxiao does not achieve the Ascendant stage, they pose no threat to us. But your Tiger tribe..."

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly.

The Tiger tribe, when disregarding Green Parasol Mountain, had once dominated the Million Mountains!

The ancient demon emperor Di Tian had even established the Demon Court, reaching into the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, attempting to unify the Eastern Wilderness.

Even though the ancient demon emperor Di Tian had fallen, the power of the Tiger tribe still existed.

Di Tian's consort, Jinxiu Xian, had disappeared after the great battle back then, leading the remaining forces of the Demon Court into hiding, so that even the decrees of Truth Crossing Hall could not reach them.

Rumor had it that besides the two sons Huang Man and Emperor Slaughter born to Di Tian early on, Jinxiu Xian had also borne him a daughter, gifted with extraordinary talent, a great demon who truly inherited the ancient demon bloodline of Di Tian.

"I can grant you freedom, but after you return, you must restrain the Tiger tribe and not actively invade human territory. Especially you, Emperor Slaughter!"

Luo Chen's gaze fell deeply upon the golden-robed man.

Only he, as the master, knew Emperor Slaughter's capabilities.

Perhaps the other's upper limit was already locked, making it extremely difficult to break through to Ascendant, but after inheriting Di Tian's Gold Origin, combined with his late-stage Sage cultivation, his combat power was terrifyingly formidable.

This was evident from the fact that Emperor Slaughter dared to single-handedly intercept the reinforcements from the Tiannan Dao Palace.

It could be said that, aside from Azure Frost back then and himself now, there were few Sages in the world who could suppress the current Emperor Slaughter.

He could not simply let such a fierce tiger return to the mountains without any conditions.

Faced with this condition, Emperor Slaughter instead breathed a sigh of relief.

He slowly raised his head, raised his palm toward Luo Chen, and swore an oath.

"I, Emperor Slaughter, am willing to establish a Great Dao oath! After returning, I will strictly restrain the Tiger tribe and not actively invade human territory. And for my entire life, I will not make a move against humans, especially cultivators of the Luo Tian Sect. If I violate this oath, may heart demons assail me, and heavenly thunder strike my crown!"

This was a very sincere Great Dao oath.

It did not restrict future breakthroughs in cultivation level, presumably because Emperor Slaughter was well aware that his upper limit was locked.

Heart demons assailing, heavenly thunder striking the crown—one internal, one external—both were the most critical factors affecting a demon cultivator's life.

As he swore the oath, his mana fluctuated, his soul manifested, and he released the barrier between himself and heaven and earth. Faintly, an invisible shackle fell upon Emperor Slaughter.

Luo Chen nodded slightly and pointed a finger from across the space at Emperor Slaughter.

"Since that is the case, then I return your freedom!"

One heavy Dao shackle fell, one heavy freedom shackle was released.

Between coming and going, Emperor Slaughter's emotions were complex.

He bowed to Luo Chen three times, then solemnly placed the Pingshan Crown upon his head.

"Master, farewell."

"Daoist, please!"

The golden-robed man stood up and strode out of the hall.

After he left, Luo Chen did not immediately leave the Danchen Hall, but waited quietly.

After a moment, Heavenjade walked in.

"Master, they have arrived."

"Let them in!"

Two figures entered the hall from outside, one after the other.

One was elegant and handsome, with a bearing like a mountain.

The other had a shaved head and large ears, with a solemn Buddha-like countenance.

Luo Chen revealed a smile. "This battle troubled you both. Thank you."

"It was merely fulfilling an agreement. Fellow Daoist Luo need not thank me." Deng Taiyue waved his hand, quite carefree. "Besides, without Fellow Daoist Kongchan and another Daoist's help, it would have been difficult for me to complete the task of blocking the reinforcements from Libei."

"Another?" Luo Chen showed a surprised expression, his gaze falling on the demon monk Kongchan.

Kongchan pressed his palms together and explained, "It was the Mad Wanderer! He has awakened and emerged from Yemo Heaven. I do not know why he suddenly appeared to help us at that time."

Luo Chen understood.

After pondering for a moment, he slowly said, "Regarding the Mad Wanderer, after I become an Ascendant, I will visit him."

Deng Taiyue's eyes lit up. "Daoist, are you about to become an Ascendant?"

Luo Chen smiled bitterly and shook his head. "Ascension is naturally necessary, but there is no rush for this moment. The injuries I sustained from the battle with Liancheng are by no means light. I must recuperate well before making further plans."

Hearing this, Deng Taiyue was slightly disappointed.

But he also knew how dangerous it was for Luo Chen to fight across realms, and it would be a joke to say he was completely unharmed.

He was merely curious about what preparations Luo Chen would make to break through to Ascendant, so he could learn from them in the future.

Perhaps seeing through Deng Taiyue's thoughts, Luo Chen suddenly spoke up to remind him. "Brother Deng, although you have comprehended the Earth Origin and reached the late Sage stage, and probably often have that illusion of resonance between heaven and earth, breaking through to the Ascendant stage in that state is truly too dangerous. In my opinion, it is better to lay a solid foundation, reach the perfection of the late Sage stage, and then seek the opportunity for breakthrough."

"This..." Deng Taiyue hesitated slightly. He naturally understood the most basic principle in Luo Chen's words—foundation was most important, haste makes waste. But in daily cultivation, that feeling of being able to take a step to heaven at any time was absolutely not false. He did not know if he would have another chance if he missed the occasional opportunity.

Should he seize the opportunity, or plan slowly?

Luo Chen did not persuade further, only mentioning one point.

"You still lack a fifth-grade spirit vein. Regarding this matter, after I achieve the Ascendant stage, I will give you an answer."

Deng Taiyue nodded. "Then I will wait for Fellow Daoist Luo's good news."

Luo Chen smiled, then his gaze fell on Kongchan.

The demon monk's heart was filled with trepidation under this gaze.

He understood that Luo Chen was probably about to fulfill his promise and grant him freedom.

This was originally what he had in mind.

But for some reason, at this moment, he instead felt some regret.

Before Luo Chen could speak, the demon monk Kongchan suddenly knelt on one knee.

"Master, this humble monk is willing to serve you for another five hundred years, to be driven by your command!"

Deng Taiyue's eyes widened beside him, as if he had never considered this possibility!

This late-stage Sage great demon emperor from the Northern Sea was actually Luo Chen's spirit pet?

And it seemed that Luo Chen was willing to grant it freedom, yet it was reluctant to leave.

What kind of reasoning was this?

Was there anyone in the world willing to bow and scrape, to be a slave and servant?

Luo Chen looked at him with a half-smile. "Have you thought it through? No regrets?"

Kongchan, disregarding the dignity of a great demon emperor, bowed his head to Luo Chen, the skull prayer beads around his neck clattering crisply.

"This humble monk absolutely has no regrets!"

"Heh, very well. No need for five hundred years. When I ascend, I will grant you your freedom."

Luo Chen smiled and did not press further.

Some chose to leave, some chose to stay—all were personal choices.

Who would ultimately regret, who would be pleased, who could say for sure?

Seeing the fatigue on Luo Chen's face, Deng Taiyue chatted with Luo Chen for a while before taking his leave.

After leaving the hall, he looked at the beaming bald monk, hesitated to speak, and finally cupped his hands.

"Fellow Daoist Kongchan, feel free to visit my Taishan Sect's Dapan Peak sometime."

"Good, good, good!"

Kongchan replied with a chuckle.

He could naturally see the meaning behind the strange expression on Deng Taiyue's face.

Perhaps he was ashamed of his kneeling submission, or perhaps he regretted the camaraderie they had once shared in battle. But how could the other know what was in his heart?

If Luo Chen truly granted him freedom, what then?

Return to the Northern Sea for bitter cultivation? That was too ascetic.

Go to the Fallen Sea to roam? That was too dangerous.

It was better to stay by Luo Chen's side, listening to his true words and guidance!

After all, this was an unparalleled existence who had slain a great power in a reverse battle!

Moreover, from his words, breaking through to the Ascendant stage next was something he had firmly in hand, without the slightest hesitation or anxiety common among ordinary late-stage Sages before a breakthrough.

At the very least, if he could get a share of that Enlightenment Pill that had caused such a stir earlier, that would be a wonderful thing!

Kongchan rubbed his bald head and looked curiously at the vast Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

"Time to explore my new home!"

...

Leaving aside the vastly different choices of Emperor Slaughter and Kongchan, and whether Deng Taiyue would take the risk to break through, Heavenjade returned to the Danchen Hall and gave Luo Chen a brief report on the subsequent situation.

The distribution of the Enlightenment Pills!

It had been agreed that anyone willing to help would receive one pill after the war ended. Even if some had fallen, Luo Chen would not be stingy and would give it to their descendants.

Even for the rogue cultivator Bo Hanjun, who had no descendants to pass it on to, Luo Chen would bestow a token, allowing his close associates to come to the Luo Tian Sect with the token in the future to claim it.

But that war, which had been almost a catastrophic disaster for the East Yuan Continent, had not actually caused much loss for the Eastern Wilderness cultivators.

After the war, statistics showed that only five Sages had fallen, most of them newly advanced.

Meng Ye of the Nine Spirits Sect, Bai Yunfei of the White Crane Sect, Qilian Zhenren of the Hundred Beasts Sect, Kongtongzi of the White Deer Cave, and Jiuxu Zhenren of the East Sun Sect.

The rest, though mostly injured, had ultimately survived.

This was a good thing, but for Luo Chen, it was somewhat troublesome.

Many remained, but there were only about ten Enlightenment Pills—not enough to go around!

Fortunately, Luo Chen's terrifying record of slaying the great power Liancheng, combined with his good reputation and credibility, meant that after a simple distribution and promises, no major disturbances occurred.

Back then, when refining the Enlightenment Pills, three batches had been completed, each with five pills, totaling fifteen.

Luo Chen had taken one himself, using the Netherwater Spring to comprehend the Water Origin.

He had also given three to Deng Taiyue.

Thus, only eleven remained.

Luo Chen took them all out and distributed them to the ten great sects and the rogue cultivator Bo Hanjun.

As for the Azure Pill Valley, Seven Mounds Mountain, and Splendid Wind Palace, they had not yet received any.

Azure Pill Valley was because Tao Wan had just formed her Sage core and was still at a low cultivation level, not yet needing this item.

As for Seven Mounds Mountain, Medicine Master did not value the Enlightenment Pill itself; what he cared about was how to refine the Enlightenment Pill. Therefore, he exchanged it for Luo Chen's complete pill formula and refining notes. This was far more important than a single Enlightenment Pill, by a thousand or a hundred times.

Luo Chen generously gave it.

Even though the two had some past grievances, two hundred years had passed, and they had long faded.

Moreover, as fellow alchemists, he could understand the other's feelings.

Most importantly, the Enlightenment Pill was not so easy to refine!

As for Splendid Wind Palace, they had not actively requested one at the time, which surprised Luo Chen slightly.

Later, he realized that they might be continuing their good relationship with Luo Chen and even the Luo Tian Sect, deliberately giving Luo Chen a way out.

Luo Chen's rise was inevitable, and the Luo Tian Sect might one day dominate the Eastern Wilderness like the Nether Origin Sect. Since that was the case, why offend Luo Chen over a single pill? By voluntarily stepping back, they could instead build goodwill.

Moreover, Luo Chen had good credibility; he would naturally make up for it later.

Why rush?

Luo Chen indeed did not intend to renege. He had already listed medicinal ingredients for each sect and commissioned them to help collect them.

Once the ingredients were gathered, he would naturally find time to refine more Enlightenment Pills.

It could be imagined that in the future, with no more internal or external troubles, the Eastern Wilderness, with the help of Luo Chen, a grandmaster of the alchemy dao, would surely enter a new era of flourishing cultivation!

But that was only the future.

For now, Luo Chen truly did not have much time.

He needed to heal, but it was not merely healing.

After everyone left, Luo Chen returned to the cold, quiet cultivation chamber.

He lifted his robe. On his chest and abdomen, a five-finger palm print, vivid in every detail, still remained.

This was left by Liancheng's divine ability.

Back then, to severely wound Liancheng, Luo Chen had forcibly taken this palm strike head-on.

Its terrifying power had once sent Luo Chen plummeting to the bottom of the sea, his physical body disintegrating, and even his soul falling into a state of extinction.

One must know, that was Luo Chen's physical body, cultivated to nearly the fifth rank!

Moreover, there was the defense of the peerless Town Prison Soul Art.

Under that single palm, heaven and earth were truly overturned!

Fortunately, the foundation of this physical body still existed, and the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit had not hesitated to exhaust its own life force, protecting its master in a crisis, allowing Luo Chen's physical body to recover.

Finally, the return of the life soul awakened Luo Chen from his state of extinction.

Only then did that heaven-shaking final sword strike come!

"This palm strike is too overbearing, even more so than the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger. But the greatest threat was also at the moment of impact. As time wears on, with my mana, I can slowly expel its residual power."

"Although the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit has exhausted its life force and fallen into a dormant state, I can replenish it later by seeking out beings with strong vitality."

"The real trouble is something I did not anticipate."

Luo Chen murmured as he closed his eyes.

Within his sea of consciousness, golden waves rippled.

The eight divided souls stood side by side, coldly staring at the black light sphere floating within the golden sea.

The main soul sat high above, emitting a grand and majestic voice.

"Zhou Fuyou, you and I are originally one body. Why persist in stubborn resistance?"
