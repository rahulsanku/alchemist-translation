# Chapter 824: Surrounded by Stars, Demon Emperor's Remains

Three days later.

Outside Shouyang Mountain at Lingtian Pass.

Carriages flowed like dragons, pedestrians wove like threads of cloth.

Countless low-level cultivators had gathered outside the mountain, spontaneously forming markets large and small, conducting all kinds of business.

To ordinary people, this scene was utterly inconceivable.

After all, Shouyang Mountain was the temporary cave dwelling of a Sage. Who would dare set up a small stall right outside someone's front door?

But this particular Sage surnamed Lu was different. A member of the East Sun Sect, upon arriving at Lingtian Pass, he had advocated for loosening many commercial restrictions.

He even led by example, setting aside a large area outside Shouyang Mountain for low-level cultivators to do business openly and honestly.

Within the market, a beam of escaping light shot up into the sky, entering the mist-shrouded mountain.

Watching that streak of light, the low-level cultivators were filled with envy.

"Another Adept has gone in, hasn't he?"

"I heard that inside the mountain, there are exchange gatherings specially held for Adepts. Many people have gained a great deal from them."

"I wonder when I'll ever be qualified to participate in such gatherings."

"Dream on! And even if you're dreaming, why not dream bigger? The higher-level Sage gatherings are what we truly yearn for."

"I heard that at the last Sage gathering, someone brought out a Demon Emperor cub..."

Just as the low-level cultivators were sighing with envy, they suddenly saw a crimson flying light streak in from outside the market, heading straight into Shouyang Mountain.

This scene drew gasps of surprise.

Lingtian Pass had a no-fly formation!

Even Adept cultivators could not fly freely except during wartime, and only in certain designated areas.

To fly straight through Lingtian Pass like this, coming directly from outside the mountain, only a Sage at the Nascent Soul stage could be completely unrestricted!

Another Sage had joined that mysterious gathering!

---

Unlike the bustling activity outside, Shouyang Mountain was somewhat quiet.

But only "somewhat."

The mountain peak and the mountainside were clearly demarcated, two groups of cultivators at different realms interacting with each other. Each showed restraint, yet still presented a scene of gathered talent.

Especially at the mountain peak. The number of Sages attending today's gathering was as high as over twenty!

This number already accounted for nearly two-thirds of the Sages at Lingtian Pass.

Placed within the entire Eastern Wilderness human cultivation world, excluding the Nether Origin Sect, it also represented half of them.

A gathering of such brilliance was unparalleled.

However, among the over twenty Sages at the exchange meeting, several small circles had subtly formed.

Looking at the circle led by Elder Lu of the East Sun Sect, one would find that the Sages within it mostly had connections to the Nether Origin Sect through their backing forces.

Then there was a group centered around Nine Spirit Lord. The people inside were mostly familiar faces, such as Elder Fu, Xiao Shiniang, Lingfengzi, and others. These people all shared one thing in common: their sect mountains had fallen, they were homeless, and most came from the fifteen domains that had already been lost.

In a certain sense, if not for this war between humans and demons, this group could be considered the rising new powers of the Eastern Wilderness human race.

After all, the domains closer to the Million Great Mountains were mostly opened up after ancient times, and the sects stationed there were emerging forces that had appeared over the past three thousand years.

The last circle was somewhat subtle.

About seven or eight people, each wearing robes with different sect insignias, each carrying an innate pride.

They did not take anyone as their leader, and gathering together was merely a subconscious act.

But to the observant, this circle belonged to the more ancient type, naturally at odds with the emerging forces, yet having a relatively ordinary relationship with the Nether Origin Sect.

They could be considered neutral forces swaying in the middle.

Among these seven or eight people, one was quite active. Whenever he spoke, the others would smile and chime in a bit.

The reason for this was not that this person had a high cultivation level—an early-stage Sage was not particularly impressive in this setting.

What mattered was his identity as a fourth-tier alchemist!

Among the hundred arts of cultivation, alchemy was paramount.

Any major sect would cultivate their own alchemists.

Ordinary alchemists were easy to train; even third-tier ones could often be raised if one was willing to invest resources.

But once it involved a fourth-tier alchemist, it was not so simple.

Three conditions had to be met simultaneously: talent, resources, and cultivation realm!

Without talent, one could not even enter the door.

Without resources, it was difficult to deepen one's skill.

Even if the first two were satisfied, long-term pill refining would greatly delay one's own cultivation, leading to a low realm.

And once the realm was low, it was absolutely impossible to meet the strong control required for refining fourth-tier pills.

Take, for example, the former Supreme Elder of Qingdan Valley, Qingdanzi. His alchemy skill had definitely touched the threshold of the fourth tier. However, because his cultivation realm was insufficient, he had failed multiple times when the entire sect pooled its resources to refine the Nascent Soul Pill.

In summary, fourth-tier alchemists were extremely rare talents throughout the entire Mountains and Seas Realm.

In the vast Eastern Wilderness, the fourth-tier alchemists who could be named numbered only six or seven.

More than half of them had been recruited by the holy land, the Nether Origin Sect.

The most famous one left outside was none other than the former Pill Emperor of the Medicine King Sect.

But this man was skilled at accumulating wealth without building good interpersonal relationships. As a result, when disaster struck, not only did no one come to his aid, but the Medicine King Sect's vast wealth attracted many to kick him while he was down.

Now, at the Shouyang Mountain Sage gathering, this person being treated kindly by everyone was another fourth-tier alchemist.

His name was Yao Ping, from Seven Hollows Mountain, and people called him Master Yao.

Everyone was currying favor with him because they had requests to make.

"Master Yao, regarding the Enlightenment Pill I mentioned last time, have you found any leads?"

"Deng Taiyue, why are you so impatient? Although the Tihu Pill is fourth-tier, it was known as a pseudo-fifth-tier divine pill in ancient times. How could it be so easy to refine? Besides, the pill formula you gave me is missing a full forty percent, especially the most important main ingredient. How could I possibly deduce the complete formula in such a short time?"

"Master Yao, regarding the Vitality-Stabilizing Pill I need..."

"That pill is not difficult. I have already refined it. But within a hundred years, I can only provide you with ten bottles at most."

"Ten bottles?"

Hearing this answer, the person who asked looked somewhat disappointed.

The Vitality-Stabilizing Pill was a fourth-tier cultivation pill that required long-term consumption to be effective.

Ten bottles in a hundred years, spread out, meant one bottle every ten years, only one pill per year. How could that have any effect?

Someone nearby laughed and said, "The Alchemy Sect is coming today, Fellow Daoist Zhang. Why not ask him for help? I heard that the Alchemy Sect's pill refining skill is superb, with few failures. If you want a stable supply..."

Before he could finish speaking, Master Yao, with his white beard, snorted coldly.

"The Alchemy Sect, Luo Chen, is nothing but a yellow-mouthed child. He has been cultivating for less than three hundred years. How profound could his alchemy skill be?"

The person who had made the suggestion smiled awkwardly. "But the fellow cultivators at Lingtian Pass have spread quite a story. Moreover, Nine Spirit Lord, who is only at the sixth level of the Sage stage, now holds a status equal to that of a Great Cultivator, all thanks to the Alchemy Sect refining pills for him."

Master Yao's expression turned somewhat ugly. He said in a deep voice, "The Alchemy Sect may have some skill, but in my opinion, he is probably someone who takes shortcuts and seeks quick success. Looking at the pills he has refined for others, they are often one-time deals, rarely involving long-term cooperation. Fellow Daoist Zhang needs the Vitality-Stabilizing Pill, a cultivation pill. The most important thing is a stable supply. In my opinion, the Alchemy Sect might also be able to refine it, but as for a stable..."

Before he could finish, Sage Zhang quickly nodded.

He could see that Master Yao was somewhat angry, so there was no need to provoke him further.

Ten bottles in a hundred years was fine. If he planned the usage time well, he could still save ten years of bitter cultivation!

Seeing his approval, Master Yao's expression improved considerably.

But once the topic was opened, he couldn't stop.

While stroking his beard, he spoke eloquently.

"There are many alchemists in this world, but few at the fourth tier. In the past, only the Pill Emperor was my equal. Now that the Pill Emperor has fallen, and Fellow Daoists Xin and Chen Mozi have long been secluded in the Nether Origin Sect, I don't even have a chance to exchange alchemy insights with anyone."

"I heard that another alchemy master has emerged in the Eastern Wilderness. I traveled a thousand miles, braving hardships, to come here, hoping to exchange ideas with him."

Everyone knew who the alchemy master in his mouth was.

Wasn't it the Alchemy Sect, Luo Chen, whom they had just been discussing!

"But I sent three visiting cards in a row, and that person avoided seeing me every time. I'm afraid he is merely all show and no substance, not daring to meet..."

The crowd suddenly understood. No wonder Master Yao, ignoring the dangers of war at the front, had left the safe Seven Hollows Mountain to come to Lingtian Pass.

So he wanted to seek advice from the Alchemy Sect!

And the other party had avoided meeting him. Could it really be, as he said, that he was unworthy of his reputation?

Master Yao's criticism had not yet ended when the atmosphere in the hall suddenly shifted.

A beam of escaping light drifted down and landed.

It revealed a tall figure, white robes, a high crown, and a handsome face.

"Another unfamiliar Sage has arrived," Sage Zhang muttered.

Master Yao nodded, not paying much attention.

Looking at his cultivation level, he was only at the third layer of the Sage stage. Although a bit stronger than Master Yao himself, it was nothing special.

With his status as a fourth-tier alchemist, he treated even mid-stage and late-stage Great Cultivators as equals, let alone a third-layer Sage.

But soon, he sensed something was wrong.

"Brother Luo, I heard you had come out of seclusion. I didn't expect it to be true. This Lord's trip was not in vain."

Nine Spirit Lord stepped forward, his face full of smiles.

The young man also smiled and asked with concern, "Has the injury you suffered back then healed?"

Nine Spirit Lord patted his left arm. "It's fine. Thanks to the healing pills you sent, it was completely healed by the second year."

Another person approached. It was Elder Fu of the Divine Talisman Sect.

The young man asked with a smile, "I heard that Fellow Daoist Yelan has come out of seclusion?"

Elder Fu said with relief, "He has come out. Although a bit hasty, his realm has finally stabilized. From now on, the Divine Talisman Sect will have a successor. Even if I were to go now, I could die with my eyes closed."

"You are joking, Elder. In the last absolute spirit battle, the figure driving the Golden Armor Giant to suppress and kill the Six-Winged Golden Cicada Demon Emperor was still vigorous and strong. You can certainly live another five hundred years."

The young man chatted and laughed while responding to others who took the initiative to greet him.

"Fellow Daoist Xiao, it's been five years. You've become even more beautiful!"

"Pavilion Master Li, we'll talk about that matter later."

"Great Palace Master, why have you come?"

...

As he walked along, it was bustling with activity.

It seemed like everyone had an excellent relationship with him!

Even those he didn't know, after they proactively announced their names, the young man would get to know them without any trace of arrogance.

After the circle led by Nine Spirit Lord had finished greeting him, the host, Elder Lu, also appeared with a smile.

"Fellow Daoist Luo, it's been years. Your demeanor is even more impressive than before!"

"Elder Lu, that sounds ambiguous. One might think you missed me dearly."

"Haha, come, let me introduce you to a few fellow Daoists!"

"That is exactly my wish. How could I not accept?"

"Celestial Master Hua, this is the fourth-tier alchemist you've been thinking about day and night, the Alchemy Sect, Luo Chen!"

...

"So he is the Alchemy Sect!"

Behind the crowd, Master Yao tightly gripped his white beard.

His immortal-like image was instantly lost in that moment.

As a fellow fourth-tier alchemist, although he had been flattered and praised after arriving here, where was such glory?

Those people couldn't even be said to be taking the initiative to get to know him; they were practically fawning and currying favor, completely lacking the dignity of a Sage!

Was the difference really that great?

Just as jealousy flared in his eyes, the footsteps of the people beside him made him widen his eyes.

"Fellow Daoist Deng, you..."

Deng Taiyue turned back to look at him, explaining with some embarrassment, "I heard that the Alchemy Sect is not only skilled in pill refining but also actively accepts incomplete pill formulas. Perhaps he can complete the full formula for the Tihu Pill. I want to go and get to know him."

After speaking, he turned without hesitation and squeezed into the crowd, becoming the next person eager to meet Luo Chen.

Master Yao stood rooted to the spot, his mouth wide open, his body trembling slightly.

Suddenly, he turned his head to look at the others beside him.

"Fellow Daoist Zhang, you're not thinking of going over too, are you?"

Sage Zhang, who had a request to make of him, naturally wouldn't speak recklessly at this moment.

"What about you all?"

The remaining few looked at each other, and then one of them said naturally, "Everyone listed in this gathering is a fellow Daoist. What's wrong with getting to know them? Besides, Master Yao, you said earlier that you wanted to discuss alchemy with him. Don't you want to meet him?"

"Yes, such a young alchemy master, with such a cultivation level, must have exceptional qualities. I am also eager to get to know him."

At this moment, Master Yao's heart was ashen.

He had only advanced to the Sage stage at an advanced age, and his future path was inevitably hopeless.

That was why he chased fame and wanted to revive Seven Hollows Mountain with his own strength.

But compared to Luo Chen, the gap was obvious.

The other party had built a Sage-level sect from scratch, possessed profound alchemy skills at a young age, and if nothing unexpected happened, he might even be recruited by the Nether Origin Sect in the future, which meant the hope of becoming an Ascendant!

One was the setting sun over the western mountains, the other a rising morning sun.

No wonder the cultivators here were so snobbish.

"But I am still a senior in the alchemy path, famous before him. If he knows what's good for him, he should come and pay his respects!"

Master Yao gritted his teeth. Even though only one or two people remained by his side, he held back the urge to bow his head and pay homage.

He was waiting for Luo Chen to come and see him.

But until the gathering officially began, that young man did not even glance in his direction.

It was as if he didn't even know such a person existed?

"Pretending to ignore me, infuriating this old man!"

...

"Now that everyone has gotten to know each other, I, Lu, will get straight to the point!"

Elder Lu sat at the head, smiling as he looked at everyone.

"We'll leave the exchange of cultivation insights for last. Let's first proceed with the part everyone is most concerned about: exchanging resources!"

As he spoke, he raised his hand in an inviting gesture.

"In the previous few sessions, I, Lu, almost emptied my entire stash. Which fellow Daoist would like to go first this time?"

As soon as the words fell, Luo Chen, who had been specially invited to sit at the upper seat beside him, spoke calmly.

"This is my first time participating in a gathering. As a newcomer, let me cast a brick to attract jade!"

As soon as he said this, everyone cast hopeful glances at him.

Luo Chen was famous as the Alchemy Sect. What he took out would surely be some high-grade pills.

Pills of this level were the rarest. Who wouldn't want them?

Even if they were useless to oneself, buying one or two would be equivalent to selling the Alchemy Sect a favor.

Just as everyone was anticipating...

A stream of light flew out and crashed heavily into the center of the spacious hall.

The smell of blood gushed out!

The residual powerful aura, without concealment, surged in all directions, startling many people's souls and causing their mana to surge.

Looking closely.

A huge creature, dozens of feet long, with an upper body like a bat and a lower body like a scorpion, appeared before everyone.

It was dripping with blood, its fur and skin gone, truly terrifying!

A complete—Demon Emperor's Remains!

Amidst the shock and awe of the crowd, Luo Chen smiled harmlessly.

"A fourth-tier Lightless Beast corpse. Apart from the fur and skin, it is basically intact."

"Anyone want it? Just exchange it with any of the three types of celestial qi: Red Celestial, Purple Celestial, or Crimson Celestial!"
