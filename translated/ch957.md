# Chapter 957: Spirit Energy Cannon Barrage, Battle in the Remnant Realm

"It's open!"

"Hurry in!"

The urgent voice rang out as Crimson Leaf Matron immediately gave the signal.

Yet none of them dared to move.

Who knew what awaited them inside?

Heavenly Master Li chuckled lightly. "This old man will go first!"

His body transformed into a streak of light, darting into the gap where the great door had opened.

The others hesitated no longer, entering one after another.

Last came Luo Chen!

By the time the two ancestors of the Golden Lion Empire arrived, all they saw was a fleeting black shadow vanishing in an instant.

Then came the thunderous slam of the bronze doors closing.

"The three of them are still inside—dangerous!"

The Third Ancestor's expression shifted. He immediately took out a token, attempting to reopen the bronze doors.

But no matter how he activated the token, the doors remained utterly motionless, as if something inside was bracing against them.

The Fifth Ancestor, who had some understanding of formations, made a brief probe and her face instantly lost color.

"There's a top-tier formation master inside who has set up an additional spirit-blocking formation, altering the structure of the imitation Life and Death Gate. There's no way to open it through normal means in a short time."

What kind of formation master could change the structure of a treasure refined by Life and Death Gate cultivators so quickly?

A name suddenly surfaced.

"Crimson Leaf!"

The Third Ancestor ground out the name through clenched teeth.

Only that old hag from the Crimson Leaf Sect possessed such formation mastery.

And according to the Golden Lion Emperor, among those eight people, there was indeed an old woman.

If the description was accurate, she must have personally seen through the flaws in the gate's restrictions, allowing them to enter so easily.

No!

In such a short time, Crimson Leaf alone couldn't have managed it.

There must have been someone familiar with the bronze doors who had prepared in advance, combined with a body-refining powerhouse of immense strength acting together, to accomplish it in one go.

He didn't know who the latter was, but the former was undoubtedly that ingrate, Heavenly Master Li.

"Fifth Sister, quickly break Crimson Leaf's formation and join up with Big Brother!" the Third Ancestor commanded.

The youthful Fifth Ancestor nodded. "I'll try my best!"

But before she could act, the Golden Lion Emperor rushed in frantically.

"Ancestor, Ancestor, save us!"

"What has you so panicked? Where are your manners?" the Third Ancestor scolded.

The Golden Lion Emperor's face was pale. "They're attacking! The Fierce Corpse Stiff Alliance has launched their assault!"

Hearing the name Fierce Corpse Stiff Alliance, the Third Ancestor couldn't help but frown.

Even though they had been secluded in the Yin-Yang Realm cultivating, they were vaguely aware of some of the turbulent undercurrents in the Southern Frontier.

There was indeed such a force, quietly stirring.

But they never expected them to dare strike at the mighty Golden Lion Empire.

The Golden Lion Emperor caught his breath and continued, "Not only that, according to reports from the beacon towers, among the enemy forces are cultivators from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

At these words, the Third Ancestor's face changed drastically. Even the Fifth Ancestor, who had been setting up formations, stopped her movements.

If the Fierce Corpse Stiff Alliance had acted alone, it could have been dismissed as internal strife within the Southern Frontier.

But bringing in the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect from the Central Continent changed everything.

By now, who in the world didn't know of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's wolfish ambitions?

They had waged war on the Northern Sea and taken over the Eastern Wilderness.

And now they were setting their sights on the Southern Frontier!

Their ambition to swallow the entire world was laid bare for all to see!

"After the Golden Lion Empire comes Unicorn Mountain, and then... our sect," the Fifth Ancestor murmured, a hint of fear in her eyes.

The Third Ancestor took a deep breath and made a decisive choice. "Set this aside for now. We'll go out and deploy troops to block the Fierce Corpse Stiff Alliance and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's army. Fifth Sister, quickly notify the sect's strong ones to prepare!"

The Fifth Ancestor nodded. "That goes without saying. But what about the Yin-Yang Realm? There are seven Sage cultivators who have broken in, including Heavenly Master Li and Crimson Leaf Matron, both late-stage Sages."

"We can't worry about that now! Compared to the sect's safety, neither the Golden Lion Empire nor Big Brother matters. Besides..." The Third Ancestor thought for a moment, then spoke with a hint of relief, "The Yin-Yang Realm is filled with layers of formations. As long as Big Brother and the others are cautious and use the formations to their advantage, they still have some chance."

The young woman opened her mouth but could only let out a heavy sigh.

"Let's hope so!"

"Let's go. They're waiting outside for us to take command," the Third Ancestor said, lifting his foot to leave.

But after a few steps, there was no movement behind him.

He turned back in confusion to find the young woman's face full of hesitation.

The Fifth Ancestor said irritably, "What is it now? Why so indecisive?"

The young woman's voice trembled with an inexplicable fear. "Third Brother, the Death Gate is inside the Yin-Yang Realm... Do you think Heavenly Master Li and the others might be heading for the Death Gate?"

The Fifth Ancestor froze completely in place.

...

A massive flying vessel, like a war fortress, floated in the sky above the Golden Lion Empire's border.

It seemed to hang there, motionless.

But upon closer inspection, this enormous ship was advancing toward the Golden Lion Empire at an undeniable, unstoppable speed.

Beams of flying light flew from the ship, plunging into the battlefield below.

On the ship, Zhan Bingtao watched the scene with disdain in his eyes.

"Such a vast empire, yet their response is so rigid. They didn't even know we were bearing down on them."

The Songyang Alliance Leader, however, looked as if he had expected this.

"Don't blame the Golden Lion Empire. From what I know, they are a branch of the Life and Death Gate. For thousands of years, no one dared to provoke them, so they naturally grew lax."

"Add to that the celestial anomaly in the Southern Frontier. The five ancestors of the Golden Lion Empire have gone into seclusion in a secret realm to cultivate, leaving only low-level cultivators behind. How could they know the great trends of the cultivation world?"

"A thousand years of peace—that's what led to today's calamity!"

Listening to the Alliance Leader's explanation and sighs, Zhan Bingtao snorted lightly and asked, "In your esteemed opinion, how long will it take to take the Golden Lion Empire?"

"Half a month!"

The Songyang Alliance Leader spoke with certainty.

"Just half a month. At most half a month. We must conquer this territory at the fastest speed, then march on Unicorn Mountain and lay siege to the Life and Death Gate!"

"Half a month, huh?"

Zhan Bingtao frowned. That was a bit short.

The Golden Lion Empire's territory was vast!

This was evident from the fact that at its peak, it could simultaneously support nine Sage cultivators.

After all, only a sufficiently large territory with abundant resources could sustain nine Sages.

By Central Continent standards, the Golden Lion Empire could rival a Great House or three or four aristocratic clans.

Compared to the Eastern Wilderness, one Golden Lion Empire was roughly equivalent to three or four small Jade Cauldron Domains.

Such a vast territory, though completely outmatched in top-tier combat power by the combined forces of the Fierce Corpse Stiff Alliance and Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, would still be too tight to fully conquer in just half a month.

He didn't understand why the Songyang Alliance Leader was in such a hurry.

But the other party had already begun making various arrangements.

"The Golden Lion Empire has high walls and deep moats, a large number of low-level cultivators, and city-protecting formations everywhere. With the scattered combat style of the various factions under my Fierce Corpse Stiff Alliance, it's hard to take quickly."

"I hope your sect can contribute more in this battle."

"Rest assured, Brother Zhan. When it comes to the battle at Unicorn Mountain later, this Alliance Leader will personally take action!"

Zhan Bingtao pondered for a long while before finally nodding slowly.

Comparatively, Unicorn Mountain had a fearsome reputation and was a tough nut to crack.

The Golden Lion Empire's territory was large, but its strength was dispersed.

And those cities scattered throughout the empire's heartland were nothing more than living targets for his Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

"Agreed!"

After he gave his consent, from the second day onward, the coalition's combat strategy changed completely.

One after another, dark, oppressive cannon muzzles aimed at the city-protecting formations that the Golden Lion citizens took such pride in.

With each cry of "Fire!" even the toughest formations began to ripple, tremble violently, and ultimately collapse into nothingness.

Heavenly Origin Spirit Energy Cannons!

When used on a large-scale battlefield, this treasure was an unstoppable tool for besieging cities and breaking through defenses!

Its only drawback was its exorbitant cost.

Each shot of spirit energy cannon fire consumed a massive amount of high-grade spirit stones.

In all of the Mountains and Seas Realm, probably only the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, which had rested and recuperated for three thousand years, could afford such extravagance.

The Songyang Alliance Leader watched it all, both pleased by the rapid progress and secretly alarmed by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's immense wealth.

Without natural defenses like the Life and Death Gate or the Nether Abyss, what fortress in the world could withstand such concentrated bombardment?

"Ah well. The Dao Sect is strong. Even if they take the entire Southern Frontier, so be it. I only seek a fifth-order spirit land to break through to the Ascendant realm!"

The Songyang Alliance Leader murmured to himself, growing increasingly anxious about Heavenly Master Li and the others' mission.

Whether the holy land could be taken was one thing; whether they could even get in was another.

If they couldn't even enter, there was no point talking about storming the sect's mountain gate.

...

Inside the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm.

A concerned voice came from Heavenly Master Li.

"Qitian, are you alright?"

The others shifted their gaze from Crimson Leaf Matron to Luo Chen, who had entered last.

At this moment, Luo Chen's hands hung by his knees, his face slightly pale, with traces of cold sweat.

Luo Chen slowly straightened up and flexed his wrists.

"I'm fine. Just overexerted myself a bit. A little drained."

Was pushing open a single door really that exaggerated?

But no one doubted it!

That bronze gate contained immense power, far beyond what a Sage cultivator could forcibly break.

Even though Crimson Leaf Matron, guided by Heavenly Master Li, had found the flaw and suggested concentrating force on a single point, it was still the worst possible approach.

Not only would it take a long time, but it would also provoke a counterattack from the bronze gate.

In comparison, Qitian had used his physical strength to push it open, and the rebound force was much lighter.

But just pushing the door had drained Qitian, who had previously exuded such powerful aura, showing just how heavy that gate was.

They had saved themselves some effort.

A beam of escaping light flew over from afar.

From a great distance, a voice shouted, "Who allowed you to enter?"

No one answered.

Before the gate, aside from Crimson Leaf Matron, the other six exchanged glances, and then two stepped forward.

Purple-Robed Marquis frowned and asked Hidden Sword Pavilion Master Fan Wujiu, "Pavilion Master, will you take it, or shall I?"

Fan Wujiu patted his sword case, and a series of hums emanated from within.

"Let's do it together. Quick and clean, to avoid trouble."

Purple-Robed Marquis didn't refuse. He shot into the sky, his body shrouded in purple light.

Fan Wujiu's movements were subtler, but black shadows spread from his sword case, and streaks of sword qi filled the surroundings.

The cultivator—presumably one of the Golden Lion Empire's ancestors—saw this, his expression shifting dramatically as he hastily retreated.

Heavenly Master Li stopped paying attention to that side and turned his focus back.

"Qitian, focus on recovering your vitality. No need to fight."

"If more enemies appear, Fellow Daoist Meng, Bai Xuanjun, lend a hand. Make sure they don't disturb Crimson Leaf's formation work."

Meng Shentong nodded, accepting the task, but then asked, "And what about you?"

Heavenly Master Li smiled faintly and waved his hand casually.

Streams of light flew from his sleeve, scattering in all directions.

Some vanished into the void, others sank into the ground.

Upon closer inspection, they were clearly special gu insects.

"Soul-Chasing Flying Ants?" Meng Shentong blurted out, but then frowned. "No, Soul-Chasing Flying Ants are quite gloomy and sinister. These are far too full of vitality."

Heavenly Master Li waved his hand. "Don't worry about that. I need to keep an eye on the intelligence these gu insects bring back to pinpoint our target."

Meng Shentong chuckled and stopped pressing.

The scene fell quiet for a moment, with only Crimson Leaf Matron busy before the gate, setting up formations to prevent the cultivators outside from breaking in.

Luo Chen watched for a moment, then stopped paying close attention.

Crimson Leaf Matron's formation mastery was indeed not bad—roughly on par with his own.

But because she had incorporated some gu insects into the formations, he couldn't glean much from it, so Luo Chen didn't bother to observe further.

Instead, he focused on the Yin-Yang Remnant Realm itself.

Looking out, everything was golden and dazzling!

The entire world seemed bathed in a layer of gilding.

Tall lion statues stood upon the vast earth, as if they were living creatures.

"These lions..."

Luo Chen's mind turned, searching for corresponding memories.

There was a record in an ancient text about the primeval wilderness.

Golden Dragon Lions!

Strictly speaking, they weren't considered powerful wild beasts.

But their lineage was quite noble—the offspring of a Nether Earth Dragon and a Fierce Lion.

They possessed extremely strong physical defense and a special attack talent that could petrify enemies into gold.

Especially the latter—once struck, the enemy would enter a petrified golden state similar to petrification.

If they couldn't break free from the golden state in time, they would fall once it was shattered.

Even a cultivator's golden core or nascent soul couldn't escape death.

"Why are there so many Golden Dragon Lion statues in this Yin-Yang Realm?"

Luo Chen frowned, sensing a hint of threat.

It felt as if those lifelike golden lion statues were alive, coldly watching their group.

He shifted his gaze a hundred li away to the battlefield.

Hidden Sword Pavilion Master Fan Wujiu's escape speed was astonishingly fast. He had overtaken the Golden Lion Empire ancestor who had shown up earlier and trapped him with sword light transformed from sword gu.

Upon closer inspection, those sword gu were shaped like leaves.

This surprised Luo Chen quite a bit.

Southern Frontier sword cultivators were indeed different from those elsewhere!

The sword gu used by Sage Heavenly Mountain had been swordfish gu.

Unexpectedly, Fan Wujiu used sword-leaf gu.

Individually, they weren't as powerful as swordfish gu, but the quantity...

Amidst the overwhelming sea of sword light, the Golden Lion Empire's Sage cultivator roared in fury.

"I am the Fourth Ancestor of the Golden Lion Empire, a disciple of Lord Youli of the Life and Death Gate. How dare you kill me?"

"Why wouldn't I dare!"

With a sharp cry, amidst the countless sword lights, a flash of purple streaked by.

Purple-Robed Marquis flickered, a purple light circling the Fourth Ancestor once before pulling back.

Swish!

That head fell so simply.

A nascent soul emerged in confusion, but before it could react, it was drowned by the overwhelming sword light.

Shua shua!

Countless sword lights flew back into the sword case. Hidden Sword Pavilion Master's gaze fell on Purple-Robed Marquis, in whose hand a soft sword trembled like a snake flicking its tongue.

"What a fine Purple Blood Soft Sword!"

Purple-Robed Marquis chuckled. "Not as good as your Thousand Leaves Sword Case."

Luo Chen, watching from afar, nodded inwardly.

Both the Thousand Leaves Sword Case, which concealed sword leaves, and that Purple Blood Soft Sword were rare treasures.

Working together, they had killed the Fourth Ancestor, who was only at the early Sage stage, in a single exchange. It was only natural.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's gaze sharpened.

Heavenly Master Li beside him shouted a warning, "Careful!"

A terrifying golden light shot out from a distant palace, hurtling straight toward the two.

At the same time, an angry roar rang out.

"You dare break into my Yin-Yang Realm and kill my fourth brother? You deserve to die!"
