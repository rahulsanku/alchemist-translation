# Chapter 930: Utterly Defeated, Divine Origin Makes His Move

The three newly ascended Sages of the Divine Sect were precisely the ten core disciples carefully selected by the Divine Fire Sect Master back in the day.

With the full support of the Five Elements Divine Sect, and having received the Five Elements Spiritual Marrow and the *Nayuan Divine Art* bestowed by the Supreme Elder, their cultivation progress advanced by leaps and bounds.

Even though seven out of the ten had failed.

Yet, in just a few short decades, three had broken through to the Sage stage—a miracle by any standard across the entire cultivation world.

Luo Chen's earlier exclamation of "profound foundations" was by no means insincere.

His own Luo Tian Sect had been established for three hundred years now.

But aside from himself, not a single genuine Sage had naturally emerged so far.

Bai Meiling was a ghost cultivator and had her own opportunities.

Although Wang Yuan possessed Sage-level combat power, he was a fourth-tier body cultivator, not an orthodox cultivator.

Lingfengzi was an outsider, and Black Dragon went without saying.

Even Junior Sister Canglong, who had only recently broken through, had done so after centuries of arduous cultivation at Icecastle, finally achieving success with Luo Chen's help.

Perhaps comparing with the Luo Tian Sect wasn't appropriate, but looking at the Divine Talisman Sect, which was established around the same time as the Five Elements Divine Sect, one could see the gap.

Elder Fu had exhausted every ounce of his energy, thinking of the sect's future even as he sat in meditation awaiting his end.

Yet, in the end, he had failed to cultivate a single new Sage, placing all his hopes on his good friend Luo Chen.

The Five Elements Divine Sect was truly impressive.

But the moment they clashed, doubt suddenly arose in Luo Chen's heart.

"Kill!"

The unfamiliar male cultivator shook his great banner, and streaks of brown dragon whirlwinds rolled in.

Luo Chen stood amidst them, unmoving.

His robes fluttered as he merely waved his wide sleeve, and the dragon whirlwinds dispersed on their own.

This sight left the unfamiliar cultivator dumbfounded.

A silver ribbon descended from above, like the Milky Way falling from the nine heavens.

Luo Chen snorted coldly, and the silver ribbon instantly went limp, losing all threat.

Yao Hong staggered into view, her face pale.

*Zheng!*

A sword cry rang out beside his ear.

Luo Chen didn't move a muscle. He simply flicked his finger.

*Ding!*

In the blink of an eye, cracks spread inch by inch across that flying sword.

Under the connection of heart and mind, Qi Emei, the sword's owner, spat out a mouthful of blood.

But her combat experience was quite rich. A spell she had already prepared was now unleashed.

A massive palm print pushed forward horizontally.

The Great Handprint of Primordial Qi!

Seeing this technique, Luo Chen's eyes lit up for a rare moment.

But only for a moment.

He pointed a finger, and the great handprint collapsed instantly.

All this was complicated to describe, yet it happened within the span of a few breaths.

Clearly, the three of them had long developed tacit understanding in fighting together.

Yet, it was utterly ineffective!

Luo Chen, in his wide robe and large sleeves, floated in midair, staring down at the three cultivators leaning on each other below. He shook his head in clear disapproval.

"Do you even count as Sages?"

"Not to mention that your lifebound treasures haven't been elevated to the true artifact level, even when casting spells, you can't draw in the world's spiritual energy to enhance their power."

"You have the realm, but not the strength. I truly don't understand how you managed to achieve this Sage realm that everyone dreams of."

These words left Qi Emei and the other two red-faced.

They wanted to argue but couldn't find the words.

Three Sages joining forces against an enemy, yet failing to even touch a single strand of his hair or the corner of his robe.

Even if Luo Chen's reputation was great and his strength immense, shouldn't it be this way?

Suddenly, a roar erupted from the side.

"Activate the sect-protecting grand formation!"

It was the Divine Fire Sect Master.

Seizing the moment the three had stalled Luo Chen, he had already directed the Five Elements Divine Sect cultivators to activate the sect-protecting grand formation.

Figures flickered as streaks of formation light flared up.

The speed was astonishing—in the blink of an eye, it spread for a hundred li, a thousand li, continuously expanding until it enveloped the entire Five Elements Divine Sect.

As the sect-protecting grand formation of a top-tier Sage sect, once fully activated, not even water could seep in, nor mosquitoes or flies enter.

Against internal threats, though its power was greatly diminished, it still possessed some killing and suppressing might.

Luo Chen paid no attention to the Divine Fire Sect Master's actions.

He had already secretly probed the Five Elements Divine Sect's grand formation before entering.

Otherwise, he couldn't have slipped silently into the sect's heartland.

If he wanted to leave, this formation couldn't stop him!

Now, Luo Chen merely looked with some regret at the three below.

Qi Emei—he had some impression of her.

He remembered that when he first returned to the Eastern Wilderness, she had fought with Zeng Yilong.

Back then, this girl was only at the early Initiate stage, right?

The human-demon war had lasted about two hundred years in total, and she had gone from early Initiate to Sage in roughly a hundred years.

Although such a miracle of forming a Sage in a century had also happened to Luo Chen,

clearly, not everyone was Luo Chen.

For her and her two fellow disciples to replicate this miracle in such a short time, they must have taken certain shortcuts.

Hence the awkward situation of having the realm but lacking corresponding strength.

"They were good seedlings, but someone stunted their growth!"

Amidst his sigh, Luo Chen raised both hands, then slowly but swiftly pushed out a single palm.

The starting posture and the hand seal he formed felt familiar to Qi Emei.

Wasn't it the same as the Great Handprint of Primordial Qi she had just used?

The next moment!

A massive cyan palm print took shape, unassuming and unremarkable.

Yet its immensely restrained power made everyone's expression change.

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "This technique is also derived from your sect's Great Handprint of Primordial Qi. Can you all withstand this move?"

As his words fell!

The Azure Yang Great Handprint was suddenly thrust forward.

Wherever it passed, the world's spiritual energy followed, making the palm print grow even larger, and the restrained power began to erupt continuously.

The three Sages, supporting each other, struck out simultaneously, each unleashing their attacks.

But beneath this single palm, the banner scattered, the red sleeve snapped, and the flying sword's cracks accelerated sharply before finally shattering completely.

*Boom!*

With one strike, the three Sages were sent flying like torn cloth sacks.

The residual force of the massive palm print was not exhausted. It rolled forward, sweeping across eight hundred li.

Only after the surrounding sect-protecting grand formation rippled did it gradually dissipate.

*Thump! Thump! Thump!*

With three crashing sounds, Qi Emei and the others fell to the ground, their auras at their lowest ebb, their faces utterly defeated.

The three of them attacking together couldn't harm Luo Chen in the slightest.

Luo Chen's single move left them completely unable to parry.

The gap between them was obvious to anyone.

If he wanted their lives, a single sword qi or a single spell would suffice.

What Luo Chen had said earlier—that they were seeking their own death—was certainly no exaggeration!

"Any more tricks?"

Luo Chen looked around.

A sense of loneliness arose in his heart. The vast Divine Sect had no worthy opponent.

"Divine Origin, aren't you going to make your move?"

His questioning voice spread across the land once more.

Rolling sound waves, carrying Luo Chen's immense divine sense, swept across a hundred li, a thousand li.

Those of lower cultivation were directly knocked unconscious.

Even the Divine Fire Sect Master, a seasoned Sage, felt his blood surge and saw stars before his eyes.

Only then did they fully realize the gap between themselves and Luo Chen.

Whether in magical power, divine sense, combat experience, or even the Five Elements Divine Sect's most renowned spell mastery—all were completely suppressed by this man.

If the Supreme Elder didn't act, who in the vast Divine Sect could stop him?

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's eyes focused, his gaze locking onto the depths of the earth beneath the shattered Divine Origin Peak.

"I've found you!"

He grinned fiercely and reached out with a claw.

Amidst the clouds, the Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw materialized, breaking through layers of rock and soil, grasping toward that place.

*Chi...*

The Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw dissipated without reason.

A figure shrouded in a black robe slowly appeared.

He gazed at Luo Chen from afar, his voice hoarse and sinister.

"You insist on finding me so stubbornly, showing your vengeful heart is firm. Why not kill them?"

Luo Chen sneered coldly. "They have no grievances or enmity with me. Why would I kill them? Today, in the end, it's a settlement between you and me!"

"Well said—clear in gratitude and vengeance!"

Divine Origin Sage praised him, then swept his gaze across the surroundings.

His severely injured junior brother, Divine Fire; the three Sages collapsed on the ground, Qi Emei and the others; and the tens of thousands of Divine Sect disciples who had retreated, only managing the formation.

"A bunch of trash!"

Cursing once, Divine Origin Sage moved!

The moment he struck, it was the same hand seal, but at incredible speed, almost instantaneous.

The Great Handprint of Primordial Qi!

Five-colored light swirled, five-element magical power surged in, and the free spiritual energy of heaven and earth continuously converged within.

It was no longer a single qi—it was clearly five qi!

Luo Chen's expression turned somewhat grave. He replicated the technique, once again manifesting the Azure Yang Great Handprint.

Two massive handprints collided in midair.

With a single strike!

*Boom!*

Terrifying power erupted uncontrollably.

Divine Origin Peak, already shattered by Luo Chen, suffered another heavy blow.

Massive amounts of rock turned to powder. The spiritual vein hidden underground was broken, and a sea of spiritual energy gushed out.

Amidst the sky-filling dust, two figures suddenly closed in, then abruptly separated.

Divine Origin Sage looked at the palm print on his chest, every finger clearly visible. He flicked off the dust and chuckled lowly. "Sage sixth layer? I didn't expect you to break through again in such a short time!"

In full combat, Luo Chen naturally couldn't hide his true cultivation level anymore.

Having obtained the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*, aided by the Alchemy Saint's blessed land, and finally comprehending the origin of fire, drawing a large mass of origin qi from the ocean of origin qi—under so many favorable conditions, he had indeed broken through to the Sage sixth layer.

Luo Chen took no pride in this.

What he was more grateful for was that he had come at just the right time!

Slowly raising his right hand, feeling the sensation of that brief contact, he murmured, "A long time ago, I noticed you were different from ordinary Sage cultivators. In your every movement, heaven and earth seemed to resonate with you."

"In the battle at Bikong Domain, Tian Duzi told me you could move freely amidst the aftermath of Ascendant-level battles."

"When the Mo Tian Domain was opened, though you and I only exchanged one move, in that move, you commanded the origin qi of heaven and earth!"

"So, you must have started walking that extreme path to Ascension long ago. As a Sage, directly communicating with heaven and earth!"

"And this path either leads to Ascension or to death—most likely the latter."

"If you were to fall on the path to Ascension, then I wouldn't know who to take my revenge on!"

Few could access the methods of Ascension.

But for Sage cultivators, most had at least some understanding of the basics.

Broadly speaking, there were three paths.

The first was a shortcut, yet the easiest to succeed—comprehending the true meaning of laws in advance.

But easier said than done. Without absolute talent, one could never comprehend the true meaning of laws.

The second was the orthodox path. Accumulate magical power, enhance the foundation of the soul, gradually transform the Sage into the primordial spirit level, and finally take the stable step toward Ascension.

As for the true meaning of laws?

That was a matter for after Ascension.

At that point, a powerful primordial spirit and unmatched divine sense would allow an early Ascendant to explore the world for laws suited to them.

And the third path?

Many knew of it, but no one dared to attempt it.

Open the Purple Mansion barrier wide, let the Sage resonate with heaven and earth, constantly merge and communicate, accept the origin qi of heaven and earth into the body, and fuse with the entire world.

As long as one achieved "the world is me, and I am the world," Ascension would be a single thought away.

But the danger was clear to everyone.

The Purple Mansion barrier was cultivated precisely to isolate assimilation by heaven and earth.

With the barrier wide open, the Sage constantly exposed—was it you becoming the world, or the world assimilating you?

In this process, cultivators could hardly maintain their true heart. Nine times out of ten, they would be assimilated, lose their will, and gradually have their Sage dissipate.

A lifetime of cultivation, gone in smoke!

Hearing Luo Chen reveal the essence of his current cultivation, Divine Origin Sage laughed bitterly.

"Do you think I wanted this?"

"Without a fifth-order spiritual vein, those of us with five-element spiritual roots have reached the limit of this realm at the Sage stage. We can advance no further!"

"Late Sage was already something I achieved with who knows how much effort."

"I had hoped to complete the Nether Origin Sect's mission and enter the Yanfu Mountain to cultivate. But at Green Parasol Mountain, my physical body was destroyed by that demon phoenix's sacred flame, and my Purple Mansion barrier was long gone!"

"To make matters worse, the Nether Origin Sect burned the bridge after crossing the river, refusing to honor their promise afterward."

"I had no choice!"

In his bitter laughter, there was a touch of sorrow.

Luo Chen shook his head. "I sympathize with your experience, but you set your sights on my physical body—that makes me very angry!"

Divine Origin Sage's greedy gaze fell upon Luo Chen.

"Yes!"

"As long as I have this body of yours, all problems can be solved!"

"Though I don't know how you converted your five-element spiritual root constitution into a single fire spirit body, the thick Purple Mansion barrier must still exist."

"As long as I obtain your body, I can abandon my current path and walk the orthodox way again!"

As he spoke, he shook his hand, and a five-colored small pagoda spun up, flying out.

Streams of light bands flew out from the pagoda, striking toward Luo Chen.

Even with Luo Chen's good temper, anger could no longer be suppressed on his face.

He could tolerate others valuing his strength and using him as a blade.

He could endure others appreciating his alchemy skills and recruiting him to refine pills.

But those who coveted his physical body—he would never forgive!

This body was no longer the one he had transmigrated into.

It was the perfect body he had obtained through countless hardships, continuous cultivation, and even completing a physical nirvana, reassembling from scratch!

Anyone who dared covet it—kill without mercy!

Han Zhan was like this, and Divine Origin was the same!

In his fury, Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat.

A small gray cauldron emerged, growing with the wind, expanding to a hundred zhang in size, crashing toward the five-colored small pagoda.

Not only that, but from the gray giant cauldron, countless crimson chains extended with a clatter, entangling and battling with those light bands.

With every collision of pagoda and cauldron, a resounding boom echoed.

With every entanglement of light bands and chains, boundless magical power was destroyed between them.

After a simple exchange of spells, Luo Chen and Divine Origin Sage directly began a clash of their lifebound treasures.

This was the most direct and dangerous battle. In an instant, it reached a white-hot intensity, representing an irreconcilable struggle to the death!

"What a fine treasure cauldron! No wonder you're the one I've set my sights on!"

Divine Origin Sage laughed darkly as a crystalline small sword shot out suddenly.

Luo Chen suppressed his anger, cut off distracting thoughts, and his mind instantly entered a state of utter calm, like an ancient well without ripples.

With a sweep of his hands, his robes fluttering, five sword pellets arrayed themselves in five directions.

"Old fiend, today you die!"
