# Chapter 706: Dragon Sect's Former Land, Place of Dao Attainment

Night fell.

Sheets of moonlight spilled over the sea surface, passing through the rippling seawater and gradually vanishing into the deep.

Looking up, the scene was magnificent, as if one were in a dream.

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his gaze, and a trace of lingering fear flashed across his pale face.

No matter what, he had never expected that the place he used to redirect disaster eastward would actually be Soulbreak Cliff, a place rumored through the Northern Sea cultivation world for countless years.

Previously, he had paid the price of one drop of heart blood to create a puppet double.

Then, under Han Zhan's instruction, using Locked Pearl Curtain and the Mao-Opening Art as principles, he had forcibly severed a wisp of divine soul from his sea of consciousness.

With this, apart from its physique, that double's aura was almost no different from his own.

At the time, Luo Chen had released the double while hiding deep beneath the sea. For this, he had even paid the price of that extremely handy wooden kite flying puppet.

This was also why he had been able to deceive the strange moth.

Now, thinking back on it, tremors came from Luo Chen's mind again and again.

As for this pain, he did not know whether it came from having actively severed his divine soul, or from fear of Soulbreak Cliff.

Tai Sui had once said that Fallen Sea was even more dangerous than Soulbreak Cliff.

But if Luo Chen were to judge, with preparations made in advance, Fallen Sea was relatively safer.

That Soulbreak Cliff, however, seemed capable of devouring the souls of all living beings. Once one stepped into it, one would be dazed and muddled, buried within without even knowing why.

What kind of fortune of heaven and earth could have formed such a forbidden land?

Luo Chen shook his head and endured the stabbing pains that came from his mind from time to time, then looked around.

"Where is this?"

Previously, while being hunted, he had flown at full strength and plunged headlong into the depths of the Demon Sea.

Without realizing it, he had long since exceeded Luo Chen's geographical knowledge of the Demon Sea.

For a time, he could not identify his specific location.

Withdraw and return?

That was impossible.

When the puppet double was attacked by the moth demon beast, it had sensed Moon Wanderer's aura.

This showed that Moon Wanderer was still relentlessly pursuing him.

Now he had lost the wooden kite, his mana was not at its peak, and his divine soul was also unstable and shaken. If he encountered Moon Wanderer, it would be ten deaths without life.

"I can only follow this road into the dark!"

In an instant, Luo Chen made his decision.

He stepped forward, using mana to cover Sang Jinghe as they advanced through the seafloor neither quickly nor slowly.

Whenever they encountered demon beasts moving through the deep sea, even somewhat weaker third-tier Demon Kings, he chose to conceal himself and avoid them.

He tried his best not to trigger new battles.

At this time, he missed Black King more and more.

If Black King were here, carrying him to roam freely through the deep sea, who knew how much worry it would save?

Unfortunately, Black King was still unconscious inside the spirit beast bag.

Previously, Luo Chen had fed him several spirit pills, and had even squeezed a few drops of essence blood containing Tai Sui vitality from his own body, forcibly preserving Black King's life.

But to make him wake again was absolutely not something that could be accomplished by letting him sleep inside a spirit beast bag.

"I have to quickly find a place to settle down!"

"Not only for my own cultivation, but also to save Black King as much as possible."

...

Time flowed quietly during that unconscious stroll along the seafloor.

Half a year later, a figure silently emerged from the sea surface.

Ahead of him was a gigantic island, its scale no smaller than Cold Light Island, which Luo Chen had once visited.

However, unlike Cold Light Island, this giant island was covered in rising and falling peaks, its ridges winding and stretching, filled with a primal atmosphere.

Perhaps those peaks were not particularly tall. Seen from afar, they looked like earthen mounds pushed up by some creature tunneling beneath the ground.

But within the mountains, the forests were lush and green, full of life.

Here and there, some lakes dotted the landscape.

Together with the thin spirit qi of heaven and earth, this place actually gave one the feeling of a paradise beyond the world.

Luo Chen was startled and uncertain, and asked softly, "Senior, is this really a fourth-tier spirit vein?"

Han Zhan replied, "It should be. I can sense a vigorous vitality flowing through the earth vein. Back when I formed my Nascent Soul in Sky Sail City, I had a similar feeling."

Luo Chen did not understand. With his spirit eye technique activated, he looked toward that giant island.

His eyes could see vitality and death intent, and were especially able to see the direction of earth-vein spirit qi.

In his early years, he had relied on this to find a first-tier spirit vein cavern beneath Slanted Moon Valley.

But observing now, he truly did not see anything strange.

At most, there was a third-tier spirit vein level in the center of this giant island.

Was it actually hidden this deeply?

"If it is a fourth-tier spirit vein, there must be a powerful demon beast occupying it."

"But from what I can see, let alone a fourth-tier Demon Emperor, there is not even a single somewhat formidable third-tier demon beast here."

"Senior, are you certain your perception is not mistaken?"

Han Zhan also hesitated somewhat.

But very quickly, he said firmly, "It should not be wrong! At least according to my perception, it is so. Moreover, with this island's majestic... vastness, its own earth-vein foundation cannot be too poor. As for why the spirit qi does not show, you will have to personally investigate."

Luo Chen thought for a while, then nodded lightly.

Over half a year, he had gone deeper and deeper into the Demon Sea.

What he had seen and heard made him trust the rumors about the Demon Sea more and more.

Powerful land demons and sea beasts were seen everywhere!

Any spirit land of decent quality was basically occupied by formidable demon beasts.

And this was still under the circumstances where large numbers of top demon beasts had been sealed inside Arctic Yemo Heaven.

Now he was alone and his condition was not very good, so he did not dare rashly raise arms.

After finally finding a strange ownerless land, no matter what, he could not let it go again.

"Then let us investigate!"

Luo Chen gathered his spirit and slowly flew out of the sea.

With a beckon of his hand, Sang Jinghe also floated out.

"Are you all right?"

Sang Jinghe shook his head. "Thank you for your concern, Senior."

Luo Chen sighed. "These past six months have had you follow me running east and west, hiding our heads and covering our tails. It has truly wronged you."

The honest Sang Jinghe said firmly, "Since I have already decided to follow Senior, a little running about is nothing. Besides, it is this junior who has dragged down your elder self."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and said nothing more.

Bringing Sang Jinghe, he stepped onto the solid ground.

As soon as he landed, his footprints sank slightly.

Luo Chen lowered his head and looked. The thick ground was actually loose soil, and for a moment had not withstood the weight of his body.

Sang Jinghe looked slightly surprised.

He bent down and grabbed a handful of soil.

Thin spirit qi emitted from the soil.

"First-tier spirit soil?"

Luo Chen was also somewhat surprised, but did not think much of it. Demon Sea islands were uncultivated by humans, and over a long time, it was not strange for them to contain spirit qi. He continued walking forward.

Sang Jinghe also hurriedly followed.

However, as the two of them walked deeper and deeper into this giant island, their originally calm hearts began to surge.

For no other reason!

The quality of the soil on this island was incomparably high!

For example, the Azure Pouch Soil Luo Chen had seen back in Five Elements Heaven's Wood Heaven Plain was seen here everywhere.

There were even other high-grade spirit soils, such as Life-Transformation Soil and Five-Color Mud.

With these spirit soils gathered together, the soil quality of this place was extremely high in grade, with the highest even reaching third tier!

Especially, Luo Chen could feel that at the very center, there might even be a chance to see fourth-tier spirit soil!

"No wonder the spirit qi concentration here is thin. So it has all merged into the spirit soil, bound there and unable to drift into the void."

Luo Chen was thoughtful.

At this moment, Sang Jinghe's face was filled with a sickly flush.

"This place is simply the holy land every spirit planter dreams of!"

Indeed!

Spirit soil was everywhere, and spirit springs gurgled. Some obscure spirit soils required for cultivating high-grade medicinal materials were also everywhere.

With such a foundation, even an ordinary Sage upper sect might not necessarily possess it.

Perhaps only those upper sects with inheritances of several thousand years could cultivate spirit soil like this.

If it were used properly, divided into categories, built into spirit fields, and arranged with formations, who knew how many rare medicinal materials could be grown here.

"So what kind of existence created such a spirit land, and why has no one touched it?"

As Luo Chen murmured, his gaze fell on a patch of loose soil.

Chit, chit, chit...

Amid rustling sounds, a long worm drilled out from the soil.

Perhaps because it saw strangers, that long worm hurriedly tried to burrow back underground.

But a mana shield descended and drew it before him.

"Dragon Earthworm?"

Sang Jinghe looked in surprise at the gray long worm twisting before Luo Chen.

Faint inspiration emerged in Luo Chen's mind as he nodded to Sang Jinghe.

"It is indeed a Dragon Earthworm, and its grade is not low. It has already reached second tier."

Dragon Earthworms had incomparably gentle temperaments.

Moreover, they were not naturally known for size. Even after growing to second or third tier, few had bodies over ten zhang long.

This kind of demon beast was the favorite of alchemy sects.

Whenever they appeared on the market, they were often bought up in a rush.

Because Dragon Earthworms were very suitable for cultivating spirit fields and improving spirit soil quality.

The mucus they secreted possessed rich water and earth spirit qi. Added to their liking for drilling back and forth through soil, loosening dense spirit soil that easily clumped, this made them even more suitable for the growth of spirit plants.

Previously, Luo Chen had not noticed them, placing all his thoughts on searching for high-tier demon beasts.

At this moment, following that conjecture in his heart, he released his divine sense and drove it into the ground as much as possible.

Sure enough!

Within the earth layers, one Dragon Earthworm after another was spread everywhere.

From those without a tier, to first-tier and second-tier ones, they were countless!

Only third-tier and above were nowhere to be seen.

Seeing this scene, the conjecture in Luo Chen's heart became increasingly certain.

"Jinghe, wait here and do not wander around. I will go and return."

Sang Jinghe nodded.

After Luo Chen left, he even took a cloth bag from his body and continuously stuffed soil into it.

As he packed soil, he said excitedly:

"These are all premium soils! I should pack more away. In the future, when I bring them back to cultivate spirit soil, there will be soil seed."

...

"You guessed it?"

"It seems Senior also thought of it!"

Low in the air, Luo Chen skimmed over the ground at extreme speed.

Circling this scenic giant island rapidly, he spoke with Han Zhan while searching for something.

Han Zhan's soft laugh came beside his ear.

"Indeed, when you saw those Dragon Earthworms, you should have discovered it as well."

"Yes. If my guess is not wrong, this giant island should be the cultivation site of that Fallen Abyss Dragon Sect. He went to the Demon-Fall Land to seek opportunity, taking away all the somewhat stronger Dragon Earthworms and leaving behind only these unpromising children and grandchildren. Because his reputation was known far and wide, even after he left for twenty years, no other formidable demon beasts dared come and lay claim to this island."

"That is my guess as well. Now that the Fallen Abyss Dragon Sect is trapped inside the Demon-Fall Land, with life and death unknown, whether or not he can emerge in the future, he will at least have to wait two or three hundred years. This island, instead, has temporarily become an ownerless land."

Two or three hundred years!

Luo Chen's eyes shone. Two or three hundred years was enough for him to break through to the Sage realm.

And now, what he needed to do was find the Fallen Abyss Dragon Sect's true cultivation place.

That Dragon Sect was extremely powerful!

With the strength of a single demon, it had dominated Yue Canglan, showing its ability.

For such an existence to have no fourth-tier spirit vein as its foothold was simply impossible.

Combined with Han Zhan's previous perception, Luo Chen was extremely certain this island contained a hidden fourth-tier spirit vein.

Abruptly!

Luo Chen's steps stopped before a deep, emerald pool.

Looking at that deep pool, Luo Chen blinked.

A natural illusion formation!

He did not hesitate at all and strode in.

The pool water was clear and cold, yet its depths could not be seen. As Luo Chen sank step by step, his body suddenly halted.

The next instant, three roars sounded.

Luo Chen's expression did not change, and a spark appeared at his fingertip.

The spark gradually brightened, transforming into a blazing sun that shone high in the darkness.

Gentle-tempered Dragon Earthworms possessed low battle strength, but there were few things that countered them.

They were skilled in water and earth and benefited the wood element. Metal objects might sever their bodies, but they could split and survive.

Only fire spirit objects countered this demon most completely!

Sure enough.

Under Luo Chen's Blazing Sun Art, those three figures hesitated.

"Unexpectedly, in a mere twenty years, three third-tier Dragon Earthworms have been born here again."

Seeing that Luo Chen was about to use the Blazing Sun Art to kill these three Dragon Earthworm Demon Kings, Han Zhan hurriedly stopped him. "This kind of demon beast, aside from cultivating spirit soil, is also an extremely excellent formation-breaking spirit beast. Killing them would truly be too great a pity."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. He listened to the old senior's suggestion and did not strike the killing blow.

The Blazing Sun Art remained active. His hands grabbed repeatedly, and three Azure Yang Great Handprints broke through the air.

When they flew back, the three Dragon Earthworm Demon Kings had already been captured.

Luo Chen casually placed three restrictions, sealing their demon cores. Then he took a deep breath and, holding the blazing sun, advanced step by step toward the dark abyss.

As he moved forward, the concentration of spirit qi around him became denser and denser.

Lower-grade third tier!

Middle-grade third tier!

Upper-grade!

Until Luo Chen landed in an abyss a full thousand zhang long, filled with damp miasma and mucus.

Ignoring this foul environment, his face was filled with wild joy.

Feeling the incomparably dense spirit qi of heaven and earth around him, Luo Chen took a satisfied breath.

"A fourth-tier spirit vein!"

"Such dense spirit qi of heaven and earth is bound within this abyss. No wonder none of it shows outside."

"This place shall be where I attain the Dao of my great Sage road!"

...

Three months later.

On the island Luo Chen had named Dragon Abyss Island, Sang Jinghe stood respectfully before a tall woman with lively brows and eyes.

"Senior Heavenjade, according to your instructions, I have already selected suitable places and planted the Purple Monkey Flowers."

"In addition, the Divine Iris has already matured. I will harvest it as soon as possible."

"Those Dragon Saliva Grasses are somewhat troublesome. Although this place is very suitable for planting them, Dragon Earthworms themselves like eating this grass. I need corresponding formation protection to ensure the countless Dragon Earthworms here do not destroy the Dragon Saliva Grass."

Heavenjade listened quietly as Sang Jinghe spoke in detail.

After he finished, Heavenjade nodded. "None of these are problems. After I report to Master, he will make arrangements. In addition, do you have any method to cultivate that Demon-Beast Heartlink Tree?"

Sang Jinghe's face revealed difficulty. "This junior's talent is insufficient. The Demon-Beast Heartlink Tree is far too high in grade, and I have never seen this kind of spirit plant before. In the short term, I cannot think of how to cultivate it."

Heavenjade frowned slightly, but did not blame Sang Jinghe.

Even Master was helpless before this kind of spirit plant, let alone a mere late-stage Initiate like Sang Jinghe.

"Forget it. Study it for now!"

"When you have time, make an inventory of the other naturally growing medicinal grasses on the island as well."

"I will go see Master first."

After speaking, Heavenjade flew into the depths of Dragon Abyss Island.

She stepped into the emerald waves and went deep into the earth abyss.

After crossing layer upon layer of darkness, she saw a trace of light.

Within her vision, a clean and tidy thatched hut stood upon underground stone.

But what drew Heavenjade's eyes most was the man crouching outside the hut, reaching out to gently stroke a large black python.

"Master, I have returned."

"Mm."

Without turning his head, Luo Chen asked, "How were the matters I instructed you to handle?"

Heavenjade immediately recounted Sang Jinghe's situation one by one.

After listening, Luo Chen was not surprised.

"Set aside the Dragon Saliva Grass in the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion for now."

"As for the Demon-Beast Heartlink Tree, let Sang Jinghe study it. Whether it can be kept alive can be discussed later."

"As for the formation matter, after I find time to meet Senior Han Zhan and discuss it with him, we should be able to produce a good method."

After speaking, he looked toward Heavenjade.

"How are your injuries?"

"They are no issue. Although there are many injuries, none are fatal. I only need to slowly recuperate."

After Heavenjade finished speaking, her gaze fell on that black jiaolong-python, and a trace of pity appeared in her eyes.

"Can Black King still hold on?"

Luo Chen's expression was not very good. "With my essence blood and spirit pills sustaining him, Black King can still barely survive for now. He has also entered a hibernating state through the Cliff Serpent method, which can preserve his life to the greatest extent. But as time passes..."

Seeing Heavenjade bite her lip tightly, Luo Chen patted her shoulder.

"Do not worry. Since he risked his life to protect me back then, I naturally will not stand by and watch him die. In my heart, I already have a rough method to save him, but there are still several key points I have not sorted through clearly. We must wait a little longer."

As Luo Chen spoke these words, a decision faintly formed in his heart.

*(End of Chapter)*
