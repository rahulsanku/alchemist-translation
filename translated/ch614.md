# Chapter 614: Lushan Talisman Notes, Elder Sang Blocks the Way

The business deal Lord Lushan spoke of was not complicated.

Over this year, through her connections in Dragon Abyss Immortal City, she had specially inquired into Luo Chen's recent situation.

She learned that although he was a wandering cultivator and had not established any force, there was still a Foundation Establishment family under his command for him to use.

That Foundation Establishment family had not been famous at the beginning, but relying on the name of Devil Lord Azure Yang, it had grown increasingly prosperous over these years. Whether the family's own businesses or Azure Yang Hall, its shop in Dragon Abyss Immortal City, everything had gradually gotten on track.

Especially after Devil Lord Azure Yang defeated Huangfu Song and forced him back, the number of wandering cultivators going to Azure Yang Hall to shop had grown larger and larger.

This was also why Lord Lushan did not question Luo Chen at all when he said he had his ways of making money.

Her business was that she wanted to cooperate with Luo Chen and place the talismans she crafted in Azure Yang Hall for consignment.

Lord Lushan's origins were in truth not especially obscure.

Like Huangfu Song, she also came from a mortal dynasty in the Northern Sea and had been an imperial princess.

But born clever, she detested the deceit and power struggles inside the imperial clan. From childhood, she liked reading Daoist scriptures and was fascinated by immortal tales.

Later, to avoid a marriage alliance, she entered a Daoist temple supported by the imperial household at a young age and became a female Daoist.

After entering the temple, she placed her main interests on cultivation and the art of talismans.

When seas had turned to mulberry fields and all things had changed, she no longer held the slightest attachment to the imperial clan and stepped alone onto the long road of immortal cultivation.

What her cultivation resources relied on was her outstanding talisman-making art.

Over these years in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, even without forming factions, she had lived like a fish in water. From this, one could see how good the quality of the talismans she traded to fellow cultivators was.

But the number of Golden Core cultivators in the Myriad Immortals Assembly was limited.

Especially when there was no major war, talismans, these one-time consumables, had an extremely low usage rate.

Over time, her source of wealth was also greatly affected.

With fewer spirit stones earned, the rent for a third-tier cave dwelling hung over her head like a sharp sword. Even someone as elegant as she was had no choice but to consider vulgar yellow and white things.

"For high-level talismans, this humble Daoist has her own trade channels. Fellow Daoist need not trouble yourself."

"However, under long-term talisman crafting, this humble Daoist has indeed accumulated a large batch of low-level talismans. I could have sold them through the Demon Hunter Office, but their price suppression is too fierce. If I sold them myself, it would be too troublesome. Thus, I thought of Azure Yang Hall under Fellow Daoist's command."

"Of course, this humble Daoist will not borrow Fellow Daoist's manpower for free. For every talisman sold through Azure Yang Hall, you may take twenty percent."

Twenty percent.

This level of concession was indeed very high.

One had to know that Lord Lushan crafted the talismans, while Azure Yang Hall provided no assistance whatsoever. That meant there was no cost at all on Luo Chen's side.

Regarding this, Luo Chen frowned and thought.

Seeing that he did not answer for a long time, Lord Lushan's elegant brows furrowed slightly.

"Fellow Daoist, the sincerity I offer is quite sufficient. Do not be too greedy."

Unexpectedly, Luo Chen laughed freely.

"Lord Lushan, you worry too much. It is not too little. It is that you are giving too much. In my view, a ten-percent commission is completely enough."

Lord Lushan froze.

Who would take benefits delivered into their hand and then give them back?

"However, I have one condition... or rather, I should say request." Under the other party's puzzled gaze, Luo Chen smiled. "Could Fellow Daoist also give some third-tier talismans to Azure Yang Hall for sale? The quantity need not be large. Supplying a few steadily each year would be enough."

"A few?" Lord Lushan lowered her head and sank into thought.

Among the hundred arts of immortal cultivation, apart from the four main arts of alchemy, formations, artifacts, and talismans, there was not much hierarchy.

Among cultivators who mastered the four arts, alchemy cultivators and formation cultivators were still ahead of artifact-forgers and talisman cultivators.

The reason was simply that alchemists had too high a training cost, while formation masters relied excessively on talent, and their respective products had too great an effect on cultivators' cultivation and on the enhancement of large forces.

By comparison, artifact-forgers and talisman cultivators were slightly cheaper.

Especially talisman cultivators. It was the sort of art easy to learn but difficult to master. In the cultivation world, many people practiced talisman-making on the side.

This also meant the market for talismans was not especially good.

But that referred only to low-level talismans.

The market for high-level talismans, like high-level pills and high-quality magic treasures, was always scorching hot.

For Lord Lushan herself, drawing a third-tier talisman also required a good deal of effort.

While maintaining her original channels steadily, squeezing out a few extra for Azure Yang Hall would affect her daily cultivation.

But no matter how much it affected her, it was a small matter.

If she could not earn enough spirit stones, she would have to say farewell to her third-tier cave dwelling in the Fulong Mountain Range.

Had she not taken the initiative to accept a ten-year long-term garrison mission precisely to raise her demon hunter star rank and obtain a higher discount?

Lord Lushan raised her head and looked at Luo Chen. "Must they be high-level talismans?"

Luo Chen nodded. "It would be best to have them. For this, even if the third-tier talismans are sold, I can take no commission on those talismans."

Uh...

Lord Lushan became somewhat confused again.

She had thought that Luo Chen had proactively reduced the commission and asked her to take out high-level talismans for the sake of greater profit. Yet Daoist Azure Yang also did not want commission from third-tier talismans. What did this mean?

She thought hard, gradually grasping the flavor of it.

"You want to borrow the reputation of my high-level talismans to bring more customers to Azure Yang Hall, thereby allowing you to gain more profit from other small businesses, yes?"

Luo Chen struck his palm in praise. "Fellow Daoist is also quite skilled in merchant affairs."

Lord Lushan smiled bitterly. She simply disliked such vulgar matters. It did not mean she was stupid.

After thinking slightly, based on the principle that merchants absolutely did not take losses, she could naturally infer Luo Chen's idea.

But for the other party to be so open and unavoidant also showed the uprightness of a gentleman.

After thinking for a moment, Lord Lushan nodded.

"Very well. Three."

"Each year, apart from those first-tier and second-tier talismans, I will provide three additional third-tier talismans. However, the effects of the talismans may vary. On this point, Daoist Azure Yang, you will have to be understanding."

She was someone who liked making talismans. Beyond common talismans, she often improved talismans and even developed new talismans.

During this process, there were successes and failures.

If those defective products she was not satisfied with could be exchanged for enough profit through Azure Yang Hall, that would clearly be a perfect arrangement.

Luo Chen agreed to this cooperation without hesitation.

"There are still three months. This humble Daoist will go back and sort through that old batch of talismans, and at the same time rush out some third-tier talismans for you. It should still be in time."

Luo Chen blinked. "Will this affect Fellow Daoist's cultivation?"

Lord Lushan shook her head with a bitter smile. "To say there will be no effect is impossible. The time is tight, after all. But compared to several decades or even a hundred years of cultivation, the loss of a mere three months does not count for much."

After saying this, she left the beach one step ahead.

Only Luo Chen remained, strolling leisurely along the beach.

When Black King returned from his wild play, what he saw was Luo Chen's smiling face.

"Master, your mood seems very good today. Are you happy because this little one's Dao has advanced?"

"You certainly think highly of yourself."

Luo Chen laughed and scolded, walking back beneath the evening glow.

His happy mood did indeed have something to do with Black King's advancement in Dao, but it was more because of this cooperation with Lord Lushan.

Once the other party's high-level talismans were sold in Azure Yang Hall, the chain reaction would not be as simple as a few third-tier talismans.

Within the Myriad Immortals Assembly, which cultivator group was most numerous?

Not the seven Nascent Soul Sages, nor the hundreds of Golden Core cultivators, but the countless Qi Refinement and Foundation Establishment cultivators.

To these people, magic treasures were hard to obtain, and formations were difficult to master. Only talismans, which could be activated beyond one's realm, were the most convenient items.

When the news spread that Azure Yang Hall had large quantities of high-quality talismans and even third-tier talismans, its doors would surely be crowded like a market.

At the same time, his original plan to sell defensive magic treasures through Azure Yang Hall would also receive a huge boost.

Perhaps before long, Azure Yang Hall would become one of the great boutique shops with a name in the Myriad Immortals Assembly.

And the profit Luo Chen gained would not be only in spirit stones.

Azure Yang Hall sold goods, naturally, but it also purchased goods.

With so many wandering cultivators trading with Azure Yang Hall, he could fully use this shop to issue missions for the resources he needed.

And what resources did Luo Chen need?

Nothing else but the three main materials of the Nascent Soul Pill.

Now that he had Peng Lake, this third-tier upper-grade spirit land, his cultivation speed advanced a thousand li a day.

In a short time, he had advanced to Golden Core Stage Five, and the date when he would break through to Golden Core Stage Six could be counted on one's fingers.

After Golden Core Stage Six came the late Golden Core stage.

At that time, he would have no choice but to consider condensing a Nascent Soul like other great cultivators.

He had already comprehended a Nascent Soul formation method.

With Han Zhan, this senior, providing guidance from the side, he could also avoid many detours.

But if he wanted to add several more chances of success for Nascent Soul formation, then the Nascent Soul Pill was also indispensable.

Although Luo Chen possessed cultivation speed comparable to a heavenly spirit root, he was not actually a heavenly spirit root. Whether he would encounter immense difficulties like during Foundation Establishment and Core Formation when forming a Nascent Soul was still hard to say.

Therefore, the Nascent Soul Pill had to be refined.

Over these years, he had gradually collected the related auxiliary materials, and they were almost complete.

Only the three main materials were choking him.

At present, Luo Chen only had one Divine Iris, obtained from the storage bag of Qian Ting, a late Golden Core cultivator.

This item was a Nascent Soul-forming spirit medicine known throughout the cultivation world to have wondrous effects on Nascent Soul formation.

It was normal for Qian Ting to have collected one.

But the other two main materials, Purple Monkey Flower and Five Elements Lotus Platform, Luo Chen had not collected even the slightest bit of over these years.

Even when he borrowed the Demon Hunter Office's intelligence system, he gained nothing.

Forget the medicinal materials, there was not even any news.

"Once Azure Yang Hall's name spreads, wandering cultivators will gather there. Perhaps I will be able to collect one or two. Even if not, news of the spirit medicines would also be excellent."

"There is still time. Slowly, over years and months, there will always be some harvest."

Luo Chen murmured.

...

Three months later.

Or rather, after Luo Chen arrived on Quyang Island, the one-year period finally reached its end.

This also meant his mission was finally over.

"Fellow Daoist Wang, who is replacing your garrison mission, arrived two days ago. You have already met him. As for the other one, although he has not arrived yet, that is no great harm. With me holding this place, no major problems will arise in the short term."

"This is the batch of talismans I prepared. Among them, there are one hundred and fifty-one first-tier talismans, and second-tier talismans are the most numerous, reaching five hundred. I rushed out two third-tier talismans. One is the Thousand-Li Divine Travel Talisman. Once activated, it can briefly erupt with extreme speed, covering a thousand li in only a few breaths. The other is the Water Giant Talisman. After refinement, it requires the owner to nurture it for half a year. Once activated, it can generate a water giant with the same realm as the cultivator's main body. This giant is not only powerful in battle, but if it is in a place rich with water vapor, it can be undying and indestructible within the time it takes to burn one stick of incense."

"As for the third talisman, it still needs a little more work. After I finish drawing it later, I will send someone to deliver it to you."

"In addition, to prevent the low-level cultivators of Azure Yang Hall from failing to understand the essence of my talismans and selling them too cheaply, I specially prepared a comprehensive talisman manual that introduces each situation in detail."

Luo Chen received a storage bag and a new book that looked freshly copied.

Lushan Talisman Notes.

Luo Chen raised a brow. "Fellow Daoist has been thoughtful."

Lord Lushan shook her head. "It is only for livelihood. Naturally, one should be thoughtful."

"In that case, this humble one will go first. After Fellow Daoist's ten-year term ends, you are welcome to visit my Peng Lake."

Luo Chen cupped his hands, then leapt into the air and flew out of Quyang Island.

When he reached the shore, he waited a short while, and a huge figure emerged from the sea.

It was Black King.

His Dao had advanced recently, and he increasingly disliked staying inside the Primordial Unity Cauldron's space.

In his words, the space inside was like a prison. Unless he was sleeping, he did not want to enter it again in this life.

Luo Chen did not force him. Since it would take several days to hurry back, and Luo Chen did not want to waste mana steering the flying boat, he simply let Black King serve as transport.

With a leap, Luo Chen landed on Black King's back and sat cross-legged.

His mana changed slightly, and a Glazed Heavenly Canopy enveloped him, blocking the wind and rain outside.

Afterward, Black King gave a cheer and dove beneath the water's surface, swimming at extremely high speed toward the Myriad Immortals Assembly headquarters.

...

With Black King's speed, the time needed to return to the Fulong Mountain Range would only be faster than by flying boat.

With nothing to do, Luo Chen simply opened the book Lord Lushan had gifted him.

Its name was Lushan Talisman Notes, clearly a record of the other party's path in talismans.

After skimming it, however, Luo Chen made another discovery.

This woman had not only recorded information about the talismans she had personally drawn. She had also compiled a complete guide to crafting basic low-level talismans.

The latter had to be read with divine sense before it could be seen.

"What is this? Returning peaches for plums?"

Luo Chen was speechless, unsure whether to laugh or cry.

Although he had yielded profit, it was for the sake of his own greater benefit.

Unexpectedly, Lord Lushan was the sort who could not accept favors from others, and had returned the gift with a complete guide to crafting basic talismans.

Although he had only skimmed it briefly, Luo Chen had already discovered that if one truly followed the guidance above and studied talisman-making, given enough time, one would have the chance to become a low-level talisman master with solid foundations.

"This Lord Lushan is someone worth befriending."

Luo Chen sighed with feeling, then continued studying the book.

Only, his mood was no longer the same as before, when he had been casually skimming to pass the time. He now paid somewhat more attention.

There was no other reason. Among the four arts of alchemy, formations, artifacts, and talismans, the one originally most suited to him was not formations or artifact-forging, nor even the alchemy art in which he took pride. It was precisely the talisman art.

Because the essence of talisman-making was preserving the spells a cultivator excelled at on special talisman skins through special techniques.

In other words, the deeper a cultivator's understanding of spells, the greater the boost to talisman-making.

And among Luo Chen's many methods, the one he had the greatest talent for was spells.

At present, he had to devote his energy to refining the Demon Emperor shed husk and cultivation, and could not also cultivate talisman-making. But it was always good to understand more.

...

As Luo Chen focused on studying Lushan Talisman Notes, time quickly passed.

Black King continued forward, and the unfamiliar scenery along the way was constantly left behind like a film playing in reverse.

Just as they were leaving the Quyang Sea Region.

Suddenly.

Black King's forward movement stopped.

Luo Chen frowned and lifted his head from beneath the sea surface.

From above came a voice.

"Daoist Azure Yang, traveling alone is rather lonely. Why not come aboard for a meeting?"

He lightly patted the jiaolong's scales, which were like metal. Black King understood and slowly floated his huge body upward.

Beneath the sea surface, a red-robed Daoist shrouded in a pale blue water barrier appeared riding a dragon.

In the sky, a huge and luxurious flying boat circled in midair, looking down at them from above.

From atop it, waves of startled cries came at this sight.

Luo Chen paid none of them any attention. He only focused on the wrinkled old man at the prow.

"Sang Jiugong."

*(End of Chapter)*
