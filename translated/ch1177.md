# Chapter 1177: Turbid World Martial Grandmaster, Divine Intent Manifests

At the center of the fourth ring.

On the central platform leading to the third ring, chaotic energy cascaded down like a waterfall, pouring ceaselessly from above to below, day and night.

Two figures, one male and one female, arrived together.

Their gazes fell upon the central platform. The two exchanged a glance, then spoke in unison with tacit understanding.

"Turbid World Martial Grandmaster, please grace us with your presence!"

A combination of two different types of astral qi—one firm, one gentle—joined forces, and the resulting sound waves forcibly charged into the energy waterfall.

The next moment.

An extremely aged figure, short and stooped, with a face like thousand-year-old withered wood, slowly walked out from within the waterfall.

The chaotic energy around him continuously scoured his body, yet could not shake his footsteps in the slightest.

Such a sight was utterly astonishing within these Nine Rings of Annihilation.

Even the three Martial Immortals of the upper three rings likely could not achieve such a feat.

But this old man traversed it as if on level ground.

All because he was the number one Martial Grandmaster within the fourth ring—Turbid World Martial Grandmaster!

Before him, whether it was the bear-like Ferocious Martial Grandmaster or the enchantingly beautiful Thousand Illusions Martial Grandmaster, both couldn't help but show expressions of admiration, instinctively bowing as they took the initiative to greet the diminutive Turbid World Martial Grandmaster.

In response to their friendly gestures, Turbid World Martial Grandmaster's tone was not particularly pleasant.

His voice, sounding as if rust had coated his throat, issued a grating sound.

"Why do you disturb me? You should know that my lifespan is short, and every moment might be my last chance. You'd best give me a suitable reason, otherwise..."

As he spoke, his turbid eyes swept over the two.

Though they contained no killing intent, they sent chills down one's spine.

Ferocious looked at the woman beside him. Thousand Illusions had no choice but to steel herself and carefully explain the reason for their audience.

...

"Leftover Immortal, alchemist, entered the Nine Rings of Annihilation just over fifty years ago, and has already stepped into the late Leakless realm." After hearing the introduction, Turbid World Martial Grandmaster extracted the key information, but still remained largely indifferent.

Both were at the late Leakless realm, both revered as Martial Grandmasters, but there were gaps even among Grandmasters.

The nine Grandmasters of the fourth ring, if they joined forces, were said to be able to slaughter all the martial artists of the lower three rings.

But he, Turbid World Martial Grandmaster, could single-handedly defeat the other eight!

This was the confidence brought by years of training, standing firm on the central platform against the torrent of chaotic energy!

Thousand Illusions Martial Grandmaster sighed. "It's not that we two are cowards, but that the battle record of that Luo Chen Martial Grandmaster is too fierce. In just half a year, he defeated eighteen experts of the fifth ring. After arriving in the fourth ring, in merely a dozen years, all six Martial Grandmasters have fallen to his hand. Including Extreme Yang, Broken Army, and Wolf Slaughter."

"Even that brat Extreme Yang lost?" Turbid World Martial Grandmaster showed a flicker of interest.

Among the nine Grandmasters, he was the strongest, but he never moved about or showed his face. Extreme Yang, however, was the most troublesome one—once a fight started, it was basically a battle to the death.

He hadn't expected that this newly promoted Martial Grandmaster, Luo Chen, had actually defeated even Extreme Yang.

But this interest was merely interest.

Especially the suggestion put forward by Thousand Illusions and Ferocious—it made him sneer with contempt.

What was this about the three of them joining forces to deal with Luo Chen, to suppress his arrogant demeanor?

Leaving aside whether he would even make a move, if he did, he would absolutely disdain resorting to a joint siege!

However, Ferocious, who had been silent all along, suddenly spoke.

With just one sentence, he made Turbid World Martial Grandmaster's eyes light up.

"He, like you, has obtained the Carefree Golden Token. He is a candidate for Immortalhood favored by the Carefree Realm Lord!"

...

Twenty years after entering the fourth ring.

Luo Chen's cultivation base had stabilized at the late Leakless realm. By outside standards, he was a revered Martial Grandmaster, considered one of the strongest below the level of Martial Immortal.

But he was still not satisfied!

Looking at the blood-red astral qi flowing around his body, it was agile yet not without a hint of sluggishness.

That hint of sluggishness was the key.

Sufficient agility meant that through years of cultivation and combat, Luo Chen had already achieved perfect control over his own astral qi, reaching a state where it responded to his will.

But that sluggishness meant that his Blood Refining Divine Astral was ultimately still lacking a certain flavor.

"Divine intent..."

Luo Chen gazed into the void, a trace of confusion on his face.

In his battle with Ice Heaven Son, he had comprehended the seed of divine intent.

But that seed had never been able to bloom and bear fruit.

He knew it was because the external pressure wasn't great enough to accelerate the growth of the divine intent seed.

Whether it was Extreme Yang Martial Grandmaster, Broken Army Martial Grandmaster, or even Wolf Slaughter Martial Grandmaster, whom he had challenged later.

These people were strong, certainly, but ultimately their strength over Ice Heaven Son was limited.

He needed a sharper whetstone!

But within the fourth ring, was there still such a whetstone?

Ordinary Martial Grandmasters could rarely pose a threat to him anymore.

If the remaining three couldn't meet his requirements...

Luo Chen's gaze unconsciously drifted toward the heights of the void...

But soon, he dismissed the thought.

Martial Immortals could indeed pose a great threat to him, but in a battle at that level, he could no longer suppress himself. He would inevitably have to utilize his physical body, even various techniques.

Thus, the purpose of tempering his astral qi would be lost.

The self-created divine ability he was still researching and polishing would also inevitably come to naught.

"I hope the remaining three can give me some inspiration!"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen no longer hesitated and set out again according to his previous plan.

Next, regardless of whether he could gain anything from battle, he would begin seclusion to break through to the Martial Immortal realm.

Even if he couldn't create the martial divine ability he desired, he wouldn't dwell on it.

At worst, after breaking through to the Martial Immortal realm, he could continue exploring.

According to plan, the next to challenge was Ferocious Martial Grandmaster.

Luo Chen had some understanding of this person's situation.

He followed a fierce and domineering style, and even his astral qi was called Overlord Astral Qi. In particular, Ferocious Martial Grandmaster also possessed a body tempered by cross-training.

From this perspective, it was somewhat similar to Luo Chen's current situation.

Therefore, he held some anticipation for this battle.

However, upon arriving at the destination, there was no one to be seen.

Luo Chen was taken aback.

"Ran away?"

It seemed absurd, but after waiting in vain for three days, Luo Chen still saw no trace of him, so he had to head for the next target.

Thousand Illusions Martial Grandmaster!

She was one of only two female Martial Grandmasters in the fourth ring.

Completely opposite to Ferocious Martial Grandmaster, this woman excelled at using softness to overcome hardness, often able to take an enemy's full-force attack with only three-tenths of her own power.

It was said that she had also incorporated some special substance into her astral qi, giving it its miraculous effect.

But just like before, Luo Chen did not find Thousand Illusions Martial Grandmaster.

At this point, Luo Chen vaguely sensed something was wrong.

But he wasn't particularly concerned.

He had some anticipation for these two planned battles, but it wasn't especially strong.

In one-on-one combat, he was absolutely confident he could take them down.

Since they had fled without a fight, he would simply go directly to the one he anticipated the most!

...

"Turbid World Martial Grandmaster, Luo Chen requests a bout!"

Amidst the surging storm, a grand voice accompanied a figure stepping out of the void, rumbling loudly. The surrounding chaotic energy was actually pushed aside in neat rows by the sound waves.

The moment he appeared, Luo Chen's gaze suddenly sharpened.

"So, you've all been waiting here for me!"

As far as his eyes could see, a man and a woman stood in midair. Before them stood a short, weathered old man with his hands clasped behind his back.

Thousand Illusions Martial Grandmaster cupped her hands. "Apologies. Everyone has come to the Nine Rings of Annihilation for bitter cultivation and to scale new heights. Meaningless battles are truly unnecessary. I hope you will understand and retreat."

Luo Chen waved his hand. "No need to apologize. I am the one who broke the rules, so I should naturally accept any form of counterattack. Since all three of you are here, why not come at me together?"

His words were plain, but the arrogance and confidence within them were utterly undisguised!

Turbid World Martial Grandmaster looked at Luo Chen, his turbid eyes flashing with streaks of light.

He did not say anything confrontational. He merely waved his hand.

The next moment, Thousand Illusions and Ferocious stepped forward together.

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

"You're not coming?"

Turbid World Martial Grandmaster did not answer. Ferocious, however, spoke in a muffled voice. "Your opponent now is us!"

"Very well, then let's begin!"

Luo Chen kept some of his attention on the short old man, then personally faced the two powerhouses.

Neither side held back. The moment they struck, wind and thunder roared, and air currents howled.

The first to engage Luo Chen was Ferocious Martial Grandmaster.

Both of them favored straightforward, unadorned moves—punch after punch exchanged head-on.

First exchange: evenly matched.

Luo Chen was surprised and urged his astral qi further.

Ferocious was even more shocked. Even though he had imagined Luo Chen was strong, he had never expected that in a direct confrontation, the other could withstand his Overlord Astral Qi and his innate divine strength.

Second exchange: the void trembled.

Luo Chen did not retreat an inch, but Ferocious's face flushed red.

He suddenly discovered that within each of Luo Chen's punches was contained thirty-six distinct variations of astral qi.

Like great waves, layer upon layer, wave upon wave, surging forward relentlessly, making him difficult to withstand.

Third exchange: both sides cast aside all restrictions and charged to close quarters.

"Roar!"

Ferocious's form suddenly changed. His Overlord Astral Qi burst forth, forming a demonic, heaven-defying bear behind him.

As he threw a punch, the bear also punched simultaneously.

Luo Chen's eyes lit up. He still slowly threw a punch.

On his body, acupoints flickered with crystalline light, as if responding.

Infinite astral qi poured out in an instant.

Boom!

Amidst the thunderous sound, Ferocious was actually unable to withstand it. The demon bear phantom behind him swayed precariously.

Just as Luo Chen was about to press the attack, a graceful figure inserted herself into the fray. Slender, delicate hands moved like willow branches in the returning wind, drifting and weaving as they blocked Luo Chen's fist.

The moment they clashed, Luo Chen felt as if his accumulated punch had struck cotton.

His surging Blood Refining Divine Astral vanished like a clay oxen entering the sea, rapidly disappearing.

"Interesting!"

Luo Chen's expression showed surprise, but inwardly he was overjoyed.

He did not withdraw. Instead, he increased his astral qi output.

For a time, the two sides fell into a stalemate.

Thousand Illusions Martial Grandmaster on the opposite side was fine at first, full of confidence. She believed she could neutralize this strike of Luo Chen's, using the technique of deflecting a thousand pounds with four ounces.

But she had not expected that Luo Chen's power was far more than a thousand pounds.

In just two or three breaths, the woman's expression changed.

"Move aside!"

A roar came from behind. A figure shot forward like thunder.

Luo Chen twisted his body, breaking off the contest of strength with Thousand Illusions Martial Grandmaster. Then, without haste, he extended both palms to block the incoming kick.

His body retreated.

But the smile on his face grew ever brighter.

"Let go!"

When he had retreated seven steps, Luo Chen's hands twisted, actually spinning Ferocious Martial Grandmaster's entire body in the air.

Then, sinking his shoulders and lowering his waist, his arm came down like a whip.

Just as it was about to strike, Thousand Illusions Martial Grandmaster followed up at the perfect moment, one hand supporting Ferocious while the other coiled around like a serpent.

Taking the chance to catch his breath, Ferocious, not yet steady on his feet, threw a punch at Luo Chen.

In an instant, the three were entangled.

Fists or palms, pointing fingers or clawing grabs.

Shoulder charges and elbow strikes, legs and feet flying.

The speed of their movements was dazzling.

One firm, one gentle—their coordination complemented each other perfectly.

In the decades since arriving at the Nine Rings of Annihilation, this was the first time Luo Chen had fought so exhilaratingly.

Though the exchange had lasted only moments, it surpassed days of grueling, bitter battle.

In the midst of combat,

his grasp of timing for strikes, his control over force, and the connection between moves became increasingly adept.

To cope with the gap between the transitions of firmness and gentleness, Luo Chen pushed his own combat rhythm to the limit.

If the opponent was domineering, he would be even more domineering.

If the opponent was gentle, he would be as light as a swan skimming water, even gentler.

And within his body, the astral qi, through these countless twists and turns of change, became even more full of spiritual nature.

However!

As the battle dragged on, the two allied Martial Grandmasters, their own astral qi waning, began to fall out of sync in their coordination.

Impatience gradually grew in Luo Chen's heart!

"Almost there, just a little more!"

His eyes, tinged with madness, fixed on the short old man standing motionless in the distance.

As if by tacit understanding.

Turbid World Martial Grandmaster, at that moment, moved!

He took a single step, as if shrinking the earth to an inch, arriving before Luo Chen.

Thousand Illusions and Ferocious swiftly retreated.

Turbid World Martial Grandmaster pointed a finger at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen was prepared. His accumulated punch directly blasted toward the old man.

Fist and finger crossed. Luo Chen's fist heavily struck the old man's body, producing a rumbling roar.

And Turbid World Martial Grandmaster's finger also landed on Luo Chen.

The ever-present Blood Cloth Robe trembled violently, trying to deflect this finger. But as the finger fell, the technique of deflecting and transferring force proved futile.

The Blood Cloth Robe shattered inch by inch.

A complex and profound astral qi was injected into Luo Chen's body.

Boom!

In the eyes of Thousand Illusions and Ferocious, they only saw the two exchange a single move. The next moment, both flew backward.

After each steadied themselves, they did not rush to make a second move.

"Who won?"

"Who lost?"

Turbid World Martial Grandmaster stood still. Amidst the chaotic and disordered aura on his body, a faint but spiritually infused will had emerged.

"So that's it, so that's it..."

Unfortunately, that will was too weak, appearing utterly insignificant before his vast astral qi.

Turbid World Martial Grandmaster murmured to himself, his eyes filled with joy.

But the next instant.

Crack! Crack! Crack!

A scene that caught Thousand Illusions and Ferocious off guard unfolded.

Turbid World Martial Grandmaster's physical body manifested a huge fist hole. The violent chaotic energy from outside took the opportunity to surge in, rapidly engulfing him.

His gaze looked toward Luo Chen in the distance. The joy contained a hint of gratitude, which ultimately turned into desolation.

"Thank you."

After the ultimate peak came sudden fall.

In his head-on collision with Luo Chen, he had ignited his own martial divine intent, but he had also taken Luo Chen's full-force strike at one hundred percent.

That was a strike nearly equivalent to a martial divine ability.

If Turbid World Martial Grandmaster had been younger, he might have survived, and then, based on the gains from this battle, advanced to a higher realm.

But his lifespan was already at its end. This strike was enough to destroy everything.

Truly, hearing the Dao in the morning, dying in the evening was acceptable.

Luo Chen, in the distance, could not hear this "thank you."

Even if he had, he would have had no time to respond.

Because the finger Turbid World Martial Grandmaster had struck him with had injected an extremely complex astral qi.

Sometimes firm, sometimes gentle, impossible to fathom. Scorching hot like the great sun, yet cold like ten-thousand-year profound ice.

Ever-changing, extremely complex.

Only now did Luo Chen realize the nature of the astral qi that Turbid World Martial Grandmaster cultivated.

He had actually absorbed chaotic energy into his body, without excessive refinement, directly nurturing it within.

That was why Turbid World Martial Grandmaster could cultivate on the central platform that everyone else avoided.

If he had truly comprehended the martial divine intent of chaotic astral qi and used it to break through to the Martial Immortal realm, he would have become the strongest martial artist in history!

But how terrifying was chaotic energy? How could a mortal master it?

Even someone as fortunate as Luo Chen only used the Primordial Unity Cauldron to absorb and transform it into easily absorbable energy.

He himself absolutely dared not touch even a trace.

But at this moment.

Turbid World Martial Grandmaster's chaotic astral qi entering his body was like adding a ladle of clear water to boiling oil.

Instantly, everything exploded within him.

Countless strands of Blood Refining Divine Astral, from the eight extraordinary meridians, from the seven hundred and twenty acupoints, all withdrew, gathering in the Qi-Blood Furnace, frantically besieging that strand of chaotic astral qi.

This was not directed by Luo Chen's divided soul, nor driven by Luo Chen's will, but the instinct of the Blood Refining Divine Astral itself.

In wave after wave of purging, a will of supreme dominance slowly but firmly coalesced, bit by bit, from the endless Blood Refining Divine Astral.

And on the outside.

A large amount of Blood Refining Divine Astral actually formed a blood cocoon, heavily enveloping Luo Chen.

Thousand Illusions Martial Grandmaster and Ferocious Martial Grandmaster looked at each other, momentarily unsure how to respond.

The three had joined forces, only to end up with this result.

What should they do now?

Thousand Illusions Martial Grandmaster hesitated. "Destroy the blood cocoon, kill Luo Chen?"

Ferocious Martial Grandmaster thought for a moment, then slowly shook his head. "Although Luo Chen acts arrogantly, in all his challenges within the fourth ring, he has never killed anyone. He practices restraint."

Thousand Illusions frowned. "But Turbid World Martial Grandmaster, he..."

Ferocious countered, "Didn't you hear him thanking Luo Chen even as he lay dying? Perhaps he had long been waiting for this day, merely lacking a suitable opponent."

Just as the two were arguing.

A grand will silently descended upon the fourth ring.

It quietly watched that blood cocoon, without uttering a single word.

(End of this chapter)
