# Chapter 859: Joining Nether Origin, the Biyou Change

Sunlight streamed through the glazed tiles, scattering dappled patterns of light across the resplendent golden hall.

Upon the lofty palace ceiling were carved exquisite murals—powerful cultivators summoning wind and rain, great demons striding across mountains and seas. Blades and swords, blood and fire adorned the scenes, as if recounting the glories of the Eastern Wilderness human race's war of expansion.

Only at the very center was there a blank area, as if left empty, waiting for new content to fill it.

Luo Chen stood quietly in the hall, his gaze sharp as he surveyed everything around him.

He had never known that such a place existed within Lingtian Pass.

Had the Lingtian City Lord not personally brought him here, he likely would never have entered this **Heavenly Water Hall** in his entire life.

And this was precisely the dwelling place of **Black Swamp Ancestor**—one of the three great ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect!

Luo Chen had once asked the Lingtian City Lord directly: if Azure Frost Demon Emperor joined the battle, how could Lingtian Pass withstand her?

The other had replied bluntly: "I'm just afraid she won't come!"

At that time, Luo Chen hadn't understood where the Lingtian City Lord's confidence lay.

Later, when he witnessed the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation, he thought that was the source of that confidence.

But when the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation was forcibly broken by the massive beast tide and the combined efforts of the five great commanders, he realized that formation might not be enough to stop Azure Frost Demon Emperor—at least not to keep her there!

Now, Luo Chen finally understood what that confidence truly was.

A **Divine Transformation** great power!

So it turned out that within Lingtian Pass, a Divine Transformation great power had been personally stationed all along.

If Azure Frost Demon Emperor truly dared to show herself, Black Swamp Ancestor would certainly not stand idly by.

After all, he was the one who had led men to personally storm Green Parasol Mountain back then!

"Quite the patience, indeed!" Luo Chen murmured to himself.

Just then, Luo Chen's soul shuddered.

When he looked up, a black-robed old man was already seated at the head of Heavenly Water Hall.

"So you are Luo Chen?"

Luo Chen's heart tightened. He lowered his head and nodded. "This junior pays respects to the Ancestor!"

The Nether Origin Sect was the only Divine Transformation sacred ground in the Eastern Wilderness, nominally commanding all the sects. Even major matters like the status of superior sects, the right to mint spirit stones, and the war of expansion required the Nether Origin Sect's permission to proceed.

As one of the three great ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect, his status and prestige were not limited to within the sect.

Throughout the entire Eastern Wilderness, anyone who saw him had to respectfully address him as "Ancestor."

When Divine Origin, Joyous Union, and the others had met Black Swamp, it was the same. Now that Luo Chen saw him, he too had to do so.

Yet after the question and answer, the hall fell into a silent atmosphere.

Luo Chen felt a gaze sweep over him—from head to toe, from outside to inside—inch by inch.

As if it had seen through him completely, leaving no secrets hidden.

Such scrutiny was excessively presumptuous, excessively rude!

But Luo Chen was familiar with it, for he often examined those of lower cultivation in the same way.

A long moment passed!

A light laugh came from above, carrying a note of approval.

"Not bad! Not bad!"

"You really are quite something!"

Luo Chen kept his head lowered and did not speak.

Black Swamp Ancestor asked slowly, "Do you know why I summoned you here?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "I do not."

"Heh, pretending not to know." Black Swamp seemed unconcerned. He knew that these juniors always appeared extremely submissive before him, feigning an inability to guess his intentions.

But Luo Chen, who had cultivated to the Sage level and was a rogue cultivator by origin—how could his mind be so simple?

Nor did Black Swamp deign to beat around the bush with Luo Chen. He asked directly, "Have you met Chu Yan?"

"I have."

"In private?"

"No, only in two public settings."

"Then why did she gift you a Heavenly Secret Pill in front of everyone?"

"This junior does not know either. Perhaps the Alchemy Saint took a liking to me as a fellow practitioner of the Dao."

"Alchemy Saint, Alchemy Sect... Hah..."

Black Swamp Ancestor chuckled softly, a laugh tinged with emotion yet also carrying a hint of mockery.

He examined Luo Chen once more. The many deeds of this man in the Eastern Wilderness surfaced in his mind, and a trace of wistfulness appeared on his face.

Those who sought the Dao were as numerous as ox hairs, but those who attained it were as rare as phoenix feathers.

Those who achieved something in the Dao of alchemy were often different from ordinary people.

Either exceptionally talented or extraordinarily persistent. And this Luo Chen—which was he?

"Would you be willing to join my Nether Origin Sect?"

The abrupt question caught Luo Chen off guard.

He clasped his hands. "This junior is already a guest elder of the Star Gate of Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. I'm afraid it would be inappropriate..."

Black Swamp Ancestor waved his hand dismissively. "Just an external affairs guest elder—do you really treasure it that much?"

Luo Chen's lips moved, but he did not refute, nor did he directly accept.

Black Swamp Ancestor frowned instead.

The other's demeanor seemed very soft on the surface, yet there was a hidden firmness within.

Wanting to negotiate terms?

That made sense. For a rogue cultivator to have made such a great name for himself, even surpassing the Nether Origin Sect's most outstanding Thunder Daozi, how could he not have some pride?

Even if he, a great power, extended the invitation personally, the other would not be so frightened as to immediately throw himself into the Nether Origin Sect.

Back then, Chu Yan had traveled far to the Central Continent. What conditions had Heavenly Origin Dao Sect offered to move her?

Black Swamp did not know, but he understood that at this moment, for this alchemist who might be the most outstanding after the Alchemy Saint, the most generous terms should be offered.

"Join my Nether Origin Sect, and you shall be rewarded with divine arts, bestowed with secret techniques, and given full support for cultivation resources!"

"Join my Nether Origin Sect, and you may choose any cultivator servants, maidservants, or concubines you desire!"

"Join my Nether Origin Sect, and the Alchemy Saint Hall shall be under your command. All the alchemy scriptures left by Chu Yan shall be yours to study!"

"In the future, if you wish to achieve the Divine Transformation realm, including myself, all three great ancestors of the Nether Origin Sect will do our utmost to assist you!"

When the last sentence fell, Black Swamp's tone became serious.

These conditions, he would only offer once.

If the other still did not know his place, then don't blame him for...

"Ancestor, my self-founded Luo Tian Sect still has about two hundred people with no place to settle. I cannot abandon them to defect to the sacred ground." Luo Chen said.

Black Swamp was speechless.

He had only thought of Luo Chen as a rogue cultivator, never imagining there were still two hundred or so ants relying on him.

Loyal and righteous, it seemed.

"This is easily done!"

Black Swamp Ancestor said casually, "My Nether Origin Sect is located in the vast Nether Origin Great Marsh, with boundless territory. You may bring them all into the sect. Alternatively, you may choose any masterless land among the thirty-six domains of the Eastern Wilderness, name it your Luo Tian Superior Sect, and settle them there."

Luo Chen raised his head and bowed deeply.

"In that case, this junior is willing to join the Nether Origin Sect!"

At these words, the black-robed elder's face lit up with joy. He called out three times:

"Good! Good! Good!"

If there had still been doubts about Luo Chen's alchemy skill at the very beginning, as he repeatedly stepped forward to refine various high-difficulty pills for others, no one questioned him anymore.

Especially—

He was so young!

Only a little over two hundred years old!

If he joined them, even if he failed to achieve Divine Transformation, he could still serve the Nether Origin Sect for seven or eight hundred years.

Thus, he would fill the void left by the Alchemy Saint's departure.

If he did achieve Divine Transformation, it would be like adding wings to a tiger for the Nether Origin Sect, with endless benefits!

Amidst his hearty laughter, Black Swamp Ancestor waved his hand.

A droplet of water suddenly materialized.

Before Luo Chen could guard against it, it instantly sank into his brow.

"This!" Luo Chen was shocked and furious.

"Don't worry. This droplet contains a divine ability of mine. In a critical moment, it can protect your safety." Black Swamp Ancestor said with a kindly expression.

With this explanation, Luo Chen's startled expression eased.

"Thank you for the gift, Ancestor."

Black Swamp Ancestor was very satisfied with Luo Chen's reaction. Then he said, "Leave your spirit beast here."

This was only to be expected. Luo Chen was not surprised.

With a thought, Di Tu, who had been waiting outside, walked into the hall.

Facing a Divine Transformation great power, Di Tu merely glanced once before collapsing to the ground, his original form forcibly revealed.

A massive golden tiger now appeared so small and fragile.

"You may go. We shall meet again when the Biyou Mountain negotiations take place!"

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment, not even looking at Di Tu's whimpering, pleading gaze. With a calm expression, he left the Heavenly Water Hall.

...

As soon as he emerged, a hearty laugh reached his ears.

"Alchemy Sect, long time no see!"

Luo Chen looked up and saw the Lingtian City Lord who had brought him here.

He forced a smile. "How can it be called long? Only three years have passed."

"A day apart feels like three autumns—how much more so three years?" The Lingtian City Lord laughed heartily, then said warmly, "But from now on, we are fellow disciples. We can see each other whenever we wish."

Such warmth was truly hard to refuse.

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth but said nothing.

"Come, come, come! Now that Junior Brother Luo has returned, how can we neglect him? I have already prepared a banquet at the City Lord's Mansion. All the notable figures in the city have been invited, all hoping to catch a glimpse of you. You don't know, but in these three years, your battle in Fenghua Domain has spread throughout the Eastern Wilderness..."

Under the Lingtian City Lord's enthusiastic invitation, Luo Chen had no choice but to follow him to the City Lord's Mansion.

As for the other's change of address to "Junior Brother," Luo Chen did not refute it.

He knew that from the moment he walked out of Heavenly Water Hall, many things were already settled.

Some things were like this—if you couldn't resist, you could only accept.

At least before accepting, he had done his best, elevating his value to the highest possible level.

The promises he had obtained were far beyond what ordinary people could imagine.

Even the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect side might not have valued Luo Chen so highly.

Since that was the case, what was there to refuse?

The banquet that the Lingtian City Lord had arranged was indeed a grand feast!

None of the attendees were below the Sage realm.

Several friends Luo Chen was familiar with had also been invited.

For instance, Nine Spirits Primordial Lord, Deng Taiyue, Lin Qingxuan, and others.

Not only that, but two other great cultivators from the Nether Origin Sect had also joined the feast—Chongxuan Sage and Hanyang Sage.

When they saw Luo Chen seated at the head, level with the Lingtian City Lord, and even above Chongxuan, Hanyang, and even Tian Duzi, although everyone was astonished, no one voiced any objection.

They knew that just before this, Luo Chen had been summoned by the Nether Origin Ancestor.

With such a grandmaster alchemist in the wilds, how could the Nether Origin Sect remain indifferent?

In earlier years, the Lingtian City Lord had invited him, but Luo Chen had declined.

Now, a Divine Transformation great power had personally invited him, with all formalities fully observed. He should have accepted.

Thus, his status had risen accordingly, making it only natural for him to sit above the others.

Moreover, with the reputation Luo Chen had built over the years and his brilliant battle record, he was qualified to sit at the head.

Amidst the clinking of cups, everyone was openly or secretly currying favor with Luo Chen.

Of course, there were also those who were disappointed.

Such as Talisman Elder and Tian Duzi.

The former because the disciple he had poured all his efforts into training had died in the great battle; the latter was full of sighs.

Under the sacred ground, who could rise to prominence?

The one who had finally emerged—a man of both strength and fame, the Alchemy Sect—had also been won over by the sacred ground.

After this great battle, the various sects and factions of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world were hollowed out, but the Nether Origin Sect had suffered no serious losses.

Once the armistice treaty was signed, perhaps the Eastern Wilderness would, like the Central Continent, completely take the surname of Nether Origin under its corroding influence!

During the feast, Luo Chen watched everything with smiling eyes and calmly accepted Chongxuan Sage's return of Danluo Mountain to him.

He expressed sympathetic sentiments about the tragic battle at Lingtian Pass.

As for the battle that had shocked the cultivation world—Bai Li Qingchuan's feat—he glossed over it briefly.

Calm and composed—that might describe Luo Chen's demeanor today.

But after returning to his long-unseen Danluo Mountain, Luo Chen's face darkened.

...

Warm fingers touched his icy brow.

There, a water droplet-shaped mark was vividly lifelike.

"I have already agreed to join the Nether Origin Sect. Why did he still use such a method?"

"Protection?"

"Restraint!"

From the moment he had seen Black Swamp, the other's first words had given Luo Chen a sense of déjà vu.

In his memory, an old man who looked like a wealthy merchant had said something similar.

"Luo Chen, is it?"

"You are Luo Chen?"

And their actions were so alike.

Even Black Swamp had gone further today!

"But do you really think this method can suppress me?"

Luo Chen snorted coldly in his heart. He already had a vague idea of how to resolve this simple restriction.

Let his soul enter his Nascent Soul, cut off his head, then use the fifth-order Withering-Bloom Divine Flame to refine that water droplet—and he would regain his freedom!

Even if things went wrong, he could simply abandon his own head!

With his Archaic body's ability to reattach severed limbs and regenerate flesh and blood, he could completely reshape his head.

But this thought came and went quickly.

If he did that, it would instantly alert the great power Black Swamp, easily causing more unforeseen changes.

Luo Chen calmed down and carefully analyzed that meeting.

"Since he sincerely wanted to recruit me, why act so petty?"

"He mentioned the Alchemy Saint Chu Yan many times. Was he afraid I would follow her example?"

"That makes sense. I was already connected to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

"But if so, Black Swamp's vision is truly rather low."

"Or perhaps it's not that his vision is low, but that he is testing me?"

Luo Chen roughly analyzed Black Swamp's behavior, but he still felt displeased.

He had always hated such actions!

But the conditions offered by the Nether Origin Sect were indeed good. Under the pressure of a great power, this was the best treatment he could negotiate.

In the end, it was the Luo Tian Sect that held him back.

Inevitably, Luo Chen thought of this.

If not for the burden of the Luo Tian Sect, he could have fled far away. How could he be oppressed by a Divine Transformation great power?

Of course, he truly had not expected that a great power of this generation would stoop so low as to use a divine ability to restrain him.

But this being the case, Luo Chen absolutely could not let the Luo Tian Sect enter the Nether Origin sacred ground anymore.

"Once the war ceases, I will find another place to settle the Luo Tian Sect, far from the Nether Origin. If anything changes in the future, I can advance or retreat freely."

Luo Chen thought silently.

He remembered that Black Swamp had personally said that among the thirty-six domains of the Eastern Wilderness, as long as it was masterless land, he could choose any one...

"Wait?"

Luo Chen was suddenly startled.

Since when did the Eastern Wilderness have thirty-six domains?

At present, only twenty-one domains were under human control, and among them, many were temporarily unsuitable for Sage-level superior sects to settle in due to the war.

The remaining fifteen domains were all on the demon race's side!

"Could it be that the two-race meeting at Biyou Mountain will bring about another upheaval?"
