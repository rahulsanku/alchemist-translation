# Chapter 827: Five Temple Immortal Brew, Shouyang Mountain Hermit (BLG, Go!)

After a lengthy exchange with Deng Taiyue, quite some time had passed.

At least on the surface, Deng Taiyue seemed like a very good person.

He straightforwardly stated that if Luo Chen encountered any shortages of medicinal ingredients while researching the Tíhú Pill, he could come to him at any time. He was even willing to lend a hand with various trivial matters.

As he left the side hall, Luo Chen learned quite a bit about Deng Taiyue.

The man possessed an earth-attribute heavenly spirit root and was exceptionally proficient in earth-element escape arts!

At his cultivation level of Sage Layer Six, there were few mountains or perilous terrains in this world that could truly obstruct him.

That was precisely why, during the great war between humans and demons, he could repeatedly venture deep into the Million Mountains to plunder all kinds of scarce resources.

Exotic flowers and rare grasses, high-grade mineral veins, even demon emperor cubs—the man could bring them all out from the Million Mountains.

That Medicine Master had been dangling the Tíhú Pill in front of Deng Taiyue all this time, likely counting on this very escape art ability of his.

Luo Chen wouldn't stoop to such a low level.

But if he truly had a need in the future, he wouldn't hesitate to speak up either.

Exiting the side hall, the lively atmosphere of discussion in the main hall continued unabated.

Many observant individuals had noticed Luo Chen and the beaming Deng Taiyue.

This scene also made many realize that Luo Chen had most likely already agreed to refine the Tíhú Pill for Deng Taiyue!

The Tíhú Pill—a pseudo fifth-rank divine pill from ancient times, said to grant cultivators an epiphany, allowing them to grasp the true meaning of laws ahead of time at the Sage realm.

Unfortunately, over the past few millennia, the pill formula had become severely incomplete. No one had yet managed to reconstruct it, let alone refine it.

Could the Pill Sect do it?

Whether they could or not, more and more Sages were seizing the opportunity to ingratiate themselves with Luo Chen.

After all, what if?

What if he actually managed to refine it? Who wouldn't want to comprehend the true meaning of laws ahead of time, adding an extra layer of security for breaking through to the Ascendant realm!

Luo Chen handled these sycophants with ease.

Occasionally, someone would request his alchemy services, but he declined, citing his current busy schedule. However, he left plenty of room in his words, saying that once he had free time, he would still be willing to refine some pills for fellow Daoists.

This attitude earned him quite a bit of goodwill.

While dealing with these people, Luo Chen also listened to others discussing the current situation in the Eastern Wilderness.

"The three great commanders have been blockading outside Lingtian Pass for several years now. Fortunately, the Lingtian City Lord created the Absolute Spirit Domain as a buffer zone back then. Otherwise, with the constant warfare at Lingtian Pass, where would we find even a moment of leisure?"

"But this is not a long-term solution. According to intelligence gathered by Fellow Daoists like Lingfengzi, aside from the Green Parasol Mountain route, the other four commanders have already stepped into territory once ruled by humanity, drawing closer and closer to Lingtian Pass."

"If they arrive at the city gates, can Lingtian Pass really hold with just us?"

"Indeed! Nine great commanders, all at the Great Sage realm! Not to mention the other demon emperors, just these nine demons making a move..."

"Don't underestimate the formations of Lingtian Pass! I heard that Tianduzi has fully mastered the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation. Relying on this formation alone, he can stand against a thousand troops and ten thousand horses. Even against commander-level existences, he alone can hold off four or five!"

"Formations are truly the crystallization of human wisdom!"

"The demon race has suffered greatly from the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation and the All-Heaven Twelve Divine Baleful Formation. I heard that in recent years, they've retreated to the rear and started setting up formations near their camps. Some fellow Daoists went to investigate and the results were laughable. Apparently, the power of those formations can at most block the attacks of a few ordinary Sages."

"Don't be too complacent. Lingtian Pass may be indestructible, but in the face of the demon army's vast torrent, it's at most a stubborn reef. Without enough Great Sages stationed here, it will eventually be washed away. When will the supreme elders of your sects be willing to come to Lingtian Pass?"

"Still early! No rush! No rush!"

...

Luo Chen observed with a smile, gradually gaining a clear picture of the internal situation among the Eastern Wilderness human forces.

Setting aside the behemoth that was the Nether Origin Sect, the Sage-level superior sects of the Eastern Wilderness were actually divided into three tiers.

The first tier had extremely close ties with the holy land Nether Origin Sect, such as the East Sun Sect and its ilk. These sects were the most ancient, with many powerful experts, and often monopolized an entire domain.

The second tier consisted of ordinary sects that had received fiefdoms after ancient times, spreading their branches and establishing their own legacies. This tier was somewhat uneven.

Within it were top-tier powers like the Heavenly Capital Sect and the Void Emperor Sect, which were in no way inferior to the East Sun Sect.

There were also declining sects like the Seven Mound Mountain and the Mount Tai Sect, which were gradually fading and struggling to produce successors.

The choices of leaders within such existences also varied greatly.

For instance, Medicine Master, feeling his age and dim prospects, had focused his energy on nurturing the younger generation. Deng Taiyue, on the other hand, sought to advance to the Ascendant realm on his own, single-handedly reversing the decline and revitalizing his sect in the future.

Different choices naturally led to vastly different styles of conduct.

The third tier was a bit more complicated to describe.

These were the newly risen superior sects that had gradually emerged over the past three thousand-plus years.

These sects had some foundation, but not deep roots. They relied on generation after generation of newly risen experts, leading the sect forward with pioneering spirit, gradually carving out a place for themselves.

It was precisely this enterprising spirit that had given birth to many top-tier experts within these new forces.

Great Sages like Divine Origin Sage and the Joyous Union Ancestor.

Those comparable to Great Sages, like Nine Spirit Primogenitor, the extremely combat-capable Golden Feather Sage, the Overlord Blade Sect Master, the Medicine King Sect's Pill Emperor, who was proficient in alchemy...

And rising stars like Luo Chen of the Pill Sect, Han Zhan of the Falling Cloud Sect, and Lin Qingxuan of the Sword Realm's Lone Swan.

If nothing unexpected happened, these new forces might one day surpass the second tier, challenge the most ancient sects, and perhaps even produce a few Ascendant-level great powers.

But the unexpected demon invasion had disrupted many such fantasies.

The Joyous Union Ancestor had died tragically. Divine Origin Sage was severely injured.

A large number of Sages had become homeless refugees, many losing even their stable cultivation grounds.

Forget about catching up—even preserving themselves had become a problem.

That was why Nine Spirit Primogenitor and the others were urgently asking those old, established forces when their strongest existences would come to participate in this great war.

But clearly, these old forces were most protective of their own wings and feathers. Unless it was a matter of life and death, they would not easily commit.

The fact that they could send representatives to Lingtian Pass now was mostly for the sake of profit.

"The holy land sits high above."

"The old forces watch from the shore, indifferent."

"Only we struggle bitterly, not knowing when this great war will end."

"Bitter, sorrowful, difficult!"

Luo Chen sighed inwardly, losing some of his enthusiasm for dealing with others.

Most Sages were shrewd individuals and naturally noticed Luo Chen's waning enthusiasm, so they shortened their time clinging to him.

Only Pavilion Master Li of the Ten Thousand Sword Pavilion chatted with him a little longer about the refining of the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid.

After the crowd dispersed, Luo Chen poured himself a cup of wine and drank it alone.

It melted on his tongue, rich with spiritual energy that circulated through his five organs and six viscera, bringing immense comfort.

"Not bad, is it?"

The host, Elder Lu, chuckled as he sat down across from Luo Chen.

"This wine is called Five Temple Immortal Brew. It was made by Flowing Cloud Sage of the Stacked Cloud Sect, using the five elemental essences and five types of spiritual ingredients, brewed over a hundred years. It has an excellent nourishing effect on our organs at the Sage realm. Every time a new batch is released, it's in high demand. As for the aged vintages, they've all been requisitioned by the Nether Origin Sect. Even their own sect only has a small amount preserved."

Luo Chen examined the spiritual wine in his hand carefully, then sensed the condition of his five organs. A look of surprise crossed his face.

It was indeed as he said!

After the spiritual wine was refined, his five organs did become more vigorous in blood and qi.

Even the bottleneck in his body refinement, which had been difficult to break through for nearly a hundred years, showed faint signs of loosening.

This wine was greatly beneficial for body refinement!

He suddenly remembered that the heart of the Lightless Beast had been bought by a cultivator named Rosy Cloud Sage from the Stacked Cloud Sect, who had said it was for brewing wine.

This Rosy Cloud Sage should also be from the Stacked Cloud Sect, a fellow disciple of Flowing Cloud Sage.

For a moment, Luo Chen deeply etched the name of the Stacked Cloud Sect into his memory, planning to send people to make contact with them in the future.

Seeing Luo Chen savoring the wine with a look of surprise, Elder Lu smiled. "It seems you really like this fine brew, Fellow Daoist. I'll give you a bottle later!"

Luo Chen rubbed his hands together, feigning shyness. "Receiving a reward without merit—such a precious item, I'm a bit embarrassed!"

Elder Lu laughed heartily. "It's not that precious. Just a hundred-year vintage, far inferior to the Stacked Cloud Sect's aged vintages stored for thousands of years. Besides, how can you say you have no merit?"

He pointed to the lively scene in the hall and transmitted his voice: "Half the credit for today's grand occasion goes to your reputation as the Pill Sect."

"Is that so?" Luo Chen blinked.

Elder Lu nodded. "Indeed. Normally, when I host banquets and gatherings, there are only a few people, maybe a dozen at most. Today, there are over twenty. Guess why they came?"

"And from today's exchanges, didn't Taiyue Sage reach a cooperation with you?"

"I only hope that when I invite you in the future, Fellow Daoist, you will frequently grace us with your presence, so that this Shouyang Mountain Discourse Gathering can flourish even more!"

With praise like that, fine wine as a gift, and the fact that it was beneficial to him, how could Luo Chen refuse?

He also understood now. Elder Lu's painstaking efforts in hosting these exchange gatherings at Shouyang Mountain from time to time were precisely to build a wide network of connections.

His own reputation served as a fine banner for Elder Lu, attracting more people to participate.

So, it was a win-win situation!

Luo Chen agreed on the spot.

...

The fine night passed quietly, and dawn broke.

Breathing in the cool morning air, streaks of light flew out from Shouyang Mountain like fireflies, scattering to various parts of Lingtian Pass.

This gathering had not been long—just over two days.

By Elder Lu's expectations, it was far from enough.

If conditions permitted, this Shouyang Mountain Discourse Gathering—from invitations, entering the mountain, to exchanges and discussions—how could two short days be sufficient?

Too rushed!

At the very least, it should last a month or so to maximize the influence of such a high-level gathering.

Unfortunately, the current situation simply couldn't support his vision.

But Elder Lu was still very satisfied with this recent event. Many powerful experts had attended, along with capable individuals proficient in various immortal arts, such as the Pill Sect, Medicine Master, Talisman Elder, and Qin Baixiang.

The foundation had been laid well. He could gradually expand in the future.

By then, as the host, he would hold a vast network of connections. With a little manipulation, he could benefit immensely. If he could fully utilize it, the entire East Sun Sect would reap endless rewards.

Luo Chen left Shouyang Mountain but did not return directly to his cave dwelling in the Western City. Instead, he first went to the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion.

It had just opened for business, and Huiniang and Caiyi were busy.

Seeing Luo Chen, Huiniang smiled and asked, "Husband, have you just returned from Shouyang Mountain Hermit's place?"

Shouyang Mountain Hermit?

Luo Chen was momentarily stunned, then realized this was likely the honorific title outsiders had given Elder Lu.

But why "Shouyang" as the name?

The next moment, he suddenly understood!

Such an honorific—wasn't it precisely because of the exchange gathering held at Shouyang Mountain?

The man had only been in Lingtian Pass for a few years. Without much fighting or killing, without paying much of a price, he had so easily established a reputation.

In comparison, his own title of "Pill Sect" had to be traced back to his Initiate period. It was accumulated step by step over time to earn such fame.

"I've been too narrow-minded. Building connections through personal reputation is slow and has mediocre results. How can it compare to Elder Lu's method of building a platform and sitting at the center?"

"This individual household can never compete with a platform builder!"

Luo Chen sighed inwardly, chatted with Huiniang for a while, then pulled Gu Caiyi into a private room for a separate conversation.

Huiniang was curious about this scene but not jealous. She was always generous and didn't think Luo Chen would show any favoritism.

Gu Caiyi, however, was a bit jealous when she entered the room and heard Luo Chen's first question.

"Has there been any reply from Heavenly Mountain Sage on the Central Continent side?"

What reply could Heavenly Mountain Sage have? Naturally, it was about the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu on Sima Huiniang's body!

Gu Caiyi shook her head. "The cultivators from the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance told me that Heavenly Mountain Sage has been sent to the Southern Frontier on a mission and can't be contacted for now."

Sent out on a mission!

Luo Chen nodded, but then sensed something was off.

"The Southern Frontier? Wasn't it the Northern Sea?"

Gu Caiyi looked confused. "I don't know either, husband. That's what the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance cultivator told me."

Luo Chen's brow furrowed deeply. How long had Heavenly Mountain Sage been in the Star Gate before being sent out on a mission?

And to the Southern Frontier, not the war-torn Northern Sea!

Could it be that the Tianyuan Dao Sect's ambitions weren't limited to the Northern Sea, but also extended to the Southern Frontier?

His questions were not something Gu Caiyi could answer.

So he temporarily set aside this topic and stated his purpose directly.

"Has the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance prepared everything I asked for?"

Gu Caiyi nodded and took out two storage bags.

"This one contains the Return True Liquid, and it's the high-quality extracted version. The price was exorbitant. I only managed to buy it through Sage Gu's connections."

"This bag contains the medicinal ingredients missing from the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid formula. Especially the eyeballs of the Ink Demon and the Thousand-Eyed Squid—it took a lot of effort to meet the quantity you requested."

Luo Chen took the two bags, carefully inspected them, and finally showed a satisfied expression.

"You've worked hard."

Gu Caiyi shook her head. "Doing things for my husband is not hard work. It's what Caiyi should do."

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "Let's go out first. Don't keep Huiniang waiting."

"Yes."

That day, Luo Chen stayed at the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion for a long time.

He occasionally showed his face in front of visiting customers, asserting his presence.

He also had some low-grade pills he had refined in his spare time placed on the shelves.

It wasn't until sunset that he returned to his cave dwelling with the two women.

Early the next morning, after a warm breakfast, the two women left one after another.

Refreshed and invigorated, Luo Chen entered his alchemy chamber.

"Pavilion Master Li must have been waiting a long time. Let's refine this Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid first!"

He muttered to himself, showing no sign of worry, but rather an expression of ease.

Clearly, refining the Jade Emperor Clear Pupil Liquid was not as difficult as he had made it out to be.

Opening his mouth, he spat out a streak of light.

It spun in the air for a moment, then crashed down onto the specially opened geothermal fire vent.

And at some point, the Red Dog Puppet had silently appeared beside Luo Chen.

"Let's begin!"

I hope BLG wins the championship today. I hope! Go for it!
