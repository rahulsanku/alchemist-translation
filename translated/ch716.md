# Chapter 716: Relics of Two Sages, the Sword Formation Reappears

The main ingredient of the Great Soul Pill was an unusual resource, but it was not rare.

In fact, Luo Chen had obtained it before.

Its name was Soul Essence.

All living beings possessed three souls and seven spirits.

They were formless and insubstantial, without shape or breath.

Only after one's realm grew profound, when the soul condensed like substance, could it manifest.

So-called ghost generals and ghost kings appeared to have solid bodies, but in truth, most of them were merely filled out by yin qi.

Cultivators, clever beyond measure through ancient and modern times, had used secret arts to extract the essence from vast numbers of illusory souls, forming what was known as Soul Essence.

According to rumor, this Soul Essence was a rare and precious resource.

When taken in conjunction with secret arts, it could greatly increase a cultivator's soul foundation.

At the same time, if someone refined Soul Essence into an offensive method, its power would far surpass ordinary soul-attack arts.

The Soul Essence Luo Chen obtained that time had come from the Myriad Immortals Assembly demon-hunter assessment mission, when Duan Li had been struck by a killing move from remnants of the Primordial Demon Sect's soul-refining branch. After Luo Chen resolved that injury for Duan Li, the Soul Essence within had fallen into his hands.

Later, Luo Chen had also relied on that Soul Essence to clarify the god-slaying, soul-breaking effect of the Dragon-Slaying Art.

It was precisely that incident that laid the foundation for the battle in his sea of consciousness ten years ago, when he personally killed Han Zhan.

Now, after all these turns, cultivating Han Zhan's Soul-Splitting Art required him to gather large amounts of Soul Essence to refine Great Soul Pills. Speaking of it, there really was a touch of fate in the matter.

Thinking of Duan Li, Luo Chen's expression dimmed. He wondered how the other party was doing in the Myriad Immortals Assembly, and whether he had been implicated by Luo Chen.

He shook his head and looked at the pill formula, pondering it carefully.

As mentioned before, the thirty-six pill formulas left behind by Demon Lord Lian Tian did not pursue overwhelming medicinal power, but broad applicability.

Therefore, Luo Chen had heard of most of the medicinal ingredients listed on it.

For the few he did not recognize, he still had some vague impressions. Most likely, the years had been long and the medicinal ingredients had changed names.

If he truly put his mind to gathering them, it would not be difficult.

Only this Soul Essence...

"Among the five continents of the Mountains and Seas Realm, the places that produce the greatest amount of Soul Essence are the Northern Sea cultivation world and the Southern Border cultivation world. On the Northern Sea side, it is because of the Primordial Demon Sect, which encouraged cultivators to slaughter the countless demon beasts of the Northern Sea and use their flesh, blood, and souls to cultivate the demon sect's demonic arts."

"Soul Essence is the thing most needed by the soul-refining branch."

"In the past, even if someone in the Northern Sea cultivation world refined large quantities of Soul Essence, the trading channels were basically controlled by the Primordial Demon Sect. Now that the Demon Sect has been destroyed, who knows where all that Soul Essence has gone?"

After thinking for a while, Luo Chen carefully put away the pill formula.

Once he returned to the human cultivation world, he would have to spend some effort asking around.

Afterward, he looked at the attribute panel again.

Unlike the mixed panel catalog of the past, this time it had been subdivided again.

Soul arts had been separated from spells and listed as their own category.

Soul-Intimidating Art, Soul-Bewildering Art, and even Mirror Flowers, Water Moon were all clearly listed there.

Overall, their number was small, far inferior to the dazzling array of spells and body arts. This also showed Luo Chen's deficiency on the path of the soul.

After a cultivator formed a Nascent Soul, the physical body was no longer so important. In the eyes of some extreme people, the fleshly body was now merely a bamboo raft crossing the sea, shielding the weak Nascent Soul from the erosion and fusion of the outer heaven and earth.

What mattered was law and soul!

The symbol of law was the Nascent Soul; the manifestation of soul was the divine soul.

If the fleshly body were destroyed, a Nascent Soul cultivator could still cling to life.

If the Nascent Soul were destroyed, then with only a remnant soul left, death would not be far.

If the divine soul were destroyed, then one's life would be completely over.

At this moment, the importance of the divine soul had been raised to first place!

Luo Chen's attainments on the path of the soul were insufficient and had become his shortcoming. This was also why, despite clearly knowing that the Soul-Splitting Art was incomplete, he still insisted on cultivating it by force.

"Food has to be eaten one bite at a time, and matters have to be done one step at a time. If I want to make up for my deficiency on the path of the soul, it cannot be done in a single day. Besides, most Nascent Soul cultivators have this same shortcoming on the path of the soul, and they do not have any good solution either. Before that, I must possess enough power to protect myself!"

Luo Chen muttered inwardly while also calculating his present methods.

Not the ordinary methods of the past, but powerful methods, ones that could contend against, or even threaten, beings of the same realm.

First and foremost was clearly his superiority in body refinement.

A fourth-tier mid-stage ancient beast physique was enough to match the third level of Nascent Soul, and even some weaker mid-stage Nascent Soul cultivators.

These body arts, paired with high-speed movement and source power reinforcement, carried a very strong threat.

With that, he had one trump card.

Then came his methods on the qi-refining immortal path.

Looking at those few spells, Luo Chen felt somewhat helpless.

The spell path he had once been most proud of was actually a little stretched thin at this moment.

The Dragon-Slaying Art was in truth an auxiliary spell, and the soul-path methods derived from it were also limited to the sea of consciousness.

As for the Origin Core Art, rather than a spell, it was more of a secret art for cultivating a second Origin Core and even a second Nascent Soul. Without a second Origin Core, it could not be used against enemies at all.

Therefore, he could only optimize his old methods from before.

Now that his realm was high, the principles of some low-tier spells from the past had become simple. It would not be troublesome to raise their power.

For example, Luo Chen already had some ideas regarding the Azure Yang Great Handprint.

But even if those ideas were realized, they could only be treated as conventional combat methods.

"Conventional is fine too. A little more is still a little more."

"As for this All-Encompassing Fire Prison, after I possess a true Nascent Soul domain, it seems to have become somewhat redundant, but perhaps it still has certain merits?"

Luo Chen recalled the moment he had formed his Nascent Soul, when the Nascent Soul escaped his fleshly body and, in that void, briefly fused with heaven and earth.

At that moment, he had faintly seemed about to grasp something, but missed it.

He now already understood what he had missed.

It was an opportunity to comprehend law!

Nascent Soul cultivators had divine souls that were too weak to fuse with heaven and earth for long, so even if such an opportunity appeared, few could maintain it long enough to thoroughly comprehend a law suited to themselves.

He was not regretful. After all, throughout ancient and modern times, beings who could comprehend law in the early Nascent Soul stage almost did not exist. For him to have had such a chance already showed his talent.

Instead, he was somewhat happy.

Because in that instant, he gained an initial understanding of heaven and earth.

"If I erase the All-Encompassing Fire Prison's main effect of restricting enemies and mainly extract its second form to enhance myself, then combine it with my Nascent Soul domain, could it change my strength?"

The main effect of the All-Encompassing Fire Prison was to stir a Golden Core cultivator's magic power into unrest, making them unable to fight as they pleased.

This effect was completely insufficient in front of Nascent Soul cultivators.

And the second form was the true-fire state!

Luo Chen had comprehended it from battle back then. With the Withering-Bloom True Flame empowering his fleshly body, every movement he made was accompanied by true flame.

What Luo Chen was thinking now was to integrate the Nascent Soul domain into that true-fire state.

"There is still some time before Dragon Abyss Island dies out. Before leaving, I should do my best to research this move."

Luo Chen settled on this plan.

Then he began taking stock of his other methods.

The Withering-Bloom True Flame absolutely could not be ignored.

A large part of why Luo Chen held such great expectations for the new move had to be credited to the Withering-Bloom True Flame.

This flame had already awakened spirit. Even if released on its own, it could count as a major killing move.

If it were integrated into his own body, every raised hand and step would inevitably become stronger!

Finally, there was his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Ten years had passed, and the Primordial Unity Cauldron had become silent once more, as though it had already completely digested the power of that tribulation lightning.

But Luo Chen could still activate it, and could even actively trigger that portion of tribulation lightning power.

This could absolutely count as a major killing move as well!

If he disregarded everything and released the power of the tribulation lightning, perhaps he could recreate heavenly punishment and lightning tribulation. How could Nascent Soul cultivators withstand that?

After this round of calculation, Luo Chen's confidence rose greatly.

Although he had no real teacherly inheritance, he had just achieved Nascent Soul and was not that weak. He still had some methods at his side.

Moreover, he still had other ways to supplement his methods.

Closing the attribute panel, Luo Chen waved a hand, and several streaks of light landed before him.

A storage bag, a white jade belt with cloud patterns, the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion, and the Soul-Summoning Banner.

For the first two, during the ten years he spent consolidating his realm, he had successively undone quite a few restrictions. He was not far from opening them completely.

As the relics of two great Sages, they might contain things Luo Chen could use.

The latter two items, he had been unable to refine before. Now that his Nascent Soul had stabilized, he could also try refining and controlling them.

"Slowly, then!"

...

Time flowed away bit by bit.

Luo Chen's movements also grew faster and faster.

The first method he completed was the optimization of the Azure Yang Great Handprint!

As the powerful spell he had relied on to make his name during the Golden Core stage, it had originally already been optimized to the point where it could grow no stronger.

But now, with the lofty perspective of a Nascent Soul realm and Nascent Soul insight, he had new ideas again.

On this day.

Above the boundless sea, Luo Chen hovered in the void.

Suddenly.

His right hand, which had been behind his back, pushed out with a palm.

A huge handprint fell onto the sea, blasting up boundless waves and leaving five deep sunken finger marks on the ocean surface.

Black King, seeing this from afar, did not think much of it.

This move of Master's was a little stronger than when he was in the Golden Core realm, but that was all. Black King felt he could withstand it head-on with his fleshly body.

Luo Chen stood quietly in the void, his expression neither sad nor joyful, yet his hands moved again.

This time, it was not his right hand, but both hands moving together, ten fingers fluttering like butterflies, hand seals changing like rain.

At first glance, Black King vaguely sensed that these were two sets of incantation gestures.

One should be the Xiantian Seal from the original form of the Azure Yang Great Handprint, the Xiantian One-Qi Great Handprint.

The other made him feel extremely uncomfortable from body to soul, as though it was filled with the scent of enslavement.

"It is the Spirit-Enslaving Seal!"

Black King's heart shook.

He absolutely had not seen wrong. This was the Spirit-Enslaving Seal cultivators used specifically to enslave spirit beasts. If cast repeatedly, it would gradually enslave a demon beast's soul.

One had to know that low-tier demon beasts could not surrender soul blood. If one wanted to enslave their souls, one could only rely on spells combined with pills to infect them over time.

And now, what Master was performing with his left hand was absolutely the Spirit-Enslaving Seal that Black King hated to the bone!

"Master is forming two seals at once. What is he trying to do?"

Amid Black King's puzzlement, Luo Chen's gathering of power ended in an instant.

He pressed his palms together, turned his right wrist, and slowly pushed out with one palm.

A transparent handprint the size of a palm slowly flew out, showing no visible power.

But after the transparent handprint flew out, its speed grew faster and faster, and its size also grew larger and larger.

The main reason for that expansion was countless strands of drifting, ownerless spiritual qi between heaven and earth gathering toward the handprint.

One zhang, ten zhang, one hundred zhang, until it reached one thousand zhang, and still it did not stop!

Boom!

The enormous handprint fell into the sea, rolling forward and pushing across eight hundred li without end!

Endless seawater evaporated, and a terrifying road to the heavens appeared within the boundless sea. At the bottom of the passage, the seabed lay bare, with hills and deep ravines. On both sides of the passage, the seawater hung high into the sky, vast waves churning, yet unable to fall.

The sight was shocking.

Black King swallowed, unable to imagine what sort of scene it would be if that move struck his body, or struck land.

Vaguely, Black King understood the principle of Luo Chen's move.

Using the Xiantian Seal as its foundation, the Spirit-Enslaving Seal enslaved the spiritual qi of heaven and earth, making the originally unremarkable Azure Yang Great Handprint break through its upper limit.

The Azure Yang Great Handprint pushed across eight hundred li!

The bit of arrogance brought about by breaking through to the fourth tier vanished without a trace before Luo Chen's palm.

Luo Chen still stood in the void. Watching the lingering might of the Azure Yang Great Handprint fade, and the immeasurable seawater surge back to drown that road to the heavens, a satisfied look appeared on his face.

To make a spell that had originally only reached the third tier break through its upper limit, and without relying on the attribute panel at that, truly made one proud.

As expected, his talent on the path of spells was beyond doubt.

After glancing at Black King, Luo Chen waved a hand and returned to the earth abyss on his own.

Three days later, good fortune came again.

Han Zhan's storage bag had been completely unlocked by him.

This speed was somewhat beyond expectation, but it was not strange.

Because this storage bag itself was not high grade, Han Zhan had not spent much effort leaving marks on it, and Luo Chen had absorbed fragments of his divine soul besides. That was why it had gone so smoothly.

Opening the storage bag, Luo Chen took out the items inside one after another.

One Myriad Souls Banner and one set of Seven Baleful Swords. Neither was strange; they had originally been Luo Chen's possessions.

He did not care much for them now. To the current him, these things had become somewhat redundant.

Afterward came spirit stones.

Holding the rhombus-shaped crystal in his hand, Luo Chen could not help freezing for a moment.

It had color, dazzling with golden light!

What did this mean?

Supreme-grade spirit stones!

And ten of them!

A full ten metal-attribute supreme-grade spirit stones. Where had Han Zhan obtained them?

Immediately, realization dawned on him.

"They should be gains from the Puppet Realm!"

"Previously, when I looked at that white tiger puppet, there were several grooves on it that were clearly meant to hold spirit stones. And for such a high-level puppet, ordinary spirit stones could not possibly drive it. Clearly, these supreme-grade spirit stones were its energy source."

"An unexpected joy, an unexpected joy indeed!"

Beaming, Luo Chen put away the ten metal-attribute supreme-grade spirit stones.

Then he took out the final object from the storage bag.

It was a wooden plaque.

A wooden plaque made from Soul-Nourishing Wood.

Holding the wooden plaque, Luo Chen shook his head. There was no remnant soul beside him anymore, so this plaque had no place to be used.

He would put it away first. Perhaps it would be useful later.

Just as Luo Chen was about to put it away, his divine sense suddenly paused.

He swept carefully inside, and some information carved on the wooden plaque flowed into his mind.

After he finished reading it, Luo Chen's face revealed both shock and delight.

Recorded within it was a sword formation!

Its name was the Great Five Elements Sword Formation!

Back then, after Han Zhan obtained Luo Chen's Seven Baleful Swords, he had wanted to explore the Demon-Fall Land, so without the original Seven Baleful Sword Array, he had deduced a sword formation on his own that could control the Seven Baleful Swords.

Luo Chen had known this long ago. Even the Soul Heaven Sword Case had been refined by him at Han Zhan's instruction.

What he did not know was the process behind it.

In his early years, Han Zhan already knew a Yin-Yang Five Elements Sword Formation. He had used it as the blueprint to deduce the Seven Baleful Sword Array.

After deducing the Seven Baleful Sword Array, he gained inspiration and began improving the early Yin-Yang Five Elements Sword Formation, ultimately producing this brand-new Great Five Elements Sword Formation.

According to what was described, if this sword formation could be assembled, its might would be endless and absolutely no weaker than the Yin-Yang Five Elements Sword Formation.

Luo Chen had always coveted things like sword formations.

Flying swords had always been the most commonly used magic treasures among cultivators, convenient, swift, and quite powerful.

Formations were synonymous with power.

When the two were combined, they could be activated at any time and make others feel utterly unable to contend.

In his early years, why had Han Zhan dared, with strength only newly entering the Nascent Soul stage, to challenge the long-famous Sage Yuding? Why had he dared to launch a pioneering war against the Proud Roar Demon Emperor? Was it not because he relied on the sword formation built for him by the strength of his entire sect?

In the Demon-Fall Land, Luo Chen had also fallen into bitter battle because of Feiyunzi's sword formation.

If he had not relied on his own fleshly body, entered the formation with his body, and lured Feiyunzi close, then under that white-gold sword formation, even if he had not died, he would have been badly wounded.

It could be said that any sword formation with a name and reputation was something countless weak cultivators dreamed of obtaining.

Luo Chen was not weak, but he did not mind gaining one more killing move.

Especially when he currently lacked methods.

But after carefully studying the Great Five Elements Sword Formation, Luo Chen's expression calmed.

There was no other reason!

This sword formation had been optimized by Han Zhan. Its power was stronger than before, and its requirements were also higher.

To assemble this sword formation, the five sword-cultivator puppets had been abandoned, but in their place it required five sword pellets of true-artifact grade, and they had to respectively correspond to the five elemental attributes of metal, wood, water, fire, and earth.

In this way, deploying and withdrawing it would be more effortless, its power more concentrated, and especially, it no longer had the weakness of puppet sword cultivators.

"This should have been a self-protection method he prepared for after he possessed a fleshly body in the future."

"But where am I supposed to find five elemental sword pellets of true-artifact grade?"

Luo Chen could only smile bitterly.

Sword pellets!

True-artifact grade!

And divided among the five elements!

Even the Yuding Sword Sect at its peak probably could not gather so many sword pellets!

With a bitter smile, Luo Chen could only put away the Soul-Nourishing Wood plaque.

He began concentrating his energy on refining the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion and Feiyunzi's belt.

...

Three years later.

Looking at the brilliant treasure light scattered all over the ground, Luo Chen held a jade slip in one hand and pinched a golden pellet in the other, his expression somewhat dazed.

On the jade slip, spiritual light flickered faintly. Its name was Feiyun Artifact-Refining Record.

It recorded the lifelong artifact-refining insights of Feiyunzi, that master artifact refiner.

But none of that was important.

What mattered was that at the end of this artifact-refining classic, it recorded the method for refining true-artifact-grade sword pellets!

Aside from being an artifact refiner, Feiyunzi was also a powerful orthodox sword cultivator.

As for that golden pellet, upon close inspection, under the seal of complex restrictions, strands of golden thread wriggled inside.

This was an unrefined metal-attribute sword pellet!

Its grade had unmistakably reached true-artifact level!

If he had only obtained these things separately, Luo Chen would not have lost his composure like this.

But if they were combined with the Great Five Elements Sword Formation he had obtained three years earlier, for worldly matters to contain such a coincidence...

"Does this count as reaching fortune after calamity, and gaining blessing because of disaster?"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen lowered his head and looked at the two objects in his hands, his eyes filled with indescribably complex emotion.

*(End of Chapter)*
