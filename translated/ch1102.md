# Chapter 1102: Collision of Sky and Ocean

Outside the Sea Emperor Palace.

Figures rapidly retreated, fearful of being caught in the crossfire.

When great powers clashed, even the residual waves were more than Sage cultivators could bear.

Had not the Eastern Wasteland Continent, where the Dao Sect’s ancestral home lay, been swallowed by the world-destroying tsunami caused by Ascendant-level great powers clashing?

Countless cultivators had died in that disaster, and even Sage True Persons could not withstand such heavenly might.

As the crowd retreated, they burned with curiosity about the battle within the Sea Emperor Palace.

Yet someone chose this inopportune moment to mock, directing their barbs at Luo Chen’s followers.

“Kongchan, you demon monk, when did you become a human’s lackey? And you brought an enemy to my Sea Clan’s sacred Sea Emperor Palace! Don’t forget, your Chanting Monk clan is also Sea Clan!”

The one mocking was Demon Emperor Saibo. His realm was far inferior to Kongchan’s.

Yet at this moment, his face was full of hatred, unconcerned with the gap in their cultivation levels.

Kongchan kept his eyes lowered, nose aligned with his heart, not responding to the taunt.

It wasn’t that he feared the other, but standing from the Sea Clan’s perspective, he had indeed acted wrongly in this matter.

Seeing Kongchan silent, Saibo sneered, “Do you really think this new master of yours has heaven-defying strength to defeat the Ancestor? Just wait to collect his corpse!”

At this, Kongchan instead smiled faintly, seemingly not worried at all.

There was no need for him to speculate about Luo Chen’s strength.

The fact that Luo Chen had possessed Ascendant-level power while still in the Sage stage was proof enough.

Now that he had truly broken through to a higher realm and dared to come directly to the enemy’s lair, it showed Luo Chen had great confidence in his own abilities.

The question was, how long could Sea Emperor Whale last before his master?

---

Inside the Sea Emperor Palace.

The atmosphere was tense, ready to explode. Void origin qi churned.

The first exchange was a direct contest for dominance over heaven and earth origin qi.

Dharma power containing origin force surged forth, manipulating vast amounts of heaven and earth origin qi to press down on the opponent.

Water vapor steamed, and countless seawater boiled as if set ablaze.

Though surprised that Luo Chen had also comprehended the Water Origin, Sea Emperor Whale was not flustered.

“This place, after all, is this lord’s home ground!”

With a sharp cry, a dark trident appeared in his hand, which he thrust directly into the hard palace floor.

The next moment.

Gurgle! Gurgle!

Bubbles eerily emerged from the ground, dreamlike and magnificent, surrounding Luo Chen.

The surrounding heaven and earth origin qi seemed to converge into those bubbles.

In an instant, Luo Chen lost dominance over this place.

“As expected, it’s someone else’s territory!”

Luo Chen sighed, but his expression remained unchanged.

With a thought, layers of black qi emerged from his body. A stream of filthy black water spread out, forming a canopy behind him.

It was the phenomenon of Qi Sea Floating Heaven!

The canopy spread, blocking the erosion of the bubbles.

He rose and fled, escaping the Sea Emperor Palace, ascending straight to the nine heavens.

Sea Emperor Whale grinned ferociously, “Now you’re afraid? Too late!”

Without hesitation, Sea Emperor Whale burst through the palace, carrying a grand momentum, charging out of the Giant Whale Sea Region.

But the moment he emerged, he felt an unbearable heat.

He looked up, and his pupils contracted.

Above, red clouds filled the sky, burning the heavens.

A vast crimson light was pressing down from the nine heavens.

It was a scorching heat that wished to incinerate all things in the world!

Sea Emperor Whale was greatly alarmed, “Fire Origin!”

There was no time to think about how a cultivator who had just broken through to the Ascendant stage could simultaneously master two great origins. The pressure he sensed had already exceeded his own limits.

“Rise!”

With a furious roar, Sea Emperor Whale swung his dark trident, lifting with both hands.

Rumble!

Before the horrified eyes of countless demons, the sea region spanning a thousand li was lifted by him.

Countless clear blue seawater rose as thousand-zhang giant waves, striking straight at the sky.

The next moment.

Waves and red clouds collided in an instant.

This was the collision of sky and ocean!

Between heaven and earth, only red and blue remained.

It was the most brilliant of colors, the most breathtaking of sights.

The world was mesmerized, but Sea Emperor Whale, the one directly involved, felt only terror.

To be able to manipulate such vast heaven and earth origin qi—in all his memory, only one person had ever done so.

Rosycloud!

But that woman, though appearing to be at the Ascendant stage, in truth did not belong to that realm at all.

For an ordinary Ascendant great power to achieve such a feat would require completely scattering their primordial spirit and fully merging with heaven and earth.

“Luo Chen, have you gone mad!”

In his furious roar, the waves retreated step by step, and the red clouds pressed down layer by layer.

Above the nine heavens.

Luo Chen, clad in white robes, sat cross-legged on a cloud.

A great cauldron stood before him.

Dharma power poured into it, and infinite suppressing force emitted a hazy light, cascading down like flowing sand.

Then, he opened his mouth and spat.

Whoosh!

A flash of red light—the Yuantu Sword shot through the air.

In the nick of time.

Clang!

The dark trident blocked the divine sword’s path.

The extremely sharp divine sword itself, with just one strike, left a deep sword scar on the trident.

Sea Emperor Whale grunted, his eyes full of disbelief.

“That spirit treasure flying sword!”

He dared not resist further. His form plummeted downward.

The thousand-zhang giant waves fell with him, and the red clouds completely descended over the Giant Whale Sea Region.

Boom…

In an instant, boundless flames burned the sea, destroying the world.

From this scene alone, the world knew that Luo Chen held the overwhelming advantage.

Yet in the clouds, Luo Chen, who had recalled the Yuantu Sword, looked at the Primordial Unity Cauldron before him, his brow slightly furrowed.

“Can’t trap him?”

He knew that Ascendant great powers were hard to kill.

The physical body was merely a vessel; the primordial spirit was the core.

As long as one was willing to abandon the vessel, the primordial spirit could merge with heaven and earth, making it nearly impossible to stop.

The reason Liancheng had died by his sword back then was threefold: first, Luo Chen had used the Three Flame Fan and the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger to destroy his physical body, exposing his primordial spirit; second, because Liancheng was backed by Heaven-Earth Island and a soon-to-arrive True Lord from a Mystic Realm, he could not flee and had to fight to the death; third, Liancheng had placed his confidence in the defensive spirit treasure Burial Bone Umbrella, never expecting that Luo Chen would find its most insignificant flaw.

Thus, Liancheng had died so miserably.

But Luo Chen was not arrogant enough to think that all Ascendant great powers he faced would have so many constraints.

Therefore, he had simulated many combat scenarios against Ascendant great powers and prepared various countermeasures.

The most important point was to use his lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, to seal heaven and earth, preventing an Ascendant cultivator’s primordial spirit from merging with the world.

But as it appeared now, it had failed.

If the opponent was determined to flee, the Primordial Unity Cauldron could not suppress them!

Luo Chen felt regret in his heart, but no frustration.

This battle, ostensibly to demand an explanation, was in truth an experiment with his own abilities.

Now that he had seen the practical results, the mission was complete.

As for Sea Emperor Whale?

“Hmph!”

Luo Chen sneered, put away the blood sword, and flickered.

Boom!

The void exploded, and his figure flashed repeatedly.

A few breaths later, void ripples undulated, sparks flickered, and Luo Chen’s form slowly materialized on a small island with clear sand and white shallows.

Before him stood a stunned Sea Emperor Whale.

“Impossible! With my Water Concealment Art, in this boundless ocean, how could you catch up?”

Luo Chen chuckled lightly, “Daoist friend, as the founding ancestor of a sect, fleeing in such a panic—isn’t that a bit shameful?”

“Better to lose face than to lose my life!” Sea Emperor Whale snorted, then leaped, plunging into the ocean.

Seeing this, Luo Chen merely smiled and took another step.

The Five-Phase Escape Arts each had their strengths and weaknesses.

Water Concealment excelled in low mana consumption and long duration, but its weakness was slow speed, and like Earth Escape, it was heavily dependent on the environment.

Fire Escape was the complete opposite—fast speed, high burst, but consuming a great deal of mana.

When Luo Chen was at the Sage stage, using Fire Escape was not very sustainable.

But this problem had essentially been solved after he reached the late Sage stage.

Now, with the Voidburst Step integrated into his Blazing Fire Escape, and with the ability to manipulate heaven and earth origin qi, there were virtually no weaknesses.

In the void, his figure flashed continuously. With each step, a fiery red lotus seemed to bloom beneath his feet.

Step by step, lotus after lotus, Luo Chen not only failed to fall behind Sea Emperor Whale but grew closer, following like a shadow!

After an incense stick’s time!

Sea Emperor Whale flew out from a patch of sea surface in a sorry state, an unnatural flush crossing his face.

It was the result of Luo Chen suddenly releasing a spell, forcefully attacking just as he was about to activate Water Concealment again, interrupting his technique.

The backlash from the interrupted escape art naturally left him uncomfortable.

He looked up. Void ripples undulated, fire lotuses appeared again, and that infuriating face was still there.

“Luo Chen, what exactly do you want?”

Luo Chen smiled without warmth, “Daoist friend, aren’t you asking what you already know?”

“Asking what I already know?” Sea Emperor Whale laughed in anger, “You clearly know you can’t do much to me, yet you insist on pestering me. Do you really think this old man has a good temper?”

For Ascendant cultivators, determining victory or defeat was easy, but fighting to the death was quite rare.

As for the frequent fall of Ascendant cultivators in the last five hundred years, it wasn’t because the iron law had been broken.

Rather, in those Ascendant battles, there was often one person’s shadow.

Rosycloud!

That demon’s methods were far from limited to the Ascendant stage!

At this moment, Sea Emperor Whale’s confidence stemmed from this point.

Luo Chen could keep up with him, and his strength was inexplicably superior, but he truly could not kill him.

And this was without abandoning his physical body!

If pushed too far, Sea Emperor Whale would fight to the death, and Luo Chen would not escape unscathed. In the worst case, Sea Emperor Whale would simply abandon his physical body.

Just as Sea Emperor Whale was glaring viciously at Luo Chen, his eyes gradually widened, and a look of fear appeared on his face.

A lifelike, extraordinarily spirited firebird was dancing gracefully in Luo Chen’s palm.

“Nirvana… Sacred Flame…”

Sea Emperor Whale swallowed his saliva, forcing out this name with difficulty.

Luo Chen looked at him with a half-smile, “Daoist friend, what do you say? Does Luo have a way to deal with you?”

Low-level Nirvana Sacred Flame, compared to ordinary source-less fire, was only slightly stronger, nothing particularly special.

But the Nirvana Sacred Flame that had undergone Nine Tribulations and Three Nirvanas could burn spirits, bodies, and even souls!

A cultivator’s primordial spirit was also within its scope!

At the very least, Sea Emperor Whale had personally witnessed, during the battle against the Primordial Demon Sect, Rosycloud using this flame to heavily injure the Soul-Refining Ancestor, who was skilled in the dao of soul refinement, nearly burning him alive.

That was a burning that even a primordial spirit escaping into the void could not avoid!

During the battle at Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, Luo Chen had revealed the Nirvana Sacred Flame, but only for an instant, and he had not displayed similar methods at that time.

But now, that firebird was devouring heaven and earth origin qi in large gulps, appearing extremely lively.

A shudder from the depths of his soul arose!

Unless Sea Emperor Whale abandoned this physical body right now, before the Nirvana Sacred Flame launched its attack, and fled thousands of miles away.

But how could he bear to do that?

Compared to humans, who focused on refining qi and spirit, demons had an innate dependence on their physical bodies. Even after reaching the Ascendant stage, they did not want to abandon it easily.

In the silence.

Sea Emperor Whale suddenly stepped back and cupped his hands in salute.

“Daoist Luo Chen, this old man was in the wrong earlier. I offer my apologies and hope you will be magnanimous!”

Luo Chen was startled. He hadn’t expected to make the other yield so quickly.

His gaze fell on the Nirvana Sacred Flame, and some doubts crossed his mind.

It seemed this flame posed an even greater threat to great demon powers!

That made sense. Rosycloud was originally from the top-tier Fire Phoenix clan among demons. The Nirvana Sacred Flame she cultivated naturally inspired more awe in demon cultivators.

He himself might not yet have unleashed the full power of this flame.

Luo Chen’s thoughts turned, and he revealed a smile, “The explanation Luo wants is not just a mere apology!”

Sea Emperor Whale was also one who could bend and stretch.

The next moment, he reached out and tossed.

Streams of light appeared between the two.

Looking closely, most were books.

Sea Emperor Whale said sincerely, “Most of the resources obtained from Heavenly Origin Dao Sect back then have already entered this old man’s stomach. I truly cannot repay them. These books mostly record Dao Sect techniques, with no small number at the fifth rank. I hear Daoist friend has founded a sect; these things might serve as inheritance foundation.”

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. When the other mentioned Luo Tian Sect, his heart sank.

“Additionally, I hear Daoist friend is passionate about alchemy and pill-making. I have a fifth-rank pill formula here, which I also present to you as a gift!”

Another stream of light fell into Luo Chen’s hand.

His divine sense swept over it. The words “Return Origin Pill” imprinted themselves in his mind.

Luo Chen tightened his grip, holding the pill formula firmly. This was indeed a great gift!

A cultivation-type pill formula for the Ascendant stage—useless to others, but for him, it was like a pillow arriving just as he felt sleepy.

“Daoist friend, is this old man’s explanation satisfactory?” Sea Emperor Whale asked cautiously.

Luo Chen’s gaze flickered. The Nirvana Sacred Flame in his hand had already dissipated, and all those technique scriptures had been collected by him.

He laughed heartily, “Sea Emperor is generous. Today’s sparring ends here!”

Sea Emperor Whale’s expression stiffened. Sparring, his ass!

Earlier, they had been fighting as if to the death, as if he wanted to utterly destroy him—and this was called sparring?

He forced a stiff smile, “Yes, yes, today was merely a sparring session.”

Luo Chen walked to his side, ignoring the other’s wariness, and said cheerfully, “Daoist friend’s banquet today hasn’t ended yet. Why not return for some drinks and conversation?”

“Please… go ahead!”

“After you!”

Luo Chen laughed loudly, flicked his sleeve, and strode forward, heading toward the Giant Whale Sea Region.

Behind him, Sea Emperor Whale’s expression shifted between dark and bright.

Among humans, such an Ascendant powerhouse had emerged, and so young at that.

This was not good news!

Could it be that, having barely escaped the suppression of the Primordial Demon Sect, they would now face Luo Chen’s thousand-year domination?

Moreover, during their earlier exchange, Luo Chen had secretly used a method to seal heaven and earth. Though weak, it had indeed existed.

If given enough time, and if that method advanced to the point where it could truly seal heaven and earth, trap primordial spirits, and be combined with the Nirvana Sacred Flame, spirit treasure flying sword, and other means, then this baleful star might truly be able to kill them, these ancient demon Ascendants.

“This Mountains and Seas Realm… I’m afraid I can no longer stay long!”

(Chapter End)
