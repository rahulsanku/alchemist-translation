# Chapter 883: Yuanjun Summons, A Shortcut to Ascension?

“Is it over?”

At this moment, Luo Chen had truly reached the end of his rope.

Taking the combined strike of three great Demon Emperors head-on had shattered his protective armor and heavily damaged his natal treasure. In the ensuing fierce battle, whether he was unleashing spells and techniques or driving the Yuantu Blood Sword, he had been consuming his mana at a prodigious rate. In particular, the activation of the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger and the two releases of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire had consumed an amount of mana so terrifying that it could not be replenished by those two jars of Golden Wind and Jade Dew.

At this moment, was he not practically trapped in a hopeless situation?

But Heaven never seals off all exits. Luo Chen still had one last chance to fight!

The Yuantu transformed into a streak of blood light and merged into his right arm. Faintly, the awakened sword spirit transmitted an unwilling roar.

Luo Chen paid it no heed. He crossed his legs and sat down on the ground.

Azure Frost was startled. Before she could react, she saw a massive, blood-filled maw burst up from the snow-covered ground, swallowing Luo Chen's entire physical body whole.

Was that the black dragon? But wasn't that Luo Chen's spirit pet? How could it be devouring its master at a time like this?

After swallowing Luo Chen, Black King cast a cold glance at Azure Frost. His eyes were filled with madness, as if Luo Chen himself were staring at her.

*Boom…*

With an undulating arch of its body, Black King carried Luo Chen deep into the earth.

Azure Frost let out a silent, mocking laugh.

Even Luo Chen's exquisite Fire Escape Art couldn't escape her tracking, let alone the crude Earth Escape Art this desolate beast had mastered?

Azure Frost took a single step, crossing several hundred zhang in an instant. She stomped down heavily with one foot.

*Come out!*

*Hum!*

A strange wave, centered on her golden lotus, spread out invisibly.

The earth surged, and the accumulated snow rolled back into the sky like tidal waves.

The three adjacent great snowy mountains, under this single stomp, instantly collapsed, tumbling down with a majestic, devastating force.

*Crack, crack, crack…*

The earth split open, revealing Black King's figure. It was as if someone had stepped on its seven-inch vital point, freezing it deep underground.

It looked up at Azure Frost, terror in its eyes. But this time, the gaze was its own.

As for Luo Chen?

*Boom!!!*

A phoenix wreathed in cyan-white flames burst forth from Black King's mouth, charging straight at Azure Frost.

Wherever it passed, hard rock melted like snow, and the ten-thousand-zhang wave of snow instantly turned into murky, filthy water.

The moment she saw the phoenix, Azure Frost was quite surprised, but not afraid. She still threw out an ordinary, unremarkable punch.

It was just a mere spell…

But the next moment, before she even made contact with the phoenix, Azure Frost abruptly withdrew her hand.

She retreated without a moment's hesitation.

This phoenix was different from the fourth-rank spell, Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain, that Luo Chen usually used. It was now brimming with spiritual intelligence, charging at Azure Frost with a maddening cry.

If one listened closely, they would find that the phoenix's cry sounded exactly like Luo Chen's own roar.

The sudden charge was difficult for anyone to react to.

Even Azure Frost was caught somewhat off guard, but she managed to withdraw her hand in an instant and, in the blink of an eye, merged into the void.

*Boom!*

The phoenix breathed out, scorching the very void until it twisted!

Within that terrifying breath, faint black cracks even appeared in the void.

But there was no sign of Azure Frost within the spatial cracks.

The phoenix hesitated for a moment, not daring to step inside.

It circled within the great snowy mountains. The three collapsed mountains nearby were evaporated by the intense heat.

Its eyes were filled with mania and violence.

But it couldn't find her!

It couldn't find its enemy!

It possessed a full body of martial power, yet had no place to use it!

A faint sigh echoed from the void.

“Why resort to such desperate, suicidal measures?”

“By merging your Sage into a fifth-rank divine fire, you can direct the attack, but you will never find me.”

“Come with me to Green Parasol Mountain! My elder sister wishes to see you. Rest assured, she will not harm you.”

Elder sister?

Who in the world could Azure Frost address as “elder sister”?

None other than Rosycloud!

Hearing these words, the mania in the fire phoenix's eyes gradually faded.

Due to the forced activation of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, the thick Sage infant was now showing signs of exhaustion, as if it might collapse at any moment.

In silence, the fire phoenix suddenly dove deep into the earth.

After a moment, Black King crawled out of the ground, opened its mouth, and spat out Luo Chen, who stumbled out.

His complexion was pale, as if his origin qi had been severely damaged.

But his eyes were still exceptionally bright.

Ripples appeared in the void, and the woman in green walked out.

Luo Chen's gaze snapped to her, still feeling a sense of powerlessness.

He said in a low voice, “Yuanjun, why do you wish to see me?”

Azure Frost said calmly, “She is very interested in you.”

Luo Chen let out a self-deprecating laugh. “Surely my reputation as an Alchemy Master has some standing with the master of Green Parasol Mountain?”

Azure Frost shook her head. “No, she simply wants something you possess.”

Something he possessed?

Luo Chen was stunned for a moment and instinctively asked what it was, but Azure Frost did not elaborate.

Just then, a magnificent flying boat pulled by a three-headed jiaolong flew over from the distance.

The flying boat stopped abruptly, and a young man stepped down from it.

He wore a whitebone Daoist robe and a skull crown, his demeanor full of sinister, imposing aura.

But this sinister aura immediately turned into utmost respect upon seeing Azure Frost.

“Hall Master, everything is ready.”

Azure Frost nodded and made an inviting gesture to Luo Chen.

“Let's go to the Whitebone Dao Palace first. There's a teleportation formation there.”

Luo Chen frowned and looked at the newcomer.

He knew this person, having met him once before.

Back when Canglong was undergoing his tribulation, this was the man who had intervened to stop him.

The master of the Great Snowy Mountains, the Whitebone Mystic Serpent!

Meeting Luo Chen's gaze, the Whitebone Mystic Serpent gave a sinister smile, even sticking out his long tongue to lick his lips.

“Hmph!”

Luo Chen snorted coldly and stepped onto the flying boat pulled by the three-headed jiaolong.

The Whitebone Mystic Serpent curled his lip. What right did a loser have to be so arrogant?

Azure Frost also walked towards the flying boat. As she passed the Whitebone Mystic Serpent, her elegant, thin lips moved slightly.

Then she wiped her arm, and the previous scorched blackness vanished in an instant. She then sat down on the soft couch of the flying boat beside Luo Chen.

Behind her, the Whitebone Mystic Serpent's expression flickered.

The words Azure Frost had just spoken echoed in his mind.

“You are no match for him.”

How could that be!

He, the dignified Whitebone Demon Emperor, master of the Great Snowy Mountains, and gatekeeper of Green Parasol Mountain, had been a Daoist for nearly a thousand years. It was understandable that he was no match for Azure Frost.

After all, Hall Master Azure Frost was a peerless genius personally taught by Rosycloud.

But what was Luo Chen?

That brat had been cultivating for less than three hundred years. How could he not be a match for him?

He glared viciously at Luo Chen, but Luo Chen had already closed his eyes, seemingly resting, completely ignoring his glare.

Taking a deep breath, the Whitebone Demon Emperor stepped onto the flying boat. Standing at the bow, he tugged on the reins around the neck of the three-headed jiaolong, which had been trembling and refusing to move forward since Azure Frost boarded.

“Go!”

Only then did the three-headed jiaolong dare to move. It pulled the magnificent flying boat, stepping into the moonlight, deeper into the snowy mountains.

By dawn, the flying boat landed in front of a whitebone Dao palace.

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes. His pale complexion had regained a bit of ruddiness, whether from the reflection of the morning glow or from having recovered somewhat.

Azure Frost glanced at him and nodded inwardly.

Although she had noticed his considerable physical cultivation foundation back then, she hadn't expected that in just over a hundred years, he would have grown to this extent.

In just half a night, the massive losses from a great battle had been mostly recovered by his physical body.

His vitality was so vast, his flesh so resilient, that it almost rivaled her own Azure Luan Divine Body.

If he had possessed a means to break through the void, that previous battle would not have been so easy for her.

If it were a hundred years ago, a Sage cultivator disrupting the world's spiritual energy might have affected her ability to merge into the void. But over the past hundred years, through continuous cultivation, she had made up for this weakness.

Now, unless an Ascendant personally disrupted the origin qi of a region, no Sage in the world could do anything to her.

Luo Chen did not know what she was thinking. Under the guidance of the Whitebone Mystic Serpent, he walked step by step to the teleportation formation in the Whitebone Dao Palace.

Looking down at the formation, whose style seemed somewhat familiar, Luo Chen's heart stirred slightly.

Back then, when Han Zhan wanted to use Green Parasol Mountain's super-large teleportation formation to escape, the destination he set was the Whitebone Dao Palace in the Great Snowy Mountains. But because of Green Parasol Mountain's void translocation, the teleportation went awry, and the two of them ended up in the Northern Sea.

Later, Han Zhan calculated that the teleportation error was not only due to Green Parasol Mountain's movement but also because the teleportation formation at the Whitebone Dao Palace had been destroyed.

Had it been repaired now?

Weren't they afraid that a human Ascendant would follow the teleportation formation and find their way to Green Parasol Mountain again?

But the thought soon struck Luo Chen as ridiculous.

With Rosycloud guarding Green Parasol Mountain, what Ascendant would dare to visit?

If this Rosycloud was the one he imagined!

“Let's go!”

Azure Frost stepped into the teleportation formation. Luo Chen silently followed. A man and a woman stood side by side, almost equal in height.

The man was young and handsome, the woman tall and aloof. Standing together, they gave off a feeling of a golden boy and jade girl, perfectly harmonious.

No one would have imagined that the two had been fighting for their lives just moments before.

*Hum!*

After a fluctuation from the formation, the two figures vanished.

The Whitebone Mystic Serpent lingered for a moment before reluctantly leaving the teleportation hall.

He was, after all, just a gatekeeper.

*Hum!*

The formation's light flashed and disappeared, but the immense mana fluctuation alarmed the guarding cultivators outside.

But before they could come to investigate, a figure flew out.

The cultivators looked at each other in confusion.

“Who was that?”

“Could it be one of the sect's grand cultivators?”

“No, even a grand cultivator would find it hard to trigger such a massive mana fluctuation, and then suppress it in an instant. Even Uncle Master Lei Daozi couldn't do that.”

“Sigh… Could it be that the Ancestor has come to Lingtian Pass?”

The war between the human and demon races was nearing its end, with only the six easternmost domains yet to be reclaimed.

The main forces of the Nether Origin Sect had mostly returned to the sect. Those still out and about had their own arrangements, and few used teleportation formations for long-distance travel.

Under these circumstances, why would the Ancestor suddenly grace them with his presence?

In a quiet room, the Lord of Lingtian City, who was silently cultivating and recovering from his injuries, suddenly felt a stir in his heart. His expression changed slightly.

He instinctively wanted to rise, but quickly realized something.

“Master?”

A figure slowly materialized in the quiet room without triggering any formations.

Nether Spring Ancestor glanced at the Lord of Lingtian City and smiled, nodding.

“It seems your recovery is going well.”

The Lord of Lingtian City smiled bitterly. “Facing an Ascendant with a Sage's body, even if it was a spirit creature with weak combat abilities, is not something we can easily handle. If not for your compassion, Master, leaving behind healing pills before you left, I'm afraid it would have taken me another ten years to recover fully.”

Nether Spring Ancestor shook his head. “Those pills were indeed good. After all, they were left behind by Chu Yan before she left.”

At the mention of Chu Yan, the Lord of Lingtian City fell silent, not daring to respond.

This junior sister had once been extremely famous in the Nether Origin Sect, even more so than the three Ascendant Ancestors.

It could be said that she was known to everyone, both inside and outside the sect.

But later, she left the Eastern Wilderness to join the Tianyuan Dao Sect, which became a taboo topic in the Nether Origin Sect.

Perhaps disciples could discuss it privately, but few dared to mention this woman in front of the Ancestor.

After a moment of hesitation, he probed, “Master, your… incarnation has come. What is the matter?”

“Oh! You can tell the difference between my incarnation and my true self now. You've improved a lot!” Nether Spring Ancestor laughed heartily.

The Lord of Lingtian City smiled bitterly again. “Having had the experience of fighting a great power like Qinghe Fairy, it's natural to be able to tell the difference between an incarnation and your true body, Master.”

Nether Spring Ancestor stroked his beard, looking satisfied. “It seems that letting you participate in that battle was the right decision, as Senior Brother Yuming suggested. Sticking to closed-door cultivation leads to no progress. Fighting the weak only fuels arrogance. Only by facing an existence a full realm above you can you make progress under immense pressure. After this battle, our Nether Origin Sect might truly produce a few seedlings for Ascension.”

He waved his hand, and Nether Spring Ancestor got down to business.

“Today, I came personally for the successor of your Alchemy Saint junior sister.”

“Successor?” The Lord of Lingtian City looked confused, but quickly understood. “You mean Luo Chen?”

The Alchemy Master who was about to take over the Alchemy Saint Hall—wasn't he the Alchemy Saint's successor?

Nether Spring Ancestor nodded. “Exactly him!”

Just as the two were talking, the Lord of Lingtian City's expression shifted, showing hesitation.

Nether Spring Ancestor sighed. “Good news doesn't leave the house, bad news travels a thousand li. The news should have arrived by now.”

The Lord of Lingtian City understood his elder's meaning and reached out to bring in the transmission talisman that had been blocked outside.

He poured mana into it, and a hurried voice immediately came out.

“The Alchemy Master is in trouble!”

The Lord of Lingtian City's pupils contracted. This transmission came from Gu Hong, who was in the Piaomiao Domain adjacent to the Luo Tian Domain. It was highly credible!

He instinctively looked at Nether Spring Ancestor.

The other man grunted and turned to walk out.

“Come with me. If anything unexpected happens, you can lend a hand.”

The Lord of Lingtian City followed without thinking, but he was shocked. What kind of accident would make his master so cautious?

But thinking that the Alchemy Master, whom the Nether Origin Sect had specifically designated, could still get into trouble, the severity of the matter must have already surpassed the level of an ordinary Sage.

The two figures quietly left the pass.

As they passed the Skyblue Domain, seeing the golden light permeating the environment, which felt cold to the touch, Nether Spring Ancestor paused.

“I left in a hurry last time and didn't have time to deal with the battlefield here. I didn't expect the impact of Emperor Heaven's fall to continue spreading.”

“After I finish the initial refinement of Qinghe Fairy, I'll have to come back and clean up the remnants of the law's true intent here. Otherwise, in a hundred years, it might affect the surrounding areas.”

The Lord of Lingtian City asked curiously, “Why not follow Junior Brother Zhang's example and send a disciple who cultivates a gold-element technique to come here and comprehend the law's true intent? I think Junior Brother Chongxuan would be a good choice. It would be a great blessing for him.”

Nether Spring Ancestor frowned. “That's a shortcut. Comprehending the law's true intent through that method will make it extremely difficult to break through to Ascension in the future.”

“But Junior Brother Zhang?”

“He's different. He's from Bai Ye's lineage. Their techniques share the same origin, and their Dao paths converge. Not only does he comprehend it like a fish in water, but the bottleneck he will face when breaking through will be relatively smaller. But even so, it will still be much harder than comprehending the law's true intent normally.”

Having said this, Nether Spring Ancestor turned back to look at the Lord of Lingtian City and advised him earnestly.

“Xiao Ye, shortcuts are not to be taken!”

The Lord of Lingtian City's given name was Ye Lingtian. But no one dared to call him “Xiao Ye,” or even address him by his full name, for many years.

Only his master could call him that.

But this also showed his master's earnest intention to teach him.

The Lord of Lingtian City bowed respectfully. “Xiao Ye will remember.”

Nether Spring Ancestor stroked his beard with satisfaction. Among his many disciples, Ye Lingtian was not the most talented, but he had the best temperament.

Coupled with his generous and sociable nature, over the years, he had not only reached the late Sage stage but also had excellent relationships both inside and outside the sect.

Once Lingtian Pass was settled, he could be sent back to the sect to try some bitter cultivation and make a push for the Ascension realm.

If he succeeded, Senior Brother Yuming would probably hand over the position of Sect Master to Ye Lingtian!

Unlike ordinary forces, the position of Sect Master in the five holy lands of the Mountains and Seas Realm was almost always held personally by an Ascendant.

Because their lifespans were long and their strength was great, holding the position for a long time could stabilize the situation both inside and outside the sect.

For a continent's holy land, stability was paramount!

“Let's go. First, head to the Luo Tian Domain. Lin Yonghua and Gu Hong have been notified and are waiting for us there.”

“I heard Luo Chen even has a son now?”
