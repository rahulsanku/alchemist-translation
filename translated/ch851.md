# Chapter 851: One Against Four, Absolute Suppression, Unmatched Battle Power!

"Attack!"

Before the words had faded, three streams of light materialized.

A compass! A bronze mirror! A banner!

These were precisely the three brothers of the Lu family's lifebound treasures, their grade at the true artifact level.

Yet at this moment, all three treasures bore fine cracks running across their surfaces.

These cracks were exactly what Luo Chen had bestowed upon them during the great battle in the Heavenly Capital Domain!

Unlike human cultivators who excelled in artifact forging, demon clan experts who refined treasures often pursued convenience and power. They did not consider the treasures' growth and transformation like human experts did, nor did they add various materials during refinement to grant their treasures high growth potential and decent restorative properties.

Therefore, when a demon clan expert's treasure was damaged in battle, it often took a very long time to recover.

Now, only a year had passed since the great battle in the Heavenly Capital Domain, and the three treasures were still far from having recovered to their peak condition.

The three Lu brothers cast pained glances at their respective treasures, then silently made the same move.

Their mana surged wildly, demonic aura spreading everywhere.

Three figures began to expand and grow wildly, finally transforming into three Nine-Zhang Deer, each two to three hundred zhang in size, standing on azure clouds in the void.

Di Tu stood to the side, somewhat surprised.

"Is the Pill Sect really that formidable? You have to reveal your true forms right from the start!"

Lu Changsheng, while fully activating his lifebound compass, replied, "This man is extremely powerful. Back then, the three of us set up a killing formation but still couldn't destroy him. Instead, he injured our treasures. Therefore, for this battle, not only did we invite you, my lord, to oversee, but the three of us also dare not hold back even a shred of our strength."

Di Tu raised an eyebrow, intuitively sensing the gravity in the words of the three Lu brothers.

"Very good. With this intention and this effort, I will report it to my father afterward."

"Then many thanks to you, my lord!"

Lu Changsheng's heart rejoiced as the needle atop his head compass had already begun spinning wildly.

Their conversation seemed complicated, but in truth, it was but an instant.

Suddenly, the compass needle stopped!

"I've found Luo Chen's true body location!"

Lu Changsheng gave a low shout, and his form began to descend rapidly.

The other two demons, sharing a telepathic connection, descended together.

The compass, bronze mirror, and banner emitted layer upon layer of light, continuously expanding until they covered nearly a hundred li of land.

From a high altitude, this descending great formation seemed intent on enveloping the vast hundred-li Azure River in one fell swoop!

At Great Azure Mountain, before the Luo Tian Hall.

A man and a woman slowly walked out.

Sima Huiniang looked at the man's tall back, her eyes filled with worry.

But she did not waste words, merely offering a graceful bow.

"I wish my husband a triumphant return!"

Luo Chen smiled faintly, stepping on a gentle breeze, ascending straight into the azure clouds.

In his line of sight, black mist blotted out the sky, almost completely covering the entire hundred-li Azure River.

This familiar scene—Luo Chen naturally knew what it was.

"Nine-Chapter Bone-Dissolving Formation, you've come again?"

A fierce glint flashed in Luo Chen's eyes, and a pale golden light shimmered brilliantly in his pupils.

His pair of eyes turned rapidly, as if searching for a flaw.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's form moved, turning into an arrow shot from a bowstring, charging straight toward the black canopy of the sky.

His speed was too fast, his body shrouded in cyan-white flames. From afar, he looked like a blazing sun.

The three Lu brothers, descending rapidly, saw this and felt a flicker of doubt.

Fearing that Luo Chen might detect them and that the formation would fail to trap him, they had made many preparations in advance.

They had summoned a large number of demon beasts to besiege the hundred-li Azure River, forcing Luo Chen to act and reveal his location. Then the three of them would strike suddenly, locking onto Luo Chen's true body with the formation.

In this way, they could once again trap him within the formation.

But now, since he had already discovered the formation they had set up, why did he not retreat but instead advance, as if he were deliberately walking into the tiger's mouth?

Though filled with doubt, the three of them could no longer change course at this point.

The formation was already set. No matter what Luo Chen did, they had to continue.

"Don't worry, big brother. Last time, he was trapped by us for a long time and only escaped after being injured. This time, he's just putting on a show!"

"Yes, as long as he enters the formation, with the combined full strength of us three brothers and Di Tu's intervention, he absolutely won't escape this time."

With his two younger brothers so confident, Lu Changsheng naturally wouldn't dampen his own spirits and boost the enemy's morale.

"Then let's invite him into the jar!"

The vast black canopy pressed down upon Azure River.

The blazing sun soared, charging straight toward the sky.

Suddenly!

*Zheng!*

Just as Luo Chen entered the formation, the sound of a sword's cry rang out sharply.

Then came a terrifying sword light, slashing upward for a thousand zhang.

*Chi...*

The sound of cloth tearing rang out. The hundred-li Azure River, shrouded in darkness, suddenly had a ray of heavenly light spill through.

Luo Chen crossed the black canopy, standing firm in the void, holding a blood sword in his hand. His cold eyes looked at the three enormous Nine-Zhang Deer, who were spitting blood and retreating.

"Repeating an old trick is useless!"

The Nine-Chapter Bone-Dissolving Great Formation was indeed ingenious.

Without relying on the advantages of time or place, it could be set up anytime and anywhere simply by the three brothers joining forces.

Moreover, it contained many variations and unpredictable changes. Any ordinary demon emperor who fell into it would face certain death.

Even a great demon emperor, upon first encounter, would have to expend considerable effort to break it.

But Luo Chen was different!

He was already a fourth-rank formation master, and he had personally experienced this formation during the great battle in the Heavenly Capital Domain.

At that time, he had relied on brute force to break it.

But in reality, he had already gained a considerable understanding of this formation.

This formation was too ethereal, like duckweed without roots. As long as one found its flaw before it fully unfolded, it could be broken "easily."

Of course, for ordinary Nascent Soul cultivators, whether finding the flaw or the so-called "easily" was extremely difficult to achieve.

But Luo Chen had a pair of illusion-breaking eyes. The fleeting flaw in the formation was instantly captured by him.

Combined with the incomparably sharp Yuantu Sword, breaking it was as easy as turning over his hand!

Standing in the void, Luo Chen shook the blood-colored long sword in his hand.

"You three defeated opponents again, daring to run wild at my Luo Tian Sect?"

His words were filled with contempt.

The three enormous Nine-Zhang Deer had already gathered together, their plum-blossom-like hooves stamping uneasily in the void.

Just then, a rough voice came from above.

"Lu Changsheng, your formation is useless. To take this man, you still need to see the blade in my hand!"

Accompanying these words was a vast, golden, resplendent blade aura.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, and without hesitation, he swung the Yuantu Sword in his hand.

Unlike before, when he needed to gather power to communicate with the world's spiritual energy, after practicing the sword path and drinking demon blood, the Yuantu Sword Spirit had established a preliminary connection with Luo Chen.

Therefore, this slash contained not only Luo Chen's pure mana but also a massive amount of the world's spiritual energy.

Sword qi and blade light collided in an instant.

*Boom!*

The void trembled, sleeves fluttered.

A burly figure holding a golden ring-blade appeared in Luo Chen's line of sight.

"So you've found a helper!"

Luo Chen sneered, his gaze falling upon the man.

"State your name!"

"I am Di Tu, son of Lord Pingshan! I have come this time to ask you, Fellow Daoist, to surrender and accompany us to Pingshan Sacred Land." Di Tu spoke with arrogance, his words showing little regard for Luo Chen.

Luo Chen frowned. The son of the ancient demon Di Tian?

This unknown enemy's background was somewhat unexpected.

According to legend, the ancient demon Di Tian had unified the Eastern Wilderness demon clan and established the Pingshan Sacred Land within the Million Mountains.

He himself had three direct descendants: Huang Man, one of the Nine Great Commanders; Di Tu, at the mid fourth-rank; and another, relatively more mysterious descendant, said to be born of his favored consort, Jinxiu Xian.

Unexpectedly, his second son, Di Tu, had come to the hundred-li Azure River to invite him, Luo Chen.

"Is this Lord Pingshan's intention?"

"Yes. The name of the Pill Sect is thunderous even among our demon clan. If possible, I do not wish to engage in great conflict with you."

"Heh, you all seem to think you have me in your grasp?"

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly as he swept his gaze around.

While he was conversing with Di Tu, the three Lu brothers had silently separated, each occupying a position.

Combined with Di Tu's position, they had perfectly surrounded Luo Chen at the center.

Seeing that the formation was complete, and especially that Luo Chen had not actively fled, Di Tu felt that victory was all but assured.

*Clang! Clang!*

He shook the golden blade in his hand, the iron rings striking the back of the blade, emitting a series of killing sounds.

"Pill Sect, we have you surrounded. With no path to heaven and no door to earth, do you still intend to struggle?"

"Haha!"

Luo Chen laughed.

"You surround me?"

The next moment, his mocking laughter abruptly ceased.

And a powerful domain, centered on him, began to erupt outward.

**Encompassing Fire Prison, open!**

Within it, any spiritual energy of the world that was not of the fire attribute would be suppressed.

The four opposing demons did not seem surprised by this and each used their Nascent Soul to communicate with heaven and earth, unfolding their own Nascent Soul domains.

But the three Lu brothers had only just begun to unfold their Nascent Soul domains when they were forcibly suppressed back by the Encompassing Fire Prison.

Forget about restraining Luo Chen—they couldn't even control the free-floating spiritual energy between heaven and earth, and could only mobilize the mana within their own bodies.

Di Tu fared slightly better, but only by a limited margin.

His domain could only protect his immediate surroundings, unable to affect Luo Chen.

With this sudden change, Di Tu immediately sensed something was wrong.

"Such a strong domain, such vast mana! You told me he was at the fourth layer of Nascent Soul?"

The three Lu brothers had no time to answer Di Tu's question.

Because Luo Chen's actions did not stop there!

The blood sword hung in the air. With one hand pointing to the sky and the other to the earth.

"Sword formation, rise!"

On the ground, the countless sword qi that had been slaughtering demon kings across the hundred-li Azure River was now drawn, transforming into countless beams of sword light that shot into the sky, surrounding Luo Chen and the four demons.

Five sword pellets, like fish, circled menacingly on the periphery.

The Great Five Elements Sword Formation, based on the principle of mutual generation and restraint among the five elements, was primarily focused on suppression.

Unfortunately, because the three sword pellets used to set up the formation had not reached the true artifact level, it had a major flaw, and its effectiveness in battle was somewhat unsatisfactory.

But Luo Chen made up for this flaw in the next moment.

He opened his mouth and spat!

A gray light materialized.

In the air, the gray light spun rapidly, and in the blink of an eye, it transformed into a hundred-zhang giant cauldron.

It was precisely Luo Chen's lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

It also possessed the power of the five elements, and its suppressive might even surpassed that of the Great Five Elements Sword Formation, which he had not yet fully mastered.

"Cover!"

The hundred-zhang giant cauldron hovered high in the sky, emitting a hazy spiritual light, completely covering the five of them.

Thus, below, the Great Five Elements Sword Formation watched menacingly; above, the giant cauldron suppressed everything from the air. Luo Chen then used his own Nascent Soul domain as a guide, fusing all three together.

In an instant, this stretch of void became stagnant, like a quagmire, making it difficult for anyone to move even an inch.

All of this, though lengthy to describe, happened in the blink of an eye, starting from when Luo Chen released his domain.

Lu Changsheng immediately sensed something was wrong and blurted out, "His earlier release of the sword formation was deliberate! It was all preparation for this moment. We've been tricked!"

But Luo Chen paid no attention to this. He reached out and grasped the Yuantu Sword again.

His white robe, without wind, fluttered and snapped loudly.

"Now, it's my turn to surround you."

"Will you surrender and become my mount, or will you put up a stubborn resistance and face certain death?"

"Choose!"

His words were filled with a domineering, world-dominating aura!

Di Tu was the first to be unable to bear it. He roared, "Arrogant nonsense! Kill him!"

Before the words had faded, he was already the first to strike, sending out waves of vast blade qi toward Luo Chen.

The three Lu brothers also acted, manipulating their treasures to strike at Luo Chen.

However, whether it was the blade qi, the azure light from the bronze mirror, or the black mist surging from the banner, in Luo Chen's eyes at this moment, they were all so slow.

He moved!

Using the Moon Step technique, he walked within his own self-created "Triple Prison," strolling leisurely, utterly at ease.

Facing each attack, he either dodged, evaded, or casually dispersed them with a wave of his hand.

The blood sword in his hand sprayed sword qi, supplemented by various spells, striking at the four demon emperors.

The demon emperors' attacks, Luo Chen could easily dodge or even take head-on.

But Luo Chen's attacks, under the suppression of the Triple Prison, were not so easy to avoid.

Unable to dodge, they could only take them head-on!

Yet, were Luo Chen's attacks so easy to withstand?

*Shua!*

The blood sword flew from his hand, slashing through the air.

Lu Chang'an, the third of the three Lu brothers, had his lifebound treasure, the banner, directly severed. Even his massive body bore a deep, gaping wound.

*Boom!*

A fire phoenix swept through. Lu Changqing, the second brother, used his bronze mirror to protect himself, but there were places he could not cover. The fur on his back melted, revealing large patches of charcoal-like flesh.

A flame dragon descended, roaring as it charged at the eldest brother, Lu Changsheng.

He swallowed his compass in one gulp, spiritual light enveloping him. His body instantly shrank, and he forcibly teleported several zhang through the void, dodging that flame dragon.

But when he reappeared, blood was already gushing from his body.

Clearly, his physical body could not withstand the forced teleportation during battle.

Di Tu, however, saw this and his eyes lit up.

"This battlefield suppresses our mana and restrains various demon arts, but it seems not to restrict physical strength."

Then, with a loud shout, he charged toward Luo Chen.

The meaning of his words was simple.

Although demon cultivators practiced the method of qi refinement, their physical bodies were not something human cultivators could compare to.

As long as they engaged in physical combat, they could ignore the Triple Prison that Luo Chen had painstakingly set up.

But just as he was charging at Luo Chen, Lu Changsheng seemed to recall a certain scene from the past and shouted in panic.

"Don't!"

However, the warning came too late.

*Bang!*

Di Tu looked incredulously at his own fist, which was being held firmly in the young man's hand, unable to advance an inch!

"Impossible!"

He frantically mobilized his physical strength, but his fist still could not move forward. Instead, the immense force exerted by the other's hand seemed about to crush his fist into powder.

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth, finding it somewhat amusing.

Under the suppression of the Triple Prison.

In terms of magical combat, he was like a fish in water!

In terms of martial combat, he was even more unrivaled under heaven!

With a wave of his left hand, the Yuantu Sword shot back, its target being Di Tu.

Sensing this, Di Tu's heart filled with panic. He wanted to retreat, but his right hand was firmly gripped by Luo Chen—unable to advance, even less able to retreat.

Gritting his teeth, Di Tu's face showed a look of madness.

The golden blade descended from the sky.

A Xuanhuang light shimmered on Luo Chen's body as the Mystic Dust Armor materialized, showing no intention of dodging.

*Kacha!*

The golden blade struck him squarely in the middle.

Di Tu retreated, pulling away.

Luo Chen held an arm in his hand, somewhat stunned.

"A lizard cutting off its tail, so decisive?"

Though stunned, it was only for a moment. With Luo Chen's combat instincts, how could he let this perfect opportunity slip?

Without a moment's hesitation, he stepped with the Moon Step and gave chase.

Not far away, the three Lu brothers watched this scene, all swallowing their saliva.

Suddenly!

Lu Changsheng saw something in the distance and shouted heart-wrenchingly, "Third brother, don't—"

Lu Chang'an's four hooves stamped wildly, erupting with all his mana as he charged out of the Triple Prison.

But what greeted him were countless terrifying strands of sword silk cutting through the air.

*Xiu! Xiu! Xiu!*

In just a few breaths, Lu Chang'an, the third of the three Lu brothers, had his massive body riddled like a beehive by the Great Five Elements Sword Formation.

A Nascent Soul emerged, panicked and flustered.

Without the barrier of the Purple Mansion, the connection between the Nascent Soul and heaven and earth became even closer. Even the Encompassing Fire Prison could not suppress it.

Lu Chang'an, disregarding the destruction of his physical body, was about to use the Nascent Soul's teleportation technique to escape this terrifying battlefield.

But the next moment, several crimson chains extended abruptly from the void, binding his Nascent Soul tightly, and directly captured it back into the giant gray cauldron in the sky.

This sudden turn of events caught everyone off guard.

Including the eldest brother, Lu Changsheng, who had tried to warn him.

He stared blankly at the giant cauldron in the sky, then shifted his gaze to Luo Chen.

The perpetrator of this evil deed was currently engaged in a wide-open, fierce hand-to-hand combat with Di Tu, not giving the other party any time or space to react.

The intense exchange of fists and feet pressed Di Tu so hard that he could barely fight back, and his body was already covered in extremely severe injuries.

It was as if the earlier scene had been the automatic operation of the sword formation and the giant cauldron.

Yet everyone knew that such mana-draining true artifacts could not possibly kill enemies on their own without someone controlling them.

Lu Changsheng watched this scene, muttering to himself, "How does he manage to simultaneously control the giant cauldron, the blood sword, and the sword formation?"

*Plop!*

The massive body of the Nine-Zhang Deer crashed to the ground, producing a tremendous sound.

"Big brother, third brother is gone!"

Lu Changqing, the second brother, roared in fury, moving his four hooves as he charged toward Luo Chen.

Hearing this, Lu Changsheng's eyes also turned red in an instant.

"Revenge for third brother! Kill him!"

*Kacha!*

Luo Chen tore off one of Di Tu's hands, and without looking back, he sneered ferociously:

"Come! Come! All of you shall be buried here today!"
