# Chapter 808: One Skilled in Attack Moves from the Heights of the Ninth Heaven

Whoosh…

A wind blew through the towering mountains and ridges, and the blood-soaked air that had filled heaven and earth seemed to fade somewhat.

But on the ground, that horrifying chasm remained, a shocking sight impossible to ignore.

Under the gaze of countless demons, Seven Rhinoceros walked up to a large boulder.

Embedded in it was a blade—a golden greatsword, now broken, only half remaining.

This was the sword that had carved out that chasm, stretching for hundreds of li, its end nowhere in sight, as if it had split the very earth in two.

"As expected of Overlord Blade!"

"His final counterattack, a dying strike, possessed such power, taking tens of thousands of our clan's strong with him."

Swish! Swish! Swish!

Several figures fell from the sky like falling stars.

Each exuded monstrous demonic aura, unmistakably fourth-tier Demon Emperor-level existences!

"Commander Seven Rhinoceros, Overlord Blade is dead!"

Commander Seven Rhinoceros glanced at them, his tone displeased. "You took too long."

Those few Demon Emperors looked somewhat embarrassed.

They hadn't expected Overlord Blade to be so tenacious. Even with the combined strength of four demons, they had fought several times without subduing him.

Moreover, his nature was fiercely unyielding. Every battle, he fought as if he didn't care about his own life, dragging others down with him, making it impossible for the demons to find a way in.

Fortunately, Commander Seven Rhinoceros had sent reinforcements.

A slender, white-robed man drifted lightly down to the golden broken blade and licked his lips.

"A human strongman's Nascent Soul—truly the most delicious food under heaven, especially one of this caliber!"

Commander Seven Rhinoceros frowned. "Gao Chang, the Great One said you must not act personally. How could you be so impulsive just now?"

The white-robed man named Gao Chang sneered. "Who told those four to be so useless? I couldn't wait, so naturally I had to hunt myself. Don't bother with me. Prepare well to attack Lingtian Pass!"

As he spoke, he bent down and reached out to grip the broken blade's hilt.

Now that its master was dead, this object would serve as a fine souvenir.

The moment he grasped the hilt, he suddenly twisted his head!

His body didn't move, but his head turned a full hundred and eighty degrees, staring straight behind him.

"The scent of food!"

Commander Seven Rhinoceros reacted instantly as well. His divine sense expanded outward, chasing after the rapidly retreating consciousness.

But the other party withdrew too quickly, almost without any entanglement, vanishing in an instant.

Direction: Lingtian Pass!

Commander Seven Rhinoceros frowned. "A human Nascent Soul's divine sense… roughly at the mid-Nascent Soul level."

"We've been discovered!"

Gao Chang said mockingly. Now satiated, although he had sensed the fresh scent of food, he no longer had the same impulse as before.

After those two sentences, the other four Demon Emperors finally reacted.

"Was someone spying on us just now?"

"Could it be that Lingfengzi?"

"No, although this person's escape speed is fast, his cultivation is only at early Nascent Soul. There's no way he could have silently spied on this place without us noticing."

"It's still the two lords who are sharp-eyed, discovering the spy in an instant."

Faced with the four demons' discussion, Commander Seven Rhinoceros showed no reaction.

He cast a subtle glance at Gao Chang. It was the other who had discovered it first!

His own cultivation, placed in the human cultivation world, was at least at the late Nascent Soul level. Yet he had been slower than Gao Chang in detecting the spy.

Just what level was Gao Chang's cultivation at?

"That's a very clever fellow. His Nascent Soul must be fresh and lively too. If you encounter this person later, remember to notify me."

Gao Chang chuckled lightly. Without any visible movement, his entire form turned into a wisp of white smoke and vanished on the spot.

After he left, Commander Seven Rhinoceros snorted coldly.

"Always showing off that pretentious escape art."

He could criticize Gao Chang, but the other four could not.

In the silence, someone asked, "Commander Seven Rhinoceros, what should we do next? Should we go help Dark Crow retake the Fire Ridge?"

Commander Seven Rhinoceros waved his hand. "No need. The Fire Ridge is nothing to fear—just a bunch of skulkers. Dark Crow and the others will take it sooner or later."

Then, his gaze swept across thousands of miles of land. Strange-shaped, ferocious demon beasts roamed the scarred earth.

After all, they were beasts that hadn't transformed, with low intelligence!

Even if they formed a demon army, they still couldn't achieve perfect discipline.

And ahead, blocking their path, was the greatest fortress in the Eastern Wilderness's ten-thousand-year history. Relying solely on these dull-witted creatures, they would never take it.

Logically, they should settle in place and wait for the other commanders to arrive before attacking together.

But Commander Seven Rhinoceros thought of Emperor Heaven's words.

"He who breaks Lingtian Pass shall be recorded as the first to achieve merit!"

Even as calm as he was, he couldn't help but grow restless.

His gaze fell on the four Demon Emperors before him, and inwardly he shook his head.

"Relying on them isn't enough!"

"I need to contact some old friends to forcefully break Lingtian Pass. Otherwise, by the time Yellow Barbarian and the others arrive, this first merit won't fall to me."

"Azure Sky has already fallen. Overlord Blade died tragically. And that unknown strongman witnessed this scene. Even if the Fire Ridge still holds, Lingtian Pass will surely be on high alert."

"So, this must be done quickly!"

Thoughts turning, Commander Seven Rhinoceros had already made his decision in an instant.

"You lot, settle in place for now. Wait for my return!"

…

On the walls of Lingtian Pass.

Teams of cultivators came and went.

Usually quite lively, they were now as silent as cicadas in winter, not daring to breathe loudly.

One by one, their gazes couldn't help but fall on that white figure that had appeared abruptly at some unknown time.

It was the Alchemist!

In the past two years or so, he had rarely come to the city walls.

Although it was said that a puppet of his was stationed at the gate tower, it was still just a puppet.

No one expected him to come in person.

Faced with the curious gazes of the crowd, Luo Chen paid them no heed.

At this moment, his heart was still shaken.

"What exactly is that white-clothed demon? Not only did it detect my divine sense instantly, but it even gave me a feeling of a natural enemy!"

"In front of it, even though my Nascent Soul is thousands of li away and protected by the Purple Mansion barrier, I still feel uneasy."

"It's as if this demon was born to be the natural enemy of Nascent Souls!"

Terrified in his heart, Luo Chen had also gleaned a piece of intelligence from that fleeting glance.

Overlord Blade was dead!

The Overlord Blade Domain had completely fallen!

Several Demon Emperors had gathered in the Overlord Blade Domain, with countless low-level demon beasts covering the mountains and plains.

In other words, they were only one step away from Lingtian Pass.

"Supreme Elder!"

A greeting came from beside him.

Luo Chen turned his head and saw Yang Pingdu leading a patrol.

He nodded and was about to leave.

But just as he took a step, he stopped and gave instructions. "The days ahead may not be peaceful. It's fine to patrol within the city, but if you lead a team outside to sweep for demon beasts, you must be careful. Keep those talismans ready at all times, as Jueqing instructed. Don't find them burdensome."

Yang Pingdu was a little surprised. He rarely saw the Supreme Elder being so "long-winded."

But the elder's concern was filled with care, and his heart warmed.

"I will follow your instructions and inform the disciples."

"Mm, be careful in everything!"

Luo Chen repeated his warning, then hurriedly left the city wall, heading straight for the city lord's mansion.

…

Lingtian Pass was enormous. If not for the city lord's mansion and Luo Chen's patrol area both being in the rear, it would have taken him a long time to get there.

But now, he arrived at the city lord's mansion before long.

Unlike Gu Zuokai, who had been shut out, Luo Chen was quickly received by the Lingtian City Lord after stating that he had matters related to the demon race to report.

"Overlord Blade is dead?"

The Lingtian City Lord sighed, seeming quite regretful about the other's fall.

"I had reserved a position for him here. He also said that if things went wrong, he would bring his disciples and retreat into Lingtian Pass. I never thought he wouldn't even have a chance to escape."

Luo Chen was stunned. He had previously thought that those Overlord Blade Sect members were reluctant to leave their homeland and didn't want to retreat.

Now it seemed they wanted to leave but couldn't!

After expressing his lament, the Lingtian City Lord asked for details.

After Luo Chen described the strongmen he had glimpsed, the Lingtian City Lord fell into thought.

After a long while, he slowly said, "Those four demons should belong to the Ten Demon Emperors under Seven Rhinoceros. Their cultivation isn't particularly high, but being able to hold off Overlord Blade means they must have some ability."

Luo Chen nodded thoughtfully. "So, that man with the rhinoceros horn on his forehead is the legendary Seven Rhinoceros Demon Emperor, one of the Nine Great Commanders?"

"That should be the case!"

The Lingtian City Lord nodded.

He paced slowly within the hall, speaking unhurriedly.

"According to the intelligence we've gathered, the Eastern Wilderness demon race, under the leadership of the ancient demon Emperor Heaven, has been forged into a single block of iron."

"He personally appointed eight late-Nascent Soul demon cultivators as commanders. Together with Azure Frost Demon Emperor from Green Parasol Mountain, they form the Nine Great Commanders."

"Each of these Nine Great Commanders is immensely powerful, comparable to our human late-Nascent Soul Grand Cultivators. Moreover, each commands at least five or six Demon Emperors, and at most ten, to do their bidding."

"Their power is unprecedented since ancient times!"

Even though Luo Chen had already learned this information from various intelligence reports, he couldn't help but swallow his saliva.

Nine Great Commanders, each comparable to a late-Nascent Soul Grand Cultivator.

Among them, there was even a peerless strongman like Azure Frost Demon Emperor.

No wonder Nine Spirit Primogenitor had been so pessimistic about the upcoming war.

He also understood why Lingfengzi had been so desperate to curry favor with him, begging for the Wind Aperture Pill to increase his strength.

Under such a great tide, no one could stand apart!

Only by increasing one's own strength as much as possible could there be a sliver of hope for survival.

Especially since above them all was an ancient demon with the Dao name Lord Pingshan and the true name Emperor Heaven!

Luo Chen couldn't help but ask, "Will Emperor Heaven personally participate in the great battle?"

At this question, the Lingtian City Lord smiled.

"You don't need to worry about that. There has always been an agreement between the human and demon races: Ascendant-level strongmen must not participate in wars. Emperor Heaven, as an ancient demon comparable to our human Ascendants, is naturally bound by that agreement."

Luo Chen's expression turned somewhat strange.

Ascendant-level strongmen must not participate?

Then what about the battle at Green Parasol Mountain back then?

The Lingtian City Lord seemed to realize the loophole in his words and explained a little.

"In this realm, Ascendants are already the pinnacle of living beings. Every move they make affects the great momentum of heaven and earth. When they fight, they manipulate the vast origin qi of heaven and earth. If they were to engage in a life-and-death battle without restraint, it would overturn mountains and seas, sink continents."

"Living beings would suffer, and the Heavenly Dao would be enraged. Either heavenly tribulation would descend, or external demons would be drawn. In short, Ascendant-level great beings would not fare well either."

"Therefore, in all previous great wars, Ascendant-level beings rarely fought each other. They usually let their subordinates battle to decide the outcome."

With this explanation, Luo Chen's heart settled a little.

But only a little.

Thinking of the demon race's Nine Great Commanders, he couldn't help but bitterly smile. "I only thought the human path was flourishing, but I never imagined the demon race, in silence, possessed such strength."

The Lingtian City Lord also sighed with emotion.

Three thousand years of accumulation since ancient times, coupled with the demon race's naturally long lifespans.

It was only reasonable that the Million Mountains had nurtured so many strongmen.

If one really wanted to compare, the Eastern Wilderness human side was not far behind. It was just that the truly top-tier experts had all accumulated within the Nether Origin Sect.

In his view, if they were to fight with all their strength, the Nether Origin Sect, with all its forces deployed, would definitely be able to overpower the Eastern Wilderness demon race.

It just hadn't reached that point yet.

He looked at Luo Chen. "The news you've brought, I will notify the various Nascent Soul Daoists stationed here. For now, please continue to hold the western side of Lingtian Pass."

Luo Chen nodded, but before leaving, he still asked worriedly, "Can we hold Lingtian Pass with just a few of us?"

"No, we cannot."

The Lingtian City Lord smiled slightly, clasping his hands behind his back as he calmly said, "Therefore, it's time to summon the Nascent Soul strongmen scattered in various places."

…

Ever since Luo Chen brought back the news of the Overlord Blade Domain's fall, the entire Lingtian Pass had become visibly tense.

The number of cultivator teams sent out to patrol the surrounding area had increased nearly tenfold.

The business in magical artifacts and talismans within the city was booming as never before.

Some powerful treasures that had originally been kept as shop treasures were bought at great cost by people gritting their teeth.

Everyone was sharpening their weapons, waiting for that battle.

No one harbored any illusions, because the cultivators of Lingtian Pass had all been forced to flee here from the east.

They had fought with demon beasts and knew what fate awaited them at the hands of demons.

And there was no retreat behind them. They had to fight to the death!

Of course, even with the determination to fight with their backs to the wall, everyone was still hoping for reinforcements from the rear.

The more people, the better!

But reinforcements would take time to arrive. Until then, no one could afford to be careless.

Luo Chen was no exception. He immediately went to visit Lingfengzi.

The other party had already refined the Wind Aperture Pill and emerged from seclusion. If he could learn more information about the powerful Demon Emperors from him, he could be slightly more composed in the coming battle.

But clearly, Luo Chen was disappointed.

For several days in a row, he couldn't find Lingfengzi.

According to the disciples of the Misty Cloud Sect, their founder had gone out of the city on a mission.

"Indeed, in this kind of situation, I can only rely on myself."

Luo Chen murmured, taking out the scroll that Wang Yuan had given him before his departure.

For Nascent Soul cultivators, learning powerful techniques often took years.

Even if he, Luo Chen, could quickly cultivate ordinary third-tier spells to a level not inferior to fourth-tier spells, it still wasn't enough.

In a short time, he couldn't raise his cultivation to mid-Nascent Soul. To increase his strength, he could only optimize what he already had.

*Wind, Moon, Thunder, Cloud: Stepping on the Immortal Path* was a good supplement.

Thunder Kick, he had already fully mastered. It could even be considered a decent killing move, having once kicked apart Feiyunzi's defensive technique.

Wang Yuan had supplemented a Moon Step.

This move was not focused on killing, but excelled at maneuvering during battle.

Swift and strange, unpredictable, combined with a body cultivator's high-speed burst, it could even make it impossible for a Nascent Soul strongman's divine sense to track.

But the above description was limited to the perfected stage.

Moreover, cultivating this technique required high standards. Magical power flowed through the meridians and acupoints, unable to circulate to the muscles and bones. Therefore, using magical power to drive Moon Step was very inefficient.

It was best to use astral qi!

Luo Chen didn't understand the changes in blood and qi, let alone astral qi. He hadn't even mastered the four types of force: bright, dark, transformative, and solid.

But he had source power!

"Source power permeates every corner of my physical body. Using it to drive Moon Step should be no less effective than a proper body cultivator's astral qi."

Luo Chen pursed his lips and carefully studied the footprint distribution on the scroll.

He memorized each one firmly, making sure not to miss any variation.

After he felt he had fully grasped it, he couldn't help but experiment in his spacious cave dwelling.

At first, he stumbled.

Gradually, he found his rhythm.

Later, the sound of wind and thunder roared within the cave, causing the formation's light barrier to shake violently.

"The space is too small. I need to find another place to practice this technique."

With a thought, Luo Chen left his cave dwelling and went directly to an open area outside the city.

…

Days passed one by one.

More and more cultivators gathered within the city.

But the demon army had yet to appear beneath the walls of Lingtian Pass, causing many to doubt the accuracy of Luo Chen's intelligence.

To this, Luo Chen offered no defense.

What he had seen in that fleeting glimpse when his divine sense was extended to its limit was no illusion.

Especially the sense of danger that white-clothed demon had given him, even greater than that of Commander Seven Rhinoceros!

He had also privately asked the Lingtian City Lord, but the other party couldn't say either, only suggesting it might be a new strongman from the Million Mountains.

Luo Chen had to let it go.

One day.

The weather was uncooperative. Thick dark clouds gathered, a fierce wind howled, thunder and lightning flashed—a sign of heavy rain to come.

Dozens of li from Lingtian Pass, a figure stood still for a long time, looking up at the sky.

"The moon waxes and wanes; the heavens have unexpected storms. The world is impermanent; the human heart is unsettled."

"So-called Moon Step takes this 'unsettled' meaning."

"If I follow the map, learning the steps Wang Yuan supplemented based on his own experience, I would be drawing a tiger but ending up with a dog."

"Wrong! Wrong!"

Amidst repeated cries of "wrong," Luo Chen laughed loudly and stepped into the sky.

As he stepped, his figure gradually became indistinct and uncertain.

Sometimes like a weak willow swaying in the wind.

Sometimes like a flash of lightning, swift as a thunderclap.

Occasionally, he would stomp heavily on the void, and his figure would suddenly dissipate, turning into several afterimages flying in all directions.

When he lightly landed on the ground, the several afterimages suddenly merged, revealing Luo Chen's face.

He casually glanced at the system panel.

**[Moon Step: Perfect 301/500]**

In just over a month, with his relentless practice, this body technique had reached the Perfect level!

Only one step away from the traditional sense of the Great Completion stage.

Even Wang Yuan, who had supplemented this technique, probably hadn't reached this level yet!

The reason it was so fast, besides Luo Chen's hard work, was also because he was naturally skilled in body techniques.

In particular, the two most important aspects of practicing this technique—physical strength and the driving energy source—were both far beyond Wang Yuan's expectations. Therefore, once he understood the trick, he reached the Perfect level in an instant.

"In this way, in battle, I have Voidburst Step for straight-line sprints, Moon Step for maneuvering, Thunder Kick for close-combat bursts, and Ninety Thousand Li for long-distance pursuits. In terms of escape techniques, I almost have no weaknesses!"

"I have one more measure of confidence for the coming war!"

Luo Chen muttered to himself with a smile.

His legs felt a little sore, the result of the high-intensity training of this technique recently. A few days of rest would fix it.

But the fact that even his physique, comparable to a desolate beast, felt sore meant he should reduce the training intensity.

"I'll stop here for today!"

Luo Chen looked at the sky. Large raindrops had begun to fall.

He walked unhurriedly toward Lingtian Pass, his mind constantly thinking about when the demon army would begin its assault.

"I was a bit nervous earlier. Lingtian Pass is tall and deep, housing tens of millions of cultivators—truly formidable."

"Although the demon army is numerous, they are scattered everywhere. The Overlord Blade Domain is just one of their forces. As long as the leading Demon Emperor is calm, he won't rashly provoke Lingtian Pass. He'll wait for the other demon armies to arrive before launching the attack."

"In that case, I can just wait out the remaining half year."

Luo Chen thought optimistically.

But just as he reached the city gate, a sudden change occurred.

Luo Chen abruptly looked up. His deep eyes pierced through the torrential rain, peering through the layers of dark clouds.

*One skilled in attack moves from the heights of the ninth heaven!*

Boom! Boom! Boom!

Amidst the deafening thunder, a massive figure crashed down, carrying the force of wind and thunder.
