# Chapter 950: Breaking the Deadlock, Hundred Poison King Gu

"Zhan Bingtao of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has come to pay respects at the mountain!"

As this arrogant voice rang out, a formidable aura pressed down upon all living beings, surging straight toward the top of the hall.

Vaguely, one could see spiritual qi rolling in like tidal waves.

"Hmph!"

Sage Songyang snorted coldly, his Sage domain slowly unfolding.

A blazing sun rose steadily above the unending waves with an unquestionable momentum.

"Fellow Daoist Zhan, this is Mijie Mountain. Do not act recklessly!"

Zhan Bingtao laughed heartily. "I am not acting recklessly. I merely wish to see whether your alliance is truly qualified to cooperate with my Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

As he spoke, his Sage domain grew even more extreme, drawing in the surrounding heaven and earth spiritual qi, forming a stalemate with Sage Songyang.

A late-stage Sage grand cultivator!

This single point revealed just how profound this unfamiliar expert's cultivation realm was.

Domain manifestation was not an exceptionally sophisticated technique, yet few ordinary Sages had mastered it.

This was because it required an extremely high degree of resonance between one's own mana and the spiritual qi of heaven and earth to produce such a phenomenon.

Back when Luo Chen was in the early Sage stage, he had been able to manifest the Encompassing Fire Prison domain only by relying on the resonance of his Withering-Bloom True Flame, taking a shortcut.

But Zhan Bingtao and Sage Songyang had derived theirs from solid, profound cultivation.

Of course, at Luo Chen's current realm, with all seals released, he too could manifest a similar domain phenomenon.

Legend had it that this was also a path of cultivation.

The initial domain manifestation, upon reaching the Ascendant stage, would draw upon the origin qi of heaven and earth to form one's own unique phenomenon. Then, bit by bit, one would outline it into a Law Manifestation, thereby communicating with heaven and earth.

Thus, late-stage Sages would gradually delve into this aspect.

At this moment, under the contest of domains between Zhan Bingtao and Sage Songyang, the Fierce Peak Grand Hall atop Mijie Mountain was plunged into a storm, pressing down so heavily that it was hard to breathe.

The other three grand cultivators sat high above, watching coldly from the sidelines, largely unaffected.

But the others...

"Break!"

Suddenly, an explosive sound rang out.

A pillar of blood and qi soared into the sky like a fierce dragon swinging its tail, forcibly breaking through the rolling waves and charging straight at Zhan Bingtao.

Sage Songyang was startled for a moment, then pursed his lips as his mana surged wildly, his Sage domain pressing forward in tandem.

Zhan Bingtao seemed somewhat caught off guard. The unfamiliar pillar of blood and qi had abruptly joined the contest, directly shattering the stalemate.

He instinctively clapped his hands.

*Boom!*

*Boom!*

The deadlock broken, he staggered back two steps.

Only then did he turn his gaze to a black-armored man in the hall.

The man grinned fiercely, glaring back without the slightest fear.

"I really dislike your attitude—looking down on everything, treating me as if I don't exist."

Zhan Bingtao bared his teeth in a grin. "Your name?"

The questioning voice echoed through the hall.

Many couldn't help but break out in a cold sweat for Luo Chen.

Just the surname Zhan alone was enough for many to guess his origins.

The Central Continent had only one sect, but within that sect were aristocratic families and humble clans scattered throughout.

Among them were twelve giant families, standing high above, controlling everything.

The Zhan family was one of the most powerful among them.

Now, this black-armored man liked to show off, interfering in the struggle between the Alliance Leader and the strongest of the Zhan family. Although he had helped Alliance Leader Songyang gain the upper hand, being remembered by this man... the consequences...

Ning Yuzhuo sneered inwardly. *"A tyrant cultivator who dares to touch the heavens—courting death!"*

But contrary to everyone's expectations, before the black-armored man could answer, Li Tianshi above had already spoken with a smile.

"Fellow Daoist Zhan, this is the newly emerged expert of our Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance, the tyrant cultivator Qi Tian. His temperament is somewhat eccentric. I hope you won't be angered by him."

Sage Songyang also nodded in agreement.

Their stance clearly indicated their support for Luo Chen.

Zhan Bingtao raised an eyebrow, his gaze constantly shifting between Luo Chen and Sage Songyang.

After a moment of deliberation, he laughed and said, "Angered? Not at all. Since your alliance has a peerless expert like Alliance Leader Songyang guarding it, and rising stars like the tyrant cultivator Qi Tian, it can be said that talents emerge in succession. This is barely enough to cooperate with my Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

These words were too arrogant.

But in truth, that was exactly the case!

Everyone knew that when the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect attacked the Northern Sea, the Penglai Immortal Sect had directly submitted, and the Cang Hai Righteous Alliance had joined forces with them.

And that Cang Hai Righteous Alliance had an Ascendant great power sitting within it.

The Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance might seem numerous and powerful, but at the Ascendant level, it was a blank slate.

If they didn't show sufficient strength, forget cooperation—even becoming a vassal would require the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's consent.

Such was the pride of the number one sacred land in the Mountains and Seas Realm!

"Fellow Daoist, please!"

Alliance Leader Songyang extended his hand in invitation.

Zhan Bingtao showed no hesitation, leaping up to one of the main seats above, sitting side by side with Alliance Leader Songyang.

With this, the previous tense atmosphere vanished, and even the subsequent conversation became much more amicable.

And what they discussed, unsurprisingly, was the details of how to deal with the Southern Frontier sacred land, the Life and Death Gate.

"The Life and Death Gate has already decayed. According to what our Dao Sect's Ancestor Liancheng said, the current heaven and earth changes in the Southern Frontier are a chain reaction caused by internal changes within the Life and Death Gate. If you all still wish to continue cultivating in this small patch of the Southern Frontier, you must resolve the calamity before you can be at ease!"

As soon as Zhan Bingtao sat down, he revealed secrets, causing an uproar in the hall.

And amidst this uproar, everyone's attention shifted away from Luo Chen.

Luo Chen's eyelids drooped low, the previous arrogant and overbearing aura already restrained.

Showing his face in front of the crowd was not his nature.

But it was the behavior expected of the identity "tyrant cultivator Qi Tian."

Moreover, under such a sudden display, it should make the Burial Life Elder take notice of him!

If the other party was truly the Devil Heart Gu Emperor, then having publicly used the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance's power to search for information about the Devil Heart Gu Emperor, and having made a move in front of him, there should be some reaction.

As long as there was a reaction, Luo Chen could respond in various ways.

As for Zhan Bingtao?

Luo Chen admitted that the man was very strong and had considerable backing.

But offending him was the tyrant cultivator Qi Tian's doing. What did that have to do with the Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen?

While Zhan Bingtao spoke eloquently about the plan to gather forces and attack the Life and Death Gate, Luo Chen's attention remained quietly fixed on the Burial Life Elder.

"No reaction?"

Luo Chen's heart gradually sank.

...

"Although the Life and Death Gate has sealed its mountain gate for a long time, a starved camel is still bigger than a horse. In the vast Southern Frontier, there are still many forces loyal to the Life and Death Gate. For example, the Unicorn Mountain, the Golden Lion Empire... and the Unicorn King's Unicorn Mountain is one of the Life and Death Gate's defensive gateways. If we want to break in, we must first cut off their wings and feathers, so that we can fight without any worries."

"Such trivial matters can be left to our Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance!"

"Mm, you are locals, more familiar with this place than us Heavenly Origin Dao Sect cultivators. It is indeed more appropriate to leave it to you. There is another matter where I might need Alliance Leader Songyang's assistance."

"Is it about imprinting the Fierce Peak Seal?"

"Exactly!"

Inside the hall, Alliance Leader Songyang, representing the entire Corpse Alliance, was discussing future plans with Zhan Bingtao.

This discussion, placed before all the Sage-level experts of the Corpse Alliance, was clearly intentional.

As for leaking intelligence, they weren't too worried about it.

This operation against the Southern Frontier sacred land was an open and aboveboard scheme. Even if there were a few spies from the Life and Death Gate in the hall, it didn't matter.

In the end, they would have to fight it out anyway!

Luo Chen was somewhat curious, however. "What is the Fierce Peak Seal?"

The person he asked was none other than the Purple-Robed Marquis beside him.

Faced with Luo Chen's question, the Purple-Robed Marquis's expression was somewhat complicated, and even his tone carried a hint of envy.

"The Fierce Peak Seal is a restriction developed by Alliance Leader Songyang, using his own natal gu insect, the Fierce Peak Gu, as the core, combined with Li Tianshi's secret techniques."

"It is placed on Sage-level cultivators to reinforce the Purple Mansion, preventing our nascent souls from being assimilated by heaven and earth during cultivation, unconsciously escaping the Purple Mansion."

"One could say that the Fierce Peak Seal is a lock. With this seal, we can recklessly unleash the power of our nascent souls in battle without fear of heaven and earth assimilation. But with this benefit comes a drawback: daily cultivation efficiency is greatly reduced, making our cultivation progress far slower than before."

"Even our divine sense becomes rigid, far less agile than before. That is why our alliance is called the Corpse Alliance."

"Some who don't understand even call us corpse cultivators."

After hearing this explanation, Luo Chen suddenly understood.

Earlier, when he arrived at Mijie Mountain, he had been wondering what method Sage Songyang had used to unknowingly gather so many experts.

So it was such a exquisite technique!

Using the Fierce Peak Gu as the core, setting up the Fierce Peak Seal to lock the Purple Mansion, preventing assimilation by heaven and earth.

In terms of specific performance, this method was somewhat similar to his own terrifying Purple Mansion barrier.

But man-made restrictions were ultimately inferior to innate Purple Mansion barriers.

At least Luo Chen was not greatly affected, and had even turned misfortune into blessing, greatly increasing his cultivation efficiency.

"You are different."

The Purple-Robed Marquis said enviously, "You are a pure body cultivator. True power resides in yourself. One force breaks ten thousand methods. You simply don't need this seal."

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "You flatter me. The path of body cultivation has long declined. Haven't you seen that even at my current realm, I am still running around seeking various body cultivation techniques?"

The Purple-Robed Marquis nodded. The reasoning was indeed sound.

After this exchange, the Fierce Peak Corpse Alliance was officially allied with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

But the specific details of the cooperation had not yet been fully discussed.

For example, if the upcoming battle was lost, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect could retreat back to the Central Continent, but how would the Southern Frontier cultivators face the wrath of the Life and Death Gate?

Or, if they won, how would the vast Southern Frontier territory be divided? Where would the Southern Frontier experts go?

In what capacity would the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect settle in the Southern Frontier?

No one asked explicitly, but everyone was concerned about these things.

Zhan Bingtao, however, seemed intent on keeping everyone in suspense, showing fatigue on his face.

"I have traveled a long way and am somewhat tired. Let's discuss the specifics another day, shall we?"

Alliance Leader Songyang's gaze swept over the restless crowd in the hall, and he finally nodded slightly.

"Today's discussion ends here. Dismissed!"

Amidst a series of exchanged glances, the ten-day Mijie Mountain Sage Exchange Meeting came to an end, and the crowd filed out.

At the top of the hall, Zhan Bingtao's gaze paused for a moment on one of the departing figures.

"Songyang, that Qi Tian is quite impressive!"

Sage Songyang said calmly, "An expert like that does not have the heart to submit to others. If you want to take him as a servant, I advise you to abandon that idea now."

Zhan Bingtao shrugged. "Even if I can't take him for my own use, I can still introduce him to Heaven-Earth Peak as a guest elder. In the future, he might even become your fellow cultivator."

Sage Songyang took a deep breath and said, "Heaven-Earth Peak is my last resort when all other paths are closed. If this campaign succeeds, you must fulfill our previous agreement and grant me a fifth-order spiritual vein from the Life and Death Gate!"

Zhan Bingtao laughed heartily. "That depends on how much you contribute to this battle and whether you can gain the recognition of Ancestor Liancheng."

"How much I contribute..."

Sage Songyang murmured, and finally his gaze grew fierce.

"What if we open the Life and Death Gate ourselves, without borrowing the power of an Ascendant?"

This "Life and Death Gate" was not the same as the sect.

Clearly, what Sage Songyang meant was the most perilous defensive line of the Southern Frontier sacred land, the Life and Death Gate.

Just like the Arctic Yemo Heaven of the Primordial Demon Sect, or the Nether Abyss of the Nether Origin Sect.

Such defensive lines could be called forbidden grounds, and were by no means easily opened by ordinary means.

Even Rosycloud had to borrow the clone of Ancestor Heavenly Origin to silently cross the Nether Abyss. How could Sage Songyang's boast not be shocking?

Zhan Bingtao's smile gradually froze. He stared suspiciously at Sage Songyang.

"Really?"

"If everything goes smoothly, there should be a three-tenths chance!" Sage Songyang replied.

"Three-tenths chance..."

Zhan Bingtao muttered, his eyes gradually brightening.

"Three-tenths is enough. This way, we can save Ancestor Liancheng some effort and focus his main strength on dealing with that great power inside the Life and Death Gate. If you really succeed, what harm is there in granting you a fifth-order spiritual vein!"

Sage Songyang breathed a sigh of relief, but his heart was inexplicably uneasy.

Was that plan truly feasible?

...

The exchange meeting was over.

But Luo Chen did not leave. He stayed in Mijie Mountain.

As he had previously thought, this mountain was indeed filled with various naturally formed spatial restrictions.

That Elder Red Leaf had relied on these natural spatial restrictions, combined with various formations, to gradually eliminate spatial fluctuations and form the stable Mijie Mountain.

"Among the seven basic formation restrictions, my weakest is spatial restrictions. Moreover, few scriptures on this subject are passed down in the world; most are closely guarded secrets of top sects."

"If I can observe these natural spatial restrictions and solidify my foundation in this area, it will be of great use whether I face the Azure Frost Demon Emperor in the future or deal with that spatial dead zone outside the Alchemy Saint's blessed land."

With this in mind, after staying behind, Luo Chen began to frequently wander up and down Mijie Mountain.

This action was actually quite reckless and impolite.

Because many Sage-level experts remained in Mijie Mountain, each with their own cultivation grounds.

If he wasn't careful, he would trespass into someone else's territory.

But because of his previous outrageous behavior and the reputation of his arrogant personality that had spread, most people tolerated him to some extent.

After all, even Alliance Leader Songyang and Li Tianshi were on his side. Unless necessary, no one would offend him.

One day.

Luo Chen stood on a cliff face, watching the wind howling outside the mountain and the turbulent clouds, golden light flickering in his pupils.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen turned and struck out with a palm, heavy and forceful.

But when it landed on its target, it was like striking into an ocean, completely unable to find purchase.

*Splash...*

A pool of blood scattered before him.

Luo Chen's eyes lit up, but his hands moved without slowing. His five fingers spread wide as he grabbed through the air.

"Come out!"

A great force grabbed straight into the depths of the blood spring.

Before he could truly grasp anything solid, a shocked voice came from within the blood spring.

"Enough!"

Luo Chen immediately stopped upon hearing this.

Then, the blood spring gurgled and flowed, condensing into a purple figure.

It was the Purple-Robed Marquis!

He looked at Luo Chen's hand with lingering fear, shocked. "My technique of transforming the body into a blood spring can offset immense force and prevent anyone from finding my true form. But why, in front of you, does it repeatedly fail?"

Luo Chen smiled without answering, instead asking, "This technique of transforming the body into a blood spring shouldn't be a native Southern Frontier body cultivation method, should it?"

The Purple-Robed Marquis nodded. "Indeed, it is not. I comprehended it from a fragmentary text. Later, I inquired about it and found it was very likely a body cultivation method from the Blood Sea lineage of the Northern Sea's Primordial Demon Sect. Unfortunately, what I obtained was only a fragment, unable to reach the level where the blood sea is inexhaustible and the true body is indestructible."

So that was it.

No wonder Luo Chen felt a sense of familiarity.

Whether it was the Blood Wanderer from before or the Blood Nightmare Moluo, both had shown similar performances.

Legend had it that after cultivating the Blood Sea lineage's techniques of the Primordial Demon Sect to great completion, one could cultivate the Blood Sea True Body, claiming to be immortal and indestructible.

Moreover, one could nurture countless Blood God Children within the blood sea, a single person capable of facing a thousand troops.

In comparison, the Purple-Robed Marquis's single pool of blood spring was indeed a bit small-time.

"It seems that in the path of body cultivation, I indeed have a great gap compared to you."

The Purple-Robed Marquis sighed with emotion, then, without waiting for Luo Chen to be modest, took out a storage bag.

"Here, for you."

"What is this?" Luo Chen curiously took the storage bag.

The Purple-Robed Marquis smiled. "What else could it be? It's what Li Tianshi promised you!"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow and took out an object from the storage bag.

It was a crystalline substance like amber.

Inside, a pure white gu insect was sleeping soundly.

"Is this the Hundred Poison King Gu?"

(End of this chapter)
