# Chapter 748: The Gap Between Heaven and Earth

Whoosh!

On the cloud-mist-shrouded plaza, Luo Chen's figure appeared.

The moment he landed, a Dao Sect disciple came forward.

Before the disciple could speak, Luo Chen revealed a jade token.

Light flickered on the jade token. The Adept cultivator only glanced at it and immediately understood Luo Chen's identity.

"So it is a Star Gate guest elder. This way, please!"

Luo Chen nodded and followed behind him, stepping into the cloud and mist. The instant he entered the plaza, it was as though he passed through a thin membrane.

In that instant, his powerful body actually felt faintly constrained.

"Is this a defensive formation?"

The guide cultivator blinked. "Senior has good eyes. This is indeed Heaven-Earth Peak's naturally generated defensive formation. It is usually not activated. This time, the Alchemy Saint's ascension is my sect's foremost great matter. Though my sect is not worried anything will go wrong, what should be done still must be done thoroughly."

So that was it.

Luo Chen subconsciously looked back at the invisible, formless defensive formation.

A defensive formation naturally generated by Heaven-Earth Peak, which contained a fifth-tier spirit vein?

He could sense the terror contained within it!

It actually made even his powerful body, comparable to a fourth-tier desolate beast, feel somewhat uncomfortable.

This was still only half activated, not fully stimulated.

If the formation fully opened, even if he attacked with all his strength, he might not necessarily break it.

Of course, it was also because his body was too powerful and therefore overly sensitive. Other people probably would not even realize such a formation had been activated on Heaven-Earth Peak.

"May I ask its name?" he asked one more question.

The Adept cultivator paused. This Senior named Luo Chen was a Star Gate guest elder. How could he not know the formation's name?

But very quickly, he remembered that the other party's identity jade token had the additional words "external affairs."

Presumably, his authority was insufficient, and there were many things he did not understand.

After hesitating, he still gave Luo Chen the answer.

"Severing Heaven-Earth Passage[TERM?]!"

Luo Chen froze. What kind of name was this?

Heaven-Earth Peak was so high its summit could not be seen. Even this vast cloud-sea plaza, seen from outside, seemed only at the mountainside.

It could be said that Heaven-Earth Peak, this ancient and extraordinary mountain, truly had a few hints of connecting heaven and earth.

Yet its naturally generated defensive formation was called Severing Heaven-Earth Passage?

He wanted to ask more, but clearly the Adept cultivator was unwilling to say more, or perhaps he did not know the formation's true effect either.

After all, from when the Tianyuan Dao Sect opened Central Continent more than ten thousand years ago to when it settled Central Continent three thousand years ago, this formation on Heaven-Earth Peak had never been activated.

How would the world know the wonder of the Severing Heaven-Earth Passage Grand Formation?

Suppressing the doubts in his heart, Luo Chen arrived at a corner of the cloud-sea plaza slightly to the south.

Before he even approached, he heard lively clamor.

"Senior Luo, we have arrived. Star Gate cultivators have been arranged here to observe the ceremony. If you have any needs, you may instruct the surrounding Dao Sect disciples at any time."

"Mm. You may go."

Luo Chen waved his hand, then walked inside.

As his figure appeared, one gaze after another turned toward him.

On the surface, Luo Chen did not change expression, but his heart suddenly tightened.

There was no other reason!

In this moment, among the several thousand gazes looking at him, the owners of nearly a hundred were at the Sage realm!

And every one of them had a realm above his!

The weaker were third or fourth level Sages, and the stronger were sixth or seventh level Sages.

And these were only the ones actively emitting magic power fluctuations.

Most had chosen to hide their cultivation. Unless Luo Chen actively probed, he could not see their specific realms at all.

"So this is the power of Heaven-Earth Peak's Star Gate?"

Luo Chen swallowed.

Even though he had repeatedly overestimated Star Gate's strength and calculated the number of Star Gate Sages again and again, even factoring in normal aging, death, lifespan exhaustion, and seated passing, he still had not expected there to be nearly a hundred Sages.

This power alone, if placed outside, would be entirely enough to settle a continent!

And this was the accumulation of Star Gate across three thousand six hundred years, and also part of the Tianyuan Dao Sect's enormous foundation!

"Fellow Daoist Luo, over here!"

While his heart shook, the familiar Elder Seven Lamps took the initiative to wave at him.

Luo Chen immediately walked over.

On the way, he also nodded briefly to Winter Mountain Hermit and Lu Qiuzhen, who were looking at him.

Clearly, they had arrived earlier than him.

After all, they were proper Star Gate guest elders. The Alchemy Saint's ascension was Star Gate's foremost great matter, so they naturally received notice earlier and prepared in advance.

Luo Chen drew in his gaze and looked toward Elder Seven Lamps. Four old men had gathered together there.

Each one's realm was not visible, but each one's calmness gave Luo Chen a pressure like deep seas and high mountains.

Could these be four of Heaven-Earth Peak's five great outside-continent elders?

He could not clearly identify them. Back when the Seven Elders had decided inside Star Gate, every person's face had been blurred, and even the Alchemy Saint had only been remembered by her frail build.

When Luo Chen reached them, two of the four left ahead of time.

Only Elder Seven Lamps and a gray-robed old man remained.

"Let me introduce you. Wilderness Wanderer Luo Chen, who joined Star Gate not long ago."

Luo Chen gave the gray-robed old man a Daoist salute to show politeness.

Then Elder Seven Lamps introduced the gray-robed old man's identity to Luo Chen.

"Offering Elder Hui Yuanhong[TERM?]..."

Only an offering elder?

It seemed somewhat different from what he imagined!

But Elder Seven Lamps was not finished. He continued, "After the Alchemy Saint ascends, one seat among Star Gate's five great outside-continent elders will become empty. At that time, Offering Elder Hui will take it."

Luo Chen suddenly understood.

At this moment, Hui Yuanhong looked at Luo Chen with interest, sizing him up again and again.

"You are the first external affairs guest elder admitted into Heaven-Earth Peak's Star Gate by the Alchemy Saint's exceptional approval?"

Before Luo Chen could answer, Elder Seven Lamps said helplessly, "Why circle around? Did I not say that earlier?"

Hui Yuanhong laughed. "Fellow Daoist Luo, do not mind. I am merely getting familiar. In the future, we will all cultivate on Heaven-Earth Peak together. On the immortal path, I hope we can support each other."

Luo Chen smiled and nodded, his tone modest. "Luo is only an external affairs guest elder, and cannot deserve Elder Hui's favor. With your profound realm, it should be you supporting Luo more. Mutual support is too much to say."

What a fine "Elder Hui"!

Hui Yuanhong was very happy. He had not yet become one of Heaven-Earth Peak's nineteen elders, yet was already addressed this way. How could he not be pleased?

Elder Seven Lamps was somewhat surprised. This Wilderness Wanderer was very skilled in worldly relations!

Hui Yuanhong smiled. "External affairs guest elder is not worth mentioning. Fellow Daoist Luo only needs to do some work for the Tianyuan Dao Sect later and establish a little merit. Turning into a formal guest elder will only take a word from us."

"Work for the Dao Sect?" Luo Chen was curious. "What matter could a sacred land ruling a continent need to trouble me with?"

"Soon, soon!" Hui Yuanhong patted Luo Chen's shoulder. "You came at precisely the right time!"

He did not specify the matter. After affectionately patting Luo Chen's shoulder, he took his leave and blended into the crowd.

Clearly, in order to deal with his imminent promotion to elder, he was enthusiastically making connections.

Luo Chen was merely one of them, and also someone relatively easy to win over. After all, Luo Chen was the last outside-continent cultivator to join Heaven-Earth Peak.

After he left, Luo Chen unnaturally twisted his shoulder.

It had been many years since someone had "valued" him like this.

Beside him, Elder Seven Lamps slowly spoke. "Do not mind it. Offering Elder Hui has been too happy lately, so it is normal for some actions to overstep."

Luo Chen gave an "mm," indicating he did not mind.

Clearly, the other party's overstepping was not merely physical. Promising that Luo Chen could become a formal guest elder was also overstepping by prematurely exercising the authority of a Heaven-Earth Peak elder.

Luo Chen looked around and could not help sighing.

"So many people!"

"Many?" Elder Seven Lamps stroked his long beard. "This is only half the strength of Heaven-Earth Peak."

Luo Chen's pupils shrank, and he blurted, "Half?"

Even though this corner still had Sages entering from time to time, it had only just passed a hundred.

But if this was only half, did Star Gate have as many as two hundred Sage cultivators?

What kind of joke was that!

Elder Seven Lamps knew he had misunderstood and immediately explained, "Star Gate's composition is not only outside-continent cultivators. This can be seen from the establishment of Heaven-Earth Peak's nineteen elders."

Nineteen elders: twelve from the Great Houses, two from the Aristocratic Clans, and five seats held by outside-continent cultivators.

In other words, Central Continent clans also occupied part of Star Gate?

Sure enough, Elder Seven Lamps's later words confirmed this guess.

"Some Sage cultivators from the major clans voluntarily join Star Gate after reaching a certain age."

"Only, they usually do not cultivate on Heaven-Earth Peak. They only come to Heaven-Earth Peak when they are about to pass away, choose a cave abode, and wait quietly for death to arrive."

"These people are not seen today because their clans will also come to observe the ceremony, so each went to their own clan's viewing platform."

So that was it.

Luo Chen now fully understood.

Not only that, through Elder Seven Lamps's later introduction, he also learned other circumstances of Star Gate's outside-continent cultivators.

Many times, outside-continent cultivators who joined Star Gate did not come alone, but brought families and followers.

Only, those descendants could not cultivate on Heaven-Earth Peak and were arranged by the Tianyuan Dao Sect in other places in Central Continent.

If those descendants had originally been in a clan model, they would continue to exist as a clan.

If they had originally been a sect inheritance model, they would be broken up and each form small cultivation clans, or merge into some local Central Continent clans through marriage.

From this point, it was very difficult for the descendants brought by outside-continent Sages to integrate into Central Continent.

But the Tianyuan Dao Sect gave them an opportunity.

That was that after they had stayed in Central Continent for five hundred years, their descendants would have the chance to enter the Tianyuan Dao Sect to study.

The reason the Star Gate viewing platform had more than a thousand people today was precisely because many of them were descendants of Star Gate guest elders and offering elders, taking this chance to reunite.

Luo Chen was curious and asked whether there had been successful precedents.

He received an affirmative answer.

From this, it seemed the Tianyuan Dao Sect truly had not spoken empty words, but had put matters into practice.

After understanding all this, Luo Chen's heart also could not help filling with emotion.

"No wonder Star Gate can absorb so many outside-continent Sage experts. It gives them hope of advancing to Ascendant and also resolves their worldly worries."

Amid this feeling, he could not help imagining.

"If I go back and bring the old acquaintances of Luo Tian Sect to Central Continent, would they also have the chance to enjoy all this?"

Central Continent's cultivation philosophy certainly did not match the path Luo Chen walked.

But Central Continent's peace of countless years was something no one would dislike. Even if there was competition, it was only healthy competition within the sect and among clans, not life-and-death killing at every turn and endless slaughter.

Such a place matched the traditional descriptions of a sacred cultivation land.

The more he understood, the thinner the self-regarding thought of being a "passing guest" in Luo Chen's heart became. He actually developed some thoughts of taking root here!

At this moment, cloud and mist trembled throughout the field. White seats formed from cloud patterns, mist, and majestic spiritual qi rose one after another.

"All cultivators, take your seats!"

By his ear, that aged, vicissitude-filled voice sounded again.

"It is Sect Master Mo!"

Elder Seven Lamps said this, then floated upward.

Luo Chen was no exception. Following a mysterious guidance, he drifted onto a cloud chair high in the air.

At this height, vast cloud and mist could no longer obstruct his sight.

Looking from south to north, there were fifty chairs facing them from afar.

At first glance, Luo Chen's gaze fell on the two chairs in the very corner.

The reason was that on one of those two chairs sat a cramped Adept cultivator.

This was the only Adept cultivator among the many Sages seated high in the clouds.

"One of the two representatives chosen by Central Continent's countless humble families?"

The basis for Luo Chen's judgment was the other chair, where early-stage Sage Dragon Abyss sat impressively!

The other party had not yet had time to elevate the humble Liu family into the ranks of Aristocratic Clans, so he was still treated as a humble family. Then the person beside him should naturally also be a humble-family cultivator.

At this moment, Sage Dragon Abyss instantly sensed Luo Chen's gaze.

He said nothing, only nodded lightly.

Luo Chen frowned. What did this nod mean?

Without thinking too much, Luo Chen's gaze wandered and swept over the other forty-eight chairs one by one.

These people should be the representatives of the Twelve Great Houses and Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans.

...

On the cloud-sea plaza, dense crowds stood below. From Qi Refinement to Adept, every realm was present.

Clearly, these people were the younger clan members brought by the major clans to observe the ceremony.

At this moment, three of them stood together, looking into the high air for something, and finally found their target among the Sages to the south.

"He is there! Senior Brother Changfengzi, is he truly that person?"

"His appearance is completely different, and his temperament is entirely different. Is that intelligence true or false?"

Facing the questions of Guan Xiaoping and Wu Tao, Changfengzi nodded with difficulty.

There was no mistake!

The intelligence collected by the Shen family was extremely accurate!

Wilderness Wanderer was Devil Lord Azure Yang, and Luo Hai was naturally Luo Chen, now seated high in the clouds.

Facing Changfengzi's certainty, Guan and Wu fell into a daze and could not control themselves.

Back then, he had merely been a rogue cultivator who guided the way for them. Now, he had actually reached this step?

And they, after several decades, had merely raised their cultivation by one neither-large-nor-small level, from early-stage Adept to mid-stage Adept.

Was the gap truly this huge?

One could even say that in response to their gazes, the other party did not lower his head to glance even once.

Between Adept and Sage, there was already the gap between heaven and earth.

...

Among the many clan representatives, Luo Chen accurately found where Fu Chaosheng sat.

After they smiled knowingly at each other, neither transmitted with divine sense.

Luo Chen continued observing the others, especially taking a few more looks at the representatives sent by the Twelve Great Houses.

All had deep cultivation and profound Dao attainments.

Then he looked toward the western side, the outermost corner.

There, cloud chairs with complex and noble patterns had likewise risen.

Those seated upon them did not exceed Luo Chen's expectations. Several were precisely people who had appeared in the Peach Garden.

West Desert eminent monks, the Southern Frontier black robe, and the young person from the Eastern Wilderness Nether Origin Sect.

As for the others, Luo Chen had no impression.

These people totaled more than ten.

After sweeping the whole field, Luo Chen had a rough concept in his heart.

"Over a hundred Sages, Central Continent clans, and representatives of three continents. These people together form the observation group for this ascension ceremony. Its specifications are already extremely high!"

"This also expresses the Tianyuan Dao Sect's regard for the Alchemy Saint's ascension."

"If I, or some other outside-continent cultivator in Star Gate, becomes Ascendant and ascends, will we also receive this treatment?"

There was certainly more than Luo Chen alone with such thoughts present.

On the southern viewing platform, every Star Gate Sage seated in the clouds had rising emotions and stray thoughts.

The three-continent representatives on the west side also had different thoughts.

The West Desert eminent monks seemed calm, but judging by their constantly turning prayer beads, they were probably not as composed as they appeared.

The young person from the Eastern Wilderness Nether Origin Sect looked somewhat gloomy, repeatedly looking around as though expecting something, yet also resisting something.

The Southern Frontier cultivator shrouded entirely in a black robe showed no expression, but their gaze occasionally glanced toward the Star Gate cultivators on the south side.

Suddenly, everyone's thoughts paused for an instant.

Everyone's attention subconsciously turned east.

Amid the cloud sea, three figures slowly appeared.

Yet everyone ignored the other two and looked at the old man at the front.

"We greet the Sect Master!"

No one knew who spoke first, and then came a mountain-and-sea roar.

Even the representatives of the three continents respectfully voiced, "We greet Sect Master Mo."

Luo Chen was no exception, respectfully greeting him with everyone.

Mo Shouzhuo[TERM?]!

The current sect master of the Tianyuan Dao Sect!

Realm: Ascendant!

He alone represented the entire Tianyuan Dao Sect!

But no one would ignore him because he seemed alone. Not only because of his realm, but also because everyone present, apart from the three-continent representatives to the north, was part of the Tianyuan Dao Sect.

Amid that mountain-and-sea greeting, Luo Chen for the first time truly felt the power of Central Continent's sole master.

Since arriving in Central Continent, Luo Chen had come into contact with Great Houses and great clans, Aristocratic Clans and humble families. What he saw was Heaven-Earth Peak and Star Gate's gathered cultivators. The name Dao Sect, which had once been so widely spread in the Eastern Wilderness and Northern Sea, had instead become less clear.

Even after visiting the Rotten Axe-Handle Mountain Range, Luo Chen had not felt much.

But in this moment, he thoroughly understood what the Tianyuan Dao Sect was.

The Twelve Great Houses were the Tianyuan Dao Sect.

The Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans were the Tianyuan Dao Sect.

Countless humble families and small clans were also the Tianyuan Dao Sect.

Heaven-Earth Peak's Star Gate was likewise the Tianyuan Dao Sect.

Yet the Tianyuan Dao Sect was only one. All people and matters added together, hundreds of Sages added together, even this Central Continent beneath their feet, whose length and width were unknown by millions of li, all counted together, were the true Tianyuan Dao Sect!

"This is a true sacred land!"

Luo Chen groaned inwardly, infinite longing in his eyes.

At this moment, Dao Sect Master Mo Shouzhuo lifted his right hand and pressed lightly in the air.

Ten thousand people fell silent; not even a crow or sparrow made a sound.

Only his faint sentence remained. "Begin."

As soon as the words fell, Heaven-Earth Peak shook.

In that moment, everyone felt this ancient mountain, which had stood in the Mountains and Seas Realm since time immemorial, seem to come alive.

An indescribable momentum began rising wildly, from small to great, weak to strong.

Boom!

Mountain wind surged, white clouds swayed.

A hazy phantom suddenly appeared in the void.

The phantom was indistinct, but its features were incomparably clear.

Brows and eyes like a painting, pupils like autumn water.

The bridge of the nose was straight, yet not lacking softness, like a work of art carved by an ancient master sculptor.

Her lips opened and closed slightly, like cherry blossoms just beginning to bloom.

Her cheeks were slightly round, giving a frail impression while also carrying a few parts of adorable liveliness.

Everyone raised their heads and looked up at that phantom face.

The young man from the Nether Origin Sect clenched his hands, fingertips stabbing deep into his skin.

"It is Martial Aunt Chu Yan!"

Seated on his cloud chair, Luo Chen fell into a trance.

"Yin soul crossing into yang, Nascent Soul transforming into spirit. This is a primordial spirit!"

In this moment, though he had already cultivated to the Sage realm he had dreamed of in youth, he still felt the gap between heaven and earth.

Before that phantom face, he was as weak as an ant.

*(End of Chapter)*
