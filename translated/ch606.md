# Chapter 606: Do You Truly Wish to Die?

Peng Island was vast — its surface covered in dense forest and circled by water birds.

Even after Luo Chen had taken up residence, the island kept its original, unpolished face, with only a handful of halls built upon it.

Beyond the Star-Dipper Hall where he cultivated and Tian Xuan's own residence, there was only one addition: the Qingyang Hall.

This was the annex hall Luo Chen used for alchemy and artifact-forging day to day.

At this moment, inside the Qingyang Hall, the Primordial Unity Cauldron hung at the center. The origin true flame burned fierce and relentless.

Luo Chen, dressed in black, watched the great cauldron as his hands moved through a steady stream of spiritual seals — raw mineral materials flowing in like streams of light.

"Choosing the Primordial Unity Cauldron as my natal law device was the right decision."

"This treasure can be used for both alchemy and artifact-forging, sparing me the effort of gathering a whole separate set of forging tools."

He said it with quiet satisfaction, his full attention on the reactions of the materials inside the cauldron.

Then — his brow furrowed. He reached out a hand, and a palm-sized lump of golden stone flew out from the cauldron.

"This piece of meteoric iron wasn't processed properly?"

"I knew it — cheap goods are cheap for a reason."

Muttering, he tossed the meteoric iron into the Withering-Bloom True Flame, subjecting it to extreme-heat calcination.

After roughly the time it took to burn half a stick of incense, he recalled the meteoric iron and let it fall onto a large stone block in the center of the Qingyang Hall.

Then he turned his wrist — and a small hammer settled into his hand.

The Flowing Fire Hammer, taken from the Golden Core cultivator of Hundred Craft Mountain.

This fire-aligned attack law device wasn't particularly high in grade — only lower-grade — but it had an excellent compatibility with fire-affinity mana. After erasing the previous owner's divine sense impression and re-consecrating it, Luo Chen had been using it as a replacement for the destroyed Mystic Flame Sword.

In combat, its offensive power was somewhat lacking, and its flying speed was too slow to reliably strike a moving opponent.

But in the forging hall, it was where it truly shone.

Small hammer in hand, Luo Chen stripped off his black outer robe, baring a torso corded with muscle.

With a roll of his arm, the muscles flexed — and he swung the Flowing Fire Hammer, striking the meteoric iron in one heavy blow after another.

Sparks flew in showers. The impurities within the meteoric iron grew fewer with each strike.

And in the attribute panel, the proficiency of the Flowing Fire Hammer Method climbed, point by point, with each blow.

"At this rate, when the Flowing Fire Hammer Method reaches mastery in a few more years and I wield this law device with full control — I think I'll surpass its former owner."

Artifact-forging consumed considerable mana.

In fact, it felt more taxing to Luo Chen than alchemy.

But it was not without its rewards.

Tier-Four Black Emperor Ointment was ferociously potent.

Even continuously running the perfected Myriad Path Convergence and using the Withering-Bloom True Flame to refine his body, Luo Chen could not absorb it perfectly.

But through the high-intensity work of forging, he discovered that his body was slowly absorbing those residual traces of Black Emperor Ointment that had accumulated in his flesh — driving his body cultivation progress steadily faster.

After recognizing this benefit, the frequency of Luo Chen's forging work had only increased over the years, and his instrument output had grown higher and higher.

Two gains at once — why wouldn't he pursue it?

Hammer shadows blurred; sparks fell like rain.

*Clang!*

The final strike landed. Luo Chen looked at the meteoric iron — now reduced to two-thirds of its original size — and allowed himself a satisfied smile.

"This quality will do for forging the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor."

Any accomplished craftsman who happened to hear Luo Chen mention "the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor" might have found that puzzling.

Wasn't that a third-tier water defense formation array?

How had it become the name of an instrument or law device?

In truth, Luo Chen had simply used a shorthand name for convenience.

The defensive law device crafting method given to him by Yu Gao from Penglai Immortal Sect combined ancient forging arts with the mainstream forging methods of the current era — an elaborate process with many demanding components, from material selection to proficiency requirements across multiple disciplines.

Over these years, Luo Chen had been working through each of those components one by one, tackling them in sequence.

He could not forge the Xuanyan Armor directly — but he could experiment on individual details of the Penglai method in isolation.

Luo Chen believed: if he could handle each component cleanly, then when he finally sat down to forge the Xuanyan Armor in earnest, his success rate would be vastly improved.

Among all those demanding components, the single hardest was that the completed law device would need to carry a full fourth-tier formation array inscribed within it.

Its name: the Yin-Yang Six Armor Formation.

Seeing it for the first time, Luo Chen had cursed — who inscribes a fourth-tier formation into a law device? This wasn't a Primordial Unity Cauldron they were forging.

But over years of research, he had come to see that the Yin-Yang Six Armor Formation held a deeper secret.

Though designated as fourth-tier, it was in practice a combination of seven third-tier formation arrays.

At the core was the Yin-Yang Array — a straightforward concept: yin and yang, heaven and earth, odd and even, hard and soft, black and gold, cloudy sky — any pair of opposites could serve as the yin-yang. What mattered was that the formation carried that dual polarity.

Han Zhan knew this field well.

And Luo Chen had mastered it easily in turn.

The challenge lay in the other component: the Six Armors.

Six third-tier defensive formation arrays.

The Dark Blue Mysterious Armor Array was one among them — and among third-tier formations, it was already famous as a preeminent water-defense array.

Beyond it lay formations such as the Bright Yellow Boulder Armor Array, the Yimu Cyan Armor Array, and others.

Every one was a standout defensive formation in the third-tier class — and for an ordinary person to learn even one would be a significant undertaking.

By comparison, the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor Array was the simplest of the six.

After years of study, Luo Chen had entered the foundation of it.

What he was attempting now was to follow the Penglai method and forge a defensive law device — inscribing just one of the six armors, the Dark Blue Mysterious Armor Array, onto it.

Using the same crafting approach, with only partial formations and materials, to forge a similar but smaller-scale law device. If it failed, the cost would not be ruinous — and if it succeeded, it would provide accumulated experience for the eventual main project.

That was the logic behind his plan.

Just as Luo Chen was examining the meteoric iron, his brow lifted suddenly.

He turned toward the direction of the lakeshore.

*The unwelcome guest has finally arrived.*

...

The Fulong Mountain Range stretched without end for ten thousand li.

Ancient lore held it to be the resting place where a great primordial beast had fallen in ages past.

It was precisely this that had made it the Myriad Immortals Assembly's base of operations — countless wandering cultivators living their secluded lives within its bounds.

Peaks rose and fell through the range; ravines cut deep between them.

Dense canopy and plunging waterfalls. Rivers and lakes winding through the landscape, filled with birds and beasts on every side.

Had this place not been so remote — and had it not bordered the old territory of the Primordial Demon Sect, making other powers too wary to encroach — the Assembly never would have settled here so comfortably.

Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake lay in the southern portion of the Fulong Mountain Range.

For years, the lake had been well-known within the Assembly, widely recognized as a coveted piece of spirit land.

Mainly because of its awkward circumstances.

Within it ran several third-tier spirit veins and twenty-some second-tier veins — the kind of land an ordinary Golden Core cultivator or Foundation Establishment practitioner might dream of.

But the Assembly refused to rent it out piecemeal.

Its rental price was nearly equivalent to a Tier-Three upper-grade cave dwelling.

This meant ordinary Golden Core cultivators would not choose it as a long-term cultivation base. And those with the wealth for it had better options.

Not that they couldn't afford to rent it — but that it offered no value for the price.

After all, not everyone could do what Luo Chen had done: lay a formation to elevate the spirit vein grade to upper-tier.

These circumstances were why Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake had sat vacant year after year.

Until rumor brought word that Demon Lord Qingyang had moved in.

And today, Peng Lake had unwanted guests.

In the sky, a magnificent cloud ship descended slowly, its immense wake churning the lake's surface into rolling waves.

The cultivators stationed at Wind-Wave Pavilion on the shore tensed and stepped forward cautiously to make inquiry.

"May I ask your honored titles? Do you have a calling card? Would you like me to inform Master Qingyang?"

He hadn't mentioned the Zhang family of Cailian Island.

A cloud ship of obvious law device caliber — its master would not lower himself to call on a Foundation Establishment family. The most likely purpose was a visit to the master of Peng Lake, Master Qingyang himself.

Much like the Moya Cave Master's visit not long ago.

On the cloud ship, Foundation Establishment cultivators were densely packed — over a hundred in number.

Those people wore cold expressions and had no interest in answering this Zhang family Qi Refinement cultivator's question.

Suddenly, the crowd parted, opening a path through the middle.

Seven figures appeared, advancing in measured steps.

Old and young, men and women.

Every one of them radiated a powerful spiritual pressure.

The pavilion guard swallowed hard, his legs going weak, barely keeping his footing.

Seven Golden Core cultivators.

At the front: a purple-robed figure, broad-browed and clear-eyed, his gaze vivid and commanding.

He glanced once at the Qi Refinement cultivator, then gave a nod to the elder beside him.

Without hesitation, the elder produced something and placed it in the cultivator's hands.

"This is our master's calling card."

"Junior, inform your master quickly — do not delay our master's affairs."

Hearing this, the guard, though still tense, felt a small measure of relief.

At least they weren't forcing their way in.

He took the card, entered the pavilion, and placed it on a stone platform inside — along with a spirit stone.

At once, spiritual light blossomed.

The golden calling card floated slowly within it, then settled gently back down after a moment.

By the lakeshore, on the cloud ship, the seven Golden Core cultivators were in quiet conversation.

"Master, this lake's owner shows no presence at all — the cultivator guarding his entrance is only at Qi Refinement. He can't even put a Foundation Establishment cultivator on watch."

"Truly laughable. Any Golden Core power worth the name would at least have a proper Foundation Establishment cultivator at the gate."

Out of the seven, only two spoke much — both elderly men of very advanced age.

Their mode of address was telling: in their mouths, the term was "Master." The others, for their part, addressed the purple-robed man as "Fellow Daoist," "Elder Brother Daoist," or "Brother Huangfu."

Huangfu Song wore a composed smile as he observed the scene.

"A treasure land with no worthy steward — this wandering cultivator Qingyang, sitting here alone, is wasting what he has."

But when his divine sense caught the subtle movement of the formation array inside the pavilion, he raised an eyebrow.

"This formation is curious — something like the Zhou dynasty's beacon signal towers of old, yet distinct. It seems to transmit the caller's message."

Someone nearby offered their read: "I've heard the master of Peng Lake is an artifact-forger with knowledge of formations — craftsmen and formation masters are rarely far apart. He may have designed this himself based on a low-tier array."

Huangfu Song let the comment pass without reply.

Nearby, a black-robed elder said with contempt: "You've all been deceived. This Qingyang is only famous among low-tier wandering cultivators. His real ability amounts to nothing more than forging an upper-grade magic instrument. Any of us, if we put in a hundred years, could match that. 'Master forger' is too generous a title."

The others smiled and nodded without adding much.

But someone was pointing into the distance.

Here and there among the peaks and clouds, streaks of light appeared and disappeared — cultivators who had heard the commotion and come from all over to watch.

On the other side, Huangfu Song was quietly irritated. These Golden Core cultivators he had gathered — most of them drawn by his Great Zhou lineage's method for forming a Nascent Infant — had no loyalty to speak of. The only ones he could truly count on were his two old servants.

But at the same time, he was quietly pleased.

Arriving at Peng Lake in full display, in no hurry, had drawn the attention of every reclusive cultivator within the Fulong Mountain Range who had come out to watch. That was precisely his intent.

With a large audience present, his eventual negotiation with Demon Lord Qingyang would be on much more favorable terms.

And even if negotiation failed — a public battle, with witnesses to the outcome, would leave Qingyang no room to dispute it afterward.

High-level cultivators cared about face.

...

Among the peaks, the various wandering cultivators who had heard the news and assembled whispered in clusters.

"What's happening? Is there going to be a fight? If so, just get on with it — why are they waiting around politely?"

"If there's no fight, then it's a visit. Isn't it?"

"Who comes calling with their whole family in tow? Seven Golden Core cultivators and a hundred Foundation Establishment ones — tell me it's a siege and I'd believe you."

"That's true. I heard the Moya Cave Master's visit to Master Qingyang was light travel — just a few attendant girls with him."

"So — courtesy first, then force?"

"That seems about right."

Lower-level cultivators clustered together; higher-level ones had their own preferred vantage points.

In fact, Huangfu Song had chosen a fortunate moment.

Some of the more sociable Golden Core cultivators had just organized a private gathering, and when the commotion reached them, they came over together to watch the fun.

Several Golden Core cultivators sat atop a peak, watching Peng Lake from afar.

"That Huangfu Song, I've heard, is descended from the blood of the Great Zhou royal clan. When the Primordial Demon Sect broke the Great Zhou as a partial punishment rather than wiping it out entirely, these remnants managed to build themselves back up."

"Looking at this now, he wants Peng Lake's spirit land as a base to stage his comeback."

"Perceptive of you."

"Ha — what perception? This news has been circulating for years. I just assumed Huangfu Song would make for the Hundred Ghosts Cavern — but it turns out he had his eye on Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake."

"Ah, you wouldn't know. The Ghost Spirit Child — one of the three Demon Hunter Office chiefs — has given up hope on reaching Nascent Soul and intends to found a family and leave behind a lineage. The Hundred Ghosts Cavern has been quietly pre-arranged as his territory these past years."

The Demon Hunter Office currently had three eight-star demon hunters — all at Golden Core Stage Nine.

They were: the Fishing Elder, the Purple Dowager, and the Ghost Spirit Child.

After reaching eight-star rank, all three had effectively taken control of the Office and rarely accepted assignments. The title "Chief" was the respectful term used for these three.

Learning that the Ghost Spirit Child had already reserved the Hundred Ghosts Cavern, one cultivator understood immediately.

The Fulong Mountain Range's spirit land was abundant in small dwellings — countless in number, many of high grade. But large territories like the Hundred Ghosts Cavern and Peng Lake were considerably fewer.

Those listening had long since pieced it together: dwellings of that scale were, in effect, reserved by the Assembly for wandering cultivators who wished to build lasting powers.

And Huangfu Song had plainly set his sights on Qingyang — who sat here alone with no backing.

"If things come to a head, do you think the Demon Hunter Office will get involved?"

"Ha — who knows? Maybe the three chiefs are already watching from the shadows."

In the midst of this chatter, someone noticed a man standing in the corner — his complexion oddly pale and unnatural.

"Fellow Daoist Duan Li — if I recall correctly, didn't you join the Assembly at the same time as Qingyang?"

Duan Li's expression was somewhat uncomfortable as he gave a nod.

He had come to this gathering hoping to learn something about treating his ongoing injuries, and had not expected the gathering to dissolve halfway when everyone ran off to watch this unfold.

Even less had he expected the man at the center of it to be Qingyang.

But on second thought — this was hardly surprising.

Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake. So many people had coveted it.

Even when Luo Chen had chosen this place years ago, Duan Li had foreseen this possibility — and had cautioned him once.

Luo Chen had gone ahead regardless. And now — here they were.

He let out a quiet sigh. "I did join at the same time as Qingyang. We've had some dealings over the years."

Someone nearby laughed. "If he starts to lose, will you help him?"

Duan Li startled, opened his mouth — and for a long time could not produce a single word.

...

Cailian Island.

Cailian Island was one of the larger islands in Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake — carrying a mid-scale second-tier spirit vein, though positioned toward the lake's perimeter rather than its center.

It had therefore been the first to detect the cloud ship's arrival.

Zhang Jiadi held his daughter Qingqing in his arms and watched the scene with a troubled expression.

Behind him stood the scattered wandering cultivators who had joined the Zhang family over the years.

A cultivation family could not be built in a day. Some of them were early relatives from Wen Xiu's birth family, but more were wandering cultivators with modest means and no one to depend on — who had voluntarily joined the Zhangs for the shelter of a powerful patron, trading their service for a cultivation land of their own.

Or so the plan had gone. In a hundred years, as the family's numbers grew, these affiliates would gradually fade to the margins — but for now, they still formed the Zhang family's backbone.

At this moment, behind him the murmuring swelled — voices heavy with worry and despair.

A droning, insistent hum — like mosquitoes, grating on the nerves.

Zhang Jiadi turned sharply and swept them with a glare.

The aura of a late Foundation Establishment cultivator exploded outward.

"Silence."

Everyone flinched.

Over these years Zhang Jiadi had been mild and easy-going — if anything, less strict than Wen Xiu. They had almost forgotten: it was the family head who held the highest cultivation among them.

Zhang Jiadi's gaze swept down the line like a blade.

"There may be a battle ahead — life and death can turn in an instant. The Zhang family is Master Qingyang's affiliate. We advance and retreat together."

"I will not hear defeatist talk again."

"Any who defy this: shall be cut down. Any who break and run from battle: shall be cut down."

A few who had been nursing private thoughts went pale at once.

Some moved to speak, but looking at Zhang Jiadi's dark expression, swallowed their words.

Zhang Jiadi drew a slow breath. Inside, he felt no great fear.

He had gone to considerable lengths to learn the full details of the Cold Light Island battle — and his esteem for Senior Immortal Qingyang ran deep into his bones.

Seven Golden Core cultivators.

In the Cold Light Island battle, the enemies Demon Lord Qingyang had faced numbered more than that.

And there was another reason — perhaps the most important.

He had staked everything he owned on Cailian Island.

If Senior Immortal Qingyang truly lost and withdrew — where would he go?

Back to being a wandering cultivator with nothing?

That, he could accept. But what about his wife and daughter?

This was precisely why, once the initial panic had passed, his resolve had crystallized in an instant.

He would stand with Senior Immortal Qingyang no matter what happened. Life or death — together.

...

Meanwhile, regardless of how each party reacted.

At the lakeshore, on the cloud ship — the crowd of cultivators who had come with Huangfu Song had grown progressively quieter.

Every face had hardened with resolve.

Because Peng Island had sent back no reply.

Huangfu Song stepped forward, standing at the prow of the ship as the wind rushed past him.

"I meant to resolve this peacefully. But it seems the master of this place does not even wish to be seen, let alone negotiate."

"In that case — let our actions speak."

He swept a hand.

With a rumble, the cloud ship surged to life — a continent in the sky — blotting out the sun as it flew into Peng Lake.

At first the ship moved fast.

But gradually its speed slowed.

An invisible retarding force covered the ship from every direction.

Beyond that, from within the lake itself, a powerful pulling force dragged the ship lower and lower.

"Night Elder — what is this?" Huangfu Song asked the black-robed elder beside him. This man had once served as the Grand Astrologer of the Great Zhou, and was highly skilled in formation arts.

The Night Elder's expression was calm. "It's a no-fly formation — the kind used in large markets and major immortal cities. Our law device-grade cloud ship is unaffected by it."

Then his gaze narrowed at the water.

"However, inside the no-fly formation there is another concealed array, and that is what is slowing the ship and pulling it down."

His fingers moved in small motions as he worked through seals, attempting to deduce the hidden formation's operating logic.

After a short while, he shook his head.

"No good — too little time. And the master of this place has laid his preparations carefully. I can't work it out quickly."

The gray-robed elder beside them said in a sharp voice: "Then we push through by force."

Huangfu Song gave a nod. "As Lord Gui says."

In the next moment, over a hundred Foundation Establishment cultivators on the ship sat down in their predetermined array, channeling their spiritual power into the ship's formation.

The ship shuddered — and surged forward again.

Huangfu Song, watching this, allowed a small smile.

"A few hundred li, gone in an eyeblink. Demon Lord Qingyang — I hope you don't disappoint me."

At the peak of his satisfaction —

Suddenly.

From within Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake, vast quantities of heaven and earth spiritual qi erupted upward.

Eight chains of mana formed in the air, binding the cloud ship immovably tight.

An enormous force crashed down.

"How can this be?"

The Night Elder's face went white. The combined spiritual power of over a hundred Foundation Establishment cultivators, augmented by the ship's own formation, should have been enough to hold off a Golden Core cultivator — let alone an ordinary no-fly array.

But the ship was plummeting, and there was no time for further analysis.

"Master, abandon the ship and fly!"

Huangfu Song's expression had long since lost its pleasure. He looked at the crowd of Foundation Establishment cultivators stumbling and lurching across the ship's deck.

"What about them?"

"There's no managing them. Unless Demon Lord Qingyang wants a blood feud — he won't strike to kill." The Night Elder raised his voice.

Huangfu Song hesitated, watching the ship race toward the water's surface below.

At last, his mind made up.

"Everyone — out of the ship."

In an instant, his Golden Core Stage Seven spiritual pressure burst outward and shot into the sky.

Six Golden Core cultivators followed close behind.

The formation's force still pressed against them, but with seven cultivators combining their strength, they had just enough to break free and fly.

On a small island within Eight-Hundred-Li Peng Lake, a black piece of wood drifted gently.

Detecting what had just happened, it gave a quiet snort.

"Luo Chen, boy — handle the rest yourself."

Over these years, Luo Chen had extracted far too much from him — the Yuan Dan Art, the Nascent Infant pill formula, along with the soul-nourishing wood technique and the Mingyuan Sect's spirit vein upgrade method on top of that.

Not to mention all those formation art exchanges and the cultivation world knowledge he had shared.

Han Zhan was no one's enforcer. Helping him deal with these small fry was already an act of great generosity.

Seven Golden Core cultivators — one at late stage, two at mid-stage, four at early stage. Given Luo Chen's current foundations, he was more than capable of handling them alone.

Besides, the Fulong Mountain Range still housed Nascent Soul cultivators in deep seclusion. He had no wish to reveal more than necessary.

...

The seven cleared the formation's suppression and climbed high — only to realize their hearts were still racing from the near thing.

Forcing their way through someone else's gate — not such an easy thing, it turned out.

They had yet to even lay eyes on the master of the place, and they had nearly been wiped out on the spot. That was far too dangerous.

They could feel it: the formation's power had not been fully unleashed.

If everything had been turned on at once, one or two among the seven would certainly have fallen.

Just as the seven steadied themselves —

A voice arrived, in an instant, from nowhere.

"Do you truly wish to die?"

*(End of Chapter)*
