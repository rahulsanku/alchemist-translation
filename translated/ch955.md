# Chapter 955: Yin-Yang Remnant Realm, Golden Lion Empire

"Fellow Daoist Meng, it's only natural you wouldn't recognize him after a century in seclusion. This Fellow Daoist Qi is a newly risen powerhouse from the Southern Frontier, his rise still recent."

Li Tian Shi said with a genial smile, his countenance kind and benevolent, as if introducing a junior of his own household.

Such an attitude made Meng Shentong almost instinctively overlook Luo Chen.

But how could one ignore that figure standing tall and imposing, a crane among chickens?

"Surname Qi?"

Meng Shentong's brow arched, as if recalling something, and he began to scrutinize Luo Chen seriously.

"Could it be that Fellow Daoist is..."

As he spoke, his eyes grew deep and obscure, as if boundless illusions were spawning within them.

The Purple-Robed Marquis's purple robes rustled in the wind.

Behind Fan Wujiu, the Sword Pavilion Master, the sword case at his back hummed faintly, issuing a warning on its own.

Only Li Tian Shi and Old Matron Hongye remained completely unaffected.

Luo Chen's eyelids drooped low, yet he too remained unmoved. Instead, he spoke calmly: "I am the Overlord Cultivator, Qi Tian!"

The words rang out like a spring thunderclap.

The illusions that had dazed everyone suddenly vanished.

Li Tian Shi and Old Matron Hongye exchanged a glance, then frowned slightly as they spoke: "Fellow Daoist Meng, why such discourtesy?"

Just now, Meng Shentong had clearly employed a subtle technique from the Luo Sect, one that pressed upon the mind.

Without a soul foundation at his level, anyone would have been affected.

The Purple-Robed Marquis and Fan Wujiu were both among those affected.

But Luo Chen's performance was somewhat strange.

He had not outwardly displayed any divine sense to resist, yet between his exhaling and speaking, there was an undeniable willpower, breaking the probing attempt through the method of thunderous words from the tongue.

Meng Shentong waved his hand. "No harm done, just a little test. After all, I know the backgrounds of everyone on this trip. Fellow Daoist Qi alone is relatively unfamiliar. It wouldn't do if he ended up holding us back. Fellow Daoist Qi, surely you don't mind?"

Would Qi Tian mind?

Of course he would!

Ever since he emerged on the scene in the Southern Frontier, his actions had been nothing but domineering and overbearing.

For someone to so openly test him was to question his ability. It would be strange if he wasn't angry.

Yet, contrary to expectations, Luo Chen's eyelids remained lowered, his expression perfectly calm.

"Testing each other is meaningless. Let's first discuss the upcoming itinerary. Once this matter is resolved, I will owe the Celestial Master nothing more, and I plan to leave the Southern Frontier. Therefore, the sooner we settle this, the better."

Leaving so soon?

Li Tian Shi's expression froze.
