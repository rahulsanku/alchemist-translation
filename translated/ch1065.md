# Chapter 1065: The Wandering Cultivator Bo Han Asks the Dao, the Alchemy Sect Returns to Lingtian

The Azure Sky Absolute Domain, which had stretched across the heart of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, suddenly dissipated today.

The pervasive, biting golden wind seemed as if it had never existed. The thin layers of clouds scattered, revealing a bright and clear sky.

Soft sunlight shone upon the earth. Vegetation could be seen sprouting with the naked eye, full of vitality. It was a scene of thriving life.

This was the true Azure Sky Domain!

And all of this change originated from the black-robed Daoist standing before the golden tiger.

Upon hearing Luo Chen declare his sect, Bo Han Jun's expression froze.

"So you are Senior Alchemy Sect?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "You know me?"

Bo Han Jun nodded, but then shook his head vigorously.

"Senior's great name thunders in my ears. Though I have never seen you, how could I, Bo Han Jun, not recognize it?"

By now, Luo Chen's name had spread across the five continents and four seas. One could say there was no one in the world who did not know him.

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment with a smile. He patted the golden tiger before him, and Emperor Slaughter's body abruptly shrank to about ten feet in length. Luo Chen took the opportunity to mount it.

The golden tiger spewed fierce astral wind, carrying Luo Chen as it flew into the sky.

Drawing closer, Luo Chen deliberately glanced at the white eyebrows on the other's face.

Bo Han Jun had never seen him, but Luo Chen had seen this man.

Just a few years ago, when passing by Fireworks Ancient Isle, the Nascent Soul cultivator hiding and cultivating in seclusion within that ancient isle was this very person.

"A wandering cultivator?"

Bo Han Jun nodded, his gaze constantly circling the golden tiger.

Was this great demon emperor the one who had caused the Azure Sky Absolute Domain to persist for over a hundred years?

And for such a powerful existence to willingly become the mount of the Alchemy Sect, just how powerful must the Alchemy Sect be?

Had he already reached Ascendant?

Luo Chen sighed emotionally. "For a wandering cultivator to achieve Nascent Soul is truly no easy feat. Fellow Daoist, your cultivation resolve is indeed firm and resolute."

A look of emotion also appeared on Bo Han Jun's face. Wasn't what Luo Chen said exactly what he himself thought?

He had risen from a humble and insignificant place, relying on the tempering during the two-hundred-year human-demon war to lay his foundation bit by bit.

And because after the great war, the power of the Eastern Wilderness sects had declined, leaving behind some fourth-order spirit veins that no one cared about, he finally had the conditions to form his Nascent Soul.

Otherwise, no matter how supremely talented he was, without the hard requirement of external resources to assist him, he feared it would be difficult to achieve the Divine Transformation great Dao.

"Senior..."

"Fellow Daoist, there is no need for that. This sect master has not yet reached Ascendant. We are both in the Nascent Soul realm; addressing me as Fellow Daoist is sufficient."

Bo Han Jun was stunned. He could not see through Luo Chen's cultivation level. Seeing that even a powerful great demon emperor like the golden tiger was willing to submit and become his mount, he had thought Luo Chen had already become an Ascendant.

He never expected...

The white-browed man's throat bobbed. This seemed even more terrifying!

Bo Han Jun exhaled a turbid breath, then suddenly bowed deeply to Luo Chen before him.

"Senior Luo's great kindness, Bo Han Jun will never forget!"

"Oh?"

Luo Chen was surprised, but then, as if remembering something, he examined the other for a moment.

"When you formed your Nascent Soul, did you take this sect's Nascent Soul Pill?"

Bo Han Jun nodded. "Yes. Back then, when Grand Elder Taiyue held the super auction, the finale was Senior Luo's ten Nascent Soul Pills. I was fortunate enough to bid for one, and later, even more fortunately, I achieved the Nascent Soul realm."

"For a wandering cultivator at the Adept realm to be able to bid for a Nascentous Soul Pill, you must have been quite wealthy. How can you call it luck?" Luo Chen chuckled softly and waved his hand. "You paid real gold and silver for the pill; it's hardly a favor."

That was what he said, but if any person or force who had obtained Luo Chen's pills truly thought that way, they would be fools.

Pills as rare as the Nascent Soul Pill, which could greatly increase the chances of forming a Nascent Soul, often had no market price.

Even if someone wanted to buy them, it depended on whether anyone was willing to sell them.

Without the Nascent Soul Pill, the various major sects could only rely on their own secret techniques to forcibly form their Nascent Souls.

For Luo Chen to have taken out ten Nascent Soul Pills in one go back then was undoubtedly a great boon for the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world, which had weakened after the war.

Especially for a wandering cultivator like Bo Han Jun, who had no spirit land for cultivation and no auxiliary pills.

But since Luo Chen said so, he could only keep this gratitude in his heart.

"Are you going to Lingtian Pass?"

"Yes, I am precisely heading there to participate in the Shouyang Dao Discussion hosted by Senior Luo. However, I don't have an invitation, which is a bit awkward."

"Haha, just travel with me!"

Luo Chen laughed heartily, sitting on the golden tiger's back as they headed towards Lingtian Pass.

Along the way, he kept chatting with Bo Han Jun.

As a wandering cultivator, the other was quite confused about Nascent Soul realm cultivation after forming his Nascent Soul. Since Luo Chen had nothing to do, he casually gave a few pointers.

Incidentally, Luo Chen also asked about the whereabouts of that batch of Nascent Soul Pills.

Under Deng Taiyue's control, seven of the ten Nascent Soul Pills had fallen into the hands of Nascent Soul sects, while the other three were distributed to the powers below.

One was a Golden Core great sect, one was a Golden Core clan, and the last was Bo Han Jun, a wealthy wandering cultivator.

Of these three parties who obtained the Nascent Soul Pills, apart from the cultivator from the Golden Core clan who failed in his Nascent Soul breakthrough and perished, both Bo Han Jun and the other person succeeded.

As for who that other person was and which sect they belonged to, Luo Chen had roughly guessed.

Qingdan Valley, Tao Wan!

As for the seven Nascent Soul Pills in greater quantity, they were obtained by the seven Nascent Soul superior sects: Nine Spirits Sect, Heavenly Capital Sect, Splendid Flower Palace, White Crane Sect, Myriad Swords Pavilion, Eastern Sun Sect, and White Deer Grotto.

Relying on these Nascent Soul Pills, five Nascent Soul True Persons emerged, while the other two sects failed.

The ones who failed were Myriad Swords Pavilion and Eastern Sun Sect.

Upon hearing the news, Luo Chen was also quite emotional.

The chances of successfully condensing a Nascent Soul were always extremely low. For a few out of a hundred to succeed was already considered good.

Even though this batch of people were all chosen prodigies from the major sects, their Dao conduct perfectly tempered by the human-demon war, two still failed even with the assistance of the Nascent Soul Pill.

The difficulty of cultivation was evident.

Taking advantage of their journey together, besides seeking advice on cultivation insights, Bo Han Jun also asked Luo Chen to look at his spirit pet.

It was a Six-Winged Frost Centipede that contained the bloodline of a desolate beast.

Its upper limit should have been extremely high, and Bo Han Jun had relied on cultivating this spirit insect from a young age to reach his current state on the path of extreme cold. However, the Six-Winged Frost Centipede itself was stuck at the fourth-order threshold.

With Luo Chen's knowledge, he naturally solved this problem for him easily.

It was actually quite simple. Many demon beasts in the Eastern Wilderness more or less had similar situations. They carried a thin Archaic bloodline, but would get stuck at the fourth order.

The solution was to choose a cultivation path.

Either choose to refine the Archaic bloodline and walk the path of a transformed demon cultivator, or choose to purify the bloodline and continue cultivating towards the Archaic direction.

Back when Luo Chen was on Xuanyan Island in the Northern Sea, the Ring-Headed Turtle clan he encountered was like this.

When those Ring-Headed Turtles advanced to the fourth order, they either chose to cultivate in the direction of transformation, or they chose to purify their bloodline and revert to becoming a desolate beast like the Mystic Turtle or the Cliff Serpent.

It was hard to say which of the two choices was superior or inferior.

Luo Chen briefly analyzed it for Bo Han Jun. The path of transformation would allow his spirit pet to cultivate faster, but he would need to be prepared to search for the corresponding cultivation techniques for it. As for the path of the desolate beast, although the cultivation speed was slower, the upper limit in this realm would be higher, and advancing to the fifth order would be relatively simpler.

Luo Chen couldn't help with the latter.

For the former, Luo Chen casually gave him a Transformation Pill.

Bo Han Jun was endlessly grateful, and couldn't help but frequently sigh inwardly. The rumored reputation of the Alchemy Sect master for being benevolent, charitable, and generous was indeed well-deserved.

He had spent his entire fortune just to purchase the Nascent Soul Pill. This Transformation Pill was clearly extremely precious, something he simply couldn't afford now. He never expected the Alchemy Sect master to generously give it to him.

Luo Chen, however, was very open-minded about it.

"This sect master was also a wandering cultivator for a very long time. I know how difficult it is for wandering cultivators. Since we've met, it's no trouble to lend a hand. However, if you want to go further, then this sect master suggests you either create your own sect or join a Nascent Soul superior sect. Otherwise, just gathering cultivation resources will consume most of your training time, which is truly not worth it."

Bo Han Jun deeply agreed, but for now, he had no intention of creating or joining a sect. He only had a profound understanding of the difficulties of being a wandering cultivator that Luo Chen had mentioned.

"The Nascent Soul realm is already incredibly difficult. I wonder if I will ever have the chance to touch the Ascendant realm in this lifetime?"

Having said this, Bo Han Jun's face showed hesitation.

"Senior Luo, there is something I don't know if I should ask."

"Generally, for things like this, it's best not to ask. But this sect master is in a good mood today, so go ahead and ask!"

"It is rumored that at the upcoming Shouyang Dao Discussion you are hosting, you will mention the opportunity for Ascendant. May I be permitted to listen?"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "Fellow Daoist, you aim high. You've only just broken through to the Nascent Soul stage, and you're already thinking about the Ascendant realm."

Bo Han Jun was a little ashamed. "It is shameful to say. First, I received the grace of Senior Luo's Nascent Soul Pill. Then, along the way, I received your guidance and teachings, and even the gift of a Transformation Pill. I should not seek anything more. But truly entering the Nascent Soul stage, I realize the vastness of heaven and earth and the distance of the Great Dao. The Ascendant realm is the dream of all my peers!"

Luo Chen waved his hand, looked deeply at the other, and then smiled freely.

"No need to feel ashamed. If you truly wish it, this sect master will grant your wish."

Upon hearing this, Bo Han Jun was both shocked and delighted.

He was delighted that the Alchemy Sect master was even more generous than he had imagined. He was shocked that such a good thing would come so easily?

He wanted to ask if there were any conditions attached, but there was no time.

Because Lingtian Pass had arrived!

The fierce tiger halted its steps. The Daoist gazed into the distance.

The once majestic Lingtian Pass was just as before.

But it had lost the grim killing intent forged by blood and fire, and gained a bit more of the bustling prosperity of the mortal world.

The original Lingtian Pass still existed, but with the collapsed city wall as a dividing line, it spread outward in all directions, covered with countless new buildings, large and small.

This unprecedented majestic city of the Mountains and Seas Realm had grown so vast that even with Luo Chen's Crimson Eye Golden Pupils, he couldn't see its end at a glance.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's gaze fixed, and his brow furrowed slightly.

Within the city, several beams of light shot up into the sky, flying towards his direction.

There were nine people in total, their momentum vast and imposing.

Wherever they passed, the entire city watched, shocked and awed.

Those were nine Nascent Soul True Persons!

As they were about to draw near, the nine people stopped in unison and shouted in one voice: "Greetings, Supreme Elder!"

The simultaneous shout echoed for ten thousand li.

The entire Lingtian Pass was shaken!

One divine sense after another probed in this direction, but upon getting close, they dared not linger for even a moment, fearing they would offend the black-robed Daoist.

They only knew one thing: the Luo Tian Supreme Elder, the Alchemy Sect master Luo Chen, had arrived!

Facing those divine senses, Luo Chen paid them no heed. His gaze only rested on the nine people before him.

At the forefront were Gu Caiyi and Cang Long.

Behind them were Wang Yuan, Lingfengzi, Haoranzi, Jadewater Dragon, and Heavenjade.

Cowering at the very back were Black King and Little White.

"Come out!"

Black King and Little White flew to the front, their faces full of grievance.

"Master, we didn't notify the sect side!"

This was Luo Chen's instruction when he sent Black King and Little White away from Green Parasol Mountain: don't let the Luo Tian Sect cultivators get involved.

Yet these seven people before him were all from the Luo Tian Sect, one could even say they were the full force of its top combat power.

If they were all wiped out, then the four hundred years of the Luo Tian Sect's management would be destroyed in one fell swoop.

Cang Long said gently, "Why blame them, Senior Brother? The matter of Black King and Little White widely distributing the Dao Discussion invitations has spread throughout the Eastern Wilderness over the years. Who doesn't know? Although our Luo Tian Sect is hidden in the Nether Abyss, we still have the Shangyou Immortal City as an external stronghold, so naturally we learned of this news."

Behind her, Haoranzi and Jadewater Dragon nodded simultaneously.

Back when Luo Chen brought these two back to the Luo Tian Sect, Haoranzi was left outside the Nether Abyss.

Later, Luo Chen left, but Sect Master Li Yingzhang did not recall Haoranzi. Instead, he sent some people out of the Nether Abyss to join Haoranzi and establish the Luo Tian Sect's external stronghold at Shangyou Immortal City.

It was both to gather information and intelligence, and to facilitate recruiting suitable young disciples.

Black King and Little White's actions were not particularly concealed, so Shangyou Immortal City naturally received the relevant news.

"But for all of you to come out..." Luo Chen shook his head, still thinking it unwise.

Gu Caiyi stepped forward, looking at Luo Chen with a stubborn expression. "Husband is about to undertake a great undertaking; how can we sit idly by?"

Luo Chen pursed his lips, looking at Gu Caiyi, whose bearing and temperament were completely different from before, and then his gaze fell on each of those behind her, one by one.

Lingfengzi had a helpless look on his face, but it was not forced.

Haoranzi smiled and nodded. Jadewater Dragon was eager to try. Heavenjade hung back, but his expression was firm.

Wang Yuan nodded at Luo Chen and said in a deep voice, "We cannot always be sheltered by you, only caring for our own carefree existence. Just like the old Flame Alliance affair, it's nothing more than sharing life and death!"

Luo Chen was stunned.

In the end, he could only sigh softly.

"Forget it!"

Luo Chen waved his hand, dismounted from the tiger's back, and walked into the crowd.

The crowd parted to make way, escorting Luo Chen as they headed towards Lingtian Pass.

Gu Caiyi walked shoulder to shoulder with him, a genuine smile blooming on her face. Finally, this time, she could follow by Luo Chen's side.

Cang Long was half a step behind, her expression gentle yet firm. This life was given to her by Luo Chen, and their relationship had advanced further; how could she not go through fire and water for him?

Behind the crowd, Black King landed beside the golden tiger, seemingly about to utter some teasing words.

But the golden tiger rolled over, revealing Emperor Slaughter's towering figure. He merely cast a cold glance at Black King, forcing him to swallow back his teasing words.

Instead, Black King muttered inwardly, "Just what kind of dogshit luck did this guy stumble upon? How did he become so powerful?"

Bo Han Jun tactfully did not follow, only watching that group from afar.

They were a group of powerful Nascent Soul True Persons, with cultivation levels higher and deeper than his own. Yet at this moment, they were all following Luo Chen with utter devotion, as if they would charge into a mountain of blades or a sea of fire together with him.

A look of reverence appeared in Bo Han Jun's eyes.

"So this is the Alchemy Sect?"

The Luo Tian Sect cultivators made no attempt to conceal themselves. They passed openly through Lingtian Pass, all the way until they reached Shouyang Mountain and disappeared from sight.

Wherever they passed, pairs of eyes followed, and strands of divine sense trembled with awe.

The Alchemy Sect master, who had vanished for many years, had once again appeared before the world!

Not long after the Luo Tian Sect cultivators welcomed Luo Chen back, a message spread from Shouyang Mountain.

[In three days, the Shouyang Dao Discussion will commence. All Nascent Soul True Persons of the Eastern Wilderness may attend!]

(End of this chapter)
