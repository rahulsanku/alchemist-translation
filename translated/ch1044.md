# Chapter 1044: Black Clouds Press Down on the City, Grandmaster Sword Seed

On a mountain path, four figures advanced at a moderate pace.

The man and woman at the front kept up a lively conversation, their words brimming with excitement.

"Big Brother is finally here! From now on, we won't have to keep our tails between our legs in the courtyard anymore!"

"That's you, Zhou Yuhao, not us. I don't know what you're so afraid of. We're all about the same age, at about the same cultivation level, receiving the same guidance and instruction—there's no reason we should be worse than them!"

"Sister Yuwei, that's not how it works! It's not that I'm afraid, I just don't want to cause trouble for the family. But now that Big Brother is here, within the Jade Pivot Court, even the entire Seven Stars Academy, we have a backer!"

Zhou Yuwei shook her head. "I wouldn't be so sure."

Zhou Yuhao didn't understand, but as they gradually ventured deeper into these mountains, he slowly began to catch on.

Now that Big Brother had arrived, he would have his own independent cave dwelling. Wouldn't that mean he'd skip the foundational courses of the Seven Stars Academy entirely?

If that were the case, his plan to latch onto Big Brother's coattails would fall flat.

Frustrated, he turned back and barked, "You two, hurry it up!"

Zhou Yuwei stopped him. "There's no need to rush them. They haven't been in the best spirits lately. This trip to visit Brother Fuyou is meant to help them relax."

"They're the ones in a bad mood?" Zhou Yuhao snorted. "Zhou Ping'an got the attention of the teaching master the moment he arrived, getting dragged off for private lessons every day. If his cultivation isn't making progress, it's purely because he's not applying himself. As for Zhou Yuqi—"

Zhou Yuhao's gaze swept past the plain-featured Zhou Ping'an and landed on the man behind him, whose expression was mournful. His face showed clear displeasure.

In his view, although Zhou Yuqi was also a member of the Zhou clan, he didn't deserve to join the Tiannan Dao Palace. He had no idea what connections had been pulled to get him in by the skin of his teeth.

Even so, the other's performance after joining had been far inferior to his own and Zhou Yuwei's.

But what truly made Zhou Yuhao dislike him was that after joining the Dao Palace, instead of sticking close to the Zhou family, he'd gone to fraternize with disciples from other aristocratic clans.

This infuriated Zhou Yuhao!

Once they met Big Brother, he'd make sure to have Big Brother teach Zhou Yuqi a harsh lesson!

The four of them pressed on, until finally the plaque of the Dawn and Dusk Palace came faintly into view.

But on the square before the palace gates, they stopped in their tracks.

Dozens of cultivators were gathered there—men and women, none appearing very old.

But the spiritual power fluctuations emanating from them far surpassed those of the four Zhou family members.

Zhou Yuwei's expression tightened. "All Initiate Realm true cultivators!"

Zhou Yuhao's voice trembled. "What are they all doing here? Are they here to cause trouble for Big Brother?"

It wasn't unreasonable for Zhou Yuhao to guess this, given how things looked.

"Kuang Pingzhang requests an audience with Junior Brother Zhou!"

"Zhou Fuyou, what are you hiding in there for?"

"Li Mountain Yuan Xuesong, I humbly request Senior Brother Zhou to come out and meet me!"

Similar shouts rang out from time to time.

Some were harsh provocations, others polite requests for an audience, making the area outside the Dawn and Dusk Palace unbearably noisy.

Yet in the face of all this clamor, the Dawn and Dusk Palace remained as silent as ever, showing no reaction whatsoever.

Standing at the very back of the crowd, the four Zhou family members trembled slightly.

"Maybe... we shouldn't see Big Brother today?"

Zhou Yuwei hesitated, also feeling that today might not be the right day to meet Zhou Fuyou.

But before they could leave, someone at the front suddenly turned around and spotted them.

"Oh? Isn't this Junior Sister Yuwei, and Junior Brother Zhou? What brings you here?"

Zhou Yuwei's face tightened. She bowed respectfully. "Yuwei pays respects to Senior Sister Xiang!"

The three other Zhou family members also bowed in deference.

They knew this Senior Sister Xiang. She was an exceptionally outstanding disciple of the Jade Pivot Court, having successfully reached Initiate two years ago and been accepted as a disciple of the Houtu Hall, nominally a registered disciple of its hall master, Fairy Bluegreen.

Senior Sister Xiang accepted their greetings with a smile, then said, "It's fine to take a stroll on a rest day, but this isn't exactly a good place for sightseeing. Zhou... hmm? Your surname is Zhou?"

As if suddenly realizing something, the smile on Senior Sister Xiang's face faded.

Her last words came out rather abruptly, her voice louder than before, drawing the attention of those nearby.

Then, the previously noisy crowd fell silent.

One after another, gazes turned toward them, pressing down on the four Qi Refinement stage Zhou family members like an immense mountain.

Zhou Yuwei's face was pale. Gritting her teeth, she said, "Senior Sister Xiang, we took a wrong turn. We'll take our leave now!"

Senior Sister Xiang's lips twitched. "You didn't take a wrong turn at all. Since you're here, why not go and see your Zhou family relative?"

As she spoke these words, the crowd parted to make way.

Zhou Yuwei swallowed hard, knowing there was no escape now.

Steeling herself, she walked toward the gates of the Dawn and Dusk Palace under the weight of countless stares.

Her heart was filled with anxiety.

If Fuyou Ge opened the door, he would likely land himself in serious trouble.

But if Fuyou Ge didn't open the door, what were they supposed to do?

Caught between hope and fear, she watched as the gates of the Dawn and Dusk Palace slowly swung inward.

A figure in white robes walked out calmly.

"It's Big Brother!"

Zhou Yuhao cried out in surprise and rushed forward.

Zhou Yuwei and the remaining two, Zhou Yuqi and Zhou Ping'an, quickly bowed in greeting.

"Come inside."

Zhou Fuyou said flatly, letting the four of them enter the main hall of the Dawn and Dusk Palace.

Then he turned, about to go back inside.

But behind him, a sarcastic voice rang out.

"Junior Brother Zhou, when you see all these senior brothers and sisters, shouldn't you offer a greeting? Has the Zhou clan, a proper Sage family, not taught you even this basic courtesy?"

These words immediately drew echoes of agreement.

"That's right. The Zhou clan is, after all, a small overseas family, not like our Central Continent aristocratic clans with their thorough etiquette."

"In my opinion, Junior Brother Zhou should go study at the Seven Stars Academy for a while. At least learn the hierarchy of superior and inferior, the rites of elder and younger, here at our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect before anything else."

"To rise to a high position overnight, it's only natural to get carried away. But this senior brother still advises you, junior brother—a young man shouldn't be too arrogant!"

These voices were particularly sharp, standing out among the crowd.

The figure in red coral robes, who had been facing away from them, slowly turned around, revealing the cold face of a young man.

Zhou Fuyou stared coldly at them. "What do you want?"

This question actually caught them off guard.

Zhou Fuyou stepped forward, standing on the steps, looking down at those below.

"First, I have already formally acknowledged a master. By the rules of seniority and hierarchy, you unregistered disciples who have not yet taken a master should respectfully bow to me and address me as senior brother!"

"Second, if you look down on my Zhou clan, then why didn't you speak up the other day when our Zhou clan's ancestor came to pay a visit?"

"Third!"

Zhou Fuyou held one hand behind his back, the other in front.

"If you've come to provoke me, then just make your move! Who's first?"

These successive declarations flushed the faces of the Initiate Realm cultivators red with anger.

Even those who had only come to watch felt that Zhou Fuyou was far too arrogant.

He had only just joined the Tiannan Dao Palace—how could he compare with these seniors who had been cultivating here for decades? He was simply asking for death!

But before they could respond, Zhou Fuyou had already made his move.

Both hands, front and back, simultaneously formed hand seals.

The next moment!

*Boom!*

Streams of black qi surged into the sky, instantly shrouding the heavens.

A sea of qi floated across the sky; black clouds pressed down upon the city!

As Zhou Fuyou pressed down with one hand, everyone was stunned.

"How dare he?"

"Taking on everyone at once?"

"He's practically courting death!"

But when the overwhelming torrent of qi descended, all their fury turned into screams.

Spells crumbled on contact.

Artifacts' spiritual light dimmed.

Even those who activated their treasures found them swaying and unstable under the assault of the black qi streams, utterly unable to escape the black canopy.

"Hmph!"

With a cold snort, Zhou Fuyou pressed down another palm from the air.

For several li in every direction, the ground trembled slightly. The Initiate Realm cultivators could no longer resist; their defensive measures began to shatter layer by layer.

Those of weaker strength even had an eerie bluish-black tinge spread across their faces—clearly poisoned.

Still not enough!

Suppressing dozens of Initiate true cultivators with his own strength alone was a difficult task for Zhou Fuyou.

So from the very start, he had used his strongest killing move.

Since he had struck, he would make sure everyone felt the pain! That they knew Zhou Fuyou was not to be trifled with!

Zhou Fuyou's expression hardened, and he raised his hand to press down a third palm!

Just then, a voice came from above.

"Little junior brother, that's enough."

Zhou Fuyou's brow twitched slightly. He formed a single hand seal.

In an instant, the pitch-black canopy that had suppressed the entire scene dispersed into streams of black qi, returning to Zhou Fuyou's body like fledglings returning to the nest.

The crowd, having narrowly escaped death, felt nothing but relief.

The way they looked at Zhou Fuyou was now filled with awe and fear.

"Senior Sister!"

Shi Miaoyin smiled and nodded at Zhou Fuyou, then turned and scolded, "Get lost!"

The Initiate true cultivators who had come to get a measure of Zhou Fuyou scrambled away in disgrace.

Once the Dawn and Dusk Palace had fallen quiet, Shi Miaoyin spoke with an apologetic tone. "My apologies. I've been lax in my discipline."

"It's fine." Zhou Fuyou wasn't particularly angry. He had roughly expected something like this.

After all, the moment he arrived, he had smoothly been accepted as a disciple by their master and obtained the Xuanzi-A-Number-One cave dwelling that every Initiate true cultivator coveted most. There were bound to be some who would come to provoke him.

What he hadn't expected was the scale of it!

It was probably also related to the commotion his ancestor had caused when entering the mountain.

Zhou Fuyou looked at Shi Miaoyin curiously. "Senior Sister, did you hear the news and come specifically to bail me out?"

Shi Miaoyin gave a wry smile. "With your abilities, little junior brother, there's no need for me to bail you out. I came today on our master's orders, to ask if you've already refined a prototype for your lifebound treasure."

Zhou Fuyou's eyes immediately lit up.

"No! What I'm using now is a killing treasure bestowed by our ancestor. However, this coral dharma robe I'm wearing has been refined by our ancestor. It's of true artifact grade, and its defensive capabilities are at the level of a high-grade dharma treasure."

Having gotten a general picture, Shi Miaoyin nodded slightly.

"That's about what our master expected. I'll send you some illustrated catalogs of famous water-attribute treasures from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Choose carefully, and then the Houtu Hall will forge it for you. Don't worry about materials."

Zhou Fuyou couldn't have asked for more.

After ordinary cultivators formed their core, they could nurture a treasure as their lifebound treasure, an object to cultivate alongside both their life and their dao.

Those looking for convenience would naturally just nurture an existing treasure.

But true disciples of major sects wouldn't take such shortcuts. Instead, they would choose to have a lifebound treasure custom-made for themselves.

From the looks of it, his master thought highly of him. He hadn't even formed his core yet, and she was already thinking about forging a prototype for him.

"I won't disturb your family reunion. If anything like this happens again, you can notify me directly."

Shi Miaoyin glanced at the four people peeking out from inside the Dawn and Dusk Palace, then left behind a transmission talisman before taking her leave.

After she was gone, Zhou Fuyou flicked his sleeve and stepped into the hall.

He was greeted by four pairs of eyes—some worshipful, some awed, some envious, some jealous.

"Big Brother!"

"Fuyou Ge!"

"Fuyou..."

Zhou Fuyou waved his hand and took the seat of honor.

"Long time no see. How have you all been these years?"

At these words, the four of them immediately began pouring out their grievances.

Clearly, the Dao Palace cultivation life that outsiders envied so much was not as easy as they had imagined.

There were factions here too, cliques, competition, and suppression.

Beyond the politics of human relationships, cultivation itself was a difficult and painful endeavor.

The one who received the most attention was Zhou Ping'an, with his five-spirit-root constitution.

Yet he was also the most frustrated person in the entire Jade Pivot Court.

His cultivation progress was excruciatingly slow. Vast resources poured into him seemed to vanish like stones dropped into the sea. His realm advancement was always the slowest.

Zhou Yuwei and Zhou Yuhao had already reached late Qi Refinement.

But Zhou Ping'an was only at the third layer of Qi Refinement, still far from even mid Qi Refinement. Word had it that the Jade Pivot Court was already considering giving up on him.

Clearly, cultivating all five elements simultaneously was an immense burden for a young man.

At only twenty-one years old, Zhou Ping'an already showed signs of premature aging.

Zhou Yuqi wasn't much better off. His spirit root aptitude was mediocre. His father had pulled strings to get him in as an auditing student.

He was already discriminated against, his cultivation speed was slow, and recently he had received some news.

"Fuyou... are my father and mother really dead?"

Facing that mournful gaze, Zhou Fuyou remained utterly calm.

"You can ask the clan elders about that."

Zhou Yuqi opened his mouth, then slumped weakly into his chair.

Zhou Fuyou didn't care much about his feelings. In truth, he didn't care much about the situation of the other Zhou family members either.

Meeting them today was merely to fulfill the obligations of clan kinship, to put on a show of backing them up.

In the Tiannan Dao Palace, cultivation ultimately depended on oneself.

After joining the Dao Palace and laying a foundation in the Seven Stars Academy, one could advance to Initiate, then choose a hall to join as a disciple.

Only upon reaching the Adept realm could one formally become a disciple of one of the four hall masters.

Those Initiate true cultivators relied more on guidance from Adept senior brothers and sisters who taught on behalf of the master, as well as their own comprehension.

This was also why so many had gathered together to come to the Dawn and Dusk Palace today.

He, Zhou Fuyou, had risen in a single step, bypassing years, decades, even centuries of effort that others had to put in, to directly become a disciple of Fairy Bluegreen. How could that not drive people mad with jealousy?

After a brief reunion, the four Zhou family members took their leave, not daring to impose on Zhou Fuyou any longer.

In the evening, the senior sister sent Zhou Fuyou an illustrated catalog.

It was a catalog of treasures!

Most of the catalog was sealed, but about a fifth of it had been unsealed.

Clearly, this was for Zhou Fuyou to choose a suitable treasure in advance.

Zhou Fuyou enjoyed reading this kind of material. It broadened his knowledge, allowing him to accumulate experience far beyond his years despite his limited age.

The treasures recorded in the catalog were no ordinary items.

Beyond describing their names, powers, strengths, and weaknesses, they also often recorded the achievements of past owners who had used such treasures.

The more Zhou Fuyou read, the more delighted he became. He felt that every type suited him, yet each one also seemed to lack something.

"My future will never be limited to just the Adept or Sage realms. The treasure I cultivate alongside my life and dao must have immense potential!"

"Therefore, the treasures of those who only cultivated to the Adept or Sage stage must have some inherent flaw."

"I need a lifebound treasure once forged by a mighty Ascendant cultivator!"

Zhou Fuyou turned to the last page.

There, a single pearl shone brilliantly.

"Calming Sea Pearl!"

---

Deep in the azure void.

An unknown amount of time had passed, and the universe felt like a formless chaos.

A Daoist in black robes sat cross-legged in meditation.

He sat there silently, but his aura was like a sharp sword, ready at any moment to pierce the heavens.

Suddenly!

The black-robed Daoist's eyes snapped open. Hundreds of apertures across his entire body trembled in unison.

Countless sword qi surged forth, forming a crimson storm in the void.

At the very center of the storm, the black-robed Daoist stared in amazement at a seed in his palm.

"Twenty years of effort, and I've finally cultivated the sword canon to the Grandmaster level."

"But this seed formed from condensed sword qi... what is it? The *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* makes no mention of anything like this."

"Could it be... like the special transformations that occurred when I advanced various spells in the past, brought about by the system?"
