# Chapter 807: Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion, Sword Canon Minor Success

An establishment like the Heavenly Fragrance Pavilion was bound to be quite famous within Lingtian Pass.

But Lingfengzi had no such leisure to come and "catch" Luo Chen. He truly had just been passing by by chance.

The two met and exchanged a few casual pleasantries.

However, under Lingfengzi's somewhat peculiar gaze, Luo Chen still felt a twinge of guilt.

He made a slight remedy.

"I've thoroughly studied your Wind Aperture Pill formula. At most three months, and I should be able to refine it."

Hearing that it would only take three months, Lingfengzi's eyes instantly lit up.

"Good, good, good! Three months is perfect!"

The word "perfect" struck Luo Chen as odd. The other party seemed to be in quite a hurry to use the Wind Aperture Pill.

Faced with his confusion, Lingfengzi lowered his voice and explained a few things.

"The situation grows more tense by the day."

"As the only one of the five Sages who can move freely, I have to go out from time to time to scout the situation. Some time ago, I went to the Azure Sky Domain. It's already been breached, completely occupied by demon beasts. Even Azure Sky Mountain has been destroyed. I couldn't find any trace of Fellow Daoist Qingkong."

Just this one sentence made Luo Chen's expression turn solemn.

Sage Qingkong was precisely the founding ancestor of Azure Sky Mountain. If even his aura was gone, one could imagine his fate.

"The Fire Ridge has complex terrain, with many cultivators hiding underground. They should be able to hold out a while longer."

"Overlord Blade Domain is locked in endless battle, living up to its name of overbearing."

"But in my view, the fall of Azure Sky Mountain means the collapse of the three domains has already begun. Perhaps before long, we'll see the demon beast army beneath the walls of Lingtian Pass."

"Under these circumstances, if I can obtain the Wind Aperture Pill, I'll be able to fully master a major technique of the Misty Cloud Sect, and my chances of survival will increase by several points."

"So, I trouble Fellow Daoist Luo."

After a brief chat, Lingfengzi hastily took his leave, clearly having other important matters to attend to.

Luo Chen's originally good mood had grown somewhat heavier from this conversation.

What had previously only existed in letters and intelligence reports was now so close to him.

He had experienced the great war between humans and demons.

During the war to open up the Xiaoyue Mountain Range, he had once charged at the forefront.

But that level of conflict was actually quite ordinary for Sage-level cultivators.

The kind of war that truly made Sages tremble with fear was the scale of the Northern Sea demon army attacking the Ice Pole Sect—an ancient sect passed down from antiquity, destroyed in a single night.

And this time, the scale was even larger!

It was a direct clash between the million mountains of the Eastern Wilderness and the thirty-six domains of the human cultivation world.

Its danger was inevitably far greater!

In the past, it was often said that Sage cultivators were extremely difficult to kill. Decades, even centuries would pass without hearing of a Sage being slain in battle.

And indeed, this was true. Anyone who could cultivate to this realm was not easy to deal with.

Everyone had their trump cards, their means. At worst, they could abandon their physical body and teleport their Nascent Soul a thousand li in an instant.

Fellow cultivators of the same rank rarely went for the kill. Most cultivated on their own, occasionally interacting to exchange resources and share cultivation insights. This was much like Luo Chen's recent life—seemingly busy, but in truth quite leisurely.

Unless it was a sealed space, like the Demon-Fall Land.

Or unless there were methods specifically designed to counter a cultivator's Nascent Soul teleportation, such as Azure Frost Demon Emperor's Sleeve Universe, the absolute suppression of the Desolate Beast Ghost Immortal Tower, or even Luo Chen's Primordial Unity Cauldron's suppression and capture.

After many great battles in the Eastern Wilderness, with Azure Frost Demon Emperor not personally intervening, several Sage cultivators had fallen one after another. Clearly, the demon side had others who had mastered means to restrain Nascent Soul teleportation.

This was the main reason why Luo Chen and the other Sages within Lingtian Pass were reluctant to easily take the battlefield.

Everyone was confident in themselves, but no one had absolute assurance when facing the strange and bizarre innate abilities of the demon race.

Luo Chen walked, and before he knew it, he had arrived at the city wall.

Under the night sky, the Black Ape Puppet stood silently behind him, making no sound.

Luo Chen gazed silently into the darkness in the distance, like a giant beast with its maw wide open, waiting at any moment to swallow Lingtian Pass whole.

"I hope the fellow daoists of Overlord Blade Domain and Fire Ridge can hold out a little longer. At least let me get through my three-year patrol duty."

"As long as I don't encounter the first wave of demon beast assault, many things will be easier to handle."

Luo Chen thought to himself.

Somewhat selfish, perhaps, but the cultivation world was just like this—better the fellow daoist die than I.

He stayed on the city wall for a while, then returned.

The Black Ape Puppet silently entered a watchtower, a large Soul Pill in its mouth, slowly refining it.

---

Just as Luo Chen had told Lingfengzi, the pill formula called Wind Aperture Pill had long been thoroughly studied by the Red Dog Puppet, which had memorized it by heart.

It was a somewhat special fourth-grade pill!

Its function was not to increase mana or elevate one's realm, but to open a new aperture within the human body.

Its name was the Wind-Hiding Aperture!

That aperture could not be used to store mana either. It was meant to store wind, and it would draw the body's entire mana to wash through it again and again.

The Wind Aperture Pill not only had to open the Wind-Hiding Aperture, but also had to use the pill as a core to stabilize it.

After thoroughly studying it, Luo Chen vaguely understood what technique Lingfengzi was roughly planning to cultivate.

Similar to refining a source true flame, the other party likely wanted to use the Wind-Hiding Aperture to refine some kind of immaculate wind—what could be called a lifebound true wind.

Only, fire has a seed, while wind is formless. The qi sea and Purple Mansion could bear a fire seed, but could not lock in formless wind. Hence the need to open a new aperture.

If the other party could truly achieve this, his overall capabilities might undergo a qualitative leap.

Just like when Luo Chen had refined the Withering-Bloom True Flame back then—whether in alchemy, artifact refinement, or combat, he had directly ascended a major level.

Since it concerned the other party's life-saving measures, Luo Chen did not delay this time.

Upon returning, he immediately began to open the furnace and refine the pill.

Three months was the conservative time he had given.

In reality, after two months, Luo Chen had successfully produced one batch, obtaining nine Wind Aperture Pills.

At this point, he could already deliver the goods.

But Luo Chen felt his hand was hot, so he simply threw the remaining two sets of materials into the Primordial Unity Cauldron for refining.

Unexpectedly, he succeeded in another batch as well, gaining another nine Wind Aperture Pills.

Eighteen in total!

When Lingfengzi came, Luo Chen gave him nine pills. The other party, unaware of the full picture, was overjoyed.

He exclaimed that Luo Chen's alchemy skill was unparalleled through the ages!

The ancestors of the Misty Cloud Sect had also refined the Wind Aperture Pill. Their best result had been a single batch yielding five pills.

This time, he had only sought one.

He had never imagined that Luo Chen would give him a full nine Wind Aperture Pills.

Luo Chen naturally just smiled, attributing it to good luck and a hot hand.

Lingfengzi certainly didn't believe that. After returning, he reported to the Lingtian City Lord that he needed to go into seclusion for a time, and would emerge at most half a year later. He requested that no one disturb him during these six months.

The Lingtian City Lord agreed.

And the news of Lingfengzi's brief seclusion carried a different meaning when it reached the ears of the other Sage cultivators.

It meant that the pill he had dreamed of had been successfully refined by Luo Chen.

Thus, Luo Chen's reputation as the Alchemy Saint grew even louder.

More and more visiting cards were sent to his residence. The realms of those seeking an audience also grew higher and higher.

Xu Xiaoliu, the temporary gatekeeper, had been quite pleased at first, but eventually couldn't withstand the pressure of those great figures' auras, no matter how well they restrained themselves or how cordially they behaved.

In the end, the gap in realm was too great. A Initiate-stage cultivator simply couldn't bear it.

In the end, the Heartsever Fairy actually replaced Xu Xiaoliu, temporarily becoming Luo Chen's gatekeeper, receiving visiting cards and greeting those who came to call.

Unfortunately, to everyone's disappointment, Luo Chen did not agree to refine pills for anyone thereafter.

The message he put out was that while refining pills for a fellow daoist, he had been struck by inspiration, achieving a new breakthrough in his alchemy. He wished to quietly contemplate for a period of time.

Thus, others naturally could not press the matter.

After all, the stronger Luo Chen's alchemy, the higher the success rate would be when he eventually agreed to refine pills for them.

In truth, the reality was naturally not this.

That Luo Chen's alchemy had improved was beyond doubt. He had long been a genuine fourth-grade alchemist. He had even refined a fifth-grade Law-Derivation Pill in the Demon-Fall Land!

But having refined a fifth-grade pill did not mean he had reached the level of a fifth-grade alchemist.

To put it bluntly, that was luck, relying on the power of the system.

However, as Luo Chen successively refined large batches of Poison-Resisting Pills, Hongyuan Pills, Great Soul Pills, and successfully produced Nascent Soul Pills, Nine-Departure Pills, and Wind Aperture Pills—these numerous fourth-grade pills of vastly different styles and difficulties—his standing as a fourth-grade alchemist was now without any doubt.

Even without relying on the system for an introduction, given enough time, he was confident he could refine any kind of fourth-grade pill.

At this point, the title of Alchemy Saint was truly well-deserved!

The main reason Luo Chen stopped accepting alchemy commissions was largely due to the influence of Lingfengzi's words.

He had his subordinates collect a great deal of intelligence, all of which indicated the situation was growing increasingly dire.

Under such circumstances, continuing to build his reputation would be beneficial for the future, but offered no immediate improvement to his current strength.

In the end, a person had to rely on themselves.

Therefore, he too began to go into seclusion.

Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi—one strand every half year. Even with some delays due to various matters, he had unconsciously refined eight strands.

He wanted to refine two more strands as quickly as possible in the remaining time, reaching the minor success stage.

At the same time, the polishing of his realm was nearing completion. He was just one breath away.

All of this required a relatively quiet environment, not allowing for divided attention.

---

During Luo Chen's seclusion, the myriad things of the world continued to turn.

Regarding the cooperation with the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance, under Li Yingzhang's promotion, the first step had been taken.

Within Lingtian Pass, at the site originally selected by the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance, a new tall building had risen from the ground, its signboard already hung.

Its name was [Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion].

Outsiders could tell at a glance that it was an enterprise of the Luo Tian Sect, and also the enterprise of Alchemy Saint Luo Chen. Moreover, due to the special nature of its location, many speculated that it was related to the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance.

However, the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion's business did not make much of a splash at the start.

The various resources it sold were only aimed at low-level cultivators. Even business involving Adept-stage cultivators was rare.

This decision came from Li Yingzhang.

He was more cautious than Luo Chen, worried about opposition from the City Lord's residence or suppression from other established sects. Thus, he did not want to take too large a step.

Even among the many commissions entrusted by the Tianyuan Merchant Alliance, he only chose the one type: purchasing demon beast corpses.

This kind of business was done by many forces within Lingtian Pass. The Luo Tian Sect was not conspicuous.

Gu Zuokai was slightly dissatisfied, but could understand the Luo Tian Sect's caution, so he could only proceed bit by bit.

They provided funds and venues, while the Luo Tian Sect provided manpower, slowly setting up the framework.

The low-level cultivators in the city were also quite cooperative. Perhaps out of admiration for Luo Chen's reputation as the Alchemy Saint, or perhaps out of gratitude for the gentlemanly conduct that many strong cultivators spoke of, they began to trade some of their harvested demon beast corpses to the Luo Tian Sect.

Even if only a portion, for the currently modestly sized Luo Tian Sect, it counted as a stable source of income.

---

Another year passed.

There was only half a year left until Luo Chen's three-year term ended.

Outside Luo Chen's cave dwelling, the Luo Tian Sect cultivators who lived around him gathered together, chatting about various matters great and small.

The most discussed topic was who would return to the sect and who would stay after the term ended.

Because of the establishment of the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion, there was currently a severe shortage of manpower.

The sect had provided some personnel, and even the old sect leader Sima Huiniang had personally come to the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion to oversee matters. But clearly, that was not enough.

Therefore, some would be drawn from this batch of sect members performing patrol duties.

The Heartsever Fairy was also present at this meeting, cold and taciturn, but not out of place.

Her earlier proactive overtures had already made many accept her.

In the eyes of Zeng Yilong and the others, the Heartsever Fairy was just like Yang Ping'an before her. Once she entered the sect, everyone was family.

They would support each other and walk forward together from now on.

Moreover, the Heartsever Fairy was also a strong cultivator. Her strength at the sixth layer of the Adept stage was a great supplement to the current Luo Tian Sect.

Just as everyone was chatting idly, the Heartsever Fairy suddenly stood up.

"What's wrong?" Zeng Yilong asked curiously.

The Heartsever Fairy carefully sensed for a moment, then sat back down.

She said it was nothing, but inwardly, she was startled and uncertain.

Just a moment ago, she had vaguely felt a heart-palpitating sensation, as if something very dangerous had appeared.

But it was only for an instant, then vanished in the blink of an eye.

"Was it from over there?"

Inside the cave dwelling.

Luo Chen looked with great interest at the Yuantu Sword lying across his knees.

With a thought, a crimson sword qi surged from the blade.

Although it appeared as a single strand, it was actually formed from the fusion of ten strands of Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi. He could separate them if he wished.

"This single strand of sword qi, under the blessing of Yuantu, would kill any Adept-stage cultivator on contact."

"If I strike with the entire fused strand, even a Sage cultivator would be injured if caught off guard."

It sounded unimpressive.

Compared to techniques like the Azure Yang Great Handprint, Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain, or even the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, it seemed far inferior.

But which of those previous techniques had not taken Luo Chen decades, even centuries, to cultivate?

And the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger was even more profound, originating from the Withering-Bloom True Flame, involving the true meaning of laws—it simply could not be compared.

Yet these ten strands of sword qi had taken Luo Chen a total of only five years to refine.

Moreover, this was merely the minor success stage of the Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon, which in theory should only be effective against Adept-stage cultivators.

The reason Luo Chen estimated it could injure Sage cultivators was due to the enhancement of the Yuantu Sword.

If he later cultivated to the intermediate stage, refining a hundred strands of Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi, he would surely be able to truly threaten or even kill Sage-level experts!

Most importantly, this technique required no pre-charging, no drawing on the spiritual energy of heaven and earth. It could be unleashed suddenly, catching people off guard!

Luo Chen was very satisfied with its power.

But what he was even more concerned about was clearly not just this.

With a thought, the attribute panel unfolded.

In the technique column, the proficiency of *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Qi* had clearly advanced from beginner to the proficient level.

Not only that, Luo Chen could sense that his speed in refining Scarlet Firmament Qi from now on would also greatly increase. It would no longer be one strand every half year, but faster.

As for exactly how much faster, he had not yet had time to experiment.

"Just as I had previously imagined!"

Luo Chen was quite excited.

According to the sword canon's records, cultivating to the intermediate stage of a hundred strands would take at least fifty years, and the great success stage of a thousand strands would take at least five hundred years.

But with the enhancement of the proficiency panel, he could greatly shorten this time.

Perhaps before long, he would be able to master an additional major technique!

Clearly, the surprises did not stop there.

Luo Chen's gaze moved upward.

On the realm column, there was a subtle change.

**[Sage Three Layers 80/100]**

His realm had finally stabilized!

The origin qi of heaven and earth he had absorbed when comprehending the true meaning of the law had been completely refined, the restlessness tempered away.

It should not have been this fast, but while constantly tempering the Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi, Luo Chen discovered that it had a considerable auxiliary effect on purifying his mana.

Combined with the day-and-night refinement of the Withering-Bloom True Flame, he had used only five years to completely stabilize his realm.

"Let me go and test it!"

Luo Chen quietly left the cave dwelling.

The Black Ape Puppet, connected by heart and mind, was already prepared, opening the way wide. Without anyone knowing, Luo Chen left Lingtian Pass.

The area for several hundred li around Lingtian Pass had already been cleared.

Luo Chen found a secluded spot and began to execute his various techniques.

Just as he had thought, after stabilizing his realm, not only had the power of these techniques increased, but most importantly, he could achieve the perfect control he had before.

The mana fluctuations that had occasionally leaked out in earlier years could now be perfectly restrained.

His entire temperament was no longer sharp and aggressive, but gradually receded, becoming as steady as a mountain, more befitting the bearing of an Alchemy Saint.

Standing before a massive deep pit.

Luo Chen was filled with joy. While maximally extending his divine sense, he thought about his next arrangements.

"This way, the cultivation I had previously halted can also resume. I can strive to break through to the mid-Sage stage as soon as possible."

"The large stock of high-grade Hongyuan Pills I have saved will finally have their use."

"The fourth-grade mid-grade cave dwelling in Lingtian Pass still has over half a year left. I can't waste it. I need to make use of it!"

After making these plans, Luo Chen prepared to return home.

Just then, Luo Chen's expression changed slightly.

Without hesitation, his divine sense, which had been testing its maximum range, began to frantically retract.
