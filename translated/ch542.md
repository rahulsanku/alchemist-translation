# Chapter 542: Absolute Burst, Capturing a Third-Tier Demon Beast Alive

Black Emperor Paste!

An unranked pill!

It sounded surprising, but in truth, it was not strange.

In the cultivation world, quite a few rare pills belonged to the unranked level.

Of course, this did not mean Black Emperor Paste was low-class, but that its grade was not fixed.

What grade the medicine reached did not depend on the pill formula itself, but on the limitations of the main material!

In other words, the better the main material, the higher the grade of the refined Black Emperor Paste.

According to the pill formula Han Zhan produced, a first-tier main material could refine second-tier Black Emperor Paste, a second-tier main material could refine third-tier Black Emperor Paste, and as for fourth-tier Black Emperor Paste, at least a third-tier main material was required for successful refinement.

As for what the main material of Black Emperor Paste was?

That was quite flexible. Scales and armor, apertured shells, shed husks, thick hides; anything on a demon beast's exterior that was hard could be used as medicine.

It was precisely by taking their meaning of firmness that the Falling Cloud Sect's puppets were created, far surpassing the puppets of the other sects in the Eastern Wasteland cultivation world.

But success was Black Emperor Paste, and failure was also Black Emperor Paste.

Fourth-tier Black Emperor Paste was far too difficult to seek. After all, it required the scales, armor, and apertured shells of third-tier demon beasts as main materials before it could be refined. Therefore, the Black Emperor Paste commonly used by the Falling Cloud Sect was generally only second- to third-tier quality refined from first- and second-tier demon beast main materials. Even third-tier stock was very, very scarce.

Back then, when Luo Chen had been injured on the Tiangu Plain battlefield, the bottle of Black Emperor Paste Han Zhan rewarded him with had only been third-tier.

As a good beginning to their cooperation, Han Zhan gave Luo Chen the pill formula for Black Emperor Paste.

After obtaining it, Luo Chen directly began studying it.

This pill formula was not complicated.

After all, it was an unranked pill.

After studying it several times, he used ready-made materials from the Swallow Islands to refine it a few times.

There were failures, and there were successes.

Among them, there were also many insights.

He discovered that this paste was best refined using "living shells". In this way, the medicinal nature could be maximized!

But although the sea regions near the Swallow Islands had many marine demon beasts, they were not known for sturdy shells, scales, and armor. If he wanted to refine them alive, the conditions were even more lacking.

Therefore, after asking Cheng Haixin and the others, he narrowed it down to several target locations.

One was the Xuanyan Sea Region, where crab-type demon beasts ran rampant and their carapaces were sturdy.

One was the Tianxia Sea Region, where shrimp-type demon beasts were countless, and even archaic beast descendants had appeared.

There was another: the famous Fallen Sea. It was said that in earlier years, that place had a large batch of turtle-type demon beasts inheriting ancient Black Tortoise bloodlines.

Among these target locations, Luo Chen quickly made a choice.

The Fallen Sea was too far, and the Tianxia Sea Region was too dangerous.

Relatively speaking, the Xuanyan Sea Region was both close and comparatively safe. It was the only proper choice.

After preparing everything properly, he set out without hesitation.

To prevent himself from getting lost, he specially called along Cheng Ji, the Cheng clan's most experienced navigation master, letting him pilot the demon-hunting ship and send him part of the way.

At this moment, aboard the demon-hunting ship,

Luo Chen held the brief pill formula Han Zhan had copied out and studied it again and again, ensuring that after reaching the destination, he could kill demons, take materials, refine pills, and temper his body in one smooth sequence.

He did not want to get there and be stuck at the most basic hurdle.

The corresponding auxiliary materials for Black Emperor Paste were also packed full inside his storage ring. When the time came, he only needed to harvest materials and refine them alive.

While he studied, a voice transmission entered his ear.

Luo Chen's thoughts moved, and he took out a blood-colored token from his storage ring.

"Is there a problem with this?"

Opposite him, Han Zhan's voice drifted from within the Myriad Souls Banner.

"There is no problem with the token itself, but keepsakes from Nascent Soul Sages generally have some tracking methods, more or less. If you do not want someone to directly find your door, it is best to handle it somewhat."

Luo Chen's brows furrowed slightly. "Even a storage ring cannot cut off that kind of connection?"

Han Zhan smiled faintly. "If it were a special storage ring, perhaps it could. But yours..."

Luo Chen lowered his head and looked at the black ring on his finger.

Accumulated Thunder Treasure Ring, obtained from the relics of the mighty figure White Night.

Its own material was not very special. It was only a storage tool used to hold Thunder Essence, and because of time's evolution, it had barely advanced to the magic treasure level.

To say it had any special effect of concealing aura or blocking sensing would truly be overpraising it.

After pondering for a moment, he directly said, "How should it be done?"

"Give me the token."

Luo Chen did not hesitate and handed over the Blood Nightmare Token.

From within the soul banner, wisps of black qi drifted out, coiling several times around the Blood Nightmare Token along a special trajectory.

Gradually, that blood color vanished.

Luo Chen raised his brow. "That simple?"

Han Zhan smiled faintly. "With my attainments in the divine soul, ordinary Sage cultivators absolutely cannot track the mark on this token. Of course, if you still do not feel assured, you can add some protective measures."

"Oh? Senior, teach me!" Luo Chen humbly requested instruction.

"Very simple. Are you not very skilled in the Concealment Veil Formation? Use divine sense to inscribe the formation and attach it to this ring. If you feel it is not appropriate, then inscribe a few more layers of formation." Han Zhan paused, then said with faint admiration, "I have never seen someone cultivate an ordinary formation like the Concealment Veil Formation to your level. If you personally cast it, and it is stacked with my method, even if the owner of this Blood Nightmare Token, Blood Nightmare Moluo, personally came searching, he definitely would not find you."

Since the words had reached this point, Luo Chen naturally had no objection.

It was merely using divine sense to carve a formation!

After opening so many storage bags over the years, he had long since become familiar with this process.

Compared to the spiritual sense of the Foundation Establishment stage, the divine sense transformed at the Core Formation stage was even stronger.

In only a few breaths, Luo Chen carved five layers of Concealment Veil Formation onto his storage ring.

At this moment, when he focused and looked, even he himself had the feeling of subconsciously overlooking this storage ring.

"This is not bad either. Most Core Formation cultivators still use storage bags. If I can keep a lower profile, I should keep as low a profile as possible."

Luo Chen smiled inwardly and put the Blood Nightmare Token Wu Qi had given him into the storage bag.

After finishing all this, he walked onto the deck.

Looking at sea and sky as one color, at the boundless blue ocean, his mood could not help becoming broad and open.

His divine sense stirred slightly.

"Return!"

After sending the voice transmission, before long, undercurrents surged beneath the sea surface.

Immediately!

"Roar!"

Beneath the sunlight, a huge black-scaled python soared from the sea surface, splashing countless waves.

On the giant python's head was a short sharp horn.

It was precisely the third-tier Black-Scale Jiaolong that had belonged to Kun Jiao of the Jiaolong Gang.

After obtaining it, Luo Chen had spent quite some effort before using the Myriad Beast Sutra to completely enslave it.

Because of this, the Black-Scale Jiaolong's vital energy had been greatly damaged.

Some time ago, when it looked at the five Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents, it had drooled with craving, trying to swallow them to supplement its nutrition.

Now that it had returned to the great sea, this Black-Scale Jiaolong was like a fish in water, utterly at ease.

It both led the way ahead and kept watch, while also catching fish, shrimp, and sea crabs to recover its vital energy.

"According to the sea chart, at the demon-hunting ship's speed, reaching the Xuanyan Sea Region will require at least a month. This month should be enough to let it recover to peak condition!"

Luo Chen estimated the time, calculating the Black-Scale Jiaolong's recovery progress.

In complete condition, the Black-Scale Jiaolong had early third-tier strength. Against human Core Formation cultivators, its chances of victory were not great, but against low-level demon beasts, it still possessed crushing superiority.

With such a demon beast helping, it would undoubtedly play a great role in the upcoming operation.

Kun Jiao's inheritance had not been limited to this demon beast.

Inside that storage bag of his, there was also a rather small fortune.

Spirit stones alone numbered over two hundred thousand. Among Foundation Establishment cultivators, that counted as quite wealthy.

The other materials were not many in type.

However, their value was also very high.

The reason was that the other materials were basically auxiliary resources prepared for Core Formation.

Luo Chen could not use them, but whether he took them out to trade or bestowed them upon those below, they were both quite good.

"Master, what are your orders?"

The Black-Scale Jiaolong lay on the deck and asked in a rough voice.

Luo Chen shook his head and casually threw out a handful of Spirit-Feeding Pills.

"Continue patrolling nearby. If you encounter a powerful demon beast, remember to warn me."

The Black-Scale Jiaolong opened its mouth and inhaled. That large handful of Spirit-Feeding Pills fell into its mouth.

It swallowed in satisfaction twice before respectfully returning to the great sea.

When it went down, the nearby Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents all trembled, deliberately keeping away from it.

Among demon beast races, realms were clearly divided and ranks were strict, even more than among humans!

...

One month later.

A scarred demon-hunting ship stopped outside a sea region filled with towering reefs.

Luo Chen raised his eyes and looked into the distance, several traces of vigilance in his eyes.

Beside him, the old man carefully said, "The Xuanyan Sea Region is overrun by crab demons, with countless kinds of demon crab races. The five great royal demon crab races are foremost among them: Overlord Crabs, Azure Emperor Crabs, Red Rock Crabs, Demon Spider Crabs, and Nine-Clawed Poison King Crabs..."

"Aside from demon crab races, many other demon races also reproduce and live here."

"And those able to live peacefully in an environment where demon crabs gather in packs are absolutely not weak either. Ring-Headed Turtle groups, Sea King Squids, Hidden Dust Sand... and so on."

"Senior, you must be careful and not careless!"

The one speaking was Cheng Ji, the Foundation Establishment old man Luo Chen had saved on the Cheng clan's demon-hunting ship when he first arrived in the Northern Sea.

This person was an experienced navigator, and was also proficient in helming, treasure appraisal, and many other skills. He was a rare top talent of the Cheng clan.

If not for Luo Chen's need, he would not have rushed such a long road to personally send Luo Chen here.

At this moment, even though much of the information Cheng Ji spoke of was something Luo Chen had already learned from books, Luo Chen still patiently listened as the other party explained it once.

Not to mention, he truly learned some unknown new intelligence from the other party.

For example, ancient texts recorded that in the past, the Xuanyan Sea Region had seven great royal demon crab races.

Now, only five great royal demon crab races remained.

This point alone allowed Luo Chen to make more sufficient preparations.

After carefully listening to the old man's instructions, Luo Chen withdrew his hesitant gaze.

"I have troubled you, Elder Cheng."

Cheng Ji hurriedly waved his hands. "I do not deserve Senior's courtesy. It shames this junior."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and beckoned with his hand.

Immediately, five huge figures appeared on the sea surface, white-bodied with blue rings, fanged and huge-toothed. They were precisely those five Blue-Ringed Fang Serpents comparable to Foundation Establishment cultivators.

"The journey back is far, and storms are unpredictable. These five demon beasts will serve as your guards!"

Cheng Ji hesitated for a moment, but in the end did not refuse.

Presumably, he too understood very clearly how dangerous it would be for him, a Foundation Establishment cultivator, to return from so far away.

If five second-tier sea serpents guarded him, with his rich experience, his chances of survival would undoubtedly be much greater.

"Many thanks for Senior's gift!"

"Mm. You may return!"

Luo Chen waved his hand. After Cheng Ji piloted the demon-hunting ship and gradually left, Luo Chen's eyes fixed on the unfamiliar sea region before him, full of ambition.

The Xuanyan Sea Region had complex terrain, hidden reefs everywhere, and a thousand islands rising.

Within it, demon crabs were numerous and water grasses flourished.

Ordinary cultivators must not lightly enter!

Even Core Formation cultivators had best not stay too long.

Very clearly, Luo Chen had traveled a thousand li to come here not simply to do one job and leave.

With a thought, the thirty-zhang-long Black-Scale Jiaolong swam over.

Luo Chen frowned. "Shrink your body!"

The Black-Scale Jiaolong looked at Luo Chen with grievance, then had no choice but to shrink its body until it was two zhang in size. This was already its limit.

Demon beasts regarded largeness as honorable and length as beautiful.

Shrinking this small, if it did not actively release third-tier aura, perhaps while swimming along, some blind shrimp soldiers and crab generals might swarm up wanting to eat it.

"You lead the way in front. If you encounter enemies, do not act on your own. All movements follow my instructions."

The Black-Scale Jiaolong hissed and quietly drilled beneath the water.

As for Luo Chen, he formed a spirit seal. Threads of black markings appeared all over his body, then vanished beneath his skin.

It was precisely the Concealment Veil Formation engraved on his body activating at full force.

Not only that, his eyes closed and opened, spiritual light blooming.

With a pair of spiritual eyes, he gazed across the surrounding thousand li.

"Go!"

Luo Chen leaped and transformed into a misty ball of spiritual light, drifting through the sky.

As he flew, he looked in all directions.

As early as when Spirit Eye Art reached Perfected Completion, it had gained the effect of peering underground and seeking spirit veins. When circulated at full force, it could even perceive the signs of life and death qi flowing.

Now, opening his spiritual eyes again, Luo Chen was precisely seeking a suitable place to settle.

After flying for roughly half an incense stick's time, Luo Chen's figure paused and sent a voice transmission.

He directed the Black-Scale Jiaolong to swim toward a large island in the distance.

He saw clearly that on that island, there were vigorous and surging spirit qi fluctuations.

Without formation restrictions, it still had a level nearly at third-tier high-grade. It was absolutely a naturally blessed feng shui treasure land.

However, just as the Black-Scale Jiaolong was about to swim ashore, Luo Chen hurriedly stopped it.

"Retreat quickly!"

He himself did not hesitate either, changing direction and leaving.

There was no other reason for this abnormal reaction. It was because on that island, several powerful auras had just emanated.

In terms of realm, they were above Luo Chen himself.

There were even waves of nauseating fishy stench spreading out.

Only after leaving the range of a thousand li did Luo Chen let out a breath of relief.

"My luck is a little poor. I just arrived and nearly barged into the nest of Nine-Clawed Poison King Crabs."

Crabs were generally eight-clawed, while Nine-Clawed Poison King Crabs were ancient variants. It was said they were descendants produced by a venomous dragon mating with an Azure Emperor Crab. The poison throughout their bodies was quite overbearing, and even Sage cultivators were unwilling to personally touch it.

Luo Chen was even less willing to meet one face to face.

This thing was not within his hunting targets either.

"Choose another!"

...

Time slowly flowed.

The stars shifted, and sun and moon turned.

Only after a full half month did one man and one jiaolong finally land on a seemingly ordinary small island.

As soon as he landed, Luo Chen said nothing and directly planted the Myriad Souls Banner into the ground.

"Senior, I will trouble you to hold the formation."

Inside the banner, Han Zhan teased, "It is only an early third-tier Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull. Is there any need to make such a fuss?"

Luo Chen's expression was solemn. He did not feel in the slightest that he was making a fuss.

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull was a famous avian demon beast in the Northern Sea.

Its temperament was violent, it especially loved fighting, and its speed was extremely fast.

For it to have a small island of residence in the crab-dominated Xuanyan Sea Region, its own ability definitely was not weak.

No matter how careful he was, it was not excessive.

Moreover, Luo Chen did not want to make a big commotion.

Sneak quietly into the village, no shooting. He was going to stay in this place for several years.

"Black Scale, go lure that Fighting Gull out. Do not let it run."

The Black-Scale Jiaolong looked at Luo Chen with grievance. Did Master not know that the natural enemies of Black-Scale Jiaolongs included Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gulls?

But under the enslavement of the Myriad Beast Sutra, it had no choice but to move out and carry out the luring task.

In truth, it did not even need to lure much.

When it revealed its enormous body and slithered onto this shrub-covered small island, a sharp bird cry rang out.

Afterward, a handsome azure great bird with two wings spanning over ten zhang rose from the shrubs, then dove down.

Its pair of sharp talons spread like steel.

The Black-Scale Jiaolong acted as if facing a great enemy, coiling into a circle, every scale on its body standing upright while its tongue hissed madly.

Not only that, the single horn atop its head radiated rich black light, trying to scare off the enemy.

The Fighting Gull did not fall for this. It joyfully flew around the Black-Scale Jiaolong.

From time to time, its two wings flapped, sending out wind blades one after another.

Judging from its posture, it wanted to exhaust the Black-Scale Jiaolong, then accomplish its feat in one stroke.

Just as one attacked and the other defended, with both still probing each other, the Black-Scale Jiaolong suddenly revealed an opening.

Its mountain-like enormous body abruptly loosened.

Seeing this, the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull revealed a humanlike look of delight.

Its two wings suddenly spread wide, and a huge vortex immediately appeared on the small island, sweeping the Black-Scale Jiaolong into it.

Not only that, it dove down, both talons directly seizing the Black-Scale Jiaolong's thick body.

The Black-Scale Jiaolong turned its head to bite.

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull opened its beak and pecked.

One jiaolong and one gull immediately drew blood on the beach.

It was also at this moment

that a sharp whistle suddenly came from the air.

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull abruptly turned its head, only to see a streak of sword light breaking through the air.

Its eyes widened in anger. Unable to care about the giant jiaolong beneath it, its two wings suddenly folded together.

Weng...

In the void, a wind vortex immediately appeared, forcefully diverting the great sword's attack direction.

Before it could relax, an explosive boom rang out in the surrounding void.

In an instant, a figure seemed to appear from nowhere, arriving above its head.

He reached out and grabbed!

The Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull tried to resist, but the Black-Scale Jiaolong's tail beneath it coiled backward, tightly entangling its body and preventing it from breaking free.

The demon beast grew anxious.

Its two enormous wings erupted with clusters of wind blades, charging toward the great hand grabbing down.

Toward this, the owner of the great hand ignored everything. His thick right hand seemed like a divine peng crossing a sea of clouds as it fiercely grabbed down, directly seizing the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull by the neck.

"Submit, or die!"

Those cold words entered the Fighting Gull's ear.

Its entire body trembled, followed by furious rage.

Just as it was about to resist, its eyes met that person's eyes, and its will could not help weakening.

"I..."

...

When night descended, on Heavenly Pivot Island, Luo Chen sat cross-legged.

The sleeve on his right arm was ragged and bloodstained. Those were wounds left by the wind blades the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull had unleashed with all its strength when he used the Perfected Completion Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw.

This beast was worthy of its battle-loving reputation. Even in such desperate straits, it still had the strength to counterattack with its life on the line.

It had even wounded Luo Chen's incomparably tempered powerful physique.

But taking such a risky strike also brought abundant returns.

In the sky, a handsome azure great bird was circling in flight.

From time to time, it lowered its head to look at the person on the island. Its eyes revealed the contradictory emotions of hatred and helplessness.

In the end, it had submitted under Luo Chen's hand and had been marked with a Spirit-Enslaving Seal, its divine soul controlled by Luo Chen.

Unless it advanced to fourth tier in a short time, it would have no way to resist Luo Chen's orders.

"Was an early third-tier Fighting Gull worth such a big effort?" Han Zhan asked by voice transmission from inside the soul banner.

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes, a faint trace of fatigue visible.

After simultaneously enslaving the Black-Scale Jiaolong and the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull, two third-tier demon beasts, he faintly felt a burden.

That was a burden on his divine soul.

He did not know whether his divine soul foundation was insufficient, or whether the grade of the Myriad Beast Sutra, this beast-enslaving cultivation art, was too low.

Rubbing his brow, he said seriously, "If this bird disappeared, it would easily draw investigation from some demon beasts in the Xuanyan Sea Region. That would alert the enemy. Also, with such a demon beast cooperating with the Black-Scale Jiaolong, I have eyes and ears in both sea and sky. After this, whether fighting or retreating, I will have enough room to maneuver."

After hearing this explanation, Han Zhan fell somewhat silent.

He knew very clearly that this had not been planned by Luo Chen. It must have been a hasty decision made after discovering the small third-tier spirit vein on Heavenly Pivot Island and seeing the Heavenly Pivot Fighting Gull.

Yet even if this decision had been hasty, it was undoubtedly perfect.

And in the end, he truly had captured a flying third-tier demon beast alive!

Recalling the offensive burst from that brief exchange, whether that short-range explosive acceleration or that unstoppable claw breaking through the air...

"I think I understand why you are so obsessed with refining Black Emperor Paste. You truly intend to go farther and farther on the path of body refinement!"

Luo Chen laughed freely and did not explain.

When his realm had been low, limited by resources and lifespan, he had not dared concurrently cultivate body refinement too much.

But now he was already at Core Formation, and his current cultivation speed could completely let him reach Core Formation Perfected Completion within his lifespan.

The extra time naturally could not be wasted.

The body refinement path could be picked up again!

Most importantly, after many years of cultivation, his Myriad Path Convergence was about to reach Perfected Completion. Combined with Black Emperor Paste, he would definitely be able to improve his physical realm in a short time.

In this way, under conventional methods, he would once again grasp a killing move that surpassed the Core Formation stage!

Luo Chen was not a fool. He would not do useless work.

He stood from the ground and patted the grit from his backside.

Luo Chen looked left and right, and finally his gaze fell into the depths underground.

"First, build a temporary cave residence!"

(End of chapter)
