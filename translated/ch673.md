# Chapter 673: The Lotus Platform Obtained, Borrowing a Treasure to Cultivate

A great lake, beautiful and dangerous, lay quietly inside the enormous Vermilion Bird Mountain.

If seen from above, it looked like flowing fire.

Deep within the dazzling magma lake, one could not help wondering whether some hidden devil lay inside, or whether this lake connected to another world.

There was no devil.

But there was indeed a sacred bird!

Deep within the earth vein, inside a halo of flowing light, a palm-sized fire sparrow slept quietly with its eyes closed and wings covering its body.

It was as though no matter how heaven and earth overturned outside, it would not wake.

Threads of red flame slowly spread outward from its tiny body as center.

Wherever they passed, mountain stone and hard rock melted, turning into scalding liquid that flowed upward and became part of the magma lake.

Yet these flames also restrained their range extremely well, lingering only within a thousand zhang.

Beyond a thousand zhang, another terrifying high temperature took their place.

Within that heat, it was as if even one's thoughts would melt.

This place was also one of the absolute forbidden zones.

Those without the corresponding opportunity would find it almost impossible to set foot here.

Drip!

Drip!

Strands of black liquid continuously fell from one figure.

It was a man of ordinary appearance. Looking carefully, his temperament somewhat resembled Han Zhan, the supreme elder of the Falling Cloud Sect.

He seemed not to care at all that the body on him was melting. His eyes stared fixedly at a small lotus platform inside the high-temperature belt.

That lotus platform, placed within the scorching heat, appeared restless and uneasy.

It seemed even it could not bear that terrifying temperature.

Yet it did not dare leave this place, because in its subconscious, there were people outside eyeing it like tigers watching prey.

Go farther in?

That produced even more instinctive fear!

Even though it had cultivated in Vermilion Bird Mountain these years and absorbed the aura of Vermilion Bird Sacred Fire [TERM?], it was only a tiny amount, enough that this place did not reject it.

The man quietly watched this scene.

Suddenly, he turned his head.

Outside the high-temperature belt, a figure came rushing over like wind and lightning.

The man's body moved. In a few steps, he arrived before Luo Chen.

"It is there!"

After saying this, he seemed unable to persist any longer. His entire body inexplicably turned into a puddle of liquid.

Afterward, a dark light flew out from within. After circling once, it merged into the soul-nourishing wood tablet hanging at Luo Chen's chest.

Looking at this scene, Luo Chen's pupils shrank slightly.

Was this Han Zhan's attainment in soul refinement?

Splitting the divine soul and casually attaching it to a puppet or refined corpse to make an avatar.

When he recalled how Han Zhan had attached his entire soul to that Sage-level puppet back then, Luo Chen's expression became even more grave.

"Senior's divine ability is extraordinary. I imagine the Split-Soul Art [TERM?] has already been cultivated to its peak."

Han Zhan was surprised. He had not expected Luo Chen to suddenly mention this matter.

He casually said, "Peak? Not at all. It is only minor accomplishment."

Luo Chen found it somewhat unbelievable.

"You can split your soul so freely, yet that is only minor accomplishment?"

Han Zhan smiled slightly.

"According to the description in the Split-Soul Art, dividing one into two is merely entry. This old man luckily accomplished it back then by borrowing Ice Castle's ice prison. Afterward, I bitterly cultivated for two hundred years and reached the number of nine souls. Only then could it be considered entering the hall. As for the so-called peak and great accomplishment, heh... I fear this old man will find it difficult to achieve in this life."

"Is it that difficult?" In Luo Chen's view, Han Zhan's soul attainments, even among the many Sage Perfected Beings he had seen, counted as first-class. He probed, "Is it limited by talent? Or is the method incomplete?"

"Neither."

Han Zhan sighed.

"It is merely missing one auxiliary soul-refining treasure recorded in the method."

As he spoke, Han Zhan suddenly came back to himself.

For no reason, why would Luo Chen flatter him and probe about soul cultivation?

So it was because of the "aftereffects" left by that earlier battle!

Luo Chen had considered his strength greatly increased and no longer feared Sage experts, yet during the first exchange, he had fallen into Shark Emperor Liujun's charming song.

The essence of that matter was obviously insufficient divine soul foundation.

This had not struck down Luo Chen's confidence, but it had allowed him to clearly recognize the gap between himself and Sage Perfected Beings.

Therefore, he was now naturally asking about it, trying to make up for the gap in this area.

After understanding this point, Han Zhan laughed softly.

"Do not think too much. In a short time, it is impossible for your divine soul foundation to transcend to the Sage level, even if I pass the Split-Soul Art to you."

Having his thoughts seen through, Luo Chen's small face reddened.

Of course, under the reflection of the Vermilion Bird Sacred Fire, one could not tell whether it was embarrassment.

He asked in a normal tone, "Why? Is there not enough time?"

"It is not a matter of time."

Han Zhan denied it, then explained, "When we Perfected Beings advance to Sage, we resist heavenly might and use our bodies to cross tribulation. Those who survive not only have their realms rise greatly, their divine soul essence also begins transforming, because heaven and earth baptize us. You lack this step. How could you compare?"

Luo Chen fell silent.

Tribulation crossing!

Clearly, this so-called calamity also came with enormous fortune.

Even if he wanted to use inner work and external objects to make up for it, they could not compare to the baptism of heaven and earth.

Seeing Luo Chen downcast, Han Zhan feared he would be discouraged and comforted him.

"In truth, you are already very good. At such an age and such a realm, to possess such a deep divine soul foundation is more than enough. Why insist on more? When your realm rises to Golden Core Stage Seven, Stage Eight, or even Great Perfection, perhaps your divine soul foundation will be infinitely close to a Sage Perfected Being's."

"If you fear being suppressed by Perfected Beings skilled in the soul path during the coming battles, am I not still here?"

"In combat ability, this old man may be lacking somewhat. But when it comes to the soul path, even before mid-stage Sage cultivators, this old man still has some confidence in contending with them. Protecting your divine soul is completely within my ability!"

Luo Chen forced a smile, and his mood did rise somewhat.

The soul path was mysterious and unfathomable.

Not every Sage Perfected Being in the world was skilled in it.

Even Shark Emperor Liujun had only used sound-wave attacks earlier, with a little soul art mixed inside.

Such methods were not much different from Luo Chen's illusion art, Mirror Flower, Water Moon [TERM?].

In fact, in his view, Shark Emperor Liujun's charming song was somewhat crude and was inferior to his attainments in Mirror Flower, Water Moon.

Methods like Han Zhan's, which could directly attack the enemy's divine soul, were probably extremely rare in the world.

Gathering his mind, Luo Chen's spiritual eyes passed through the flowing magma and landed on that small lotus platform.

Their earlier conversation had not been for passing time.

During that process, he had been observing the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

This lotus had indeed reached an extremely high level of intelligence. It knew this place was dangerous, so it deliberately shrank its main body to avoid being scorched over a large area by the high temperature.

At the same time, shrinking its body also made it easier to hide.

If not for Han Zhan's refined corpse avatar following it the whole way, even if Luo Chen found this place, he might not have been able to locate the Five Elements Lotus Platform's hiding spot.

Next, it was time to collect this spiritual object!

As for how to collect it?

Luo Chen had already formed a plan!

As a senior alchemist who had reached the fourth tier, he was thoroughly familiar with harvesting all kinds of spirit medicines.

Especially before entering Demon-Fall Land [TERM?], he had already found several methods to collect the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

The simplest and most brutal method was to use overwhelming ability to isolate heaven and earth spiritual qi and directly restrain it.

Next was to use tools not belonging to the five elements to harvest it.

As for why one had to use something outside the five elements?

The principle was simple. The Five Elements Lotus Platform possessed all five types of spiritual power at once. The Dao of generation and restraint was almost instinctive to it.

Whether earth-and-wood jade boxes or metal-and-fire sharp blades, it could instinctively avoid them. Even if caught, it could escape.

Thus, something outside the five elements would not be affected by this.

Finally, there was one more method!

That was to take a magic treasure that possessed all five elements and use it to collect the object.

Possessing all five elements meant one did not fear the Dao of five-element generation and restraint. This followed the principle of shared origin.

The reason Shark Emperor Liujun could not forcefully collect it was likely because he lacked the corresponding treasure for collection.

And Luo Chen happened to have such a treasure!

With a thought, Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat gently.

A small gray cauldron spun out and appeared above the lake.

With mana empowering it, the small gray cauldron expanded. In the blink of an eye, it became several zhang huge.

It was his lifebound magic treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

Luo Chen formed a spiritual seal. The Primordial Unity Cauldron began spinning rapidly, and a powerful suppressive force immediately spread outward, instantly covering the Five Elements Lotus Platform in the distance, which believed it had hidden itself well.

Afterward, Luo Chen flicked his fingers repeatedly.

Swish!

Swish!

Swish!

...

One mana chain after another flew out with clattering sounds. Their color was dark gray, unlike the scarlet chains Luo Chen normally used.

Clearly, in controlling the Primordial Unity Cauldron at this moment, Luo Chen had already converted five-element mana in advance. This was why the nine chains that emerged were gray and misty.

The nine mana chains flew out like spiritual snakes shaking their heads and wagging their tails, and quickly reached the Five Elements Lotus Platform.

Danger had arrived. The Five Elements Lotus Platform, already intelligent, naturally was unwilling to await capture.

The lotus platform turned, and its body suddenly grew larger, reaching roughly five zhang. It stood within the sea of fire like a fire lotus!

The nine chains bound its body and tried to pull it back.

But when the two sides wrestled, the Five Elements Lotus Platform remained unmoving!

"This!"

Shock appeared on Luo Chen's face.

Han Zhan looked at this scene and sighed.

"That Tai Sui was right. This lotus platform's Dao practice is indeed deep. It lacks only one step to advance to the fifth tier."

Luo Chen frowned. He had also understood.

The reason Shark Emperor Liujun had not directly taken this object away, apart from lacking the corresponding treasure, was probably largely due to the Five Elements Lotus Platform's own resistance.

That was why he could only make do by borrowing the treasure in Vermilion Bird Mountain to heal.

Even during the great battle, when the Five Elements Lotus Platform forcibly fled and Shark Emperor Liujun was too occupied to split his attention, he could not suppress it.

If so, even if Luo Chen collected this object now, he would probably find it difficult to put it into a storage ring and take it away.

This was troublesome.

"I was insufficiently prepared. Blood Wanderer spent enormous effort to collect the fifth-tier Withered Blood Vine. This Five Elements Lotus Platform is only one step away from the fifth tier. One can imagine how difficult it will be for me to collect it."

"But no matter how troublesome things are later, I must first take it away from this place!"

He saw very clearly that within Vermilion Bird Mountain, the Five Elements Lotus Platform possessed the same home-ground advantage as him. Its strength did not end and flowed without stop.

Therefore, he could only first take it away!

Luo Chen pursed his lips, grew ruthless, and poured out the little remaining mana in the second Origin Pill.

The nine chains immediately tightened.

"Come out!"

A silent wrestling contest quietly unfolded between one man and one lotus.

Later, Luo Chen even exhausted the mana in the Origin Pill and began using the mana of his lifebound Golden Core. Only then did he barely pull the Five Elements Lotus Platform a few hundred zhang.

At that moment...

"If you do not mind, let this old man lend you a hand."

"Hm?"

Luo Chen was surprised for a moment, then accepted Han Zhan's help.

He only felt the Primordial Unity Cauldron suddenly sink. Then a mysterious power empowered the nine mana chains.

Clatter...

The chains swayed, and Luo Chen pulled himself backward.

The Five Elements Lotus Platform was also forced to move outward.

During this process, Han Zhan sighed.

"In only several dozen years, you nurtured the Primordial Unity Cauldron to such quality. It is truly impressive."

A refined-corpse body was already hard enough. Yet inside the high-temperature belt derived from the Vermilion Bird Sacred Fire, it still melted away completely in a short time.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron, however, was completely unaffected!

From this, one could see the high quality of this treasure.

In Han Zhan's view, the Primordial Unity Cauldron lacked only an opportunity to be baptized by heaven and earth.

At that time, this treasure would surely advance into the ranks of true artifacts and control heaven and earth spiritual qi for its use.

Luo Chen did not speak and continued driving his mana.

Time slowly passed.

...

One day later.

"It came out!"

Tai Sui excitedly looked at the tiny figure on Vermilion Bird Mountain.

Beside him, Moya Cave Master, who had already awakened, slowly opened his eyes. Looking at Luo Chen's figure and that enormous lotus platform bound and dragged out by nine chains, a complicated expression appeared in his eyes.

All for this thing, he had nearly been buried here.

Was this trip worth it or not?

Later, could he leave Demon-Fall Land alive and safe?

If he fell to Dao Lan's end...

He pursed his lips and asked hoarsely, "Does this spirit medicine truly help cultivators form a Nascent Soul?"

"It should not."

Tai Sui shook his head, then said, "The Five Elements Lotus Platform absorbs five-element spiritual qi to grow. It is born incomparably pure and clear, extremely compatible with heaven and earth spiritual qi. To cultivators, its greatest use is actually as an auxiliary cultivation object, helping cultivators filter impurities within heaven and earth spiritual qi and directly absorb it."

"Directly absorb?" Moya Cave Master froze, his face showing hesitation. "Without refining?"

"No need to refine! With a cultivator's Nascent Soul combined with the Five Elements Lotus Platform, they can directly absorb heaven and earth spiritual qi most suited to themselves."

"Nascent Soul?"

"Correct. The Five Elements Lotus Platform is a treasure specifically used by Sage Perfected Beings. It is normal that you Golden Core cultivators have never seen one."

Moya Cave Master became even more puzzled.

"Then why did Devil Lord Azure Yang spend so much effort looking for this object?"

They Golden Core cultivators did not have Nascent Souls within their bodies. They simply could not withstand violent heaven and earth spiritual qi pouring into them. They had to slowly capture and absorb it, then run it through their cultivation method, filter it through their meridians, and purify and refine it bit by bit before it could transform into mana compatible with themselves.

For Luo Chen to do this as preparation after advancing to Sage could not even be called planning for a rainy day. It was simply worrying about the sky falling.

Tai Sui shrugged.

"I do not know. But he is a high-tier alchemist, so perhaps he has some method to turn it to his own use!"

Moya Cave Master was speechless.

All along, he had treated Luo Chen as an artifact-forger like himself.

Yet he had forgotten that this person's strongest ability was actually alchemy!

If the other party had some method to refine the Five Elements Lotus Platform and turn it into a support resource for Sage formation, that would make sense.

"Sage formation..."

He murmured, and a trace of longing appeared in his eyes.

Tai Sui seemed to sense the desire in his heart and smiled.

"Cave Master, you need not belittle yourself. After you help my main body escape, I will guide you into the inheritance land. If you can receive a little of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's artifact-forging inheritance, a mere Sage realm will be within easy reach."

Was it truly that simple?

Moya Cave Master was half-believing and half-doubting.

Tai Sui said quietly, "Think of Fairy Feileng."

With just that sentence, Moya Cave Master's heart shook, and his confidence increased greatly!

Boom!

A loud sound came from the distance.

The five-colored lotus platform landed beside a rolling magma river.

In the sky, a gray great cauldron emitted gray light, covering it and preventing it from struggling away.

With one glance, Moya Cave Master's eyes shone.

"What magic treasure is that gray cauldron?"

Tai Sui's focus was different. He watched Luo Chen leap and sit upright atop the lotus platform, his aura rising and falling as if he were cultivating.

"Hm? He also knows the Five Elements Lotus Platform can be used to assist cultivation?"

"But without a Nascent Soul, how can he withstand the violent spiritual qi it draws?"

*(End of Chapter)*
