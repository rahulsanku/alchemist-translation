Within the pill chamber.

A figure lay collapsed on the ground, their condition unknown, life or death, only a red light shield surrounding them.

The reason for this situation, aside from the alchemist himself, lay in the potency of the pill being refined, as well as the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

The pill force of the medicine was too strong, yet it could not overpower the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which suppressed it tightly.

The vast medicinal power, after its violent eruption, began to slowly retract, surging into nine pill pellets that resembled stars.

Faintly, from within the void, there came low roars and growls.

Whether this was due to the medicinal power retracting too quickly, or because the Nine Separation Pill itself was too peculiar, he could not tell.

"Most pills emphasize fusion and condensation before completion, extreme internal containment."

"This Nine Separation Pill is quite special; it insists on seeing heaven and earth before it is willing to coalesce."

Luo Chen chuckled, the buzzing roars sounding in his ears.

But as he listened to those roars, Luo Chen's smile gradually faded, and his expression turned somewhat uncertain.

This kind of "pill resonance" sounded neither like an ox nor a wolf.

It was low and heavy, yet not lacking in clear, sharp desolation.

Among the main ingredients, there was a Nine Páozi's golden core, but how could a Nine Páozi make such a sound?

"Nine-Headed Howler: Born with nine heads, each with its own will, temperament inconsistent, some cruel, some docile. Taboos for this demon: thunder and lightning, the sharpness of metal..."

Following these memories, Luo Chen continuously synthesized all the information.

In its earliest form, the Nine-Headed Howler did not have nine heads, but rather fleshy tumors. Whenever a tumor grew a head, it would master one ability.

That demon fruit, translated into the terminology of human cultivators, was actually spirit essence.

The ultimate essence products of all things in heaven and earth.

Bit by bit, drop by drop, Luo Chen finally reached a judgment.

The technique practiced by Nine Spirit Lord indeed originated from the demon realm, and was even created using the Nine-Headed Howler as a template.

Therefore, his Nascent Soul would have tumor-like growths on it.

And he was also skilled at refining the Nine Heavens Qi!

After figuring this out, Luo Chen suddenly had a moment of epiphany.

Even if these things did not provide him with any practical improvement, his knowledge had indeed grown.

But another question also suddenly arose.

How could there be techniques created using demons as templates within the Mountains and Seas Realm?

He had never heard of demons existing in this realm.

Even that Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had actually come from beyond the heavens.

Beyond the heavens...

Somehow, Luo Chen thought back to the matter of Alchemy Saint Chu Yan's ascension. The place she went to was definitely not the legendary Immortal Realm.

The spiritual mechanism that had lifted her up had been intercepted by the Tianyuan Dao Sect Sect Master.

She had left through the Star Gate, going to another place.

There were also rumors in the cultivation world that although the Mountains and Seas Realm had human natives, it had always been dominated by desolate beasts.

It was not until ancient times that the Five Sacred Lands appeared out of nowhere, slaughtering desolate beasts on a massive scale.

To protect themselves, the desolate beasts abandoned their original path and transformed into the rapid cultivation path of demon beasts taking human form, thus giving rise to the ancient human-demon war.

Luo Chen still gazed upward at the ceiling of the pill chamber, but his gaze seemed to pierce through the roof, looking outward, beyond the nine heavens.

"Any strong individual, any powerful force, leaves traces wherever they go, and their rise is sure to stir waves."

"The Five Sacred Lands appeared abruptly, with no traceable origin. The only explanation is that they were not natives of the Mountains and Seas Realm at all, but came from another world. That world might be behind the Star Gate in Central Continent..."

"Not only that, but that world is also connected to the demon realm. Therefore, when they descended upon the Mountains and Seas Realm, they also brought numerous wondrous techniques and methods, and they were systematic and complete."

"So, one of the three lineages of the Primordial Demon Sect can also cultivate pure demonic qi. I overlooked this before."

"Pioneering wars, pioneering a domain, a sea—if you look at it on a larger scale, can the descent of the Five Sacred Lands also be seen as a pioneering war? Only, the scale was larger, pioneering an entire world?"

One thought after another, one speculation after another, continuously burst forth in Luo Chen's mind.

Bizarre, unimaginable, yet, through the connection of various clues, reasonable and constantly approaching the truth.

And the source that led to all these conjectures was merely the nine pills floating in the air.

Luo Chen's expression suddenly stirred, and he beckoned to the Nine Separation Pill in the air.

*Ding-ding, dong-dong.*

Like large and small pearls falling onto a jade plate, Luo Chen collected the pills, picked up Qi Yi, and left the pill chamber.

Within the most solemn Luo Tian Grand Hall on Great Azure Mountain, Li Yingzhang sat at the head, his gaze falling upon the three people and one beast in the hall.

Like a snake, like a python, with a prominent head and horns!

His Chiwen Sword was different from ordinary magical treasures, following a rather unorthodox refining method, incorporating a demon spirit into the blade, possessing considerable power.

For the Chiwen Sword's sword spirit to be so wary, the only explanation was that the other party was very likely also a dragon lineage, and a higher-level superior existence!

A fourth-tier dragon lineage Demon Emperor!

Unless, someone or something she could trust appeared.

"Is it the master?"

"No!"

"What a proud person!"

"Little Black pays respects to the Mistress!"
