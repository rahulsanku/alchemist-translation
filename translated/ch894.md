# Chapter 894: Nether Origin Rises from the Depths, the Alchemy Sovereign Descends

From Luo Tian, to Ling Tian, and then to Shangyou—though it seemed a long journey, in a daze it was but an instant.

When the youth closed his eyes, heaven and earth had already changed.

"You may open your eyes now."

A gentle voice sounded at his ear, and Luo Lingxi opened his eyes.

He looked outside. Cultivators clad in black robes, their faces full of murderous solemnity, were staring at him and his companions.

Instinctively, he tightened his grip on the large hand holding his.

"It's fine."

The next moment, those cultivators respectfully bowed.

"Greetings, Uncle-Master Ye!"

The Ling Tian City Lord smiled and waved his hand, pointing toward Luo Chen, who had just stepped out of the teleportation formation.

"This is the famed Alchemy Sovereign of the Eastern Wilderness, and in the future, he will also be the Alchemy Sovereign of my Nether Origin Sect."

Those cultivators were startled, then once again bowed respectfully to Luo Chen.

"Greetings, Alchemy Sovereign!"

Because of this bow, Luo Lingxi felt the immense pressure brought by being watched instantly dissipate.

He looked up at his young father. The other's expression was as usual, as if long accustomed to this scene.

He merely nodded slightly, saying nothing more.

Seeing this, the Ling Tian City Lord invited, "Let's go. We'll stay in Shangyou City for half a day. The sect has arranged a ferryman, and then we can enter the Nether Origin."

Luo Chen led Luo Lingxi out of this heavily guarded teleportation hall.

When he looked back, he happened to encounter several probing divine senses.

Following the direction from which the divine senses emanated, Luo Chen quickly traced them to their source and couldn't help but nod slightly.

"As expected of a sacred land!"

"For just a teleportation hall, they've not only arranged for a large number of Adepts to stand guard at all times, but also have three Sages watching over it."

The Ling Tian City Lord laughed. "The teleportation hall is a small matter, but the Nether Origin Sect's gateway is a great one. If we lose this Shangyou City, then the Nether Origin Sect will have its door blocked. We have to squeeze out this much manpower. And this is during peacetime. If we go back ten or twenty years, during the human-demon war, there would only be more Nether Origin Sages in Shangyou City."

Luo Chen felt this made sense.

A sect's gateway could not afford any mistakes.

Take the Primordial Demon Sect, for example. Although he had never been to their true mountain gate, the Arctic Yemo Heaven, he had seen their gateway, the Surging Sea Immortal City, from afar.

At that time, the vast immortal city was already a ruined city.

But on the foundation of that ruined city, formation grandmasters had laid down a great number of formations, sealing the Arctic Yemo Heaven tightly.

This had also led to the Northern Sea demon army being trapped by the Primordial Demon Sect for a hundred years.

If the Primordial Demon Sect hadn't lost the Surging Sea Immortal City back then, could they have avoided being beaten at their own doorstep?

If the Northern Sea demons had sent people to guard the Surging Sea Immortal City before they broke in, would they have escaped their predicament sooner?

These questions had no ifs.

Under the enthusiastic guidance of the Ling Tian City Lord, Luo Chen toured the bustling and prosperous Shangyou City in a cursory manner.

Three figures gradually disappeared into the crowd.

And in the palace complex near the teleportation hall, the three divine senses that had been probing earlier slowly returned to their owners.

"What keen divine sense perception, what a strong soul foundation. Is that the Luo Chen whom Senior Brother Chongxuan said repelled a Great Demon Emperor with a single finger?"

"Even though he was protecting a youth when the teleportation ended, why couldn't I probe the details of his cultivation base despite focusing all my attention on him?"

"The Alchemy Sovereign has arrived. Quickly report to the clan head!"

...

Within the Nether Origin Sacred Land.

On a secluded island, the light of a formation suddenly flickered, blocking a visitor's direct entry.

A gentle woman walked out of her alchemy chamber with a displeased expression and summoned her disciple.

"Didn't I say that this century is a crucial time for my alchemy research and I'm not seeing any guests? Which fellow disciple is so rude as to force their way onto my Sulfur Jade Island?"

The disciple replied fearfully, "Reporting to Master, it's Uncle-Master Lu!"

The gentle woman frowned and murmured softly, "Senior Brother Lu Chun?"

Just then, a figure had already appeared in the distance, flying over in a manner that seemed slow but was actually fast.

"Sister Chu, working behind closed doors won't yield any results. Why be so unwelcoming to your senior brother?"

Chu Yu took a deep breath, cast a Cleansing Art on herself to remove the complex pill qi, and quietly waited for the newcomer.

With a few flickers, the figure had crossed a hundred li and landed before Chu Yu.

His bearing was free and easy, his manner unhurried.

Chu Yu said flatly, "Senior Brother's escape art is exquisite, ignoring my Sulfur Jade Island's formation. It seems your Dao cultivation has advanced again!"

Lu Chun laughed heartily, his cultivation base at the fourth layer of the Sage realm clearly revealed.

He had indeed broken through the early Sage stage, which could be considered a great advancement in Dao cultivation.

But...

"Compared to my junior sister praising my Dao cultivation, I would rather you praise my alchemy achievements."

Chu Yu twitched the corner of her mouth. "Unless one breaks through to Ascendant, even a late-stage Sage, for us alchemists, isn't much different from an early-stage Sage."

"No!"

Lu Chun clearly had a different view on this.

"Has Junior Sister not noticed that the Alchemy Saint Hall tests not only our alchemy skills but also has requirements for cultivation realm?"

Chu Yu sneered. "So this is the reason why Senior Brother, after mastering the refining methods of only three types of fourth-grade pills and obtaining the title of Lesser Grandmaster, stopped delving into research and shifted his focus to cultivation instead?"

Lu Chun's face flushed slightly, but he didn't hypocritically hide it.

He admitted generously, "That is indeed my thought."

Chu Yu shook her head. "Then you have taken the wrong path. If merely having a high cultivation realm and dabbling in alchemy could obtain the Alchemy Saint's inheritance, would the three ancestors begrudge spending some time trying their hand at alchemy and pill-making?"

Lu Chun opened his mouth to refute but couldn't find the words.

For Ascendant powerhouses, their lifespans were already far longer than ordinary cultivators.

Coupled with their powerful souls and deep magic power, once they wanted to dabble in a certain immortal art, as long as they spent some time and effort, they could always achieve some success.

Reaching the pinnacle might be difficult, but the Lesser Grandmaster realm of establishing one's own school was definitely not hard.

If he tried to take a different path, raising his cultivation realm to compensate for his lack of alchemy skill, compared to them, it would be utterly laughable.

Had he really taken the wrong path?

This thought kept echoing in Lu Chun's mind, who had only recently broken through in cultivation realm, causing his face to alternate between red and green, and his magic power fluctuations even became chaotic.

Chu Yu frowned and lightly shouted, "Senior Brother!"

Lu Chun snapped back to reality, immediately realizing something was wrong. He stammered, "Junior Sister, I..."

Chu Yu sighed. She didn't show any sign of gloating.

Compared to her senior brother's rudeness in forcing his way onto the island, what she saw more clearly was the closeness of their relationship.

Her Chu family was not a prominent clan in the Nether Origin Sect. Before the appearance of their ancestor, the Alchemy Saint who never married, they had only produced one early-stage Sage predecessor.

It wasn't until Alchemy Saint Chu Yan burst onto the scene that the Chu family developed.

But with the departure of Ancestor Chu Yan, the Chu family, which hadn't even developed for a few hundred years, was suppressed by other factions within the sect.

The fact that she, an alchemy grandmaster, could only live in obscurity on tiny Sulfur Jade Island showed how difficult the Chu family's current situation was.

And over the years, the only one who had shown closeness to the Chu family was the Lu family!

Although many said the Lu family was trying to steal the Chu family's alchemy techniques—and they had indeed used those techniques to cultivate Lu Chun into a Lesser Grandmaster—sometimes, possessing resources while no one dared to get close was also a kind of tragedy.

That Senior Brother Lu Chun showed such sincerity before her was an important reason Chu Yu could tolerate his rudeness in the details.

"Senior Brother, tell me your purpose for coming!"

Because of this distraction, Lu Chun no longer dwelled on his earlier distracting thoughts. He suppressed them at the bottom of his heart and began to explain to Chu Yu.

"The Alchemy Sovereign is about to enter the Nether Origin!"

"Alchemy Sovereign?" Chu Yu first showed confusion, but then her bright eyes lit up. "That Alchemy Sovereign?"

Among the hundred immortal arts, all lead to the great Dao.

Those who reach the pinnacle are called Saints; those who establish their own school are called Grandmasters; those who enter the hall and ascend the chamber can be called Masters.

But these titles were often tacitly understood, and few used them to refer to themselves.

Unless, they were universally recognized!

Just like Ancestor Chu Yan's title of Alchemy Saint.

Below the Alchemy Saint, there were still quite a few alchemy grandmasters in the present age.

The Nether Origin Sect, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, the Primordial Demon Sect... and even among the demons, there were capable alchemists.

But only Luo Chen alone held the title of "Alchemy Sovereign," which was truly arrogant.

Lu Chun nodded. "It is Luo Chen!"

Chu Yu thought for a moment and said matter-of-factly, "If you count the time, he should indeed be coming. What's so strange about that?"

"There's nothing strange about that, but Junior Sister, you must know that he is coming to take over the Alchemy Saint Hall. And the prerequisite for taking over the Alchemy Saint Hall is to obtain the Alchemy Saint's inheritance. Junior Sister..."

Before he could finish speaking, he was interrupted by the woman's resounding voice.

"No, the Alchemy Saint's inheritance is mine!"

The woman, who usually displayed a gentle temperament, now showed an attitude that brooked no argument.

To others, the Alchemy Saint's inheritance was just an inheritance.

But to her, obtaining the Alchemy Saint's inheritance was an antidote to restore the Chu family's glory and to break free from the predicament of being constantly suspected by the sect's cultivators over the years.

And it was the only antidote!

Lu Chun nodded in agreement. "The Alchemy Saint's inheritance could be yours, it could be mine, I could even grit my teeth and accept Chen Mozi or Xin Yisheng obtaining it, since they have served the Nether Origin Sect for centuries. But Luo Chen? What right does he have?"

"He has rendered no service to our sect, nor has he ever contributed. His alchemy skills are only boasted about by others; we haven't seen his true ability."

"If such a petty thief steals the Alchemy Saint's inheritance, the world will surely laugh at the Nether Origin Sect for having no one!"

Chu Yu's expression was cold. She half agreed and half disagreed with her senior brother's words.

A man with a great reputation was never without substance. Luo Chen was definitely not that worthless.

But the matter of the inheritance could indeed not fall to an outsider!

Lu Chun continued indignantly, "Although we all think this way, we can't go against the ancestors' will. I don't know what the ancestors are thinking, actually planning to promise the Alchemy Saint Hall to a wild-born Luo Chen instead of us who have painstakingly studied alchemy for years."

"I don't care what the inside story is, but for Luo Chen to smoothly take over the Alchemy Saint Hall is absolutely impossible!"

Chu Yu looked at Lu Chun. "Can Senior Brother still defy the ancestors' decree? Moreover, the prerequisite for taking over the Alchemy Saint Hall is to obtain the inheritance and gain my ancestor's recognition. In the end, it will come down to a contest of alchemy skills."

"That is the final hurdle!" Lu Chun glanced at the gentle woman and said with confidence, "He has to get to the Alchemy Saint Hall first."

"Hmm?"

"Regardless of whether this man has real skill, his fame and sharpness are undeniable. It is precisely by relying on this reputation that the ancestors agreed to open the once-a-century Alchemy Saint Hall inheritance trial early for him, even disrupting our rhythm. Today, I will give him a show of force, to dampen his spirit. At that time, the strong within the sect will not be fooled by the halo over his head, and perhaps the century inheritance trial won't be opened so hastily." Lu Chun said confidently, as if everything was under control.

Chu Yu frowned slightly. "But this is ultimately a trick outside the Dao of alchemy."

"Means are not judged by high or low, only by results!"

Lu Chun's magic power surged as he prepared to leave.

"Junior Sister, would you like to accompany me and watch a good show of the Sinking Nether Origin?"

Chu Yu wanted to refuse, but another voice kept tempting her in her mind.

Why didn't the ancestor trust his own descendants, but instead believed in an outsider he had never met?

And what kind of person was this Luo Chen, really?

In the end, she followed Lu Chun off Sulfur Jade Island and went to the shore of the Nether Origin.

When she arrived, her heart was still in turmoil.

Part of her hoped he would show his incompetence and be a man of unearned reputation, so that the Alchemy Saint's inheritance would not fall to another. Another part of her hoped he had real skill and great ability, proving that the ancestor had not misjudged.

While her heart was conflicted, Lu Chun beside her said in a low voice:

"Chen Mozi, Xin Yisheng, and the others have also come!"

...

Every sacred land has a natural barrier!

This was not a rumor, but a fact.

It was because the five sacred lands of the Mountains and Seas Realm had been carved out from the demons through bloody battles, seizing spiritual mountains and lands to establish their mountain gates.

To prevent demon counterattacks, when the mountain gates were first established, they specifically chose dangerous places with natural barriers.

The Arctic Yemo Heaven of the Northern Sea's Primordial Demon Sect, the Life and Death Gate of the Southern Frontier's Life and Death Gate, the Verdant Screen of the Western Desert's Hanging Temple, and the Nether Abyss of the Eastern Wilderness's Nether Origin Sect!

This was Luo Chen's first time facing the Nether Abyss!

A pitch-black abyss, boundless in extent, unfathomable in depth, with no visible seawater flowing, lay stretched out beneath his feet.

It was like the gaping maw of some giant beast, waiting at any time for living beings to stumble in ignorantly, and then swallow them whole.

Even though he prided himself on having made new breakthroughs in his current strength, he dared not force his way in.

He only felt that the danger within was certainly no less than that of the Fallen Sea.

"What is hidden inside?"

As Luo Chen grew wary, he shifted his attention to the air.

Golden light flickered in his eyes.

After a long while, he withdrew his gaze, still shaken.

"Such a no-fly formation is unheard of and unseen!"

The Ling Tian City Lord beside him looked as if he shared in the glory, praising with incomparable reverence, "This formation was jointly laid down by the Nether Origin ancestors back then, with the fifth-rank Nether Origin spirit vein as its foundation and the Nether Abyss as its formation eye. It is on par with the Severing Heaven-Earth Passage of the Central Continent's Heaven-Earth Peak. Even Ascendant powerhouses dare not fly across it."

"Not only that, but in the three thousand years since the ancient era, this formation has been continuously repaired and supplemented by our sect's powerhouses, with many killing formations added."

"Especially a thousand years ago, Ancestor Bai Ye integrated his most proud Nine Heavens Universal Great Thunder Art into it. Once someone tries to force their way through, it will trigger the fury of the nine heavens, bringing down a heavenly tribulation to blast the invading enemy into dust."

So-called formation foundations and formation eyes were just general terms, revealing no real details.

But using human power to trigger a heavenly tribulation—such a method was enough to strike fear into the heart of any Sage cultivator who had experienced a tribulation.

Luo Chen thought of his own experience of using a transformed lightning beast to trigger a heavenly tribulation in the Medicine King Domain. Compared to this, it seemed like mere child's play.

He also thought of the Accumulated Thunder Nine Mountains that had stood for a thousand years in the Jade Cauldron Domain. Over that millennium, the nine thunder mountains had not disappeared; instead, the thunder had continued unabated, almost possessing the power to annihilate all life.

Luo Chen blinked. "Ancestor Bai Ye? Could it be the one from the Jade Cauldron Domain..."

The Ling Tian City Lord's expression darkened. Just as he was about to explain, the Nether Abyss below suddenly had land rising from its depths.

Luo Lingxi gripped Luo Chen's large hand, his eyes shining as he pointed into the abyss.

"There's a monster!"
