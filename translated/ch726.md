# Chapter 726: Revisiting Old Places, the Blood God Reappears

Luo Chen's trip to Molan Court had happened by chance.

He had come by coincidence and left in haste.

As for the righteous-demonic War-Ceasing Assembly, he did not pay much attention to it. To him, the Northern Sea cultivation world did not have many people or matters worth concern; he was only a passerby.

Therefore, after achieving his established goal, and in order to avoid hidden dangers brought by his earlier high-profile actions, he quietly led his people away.

Old Monster Netherfire's soul essence was in hand. As for the other two bottles of soul essence Fu Chaosheng had promised, as well as more beyond that, those would have to wait until after they reached Central Continent.

But that was no matter. One bottle of soul essence on the level of ten million people was enough for him to refine many Great Soul Pills [TERM?].

Afterward, so long as he gathered the relevant supplementary materials, he could begin pill refinement.

And in this regard, there was no need for Luo Chen to run himself ragged purchasing everywhere.

The third condition he reached with Fu Chaosheng was both a condition and a transaction.

That was the hope of borrowing the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance's power in Fu Chaosheng's hands to collect the resources he needed.

The simple word "resources" covered many things.

These included supplementary materials for Great Soul Pills [TERM?] and supplementary materials for fourth-tier Hongyuan Pills [TERM?].

If Luo Chen truly had to collect these two categories personally, the time consumed would be difficult to estimate.

In addition, Luo Chen also entrusted Fu Chaosheng with helping him collect cultivation arts and secret techniques related to the fire attribute, spell tomes, and so on. As for body refining, he would take what came along; if any could be collected, then collect a copy as well.

Fu Chaosheng was not helping Luo Chen collect these resources for free.

Luo Chen had to pay!

Fu Chaosheng also did not doubt Luo Chen's financial strength. During the Sage trade in Return Origin Palace, Luo Chen had thrown out one hundred million, already displaying abundant wealth.

Of course, all of the above was related to cultivation.

Luo Chen's other goal had also been invisibly achieved during their negotiations.

That was using the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance's route to go to Central Continent!

The Northern Sea's geography was vast. Even if Luo Chen had a map, if he wished to cross the boundless ocean and reach Central Continent, even leaving aside the dangers along the road, time alone would cost a great deal.

The Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance was different. They had their own merchant caravans, mature and safe routes, and even some short-distance teleportation formations that could directly cross certain natural barriers and forbidden lands.

The time that could be saved through this was at least tenfold!

Leaving aside the progress of the righteous-demonic War-Ceasing Assembly at Molan Court, and ignoring how Old Ghost Nanqi and the others might fly into rage after discovering Luo Chen had vanished, Luo Chen at this moment was rather leisurely.

Hiding inside Black King's belly, he had fully immersed himself in the beast-hide booklet in his hand.

This was a fourth-tier main cultivation art of the fire attribute!

It was something Fu Chaosheng carried with him. It was not a Heavenly Origin Dao Sect cultivation art, but something he had purchased over these years.

After they had reached the third condition, Fu Chaosheng had given Luo Chen this cultivation art.

For this, Luo Chen spent a full five million spirit stones!

And that was still the friendship price!

Luo Chen had no choice. Fourth-tier cultivation arts were hard to find!

Although this cultivation art, named the True Scripture of Flame Sovereignty, was rather ordinary, it was very complete, almost continuous from Qi Refinement to the Sage stage.

Its specific content was rather general, unlike today's cultivation-world methods that were divided in fine detail, but so long as one spent some effort modifying it, the Qi Refinement chapter, Initiate chapter, Adept chapter, and so on could all be extracted.

In this way, relying on this cultivation art alone was already enough to open a mountain, establish a sect, and cultivate disciples from nothing.

Luo Chen was reading it bit by bit from the beginning, without the slightest omission.

Although the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture given to him by Nether Spring was called the number-one fire-attribute cultivation art beneath heaven, it only had the Golden Core chapter.

Even though Luo Chen had cultivated it to an unprecedented Great Completion realm, and after reaching the Sage stage could still use it to cultivate, in Luo Chen's eyes it still lacked something.

In the past, he could tolerate common main cultivation arts like the Evergreen Art [TERM?] and Burning Wood True Art [TERM?] because he had no choice.

But now, as a dignified Sage, if he were still so casual about his main cultivation art, that would be somewhat unreasonable.

Thus, he had always wanted to seek a better and more suitable fourth-tier main cultivation art.

If there was no more suitable one, then even if he had to create one himself, he would still make something better!

The large number of achievement points accumulated on the attribute panel were not just for show.

Luo Chen had entrusted Fu Chaosheng to collect fire-attribute cultivation arts for him, no matter what grade, and no matter whether they were complete or incomplete. He would refuse none.

From this point of view, Luo Chen had already prepared himself to draw on the strengths of a hundred schools and create his own cultivation art.

Black King traveled while Luo Chen studied the cultivation art. Stars and moon turned overhead, and time passed swiftly.

On this day, Luo Chen closed the beast-hide booklet.

There was some waning interest on his face.

The True Scripture of Flame Sovereignty was complete, yes, but it was a bit too conventional.

In terms of cultivation speed increase, even at Great Completion it would probably be about the same as the current Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture.

Especially since it lacked specific descriptions of resonance between heaven and man, and of merging spirit with heaven and earth. It merely mentioned that at this step, one should choose a safe and quiet spirit-vein land as much as possible, release the Nascent Soul, and sense the true meaning of laws between heaven and earth.

Of course, it was not completely without gains.

At the very least, the part mentioning opening all the apertures in the body and breathing in the spiritual qi of heaven and earth according to the circulation principles of the stars in the heavens had value for reference.

Luo Chen's full-body apertures had all been opened as early as the Initiate stage.

Over these years, he had merely treated them as another place for storing spiritual qi besides the qi sea.

If he could make use of them and integrate them into his main cultivation art, it would likely add some boost to his cultivation speed, however much.

"But the two fourth-tier fire-attribute spells recorded here, if I can master them, should greatly help my methods against enemies!"

Luo Chen thought back to the descriptions of those two fourth-tier fire-attribute spells and was rather moved.

One was called Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain, and the other was called Flame Dragon Descends.

The former was similar to the Azure Yang Great Handprint, an area attack with an extremely broad striking range. When fully unleashed in the hands of a Sage cultivator, it possessed the might to destroy mountains, sever seas, burn cities, and annihilate kingdoms.

The latter was a spell created in imitation of the thunder tribulation cultivators drew during tribulation, targeting only a single individual.

It did not sound as domineering as Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain, but think about the nature of thunder tribulation.

Impossible to evade, impossible to flee; one could only resist it head-on!

When Flame Dragon Descends was cast, it required not only mobilizing magic power, but also stirring the wandering fire-attribute spiritual qi between heaven and earth. Even on the soul side, one had to do everything possible to capture the enemy's movements, thereby achieving the effect of "tracking strikes."

"I will first try practicing them myself. If I can learn them naturally, that would be best. If I truly cannot, I still have plenty of achievement points to help me enter the door."

Luo Chen smiled faintly and found something to do for the next leg of the journey.

...

Mo Gan Mountain.

It faced the sea on three sides, with only one narrow path precariously connecting it to the small island behind.

On the shaded side of the mountain, an enormous dragon head slowly surfaced from beneath the water's shadow.

"Master, we have arrived."

The dragon's mouth opened slightly, and a white-robed Daoist walked out from within. His soft black hair draped behind him, held in a simple topknot by a single wooden hairpin.

After him, a tall woman with proud brows and eyes, and an honest-faced middle-aged man, walked out one after another.

The white-robed Daoist beckoned with his hand, and the enormous body beneath the shadows transformed into a miniature dragon the size of a loach and slipped into his sleeve.

He looked at Mo Gan Mountain before him and the familiar yet unfamiliar sea region behind, then nodded slightly.

"Correct. This is the Mo clan fishery from back then."

Heavenjade was somewhat surprised. "How did it become like this? I remember that at the time, the sea was filled with fish cages, and the spiritual qi was fairly abundant."

Luo Chen said wistfully, "After successively experiencing the battle between me and the Adept forces of Coldlight Island, and then enduring the aftermath of a Sage battle, the fact that the main mountain still remains is already fortunate. How can we demand more?"

After casually sighing, Luo Chen had already stepped onto land.

Heavenjade and Sang Jinghe followed close behind.

They did not go up Mo Gan Mountain, but instead followed that narrow path, walking step by step toward the small island ahead.

Halfway along, Heavenjade and Sang Jinghe received orders and flew elsewhere to search for targets.

Luo Chen alone boarded a small boat and headed toward the ruins of Jadecold City.

On the boat, the boatman at Qi Refinement warmly introduced Luo Chen to the general situation of the current Coldlight Archipelago.

Yes, Coldlight Archipelago, not Coldlight Island.

As the former largest island in the southeast, Coldlight Island had been shattered into pieces in that Sage battle.

The entire continental plate had broken apart, and countless spirit veins had collapsed.

Even after several decades, with the local natives constantly repairing it, it could not be restored. Instead, they gathered the spirit veins, built formations around the islands, and successively divided it into thirteen islands of varying size.

"That battle was tragic!"

"Jadecold City, which once overawed Coldlight Island, directly became ruins."

"The sect treasure land of Refining Edge Hall, where rogue cultivators could scarcely seek a single treasure, was destroyed. Hundred Crafts Mountain survived, but for the following several decades, the Hundred Crafts Mountain Master never appeared. That great Adept sect also split into several small families amid struggles for authority."

"The Qian clan [TERM?] is gone, completely gone. Their clan head died at the hands of that famous Devil Lord Azure Yang. When the Sage battle broke out, no one was there to sit in command, and their residence was directly destroyed. Later, many rogue cultivators swarmed in and robbed the rather wealthy Qian clan [TERM?] clean. Heh, if you ask where so many powerful rogue cultivators came from, who knows whether those people calling themselves rogue cultivators were tribulation cultivators disguised by other forces?"

"The Wu clan, on the other hand, is living quite well. They were originally rather miserable, with their clan Adepts either dead or injured. But later they brought back Xiang Baiyang, who had been healing outside, and the clan instantly had a leader. With him presiding, the Wu clan has already recovered its vitality and can be counted as the number-one power of the current Coldlight Archipelago."

"Still, seeing the former largest island in the southeast, once illustrious and bustling with cultivators, decline to its present state really does make one sigh."

Luo Chen listened with a smile as the boatman introduced the current situation of the Coldlight Archipelago, occasionally echoing a word or two.

"In my view, it is not without benefit. If Coldlight Island had not become like this, then during the righteous-demonic war, this ownerless land would surely have been enveloped by war. How could rogue cultivators like you live and work in peace?"

The boatman froze, then grinned. "Superior's insight is lofty."

Luo Chen, disguised as an Adept cultivator, smiled, then released his divine sense. Following the boat's movement, it swept bit by bit over the surrounding islands.

For some reason, he felt somewhat surprised.

He murmured softly:

"A formation?"

The boatman heard this murmur and explained, "In that great battle, Coldlight Island was severely damaged, and its spirit veins were difficult to restore. Later, someone sought the island-linking formation method from the Cang Hai Alliance and used it to gather the scattered spirit veins. Afterward, the remaining forces of the various families used this as a foundation to arrange their own formations, which created the current star-strewn layout of the archipelago. Because of this, each family is rather exclusionary. If one wants to go to their territory, one has to be carried in by small boats like ours, otherwise it can cause unnecessary conflict."

The Cang Hai Alliance's island-linking formation method?

Bright light bloomed in Luo Chen's spirit eyes as he swept them in every direction. There was indeed a familiar feeling.

Similar formations had also been arranged on the Swallow Islands, but because they lacked the main formation disk, they were missing three parts of the proper charm.

The Molan Court side was even more mature, and also contained the formation principles of the island-linking formation.

From this point of view, Luo Chen's previous inference had been entirely correct.

The Cang Hai Alliance's formation had now spread across the Northern Sea and was not limited to the Cang Hai Alliance's internal forces using it themselves.

However, what Luo Chen found strange was that this formation brought earth-vein resonance and the sense of thriving vitality. How could there be this oppressive feeling?

"Is it because I have advanced to the Sage stage and feel heaven and earth more deeply, so I am also more sensitive to the restrictions of formations?"

Luo Chen shook his head and did not investigate too deeply.

The small boat rocked and swayed as sea waves rippled.

After traveling all the way forward, it finally stopped before an enormous ancient tree.

The ancient tree was lush with branches and leaves, like an enormous umbrella spread open over the azure sea.

Its trunk, however, was entirely submerged beneath the sea surface.

"Superior, we have arrived. This is Jadecold City!"

Even though he had already learned the current situation of Jadecold City from Fu Chaosheng, seeing this scene still shocked Luo Chen.

That once vast and majestic city now had nothing left. Only an unwilling spirit tree stood in its original place.

And all the city ruins had been swallowed by the sea!

Luo Chen stood quietly at the bow of the boat, looking down beneath the sea surface as if staring into an abyss.

That great battle set off by Blood Nightmare Moluo, Fairy Jadecold, and Old Daoist Jiyuan had created the current tragic state of Coldlight Island. Who knew how many people had perished at the time?

Countless mortals, thousands upon tens of thousands of cultivators, perhaps something like that?

Perhaps his estimate was even somewhat conservative.

At this moment, a graceful figure flew back.

"Master, I have investigated clearly. That Xiang Baiyang is precisely the elder of the Xiang brothers from Refining Edge Hall back then."

Luo Chen smiled.

In the battle to surround and kill him back then, most of those people had died. Even the Hundred Crafts Mountain Master had died on Seven Stars Island.

Only a pair of twins who could join hands to match a late-stage Adept had existed, and the elder brother had broken through the grand formation and survived by luck.

He remembered that they were people of Refining Edge Hall.

Now it seemed that fellow also had ties to the Wu clan!

"Good. The Wu clan more or less participated in that matter back then, so let this account be settled together!"

Luo Chen casually tossed the boatman a mid-grade spirit stone, then stepped into the air and flew toward a medium-sized island not far from the Jadecold City ruins.

Heavenjade followed close behind.

The boatman seemed to realize something. Curiosity and eagerness appeared on his face, but in the end, he still gritted his teeth and controlled the boat to quickly leave this region.

...

Baiyang Island!

The island where the Wu clan now resided was called this, named after the clan's grand elder, Xiang Baiyang.

It sounded somewhat strange. How could the grand elder of the Wu clan have the surname Xiang?

The reasons behind it were rather complicated.

If Fu Chaosheng had not casually mentioned it, and Heavenjade had not deliberately investigated, Luo Chen would likely have found it hard to know the reason.

Xiang Baiyang himself had a slight bloodline connection to the Wu clan, only he and his younger brother had separated from the Wu clan when they were young and entered Refining Edge Hall.

Later, Refining Edge Hall was destroyed, his younger brother also died, and the leaderless Wu clan invited him back through marriage alliance. After several decades, not only had the Wu clan become the number-one family of the Coldlight Archipelago, even Xiang Baiyang himself had cultivated to the seventh level of the Golden Core Stage.

"A late-stage Adept great cultivator?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly, standing in the air and looking at the island below, which spanned several hundred li.

Mortals flourished upon it, and cultivators came and went like clouds. It truly had some signs of revival.

And within his perception, Xiang Baiyang's aura shone like a star among fireflies.

He was indeed late-stage Adept.

And slightly weaker than such a star were four or five more lights, likely Adept cultivators as well.

Sang Jinghe asked curiously, "Sage, what do we do next?"

Heavenjade said hatefully, "Why not kill them all!"

That sentence, "kill them all," made Sang Jinghe's scalp go numb.

He had never imagined that Fairy Heavenjade, who had spent every day with them and seemed gentle and kind, would be so ruthless.

At least several tens of thousands of people lived on this island!

What he did not know was that in that encirclement against Luo Chen back then, Heavenjade had also been heavily injured. If Luo Chen had not done everything possible to heal her afterward, Heavenjade's life would probably have been ruined.

Especially since demon beasts were unruly by nature. Even after transforming, their temperaments remained incomparably cruel.

Luo Chen waved a hand. "There is no need to go that far. Though they offended me, it was only the family higher-ups who committed sin. This time, I have come for revenge, so only the chief culprits will be executed!"

As his words fell, he no longer concealed his aura.

A heaven-surging pressure instantly enveloped the entire Baiyang Island.

At this moment, every cultivator on the island was shaken.

Seven streaks of escaping light flew out from the island with difficulty.

Seeing Luo Chen's clearly hostile appearance, they subconsciously wanted to speak.

But Luo Chen wasted no words. He flicked a finger, and a spark rose from his fingertip.

In the blink of an eye, it transformed into a round blazing sun.

Then the blazing sun abruptly divided, and seven great suns blasted toward the seven people.

"Senior, you..."

Before the cry of alarm could even spread, the seven immediately screamed in misery, unable to put up even resistance.

Ordinary Adept cultivators, before a Sage, where would they have any power to resist?

The suppression of the domain alone was enough to make them stretch out their necks for slaughter.

After taking revenge, Luo Chen had no lingering attachment. Ignoring the stunned tens of thousands below, he turned to leave.

But before his departing step could fall, Luo Chen stopped.

His gaze landed on one of the fireballs.

That person was actually not dead yet!

Inside, blood light enveloped him. Amid continuous screams, he was still resisting desperately.

"He does have some ability. Then... no!"

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly, and his right hand clawed through the air.

However, the fireball exploded apart, and a figure flew out from within. His appearance was wretched beyond measure, but his aura was continuously rising, forcibly scattering this claw.

That dense blood light and seemingly real yet false aura both stirred up an unforgettable scene in Luo Chen's memories.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's expression thoroughly darkened.

"Blood God Child!"

*(End of Chapter)*
