# Chapter 863: Luo Chen's True Body, Hundred-Leg's Death

Hundred-Leg Great Demon Emperor—the weakest among the nine great commanders, yet the one with the most fearsome reputation!

It sounded contradictory. If he was the weakest of the nine demons, why did he have the fiercest reputation?

But for Luo Chen, this question was easy to understand.

Reputation was never built on the flattery of one or two people, but spread by a vast multitude.

The reason Hundred-Leg Great Demon Emperor's fearsome name resounded throughout the cultivator community was simply that this was a powerful being who completely disregarded decorum.

In several battles, he struck without any restraint, never observing the rules of general against general, king against king.

He deliberately avoided the great human cultivators, often targeting those weaker early and mid-stage Nascent Soul cultivators. He didn't even begrudge using his mana to kill Golden Core cultivators.

It was precisely because of this brutal style that he had earned such a monstrous reputation among countless low-level cultivators.

During those nine days and nine nights of attack and defense at Lingtian Pass, seven Nascent Soul cultivators had died.

Three of them had fallen to his hand.

In particular, the two Nascent Soul cultivators from the Nether Origin Sect were casually killed by him, which once enraged Sage Hanyang of the Nether Origin Sect so much that he pursued Hundred-Leg for an entire day and night.

And this demon's complete lack of decorum was not only due to his personal style but also related to his combat methods.

With just one glance, Luo Chen saw the many magical treasures on those hundred arms and hundred hands!

These were no demon-refined treasures.

They were orthodox magical treasures refined by human artificers.

And among them, several were actually true artifacts!

Clearly, this demon had slaughtered human cultivators on a grand scale, seizing their magical treasures to match his many arms.

Clang, clatter, crackle.

Amid the collision of countless magical treasures, the enormous centipede slowly rose upright, its divine sense firmly locking onto the unmoving Luo Chen.

"Scared?"

"Don't worry! After I capture you, that blood sword in your hand will also be one of my spoils of war!"

With just one look, Hundred-Leg saw the power of the sword in Luo Chen's hand, and a deep greed appeared in his eyes.

Faced with this provocation, the corners of Luo Chen's mouth twitched as he slowly raised his blood sword.

"You want it? Then come and take it!"

The next moment, Hundred-Leg charged at Luo Chen with earth-shaking might.

Mana surged, treasure light flared.

In an instant, magical treasures of every color enveloped Luo Chen.

"Break!"

Luo Chen shouted softly, and Yuantu Sword slashed out a dazzling sword light, scattering the surrounding treasures in all directions.

Soaring into the sky, Luo Chen frowned again.

Because of the turbulent heaven and earth origin qi, he couldn't control the free-floating spiritual energy in the void, and even the power he could draw from Yuantu Sword was greatly diminished.

He glanced at the pursuing Hundred-Leg, and while executing Moon Step to narrowly dodge attacks from various magical treasures, he pondered his countermeasures.

In such an environment, ordinary cultivator techniques would likely have their power greatly reduced.

He could only rely on his own mana!

But without external spiritual energy to replenish, whether it was high-level spells or powerful true artifacts, the mana consumption was terrifyingly high.

This Hundred-Leg commander had abandoned demon arts from the start, directly manifesting his true body to fight. Clearly, he too was aware of this.

If that was the case...

Luo Chen hesitated no longer. He pressed his thumb against his chest.

"Transform!"

*Boom!*

Rolling source power flowed through his entire body, and his form began to expand and grow wildly.

Not only that, but Mystic Dust Armor responded to the movement, tightly wrapping his entire body, leaving only a pair of brilliant eyes gleaming with faint golden light.

In the blink of an eye, a nine-zhang giant appeared in the world.

Then Luo Chen charged forward, gripping the blood sword—now magnified more than ten times by mana—and stabbed fiercely at Hundred-Leg.

Facing the charging Luo Chen, Hundred-Leg was unimpressed.

Even if his size had increased somewhat, he was still insignificant in Hundred-Leg's eyes.

"Just an ant after all!"

A flying sword came abruptly, stabbing straight at the back of Luo Chen's head.

Luo Chen's form suddenly flickered, nimbly flying behind Hundred-Leg, and the flying sword missed.

From left and right, a hammer and a stone tablet attacked simultaneously, but under a horizontal sword slash, the two top-grade magical treasures were cut in two.

With no more obstacles, Yuantu Sword stabbed straight at Hundred-Leg's back.

*Clang!*

A tortoise-shell-like shield suddenly appeared.

Luo Chen's expression hardened, and with immense force, he pressed his sword forward, stabbing hard.

"Break!"

*Sss...*

Like a heavy hammer striking, the tortoise shell shattered into pieces.

But just as Luo Chen wanted to press further, four more giant arms from above, below, left, and right swung magical treasures at him.

Luo Chen dared not take the risk. He turned back and struck out with a palm, using powerful mana to clear a path.

He floated lightly away like a colorful butterfly, but the next moment, his form suddenly shot upward, straight into the sky. Both hands gripping the blood sword, he slashed downward!

Hundred-Leg sneered, raising a sword and a saber with his left and right arms above his head.

*Crack!*

In an instant of contact, the sword and saber turned to powder.

But suddenly, a fist wrapped in silver-white light shot out from Hundred-Leg's chest.

Caught off guard, Luo Chen, who was fully focused on his downward slash, took the blow head-on.

*Bang!*

His chest visibly caved inward.

"You're too green!" Hundred-Leg laughed wildly, sending more magical treasures flying at Luo Chen.

Without a word, Luo Chen let source power flow through his chest, restoring it to its original state, then used Moon Step to dodge repeatedly in the air.

Seizing an opportunity, he charged back within a hundred zhang of Hundred-Leg.

His pair of golden pupils spun unceasingly, trying to see through his opponent's weakness.

But every time he used his agile speed and the sharpness of Yuantu Sword to gain a local breakthrough, more magical treasures would strike at him.

His nine-zhang body, which usually seemed so imposing, now appeared utterly insignificant before Hundred-Leg Great Demon Emperor's seven-to-eight-hundred-zhang form.

When Hundred-Leg swung his hundred arms, slapping at Luo Chen was like swatting a mosquito.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's eyes lit up as he found a gap in the shell.

"Right there!"

When another magical treasure in the shape of a wolf-tooth club came smashing down, Luo Chen no longer dodged. He took the blow squarely on his back.

Using the force, he surged forward.

With a sonic boom in the void, Luo Chen kicked out with one foot.

Thunder Kick!

In a flash of lightning, Luo Chen's foot struck that gap in the shell.

Source power exploded, and the terrifying force kicked Hundred-Leg stumbling backward.

Just as a look of surprise appeared on Luo Chen's face, he suddenly sensed something wrong.

His right foot, which he was trying to withdraw, seemed to have sunk into a quagmire.

At the same time, four arms around him flicked their fingers, sending out four golden ropes that bound Luo Chen's hands and feet in an instant.

Luo Chen gritted his teeth and shook, releasing source power to burst the golden ropes, but his entire body was already exposed with a huge opening.

In his line of sight, a sharp black awl was falling from above.

Without hesitation, Luo Chen thrust Yuantu Sword upward.

*Ding!*

The sword tip and the awl met, point to point!

But clearly, Hundred-Leg had lured the enemy in deep and set this trap. How could it only have one or two moves?

In an instant, the black awl's ink-like color spread, and four more awls materialized around it, suddenly falling.

*Puff!*

*Puff!*

*Puff!*

*Puff!*

Four awls directly sank into Luo Chen's nine-zhang body, and even Mystic Dust Armor couldn't stop them.

Under the influence, the black awl that had been locked in stalemate with Yuantu Sword also began to press down slowly.

Luo Chen tried to pour in more mana, but found that the surging mana within his body had become difficult to mobilize.

"It's those four awls—they've sealed my mana!"

Luo Chen's expression turned fierce. His mana was suppressed, but he had more than just mana.

Source power poured out like a torrent, and Yuantu Sword directly flipped the black awl away. Not only that, but a great seal that looked like a mountain, which hadn't even fallen yet, was sliced in two by Yuantu Sword.

Taking this opportunity, Luo Chen slapped his palm against Hundred-Leg's gaudy shell and used the force to retreat.

*Thump! Thump! Thump!*

His heavy body left huge footprints on the ground.

Luo Chen half-knelt, leaning on his sword, his expression dark and gloomy.

He had never imagined that in his own specialty—body refinement—he would be ambushed like this.

This Hundred-Leg hadn't abandoned body refinement like ordinary demons. He, like Luo Chen, cultivated both mana and body!

Even if he didn't walk the path of blood qi, his physique was definitely at the fourth rank!

And now, his mana was temporarily sealed by the four awls, putting him at a disadvantage.

Among the broken walls and ruins, Hundred-Leg watched this scene with a ferocious expression, his voice carrying a wild, arrogant laugh.

"Hahaha, how does it feel, this Five-Element Town Mana Awl from Tianfan City?"

Hundred-Leg paid no attention to the large hole Luo Chen had kicked in his abdomen. He moved his steps, walking toward Luo Chen.

"Now that you can't use your mana, and your physique is inferior to mine, how can you still resist?"

"Come back with me!"

"Don't worry, I won't hand you over to Aokuang. After all, your skill in alchemy and pill-making is famous even among our demon race."

Luo Chen lowered his head, saying nothing.

Hundred-Leg was unconcerned. He knew that human cultivators were all like this. Making them take the initiative to beg for mercy required some methods.

He would just apply a little more pressure.

At worst, he would break this man's limbs and tie him up with Binding Gold Ropes.

But this man's combat techniques weren't very refined either. How had Qixi lost to him?

Was it because of that blood sword?

Hundred-Leg could tell that the blood sword was extremely extraordinary. This man probably hadn't even activated half of its power.

If he obtained it and fully activated its power, his strength would surely soar by leaps and bounds.

Even if Aokuang were present, he would have some confidence to deal with him!

"Applying pressure bit by bit is best, lest this man's temper be too fierce and he self-destructs his treasure. Then I'll lose more than I gain."

Thinking this in his heart, Hundred-Leg had already approached within a few li of Luo Chen.

Suddenly, his expression changed slightly, and he subconsciously looked around.

But after careful observation, a look of hesitation appeared on his face.

"There's nothing!"

"Why did I just feel a sense of being watched by the Great Desolation?"

"No!"

Hundred-Leg's expression turned stern, and his gaze fell on the man below.

Strands of blood-colored mist, like steam, burst out from the man's pores, completely enveloping his nine-zhang body.

And this mist grew thicker and larger, even covering part of the thousand-li Tai Lake beside them.

"This guy is not right!"

Without hesitation, Hundred-Leg raised one arm, and a small mountain flew out from it, spinning.

The small mountain grew with the wind, instantly turning into a towering peak.

He waved his hand, and the mountain came crashing down.

*Boom!*

With a deafening sound, under Hundred-Leg's horrified gaze, the mountain was directly sent flying.

Countless blood-colored mists suddenly retracted, pouring into the giant's body.

When the blood mist dissipated, a giant five hundred zhang tall, completely shrouded in black armor, slowly stood up on the earth.

The surrounding low peaks only reached his waist.

His golden eyes radiated endless brutality.

He stared fixedly at Hundred-Leg, then bent his legs.

Rock-like muscles seemed compressed to the extreme.

Large tendons flowed beneath the armor, like bowstrings pulled taut.

The next moment!

*Bang!*

The giant shot out like a cannonball, charging straight at Hundred-Leg.

Hundred-Leg's expression turned fierce, and his hands, wrapped in silver-white gloves, struck out fiercely.

*Smack! Smack!*

The giant's palms gripped those silver-white arms, and then a terrifying force came like a tidal wave.

Hundred-Leg's eyes were filled with terror. He found that in terms of pure strength, he was no match for his opponent.

His massive true body was now retreating uncontrollably.

*Rumble!*

*Rumble!*

One by one, the broken palaces collapsed into rubble.

Vast clouds of dust blotted out the sky and covered the sun.

*Plop!*

Spray flew up.

The two enormous bodies fell into Tai Lake.

Hundred-Leg looked down. His two arms had been forcibly pressed into his own chest.

Broken bones and severed tendons brought unbearable pain.

Hundred-Leg roared in fury.

"Luo Chen!!!"

Luo Chen roared back.

"Hundred-Leg!!!"

*Boom!*

Rolling waves surged back to the sky.

Ten thousand hectares of lake water exploded into rain.

...

Vast Tai Lake stretched for a thousand li.

The lake's name came from the Nascent Soul sect Taihu Palace, but its own foundation was a complete fourth-rank water vein.

Now, with two powerhouses fighting fiercely in the lake, the water vein was in turmoil, and a large amount of spiritual energy was leaking out.

Between heaven and earth, mist and rain swirled.

In normal times, it would have been a beautiful, unparalleled wonder.

But at this moment, it was merely a fragile backdrop to the simplest, most direct hand-to-hand combat.

In the great lake.

Luo Chen and Hundred-Leg engaged in a battle with no tricks.

No spells surging, no flying through the sky or escaping underground. There was only fist and foot.

The giant swung his palm, struck both ears, followed by an iron elbow to the mountain, then clasped his hands together, gathered source power into a blood-red light ball, and blasted out with a palm.

Hundred-Leg stepped on the waves, stumbling backward.

One magical treasure after another, under his control, smashed into Luo Chen.

Luo Chen didn't even look at these attacks, letting them land on him.

Axes and sabers struck, but the black armor remained unmoved.

Flying swords and spears stabbed, but the knotted muscles rebounded them.

Hundred-Leg watched this scene in shock, unable to believe it in his heart.

Their demon race had a natural physical advantage in body refinement.

But how could a human achieve such a level?

Even when he summoned a true artifact flying sword obtained from the Nether Origin Sect, which pierced through the black armor's defense, it still couldn't advance further, caught between hard bones and terrifying muscles.

Hundred-Leg's heart turned ruthless. With one hand holding the sword, he waved at the distance with the other.

"Mountain, come!"

The giant mountain that Luo Chen had sent flying earlier came rumbling back, crashing toward Luo Chen.

Faced with this, Luo Chen grinned fiercely, opened his mouth, and spat.

Primordial Unity Cauldron flew out, spinning, growing to a hundred zhang in size.

Usually, the hundred-zhang Primordial Unity Cauldron seemed like a small mountain, but now in Luo Chen's hands, it was so light and nimble.

He lifted the giant cauldron and swung it.

*Bang!*

A heavy sound rang out, and the giant mountain flew back again.

A towering sword light slashed out from Hundred-Leg's hand.

Luo Chen swung Yuantu Sword, using source power instead of mana, and slashed out a vast sword light.

*Clang!*

At this point, both sides had abandoned all other methods.

Luo Chen's mana was sealed, his heart willing but his strength insufficient. He relied purely on his powerful physical body and Mystic Dust Armor to endure.

As for Hundred-Leg, those ordinary magical treasures were useless against Luo Chen, only wasting mana and divine sense.

Only!

Each held a sword, slashing at each other.

One cauldron, one mountain, summoned and smashed.

In this constant collision, the tremendous recoil force made their internal organs creak, and even caused them to spit blood.

Comparatively, Luo Chen seemed to be in better shape. His five organs had been nourished for many years by the thousand-year Wumiao Immortal Brew, making them extraordinary.

But even so, in the repeated collisions, it turned into a pure exchange of injuries.

Mist and rain shrouded the sky, sun and moon lost their light.

Unbeknownst to them, this fierce battle had already reached the dead of night.

After another collision.

*Boom!*

Hundred-Leg looked in disbelief at the mountain rocks falling like rain.

His greatly valued mountain true artifact had been smashed to pieces by the opponent's giant cauldron.

"You ruined my treasure—you deserve to die!"

Both hands gripping the sword hilt, the true artifact flying sword from the Nether Origin Sect, driven by mana, grew wildly in size, then slashed down.

Luo Chen tossed Primordial Unity Cauldron high into the air, then swung Yuantu Sword again.

*Boom!*

Another inconclusive clash.

But in this collision, something that left Hundred-Leg dumbfounded happened again.

He slowly raised his hand and looked at the sword in his hand.

It was broken!

A true artifact personally refined by a Nascent Soul cultivator from a Holy Land of Transformation had actually broken!

Luo Chen watched coldly, knowing that his temporary change in combat style had been effective.

Against Hundred-Leg's countless arms and numerous magical treasures, so-called agility was actually a weakness.

Only by meeting force with force could he confront the opponent's weakness head-on!

Yes, this Hundred-Leg Great Demon Emperor wielded many magical treasures, with attack and defense, pulling and trapping, sneak attacks and ambushes. He seemed proficient in everything, without any flaws.

But in reality, he was proficient in everything but master of none.

His soul was not outstanding, and his magical treasures were of varying quality.

Especially those treasures—none of them were personally refined and nurtured by Hundred-Leg, but obtained through trickery and plunder.

As long as he was forced into a direct confrontation, these small flaws would keep magnifying!

As for his five-hundred-zhang giant form, that was actually his true body!

The usual nine-zhang form was merely the optimal size for dealing with human cultivators.

As long as he was willing to use source power, he could transform into a hundred-zhang giant.

"This battle has dragged on too long."

"Let's end it!"

Luo Chen formed a sword finger with one hand, raised the blood sword with the other, and strode toward Hundred-Leg.

The murky lake water stirred with his movement.

Hundred-Leg's expression changed, and he actually turned to flee!

"Last time I let Qixi escape. How could I possibly let a tiger return to the mountain this time!"

Luo Chen slashed down with his sword.

Hundred-Leg's body was cut in two.

At that moment, a golden light shot out from within his body, tearing through the air.

*Sss!*

In an instant, it struck Luo Chen's brow.

The massive giant, holding the enormous blood sword, stood frozen in Tai Lake.

Clear moonlight spilled over him, as if covering him with a layer of gauze.

From the centipede cut in two, a figure climbed out with difficulty.

He gasped for breath, looked at Luo Chen, and showed a look of lingering fear.

This guy was no cultivator at all!

He was a fucking desolate beast!

And because he had mastered magical treasures, he was even more troublesome than those great desolate beasts in the wilderness!

If he hadn't kept a hidden trick up his sleeve, he would have really fallen here tonight.

"Fortunately, I nurtured a Soul-Weaving Needle when I transformed. Although I didn't master the method and its power is average, with a surprise attack, ordinary people still can't block it. Especially since his mana was sealed by me, he could only sit and wait for death."

Hundred-Leg slowly stood up, ready to gather his broken body.

But with a sidelong glance, his entire body froze.

The giant, standing motionless, had the sword finger of his left hand slowly rising.

A surging wave of mana fluctuations spread out, no longer able to be concealed.

"How is it possible..."

Hundred-Leg swallowed his saliva, no longer caring about his broken body, and frantically rushed toward the sky.

At that moment!

From the murky Tai Lake, a giant python broke through the surface, opened its fanged mouth, and let out a sharp hiss at Hundred-Leg.

Struck by this, Hundred-Leg's soul paused.

Although he broke free from the influence in an instant, everything was already too late.

A finger, lightly pressed out, landed on his body from across the void.

Without his true body to shield him, how could this mere Nascent Soul withstand the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger!

In an instant, the originally tender centipede Nascent Soul became wrinkled and shriveled.

The mana that condensed the Nascent Soul also began to uncontrollably dissipate and disappear.

Hundred-Leg stared blankly at this scene, still unwilling to believe his end.

"Why wasn't he affected by the Soul-Weaving Needle? It clearly hit his brow and entered his sea of consciousness?"

"When did his mana unseal? How can he use divine techniques?"

"Why is there a helper for him in Tai Lake? That shadow seems to be the Netherworld Charm Python, but that thing died long ago?"

Countless questions eventually condensed into one thought.

"I shouldn't have stopped."

*Pfft!*

A generation of great demon emperor, his Nascent Soul collapsed like a bubble, and his vast mana dissipated naturally like withered vegetation, not even causing a spiritual energy storm.

In the still-churning Tai Lake, only that giant stood under the bright moon.

The giant's rough, rock-like fingers slowly loosened. He turned his neck and looked down at the rolling, surging lake water.

A giant python's shadow swam toward him, but when it was about to get close, it timidly stopped.

"Master?"

The giant grinned.

(The chapter ends)
