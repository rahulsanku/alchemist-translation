# Chapter 841: Lingtian Breached, Heavy Casualties

When they came, the pursuit had been swift and urgent.

On the return journey, however, there was no rush at all.

For this war, ever since the Nether Origin Sect cultivators had stood by with folded arms, Luo Chen had not a single thought left.

Those truly capable of steering the course of this war were not the countless self-sacrificing cultivators of Lingtian Pass, but the Ascendant sacred ground—the Nether Origin Sect.

Yet even they harbored ill intentions, just as Luo Chen had predicted years ago, watching coldly as the various Sage-level factions were destroyed one after another, ruined one after another.

Even if this war were ultimately won, what meaning would it hold?

In the end, it would only be making a wedding dress for the Nether Origin Sect!

"One must cherish life, cherish oneself, cherish the lives of all those who follow me."

"At the very least, do not offer up precious life to such a war."

"Not worth it!"

Seated cross-legged on Black King's back, Luo Chen's expression was calm, his heart as still as water.

...

A few days later, Luo Chen returned to Hundred Li Green River.

"Master, won't you take me along?"

"No need."

"At a critical moment, little Black can still help you a bit."

"Heh, as long as no Grand Cultivator makes a move against me, ordinary enemies are still beneath my notice. But if a Grand Cultivator does act, you wouldn't be able to help me much either. Better to stay and guard the home well."

Black King let out an "oh" and listlessly burrowed deep into the earth's veins.

Li Yingzhang stood to one side, with Tianxuan and Min Longyu behind him.

Seeing Luo Chen return safe and sound, they all couldn't help but reveal joyful smiles.

Luo Chen smiled gently and inquired about recent events, but the answer caught him somewhat off guard.

"Lingtian Pass has been breached!"

Even though he had long guessed that Lingtian Pass would be attacked, Luo Chen still hadn't expected that the pass—with its high walls and deep moats, protected by a fifth-rank formation—would be broken in the short time since they had left.

Li Yingzhang explained with reverence: "Three days after you, Supreme Elder, left, the demon beast army launched a total assault on Lingtian Pass. After another three days of fierce fighting, the five Great Commanders appeared simultaneously and forcefully attacked the Black Tortoise Four Directions Grand Formation. Tian Duzi presided over the formation to resist them, and it was barely holding in a stalemate. But at the critical moment, Commander Huang Man invoked the Ancient Demon Emperor's Heaven-Severing technique and forcibly broke it. Tian Duzi suffered severe backlash from the formation, gravely wounded. And without the formation's protection, thirty Demon Emperors led tens of millions of demon beasts charging straight in, slaughtering into Lingtian Pass..."

As he listened to that scene, Luo Chen's heart instantly tightened.

"What happened afterward?"

"Fortunately, reinforcements arrived in time. The teleportation formation within Lingtian Pass seemed to have been prepared in advance. When the Demon Emperors charged in, the Nether Origin Sect teleported over a dozen Sage-level cultivators. Cooperating with the strong within the city, they barely managed to drive the Demon Emperors out."

Luo Chen frowned. "How could they drive them out so easily? With the five Great Commanders present, relying on ordinary Sages alone?"

Li Yingzhang shook his head. "The Nether Origin Sect also sent two Grand Cultivators to assist. Moreover, the five Great Commanders didn't linger in battle. After breaking the formation, it seemed some accident occurred. Unable to secure a quick victory, they voluntarily withdrew."

A strange look crossed Luo Chen's face. He calculated on his fingers—the time of the five Great Commanders' withdrawal was almost exactly when the great battle at Tian Du Sect had ended.

Had those Commanders gone to rendezvous with Jinxiu Xian and the others who were being pursued?

"Although the Demon Emperors retreated, the casualties inflicted on the cultivators defending Lingtian Pass in that battle were enormous—nearly half! On our Luo Tian Sect's side alone, a hundred died in battle. Among the Adept-level elders, Fu Jiusheng and Chu Kui unfortunately..."

Luo Chen listened silently to all of this, his calm face revealing no emotion.

After a long while, he asked faintly, "Where did this intelligence come from?"

Li Yingzhang's expression was heavy. "The Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance voluntarily informed us."

Luo Chen let out a cold laugh. Through the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion, the Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance had indeed managed to infiltrate, even able to obtain such core intelligence.

He hesitated no longer. After giving a few instructions, he turned and flew toward Lingtian Pass.

Behind him, Li Yingzhang watched his retreating figure. When it disappeared from sight, his steady face finally revealed a trace of helplessness.

In one battle, the Luo Tian Sect—which had never had much foundation—had lost another third of its strength.

As the one in charge, his heart ached the most.

...

At daybreak, the first light of dawn gradually emerged.

A figure descended outside the western city gate.

Gazing at the heavily cracked city walls and the blood-dyed earth, Luo Chen said nothing and flew straight inside.

There was no familiar suppression force from a no-fly formation—this meant that the no-fly grand formation within Lingtian Pass had also been destroyed in that battle.

Coupled with the broken Black Tortoise Four Directions Grand Formation, this Lingtian Pass now had no restrictions whatsoever against Demon Emperor-level experts!

He flew very slowly, letting the stench of blood in the air drift into his nose, while the buzzing clamor of the city filled his ears.

A beam of light shot up, blocking his path.

But upon seeing his true appearance, the person immediately revealed a respectful expression.

"Senior Alchemy Sect!"

"Mm."

Luo Chen nodded, looking at this unknown Adept-level cultivator with a gentle expression.

"Do you know where my Luo Tian Sect cultivators are currently stationed?"

The Adept answered without hesitation. "They should be within Danluo Mountain."

"Thank you."

Luo Chen casually handed over a bottle of pills. Ignoring the other's repeated thanks, he drifted lightly into the city.

Everywhere he passed, devastation met his eyes.

Countless buildings had collapsed. Amid the broken walls and ruined foundations lay the corpses of demon beasts and cultivators, clearly not yet gathered for burial.

This was still the relatively safest western district.

It was hard to imagine the other three areas, especially the eastern district, which had borne the greatest pressure of attack and defense.

Luo Chen dared not dwell on it. He headed straight for Danluo Mountain.

Soon, he saw the shadowy figures of people on Danluo Mountain.

He actively revealed his form, dispersing his mana fluctuations.

Someone spotted him immediately and shouted loudly:

"The Supreme Elder has returned!"

Instantly, everyone on the mountain stopped what they were doing and flew toward him in unison.

The moment they saw Luo Chen, the confusion in each person's eyes vanished, as if they had found their backbone.

Luo Chen's gaze swept over everyone. Some were listless, some bore injuries, and not a few were missing limbs. Even Yang Ping and the other Adept-level cultivators were visibly in poor condition.

A sparse hundred or so people—this was the remaining strength of the Luo Tian Sect.

"All of you, disperse. Rest well and don't leave any hidden problems."

Luo Chen offered a word of comfort without going through any formalities.

After the low-level disciples dispersed, Zeng Yilong, Jueqing the Fairy, and the others followed behind Luo Chen into Danluo Mountain.

At the same time, they told him the details of the previous battle.

"Three days after you, Uncle-Master, left, the demon beast army launched a total assault, surrounding Lingtian Pass completely."

"When the Black Tortoise Four Directions Formation was broken, a large number of low-level demon beasts charged in."

"The Death-Defying Army we had previously formed lost its defensive stronghold and scattered like loose sand, immediately routed."

"Under Master's command, we retreated to Danluo Mountain to defend..."

Luo Chen walked unsteadily up Danluo Mountain, his divine sense spreading out. The condition of the mountain's formation also entered his mind.

The formation's foundation had been shaken, and even the flow of earth-vein qi showed signs of obstruction.

Fortunately, the main vein had not dispersed. With some repair and guidance, this mountain could still be considered a fine place for cultivation.

"I've already contacted the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion. With Heavenly Origin Merchant Alliance experts protecting it, there should be no major problems," Zeng Yilong said.

Luo Chen's footsteps stopped outside a damaged hall. "Where is your master?"

Zeng Yilong's master was naturally Wang Yuan.

At this question, Zeng Yilong's eyes showed reverence.

"Master is resting inside the hall. At that time, Danluo Mountain attracted a fourth-rank Demon Emperor. It was Master who personally fought a great battle against it, barely holding out until the Nether Origin Sect's reinforcements arrived."

These words were spoken lightly, but in reality, the danger had been extreme.

From the moment Luo Chen saw Wang Yuan, he understood everything.

"The fire seed I gave you—you've already used it?"

Wang Yuan nodded. "In that situation, only by borrowing that fire seed of yours could I fight a Demon Emperor with full force. I was somewhat exhausted afterward, but after a few days of rest, I've gradually recovered my vitality."

"Let me examine you anyway!"

Wang Yuan smiled and did not refuse Luo Chen's goodwill.

As one medical spell after another was cast, combined with various spiritual medicines, Wang Yuan's complexion visibly returned to normal.

Clearly, his condition was not as good as he claimed.

During this time, the cultivators from the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion, led by Sima Huiniang and Gu Caiyi, also hurried to Danluo Mountain.

While treating Wang Yuan, Luo Chen handed over the various wound medicines he had stockpiled over the years to the two women, telling them to use them to treat the sect's disciples.

It seemed that everything was slowly recovering.

But Luo Chen knew that Lingtian Pass had been breached once. Having tasted the sweetness, the demon beasts would surely come again.

And since the Nether Origin Sect had already made a move, it meant they were officially entering the war. The subsequent battles would be even more intense.

To this, he remained unmoved.

Three days later, Luo Chen withdrew his hands.

Looking at Wang Yuan, who slowly opened his eyes, Luo Chen instructed:

"Your physique is very strong, so you could barely withstand the power of my fire seed. But that was only once. I cannot give you a second fire seed. If you activate it again, there will be only death."

Wang Yuan seemed unconcerned. "No matter. I couldn't rely on external objects forever anyway. In the end, I must depend on myself."

Luo Chen nodded. After thinking for a moment, he took a miniature mountain from his storage ring.

"This is?" Wang Yuan asked curiously.

Luo Chen explained, "This treasure is called Baizao Mountain. It was the inherited treasure of a overseas Adept-level sect. Although it's a magic treasure, it's extremely heavy. I saw that your weapon was destroyed in the last battle, so I'll give you this mountain."

Speaking thus, he showed some regret.

"Actually, after I refined Motian Cliff back then, I collected the remaining materials of that true artifact. I originally planned to merge it into Baizao Mountain before giving it to you, but I've never had a suitable opportunity over the years, and I haven't mastered the relevant fusion method."

Wang Yuan wasn't particularly bothered. He took Baizao Mountain, weighed it in his hand, and revealed a satisfied expression.

A body cultivator's strength came from astral qi, which was completely different from a cultivator's mana.

Therefore, the weapons they needed had to be compatible with astral qi.

Luo Chen had very few body cultivator weapons on hand—only a Ten-Thousand-Jun Halberd, which he had already given to Zeng Yilong.

When he had first returned to the Eastern Wilderness, he had indeed planned to merge the Motian Cliff materials into Baizao Mountain before giving it to Wang Yuan. But he had never been able to figure out how to modify it for astral qi activation.

For now, he could only give Baizao Mountain to Wang Yuan as is.

At least this thing was heavy enough!

Suddenly.

"What are your plans next?"

Facing Wang Yuan's question, Luo Chen raised his eyelids. "Why do you ask?"

Wang Yuan said naturally, "Lingtian Pass was breached this time. Even if the formation is repaired later, it will no longer be an absolutely safe place. The Nether Origin Sect has also begun joining this war. These past few days, low-level Nether Origin Sect cultivators have been arriving continuously, clearly preparing to launch a counteroffensive. Under such a great tide, if you have no plans at all, then the Luo Tian Sect will inevitably be ground down to not a single soldier left. Don't tell me you'll be indifferent to all this?"

Luo Chen fell silent.

After a long time, he revealed a bitter smile.

"You also spoke of the great tide. Under such a surging tide, without becoming an Ascendant, one is ultimately just a chess piece. What I can do is far too insignificant."

"Ascendant..."

Wang Yuan sighed. That was far too distant.

"Let's take it one step at a time, one look at a time!"

Luo Chen offered a word of comfort, then stood up and walked straight out of the cave dwelling.

As if by appointment, a messenger arrived—a cultivator from the Nether Origin Sect.

"Senior Alchemy Sect, the City Lord's Mansion invites you!"

Luo Chen was not at all surprised by this. He had not concealed his movements since returning these past few days. The strong within the city were certainly aware of this news.

Then let's go pay a visit!

He also wanted to know the specific circumstances of the various battlefields, and what exactly the Nether Origin Sacred Ground planned next!

...

The familiar City Lord's Mansion was still majestic and imposing.

The washed white jade plaza was clean and new, as if nothing had happened.

But Luo Chen, who arrived first, gradually learned just how brutal the previous great battle had been as he met the later arrivals one after another.

"Xiao Shiniang is dead! Fireworks Ancient Isle has vanished like smoke."

"Mu the Sage died in battle, his very body torn into pieces by two Demon Emperors. I heard that the Mu residence in his Mu family was destroyed by a surprise attack from Demon Emperor forces before he fell. So during the great battle at Lingtian Pass, his mind was unsettled, leading to such a miserable end."

"Heh, speaking of which, if my Misty Void Sect's disciples hadn't been lost long ago, leaving me a solitary man who only needed to protect himself, I'm afraid you wouldn't be seeing me today either, Alchemy Sect."

Ling Fengzi's tone carried laughter, but his expression was desolate—where was the joy?

Luo Chen asked, "What about the others?"

Ling Fengzi told Luo Chen what he had learned.

"Xianhe the Daoist is fine. The Crane Sage within his sect emerged from seclusion and fought alongside him. Their strength is formidable."

"Elder Fu's situation is not good. He was severely wounded in the Lingtian Pass battle. Two days ago, Yelan the Sage hurried to Lingtian Pass to take over the Spirit Talisman Sect's disciples... Ah, isn't that him?"

As he spoke, a Sage-level cultivator entered the hall.

After a sweep of his gaze, it stopped at Luo Chen's side. Then the middle-aged man with a steady temperament walked toward them.

"I am Yelan. Greetings to Alchemy Sect, greetings to Ling Fengzi."

Luo Chen returned the greeting with a "Fellow Daoist," then asked about Elder Fu's condition.

Yelan's face showed grief. "His condition is very poor. His foundation has been damaged. I'm afraid..."

Luo Chen sighed. "After today's business is concluded, I'll find time to visit Elder Fu."

The people in the hall gradually increased.

Some, upon seeing Luo Chen, took the initiative to come and converse.

Even Yao Xiansheng, who had previously been at odds with him, gritted his teeth and approached.

But after learning that Seven Ridges Mountain had been destroyed, just as Fenghua the Fairy had said, his face was ashen, his entire spirit seemingly scattered.

Only then did Luo Chen deeply realize just how devastating this battle had been for the major powers of the Eastern Wilderness.

Attacked from the rear, breached at the front.

One after the other, the losses were truly heavy!

And this was clearly not the limit.

More familiar faces appeared in the hall.

Jiuling Yuanjun, Deng Taiyue, and others all showed up. But ultimately, some familiar faces were missing.

"Where is Hua the Celestial Master?"

"Perished together with the Void Emperor Sect!"

"Where is Pavilion Master Li?"

"Died under the palm of the Great Demon Emperor Bi Zheng! Not only that, but Tie Guan the Daoist was also severely wounded and returned to White Deer Grotto to recuperate."

Speaking of that battle, Jiuling Yuanjun revealed a lingering fear.

He looked at Luo Chen and also asked about the general situation.

The answer he received shocked him as well.

"Jin Ling was no weakling, yet he also fell?"

The appearance of the name Jin Ling seemed to trigger a chain reaction, instantly drawing a cold gaze.

A black-robed old man stepped into the great hall, his fierce aura pressing down on Luo Chen.

"Luo Chen!"

(Chapter End)
