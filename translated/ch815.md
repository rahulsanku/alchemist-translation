# Chapter 815: Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, Instant Annihilation

White Daoist robe, tall crown, black hair—his face was extremely young!

At a single glance, the hook-nosed man’s pupils contracted sharply.

He spoke in a cold voice. “Could it be that you are the Daoist who stopped Thunder Prison back then?”

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. “I didn’t expect I’d have some small reputation on your side!”

As he spoke, scorching mana fluctuations began to unfold in succession.

Vast and surging, filled with an overbearing dominance that acknowledged no equal!

The Encompassing Fire Prison unfolded in an instant.

In that moment, all other attribute heaven-and-earth spiritual qi within the vicinity was forcibly pushed aside, leaving only fire-attribute heaven-and-earth spiritual qi.

Divine Fire Sage, who had been standing extremely close, changed expression drastically.

His domain also controlled fire-attribute heaven-and-earth spiritual qi, yet in that brief span of time, he had actually lost his grip over the free-floating spiritual energy.

Shooting a wary glance at Luo Chen, Divine Fire Sage immediately withdrew from that area.

And the Demon Emperor who had been battling Divine Fire also felt quite uncomfortable, tacitly exchanging battlefields with Divine Fire Sage.

If even they, as bystanders, felt this way, then the hook-nosed Demon Emperor facing Luo Chen directly naturally felt it even more keenly.

He could clearly sense his own Sage domain being suppressed by a far greater power, making even its extension extremely difficult.

Thus, there was no advantage of time or place to be had.

“This person is far more troublesome than I imagined!”

With a thought, he hesitated no longer and shot straight into the sky.

“If you have the courage, come fight me in the heavens!”

Luo Chen smiled faintly and pursued without the slightest hesitation.

Not only that, but with both hands forming seals, as he advanced, the Azure Yang Great Handprint manifested in the world once more.

The continuously accumulating spiritual energy caused the scope of this majestic handprint to expand unceasingly.

*Rumble…*

From afar, it looked as though someone had extended a giant hand, seeking to grasp the very firmament.

The hook-nosed Demon Emperor naturally dared not wait for death. When he reached ten thousand zhang in the sky, he suddenly turned around.

Stepping on a flying shuttle, he flashed forward.

*Swoosh!*

Shuttle and azure palm crossed paths.

The vast mana abruptly scattered.

Even though layers upon layers of halos burst forth in the sky, this technique was ultimately broken.

After passing each other, Luo Chen turned around, revealing an expression of keen interest.

“An interesting treasure. A special method of attack.”

“Daoist, leave your name!”

That man slowly drifted upward, facing Luo Chen directly. The flying shuttle danced affectionately beside him like a single fish swimming.

“Under the command of Commander Seven Rhinoceros, I am Frost Eagle!”

Before his words had even faded, he had already struck first.

From that brief exchange just now, he had already discovered that Luo Chen’s mana reserves were unimaginably vast.

Whether it was the domain he displayed or the casual spell technique he had thrown out, neither was something an ordinary early-stage Sage could match.

He was at least on the same level as those Demon Emperors of the fifth or sixth Sage layer he had seen in the demon army.

Facing such an existence, if he did not seize the initiative in battle, it would be no different from waiting for death!

He raised his hand, and the flying shuttle vanished from beside him in an instant.

When it reappeared, it was already within three zhang of Luo Chen.

*Whoosh!*

The shuttle pierced through. Not only that, but a massive, pale cyan eagle claw also grabbed out from the void.

Seeing this, Luo Chen was not flustered. He took a light step.

In the narrowest margin, he barely dodged the pincer attack of both techniques.

“Eh? What technique is this?” Frost Eagle Demon Emperor let out a surprised exclamation upon seeing both his overt and covert methods miss.

But he naturally would not stop his actions.

The flying shuttle once again vanished into the void, hiding in the shadows.

And as both hands grasped at the air, one enormous cyan eagle claw after another materialized in the sky, grabbing at Luo Chen from all sorts of unexpected angles.

The overt and covert shifted, making it all the more difficult to guard against!

After all, that flying shuttle could even break Luo Chen’s Azure Yang Great Handprint. If it struck the body by accident, an ordinary Sage could not withstand it.

Yet what was infuriating was that no matter how many eagle claws he sent out, that human Sage who called himself the Pill Sect Master always managed to dodge by a hair’s breadth at the very last moment.

The footwork was so exquisite, the evasions so uncanny, that his eyes could no longer keep up. He had to release his divine sense completely just to catch a glimpse of a flaw in the opponent’s movements.

*Swoosh!*

Amidst the dense web of claw shadows, the flying shuttle reappeared!

This time, he wouldn’t escape!

Luo Chen abruptly halted his body, revealing a helpless expression.

A perfect-grade Moon Step could not ultimately completely escape the divine sense lock of a Sage-level Demon Emperor, especially when this flying shuttle treasure was even faster than an ordinary flying sword.

A red light flashed on his right hand. He extended two fingers and pointed them toward the back of his head.

A gentle flick.

*Ding!*

The flying shuttle, as if struck by a heavy blow, staggered backward.

In the distance, Frost Eagle’s face paled slightly, and he could not help but let out a muffled groan.

His face was filled with disbelief.

To use the fingers of his physical body to forcibly flick away his true-artifact-level flying shuttle—what kind of technique was this?

“No, neither the translocation technique earlier nor this finger-flicking of the flying shuttle now are any kind of spell technique. They are pure physical power!”

“This person not only possesses overwhelming mana but also a terrifying physique beyond imagination!”

“I am no match for him!”

At this thought, his face showed no fear, but instead revealed a ruthless determination.

He clasped his hands together, then abruptly parted them!

Like spreading open a pair of wings, surging demonic qi filled every direction.

A biting wind howled, and mist spread.

A massive cyan eagle, nearly two hundred zhang in size, displayed its majestic and spirited form in the sky.

Its wings beat violently, and gusts of frost and ice swept toward Luo Chen with the gale.

In the blink of an eye, the entire sky seemed to be covered by wind and frost.

Even Luo Chen’s Encompassing Fire Prison was temporarily suppressed, unable to affect the enemy.

Seizing this opportunity, the cyan eagle trembled its wings and pounced toward Luo Chen.

Just as it was about to enter the storm of wind and frost, the eagle smoothly rose upward and, without hesitation, flew eastward.

After going all out, he had chosen to flee!

Before leaving, he turned his head and took a deep look in the direction where the wind and frost had enveloped, as if trying to imprint Luo Chen’s image in his memory.

It was this very glance that made his pupils contract sharply.

He had absolute confidence in his centuries-cultivated Cyan Mist Wind and Frost. This technique was enough to threaten any mid-stage Sage.

But at this moment, at the center of the storm, a white-robed man braved the heavy wind and frost, walking out step by step.

Strolling leisurely, utterly composed!

“Fellow Daoist Frost Eagle, fleeing without a fight is somewhat discourteous to this sect master!”

The Pill Sect Master laughed lightly as his clenched right hand slowly unfurled like a lotus blossom.

At the same moment, Frost Eagle felt his heart clench sharply.

Looking down, he saw a wisp of flame, the color of bone-white, rising coldly from within his body.

When had he been hit by this technique?

He had no time to think. He could only watch helplessly as his originally magnificent and smooth cyan feathers lost their luster of life in the blink of an eye, becoming utterly dead and still.

Not only that, but his vigorous and powerful wings grew heavy and sluggish, as if drained of all strength.

And the white flame was continuously seeping into the flesh and blood of his body, and even into the meridians, acupoints, and Purple Mansion barrier that had formed after his transformation.

Once it fully penetrated, the consequences…

In an instant, Frost Eagle’s soul fled in terror.

“Get out of me!”

The nascent soul within his body glared angrily, opening its mouth to spew out a great torrent of mana, transforming it into nascent soul true fire in an attempt to expel the strange white flame that had somehow attached itself to him.

Yet, the moment that nascent soul true fire touched the white flame, it instead intensified the burning of this fire.

An unbearable, searing pain caused Frost Eagle to lose all speed.

His enormous body could not stop falling.

When he reached mid-air, a cyan light mass emerged from within the flying eagle’s body.

It cast a hateful glance at Luo Chen, then pounced toward a certain spot in the void.

*Swoosh!*

The flying shuttle reappeared, enveloping it, and then, with a trembling hum, vanished once more into the void.

Luo Chen stood motionless in place. Seeing this scene, he could not help but nod.

“Struck by my Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, you immediately abandon your body and flee with your nascent soul, like a gecko cutting off its tail. Truly decisive and extraordinary!”

“Unfortunately, it’s too late!”

He made no great movement. He merely placed his index and middle fingers together, pointing them toward a certain spot in the void.

Then, his middle finger bent slightly.

Accompanied by a soft cry from his mouth.

“Collect!”

*Hum…*

The next moment, a giant mouth materialized in the void!

Looking closely, where was there any giant mouth? It was actually a great cauldron!

The great cauldron was dull and unremarkable, yet its surface was covered with intricate patterns—birds, beasts, insects, fish, mountains, rivers, and seas—all vivid and lifelike, as if they had been born that way.

And the flying shuttle was now like a fish caught in a net, frantically charging left and right within the cauldron’s mouth, trying to escape.

But how could a single fish leap out of a boundless ocean?

“Stop struggling!”

Luo Chen smiled faintly, and then, within the Primordial Unity Cauldron, black electric arcs suddenly burst forth.

*Crack!*

*Crackle!*

*Sizzle!*

Sharp screams came continuously.

Vaguely, sounds of pleading could be heard.

“Spare my life, Daoist!”

“I am willing to surrender, to be your mount.”

“Such a heart of iron and stone, you will die a miserable death…”

The screams gradually weakened.

When the black electric arcs flashed and vanished, a wisp of green smoke drifted up.

Only the flying shuttle remained, floating alone and bewildered within the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

*Thud!*

On the ground, the massive body of the giant flying eagle suddenly crashed down.

Dozens of demon beasts that had been too slow to dodge were directly crushed into meat patties.

The nearby living beings, both human and demon, all stopped their actions simultaneously, staring at this scene in shock.

That body was full of surging life force, yet utterly devoid of any will—clearly a soulless corpse.

A cultivator’s expression was dazed as he murmured, “A Demon Emperor… has fallen!”

The fall of the Demon Emperor’s corpse seemed like a signal.

Countless low-level demon beasts, in a panic, retreated like a receding tide.

And all the cultivators stood frozen in place, their gazes turning toward the nine heavens above.

A figure, white-robed and tall-crowned, descended slowly.

Vast mana fluctuations pressed down, forcing the bloody flames of war on the ground to retreat continuously.

His body was spotless, not even a single strand of his bound black hair was out of place.

His figure stopped in mid-air above the corpse of Frost Eagle Demon Emperor. He was silent, as if observing something.

Around him, the clear sound of someone swallowing saliva could be heard.

Then came a weak voice: “Lord Pill Sect Master.”

Pill Sect Master was already a respectful title, and the addition of “Lord” added even more fear!

Luo Chen glanced at that person and nodded slightly.

The next moment, a sound like mountains roaring and seas howling erupted.

“Lord Pill Sect Master has conquered the enemy and slain the demon!”

“The beast tide is retreating! A great victory! A great victory!”

Amidst the cheers, a graceful figure flew down from the city wall and arrived beside Luo Chen.

She could not help but lower her head to look at the charred corpse on the ground, which was like a small mountain. Between her nostrils, the air was filled with the scent of roasted meat.

*Gulp!*

“Fellow Daoist Luo… you killed him?”

“Mm.”

The Second Palace Master of Fenghua Palace parted her red lips slightly, her face full of disbelief.

How long had it been since the two of them had gone up to the heavens to fight?

At most, it hadn’t even been the time it takes to drink a cup of tea!

A generation of Demon Emperor, just like that, died so easily?

That was a Demon Emperor at the third Sage layer!

Moreover, judging from its original form, it was a bird of the eagle family, most skilled in speed.

An existence like that, even if it couldn’t win, could still run. How could it have even left its original body behind?

But the facts were right before her eyes, leaving her no choice but to believe.

The Second Palace Master asked with a hint of hope, “Did the demon’s nascent soul escape?”

“Unfortunately, that demon’s luck was poor. He met me.”

Luo Chen toyed with an object that looked like a weaving shuttle in his hand, his tone flat, as if he had done something utterly insignificant.

“Both soul and spirit are destroyed. Hard to enter reincarnation.”

The Second Palace Master stared at the flying shuttle and fell silent.

A man and a woman stood quietly in the center of the battlefield.

Nearby, low-level cultivators began to clean up the battlefield, but they still could not stop casting glances at the two of them.

After about two or three minutes.

Luo Chen suddenly spoke: “They’re back!”

As if by prior agreement, three figures flew back simultaneously from different directions.

Golden light arrived first.

As soon as Golden Feather Sage landed, his gaze shifted from the cyan eagle corpse to Luo Chen.

Even though he had sensed this scene from dozens of li away, he still found it hard to believe.

Then came a blaze of red light, as Divine Fire Sage returned.

He was slightly disheveled, his aura somewhat unstable, but overall unharmed.

But the yellow light that landed last, after dispersing its mana, could not suppress itself. Old Ghost Motian spat out a large mouthful of clotted blood.

On his chest, there was clearly a black palm print, and even his protective Dharma robe had been pierced through.

“Fellow Daoist Motian, it seems your situation is not too good! I have some slight knowledge of medicine and alchemy, and I do have some healing spiritual medicines. Do you need my help?”

Luo Chen laughed loudly, his voice so clear that it could be heard for several li in every direction.

Deliberate!

Old Ghost Motian’s face was deathly pale as he glared fiercely at Luo Chen.

But that vicious glare, in the eyes of several Sages, carried an unconcealable weakness.

“No need!”

Without much conversation, Old Ghost Motian went straight into Lingtian Pass.

Luo Chen smiled faintly, as if completely unconcerned by the other’s rude behavior.

He turned his gaze and landed on Golden Feather and Divine Fire.

At just one glance, Divine Fire Sage could not control himself, and mana fluctuations surged.

“What are you going to do…”

Golden Feather raised his hand, interrupting his junior brother’s words.

He looked coldly at Luo Chen, a hint of wariness in his eyes.

“Congratulations, Daoist, for slaying a powerful enemy in formation!”

Having said that, he led Divine Fire Sage away from the devastated battlefield.

Only the Second Palace Master remained by Luo Chen’s side.

The atmosphere seemed somewhat tense.

The Second Palace Master revealed an indignant expression. “If it weren’t for Fellow Daoist Luo quickly killing that demon and intimidating the other three Demon Emperors, how could they have extricated themselves so quickly? Not only do they not show gratitude, but they put on such a face!”

Indeed.

In a battle at the Sage level, unless there was an absolute gap in realm, it would drag on for at least half a day at the very least.

Old Ghost Motian had lost his true artifact, Motian Cliff, and his own realm was insufficient. If the battle had gone on longer, let alone extricating himself, it was uncertain whether he could have returned alive.

Luo Chen, however, did not mind and comforted her a few words.

In his view, putting on a face?

Heh, nothing more than bluster and bravado!

The Second Palace Master spoke a few more indignant words on Luo Chen’s behalf, then pointed at the huge thing on the ground and asked, “Fellow Daoist Luo, what do you plan to do with this Demon Emperor’s corpse?”

Looking down at the charred corpse of Frost Eagle, Luo Chen licked his lips.

This was no small prize!

It looked pitch black, burned to charcoal, but in reality, the inside was completely undamaged.

That Frost Eagle Demon Emperor had seen the situation was wrong and immediately abandoned his body with his nascent soul. Luo Chen, naturally, had not continued to burn his original form.

And a Demon Emperor-level demon beast corpse was a treasure from head to toe!

Not to mention the flying shuttle, just this corpse alone had made Luo Chen a fortune.

And most importantly, through this battle, Luo Chen had tested the strength of his fifth-stage Withering-Bloom Divine Fire!

“Perhaps I was being too cautious earlier?”

Thinking this in his heart, Luo Chen said calmly, “Have someone carry it back into the city. Leave some of the flesh and blood materials. As for the rest, anyone who is interested can purchase it from the Luo Tian Treasure Pavilion.”

The Second Palace Master nodded and immediately ordered people to handle the Demon Emperor’s corpse.

In fact, she didn’t even need to call anyone.

Luo Tian Sect cultivators who had been waiting nearby for some time greeted Luo Chen with reverence, then eagerly lifted the enormous Frost Eagle corpse and carried it toward Lingtian Pass.

Wherever they passed, an uproar arose!

Aside from the few corpses left behind when the eight Demon Emperors had violently attacked Lingtian Pass, this was the first Demon Emperor-level existence to fall before the battle lines since the war at Lingtian Pass had begun.

And the one who killed him was none other than the already famous Luo Tian Supreme Elder, Pill Sect Master Luo Chen!

For a time, even though the fighting in other city districts had not yet ceased, the discussions about Luo Chen within the city had already drowned out everything else!
