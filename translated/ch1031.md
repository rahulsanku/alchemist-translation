# Chapter 1031: Extreme and Radical, Late Sage Stage

"Apologize!"

The bone-chilling words came from Zhou Fuyou's mouth, and the temperature around them seemed to drop several degrees.

His gaze was fixed on the youth surrounded by a crowd of sycophants.

Zhou Fuyou knew who he was.

Zhou Yuhao, whose father was a true Initiate cultivator.

But the real reason so many people fawned over him was his grandfather.

Seven years ago, Zhou Yuhao's grandfather had received personal guidance from the clan ancestor and broken through to the Adept stage in one go. He was now a figure of considerable importance within the clan.

Because of this, his branch of the family had suddenly risen to prominence.

Though Zhou Yuhao was still young, he had clearly understood his family's special status from an early age and often threw his weight around.

Now, intimidated by Zhou Fuyou's threat, he first took a step back instinctively, but then came to his senses amid the crowd of companions surrounding him.

What status did he have, and what status did the Zhou Fuyou siblings have?

Why should he be afraid of him?

Zhou Yuhao sneered, "Am I not speaking the truth? Even if this sickly girl tests positive for a spirit root in the future, she probably won't be able to cultivate. Why waste effort here? In my opinion, she might as well move to Coral Town early and save the clan some resources."

As soon as he said this, the companions around him chimed in agreement.

"Yuhao is right!"

"People should know their place. Our Zhou clan doesn't have many resources to begin with. All resources should be used where they matter most. Wasting them on such a sickly girl is too extravagant."

"I heard that besides the bereavement compensation, their family is still receiving treatment equivalent to Initiate cultivators?"

"Zhou Qingyu doesn't even deserve to train with us!"

...

Voices clamored, gazes like thorns.

Zhou Qingyu's face was pale, her lips trembling slightly.

Especially when she looked at a few friends her age, and they averted their eyes, tears welled up in her eyes.

Instinctively, she gripped the sleeve of the youth in front of her.

"Brother, let's go back!"

Zhou Fuyou's heart felt as if it were being squeezed. Gently, he released his sister's hand.

As his sister looked on in confusion, Zhou Fuyou completely blocked her from view.

"I'll say it one last time. Apologize!"

Zhou Yuhao across from him not only refused to apologize but even made a face.

"No way, no way. What can you do about..."

*Thump!*

A sharp whistling sound seemed to explode across the square.

Before anyone could react, a figure crossed several large strides and charged directly in front of Zhou Yuhao.

Zhou Fuyou grabbed Zhou Yuhao, who was a head shorter than him, with one hand and swung the other hand in a slap.

*Smack!*

"Apologize!"

No one had time to react. No one expected Zhou Fuyou to be so fast.

Including Zhou Yuhao himself, who was stunned senseless by the slap and couldn't even speak.

*Smack!*

"Apologize!"

When the second slap landed, he finally came to his senses and roared in fury, "How dare you hit me?"

*Smack!*

"My father is a true Initiate cultivator! My grandfather is an Adept Superior!"

*Smack!*

"Help me! Someone help me!"

*Smack!*

Three teeth fell to the ground.

Zhou Fuyou's cold gaze swept across the surroundings. The few who had been about to step forward involuntarily stopped in their tracks.

With a cold snort, Zhou Fuyou used his single arm's strength and, in full view of everyone, lifted Zhou Yuhao by the neck.

"I'll ask you one last time. Will you apologize or not?"

Zhou Yuhao was already a head shorter than Zhou Fuyou, and now he was lifted to the same height.

Meeting those eyes that looked at him as if he were already dead, he finally became afraid. His face, flushed red like a pig's liver, moved as he struggled to speak.

"S-Sorry."

*Thump!*

Zhou Fuyou released his hand and turned to leave.

Zhou Yuhao collapsed to the ground, his throat making sounds like a broken bellows. His pants, wet at some point, gave off a foul, fishy smell.

He stared at Zhou Fuyou's retreating back, his eyes filled with fear.

Everyone involuntarily made way, watching as Zhou Fuyou took his sister's hand and left the square.

...

On a path paved with bluestone slabs.

The siblings walked neither quickly nor slowly.

"Brother, I don't want to go to the square to exercise tomorrow."

"No, you must go. As long as big brother is here, no one dares bully you."

"But they..."

"Other people's opinions don't matter. As long as we live well, that's enough. And exercising, making ourselves strong—that's the only thing we can rely on."

"Okay, Maomao will listen to big brother!"

Hurried footsteps came from behind.

Soon, Zhou Yuqi caught up, panting heavily.

"Fuyou, how could you hit Zhou Yuhao just now? Their family has an Adept Superior. By offending them like this, in the future..."

Zhou Fuyou stared coldly at Zhou Yuqi. "Was I supposed to let him bully my sister?"

Zhou Yuqi hesitated. "But..."

Zhou Fuyou directly interrupted him. "No buts. No one can bully Maomao, unless I'm dead!"

The last five words were spoken with absolute finality, leaving no room for doubt.

Zhou Yuqi stood there dumbfounded, as if seeing this childhood friend for the first time.

He returned home somewhat dejectedly.

His mother seemed to have heard about what happened earlier and called him over to ask about it in detail.

Zhou Yuqi answered absentmindedly, but at the end, he suddenly had a realization.

"Mother, why don't you adopt Fuyou and Maomao?"

"Adopt?" Zhou Yuqi's mother heard these two words and couldn't help but curl her lips into a smile. "Alright, tomorrow I'll go find the clan leader to submit the request."

Zhou Yuqi thought he had helped his friend and jumped up happily.

...

In the dead of night.

In the empty Drink-Green Waterside Pavilion, a shadowy figure drifted over.

From a distance, she quietly watched the boy and girl inside, a strange expression on her face.

"Master's clone seems to have a completely different personality from the master himself?"

"He doesn't look like a reckless person, but he didn't care at all about the consequences of his actions. And he's very extreme. I even sensed a hint of killing intent during the day."

"He's so young. How could his personality be so extreme?"

"Also, didn't Master say he was already at Qi Refinement stage? Why can't I detect any spiritual power fluctuation on him at all?"

Xiaobai had only arrived today.

Traveling from the Western Continent to the Tiannan Continent was equivalent to crossing half of the Central Continent, and with the sea separating them, it had taken her over half a year.

Yet the first time she saw Zhou Fuyou, countless questions arose in her mind.

Just as she was pondering deeply, a soft sound came from beside her.

*Drip!*

"Is it raining?"

Xiaobai looked up at the sky in confusion. It didn't seem like weather for rain today!

When she lowered her head in puzzlement, a figure had silently appeared beside her.

"Coming uninvited—Fellow Daoist is somewhat rude, don't you think?"

Xiaobai's face changed dramatically as she stared fixedly at the vicissitudinous figure exuding a thick aura of decay.

"Who are you?"

"I should be the one asking you that!"

The vicissitudinous figure turned sideways, staring at Xiaobai with a suspicious expression.

"I am Zhou Yunshen, clan leader of the Zhou clan. You carry the aura of an old acquaintance. Are you one of Luo Chen's people?"

Hearing the other party introduce himself, Xiaobai finally breathed a sigh of relief.

She nodded and said, "Luo Chen is my master. My name is Bai Meiling."

Zhou Yunshen confirmed his speculation, then turned his gaze to the two figures under the lamplight.

"Did you come on your master's behalf to see Fuyou?"

"Yes!"

"Didn't he say not to interfere with Fuyou's growth?"

Xiaobai hesitated for a moment, then shook her head. "That's none of your business!"

"Don't want to say?" Zhou Yunshen let out a low laugh. He didn't press further and simply watched the night scenery in the courtyard.

This quiet night scene was soon broken.

A dozen people, surrounding a richly dressed woman, burst into Zhou Fuyou's home, shouting things like "Beat that little brat to death!"

But before they could even see the Zhou Fuyou siblings, a sturdy man blocked them in the courtyard.

"Get back!"

The group stood frozen in place.

Someone recognized the sturdy man's identity.

"It's the clan leader!"

Zhou Junxiao said sternly, "Children fighting among themselves, and you adults have the nerve to get involved? Should I call Zhou Xiaocheng over here?"

Zhou Xiaocheng was Zhou Yuhao's grandfather, the deep background these people relied on.

The group slunk away from the courtyard.

Zhou Junxiao looked displeased. When he turned around, he saw the boy standing under the eaves.

Zhou Junxiao forced a smile. "Don't be afraid. This is the Zhou clan. Your parents died fighting for the clan. No one dares bully you. Tomorrow, come to the clan meeting hall. Your Second Grandfather has something to discuss with you."

The boy nodded slightly, the cold wariness in his eyes visibly fading.

Far away.

Xiaobai said in surprise, "You're also interfering with Zhou Fuyou's growth?"

Zhou Yunshen chuckled lightly. "He is, after all, a Zhou."

Then Zhou Yunshen asked, "Fellow Daoist Bai, are you passing through, or do you plan to stay for a while?"

Bai Meiling hesitated for a moment before finally saying, "I might need to stay for some time."

Zhou Yunshen let out an "oh" and then said, "In that case, I suggest you go live in the White Egret Marsh. After all, you are a ghost cultivator. Staying too long where my Zhou clan members gather could easily affect these low-level clansmen."

Xiaobai's heart skipped a beat. She knew her true nature had been seen through at a glance.

And she had hidden so well earlier. How was she discovered?

Master had said before that unless a great cultivator deliberately searched, ordinary Sages would never be able to find her.

Master was absolutely right!

If there was an unexpected situation, there was only one possibility—this old man, whose lifespan was nearly exhausted, had undergone some unforeseen change.

Xiaobai quietly cast her divine sense toward the old man.

At that moment, even though his body stood right before her, Zhou Yunshen seemed absent from her divine sense perception, leaving only empty, vast heaven and earth.

Melting into heaven and earth?

Xiaobai suddenly woke with a start, staring at Zhou Yunshen with fear in her eyes.

This situation was not unfamiliar to her. She had often seen it in her master before.

"You!"

Zhou Yunshen smiled faintly. "Thanks to your master's blessing, I have gained a little something. Fellow Daoist Bai, go to the White Egret Marsh. You don't need to worry about Zhou Fuyou here."

Xiaobai took a deep look at the old man and left the Drink-Green Waterside Pavilion.

...

Deep within the White Egret Marsh.

After settling down, Xiaobai hesitated for a long time before cautiously activating the jade token Luo Chen had given her.

Soon, a voice like clashing metal came from the token.

"Xiaobai?"

"Master, it's me!"

"What is it?"

"Your clone has some unexpected circumstances. Both parents are dead, and his situation within the clan is poor. As a result, his personality has become quite extreme. In Xiaobai's opinion, if not guided properly, this extreme personality will not be conducive to cultivation and will likely lead to an early death."

"Mm, I understand."

"Also, you said he had reached Qi Refinement stage, but I didn't observe any spiritual power fluctuation on him. That's somewhat strange."

"I understand."

"Finally, the Zhou clan ancestor Zhou Yunshen said that thanks to your blessing, he has gained some insight. From Xiaobai's observation, he has actually comprehended the true meaning of the laws and quietly entered the late Sage stage."

"Oh? Any other news?"

"No."

"Treasure the remaining two chances. This jade token only has three transmission opportunities."

"Understood!"

Ending the brief exchange, Bai Meiling looked at the jade token, now a third dimmer, and let out a slight sigh.

Though somewhat unexpected, everything was still under control.

Meanwhile.

In the blue void millions of li away from the Tiannan Continent, Luo Chen looked at the jade token in his hand with surprise.

The jade token he had given Bai Meiling was not an ordinary transmission token. He had made it with a special technique, leaving behind three separated divine thoughts, which allowed for such ultra-long-distance cross-continental communication.

And these divine thoughts were used once and lost forever.

He had specifically reminded Xiaobai earlier to use the jade token only in unexpected situations.

Now that she had wasted one opportunity so quickly, had something unusual happened?

With a thought, the corresponding divine thought returned to his sea of consciousness.

In an instant, he knew all the intelligence Xiaobai had transmitted.

"Both parents dead, personality extreme and radical?"

"Can't detect any spiritual power fluctuation?"

"Zhou Yunshen comprehended the true meaning of the laws and entered the late Sage stage?"

Luo Chen muttered these three pieces of news, a look of interest appearing on his face.

The clone had clearly entered the Dao to cultivate, yet there was no spiritual power fluctuation. After a moment's thought, Luo Chen arrived at the answer.

It must be caused by the Netherwater Spring!

Before Zhou Fuyou was even born, he had dispersed the Netherwater Spring and merged it into the clone's body.

With this extraordinary treasure on him, as long as the clone was willing, it was the best concealment method in the world. Except for Ascendant-level great powers, no one could see through his specific cultivation realm.

As for Zhou Yunshen comprehending the true meaning of the laws...

Luo Chen thought about it and also attributed it to the Netherwater Spring.

He himself primarily cultivated fire methods, yet back then he had been able to comprehend the water origin from the Netherwater Spring. Zhou Yunshen, who primarily cultivated water methods, would naturally have a certain chance of comprehending the water origin under such "close contact."

His judgment was correct. Since the birth of his clone Zhou Fuyou, the White Egret Marsh had been raining endlessly all year round—this was the influence of the Netherwater Spring.

As long as Zhou Fuyou's cultivation level grew higher in the future, this external influence would be brought under some control.

Zhou Yunshen's comprehension of the water origin was also influenced by the clone. In other words, it truly counted as "thanks to Luo Chen's blessing."

However, this matter itself had little impact. After all, Zhou Yunshen was already advanced in years. Even if Luo Chen had helped extend his lifespan by a few years back then, it wasn't enough for him to charge toward the Ascendant stage.

On the contrary, it might bring some benefits to the clone's growth.

What truly concerned Luo Chen was the personality of his clone, Zhou Fuyou.

"Extreme? Radical?"

"Is it caused by the great tragedy of losing both parents, or is it his inherent personality?"

"Will this extreme nature affect my future retrieval of the life soul?"

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen fell into hesitation.

To be safe, what he should do now was use some means to secretly influence the clone, making it easier to retrieve the life soul in the future.

But externally applied means were easily detected.

In the end, Luo Chen decided to hold still.

"I can suppress and refine even ghosts and gods. What is a mere clone!"

(Chapter End)
