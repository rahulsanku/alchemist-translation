# Chapter 1060: Truth Crossing Hall Master, Commanding the Demons

Crisp footsteps echoed through the underground palace. Nether Spring frowned as she hurried to the central teleportation array.

"The Green Parasol Mountain formation has always been hidden. Why did it suddenly activate today?"

"Which blind fool is it?"

After descending the last step, the spacious underground palace scene instantly came into view.

Yet as far as the eye could see, it was completely empty, with not a single figure in sight.

Nether Spring was stunned, almost thinking she had sensed incorrectly.

But upon seeing the gradually fading light atop the teleportation array, she immediately reacted.

"Someone really did teleport over, and from the branch array in the Northern Sea at that. But where is the person?"

She looked around, somewhat bewildered.

Meanwhile, in another void space.

Luo Chen, who had just arrived at Green Parasol Mountain, was shifted here. Even though he deliberately made no resistance, the ability to teleport someone else out of thin air was no ordinary means.

Luo Chen's expression was complicated as he looked at the woman standing quietly in the void.

Her figure was still as tall and slender, her long, shapely thighs firm and powerful. Her originally cold and aloof temperament was now even more ethereal.

She stood there quietly, her face exquisitely beautiful like a banished immortal maiden. Though clearly present in this realm, she seemed as if she might ride the wind and depart at any moment.

"You really did ascend to Ascendant!"

Azure Frost calmly looked at him. "You've said that twice."

Luo Chen smiled bitterly. He had indeed said it twice.

The first time was a shocked question; the second was helplessness after confirming it face to face. The emotions contained within this might only be known to him alone.

Once upon a time, he had regarded Azure Frost as his imaginary rival, a benchmark to chase!

When experimenting with certain ideas or creating new killing moves, he would subconsciously think, "How would this fare against Azure Frost?"

This was because Luo Chen had once been utterly defeated by Azure Frost without any ability to fight back. Even those Sage-level cultivators he had once looked up to, those famous grand cultivators, were no match for Azure Frost.

He had continuously worked hard to improve himself, eventually catching up to Azure Frost step by step.

From exhausting all his strength just to injure her, to mutual compromise in life-and-death battles—especially after the Western Desert Buddhist Land battle, when the world ranked the demoness Azure Frost alongside Formless Demon Moluo, he had once felt a sense of pride.

In recent years, Luo Chen's strength had increased tremendously.

Whether it was stepping into the ninth layer of the Sage stage, or mastering the Million Baleful Souls Banner, the Blood Jade Three-Flame Fan, and even the Great Perfection of the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*—one by one, great methods capable of threatening Ascendant-stage cultivators had been grasped by him, making him feel that he had completely surpassed Azure Frost.

Until this moment.

Azure Frost had ascended to Ascendant, once again walking ahead of him.

Luo Chen sighed, maintaining his composure as he cupped his hands in salute. "Congratulations!"

Unexpectedly, Azure Frost looked at him indifferently. "You need not belittle yourself. When you first entered the Adept stage, I was already at the late Sage stage."

Luo Chen was startled. The thoughts in his heart had been seen through by her.

Was it because of the Same-Life Shared-Death Gu?

"Now, I have merely taken the first step. You will catch up sooner or later. After all, you have also condensed your primordial spirit and are only one step away from Ascendant, are you not?"

Luo Chen was speechless, then laughed at himself. "I suppose I was too attached."

"You came back from the Northern Sea?"

"Mm, I went there to handle some matters. To save time on the journey, I used Green Parasol Mountain's teleportation array."

"You still have time to go to the Northern Sea? Don't you intend to retrieve your life soul?" Azure Frost's willow-like brows furrowed slightly, as if she didn't understand how Luo Chen could still have such leisure.

Regarding the matter of Luo Chen's incomplete life soul, Azure Frost knew the inside story.

And having experienced breaking through to Ascendant herself, she was even more aware of how crucial a complete soul was for taking that step.

Especially for Luo Chen—his foundation was so profound that it was unprecedented in millennia. As long as his life soul was complete, taking that final step would be a natural and effortless matter.

Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath. "I have already made arrangements for this matter. The day I make my move is not far off."

Speaking thus, he looked at Azure Frost with a half-smile.

"You care so much about me. Could it be that you want to help me? Perfect timing—now that your cultivation has greatly increased and you possess such formidable spatial laws, I imagine you can hold your own even against older-generation Ascendant powerhouses!"

Azure Frost ignored Luo Chen's teasing and said calmly, "I haven't fought them, but I imagine I wouldn't be inferior."

"Then come help me!" Luo Chen's spirits lifted as he spoke eloquently. "The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is, after all, the most deeply rooted sacred land in the Mountains and Seas Realm. On the surface, only Ancestor Liancheng remains as an Ascendant great power, but who knows if they might still unleash some Ascendant-level combat strength. I've contacted an Ascendant powerhouse from the Northern Sea. If I have your assistance as well, then this matter will be all but certain."

Azure Frost cast him a cold glance. "And why should I?"

Why should Azure Frost help him?

To this question, Luo Chen was full of confidence.

Of course, it was because of the life-and-death connection between the two of them!

But when this answer reached his lips, seeing Azure Frost's indifferent gaze, Luo Chen couldn't bring himself to say it.

If he used that as leverage, not only would he reveal petty behavior, but he would also incur her displeasure for no reason.

Especially since the Same-Life Shared-Death Gu that Luo Chen had planted back then was only at the fourth rank. For the ancient demon Azure Luan, who had now ascended to Ascendant, it was hard to say how much binding force this fourth-rank gu insect still possessed.

Luo Chen put away his smile and pondered for a moment.

After a long while, he said solemnly, "Helping me is not just helping me—it's also helping yourselves!"

Azure Frost's nature was cold and aloof, devoted to cultivation, but that didn't mean she was foolish.

"Your words are half-true, half-false."

Luo Chen didn't deny it. The false part was his selfishness, while the true part was indeed consideration for Green Parasol Mountain.

He spoke slowly. "A hundred years ago, Rosycloud Sovereign forcefully attacked the Central Continent, causing the land to sink, the Dao Sect to collapse, and the fallen to number in the billions. Since then, the two sides have forged an indelible blood feud!"

"Over the years, the great powers of the Dao Sect have not dared to set foot in the Eastern Wilderness, fearing the Sovereign's might. But the fact that the Sovereign has not again attacked the Central Continent to eradicate the roots suggests that she was severely injured and unable to continue fighting."

"If it were only that, then so be it. As time passes, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect recovers its strength, and Rosycloud Sovereign heals her injuries—at worst, they would fight another battle."

"But as far as I know, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is currently setting up formations on several continents, intending to reunite the Central Continent and rebuild Heaven-Earth Peak. Once they complete this step, they can make contact with the upper realm, and might even recreate the scene from back then when many great powers descended upon the Central Continent."

"Given the blood feud between the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and your sister Rosycloud Sovereign, countless experts will surely cross the sea and forcibly ascend Green Parasol Mountain at that time."

"Azure Frost, do you want to see that happen?"

Azure Frost's expression shifted slightly, and her slightly curved brows gently furrowed.

Regarding her sister's situation, she had never revealed it to outsiders, but Luo Chen's guess was actually not far off.

Ever since the great battle with Devil Lord Heaven-Refining a thousand years ago, when she fell into this realm, Rosycloud Sovereign had been severely injured and was forced to use the Nirvana method.

After a thousand years, she had re-cultivated back to the Void Refining stage.

However, because her sister was not a native of this realm, the Void Refining stage was severely suppressed by the world's laws, unable to fully display its power, and could only use Ascendant-level means.

Although she had subsequently achieved many proud feats—annihilating the Primordial Demon Sect, burying the Nether Origin Sect, and severely wounding the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect—she had ultimately accumulated tremendous injuries.

In particular, the dying strike of the Nether Origin Sect's Abyss great power had attracted heavenly tribulation, and the Heavenly Origin Five Elders had activated the Void Refining-level Great Desolate Soul-Drawing Banner at Broken Soul Cliff, bringing unimaginable trauma to her sister.

What Luo Chen said about "Rosycloud Sovereign unable to continue fighting" was the truth!

This was also the reason Azure Frost had returned to Green Parasol Mountain and, after a hundred years without leaving the mountain gate, forcibly broken through to the Ascendant stage.

She wanted to protect her sister during her weakest period!

As long as her sister recovered from her injuries, then no one in the Mountains and Seas Realm, vast as it was, would be a match for her!

But now the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was taking a shortcut, rebuilding Heaven-Earth Peak to summon great powers from the upper realm to descend.

"Without the Star Gate, can Heaven-Earth Peak accomplish this?" Azure Frost murmured to herself.

Luo Chen caught that word. "Star Gate?"

Azure Frost hummed in acknowledgment, a bit more confidence appearing on her face. "Yes, the Dao Sect's Star Gate was taken by my sister. Without this spatial spirit treasure for crossing the void and traversing the cosmos, ordinary great powers cannot descend given the distance between the Mountains and Seas Realm and the Xuan Realm."

A flicker appeared in the woman's hand, and an ancient gate shimmering with starlight materialized in her palm.

Just as Luo Chen wanted to look more closely, the gate vanished from Azure Frost's hand in an instant.

So that was it—Rosycloud Sovereign's attack on the Central Continent back then was for this spirit treasure?

As Luo Chen realized this, bitterness welled up in his heart.

If the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, having lost the Star Gate, could not summon great powers from the upper realm, then Green Parasol Mountain faced no danger at all, and Azure Frost had no reason to help Luo Chen.

Still unwilling to give up, Luo Chen brought up a term Li Canghai had mentioned.

"The Northern Sea formation grandmaster Li Canghai mentioned that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has set up a formation called the God-Descending Formation on Heaven-Earth Island. Could this formation summon across realms?"

"God-Descending Formation?" Azure Frost looked somewhat confused.

So she didn't know about this formation either!

Luo Chen sighed, finding it difficult to convince her.

Forget it!

In the end, he would have to rely on himself!

With a thought, Luo Chen took a step forward, and his entire person left that void space.

Looking around, he was inside the Daytime Void Hall. Azure Frost stood ahead, appearing deep in thought.

Luo Chen walked toward the hall's exit. As he passed by her, he said with feigned ease, "If I can successfully retrieve my life soul and step into the Ascendant stage, I will voluntarily dissolve the Same-Life Shared-Death Gu contract. If I fail, with your current cultivation, you should be able to withstand the contract's backlash. Thinking about it this way, your successful ascension to Ascendant now is actually a good thing."

Having said this, he stepped out of the Daytime Void Hall with heavy footsteps.

Sometimes, seeing hope and then losing it was far more painful than having nothing at all.

But after leaving the Daytime Void Hall, Luo Chen had already adjusted his mindset. After all, from the very beginning, he had never factored Azure Frost's combat power into his calculations.

"Sister Azure Frost, someone just teleported... Eh, Luo Chen?"

Nether Spring, who had hurried over to the Daytime Void Hall in a rush, wanting Azure Frost to investigate the earlier anomaly with the teleportation array, stopped at the hall's entrance.

Nether Spring showed a look of realization. "Luo Chen, so it was you who came through the teleportation array earlier!"

Luo Chen looked at her calmly. "Deputy Hall Master Nether Spring, shouldn't you address me as Truth Crossing Hall Master, or Lord Luo Chen?"

Nether Spring was stunned, then curled her lips.

She didn't call him that, but she didn't deny it either.

Luo Chen himself didn't care about forcibly correcting her address; it was merely a simple reminder.

But as he spoke the identity of "Truth Crossing Hall Master," Luo Chen's footsteps stopped at the entrance of the Daytime Void Hall.

"What are you still standing around for?" Nether Spring chirped beside him, her voice not particularly pleasant—unsurprising, given that her true form was a crow.

Luo Chen ignored her words, his gaze sweeping far across Green Parasol Mountain.

Especially at the hall on the opposite mountain peak facing the Daytime Void Hall—Truth Crossing Hall!

"Nether Spring, as the Truth Crossing Hall Master, my position shouldn't just be an empty title, should it?"

Nether Spring looked at him warily. "What do you want to do?"

The corners of Luo Chen's mouth lifted slightly. From his storage ring, he took out a large seal and a white jade bamboo slip that seemed neither bamboo nor jade.

The seal was the Truth Crossing Seal, the symbol of authority over Truth Crossing Hall. It could also be used to enter the only fifth-rank cave dwelling within Truth Crossing Hall.

The bamboo slip was the Demon Cultivator Register, recording all the powerful demon cultivators who had submitted to Green Parasol Mountain!

Those who could be called "demon cultivators" were by no means low-level demon beasts. Even third-rank demon kings were not qualified.

Only fourth-rank demon emperors could be called Sage-stage demon cultivators.

Because after transforming, demon emperors walked the same path of transformation and core formation as human cultivators, they were called Sage-stage demon clan cultivators, or Sage-stage demon cultivators for short.

With the seal in one hand and the register in the other, Luo Chen said with a hint of cruelty, "In the name of the Truth Crossing Hall Master, I should be able to command all the demon cultivators on this register, shouldn't I, Deputy Hall Master?"

Nether Spring frowned. "Indeed you can. This white jade bamboo slip was personally made by the Sovereign. All demon cultivators on the register must obey the Truth Crossing Hall Master's orders."

Speaking thus, as if thinking of something, she quickly added, "Except for me!"

Luo Chen glanced at her, a look of understanding appearing on his face.

"That's enough. That's already enough!"

Muttering these words, he left the Daytime Void Hall without looking back.

...

Three days later.

At the feather clan's sacred land, Green Parasol Mountain.

Luo Chen stood at the entrance of Truth Crossing Hall, watching as birds spread their wings and soared, flying out from Green Parasol Mountain toward the depths of the Million Mountains.

They came in groups of three or five, each squad carrying tasks issued by the Truth Crossing Hall Master—to go deep into the Million Mountains and invite the Sage-stage demon cultivators who had submitted to Green Parasol Mountain.

Nominally, it was to come and pay homage to the new Truth Crossing Hall Master, but Luo Chen's intention was certainly not limited to this.

If he wanted to deal with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, relying solely on his own connections and the Sage-stage experts of the Eastern Wilderness would still be somewhat insufficient.

If he added the demon emperors of the Eastern Wilderness, it should be enough.

It could almost be considered gathering the strength of an entire continent's experts!

Luo Chen's hand flickered, and Black King landed on the ground.

Little White also stood respectfully beside him.

"You two go as well!"

"Deliver my invitations to the major sects of the Eastern Wilderness, including the Eastern Sun Sect."

"Seven years from now, at Shouyang Mountain on Ling Pass, Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen will once again hold a Dao Discussion Assembly!"

Black King respectfully accepted the order. Running errands for Luo Chen was something he had done many times.

Little White hesitated for a moment, then asked, "What about the Luo Tian Sect?"

Luo Chen thought for a moment, then waved his hand. "No need to involve them. This battle is too dangerous. I don't want them to participate. Let them continue living well in the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land!"

Little White nodded, then stepped onto Black King's head.

"Master, we will definitely complete the mission!"

Black King seemed somewhat dissatisfied with Little White's presumptuous behavior, but dared not act recklessly in front of Luo Chen. He said to Luo Chen, "This one too," then carried Little White down Green Parasol Mountain, heading toward the thirty-six domains of the Eastern Wilderness.

Watching the birds fly into the distance and the black dragon burrow into the ground, Luo Chen exhaled a long turbid breath.

He turned and entered Truth Crossing Hall. Holding the Truth Crossing Seal, he headed toward the fifth-rank cave dwelling he had never set foot in before.

Though he had not yet ascended to Ascendant and had no plans to break through now, he still wanted to see what the fifth-rank spirit vein that everyone dreamed of was really like.

When the formation slowly opened before the spiritual light emitted by the Truth Crossing Seal, when the vast, ocean-like spiritual energy rushed toward his face, Luo Chen's eyes shimmering with brilliant golden light finally understood why everyone who broke through to Ascendant yearned for a fifth-rank spirit vein.

"So that's how it is."

(End of chapter)
