# Chapter 1053: Thousand Skies Sword Seed, Arctic Demon Seal

After completely withdrawing from the Lili Continent, the Frost Eagle Flying Shuttle spread its wings once more.

Xiaobai was still controlling the shuttle.

However, she was somewhat puzzled, so she took the opportunity to ask Luo Chen.

"Master, do you not need me to return to the Tiannan Continent to monitor and protect your clone anymore?"

Luo Chen, lost in thought, replied without raising his head. "No need."

"But..."

Knowing Xiaobai's confusion, Luo Chen looked up and said calmly, "He's already at the Adept stage and has enough ability to protect himself. Moreover, the Tiannan Dao Palace is the best protector. Since you can't get close to the Tiannan Dao Palace, there's no use for you there."

Xiaobai suddenly understood.

During the twenty years she had spent monitoring Zhou Fuyou, she indeed hadn't played much of a role.

With her cultivation level, she might have been able to fool ordinary cultivators, but the Tiannan Dao Palace had many experts within, and she simply couldn't enter.

Having secretly protected Zhou Fuyou until he reached the Adept stage was already enough.

Luo Chen's faint voice came again from beside her.

"Most importantly, Fu Qinglan has already seen you."

Luo Chen sighed, wondering how his clone had chosen to take Fu Qinglan as a master.

Was there some kind of karma at play in the unseen?

Back then, he had acted as Fu Qinglan's Dao protector, and now Fu Qinglan was passing on the Dao to "him."

But Luo Chen knew Fu Qinglan was a very clever woman. Through the traces he had left in the Burial God Valley and the matter of Black King, she had vaguely sensed his ill intentions toward the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

If Xiaobai continued to be exposed on the Tiannan Continent, it would inevitably lead Fu Qinglan to make deeper connections.

After all, the Zhou family that Zhou Fuyou came from had traveled to the Central Continent together with Luo Chen back then and had joined the Star Gate together.

With a faint sigh, Luo Chen took out a token.

Xiaobai looked at it curiously. "Master, what is this?"

Luo Chen explained, "Given to me by Li Canghai. The Arctic Yemo Heaven suffered severe terrain damage during the battle that destroyed the Primordial Demon Sect. Later, the Soul-Refining lineage's ancestor and Li Canghai successively set up two grand formations to seal the interior. Even though most of the demon beasts inside have escaped, those two grand formations are still functioning. If I want to enter the Arctic Yemo Heaven easily, I need Li Canghai's token."

Xiaobai nodded but then showed a puzzled expression.

"That senior seems very friendly toward you, Master?"

"Friendly?" Luo Chen's lips curled slightly, and he let out a light laugh tinged with a hint of sarcasm.

Xiaobai didn't understand. At least on the surface, their interaction had seemed quite amicable!

But in Luo Chen's view, that friendly meeting hadn't been as harmonious as it appeared.

From the very beginning, Li Canghai hadn't wanted to see outsiders, which was why he had set up the Mist-Hidden Mind-Confusing Grand Formation.

Even after Luo Chen broke through and demanded his spirit beast Black King, Li Canghai hadn't been friendly.

The change in attitude came after their exchange.

Only after Luo Chen used his master-level Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi to withstand a strike from a Ascendant-level cultivator did Li Canghai truly begin to value him.

In the world of immortal cultivation, the strong prey on the weak, and strength commands respect. This had been vividly demonstrated.

Because of his strength, Luo Chen had gained Li Canghai's recognition, acknowledging that he had the qualifications to communicate as an equal. And because Luo Chen had pointed out Li Canghai's true target—the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect—the two had become strategic friends, allowing them to converse with ease.

But at that point, it was still only recognition.

What truly counted as friendship came after the matter of the *Spirit-Nourishing Pill* pill formula.

Li Canghai had used that as a test, confirming Luo Chen's alchemy mastery, and only then developed a desire to befriend him.

If Luo Chen could truly advance to the Ascendant stage in the future, then an alchemist of the Saint level was someone no Ascendant great power would willingly make an enemy of.

The news that the Arctic Yemo Heaven contained a vast amount of Nine Heavens Firmament Qi was Li Canghai's gesture of goodwill and investment.

*"He wants to befriend me, but he's also afraid that if I lose this great killing move, I won't have enough strength to deal with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect in the future!"*

Luo Chen thought silently to himself, then returned to a room in the center of the shuttle.

After settling down, Luo Chen chanted the *Moonlight Cleansing Heart Mantra* to calm his spirit, and his divine sense slowly entered his Purple Mansion.

The Nascent Soul, which bore the exact same face as Luo Chen, opened its eyes at the same time.

The surface of the Nascent Soul was covered in a thick, solid suit of armor, its patterns ancient and natural, like the shell of a great turtle that had lived for countless ages.

In the Nascent Soul's hands was a small blood-colored sword.

On the blade, a blood groove ran horizontally, and a strange light flickered within it from time to time.

Because of this strange light, the fire spirits of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire and the Nirvana Sacred Flame nearby didn't want to get too close, as if they disliked it.

The sword was Yuantu, and upon closer inspection, that light was actually a sharp seed, like an awn.

A sword seed!

After reaching the master level in the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*, a sword seed had suddenly condensed.

Even after Luo Chen had exhausted all his Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi in his battle against Li Canghai, this sword seed still remained.

Now, as Luo Chen's Nascent Soul circulated its mana, the sword seed spun rapidly, actually generating strands of Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi once more.

If the founder of the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* had seen this, his eyes would have bulged and his jaw would have dropped.

A sword technique that was supposed to consume a large amount of sword qi in a single burst to achieve power comparable to a divine ability had, in Luo Chen's hands, gained the power of endless, unceasing regeneration!

Seeing the newly generated Scarlet Firmament Sword Qi, the little Nascent Soul figure showed a human-like smile.

The battle with Li Canghai had truly been immensely rewarding!

Not only had he made a friend, but he had also briefly measured his current strength. Most importantly, he had experimentally verified the function of the Thousand Skies Sword Seed!

*"I hope this trip to Yemo Heaven will also yield some gains."*

...

During the journey, Luo Chen wasn't solely immersed in cultivation. He also called out Black King to inquire about the current situation in the Northern Sea.

Black King had managed to escape from the Southern Ocean back then and had eventually returned to the Northern Sea.

"I first went to the Swallow Islands to check. Your Dao companion, Master, is still alive and well. Her cultivation level is neither high nor low, around the third layer of the Adept stage."

"Then, following the route I remembered, I returned to my hometown from when I was young."

"Along the way, I captured a few third-rank demon kings and roughly inquired about the situation in the Northern Sea. It's actually quite complicated. Demon kings at that level don't know much."

"Initially, the Northern Sea Demon Alliance launched a large-scale invasion of the human cultivation world. Because that old man Li Canghai didn't care or resist, large swaths of territory were occupied by the demon race. Many cultivators were forced to flee westward, and the demon race was ascendant for a time."

"But when the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's army arrived in the Northern Sea, the situation underwent a earth-shattering change."

"The Dao Sect's experts were too powerful. Wherever they passed, they were invincible. With Li Canghai's Cang Hai Righteous Path Alliance acting as a local collaborator, within just thirty years, eighty percent of the vast Northern Sea had fallen into the Dao Sect's hands."

"The few human cultivation territories still occupied by the demon race were the Myriad Immortals Assembly, where we once lived together, and the sea region that was once the Primordial Demon Sect's mountain gate."

"If things had continued like this, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect unifying the Northern Sea was beyond doubt. Even counterattacking the Demon Sea and taking the entire Northern Sea into their fold wouldn't have been impossible. But later..."

Later events, the entire world knew.

Rosycloud had attacked the Central Continent, nearly destroying the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, forcing the Dao Sect cultivators scattered across the four continents to hastily withdraw and defend.

Even the Northern Sea cultivation world, of which they had occupied eighty percent, was decisively abandoned.

Whether they were defending the Dao Sect or returning to fight for power and profit, Luo Chen didn't know. The only thing he knew was that the current Northern Sea cultivation world had fallen into the most chaotic era in its history.

Now, the Northern Sea cultivation world was even called the Sea of Chaos.

Li Canghai, this human great power, simply didn't bother with affairs. He even seemed to have reached some kind of tacit understanding with the two ancient demons of the Northern Sea.

The cultivators and demon beasts below were each carving out territories and building their own forces, attacking each other, gradually forming a chaotic and dangerous situation where they were intertwined.

If the ancestors of the Primordial Demon Sect, who had opened up the Northern Sea cultivation world, could see this scene, they would probably be resurrected from anger.

All the painstaking efforts to build such a grand situation had collapsed to this extent.

Of course, they might not be resurrected. Perhaps they weren't dead at all and were living well in the Immortal Realm?

Luo Chen didn't care about the situation in the Northern Sea cultivation world. The so-called racial divisions, in his view, were nothing more than that. All living things have spirits. After cultivating to a high realm, they are all beings pursuing the Great Dao. If he had spare strength, he would naturally protect one side. Clearly, he didn't have much extra strength at the moment.

What he cared about now was news of the Arctic Yemo Heaven.

The Northern Sea Demon Alliance had taken advantage of the opening of the Demon-Fall Land back then to channel the power of the Fallen Sea into the Arctic Yemo Heaven, breaking open a corner of the formations set up by the two great powers, and thus escaped.

But that didn't mean they had completely abandoned that most essential cultivation site in the Northern Sea.

From the nearest demon sect gateway, Taohai Immortal City, to the more distant Fulong Mountain Range's Myriad Immortals Assembly, all were under demon race control.

Including the Three Oceans defensive line in between!

"Master, the Myriad Immortals Assembly and the Three Oceans defensive line can be dealt with later. But to enter the Arctic Yemo Heaven, we must pass through the ruined city of Taohai. That won't be easy!" Black King said this, but his eyes clearly showed eagerness.

He had already learned from Xiaobai about Luo Chen's feat in the Western Desert, joining forces with the demoness Azure Frost to defeat a group of Buddhist experts. He wished he had been there himself.

Now, seeing that Luo Chen could even spar with that old man Li Canghai, it was clear his strength had advanced further.

This time, breaking into Yemo Heaven, why not go on a killing spree!

It would also vent the frustration he had been feeling lately.

However, Luo Chen just glanced at him faintly and said with some annoyance, "Don't stir up trouble. I don't want to fight recently!"

"Oh."

Black King backed down, lying dejectedly on the floor.

...

Taohai City!

Once the gateway of the Primordial Demon Sect, it had been called an Immortal City.

Later, after a great battle, it became a ruined city.

Now, a large number of demon beasts were entrenched within it. In the eyes of many cultivators, it was an out-and-out demon city.

There were some remnant treasures inside, but no cultivator dared to have any evil thoughts, because not only were there low-level demon beasts, but there was also a terrifying great demon emperor in residence.

In the Taohai Demon City, at the location of the former city lord's mansion, the solemn sound of chanting sutras could be heard from time to time.

If one got closer, they would even find brilliant Buddha light and a majestic, solemn appearance, as if it were a Buddhist temple.

Such a building was completely out of place in this demon city.

But no demon beast dared to object, because the one living inside was the strongest in the demon city—the demon monk Kongchan!

The demon monk Kongchan's original form was a well-known humanoid demon beast in the Northern Sea called the Chanting Monk.

Born understanding human speech, with an extremely peaceful yet extremely cruel temperament.

This demon monk Kongchan was an outstanding member of the Chanting Monk race. Not only had he successfully transformed, but he had also cultivated to the late Sage stage, making him a truly famous great demon emperor!

At this moment, inside the Buddha hall, Kongchan was silently chanting sutras, his prayer beads constantly turning.

When footsteps sounded outside the Buddha hall, his movements slowly stopped.

"Two fellow Daoists, you've finally arrived!"

Two figures walked into the Buddha hall.

One was dressed in blue with blue hair, handsome in appearance with an eerie temperament.

The other wore a wide robe with large sleeves, broad-shouldered and stout, with a gentle smile on his face.

Kongchan turned around, still sitting cross-legged on his cushion, looking at the two.

"Liujun, Taisui, long time no see!"

The two also bowed to Kongchan and each chose a cushion to sit on.

Kongchan looked at Taisui, a look of approval on his face. "This body you've seized seems to be a very good fit, Fellow Daoist. In just a hundred years, you've broken through to the Sage stage again."

Taisui snorted coldly and did not respond.

This human fleshly body with a supreme wood spirit root seemed to fit him well, but in reality, it was only so-so.

Far inferior to the other archaic beast body he had set his sights on back then.

Unfortunately, not only had that body escaped, but it had also taken his original form with it.

Now, after spending a hundred years, he had barely managed to form his Nascent Soul. It was truly unsatisfactory.

Kongchan then looked at the blue-haired, blue-clothed man. "Liujun, how are things on your end?"

Shark Emperor Liujun shook his head and sighed. "It's not easy. Channeling the power of the Fallen Sea into Yemo Heaven is simple, but pulling it back is definitely not something that us Sage-level demon cultivators can easily do."

"It has to be done, even if it's difficult!" Taisui gritted his teeth and said, "The Demon-Fall Land only opens once every three hundred years. The condition for its opening is absorbing enough spiritual energy. The Fallen Sea is an important part of the formation arrangement. If we can't guide that power back, forget three hundred years—it might be five hundred, or even a thousand years before it can be opened again!"

After speaking, Taisui's gaze swept over the two.

"Don't you two covet the legacy of the first-generation Void Refinement True Lord?"

This question made the breathing of both demon emperors quicken.

Especially Shark Emperor Liujun, who had been inside once and knew how many treasures there were, and how many good places outsiders had yet to truly explore.

Demon Monk Kongchan collected his mind and asked calmly, "Is there truly a method for Ascension inside?"

Taisui sneered, "If you don't believe me, do you not believe Liujun either? Or perhaps, you don't believe the intelligence that the Fallen Abyss Dragon Sovereign, who was trapped inside, has already become an Ascendant?"

The Fallen Abyss Dragon Sovereign!

He was a generation of strongmen among the demon race. He had charged into the Demon-Fall Land together with them back then, but unfortunately, he hadn't managed to escape later.

But according to Taisui's information, the Fallen Abyss Dragon Sovereign had already advanced to the Ascendant stage inside!

Shark Emperor Liujun nodded. "It's true. The reason he stayed in the Five Elements Heaven's Yue Canglan back then was because he sensed the power of the Water Origin within. Whether he was assimilated or truly comprehended it, there must certainly be an opportunity for Ascension there."

After confirmation once more, Demon Monk Kongchan's eyes showed a greedy light.

Currently, there were only two ancient demons in the Northern Sea.

If he could use the Demon-Fall Land to advance to the Ascendant stage, he could stand alongside the two great powers, Great Sage Beili and Sea Emperor Whale.

However, one problem stood before them.

The two grand formations set up by the Soul-Refining lineage's ancestor and Li Canghai were both extremely difficult to enter and exit. How could the three of them manage to pull back the power of the Fallen Sea and open the Demon-Fall Land ahead of time?

Just as they were troubled, Demon Monk Kongchan's expression suddenly changed. He looked up toward the depths of Taohai City.

There, a halo of light slowly rippled, and a portal seemed to open in the void.

"Someone is forcefully breaking through the Arctic Demon-Sealing Grand Formation!"

"Who is it?"

"I don't know, but there are few in the world who dare to forcefully break through such a formation. The last one was that madman!"

"Never mind that. Seize the opportunity. We're going in too!"

Without hesitation, the three transformed into beams of light and shot straight toward the portal that was about to close.
