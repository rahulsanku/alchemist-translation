# Chapter 751: Just as Now, Just This Thing, I, Luo Chen, Will Not Yield!

Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven stretched an unknown length and breadth, and no one knew where its borders lay.

The scenery within was incomparably real, seemingly no different from the outside world.

Five or six days had already passed since the grand competition began.

Outside a certain valley.

Five or six Adept cultivators stared warily at the scene inside the valley, where a dozen or so white argali sheep were resting in perfect ease.

They watched for a while before finally making up their minds.

"We are not as formidable as Senior Brother Wang, who can hunt illusion beasts alone with one sword. Cooperation would be better."

"First set up a formation, then break them one by one. Any objections?"

"How do we divide the illusion cores once we get them?"

"Equally! If the number is uneven, we divide them in order and make up the difference later. If someone unfortunately dies and is eliminated..."

At this point, Changfengzi paused.

Wu Tao beside him took over without hesitation. "Life and death are decreed by fate, riches and honor by Heaven. For those who leave, their spoils will be divided by those who remain. But between companions, no one may use despicable methods to deliberately plot harm!"

Changfengzi blinked in satisfaction, then looked toward the others.

"Do you agree to this plan?"

Of the other four, Guan Xiaoping was the first to respond, and after a moment's hesitation, the remaining three agreed as well.

Only, faintly, their positions drew closer together.

The Liu, Guan, and Wu families had always been on good terms. Even without using schemes and tricks, the strength they could wield by grouping together was not something the others could oppose alone.

"Good. Since that is settled, let us begin!"

Changfengzi wasted no more words. He took out an array disk and formation flags and handed them to the others to set up one by one.

He himself held a talisman and carefully flew into the air above the valley.

When everything was ready.

He immediately activated the talisman with magic power, and under the talisman's guidance, thread after thread flew in from all directions, covering the entire valley.

"Move!"

Changfengzi was the first to charge into the valley, and the others, unwilling to fall behind, immediately followed.

In an instant, the valley rang with a great clamor as magic treasures flew wildly.

Amid multicolored light, large swaths of white mist scattered outward.

Yet just as the white mist was about to fly out of the valley, it was blocked by the formation arranged in advance.

Half an hour later.

A slightly exhausted Changfengzi took out a gourd and opened its mouth toward the white mist floating in the air.

"Collect!"

Strands of white mist began to be sucked into the gourd, reluctant and unwilling.

After drawing in more than half, Changfengzi looked at the thick white mist still remaining and was still somewhat dissatisfied, but when he shook the gourd and heard the clear clinking sound within, a smile appeared on his face.

Guan Xiaoping leaned over excitedly. "Senior Brother, this time we may collect more than a dozen illusion cores!"

Changfengzi stroked his elegant long beard. "This is only the beginning."

Wu Tao also nodded excitedly. "Right, this is only the beginning. Later, we will collect even more illusion cores!"

The remaining two also came closer, but unlike the original three, there were now only two of them left.

They looked at each other, finding it somewhat impossible to accept.

They had only just begun, yet one person had already been lost.

If things continued later.

They could not keep cooperating with these three!

They had already vaguely sensed before that although Changfengzi and the others had not used any plots or tricks, when supporting other people, they had clearly held back.

They were plainly planning to wear down their companions in order to obtain more illusion cores!

The two exchanged a glance and already reached a tacit understanding.

First, they would take their share of these spoils. Afterward, they would part ways.

When the time came, the two of them could also cooperate, recruit others to hunt demon beasts together, and then imitate Changfengzi and the others' methods.

"Senior Brother, let us press on and condense the remaining cloud qi into illusion cores too!"

"That is precisely my intention. However, the strongest illusion beast here is at the late third tier. Condensing it will still take some effort."

Changfengzi said this.

After resting briefly, he formed hand seals and urged the gourd on, continuing to absorb the cloud qi in the valley.

One core, two cores...

More and more illusion cores condensed within the gourd, until only the largest mass of cloud qi remained.

It was at this moment.

Boom!

A giant hand of azure and red reached down from beyond the sky.

The formation covering the valley stirred in response, wanting to block it, but beneath that great hand condensed from vast magic power, it crumbled in an instant.

Inside the valley, all five people went deathly pale.

Amid his shock, Changfengzi blurted out, "Azure Yang Great Handprint!"

The hand condensed from magic power had slender knuckles and lifelike palm lines.

It gripped that largest mass of cloud qi, then withdrew.

High in the sky.

Fu Qinglan stared in surprise at the spell the man beside her had cast, released in an instant and without the slightest hint of strain.

There was actually someone who could cultivate a spell to such a freely controlled state?

Luo Chen opened his right hand, and a small white pellet, like a jujube pit, appeared in his palm.

"So this is an illusion beast's core?"

They had traveled at an unhurried pace for five days, slowly making their way in from the outer edge of Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven, and had finally encountered an illusion beast for the first time.

There were even people who had helped scatter its body in advance, saving him unnecessary effort.

Fu Qinglan smiled faintly. "This is only a third-tier illusion beast, so it cannot automatically condense an illusion core. To me, it is of no use. But if taken outside, capable people of my sect can use it to make magic treasures, pills, talismans, and so on. Magic treasures made from it in particular can generate large numbers of illusions, making them especially suitable for low-level disciples to temper their Dao hearts."

Luo Chen gazed at the illusion core in his hand, a thoughtful look appearing on his face.

He remembered very clearly that, back then, he had once picked up a treasure from a street stall in Tianlan.

Its name was the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror!

Although it had only been a damaged magic treasure, it was still one of the rare occasions in his life when he had found a bargain.

Now, sensing the strange power inside the illusion core, then combining it with Fu Qinglan's words, he faintly felt that the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror's main refining material might have been the core of an illusion beast.

In that case, the relationship between Ghost God Valley, the Adept great sect of the Eastern Wilderness, and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect of Central Continent became even more intriguing.

Then his thoughts turned again.

If he were to refine this illusion core himself, how should he begin?

Luo Chen still had some attainments in the way of artifact forging.

In a short time, he had already thought of several formations suitable for arranging on this illusion core. If he then found a carrier and combined it with methods of the soul path, perhaps he could recreate the wonders of the Ghost God Heart-Inquiring Mirror.

Fu Qinglan did not interrupt Luo Chen's thoughts. Later, hunting illusion beasts and collecting illusion cores would still require Luo Chen's efforts.

The more familiar he was with this object, the smoother things would be later.

However, the people below could no longer wait.

Changfengzi gritted his teeth and raised his head. "Martial Uncle Fu, Senior Luo... that is our spoil!"

Luo Chen glanced at him and remained unmoved.

Fu Qinglan, however, smiled. "Do not worry. Dao Brother Luo is only curious because this is his first time seeing it. He will return it to you shortly."

Afterward, she said quietly to Luo Chen, "Third-tier illusion cores are useless to me. Only fourth-tier illusion cores can aid my cultivation. If Dao Brother wishes to study such things, after the grand competition ends, this little sister can exchange a few for you from the sect."

Luo Chen gave a hum of acknowledgment, then raised his hand and tossed it.

In the valley, Changfengzi hurriedly caught this highest-grade illusion core.

When he raised his head again, Luo Chen and Fu Qinglan had already vanished from the sky.

He opened his mouth, wanting to say something, but the other two fellow disciples beside him were already impatient to divide the illusion cores.

Changfengzi naturally had no objection. He divided the other illusion cores among them, leaving only the one Luo Chen had helped condense for himself.

"Changfengzi, our cooperation ends here!"

"Farewell!"

Changfengzi was stunned and had no time to urge them to stay before the two hurriedly left.

Guan Xiaoping wrinkled her little nose. "Those two do not know what is good for them."

Changfengzi waved a hand. "No matter. Let them go. Later, if we meet fellow disciples, we can invite them in and hunt illusion beasts together."

Wu Tao chuckled. "Exactly. With them helping, hunting illusion beasts will be much easier for us. Once the number is enough, we will leave the grotto-heaven. When that time comes, our rankings in the grand competition will surely be ahead of theirs."

Changfengzi smiled and nodded, but that smile was rather forced.

He knew very well how powerful the formation he had arranged above the valley had been.

It was a powerful formation fully capable of withstanding one strike from a fourth-tier illusion beast!

Yet beneath Luo Chen's plain and unremarkable palm, it had not had the slightest power to resist.

"The former Devil Lord Azure Yang has actually grown this powerful. If the family head meets him, will there truly be no problem?"

...

After crossing the valley, Luo Chen and Fu Qinglan continued onward toward the center of Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

Every word spoken inside the valley entered his ears under the probing of his divine sense.

He had naturally recognized Changfengzi and the other two old acquaintances from the Northern Sea at a glance.

Back then, these three had still seemed rather immature. Now, after a trip to the Northern Sea and their return, their cultivation had not improved too much, but in terms of battle experience and scheming, they were enough to make one look at them anew.

Sure enough, it was just as he had judged before.

These younger-generation disciples of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had received excellent instruction since childhood, and their foundations were laid firmly. So long as they were given a little tempering, they could distinguish themselves.

By comparison, the Qi Refinement disciples of the Luo Tian Sect he had once run had needed to go onto the battlefield. That truly had been rather like pulling up shoots to make them grow.

But halfway through reflecting on this, Luo Chen laughed softly.

Times had been different, circumstances had been different, and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was such a colossal entity. How could a mere Luo Tian Sect have the conditions to cultivate disciples this way?

His reflection was somewhat laughable.

Fu Qinglan did not understand why he had suddenly laughed for no reason, and instead continued introducing the general situation of Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven from the side.

"On the outer edge of the grotto-heaven, large numbers of low-level illusion beasts are entrenched, but those are not our target."

"Fourth-tier illusion beasts are the top priority!"

"But illusion beasts are completely different from the demon beasts seen in the outside world. They are transformed from Mirage Dragon qi, gathering and scattering without form, their whereabouts uncertain. The more powerful an illusion beast is, the less likely it is to live in groups. They are scattered within the depths of the grotto-heaven, dormant and hidden."

"Finding fourth-tier illusion beasts is the first great difficulty."

"After we find one, killing it and collecting its illusion core will be even more troublesome. When the time comes, I will need Dao Brother to exert yourself greatly."

Luo Chen's expression was calm as he looked into the distance at the mountains and valleys, his face full of confidence.

"If late third-tier illusion beasts possess only that level of strength, then ordinary fourth-tier illusion beasts are, to me, merely there for the taking."

Fu Qinglan's eyes brightened. "In that case, I will trouble Dao Brother."

She increasingly felt that her grandfather choosing this person as her Dao protector had been the most correct decision possible.

All those previous doubts had long since vanished like smoke.

...

"Xiaofan, are you certain there is a fourth-tier illusion beast in this forest?"

"Absolutely!"

Outside a dense forest, two figures stood shoulder to shoulder.

One had swordlike brows and bright eyes, with a long sword on his back.

The other had exceptionally lively features and looked as young as a teenager.

Lin Bufan looked at the forest before them and spoke with assurance.

"Back then, when the Alchemy Saint cultivated for ascension on Heaven-Earth Peak, she drew in many spiritual impulses. Although I could not capture that kind of spiritual impulse, I remembered the feeling. Later, I lingered on Heaven-Earth Peak for nearly half a year and became very familiar with the scene when spiritual impulses appeared."

"When the Alchemy Saint ascended, the spiritual impulses she drew in were intercepted by the sect master. A portion of them was unexpectedly thrown by the sect master into Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven."

"These fourth-tier illusion beasts that hid themselves extremely well have all awakened and are absorbing those spiritual impulses!"

"Jingtang, make your move!"

Lin Jingtang trusted this younger-brother-like figure very much. Leaving battle strength and realm aside for the moment, the other party's talent in cultivation was something he could not hope to match.

If he said there was a fourth-tier illusion beast inside, then his words naturally had substance.

The long sword on his back was slowly drawn.

"In that case, let me find it, seize its core for your cultivation, and help you take another step forward on the great path to Ascendant."

As he spoke, he stepped into the verdant forest.

The moment he entered, the creatures inside sensed his piercing killing intent.

In an instant, large numbers of vines came rustling from above and below.

Lin Jingtang was unafraid. The treasured sword in his hand danced without pause, scattering sword light after sword light, and all the vines shattered.

He advanced all the way, with Lin Bufan following closely behind.

Before long.

"There!"

"It has disguised itself so well!"

Lin Jingtang praised it, then slashed diagonally with his treasured sword, sending an enormous sword light toward a withered tree.

At this moment, the withered tree abruptly came alive!

A length of branch broke through the air and struck sideways.

The powerful force directly smashed the sword light apart.

Not only that, still more branches rose up, blocking out the sky and covering the entire forest.

Lin Jingtang drew a deep breath. "Xiaofan, retreat a little for now. This thing is troublesome, and it also seems unaffected by my Sage domain. I will need to spend some more effort to deal with it."

Lin Bufan nodded and quickly retreated some distance.

Without needing to look after him, Lin Jingtang had no more misgivings, and all his abilities unfolded in full.

Within the vast forest, terrifying fluctuations of magic power immediately erupted.

...

Clang!

With the sound of a bell, the ancient forest stretching a hundred li turned into powder at a speed visible to the naked eye.

As the powder drifted, a streak of white light suddenly flew out.

The next moment, Lin Jingtang, with swordlike brows and bright eyes, soared into the sky holding a great golden bell in his hand.

"Where do you think you are running!"

The great bell released golden light that shone from afar upon that streak of white light, leaving it nowhere to hide.

Lin Jingtang held the great bell and pursued from a distance, keeping it from leaving the range of his divine sense.

The two, one ahead and one behind, chased each other without pause.

Until, within Lin Jingtang's divine sense, another enormous divine sense like the sun at high noon appeared.

His face changed, and he shouted explosively, "You dare!"

The answer to him was a soft laugh.

"Why would I not dare? Such a treasure naturally belongs to the fated."

Lin Jingtang's face turned ashen. In his divine sense, he saw, a thousand li away, a middle-aged man in white robes with gray hair reach toward that streak of white light from afar.

Within the boundless cloud layer, a colossal claw that blotted the sky immediately appeared.

Lin Jingtang's pupils shrank. Was this a spell, or a body technique?

But immediately, a sneer curled at the corner of his mouth.

If the man thought he could subdue a fourth-tier illusion beast with this, he was thinking far too simply.

Sure enough, that sky-filling claw emerging from the clouds had clearly already seized that streak of white light, but in the next instant, white cloud qi seeped out through the gaps between the claw's five fingers.

The cloud qi that seeped out gathered back into one in the blink of an eye.

Luo Chen gave a soft sound of surprise.

Even the Cloud-Seizing Divine Claw, used with his full strength, could not hold this heavily injured fourth-tier illusion beast?

However, he was not entirely unprepared.

With a thought, his Sage domain enveloped heaven and earth.

The white light's escape speed remained the same, completely unaffected.

Luo Chen frowned. Even a Sage domain was useless?

"What exactly is this illusion beast? It seems I must show something real."

He made no visible movement, merely raising his right hand upright before his chest and forming a seal.

The next moment, multicolored light spread from within his body, then transformed in an instant into thick scarlet chains.

"Lock!"

With a soft shout, nine scarlet chains thrust straight into the void and locked down that streak of white light.

No matter how it struggled, it could not break free.

In the end, it revealed its true body, and it was shockingly a white withered tree.

A fourth-tier illusion beast with such an appearance?

Amid Luo Chen's surprise, he saw a handsome man arrive before him.

Looking at the great bell, realization dawned in his heart.

"The Fright Bell. You are Lin Bufan's Dao protector, Lin Jingtang?"

Lin Jingtang first glanced at the withered-tree illusion beast locked by the nine scarlet chains, his heart filled with astonishment. After his life-bound magic treasure, the Fright Bell, advanced to a true artifact, its suppressive power had greatly increased.

It was precisely because of this that the family head had specially sent him to serve as Lin Bufan's Dao protector, so that he could suppress and collect the cores of illusion beasts.

Yet he had not expected that an illusion beast he had failed to suppress even after exhausting tremendous effort would actually be captured by the other party with one casual method.

His gaze finally fell on the white-robed, gray-haired man.

"Wilderness Wanderer!"

"Since you know I am protecting Lin Bufan, you should know that Xiaofan and Fu Qinglan, whom you protect, are friends."

"Since they are friends, how can you cut in and steal another's love?"

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow.

"Is that so?"

Behind him, Fu Qinglan slowly emerged.

According to their agreement, anything related to battle was to be decided by Luo Chen.

But when it involved her friends...

Fu Qinglan nodded. "Little Lin is indeed on good terms with me. Since this illusion beast was discovered by them first, then let us yield it to..."

Luo Chen laughed.

His laugh was somewhat dismissive, revealing such clear mockery.

Lin Jingtang, who had originally relaxed, immediately had a bad premonition.

Sure enough!

"If he is a friend, you must yield. Then if it were your fellow senior and junior disciples, would you yield?"

"If it were the three disciples of the Great Houses, especially when facing Shi Ju, would you yield as well?"

"This object concerns your opportunity to reach Ascendant. Can you simply hand it over?"

"Fu Qinglan, since you bear the responsibility of reviving your family, you should know that, at many times, many things cannot be yielded."

"Just as now! Just this thing!"

Ever since arriving in Central Continent, Luo Chen had always been calm and indifferent, yet at this moment, he seemed extremely aggressive.

Sentence after heavy sentence, question after question, caught Fu Qinglan completely unprepared.

Clatter...

The nine scarlet chains wrapped around the withered-tree illusion beast and flew toward Luo Chen, while a seal had already formed in his hand, his eyes filled with icy coldness.

"Since I am her Dao protector, then within this grotto-heaven, in all matters concerning her great path, I, Luo Chen, will not yield!"

*(End of Chapter)*
