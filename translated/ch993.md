# Chapter 993: Xuan Du Preaches the Dao, Third Visit to Green Parasol Mountain

**Pure Land of Lapis Lazuli.**

Within the Moonlight Lapis Lazuli Grand Hall.

Elder Xuan Du sat cross-legged at the upper seat, with Luo Chen and Azure Frost seated to his left and right. Below the hall sat sixteen monks whose auras were withered and whose kasayas were stained with blood.

Luo Chen could feel the scorching intensity of the hateful gazes behind him, as if they wanted to bore holes through him.

In this battle, the Hanging Temple had come with overwhelming force, deploying their finest elites.

Yet in a single battle, most of the thirty-six eminent monks had fallen by his sword, and the most talented head of the Prajna Hall had died at Azure Frost's hands.

To say they bore no hatred would be far too naive.

But Luo Chen had no guilt.

Having accepted a commission, he was loyal to the task. He had done his utmost, regardless of right or wrong.

Above.

Elder Xuan Du spoke unhurriedly: "Fellow junior brothers, how can we cultivators harbor hearts full of hatred and resentment? We must abstain from anger and wrath."

His voice was slow and measured, carrying the resonance of Buddhist chanting.

It gradually calmed people's hearts.

Although Luo Chen could still feel some hateful gazes, they were indeed much fainter than before.

He twitched the corner of his mouth, ignored them, and looked up at Elder Xuan Du.

"Great Master, have you achieved the Ascendant realm?"

Elder Xuan Du first nodded, then shook his head.

This behavior left Luo Chen uncertain.

Elder Xuan Du sighed. "Achieved, yet not achieved."

Luo Chen frowned. Recalling what Azure Frost had said earlier, he had a vague guess.

Elder Xuan Du's current state was indeed at the Ascendant realm, but it probably couldn't be maintained for long.

That Buddha's Left Hand had indeed gravely wounded him just before his great accomplishment was complete.

Now appearing well was merely a castle in the air, a flower in a mirror.

"No matter. This current state is enough to provide the power I need for what comes next. Our cooperation with you two can be considered complete."

"This humble monk thanks you!"

Azure Frost nodded slightly.

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Since it's cooperation, there's no need for thanks. Great Master is too courteous."

Elder Xuan Du squeezed out a smile. "Next, I will send you to the Buddha Mother Temple first. You can activate the teleportation formation to leave the Western Desert. As for what happens afterward, that is internal Buddhist sect affairs and has nothing to do with you."

Luo Chen and Azure Frost exchanged glances, visibly relieved.

The other party hadn't broken their agreement—this was the best outcome.

But when the other eminent monks heard this, some with weaker minds clearly showed dissatisfaction.

Elder Xuan Du pretended not to see and instead began discussing some insights on advancing to the Ascendant realm.

As expected, once he started speaking, the Buddhist monks forgot about Luo Chen and Azure Frost, focusing entirely on listening.

Luo Chen and Azure Frost were somewhat awkward being in the hall, but since Elder Xuan Du hadn't chased them out, they simply thickened their faces and stayed to listen attentively.

The Buddhist path to Ascension involved attaining Buddhahood and reaching the Arhat realm, which differed greatly from demons advancing to Ancient Demon or cultivators advancing to Ascendant.

But stones from other mountains can polish jade. Ultimately, it was all about the transformation of the soul and control over heaven and earth's origin qi.

Therefore, Luo Chen and Azure Frost listened carefully and gained much benefit.

Time passed unknown, and Elder Xuan Du's sermon had reached its end.

He looked at his surviving fellow disciples with pity in his eyes.

"This humble monk's breakthrough borrowed the power of the spirit treasure Pure Land of Lapis Lazuli, so there are many imperfections. You may trust these experiential insights, but you cannot fully believe them. I hope you will carefully consider them in light of your own circumstances."

The group of monks awoke as if from a dream, chanting Buddha's name in unison, grateful for Xuan Du's sermon.

This scene appeared quite strange.

Earlier they had been shouting about killing, yet in an instant they were harmonious as before.

Perhaps this was what they called the Buddhist path!

Or perhaps it was related to Elder Xuan Du himself having originally come from the Hanging Temple.

Luo Chen remembered what Xuan Du had said and bowed respectfully.

"Thank you for your guidance, Great Master."

Elder Xuan Du smiled slightly. "It is good that the benefactor has gained some understanding. If you achieve something in the future, simply light a stick of incense for the Buddha."

Luo Chen was startled, not understanding the meaning.

Elder Xuan Du did not explain further. He casually drew a line in the void.

Azure Frost sensed it and turned to look.

Within the Pure Land of Lapis Lazuli, a door of light appeared.

"The Buddha Mother Temple has arrived."

Luo Chen and Azure Frost rose simultaneously and walked toward the hall's exit.

Behind them followed over a dozen gazes with complex meanings, some containing hatred.

The two acted as if they hadn't noticed. With this matter concluded, what did a little hatred matter?

Their goal was merely to save several years of effort and return to the Eastern Wilderness in one go.

Watching their departing figures, Elder Xuan Du hesitated for a moment, then called out to stop Luo Chen.

"Benefactor Luo Chen."

"Hmm?" Luo Chen turned around in confusion.

Elder Xuan Du looked at him seriously. "Your karma of killing is too heavy. Your cultivation path will inevitably be filled with tribulations. You should practice the *Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Mantra* more diligently."

Luo Chen laughed heartily. "Thank you for the reminder, Great Master. Luo will naturally cultivate diligently."

Having said this, he and Azure Frost passed through the door of light and left this pure land stained with blood.

Elder Xuan Du sighed, unsure if the other had truly taken his words to heart.

He slowly withdrew his gaze. Feeling the eyes of his fellow disciples, he pressed his palms together.

"It is time to see my senior brother..."

---

The battle in the Pure Land of Lapis Lazuli gradually spread as Great Brahma Heavenly Son and the other high-level cultivators returned to the Monan Royal Court.

The might of the demoness Mara was astonishing.

The two had joined forces to overpower the eminent monks of the Hanging Temple.

Especially Luo Chen of the Danxia Mountain from the Eastern Wilderness—his killing intent was immense, his demonic might so fierce it was rarely seen in the world.

In a single battle, fully twenty eminent monks had died directly by his hand, including the Monan strongman, the head of the Tuluo Assembly.

The name "Three-Headed Mara" would gradually spread across this land in the years to come.

But for the present, the impact was not that great, because everyone's attention was focused on the Hanging Temple.

The Pure Land of Lapis Lazuli had descended outside the Green Kingfisher Screen!

The newly ascended Arhat, Elder Xuan Du, was confronting his senior brother Mahakala, intending to seal the mountain gate!

---

"Can he succeed?"

Azure Frost, who was channeling the power of the Peacock Buddha Mother statue to slowly activate the teleportation formation, paused slightly at the question behind her.

"Do you want him to succeed?"

"If he doesn't succeed, he'll die." Luo Chen sighed. "If I wasn't mistaken at the time, Xuan Du's realm was unstable. He only had the strength for one battle. If he fails, it will be the end of his path."

Azure Frost's eyelashes trembled. "So?"

Luo Chen shrugged. "So naturally I hope he succeeds. After all, we indirectly caused Mahakala such great trouble. If Xuan Du fails, Mahakala might come looking for us in the future."

Azure Frost said flatly, "I'm not afraid."

She had the backing of Green Parasol Mountain, also a sacred land. She truly wasn't afraid of Mahakala.

Luo Chen was helpless. "You're not afraid, but I am! After all, he's an Ascendant great power. If he really comes knocking, what am I supposed to do? Besides, you and I are now bound by life and death. Can't you wish me well?"

The corner of Azure Frost's mouth lifted slightly, forming a beautiful curve. "After I rescue my sister, I'll have her undo the life-and-death gu."

Luo Chen pretended to be indifferent. "She can try! Although it's only a fourth-rank life-and-death gu, once bound, unless you and I advance to the Ascendant realm, there's no way to undo it."

Faced with these words, Azure Frost fell silent.

The already quiet temple became even more still, with only the sound of the Peacock Buddha statue slowly turning echoing through the space.

Suddenly.

"Are you very afraid of my sister?"

Luo Chen chuckled. "Listen to you. Who in this world isn't afraid of Rosycloud?"

Azure Frost's hand fell on her chest. "Back in the Sunken Sand Valley underground cavern, you and I seemed locked in a death struggle, but in reality, you could only severely wound me, while you could destroy my heart. With the Nether Soul Pearl empowered by your physical strength, once I died, my soul would be like a candle in the wind. In that situation, you could have killed me, but you didn't. Instead, you proposed those seemingly harsh conditions that benefited both sides. You were wary of my sister!"

This time, it was Luo Chen's turn to fall silent.

Because Azure Frost was not wrong.

From the fact that Rosycloud was willing to risk danger alone and force her way into Broken Soul Cliff to save Azure Frost, it was clear that Azure Frost was extremely important to Rosycloud—far more than just face.

Not knowing whether Rosycloud was still alive, he simply didn't dare kill Azure Frost.

Having witnessed firsthand the battle where Rosycloud destroyed the Nether Origin Sect, Luo Chen had long formed a deep impression that this demon was not to be fought head-on.

He didn't want to provoke such a great enemy.

And Azure Frost, who had been nearly mad at the time, had been trying to kill him at every turn.

Only under conditions of mutual destruction could they reach a life-and-death pact. That way, even if Rosycloud was still alive, she wouldn't kill him for his last-minute betrayal on Broken Soul Cliff.

Faced with Luo Chen's silence, Azure Frost said no more.

She only gave Luo Chen a deep look, then activated the teleportation formation.

Light flickered. The two figures silently stepped into it and vanished in an instant.

---

**Eastern Wilderness!**

**Green Parasol Mountain!**

Once the sacred land of the Feather Clan, now the only demon sacred land among the million mountains.

But unlike the imagined bustle, over the past ten years it had become increasingly cold and lonely.

In the sky, flocks of birds were no longer seen soaring.

Among the mountain ranges, grass and trees had withered and yellowed, as if their vitality had been forcibly drained.

Even so, the three majestic palaces still stood tall.

Suddenly!

At the location of the Daytime Void Hall, a pillar of light shot into the sky.

Several powerful auras sensed it and cast their divine sense over.

"Is the Daytime Void Hall Lord back?"

Figures left their cave dwellings one after another, flying toward the Daytime Void Hall. But upon arrival, they were blocked by the powerful formation and could only wait quietly.

Deep underground.

Above the massive teleportation formation, a wisp of green smoke drifted up.

Luo Chen looked at the ground in confusion.

"Why destroy this corner?"

Azure Frost descended from the platform, explaining as she walked. "When Xuan Du sent us away, the Buddha Mother Temple teleportation point was already exposed. To prevent complications, it's better to sever the connection between the two formations first."

Having said this, she turned back to stare at Luo Chen.

"This trick was learned from you."

Luo Chen was speechless. She was clearly referring to what he had done at the Northern Sea teleportation point.

Even now, a corner of the Northern Sea teleportation formation was still in his storage pouch.

But based on Rosycloud's later return to Green Parasol Mountain, the Northern Sea teleportation formation had probably been repaired.

He didn't expect her to still remember that.

He smiled awkwardly, descended the platform, and walked outward.

The two walked out one after the other, a scene quite reminiscent of when Azure Frost had "invited" Luo Chen to Green Parasol Mountain.

But this scene was no longer comparable to those days.

Soon, the two arrived above the Daytime Void Hall.

The hall's formation opened, and a figure flew straight in.

"Azure Frost, I knew you were still alive!"

Hearing this voice, Azure Frost rarely showed a hint of a smile.

"Nether Spring..."

Before she could greet him, Nether Spring saw the man standing to the side and blurted out.

"Luo Chen!"

Then, Nether Spring flew into a rage.

"Seize him!"

A figure turned into a thunderbolt, breaking through the air from outside the hall.

Luo Chen frowned. Raising a hand, he formed a massive palm print that seemed slow yet was actually fast, pushing out flatly.

Silently, the thunderbolt came as fast as it went.

Nether Spring was stunned, looking at Luo Chen in disbelief.

Only then did Azure Frost finish her words. "Nether Spring, don't act rashly first."

Nether Spring said hatefully, "This kind of turncoat! Even if he doesn't appear on Green Parasol Mountain, I would find an opportunity to go out and kill him, let alone now that he's right in front of me."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. "Turncoat? Where does that come from?"

Nether Spring sneered. "On Broken Soul Cliff, you turned your coat and attacked Lord Yuan Jun and Azure Frost. Do you think the world doesn't know?"

"Where did this news come from?"

The one asking this question was not Luo Chen, but Azure Frost.

Facing Azure Frost's somewhat expectant gaze, Nether Spring took a deep breath and said, "This was personally spoken by Lian Cheng, a great power from the Tianyuan Dao Sect in the Central Continent. The news has already spread throughout the Eastern Wilderness."

Upon hearing this, Azure Frost's face instantly turned deathly pale.

If Lian Cheng was still alive, then her sister...

But before she could think further, Nether Spring quickly told her good news.

"Lord Yuan Jun returned several years ago. Don't worry."

Azure Frost shifted from sorrow to joy. Without hesitation, she rushed out of the Daytime Void Hall and flew toward the Profound Mystery Hall.

Inside the hall, upon hearing that Rosycloud had already returned, Luo Chen's expression changed slightly.

Seeing Nether Spring still glaring at him like a tiger watching its prey, Luo Chen couldn't help but smile bitterly. "The inside story of this matter is quite convoluted and hard to explain in a short time. In short, I have my difficulties and there are misunderstandings. Lord Nether Spring, why must you be so aggressive?"

"Difficulties? Misunderstandings? Aggressive!" Nether Spring's face twisted into a cold smile. "Since it's hard to explain in a short time, then stay and explain clearly!"

As he spoke these words, several figures appeared one after another at the entrance of the Daytime Void Hall.

There was Pure Crane, an old acquaintance, and several unfamiliar Sage-level demon cultivators.

With heavy footsteps, a furious Thunder Prison walked in.

He was the one Luo Chen had just sent flying with a palm strike.

Thunder Prison had never imagined that his sudden attack would be so easily repelled. For someone as proud as he was, this was absolutely unacceptable.

And of all people, it was Luo Chen!

Thunder Prison stared fixedly at Luo Chen, his aura swelling, lightning flickering all over his body, looking as if he wanted to devour someone.

Faced with powerful enemies on all sides, Luo Chen did not seem particularly nervous. He sighed and said, "If things were as you imagine, why would I return together with Lord Azure Frost?"

Nether Spring was stunned.

Just then, a vast divine sense descended from the sky.

All the demon cultivators knelt in unison.

"We pay homage to Lord Yuan Jun!"

Luo Chen's expression changed slightly. He bowed deeply. "I pay homage to Lord Yuan Jun."

That divine sense fixed on Luo Chen for a moment before disappearing.

But even in that brief instant, cold sweat broke out on Luo Chen's forehead.

For someone who had achieved success in body refinement, this was something he would never have imagined possible, showing the immense pressure Rosycloud brought.

After Rosycloud's divine sense departed, Nether Spring seemed to have received instructions. He looked at Luo Chen with a complex expression.

"Come with me!"
