# Chapter 989: Soaring Strength, Instant Defeat of Three Cultivators

Within the Glazed Pure Land, a sudden heaven-shaking roar arose.

From within to without, it rolled forth like a vast ocean.

The first to notice was naturally Zen Master Xuandu, who was in the midst of breaking through to the Ascendant realm.

With but a single thought, the scene within the boundless pagoda forest was completely reflected in his mind.

The moment he saw Luo Chen's true form, he couldn't help but frown slightly.

"Why do the three heads neither share the same visage nor face the same direction?"

"This golden body is clearly constructed with Buddhist divine powers, yet it carries a hint of demonic and eerie strangeness."

"What exactly has he cultivated into something?"

Luo Chen's mastery of *Three Heads and Six Arms* had been far too swift, to the point that even Zen Master Xuandu, who had bestowed this technique, could not fathom whether Luo Chen's cultivation direction was right or wrong.

But at this moment, there was no time for Zen Master Xuandu to worry or regret.

Luo Chen's roar, following the collapse of the Eight Cold Minor Hells, had already spread throughout the entire Glazed Pure Land.

The group of experts who had entered this place immediately felt an overwhelming, sky-toppling power rushing toward them.

Brahma Heavenly Son shot into the air, crossing layer upon layer of pagoda forest, and gazed at that "monster."

With just one look, his pupils involuntarily constricted.

"Three Heads and Six Arms?"

"Not right!"

As the strongest cultivator of the Monan Royal Court, and a grand cultivator who could be considered outstanding even in the entire Western Desert, he had seen others use this divine power before.

But never had he seen anyone so bizarre.

Behind him, the three leaders Xiao Yan, Tanpo, and Tuluo had already caught up, standing far away as they gazed at the giant roaring up to the heavens.

In each other's eyes, shock involuntarily surfaced.

At that moment!

One of the heads of that three-headed, six-armed monster looked over from afar, its crimson eyes and golden pupils blazing with fierce divine light.

"Is this the enemy who seeks to hinder Xuandu's breakthrough?"

In a rumbling echo, the monster's six arms simultaneously formed hand seals, and a sea of heaven and earth spiritual energy converged toward it.

No need for further words!

In the blink of an eye, the spell took shape instantly.

A massive palm imprint struck through the air, pushing forward a thousand zhang, its target directly aimed at Brahma Heavenly Son.

Not only that, behind that enormous palm imprint, a fire phoenix spread its wings, scattering patches of fiery meteors, exceptionally majestic. A flame dragon descended into the world, roaring as if about to annihilate all.

Azure palm, fire phoenix, flame dragon—three techniques unleashed simultaneously, targeting four people!

Brahma Heavenly Son's eyes gleamed with sharp light. "Facing four alone—what boldness!"

Without hesitation, he likewise struck out a palm, the surrounding heaven and earth spiritual energy moving with him, forming a swastika palm imprint.

It was the Brahma Sacred Seal, the technique that had made Brahma Heavenly Son famous!

One azure and one golden, two enormous palm imprints collided under an undeniable impact.

*Boom!*

The extreme collision of dharma power, the vast release of spiritual energy, stirred everyone's robes to flutter loudly and their faces to tremble.

The other two techniques were not so easily dealt with.

Xiao Yan and Tanpo, having a good private relationship, acted in tacit understanding, striking out together and unleashing their dharma treasures, intending to receive the spreading-winged fire phoenix.

That bitter-faced, gaunt Tuluo leader, half-unrobed, revealed knotted muscles and punched toward the descending flame dragon!

*Boom!*

*Boom!*

Almost simultaneously, violent explosions resounded throughout the Glazed Pure Land.

Then, two figures spat blood and retreated dozens of zhang in disarray.

Tuluo leader also grunted, his feet forcibly sinking deep into the earth.

Only Brahma Heavenly Son still stood in the distance, his hands clasped behind his back.

But if one looked closely, one could clearly see a slight tremor in the right hand that had struck out.

He frowned slightly and immediately called out loudly, "Fellow Daoist, you misunderstand!"

Amidst the hazy currents of qi, a figure suddenly flew out of the white pagoda forest.

No longer the strange three-headed, six-armed appearance, only a tall, upright figure outlined by a white Daoist robe, a handsome face beneath a Ping Mountain crown, and a pair of eyes that, though their divine light was restrained, seemed capable of seeing through all things in the world.

The moment Luo Chen emerged from the forest, his gaze fell upon the four people.

"A misunderstanding? Could it be that you are not here to hinder Zen Master Xuandu?"

Brahma Heavenly Son looked at Luo Chen's original form, especially comparing it to the earlier monster, and immediately felt a three-part admiration.

He immediately explained, "I revere Buddhism, but I am not blindly faithful. Entering this time was merely to witness the famed Pure Land scenery."

"Oh? Is that so?" Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, then smiled ambiguously as he looked at the other three. "And what about you?"

Xiao Yan glared angrily. He was a nation's sovereign, born noble, and had never been so embarrassed.

The other party had started fighting without a word—truly a brute.

Especially since he and Tanpo had joined forces and still barely failed to receive that move—it was an utter disgrace.

But before he could speak, the extremely charming Tanpo pulled him back.

She shook her head solemnly at him, then looked at Luo Chen and gave a graceful bow.

"Fellow Daoist, you worry too much. We two also merely came in for a look."

Luo Chen gave an "oh," his gaze falling upon the ascetic monk who was deeply embedded in the ground, his golden body mottled.

Tuluo leader raised his head, meeting Luo Chen's gaze with neither humility nor arrogance.

"Originally a transcendent expert from beyond, how could I degenerate into a demonic heretic?"

Luo Chen smiled freely. "It seems this fellow Daoist has come to subdue demons and vanquish evil. In that case, I have no hesitation in striking."

Amidst his laughter, under the horrified gazes of the crowd, his figure suddenly vanished.

Only a few scattered, brilliant sparks of fire remained in place.

Tuluo leader's expression changed drastically, and he instinctively punched toward the space before him.

*Boom!*

This punch seemed to collide with hard reef, shaking out endless qi force.

Amidst the spreading sparks, Luo Chen's figure slowly materialized.

In the distance, Brahma Heavenly Son murmured softly, "It's the Fire Concealment Art."

Xiao Yan and Tanpo exchanged glances, looking at each other in bewilderment.

They had not seen few Five-Phase Escape Arts, but never such a brilliant Fire Concealment Art.

Without the slightest trace of smoke or fire, without any visible hand seals or incantations, he had simply vanished like a ghost into the void.

How was this done?

They did not know. Only Brahma Heavenly Son's face showed a flicker of doubt.

Ordinary Fire Concealment Arts naturally could not achieve such a level.

But if it were someone who had comprehended the origin of fire, they could naturally capture the ubiquitous fire-attribute spiritual energy between heaven and earth and then conceal themselves with ease.

Rumor had it that the Eastern Wilderness' Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen was indeed a peerless genius who had comprehended the true meaning of laws ahead of time. Could this be the origin of fire?

Their doubts went unanswered.

And as for Tuluo leader, the one directly involved, he felt an unmatched, overwhelming force surging toward him like a mountain toppling and a sea overturning.

Moreover, a crude and primitive power, following the point of contact between the two, surged through his entire body.

Sleeves rolled back, shattering inch by inch.

Sinews and muscles bulged, blood boiled.

Seeing this, Luo Chen raised an eyebrow.

"An ascetic cultivation method? Quite the defense!"

With a word of praise, Luo Chen suddenly withdrew his foot, then closed in.

His entire form turned into a blurry shadow, continuously stepping with ghostly footwork, circling around Tuluo leader.

Though his figure was ethereal and ghostly, his strikes were like a violent storm.

Fingers, palms, fists, claws, flicks, slaps, pinches, smashes—eight moves in succession, with source power infused, then detonated together.

Overlord Removes Armor!

Having done all this, Luo Chen drifted backward.

Tuluo leader still stood in place, his eyes somewhat dazed.

The next moment!

*Bang! Bang! Bang!*

On his gaunt yet powerful fleshly body, one after another, bursts of blood mist exploded.

"Ah!"

A scream rang out simultaneously.

In the air, Brahma Heavenly Son's pupils contracted.

"Tuluo leader's Chandana Golden Body has been broken!"

As the most famous body-tempering expert of the Monan Royal Court, Tuluo leader's Chandana Golden Body was renowned far and wide. It was said to be a powerful Leakless Golden Body capable of withstanding true artifacts.

This was evident from how he had alone withstood Luo Chen's Flame Dragon Descends earlier.

Yet, after only two exchanges, it had been broken by the other party.

"If it were me, how many moves would it take?" Brahma Heavenly Son asked himself, but the answer was already clear—at least over ten moves.

"Don't!"

"No!"

Exclamations came from the side.

Brahma Heavenly Son looked again. At the tip of the white-robed Daoist's finger, a sharp and fierce white-gold sword qi had already formed.

He was about to commit murder!

With his golden body broken and his astral qi venting outward, Tuluo leader knelt awkwardly on the ground, watching the white-robed Daoist approach him step by step, despair in his eyes.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's steps halted.

He looked helplessly toward the distant, moonlit Buddhist temple.

"Master, you asked me to protect you, yet you also ask me to hold back. That's quite difficult to manage!"

Even as he said this, the white-gold sword qi in Luo Chen's hand had indeed dissipated.

He glanced at Tuluo leader, gave a light snort, and without further mockery, flew alone to a pavilion.

Xiao Yan and Tanpo exchanged glances, then carefully descended, helped Tuluo leader up, and took out pills to heal his wounds.

Though they indulged in pleasure, they also believed in and served the Buddha.

Ascetics like Tuluo leader were greatly admired by them.

Brahma Heavenly Son glanced at them, then walked alone into the pavilion.

"Fellow Daoist says you are protecting Zen Master Xuandu. May I ask who the enemy is?"

Luo Chen looked up at this tall, splendidly robed man who resembled a heavenly god, and the corners of his mouth lifted slightly.

"You know my origins, yet I do not know your name. Shouldn't you first introduce yourself?"

Brahma Heavenly Son was startled, then laughed silently.

He was quite famous in the Western Desert, and few high-level cultivators did not know him, so he had subconsciously forgotten this.

This Luo Chen had come from outside the continent and had not risen for very long; it was normal that he did not know him.

He performed a Daoist salute and said solemnly, "Lord of the Monan Ludou Kingdom, the world calls me Brahma Heavenly Son."

Hearing the other's Dao title, Luo Chen couldn't help but regard him more highly.

Although Brahma Heaven was not the mainstream Buddhist faith of the Western Desert, it was still known as the creator god in its branch.

For the other party to use this name as his Dao title and still be alive and well showed that his abilities were no small matter.

Especially since he had alone received one of Luo Chen's moves earlier, which further demonstrated his extraordinary nature.

Seeing that the other party had proactively extended goodwill, Luo Chen waved his hand, and a wine flagon appeared on the stone table of the pavilion.

Seeing this, Brahma Heavenly Son smiled bitterly. "This is, after all, a Buddhist Pure Land. Drinking wine would be somewhat blasphemous."

Luo Chen thought about it and agreed, putting away the flagon. "Then you do the honors!"

Brahma Heavenly Son shook his head helplessly, took out an exquisite tea set, used true flame to boil water, and began brewing spiritual tea, while the curiosity in his eyes remained undiminished.

Since the other party had set up the stance, Luo Chen naturally had nothing to hide.

He said slowly, "Hanging Temple!"

The great enemy of the Glazed Pure Land was the Western Desert's sacred land, Hanging Temple?

Brahma Heavenly Son, who was about to pour tea, froze, his hand suspended in midair, disbelief on his face.

"How is that possible?"

"How is it impossible?" Luo Chen countered.

Brahma Heavenly Son said subconsciously, "The Glazed Pure Land itself originated from Hanging Temple. Zen Master Xuandu was once one of the eight great heads, the head of the Medicine King Hall. Why would Hanging Temple deal with him?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly, actively took the teapot, and poured himself a cup.

"You yourself know that Zen Master Xuandu came from Hanging Temple. In the entire Western Desert, besides Hanging Temple, who else would dare to be his enemy?"

Brahma Heavenly Son was speechless.

The logic... did seem to hold?

He suddenly recalled Zen Master Xuandu's warning when they first entered the Glazed Pure Land.

A Buddhist tribulation?

An internal calamity within Buddhism!

Brahma Heavenly Son couldn't help but smile bitterly. "This is truly an undeserved disaster for me!"

Luo Chen took a sip of the spiritual tea and nodded slightly in approval. The tea was excellent!

He laughed. "It is not too late to leave now."

But to his surprise, Brahma Heavenly Son shook his head.

"Since I am here, there is no reason to leave. I also wish to see how Fellow Daoist will display your divine might and repel the powerful enemy."

Luo Chen looked at him with a half-smile. "I only hope that Fellow Daoist does not switch sides at the critical moment."

Brahma Heavenly Son waved his hand. "I fear Fellow Daoist does not know that when Hanging Temple once invited me to be the head of the Prajna Hall, I declined. In this coming battle, I have no stance, merely an observer."

Then, he asked curiously, "When will the Hanging Temple group arrive?"

"I do not know, but they will come eventually."

"How many experts will come?"

"Also unknown, but presumably not a few."

"Then does Fellow Daoist have confidence?"

"Alone, I might be somewhat strained, but this battle is not mine alone."

Brahma Heavenly Son instinctively turned his gaze to another place.

An endless bamboo sea, verdant and green.

With his divine sense, he could naturally detect another powerful aura within, restrained but ready to be unleashed.

The rumored number one under Ascendant, the Demon Emperor Azure Frost, was also going to participate in this battle?

And according to intelligence, this Alchemy Sect's Luo Chen had once engaged in a life-and-death fierce battle with Demon Emperor Azure Frost in the vast desert.

If before he had not believed that Luo Chen's strength could be so formidable, after that brief exchange, he was now utterly convinced that this Eastern Wilderness expert was enough to stand alongside Azure Frost.

Thus, with the two strongest under Ascendant in the current Mountains and Seas Realm joining forces, what kind of lineup would Hanging Temple have to send to defeat them and prevent Zen Master Xuandu from breaking through to the Ascendant realm?

Brahma Heavenly Son did not know, but his curiosity had been thoroughly piqued.

He pursed his lips and smiled. "Waiting is always tedious. I wonder if Fellow Daoist knows how to play chess?"

Luo Chen's eyes lit up. "I have some slight experience, but I am not proficient. If Fellow Daoist wishes, I am willing to accompany you for a game or two."

"No, it is I who will accompany you."

With a light laugh, Brahma Heavenly Son took out a chessboard and pieces, placing them on the stone table.

A breeze blew out from the bamboo sea, and the pavilion fell silent for a time.

Their conversation had not been shielded from others.

Thus, Xiao Yan and his two companions also heard it.

Learning that Hanging Temple was about to invade, the three of them had expressions that were wonderfully varied.

Especially Tuluo leader, whose golden body had been broken by Luo Chen, showed a look of eager restlessness on his face.

Luo Chen acted as if he had noticed nothing, concentrating on the small chessboard.

His chess skills were not considered proficient; they were merely something he had picked up from some guidance by Fu Qinglan when he was bored in the Central Continent.

Playing against Brahma Heavenly Son, he quickly fell into a disadvantage.

But he did not mind, nor did he even invest much of his mind into it.

All his attention was instead focused within his body.

After the initial completion of the *Three Heads and Six Arms* divine power, although the essence of his fleshly body had not strengthened much, his strength had indeed increased greatly.

The reason for this increase lay in the separation and differentiation of the chaotic powers within him.

Now, his dharma power flowed along meridians and acupoints into his Purple Mansion, source power filled his flesh, blood, sinews, and bones, while other attribute powers were distributed among his internal organs.

Once in battle, dharma power moved as if it were an extension of his arm, and source power was everywhere.

He imagined that if Azure Frost were to again use the same methods as before, he would probably be unable to prevent Luo Chen's flesh and blood from regenerating, allowing him to maintain his peak state at all times.

And if he were to unfold the *Three Heads and Six Arms* divine power, Luo Chen's strength would increase even more.

Because after the power differentiation, he would no longer be constrained to only being able to execute one or two techniques at a time.

At that point, with his profound soul foundation, his powerful and versatile fleshly body, and the coordination of various powers, he could easily unleash multiple techniques simultaneously.

Like the earlier Azure Yang Great Handprint, Fire Phoenix Scorches the Plain, and Flame Dragon Descends—three great spells unleashed at once!

That was just a small test.

If combined with various dharma treasures...

"I wonder, if I were to fight Azure Frost again now, what would be the odds of victory or defeat?"

The chess game had, without anyone noticing, changed board after board.

Brahma Heavenly Son had been in the lead the entire time, so he had ample energy to observe Luo Chen.

What surprised him was that from beginning to end, the other party had never shown any sign of worry.

As if the great enemy, Hanging Temple, could not affect him in the slightest.

"Is it arrogance, or confidence?"

No one answered him.

The only thing that could verify it was the battle that was imminent.

And that day...

"They're here!"

Three days later, Luo Chen swept away the chess game, which was heavily in his opponent's favor.

Brahma Heavenly Son was helpless. "I was about to win!"

Luo Chen slowly stood up, clasped his hands behind his back, and looked into the distance.

Playing chess, he was no good.

But flipping the table, he had plenty of experience.

(Chapter End)
