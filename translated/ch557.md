# Chapter 557: Waiting by the Stump, a Cliff Serpent's Body

Over the vast and open sea, wild winds swept across the water.

Then, from somewhere in the distance, a massive figure came shooting like a meteor — and when it stopped, the sea exploded upward in countless sprays of white.

The rain had passed. Sunlight broke through the spray, scattering rainbows in the mist.

Within that hazy radiance, the enormous silhouette shrank slowly. The fire-armor dissipated. The wings folded. Layer by layer, vapor-like surges of energy pushed the surrounding seawater outward.

In a few moments, as the spray fell back to the sea, Luo Chen stood in his original form.

He looked down at himself — bare and completely uncovered — and frowned.

Both the Heavenly Roc Transformation and Wang Yuan's Giant Spirit Transformation had this problem: the clothes didn't survive the shift.

When his cultivation had been lower, the changes only stretched him one or two zhang, and ordinary spell robes could expand with him.

But now, having advanced to Archaic Tier Three late stage, full deployment easily reached tens of zhang — far beyond what even high-grade spell robes could endure.

"Well. It's a minor problem."

He cast a healing technique, made a brief pass at his wounds, and pulled on a loose ivory-white daoist robe.

Then he sank beneath the sea's surface and waited quietly.

At full-throttle speed, the distance he had covered in that time put him well over a thousand li from the Xuanyan Sea Region.

No ordinary third-tier demon beast could catch up.

Especially the ones on Xuanyan Island — demon crabs and demon turtles, neither known for speed — and now most of them had taken serious wounds in the heavenly tribulation besides.

The only ones with any energy left to chase Luo Chen were Golden Claw and a few great demon kings — and all of them had just eaten a Azure Yang Great Handprint that had been quietly gathering power for a long time. They had their own problems to deal with.

So Luo Chen wasn't concerned about being pursued.

He was waiting here to regroup with Han Zhan and Black King.

Their rendezvous point had been identified by Han Zhan through his formation reading of Xuanyan Island — one of a few hidden escape routes that the Sealing Divinity Compass's Misty Cloud Dense Fog Formation could use to slip away cleanly.

And Luo Chen had bought them enough time.

There shouldn't be any complications.

About half a stick of incense later, Luo Chen opened his eyes from his breathing exercises with a slight frown.

"Why aren't they here yet?"

*Splash.*

He rose to the sea's surface, deploying both his enhanced eyes and his divine sense toward a certain direction — but there was still nothing.

Strange.

This was the agreed meeting point. And still no sign of them.

A cold thread ran through Luo Chen's chest.

*Han Zhan wouldn't betray me and run, would he?*

If that were the case, Luo Chen would have genuine cause to grieve.

Han Zhan was currently holding everything — Luo Chen's belongings included.

The Myriad Souls Banner, nurtured over long years and growing sharper by the day. The indispensable Sealing Divinity Compass. And the Xuanyan Armor that Black King had snatched from right under the demon crabs' claws.

Those three items alone, in the increasingly turbulent Northern Sea cultivation world, were worth more than a million spirit stones combined.

The Xuanyan Armor especially — the physical remains of a fourth-tier transformation-stage demon beast. Handled correctly, it might produce some extraordinary treasure.

He wouldn't have taken that risk otherwise.

Oh, and also.

Black King.

This Black-Scale Giant Python had once been a specimen of mediocre aptitude by any standard. But after receiving cultivation support from both Kun Jiao and Luo Chen himself — especially the massive doses of supreme-grade Imperial Moonlight Nectar — its hidden potential had slowly been unlocked. Its future was genuinely limitless.

If Han Zhan had made off with it, Luo Chen wouldn't even have anywhere to cry.

Only now did Luo Chen belatedly realize: the Great Dao oath was not omnipotent.

The oath Han Zhan had sworn to him only restricted Han Zhan from taking any action harmful to Luo Chen before he advanced to the Sage realm.

It said nothing about leaving of his own accord.

In an instant, the elation from his extraordinary haul began to dim.

"No. I need to go look."

He checked his own condition. Mana still over half. His body bore wounds, but none that would limit normal combat capacity.

If he ran into an enemy, he had more than enough to protect himself.

Following that direction, Luo Chen summoned a cloud of mana and activated his Concealment Veil Formation, drifting quietly above the sea's surface.

As he flew, he kept his soul-sense on alert.

Black King was his spirit beast — when he had enslaved it, he had taken its soul-blood, creating a one-way connection. As long as the distance wasn't too great, he could sense a rough directional bearing on his beast at any time.

After flying for several minutes, the cloud came to an abrupt halt over the water.

Luo Chen looked upward. A streak of escape-light was fleeing in frantic haste — and the powerful aura radiating from within it was unmistakably Adept Stage Seven.

A particularly familiar aura at that.

"That's Gu Shaoshang!"

"What's he doing here? And looking like he's running for his life?"

Even as Luo Chen puzzled over it, the sea ahead opened in a vortex, and a massive black-scale giant python surfaced — enormous in girth, the single sinister horn on its brow visible, its presence carrying a hint of something formidable even without needing to look at it.

Black King!

Luo Chen's heart lifted. He dropped the Concealment Veil Formation.

Black King felt it too, and swam over quickly.

"Master, Black King has not disgraced the mission!"

As it spoke, it opened its maw and exhaled. A globe of amber radiance floated upward, hanging in the air between them.

One look was enough for Luo Chen to feel a sense of ancient weight.

Looking carefully through the amber glow, he could make out a turtle shell spinning slowly within — its surface covered in dense, intricate markings, each section forming hexagonal patterns.

Such perfectly ordered shell-markings were exceptionally rare in the cultivation world. Only the most gifted bloodlines could form them.

"So this is the Demon Emperor's relic — the Xuanyan Armor?"

He reached out and it settled into his hand.

He examined it carefully for a while, then tucked it gently into his storage ring, and looked at Black King.

"What was that with Gu Shaoshang?"

Black King was confused. "I don't know either. When I first escaped through the deep-sea spring, I found him waiting outside. It was Senior Han who personally made a move — startled him off."

*Startled him off?*

Even as Luo Chen puzzled over that, a scale on Black King's back flipped open and the Myriad Souls Banner flew out from within.

He caught it, and Han Zhan's voice drifted into his ears.

"That young one was formidable — skilled in formations. He probably did the same as us: read the hidden escape routes on Xuanyan Island, then waited along our path like a hunter waiting by a stump."

"You fought him?"

"Certainly not. I'm a proper Sage Sage — I'm not going to keep throwing hands with Adept juniors time after time. I merely released a wisp of Sage aura. That was enough to frighten him away."

Ah, that explained it.

Luo Chen nodded — but still had a lingering question. If Han Zhan had driven him off so quickly, why had they still been delayed so long?

Perhaps knowing Luo Chen's cautious habits, Han Zhan added a few more words of explanation.

It turned out that the Ring-Headed Turtles were also aware of the island's hidden escape routes.

After the patriarch and Great Elder fell, Ring-Headed Turtle demon kings began leading the surviving clanspeople out through those same routes — and their path overlapped with Han Zhan and Black King's.

To avoid a disastrous collision, Han Zhan and Black King had been forced to slow their pace, lingering far longer than planned before finally leaving Xuanyan Island.

The moment they emerged, they ran directly into Gu Shaoshang waiting in ambush — and that was the scene Luo Chen had arrived to find.

"Enough. We're still too close to the Xuanyan Sea Region. Better to leave first, just to be safe." Luo Chen said.

"Are we heading back to the Swallow Islands?" Han Zhan asked.

Luo Chen shook his head. "I need to process the batch of third-tier demon crab materials before going back. Otherwise if we wait too long, they'll no longer be suitable for Black Emperor Paste refinement."

As he spoke, he gathered the Myriad Souls Banner in his hand. Han Zhan slipped back inside at the right moment, and at the same time passed out the Sealing Divinity Compass.

Black King shrank down and curled beneath Luo Chen's mana cloud.

With a seal formed, the gray mana cloud quietly dissolved into the horizon.

...

On a small, spirit-lacking island several tens of thousands of li from Xuanyan Island.

A seagull perched atop it.

That day, a streak of escape-light descended.

The seagull flew over immediately. "Master, you're back."

"You came back before us. Has anyone visited the coral trench cavern?" Luo Chen asked, looking at Heavenjade.

Heavenjade shook her head quickly. "I checked. No one has. Even the low-tier sea demons that occasionally wandered into the coral trench were caught and eaten by the blood coral. That place still hasn't been discovered."

Luo Chen nodded. Undiscovered meant reasonably safe.

He could process the demon crab shells there first, then leave the Xuanyan Sea Region at his leisure.

He instructed Heavenjade to stand guard nearby and stay alert for enemies, then brought Black King back to the coral trench.

The moment he arrived, he retrieved the Sealing Divinity Compass and began resetting the Misty Cloud Dense Fog Formation.

"Still this careful." Han Zhan smiled from beside him.

Luo Chen continued guiding the compass while replying offhandedly: "When you're away from home, being careful is always right. If I hadn't been, I'd have been surrounded by Demon Spider Crabs back on Heavenly Pivot Island."

Once surrounded, breaking out was never simple.

The breakthrough at Xuanyan Island had gone so clean because he had prepared well in advance.

But if he were caught in a trap inside his own cavern, even going all out might not guarantee escape.

And if the enemy were the Demon Spider Crab type — specialists in perception and sealing — there would truly be no way out.

As he worked through the formation, Han Zhan said suddenly: "Expand the formation's coverage."

"Hmm?"

"About one li should do." Han Zhan estimated.

One li — roughly five hundred zhang in diameter. Luo Chen typically converted it that way.

He didn't entirely understand, but he followed the instruction.

In an instant, bolstered by his mana, the Sealing Divinity Compass blazed with layered spiritual light, and the formation lines inscribed in advance began spreading outward.

Currents shifted. A red mist spread.

The coral trench, already awash in red light from the blood coral, grew a shade deeper still.

Someone who didn't know might have assumed a third-tier blood coral had bloomed here.

Once the formation was set, Han Zhan said: "Have you noticed anything new inside your Myriad Souls Banner?"

Luo Chen blinked.

He genuinely hadn't checked.

Though he had personally refined the banner and bound it to himself, in ordinary times it was almost entirely in Han Zhan's operation — it served as Han Zhan's home, fed and housed him, so to speak.

He had never paid much attention to its contents.

Now, his divine sense probed inside.

And Luo Chen's expression became one of astonishment.

Souls.

Countless souls.

Not just the original two Ghost Kings and the over a thousand Ghost General-level souls that had been there before — there were now tens of thousands of dense, tightly packed lower souls as well.

And beyond that, more than ten remnant souls of third-tier demon kings.

"What are these?" He had barely taken half of it in before he had to ask out loud.

"Heh heh."

Han Zhan smiled mildly. "When I was monitoring the battlefield for you, it's not as though I was idle. Collecting a few ownerless remnant souls is no great effort."

*No great effort?*

Luo Chen was momentarily without words.

But understanding came quickly. This was why the demonic soul practitioners who mastered the soul path liked to haunt ancient battlefields — gathering powerful souls.

The most extreme among them would actively stir up conflicts and orchestrate wars, large and small.

Even low-level soul-refining demon cultivators were known to, from the shadows, manipulate wars in mortal kingdoms.

It was for one simple reason: the profit was immense.

On a battlefield, the intensity of blood-qi and killing-intent was incomparable.

A soul that survived that environment — even just a remnant — if nurtured carefully, would become a formidable weapon.

The tens of thousands of lower souls Han Zhan had gathered were nothing to the five royal clans' armies of millions. But for the Myriad Souls Banner, they were an enormous influx of raw power.

And those ten-odd third-tier remnant souls — if they could devour each other and consolidate over time, they might eventually produce a few complete third-tier Ghost King-level existences.

When that day came, the Myriad Souls Banner's combat power would be multiplied several times over.

This had probably been Han Zhan's intent all along.

Previously he had held back some of its capabilities — not just to conserve his soul's essence, but also because there was nothing to work with. Now with a foundation like this, the banner could be wielded with full flexibility in the future.

"For me, this is all gain. A powerful new weapon from nothing — and it fills the gap left by losing the Mystic Flame Sword."

A smile spread across Luo Chen's face.

Before he could even voice the compliment, Han Zhan said: "Take a closer look inside."

"There's more?"

Curious, Luo Chen sent his divine sense deeper into the banner.

The further in he went, the more souls crowded together — in his first quick scan he hadn't noticed. Looking carefully now, he realized all the battle souls seemed to be congregating around one particular thing.

When Luo Chen saw what that thing was, his expression changed entirely.

"This—!"

A cry escaped him.

He sent mana flooding through the banner. The Soul-Refining Banner expanded as a strong wind hit it, growing to its full nine zhang length.

The banner's gateway opened wide, and something two or three zhang across slowly "swam" out from within.

The moment it appeared, an aura spread through the cavern.

Black King, crouching at the cavern entrance, raised its head in puzzlement and unease.

"Master, what is that?"

Luo Chen didn't answer, focusing all his mana on guiding the shape outward.

One zhang... two zhang... three...

It wasn't until one hundred zhang had emerged that the full form was revealed.

A massive serpent.

A hundred zhang of serpent.

That vast body coiled and curved through the coral trench, and even without a trace of life remaining, the residual pressure of its aura made the surrounding blood coral press itself flat, as if by some deeply ingrained instinct.

Luo Chen stared at the scorched, wound-covered body — like a piece of dead wood or charred coal.

He swallowed once, and looked at Han Zhan.

"A Cliff Serpent?"

The slightly translucent face of Han Zhan showed a lingering smile, edged now with a hint of fatigue.

"That's the Ring-Headed Turtle that failed the tribulation. But though it failed, it shed its former limitations and attained the Cliff Serpent body."

"This... this..."

Luo Chen shook his head again and again, unable to stop.

"You really have given me quite a surprise, Senior."

Han Zhan said with tired levity: "I can't help you much in actual combat. But little things like this are trivial."

*This* was not a little thing.

A Cliff Serpent corpse — transformation failed, tribulation not survived, but still, technically, the body of a third-tier Perfected Completion peak great demon king.

There would be no shortage of buyers willing to pay top price for this.

Even for personal use, certain materials on it would have considerable applications.

Selling it — Luo Chen was reluctant to part with it.

But keeping it for personal use — what exactly would that look like?

As he turned the question over, something rubbed against his shin.

He looked down. Black King was carefully, hopefully nuzzling against him.

"Master, that..."

Luo Chen's brow furrowed. "This is not something you can eat."

"Actually, it can." Han Zhan smiled.

Luo Chen looked up.

"When I asked the little one to make its move earlier, I promised it some conditions — and one of those was this Cliff Serpent body. Go ahead and give it to him. The transformation failed, and then it took a tribulation amplified dozens of times over. What's left is genuinely of limited use for your purposes."

Black King also looked at Luo Chen with hopeful, beseeching eyes. "Master, when I grabbed the Xuanyan Armor for you, Golden Claw almost beat me to death."

Luo Chen's expression shifted back and forth. His divine sense went in and out of the Cliff Serpent body several times in succession.

After repeated examination, he had to admit Han Zhan had a point.

This snake body — which looked barely different from charcoal — had genuinely lost nine parts in ten of its spiritual nature. Whether used for pill refinement or magic treasure crafting, its efficacy would be mediocre at best.

After all, this was not a demon beast with any natural resistance to lightning. On the contrary, it was a water-type great demon with a natural aversion to heavenly thunder.

That a complete body had been preserved at all was already a fortune among misfortunes.

By comparison, his true gains from this venture remained the dozen-odd demon crab king bodies and the Xuanyan Armor Black King had taken for him.

After thinking it through, Luo Chen drew his lingering gaze away.

"Fine. It's yours. Don't let it stop you from working afterward."

Black King immediately curled its body against the ground, bowing its head to Luo Chen repeatedly in the closest thing a giant python could manage to prostration.

"Master's generosity is boundless! From now on, Black King will serve you body and soul, to the very last breath!"

Heh — this beast was truly getting more and more fluent in the human tongue with each passing day.

But looking at the hunger in Black King's eyes, Luo Chen realized: this failed-transformation Cliff Serpent body might be another enormous stroke of fortune for Black King.

And if Black King grew stronger, that was nothing but good news for its master.

Adding it all up —

The last of the weight in Luo Chen's chest lifted. He laughed quietly.

"This Xuanyan Island trip was truly a magnificent harvest. The biggest windfall of my entire life."

His heart light, he walked into the cavern.

*(End of Chapter)*
