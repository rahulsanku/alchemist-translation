# Chapter 1195: Madness in the Guixu, the True Spirit Execution Venerable

*Crack!*

*Crack!*

*Crack!*

Like the sound of glazed jade shattering, crisp echoes resounded through the solemn and majestic Ancient God Dao Field.

A golden claw, covered in ferocious scales, slowly extended out from the spatial rift.

This scene left everyone dumbfounded.

This was the Ancient God Dao Field!

This was the Guixu that devoured everything, destroyed everything!

Why would such an existence appear here?

Even the experienced Guixu martial artists were at a loss in this moment.

*Clang!*

Accompanied by a loud noise, another claw reached in, seemingly gripping the edge of the rift.

Between the two claws, a golden halo continuously spread, dyeing everything gold.

Along with it came an angry roar.

"Foreign domain evil god, how dare you invade my Golden Execution Great Realm!"

The terrifying roar was unbearable, the soul-shaking impact destroying everything.

When the rift grew large enough, the existence behind it could no longer restrain itself and reached across the void, swiping a claw down at the Thirty-Three Layers Divine Mother Hall.

"Not good, it wants to destroy the Source of Compassionate Light!"

The Carefree Domain Master blurted out.

The next moment.

The Thirty-Three Layers Divine Mother Hall burst forth with brilliant light.

An intense milky-white power converged, forming a gentle and compassionate female figure.

The illusory body wore no gold, jade, or pearl ornaments, only a flowing white gauze robe.

The edges of the robe were adorned with countless star-tear crystals like stars, appearing even more sacred and compassionate.

Her right hand held a pure lotus of light, with a spiral galaxy-like vortex rotating in its center, reflecting worlds she seemed to have saved.

Her left hand hung down, lightly forming a Compassion Seal, the five fingers intertwined as if containing infinite power.

It was precisely the image of the Compassionate Light Divine Mother that all Guixu martial artists had seen in their consciousness when they first entered.

Facing the golden giant claw descending with destructive force, the Divine Mother's left hand seal remained unchanged, but the milky-white radiance dripping from her fingertips transformed into countless specks of light and white sparrows, charging forward with the desperate determination of moths flying into a flame.

*Boom!*

A deafening explosion, followed by continuous blasts.

In the explosions, the Divine Mother image became increasingly illusory, even teetering on the verge of collapse.

Until the golden giant claw slowly withdrew, the Divine Mother image could no longer hold and shattered, turning into thousands of milky-white streams returning to the Thirty-Three Layers Divine Mother Hall.

This scene tugged at the hearts of all Guixu martial artists.

If it were before, they wouldn't understand what this meant.

But after Corpse's explanation, everyone knew that the Thirty-Three Layers Divine Mother Hall had suffered severe damage.

The Source of Compassionate Light itself had already lost its way, devoid of self-awareness.

The divine image it could condense was at most an instinctive reaction to danger.

Once was fine, but if it happened a few more times... the Divine Mother Hall would be destroyed, and their chance to leave the Guixu would drop to zero.

Everyone's expression changed!

At that moment, from the increasingly torn rift, an even angrier roar came.

As if failing to destroy the Source of Compassionate Light in one blow had greatly infuriated that existence.

A massive head forcibly squeezed in.

The face was entirely red, all flesh without skin, as if the facial skin had been peeled off.

There was no nose, but the nasal root remained, as if the nose had been gouged out.

The only unique feature was the criminal-like tattoo beneath the eye sockets, which were entirely white eyeballs.

When this head appeared, countless gazes immediately fell upon it.

It breathed heavily, looking down from above.

A trace of the foreign domain evil god's aura lingered on each person.

"A group of ants, you are the evil god's followers? Seeing the great Golden Execution Venerable, why do you not kneel?"

"Golden Execution Venerable? Could it be that you, senior, are a Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerable?"

The Carefree Domain Master slowly floated up, arriving beneath that terrifying face, speaking calmly without a trace of fear.

The existence called Golden Execution Venerable sneered, "Do not impose your human titles upon me. I am a great True Spirit!"

The Carefree Domain Master nodded thoughtfully.

"Though not a human Great Vehicle, you are a powerful True Spirit comparable to a Heavenly Venerable. You must have condensed a complete law force to cross the Guixu world."

His words seemed like self-talk, but his voice was quite loud, reaching every martial artist below.

Especially the phrase "complete law force"!

Instantly, the martial artists who had been wearing worried expressions suddenly burst forth with unimaginable brilliance in their eyes.

Golden Execution Venerable was stunned.

Those eyes were full of hunger, excitement, eagerness, and even a desire to try—everything except the fear of lower beings toward higher ones.

Why was this?

Luo Chen was also among the crowd.

When he heard the Carefree Domain Master deliberately say those words, he instantly realized something.

The Endless Guixu was a naturally formed digestive place of the universe.

Between the cycles of epochs, all living beings, gods, ghosts, demons, and even star realms would be devoured and destroyed by it.

For martial artists, it was an even greater prison.

Those who could cultivate to the Martial Dao Human Immortal realm all thought about how to escape.

For thousands of years, countless Martial Dao Human Immortals had thought of countless ways to leave.

The spatial vortex called the Destruction Gate, the Chaotic Thunder Pool touching the sky, the ultimate place where stars are destroyed...

Each method was extremely dangerous, but people still went forward one after another.

Among these methods, there was a rumor that had been passed down among every Martial Dao Human Immortal.

That was to hunt and kill a Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerable!

Legend had it that beings at the Heavenly Venerable level, who mastered complete law force, could ignore the shackles of this place and forcefully escape.

Legend had it that such beings had left before.

Legend had it that someone had fought with a Heavenly Venerable-level being that accidentally wandered in. Though no one knew if they succeeded in the end and left.

In any case, this was a path that might be more promising than the Ancient God Dao Field!

Three figures rose simultaneously, standing beside the Carefree Domain Master.

Heavenly Abyss Lie Su was excited: "A True Spirit is also a Great Vehicle!"

Heavenly Cry Old Monster half-cried, half-laughed: "The Guixu restricts mana and spiritual power. The strength of beings from outside is reduced to less than one percent."

Corpse Old Demon said coldly: "The Ancient God Dao Field cannot be lost. A Great Vehicle primordial spirit can serve as a path of survival."

The Carefree Domain Master exhaled a turbid breath and slowly uttered one word: "Kill!"

The next moment, the four Destruction Kings struck simultaneously.

But it wasn't an attack. Instead, they transformed into streams of astral power, forming giant hands that reached toward Golden Execution Venerable's body.

They wanted to pull it in!

Faced with such rude behavior, Golden Execution Venerable was enraged.

The foreign domain evil god was already hateful, but these evil god followers were even more infuriating.

"Ants, you seek death!"

It opened its mouth and spat out, endless golden light scattering, filled with sharp blades.

The Heavenly Cry Old Monster snorted from his abdomen, then opened his mouth that had never spoken before.

"Xu..."

A fierce astral wind spewed out, heading straight for the golden light.

It was the strongest among astral winds—the Unforgivable Heavenly Wind!

When the two met, it was almost one-sided. The Unforgivable Heavenly Wind was no match for the golden light blades.

But the next moment, brilliant starlight lit up.

Heavenly Abyss Lie Su charged straight in!

The terrifying golden light swept over his body, but he felt nothing.

His body was constantly torn apart and constantly reorganized, showing the indestructible nature of starlight in the changing light and darkness.

It was his Star Indestructible Body, cultivated for thousands of years!

As he charged in, more and more starlight erupted, dispersing the golden light.

Corpse Old Demon wasn't idle either. No one knew what he did, but the void shook, and then sections of pale white bones suddenly emerged on Golden Execution Venerable's body.

Those bones pierced through the flesh and blood, using the flesh and blood as nourishment to grow wildly.

The three kings worked together to block Golden Execution Venerable's angry blow, even causing some damage to its body.

But the most terrifying was the Carefree Domain Master. His hands grasped the void, and ethereal Carefree Astral Power condensed into chains, binding Golden Execution Venerable's head.

Under his force, Golden Execution Venerable was pulled inch by inch into the Ancient God Dao Field.

"What are you waiting for!"

The Carefree Domain Master's clear shout echoed through the universe.

In the Dao Field, all martial artists could no longer hold back.

If it were before, they might still have been afraid, but the four Destruction Kings had already acted, charging ahead.

At this point, why wait any longer!

Over a hundred figures, like arrows from a string, charged toward Golden Execution Venerable without looking back.

At the forefront were those known as Epoch Monsters.

They had been suppressed in the Guixu long enough.

Countless days and nights of cultivation had granted them extraordinary power, but they could only be confined to this small world, with nowhere to use it.

To survive, they had sealed themselves time and again, day after day feeling the slow but undeniable loss of vitality.

That pain was like witnessing a slow execution happening to their own bodies.

Now that someone wanted to destroy their hope of leaving, and that person was also one of the hopes of leaving, who could bear it?

Over thirty Epoch Monsters struck with their ultimate killing moves.

The power each instantly unleashed was no less than that of the four Destruction Kings.

Following closely were the Martial Dao Human Immortals of the Heavenly Wind Division and the Myriad Manifestations Star Sect.

One side surrounded a black banner, swallowing fierce astral wind. When the banner shook, heaven and earth overturned.

The other side formed a formation, astral power flowing, erupting in five colors as a massive star slowly condensed.

Finally came the current leaders of various forces summoned by the Carefree Domain Master, all revealing their trump cards.

There was Primordial Magnetic Divine Power breaking the five elements, Red Lotus Karmic Fire burning everything, endless ice and snow sweeping all, heavy towers pressing down from above...

Luo Chen and Yuan Qingzang were also among them. The two exchanged glances and struck in tacit understanding.

Violent thunder light surged like waves, forming a heaven-destroying momentum, rushing toward Golden Execution Venerable.

Martial Dao Divine Ability—Raging Current Destruction Realm!

Under this united attack, Golden Execution Venerable seemed caught off guard. A layer of golden blood film only had time to appear on its body before it was drowned by the overwhelming attacks.

*Boom! Boom! Boom!*

Continuous explosions burst like fireworks in the sky.

The entire Ancient God Dao Field shook.

The originally stable spatial gaps actually produced fierce spatial cracks.

"Did it work?"

Yuan Qingzang's murmur sounded in his ear.

Luo Chen's eyes trembled. He grabbed the void and threw Yuan Qingzang behind him.

The next moment.

A golden tail swept across, and who knew how many figures fell from the sky.

At the same time, a terrifying, peerless ferocious aura surged and spread, even suppressing the peaceful sacred aura of the Thirty-Three Layers Divine Mother Hall.

Luo Chen looked up.

In the sky, a bottomless void rift appeared, shocking to the eye.

Outside the rift was a monster about ten thousand zhang long, covered in golden scales.

Golden Execution Venerable had been pulled in!

But the battle situation was not necessarily better than before.

Such an existence had a realm comparable to Great Vehicle, but its true body was a True Spirit.

Its physical body was terrifying, completely no less than a cultivator of the same level!

It coiled in the sky, its white-eyed pupils looking down at the dense swarm of ants below, roaring in anger, venting the endless fury in its heart.

"You all deserve to die!!!"

In its rage, terrifying golden light erupted wave after wave.

Where it passed, those of lower cultivation couldn't dodge in time, silently dissolving like spring snow under the sun.

But the strong showed no fear, their eyes wild with madness as they charged at it again.

Yuan Qingzang was also among them. Blood-red thunder light had somehow wrapped around his body. This was presumably the real killing move he had once mentioned to Luo Chen.

Its power was extremely strong, no less than a strike from a Destruction King.

But the most eye-catching were not these Martial Dao Human Immortals, but the four Destruction Kings.

The Carefree Domain Master stood alone, still blocking the front.

In his hand, a mirror full of immortal intent had somehow appeared.

The mirror emitted piercing cyan light, strand after strand wrapping around Golden Execution Venerable's ten-thousand-zhang body.

Corpse Old Demon flew to the rift exit, blocking its retreat. Surging air currents surged out, seeming like astral power yet also like demonic qi.

Heavenly Cry Old Monster and Heavenly Abyss Lie Su descended below.

"Raise the Unforgivable Grand Formation!"

Accompanied by Heavenly Cry Old Monster's suppressed sobbing, all his clansmen flew into the black banner. An aura of tearing everything apart immediately emerged.

Heavenly Abyss Lie Su returned to his Myriad Manifestations Star Sect disciples. The starlight on his body did not dim but grew stronger.

His body gradually became magnificent, all his disciples merging with him.

The next moment, a nearly ten-thousand-zhang starlight giant rose.

His right hand grasped, and an ancient war halberd condensed.

Holding the halberd with both hands, the starlight giant slashed at Golden Execution Venerable.

Luo Chen watched these scenes, his eyes twitching wildly.

Too crazy!

It was simply a fight to the death!

He had another path. Did he have to be this crazy too?

At that moment, the Carefree Domain Master's cold voice came faintly.

"Luo Chen, what are you still hesitating about?"

Among the four Destruction Kings and over a hundred Martial Dao Human Immortals, the Carefree Domain Master valued Luo Chen the most.

He believed in the other's talent and also believed in his strength.

So, he needed Luo Chen.

This battle could not afford to lose!

Facing the Carefree Domain Master's invitation, Luo Chen's face hardened.

"One move. I will only allow you one move, as repayment for past kindness!"

Without time to think, Luo Chen had already made his choice.

His astral power flowed, all his apertures shining like stars. Martial Dao gods, identical in form to him, opened their eyes.

Then, he clenched his fist and punched out.

Within it were hundreds of fist shadows, all merging into one fist.

One punch out, the void shattered.

Seven Hundred Divine Kill!

Facing the mad attacks of the Guixu martial artists, Golden Execution Venerable grew even angrier, with three parts of fear mixed in.

These people were all madmen!

Seeming like followers, they were crazier than fanatics!

And this place seemed to greatly suppress him.

If the battle continued, he might... die.

When this thought emerged, Golden Execution Venerable roared to the heavens.

All its golden scales suddenly fell off, emitting extremely dazzling light, like heavenly blades falling from the sky.

Golden Execution Great Decapitation!
