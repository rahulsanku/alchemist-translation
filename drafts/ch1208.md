# Chapter 1208: Under Yao Xi's Gate, Where Spirit Veins Converge

"Xuan Luo!"

When his name was called, Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly, a hint of surprise and delight appearing on his face as he stepped forward voluntarily.

Yao Xi Sage glanced at him, then continued reading the names.

"Ji Yue!"

"Feng Zhiyan!"

Soon, another man and woman were called out.

Such a commotion made many people realize what was happening, and they looked on with expectant eyes.

However, that was where it ended.

Yao Xi Sage's gaze fell upon the three of them, her tone calm and even carrying a hint of laziness.

"You three, enter my tutelage, temporarily as registered disciples."

All three were overjoyed, each addressing her as Master.

Then Yao Xi Sage swept her gaze over the assembled cultivators.

"That will be all. Jiuzhang, I'll leave the rest to you."

As the senior senior brother of Jade Spring Peak, Wan Jiuzhang was also somewhat helpless.

His master was simply too lazy. Even on such an occasion, she wouldn't say a few more words to inspire people, only focused on completing the task.

Never mind.

He would say more later to strengthen these foreign cultivators' sense of belonging to the sect!

Yao Xi Sage seemed quite confident in Wan Jiuzhang's abilities. After giving her instructions, she wrapped several Jade Spring Peak disciples with her mana, including Luo Chen and the other two.

Then, transforming into a streak of light, she disappeared above the Heart-Inquiring Hall.

After she left, Wan Jiuzhang gave a bitter laugh.

Among the thousand-plus newly admitted disciples, only three had been directly selected into the inner sect. This ratio was truly too small.

Those who remained all showed expressions of dejection.

Letting out a turbid breath, Wan Jiuzhang walked before the crowd.

"Congratulations on joining my Suling Sect. From now on, you are no longer wandering, rootless rogue cultivators."

"Although you will initially cultivate in the outer sect, you must not slack off. Those who perform well in outer sect cultivation can enter the inner sect for study through the outer sect examination. If there are outstanding individuals, they may even catch the eyes of the sect's powerful figures, and there is a great opportunity to be accepted as a personal disciple."

"There is precedent for this. For example, my Jade Spring Peak's little junior sister Liu Muhan came this way back then. All of you should take her as your model!"

After this speech, the originally low emotional atmosphere gradually became elevated.

Yes!

They were no longer rootless rogue cultivators from now on.

Having entered the Suling Sect, they would henceforth have this Nascent Soul-level force as their backing, and spirit mountain caves as their foothold.

When spoken of, they would be disciples of a great sect.

Seeing this scene, Wan Jiuzhang breathed a sigh of relief and smiled as he released a flying boat.

"Junior brothers and junior sisters, follow me!"

...

Within the Sage's escaping light, Luo Chen's gaze flickered.

Tens of thousands of rogue cultivators, a thousand selected.

Among the thousand, only three had caught Yao Xi Sage's eye.

No!

Strictly speaking, they hadn't truly entered Yao Xi Sage's eyes yet; they were merely registered disciples.

To become formal disciples was not something that could be accomplished overnight.

From this perspective, the so-called Entry Assembly was not particularly valued by the Suling Sect. They must have other recruitment methods.

But for himself, it was enough.

The Sage's flying light was extremely fast, and within the sect's mountain gates, there were almost no obstacles.

Perhaps considering the three newly accepted registered disciples, Yao Xi Sage deliberately slowed down after flying for a while.

"Enjoy the scenery. From now on, this will be your home."

She said it very casually.

But for Ji Yue and Feng Zhiyan, it was quite exciting. They opened their eyes wide to look at the scenery passing by along the way.

Unlike the outer areas divided into patches of spirit fields, the internal structure of the Suling Sect was truly worthy of an immortal sect's splendor.

Strange mountains stood tall, filled with abundant spiritual energy.

Rivers and lakes were scattered like stars in the sky.

Golden Core cultivators and Nascent Soul Sages, rarely seen in the outside world, were everywhere here.

Occasionally, powerful auras as deep as the abyss flashed by, shocking their perceptions.

This was the Suling Sect?

Yao Xi Sage's voice entered their ears like clear spring water.

"My Suling Sect originally had nine great Nascent Soul cultivators, considered outstanding even among Nascent Soul-level forces. Two hundred years ago, the Sect Master entered the Heavenly Origin, with the help of friends, successfully broke through to the Nascent Soul stage, achieving the name of a True Lord. Our sect also rose with the tide, becoming a Nascent Soul-level force."

"Below the Nascent Soul and Nascent Soul cultivators, the number of Nascent Soul Sages within the sect far exceeds ordinary forces, numbering over a thousand."

"They do not all stay within the sect to cultivate but are scattered in various places, guarding regions for the sect and managing various industries."

"In fact, it's not just Nascent Soul Sages. Golden Core and Foundation Establishment cultivators below the Nascent Soul stage are also assigned tasks every so often. You will not be an exception in the future."

A Nascent Soul True Lord!

Eight great Nascent Soul Elders.

Over a thousand Nascent Soul Sages.

Countless low-level disciples—this was the foundation of the Suling Sect.

Through Yao Xi Sage's introduction, the three newcomers, Luo Chen and the others, gained a more concrete outline of the Suling Sect's impression, no longer as rough as hearsay from the outside.

A vague sense of belonging quietly sprouted from the bottom of their hearts.

Even Liu Muhan and the others who accompanied them, despite having cultivated within the sect for many years, couldn't help but show proud expressions.

Belonging, pride... if one added more emotions like closeness, attachment, and faith, fused together, that was the so-called sect cohesion.

Luo Chen was very familiar with this set; after all, he had once established a force like this.

But even so, hearing Yao Xi Sage's introduction, he was still shocked by the Suling Sect's strength.

Not only that!

If a mere Nascent Soul-level force like the Suling Sect had such foundations, how strong must the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, known as one of the Nine Great Sacred Sects, be?

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect of the Mountains and Seas Realm was probably not even the tip of the iceberg!

Strictly speaking, he, Luo Chen, also had enmity with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

After all, the branch in the Mountains and Seas Realm was destroyed by him leading a team, and he had once clashed with the White Tiger True Lord who descended from the Mystic Realm.

He was probably already on the wanted list of the Mystic Realm's Heavenly Origin Dao Sect under the name Luo Chen.

Thinking this, Luo Chen felt quite wise about his decision to use a pseudonym to infiltrate the Suling Sect.

Otherwise, if exposed, no matter how strong he was, he wouldn't escape the pursuit of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect at the moment.

"Jade Spring Peak, we've arrived!"

Just as Luo Chen was feeling relieved, Yao Xi Sage withdrew her escape light.

The three of them followed the sound and saw a magnificent, even dreamlike mountain peak before their eyes.

Under the sunlight, clouds and mist swirled, like an immortal mountain.

Waterfalls hung like the Milky Way from the Nine Heavens, suspended on various parts of the mountain.

The flying waterfalls splashed countless droplets, like steaming fragments, condensing mist into jade.

This was Jade Spring Peak!

As the group descended to Jade Spring Peak, their feet touched the ground, and there was a soft feeling.

Looking down, the gaps between the mountain rocks were filled with lush green moss, spreading out continuously like the finest emerald velvet carpet.

Everywhere one looked, there were gnarled and ancient jade-bone pines, narrow sword-like pure-heart orchids, and more strange plants that Luo Chen didn't recognize.

What a quiet and elegant cultivation holy land!

Amidst the dazzling sights, the respectful greetings of Jade Spring Peak cultivators sounded from time to time.

"Master," "Grandmaster"—these names were incessant.

Clearly, Yao Xi Sage was not an unknown new Nascent Soul within the Suling Sect. She had long established her own lineage and passed down her personal Dao tradition.

After greeting, those cultivators would often subconsciously look at Luo Chen and the other two, probably knowing their origins.

Curiosity showed in their eyes.

Occasionally, when their eyes met, they would reveal friendly smiles.

Soon enough.

Yao Xi Sage led the three into a palace called the Jade Spring Palace.

Luo Chen lowered his head, able to feel the woman sitting at the upper seat constantly watching them, and even several streams of divine sense sweeping over them.

He didn't do much defense, merely using his powerful divine sense to conceal his strong astral qi.

As for his immortal cultivation realm?

No need to hide; at this moment, he indeed only had the early Foundation Establishment stage.

After a long while, a lazy voice came from above.

"Taking you in is merely to fulfill the sect's task for me. It doesn't mean you can rest easy."

"Registered disciples can also be expelled."

"Therefore, cultivate well! In ten years, I will test your progress. If you can satisfy me, you can then become formal Jade Spring disciples. Oh, in the sect's terminology, it should be called true disciples."

As she spoke, two jade slips and one book flew before the three of them.

Before Luo Chen and Ji Yue were jade slips; before Feng Zhiyan was a paper book.

It wasn't discrimination, but because Luo Chen and Ji Yue were at the Foundation Establishment stage and had already given birth to spiritual sense, they could quickly absorb information from jade slips.

Feng Zhiyan, however, was only at the Qi Refinement stage and couldn't yet release her spiritual sense, so she could only gain knowledge by reading paper books.

"Muhan, you know the procedures best. Take them to settle in!"

"Yes, Master!"

Liu Muhan bowed respectfully and led the three out of Jade Spring Palace.

...

"This is the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall. Later, I'll take you to receive your identity tokens, sect robes, and corresponding magical artifacts, spirit stones, cave dwellings, etc. Junior Sister Feng, since you haven't reached Foundation Establishment, you can't have a personal cave dwelling yet. I'll arrange something separately for you."

"You probably won't come to the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall often, unless you lose your identity token and need a replacement."

"But you must remember the routes to the adjacent Transmission Hall and External Affairs Hall, as you'll definitely visit them often. The Transmission Hall is where the sect's seniors give lectures and transmit techniques to inner sect disciples. It's not limited to any particular peak or mountain; even true disciples can come here to listen to instructions. The External Affairs Hall is simpler—a place to issue tasks, both internally and externally. Since the sect is too large and resources distributed are limited, to accelerate cultivation, we low-level disciples have to earn rewards by taking on tasks. But remember one thing: when taking tasks, you must act within your abilities and never exceed your capabilities."

"Besides the three halls, our sect also has five courts. They are the Spirit Medicine Court, Alchemy Court, Formation Court, Hundred Crafts Court, and Enforcement Court."

"From the names, you should know what these five courts do. The Hundred Crafts Court is special; its internal situation is more complex, involving the hundred arts of immortal cultivation. Although not as specialized as the other three courts, there are still many masters and experts stationed there."

After receiving the necessary daily items, Liu Muhan led the three around the sect.

But the Suling Sect's territory was simply too vast; it was impossible to visit every place. Therefore, for several locations, Liu Muhan merely pointed in a direction from afar.

The three halls and five courts were the basic organizational structure of the Suling Sect.

They could also be seen as public facilities!

Their coverage involved the entire sect, from top to bottom.

True disciples from various peaks and mountains could cultivate and train here.

Especially the five courts; any disciple with a particular skill could join them and develop their expertise.

However, these matters were still too distant for Luo Chen and the others.

"For now, your urgent task is to set aside all distractions and cultivate hard for ten years, striving to gain our master's recognition and become true entry disciples." Liu Muhan said this, her words carrying earnest instruction.

Feng Zhiyan was a lively person. After leaving Jade Spring Palace, she revealed her true nature.

Seeing that Liu Muhan was easy to talk to, she boldly asked, "You call her 'old venerable,' but she looks very young to me?"

Liu Muhan smiled and shook her head. "The techniques of our Jade Spring Peak lineage mostly have the effect of preserving beauty and complexion, so you can't tell the actual age from appearance. To tell the truth, our master is probably over four hundred years old."

"So old?" Feng Zhiyan was extremely surprised.

Liu Muhan hummed in acknowledgment. "As far as I know, our master formed her Nascent Soul about two hundred years ago. So at that time, she barely had the opportunity to attend the internal lectures after the Sect Master's breakthrough. Perhaps you don't know, but the lectures of a Nascent Soul True Lord are not something just anyone can listen to. At the very least, one must be at the Nascent Soul stage to audit. And since auditing the Sect Master's lectures, our master's cultivation progress has been advancing by leaps and bounds. Although she rarely shows it, from what some senior brothers speculate, our master is probably already close to the late Nascent Soul stage."

"Late Nascent Soul stage!"

This time, it wasn't just Feng Zhiyan.

Even Ji Yue, who had been silent all along, exclaimed.

The late Nascent Soul stage was already a major figure in the immortal cultivation world, fully capable of guarding a region.

Above that were the great Nascent Soul cultivators who could establish their own sects!

Liu Muhan looked as if she shared in the glory, imagining, "Our master's cultivation time is extremely short, but her talent is outstanding. Together with Uncle Li Ting and five others, they are known as the Seven Sages of Suling, the most likely to break through to the Nascent Soul stage. If that day really comes, perhaps our Jade Spring Peak could also become a lineage on par with the eight great Nascent Soul Elders."

After the imagination, she returned to reality.

"Alright, I'll first take Xuan Luo and Ji Yue to choose their cave dwellings!"

Liu Muhan held a map, on which red lights flickered, but there were still many marked white dots in a dim state.

Those white dots were unowned small cave dwellings.

Ji Yue had long been itching to choose. He was the only one among the three with a heavenly spirit root, not old, only twenty-six years old.

This was also the reason he had caught Yao Xi Sage's eye and been accepted as a registered disciple.

With such talent, he naturally needed a cave dwelling with extremely abundant spiritual energy.

Soon, Ji Yue found a place to his liking.

Then it was Luo Chen's turn.

When Luo Chen chose an underground cave dwelling with ordinary spiritual energy, Liu Muhan couldn't help but frown.

"Junior Brother Xuan Luo, are you sure about this place?"

Ji Yue also released his spiritual sense to inspect it and subconsciously frowned.

In their view, this place had neither abundant metal-element power nor fire-element earth veins. Instead, earth-element spiritual energy was quite plentiful.

This didn't suit Luo Chen's physique, did it?

However, Luo Chen firmly nodded.

"This place will do! Although the spiritual energy is average, it has the advantage of a quiet surrounding environment with no one to disturb. Moreover, earth generates metal, which is also beneficial to me."

Seeing Luo Chen insist, Liu Muhan no longer stopped him. She let the two release their spiritual sense and leave their marks on the cave dwelling map.

The white dots flickered and turned red, indicating the place was now occupied.

"Alright, you two will need to clean your cave dwellings next and prepare for the ten years of hard cultivation ahead, so go directly there! Junior Sister Zhiyan, come with me to the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall to return the map, and then I'll take you back to Jade Spring Peak to cultivate."

"Goodbye, Senior Sister Liu!"

"Goodbye, Senior Sister Liu!"

After everyone had left, Luo Chen hovered above a basin, a smile appearing on his calm face.

Although the place he had chosen looked ordinary, under his spiritual eyes, it had its own special features.

This place was the convergence point of the surrounding spirit veins.

It wasn't just the overflow of spiritual energy from a single spirit vein; it also connected to several other large spirit veins.

Only because the earth's energy had sunk, making the spirit veins turbid and the spiritual energy not abundant.

But for Luo Chen, who cultivated the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra* and could convert various types of energy, this was not a problem at all.

To him, although this place appeared to be only a second-order spirit vein, its actual effect was comparable to a third-order spirit vein.

If he were ruthless enough to extract heavily from the other main veins, the effect would be...

"Such a commotion would be too great and easily attract attention. Better to cultivate peacefully until the Golden Core stage, then change to an independent cave dwelling!"

Luo Chen smiled and descended into the basin.

Using the cave dwelling token, he entered an underground cavern.

The tables, chairs, and beds inside were intact but covered with dust, showing that no one had cultivated here for a long time.

Luo Chen didn't mind. Later, on the basis of this cave dwelling, he would extend further downward. The convergence point of the spirit veins there would be his true cave dwelling.

Before that.

Luo Chen's hand flickered, and a jade slip appeared before him.

It was the technique given by Yao Xi Sage earlier.

*Metal Transformation Art*

With a sweep of his divine sense, Luo Chen first nodded, then shook his head.

It was indeed a good Foundation Establishment stage technique that could greatly enhance a cultivator's spiritual power.

But this was only a partial remnant; there should be a continuation above. And judging by its upper limit, it probably wasn't limited to just the Golden Core or Nascent Soul stage.

"A technique that leads directly to the Nascent Soul stage?"

"It's worth practicing!"

"Whether to deal with Yao Xi's inspection or to obtain the follow-up techniques later to facilitate my advancement to the Divine Transformation stage."

Luo Chen smiled faintly, then waved his sleeve.

Within the cave dwelling, dust rolled like a dragon, thrown outside the cave, instantly leaving everything spotless.

Then!

Luo Chen slapped the ground with one palm.

Rumbling sounds came from beneath the cave dwelling.

Wearing a set of sect's green Daoist robes, Luo Chen's figure transformed into a streak of light and disappeared into the depths of the earth veins.
