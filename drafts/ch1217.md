# Chapter 1217: Brilliant Display, Bountiful Rewards

On this day.

Miaodan Peak was shrouded in clouds and mist, a scene of magnificent grandeur.

Streaks of light flew in from various peaks within the Sulling Sect, a truly spectacular sight.

On the square before the hall atop the peak, what was once quiet and desolate was now nearly packed with a sea of people.

Alchemists bearing tokens with two or three stripes had entered early, waiting with great anticipation for the upcoming spectacle.

Suddenly, a dharma cloud spread across the sky, blotting out the sun and inspiring awe.

"It's a Sage!"

The dharma cloud arrived above Miaodan Peak, then abruptly contracted, and a figure descended before the hall.

Paying no heed to the watching crowd, he folded his arms across his chest and closed his eyes to rest.

Someone had already recognized him—it was Pure Lotus Sage of Lotus Flower Peak, who was also a fourth-rank alchemist.

Although he hadn't joined the Alchemy Institute, he was a frequent visitor to Alchemy Emperor Mountain, often entering the institute to interact with many high-rank alchemists.

No one had expected that the mere refinement of a second-rank pill would attract such a prominent figure.

But the guests who arrived one after another were no less significant than him.

From behind the Alchemy Institute, the sound of bells rang out.

Then several streaks of light descended together.

The aura of one of them surpassed even that of Pure Lotus Sage.

When that person arrived, even Pure Lotus Sage opened his eyes and took the initiative to greet him.

"Dean!"

This person was none other than the dean of the Alchemy Institute, He Lanshi, respectfully called Sage Known North by outsiders. His cultivation was at the late Sage stage, and he was renowned as a Grandmaster of the Alchemy Dao!

In the past two hundred years, he had been hailed as the foremost among the Seven Sages of Sulling!

Dean He smiled slightly. "I never expected that the refinement of a mere second-rank pill by our institute today would bring you here in person, junior brother."

Pure Lotus Sage shook his head. "Though that pill is good, it naturally has no effect on the likes of us. I came for that legendary alchemy genius."

"Xuan Luozi?" Dean He spoke the name, then nodded in understanding. "He is indeed a rare alchemy genius!"

With extraordinary alchemy talent, he had refined a first-rank pill on the day he entered the institute, and succeeded with three types within three months.

Moreover, at the pill-testing meeting, he had boldly proposed his own ideas, opening new avenues of thought for the Alchemy Institute.

Three years later, he had easily become a second-rank alchemist.

Finally, he joined He Jun's group, personally refined his ideas, and created the Circulation Traveling Qi Pill suited to the current cultivation environment.

Such talent, even if he was merely an Initiate cultivator and currently only a second-rank alchemist, was enough to earn the favor of important figures.

Most of those present today had come for the Circulation Traveling Qi Pill.

But among the few high-rank cultivators, the majority had come for Xuan Luozi himself!

And indeed!

When the hall masters and deans of the other three halls and five institutes arrived, they too discussed Xuan Luozi.

They all praised the Alchemy Institute for producing a true genius!

"Oh? Why has Senior Brother Iron Horse also come?" Pure Lotus Sage scanned the crowd and was surprised to find a solitary, aloof middle-aged man standing on the other side.

Even Dean He was somewhat surprised. "The Tian'ge Peak lineage of Junior Brother Iron Horse has never been interested in the alchemy path. They advocate cultivation without reliance on external things, purely through bitter training and tempering. For him to come is truly unexpected."

No one went over to greet him, as Iron Horse Sage had a somewhat eccentric personality and wasn't sociable.

They simply waited patiently, their gazes falling on the newly built high platform in the square.

An opening had been made in the fire vein connected from underground, and a blazing earthfire burned fiercely.

A pitch-black, half-man-high pill furnace sat atop it.

Around it stood six Initiate cultivators, each holding a wooden tray laden with a large quantity of medicinal herbs.

Though those herbs were valuable, they weren't hard to find; most were cultivated and grown within the Sulling Sect.

High-rank alchemists only needed a single glance to understand.

Those herbs were presumably the raw materials for refining the Circulation Traveling Qi Pill.

But as something improved from an ancient incomplete formula, how could it be so simple? There must be mysteries in the actual refinement process.

Two figures walked out from behind the crowd, and wherever they passed, everyone made way.

One was old, like a flickering candle in the wind.

The other was young, like a newborn sun.

Gongsun Mao surveyed the crowd, a hint of pride in his eyes, but in the end, it only turned into words of encouragement.

"Xuan Luozi, don't be nervous!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Alright."

He calmly walked past Gongsun Mao, met the expectant gazes of He Jun and the other six, and under the watchful eyes of the crowd, ascended the pill platform.

Without excessive formalities, he simply clasped his fists in a salute to everyone and briefly introduced himself.

"True Disciple under Sage Yaoxi of Yuquan Peak, second-stripe alchemist of the Alchemy Institute, Xuan Luozi!"

Then, as if no one else were present, he formed a hand seal.

In an instant!

A fierce earthfire burst forth from the opening, beginning to scorch the pill furnace intensely.

The originally black furnace turned red under the earthfire's heat.

Not only that, but Luo Chen also took out a bottle and poured a specially prepared liquid into the furnace.

After the liquid entered the furnace, the red color immediately faded. As the furnace temperature rose and the color changed again, Luo Chen poured in another special liquid.

After repeating this cycle three times, Luo Chen began to draw the medicinal herbs from the wooden trays held by He Jun and the other six into the furnace.

A series of intricate alchemy techniques unfolded in Luo Chen's hands, using different methods to process a large number of auxiliary and main ingredients.

The previously noisy square had fallen silent.

Everyone's gaze followed Luo Chen's figure, following the hand seals he cast.

Laymen couldn't see clearly, feeling that all the techniques were much the same—merely differences in spiritual power output and rhythm.

But in the eyes of the alchemists, Luo Chen's techniques were as beautiful as those of the most skilled carving master, each change bringing about an effect like the work of a divine craftsman.

The refinement of a second-rank pill was simple in some ways, complex in others.

Time passed slowly amidst Luo Chen's methodical refinement.

No one showed impatience; they all waited for that final moment.

From the rising sun to its zenith in the sky.

When the evening glow spread for a thousand li, a long-suppressed fragrance of pills had somehow already filled the nostrils of the crowd.

Clang!

Suddenly, the sound of the furnace lid opening was heard.

A smile flickered across the handsome, almost demonically beautiful face of the young man. With a wave of his hand, ten round, golden, star-like pills flew out, finally landing on the jade tray held by Gongsun Mao below.

Success on the first try!

Luo Chen clasped his fists once more, his etiquette impeccable.

The pill was complete, the ceremony finished.

Next came the pill tasting.

Gongsun Mao, holding the jade tray, stepped up the high steps one by one under everyone's gaze, arriving before the leaders of the three halls and five institutes.

Dean He took one pill and also handed one to Pure Lotus Sage.

The last one was kept for Gongsun Mao.

Everyone tasted and appraised them, from color and appearance to the concentration and dispersion of medicinal power, and even the various alchemy techniques Luo Chen had displayed during the process, as well as the most basic raw materials.

Finally, a consensus was reached!

The Circulation Traveling Qi Pill was a success!

And it had fully met the standards for large-scale mass production!

When this news spread, the entire venue erupted.

He Jun's group gathered around Luo Chen, cheering.

Even for Luo Chen, such a scene brought a genuine smile to his face.

He didn't deliberately control his expression, didn't intentionally suppress his Dao heart, but enjoyed the praise from everyone with great pleasure.

He had already understood.

The transformation of the mind wasn't about constant suppression.

In the past, whenever a stray thought arose in his mind, he would immediately cut it off, lest it affect his cultivation and invite inner demons.

But he didn't know that constant suppression would only brew greater inner demons.

This was evident from the frequent demonic obstacles he encountered in his early days.

A cultivator should instead practice a balance of restraint and release.

Experience various emotional changes firsthand, and ultimately emerge unstained from the mud, maintaining a clear Dao heart.

Perhaps another term could describe it.

Unobstructed thoughts!

Just like this, idly listening to flowers bloom and wither, sitting and watching clouds rise and fall.

When there are no more attachments, that will be the day his mind achieves great accomplishment.

Unnoticed by anyone, Luo Chen's vast soul became quieter, less conspicuous.

But at this moment, everyone's attention was on Luo Chen.

The greatest contributor to this successful refinement of the mass-producible Circulation Traveling Qi Pill was Gongsun Mao.

He had found the incomplete formula, supplemented it, and single-handedly driven this plan forward.

But he was already very old, and having used life-extending methods, his lifespan had long exceeded the five-hundred-year limit of the Adept stage.

No matter how much reward was given to him, it was insignificant.

And Luo Chen was the most crucial link in completing this plan, having truly verified its feasibility.

He was also likely to be an important figure in leading the mass production of the Circulation Traveling Qi Pill in the future.

In such a scenario, if there were no rewards, it would be unacceptable.

Dean He stepped forward, smiling warmly. "I bestow upon Xuan Luozi the token of a third-stripe alchemist, award him a cave dwelling on Lingdan Peak, and grant him access to Alchemy Emperor Mountain. He may freely peruse all pill formulas and alchemy texts within the Alchemy Institute. If he encounters any difficulties on his alchemy path, he may consult any high-rank alchemist, and no one shall withhold their knowledge."

As soon as this reward was announced, everyone understood Dean He's intention.

He had granted Luo Chen unimaginable authority!

This was to use the full strength of the entire Alchemy Institute to nurture Luo Chen!

"Furthermore, I have observed that you possess extremely skilled techniques in fire control, yet lack a source true flame. I hereby bestow upon you a fifth-rank fire seed. May you refine it, nurture it well, and let it aid you in advancing further on the alchemy path in the future!"

As he spoke, a flame flew out from his palm.

The flame was like flowing gold, burning like the sun's core, majestic as an emperor, inspiring fear in all living things.

"It's the Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame!"

Gasps of surprise rose from the crowd.

Everyone knew that Dean He was renowned as the foremost of the Seven Sages, excelling in both alchemy and swordsmanship.

He primarily cultivated the sword path, but his reputation in alchemy surpassed even his swordsmanship. What truly enabled his great achievements in alchemy was a domineering strange fire he had refined in his early years, which he had cultivated to the level of a fifth-rank divine flame.

That was the Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame!

No one expected that he would actually split a seed of his own source true flame and bestow it upon Xuan Luo!

Even Luo Chen was caught off guard.

The drifting fire seed was quite docile.

But the terrifying high temperature it emitted had the power to burn away all spiritual energy.

This was a divine flame with a fundamental rank as high as the fifth rank!

After such extremely high authority rewards, there was even this strange fire, so helpful to alchemists, as a gift.

Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly, and he clasped his fists. "Thank you for the gift, Dean!"

Dean He stroked his beard and smiled. "No hurry, no hurry."

As Luo Chen looked on in confusion, Dean He turned to the others beside him with a beaming smile.

"All of you have rarely come to watch such a good show. In the future, if the Circulation Traveling Qi Pill is truly mass-produced, your disciples will surely benefit greatly. Shouldn't you show some appreciation?"

The dean of the Spirit Medicine Institute laughed and scolded, "No wonder you made such a big show of inviting us to participate in the pill-tasting meeting. So you old fellow were setting a trap for us here!"

Though it was a scolding, she didn't refuse.

After a moment's thought, she said leisurely, "I came in a hurry and didn't prepare any gifts. I don't have anything suitable for an Initiate cultivator on me. So... Xuan Luo, from now on, whenever you need medicinal herbs for your personal alchemy, come to my Spirit Medicine Institute and you'll always get a twenty percent discount. How about that?"

A twenty percent discount seemed ordinary.

But how many pills would a genius alchemist refine in a lifetime?

If the Spirit Medicine Institute dean's promise remained in effect forever, the spirit stones saved would be an astronomical figure.

Luo Chen naturally wouldn't refuse and quickly thanked her.

The other deans and hall masters, seeing this, couldn't lose face and also gave their rewards.

The reward from the dean of the Hundred Ingenuities Institute wasn't generous, but it was very suitable for Luo Chen's realm.

A treasure embryo, a metal-attribute flying sword!

If Luo Chen could successfully advance to the Adept stage, this excellent treasure embryo would save him a great deal of effort and could be cultivated as his lifebound treasure.

The Formation Institute gave a reward similar to the Spirit Medicine Institute's—whether it was Luo Chen's cave dwelling, alchemy chamber, or his own medicine garden, wherever formations were needed, the Formation Institute could dispatch high-rank formation masters to help arrange them without charge.

The Law Enforcement Institute simply said one thing: if he needed people, just ask!

Then came the three halls.

The hall master of the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall had nothing impressive to offer, only spirit stones. He rewarded Luo Chen with a hundred high-grade spirit stones in one go, worth a million low-grade spirit stones—a huge sum for an Initiate cultivator personally.

The External Affairs Hall was always responsible for distributing sect contribution points. This time, they rewarded Luo Chen with a million sect contribution points, and also rewarded He Jun and the other six, which was the reward they deserved for their contributions to the sect.

The Scripture Transmission Hall gave Luo Chen the authority to freely enter the first to eighth floors of the Scripture Pavilion, and also offered sufficient discounts for borrowing and copying texts.

After a round of rewards, Luo Chen, who had only been known as a genius, instantly became a wealthy man.

It made everyone's eyes turn red.

Although the rewards from the three halls and five institutes were mostly given out of respect for Dean He, they also showed the sect's determination to nurture true geniuses!

This also greatly stimulated the determination of the Alchemy Institute and the other three halls and five institutes to research new things.

Who knew, if they could also make innovative breakthroughs similar to the Circulation Traveling Qi Pill, the good fortune that had befallen Luo Chen might fall on them.

And amidst the bustle, a voice as cold as ice and as hard as metal suddenly rang out.

"Xuan Luo, would you be willing to become my disciple?"

Everyone was startled and looked toward the source of the voice.

It was Iron Horse Sage, from the Tian'ge lineage, possessing the late Sage stage, and together with Dean He, Li Ting Sage, Yao Xi Sage, and three others, known as one of the Seven Sages of Sulling!

At this moment, his gaze was burning, fixed intently on Luo Chen, with the fervent look of one who had discovered a peerless uncut jade.

A Sharp Gold Body, truly a Sharp Gold Body.

And the metal-attribute spiritual power he displayed was extremely pure, indicating that the corresponding chapter of the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Mystic Scripture* he cultivated had reached an extremely high level.

This was a true genius!

Not just in alchemy, but in cultivation as well.

To have him focus solely on alchemy was to put the cart before the horse.

Such talent should not be hindered by external things, but should concentrate on cultivation, with the full strength of the sect supporting him.

In the future, he would form his core, condense his sage embryo, break through to the Ascendant stage, and become the pillar of the Sulling Sect!
