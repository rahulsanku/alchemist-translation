# Chapter 1232: Five Spirits Dao Domain, Purple Mansion Reborn

Within the Ink Jade Space.

The tall tower stood, and the Daoist sat in stillness.

A vast amount of demonic qi surged forth, forming a massive vortex that poured into Luo Chen's body, causing his blue robes to rustle violently.

Within him, a long-lost sensation of transformation was sweeping through his entire being!

The Three Flowers Gathering at the Crown, the Five Qis facing the Origin.

Shattering the core, forming the Nascent Soul, entering the Dao and claiming Truehood!

Because of this feeling, even his vast divine soul, which had lain dormant for so long, seemed to become joyful.

With a thought, a small infant flew out from the crown of Luo Chen's head.

Its body was robust, far exceeding that of an ordinary Sage, and its eyes were incredibly lively.

Five-colored spiritual qi swirled around it, like ribbons, like flowing light, appearing utterly dreamlike.

As the Nascent Soul breathed, the surrounding spiritual energy circulated, naturally forming a Sage domain.

The first to manifest was the Golden Gate Dao Domain, born from cultivating the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Mysterious Chapter*. Pages resembling heavenly scriptures floated within it, each page containing immense power.

Then came a domain phenomenon of cyan-green and grey-white colors—this was the Withering-Bloom Dao Domain!

Immediately after, vast dharma power stirred the world's spiritual energy, exploding outward for a thousand li, creating a scene of a sea of qi floating in the sky!

The domain shifted again!

The familiar All-Encompassing Fire Prison once again appeared around Luo Chen. Vast fire-attribute spiritual energy coiled around the Nascent Soul, manifesting layers of fiery prison houses.

Finally, the blazing flames settled to the ground, forming an Earth Element Dharma Domain that could not be destroyed for ten thousand generations!

Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth—five domains cycled endlessly on Luo Chen's body, rotating in a perpetual loop.

And deep within each domain, faint traces of lightning and thunder could be seen.

Luo Chen joyfully felt his Nascent Soul controlling the world's spiritual energy. This was a sensation he had not possessed for a long time, an instinct that had once persisted for over three hundred years.

Now, it was finally regained.

But he was still not satisfied!

He carefully guided his vast divine soul into the Nascent Soul.

Soon, the little Nascent Soul figure felt a sensation of fullness.

One must know that the foundation of Luo Chen's newly condensed Nascent Soul was far deeper than that of ordinary Sage cultivators. Even late-stage grand cultivators who had cultivated for many years might not match his current strength.

Yet even so, it still could not bear his complete divine soul, showing just how powerful Luo Chen's soul was.

It seemed that unless he achieved the Ascendant stage, this soul would have no place to reside, forced to remain hidden deep within his sea of consciousness.

Luo Chen was not anxious. When the Nascent Soul was about to reach its limit, he severed part of his soul, leaving only a small fraction within the Nascent Soul.

Thus, the previously somewhat dull little Nascent Soul figure instantly became lively.

Then!

Dharma power surged, and the five domains emerged together.

With the empowerment of divine sense, the five Sage domains gradually merged.

White-gold, cyan-green, azure-blue, crimson-red, and then mysterious yellow settling.

Five colors intertwined, five spirits coexisting.

"This is the Five Spirits Dao Domain!"

The moment this domain was born, the entire Ink Jade Space trembled, as if it were about to be crushed.

It was unimaginable—if facing an ordinary Sage cultivator in battle, how could an opponent possibly execute their techniques under the suppression of Luo Chen's domain power?

It seemed that once the domain was unleashed, it would be enough to crush any Sage cultivator.

But the changes brought by Luo Chen's Nascent Soul formation did not stop there!

Within his robust physical body, the new qi sea constructed through the flesh-and-blood transformation technique was rumbling as it evolved into the Purple Mansion where the Nascent Soul would reside.

Just as the Purple Mansion was about to take shape, a deafening sound of shattering came from deep within his flesh.

The old Purple Mansion, which had once borne Luo Chen's vast primordial spirit, was visibly cracking.

Originally, this old Purple Mansion was already severely damaged. Now, stimulated in this way, it shattered faster and faster.

But the Purple Mansion barriers that crumbled merged into the new Purple Mansion.

Rising from destruction, reviving amidst ruins.

A Purple Mansion, like an immortal palace or imperial court, was gradually being born between decay and renewal.

This process was extremely fast, yet also extremely slow.

From Luo Chen's perspective and even his divine sense, it felt incredibly rapid, but for his physical body and reality, it was agonizingly slow.

The little Nascent Soul figure had been hovering above his crown, not rushing back, but instead breathing in the world's spiritual energy to supply the generation of the new Purple Mansion.

Within the Ink Jade Space, due to the abundance of Netherworld Demon Moss, most of the qi generated was demonic qi.

During this breathing process, the five-colored, resplendent Nascent Soul gradually took on a tinge of black.

Luo Chen paid it no mind.

This level of "demon contamination" could be easily transformed into pure dharma power by circulating the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra* later, without affecting anything.

As Luo Chen focused on constructing his new Purple Mansion, time began to flow rapidly.

Days passed, years passed.

Another ten years later.

A thunderous roar that no one could hear exploded within Luo Chen's body.

Boom!

The new Purple Mansion was born!

It was positioned above the qi-blood furnace obtained from body refinement.

Immediately, the little Nascent Soul figure that had been lingering outside fled back inside.

Like a wanderer returning home, a fledgling returning to its nest.

And the Purple Mansion, far thicker and vaster than before, gave him a sense of security he had never felt before.

As dharma power circulated, the ethereal, otherworldly aura of an immortal once again appeared on Luo Chen's body.

Luo Chen examined himself internally and nodded with satisfaction.

"This step is about done."

Suddenly!

His expression shifted slightly, and his divine sense focused on a single point.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron, which he had previously hidden, emerged.

This time, it did not enter the Purple Mansion.

Instead, it settled between the qi-blood furnace and the Purple Mansion.

In an instant, astral qi surged upward, dharma power sank downward, and two extremely powerful forces converged within the precious cauldron.

A method of nurturing that had never existed before appeared, driven by Luo Chen's instinct.

And the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which had not evolved for a long time, began an imperceptible but unstoppable transformation.

A realization dawned on him.

"Perhaps this is the final step for the Primordial Unity Cauldron to achieve the status of a Dao Artifact!"

In the past, the Primordial Unity Cauldron had been nurtured with dharma power to advance to a true artifact.

In the Guixu, it had absorbed countless law fragments and even condensed a complete law great dao, achieving the prototype of a Dao Artifact.

At that time, Luo Chen could only nurture it with astral qi, lacking the dharma power it should have had.

Now, with the Purple Mansion newly formed and the Nascent Soul breathing automatically, both dharma power and astral qi acted upon the Primordial Unity Cauldron simultaneously, finally stirring his lifebound treasure.

Luo Chen could hardly estimate when the Primordial Unity Cauldron would reach completion.

But he did not intend to be stingy with his dharma power and astral qi. He was prepared to expend effort, nurturing it day and night.

Even if it slowed his cultivation speed somewhat, it would be worth it.

If one day he could activate the Primordial Unity Cauldron, transformed to perfection, the boost to his combat power would be immeasurable.

As for now?

Luo Chen raised his head and looked into the distance at the entrance.

The teleportation formation he had damaged was showing a gap.

From outside to inside!

"The sect's elders have finally found a way to enter this space."

Luo Chen smiled slightly, his gaze falling on the first level of the Five Turbidities Tower.

"It's time to let Jiang Huang wake up."

...

Whoosh!

The wind rose from somewhere, but to the ears of the two late-stage Sage cultivators, they knew where it came from.

"The reverse spirit channel has opened!"

Within the ancient Daoist temple, various formations were operating.

Sage Youhuang was at the center, fully controlling the formations, facing the demon statue from afar.

This was a profound technique within formations, known as using formations to control formations.

And what was being controlled was a teleportation formation, creating a rare reverse spirit channel.

Although the scale was small, fortunately, the space behind the demon statue was very close to this place, and the reverse spirit channel was relatively stable.

"Yaoxi, go in quickly!" Sage Youhuang shouted, a look of strain on her face.

Sage Yaoxi nodded, not daring to delay.

She turned into a streak of light and disappeared into the reverse spirit channel.

When she reappeared, she was inside the Ink Jade Space.

Seeing the Netherworld Demon Moss covering the ground, she couldn't help but show a look of surprise.

Even though she had heard about it from her disciples earlier, seeing it in person was still shocking.

Beneath the noses of the three Ascendant-level powers in the Xuan Realm, there was actually such a large-scale demonized area.

Based on the intensity of her divine sense, which spread across most of the space, she still couldn't see the end of the demon moss's spread.

It was unimaginable how long it must have taken to reach this extent.

Taking a deep breath, Sage Yaoxi already had a sharp weapon in hand, with a jade ruyi hovering above her head.

After all, there was an enemy at the Sage level here.

If things went wrong, she would have to retreat.

Fortunately, her divine sense detected only two Sage cultivators, both at the early Sage stage.

And both of them gave her a familiar feeling.

"Oh?"

Yaoxi was stunned, then overjoyed. She turned into a streak of light and shot away.

Moments later.

Sage Yaoxi looked at the two before her with a complex expression.

One was clad in dark green battle armor, half-conscious and half-awake.

The other had demon moss patterns covering his face, handsome yet eerie.

"Jiang Huang, Xuan Luo."

The two stood outside the pagoda, each bowing respectfully.

"Master!"

Feeling the strange dharma power aura on the two, and their unmistakable Sage realm, Yaoxi's emotions fluctuated.

"How did this happen?"

Before the two could answer, she looked around again.

"Where is the enemy?"

Luo Chen remained motionless, while Jiang Huang raised her head with a confused look and spoke in a hoarse voice.

"Master, the enemies are all dead!"

...

Within the Daoist temple.

Sage Youhuang struggled to maintain her position, the surrounding formations showing signs of breaking.

The reverse spirit channel was not easy to maintain.

This technique was usually exclusive to Ascendant-level experts who could control the world's origin qi, a great divine ability said to be able to reversely open the barriers between two realms.

Although she had used formations to achieve it cleverly, her peak mid-Sage realm was still barely enough.

Her friend had been inside for a while.

In her perception, there had been no battle over there.

But why hadn't she come out yet?

Just as she was about to send a warning signal, the blood-red mouth of the demon statue suddenly opened.

Amidst a surge of spiritual light, three figures slowly emerged.

At that moment, Sage Youhuang could no longer sustain the formation and interrupted its maintenance.

Chi...

Like a candle flame being extinguished, the surrounding formations all dimmed.

Luo Chen glanced at the nearby situation, a thoughtful look in his eyes.

No wonder two Sage cultivators could enter the Ink Jade Space. He hadn't expected Sage Yaoxi to invite such a formation expert, actually opening a reverse spirit channel.

He didn't know this technique.

But since the other party could do it, it seemed the Suliing Sect had records of this aspect.

After returning, he could take the time to borrow and read them.

While he was observing his surroundings, Sage Youhuang's gaze also fell on the three.

She already knew Luo Chen's appearance, but the other female cultivator was somewhat unfamiliar.

"Oh? Yaoxi, who is this?"

Sage Yaoxi said with some complexity, "My former disciple, Jiang Huang. Don't you recognize her?"

Sage Youhuang was stunned.

Sage Yaoxi sighed, unsure whether she was happy or worried. She waved her hand and said, "This is Sage Youhuang of Guzhu Peak. She came specifically to help with Xuan Luo's matter. Why don't you quickly thank your shishu?"

Jiang Huang looked at Sage Youhuang with confusion, still appearing mentally unclear.

But Luo Chen had already cupped his hands in salute. "Xuan Luo thanks Shishu!"

Sage Youhuang nodded, then looked at Sage Yaoxi with a face full of doubt.

"What exactly happened? Weren't we supposed to rescue a mid-stage Initiate disciple? How did he come out as a Sage, and you even found a disciple who went missing two hundred years ago?"

Sage Yaoxi shook her head. "It's a long story. I'll tell you on the way back."

Sage Youhuang hummed in acknowledgment. "Alright, let's clean up and have the sect's disciples take over the guard duty. Don't let any other wandering cultivators in."

Before leaving, Sage Youhuang made some arrangements.

She repeatedly instructed the disciples who would be stationed there, not only forbidding outsiders from entering but also prohibiting sect disciples from going inside.

This not-so-vast Ink Jade Space would be properly handled by the Suliing Sect later.

Whether to remove the demon moss, cleanse the demonic qi, or utilize it for other purposes was a matter for another discussion.

Luo Chen and Jiang Huang boarded Sage Yaoxi's flying carriage, heading back to the Suliing Sect after twenty years away.

...

A massive flying boat cut through the sky, leaving only an empty trail hanging in the heavens.

On the flying boat, pavilions and towers were everywhere, and flying springs and waterfalls cascaded.

Its specifications were clearly not those of an ordinary Sage.

In fact, this Flying Spring Treasure Boat was not something an average Sage cultivator could possess. It was once the personal carriage of Tai Su Lingjun during her Ascendant stage.

Later, it was bestowed upon Sage Yaoxi.

At this moment, beneath a small mountain, two figures moved with lotus steps, speaking softly.

"So Jiang Huang saved Xuan Luo and even killed the demon clan expert who had been controlling her?"

"That should be correct. I examined the demon's corpse, and it was indeed killed by my self-created Flying Waterfall Sword Technique. Within the entire Suliing Sect, only Jiang Huang and I have such attainments."

"But wasn't that demon at the late Sage stage?"

"Realm is secondary. The other party was of the Po Luo race, only skilled in soul-fixing and soul-seizing, not good at killing, and extremely weak in combat. While initiating Xuan Luo, their mind was greatly divided, giving Jiang Huang the opportunity to strike and kill them. But the consequences were also severe. After killing her master, Jiang Huang suffered severe soul backlash, which is why she is in her current state."

"Alas, this female disciple of yours has truly suffered many calamities!"

"Being able to return alive is the greatest fortune. Those calamities can be considered tempering on the path of cultivation. And didn't misfortune turn into blessing? Both of them have formed their Nascent Souls."

"But I feel their current state is very unstable. Jiang Huang is mentally unclear, Xuan Luo's realm fluctuates, especially the Netherworld Demon Moss rooted in their bodies and even their Nascent Souls. This is by no means the normal path to forming a Nascent Soul. If this continues, not to mention the hope of future Ascension, even their lives might be in danger!"

"This matter..."

Sage Yaoxi was equally worried, but her expression gradually became resolute.

"Two disciples have been lost and found again. I absolutely cannot ignore this. I will request the sect's Ascendant elder, even the Sect Master himself, to personally take action and save them!"

Sage Youhuang was not optimistic.

The matter of demon moss attaching to the body was frequently recorded in ancient texts.

Whether human cultivators or demon clan experts, once infected, it was very difficult to remove.

The Netherworld Demon Moss could even root itself in the soil of the Xuan Realm, transforming an entire region.

This was a top-tier method specifically developed by the Mahayana experts of the Demon Realm to invade the Xuan Realm.

To remove it, only one or two methods had been found so far, and the conditions were extremely harsh.

Sage Youhuang thought for a moment and said softly, "How about letting them also participate in the Demon-Suppressing Trial, using the method of demon marrow drills to save themselves?"

Sage Yaoxi pondered silently, somewhat hesitant.

"The Demon-Suppressing Trial is too dangerous. That is the last resort. Let us return and seek the decision of the sect's elders!"
