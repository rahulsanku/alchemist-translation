# Chapter 1198: All Preparations, Before Departure

Within the sea of consciousness, the waves gradually subsided.

Luo Chen's main soul flickered with faint light, as if contemplating something.

After shattering the residual soul attack lingering within the golden light just moments ago, he had captured some information from it.

This information was extremely complex and chaotic, but when summarized, it roughly pertained to the law-based divine ability that Gold Punishment Venerable had unleashed.

Its name—**Gold Punishment Great Execution**!

It was a divine ability of immense power and terrifying lethality.

When unleashed, drawing upon one's own flesh and blood while channeling the origin qi of heaven and earth, it was enough to sever a star with a single strike.

Unfortunately, Gold Punishment Venerable could not choose his battlefield. Within this Return to Void, devoid of spiritual energy and with shattered laws, he simply could not drive this technique to its peak.

"If I could learn this technique, then when I later rebuild my Five Elements spirit root, it might become a major trump card."

Luo Chen's divine thought flashed past, and then he sensed something amiss.

He quickly forced his main soul into a state of feigned slumber.

All information related to the Gold Punishment Great Execution was also suppressed.

Opening his eyes, he looked at the sky.

Sure enough, streaks of silver-white lightning, like writhing dragons and serpents, had begun to converge.

These were all attracted by his use of his massive main soul.

Even though he had now absorbed the Chaotic Thunder Pool and could withstand the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound within a small range, Luo Chen still did not want to invite trouble.

Especially at this most dangerous moment.

As expected, after his main soul fell dormant, the chaotic thunderstorm gathering in the sky seemed to lose its target and gradually dissipated.

Luo Chen smiled and rose calmly.

After removing the residual power of the divine ability, he felt completely at ease.

His apertures opened and closed freely, no longer obstructed.

Even without the protection of the Primordial Unity Cauldron's chaotic gray light, the surrounding chaotic energy could no longer pose much of a threat to him.

Luo Chen did not rush to deal with miscellaneous matters. Instead, he took time to make a trip outside.

...

On Wind Snow Island.

Luo Chen met his old friend, the newly ascended Martial Dao Human Immortal, Xiao Lin.

After offering congratulations, he stated his purpose to the three Human Immortals.

"I plan to close the Ninefold Immortal Tower ahead of schedule. For the next hundred years, I will focus entirely on seclusion and cultivation, making the final push to leave the Return to Void."

Xiao Lin already understood fragments of the truth about the Return to Void and had also learned from Fang Qingxue and Gu Xun about the impending destruction. He was not surprised by Luo Chen's decision.

He just asked curiously, "What about the many martial artists still inside the tower?"

Luo Chen replied, "Except for Ice Son and Wild Barbarian Martial Ancestor, who still have hope of breaking through to the Human Immortal realm, all other martial artists will be expelled."

He spoke these words very calmly, but they had already pronounced a death sentence for those about to be expelled.

The three did not question this ruthless decision.

Perhaps Luo Chen's action was cruel to them—expelling them meant waiting for death.

But staying behind was also waiting for death, was it not?

When great calamity looms, everyone can barely look after themselves. Where would they find the strength to protect others?

Going out early might give them a billion-to-one chance of finding a way to survive.

The three's silence clearly indicated their acceptance of Luo Chen's arrangement.

With no further doubts, Luo Chen spread his martial dao divine will before them.

In an instant, his unique will enveloped every corner of the Nine Rings of Destruction.

All the martial artists still inside the tower fell silent at once, hearing Luo Chen's proclamation.

"I am Luo Chen, current master of the Ninefold Immortal Tower. I must announce to all Nine Ring martial artists that in a hundred years, the cycle of epochs will turn, and the era of Great Destruction will arrive. At that time, all things will return to nothingness; nothing will remain. Everyone, seek your own path to survival!"

As his words fell, the beings within the tower were shocked.

But before they could react, chains of gray light extended from the void, binding them and casting them out directly.

Only Ice Son and Wild Barbarian Martial Ancestor from the Fourth Ring were left behind.

Outside the tower.

The group of Leakless Realm martial artists finally reacted.

Some tried to re-enter the Ninefold Immortal Tower, but were directly repelled by the increasingly turbulent chaotic energy surrounding it.

Those chaotic energies even formed nebula-like masses, enveloping the entire Ninefold Immortal Tower.

In an instant, despair filled the crowd.

Some began to curse Luo Chen loudly, their harsh words unceasing.

Unfortunately, no matter how foul their language, there was no response.

Some, in their despair, began to beg Luo Chen bitterly, but still received no reply.

Eventually, some took extreme measures, attempting to attack the Ninefold Immortal Tower. But all their attacks, upon contacting the increasingly violent chaotic energy, vanished like mud thrown into the sea.

These martial artists had to accept the reality: they could never return to the Ninefold Immortal Tower, and they must face the coming terror.

"This is the tragedy of the weak. You two, work hard!"

Xiao Lin returned to the Fourth Ring and said this to Ice Son and Wild Barbarian Martial Ancestor.

He briefly explained why Luo Chen had kept them behind.

After leaving a few words of encouragement, he returned to the Third Ring and ultimately chose to settle on Lichen Island.

This place, Luo Chen had already ceded to him.

Because Luo Chen would be cultivating in the Second Ring next.

Xiao Lin looked up at the sky. Though he could not see Luo Chen's figure, he could still feel the vast will that the other had revealed, like the tip of an iceberg.

He murmured to himself, "Luo Chen, can this path really be walked?"

...

In the past, Luo Chen cared about how others saw him, because reputation was a useful weapon.

But now, Luo Chen no longer cared about such things, especially within the Return to Void.

Under the Great Destruction, there would be great terror!

At that time, everyone would have to fight for themselves.

He did not have the compassionate mindset to treat all beings kindly, so he had to be selfish.

Completely closing the Ninefold Immortal Tower, transferring chaotic energy to assist his accelerated cultivation—this was the most urgent matter at hand.

Now!

From the Fourth Ring downward, all chaotic energy was channeled through the Ninefold Immortal Tower to the Second Ring.

The surrounding energy was so turbulent that even the Primordial Unity Cauldron could not absorb it all at once.

But before cultivation, some trivial matters needed to be briefly handled.

His gaze fell upon the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

After absorbing the Chaotic Thunder Pool, the Yuan Magnetic Divine Power, and the Star Crystal, this lifebound treasure of his was undergoing unknown changes.

And these changes were for the better.

Luo Chen raised his hand.

Instantly, three forces, each independent yet equally powerful, emerged from the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Under Luo Chen's divine will, they gradually fused into something even more terrifying.

Starlight flickered, thunder rumbled, and as they struggled against each other, they were also drawn together by a mysterious magnetic force.

Through his connection with his lifebound treasure, Luo Chen could easily control this power.

He struck out into the distance, and a streak of light flashed, erupting at the point of impact with waves of Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound.

"I imagine that even if the enemy has defensive techniques, it will be difficult to resist this move."

"Starlight is omnipresent, thunder arrives in an instant, and the Yuan Magnetic Divine Power overcomes all Five Elements. Whether used for frontal assault or ambush, this divine ability inherent to the Primordial Unity Cauldron is formidable enough."

"Let's call it... **Heavenly Star Yuan Magnetic Divine Thunder**!"

Luo Chen could not be bothered to think of a fancy name. He took one characteristic from each of the three forces and named it accordingly.

Then, Luo Chen took out an object.

The Vacuum Gu!

Compared to before, the Vacuum Gu's changes were increasingly obvious.

Its size had not changed much, but the glacier-like folds on its surface had become more intricate.

All of this was thanks to the large amount of Void Sprout Crystals Luo Chen had obtained from Corpse Old Demon's clone.

Even now, he had not finished consuming them.

Observing closely, Luo Chen's brow furrowed slightly.

"Why, after eating so many Void Sprout Crystals and a vast number of Spatial Prism Apertures, has it still not broken through to the fifth rank? Is it really that difficult to cultivate a Divine Gu?"

Luo Chen had some understanding of gu insects, but ultimately not enough.

Whether it was refining gu, using gu, or raising gu.

But after experiencing the many miraculous effects of gu insects, he had never forgotten this special existence.

Whether it was the heart-to-heart connection of the Spirit-Rhinoceros Gu, the power theft and conversion of the Devil Heart Gu, or the forced binding of the Life-and-Death Gu.

Especially the Fortune and Misfortune Gu!

His arrival in the Return to Void was "indebted" to a trace of Misfortune Gu aura left by Netherleave Divine Lord.

Although the Return to Void environment was harsh, it had indeed allowed his strength to advance by leaps and bounds in a short time, with the hope of breaking through to a higher realm before leaving the Return to Void.

The time spent, even counting what came after, was only five hundred years.

Not to mention the techniques, divine abilities, and resources gained along the way.

In his view, the path of gu insects was another way to cultivate the origin of the Heavenly Dao. Otherwise, he could not explain the peculiar effects those gu insects possessed.

If he could cultivate a Divine Gu of his own, it would surely be of great benefit, whether for escaping the Return to Void or for future cultivation.

"Could it be that Old Ghost Nanqi deceived me back then, holding back on the method to cultivate the Vacuum Gu?"

"Or perhaps even he himself did not know the complete cultivation method?"

With an uncertain expression, Luo Chen took a deep breath.

He carefully took out an object and pushed it before the Vacuum Gu.

"Can you eat this?"

A stone emitting a five-colored light floated quietly before him.

The Vacuum Gu, its body like pure dark glass, trembled slightly, then flew up and directly lay on top of it.

From its toothless mouth, a large amount of liquid was secreted, enveloping the entire five-colored stone.

Seeing this, Luo Chen's spirits lifted greatly.

"This gu can actually eat Star Crystals!"

His trip to the Sea of Stars had been very fruitful.

Not only had he perfected his technique and increased his realm, but his lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, had also undergone a transformation.

In addition, he had obtained an extra Star Crystal.

According to Danyang, the current master of the Star Sect, this thing was harmful to martial artists, greatly hindering martial dao divine will. But for cultivators, it was an excellent material for refining artifacts.

Adding just a piece could explosively increase the power of a cultivator's magic treasure.

Luo Chen had originally planned to refine it into the Yuantu Sword later.

But with escape from the Return to Void imminent, what he needed was not an offensive method. So he set his sights on the Vacuum Gu.

Based on his inference, the Vacuum Gu was related to spatial attributes, hence its need to consume large amounts of spatial-attribute materials.

And as the ultimate concentrated crystal core of a star, it should also possess related spatial attributes.

Now, the test proved effective!

"I hope you can undergo a transformation before we depart!"

With hopeful feelings, Luo Chen carefully put the Vacuum Gu away.

Then, he took out another object.

A white bone.

After staring at the bone for a long time, Luo Chen ultimately chose to seal it and put it away.

He had already determined that this object was not a bone from Corpse Old Demon himself.

Moreover, it was highly likely related to the ancient god in the Ancient God's Dojo, the one known as the Source of Compassionate Light.

That ancient god had long since scattered its will, but even its residual power could directly block Gold Punishment Venerable's full-force strike. It must have possessed earth-shattering power in life.

Luo Chen did not want to get involved with such an existence for now.

In fact, this was also one of the reasons he had given up on leaving through the Ancient God's Dojo.

It was not that he was afraid of Corpse Old Demon.

As he had said before, in a direct confrontation, who would live and who would die was still unknown.

Finally!

Luo Chen slapped the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Dong!

A tail, fully three thousand zhang long, lay across the chaotic void.

Its flesh and blood were mangled, a terrible sight.

Yet the terrifying power contained within it made it impossible to look away.

"Finally, this portion of the Great Vehicle True Spirit's fleshly body!"

Luo Chen licked his lips, a hint of greed in his eyes.

Such a thing, if placed outside, would be the ultimate material.

Whether for alchemy, artifact forging, formation setting, talisman making, or cultivating secret arts.

For a cultivator, there were many ways to use such excellent material.

But unfortunately, this was the Return to Void.

Not to mention whether Luo Chen had the means to use it, even if he did, he would abandon it.

Because for him, at this moment, all external enhancements were inferior to improving his own hard power.

Refining the Heavenly Star Yuan Magnetic Divine Thunder was one thing, letting the Vacuum Gu devour the Star Crystal was another. As for this nearly one-third of Gold Punishment Venerable's fleshly body, the most suitable method of use for him at present was only one.

Eat!

Luo Chen had once gained a glimpse into the path of True Spirits, so he naturally knew what power a True Spirit's fleshly body contained.

Not to mention the power of the flesh and blood itself, the source blood containing vast qi and blood power was a great tonic for martial artists.

Now that Luo Chen was stuck at the Great Perfection of the Human Immortal realm, this supplement would surely allow him to take another step forward.

A higher realm would also bring him a greater chance of escape when leaving the Return to Void.

Not only that, but for the improvement to the next realm, he had already made plans while completing his technique.

His gaze fell upon the Primordial Unity Cauldron. Luo Chen did not absorb it into his body but left it outside.

Still, he separated a martial dao deity to control it on his behalf.

Soon, the surrounding chaotic energy, like ten thousand rivers returning to the sea, was continuously absorbed into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Luo Chen temporarily took none of the energy refined from it.

After making all these arrangements, he opened his gaping mouth and bit directly into Gold Punishment Venerable's fleshly body.

At the same time, the Blood-Refined Divine Astral within his body began to circulate wildly, driving the qi and blood furnace along the path of the new technique.

This so-called new technique was the completed *God-Devil Formless*.

But as early as in the Sea of Stars, Luo Chen had already given it a more suitable name.

The *Chaos Indestructible Scripture*.

If everything went smoothly from here, he might be able to cultivate the physique he had envisioned.

Within the chaotic sea, Luo Chen, accompanied by the Primordial Unity Cauldron, with the Door of Destruction hanging above his head, continuously chewed the True Spirit's flesh and blood. His aura grew stronger as it became more perfectly unified.

And the passage of time became increasingly rapid.
