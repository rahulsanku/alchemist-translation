# Chapter 1226: Ember Awakening, Leaving the Sect

Alchemy Courtyard, Danhuang Mountain.

Ning Fu stood quietly outside the Mountain-Sea Palace.

Only after a long while was she granted permission to enter.

Once inside, she curiously looked around.

Was this the place where the Alchemy Saint had once briefly resided in the Sùlíng Sect?

Though newly built, considerable effort had been invested.

Ancient and grand in style, truly extraordinary!

She had heard that after the Alchemy Saint departed, the Mountain-Sea Palace had not been abandoned. Instead, its halls had been distributed among a group of Four-Pattern Alchemists.

In a certain sense, this was also a kind of reward, a form of inspiration.

Which alchemist wouldn't wish to one day live where the Alchemy Saint once dwelled, to gaze upon the traces and elegance left behind by a master of the alchemical dao?

Guided by a disciple, Ning Fu soon arrived at a side hall.

A middle-aged man was walking out from within. Seeing Ning Fu, he revealed a gratified smile.

"Xiao Fu, congratulations on refining a third-grade pill and officially becoming a Three-Pattern Alchemist."

Ning Fu bowed respectfully. "I owe this achievement entirely to Seventh Uncle's guidance."

Ning Li Sage waved his hand. "No need to flatter me. This accomplishment is all your own doing, especially your excellent performance in refining the Circulation Qi Pill."

Having said this, his expression turned serious.

"Although alchemy knows no distinction between male and female, our sect doesn't actually have many female alchemists. If you can achieve something, then female cultivators seeking pill refinement will naturally favor you. This will greatly help elevate your status within the clan. Since you intend to work hard in this area, I will also give you extra attention. In the future, when I preside over the refinement of fourth-grade pills, you can assist me and observe from the sidelines. Perhaps one day, you too may have a hall in this Mountain-Sea Palace, just like Xuan Luozi."

At the mention of Xuan Luozi, Ning Fu's face clearly showed curiosity.

"Seventh Uncle, where is he?"

Ning Li Sage looked at her teasingly. "You've only just arrived, and already you're asking about your junior apprentice brother Xuan Luozi? Could it be that..."

"Seventh Uncle!"

Ning Fu rarely acted coy, revealing a charmingly naive expression.

Ning Li Sage laughed heartily, then explained, "Xuan Luozi's talent in the alchemical dao is exceptionally high. He can already stably refine third-grade pills. A few years ago, he gained some insights in cultivation, saved up a batch of pills, and returned to Jade Spring Peak for seclusion. Perhaps by the time he emerges, his cultivation will already be at the mid-Adept stage."

Hearing this, Ning Fu was momentarily stunned.

The days she had worked alongside Xuan Luozi, studying the Circulation Qi Pill day and night, were still vivid in her memory.

He had entered the Spirit Pill Peak ahead of her.

She had thought she could quickly catch up, but unexpectedly, he had ascended to Danhuang Mountain in a single bound.

Now he could already stably refine third-grade pills. She imagined that after another few decades of accumulation, even fourth-grade pills would be well within his reach.

The gap between herself and him had become as vast as heaven and earth.

Noticing the young woman's loss of composure, Ning Li Sage's keen eyes perceived something.

He pondered for a moment, then decided to remind this little niece.

"Xuan Luozi is different from ordinary cultivators, and even from geniuses in the conventional sense. He is not only skilled in alchemy and pill refinement but also quite talented in cultivation. A person like this will never be content with mediocrity. If you wish to keep up with him, you must excel in one particular area. If you try to juggle everything, the gap between you will only grow wider..."

As he spoke, Ning Li Sage recalled that Xuan Luo had once been praised by the Alchemy Saint and had also caught the eye of Sect Master Tai Su Lingjun.

His tone grew heavier. "Perhaps you should abandon certain thoughts."

Ning Fu opened her mouth, wanting to speak but hesitating.

She herself was considered a genius in the eyes of her clan and fellow disciples, yet now she was being admonished by an elder like this. Anyone would find it hard to accept.

Moreover, the Ning family also held considerable influence within the Sùlíng Sect.

But thinking of Xuan Luo's achievements in these few short decades, that pride of hers involuntarily diminished by three parts. A look of dejection appeared on her face.

"Seventh Uncle, I understand."

"Mm. Come with me. I'll show you around the Mountain-Sea Palace, and then we'll visit Danhuang Mountain."

...

Luo Chen had no idea that because of his excellence, a certain young woman's budding affection had died before it could bloom.

His attention was entirely focused on the golden core within his dantian.

In his qi sea, dharma power roiled, radiant light overflowing in all directions.

A five-colored golden core, like a star, slowly revolved within.

The five colors represented the power of the five elements.

But unlike ordinary five-attribute golden cores, five chapters of mysterious scriptures clung to Luo Chen's core.

Each chapter of mysterious scripture was inscribed with all kinds of spells.

From low to high, everything was available.

Even fourth-grade spells, equivalent to the Sage stage, were numerous upon them.

After years of nurturing, the might of these five chapters of mysterious scriptures was no less than the natal true artifacts that those old Sage monsters had nurtured for centuries.

Moreover, their upper limit was extremely high, their power determined by the grade of the spells inscribed upon them.

If one day he could truly inscribe divine ability seeds onto them, that would be the moment this technique reached its pinnacle.

At that time, even if he couldn't unleash divine abilities instantly, the speed at which Luo Chen could execute them would far surpass normal Ascendant-stage great powers.

Once he entered a combat state, let alone those of the same rank, even a Void Refining True Lord a full major realm higher might suffer a great loss if caught off guard.

But that was a matter for later.

For now, Luo Chen focused entirely on his natal golden core.

From time to time, he mobilized the Heavenly Star Magnetic Divine Thunder within the Primordial Unity Cauldron, releasing strands of electric arcs to purify and temper the five-colored golden core.

By purifying and tempering it with thunderous power first, by the time he formed his nascent soul, he would no longer fear the thunder tribulation.

"As I expected, the Five Elements Divine Pill has greatly aided my cultivation during the Adept stage."

"In just ten years, without excessively drawing on the Sùlíng Sect's earth vein spiritual qi, I have smoothly advanced to the late Adept stage."

"The matter of forming the nascent soul is imminent!"

Luo Chen opened his eyes, calculating silently in his mind.

He wasn't sure if the Sùlíng Sect had already decided on candidates for the Demon-Suppressing Trial.

Only after he himself broke through to the Sage stage could he take the initiative to ask; otherwise, he would only make a fool of himself.

Making a fool of himself was a minor matter; arousing suspicion would be troublesome.

He was willing to trust Chu Yan because they had once shared a good karmic connection, but that didn't mean he was willing to trust others.

However, before leaving seclusion, he still needed to thoroughly examine himself.

Luo Chen's gaze fell upon the attribute panel, moving downward until it stopped at the achievement points count.

[Achievement Points: 1058]

Seeing this number, even though Luo Chen had seen it before, he couldn't help but reveal a delighted smile.

An unprecedented number, having surpassed one thousand!

These were all obtained from extensively practicing spells, pill formulas, and array secret arts in recent years.

Even if he were to invest these achievement points into continuing the deduction of the *Chaos Indestructible Scripture* right now, he was confident he could deduce something.

But there was no rush!

He wanted to save them for a more suitable time.

For instance, the Void Refining stage!

The higher one's realm, the fewer achievement points were consumed to deduce higher-level techniques.

The *Chaos Indestructible Scripture* was not merely a body-tempering technique. Because it excelled at refining energy, it was no less than other top-tier techniques in the path of qi cultivation.

Therefore, he planned to wait until he broke through to the Void Refining stage, then use both the Void Refining stage and the Destruction stage to deduce simultaneously, attacking from two fronts.

Next was the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame.

Luo Chen moved his mind.

Instantly, from the fire vein deep underground, a meteor, as if summoned, rushed towards him.

Luo Chen looked up. After decades of nurturing in the fire vein, plus devouring a Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame, the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame had finally recovered somewhat.

From within the ember came a faint call.

It was the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit!

He had originally wanted to bring it along, but after careful consideration, he decided to leave it behind for now.

"Not enough!"

"The current Withering-Bloom Divine Flame is of little use to me, whether for combat or auxiliary cultivation. Let it continue recovering within the sect!"

Luo Chen signaled the Withering-Bloom Ember to continue sinking down.

But just before the ember departed, he noticed a faint golden pattern had appeared on it.

Was it because it had devoured the Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame?

Luo Chen was thoughtful.

Restoring the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame was something he was familiar with, having done it once before.

If there were living beings with immense vitality, they would be the best ingredients.

But now it seemed that if there were powerful source-less fires, they might accelerate its recovery.

This was related to the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame's growth experience!

Originally, it had been merely an ordinary third-grade flame.

It had evolved by absorbing a large number of third- and fourth-grade strange fires in the Demon-Fall Land.

Although its main characteristic was still the dao of withering and blooming, it retained the instinct to devour various fires.

"Perhaps I should consider a different approach later?"

Luo Chen thought to himself.

Then, he rose and walked out of his cave dwelling.

First, he observed the medicine garden for a while.

Among the uneven ordinary medicinal herbs, a transparent, colorless little grass grew quietly. A dark-golden, glazed gu insect lay sleeping on top of it.

It was the Boundless Grass and the Vacuum Divine Gu.

Nourished by the aura of the Vacuum Divine Gu, the Boundless Grass was growing extremely quickly.

In Luo Chen's estimation, it was not far from maturity.

He walked over, took out a small bottle from his storage ring, and poured it onto the Boundless Grass.

Drops of green liquid fell in a drizzle.

This was the Heavenly Dew he had specially refined based on the Sùlíng Sect's pill formula. It contained vast wood-attribute spiritual qi and could accelerate the ripening of medicinal herbs.

After that, he took out a large number of Spatial Prism Apertures, crushed them into powder with his physical strength, and sprinkled them around the Boundless Grass.

"Grow quickly!"

Perhaps because the spiritual mechanism of the mystic realm was thin, making it difficult for these special high-grade spirit plants to awaken their spirits, not only had they not opened their spiritual wisdom to become spirits, but even their growth rate was slow.

To cultivate the Boundless Grass, Luo Chen had invested considerable effort.

But he was quite looking forward to the scene after it grew up.

After doing all this, he closed the cave dwelling's doors and activated the surrounding formations.

Unless Jade Spring Peak's Yaoxi Sage personally intervened, it was unlikely that any other sect Sage would open his cave.

Luo Chen hesitated no longer and began to carry out his plan.

...

External Affairs Hall.

Whether it was the three halls or five courtyards, they were always bustling with people, lively and crowded.

But while crowds lingered in other places, at the External Affairs Hall, cultivators came and went, with few staying long.

Because this was where tasks were issued and accepted.

Just as Luo Chen arrived at the External Affairs Hall and was looking around, an Initiate-stage cultivator approached him.

"Senior Brother, are you here to issue a task or accept one?"

Luo Chen smiled. "Accept one."

The man's spirits lifted. "Senior Brother looks like he rarely comes to the External Affairs Hall, so you might be unfamiliar with it. If Senior Brother is willing to give a little reward, this junior brother would be happy to answer your questions."

To prevent Luo Chen from refusing, he proactively offered some information.

"Although they are all tasks, there are significant differences. The task boards here are mixed together, containing both personal tasks and sect tasks. The number is extremely cumbersome, and hidden difficulties are rarely stated clearly. For example, sect tasks are often tedious with many procedures, but the benefit is that you can directly obtain sect contribution points. Personal tasks offer generous rewards, but most are quite niche. This junior brother frequents this place and has compiled a task reference document himself, updated regularly. If Senior Brother wants to save trouble, why not tell me your requirements, and I'll immediately find suitable tasks for you."

This was indeed Luo Chen's first time accepting a task.

Generally speaking, ordinary Sùlíng Sect disciples would take on some tasks in their spare time to obtain cultivation resources.

But he was different and didn't need to.

Because of his alchemy, not only did he hold a very high status, but he also never had to worry about cultivation resources. He even had a large number of sect contribution points on him.

He wasn't very familiar with the procedures here.

He hadn't expected that such a profession had emerged in the External Affairs Hall.

He glanced at the crowds gathered before the task boards and thought for a moment before asking, "How should I address you, Junior Brother?"

"Easy enough. Senior Brother can call me Luo Hu."

Oh, even the same surname.

Luo Chen smiled. "Why is Junior Brother so familiar with this place?"

Luo Hu sighed emotionally. "I entered through the Recruitment Assembly sixty years ago. Unlike Xuan Luozi from the same cohort, who was so fortunate, I wasted a full ten years in the outer sect before barely entering the inner sect. Without a master's guidance or a spirit mountain to rely on, I could only depend on the elders of the Teaching Hall for instruction in my daily cultivation. To obtain cultivation resources, I naturally often accepted tasks. After a while, I became familiar with this place. But finding suitable tasks, accepting them, and completing them took too much time. Later, I discovered that many people shared my troubles. So I thought, why not solve this problem for everyone and collect a little reward?"

Luo Chen nodded, no longer beating around the bush. He asked directly, "Do you have any tasks suitable for a mid-Adept stage cultivator, far enough from the sect, and not too dangerous?"

Luo Hu was startled, looking at Luo Chen in surprise.

He had only thought the other party had refined dharma power and achieved some success, but he hadn't expected that, despite his young age, he was already an Adept-stage Superior.

But after the surprise came secret delight.

Adept-stage cultivators were usually generous when they acted.

If he could satisfy this person, earning a batch of spirit stones, he could cultivate peacefully for a while.

"For the kind of task Senior Brother described—not dangerous means the reward won't be high, but it's far away and time-consuming, with high realm requirements. That means very few people will accept it."

"Tasks like this, if left hanging for a long time, sink to the bottom of the boards. It's hard to find them on your own."

"But this younger brother does have some compiled ones here. Senior Brother can take a look."

A jade slip was handed over from the other party.

Luo Chen swept it with his divine sense and quickly selected one.

"This one!"

"So fast?" Luo Hu was stunned, not expecting the other party to choose so quickly. But after seeing the task requirements, he frowned and kindly reminded him, "Senior Brother, this kind of task has too low a reward and relies on luck. People usually only complete it if they happen to be passing by. Are you sure you want to accept it?"

Luo Chen smiled. "It's fine. Doesn't it also say that it's okay not to complete it? It's been hanging there all along anyway."

As he spoke, he tossed over a few high-grade spirit stones.

For an Initiate-stage cultivator, this reward was already generous, especially since it was easily obtained just by exchanging information.

Luo Hu was very satisfied and immediately led Luo Chen to register.

When the name [Xuan Luozi] appeared on the ledger, he realized who the other party was.

It was that genius who had entered the sect alongside him back then!

But in just a few decades, he had already cultivated to the mid-Adept stage!

By then, Luo Chen had already left.

Luo Hu regretted not seizing the opportunity to build a good relationship with this fellow cohort senior brother.

After leaving, Luo Chen did not immediately set out on the task. Instead, he first went to Danhuang Mountain.

He informed some elders that he was going out on a task.

During this process, he was naturally advised against it.

But using the excuse that he had achieved some success in cultivation and wanted to travel and broaden his horizons, no one stopped him.

On the contrary, several familiar Sage-stage cultivators generously gave him jade tokens, talismans, and other life-saving measures, telling him to be careful.

After handling all these social obligations, Luo Chen finally left the sect with an air of detachment.

This trip would take some time.

But when he returned, he, Xuan Luozi, would be at the Sage stage!
