# Chapter 1258: Nine Yang Sword Lord, Fellow Sufferers

At Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak.

Inside a pill room wreathed in curling smoke and fire.

Chu Yan's little face was flushed red as she stared at the furnace, watching the pill pellets slowly flying out from within. Her eyes were filled with delight.

"So that's how it is!"

"Just a little more, and I would have had it. Back then, I was merely one thought away from going down the wrong path, yet I never imagined it could be completed through such a method."

"Crushed Star Sand! A cultivator's divine sense is like the countless sands of the Ganges—difficult to condense, especially in a state of sudden enlightenment where thoughts scatter wildly. By leveraging the property of Crushed Star Sand—fragmented like gravel yet capable of automatically gathering—it can assist in condensing divine sense into a single point. Then, with the Bodhi Wood to enlighten, the initiation becomes an instant matter!"

"Luo Chen, you're so clever. How did you think of this?"

Looking at the woman's excited, even somewhat adorable demeanor, Luo Chen felt a momentary daze.

They had both been cultivating for over a thousand years.

He rarely saw such a demeanor among fellow cultivators.

It was as if Chu Yan still harbored the most fervent, childlike heart toward the Dao of alchemy, unchanged from the very beginning.

Ahem.

He cleared his throat lightly, casually dispersing the smoke and dust in the room, and gave all the resulting Enlightenment Pills to Chu Yan.

"It's not really a matter of cleverness. I simply stood on the shoulders of you and Mr. Xuan Yan, supplemented by a great deal of experimental trial and error, slowly finding the most suitable substitute materials."

He spoke the truth.

Although the attribute panel had helped him confirm the final step of whether the pill formula was complete, the theoretical support in the early stages and the extensive experimental research in the middle were the true keys.

Chu Yan took the Enlightenment Pills and examined them carefully, her gaze occasionally falling on the alchemy furnace.

"What's wrong?"

"Three parts more of the fire's mundane breath. It affects the quality of this pill somewhat."

"Oh, perhaps my skill is lacking."

Chu Yan shook her head. "No, I observed your entire technique. I couldn't find any flaws. In fact, it felt even more solidly grounded than my own. The problem lies with this furnace. It shouldn't be the alchemy vessel you're most accustomed to, right?"

Luo Chen pursed his lips.

This woman was remarkably perceptive—or rather, when it came to anything related to the Dao of alchemy, she was sharper than anyone else.

She could detect from the quality of the finished pill alone that this furnace was not his most familiar tool.

And indeed, it wasn't. His most familiar vessel was ultimately his lifebound treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

If he had used that cauldron to refine the Enlightenment Pills, they would not have been merely mid-to-upper grade, but at least upper grade, and perhaps even supreme grade.

Just as the two were passionately discussing the Dao of alchemy, a voice suddenly pierced through the clouds and mist like a sharp sword, directly entering Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak.

"Junior Sister Chu, your senior brother has come to trouble you. Would you open the door for a chat?"

Hearing this voice, Luo Chen's expression shifted for a moment.

A Void Refining True Lord!

Across from him, Chu Yan frowned. She turned around, her rosy little lips parting, but the words that came out were ice-cold.

"Nine Yang Sword Lord, I am in the middle of an important moment refining a furnace of pills. I cannot be disturbed."

Luo Chen was taken aback.

Such cold words were utterly different from the adorable, lively, and cheerful woman who had been before him for the past half-month. He even felt as if she had become a different person.

No!

She hadn't changed. According to Yu Jiajia, Alchemy Saint Chu Yan had always been indifferent and aloof within the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, earning the nickname "Cold Beauty."

"Junior Sister, why must you keep people at arm's length? I am not disturbing you without reason. Please release the restriction."

"Just say what you have to say directly!"

"Very well. The Great Sovereign is about to emerge from seclusion, and the sect will hold a celebration soon. Additionally, the recent interrogations of disciples from minor sects have stirred up some resentment. Therefore, we need you to refine some pills suitable for the Initiate and Ascendant stages. They can both appease them and showcase the magnanimity of our Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

"I understand. The Pill Saint Hall already has this on record. When the celebration comes, someone will deliver them."

"Alright, then. Junior Sister, focus on your pill refining! I won't disturb you."

Outside Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak, Nine Yang Sword Lord felt somewhat disappointed.

He had come under the pretext of business today, yet still failed to see his beloved's face. Truly a pity.

He sighed and was about to leave when he noticed a graceful figure approaching from outside.

"Yu Jiajia?"

Yu Jiajia was in the midst of flying when she found someone blocking her path.

She paused. Upon seeing his true face, she hurriedly bowed.

"Disciple pays respects to Sword Lord."

Nine Yang Sword Lord waved his hand. "How is your father? I heard that during this Demon-Suppressing Trial, his physical body was destroyed, and he had to escape with only his primordial spirit. What is his current situation?"

"Thank you for your concern, Sword Lord. The sect has contacted the branch of the Corpse Heaven Sect in Youxuan Continent, planning to purchase an Ascendant-level physical body for my father to seize. It should not affect his Ascendant cultivation."

Nine Yang Sword Lord nodded. "With your ancestor's legacy and your father's contributions to the sect, an Ascendant-level physical body should not be a problem."

After a brief exchange of pleasantries, he looked curiously at the direction Yu Jiajia was heading.

"Are you going to Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak?"

Yu Jiajia hesitated.

Nine Yang Sword Lord smiled slightly. "It's fine. I just came from there. Chu Yan is in the middle of an important pill-refining session. I was merely worried you might disturb her."

Yu Jiajia visibly relaxed. She replied, "That shouldn't be a problem, Sword Lord. I've just come from the Pill Saint Hall, where I withdrew a large quantity of medicinal herbs specifically to deliver to Elder Chu. It's to assist her in pill refining."

So she really was refining pills?

Nine Yang Sword Lord was somewhat surprised. He had thought she was just brushing him off.

But then Yu Jiajia added, as if explaining, "Elder Chu has recently been discussing the Dao of alchemy with a friend. She's extremely interested and needs a large amount of herbs for experiments. The Pill Saint Hall has been exceptionally busy, so I had to step in. I've been somewhat remiss in the interrogation work with the various sect disciples. Please forgive me, Sword Lord."

The overall person in charge of interrogating the disciples of the various sects was Nine Yang Sword Lord.

Because he possessed the divine ability of a sword-heart that could see through all things, it was easy for him to discern truth from falsehood in others' words.

However, Nine Yang Sword Lord was rather lazy about such matters, so he had delegated the work to other disciples, Yu Jiajia being one of them.

He didn't really care about it, but when he heard the word "friend," his eyes narrowed slightly.

"May I ask who this person is?"

...

"Nine Yang Sword Lord—who is he?"

In the pill room, Luo Chen asked with great interest.

Chu Yan curled her lips, her expression showing some disgust.

Yes!

That was exactly the look.

Luo Chen couldn't help but smile. He found that, aside from matters related to the Dao of alchemy, Chu Yan's expressions changed without any concealment. It was truly amusing.

"Someone like us, and yet different from us."

"Uh..."

This time, Luo Chen was genuinely surprised.

Chu Yan snorted lightly. "He's like us because he also ascended from the Mountains and Seas Realm."

Luo Chen suddenly understood.

He remembered now. He had indeed heard this name from others before.

It seemed that this person was indeed also from the Mountains and Seas Realm.

"Where is the difference?"

"The difference is that you and I are both natives of Mountains and Seas—you a rogue cultivator, me from the Nether Origin Sect—while he is a legitimate direct-line descendant of the Dao Sect. Not only was he heavily cultivated back in the Mountains and Seas Realm, but in the main sect of the Xuan Realm, his He family is also one of the great houses within the sect."

Luo Chen had almost forgotten.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was not primarily based on master-disciple inheritance, but on family bloodlines.

Back in the Mountains and Seas Realm, there had been the saying of "Twelve Great Houses, Thirty-Six Aristocratic Clans." It seemed that the scale here in the Xuan Realm was likely even larger.

Luo Chen let out an "oh," then said, "It seems Fellow Daoist Chu doesn't care much for these playboys from the great families and clans?"

Chu Yan waved her hand. "It's not quite that. Although he comes from a great family, he's no ordinary playboy. On the contrary, this person has exceptional talent and works hard at cultivation. He is one of the few outstanding figures within the Dao Sect. Moreover, back in the Mountains and Seas Realm, he once improved a fragmentary sword canon left behind by an unknown powerful figure, using it to cultivate the Nine Firmament Sword Qi and attain the Ascendant Dao..."

A fragmentary sword canon. Nine Firmament Sword Qi.

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly.

Could it be that the other party also cultivated the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon*?

Luo Chen recalled another matter.

He had once released a suppressed Kui Niu ghost deity on the Tiannan Continent, which had split off from the Central Continent.

At that time, that Kui Niu ghost deity had been suppressed by a crude method used by an avatar descended by a great power from the Xuan Realm's Dao Sect. However, the sword scars that had split it into nine parts were all terrifyingly powerful.

Looking at it now, it might also have been this person's handiwork.

"The reason I dislike this person is not because of his personal character, but simply because he comes from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. That's all."

Chu Yan's tone was melancholic. The excitement from their earlier discussion of the Dao of alchemy had somehow dissipated.

Seeing this expression, Luo Chen felt an inexplicable sense of pity.

No!

It wasn't pity, but rather a feeling of fellow suffering!

Luo Chen had once experienced something similar. Back then, his life soul had been controlled by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, ultimately forcing him to personally lead his people to destroy the Mountains and Seas Heavenly Origin.

Now, Chu Yan's situation was not unlike his own back then.

The only difference was probably that Luo Chen had once had the chance to fight for his life, while the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect that Chu Yan faced was a behemoth that made one unable to even conceive of resistance.

Moreover, there had been similar speculations from the Sushang Sect.

"You..." Luo Chen hesitated, then finally spoke softly, "Are you under someone's control?"

Chu Yan glanced at Luo Chen. "You guessed it?"

Luo Chen sighed. "It's not that I guessed. It's that I've had a similar experience."

Chu Yan was puzzled. "Back then, didn't you not join the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect? You only became an external guest elder. No, wait—did you enter the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven?"

Luo Chen nodded regretfully.

"No wonder. I was wondering why you would later attack the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect for no reason. Everyone said you had fallen into demonic ways, but now it seems you were much braver than I."

Chu Yan now also felt a sense of fellow suffering. The look she gave Luo Chen held even more admiration.

But she changed the subject, pointing at her palm-sized little face.

"However, I am different from you. It's not that my life soul was taken, but that my primordial spirit is under restriction."

Luo Chen understood. For a cultivator who had reached the Ascendant or Void Refining stage, their primordial spirit would necessarily be complete and intact. It was impossible for a life soul to be missing.

The only possibility was that someone had placed a restriction on Chu Yan's body.

And the only person within the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect capable of doing that was one.

"The Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign?"

Chu Yan nodded lightly. "Yes. My life and death are in his hands. With just a slight thought from him, my primordial spirit will collapse—a thousand years of bitter cultivation, life, soul, all destroyed in an instant."

Well, then!

What had started as a meeting of like-minded enthusiasts had now turned into a "fellow sufferer" support group.

Just as the two were feeling a mutual sympathy, a knock sounded.

"Reporting to the elder, the materials you requested have been brought."

The door opened automatically.

Yu Jiajia's respectful figure appeared before the two.

Chu Yan waved her hand, and a storage pouch landed in her grasp.

"You may go... wait. When you entered the mountain, did you encounter anyone?"

Yu Jiajia hesitated for a moment, then replied, "I encountered Sword Lord. He asked a few casual questions."

"What questions?"

"Whether you were refining pills, and..." Yu Jiajia's gaze fell upon the young man standing before the furnace—graceful, handsome, and extraordinarily dashing.

Chu Yan frowned. "Enough. You may go!"

Before leaving, Yu Jiajia added, "When I was collecting the herbs at the Pill Saint Hall, the steward there asked me to inquire about the distribution ratio for the pills to be bestowed upon the cultivators of the subordinate sects. Additionally, there is a shortage of pills suitable for Ascendant-level cultivators. They estimate that you will need to return and refine another furnace."

Having said this, she respectfully withdrew.

After she left, Chu Yan sighed in frustration. "Luo Chen, I'm afraid you've caught the attention of Nine Yang Sword Lord now."

Luo Chen smiled. "Surely a dignified Void Refining True Lord wouldn't be so petty as to be jealous of a mere Initiate-stage cultivator like me, would he?"

The word "jealous" was spoken with a subtle tone.

Chu Yan shot him a glare, then said frankly, "I came from the Nether Origin Sect. Back then, I joined the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect because I had hit a bottleneck in my alchemy. The Nether Origin Sect still exists in the Xuan Realm and is also one of the Nine Great Saint Sects. The Heavenly Origin Sect forcibly kept me and placed restrictions on me—this is not the way of the righteous path. Therefore, they have always wanted me to fully join the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Nine Yang Sword Lord received orders from the Sect Master to actively pursue me, hoping I would become his dual-cultivation dao partner. Anyone who obstructs this matter will be struck down by him, the He family behind him, and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Do you think they will be generous in this matter?"

Luo Chen nodded repeatedly. The Dao Sect not only wanted Chu Yan's body but also her heart!

His close association with Chu Yan, combined with the extraordinarily handsome appearance of this newly formed body, would indeed easily arouse suspicion.

But he wasn't too concerned.

"It's fine. Once this matter is concluded, I will probably be leaving."

"You're leaving? Going where?" Chu Yan's eyes lit up, her gaze clearly revealing a longing for freedom.

Looking into those deer-like eyes, Luo Chen's heart softened. The words "I want to take you with me" almost slipped out.

But he knew it was impossible.

Not to mention how he would take Chu Yan away right under the nose of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, the mere fact of the Body Integration-level restriction placed on her was something he could not resolve at his current level.

If he truly angered the Dao Sect, the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign would, with a single thought, cause Chu Yan's primordial spirit to collapse, and she would perish, body and soul.

"I haven't decided yet. Probably to the nearest Blood Rain Continent..."

As he spoke, a thought suddenly arose in Luo Chen's mind.

Primordial spirit collapse?

It seemed he might not be without a solution after all!
