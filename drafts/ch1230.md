# Chapter 1230: Jade Spring Jiang Huang, Po Luo Mao Qiu

Hum...

Within the ancient temple, spiritual light pulsed and surged.

Inside the central head of the enormous demonic statue, a blood-red maw hung low, and a vortex began to rotate counterclockwise.

Suddenly!

Four figures stumbled out from within it.

Before they could even steady themselves, one of them instinctively tried to charge back inside.

Beside her, Lu Zhenqiu quickly grabbed the other's arm. "Senior Sister Liu, don't be impulsive! Don't re-enter the tiger's den!"

Liu Muhan's eyes were bloodshot. It was as if she could still hear Junior Brother Xuan Luo's words echoing in her ears: "Take her and leave."

"How can you tell me not to be impulsive? Junior Brother Xuan Luo is still trapped inside!"

"Muhan, calm down! The teleportation formation can no longer function. Look carefully!"

At Lu Zhenqiu's sharp shout, Liu Muhan's body stiffened. She stared blankly at the demonic statue.

The blood-red maw that had been hanging open had gradually closed, returning to its original state of a slight, small-lipped smile.

As for the teleportation formation within its mouth, there was no longer a trace of spiritual light.

Any cultivator with even a basic understanding of formations would recognize this as a sign that the teleportation formation had failed.

Lu Zhenqiu sighed and said in a low voice, "It seems that Xuan Luozi was afraid that the enemies in that ink-jade space would pursue us, so he destroyed the teleportation formation on the other side himself."

Liu Muhan's body couldn't help but tremble, her eyes filled with pain.

"It shouldn't have been him. It should have been me. I entered the sect earlier than he did, I have more experience. Especially this mission involving the disappearance of spiritual energy—it had nothing to do with him. I should have stayed behind to cover the retreat."

Lu Zhenqiu opened her mouth, wanting to say something.

For instance, that Xuan Luo had a higher cultivation level and greater strength. Or that it was Xuan Luo who had insisted on bringing everyone to such a dangerous place.

Logically, none of this was Liu Muhan's fault.

But the image of Xuan Luo standing alone at the pass, sword in hand, covering their retreat, kept flashing through her mind. Those words simply wouldn't come out.

She steadied herself, forcing her thoughts to be clear.

Compared to Liu Muhan, Lu Zhenqiu had been training in the Enforcement Court ever since her Initiation stage. She had rich experience in executing missions.

She had even encountered some predicaments that ordinary enforcement squads couldn't handle.

Soon, she had sorted out her thoughts.

"The true situation of this mission is far more complex and dangerous than what was posted on the surface. We must report back to the Three Halls and Five Courts."

"This matter involves an unknown demonic qi, and the sect's elders must be informed."

"Especially since Xuan Luo is trapped inside. His talent is exceptionally high, and many important figures in the sect hold him in high regard. They won't just let this go. We must have the sect's Sages handle this matter. In particular, your master, Sage Yao Xi, and also..."

As she spoke, Lu Zhenqiu thought of how she herself had benefited from Luo Chen's rear-guard action, allowing her to stand here safely now.

She pursed her lips. "I will report this matter to my master, Sage You Huang, and beg her to take action. We must rescue Xuan Luo. Alive, we see the person; dead, we see the corpse!"

She spoke those last words with absolute finality.

Liu Muhan seemed to have found a pillar of support. She nodded repeatedly.

"Yes! Not only must we report to the sect, but we must also ask the Sages to take action. And the Ascendant elders! I heard that Elder Gu Mu also thinks highly of Junior Brother Xuan Luo. He will definitely help rescue him."

Lu Zhenqiu looked at her with concern. This was the first time she had seen Liu Muhan so distraught.

The other party was originally a loose cultivator who had only entered the Su Ling Sect through the Spirit-Entry Assembly. Her mental fortitude far exceeded that of ordinary sect disciples.

During the execution of the mission earlier, she had even taken the lead.

Even when a great battle suddenly erupted, Liu Muhan had remained calm in the face of danger and was prepared to stay behind to block the enemy herself. She was truly responsible.

This loss of composure was more a case of concern clouding her judgment.

But before leaving, as she looked at that demonic statue, Lu Zhenqiu felt more regret in her heart.

"I'm afraid he can't be saved."

---

Within the ink-jade space.

A blood-red figure hovered in mid-air, countless strands of black hair flowing behind him.

Like a god, like a demon!

All the enemies charging towards him couldn't even get close. Under the spreading shockwaves of those blood-red astral qi, they turned into blood mist and powder, finally adhering to the blood-colored cloth robe on his body.

Such a sudden change seemed to have stunned the master of this place.

The next moment, two figures soared into the air.

One was clad in armor, like a giant beast charging a city, rushing to kill Luo Chen.

The other held a sword, and with a single stroke across the void, a black waterfall cascaded down from the nine heavens, surging and washing over everything.

"Oh?"

Luo Chen had intended to sweep away the remnants of the demonic cultivators here with the force of a thunderbolt.

But when he saw that latter move, his expression shifted slightly.

He casually brushed his hand, and the armored demonic cultivator crumbled like a pile of sand and stone, becoming nourishment for the blood-colored cloth robe.

As for the surging black waterfall of sword light, Luo Chen extended two fingers and gently pinched.

*Clang!*

With that pinch, the black waterfall of sword light came to an abrupt end.

And the long sword in the distant figure's hand lost control, coming to a silent halt.

Luo Chen beckoned with two fingers, and that person was pulled over without any resistance.

Luo Chen looked carefully.

The newcomer had a seductive figure, wrapped in a suit of ink-green armor.

Her black hair was like a waterfall, her features delicate and beautiful.

Only her eyes were filled with a fierce aura. Even under control, she stared at Luo Chen with murderous intent.

Luo Chen recalled the earlier sword strike and finally found the familiar source.

"Jade Spring Peak, Heavenfall Sword Art!"

He was certain he hadn't misjudged, because that kind of sword technique was very rare. It was a self-created technique by Sage Yao Xi, inspired by the flying waterfall of Jade Spring Peak.

When executed, it could compress one's own Sage domain into the sword qi. Even if one's cultivation level was inferior to the enemy's, because it was so condensed, it could still break through the enemy's domain.

Moreover, Adept-stage cultivators could practice it, and its power was astonishingly great.

Back then, there was a true disciple of Jade Spring Peak who had made quite a name for himself by relying on this sword art.

That person was...

Luo Chen looked at the woman before him, whose killing intent was surging. His eyes emitted a strange light, like a vortex, pulling her mind into it.

"Senior Sister?"

In an instant, the soul memories related to this woman flipped rapidly before Luo Chen's eyes like a book.

*Mentally deranged, breakthrough to Sage, demonic qi initiation, captured after defeat, unable to win in battle, accepted the mission to clean up the sect's mess, made a name with the sword in the Su Ling Sect, became a disciple of Yao Xi at the foot of Jade Spring Peak.*

From back to front, they flipped over like flying pages.

The memory fragments stopped at the appreciative gaze of Sage Yao Xi.

At this point, Luo Chen finally confirmed that this controlled female cultivator was indeed the original owner of the Jade Ravine Cave Mansion he currently occupied—the Senior Sister of Jade Spring Peak, Jiang Huang!

He had heard before that she had gone missing, and even Sage Yao Xi had personally searched for her two hundred years ago without finding a trace.

He never expected that she had been trapped here.

And by some twist of fate, she had even broken through to the Sage stage through demonic qi initiation.

Luo Chen did not forcibly try to restore her consciousness. That would not be conducive to his next actions.

But he couldn't just kill Jiang Huang outright either.

After all, he had been freeloading at Jade Spring Peak for quite a while. When he formed his core, Wan Jiuzhang had even arranged the best cave mansion and the best travel carriage for him. Saving their Senior Sister could be considered repayment.

For now, though, it was best for her to stay put obediently!

Luo Chen gently pointed a finger at the center of her brow.

Her eyes, which had been brimming with killing intent, suddenly went blank, and she closed them dazedly.

All of this takes a long time to describe.

But Luo Chen's killing one, capturing another, and soul-searching to read memories actually happened in an instant.

The master of the ink-jade space in the distant tower only felt that as soon as he had sent out his two demon generals, the connection was immediately lost.

To be able to do this was definitely not something an Adept-stage cultivator could achieve.

Of course, from the moment Luo Chen revealed his aura and effortlessly annihilated his hundred Adept-stage demon servants, he hadn't regarded him as an Adept-stage cultivator.

But he still hadn't expected that his two carefully cultivated Sage-stage demon generals would be so vulnerable.

"Ascendant?"

Just as he was in shock, he felt the tower shaking violently.

He looked up sharply and saw a giant hand grabbing at him from across the void.

Unwilling to sit idly and wait for death, he unleashed his strongest technique, trying to blast through that giant hand.

Countless Nine Nether Demon Mosses rose from the earth, wrapping around the giant hand.

But such an attack was like a mud cow entering the sea, with no reaction at all.

The blood-red giant hand closed with a bang!

*Hong!*

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his giant hand, not bothering with the few traces of ink-green moss that had stained the back of it. His gaze fell upon the despairing creature within.

Its body was round and plump, covered in fluffy white fur.

Its lips were unusually large, spitting out bubbles of demonic qi.

Its ink-green eyes rolled around, and even in despair, it couldn't hide its endearing, chubby appearance.

There was not a trace of the fierce and vicious demeanor it had displayed when transmitting its voice earlier.

"A demon?"

This appearance seemed familiar to Luo Chen.

As his memories stirred, Luo Chen finally found the source.

The Mountains and Seas Realm, the Demon-Fall Land, within the Pill Realm.

There was a trial for refining pills, which contained a large number of ancient texts, including records of demon races.

Among them was a record of a special demon race called the Po Luo.

This race, aside from being skilled at soul-seizing and soul-stealing, was not particularly strong. On the contrary, because of their cute appearance, they were often kept as demon pets by powerful demons.

He didn't expect to encounter one today, and one that had, quite rarely, cultivated to the late Sage stage, only a step away from Ascendant.

"You are of the Po Luo race?"

The creature was stunned for a moment, then nodded repeatedly.

"Great one, spare my life!"

Luo Chen sneered. "Spare your life? Then who will spare the lives of the human cultivators you have enslaved?"

The Po Luo demon couldn't refute that, but immediately said, "Mao Qiu is willing to acknowledge the great one as master!"

This time, it was Luo Chen's turn to be taken aback.

Such a quick surrender, such groveling, was something he had rarely seen in his life.

Perhaps this was the deep-seated servility hidden in the bones of the Po Luo race after being kept as demon pets for millions of years?

Luo Chen didn't give a direct answer, but instead asked, "Your name is Mao Qiu?"

Mao Qiu blinked its large, ink-green eyes. "Yes, that name was also given to me by a human cultivator. She wanted to make me her spirit pet, but I secretly ran away. I also discovered this small space, carefully cultivated the demon moss, and turned it into a cultivation cave."

"A human cultivator?" Luo Chen frowned.

Mao Qiu nodded vigorously. "Yes! As long as the great one is willing to spare my life, I will tell you everything I know without holding anything back."

Luo Chen stroked his chin, then gave a light smile.

"That's too troublesome."

"Huh?"

Just as Mao Qiu was stunned, its eyes had unconsciously met Luo Chen's.

"Soul search..."

The thought had just flashed through its mind when it fell into a daze.

On Luo Chen's side, he had already begun to quickly browse through the creature's life experiences.

With his powerful soul cultivation, as long as he wasn't too violent or too thorough in his search, he could perform a soul search on beings below the Ascendant stage without damaging their origin.

The earlier soul search on Jiang Huang was an example.

Now, using the same technique, it was still effective.

A little Po Luo demon, having followed all sorts of strange and bizarre demon elders since childhood, had seen nothing but worried faces.

Until one day, it was noticed by a young human female cultivator who wanted to take it as a spirit pet and even gave it a name.

It seized the opportunity to escape, found a spirit land, planted demon moss, and, just like others had taken it as a pet, began capturing humans to make into demon servants.

When it encountered someone with outstanding talent, it would use the demonic qi initiation method to help them become demon generals. It had tried this several times, but only two had succeeded.

The soul-search memories ended there. The creature's life experiences were frighteningly simple.

Or perhaps it wasn't that simple.

Luo Chen frowned. He found that this little thing's sea of consciousness was structured completely differently from ordinary beings.

It was like the fluffy white fur on its body—layer upon layer, with memories hidden within.

When searching, although his divine sense swept through like a comb, there were always omissions.

"The Po Luo race is skilled at soul-seizing and soul-stealing. This isn't a skill acquired through later cultivation, but an innate talent. It must be related to this unique structure of the sea of consciousness."

If the creature hadn't spoken willingly, to truly explore its deeper memories, he would have had to completely destroy its sea of consciousness and soul.

Luo Chen hesitated.

Especially in the memories he had seen, he noticed that human cultivators occasionally appeared around Mao Qiu during its childhood. This made him realize its identity.

It wasn't a pure demon from the demon realm, but a remnant of the demon race left behind in the Xuan Realm!

He thought for a moment, then awakened its consciousness.

Mao Qiu, in a daze, woke up again.

Luo Chen's voice came from beside its ear.

"You are a remnant of the demon race?"

Mao Qiu understood that it had just been soul-searched. But relying on what its demon elders had said about the uniqueness of their race, it believed that it hadn't been completely searched, so it still had value.

Its eyes rolled, and just as it was about to speak, it saw Luo Chen's face darken.

"I may not be able to see the whole picture, but I know a little. If even half of what you say next doesn't match up, grinding your bones to dust and shattering your soul will be your fate."

"I am a genius of the Po Luo race, I don't want to die yet." Mao Qiu trembled all over, then quickly said with absolute certainty, "Master guessed correctly, Mao Qiu is indeed a remnant of the demon race."

Whoa!

Just a moment ago it was "great one," and now it was directly calling him "master."

It was truly afraid of death!

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth. "Where are you from?"

Although demon remnants were special, there were quite a few in the Xuan Realm.

There were three known branches on the You Xuan Continent.

They were located in the Youdu Source Court, the Heavenly Phoenix Clan, and the middle ground between the Nine Great Holy Sects' Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and the Immortal Danling.

Especially the last branch, which was tasked with observing the movements of the demonic cave.

It was called the Fudi Branch.

Mao Qiu replied, "I come from the Fudi Branch."

That was a plausible origin, and not too far away. Given Mao Qiu's cultivation level when it first escaped, it was the most reasonable distance for it to have traveled here, and it matched up.

But Mao Qiu's next words made Luo Chen freeze.

"Our Fudi Branch originated from a small tribe brought into the Xuan Realm by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining during the Xuan-Demon War ten thousand years ago. Two thousand years ago, the Devil Lord disappeared, and the hearts of the tribe were scattered, suffering oppression from humans. Later, most of the strong members of the tribe were recruited by the Immortal Danling and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Mao Qiu was born late, and the elders of the tribe had no time to look after me, so I ran away."

Devil Lord Heaven-Refining!

Luo Chen took a deep breath, a complex expression appearing on his face. (End of chapter)
