# Chapter 1207: The Heart-Questioning Path, Unity of Intention and Thought

"Metal-Edge Body!"

As soon as these words were spoken, they immediately stirred commotion all around.

Then, like a small pebble falling into a calm lake, ripples spread outward in layers.

Countless wandering cultivators who had been craning their necks in anticipation heard this news and couldn't help but exclaim in surprise, their envy plain to see.

The Metal-Edge Body sounded ordinary, but it was actually one of the many spiritual constitutions.

Those who possessed this constitution were born with an extremely strong affinity for metal-attributed spiritual qi. Whether absorbing or refining it, they achieved twice the results with half the effort.

Not only that, but the dharma power nurtured by such cultivators would also be exceptionally sharp and fierce, no less formidable than ordinary magic treasures.

It was just like the Fire Spirit Body that Luo Chen had once possessed. The fire-attributed dharma power cultivated by a Fire Spirit Body was violent and domineering, providing an extremely strong amplification for large-scale offensive spells.

Wan Jiuzhang's gaze fell upon Luo Chen, a look of understanding appearing on his face.

"No wonder he's not a heavenly spirit root and even has a mutually restraining fire spirit root, yet still managed to reach the Initiate stage in his twenties. Metal-Edge Body, tsk tsk... Who would have thought that among the motley crowd of wandering cultivators, there would be such a pearl lost in the sea."

Luo Chen put on an anxious expression and asked in a low voice, "Then, Senior, what about my first round of testing?"

Wan Jiuzhang laughed heartily. "Naturally, you've passed. Prepare for the next round!"

Luo Chen was overjoyed and repeatedly expressed his thanks.

"What are you thanking me for? This is your own aptitude. Fortunately, you joined the Spirit-Entry Assembly. If you successfully join the Su Ling Sect, there will naturally be sect seniors to help you change your cultivation technique. Otherwise, if you continue cultivating like this, your fire spirit root will eventually counteract your metal spirit root, and might even cause defects in your Metal-Edge Body."

Wan Jiuzhang spoke thus.

In truth, what he said was not false.

Often, a single spiritual constitution was formed through the infiltration of a spirit root, corresponding only to a single-attributed spirit root.

Occasional exceptions were those mutually reinforcing auxiliary spirit roots that were primarily based on the main spirit root.

For Luo Chen, whose spirit roots were mutually restraining, dragging it out would indeed become a burden later on.

Under the guidance of a disciple, Luo Chen headed toward the display platform for the second round.

Along the way, that Initiate-stage disciple actively struck up a conversation with Luo Chen.

"With Junior Brother Xuan Luo's Metal-Edge Body, you could actually join our sect even without going through the second round of assessment. But the process must still be followed, to avoid causing resentment in others. I hope Junior Brother Xuan Luo understands."

He was already calling him Junior Brother Xuan Luo before he had even joined the Su Ling Sect.

This showed just how much influence the name of a spiritual constitution carried.

Even in the Xuan Realm, where cultivators were countless, spiritual constitutions were not something that could be found everywhere on the streets. They appeared only once in a million or so people.

Luo Chen wore a humble smile on his face, but in his heart, he was perfectly clear about the origin of his Metal-Edge Body.

The Great Vehicle True Spirit, Metal Punishment Sovereign!

That one-third of his flesh had had too great an impact on Luo Chen.

Not only had it helped him break through to the Destruction Realm and successfully cultivate the Chaos Indestructible Body, but the residual metal-attributed origin power had also subtly transformed his constitution.

Originally, Luo Chen's Five-Spirit Dao Body had been hidden, with the Fire Spirit Body manifesting externally.

But under the influence of Metal Punishment Sovereign's flesh and blood, a Metal-Edge Body had actually been generated.

Luo Chen did not prevent this very obvious change.

In his view, whether it was the Fire Spirit Body or the Metal-Edge Body, they were merely one type within the Five-Spirit Dao Body.

When his Dao Body revived, his physical form would ultimately be unified.

The voice of the Su Ling Sect disciple came to his ears.

"We've arrived. I wonder what skill Junior Brother Xuan Luo wishes to demonstrate?"

It was still early, and there were no other wandering cultivators on the display platform.

Facing the gazes cast upon him, Luo Chen smiled faintly and skillfully formed a hand seal he had not used for a long time.

His index and middle fingers were extended and pressed together, the remaining three fingers curled into his palm, and his thumb pressed firmly against the nails of his ring and little fingers.

Seeing him assume this posture, the cultivators present understood.

It was a sword art!

Indeed, wandering cultivators loved to practice flashy sword arts.

The next moment, golden light rippled before Luo Chen, and one after another, sharp golden small swords materialized.

With his light shout, the numerous small swords flew back and forth like swimming fish. Their killing power was not great, but their control was extremely smooth.

The crowd was momentarily stunned, then couldn't help but laugh.

"Who would have thought it was the Grass-Cutting Geng Metal Sword Art!"

"This Xuan Luo is quite thoughtful."

"He uses it so skillfully; it's clear he's put in considerable effort. He really came here to join our Su Ling Sect."

The Grass-Cutting Geng Metal Sword Art sounded like a grand name.

In reality, it was merely a second-order spirit-planting technique.

Its killing power was not great; it was specifically used for killing insects and weeding in medicine gardens, and was also frequently employed for harvesting spirit grains.

Ordinary cultivators would not specifically practice such a relatively useless spell.

Ordinary cultivators would not specifically practice such a relatively useless spell.

Only the Su Ling Sect and the spirit planters associated with their industry would specifically practice this technique.

When Luo Chen demonstrated it, the disciples chuckled lightly, already acknowledging his skill.

When Luo Chen demonstrated it, the disciples chuckled lightly, already acknowledging his skill.

Seeing their reaction, Luo Chen knew that the second round was also passed.

Speaking of it, this Grass-Cutting Geng Metal Sword Art was something he had learned from an elderly woman when he first entered the cultivation world. Over the years, it had never been of any use.

Today, however, it was used to "show off" a little.

The disciple who had guided him earlier walked over and said with a smile to Luo Chen, "With just this skilled display of the Grass-Cutting Geng Metal Sword Art, in previous years, it would have been enough to join the outer sect of our Su Ling Sect. But this year is different, and with your aptitude and cultivation being so excellent, it would be a waste to cultivate in the outer sect. Go on to the third round. If you pass, you might leap over the dragon's gate and directly enter the inner sect."

"Thank you for your guidance, Senior Brother."

Luo Chen politely replied, then alone stepped onto the nine hundred and ninety-nine azure jade steps.

That Su Ling Sect disciple watched his departing figure gradually swallowed by the clouds and mist, a wistful expression appearing on his face.

With such outstanding talent, no other sect would be willing to send him as a spy. He must surely be of clean background.

This third round would probably be just like the second, merely a formality.

Once he joined the Su Ling Sect and received the sect's resources for cultivation, it would be like a dragon entering the sea.

Don't look at how he was still a bit higher in cultivation than him now; in a few years, he would inevitably be surpassed.

His earlier politeness was also to avoid offending him.

He hoped that when the other party achieved fame and success in the future, he would still remember this small favor of guiding the way today. Even just recognizing his face would be enough.

...

Contrary to what that disciple thought, the third round was the true test for Luo Chen!

The Heart-Questioning Path!

Questioning what heart?

For wandering cultivators, it questioned the Dao heart, clarified the original heart, and from then on, strengthened the path of cultivation.

But for someone like Luo Chen, who harbored ulterior motives, what it questioned was his improper intentions.

He had never experienced the Heart-Questioning Path and did not know its intensity.

If he performed too weakly, he might truly sink into it.

If he performed too well, exposing his divine soul, which was comparable to a late Ascendant stage cultivator, that would be the most troublesome thing.

For this reason, during the three days of waiting, Luo Chen had painstakingly split off a very weak fragment of his soul.

Its intensity was roughly around the Initiate stage.

This was a very delicate task for him. Even though he had experienced splitting his divine soul many times before, it still required a great deal of effort.

It was simply too weak.

And within this split soul, he had also injected a wisp of martial divine intent, to ensure that he would not truly lose his original heart.

"I hope everything goes smoothly!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath and truly stepped onto the Heart-Questioning Path.

...

Above the nine hundred and ninety-nine azure jade steps, a magnificent palace sat atop the mountain peak.

Outside the palace, cultivators stood in abundance.

Inside the palace, a beautiful woman sat in meditation.

A stunningly beautiful palace-dressed young matron sat in the center of a formation, her gaze fixed on a water mirror before her.

Amidst the shimmering ripples, the figure of a handsome, sharp-edged young man appeared.

As he stepped up the stairs step by step, his divine soul began to fluctuate violently.

Scenes of memories hidden deep in his heart naturally appeared in the water mirror.

This was not a soul-searching technique, but a frequency of inducement created by combining a magic treasure with formation dao, and using the divine sense of a Sage as the dominant force.

It did not search the soul, but merely used external force to induce, causing people to automatically expose the darkest things in their hearts.

In this way, it could investigate the true thoughts of the wandering cultivators at the Spirit-Entry Assembly, while also avoiding damage to their divine soul foundation, lest it affect their future normal cultivation.

The azure water mirror, fleeting shadows.

Scenes arose spontaneously.

In an instant, a shocking hatred flickered out.

"Hmm? Why is there such a strong heart of hatred?"

As the palace-dressed beauty wondered, she saw amidst the layers of illusions, birds of prey wreaking havoc, descending upon a small town, and wantonly slaughtering mortals.

Among them, a youth struggled and resisted, eventually escaping with his life.

Later, he cultivated diligently, vowing revenge.

The palace-dressed beauty suddenly understood, recalling the information left by the other party on the wandering cultivator registry.

He was from Green Gorge Town, near the Sunset Plains.

That place could indeed encounter low-level demon beast attacks.

And across the Sunset Plains was the territory of the Heavenly Phoenix Clan, where many feathered bird demons could be found.

"So that's how it is!"

"Although a heart of hatred is not advisable, it can also serve as a great driving force for cultivation."

"Who would have thought this youth also had such a tragic past."

With a light laugh, the palace-dressed beauty reached out and brushed the water mirror. The rippling surface immediately calmed down.

At the same time.

On the Heart-Questioning Path, Luo Chen calmed down from his daze.

A flicker of surprise passed over his lowered face.

He had not expected that this Heart-Questioning Path of the Su Ling Sect had some real skill.

It was not an illusion, nor a forced soul search. Instead, it used a special frequency of divine soul oscillation to actively induce the person within to recall the most secret things in their memory.

In that instant just now, the weak split soul he had split out had actually been stirred.

It had exposed a deeply hidden hatred!

Hatred for Rosycloud!

Fortunately, he had been prepared and had planted a wisp of martial divine intent within it. Using the instinctive resistance of his body, he had awakened the split soul, thereby displaying the pre-conceived scene of demon beasts harming people.

This also made Luo Chen vigilant.

The methods of the Xuan Realm were vastly different from those of the Mountains and Seas Realm!

Even divine soul attacks were played out in such a way.

And while being vigilant, Luo Chen also vaguely missed that strange feeling from just now.

This was the first time he had placed martial divine intent and soul divine thought together. Although they had not merged, their coordination had given him an experience he had never had before.

Divine intent, divine thought.

Intent precedes thought; it is the purest instinct of the body.

Intent arises from consciousness; it is the correct feedback given by a cultivator's soul after perceiving the outside world.

There is no distinction between high and low between the two.

The body's instinct is faster, but not necessarily correct.

The soul's cognition often seeks benefit and avoids harm, but its reaction can be a beat slower.

If I could unify intention and thought...

Inexplicably, this thought arose in Luo Chen's mind.

"Xuan Luo, are you alright?"

Unbeknownst to him, Luo Chen had already walked past the nine hundred and ninety-nine azure jade steps and passed the Heart-Questioning Path.

Beside him was Liu Muhan, her face full of concern.

Seeing his dazed expression, she explained, "This Heart-Questioning Path is a method our Sect Master obtained from the demon clan remnants of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. It is still being used for the first time and is not yet refined. I hope it hasn't affected you too much."

Luo Chen blinked. "What are demon clan remnants?"

Liu Muhan was startled, then chided him playfully, "You still have the leisure to care about that!"

"Oh, I'm fine. I just recalled some memories I'd rather not think about." Luo Chen comforted her.

Liu Muhan nodded. She roughly knew the situation of the Heart-Questioning Path, and then a smile appeared on her face.

"Congratulations on passing the third round, the Heart-Questioning Path assessment. From now on, you are a disciple of our Su Ling Sect."

"Outer sect or inner sect?"

"You've done your research quite thoroughly. The specific assignment will have to wait until the Spirit-Entry Assembly is over. Wait outside the hall for now!"

Seeing that Luo Chen was fine, Liu Muhan arranged for him to wait aside, while she went to greet the wandering cultivators who had passed the subsequent assessments.

Luo Chen had no objection.

He found a spot outside the palace where he could see the Heart-Questioning Path below, and leaned against the railing.

On the surface, he seemed to be watching the scenery of the wandering cultivators below attempting the third round, but in his mind, he was constantly recalling the marvelous feeling that had flashed by earlier.

The high degree of unity between body and soul, the perfect fusion of divine intent and divine thought.

In that state, he had even felt a sense of omnipotence.

If he could grasp that feeling...

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly.

...

Because of the promotion to a Void Refining power, this year's Spirit-Entry Assembly had far more participants than before!

Therefore, the duration was also different from usual.

From start to finish, it took about half a month in total.

During this time, strong cultivators from the Su Ling Sect would occasionally come to observe.

And the number of people outside the mountain-top palace also grew.

By the end, it had exceeded a thousand!

Although it was insignificant compared to the tens of thousands who had participated in the assembly, for a sect to accept over a thousand disciples at once was definitely considered a grand opening of the mountain gates.

Luo Chen was regretful that he did not see Huang Yuanhua among the final crowd.

He had failed.

When attempting the third round, his Dao heart was not firm, and he had stopped in his tracks.

He had been escorted down midway.

There had actually been signs of this earlier.

Huang Yuanhua's performance had not been particularly good.

Although his early Initiate stage cultivation was higher than many of the Qi Refinement wandering cultivators present, in the end, there was no difference in Dao heart between Qi Refinement and Initiate stages. What truly created the gap in cultivation was merely the difference in cultivation time and resource allocation.

And for the Su Ling Sect, these were the least important things.

Fortunately, Luo Chen and Huang Yuanhua had exchanged contact information, so they might still meet again in the future.

When Wan Jiuzhang announced that this year's Spirit-Entry Assembly had officially and successfully concluded, the doors of the Heart-Questioning Hall finally opened.

A graceful figure walked out, welcomed by the crowd of disciples.

Over a thousand wandering cultivators—no, they should now be called over a thousand Su Ling Sect disciples—shouted in unison, "Greetings, Sage!"

Luo Chen was among them, standing in the front row.

He even had the leisure to secretly observe the other party.

This woman was extraordinary. She had single-handedly maintained the Heart-Questioning Path for over ten days without showing any fatigue, which showed how profound her divine soul foundation was.

Just as he was observing her, a pair of watery, shimmering eyes met his.

Yao Xi Sage's red lips parted slightly.

"Xuan Luo!"
