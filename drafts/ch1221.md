# Chapter 1221: Core Formation Ceremony, Mountains and Seas Palace

On Jade Spring Peak.

Two figures walked side by side.

The man was handsome and spirited, the woman graceful and lovely.

Jade belts hung high, a secluded stream ran beside them, and the crashing sound of the waterfall did not affect their cheerful conversation in the slightest.

Liu Muhan pointed to the formations near the medicine garden in the distance and explained, "All the surrounding formations are complete. If you find the waterfall noise bothersome, you can activate the formations to block it out. As for this medicine garden, although some herbs are planted here, most are ordinary. Xuanluo, if you want to grow some medicinal herbs, you can remove them anytime and plant new ones."

Luo Chen nodded, withdrawing his gaze from the medicine garden and looking toward the cave entrance that emitted a hazy glow under the sunlight.

This would be his cave dwelling on Jade Spring Peak from now on.

Senior Brother Wan Jiuzhang had prepared it for him before going into seclusion.

Now, Liu Muhan, who had a good relationship with him, was handling the handover while introducing the general layout.

"The spiritual energy here is rich, the scenery beautiful—even within the sect, this is a rare and excellent cave dwelling. Why was no one using it before?" Luo Chen asked casually.

Unexpectedly, Liu Muhan's expression dimmed.

"It's not that no one lived here. It's just that the previous occupant went missing decades ago during a sect mission. And because of that person's special status, this cave dwelling has been left vacant ever since."

Luo Chen became interested. "Oh?"

Liu Muhan sighed and recounted the past.

The previous occupant of this cave dwelling was a female cultivator who held a high status on Jade Spring Peak—she was the senior sister.

Although her cultivation level was slightly lower than Wan Jiuzhang's, her conduct was extremely assertive, and her combat power was exceptionally strong!

When she took on missions, she always acted alone.

One could say that back then, among the second-generation disciples of Jade Spring Peak, Wan Jiuzhang handled internal affairs while that senior sister managed external matters.

But sixty years ago—ten years before Luo Chen joined the sect—she accepted a mission issued by the Enforcement Hall through the External Affairs Hall. After leaving the sect, she vanished without a trace.

For this, Sage Yaoxi personally left the mountain and used various secret techniques, but could not find her whereabouts.

With the Entry Spirit Assembly approaching, the matter was eventually dropped.

One could say that Senior Brother Wan Jiuzhang's rush to go into seclusion and break through to the Sage realm was partly related to the senior sister's disappearance.

Jade Spring Peak had lost a senior sister who was strong in external affairs. With Wan Jiuzhang's gentle temperament, it was difficult for him to establish the peak's reputation within the sect.

Sage Yaoxi didn't manage affairs, so Wan Jiuzhang had to make plans from the perspective of cultivation level.

If he could break through to the Sage realm, Jade Spring Peak's status would surely rise to another level.

Fortunately, Luo Chen's meteoric rise had greatly improved the senior brother's mood. He went into seclusion with a smile and had even arranged everything for Luo Chen in advance—the cave dwelling, cultivation resources, and all other trivial matters.

"In Senior Brother's words, even if he fails to break through, it doesn't matter. From now on, the disciples of this generation on Jade Spring Peak will be led by Xuanluozi, who will surely bring glory to Suling!"

Liu Muhan said this, her eyes carrying a hint of hope.

Everyone understood what it meant to form a golden core at the age of sixty.

As long as no accidents happened, reaching the Sage realm was a certainty!

Luo Chen smiled wryly. "Senior Brother, I haven't even seen you, and you're already putting pressure on me."

Liu Muhan laughed and teased, "Xuanluo, are you perhaps afraid?"

"Afraid?" Luo Chen laughed heartily. His clear laughter startled birds into flight and fish to leap from the stream.

Although he said no further words of encouragement, the strong confidence contained in his laughter was something even Liu Muhan could feel.

It was as if the Sage realm was nothing in his eyes, something he could obtain with a mere reach of his hand.

"Sister Liu..."

"Although I entered the sect earlier than you and am older, your cultivation at the second layer of Core Formation is now above mine. Calling me 'senior sister' or 'junior brother' feels too formal. Just call me Muhan."

Luo Chen did not stand on ceremony and said generously, "Muhan, you are more familiar with the sect than I am. Please help me with something!"

Liu Muhan didn't even ask what it was and directly nodded. "Alright!"

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised by the woman's straightforwardness, but he didn't entrust her with anything troublesome.

"It's not difficult—just sending out some invitations. I've come to Suling Sect and have met very few fellow disciples, only some from Jade Spring Peak and the Alchemy Academy. Since I've come out of seclusion after forming my core, I might as well hold a small gathering and invite some fellow disciples over for tea and discussion of the Dao."

Liu Muhan blinked. She had forgotten about this.

For an individual, forming a golden core was a major event, but for Suling Sect, which produced many experts, it was merely a small matter.

It was impossible to hold a grand core formation ceremony like those small families or minor sects would painstakingly organize.

Usually, cultivators would invite a few senior and junior brothers and some familiar friends within their own master's lineage for a small celebration.

That was what she had done.

But this convention did not apply to Xuanluo.

Forming a core at sixty, a genius in alchemy—either title alone was enough to invite guests of considerable weight.

This was probably why Senior Brother had arranged all the trivial matters for Luo Chen before going into seclusion.

It wasn't just this cave dwelling.

Even the golden core Dao robe, travel boat, and other items that could display status had all been prepared.

After realizing this, Liu Muhan smiled and said, "If you invite people under your name, the number of guests will not be small, and the amount of wine and food involved will be considerable. If you trust me, leave it all to me to handle!"

Luo Chen thought for a moment and then entrusted the entire core formation gathering to Liu Muhan.

He also offered to cover the corresponding spirit stone costs, but she refused.

Jade Spring Peak had its own reserve spirit stones for such expenses.

After they had talked enough, Liu Muhan hurriedly left.

Luo Chen entered the Jade Stream Cave Dwelling.

The interior was fully furnished, faintly retaining some of the personal style of the former senior sister.

Besides the study, bedroom, and meditation chamber, there was even a large open space.

Luo Chen glanced at it and understood what this empty room was for.

The cave floor was made of special ore that could absorb magical power, and the surrounding walls were covered with numerous sword marks.

It seemed this was where the missing senior sister practiced sword techniques in her spare time.

This also matched Liu Muhan's earlier description.

That senior sister's cultivation level wasn't very high, but her combat power was extremely strong!

Unfortunately, it was this confidence that fostered an arrogant personality, causing her to take on an external mission alone and eventually disappear without a trace.

Luo Chen shook his head and didn't comment further. He merely waved his hand and made some slight adjustments.

He transformed this Jade Stream Cave Dwelling into a residence suitable for himself.

Although he would likely still be active around the Alchemy Academy for some time, there was still a while before he broke through to the Sage realm. This cave dwelling had rich spiritual energy and shouldn't be wasted. When he occasionally returned to cultivate, it would naturally be more pleasant to have a comfortable environment.

While Luo Chen was arranging his cave dwelling, Liu Muhan had already begun to act.

First, she withdrew a sum of spirit stones from the managing second senior sister of Jade Spring Peak in Luo Chen's name, then sent people to the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall to purchase corresponding wine and ingredients.

Next, she mobilized a large number of Foundation Establishment disciples from the peak, sending out invitations according to the list Luo Chen had provided, starting from the nearest and working outward.

When this task was assigned, those low-level disciples did not refuse at all. Instead, they were very enthusiastic.

One by one, they patted their chests and promised to handle the matter beautifully, never disgracing Senior Brother Xuanluo or Jade Spring Peak!

There was a reason for their eagerness.

Ever since the Circulation Qi Pill was refined over thirty years ago, after more than three decades of preparation, it had begun small-scale mass production within Suling Sect.

Externally, it occasionally appeared on the shelves of some Suling Sect immortal city shops.

The miraculous effects of this pill, spread by word of mouth among cultivators who had taken it, had gained some renown in the cultivation world.

Unfortunately, the pill was hard to come by. Whenever it was placed on shelves, it would be swept clean by those small and medium-sized forces.

As for within the sect, although it wasn't as hard to obtain, the supply was still limited.

Only Jade Spring Peak was different!

The Alchemy Academy left enough supply for Jade Spring Peak almost every month.

This allowed the Foundation Establishment disciples of Jade Spring Peak to grow in cultivation far faster than their peers.

Everyone knew the reason.

It was all because of Xuanluo!

Therefore, when it came to matters involving Luo Chen, the Foundation Establishment disciples of Jade Spring Peak naturally put in their full effort.

Not only did they handle the task, but they also made sure to do it beautifully!

Through their efforts, invitations were sent to Core Formation cultivators of various peaks and lineages.

Most were people who had sent transmission talismans to Luo Chen while he was in seclusion.

A smaller portion were those who, before Luo Chen, had been known as geniuses and had once shone brightly in Suling Sect.

Another part were outstanding individuals who had performed excellently in the sect's grand competition.

In short, through the efforts of these disciples, the news that Luo Chen was about to hold a core formation ceremony spread far and wide, even reaching the ears of some Sage realm cultivators.

But no one came out to reprimand him. Instead, some Sages privately praised the move, considering Luo Chen a man of ambition.

Later on.

Some Core Formation cultivators who had not received invitations actually sought out Liu Muhan, who was managing the event, asking for an invitation so they could personally get to know Luo Chen.

Thus, what was originally a small gathering truly transformed into a large ceremony.

Liu Muhan found it both amusing and exasperating, but she became even more dedicated to the matter.

Even the venue for the ceremony was changed.

From Luo Chen's cave dwelling to an open area on Jade Spring Peak.

Liu Muhan also gained a lot from this, getting to know some Core Formation geniuses within the sect whom she had never had the chance to meet before, expanding her network.

Time gradually passed.

In just half a month, this golden core ceremony was held on schedule.

Core Formation cultivators from various peaks and lineages—except those who hadn't received invitations, were in seclusion, or were out on missions—basically all came, as long as they had no grudges or conflicts with Jade Spring Peak.

At that time, streaks of light were like threads, cultivators like clouds.

Jade Spring Peak was bustling with excitement!

Liu Muhan, who was in charge of the ceremony, was nearly overwhelmed. Fortunately, the hall master of the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall had foreseen this and sent a large number of cultivators to help stabilize the scene.

The corresponding wine and food were even provided free of charge.

Luo Chen was "flattered by the unexpected favor."

But the hall master of the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall only said it was to make up for the meager rewards at the previous pill tasting gathering and to support the younger generation.

The favor did not stop there.

At the second senior sister's place, where gifts were received, there were even occasional congratulatory gifts sent by Sage realm cultivators through their disciples.

In the eyes of others, this was simply unimaginable.

A cultivator forming a golden core—having peers come to support him was one thing, but even Sage realm cultivators were moved.

But in Luo Chen's eyes, it was only natural.

Given his age and talent, reaching the Sage realm in the future was inevitable.

And because of his previously displayed alchemy talent, he would very likely become a fourth-tier or even fifth-tier alchemist in the future.

Those Sage realm cultivators were not befriending the current Luo Chen, but the future Luo Chen.

After all, among the thousand-plus Sage realm cultivators of Suling Sect, there were only about a dozen fourth-tier alchemists. How could they fully satisfy their pill needs?

By befriending the future Luo Chen, they wouldn't have to worry too much about pills in the future.

In short, Luo Chen's core formation ceremony was held amidst such lively excitement and ended with all guests satisfied.

The ceremony allowed Luo Chen to get to know many outstanding geniuses of the younger generation in Suling Sect.

It also made more geniuses truly remember Luo Chen.

How to describe this special core formation ceremony?

Perhaps it could be summed up by a sentence from Feng Zhiyan, whose eyes were full of admiration after the celebration ended.

"So-called geniuses are merely the threshold for getting to know Senior Brother Xuanluo!"

...

"How do you feel? You've been basking in glory lately, haven't you?"

"Uncle Ning, please don't tease me."

"Haha, no need to be shy. Young people should have the vitality of youth. Though I'm not saying you should look down on everyone, there's no need to be restrained. Be free and unrestrained."

On Danhuang Mountain, Sage Ningli said this to Luo Chen.

It was as if he saw his younger, spirited self, his eyes full of appreciation.

Luo Chen cupped his hands. "I should thank Uncle for that valuable congratulatory gift."

"It's just a small gift, not worth mentioning." Sage Ningli waved his sleeve, then said seriously, "Now that your matters are settled, you should pay more attention to the Mountains and Seas Palace."

Luo Chen nodded solemnly.

As they spoke, the two had arrived outside a palace that was solemn, grand, and even majestic.

Before entering, Luo Chen's gaze froze on the wall reliefs and the upturned eaves.

Carved on them were a large number of birds and beasts.

Though not very lively in spirit, a sense of primal wildness rushed toward him.

"These are... archaic beasts?"

Sage Ningli glanced at Luo Chen in surprise, then nodded. "You have good knowledge. What's carved on them are indeed archaic beasts. According to the records in the classics, Alchemy Saint Chu Yan's homeland—the Mountains and Seas Realm—was once infested with countless archaic beasts. Therefore, we specially carved the archaic beasts recorded in the illustrations onto these buildings."

The corner of Luo Chen's mouth twitched. He wanted to tell them that the Mountains and Seas Realm now had very few archaic beasts left.

Except for some desolate wilderness areas, whether in human territories or demon territories, archaic beasts only existed in ancient legends.

Chu Yan himself had probably never seen many living archaic beasts.

Oh, that mysterious turtle was an exception!

But he could understand.

So-called classics often had time limitations.

Especially Suling Sect's understanding of the Mountains and Seas Realm, which was mostly limited to several thousand or even ten thousand years ago.

At that time, the Mountains and Seas Realm indeed had countless archaic beasts dwelling in it.

Sage Ningli didn't notice Luo Chen's change in expression and led him into the Mountains and Seas Palace.

As they walked, he gave Luo Chen a general introduction and also handed him a book.

It was a log written by the cultivator in charge of building the Mountains and Seas Palace.

It marked every pavilion, tower, pond, and stream, explaining their allusions and meanings.

Luo Chen listened, and an irrepressible look of nostalgia appeared in his eyes.

Although the Mountains and Seas Palace built by Suling Sect based on the classics had many places that were out of step with the times, there were even more places that evoked Luo Chen's memories of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Such as the Star-Gazing Tower in the center, shaped like a pillar connecting heaven and earth, resembling the Heaven-Earth Peak landmark of Central Continent.

Such as the pavilions and towers scattered around, mostly floating on the lake's surface, like the Northern Sea archipelago.

Especially a stream diverted from elsewhere, occupying a third of the entire Mountains and Seas Palace's area, which made him even more nostalgic.

It was as if this was the Nether Abyss that once surrounded the Nether Origin Sect!

Nether Origin...

Luo Chen suddenly realized something.

He tentatively asked, "Since taking on this task, this disciple has consulted some classics and asked some people. According to some rumors, although that Alchemy Saint came from the Mountains and Seas Realm, his origins were with the Nether Origin Sect. On the Youxuan Continent, the Nether Origin Sect is also one of the Nine Great Sacred Sects. Why did the Alchemy Saint not return to his main sect after ascending to the Xuan Realm, but instead stay within the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

The moment this question was posed, Sage Ningli abruptly turned his head.

The atmosphere fell silent for a moment.

Luo Chen pursed his lips, putting on a nervous expression. "If it's inconvenient to say, Uncle need not bother with it."

"You've prepared thoroughly, even finding out about such secret matters."

Sage Ningli looked around, then sighed and sent a divine sense transmission.

"There's nothing to hide. To prevent you from angering him later, I might as well tell you now."
