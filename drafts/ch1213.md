# Chapter 1213: The Secret of Spiritual Opportunity, Opening Mountains and Seas

Without waiting for the crowd to raise doubts, Luo Chen began to speak freely.

"Circulating Qi, automatic cultivation. It doesn't harm the body, doesn't consume the spirit. It uses medicinal pills to drive the cultivator's golden core to rotate automatically, absorbing and refining external spiritual energy."

"Its characteristic is the massive medicinal power of the pill itself, which can last for a very long time. The main ingredient, Zhouyuan Fruit, is a rare essence of spiritual energy, sufficient to achieve this. But it's difficult to cultivate, which is why you all thought of replacing the main ingredient with other similar medicinal herbs to achieve a weaker effect."

Above, Gongsun Mao nodded, his eyes carrying appreciation for Luo Chen.

Regardless of what Luo Chen would say next, at least his previous words had succinctly summarized the main line of thought that he and the group of three-pattern alchemists from the Spirit Pill Peak had discussed.

In terms of theory, this junior brother named Xuan Luo had already entered the door.

No one interrupted, and Luo Chen continued to speak freely, but his tone suddenly shifted.

"The idea of replacing the main ingredient isn't wrong, but the emphasis is misplaced!"

"There are many medicinal herbs containing massive spiritual energy in our sect and even throughout the Youxuan Continent, most of which can achieve similar effects. However, the third-grade Circulating Qi Pill corresponds to Golden Core cultivators, while the second-grade pill corresponds to Foundation Establishment cultivators. The greatest essential difference between the two seems to be the Golden Core Dao Fruit, but in reality, it's divine sense!"

This time, Gongsun Mao's brow furrowed deeply.

He didn't even need to speak; someone had already refuted Luo Chen on his behalf.

"Fallacy! Isn't the gap between a Foundation Establishment true cultivator and a Golden Core superior precisely the condensation of the golden core?"

"Once a golden core is swallowed into the abdomen, my fate is determined by me, not by heaven. This shows the importance of the golden core to cultivators."

"Green youth, this is not a place for you to spout paradoxical theories!"

...

Amidst the rising and falling refutations, He Jun couldn't help but smile bitterly.

Earlier, he had asked these senior brothers to be more tolerant of Luo Chen.

But he had forgotten that on the path of alchemy, precedence is determined by achievement. There is no such thing as friendly warmth between senior and junior brothers.

Right is right, wrong is wrong!

Once there is a difference in philosophy, there is no room for tolerance.

In the corner of the pill hall, Wan Jiuzhang's expression was quite tense.

Even though his cultivation level surpassed everyone present, he couldn't interject at all. This was a discussion among alchemists.

However, regarding Luo Chen's claim about the essential difference between Foundation Establishment and Golden Core cultivators, he was lost in thought.

Was it not in magical power, but in divine sense?

"Silence!"

An aged voice suddenly rang out, suppressing the noise in the hall.

Gongsun Mao narrowed his eyes and looked at Luo Chen.

"Continue."

Luo Chen smiled slightly, not bothering to argue with the crowd about the essential differences in cultivation realms. Instead, he continued to speak freely along the direction of his previous theory.

"The gap in divine sense means that Foundation Establishment cultivators cannot cultivate frequently, let alone go into seclusion for decades or even centuries at a time."

"As such, the second-grade Circulating Qi Pill you want to mass-produce will place immense pressure on Foundation Establishment cultivators. Unless someone's soul foundation surpasses the Foundation Establishment stage and reaches the Golden Core stage, can they meet your theoretical requirements."

"In that case, in my view, the emphasis should not be on the main ingredient."

"Supplementary ingredients! Supplementary ingredients that can stimulate the soul activity of Foundation Establishment cultivators—that is the key!"

"Only by replacing the Ice Heart Flower with other, more potent medicinal materials can it be truly possible to mass-produce a Circulating Qi Pill suitable for Foundation Establishment cultivators."

"That's about it. Thank you, Master Gongsun, for listening to my humble opinion."

Luo Chen cupped his hands in a salute, flipped his green robe, and sat down calmly.

Earlier, due to Luo Chen's outrageous remarks about cultivation realms, the atmosphere in the hall was quite tense. But after he truly finished speaking his thoughts, although the atmosphere was still tense, some people had actually begun to think along the direction Luo Chen had envisioned.

Others sensed something was wrong and found it somewhat unbelievable.

Had someone actually believed the theory of this green youth?

But when they discovered that even Master Gongsun was silently pondering, all the noise quieted down.

After a long while.

Gongsun Mao spoke slowly in contemplation: "Xuan Luo, you mean that Golden Core cultivators have strong enough divine sense to withstand the soul pressure brought by the automatic cultivation of the third-grade Circulating Qi Pill, but Foundation Establishment cultivators cannot. Even the second-grade Circulating Qi Pill we envisioned would be too burdensome for them. Therefore, you believe we should strengthen the soul-type supplementary ingredients and not be so particular about the main ingredient."

"Am I correct?"

At the last sentence, he looked at Luo Chen with blazing eyes.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Master has summarized it well. That's more or less what I mean."

Gongsun Mao nodded. "An original idea. Although it differs from our mainstream thinking, it has indeed opened up a path we never considered. It may be worth a try."

When the words "worth a try" were spoken, everyone present in the hall realized what it meant.

It meant that Master Gongsun would incorporate Luo Chen's ideas into subsequent experimental pill refining.

Some expressed disapproval of this move, believing it would increase the workload and weaken everyone's investment in the original theory.

But others thought it was worth a try. What if the previous theories didn't work out?

"He Jun, you are familiar with Xuan Luo. You will be responsible for verifying his ideas! Whatever people and resources you need, just ask. The Pill Refining Institute will provide them."

Upon hearing this, He Jun's eyes lit up.

This meant he could lead his own team.

Not to mention whether results could be achieved, just the resources that could be mobilized during the experimental process was enough to excite him.

"He Jun is unworthy, but I will do my utmost!"

Gongsun Mao nodded, then looked at Luo Chen again.

This time, the appreciation in his eyes reached its peak, with even a hint of gratification.

Before his own lifespan ran out, to encounter such an outstanding junior—that feeling was truly indescribable.

"Xuan Luo, your talent has been verified. But the courage you showed today—not fearing authority, daring to boldly propose your own ideas—is even more commendable. The path of alchemy has accompanied the cultivation world for who knows how many epochs since we cultivators could practice it. Its theoretical system has almost developed to its extreme. Too many people are trapped within it, constrained by old ideas. This courage of yours may allow you to walk the path of alchemy more smoothly."

"Take out your alchemist token!"

Luo Chen, puzzled, removed the alchemist token from his waist.

Everyone in the hall seemed to realize something and looked at him with considerable envy.

They saw Gongsun Mao also take out his own four-pattern alchemist token and strike it with a stream of magical power.

Instantly, flowing light transformed and covered Luo Chen's token.

Luo Chen reached out to take it and saw numbers flickering on it.

[Ten Thousand]

He immediately realized what this was—sect contribution points, a full ten thousand!

An amount that an ordinary two-pattern alchemist could never accumulate in a lifetime.

"Master, this..."

"Consider it a reward for the ideas you proposed today, and an old man's support for a junior."

Gongsun Mao revealed a gentle smile. "After all, I don't have much time left. Hoarding contribution points is of little use. Don't slack off either. If you and He Jun truly succeed, the sect's rewards for you will only be greater."

Luo Chen nodded with a complex expression.

His original plan was to accumulate sect contribution points by refining pills.

Although ten thousand was a large amount, if he slightly revealed his extraordinary pill-forming rate, he could accumulate it in a short time.

But he never expected that just a few words today could save him several years of effort.

Perhaps, as the other party said, it was support for a "junior."

After this interlude, the pill-testing meeting continued.

But subtly, it had already split into a large group and a small group.

The large group continued along the theoretical direction of Gongsun Mao and the others, while the small group was organized by He Jun.

Among them were alchemists Luo Chen had just met, such as Ning Fu and Zhu Xiao.

There were also those he didn't know but who were interested in the theoretical direction he had proposed and actively joined.

However, because Luo Chen was ultimately only a first-pattern alchemist, his participation was not very high.

He didn't plan to put much effort into this either.

After all, he had already performed brilliantly enough earlier. Let He Jun and the others carry out the practical experiments!

...

Late at night, the first day of the pill-testing meeting temporarily ended.

It would continue in the future. Even when they truly began attempting to refine the Circulating Qi Pill, similar meetings would be held irregularly for everyone to exchange ideas, find problems, and solve them.

When the meeting adjourned, many people took the initiative to come forward and get to know Luo Chen.

The private transmission jade talismans he received alone numbered no less than ten!

It showed how outstanding his performance today had been!

Luo Chen didn't leave the Pill Refining Institute directly. Instead, he waited for a while in the Step Pill Court.

When Wan Jiuzhang appeared with an excited expression, he left together with him.

"Senior Brother seems to have gained something?"

"Indeed, a two-pattern alchemist agreed to help refine the pills. It's also their specialty. It should be possible to see the finished pills soon."

"That's excellent."

"This is also thanks to Junior Brother's introduction!"

After Wan Jiuzhang sighed with emotion, he couldn't help but look at Luo Chen with a strange gaze.

Before Luo Chen could ask, he took the initiative to say, "If Junior Brother has any needs for medicinal herbs in the future, feel free to come to me. Jade Spring Peak will fully support you."

Luo Chen didn't refuse and said with a smile that he certainly would.

The two finally parted before the Kun Bottle Cave Dwelling.

Wan Jiuzhang personally watched Luo Chen return home before leisurely flying toward Jade Spring Peak.

Luo Chen's stunning speech in the Pill Refining Institute earlier still surfaced in his mind from time to time.

Even Master Gongsun thought so highly of Luo Chen. How could he not represent Jade Spring Peak in fully supporting their own?

Perhaps in a hundred or two hundred years, Jade Spring Peak could also produce an alchemy master!

...

The next day.

Luo Chen did not go to the Pill Refining Institute to continue participating in the pill-testing meeting.

After all, in the eyes of others, he was ultimately just a first-pattern alchemist, not suitable for directly participating in the experimental refining of second-grade pills.

Moreover, alchemy was just a process for him.

The result was to obtain a large number of contribution points to achieve other goals.

Early in the morning, Luo Chen hurried to the Scripture Transmission Hall.

Unlike others waiting for high-level cultivators to give lectures and transmit teachings, Luo Chen went straight into the Scripture Pavilion!

As a Nascent Soul force with deep pockets, the Su Ling Sect naturally had a considerable collection of scriptures.

The nine-story-high Scripture Pavilion was enough to illustrate this.

The moment he entered the pavilion, a divine sense shone brightly upon him.

Luo Chen took out his alchemist token, and the number of contribution points on it made the other party pause.

"Floors six and below can be borrowed and copied. The corresponding contribution points are marked on the side."

"Disciple understands."

Luo Chen replied and first entered the first floor.

That divine sense lingered for a long time before slowly withdrawing.

"An Nascent Soul cultivator guarding the Scripture Pavilion? That's quite thoughtful."

Luo Chen twitched the corner of his mouth and began to walk around the spacious first floor.

From time to time, he would take out a book and browse it briefly, but in reality, he had already released a strand of divine sense, sweeping across the entire Scripture Pavilion.

Only when he reached the ninth floor did he encounter an obstacle.

That was a Nascent Soul-level soul restriction!

Luo Chen understood in his heart that the nine-story Scripture Pavilion of the Su Ling Sect was probably divided by cultivation level.

Floors six and below corresponded to the Golden Core stage and below.

Floors seven and eight were places where Nascent Soul true cultivators could set foot.

As for the ninth floor, it was presumably personally established by Tai Su Lingjun, accessible only to Ascension-stage great cultivators.

But for Luo Chen, it was enough.

Nascent Soul true cultivators, whether in the Mountains and Seas Realm or the Xuan Realm, were an important force that could not be ignored.

The information and news they could access were enough to be considered secrets of the cultivation world.

His body lingered on the first floor, slowly moving to the second and third floors, and even the fourth floor.

But his divine sense was already roaming freely among the rich collection of books on the seventh and eighth floors.

He didn't pay much attention to cultivation techniques and scriptures.

His attention was mostly focused on some travelogues or rare manuscripts.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's divine sense moved, silently probing into a light barrier.

Inside, a jade slip was quickly perused by Luo Chen without anyone noticing.

[Discussions on Spiritual Opportunity]

This was precisely what he was most concerned about.

Ever since arriving in the Xuan Realm, he had discovered that the number of spiritual opportunities observable with his Crimson-Golden Pupils was extremely scarce.

At that time, he thought it was because of the numerous sects on the Youxuan Continent, each using secret techniques to confine spiritual opportunities.

But after truly entering the Su Ling Sect, he was shocked to find that whether it was the fourth-grade spirit vein or the fifth-grade spirit veins where the eight Ascension-stage elders resided, the amount of spiritual opportunity was extremely thin.

Let alone allowing Ascension-stage great cultivators to comprehend the origin of laws daily, even supporting late-stage Nascent Soul cultivators to break through to the Ascension stage was difficult.

In other words!

Nascent Soul cultivators on the Youxuan Continent could hardly absorb spiritual opportunities to comprehend the origin of laws in advance.

To break through to the Ascension stage, they could only honestly follow the normal cultivation process.

Accumulate massive magical power, condense the primordial spirit, and break through to the Ascension stage naturally.

Then, in the ocean of origin qi, grope for the origin of laws suitable for themselves.

Luo Chen naturally didn't want this, so he had been thinking about why this situation occurred.

This book, *Discussions on Spiritual Opportunity*, might be able to enlighten him.

As his divine sense delved deeper, the information within entered his mind one by one, and his expression gradually changed subtly, even becoming serious.

Spiritual opportunity!

It is not a power, but a consciousness born from a realm of heaven and earth.

This consciousness drives the growth of all things within a realm of heaven and earth, deriving all kinds of great dao laws.

Therefore, people often say that heaven and earth have spirit. This "spirit" never refers to spiritual energy, but to the elusive and unfathomable "spiritual opportunity."

Ordinary people simply cannot capture this existence. Occasionally, when they do, they enter a state of enlightenment, comprehending many things overnight, saving decades or even centuries of effort.

Hence, there has always been a saying: "The spiritual opportunity of the universe is profound and obscure, chaotic and dim, the great dao is difficult to seek, and living beings are shrouded. Only when heaven bestows spiritual opportunity, clear and unobstructed, can the clouds be parted and the fog dispelled."

But in *Discussions on Spiritual Opportunity*, a different claim was put forward.

Or rather, the author observed a phenomenon.

That is, on the Youxuan Continent, and even throughout the entire Xuan Realm, the amount of spiritual opportunity is extremely scarce.

Even fifth-grade spirit veins find it difficult to naturally produce a large amount of spiritual opportunity.

Regarding this phenomenon, some speculated that it was absorbed by the Great Vehicle-stage Heavenly Sovereigns at the apex of the realm, while others speculated that it was caused by the Xuan Realm itself.

Especially the latter, which gained recognition from many.

The Xuan Realm was too vast, too ancient.

So ancient that the consciousness of this realm had solidified.

Moreover, over billions of years, countless living beings had lived and died, too many distracting thoughts filling it, confusing the spiritual opportunity.

A strong piece of evidence supporting this view was that some powerful cultivators, when exploring some newly born small worlds, had discovered a large amount of abundant spiritual opportunity.

Upon seeing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but fall into deep thought.

In his memory, it seemed that more than one person had said similar things to him.

The Taisui in the Demon-Fall Land, Fu Chaosheng of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and so on.

They had all mentioned that the Mountains and Seas Realm had abundant spiritual opportunity, far surpassing other places, hence the proliferation of geniuses.

"Newborn small worlds have more spiritual opportunity?"

As Luo Chen pondered, the subsequent content of the book flooded into his mind.

When he saw a certain record, his mind was shaken.

[Ten thousand years ago, the demonic nest stirred, space overlapped, and someone discovered a barbaric world filled with spiritual opportunity. The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect took the lead, joining forces with the Primordial Demon Sect and the Nether Origin Sect, two of the nine great holy sects of the Youxuan Continent, as well as the Life and Death Gate of the Wanling Continent, to jointly wage a war of conquest. During this period, many small forces also participated.]

[Over the past ten thousand years, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has reaped great benefits from that small world, hence the proliferation of geniuses within the sect, with many great cultivators and true lords advancing.]

[That barbaric world was named the Mountains and Seas Realm!] (End of chapter)
