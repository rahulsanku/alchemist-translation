# Chapter 1222: The One Who Destroyed Heavenly Origin—Who Was That?

"Ten thousand years ago, the demon lair stirred, and the Xuan Realm trembled. At that time, the demon realm invaded, sparking a great Xuan-Demon war..."

Ning Li Sage recounted the past events, the general content largely matching what Luo Chen had secretly read in the library before.

But this time, the focus was concentrated on the latter part.

"After the demon lair was suppressed and sealed, the spatial passages connecting the two realms naturally contracted. The major sects all lost their control over the Mountains and Seas Realm. Only the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was different—they had always held tight control over a spatial passage that led directly to the Mountains and Seas Realm."

"This meant that all the benefits related to the Mountains and Seas Realm were in the hands of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. How much they distributed depended entirely on their whim."

"At first, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would still share some benefits with the other major sects. But as time passed, they used various excuses—the high cost of maintaining the spatial passage, the frequent interruptions in contact, and so on—to reduce their distributions, until they stopped giving anything at all."

"If it were just that, it might have been tolerable. After all, the major powers that had been the main pioneers had already recouped their investments from the beginning. But there was a key factor involved—talent!"

"The Mountains and Seas Realm may not be large, but its spiritual energy is abundant, making it easy for geniuses to emerge..."

When he spoke the word "genius," Ning Li deliberately glanced at Luo Chen.

He also explained to Luo Chen what kind of genius the Mountains and Seas Realm produced!

It had nothing to do with cultivation speed, but rather with the comprehension of true law intent.

According to legend, the prodigies of the Mountains and Seas Realm could comprehend true law intent ahead of time at the Sage stage. Breaking through to the Ascendant stage was much smoother for them compared to Xuan Realm Sages, and their cultivation after Ascension was also easier than that of Xuan Realm cultivators.

"Back when the major sects all had spatial anchors, they could pull up some talents every so often. But once only the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect controlled the spatial passage, this avenue was completely cut off."

"Whether it was the Nether Origin Sect or the Primordial Demon Sect, cultivators who could ascend to the Xuan Realm simply vanished."

"On the other hand, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect kept receiving prodigies. For example, Nine Yang Sword Lord, Hundred Poison True Lord, and even the Alchemy Saint! These people all came from the Mountains and Seas Realm. When they first arrived, they were unknown nobodies, but before long, they all successfully broke through to the Body Integration stage and became True Lords of their generation."

"If these people had all come from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, that would have been one thing. But as time passed, people gradually discovered that some of them might have originated from other sacred sects."

The first to be discovered was Hundred Poison True Lord. When he fought against enemies, the techniques he used were unmistakably the gu arts of the Life and Death Gate from the Wanling Continent. His body, tempered by a hundred poisons, was truly impervious to poison, immune to both water and fire—so domineering!

Hundred Poison Body Tempering?

Luo Chen's mind stirred.

He had also cultivated a gu-tempering body cultivation technique with the exact same name.

Back then, Li Tian Master had said it was his own creation.

Now it seemed there was some exaggeration in that claim. The origin of the technique was probably inseparable from this Hundred Poison True Lord.

"This speculation was confirmed once again when the Alchemy Saint Chu Yan ascended. Because many people could determine that the Alchemy Saint Chu Yan's origins lay with the Nether Origin Sect, another of the nine sacred sects!" Ning Li Sage said slowly, his expression quite solemn.

Luo Chen was puzzled. "After these Mountain-Sea cultivators ascended, didn't they want to return to their main sects?"

Ning Li Sage shook his head. "Some suspect that they were all controlled by the Dao Sect using secret arts. Even if they had a certain degree of personal freedom, their life and death were in others' hands. This would also explain why the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has developed so rapidly in recent years, with geniuses and experts emerging in an endless stream."

He looked at Luo Chen and said seriously, "This is what I need to warn you about—never ask related questions. For top experts to be controlled by others is already a humiliating matter. If you carelessly touch on this topic and they take offense... don't take this lightly. Hundred Poison True Lord once had a reputation for bloodlust, and this is why."

Luo Chen nodded solemnly, indicating that he had taken the warning to heart.

He wouldn't say things he shouldn't.

But another question rose to his lips.

"Originally, it was a small world jointly developed by various major powers, yet in the end, only the Dao Sect enjoyed it exclusively. The small powers might let it slide, but what about the sacred sects on the same level as the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect? Especially since this involves the most critical matter of technique inheritance."

At this question, Ning Li Sage laughed heartily.

"You see clearly. There was indeed a period of contention in the middle. Apart from the Life and Death Gate, which is far away on another continent, the Primordial Demon Sect and the Nether Origin Sect on the Youxuan Continent did make trouble for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. There were constant small-scale conflicts that nearly escalated into a full-scale sacred sect war."

"But this matter changed six hundred years ago."

Six hundred years!

Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly.

Ning Li Sage wore an expression of someone enjoying gossip as he recounted the hearsay secrets.

"According to legend, a remarkable peerless expert emerged from the Mountains and Seas Realm itself. At the Sage stage, they defied and attacked the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Not only did they destroy the sect and annihilate its members, but they also severed the only passage connecting to the Dao Sect in the Xuan Realm. This caused the Dao Sect to lose control over the Mountains and Seas Realm as well. The other sacred sects, whose interests had been damaged, found this greatly satisfying. Coupled with some compensation from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, the matter of the Mountains and Seas Realm was quietly dropped."

Luo Chen's brow twitched imperceptibly. He asked in a low voice, "A Sage cultivator destroyed the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect in the Mountains and Seas Realm? Such a humiliating secret—how did it leak out?"

Ning Li Sage chuckled. "Naturally, it wasn't something they spread themselves. The other two sacred sects and the Heavenly Phoenix Clan deliberately disseminated it. Probably to tarnish the Dao Sect's reputation and suppress their momentum!"

As he spoke, a look of reverence involuntarily appeared on Ning Li Sage's face.

"Sage stage, ah. I wonder how they managed to defy and overthrow an Ascendant-level sect. That legendary figure who destroyed Heavenly Origin in the Mountains and Seas Realm must have been exceptionally talented. Five or six hundred years have passed—they've surely already advanced to the Ascendant stage, and even the Body Integration stage is not out of reach."

Luo Chen outwardly agreed with the other party, but his mood was somewhat subtle.

He hadn't expected that in the Xuan Realm, there were still legends circulating about him.

Even if only on a small scale.

Amidst the secret delight, Luo Chen also felt a sense of vigilance.

To the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, the Mountains and Seas Realm was not just a place to harvest resources—it could be seen as a cradle of talent.

Although the culprit who destroyed the Mountain-Sea Dao Sect was Rosycloud, it was he who had severed the final hope of reconnecting the two realms' Dao Sects.

Rosycloud had the Heavenly Phoenix Clan as her backing, so the Dao Sect couldn't touch her.

But he didn't!

If he were truly exposed by accident, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would probably spare no effort to hunt him down.

His decision to conceal his identity and develop quietly had truly been the right one.

"Alright, I've told you what needs to be said. Xuan Luo, you're a smart person and should know your limits. Next, let's go look around elsewhere!"

Ning Li Sage ended this topic and continued showing Luo Chen around the Mountain-Sea Palace.

Some areas were still in the finishing stages and would be handed over to Luo Chen for supervision.

When it was time to part, Ning Li Sage once again brought up the importance of the Alchemy Saint's visit.

It would be greatly beneficial not only to the Sulling Sect but also to Xuan Luo personally, and he urged Luo Chen to take it seriously.

After Ning Li Sage left, the vast Mountain-Sea Palace was left with only Luo Chen, aside from the Baiqiao Academy spirit architects still working on construction.

He stood by the shore of the "Little Nether Abyss," his expression somewhat peculiar.

His gaze seemed fixed on the gurgling flow of the Little Nether Abyss, yet also seemed to be looking toward some unknown place.

After a long while.

He let out a faint sigh.

The Mountain-Sea Palace was, after all, not the Mountains and Seas Realm. With that person's temperament, she would surely look down on such superficial efforts.

Instead, he himself would need to be careful not to reveal any clues.

With his current soul power, he could deceive all Ascendant-level experts. Especially after undergoing his heart-mind transformation and inscribing the Five-Colored Mystic Chapter, as long as he didn't actively attract attention, even Body Integration True Lords would find it difficult to see through his disguise.

But one must always prepare for the worst!

For this, he needed to make some additional safeguards.

Luo Chen recalled the scene when he entered the Heart-Questioning Path during the Initiation Assembly.

That was the first time he had combined his martial divine will with his soul divine sense, thereby bypassing the heart-questioning examination.

Back then, he had a rough idea but had never put it into action.

But this time, perhaps he should give it a try, to avoid failing this test.

Uniting will and intention—let's give it a try.

...

The once-famous Xuan Luozi had gone quiet after his Adept Establishment Ceremony.

But before the Sulling Sect disciples could ponder this, another major event was announced.

The Heavenly Origin Alchemy Saint would visit the Sulling Sect!

Just four simple words, yet they caused a tremendous stir.

First and foremost was the identity of the visitor—a member of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Although the Sulling Sect was an independent sect, like the surrounding powers both large and small, it fell under the rule of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

In a sense, they were actually vassals of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!

Even their largest medicinal herb business was inextricably linked to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Now that someone from the Dao Sect was coming, and a Body Integration True Lord at that, they naturally had to receive them well.

Moreover, this person was not only a Body Integration True Lord but also bore the title of Alchemy Saint.

This reputation was highly recognized even within the Dao Sect, where experts abounded.

Not to mention that their own Sect Master had received help from this person to successfully break through to the Body Integration stage.

Now that the Heavenly Origin Alchemy Saint was coming to stay at the Sulling Sect for a while, the entire sect was mobilized.

Externally, they cleared out the surrounding riffraff and drove away rogue cultivators to avoid disturbing the honored guest.

Internally, they began tidying up every mountain peak, ensuring that everything looked pleasing to the eye.

The scale of it... to use Luo Chen's occasional complaint, it was like a higher-up leader inspecting a primary school, and the school starting a thorough cleaning in advance.

Fortunately, the Alchemy Academy had started preparations early, so by the later stages, the tension had eased somewhat.

Luo Chen also understood why the fourth-tier alchemists on Alchemy Emperor Mountain couldn't spare the time to manage the Mountain-Sea Palace.

It turned out that those people had recently been organizing various difficult alchemy questions to facilitate discussion and consultation when the opportunity arose.

These questions might have troubled them for decades or even centuries without resolution.

Now that they had the chance, they naturally didn't want to miss it.

And managing the Mountain-Sea Palace was also an opportunity for Luo Chen to enter the eyes of the important figures.

Amidst such hurried preparations, three years passed in the blink of an eye.

Only thirteen years had passed since Luo Chen formed his core.

The Sulling Sect's only fifth-tier alchemist, Elder Gumu, had returned.

His return was a signal!

A few days later.

A procession arrived outside the Sulling Sect's mountain gate.

The number of people was not large—only about a hundred—but the scale was extremely high.

Dark birds pulled the carriage, and canopies shaded the clouds.

Sun-Moon treasure banners hung high, and Mountain-River jade slips stood side by side.

Golden boys and jade girls attended, while Ascendant-level experts provided protection.

The origin qi of heaven and earth lingered nearby, and true law intent repelled petty troublemakers.

The Sulling Sect's mountain gate opened wide, and all formations were fully activated.

All high-level cultivators above the Adept stage came out to welcome them, their spirits high and their expressions radiant.

Clouds and rosy clouds paved the path, and jade chimes transmitted sound.

With each chime, golden ripples spread through the cloud path.

Then, celestial maidens in rainbow garments played zithers and held sheng pipes, performing the "Grand Music of Heaven's Equilibrium," lively yet dignified and grand.

On the treasure carriage, two graceful figures sat side by side.

Both possessed peerless beauty, like the sun and moon shining together.

One wore green robes, the other a white dress.

But the woman in green was bright and poised, like a blooming peony, elegant and noble.

The woman in white, however, had a tranquil expression, with a hint of melancholy.

Looking at the grand spectacle before her, Tai Su Lingjun smiled. "Sister Chu Yan, what do you think of my Sulling Sect?"

Chu Yan revealed a genuine smile. "A magnificent sight, with disciples like dragons—truly a leader among Body Integration-level powers."

Tai Su Lingjun pouted. "Sister, you're being too insincere. While I'd love to hear your praise, this isn't the way to do it! I'm far from being a leader—I can't even compare to the Five Elements Divine Sect, let alone that."

"Having gained the Dao Sect's favor, you'll surpass them in the future." Chu Yan said this, her gaze sweeping over the grand reception. She sighed. "I only came out to relax. Why did you go to such trouble, Lingjun?"

Tai Su Lingjun waved her hand. "Compared to helping me break through to Body Integration, this is nothing. Come, let me first take you to settle in, and then we can tour my Sulling Sect properly."

As she spoke, she looked at the hundred-person procession following Chu Yan.

"You don't need to follow. I've arranged accommodations for you myself."

Among the hundred-person group, an elderly Ascendant cultivator seemed about to speak, but Tai Su Lingjun silenced him with a glare.

"Within my sect, with me personally accompanying her, do you think anything could go wrong?"

With that, there were no further objections.

Several Ascendant elders flew out from within the Sulling Sect and enthusiastically led the hundred or so people to other areas.

Meanwhile, Tai Su Lingjun brought Chu Yan to the Alchemy Academy.

A complex yet familiar fragrance of alchemy entered her nose, and Chu Yan's brow relaxed slightly.

Tai Su Lingjun nodded imperceptibly. Although she was out to relax, a familiar environment would make it easier for her to let down her guard.

This young Alchemy Saint sister, in her opinion, was quite innocent.

She hoped this trip would bring her some peace of mind.

"This is the place. Your residence for the next three months—its name is the Mountain-Sea Palace."

Upon hearing this, Chu Yan looked over, and part of the palace's layout came into view.

Some scenes that were both familiar and yet not quite right made her part her lips slightly.

After a long moment, she said softly, "Lingjun, you've gone to too much trouble."

"Didn't I tell you before? Call me sister—it's more intimate." Tai Su Lingjun said as she led the woman down to the palace's main gate.

There, a group of cultivators was already waiting.

Each of them greeted the Alchemy Saint with the fullest, most reverent posture.

"Greetings, Alchemy Saint!"

With just one glance, Chu Yan knew that these people were alchemists like herself.

She was about to say something when her gaze suddenly froze.

At the end of the crowd, a young man stood with his hands clasped in greeting along with everyone else. A subtle, mysterious feeling arose within her.

"Who is that?"
