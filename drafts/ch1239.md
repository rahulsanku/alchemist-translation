# Chapter 1239: Fire Tree Golden Blossoms, Complete Awakening

Leaving the Suwang Hall.

Though the group of Nascent Soul cultivators were physically exhausted from refining their treasures, their spirits were high. They had already been confident about the Demon-Suppressing Trial, and now with the protective means bestowed by their sect elders, they were even more assured.

Luo Chen and Jiang Huang followed behind Yao Xi, strolling and chatting.

Mostly Yao Xi spoke while the two listened.

"Jiang Huang, your cultivation is the lowest and your strength the weakest, which is why Elder Taiheng bestowed you with a defensive robe. When you enter the demon cave, don't act rashly. First use the Lotus Brand to gather with us, and only seek self-preservation. As for the Demon Marrow Diamonds you need, your master will naturally find them for you."

"Disciple has burdened Master."

Yao Xi shook her head, not thinking this way.

In the past, she hadn't fulfilled her duty as a master. This time, she could make up for it somewhat.

Moreover, with the Lotus Brand on them, the ten Nascent Soul cultivators of the Sushui Sect could gather together. Finding Demon Marrow Diamonds would likely be far easier than for Nascent Soul cultivators of other sects.

She shifted the topic to Luo Chen.

"Going out with Elder Tiange, did you learn anything?"

Luo Chen said with slight excitement, "I learned many things. Elder Tiange imparted his knowledge to me without reservation. Disciple has now touched upon the threshold of law-domain divine abilities."

"Is that so? That's good..." Yao Xi said softly, her tone carrying an indescribable mix of envy and relief. She smiled gently, "With your rapid cultivation, Xuan Luo, and your outstanding talent, you might even surpass your master someday."

Luo Chen cupped his hands, "Master flatters me. Disciple would never dare."

Would never *dare*, not *could not*!

Yao Xi was startled, sensing the powerful confidence hidden beneath his self-deprecating words.

She had taken on the task of overseeing the Initiation Assembly, casually accepting three rogue cultivators as disciples to fulfill an obligation.

Yet unexpectedly, a true dragon had emerged from the muddy pond!

If that day truly came, when Luo Chen surpassed her, the image...

Yao Xi murmured, "That would be a good thing too!"

While the master-disciple trio chatted as if taking a leisurely stroll, the people ahead suddenly stopped.

Dean He looked back and smiled as he issued an invitation: "Yao Xi, I plan to hold a gathering in two days, convenient for all fellow brothers and sisters to connect before the Demon-Suppressing Trial. Will you come?"

Yao Xi naturally accepted with pleasure.

They had prepared for the Demon-Suppressing Trial for a long time; missing a month or so of bitter cultivation wouldn't matter.

Jiang Huang also accepted.

But when it came to Luo Chen, he politely declined.

"Disciple received guidance from Elder Tiange and felt something stir within. I plan to make good use of this coming month. As for the gathering, I'll have to pass. There are still some days before the Demon-Suppressing Trial. During the journey, I will naturally visit all the esteemed elders."

Dean He understood and didn't insist.

Watching Luo Chen bid farewell to Yao Xi and fly alone toward the Danhuang Mountain, he stroked his beard and smiled.

"Xuan Luozi is young yet has cultivated to this level. It seems that besides talent, he is also diligent enough!"

Beside him, Iron Horse nodded in agreement.

Li Ting looked on with considerable curiosity, still unable to connect Luo Chen with that rogue cultivator who had been drawn in by the Initiation Assembly at Taisu Immortal City.

A mere rogue cultivator had actually succeeded in forming a Nascent Soul at barely a hundred years old?

Among the Seven True Ones of Sushui, the other three were noncommittal.

"Using the method of demonic qi infusion to form a Nascent Soul is merely a lucky shortcut. The consequences will be endless."

"He took the spot of Junior Brother Qian, didn't he?"

"I only hope this child doesn't drag us down once we enter the demon cave."

Hearing these idle words, Yao Xi's face immediately darkened.

"Hongyan, Shenglin, Qianxiu, what do the three of you mean?"

Seeing that fellow disciples were about to quarrel, Dean He coughed heavily.

With his prestige, he immediately suppressed the brewing argument.

In the ensuing silence, Dean He said solemnly, "Have you forgotten the Sect Master's instructions? On this journey, we must unite and help each other, never allowing discord among brothers. Xuan Luo is a junior; we should be tolerant. Moreover, with his talent, given time he will surely become a pillar of the sect. Do you lack even this capacity to accommodate talent?"

The three who had spoken earlier all bowed their heads in acknowledgment.

"Thank you, Senior Brother, for the wake-up call!"

---

Luo Chen, who had left the Suwang Hall, knew nothing of the controversy sparked by his presence.

With Tai Su Lingjun there, he couldn't freely release his divine sense, which rivaled that of a peak Ascendant cultivator.

Peak Ascendant was still, after all, the Ascendant level.

A moment of carelessness and he would be detected by a Void Refining True Lord.

But he was well aware that his exceptional participation in the Demon-Suppressing Trial would inevitably cause a stir.

Whether it was the conflict of interest from taking someone else's spot, or the worry that his ostensibly low strength would hold others back—these were all to be expected.

Such problems couldn't be solved by attending a few sect gatherings and building connections.

That was why he had declined without a second thought.

Besides, he truly had matters to attend to within this month.

Not the "something stirring within" he had mentioned earlier, but alchemy-related matters.

As soon as he returned to the Mountain-Sea Travel Palace.

Luo Chen entered the pill chamber.

With a wave of his hand, a storage pouch floated in midair, and various medicinal herbs rained down like falling rain.

These were all collected over more than ten years by that fellow disciple named Lü Su, whom he had previously commissioned through Bu Danting.

After an inspection, Luo Chen nodded with satisfaction.

Though some auxiliary ingredients were missing, the main materials he needed had basically all been gathered.

It was worth the compensation he had paid.

As for the missing auxiliary ingredients, they weren't a problem for him. He only needed to contact the Spirit Medicine Court to easily collect them.

Luo Chen's gaze fell upon the dense array of various medicinal herbs.

With a wave of his hand, two streams of light flew over.

One resembled a teardrop, named Tanxing Tear—the main ingredient for refining Soul-Nourishing Pills.

The other looked like magma, flowing sluggishly, with a faint residual heat that burned the palm.

Luo Chen was very familiar with this item. It was Withered-Source Fire Marrow, a special mineral material born from the source land after a source-less fire departed.

He had obtained it twice back in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

This item was of great use to Luo Chen!

By refining it into Two Modes Pills, he had a high chance of completely awakening the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame!

If one pill wasn't enough, then he would make several!

He absolutely had to awaken the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame.

If earlier he had merely been grateful for the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame saving him multiple times, now that his cultivation was gradually recovering, he increasingly missed the convenience of having the divine flame by his side.

Whether for alchemy, artifact refining, cultivation, or combat.

Without the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame, everything was endlessly troublesome.

Moreover, to quickly remove the demon moss on his body, he had to use true fire to refine the Demon Marrow Diamonds.

Although he didn't care much about this small matter, having the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame's help would make it much more convenient.

Luo Chen pondered for a moment and made his decision.

That night, after inventorying all the medicinal herbs and drafting his plan, he went to the Spirit Medicine Court early the next morning.

After collecting a large number of backup auxiliary ingredients, Luo Chen began a month-long alchemy session.

In truth, with his alchemy skills, mere fourth-grade pills wouldn't require an entire month.

After about two or three days, he had successfully refined the Soul-Nourishing Pills.

And in considerable quantity!

Enough to repair Jiang Huang's soul damage.

After that came the Two Modes Pills.

---

That day.

Inside the Heavenly Fire Pool of the Sushui Sect.

Several figures flew in, then withdrew their light, propped up their mana shields, and carefully moved through the area.

From time to time, they bent down to pick some red, glossy fruits and place them in their baskets.

This place was one of the few fire-spirit lands within the Sushui Sect, with a spirit vein rank as high as fourth grade.

However, because the heaven and earth spiritual energy was too mixed and turbulent, it wasn't suitable for cultivators to reside in.

But the Sushui Sect hadn't abandoned this place. Under the joint efforts of the Spirit Medicine Court and the Formation Dao Court, it had gradually become a medicinal garden rich in fire-attribute herbs.

It had been several thousand years.

Over those millennia, the Red Venerable Fruits produced here had become renowned both inside and outside the sect.

This fruit was not only remarkably effective for fire-attribute cultivators in their cultivation but was also superb for alchemy and medicine!

The only problem was probably that Red Venerable Fruits were scarce and difficult to pick—at least Golden Core cultivators were required for the task.

The few figures within the Heavenly Fire Pool at this moment were precisely after the Red Venerable Fruits.

But judging from the expressions on their faces, their harvest seemed meager.

A Golden Core cultivator straightened his back and looked at the red fruit in his hand, which emitted faint fire power. His expression was somewhat dejected.

He couldn't help but look up at another cultivator not far away.

"Senior Brother Tang, how is it on your end?"

The middle-aged man called Senior Brother Tang turned around, his face full of frustration.

"Not great. I picked seven Red Venerable Fruits, but their quality is extremely poor. Strangely enough, though they meet the age requirement, their fire power is extremely weak, nowhere near the standard of five-hundred-year Red Venerable Fruits. Junior Brother Zhu, what about you?"

Junior Brother Zhu sighed, "Same for me. I imagine the other senior brothers are in a similar situation."

Senior Brother Tang stopped his work and said with confusion, "Strange. Ever since the Heavenly Fire Pool was developed into a fire-type spirit plant garden, it's been fine for thousands of years. How has it been producing less and less over the last hundred years? It even gives me the illusion that the fire energy here has been secretly drained."

Junior Brother Zhu hesitated, "If this continues, we probably won't be able to complete the tasks assigned by the Spirit Medicine Court. How about... we report this situation to the Spirit Medicine Court and have them investigate?"

Senior Brother Tang thought for a moment and nodded slightly.

"That's fine, but let's discuss it together with the other fellow brothers after they finish their work before reporting it."

Having made this decision, the two felt relieved.

They simply stopped picking fruits altogether. After all, the ones they had gathered didn't meet the standards. Even if they handed them over, the Spirit Medicine Court probably wouldn't be satisfied.

While they waited, Senior Brother Tang, who had a slightly higher cultivation, suddenly furrowed his brow.

Junior Brother Zhu, sharp-eyed, asked, "What's wrong?"

Senior Brother Tang looked around in confusion. Just as he was about to say nothing was wrong, his body suddenly swayed.

Losing his balance?

The thought had just emerged in his mind when it was immediately dismissed.

It turned out he hadn't lost his balance—it was an earthquake.

Looking around, the entire Heavenly Fire Pool, spanning dozens of li, was shaking.

The tremor started mild, then grew intense.

The several Golden Core cultivators had already stopped what they were doing and gathered together, utterly bewildered, not knowing what was happening.

Suddenly!

"Look, at the center of the Heavenly Fire Pool!"

Several gazes shot over simultaneously. When they saw what was there, their pupils contracted.

In that pool that resembled a boiling cauldron of oil, a towering tree had somehow grown!

The tree was cyan-white, with a trace of golden light swirling around it.

As its branches spread, golden flames bloomed and extinguished like fireworks, forming blossoming golden flowers.

The group of Golden Core cultivators stared dumbfounded at this scene.

After a long while, someone murmured, "A strange fire taking form—it should be at the fourth grade!"

Hearing this, someone instinctively revealed a greedy look.

But before they could act, an even more violent tremor came.

"Run!"

"The earth fire is about to erupt!"

"Quickly report to the Nascent Soul True Person to come and suppress the Heavenly Fire Pool. Don't let it destroy thousands of years of arrangements here!"

The Golden Core cultivators hurriedly mounted their escape light and fled outward.

Among them, the quick-witted ones had already taken out their transmission jade tokens to notify familiar Nascent Soul True Persons.

About half an incense stick later.

A figure appeared above the Heavenly Fire Pool.

It was the rather famous Pure Lotus True Person within the sect!

Looking down at the Heavenly Fire Pool, which was continuously erupting with billowing black smoke and flames, he frowned.

He then employed great means to begin channeling the erupting earth fire.

At the same time, he released his divine sense, desperately searching for the trace of the fourth-grade strange fire that the disciples had mentioned.

It wasn't that he was being overly diligent.

As an alchemist, he had only refined a third-grade source fire so far, which provided too little benefit to his alchemy.

If he could obtain a fourth-grade strange fire here, that would be perfect.

But the more he searched, the more alarmed he became.

The aura contained within the fire energy here was terrifying, far beyond what an ordinary fourth-grade strange fire could match.

In his opinion, even Dean He's Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame was inferior.

And Dean He's Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame was a solid fifth-grade divine flame!

In other words, the strange fire born here might be at the fifth-grade level, naturally containing a trace of law-domain true intent?

This shock turned into joy.

If he could obtain this fire, if it was compatible with him, he might even be able to glimpse the Ascendant realm!

But after he had completely suppressed the entire Heavenly Fire Pool, with no more black smoke in sight, he still hadn't found any trace of the strange fire.

"Where did it run? Has it already developed intelligence?"

The magma flowed sluggishly beneath his feet, and figures flickered outside the mountain.

Pure Lotus True Person's mood shifted from joy to disappointment, then to unwillingness. He shouted loudly:

"Search!"

"All of you, search for me!"

"Whoever finds traces of the fire tree will be heavily rewarded by me!"

Instantly, the crowd of cultivators who had gathered upon hearing the news began to move.

As for the several Nascent Soul True Persons, they approached Pure Lotus True Person to ask what had happened.

Pure Lotus True Person had no mind to explain, his face full of anxiety.

---

On Danhuang Mountain.

Inside the Mountain-Sea Travel Palace, Luo Chen sat cross-legged in the pill chamber, his eyes closed as if in meditation.

Suddenly!

From the earth fire mouth, branches spread out.

Following a close, intimate connection, they gradually extended to Luo Chen's body.

A sense of attachment, like father and son, arose spontaneously.

Luo Chen opened his eyes, looking at the blooming fire tree and golden blossoms, revealing a relieved expression.

"It took a full seven Two Modes Pills to feed you, but you've finally awakened."
