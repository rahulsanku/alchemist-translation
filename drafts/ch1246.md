# Chapter 1246: Yuantu Shows Its Might, Demon Lair Anomaly

Eight hundred li, crossed in an instant.

Amidst the surging wind, Luo Chen's figure appeared before a towering peak.

Looking up at the mountain that pierced the night sky, Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

Within the underground demon lair, there were mountains of such scale?

The Dark Nether Demon Lair was truly bizarre!

As he marveled, the creatures dwelling atop the mountain seemed to sense the uninvited guest.

In an instant, bursts of explosive sounds rang out.

Among them, one voice was especially piercing, as if it could tear through souls themselves.

"A demon spirit comparable to a late-stage Sage?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself, his expression shifting slightly.

He was naturally unafraid of such an existence.

But according to the introduction from the Sulling Sect's Ascendant elder, the Sages participating in the trial would initially enter through the Demon-Suppressing Tower. Although randomly teleported, they would basically all end up on the periphery of the Dark Nether Demon Lair.

In such places, let alone late-stage Sage demon spirits, even the weakest low-level demon spirits were extremely rare.

Now, things had completely exceeded expectations.

"Someone said earlier that this demon-suppressing trial was far more dangerous than before. Now, there are signs of it," Luo Chen mused.

As he pondered, the demon spirit on the mountain had already made its move.

In but a moment, a sky-covering mass of demonic qi gathered into clouds, rolling toward Luo Chen.

Luo Chen's eyes widened, clearly seeing the creatures within the demon clouds.

Some wore armor and carried weapons; others had horns growing from their heads.

Some had blood-red eyes; others were covered in blue all over.

Humanoid forms were everywhere, but even more were creatures resembling beasts and birds.

Truly a bizarre assortment, all different.

"Formed from demonic qi, possessing shallow intelligence but not true demons, acting purely on instinct—hence called demon spirits."

After observing for a moment, Luo Chen couldn't be bothered to investigate further.

With a flick of his hand, an ancient great sword suddenly appeared.

The moment it emerged, a long-dormant consciousness instantly awakened.

Before this consciousness could show itself to Luo Chen, it sensed the surrounding situation.

The familiar aura made it reveal a natural closeness and joy.

Luo Chen, sharing a mind with the Yuantu Sword, naturally detected this ecstasy.

Although the Yuantu Sword had been elevated to a spirit treasure, its sword spirit was different from traditional artifact spirits.

It was formed by the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining taking a demonic beast called *Tusu* from the demon realm and refining it into the sword, bound to it in life and death.

Now, this Dark Nether Demon Lair environment was likely not much different from the demon realm.

Little Tu had returned home like a wandering son.

He smiled slightly.

"Go!"

Without making any particular motion, he merely extended his hand, and the Yuantu Sword shot into the sky, diving straight toward that demon cloud.

One might call it a wandering son returning home, but the Tusu beast was born to devour its own kin—there was no warmth or tenderness.

Just like now!

The Yuantu Sword plunged into the demon cloud like a fish in water. Waves of demon spirits rushed forward, wrapping around it in a dense mass.

Then, countless blood-red sword lights scattered, cutting through the layered clouds, revealing the sky's light.

In just three to five breaths, the demon cloud that would make ordinary Sages flee in terror was already shredded into pieces.

"Screech!"

The earlier piercing voice sounded again.

From within the demon cloud, a pair of pitch-black wings, a full hundred zhang wide, spread open.

Bones stark white, demonic qi forming feathers.

A ferocious great bird revealed its form. Facing the overwhelmingly demonic Yuantu Sword, it did not retreat but instead showed fierce light in its eyes, spewing blazing black flames from its mouth.

Its wings beat, stirring up gusts of wind, and it screeched as it clawed at the Yuantu Sword.

In the void, the sound of metal clashing rang out repeatedly.

Luo Chen watched this scene calmly from afar.

Without his personal control, even with the power of a spirit treasure, the Yuantu Sword could not easily take down this demon spirit comparable to a late-stage Great Sage.

But he was not anxious.

This was merely because the sword spirit was still in a "playful" state.

However, he could not afford to prolong the battle.

Luo Chen recalled the earlier reaction of the lotus brand.

If this battle attracted other cultivators, it would be quite troublesome.

"Alright, Little Tu, stop playing. Finish this one quickly."

Receiving the order, the Yuantu Sword suddenly paused.

The next moment!

"Roar!"

A ferocious little beast emerged from the sword body, wrapping around the Yuantu Sword as it shot toward the great bird demon spirit.

The demon sword took form; the fierce spirit showed its might!

Just one contact.

*Chi!*

That great bird demon spirit was like a water bag pierced by a sharp awl, suddenly losing all its strength.

All the demonic qi throughout its body gushed out like water from a punctured bag.

This surging demonic qi did not go elsewhere—it was all absorbed by the Yuantu Sword.

And that was not all!

After absorbing the great bird demon spirit, the ferocious little beast was still unsatisfied. It opened its gaping maw and sucked hard at the nearby broken demon clouds.

*Whoosh!*

Like wind sweeping away clouds, there was no longer any sound in the area.

After doing all this, the long sword turned into a stream of light and returned to Luo Chen's hand.

Luo Chen looked down, somewhat surprised.

Previously, the Yuantu Sword had been bloodthirsty, and it was through this method that he had tamed the sword spirit, gradually nurturing it to the spirit treasure level.

Now, the Yuantu Sword was also interested in these demon spirits.

After devouring the demon spirit that had occupied this mountain peak, the Yuantu Sword had clearly increased in spirituality.

Luo Chen pondered for a moment, feeling the restlessness from the great sword, and finally nodded slightly.

"Don't worry. You'll have plenty to eat later!"

With that, Luo Chen leaped up and disappeared into the mountain peak.

Moments later, dust scattered from within.

Streams of light flew out, landing around the mountain peak. Under the cover of those streams of light, the demonic qi that had originally gathered here became much sparser.

An outsider looking on would never think this place could give birth to a demon nest, only seeing it as an ordinary spot in the Dark Nether Demon Lair.

Inside the mountain peak.

Luo Chen, having set up his formation, clapped his hands with satisfaction.

His years at the Sulling Sect had not been wasted.

He had devoted considerable effort to studying techniques and arts, not limited to spells and pill formulas. He had also put significant work into the dao of formations.

For instance, this set of *Earth-Trapping Spirit-Gathering Formation* was a top-tier formation from the formation academy, often used only when large sects established their mountain gates.

In Luo Chen's hands, let alone Sage cultivators, even if an Ascendant great power passed by, unless they specifically used their divine sense to carefully probe, it would be very difficult to detect anything amiss.

After doing all this, Luo Chen immediately sat cross-legged in the simply excavated cave dwelling.

Now, everything was prepared, and no powerful beings were nearby—perfect for him to devote himself fully to cultivation.

Feeling the dense demonic qi here, the *Chaotic Indestructible Scripture* slowly began to circulate.

The speed was not fast.

But with the enhancement of the Five-Spirit Dao Body, the absorption, transformation, and utilization of demonic qi reached an astonishing degree.

Massive amounts of demonic qi surged in like giant waves, pouring entirely into Luo Chen's body.

With this nameless mountain peak as the center, large quantities of demonic qi were drawn in.

In some places where demonic qi was already sparse, it was completely drained. The dark green Nether Demon Moss on the ground, deprived of demonic qi nourishment, gradually turned yellow and withered.

If Luo Chen knew that his unrestrained absorption of demonic qi would cause the Nether Demon Moss to wither, he would probably laugh and cry.

There was no need to use the demon-removing liquid to clear the demonic qi one by one.

As for the demonic contamination state, it had long since disappeared.

After all, that was merely his disguise. How could his physique be contaminated by demonic qi? On the contrary, the demonic qi was devoured by him.

And so, under this cultivation, Luo Chen's long-suppressed immortal dao realm began to skyrocket.

In just a few days, he broke through to mid-stage Sage.

And this trend showed no sign of stopping.

---

More than a thousand li from the nameless mountain peak where Luo Chen was in seclusion.

A figure frowned as they surveyed their surroundings.

"Demonic qi everywhere, demon spirits all around."

"The range my divine sense can release is less than fifty li. It almost feels like I've returned to my Initiate days."

"Affected by this, even my conventional combat power is greatly reduced. Even though I spent a year in Guangling County adapting to a similar environment, I can only exert about seventy to eighty percent of my full strength."

"The priority now is to find fellow sect members, so we can rely on each other!"

But...

Yao Xi Sage touched her brow. The lotus brand had lost its reaction.

Earlier, when she first arrived here, there had been a momentary reaction.

It indicated that a fellow Sulling Sect member was nearby.

But only for a moment.

Then there was no further reaction!

Yao Xi Sage understood what this meant.

"Already fallen?"

Yao Xi Sage bit her red lips, her expression very complicated.

Even though she knew the demon-suppressing trial was extremely dangerous, and many sects were reluctant to send their top geniuses, only dispatching ordinary Sages or elderly Sages with no hope of breakthrough who intended to make a final gamble, she had not expected one of her fellow sect members to fall in such a short time.

And most importantly, the random teleportation range was initially the peripheral areas, where there should not be too powerful demon spirits.

Falling under these circumstances indicated that the fellow member was very weak.

And a very weak fellow member...

"Youhuang, Xuanluo, Jianghuang..."

She dared not think about who it might be. Whoever it was, she would find it hard to accept.

The priority now was to rendezvous with the remaining fellow sect members!

Yao Xi Sage took out the map she had previously copied and began to study it carefully.

But after several examinations, it did not match this place at all.

"Did the elders not record it, or has the terrain of the Dark Nether Demon Lair undergone earth-shaking changes in the past thousand years?"

Unable to decide, Yao Xi Sage knew she could not stay here long.

She summoned her lifebound magic treasure, constantly maintaining her mana protective barrier, and turned into a stream of light flying toward the inner circle.

Along the way.

Whenever she encountered places with lush demon moss, she would take out her demon-gourd and pour demon-removing liquid down, then use her mana to drive it, uprooting the Nether Demon Moss in that area.

This was the main task for those participating in the demon-suppressing trial.

As for seeking opportunities and achieving breakthroughs through enlightenment, those were merely opportunities outside the trial.

But wherever demon moss flourished, demon spirits often dwelled.

Yao Xi Sage soon encountered demon spirits, and battle was inevitable!

---

Encounters like hers were common throughout the periphery of the Dark Nether Demon Lair.

In fact, due to the bizarre changes in the demon lair this time, many who had prepared in advance found their intelligence wrong and suffered heavy losses directly.

On a plain.

Several figures simultaneously withdrew their magic treasures, their faces ugly as they looked at the ground.

"Dead?"

"Divine sense can't detect anything. No aura at all. He should be dead."

"Unexpectedly, right after entering, we encountered the most troublesome formless demon spirits. And our cultivators' divine sense is suppressed by more than a hundred times, making it very difficult to detect them."

"Fortunately, Fellow Daoist from the Shadow Sect is skilled in this dao. He detected it quickly and responded appropriately. It's just a pity for Fellow Daoist Jin from the Divine Hand Valley."

As they spoke, they looked at another spot on the ground.

A person lay there, eyes wide open. Though his body was still warm, his aura was completely gone.

This was a sign of soul extinction!

Fellow Daoist Jin from the Divine Hand Valley was the first to be hit. He suddenly went mad for no reason, his mana wildly surging, with almost no warning.

The Sage from the Shadow Sect sensed something was wrong and joined forces with the others to surround the man. The moment the formless demon spirit fled, they struck together and killed it.

But Fellow Daoist Jin had already fallen.

He was an unadulterated late-stage Sage!

Outside, he would be the pillar of a sect, revered by thousands.

Now, in this Dark Nether Demon Lair, he had died without accomplishing anything.

The others felt a sense of mourning for their own kind.

Suddenly, a figure flew out, arriving above the corpse, and reached out.

The other's storage magic treasure fell into his hand.

Everyone was stunned.

"Fellow Daoist Tiansha, what are you doing?"

Tiansha of the Shadow Sect curled his lips. "What am I doing? I avenged him. Taking some compensation—I think he would approve from the underworld. What, do you want to return his belongings to the Divine Hand Valley?"

Naturally, no one had that intention, but their thoughts were even more naked.

"The Divine Hand Valley specializes in alchemy. Fellow Daoist Jin was busy picking the Obscure Bright Demon Flowers earlier, which was why he let his guard down and was hit."

"These flowers bloom three at a time. They have great miraculous effects for us Sages breaking through to Ascendant. How can you enjoy them alone?"

"That's right. You weren't the only one who avenged him!"

Tiansha laughed heartily. "There are five of us here. Three flowers can't be divided. Why not let me enjoy them alone!"

With that, his figure gradually dimmed.

Then he transformed into several shadows, fleeing in different directions, gradually becoming ethereal.

"It's the Shadow Escape Art of the Shadow Sect. Though exquisite, it's not fast."

"Chase him!"

With someone taking the lead, the others naturally would not fall behind.

Even if Tiansha was a Sage from the Shadow Sect, a Void Refining power, so what?

In the demon-suppressing trial, everyone was equal.

Besides, they each had their own Void Refining backing. Why should they fear the Shadow Sect!

In an instant, several figures scattered, beginning to chase and fight across the plain.

Such a scene, in the eyes of a nearby observer, seemed somewhat farcical.

Earlier, they had united against the demon spirit; now, they had turned to internal strife.

But this was the norm of the demon-suppressing trial.

As long as they completed the tasks of clearing demon moss and dispersing demon nests, the great powers of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect would turn a blind eye to the actions of the lower sect cultivators.

However, for this Dao Sect great power passing through, he could not turn a blind eye.

His expression was quite grave.

"What the True Lord said is indeed true!"

"The changes in the Dark Nether Demon Lair this time far exceed the last millennium. Not only has the area expanded several times, but the demon spirits born are also far beyond before."

"If it were only that, it would be tolerable. But the denser the demonic qi, the more stubborn the demon nests that are born."

"One can imagine—if the peripheral areas are like this, then the interior, and the central demon abyss that the True Lord warned about, will have deteriorated to what extent?"

"No!"

"I must notify the younger generation of our sect, have them focus on the demon moss clearing task, and also restrain the other sect disciples they encounter. We cannot continue this unrestrained infighting."

With a thought, he opened his mouth and gently exhaled.

A bubble floated out, swaying as his lips moved, as if carrying many messages.

With a point of his finger, the bubble shattered.

At the same time, the Dao Sect Sages scattered throughout the demon lair felt a stir in their hearts and took out a talisman.

On it, a bubble appeared, then shattered and vanished in an instant.

But the information within had already reached each person.

"We obey the elder's command!"
