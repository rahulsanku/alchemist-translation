# Chapter 1193: Four Major Powers, Epoch Monsters

Red Lotus Lord, Chonghai Tower Master, Snow Cliff Ancestor, Ice Fire Old Monster…

Luo Chen had not known these people before, but now he matched names to faces one by one.

Under his perception, none among them were weak.

At the very least, they all possessed the mid-stage Martial Human Immortal realm.

Those as powerful as Red Lotus Lord were in no way inferior to Yuan Qingcang beside him, being genuine late-stage Martial Human Immortals.

With such a force united, even Corpse dared not underestimate them.

His expression was extremely ugly as he said hoarsely, "This junior spouts nonsense, but do you actually believe him?"

However, Red Lotus Lord immediately retorted, "The message left by my clan's Red Leaf Elder is enough to corroborate Luo Chen's words! After we left, you slaughtered our tribes and transported people to your Corpse Remains Immortal Domain."

Chonghai Tower Master, dressed in blue robes, was even more furious.

"Corpse, we respected you as a senior and never slighted you all these years. Why did you do something so destructive, digging up our very foundations?"

Snow Cliff Ancestor from Snow Cliff Gorge said nothing, but Ice Fire Old Monster sitting beside him could not help but speak coldly, "Corpse, you need to give us an explanation!"

Faced with everyone's pressure, Corpse could not utter a single word.

If it were only these four, it might be manageable, but they were not alone.

These tribes had multiplied in the Nether Ruins for a long time and had connections with many other forces.

It was a case of pulling one hair and affecting the whole body.

But he was different. For nearly ten thousand years, he had always acted alone, without any external support.

Especially with that fellow watching him covetously from the side.

Corpse coldly looked at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen, who had been cheerfully watching the show, met his gaze without fear, looking as if he had nothing to worry about.

This fellow had withstood his full-force strike left outside; he was certainly no easy opponent.

If it really came to blows…

Just as Corpse fell silent, someone suddenly spoke with a sneer.

"I was wondering earlier why Corpse, who knows the Ancient God Dao Field best, was the last to enter. So you were making these little moves outside!"

It was Tianxuan Liesu.

He smoothed his Purple Star Robe and asked with a half-smile, "Leaving a clone, transporting other tribal populations—I'm very curious, Corpse, what exactly are you planning outside?"

As he said this, he was not staring at Corpse but instead looking at Luo Chen.

Luo Chen's spirits lifted, and he was about to speak when Carefree Realm Master called out to him.

"Luo Chen!"

"Hmm?" Luo Chen was puzzled and looked over.

Carefree Realm Master sighed, his gentle gaze sweeping over everyone.

"Everyone… why must it come to this?"

Red Lotus Lord said solemnly, "Carefree Realm Master, do you intend to interfere in this matter?"

Carefree Realm Master waved his hand. "I don't want to take any side, but I am merely speaking the truth."

He paused, then said seriously, "At this moment, all of you have gathered here, which already means abandoning everything in the Nether Ruins. Whether it be power, wealth, or bloodline clansmen, you have staked everything. Why let what has already been abandoned affect the plans ahead?"

These words were harsh!

But they were the truth.

Since they had already abandoned it, why speak of grievances? It was merely a matter of saving face.

For the sake of face, would they affect the unity they needed now?

Red Lotus Lord and the others' expressions shifted, caught in a dilemma. But those Martial Human Immortals who were originally on good terms with them might not be so steadfast.

Carefree Realm Master spoke again, his gaze settling on the frowning Luo Chen.

"Moreover, Fellow Daoist Luo Chen destroyed Corpse's clone left outside and ruined his backup plan. That can be considered avenging you all. Why not let it go at that?"

The others also looked at Luo Chen.

Facing everyone's gaze, Luo Chen relaxed his brow and said with a light smile, "I have no objection. After all, I didn't suffer any loss; in fact, I even gained some small harvests. As long as Old Demon Corpse doesn't hold a grudge, I can sit down and have a good talk."

No one expected that the first and only person to speak for Corpse would be Carefree Realm Master, who had a better relationship with Luo Chen.

But upon careful thought, at this time, in this place, under these circumstances, only Carefree Realm Master had that qualification.

And Luo Chen, who had just arrived, indeed gave Carefree Realm Master this face.

Carefree Realm Master nodded gratefully at Luo Chen, then looked at Corpse.

"Fellow Daoist Corpse, I assume you don't want to incur the wrath of the masses, do you?"

Faced with Carefree Realm Master's mediation, Corpse's expression had already relaxed considerably.

But the words "small harvests" from Luo Chen's mouth felt extremely grating to the ears.

That bone must have fallen into Luo Chen's hands!

Now the others' killing intent toward him had weakened considerably. Carefree Realm Master seemed to be on his side as well. If he forcefully…

"In my opinion, Fellow Daoist Luo Chen's strength is not bad; he is enough to replace Corpse."

Corpse glared viciously at the speaker—it was Tianxuan Liesu.

The other party did not care about Corpse's gaze at all and said without restraint, "You have too many moves. You are far more familiar with the Ancient God Dao Field than we are. If we really go in, I'm worried you might cause trouble."

Corpse's expression turned cold again.

What Tianxuan Liesu had pointed out was the most critical point!

To open the gate of the Ancient God Dao Field, at least four Destruction Realm martial artists were needed as leaders.

This was also why he had previously dared to veto Carefree Realm Master's proposal to bring Luo Chen into the Ancient God Dao Field.

But Luo Chen's strength had been recognized by Carefree Realm Master and Tianxuan Liesu.

Thus, he could replace Corpse.

And now, having incurred the wrath of the masses while being alone, if he insisted on persisting…

Taking a deep breath, Corpse said coldly, "Past grievances are wiped clean."

Hearing this, Carefree Realm Master was relieved.

He laughed heartily. "Fellow Daoist Corpse, excellent!"

Tianxuan Liesu snorted and did not continue his sarcasm.

The rarely speaking Tianku Old Monster let out a strange laugh, his voice vibrating.

"This is perfect! Old Demon Corpse has lost his backup plan, so he will have to give his all. Everyone has no more attachments and can stake everything. And with this Fellow Daoist Luo Chen, whose strength is not inferior to ours, our chances of success have greatly increased!"

Luo Chen stood outside the square, watching this scene with a smile.

Clearly, what Tianku Old Monster said was exactly the situation Carefree Realm Master wanted most.

That was why he had mediated so vigorously just now, to avoid a fight.

Luo Chen had no objection. As he had said earlier, he had not lost anything.

Yuan Qingcang, standing behind him, had witnessed this entire verbal exchange, his heart churning.

Whether it was Luo Chen's alliances and provocations, stirring up everyone's anger toward Corpse, or Carefree Realm Master's mediation, or Tianxuan Liesu's occasional sarcastic remarks—none of it was simple swordplay.

Yet the conflict within far exceeded that of blades and swords.

Compared to them, he, who had just emerged from the Sky Tower Forest, was as naive as a blank sheet of paper.

So there was a power beyond pure strength—the power of words!

But if Luo Chen knew his thoughts, he would probably scoff.

Whether words or schemes, they must be built on absolute power.

If he had not effortlessly blocked Corpse Old Demon's strike, how could he stand here speaking so freely?

With a single step, Luo Chen crossed space and appeared in the square.

"This seat deserves a place here!"

…

As someone already accepted to be on the same level as Corpse Old Demon, Luo Chen surprisingly did not set up his own separate corner.

He merely sat down behind Carefree Realm Master on the left.

Beside him, aside from Yuan Qingcang, was another person he had met two hundred years ago—Mu Bugui.

Now seated, Mu Bugui was introducing the general situation of the people in the square.

They were divided into four major powers!

Tianxuan Liesu was the strongest!

Under his command were more than thirty Martial Human Immortals, all from the Myriad Manifestations Star Sect, obeying his orders alone.

In terms of cultivating talent, the established sect system indeed had its merits.

Next was Carefree Realm Master.

From the Great Destruction Realm, even including Luo Chen, there were only three Martial Human Immortals.

But many tribal leaders had excellent relationships with Carefree Realm Master and had come at his invitation, nominally following his command.

Such as Red Lotus Lord and the others, the silent Bei Feng Old Man, and some others.

The total number was not large, only a dozen or so, but as clan leaders, they almost all had trump cards up their sleeves, and no one dared underestimate them.

This was evident from how Red Lotus Lord and the others had dared to threaten Corpse Old Demon together earlier.

Next was Tianku Old Monster.

He had brought twenty Martial Human Immortals!

All from the Tianfeng Tribe, their number even exceeding Carefree Realm Master's side.

Each one, like Tianku Old Monster, wore gray robes with unique totem patterns on their faces.

It was said that their martial arts system was vastly different from other places.

Their cultivation speed was extremely fast, but their combat power was relatively average.

Thus, despite their numbers, they were not as strong as Carefree Realm Master's side.

After learning this, Luo Chen could not help but sigh at the harshness of the cultivation environment in the Great Destruction Realm.

Even including Fang Qingxue and Gu Xun, who remained in the realm, there were only five Martial Human Immortals in total.

The gap with the reputedly equal Star Sea and Tianfeng Tribe was enormous.

But he could understand. After all, cultivating with chaotic energy was truly inconceivable.

Ordinary martial artists cultivated cautiously and slowly, not to mention the constant risk of death.

Over so many years, producing Luo Chen and the other four was already fortunate.

"And the last power?"

Luo Chen looked at the group sitting behind Corpse.

Almost every one of them was extremely old, with a thick aura of death.

And each exuded a sufficiently powerful presence, in no way inferior to Yuan Qingcang, Red Lotus Lord, and the others.

"Them…"

Mu Bugui sighed, then explained via voice transmission, "Strictly speaking, these people do not belong to any power. They simply do not follow the commands of the four Destruction Realm kings. They have gathered here only to find a way out."

Luo Chen frowned and asked, "Then their identity?"

Mu Bugui explained word by word, "Epoch Monsters!"

"Oh? Quite a grand title!" Luo Chen became interested, his gaze burning as he swept over that group, as if trying to see something.

In response to his gaze, some closed their eyes and ignored him, while others stared back coldly.

In short, he did not see any fear of him.

This was quite absurd.

After all, Luo Chen had just displayed extremely strong combat power, and even Carefree Realm Master and Tianxuan Liesu had acknowledged his strength.

Mu Bugui transmitted, "Each of these people is quite old, even older than us. Some of them are even older than Carefree Realm Master and the others, having lived for who knows how many thousands or tens of thousands of years. The reason we don't know their names is that their active periods were not recent. They all became famous long ago, breaking through to Martial Human Immortal early on. But they were all trapped in the Nether Ruins, unable to leave. To find a way out and to survive, each used secret techniques to seal themselves. Only when the epoch turns do they actively break the seal, seeking that one and only opportunity."

"There were actually many such people, but in the end, only this number survived."

"Because they were sealed for too long, the tribal forces they originally belonged to have long since vanished. Now they are left as solitary individuals. So they chose to sit behind Corpse, because strictly speaking, Corpse is the same as them. The only difference is that Corpse occasionally appears in the world. But these monsters must never be underestimated. Each one possesses divine abilities and killing moves that once dominated the Nether Ruins. Even the four Destruction Realm kings do not easily provoke them."

After listening to Mu Bugui's introduction, Luo Chen nodded solemnly.

No wonder he sensed such a thick aura of death from these people.

Sealing themselves with secret arts, enduring their lifespan limits, breaking the seal when the epoch turns, seeking a single thread of life.

Such existences, called Epoch Monsters, were not an exaggeration.

But Luo Chen still had one doubt.

"Among these people, aside from Corpse, why is there not a single Destruction Realm?"

Mu Bugui was momentarily speechless, not knowing how to answer.

It was Carefree Realm Master ahead who gave the answer.

"Although these Epoch Monsters have survived, they are merely lingering on. Even if they escape, they cannot escape their lifespan limits. Unless they have other fortuitous encounters, they will ultimately die. As for those as strong as Destruction Realm kings, every one of them was the strongest of their era. How could they choose to merely survive? Confident as they are, they long ago sought their own way out. As for whether they succeeded, that is another matter."

Luo Chen suddenly understood.

Thinking carefully, it was indeed the case.

Not to mention others, take himself.

Although he had not yet reached the Destruction Realm, his strength was about the same. Deep down, had he not entertained the thought of leaving through the Destruction Gate?

Carefree Realm Master continued.

"Such an existence once appeared in our Great Destruction Realm."

"Who?"

"Xiao Lan."

Luo Chen was startled and blurted out, "The Immortal Remnant Xiao Lan?"

Carefree Realm Master sighed emotionally, "It was him. Originally at the Void Refining realm, after drifting to the Nether Ruins, he cultivated to the Destruction Realm in a thousand years. Truly a heaven-defying talent. After leaving behind the Immortal Remnant City lineage, he left the Great Destruction Realm to seek a way out, and ultimately disappeared without a trace."

Luo Chen recalled the texts he had seen in the Xiao family library of Immortal Remnant City, which indeed recorded the deeds of Immortal Remnant Xiao Lan leaving the city.

So he, like them, had been painstakingly searching for a way out.

Only earlier.

Just then, a dignified gaze swept over.

Luo Chen looked and saw Tianxuan Liesu.

He nodded slightly, then looked at the others.

"Everyone, you have rested enough. Let us continue!" (Chapter End)
