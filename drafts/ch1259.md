# Chapter 1259: Devil Seed Transformation, Someone from Nether Capital

"Let's go!"

"Go where?"

"The Alchemy Saint Hall. Didn't Yu Jiajia just say there were some deficiencies in the pill department? I'll go check on the situation."

Chu Yan said casually, but she didn't notice the subtle change in Luo Chen's expression.

The two left Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak and headed toward the Alchemy Saint Hall.

Along the way, they occasionally encountered some Dao Sect cultivators, all of whom appeared busy.

It seemed that the so-called celebration for the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign's emergence from seclusion was indeed already in preparation.

When these Dao Sect disciples encountered Chu Yan, they all stopped to respectfully greet her, occasionally casting curious glances at Luo Chen following behind her. Shock appeared on each of their faces.

Everyone knew that the Alchemy Saint was aloof and proud within the sect, never sparing a kind word.

Let alone allowing a man to stay by her side—she barely spoke a few words in ordinary interactions.

Who was this person, to have such good fortune?

Chu Yan's status within the Dao Sect was somewhat special. Although many knew the inside story, it in no way diminished the reverence for her position.

This was evident from the fact that the Alchemy Saint Hall was established in the core area of the Dao Sect.

As they passed a majestic and imposing mountain peak, Chu Yan casually remarked, "This is Yao Mountain, where that one is in seclusion. When you have your audience, it should also be here."

Luo Chen nodded silently.

Yao Mountain—the Dao field of the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign!

It could also be said to be the birthplace of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

The density of spiritual qi here was such that even with the sealing of formations and restrictions, one could still sense a trace of it from outside.

After passing Yao Mountain, they arrived at the Alchemy Saint Hall.

The distance between the two was extremely close, no more than a few li.

As soon as Chu Yan entered the hall, an elderly Ascendant cultivator actively came forward to greet her.

"How is the situation?"

"We're a bit short-handed. Everyone is in the main hall tending to that furnace of medicine, and we can't spare anyone to refine the pills needed for the celebration. That's why we had to disturb you."

"Mm, I understand. You may withdraw!"

Chu Yan waved her hand and led Luo Chen toward the main hall.

The Ascendant cultivator looked at Luo Chen with curiosity.

Chu Yan stopped and explained, "Xuan Luo, a pill dao prodigy from the Sushu Sect. I'm showing him around."

"Oh, so he's the outstanding junior that old Gu Mu has been constantly talking about!"

The old man suddenly understood and looked at Luo Chen with considerable interest. He seemed to want to know what exceptional qualities this young man possessed—besides his extremely handsome appearance—that made Gu Mu value him so highly, to the point that even Chu Yan was willing to keep him by her side.

After explaining, Chu Yan said no more.

Luo Chen, however, was somewhat puzzled.

As if seeing through his confusion, Chu Yan proactively transmitted her voice: "This man is Du Bailing, a long-established alchemist of the Dao Sect. He handles the trivial daily affairs of the Alchemy Saint Hall for me. Most importantly, he is a direct descendant of the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign."

Luo Chen suddenly understood. No wonder Chu Yan treated this man with such solemnity.

He was indeed different from others.

Entering the main hall, Luo Chen immediately felt a wave of scorching heat rushing toward him.

His spirits lifted as he looked toward the center of the great hall.

There stood a massive pill furnace in the center, with chains extending from surrounding dragon-coiled pillars to lock it in place.

In front of the furnace were two statues—one dragon, one phoenix—each spouting wisps of golden and black flames, continuously burning the enormous furnace.

Not only that, but in every corner of the great hall sat figures, occasionally channeling their mana to strike the furnace.

Every single one of these cultivators possessed formidable auras.

The weakest were at the Sage realm, and the stronger ones even surpassed Elder Gu Mu.

Most importantly, all of them were alchemists!

"Such a setup—what kind of pill are they refining?"

Luo Chen was deeply shocked. Even when he refined some fifth-grade pills, he had never seen such a luxurious lineup.

This time, Chu Yan did not explain. She simply stood there, as if making time for Luo Chen to observe.

Because of her actions, she frequently drew the attention of the alchemists inside the hall.

After a moment, Chu Yan beckoned and led Luo Chen out of the main hall to a side hall.

"Did you notice anything?" Chu Yan asked with interest.

Luo Chen hesitated, then slowly said, "The coiled dragon pillars lock the furnace, drawing upon earthly qi. The furnace manifests sun and moon, gathering their essence. Those two flames, one gold and one black, are clearly high-grade divine fires of opposing natures. I suspect the pill being refined inside is one with opposing properties. Most importantly, the entire main hall has been set with an extremely powerful formation aimed at nurturing. It seems the pill inside has taken shape, but there is still much work before it truly becomes a pill."

Chu Yan nodded with satisfaction. "Not bad. In such a short time, you've discerned so much. It shows that although your learning is broad, you are very proficient in all aspects. As for the pill inside the furnace..."

Chu Yan transmitted her voice softly, "It is the Sacred Demon Origin Liquid, refined using a drop of Nether River Divine Milk as the main ingredient!"

She stopped there. As for what effects the so-called Sacred Demon Origin Liquid had, what realm of cultivators it was for, or who it was being refined for, Chu Yan did not elaborate.

But even so, Luo Yan could hardly conceal his shock.

He had heard of the name Nether River Divine Milk before.

Not from the scriptures of the Xuan Realm, but from a volume of alchemy texts inherited from the Alchemy Saint in the Demon-Fall Land back in the day.

Legend had it that in the Demon Realm, there was a river that had flowed for billions of years, called the Nether River, bearing the great name of "Source of Ten Thousand Demons"!

The Nether River Divine Milk was a special product derived from it.

Its effects were incredibly heaven-defying. Aside from marrow-cleansing, tendon-reforming, and spirit-root washing, it could also shed the mundane qi from one's primordial spirit.

What mundane qi was, Luo Chen did not know.

But he knew that if a Body Integration cultivator consumed large amounts of this substance, they had a high probability of breaking through to the Great Vehicle stage.

He even suspected that the Spirit-Molding Sacred Spring arranged in the Demon-Fall Land back then was highly likely a spiritual spring diluted by the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining from Nether River Divine Milk.

Now, Chu Yan was using this as the main ingredient to refine a pill, mobilizing such vast human and material resources. The pill being refined must be heaven-defying.

This was evident from the fact that the pill had not yet truly emerged from the furnace.

It seemed that even with Chu Yan's abilities, she could not directly refine this substance, which was why she sought external nurturing methods to try and complete the pill.

Chu Yan had been observing Luo Chen. From the fleeting shock on his face, she knew he might understand some relevant content.

But she did not guide or explain too much.

She merely took out her most commonly used alchemy tool.

A snow-white jade pot.

Its name was the Sun-Moon Pot, said to encompass both sun and moon.

This was absolutely incomparable to the Pot Containing Sun and Moon, the former sect-suppressing treasure of the Medicine King Sect. The laws swirling around it made even Luo Chen's heart tremble.

This must be her lifebound treasure, nurtured with her own origin mana!

"Although I can independently refine the Devil Seed Transformation Pill, time is a bit tight. You'll assist me! Once we finish refining it early, we can still find time to continue discussing pill dao." Chu Yan said this.

Although Luo Chen's pill dao attainment was not yet as profound as hers, during their earlier discussions, Luo Chen would occasionally propose theories and ideas that refreshed her perspective.

These were completely different from the alchemy systems of the Mountains and Seas Realm and the Xuan Realm.

They had the style of the Demon Realm, as well as a unique charm unlike any other pill dao system in the world.

Especially regarding pills related to the physical body, even Chu Yan found them quite astonishing after hearing them.

What she did not know was that these were theories Luo Chen had occasionally researched during his long confinement in the Return Ruins, exploring ways to stimulate human potential.

Thus, she enjoyed—even cherished—discussing pill dao with Luo Chen.

Luo Chen nodded, took a pill formula from her hand, and quickly read through it.

He was fine until he looked, but then he froze.

"Devil Seed Transformation Pill?"

"Yes. This pill is a fifth-grade divine pill intended to be bestowed upon Ascendant cultivators who perform outstandingly in the Demon-Suppressing Trial during this celebration." Chu Yan answered casually, her gaze falling on Luo Chen. Suddenly, she thought of something: "Although you've hidden it well, you must already be at the Ascendant stage, right? Those pills rewarded to Sage cultivators are useless to you. How about I secretly give you one when the time comes!"

Luo Chen opened his mouth but ultimately couldn't say anything to refuse.

Because if this pill truly had the effects described on the formula, he couldn't muster any intention to refuse.

Devil Seed Transformation Pill—planting a devil seed in the Dao heart, deriving a Law Manifestation!

This was a high-grade divine pill that could help Ascendant cultivators derive their Law Manifestation ahead of time.

The pill dao theories involved were extremely profound. Upon first glance, Luo Chen couldn't make sense of them.

And the sheer number of medicinal ingredients covered made Luo Chen's head spin.

He had no idea where Chu Yan had obtained this pill formula.

"Study it for two days. I'll prepare in advance. When we truly begin, just follow my instructions."

"Alright!"

Luo Chen agreed without hesitation.

And some of the hesitation in his heart dissipated at this moment.

For Chu Yan to bring him along to refine pills at a time like this was clearly an act of promotion. And the Devil Seed Transformation Pill was undoubtedly extremely precious to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, yet she casually said she would secretly give him one.

Added to everything before, what was there for him, Luo Chen, to hesitate about?

Even if handing over the [Spirit-Stabilizing Pill] formula might bring him great trouble, as long as he left the Youxuan Continent, those troubles wouldn't find him.

Yes!

The method Luo Chen had to resolve primordial spirit collapse was the sixth-grade devil lord pill formula he had recently obtained—the Spirit-Stabilizing Pill!

This substance did not prevent primordial spirit collapse. Rather, at the moment of collapse, it could stabilize the primordial spirit, forcibly gathering it back to wholeness.

If Chu Yan had this pill by her side, even if the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign caused her primordial spirit to collapse with a single thought, she could still preserve a thread of life.

...

Two days passed in the blink of an eye.

Luo Chen thoroughly read the pill formula. Although he couldn't claim to have memorized it by heart, he had committed all the important nodes in the refining process to memory.

And Chu Yan already had some experience refining this pill. Once the preparations were complete, she directly began.

Luo Chen assisted on the side, occasionally processing medicinal ingredients on the spot, sometimes activating his true flame to maintain the Sun-Moon Pot's temperature.

Even though she had witnessed Luo Chen's alchemy techniques before, Chu Yan couldn't help but inwardly praise the solid foundation of his pill arts. Even when hastily taking on a new pill formula, he could remain calm amidst the chaos, methodical and orderly.

This was far more relaxing than cooperating with those Ascendant alchemy masters.

In her relaxed state, Chu Yan also casually chatted about this pill.

"This pill formula was derived from my understanding of the Sacred Demon Origin Liquid formula. At the time, I researched three relatively lower-grade formulas. One of them was called the Great Yin Pill, which was the pill that helped your sect's founding ancestor, Grandmaster Tai Su, break through to the Void Refining stage. However, that pill is very dependent on the individual cultivator's constitution, so it doesn't have universal applicability."

"The Devil Seed Transformation Pill is also one of them. This pill is a shortcut for Ascendant cultivators to cultivate their Law Manifestation."

"It has both advantages and disadvantages. The benefit is that one can derive their Law Manifestation ahead of time. The drawback is that this pill easily breeds inner demons in the cultivator, so only those with extremely powerful soul foundations can take it."

"To be honest, I don't know why the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign wants to bestow this pill upon Ascendant cultivators who perform outstandingly in the Demon-Suppressing Trial."

"In my opinion, even Dean He of your Sushu Sect's Pill Academy doesn't have a soul foundation that meets the requirements."

"As for you, although you've hidden it well, your soul foundation must be extremely powerful. Perhaps you are more suited for this pill."

Luo Chen listened silently, his hands never stopping.

About ten days later.

A strange pill fragrance wafted through this side hall.

If it weren't for the two of them having deep cultivation bases and profound realms, ordinary Sage cultivators—or even Ascendant cultivators—would have their minds shaken just by smelling this pill fragrance.

Luo Chen looked at the pills on the wooden tray.

They were the size of wax pills, pure white all over, but deep within the white, there was a profound black dot.

White within black, the Dao heart giving birth to a devil!

This was the Devil Seed Transformation Pill!

Chu Yan waved her hand. Two of them flew toward her, and the remaining one was placed before Luo Chen.

"That one is yours. Keep it well and don't let others see it. Otherwise, even I will get into some trouble."

Luo Chen nodded. He was also well aware of the value of this pill.

Just from the rare medicinal ingredients needed during the refining process, he knew that refining these three pills had cost even the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect a considerable price.

But this also showed the immense wealth of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

If they could gather so many rare ingredients, then with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's immense strength, they should also be able to gather the complex materials needed for the Spirit-Stabilizing Pill!

Moreover, the exceptional alchemy skills Chu Yan had displayed during the previous refining process were sufficient to refine the Spirit-Stabilizing Pill.

With this thought, Luo Chen hesitated no longer. With a flick of his wrist, a page of a book appeared before Chu Yan.

"This is..."

Chu Yan was startled as she took the page.

Looking at the handwriting on it, she murmured to herself.

"Stabilizing and gathering all thoughts, the spirit soul never ends..."

Suddenly, she raised her head, her burning gaze falling upon Luo Chen.

Luo Chen did not flinch or avoid it. He simply nodded gently.

...

A month later, at the Demon-Suppressing Tower.

Four figures stood respectfully outside the tower, as if waiting for something.

After a moment, a streak of light appeared in the sky.

Seeing that only one person had arrived, the four figures were somewhat surprised. But when they saw his true appearance, they all bowed respectfully.

"Azure Dragon of the Heavenly Origin Four Generals, pays respects to Great Sovereign Xixiang!"

"White Tiger..."

"Vermilion Bird..."

"Black Tortoise..."

Lin Xixiang waved his hand casually and said, "I've already heard the detailed situation from someone on the way here. You've worked hard. From now on, this place will be temporarily taken over by my Nether Capital Source Court. You may leave."

Vermilion Bird hesitated and said, "Just Great Sovereign alone?"

Lin Xixiang smiled and asked in return, "The four of you could guard this place. Could it be that I alone cannot?"

Vermilion Bird quickly shook his head, "We dare not presume!"

"Alright, go on. I hear your sect is holding a celebration. Give my regards to the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign as well. Come to think of it, it's been nearly a thousand years since we last met. Tell him to find time to come have a drink with me."

"Yes!"

The Heavenly Origin Four Generals lingered no longer and all left the Demon-Suppressing Tower.

Beneath the golden, radiant high tower, Lin Xixiang watched the four departing figures, his eyes narrowing slightly.

That drink... I'm afraid it won't happen.
