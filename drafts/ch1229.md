# Chapter 1229: Demon Statue, Ink Jade Space

*Boom!*

A rainbow-like sword beam slashed down from above.

The great river split open instantly, waves standing apart like walls on either side.

Such was the terror of a single sword strike.

Yet the sword light hung precisely above the ancient Daoist temple, not damaging its body in the slightest.

This level of exquisite control demonstrated the profound strength of the one who had struck.

Everyone turned to look.

Especially the female Adept who had been traveling with Liu Muhan. When she saw the gleaming golden sword in Luo Chen's hand, she couldn't help but cry out in surprise.

"Liuhong Sword!"

Luo Chen twirled the sword in a flourish and asked with a smile, "Senior Sister, do you recognize this sword?"

She waved her hand. "No need to call me senior sister—my cultivation level is actually lower than yours. Just call me by my name, Lu Zhenqiu."

Liu Muhan took the opportunity to introduce her. "Junior Sister Lu is from Solitary Bamboo Peak, a true disciple under my master's friend, Sage Youhuang. She has also joined the Enforcement Court. Although our team is small for this mission, it's actually a collaboration between the Spirit Medicine Court, the Enforcement Court, and the External Affairs Hall."

Luo Chen nodded in understanding.

When a sect's remote industrial property experienced a decline in medicinal herb production—and one that had persisted for two hundred years at that—even if the amount involved wasn't significant to Suli Sect itself, the personnel issues involved couldn't be judged by the scale of the amount alone.

The Spirit Medicine Court issued the task, and the External Affairs Hall recruited people to complete it—that was only natural.

The Enforcement Court's involvement was more to see if there was any behind-the-scenes manipulation, embezzlement, or fraud.

After introducing Lu Zhenqiu, Liu Muhan was also very curious.

"This sword of Xuan Luo's—I'm seeing it for the first time too. Junior Sister Lu, how do you recognize it?"

Lu Zhenqiu looked enviously at the golden longsword in Luo Chen's hand. "This sword was personally forged by the Dean of the Hundred Ingenuity Court. It was made from the sharpness of five metals as its foundation, supplemented by hundreds of mineral materials, and infused with a trace of precious Lianxiao Qi. When the sword embryo was completed, it already possessed the quality of a top-grade dharma treasure. Many said that if this sword found a worthy master, nurturing it into a true artifact in the future would be a natural progression. At the time, many senior brothers and sisters in the Enforcement Court wanted this sword, but the Dean of the Hundred Ingenuity Court couldn't bear to part with it. I never expected that the sword would end up in your hands, Xuan Luo, and that you've already nurtured it into a lifebound dharma treasure."

Luo Chen held the sword level and swept his gaze across it.

He hadn't expected this sword to have such a reputation.

Back at the Pill Tasting Assembly, Dean He had goaded the hall and court deans into offering rewards.

Some of them had been rather "stingy"—like the Dean of the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall, who had only given some spirit stones.

Later, during Luo Chen's Adept Establishment Ceremony, the Dean of the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall had sent a large amount of manpower and resources to help him hold the ceremony perfectly, which could be considered compensation.

At the time, some people had also thought the reward from the Dean of the Hundred Ingenuity Court was stingy.

He had only given a dharma treasure embryo.

But no one expected that the sword embryo he gave had such a reputation within the sect.

If nurtured well, it could even become a true artifact in the future.

Of course, Luo Chen also knew this sword was extraordinary—otherwise, he wouldn't have spent time nurturing and refining it.

What he valued most was what Lu Zhenqiu had mentioned: the trace of Lianxiao Qi fused within the Liuhong Sword.

Lianxiao Qi and the Chixiao Qi that Luo Chen had previously cultivated both belonged to the Nine Firmament Qi!

Though different in nature, they shared the same origin.

After refining this sword, executing the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* came as naturally as a fish in water.

The rainbow-like sword beam from earlier was clear proof!

Luo Chen said with a smile, "This sword was gifted to me by the Dean of the Hundred Ingenuity Court when I was at the Initiate stage. I didn't expect it to be such a precious item."

Lu Zhenqiu cast another envious glance, then asked curiously, "Speaking of which, I wonder why Xuan Luo has come here?"

Luo Chen turned his gaze toward the ancient Daoist temple below.

"I accepted a task to find a certain spiritual plant. I didn't expect to run into you all, and it seems we share the same goal."

The others also realized what was happening.

No wonder they had encountered him tonight.

Liu Muhan waved her hand and took the lead. "Let's go down and take a look!"

The group of seven left two Initiate cultivators outside to keep watch, while the remaining four didn't hesitate and flew down together.

As their figures disappeared, the river waves that had lost their magical power support crashed down, and the river surface leveled once more.

...

The Daoist temple had no name, its doors tightly shut.

There were no formations protecting the surroundings, only the surging river water serving as cover.

But upon landing here, one could sense its extraordinary nature.

While Liu Muhan was surveying the surroundings, she saw Luo Chen bend down and reach out to collect some moss growing on the ground.

"Xuan Luo, is this your mission target?"

"Mm, it should be."

Luo Chen examined the moss in his hand. It was pitch black, with eye-shaped patterns at its center.

It was precisely the Phoenix Eye Obsidian Moss that the Sage from the Spirit Medicine Court had specifically requested!

Looking around, a large patch of it grew here.

It seemed that the rogue cultivator named Huang Wei hadn't deceived him—this place indeed produced Phoenix Eye Obsidian Moss in abundance.

But upon closer inspection, Luo Chen couldn't help but frown slightly.

This spiritual plant looked exactly as recorded in the classics, yet it gave off a rather sinister feeling.

With a thought, he gently pressed a finger on the moss.

It was the Falling Petal Hand from the basic alchemy arts of the Xuan Realm!

It could instantly maximize the medicinal properties and spiritual energy of the herbs.

The next moment, a chilling scene appeared.

A wisp of black qi emerged abruptly.

Luo Chen's gaze sharpened—he had already recognized what this black qi was.

Beside him, Liu Muhan seemed oblivious, but Lu Zhenqiu said hesitantly, "This is... demon qi?"

Luo Chen glanced at her. "Lu Zhenqiu, you have good eyesight."

Liu Muhan's expression changed slightly. "Junior Sister Lu, is this really demon qi?"

Lu Zhenqiu swallowed. "It should be correct. As Enforcement Court cultivators, we occasionally go out on missions and have encountered remnants of the demon race. The power they emit is exactly this kind of energy. But strangely enough..."

Lu Zhenqiu looked around, utterly confused.

"There's no demonic cave here, nor any trace of living beings. Why would demon qi be generated? And it seems to have been soaking into this moss for a very long time, otherwise it wouldn't be hidden so deeply."

Luo Chen clapped his hands and straightened up.

His magic power circulated as he collected a large patch of Phoenix Eye Obsidian Moss.

Then, his gaze fell upon the nameless Daoist temple before him.

"Phoenix Eye Obsidian Moss is nearly extinct in the Xuan Realm. The reason is that it's not originally a native spiritual plant of the Xuan Realm—it mutated after being contaminated by demon qi during the Xuan-Demon War ten thousand years ago."

"After the demon realm was repelled and demon qi was cleansed, Phoenix Eye Obsidian Moss gradually went extinct."

"For so much of it to grow here, there must be a source of demon qi. Coupled with the phenomenon of wood-attribute spiritual energy loss on Green Smoke Mountain..."

"It seems the answer lies inside this Daoist temple!"

As he spoke, he strode to the temple's large doors, which had no patterns or carvings, and reached out to push them open.

Behind him, Liu Muhan's expression changed.

"Xuan Luo, don't act rashly!"

Lu Zhenqiu also quickly advised, "If demon qi is involved, this isn't something we Adepts can handle. It's better to report to the sect."

But even as they spoke, the large doors had already creaked open under Luo Chen's push.

Luo Chen turned back and smiled.

"It's fine. If it's hiding so furtively, even if there's danger, it won't be too great."

With that, he strode inside.

Liu Muhan sighed inwardly. This genius junior brother of hers—he had been too smooth-sailing all along, too proud.

The Xuan Realm was vast, and there were always those more capable.

Even if this Daoist temple was hiding furtively, that didn't mean its occupants were weak.

Perhaps it was precisely to guard against those great powers at the Sage and Ascendant levels?

But she couldn't let him face danger alone!

Liu Muhan and Lu Zhenqiu exchanged glances, then led the two Initiate disciples into the temple together.

Before entering, they had already taken out their respective dharma treasures, extremely cautious.

But there was no movement inside.

The temple was empty, with no danger in sight.

Only Luo Chen stood alone in the center of the main hall, looking up in a certain direction.

Behind the incense burner table and offering altar.

A pitch-black statue with three heads and six arms silently gazed at the newcomers in the faint light streaming through the open doors.

The three heads were positioned on the left, right, and center. The left head had eyes blazing like molten copper, fangs biting through its own lower jaw. The right head had no mouth or nose, its entire face like black paper, with only a hint of spirit in its eyes.

The central head, however, had lowered brows and a gentle smile, its thick lips slightly parted, radiating an aura of peace.

Though the statue was an inanimate object, it sat there as if it were a living creature, quietly observing the world.

The five people stood before this statue, momentarily speechless, stunned into silence.

Until a magnetic voice rang out.

"Four eyes, one mouth, channeling spiritual energy. This statue forms a spirit-gathering formation, absorbing surrounding spiritual energy. It seems this is the reason for the loss of wood-attribute spiritual energy on Green Smoke Mountain and even the Gray Triangle."

Liu Muhan couldn't help but look at Luo Chen.

"But the statue is empty—where did all that spiritual energy go?"

"The answer is right here!"

Suddenly!

Luo Chen leaped into the air and struck the crown of the statue's central head with a palm strike.

The next moment, that mouth suddenly opened wide.

As the mouth opened wide, what had been gently smiling lips transformed into a blood-red maw, as if eager to devour.

At the bottom of the maw, a formation could faintly be seen in operation.

It looked like a teleportation formation?

Luo Chen's eyes lit up.

"It's inside!"

Before his words had even faded, he had already charged in.

"Xuan Luo!"

Again with this.

Liu Muhan's heart raced as she quickly followed him in.

Lu Zhenqiu also led the others in rushing inside.

...

Just as Luo Chen had said earlier.

If this Daoist temple was hiding so furtively, even if there was danger, it wouldn't be too great.

Even the Sage or Ascendant-level powers that Liu Muhan and the others had worried about meant nothing to him.

If the enemy was at the Refining Void or Body Integration stage, he was confident he could escape.

As for a Great Vehicle stage Heavenly Venerable—absolutely impossible!

So compared to these dangers, Luo Chen was more eager to know where this demon qi came from.

He intended to participate in this Demon-Suppressing Trial. If he could learn more about the demon realm and the demon race beforehand, that would naturally be best.

So from start to finish, he had not hesitated at all.

But upon entering the space behind the statue's gaping maw, a sense of disappointment welled up in Luo Chen's heart.

His divine sense instantly spread throughout the entire space.

There were people inside!

More than one!

Living beings full of life numbered in the hundreds.

Among them were even three Sage-level existences.

Of those three, one had a rather high cultivation level—around late Sage stage, only a step away from the Ascendant stage.

For an ordinary cultivator, this secret realm was indeed dangerous.

But for Luo Chen, the enemy's cultivation level represented the upper limit of the intelligence he could gather.

Sage stage...

Luo Chen was very disappointed.

But in that moment of disappointment, a trace of surprise rose to his face.

He discovered that the ground of this space was covered with a large amount of deep green, almost black moss.

It wasn't Phoenix Eye Obsidian Moss, but Nine Nether Demon Moss!

According to the classics, when the demon realm attacked the Xuan Realm, they used this Nine Nether Demon Moss to transform the environment, converting spiritual qi into demon qi!

In an instant, connecting the dots, Luo Chen understood everything.

Someone had occupied this sealed small space, secretly absorbing external wood-attribute spiritual energy, cultivating Nine Nether Demon Moss, and practicing demon arts.

It was that simple.

Luo Chen didn't care who that person was.

But this Nine Nether Demon Moss was a major trial in the Demon-Suppressing Trial, and he could take the opportunity to adapt to it in advance.

Unlike Luo Chen's alternating feelings of disappointment and surprise, the four people who entered behind him—Liu Muhan and the others—were stunned.

Looking out, under the dim sky, the ground was covered with black-green moss, like an ink jade carpet spread across the entire earth.

The impact of such a scene was no less than that of the demon statue outside.

And the tall tower standing in the distance proved that someone was active here.

Just as they entered.

There was movement from the tall tower.

*Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!*

*Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!*

Figures radiating powerful auras shot out, charging toward them with murderous intent.

A voice full of malevolence rumbled through the void.

"Barging into this lord's seclusion—you will stay here!"

Due to this sudden voice, everyone's spirit shook, and they stood frozen for a moment.

When they came to, the two leading women's faces turned pale.

"A Sage!"

But there was no time to react.

In an instant, the enemy was before them.

Those who came were all Adept-level existences.

Each was dressed in black, their bodies shrouded in demon qi, with sharp fangs and claws, hideous beyond compare.

Battle erupted instantly!

Luo Chen was at the front. The Liuhong Sword transformed into countless sword lights, like rainbows tearing through heaven and earth.

With a single move, he single-handedly engaged three opponents.

Liu Muhan and Lu Zhenqiu were worthy of being true disciples of Suli Sect—their combat skills were not lacking. After the initial panic, they quickly calmed down and activated their dharma treasures to face the enemy.

The two Initiate cultivators stood back-to-back, supporting each other.

In an instant, the five-person team erupted with considerable power, holding back the attackers.

Liu Muhan fought while keeping an eye on Luo Chen with her divine sense.

She found that this junior brother not only had exceptional talent in cultivation but also possessed considerable combat skills. His long sword moved with perfect ease, and he held his own against three opponents without falling behind.

If only these were the only enemies, it would be fine.

Even if it were difficult, they should be able to handle them.

But the unknown existence that had issued that grand voice earlier had yet to make a move, weighing heavily on the woman's heart.

When she caught sight of more than a dozen figures shooting out from the distant tower in her peripheral vision, despair crept onto her face.

"We can't fight anymore! Leave quickly! Junior Sister Lu, take the people and retreat first!"

Her left hand extended, and a jade bracelet on her fair wrist began to emit spiritual light.

Then a huge halo rose up, enveloping the battle group.

For a moment, everyone felt their strength greatly increased and their magic power flowing smoothly.

Meanwhile, the enemies trapped within saw their black qi begin to melt like snow under the spring sun.

Lu Zhenqiu had already begun to move upon hearing Liu Muhan's words earlier.

She led the people back to the entrance, where the teleportation formation was still operating.

"Senior Sister Liu!"

Liu Muhan didn't look back. "You go first! Xuan Luo, you go too! I'll hold them off!"

Luo Chen said loudly, "The teleportation formation is short-range, but if someone interferes, it will definitely be interrupted. Someone needs to stay here to block the pursuers."

Liu Muhan's face showed determination. "That's why I said I'll hold them off!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "It was my stubbornness that brought us in. How could I implicate Senior Sister? Let me hold them off instead!"

As he spoke, he suddenly struck Liu Muhan with a backhanded palm.

So sudden that she couldn't react in time, and didn't even know why her magic power suddenly stalled. She stumbled back without resistance and landed precisely on the teleportation formation.

Perfectly accurate!

"Lu Zhenqiu, take her and leave!"

In his roar, Luo Chen's magic power surged explosively. The Liuhong Sword transformed into countless rainbow beams, single-handedly engaging all the enemies.

On the teleportation formation, Lu Zhenqiu gritted her teeth and grabbed the struggling Liu Muhan.

As spiritual light flickered, the four of them vanished.

Just after they left, all the rainbow beams suddenly collapsed.

From the distant tower, a surprised voice came.

"An Adept with this kind of strength?"

But Luo Chen acted as if he hadn't heard. He strolled leisurely to the teleportation formation and casually slashed down with his sword.

A corner of the formation was instantly damaged.

Sealing off both locations, cutting off his own retreat.

Such an action surprised the master of this place even more.

"You're not leaving?"

Luo Chen ignored the increasingly numerous Adept-level demon cultivators charging at him. He simply gazed at this ink jade space paved with Nine Nether Demon Moss, a smile on his face.

"This place is not bad. It can serve as my location for establishing my Sagehood."

As he spoke, his aura gradually rose.

From beneath his green Daoist robe, wisps of blood-colored gas emerged.

These blood energies seemed to possess their own intelligence. After appearing, they moved like dragons and snakes.

Surrounded by blood energies, Luo Chen's aura suddenly changed. His expression turned ice-cold, and an extremely destructive power filled the entire ink jade space.

At this moment, he seemed to be the true great demon!
