# Chapter 1251: Vermilion Bird Sweeps the Demon Lair, Xuan Luo Attests the Great Dao

In one year's time, the demon lair had been turned upside down.

That avian demon spirit had burst forth from the central demon abyss, launching a great slaughter throughout the demon lair as if patrolling its territory.

Any Initiate cultivator exposed to the open met their end on the spot.

Even several Ascendant great powers, caught off guard, had fallen.

Fortunately, someone discovered it quickly and promptly notified the Demon-Suppressing Tower.

Thus, Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird descended!

The commotion at that time was immense!

The sky above the entire Dark Demon Lair was covered with overwhelming golden light, as if it wanted to purify everything in the world.

That was the external manifestation of the Demon-Suppressing Tower using its great power to forcibly summon Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird.

Upon entering, Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird immediately understood the general situation and charged straight at that avian demon spirit.

A battle at the Sage level thus commenced!

The development of the battle had some twists and turns, but overall there were no major surprises.

Even though Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird was suppressed by the demon lair's environment, she was still a powerful existence at the late Sage stage. At her peak, she had once cooperated with Devil Lord Heaven-Refining to besiege Rosycloud. Facing a mere demon spirit, she held the upper hand from the very beginning.

The so-called twists and turns mainly lay in the fact that the demon spirit, relying on the Dark Demon Lair's environment, could continuously regenerate after being dispersed.

Although Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird was somewhat surprised, in her fury she had already decided to exterminate it completely.

Disregarding the suppression of the heavenly and earthly environment, she forcibly unleashed her origin divine flame, spending several months to burn the demon spirit to ashes.

After doing all this, she did not withdraw.

Forcibly descending using the Demon-Suppressing Tower's power required a tremendous price.

And that price would inevitably have to be compensated by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect later.

Since she had already come in, she might as well not waste that price. She would simply accelerate the destruction of the demon nests and end the Demon-Suppressing Trial early.

In this way, losses could also be minimized.

Thus!

After burning the demon spirit to death, Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird, like that demon spirit, began a massive sweep through the Dark Demon Lair.

The only difference was that one targeted human cultivators, while the other targeted demon nest spirits.

As Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird swept from the outside inward, demon spirits fell wherever she passed, whether high or low rank.

Those demon nests standing in various places also suffered the most thorough destruction under her great power.

It could be imagined that after this ordeal, the Dark Demon Lair would certainly be severely weakened.

Even if it recovered for another thousand years, it could never reach its current prosperity.

"It's rather a bargain for the Primordial Demon Sect, who will be in charge of the next Demon-Suppressing Trial."

"No, that's not quite right either."

"The Primordial Demon Sect cultivates demonic techniques with a human body and requires a great deal of demonic qi. The more thoroughly I do this, the less benefit they will gain next time."

With this thought, Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird had an idea.

Since she was already here, why not do it even more thoroughly?

She turned her gaze toward the central demon abyss!

The endpoint of the Demon-Suppressing mission had always been the central demon abyss, which was also the source of the Dark Demon Lair!

Thus, she went alone, charging into the central demon abyss!

...

In the abyss shrouded by endless mist, a woman in fiery red robes hovered high in the air, looking down at the demon abyss below.

Suppressed, deep beast roars reached her ears from time to time.

Moreover, seven powerful auras, not inferior to the previous Sage-level demon spirit, were scattered in all directions, eyeing her covetously.

Perceiving this scene, Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird revealed a cold smile.

"Who would have thought that in a thousand years, the Dark Demon Lair would have grown to such a scale. I wonder if it's because the previous sect failed to complete the Demon-Suppressing mission, or if it's the spatial tremors caused by an unknown impact on the Xuan Realm as mentioned by Nether Capital, allowing more demonic qi to be transported from the demon realm?"

Although she was powerful, her status had not yet reached the apex of this realm.

She did not fully understand all the secrets involved.

But since she had already come this far, she would strangle all danger in the cradle.

Taking a deep breath, Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird formed a hand seal, then slowly spread her hands.

She did not take those Ascendant-level demon spirits seriously, but Sage-level demon spirits were not so easy to eliminate.

Especially since she was still suppressed by the environment here.

Therefore, she prepared to go all out!

The hand seal took effect, and her hands slowly opened.

The next moment.

*Caw!*

A sharp cry issued from her mouth, spreading for a thousand li.

Then, a majestic phantom rose behind her.

Its color was crimson red, extremely hot.

Its wings spread open, like the arms of a deity.

On its slender, elongated neck, a divine bird's head materialized—it was the legendary Vermilion Bird!

When the Vermilion Bird Dharma Image appeared, heaven and earth stirred.

Streams of origin qi of heaven and earth surged forth, gathering and intertwining around the Dharma Image, forming ribbon-like currents of air.

An aura that was both peaceful and sacred, yet also domineering and extreme, instantly covered a thousand li.

After the Vermilion Bird Dharma Image was born, it enveloped Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird within.

As the Perfected Lord's cold eyes gazed at the demon abyss, the quite solid Vermilion Bird Dharma Image also opened its indifferent eyes.

"Although suppressed by the demon lair, I can still exert sixty to seventy percent of my power. Enough to annihilate the demon spirits here!"

With a sharp cry, Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird charged straight into the demon abyss.

Wherever she passed, none could stand against her!

Any Ascendant demon spirit that roared out was no match for her, barely lasting a single exchange.

Even when Sage-level demon spirits came to intercept her one by one, they could not completely stop her advance.

Instead, under the burning of her Vermilion Bird divine flame, they were forced back step by step.

Fierce!

At this moment, only these two words could describe the bearing and aura of Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird.

Not only that, but the demon nests within the abyss, which had condensed dense demonic qi, were also broken apart one after another by her casual actions.

Spirit nests began to form, and a great amount of spiritual energy emerged.

It could be imagined that given the depth and breadth of the central demon abyss, the number of demon nests within it, how vast would be the amount of spiritual energy generated after they were all broken?

Even if it couldn't compare to some newborn small worlds, it was enough to rival those planets heading toward destruction.

However, as Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird advanced, she gradually discovered a strange phenomenon.

The closer she got to the center, the more stubborn the demon spirits' resistance became.

They even did not hesitate to actively extract demonic qi from the demon nests to accelerate their body repair.

Combined with the earlier actions of that avian demon spirit actively going out to sweep human cultivators.

A horrifying guess surged into her heart.

"Has the Source Court seal loosened?"

The nine great holy sects had a certain understanding of places like the Dark Demon Lair.

This was one of the spatial weak points of the Xuan Realm!

The demon realm had used this to enter the Xuan Realm back then.

Although the Xuan Realm's native forces had ultimately won the Xuan-Demon War and driven back the demon realm's army, the demon realm's Holy Ancestor had still left behind some means to prevent those spatial weak points from automatically healing.

For this reason, the Source Court-level forces on each continent of the Xuan Realm had set up corresponding seals.

The Dark Demon Lair was one such case!

The Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerable of the Nether Capital Source Court had personally taken action, first setting up a Ninefold Severance Seal at the spatial weak point, then using the Demon-Suppressing Tower, a heaven-reaching spirit treasure, to draw upon the laws of heaven and earth to forcibly imprison it, preventing its expansion.

If the Ninefold Severance Seal were broken or loosened, the consequences would be unimaginable.

It was very likely that the demon realm's army would once again sweep in!

Thinking of this, Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird no longer held back. Going all out, she took an even more extreme approach!

Strands of Vermilion Bird divine flame sprayed from her mouth, no longer touching and withdrawing, but remaining on those Sage-level demon spirits.

This method certainly caused great harm to her, at least affecting the power of her origin divine flame.

But the benefits were also obvious.

The divided Vermilion Bird divine flame could remain on the demon spirits forever. No matter how many times they were reborn, they would be met with the burning of the divine flame.

After doing all this, Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird paused no longer and charged straight for the Ninefold Severance Seal.

Just then!

In a remote corner of the demon abyss, a shocking tremor suddenly arose.

The Vermilion Bird looked back, only to see a vast sea of demonic qi rolling away, breaking through the mist that had shrouded the demon abyss for ten thousand years, revealing the brilliant golden light erupting from the Demon-Suppressing Tower above the demon lair.

Her heart was greatly alarmed, and she blurted out.

"Damn, could it be that another powerful demon spirit has been born?"

But upon closer observation, she found something amiss.

That monstrous demonic qi, after converging together, quickly transformed into five-element spiritual qi, and even absorbed the origin qi of heaven and earth to merge into a giant with a blurry face.

Such a scene was not unfamiliar to her, who had seen much of the world.

A sign of Ascension!

After her initial shock, Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird revealed a look of astonishment.

"Could there be a cultivator breaking through to Ascension here? Which sect's disciple is so bold as to be reckless?"

"And what's with this divine sense intensity?"

If it were ordinary times, she would naturally go over to see what was happening.

But right now, there was no time for that.

Because as she was distracted, the seven demon spirits trapped by her divided Vermilion Bird divine flame actually took the initiative to self-destruct their bodies.

Then, seven streams of light flew toward the center of the demon abyss.

Seeing this, Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird's heart sank.

"These creatures are targeting the Ninefold Severance Seal set up by the Nether Capital Source Court!"

She hesitated no longer. The Vermilion Bird Dharma Image enveloped her, spread its wings, and rumbled across the abyss, charging forward with the most resolute attitude.

When she arrived at the seal's location.

The seven streams of light had already disappeared.

On the pitch-black earth, gray-white traces flickered with an inexplicable light.

In the center of the enormous formation, a left hand was slowly extending.

That hand was covered in black scales, with laws and principles interwoven upon it. When seen, it seemed to exist in another time and space.

At this moment, it was about to break through the boundary!

Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird's eyes were filled with terror.

"Holy Ancestor of the Demon Realm!"

...

In a remote corner of the demon abyss.

A giant, whose true features could not be seen clearly, with a illusory face but a solid body, slowly flew out.

A vast sea of demonic qi surged forth, and upon encountering the giant, it transformed into five-element spiritual qi, naturally absorbed by him.

The demon spirits lurking nearby seemed to realize something and swarmed out, wanting to disrupt this person's breakthrough.

But as soon as they approached, sword qi capable of cutting through everything came down like a torrent.

Moreover, a blood-red sword light darted among them, destroying those ignorant demon spirits in the most violent manner.

Truly, not a single disturbance was allowed!

The giant seemed oblivious to all this.

In his plan, breaking through to Ascension again required no pill assistance, after all, he had experienced it once before.

He only needed to go through the process again; it wouldn't take much time.

However, there was an unexpected joy.

During the breakthrough, through the connection between the demon moss within his body and the vast amount of Nine Nether Demon Moss outside, he captured a glimmer of the origin of wood.

Following the breakthrough opportunity, he directly entered the ocean of origin qi and precisely found the laws belonging to the origin of wood.

It was different from the sharpness of the metal origin, the explosiveness of the fire origin, and also completely distinct from the non-contending gentleness of water and the deep heaviness of earth.

It was a power of the most tenacious, unceasing vitality.

Swords could not cut it, wild fires could not burn it, floods could not wash it away, rocks could not block it.

As long as a single breath remained, it was endless, supporting heaven and standing upon earth!

"This is the origin of wood!"

Luo Chen felt it, his heart joyful and festive.

At this point, it should be about enough.

If following the original plan, he could now end the breakthrough.

But before ending, he slightly sensed his own soul strength, and a result presented itself before him.

A Sage-level soul!

This result was unexpected, yet within reason.

Back in the Mountains and Seas Realm, he already had a soul foundation surpassing his peers.

During the five hundred years of return-to-void, although he could not cultivate immortal techniques, his soul foundation had been tempered by the years, solidifying its foundation and reaching the late Ascension stage.

It could be said that those five hundred years were not wasted.

It was not just about achievements in martial arts.

All along, Luo Chen's soul, relying on technique cultivation, pill assistance, and multiple experiences with heart-demon obstacles, had far surpassed others.

But it had always lacked a certain sedimentation of time and years.

In fact, this was the normal way for most cultivators to grow their souls.

Time builds everything!

The five hundred years of return-to-void had made up for this shortcoming.

And after arriving in the Xuan Realm, the re-cultivation of realms and the perfection of mental transformation had left his soul foundation stuck at the peak of Ascension.

Until this moment!

Leveraging the opportunity of Ascension, his soul foundation leaped to the Sage level.

There was no doubt about this.

Because the divine sense he emitted had a momentary collision with a powerful divine sense from afar.

Although still inferior to the other party, it no longer had that gap between heaven and earth.

The two were on the same level, only differing in quantity.

"It's Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird. Why has she also entered?"

A doubt surfaced in his mind.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's gaze fixed.

In the deepest part of the vast ocean of origin qi, a black light flickered faintly.

That light was impossible to capture, yet extremely powerful, filled with an image of destroying everything.

It was even far above the five-element laws he had comprehended.

Perhaps the only thing that could compare to it was that wisp of gray light that had once been integrated into Luo Chen's lifebound treasure, which could be mentioned in the same breath.

What kind of law or great dao could be comparable to what Luo Chen had named "Order Light"?

Most importantly, Luo Chen felt a familiar aura on that black light.

As if he had seen it somewhere before?

Where was it?

Luo Chen's thoughts drifted uncontrollably, and a memory hidden deep in his heart slowly had its pages turned.

It was in the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

To comprehend the origin of water, he had used the Netherwater Spring and taken an Initiation Pill to forcefully gain insight.

Although the outcome was successful, the process was not easy.

Especially at the moment of comprehending the origin of water, the origin of fire clashed with it. The extreme collision between the two once attracted a destructive force from heaven and earth.

That force, he could not penetrate, could not suppress, and could not even hide his soul aura to avoid it.

For this reason, he had to take a risk, using the Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas method of the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* to extinguish his soul.

Only then did that force quietly dissipate, not destroying Luo Chen, nor destroying the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land.

Now, this force had appeared before Luo Chen again.

One burst of energy, then it fades, and a third time it is exhausted.

Luo Chen knew that opportunities like this could not be repeated. If he missed this chance again, he might never have another in his lifetime.

At least, he could not re-cultivate the Ascension realm again.

So, he would go all out!

Luo Chen's divine sense began to delve deeper and deeper, wanting to enter the deepest part of the ocean of origin qi and touch that black light.

Fortunately, he had not spent much time comprehending the origin of wood earlier, and he was still in the state of breaking through to Ascension.

At first, the descent of his divine sense was relatively smooth.

However, the deeper he went, the more difficult it became, with a feeling of running out of steam.

This was a very surprising thing.

With Luo Chen's divine sense, now comparable to a Sage Perfected Lord, and in the state of breaking through to Ascension, wanting to comprehend a true meaning of law should never be so difficult.

After all, when he first broke through back then, he had even encountered a high-end fire-type law true meaning like the Nirvana True Intent.

But this also showed how powerful the law represented by that black light was.

Still above the Nirvana True Intent!

Luo Chen's divine sense hardened, charging forward without hesitation.

Manifested externally, the primordial spirit giant trembled wildly, as if mustering all its soul power.

Even the demonic qi surging from ten thousand li around was momentarily scattered.

But human strength has its limits. Sometimes, wanting to do something is not the same as being able to do it.

When he reached two-thirds of the depth, the pressure from all directions in the ocean of origin qi, enough to crush everything, surged in, and Luo Chen's divine sense wavered precariously.

Just then!

Strands of gentle power, as if attracted, flew in automatically from the outside, merging into Luo Chen's primordial spirit.

His divine sense was nourished, generating new strength.

And that power was more like the will of heaven and earth, making Luo Chen move even more smoothly in the ocean of origin qi.

The speed that had stalled increased again!

It was spiritual energy!

Luo Chen understood instantly.

In this breakthrough, he had not planned to last too long, nor had he intended to comprehend a new true meaning of law, so he did not care much about the amount of spiritual energy.

What he had collected on the way was actually enough.

But in the face of wanting to dive deep into the ocean of origin qi, it was somewhat insufficient.

However!

"Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird, whether you did it intentionally or unintentionally, Luo Chen remembers this kindness."

Perfected Lord Vermilion Bird had charged into the central demon abyss, breaking apart the high-level demon nests everywhere, thus generating a large amount of spiritual energy.

At this moment, it became the final boost helping Luo Chen dive deep into the ocean of origin qi!

As if in an instant, yet as if eons had passed.

Before he knew it, Luo Chen had arrived before that black light.

His divine sense charged straight in.

In that instant, countless scenes, like dream bubbles, were born and shattered, appearing one by one before his eyes.

Spiritual power collapsing, physical body shattering, soul extinguishing—the endpoint of each of the Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas.

In the Alchemy Saint's Blessed Land, the clash of water and fire, the scene of destruction that heaven and earth could not tolerate.

In the return-to-void land, the great annihilation that swept everything at the end of all things.

There were even terrifying scenes of continents collapsing, cloud seas dispersing, stars being destroyed, and the universe expanding infinitely before turning into nothingness in an endless great explosion.

When Luo Chen opened his eyes.

His divine sense was in a strange state, extending infinitely.

He saw, at the junction of two realms, a black humanoid creature floating in a great river without source or end, extending a left hand like a heavenly pillar, striking through the void.

That hand broke through layers of spatial restrictions, slapping a tiny fire sparrow, sending it flying beyond sight, its whereabouts unknown.

In this process, the spatial barriers between the two realms shattered inch by inch, unleashing terrifying destructive power that acted upon that left arm.

That person also seemed to see Luo Chen.

In his cruel gaze, there was a hint of confusion.

As if wondering why an insect could see him?

Just then.

A blood-red sword light shot up into the sky, and under the enhancement of that destructive power, it stabbed at that person.

The person immediately roared, and his left hand grabbed out again.

*Boom!*

The sword light and the left hand collided violently.

An unparalleled destructive force erupted instantly, pushing that left hand back layer by layer.

The spatial barriers shattered inch by inch, and the left hand was continuously pushed back, ultimately returning powerlessly to its original position.

In the great river, the black humanoid creature suddenly stood up, staring fixedly at the sky.

Under the three moons hanging in the sky, a blood-red sword tip was slowly retracting.

"Destruction Great Dao! The Xuan Realm actually has one who has comprehended this dao!"

"Heaven's will!"

"Heaven's will is against me! I was just a little short of opening the Ninefold Severance Seal. Is it not yet time to invade the Xuan Realm?"

Inside the central demon abyss.

No!

Perhaps it could no longer be called a demon abyss.

The landscape here had long been changed by the previous battle. The terrain had sunk by who knows how much, and the cliffs on both sides had been flattened like wheat blown by the wind for thousands of li.

Now it should be called a plain.

Luo Chen's primordial spirit returned to its position, and he slowly opened his eyes.

In his cold gaze, there was a madness that seemed to want to destroy everything.

His hands, including the sword in his hand, were trembling at this moment.

Countless thoughts surged into his heart, thousands of words but not knowing where to begin.

In the end, there was only a murmur.

"Without seeing the light of extinction, how can one speak of the dao of longevity?
Daring to go to the place of ending, only then can one attest to the great creation!"
