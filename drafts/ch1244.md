# Chapter 1244: Vermilion Bird True Lord, the Trial Begins

Swish! Swish! Swish!

Swish! Swish! Swish!

Sharp whistling sounds rang out from the sky as streaks of light descended before the golden giant tower.

As the streaks of light dissipated, figures of various sect geniuses were revealed.

On their faces were expressions of tension or anticipation. Some remained calm and silent, while others frequently looked around.

Only those leading Divine Transformation cultivators seemed relatively relaxed.

Knowing the complete procedure, they did not assume a battle-ready stance after landing. Instead, they chatted with close friends and acquaintances.

"Compared to previous sessions, the number of participants in this trial seems much higher?"

"Indeed. In the past, there were about a hundred people, but this time there are a full two hundred!"

"Why is that?"

"I heard the situation inside the Netherdark Demon Cave is more severe this time, far exceeding previous sessions. Therefore, more Sage cultivators are needed to clear the demon moss."

"More severe? What does that mean?"

"Not sure. In any case, demon cave agitation is never a good thing. If not handled properly, it could lead to a demon realm invasion like ten thousand years ago."

"If that's the case, wouldn't this trial be even more dangerous for the Sage cultivators?"

"Yes. The casualty rate in the past was already as high as ten to twenty percent. This time, it's likely to rise. Did your sect prepare suitable defensive measures for the juniors?"

Though they appeared relaxed, the topics they discussed were quite heavy.

Perhaps only the leading Ascendant cultivators from the five major Law Manifestation powers maintained calm expressions on the surface.

Take the one from Azure Dragon Sect, for example. Relying on his ancestor's status in the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and his specific understanding of this trial, he didn't show excessive worry.

Instead, he pulled Elder Gumu and three others together, chatting and laughing while commenting on the juniors from various sects.

"Haogiong Old Monster, I heard your Five Elements Divine Sect has produced an extraordinary genius. Excellent aptitude, cultivated to the peak of the Sage stage in just five hundred years, only a step away from the Ascendant stage. Did he come this time?"

"He didn't come, and there's no need. That child only needs to cultivate and break through step by step."

"Ho! Such confidence! Then who will hold down the fort for your Divine Sect this time? The top spot in this trial might change hands."

"Fellow Daoist Li, you worry too much. My sect's Chongguang is also a seasoned Sage. With him leading, there won't be a problem."

"A seasoned Sage, huh? I'm afraid he has no hope of breaking through and came here seeking a chance for sudden enlightenment!"

Elder Li of Azure Dragon Sect was unconcerned. He then looked at a woman in green robes who was smiling.

"Chenyan Ji, who did your Biluo Sect send?"

The woman named Chenyan Ji covered her mouth with a laugh. "Such a minor junior is worthy of your concern, Fellow Daoist?"

Elder Li waved his hand. "I'm just bored and looking for a topic to discuss."

Chenyan Ji replied without hesitation, "Green Butterfly Fairy, what do you think?"

Elder Li was genuinely surprised this time. "Green Butterfly Fairy? Isn't she the daughter of your sect's Green Robe Ancestor? You're willing to send her to participate in the trial?"

Chenyan Ji smiled. "As an ancestor, one must lead by example. While descendants enjoy blessings, they should also contribute to the sect."

Elder Li nodded, finding this quite reasonable.

"The strong are never cultivated through training alone. Although the trial is dangerous, the opportunities are also plentiful. Green Robe Ancestor is not only generous but also more open-minded than some people!"

As he spoke, he glanced at Haogiong Old Monster from the Five Elements Divine Sect beside him.

The other party twitched the corner of his mouth but did not respond to this remark.

Seeing that Elder Li had finished his lament and didn't ask further, Chenyan Ji couldn't help but speak up.

"Fellow Daoist Li, why not ask about the geniuses from Shadowy Teaching and Essence Spirit Sect?"

Elder Li shrugged. "What's there to ask? The people from Shadowy Teaching always hide their faces. Even if I ask, they probably won't tell. As for Essence Spirit Sect, I've already seen them. The leader is Helan Shi, known as the Dual Master of Alchemy and Sword. I know that junior. He was once equally famous as my sect's Elder Kong Zhaonan, and they have private dealings. One is called 'Knows the North,' the other 'Zhaonan.' Kong Zhaonan succeeded in becoming an Ascendant a hundred years ago. I imagine Helan Shi is not far behind."

Chenyan Ji said in surprise, "Elder Li, you have such confidence in Essence Spirit Sect? Fellow Daoist Gu, what do you think?"

The somewhat silent Elder Gumu forced a smile. "Fellow Daoist Li is too generous."

Elder Li snorted. "Tch, you people are so hypocritical. You're all full of confidence, yet you put on this act. If you ask me, why not make a bet? Let's see which of our five sects has the most surviving disciples after this trial?"

"Fine!"

Unexpectedly, the one who responded was a black-robed man who had never spoken during the previous discussion.

Elder Li's expression froze. "Shadowy Teaching excels in shadow escape and life-preservation techniques. This bet plays right into Fellow Daoist Pei's hands."

The black-robed man waved his hand. "I won't take advantage of you. Let's make two bets. One is to see the number of surviving disciples after the trial ends. The other is a hundred years later, to see which sect produces the most Ascendant cultivators among this batch of survivors. How about that?"

"Not bad. That way, no one can just muddle through," Chenyan Ji said with a smile.

Haogiong Old Monster from the Five Elements Divine Sect also said coldly, "Fellow Daoist Pei speaks well. Setting a century-long bet is quite interesting."

Elder Gumu thought about the ten people from Essence Spirit Sect participating in this trial. Xuan Luo and Jiang Huang could be ignored, and Youhuang Sage was also slightly inferior.

But the remaining Seven True of Essence Spirit were quite excellent.

Especially Helan Shi, Iron Horse Sage, and Li Ting. All three had great hope of breaking through to the Ascendant stage.

Participating in this bet was not unacceptable.

Above all, he could not let down the reputation of Essence Spirit Sect!

"I also agree. What about the stakes? Since there are two bets, naturally there should be two stakes! I may not have much else, but a Separation Union Pill and a thousand-year-old Red Essence Mushroom are still worthy of being offered."

As soon as these words were spoken, everyone fell silent.

Haogiong Old Monster squinted at Elder Gumu. "Your sect has been flourishing in recent years. You can even take out a thousand-year-old Red Essence Mushroom for a bet."

Elder Gumu smiled slightly. "What can I say? My sect excels in cultivating spirit plants. A mere thousand-year-old medicinal herb is nothing."

These words were both self-deprecating and proud.

Elder Li clicked his tongue in admiration. "Red Essence Mushroom is useless to me, but that Separation Union Pill is a top-grade fifth-tier divine pill. I was fortunate enough to receive one from my sect's ancestor back then. It saved me a full hundred years of cultivation. Was that pill also refined by you, Old Man Gumu, and offered to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

Elder Gumu nodded confidently. "Indeed, I refined it. In recent years, the Dao Sect has had an Alchemy Saint in residence, so they no longer need offerings of Separation Union Pills. That's how I came to have some surplus to use as stakes."

Elder Li thought for a moment, then gritted his teeth. "In that case, I won't be stingy either. I'll use a recently comprehended Ascendant secret technique and a piece of Star Gold as stakes!"

The preciousness of an Ascendant secret technique went without saying.

Star Gold was also a very rare mineral material.

It was said that this substance was of the same origin as the legendary Star Crystal.

Even if it only had one or two percent of the effect, adding a piece to one's natal magic treasure could greatly increase its foundation, making it not impossible to break through to the spirit treasure level.

From this, it could be seen that Elder Li was extremely confident in this bet.

Seeing that the two of them had each taken out precious and rare items, the other three thought for a while and also each brought out treasures no less valuable than these.

After the bets and stakes were completely settled, the atmosphere among them seemed to become harmonious again.

"Why is it taking so long this time? Hasn't the Demon Suppression Tower opened yet?"

Just as everyone was puzzled.

In the distance, the sounds of a dark bird soaring and a jiaolong roaring were heard.

Everyone turned to look. A huge treasure carriage, pulled by dark birds and jiaolongs, was rapidly approaching.

On the treasure carriage, figures were numerous. At a glance, there were no less than a hundred people!

And each one had a full primordial spirit and blazing eyes.

A noble aura was evident on the outside.

Seeing this scene, someone exclaimed in shock, "Heavenly Origin Dao Sect? Are their Sage disciples also participating in the Demon Suppression Trial?"

Among the crowd, Luo Chen was also somewhat surprised.

The Demon Suppression Trial, while sounding nice—supposedly helping Sage cultivators break through to the Ascendant stage—was actually a very dangerous and arduous task.

After entering, their mission was not just cultivation. They also had to clear demon moss and cooperate with the Dao Sect's great powers to disperse the formed demon caves.

This kind of trial was usually carried out by whichever of the Nine Great Holy Sects was responsible for guarding the area. Occasionally, some slots would be given to the vassals of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

After all, the Netherdark Demon Cave was located within the Dao Sect's territory.

But according to past records, the Nine Great Holy Sects themselves did not send many Sage disciples into the demon cave.

Especially the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. In the previous session, which was still during the period of opening up the Mountains and Seas Realm, they did not send a single Sage disciple to participate in the trial.

"The Mountains and Seas Realm..."

A flash of inspiration struck, and Luo Chen suddenly understood something.

For the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, which once possessed the Mountains and Seas Realm, they didn't really care about the abundance of spiritual opportunities.

He had seen the Dao Sect's master of the Mountains and Seas Realm use the power of a spirit treasure to collect spiritual opportunities.

But now!

The passage to the Mountains and Seas Realm had been severed by him and Azure Frost working together, and the Star Gate had been taken away.

The Dao Sect had lost a place to cultivate Ascendant talents, which was why they had taken an interest in the Demon Suppression Trial.

The treasure carriage stopped above the Demon Suppression Tower.

The Dao Sect's great power on the carriage did not come down. First, he cupped his hands in the direction of the Demon Suppression Tower, then dispatched his disciples to fly out.

Seeing this, Elder Gumu and the others did not go forward to greet him. After nodding in acknowledgment, they looked at each other.

"Should we continue the bet?"

Having Dao Sect Sages participate and not having them were two completely different matters.

If so, then the difficulty for this batch of disciples from various sects to gain anything in the Netherdark Demon Cave would skyrocket.

Elder Li of Azure Dragon Sect chuckled. "Of course we continue! You all agreed just now."

Seeing his smile, the other four naturally understood.

Clearly, the other party had known this news long ago.

Given the relationship between Azure Dragon Sect and the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, their disciples would naturally receive the other party's protection.

It was hard to say how many Ascendant cultivators would emerge in the future, but in terms of survival rate, they would definitely be superior.

As for the first bet, Elder Li was naturally sure to win!

The others couldn't go back on their word and had to reluctantly accept the bet.

Just then, the giant tower in front of them emitted a rumbling sound.

The gate opened, and a figure walked out.

It was the genius of the Earth Prostration lineage—Xiu Teng!

He looked at everyone and said solemnly, "Ascendants, stop here. Sages, please enter!"

Subsequently, more than three hundred Sage cultivators, under the encouraging or worried gazes of their elders, filed into the Demon Suppression Tower one after another.

...

The scenery inside the tower was not particularly majestic.

What met the eye were more crisscrossing lines of talisman marks lying horizontally on the ground.

There were also ten figures sitting cross-legged on them.

Each one exuded an extremely powerful aura, no less than the leading Ascendant elders of the various sects from before.

In other words, these ten people were the true main force of the upcoming Demon Suppression Trial, the Dao Sect's great powers responsible for dispersing the demon caves!

As soon as everyone entered, their pupils contracted and their bodies tensed up.

Luo Chen was no exception.

But what made him nervous was naturally not the ten Dao Sect great powers, but the talisman marks on the ground.

"These are not ordinary talisman marks, but origin talisman marks containing complete law intent."

"Mainly for suppression, confinement, elimination, and even destruction!"

Especially the last point. Luo Chen could feel an extremely powerful force from within.

It seemed like it could be detonated instantly!

That was the final resort. If everything else was useless and a demon from another realm really broke out from here, then this formation would surely explode instantly.

Its power would be enough to destroy everything.

Even the entire Guangling Prefecture, and even the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, would be affected.

Luo Chen took a deep breath. Being able to arrange such means was no longer something ordinary cultivators could do.

In his opinion, even a Body Integration Great Venerable could not achieve such a feat.

Only a Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerable, standing at the apex of the Mystic Realm, could lay down such a shocking formation.

In comparison, the golden, magnificent, and majestic Demon Suppression Tower seemed more like a cover.

"Using a heaven-reaching spirit treasure as a cover? The Nether Capital Source Court truly has a bold hand."

Luo Chen was extremely shocked in his heart.

From the arrangement here alone, he had glimpsed a corner of the Profound Mysterious Overlord's iceberg.

Just as everyone was tense.

A heavy voice suddenly rumbled in their ears.

"Xiu Teng, distribute the demon-eliminating liquid."

"As you command, True Lord!"

Xiu Teng respectfully bowed to the upper level, then came before everyone. With a wave of his hand, streaks of light fell into everyone's hands.

Luo Chen looked down. It was a gourd.

Xiu Teng said loudly, "This is the demon-eliminating liquid refined by our Earth Prostration lineage. It has a miraculous effect on clearing demon moss. Although it cannot eradicate it completely, it can cause it to wither. You should use your dharma power to drive it and pour it on areas dense with demon moss. Remember, do not be perfunctory. This demon spirit gourd also has the function of recording and summoning. After you come out, we will inspect it."

So this was the legendary demon spirit gourd?

Luo Chen shook it. The sound of waves came faintly from inside, along with some vague complaints.

Refining the demon spirits unique to the Netherdark Demon Cave into magical instruments without harming their intelligence.

This technique... Luo Chen thought of the Yuantu Sword.

Just as Xiu Teng finished his instructions, a sharp voice sounded again inside the tower.

"The Netherdark Demon Cave is personally suppressed by the Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerable of the Nether Capital Source Court. Its internal laws restrict power. We True Lords cannot easily enter, and even Ascendant cultivators can only enter a maximum of ten at a time."

"This Demon Suppression Trial is different from the past. Demon caves are frequently agitated."

"Therefore, if powerful demon spirits above the Ascendant stage appear, you ten should summon me!"

The ten seated Ascendant great powers all opened their eyes and replied respectfully, "As you command, Vermilion Bird True Lord!"

The moment that voice appeared, Luo Chen's expression changed.

Now, hearing the others' address, he was even more surprised.

Wasn't it said that the ones stationed here were the White Tiger True Lord and the Black Tortoise True Lord, two of the four great generals of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?

How had it become the Vermilion Bird True Lord?

No one answered this doubt for him.

Everyone had already walked to the center of the formation one after another.

As the light flickered, figures disappeared one by one into the Demon Suppression Tower.

Luo Chen walked up with his fellow disciples from Essence Spirit Sect. They looked at each other, encouraging one another.

Then, with a flash of light.

When Luo Chen opened his eyes, everything he saw was dim and dark. The earth was a vast expanse of dark green.

Only in the sky did strands of golden light occasionally flicker.

"So this is the Netherdark Demon Cave?"

Luo Chen muttered to himself, the corners of his mouth lifting slightly. Then he turned into a streak of light and disappeared from the spot.
