# Chapter 1256: Flying Star No More, Yet Old Friends Remain

Though the Xuan Realm was vast, it should not have held so many Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerables!

Especially now, it had almost become deformed.

On smaller continents, there were three to five Great Vehicle cultivators.

On larger ones, like the Youxuan Continent, over a dozen Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerables were crowded together.

A Great Vehicle cultivator was one who had cultivated a single law's true intent to great perfection and beyond!

Such beings, existing within a single realm, were almost equivalent to incarnations of their corresponding laws.

They would be instinctively repelled, expelled, and even lifted up by this heaven and earth, forced to ascend and leave this realm.

This was similar to how Ascendant cultivators in small worlds were compelled to ascend.

The principle was essentially the same.

Yet over countless years, the number of Great Vehicle cultivators in the Xuan Realm only increased, never decreased.

The reason, when traced to its root, was only one thing.

The Immortal Path had been severed!

Without the guiding immortal light, even a Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerable dared not ascend directly.

Even if they lingered in the Xuan Realm, they had to endure the terrifying heavenly tribulation that came once every ten thousand years, forcing them to merely survive here.

Over time, a special realm division even appeared in some classics—the Tribulation Transcendence stage.

In reality, this stage was no different from Great Vehicle, only distinguished by how many tribulations one had survived.

Those who had survived one tribulation were in the Tribulation Transcendence stage, and those who had survived seven or eight were also in the Tribulation Transcendence stage.

Take the legendary Nine-Tribulation Heavenly Venerable who roamed the Xuan Realm—he had survived nine Great Vehicle heavenly tribulations.

Strength was measured by the number of tribulations survived; the more one had survived, the stronger one was.

Of course, this was not absolute.

Each successive heavenly tribulation grew more terrifying. To cope with these tribulations, Great Vehicle cultivators nearly exhausted their resources. Some grew poorer with each tribulation, their strength weakening accordingly.

The main reason strength weakened with each tribulation was that, except for the first Great Vehicle heavenly tribulation, subsequent tribulations offered almost no heavenly energy in return.

They were purely punitive lightning tribulations.

Thus, later tribulations were also called—Immortal-Falling Tribulations.

They destroyed those cultivators who should have ascended to become immortals but tried to linger in the Xuan Realm.

All this information flowed through Luo Chen's mind. This was the benefit of heading straight to the Suling Sect's Scripture Pavilion upon first arriving in the Xuan Realm.

By reading extensively, one could always find corresponding answers.

And this answer was precisely related to the Flying Star Platform before him.

The Immortal Path had been severed. Ascendant cultivators from the Mountains and Seas Realm likewise could not ascend directly to the Immortal Realm.

Either they were reckless like Luo Chen, drifting through the universe without knowing the truth.

There probably weren't many such people.

After all, cultivators from small sects and scattered cultivators found it difficult to reach the Ascendant stage.

But such people should not be limited solely to the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Other small worlds likely had similar beings, forced to drift through the universe.

Those fortunate might find a great thousand world like the Xuan Realm, capable of bearing Great Vehicle cultivators and those below. Those less fortunate...

Luo Chen sighed inwardly, his gaze falling upon the Flying Star Platform.

Or else, those in the know were teleported to the Xuan Realm through the Heaven-Earth Peak Star Gate.

But now, that path had been severed by him and Azure Frost working together.

Even the Star Gate had been taken by Rosycloud.

Hence, the Flying Star Platform had fallen into such disuse.

Only six or seven hundred years had passed since that battle.

"Not right!"

Luo Chen's hand within his sleeve suddenly moved.

It was the Void God Gu, Kongkong'er, reacting.

"Although this place is ruined, the spatial energy is quite rich. It should be a spatial node, otherwise Kongkong'er wouldn't react like this."

"This node should correspond to the Mountains and Seas Realm!"

"If..."

Luo Chen's eyes showed a contemplative gleam, a hypothesis vaguely surging in his heart.

If he could recreate the super teleportation formation here, supplemented by a spatial spirit treasure like the Star Gate, and establish contact with those in the Mountains and Seas Realm, using that realm as a coordinate to anchor...

He could likely very easily reconstruct the teleportation formation that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect used to receive Ascendant cultivators from lower realms.

Or even, if there were powerful, daring individuals in the Mountains and Seas Realm, they could completely capture this corresponding spatial node and directly sneak into the Xuan Realm.

"Xuan Luo?"

A call came from beside him, and Luo Chen woke as if from a dream.

Elder Gummu smiled at him. "What were you thinking, so lost in thought?"

Luo Chen shook his head, forcing a smile. "Just a little unsettled, nothing much."

Elder Gummu patted his shoulder. "It's normal. Your cultivation has advanced too quickly, leaving your state of mind unstable. Once this matter is settled, return to the Sulling Sect and go into seclusion for fifty or sixty years, and this won't happen again. Also, I heard that the Alchemy Saint has a unique pill that aids in tempering the state of mind. Once we settle down, I'll go and ask for one for you..."

As he spoke, Elder Gummu grew somewhat hesitant.

He remembered that Xuan Luo seemed to have a good relationship with the Alchemy Saint, who favored him greatly. Perhaps he wouldn't need to ask; if Xuan Luo went himself, seeing his current condition, the Alchemy Saint might directly bestow one upon him.

"Then I thank the elder."

"It's nothing. We've arrived at Juanke Mountain."

As he spoke, Elder Gummu led a group of cultivators off the large ship. The cultivators from other sects did the same, and even the Dao Sect Sages who had participated in the Demon-Suppressing Trial entered Juanke Mountain one after another.

Throughout the process, the armored guards bearing halberds on the ship stood on either side, their gazes fixed on everyone.

It was as if they were under surveillance.

Luo Chen made no move, calmly entering Juanke Mountain like the other cultivators.

Once inside, he didn't even perform his daily cultivation.

The only cultivation he did was probably the Soul-Splitting Art!

He began to recultivate it, practicing from beginning to end, in order to heal the splitting trauma on his divine soul.

Thus, a few days later, Juanke Mountain made a move.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect sent a notice, inviting every cultivator to participate in an interrogation.

Luo Chen was among them.

...

Inside a room, it was so quiet you could hear a pin drop.

A voice came from behind a screen.

"Xuan Luo of the Sulling Sect, recount your experiences in the Demon-Suppressing Trial one by one."

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow. This kind of talk felt more like an interrogation.

Wasn't visiting the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect after the trial supposed to be a reward and grace?

It seemed the anomaly within the Dark Abyss Demon Cave had made the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect very concerned!

Speculating in his heart, he spoke without hesitation.

"After entering the Dark Abyss Demon Cave, I was in the outer area and didn't see any demon spirits. Then, following the secret technique bestowed by my sect master, Tai Su Lingjun, I sensed my fellow disciples. I intended to clear the Nine Nether Demon Moss while going to rendezvous with them, but while passing through a place in the outer area, I unexpectedly discovered a demon cave that had been scattered by a senior from the Dao Sect. At that time, spiritual energy was abundant, and I felt my divine soul was quite active. I thought it was a good place for cultivation, so I simply stopped and cultivated there. This process lasted a full three years, ending only when the Demon-Suppressing Tower opened."

"Give the specific location in detail!"

"Uh, this..."

"Don't try to muddle through. Furthermore, as far as we know, you had a demonic contamination before the trial. Where did that contamination come from?"

"This matter involves sect secrets, I'm afraid..."

"Hmph, before my Dao Sect, what secrets are there? Just speak. As for your sect master, Tai Su Lingjun, there will naturally be seniors from this sect to appease her."

"Very well!"

Luo Chen sighed and recounted the matter of the Gray Triangle Ink Jade Space in detail.

He didn't omit Jiang Huang either.

At the end, he also explained his route during the Demon-Suppressing Trial in detail.

Silence came from behind the screen.

But with his divine sense, Luo Chen could hear clearly.

"Junior Sister Yu, does it match?"

"This junior brother from the Sulling Sect isn't lying. It matches what Jiang Huang said."

"Could the two have colluded?"

"With the formation suppression from our sect's experts, if they wanted to lie, they would need the divine soul cultivation of a Refining Void True Lord. Moreover, the route map he later gave also matches the usage traces of the Demon Spirit Gourd. I asked my father, and he did indeed shatter an outer demon cave on that route. At the time, he even lamented how such a demon cave with rich demonic energy could appear in the outer area."

"How is your father? I'd like to find time to visit him."

"Alas, his physical body was destroyed, and only his primordial spirit escaped. It's not suitable for visiting. Let's settle this matter first!"

"The junior sister speaks sense!"

After a moment of silence.

The voice came from behind the screen again.

"You may go back first. Think about whether you've missed anything. In two days, we'll invite you again to cooperate with our investigation."

Again?

Luo Chen frowned and cupped his hands in farewell.

After he left, the screen was removed, revealing two figures.

One was a gentle woman with a delicate, refined temperament.

The other was a handsome young man with flying eyebrows.

The man looked at the woman's slightly furrowed brow and asked doubtfully, "Junior Sister Yu, is there a problem with what this man said?"

Junior Sister Yu shook her head. "He's fine. I was just thinking that any cultivator who has achieved something in cultivation is a person of restraint. This kind of interrogation is too offensive. Once is enough. If we do it a few more times, I'm afraid it will anger them and even the sects behind them."

The senior brother sneered disdainfully. "What's there to fear from these small sect cultivators?"

Junior Sister Yu sighed. "If there were nothing to fear, why set up this screen? Isn't it precisely to avoid forming grudges with them?"

The senior brother was startled, then nodded repeatedly. "The junior sister is still clever. I didn't think of that."

"Try to be as gentle as possible in the next interrogation. Among these people, there might be future elders or sect masters of some sect." Junior Sister Yu smoothed her hair and was about to say more when she suddenly paused, a jade talisman appearing in her hand.

The man beside her looked over curiously.

"This is... a token from that one?"

Junior Sister Yu's eyes brightened as she carefully listened to the message within the jade talisman.

After listening, her expression was somewhat strange.

"What's wrong, Junior Sister?"

"Nothing. I need to go out for a while. Senior Brother, please continue questioning the others!"

With that, Junior Sister Yu trotted out of the room.

...

After coming out of that little dark room, Luo Chen stroked his chin and nodded after a long moment.

It seemed that the Demon Spirit Gourd also had the function of recording the trial cultivators' range of activity.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect used this to verify the truth of the trial cultivators' words.

Fortunately, he hadn't lied. He had indeed walked that route.

But only part of it. Later, he had charged straight from that route into the central area, entering the Central Demon Abyss.

However, with such a huge commotion in the Central Demon Abyss, with a Demon Realm Saint Ancestor crossing over, and only sending the Four Great War Generals to guard the Demon-Suppressing Tower, letting these Sage-level juniors interrogate them...

Wasn't that a bit perfunctory?

As Luo Chen thought about it, he laughed to himself.

Calling it perfunctory was also acknowledging that they had done their best.

The Four Great War Generals were all at the late Refining Void stage or even peak—they were already the strongest combat power of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

They couldn't possibly have the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign personally appear!

And for these Sage-level cultivators, with the formation and the Demon Spirit Gourd cooperating, it was enough to extract information.

He figured that aside from Dean He, who needed to be questioned by the Dao Sect's great powers, everyone else had encountered the same setup as Luo Chen.

But the thought of having to go through this once or twice more made Luo Chen speechless.

With this time, he might as well continue cultivating the Soul-Splitting Art, striving to establish that mutually restraining yet mutually strengthening connection between the nine newly split sub-souls and his main soul.

Just as Luo Chen was grumbling to himself, hurried footsteps sounded behind him.

"Xuan Luo, wait!"

Luo Chen turned around and looked at the woman who had come running.

It was one of the two from behind the screen just now.

Her white dress fluttered, her black hair flew, and she had that gentle, lovely quality mixed with the unrestrained nature of youth.

Compared to her, his own demeanor was a bit too mature.

He stopped and smiled at the approaching woman.

"Fellow Daoist, is there something you need?"

The woman stopped at an appropriate distance of about three feet from Luo Chen. She smoothed the hair on her cheek and smiled. "My surname is Yu, given name Jiajia. The Sulling Sect has always had a good relationship with my Dao Sect, so you may call me Senior Sister."

Luo Chen gave an "oh." "Senior Sister Yu."

Yu Jiajia stated her purpose. "I heard that not long ago, an elder from my sect went to stay at the Sulling Sect for a while, and you accompanied her?"

A gentle figure appeared in Luo Chen's mind.

He nodded.

Yu Jiajia revealed a smile. "That person said that since you've come to the Dao Sect, she should return the favor as a host. I am here on her orders to invite you to Sitting Seclusion Peak."

The Alchemy Saint, Chu Yan?

Luo Chen gladly accepted the invitation. If he could avoid the subsequent interrogations, that would be a good thing.

On the way to Sitting Seclusion Peak, he casually asked about Chu Yan.

"You seem to admire her?"

Yu Jiajia was somewhat surprised. "You can tell?"

Luo Chen smiled. "The eyes don't lie."

Yu Jiajia giggled. "I do admire her. To cultivate to the Refining Void realm as a woman is rare even within the sect."

Luo Chen was unconcerned. "The path of cultivation knows no gender. Besides, doesn't your sect also have top female experts like the Vermilion Bird True Lord?"

Yu Jiajia shook her head. "It's different. Elder Chu is not only profound in her realm but also has a peerless alchemy skill. Even the Great Sovereign treats her with courtesy. That's something even the Vermilion Bird True Lord cannot achieve."

"You want to become someone like that too?" Luo Chen asked.

Yu Jiajia smacked her lips. "Who wouldn't want that? But Elder Chu said I have no talent for alchemy. Even if I worked hard my whole life, I'd only be an ordinary alchemist. If it weren't for the fact that when she ascended, my father was on duty at the Flying Star Platform to receive her, she probably wouldn't have bothered to guide me at all."

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised. "Is Elder Chu very aloof?"

"Aloof?" Yu Jiajia was even more surprised. "Alchemists are eccentric and unsociable. Isn't that normal?"

The logic was sound.

But the Chu Yan in Luo Chen's memory had never been associated with aloofness.

Even when she ascended back then, she could bestow a Heavenly Secret Pill on Luo Chen, whom she barely knew.

Why had her temperament changed so drastically upon arriving in the Xuan Realm?

"Junior Brother Xuan Luo, are you very skilled in alchemy? Otherwise, why would Elder Chu take the initiative to invite you?"

Just as the two were chatting, their destination had arrived.

It wasn't far from Juanke Mountain, only about a hundred li away.

Because the disciples of other sects weren't suitable to be placed in the sect's core area, and the matter of the Demon Cave anomaly was involved, the Juanke Mountain where they were housed was quite remote.

And the place where Chu Yan lived was also equally remote.

Sitting Seclusion Peak?

Luo Chen thought of the Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect back in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Looking at the mountain shrouded in mist and clouds, with only fragments visible, Luo Chen narrowed his eyes.

The next moment, the clouds and mist parted.

A figure walked out from within.

Her face was clean, without makeup, yet it could not hide her peerless beauty.

Dressed in palace attire, she was as cold and ethereal as a moon palace fairy.

But upon seeing Luo Chen, she gave a graceful bow and revealed a smile.

"Fellow Daoist Xuan Luo, it has been a long time."
