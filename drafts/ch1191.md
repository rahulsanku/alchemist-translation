# Chapter 1191: The True Body Appears, Star Crystals

The Sea of Stars blazed with starlight.

One by one, stars fell from the extreme heavens.

Like a torrential downpour, they seized the hearts and minds of all who witnessed them.

Luo Chen opened his crimson-golden eyes. After sixty years of seated meditation, his gaze showed no trace of fatigue—only brilliant, piercing light.

His sharp eyes fixed upon the first falling star. It descended from the firmament at a speed unimaginable to mortals, friction with the atmosphere trailing long tails behind it.

He had seen such a sight before.

In years past, when he traveled through the desolate wilds of the Mountains and Seas Realm, he had witnessed falling meteorites. That experience had inspired him, combining with the power of fire origin laws to create a divine ability of his own—Starfire Burns the World!

Back then, his focus had been on the starfire.

But now, Luo Chen's entire mind and spirit were fixed upon the star's body itself.

As it plummeted, the enormous celestial body rapidly disintegrated.

This scene was exactly like a martial artist's transition from prosperity to decline.

No matter how strong a martial artist was—even if they hung high in the firmament like the sun for millions of years—they could never escape the fate of final downfall.

Luo Chen's thoughts ran deeper.

The *Demon Phase Art* taught abandoning the fixed physical body to transform into a boundless sea of blood. As long as the blood sea remained undying, the physical body was immortal.

The *Pure World Withered Bone Bodhi Method* held that the physical body would eventually end, and only bones could endure eternally. Thus, one cultivated only the skeleton, relying on the divine power of incense offerings to achieve eternal life.

Fang Qingxue had relayed what the Lord of Carefree Realm said: martial arts was a bitter sea, and martial artists sailed within it. The physical body was the boat, divine will the oar, and endless energy the seawater. None of the three could be lacking. Thus, one first cultivated the physical body, then absorbed qi to transform it into astral power. With this foundation, one finally comprehended one's own divine will, using it as an oar to cross the bitter sea of martial arts. But the bitter sea was boundless. Relying on only one kind of power, one could never reach the other shore. Therefore, one needed to pursue the unity of all three. Only then could one move through the water like a fish and cross the bitter sea!

As he pondered, a thunderous roar shattered the cosmos.

*Boom!*

The star had landed!

An extremely terrifying, unimaginably vast force erupted a thousand li away.

Like a monstrous tidal wave, it surged in all directions, centered on the place where the star had fallen.

The Myriad Manifestations Star Sect was prepared.

From deep within the sect's forbidden grounds, a massive white stone sundial rumbled forth, emerging from the earth.

The Star Sect Master led the sect's core power, seated beneath the sundial. Each released their lifebound astral power, exerting every ounce of strength to activate the sundial.

A brilliant starlight barrier unfolded, encompassing the surrounding hundred li.

Fortunately, tonight was the grand ceremony of the Myriad Manifestations Star Sect. All disciples were gathered here. This barrier was enough to protect them all.

As for the martial artists and commoners elsewhere in the Sea of Stars, they could only rely on their own fortune.

After blocking the first wave of impact, the Star Sect Master breathed a sigh of relief. Then, subconsciously, he looked toward the Purple Forbidden Enclosure, one of the Three Enclosures.

With just one glance, he froze.

That senior had made no defensive measures at all. He simply exposed his physical body to the vast impact of the star's destruction.

"What is he trying to do?"

A sudden shout rang out.

"Luo Chen, what are you doing?"

It was Yuan Qingcang!

At this moment, his entire body blazed with thunderous light, forming a thunderball that protected him. Amidst the vast impact, he stood as steady as a reef.

Faced with his companion's reminder, Luo Chen seemed not to hear. He continued staring at the high sky.

He let the vast force crash against him, shredding his white robes to pieces.

A layer of blood-cloth robe had originally covered his body surface, but that too retracted instantly, revealing his thousand-tempered true spirit fleshly body.

Standing within the stellar storm, Luo Chen's eyes grew brighter and brighter.

"Here it comes again!"

Tonight, more than one star would fall!

Perhaps because the end of the era was approaching, anomalies frequently occurred within the Return to Nothingness.

Falling into the Sea of Stars, they became even more violent.

Those remnant stars, unknowingly drawn into the Return to Nothingness at some point, had finally met their end tonight.

This time, it was three stars!

Such a simple, insignificant number brought destruction to this land!

Luo Chen sat cross-legged on the Purple Forbidden Enclosure, letting the air currents batter him, letting starlight scatter over him. The boundless, vast force madly pounded against his reef-like form.

A sensation of his flesh nearly tearing apart arose.

Ignoring the pain, countless thoughts surged through Luo Chen's mind. His qi and blood boiled like oil. The seven hundred and twenty martial gods hidden within his acupoints seemed to come alive.

On his attribute panel, the remaining hundred-plus achievement points from the past sixty years began to be consumed madly.

The technique named *God-Devil Formless* was being deduced at an unimaginable speed.

This was the feeling!

The immense external pressure was driving the unity of flesh, astral power, and divine will.

One step!

Just one more step!

If he could take it, he would achieve the unity of all three, forming a whole like "Chaos"!

"Not enough!"

Luo Chen suddenly looked up at the sky, his eyes filled with madness.

The external pressure was still insufficient.

His perception was still insufficient!

With a thought, Luo Chen let out a soft shout.

"Appear!"

The next moment, under Yuan Qingcang's shocked gaze, a giant rose from the ground, standing tall atop the Purple Forbidden Enclosure.

The giant was a thousand zhang tall, its top impossible to see at a single glance.

Its legs were like heavenly pillars, its six arms like demon god limbs.

Its exposed skin was bronze-colored, yet faintly tinged with gold, giving a solemn and majestic feeling.

Covering its skin were patterns like ancient tree rings or demonic beast scales.

"Roar..."

With a roar from the giant, patches of light bloomed on its pale golden body surface.

Those were acupoints!

Within each patch of light sat a "Luo Chen" in meditation.

At this moment, seven hundred and twenty Luo Chens opened their eyes simultaneously.

Instantly, seven hundred and twenty streams of martial divine will surged forth, accompanied by violent and boundless blood-refined divine astral power that erupted like a massive blood cloud covering the nine heavens and ten earths.

Yuan Qingcang stared blankly at this scene, muttering to himself.

"Is this his full-strength form?"

Whether it was the battle with himself back then, or when he took the Corpse Immortal's finger strike head-on, those were merely the tip of the iceberg of Luo Chen's true power.

Only now did Yuan Qingcang understand what it meant to say there was always a sky beyond the sky, a man beyond men.

And Luo Chen, revealing his full-strength form now—was he trying to resist heaven with his own power?

The fall of three stars could not shake Luo Chen in the slightest.

What followed was a rain of stars, continuous and unending!

Hundreds, thousands of stars crashed into the earth. Mountains crumbled, great rivers were severed.

Endless storms raged on all sides. The very space of the entire world seemed unable to bear it, tearing open spatial rifts one after another.

The disciples of the Myriad Manifestations Star Sect watched this scene in utter despair.

That sundial, of unknown origin, now had its pointer trembling violently, its starlight flickering wildly, on the verge of collapse.

The Star Sect Master was dumbfounded, yet powerless to resist.

In his eyes, there was only that giant, standing tall between heaven and earth, bearing the brunt of the impact.

He could not sit by and watch the Myriad Manifestations Star Sect perish here. Gathering his remaining strength, he raised his head and roared.

"Senior, save us!"

His words fell like a stone into the ocean—no response at all.

The current Star Sect Master's face was filled with despair.

Was the foundation of the Star Sect, entrusted to him by his elders, to end here?

Just then!

The giant opened its mouth and spat out a small gray cauldron. It flew out, growing larger as it faced the wind, until it was about a hundred zhang in size, hovering above the sundial.

As the gray cauldron emerged, streams of chaotic gray light cascaded down like thousands of silk ribbons.

Where the ribbons passed, they seemed to fix the earth, wind, water, and fire. They seemed to fix time and space.

The universe instantly became clear.

The current Star Sect Master was stunned, then overjoyed.

"Everyone, lend me your strength! Restart the Myriad Manifestations Instrument!"

"Yes!"

All the Star Sect disciples responded, releasing all their power, channeling it into him.

The complex force was unbearable, causing him great pain.

But in the end, he slowly sorted out the sundial called the Myriad Manifestations Instrument.

The sundial, which had been trembling violently, regained its stability. Streams of starlight scattered outward, barely shielding the disciples of the Myriad Manifestations Star Sect.

Yuan Qingcang watched this scene and shook his head inwardly.

But he said nothing. He was merely curious about that gray cauldron.

He sensed a familiar power from it—could it be his own Chaos Thunder Pool?

No!

It wasn't just the Chaos Thunder Pool.

There seemed to be that power that Green Robe had once controlled?

Magnetic divine power?

Just then, the gray cauldron rotated, emitting a tremendous suction force. It covered a thousand li, ten thousand li, crossing spatial distances as if it were omnipresent.

Streams of starlight, as if attracted, were all drawn into it.

Even the remaining star bodies themselves entered the belly of the cauldron like prey into a giant beast's mouth.

"This... this..."

Yuan Qingcang was completely lost.

He could not understand what Luo Chen was trying to do, nor could he understand what that small cauldron was.

Everything happening now exceeded his comprehension.

And the scene of falling stars destroying the world continued around them.

Luo Chen bathed in the light of stellar destruction. Even his thousand-zhang body became indistinct.

Vaguely, sounds of Buddhist chanting echoed through everyone's hearts, calming their restless minds.

Time flowed rapidly. In the blink of an eye, it was dawn.

Sunlight, far more piercing than before, spilled onto the scarred earth.

The Star Sect disciples, already collapsed from exhaustion, opened their eyes, breathing the air that was turbid yet fresh and invigorating after their survival.

The Star Sect Master subconsciously looked toward the Three Enclosures.

But the reliable figure at the Purple Forbidden Enclosure was gone.

He was startled, then flew over.

The Purple Forbidden Enclosure had already collapsed. Amidst the ruins, only a corner of a high platform remained.

When he arrived, he found that white-clad figure.

The Star Sect Master breathed a sigh of relief and immediately descended into the ruins, kneeling on one knee.

"Junior, on behalf of the entire Myriad Manifestations Star Sect, kowtows in gratitude for Senior's life-saving grace."

Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes. His gaze was peaceful, without a trace of sharp light, as if he had returned to simplicity.

He said calmly, "No need to be concerned. Consider it repayment for lending me the Purple Forbidden Enclosure for sixty years of cultivation."

The Star Sect Master shook his head repeatedly. "If it were just me alone, that would be one thing. But Senior preserved the lives of ten thousand people from the entire sect, as well as our sect's orthodoxy."

Luo Chen smiled. He extended his hand.

Under the Star Sect Master's puzzled gaze, a five-colored stone covered in edges and corners suddenly appeared.

"A Star Crystal?"

"You recognize this thing indeed!" Luo Chen weighed the five-colored stone in his hand, asking curiously, "Explain it to me."

"Of course."

The Star Sect Master nodded, then began to explain in detail the origin, function, and taboos of this object.

The five-colored stone was called a Star Crystal.

In myths and legends, it was a substance used to mend the heavens.

In the records of some cultivators, this object was recorded as an extremely rare heavenly material among heavenly materials and earthly treasures. It was on par with earthly treasures like Xirang and Weak Water!

It originated from stars, being the core of a star.

But such a thing did not appear under normal circumstances. It only condensed and appeared when a star fell—and even then, only when a previously very powerful star fell.

Such a thing was of great use to cultivators!

Refining even a little into a magic treasure would grant it heaven-destroying, earth-obliterating power.

As for the principle behind it, no one knew to this day.

For martial artists, however, this object was somewhat useless. It did not communicate with qi and blood, was difficult to fuse with divine will, and was like a stubborn stone, completely unusable.

On the contrary!

If a martial artist kept it by their side for long, they would be affected by the Star Crystal. Their originally active divine will would gradually become sluggish and dull, greatly affecting their own cultivation.

After hearing this, Luo Chen fell into thought.

He had harvested many of these five-colored stones last night—seven or eight pieces.

Most had been absorbed by the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Only the smallest piece in his hand was one he had collected from the star debris after dawn.

He had tried it at first, and indeed, as the Star Sect Master said, his martial divine will became sluggish as soon as it approached, not as active as before.

It seemed the other party was not deceiving him.

Luo Chen thought for a moment, then asked directly, "You are so familiar with this object. It seems you have harvested quite a bit in the past?"

The Star Sect Master smiled bitterly. "To be honest with Senior, my Myriad Manifestations Star Sect has been established here for nearly ten thousand years of inheritance. We have encountered countless star falls, so naturally we have collected many Star Crystals. This time, Senior saved my entire sect. The great kindness is beyond words. I should repay it with all my strength and naturally would not begrudge these external objects. But unfortunately..."

He shook his head, ashamed. "All the Star Crystals in the sect were taken away by the Star Sect Master from two generations ago."

"Two generations ago... Heavenly Manifestation Array?" Luo Chen understood.

The Star Sect Master nodded. He had no dissatisfaction with Luo Chen directly calling their sect's ancestor by name.

In his view, Luo Chen, who had single-handedly resisted the falling stars last night, was absolutely not inferior to their ancestor, Heavenly Manifestation Array, in terms of strength.

Calling him by name was merely addressing him as an equal.

He thought for a moment and said, "I wonder if Senior wants anything else? If the Star Sect possesses it, I will naturally repay with all my strength."

Luo Chen waved his hand.

The truly good things had probably all been taken away by others. What remained was only scraps.

His gaze swept across the former Sea of Stars. It was now dim and boundless.

Only three great suns hung high in the void, mercilessly scorching the earth.

The Myriad Manifestations Star Sect, which had once possessed the aura of an immortal sect, was now reduced to broken walls and ruins, a picture of decay.

"This place is no longer livable. Find another location."

Luo Chen said this as he stood up.

The Star Sect Master sighed. "Our sect's orthodoxy is here. How can we easily change our mountain gate? Besides, after last night's simultaneous fall of stars, the energy here is even richer than before. If we tidy things up properly, our Star Sect may yet rise again in the future."

"The future?"

Luo Chen carefully examined the Star Sect Master, who looked haggard and worn, his entire body in ruins. He said no more and prepared to leave.

"Senior, won't you stay a little longer?"

"No need. Someone is waiting for me."

The Star Sect Master looked up. In the distance, Yuan Qingcang had been waiting for a long time.

He could only bow deeply.

"Danyang bids farewell to Senior!"
