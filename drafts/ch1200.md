# Chapter 1200: Azure Frost of the Mystic Realm, the Space of Destruction

The Mystic Realm!

The Mystic Nether Continent.

Within the Heaven Phoenix Clan.

Inside a spirit mountain cave dwelling, a woman sat cross-legged.

A simple green robe could not conceal her peerless beauty.

Spiritual energy swirled around her, and ripples frequently appeared in the void.

She sat there quietly, yet it was as if she was not there at all, as if the next moment she would vanish into the void and disappear from this world.

As her cultivation art circulated, the aura emanating from the woman grew increasingly dense, no longer suppressible.

Nor did she wish to suppress it.

The next moment!

*Boom!*

Her aura burst forth in full force. A massive primordial spirit flew out from the crown of her head, manifesting across the sky.

Rolling spiritual energy surged in like tidal waves.

A majestic and spirited Luan bird phantom soared across the sky, madly devouring the origin qi of heaven and earth.

Affected by this, the entire Heaven Phoenix Clan was shaken.

One figure after another flew out from the spirit mountains and great rivers, gazing with anticipation toward that location.

Some of these figures had taken human form, but most were avian demon beasts.

Seeing this scene, everyone knew that one of their own was about to break through to the next realm!

Not only these low-level beasts.

Within the Heaven Phoenix Clan, the sole Great Vehicle stage Heaven Phoenix also cast her gaze upon the location of the spiritual tide.

"As expected of a child specially brought up from the lower realm by Phoenix'er. Her talent is so outstanding. Clearly, she primarily cultivates the most difficult and powerful spatial laws, and also practices body refinement on the side. Yet she still managed to successfully condense a phantom in five hundred years. As long as she crosses this step, she will surely and smoothly enter the Law Manifestation stage. Her future achievements will not be inferior to Phoenix'er or Lie Yu."

But the next moment.

The Heaven Phoenix suddenly paused.

Where her divine sense reached, she saw the rolling spiritual tide suddenly dissipate, and the majestic Luan bird phantom soaring through the sky abruptly collapse.

Such a sudden change was truly unexpected!

To pursue faster cultivation speed, their Heaven Phoenix Clan had long abandoned the most ancient True Spirit path, instead pursuing the human method of Qi Refining immortality.

Therefore, the process of breaking through realms was no different from that of humans.

Aside from the heavenly tribulations faced during the Sage stage, Body Integration stage, and the Great Vehicle ascension, normal breakthroughs to major realms did not attract lightning tribulations.

It was more about internal transformation!

As long as one's foundation was sufficient, breaking through was a matter of course.

The celestial phenomena brought about by a realm breakthrough were merely a gift from the world, a process that usually lasted for a period of time, allowing one to absorb external energy to stabilize the current realm.

Therefore, it was rare for someone to fail after successfully taking that step.

It was too unreasonable.

Could it be that the child had not yet adapted to the heavenly laws of the Mystic Realm?

But that shouldn't be the case either!

She had already been in the Mystic Realm for five hundred years!

What needed to be transformed had been transformed, what needed to be adapted had been adapted. Such a mishap should never occur.

"Could it be that her Dao heart has a problem, causing the phantom to collapse?"

The Heaven Phoenix Ancestor, not knowing the truth, spread her divine sense toward the cave dwelling.

"Azure Frost, what happened? Why did you fail at the critical moment?"

Inside the cave dwelling.

The woman in green had a pale face, with faint traces of blood at the corner of her mouth.

She wiped away the blood and replied respectfully, "I have worried the Ancestor. It is because this child's cultivation is insufficient and still needs more polishing."

"Cultivation insufficient?"

The Heaven Phoenix Ancestor was half-convinced.

Nevertheless, she still spoke words of comfort to Azure Frost, telling her to recuperate well and prepare for the next breakthrough.

After the Ancestor's divine sense departed, Azure Frost fell into silence.

She blinked her eyes, as if the scene from before still lingered and would not disperse.

She had seen it!

In a chaos.

A thousand-zhang giant was instantly destroyed by a terrifying storm, with no power to resist.

Even the Life-and-Death Gu that was the only connection between the two of them was completely destroyed.

At that moment, even the faint and extremely weak connection backlashed upon her, causing her to be unable to take the step that should have been easy.

"Luo Chen, in the end, you still died?"

The woman murmured. On her face, which had always been calm and cold as frost, a trace of guilt appeared.

That man was a true unparalleled genius!

Not only was his talent extraordinary, but his Dao heart was also unshakably firm. In terms of combat ability, he was no less than her.

Even if he could not successfully ascend, as long as he found a great world no less than the Mystic Realm, he could still achieve extraordinary accomplishments.

In five hundred years, his progress might even surpass hers.

But because of her sister, Rosycloud, not only was his dream of ascension shattered, but his subsequent path of cultivation was also forcibly cut off.

After suffering through five hundred years, he had finally fallen.

The woman unconsciously pressed her hand to her heart, then slowly closed her eyes.

A faint murmur echoed from her mouth.

"I'm sorry..."

---

*Crack!*

A sharp, piercing sound rang out in everyone's ears.

Fang Qingxue looked around and saw that the walls of the Ninefold Immortal Tower protecting them were covered with one hideous crack after another.

And this was inside the tower!

One could only imagine that the defensive layer outside the tower had already been completely destroyed.

She finally understood why her ancestor, Wuyou Realm Lord, had held the Ninefold Immortal Tower for thousands of years but had never attempted to use this treasure to cross the Gate of Destruction.

The pressure, both internal and external, was unimaginable to ordinary people.

Now, having only just entered the Gate of Destruction, the Ninefold Immortal Tower could no longer hold up. What about the journey ahead?

"Brother Luo, what do we do?"

Everyone's gazes simultaneously fell upon the white-robed man sitting at the formation's core.

At that moment, Luo Chen suddenly opened his eyes.

He said gravely, "The Immortal Tower will be destroyed. Everyone, look out for yourselves!"

As he spoke, the Golden Erosion Array Diagram suspended above his head also began to show cracks.

The entire Ninefold Immortal Tower emitted a creaking, groaning sound of unbearable strain.

Fang Qingxue and the other three dared not be careless. Taking a deep breath, they each released their protective astral qi to shield themselves.

Not only that, but they also added various protective treasures.

Before everyone's eyes, Fang Qingxue draped a thin gauze robe over herself. Its treasure light flowed, appearing extraordinary. Then she released a flower basket that enveloped her entirely. She glanced at Luo Chen, somewhat hesitant.

Gu Xun's expression was grim. He first swallowed a black pill, then put a string of prayer beads on his hand. Not only that, but a damaged ancient bell also hung high above his head. Finally, he scattered a handful of crystals that formed a tiny space, trapping him inside.

In comparison, Xiao Lin and Ice Heaven Son were much more impoverished.

The former only had time to slap a yellowed talisman onto himself, which exuded powerful spiritual energy without any concealment.

Luo Chen glanced at it, somewhat surprised, but instantly understood.

That talisman should have been left behind by the former immortal Xiao Lan. Xiao Lin had only simply activated it.

But since he did not understand the methods of Qi Refining immortality, no matter how powerful that talisman had been before, it could probably only exert half its effect now.

Ice Heaven Son, on the other hand, looked utterly bleak, able only to repeatedly urge his protective astral qi.

Return-to-Void martial artists pursued the martial path and did not rely on external objects.

Moreover, he had only recently advanced to the Human Immortal realm and had no time to find protective treasures.

Thus, he could only face the coming storm in this nearly "naked" state.

He instinctively cast his gaze toward Luo Chen.

At this moment, Luo Chen had already walked to the front of the group. A blood-colored light circulated throughout his body, forming a blood-colored cloth robe.

With both hands extended forward, a divine astral light shield materialized.

Not only that, but starlight and thunderbolts burst forth from within his body, completely surrounding him.

"Here it comes!"

No one knew when this voice was uttered.

Immediately after, a deafening explosion rang in everyone's ears.

*Boom!*

The Ninefold Immortal Tower shattered with a bang.

A terrifying shockwave surged inward from the outside.

Luo Chen stood at the very front, letting out a muffled grunt.

He had withstood the first wave!

But he still couldn't help staggering backward.

Seeing this, Fang Qingxue's face finally hardened. She took a step forward and moved to Luo Chen's side, her overhead flower basket also encompassing him.

The surrounding air currents swept past, rustling the gauze robe on her body.

Gu Xun, Xiao Lin, and Ice Heaven Son were relatively further back, so they did not bear the brunt of the impact after the Ninefold Immortal Tower's destruction. But the subsequent aftershocks still rolled in relentlessly.

The speed was too fast. The three had no time to react and could only defend passively.

*Rip...*

A tear appeared on Xiao Lin's talisman, and a trace of blood appeared on his face.

Gu Xun's whole body shook. The empty sprout crystal hut protecting him clattered and clanged.

As for Ice Heaven Son, his eyes widened, his pale, thin lips slightly parted, but no sound came out.

*Boom!*

As the aftershock passed, Ice Heaven Son's entire body was torn to pieces, vanishing into this strange space.

Sensing this, the four survivors were all greatly shocked.

"Don't stop! Keep moving forward!"

Luo Chen's voice rang out, calm, even ice-cold and severe.

He took large strides, leaving the protection of the flower basket, and moved forward unwaveringly.

Fang Qingxue looked at Luo Chen's back, gritted her teeth, and followed.

The two behind also hurriedly followed.

---

Looking out with his Red-Eyed Golden Pupils.

There were pitch-black cracks, transparent nodes expanding and contracting, and clusters of gas flowing and impacting.

Occasionally, a meteor the size of a star would appear, only to be ground to nothing in an instant.

A feeling of universal destruction, of all things returning to nothingness, arose in Luo Chen's heart.

For a moment, he was even dazed, as if some fleeting insight that had flashed by before was reappearing.

But soon, Luo Chen gathered his stray thoughts and calmed down.

The spatial vortex known as the Gate of Destruction had once been only a hundred zhang in size.

But as the Return-to-Void was destroyed, it had expanded to who knows how large.

A thousand zhang, a hundred thousand zhang, a million zhang, or even ten million zhang?

Luo Chen did not know. He only knew that here, he was walking on thin ice. One wrong move and he would fall completely.

Even though he had cultivated the Chaos Indestructible Body, theoretically allowing his bones to never be destroyed and his blood to regenerate from a single drop, as long as his martial divine will remained, he could recover time and again. But in this space where spatial storms could erupt at any moment, no matter how strong the flesh, it would only be reborn again and again, torn apart again and again, until there was no more power to regenerate.

He could only rely on his instincts to move forward, forward, and forward again!

Time passed, unknown for how long. A miserable cry came from behind.

Luo Chen did not look back. He knew that another of his companions had fallen.

It should be Xiao Lin!

That talisman wouldn't last much longer.

Then who would be next?

Luo Chen had no mind to think. The terrifying feeling of universal destruction always hung over his heart.

The Heavenly Star Primordial Magnetic Divine Thunder was almost exhausted.

The blood cloth robe formed by his martial divine ability was already tattered.

A fierce look flashed across Luo Chen's face. He raised his hand.

*Whoosh!*

A large umbrella unfurled like a flower.

The spirit treasure, Burial Bone Umbrella!

The astral qi within his body was rapidly converted through the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra*, forming long-unused mana, barely enough to drive the Burial Bone Umbrella.

The moment this umbrella appeared, its artifact spirit awakened.

It stared in horror at this scene, continuously sending out danger signals.

But Luo Chen ignored it all, continuing forward.

"Be careful!"

A warning came from behind.

Luo Chen had already seen it. In his line of sight, a massive fireball like a great sun came roaring in.

That should be a fallen star, sucked in by the spatial vortex here.

Luo Chen held the umbrella in one hand and made a grasping motion with the other. The Yuantu Sword fell into his hand.

"Slash!"

A towering sword qi erupted violently.

It cleaved the terrifying falling star fireball in two.

The roaring sound of the meteor flying past echoed in his ears. Before Luo Chen could breathe a sigh of relief, he heard the sound of the umbrella above his head cracking.

He knew that the mana he had hastily converted from his Blood-Refined Divine Astral, having not undergone primordial spirit tempering, could not fully drive this spirit treasure.

Especially—

Luo Chen looked around. The surging chaotic energy contained even more terrifying power.

It was the cosmic force carried by the countless fragments of laws filling this place.

Even a spirit treasure with its own consciousness could not withstand such force.

"Luo Chen, where are we going?"

Gu Xun's hoarse voice came from behind.

The empty sprout crystal hut had long been destroyed, and the ancient bell above his head had also disappeared at some point.

All that was left to protect Gu Xun was the prayer beads in his hand and the black light constantly emanating from his body.

Under the blessing of that black light, Gu Xun's aura continuously surged, but his life force was rapidly draining away.

Where to go?

Luo Chen looked around. This space was indistinguishable from day or night, had no direction, and even up, down, left, and right seemed reversed.

Only the intent of destruction filled it.

If before, he had only thought of taking it one step at a time.

But at the moment he reorganized his Chaos Indestructible Body, he had faintly sensed a place in the outside world.

That place was like a coordinate, guiding him forward, preventing him from losing his current direction.

"Follow me closely!"

After saying this, Luo Chen never spoke again.

The three of them trudged on with difficulty through the space of destruction.

Another period of time passed.

*Rip!*

The moment he heard this sound, Luo Chen knew the Burial Bone Umbrella was destroyed.

It must have been hit by an energy cluster containing metal-attribute laws. There was no room for salvation. It shattered on the spot.

Luo Chen felt some regret, but had no time to mourn the loss of the spirit treasure.

He opened his mouth and spat.

A small cauldron flew out with a *whoosh*.

The small cauldron emitted a gray light, enveloping him completely.

With the blessing of this gray light, Luo Chen's steps became much lighter, and his forward speed nearly doubled.

Behind him, Fang Qingxue saw this and couldn't help but be stunned.

The flower basket in her hand was long gone, replaced by another treasure.

In her three thousand years of governing the Great Destruction Realm, Wuyou Realm Lord had collected countless treasures.

Even though she had taken the top tier with her, what she left for Fang Qingxue were still rare and excellent items, some of which were rumored to be heaven-reaching spirit treasures in the outside world!

Refined by special methods, they could also be driven by one's natal astral qi.

But the effect...

She instinctively looked back.

All she saw was Gu Xun's prayer beads shattering, his black aura suddenly dissipating, and his entire body, like a withered tree, being swept away by the vast air currents to an extreme distance.

Another companion had fallen.

Fang Qingxue felt her steps growing heavier and heavier. Watching Luo Chen drift further and further away from her, the despair in her heart grew more and more intense.

She knew that here, she could only rely on herself and should not expect anything from others.

Especially since Luo Chen had already been righteous enough, controlling the Ninefold Immortal Tower to bring them in, withstanding the initial impact, and leading the way all along.

But the longer she stayed in this place, the more lost and confused she became inside.

"Can I really get out?"

*Hum...*

The green lamp in her hand trembled, and its flame suddenly went out.

Fang Qingxue felt her steps heavy, unable to take another step forward.

Another storm came from ahead, and she uncontrollably retreated backward.

Just then.

*Whoosh!*

Five blood dragons roared in, turning into a large hand that grabbed her and pulled her forward.

The chaotic gray light poured down, enveloping her. She felt an immense relief.

She instinctively looked up at Luo Chen. He said not a word, still staring firmly ahead, continuously taking steps.

In silent silence, the two continued forward.

Time passed, unknown for how long. The gray light above their heads grew dimmer and dimmer, as if it would disappear at any moment.

When another spatial storm struck, Luo Chen was on high alert, frantically driving the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

A dull *thump, thump* beat against two uneasy hearts.

"*Pfft!*"

A mouthful of blood sprayed out. Luo Chen's face was pale.

He had managed to withstand this wave.

But this time, it was not just one wave of spatial storm impact.

It seemed they were approaching the exit. The spatial storms outside were erupting wildly. Those spatial cracks came like giant nets, leaving no room to dodge.

*Rumble!!!*

Another round of spatial storms struck. Fang Qingxue finally made her decision.

"Brother Luo, thank you."

She voluntarily walked out of the protection of the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Luo Chen only had time to turn his head and glance, seeing the young girl swept away by the spatial storm, disappearing from his sight.

He opened his mouth, but in the end, said nothing.

For the sake of Wuyou Realm Lord's face, for the sake of the favor Fang Qingxue had shown by using her flower basket to protect him when they entered this place, he had not stood by and watched her die. While he still had strength to spare, he had protected her.

But now, he could barely save himself. In the end, he could only look after his own future.

What followed was wave after wave of spatial storm impacts. Luo Chen was caught in the middle, like a fisherman in a tsunami.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron was pushed to its limit time and again, its gray light constantly blooming.

That was the power of complete laws, enough to resist the law fragments in the chaotic energy.

But human strength had its limits. The Primordial Unity Cauldron was, after all, not yet a legendary Dao artifact.

When the terrifying seven consecutive storms swept past, the gray cauldron had no more radiance, and even a crack appeared on its surface.

Luo Chen endured the pain and withdrew it into his body.

For the rest of the journey, he would have to rely on his newly cultivated Chaos Indestructible Body to endure!

Not only that, but he also drove the dormant Vacuum God Gu within his body.

Although the Vacuum God Gu could now only trigger weak spatial turbulence, unable to directly contend with these spatial storms, it could at least have some effect.

But unexpectedly... the further he advanced, the smaller and fewer the spatial storms he encountered!

It was as if that wave of seven consecutive storms had been the last darkness before the dawn.

"What a pity."

The image of the young girl Fang Qingxue's melancholy face before she left flashed through Luo Chen's mind. He could only take steps forward alone.

And the destination in his mind became clearer and clearer, as if it were just one step away.

The Vacuum God Gu seemed to sense this. It spread its dreamlike starlight wings and carried Luo Chen flying outward.
