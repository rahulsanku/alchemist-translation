# Chapter 1254: Demon-Scrutinizing Treasure Mirror, Four Generals Assembled

Within the Demon-Suppressing Tower.

A vortex was slowly rotating.

The power of the formation activated, and figures flew out from within it one after another.

Everyone who emerged couldn't help but show expressions of relief.

A small number, however, were different, still carrying a sense of lingering dissatisfaction.

Yun Dongjun of the Great Soul Gate was one such person.

"A pity. I had clearly entered an enlightened state by borrowing the spiritual opportunity, and was on the verge of comprehending the true meaning of the laws, only to be forcibly interrupted."

"In the end, it was still a matter of insufficient time. All blame that bitch Ding Ling'er."

"Still, in half a year's time, I managed to condense my primordial spirit ahead of schedule. After returning to the sect, I can attempt to break through to the Ascendant stage."

Regarding breakthroughs to the Ascendant stage, there had always been only three methods.

Either follow the rules strictly, relying on years of accumulation to forcefully break through. This method had the lowest probability; out of a hundred Sage cultivators, it was rare for even a few to succeed.

Then there were the other two methods: condensing the primordial spirit in advance, or comprehending the true meaning of the laws ahead of time.

Regardless of which, as long as a cultivator had sufficient talent and accomplished it early, the subsequent breakthrough to the Ascendant stage would be much easier, naturally resulting in a success rate above fifty percent.

With the aid of secret techniques, this success rate could be increased even further.

As Yun Dongjun regretted, his gaze unconsciously swept across the entire scene.

The first ones he saw were naturally Ding Ling'er and Xiao Fufeng.

Facing Yun Dongjun's gaze, those two deliberately looked away.

"This grudge, I have noted it. Once I break through to the Ascendant stage, I will come back to deal with you two."

Sneering inwardly, his gaze swept over other places.

Most of the sect cultivators now appeared isolated and alone.

This included the two major sects, the Biluo Sect and the Shadow Sect. Of the ten people each had sent to participate in the Demon-Suppressing Trial, only a scant few remained.

The ugly expressions on Green Butterfly Fairy and the others were enough to tell of the severity of their losses.

The Azure Dragon Sect and the Five Elements Divine Sect were not much better off, with only four survivors each.

Yun Dongjun sighed, understanding why this was.

That Void Refining Demon Spirit that had charged out from the Central Demon Abyss was simply too powerful.

Under its wanton slaughter, the casualty rate among the disciples of the various sects participating in the Demon-Suppressing Trial had reached two-thirds of the historical average, or even more.

This could be seen from the number of Dao Sect Sage cultivators who were gradually being pulled out afterward.

This was the most powerful and numerous force in the Demon-Suppressing Trial, with over a hundred people.

Yet now, only a dozen or so remained.

The strongest and most numerous naturally made them the most obvious target, and they had suffered the greatest blow.

However, one thing was somewhat unexpected.

Yun Dongjun's gaze fell upon a group of people in the corner.

A full six people!

And each of them had obvious fluctuations of magical power emanating from their bodies.

That indicated that they had all gained tremendous benefits during the trial, to the point where their breakthroughs in cultivation level were so recent that they hadn't had time to stabilize their realms, hence the outward fluctuations of magical power.

In particular, the composition of their cultivation levels was very unique.

Four late-stage Sage cultivators, one early-stage Sage cultivator, and one Ascendant cultivator!

"It's the Sushan Sect!"

Yun Dongjun remembered these people. Before the trial, this group had lived next door to them, and their faces were all familiar.

But he hadn't expected them to maintain a relatively complete number of personnel, and each of them had gained something.

"That one is Sage Zhibei. With accumulated experience finally bearing fruit, he actually broke through to the Ascendant stage in one go."

Yun Dongjun was very envious.

And thinking of Ascendant cultivators, he unconsciously recalled the person who had saved him earlier.

Was it a Dao Sect Ascendant?

He unconsciously swept his gaze across the entire scene.

But he found that of the ten Dao Sect Ascendants who had entered earlier, only three had now appeared intact within the Demon-Suppressing Tower.

Several other powerful auras were involuntarily revealing themselves.

They were primordial spirits!

Yun Dongjun dared not look too closely, fearing he might offend those primordial spirits.

He understood that those exposed primordial spirits belonged to the Dao Sect Ascendants who had fallen victim to the Void Refining Demon Spirit within the demon lair.

They were either severely injured, unable to conceal their primordial spirit aura, or their physical bodies had collapsed, with only their primordial spirits managing to escape.

However, to his surprise, none of these Dao Sect Ascendants matched the one who had saved him.

"Could it be that it wasn't a Dao Sect Ascendant, but rather some cultivator from another sect who happened to break through to the Ascendant stage during the trial and saved me by chance?"

As he was pondering this, more people continued to emerge from the vortex.

When a tall, handsome young man with a lean figure walked out, the Sushan Sect group exclaimed in surprise and gathered around him.

Yun Dongjun also glanced at him in passing, but quickly looked away.

A mid-stage Sage cultivator.

Although it was strange that the Sushan Sect had sent such a low-realm cultivator to participate in the trial, it had nothing to do with him.

He was not the person he was looking for.

...

"Xuan Luo!"

"Junior Brother Xuan Luo, you're fine. Thank goodness."

"I knew you were still alive. I always said you had the blessings of heaven."

"Are you injured anywhere?"

The moment Luo Chen appeared, his fellow disciples from the Sushan Sect surrounded him, showering him with concern and inquiries.

Such a lively scene naturally attracted the attention of cultivators from other sects.

Especially since the Sushan Sect still had so many survivors, making them even more conspicuous.

Dean He coughed lightly, signaling everyone to quiet down.

Then he looked at Luo Chen with some surprise. "Eh, Xuan Luo, your cultivation level?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Disciple discovered a demon lair that had been scattered by someone in the outer area. Not only did I use the demon marrow crystals I picked up to remove my demonic contamination, but I also took the opportunity to cultivate in seclusion for three years. By a stroke of luck, I broke through to the mid-Sage stage."

Three years' time, leaping directly from early Sage to mid-Sage—this would be shocking news to the outside world.

However, the few members of the Sushan Sect had no doubt at all.

Because they too had experienced that heaven-defying cultivation environment where spiritual apertures formed and vast amounts of spiritual energy permeated the air.

Except for Jiang Huang, who needed to remove her demonic contamination and hadn't gained much in terms of cultivation level, the rest had all benefited to varying degrees.

Yao Xi had even managed to grasp a hint of the true meaning of the laws.

Dean He, in particular, had broken through to the Ascendant stage after years of accumulated experience!

How could they doubt that Xuan Luo had encountered such an opportunity? Instead, they were overjoyed.

Yao Xi asked with a hint of reproach, "Why didn't you use the Lotus Brand's resonance to rendezvous with us after you entered?"

Luo Chen answered seriously, "It wasn't that disciple was unwilling, but the distance was simply too great. I couldn't sense any of you esteemed elders at all. Moreover, with my low strength, I was afraid of holding everyone back. Since I happened to find a suitable place, I simply found a spot to cultivate in seclusion."

"You..." Yao Xi was both annoyed and fond, annoyed that this disciple had made everyone worry for so long, yet fond of his consideration for his fellow disciples.

Her good friend Youhuang smiled and interjected, "If Xuan Luo was in the outer area for too long, it would indeed be difficult for him to sense us, since we entered the middle zone very early on."

The straightforward Tie Ma also remarked with emotion, "Actually, it might not be a bad thing. If Xuan Luo had rushed to rendezvous with us, he might have run into that Void Refining Demon Spirit and lost his life. Now, he has not only succeeded in purging the demon but also greatly increased his cultivation level. What's not to be happy about?"

Jiang Huang stood behind Luo Chen, looking at his back, and a certain feeling in her heart grew stronger.

"Junior Brother..."

"Congratulations to Senior Sister as well for successfully purging the demon. Although your cultivation level hasn't increased much, you will surely have smooth sailing in your future cultivation!"

Luo Chen turned around with a smile and said this to Jiang Huang.

Jiang Huang bit her lip and nodded gently.

She suppressed that feeling.

It probably wasn't him!

"Alright, we'll talk about everything once we get out!"

Dean He timely interrupted everyone's concern for Luo Chen, although he himself was more worried about Luo Chen than anyone else. But the fact that he was still alive was enough.

At this moment, the main priority was to deal with the current situation.

The trial was over, so why was the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect still keeping everyone confined within the Demon-Suppressing Tower, including the Dao Sect cultivators themselves?

Time passed quickly. When the last trial cultivator walked out of the vortex, it disappeared in an instant.

Just then.

A broad and grand voice echoed from within the tower, carrying a hint of intimidation.

"Everyone, hand over your Demon Spirit Gourds!"

No one hesitated. Each person handed over the Demon Spirit Gourd that had been issued earlier.

Most of the demon-removing liquid inside had been consumed, but there was still some surplus, related to the fact that Vermilion Bird True Lord had personally taken action to wipe out too many demon lairs.

Some of the demon-removing liquid had no use left.

A few people could not produce their Demon Spirit Gourds, claiming they had been destroyed in the previous battles.

The broad voice from above did not pursue the matter with those few.

After all the Demon Spirit Gourds had disappeared within the Demon-Suppressing Tower, the voice sounded again.

"Next, you may leave the Demon-Suppressing Tower. However, when you leave, do not restrain your aura. Relax your mind and spirit. There will be a small inspection. If you resist too much, it may easily invite backlash."

Everyone was somewhat surprised.

But hearing that they could leave this place where they had stayed for three years, they were naturally eager to do so.

Luo Chen followed the crowd as they walked out, his gaze unconsciously looking upward toward the exit.

There, unknown to him, a rhomboid bronze mirror had been suspended, emitting a pure white light.

"A treasure used to detect the mind and spirit, inspect magical power, and identify demonic spirits?"

This speculation was verified as those ahead passed through the light of the bronze mirror.

When each person passed through, more or less black qi emerged from their bodies, but the amount was not large, very slight.

This was caused by being in the demon lair environment for too long.

Although it wouldn't lead to a state of demonic contamination, their magical power had become somewhat impure.

"Everyone, don't panic. Just do as Black Tortoise True Lord instructed."

Dean He comforted everyone, then strode boldly through the exit of the Demon-Suppressing Tower.

The light of the bronze mirror shone upon him, and the pure white light instantly turned a blazing red, very much in line with Dean He's own magical power attribute.

Watching this scene, Luo Chen had already formed an idea in his mind.

The principle of this bronze mirror was perhaps similar to the Heart-Inquiring Mirror he had obtained in his early years, and the Dustbound Immortal Realm he had later refined.

They were all products of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's artifact-refining methods.

However, in terms of grade, this one was much higher, likely already at the level of a spirit treasure.

If that were the case, it would actually be much easier.

A rigid object without intelligence was harder to fool.

And the artifact spirit of a spirit treasure generally had low intelligence, acting only on instinct and its master's commands.

He wouldn't even need to conceal anything. He just needed to...

Luo Chen's mind moved. The Blood Cloth Robe covering his body retracted into his body, and his magical power shifted, instantly becoming sharp and aggressive.

When he stepped across that bronze mirror, golden light immediately surged forth.

Then a certain consciousness fell upon him. Luo Chen faced it calmly.

That consciousness observed for a while, did not linger, and directly departed.

Huff...

In the span of a breath, Luo Chen's figure appeared outside the tower.

Looking around, he saw Dean He and Elder Gumu and others waiting with concern. The Ascendant elders of other sects also showed anxious expressions as they waited bitterly.

Besides that...

Luo Chen's gaze swept over the area near the Demon-Suppressing Tower. At some point, a large number of cultivator troops had been stationed here.

Each team was led by an Ascendant cultivator, densely surrounding the Demon-Suppressing Tower.

Moreover, in Luo Chen's perception, several other powerful auras were hidden in the shadows.

"Have they even sent True Lord-level experts?"

Thinking this, he kept walking and arrived before his fellow disciples.

"Greetings, Elder Gumu!"

Elder Gumu examined Luo Chen for a moment, then nodded with a gratified smile. "Good, good, good. Go wait at the back."

He was happy, but the Ascendant elders of the other sects could not be happy. Their expressions were as gloomy as water.

Earlier, they had heard that this Demon-Suppressing Trial was different from before, more dangerous.

But danger often went hand in hand with reward. Since they weren't going in themselves, they hadn't been too worried.

However, when the three years had passed and the true results were laid before them, many still could not accept it.

The casualty rate was as high as one-third!

Some Ascendant elders of certain sects had even learned from the cultivators who had come out earlier that their sect's Sage cultivators had been completely wiped out.

This made the elders of the remaining sects even more worried.

"Why aren't they letting them out yet? It's never been this slow before!"

"I imagine it has something to do with that Void Refining Demon Spirit. It was the culprit behind such heavy casualties."

"Damn it. In a thousand years, not even we can cultivate to the Void Refining stage. How could a high-level demon spirit of the Void Refining level appear inside?"

"Regardless, with such a major incident, it's understandable that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is being cautious. Fellow Daoists, please be patient!"

The last sentence was spoken by Elder Gumu.

He was quite relaxed, but the others had no good expressions.

It was said that in this Demon-Suppressing Trial, the Sushan Sect had suffered the fewest losses and gained the most.

Not to mention anything else, just the Ascendant aura emanating from Sage Zhibei, Helan Shi, standing beside Elder Gumu, was enough to make up for all losses.

The nearly one hundred trial cultivators were checked one by one. It didn't actually take much time.

Although some people with dense demonic qi took a bit longer, within an hour, all the trial cultivators had come out of the Demon-Suppressing Tower.

Compared to the initial three hundred trial participants, the current scale of less than a hundred—including the Dao Sect Sage cultivators—was a result that many sects found hard to accept.

But no matter how grieved they were, they could only return to their sects now.

Just as everyone was about to disperse, a figure appeared before them.

"Don't forget the tradition of past trials. Cultivators who successfully emerge from the Demon-Suppressing Trial may enter the Dao Sect for a visit and tour!"

"So, please!"

A large ship, covered in restrictions and exuding a fierce aura, had already floated above the Demon-Suppressing Tower.

The Dao Sect Ascendant smiled kindly at the cultivators of the various sects and extended his hand in an inviting gesture.

...

Inside the Demon-Suppressing Tower.

After everyone had left, four figures materialized.

Three men and one woman!

One was as ancient as decaying withered wood, one was brimming with surging killing intent and fierce energy, one was broad, gentle, and scholarly with the bearing of an elder, and the last one had a rather low and depressed aura.

The three gazes fell upon Vermilion Bird True Lord, each with a different meaning.

"Junior Sister Vermilion Bird, are you alright?"

"It's fine. With Senior Brother Black Tortoise's help, the situation has been temporarily stabilized. After returning to the sect and cultivating in seclusion for a while, I should be able to recover."

"That's good!"

"Senior Brother Azure Dragon, why did the Sect Master only send you here? Why didn't he come himself? After all, that was a Demon Realm Saint Ancestor. Relying on us alone, I'm afraid we can't hold the line."

"The Demon Realm Saint Ancestor is powerful, but in the end, he hasn't crossed over. Besides, hasn't the seal not been broken yet? As for the Sect Master, he has other important matters. Don't ask."

After Azure Dragon True Lord finished speaking, another rough and overbearing voice rang out.

"So what if it's a Demon Realm Saint Ancestor? Under the immense pressure of crossing between two realms to fight across the boundary, he can't even exert one or two percent of his full strength. Vermilion Bird, to be severely injured under such circumstances, it seems you've been slacking in your cultivation these past years!"

Vermilion Bird True Lord glanced at the middle-aged man full of killing intent and chuckled lightly. "White Tiger, it seems you have a lot of experience with cross-realm battles?"

White Tiger was taken aback, then snorted coldly and said nothing more.

Black Tortoise True Lord sighed. "Enough. Let's first look at the results of the Demon-Scrutinizing Treasure Mirror's inquiry. There are a few worth noting. Come with me to examine them, then send the list to the sect so they can proceed with the next round of screening."

As he spoke, he reached out and beckoned.

A stream of light flew out from within the Demon-Suppressing Tower, transforming into a rhomboid treasure mirror.

Then, figures began to appear on its surface.

With just one glance, White Tiger True Lord's gaze fell upon a lean young man.
