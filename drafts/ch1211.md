# Chapter 1211: Terrifying Talent, Another Divine Ability Gained

When Luo Chen returned from Step Pill Court to the mountain-foot hall of Marvelous Pill Peak, he was greeted by gazes filled with complex emotions.

But beneath all those emotions lay a thick layer of envy.

After all, most of those still remaining in the hall were failures who hadn't passed the talent assessment and were paying their own way to learn alchemy. Naturally, they were immensely envious of Luo Chen's success on his first try.

Luo Chen paid them no heed. Using his Alchemy Court token, he selected a room.

After closing the door, he first sat quietly for a while.

Only after sweeping his divine sense around and confirming no one was watching did he carefully open his mouth and spit something out.

A faint green light, wreathed in fiery aura.

It was the Withering-Bloom Fire Seed!

After three years of absorbing earth vein fire qi in Tai Su Immortal City, the Withering-Bloom Fire Seed had recovered a sliver of its vitality.

Though not much, its condition was far better than the deathly stillness before.

Now that he was in the Spirit Herb Sect, and moreover in the Alchemy Court—a place with abundant fire veins—Luo Chen naturally wouldn't waste the opportunity.

"Go!"

With a toss of his hand, the Withering-Bloom Fire Seed drilled into the earth fire vent.

Under Luo Chen's divine sense control, the fire seed continuously shifted and sank, plunging deep into the fire vein.

This way, it wouldn't be easily discovered.

After doing this, Luo Chen casually tossed out his hand, and streaks of light flew out one after another.

There was a pill furnace, a book, and seven or eight types of very young medicinal herbs.

This was the Alchemy Court's way of training its disciples.

"Quite generous," Luo Chen remarked.

At least the Luo Tian Sect he had founded back then had never been this generous in training unranked alchemists.

Whether it was the low-grade pill furnace or those medicinal herbs, they all cost a fair number of spirit stones.

Back then, when the Luo Tian Sect trained alchemists, they went through a long, tedious process of identifying herbs, tending fires, and doing miscellaneous chores.

It would take years before they'd let someone actually start refining pills.

Only the Spirit Herb Sect, which specialized in the medicinal herb business, could afford to be so lavish.

Luo Chen picked up the thick book.

*Preliminary Introduction to the Dao of Alchemy*

Luo Chen calmed his mind and quickly skimmed through this alchemy manual issued by the Alchemy Court.

He hadn't known what to expect, but upon reading it, he realized how profound the author's intentions were.

The book didn't start with various alchemy practice methods. Instead, it recorded a large amount of alchemical theory.

Beginners might find it confusing at first glance, but once they actually started practicing, these theories would be verified one by one in practice.

It could be called a general outline!

After that, it listed three pill formulas.

They were the Inedia Pill, the Golden Deficiency Powder, and the Foundation Strengthening Liquid.

One was for fasting, one for strengthening the body and nourishing the five organs, and the last for expanding spiritual power.

These three formulas not only came with detailed main and auxiliary ingredients and their proportions, but also the corresponding alchemical techniques needed for refining.

The only thing missing was probably the details of fire control during refining and the timing for extracting the pills.

And this missing part, in Luo Chen's view, was precisely the most profound aspect that beginning alchemists needed to master.

After the three pill formulas came the "Hundred Herbs" section, which took up the largest portion of *Preliminary Introduction to the Dao of Alchemy*.

The number of medicinal herbs recorded there, along with illustrations, reached as many as ten thousand types!

There were even some low-grade herbs that Luo Chen didn't recognize.

After Luo Chen quickly finished scanning *Preliminary Introduction to the Dao of Alchemy*, he fell into silence for a while.

It was a long time before he let out a leisurely sigh.

"What a truly good book!"

To be honest, this book was almost no help to Luo Chen. It only let him learn two first-grade pill formulas and some low-grade herbs.

What truly made him sigh was the book's exquisite design, its detailed content, and the depth of the author's intentions.

First, it gave alchemical theory as a general outline.

Then it listed pill formulas and techniques, progressing from shallow to deep, allowing beginners to gradually master alchemy through practice.

Finally, it appended a massive collection of herb illustrations to build a solid foundation for beginners.

One could say that if someone perfectly followed this *Preliminary Introduction to the Dao of Alchemy* to learn alchemy, even without a master's guidance, they could achieve some success.

It would also provide a deep foundation for later refining higher-grade pills.

In comparison, the alchemical inheritance Luo Chen had left in the Luo Tian Sect was much cruder.

But Luo Chen didn't belittle himself.

It wasn't that he was inferior to the author; it was just that the author, backed by the Spirit Herb Sect's abundant resources, could compile such a logically sound introductory textbook.

"Author Gongsun Mao. I'll remember that name."

Luo Chen put down the book, pondered for a moment, and then began to work.

He planned to first refine a first-grade pill and become a formal alchemist.

The alchemy talent he had displayed earlier now needed to be turned into real results, laying the groundwork for his later stunning achievements.

"I'll go with the Inedia Pill. One of the most familiar pills to me."

Luo Chen smiled, set up the pill furnace over the earth fire vent, and began feeding in the herbs needed for the Inedia Pill.

...

In the mountain-foot hall of Marvelous Pill Peak.

Figures stood at their respective earth fire vents, practicing controlling the flames with their spiritual power. Everyone's face was taut with concentration.

Suddenly!

A faint, barely perceptible fragrance drifted over from behind.

Someone sniffed the air and was startled.

"Pill fragrance?"

"So concentrated. Could it be the aroma of a first-grade pill?"

"Who's refining a first-grade pill here?"

"Could it be that guy?"

Amidst the murmuring, everyone unconsciously stopped what they were doing.

Incredulity was written on every face.

That guy had taken his talent assessment in the morning, and by the afternoon, he'd already refined a first-grade pill?

Impossible, right?

But when Luo Chen strode out, his body wreathed in thick pill fragrance, impossibility became reality.

"Junior apprentice-brother, do you know how to get to the Pill Hall of Marvelous Pill Peak?"

A Qi Refinement cultivator stared blankly at Luo Chen and instinctively pointed upward.

"At the peak."

Luo Chen smiled and nodded. "Thank you."

With that, he walked away.

Behind him, the crowd exchanged bewildered looks.

After a long while, someone withdrew his spiritual power, dispersing his control over the earth fire. He patted his dust-covered Dao robe with a look of dejection.

"Senior Apprentice-Brother Zhou, your practice time for today isn't up yet. Aren't you going to continue?" someone beside him asked in confusion.

Senior Apprentice-Brother Zhou sighed, looked at Luo Chen's retreating figure, and smiled bitterly. "What's the point of continuing? People with talent just pick it up and succeed—they can do whatever they want. But those of us without talent, no matter how well we practice the basics, will at most become low-grade alchemists. Forget it. I won't waste these spirit stones anymore."

With a sigh, Senior Apprentice-Brother Zhou left dejectedly.

After he left, as if triggering a chain reaction, several others gradually departed.

Clearly, these people had all been discouraged by Luo Chen's stunning performance.

Luo Chen naturally knew nothing of this.

Leaving the hall, he mounted a magical flying sword issued by the sect and headed straight for the mountain peak.

Of course he knew where the Pill Hall of Marvelous Pill Peak was. Even the locations of various pill rooms and cave dwellings on the mountain were clearly visible under his divine sense.

Asking that question earlier was just to appear normal.

Arriving at the Pill Hall, Luo Chen glanced thoughtfully into its depths, then approached a duty alchemist.

He took out the Inedia Pill he had just refined for inspection.

After it was done, an extra cloud pattern appeared on his identity token.

From then on, he was a First-Cloud Alchemist of the Spirit Herb Sect's Alchemy Court.

Becoming a First-Cloud Alchemist came with different treatment.

Previously, he could only receive one batch of first-grade pill materials per month. Now he could receive three batches per month.

He promptly applied for the materials for the Golden Deficiency Powder and the Foundation Strengthening Liquid as well.

The duty alchemist didn't make things difficult for him and approved the application without trouble, telling him to go directly to Step Pill Court to collect the herbs.

As for the shock Luo Chen caused Lü Su in Step Pill Court, that goes without saying.

After Luo Chen left, the duty alchemist, seeing no one else around, hurried back to the inner hall.

The inner hall was bustling with noise.

Hundreds of low-grade alchemists were gathered together, engaged in heated discussion.

"The Zhouliu Circulation Qi Pill is an ancient incomplete formula to begin with. Completing it is already difficult enough—how can we talk about mass production?"

"Exactly. Just cultivating the required main ingredients is hard enough. The quantities needed for mass production are simply impossible."

"But Master Gongsun has already found several substitute herbs and is in the process of verifying them. If we can help him perfect the idea and verify the substitutes, then our Spirit Herb Sect will have a unique second-grade pill."

"Second-grade? Isn't the Zhouliu Circulation Qi Pill third-grade?"

"After the main ingredients are replaced, the medicinal power will naturally decrease. Why else would Master Gongsun not conduct his experiments at Spirit Pill Peak and instead come to our Marvelous Pill Peak..."

...

He Jun was also among the discussion crowd.

But because he had stepped out earlier, he was still just listening and hadn't joined the conversation.

Hearing movement behind him, he instinctively turned his head.

"Junior Apprentice-Brother Peng, what's wrong?"

Junior Apprentice-Brother Peng waved his hand. "Nothing major. Just a fellow disciple who refined an Inedia Pill and became a first-grade alchemist. I helped him process the paperwork so he can apply for more herb shares later."

"Oh."

He Jun nodded, not finding it strange.

It was just a first-grade alchemist, after all.

Just as he was about to rejoin the discussion on "mass production of the Zhouliu Circulation Qi Pill," his heart stirred, and he casually asked, "Which junior apprentice-brother was it?"

Junior Apprentice-Brother Peng still remembered the young man. "His name is Xuan Luo."

He Jun blurted out, "Xuan Luo from Jade Spring Peak?"

Junior Apprentice-Brother Peng was puzzled. "How did you know?"

He Jun opened his mouth, somewhat incredulous. After a long pause, he said faintly, "He passed the talent test this morning. I was the one who presided over it."

This time, even Junior Apprentice-Brother Peng was stunned.

...

Kunping Cave Dwelling.

Named for its location in a well-shaped basin that resembled a bottle mouth, this was where Luo Chen now lived.

After leaving the Alchemy Court, he had returned directly to Kunping Cave Dwelling without visiting the other three courts or four halls.

Things had to be done step by step. There was no need to rush.

Besides, he had endured five hundred years of seclusion in the Guiyuan Hall. What was a little more time?

Back in his cave dwelling, he didn't even study the formulas for the Golden Deficiency Powder and Foundation Strengthening Liquid much. Instead, he opened the jade slip that Yao Xi had bestowed upon him that morning.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged, the jade slip spread before him.

Under his divine sense perception, three restrictions were laid upon it.

These three divine sense seals locked the Adept chapter, the Sage chapter, and the Ascendant chapter respectively.

Their purpose was to prevent Luo Chen from reading the cultivation technique ahead of time and becoming impatient for quick results.

But for Luo Chen, Yao Xi's concern was unnecessary.

Moreover, these divine sense seals couldn't stop him.

His divine sense flowed, plunging straight into the jade slip like a knife cutting through tofu. In an instant, he broke through the three divine sense imprints.

Suddenly, golden light blazed forth!

Characters seemed to come alive, appearing before Luo Chen like a jade wall.

Looking up, all the characters fell into his eyes.

The Qi Refinement and Initiate chapters, he had long since perfected.

They were nothing more than:

[Gold Essence Transforms Qi, Spirit Illuminates All Meridians]

[Apertures Store the Pivot of Arms, Molten Gold Enters the Sea]

And the general outlines of the three subsequent realms were also presented one by one.

[Dan Inscribes Mystic Chapter, Three Treasures Gather Gold]

[Supreme Yi Mystic Pivot, Golden Palace Dao Domain]

[Sage to Ascendant, Origin Source Forms Manifestation]

"So this is the *Supreme Yi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Mystic Chapter*? The higher the realm, the more extraordinary it becomes."

Upon his first browsing, Luo Chen could perceive its subtlety.

If the first two realms hadn't yet completely distinguished themselves from common cultivation techniques, then starting from the Adept stage, the cultivation method became entirely different.

Not only did one need to condense a golden core, but one also had to inscribe a treasure seal within the core using divine sense.

This way, when the three treasures of essence, qi, and spirit merged, the treasure seal would assist, making breaking through to the Sage realm much easier.

Moreover, this [Mystic Chapter Treasure Seal] could be nurtured as a natal magic treasure, becoming more powerful at later stages.

Even the Purple Mansion formed would differ from ordinary ones, called the Supreme Yi Mystic Pivot.

The Sage domain formed would be called the Golden Palace Dao Domain, with power far exceeding that of same-rank domains.

Most importantly!

This scripture completely recorded the techniques for cultivating a dharma manifestation at the Ascendant stage.

For Luo Chen, this was like finding a pillow just when he was feeling sleepy.

He had been lacking cultivation manuals for this very aspect.

Beyond that, the *Supreme Yi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Mystic Chapter* also contained a large number of spells, and even a natal divine ability!

Luo Chen set aside the high-grade metal spells for now.

He studied the natal divine ability in detail, and the more he looked, the more he felt its grandeur!

Starting from the Adept stage, one inscribed the [Mystic Chapter Treasure Seal]; at the Sage stage, one derived the [Golden Palace Dao Domain]; upon reaching the Ascendant stage, one comprehended the origin of metal. Then, using these two methods to draw upon the origin qi of heaven and earth, one could execute a great divine ability called the *Soldier Realm Torrent*.

According to the description, when this technique was unleashed, ten thousand weapons would fly forth like a mighty torrent, enough to destroy everything!

"This is what a top-tier cultivation technique looks like!"

Luo Chen let out another similar sigh.

Originally, he had only planned to practice it casually to satisfy Yao Xi's spot checks.

But now it seemed this cultivation technique was worth cultivating seriously.

There was much to borrow from it. If he could transplant it into his five-element simultaneous cultivation, it might yield unexpected effects.

After all, Luo Chen would later need to re-condense his golden core, nascent soul, and even primordial spirit.

Next, Luo Chen began to seriously study the *Supreme Yi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Mystic Chapter*, constantly comparing it with his past cultivation path.

He truly benefited greatly.

In his view, the Mystic Chapter Treasure Seal had similarities with the sword seed condensed from the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* he had cultivated in the past.

Both cultivation methods also carried the meaning of "myriad transformations."

That meant he could completely use this cultivation technique to recreate that *Thousand Skies Converging Flow*, a technique comparable to a divine ability.

And not only that.

What if the inscribed Mystic Chapter Treasure Seal wasn't limited to the metal element, but encompassed all five elements?

While studying the cultivation technique, Luo Chen didn't stop his alchemy practice in the outside world.

Over the next three months, he successively refined the Golden Deficiency Powder and the Foundation Strengthening Liquid, two first-grade pills.

He began to attract attention on Marvelous Pill Peak.

Until He Jun came calling again.

"Master Gongsun will come to Marvelous Pill Peak tomorrow to host a pill-testing assembly. Are you interested in attending as an observer?"
