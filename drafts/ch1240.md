# Chapter 1240: Chaos Myriad Spirit Flame, the Might of a True Lord’s Chariot

With a casual wave of his hand, flames swirled.

The fiery tree and golden blossoms vanished, reverting to their most primordial form—a flickering flame that settled into Luo Chen’s palm.

Sensing the lively fire spirit within, a smile tugged at the corners of Luo Chen’s mouth. His divine sense gently extended, carefully examining the current state of the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame.

A hundred years of nurturing within a fire vein, plus a Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame as a seed, and seven Two Extremes Pills—all of this had finally revived its slumbering spirituality.

But in terms of power, it had yet to recover to its peak.

This was within Luo Chen’s expectations.

After all, back when it had protected him, it had completely drained its origin true flame’s power, nearly extinguishing itself entirely.

During this century of fire-vein nurturing, Luo Chen hadn’t dared to make too much noise, lest he draw attention. So the firepower he had absorbed was somewhat insufficient.

That Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame, though possessing a fifth-grade rank, only had the power of a third-grade flame—much like the Nirvana Sacred Flame Luo Chen had first obtained. Its rank was high, but its strength was weak.

For the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame, it was hardly a substantial supplement.

The newly awakened Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit even clearly transmitted a signal of “hunger.”

It was very hungry!

It needed food!

And Luo Chen had always known the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame’s diet.

Either beings imbued with powerful vitality, or various source-less fires.

Fortunately, in anticipation of the fire spirit’s revival, Luo Chen had indeed prepared a little “snack” for it.

“I wonder if you’ll be accustomed to eating a cultivator’s self-cultivated origin divine flame?”

With a soft chuckle, Luo Chen’s nostrils flared slightly. As he exhaled, two wisps of true flame slowly drifted out.

One was black, radiating a biting cold, like the solid ice of an ancient, frozen pool.

The other was white, its scorching heat stinging the face, as if facing the great sun directly.

The moment the two wisps of true flame appeared, they transformed into two yin-yang fish, quickly intertwining, their heads and tails connected, forming a taiji pattern.

The entire space of the meditation chamber seemed to grow somewhat illusory at that moment.

If any knowledgeable expert from the demon realm were present, they would surely recognize this intertwined divine flame—the Yin-Yang Extreme Dao Flame!

Not naturally formed, but a high-grade divine flame cultivated by human effort.

It simultaneously contained the extreme cold of water and the extreme heat of fire.

Its power was immense, capable of both freezing space and melting flesh and blood. It was one of the most difficult divine flames to cultivate.

Aside from the earliest, unverifiable demonic sage ancestor, anyone else trying to cultivate this thing faced extreme difficulty. Even with the aid of Two Extremes Pills, it was by no means easy.

But for Luo Chen, what had once seemed troublesome—the Yin-Yang Extreme Dao Flame—he had effortlessly cultivated in just the ten-plus days he had spent waiting for the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit to revive.

The reason it was so simple was closely related to his profound foundation in the immortal path.

Luo Chen himself had comprehended the two fundamental laws of water and fire. Coupled with his long experience in refining flames, and finally utilizing the remaining three Two Extremes Pills, it was only natural that he succeeded in one go!

However, the quantity wasn’t very large. This thing consumed a great deal of mana, especially for a Sage-level cultivator.

That was why Luo Chen called it a “little snack.”

Gazing at the newborn taiji yin-yang fish, the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit suddenly darted forward, extending countless branches that surrounded it.

It looked utterly salivating.

This familiar scene made Luo Chen chuckle silently.

It reminded him of when the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit had faced the golden bird fire spirit of the Nirvana Sacred Flame, and the blue swimming fish of the Shark Emperor True Flame.

In the past, Luo Chen had suppressed the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit’s natural devouring nature in pursuit of having more trump cards.

But now, Luo Chen would no longer suppress it!

One didn’t need too many trump cards—just enough strength.

Otherwise, no matter how many trump cards one had, if faced with an opponent whose strength far exceeded one’s own, even with cards in hand, they couldn’t be played.

So.

“Eat!”

At Luo Chen’s command, the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit joyfully pounced.

Or rather, countless branches plunged directly in, sucking up the two struggling fish like a straw and swallowing them in one gulp.

If any demon realm expert saw this, they might be utterly heartbroken.

But Luo Chen couldn’t care less.

He had already understood the principle of this flame. As long as he was willing to expend mana, he could refine a few more at any time.

What he cared about was something else.

Watching the flame, now sated, swaying back and forth in his palm, his expression turned somewhat strange.

“This little thing has devoured so many different third- and fourth-grade source-less fires, absorbed the Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame containing the metal origin, and the Yin-Yang Extreme Dao Flame of water and fire dual origins. The original Withering-Bloom true intent within it, though still present, has been greatly diluted.”

“Can it still be called the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame?”

After another near-death rebirth, the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit had grown even closer to Luo Chen.

As if sensing what Luo Chen was thinking, the fire spirit suddenly leaped from his palm.

Then, the flames blazed brightly!

A ten-foot-tall fire tree rose from the ground.

Luo Chen looked and was momentarily stunned.

The fire tree’s branches forked, and on each branch, the source-less fires it had once absorbed were fully displayed.

The most recent ones: the Yin-Yang Extreme Dao Flame and the Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame.

Some from long ago, lower-grade source-less fires.

Such as the translucent Glazed Fire, the blooming Red Lotus Earth Fire, the colorful, butterfly-winged Multicolored Demon Butterfly Flame, the ethereal, cloud-like Burning Heaven Fire, and so on.

Even the Azure Yang Demon Flame Luo Chen had casually refined, and the Shark Emperor True Flame he had accidentally absorbed during a battle with Shark Emperor Liujun, had each grown a branch.

Finally, Luo Chen’s gaze froze. At the very top of the tree, he saw a faint phoenix silhouette.

“Nirvana Sacred Flame?”

In an instant, a shiver ran through his entire body.

But as his divine sense swept over it, he breathed a sigh of relief.

This golden phoenix silhouette was the Nirvana Sacred Flame, yet it wasn’t.

In terms of nature, it was almost indistinguishable from the Nirvana Sacred Flame Luo Chen had once possessed. But its core was still the flame now called the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame.

Luo Chen pondered, then asked, “Did you absorb and evolve it during those years you spent day and night with the Nirvana Fire Spirit?”

The fire tree swayed, its branches nodding.

Luo Chen smiled bitterly, mocking himself for his earlier overreaction.

Rosycloud had become a nightmare for him.

If he couldn’t eliminate this person, or face her again, he would never achieve a clear mind.

Perhaps it wouldn’t affect his next breakthrough to Ascendant, but when he later advanced to the Void Refining stage, or even the Body Integration stage where heavenly tribulations would descend, this obsession would surely come knocking.

Sighing, Luo Chen silently gazed at the “flamboyant” fire tree.

“Calling you the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame is no longer appropriate.”

“I cultivate the *Chaos Indestructible Scripture* and possess the Chaos Indestructible Body. As my origin true flame, you should naturally be named after Chaos. And since you devour all flames and have given birth to spirituality, I shall call you…”

“The Chaos Myriad Spirit Flame!”

A simple, crude naming method—but it had always been Luo Chen’s style.

The fire spirit, understanding his thoughts upon hearing its new name, tilted its trunk and propped up its branches, as if a person were thinking.

Seeing this pretentious act, Luo Chen couldn’t help but laugh and scold.

“Get in here!”

Opening his mouth, he inhaled. The fire tree turned into a stream of light, straight into Luo Chen’s Purple Mansion.

Once inside, the fire tree first felt a little unfamiliar, as if surprised that home had changed after so long away.

But it quickly grew bored with the new scenery. It directly rooted itself near the Nascent Soul, stretching out its form again, absorbing the occasional mana leaking from the Nascent Soul’s Dharma body.

Luo Chen patted his lower abdomen and nodded slightly.

This revival, though the fire tree’s power hadn’t reached its peak, its spirituality was far greater than before.

Perhaps it was the effect of the seven Two Extremes Pills.

That thing was originally refined from the withered-source fire marrow left after source-less fires departed. Besides cultivating the Yin-Yang Extreme Dao Flame, it also promoted the growth of all kinds of flames.

He didn’t have many materials left. In the future, he could conveniently collect more herbs and refine more, treating them as pills for the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame… no, for the Chaos Myriad Spirit Flame to eat.

As for now…

Luo Chen brushed his robes, flicking away the smoke and fire, and calmly rose to leave the alchemy chamber.

The one-month deadline had arrived. It was time to travel far and participate in the so-called Demon-Suppressing Trial.

…

That day.

The sky was clear, with not a cloud in sight for ten thousand li.

A massive object hovered at the mountain gate of the Sushu Sect.

It was neither a boat, nor a carriage, nor a chariot. It was, in fact, a palace complex of layered peaks and ridges.

Upon it stood halls and pavilions, countless towers and kiosks, exotic flowers and rare herbs everywhere, and cranes and auspicious beasts visible in every direction.

Countless streams of spiritual qi sprayed out from beneath it, forming ribbons that hung and swayed.

Even more dazzling, auspicious clouds rose up, conjuring many mirage scenes.

Truly a spectacle of a thousand sights!

Luo Chen stood outside the massive palace complex, a look of shock on his face.

It wasn’t feigned, but genuine.

“Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace! Is this the personal chariot of a Void Refining True Lord? What magnificent style!”

His arrival was not concealed in any way.

The cultivators atop the heavenly palace soon spotted him.

Immediately, a glazed golden bridge was laid down, as if to welcome him.

Luo Chen stepped up. When he reached the bridgehead, an elderly Daoist was waiting for him with a smile.

“Greetings, Uncle-Master Zhibei!”

He Lansi waved his hand. “Compared to the title of Uncle-Master, I’d rather hear you call me Dean.”

Luo Chen complied readily. “Dean, you’ve been waiting long.”

“Not long at all. I just arrived a little early.” He Lansi smiled slightly, then asked with a hint of pride, “Well, what do you think? Isn’t the True Lord’s chariot impressive?”

Luo Chen sighed with emotion. “Impressive is an understatement. It’s simply breathtaking!”

The Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace was the personal chariot of the current master of the Sushu Sect, Grand Pure Spirit Lord.

For this Demon-Suppressing Trial, to ensure the Sushu Sect didn’t lose face, it was lent to Elder Gumu to carry everyone to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

After hearing this, He Lansi also sighed. “Yes, when I first saw this palace, I was just as shocked as you are, unable to contain myself. From the outside, it’s a spectacle of several thousand zhang in circumference. But once you truly tour it, you’ll understand the secret of ‘mustard seed containing Sumeru’ within!”

Luo Chen nodded, already having a good idea.

He had just swept it with his divine sense from the outside.

It appeared to be only a few thousand zhang in scale, but even with his Ascendant-level peak divine sense, it took him some time to see it all.

In other words, the internal space had already exceeded ten thousand li!

This size was not just a palace; even the mountain gates of some Ascendant-level powers might not be this magnificent.

He Lansi continued, “A True Lord should have the bearing of a True Lord, especially as the master of a sect! This Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace was custom-made by the Lord Sect Master at great expense, inviting the top refining masters of the Nine Great Saint Sects’ Boundless Mountain. It took over a hundred years to complete. Inside, it contains countless spirit veins of all sizes. Even a fifth-grade spirit vein was moved in from within the sect. Ascendant-level great powers can also cultivate here long-term. Its functionality is very comprehensive: cultivation and residence, flying and travel, planting flowers and grass, raising spirit beasts, attack and defense—it can be said to cover everything. On ordinary days, the Sect Master doesn’t use this treasure much. But when it is used, even cross-continental travel won’t delay one’s own cultivation. Look over there. Can you feel the rich spiritual qi in that lake water? In fact, a spirit-eye spring was transplanted into it…”

Dean He’s introduction echoed in his ears, and Luo Chen nodded repeatedly.

But in his heart, that phrase echoed: “A True Lord should have the bearing of a True Lord!”

Speaking of it, he had actually seen a chariot of this level before!

In the Mountains and Seas Realm!

That Demon-Fall Land, considered a place of opportunity by countless Northern Sea cultivators, was in a sense, wasn’t it also the chariot of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining?

Its internal three-layer structure had its own style.

The Leaving Forgetting Heaven deterred the weak-minded, the Five Elements Heaven cultivated various spirit plants and ores, and the Bright Radiance Heaven was the true place for cultivation and residence.

Its actual area seemed only within the Fallen Sea, but in truth, it was vast and boundless.

With Luo Chen’s Adept-level cultivation back then, he hadn’t seen its full scope even when he left.

Comparatively, that Devil Lord’s palace, named Bright Radiance Heaven, seemed even more exquisite than Grand Pure Spirit Lord’s Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace.

That was only natural. Devil Lord Heaven-Refining was himself a top-tier existence in the art of refining. He might not be inferior to the refining masters of Boundless Mountain.

“If I have the chance in the future, I must also have a personal chariot like this. Not only for my own use, but also to carry my family, friends, disciples, and followers…”

This ambitious thought, when it touched upon family, friends, disciples, and followers, was like being doused with cold water, instantly cooling down.

There was one thing that had always lingered in his heart.

He knew it, but never delved into it.

That was his legacy left in the Mountains and Seas Realm. If it flourished, five hundred years after his ascension, there would surely be some who had cultivated to the Ascendant stage.

If those people remained within the realm, preparing to cultivate to the late Ascendant stage before ascending, that would be fine.

But if they followed his example, hastily ascending at the early Ascendant stage, what would be the consequences?

The celestial realm’s guiding power was nearly nonexistent. One could say the immortal path was severed!

The only path to the Xuan Realm—the Star Gate once set up by the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect in the Central Continent—had also been taken away.

Then, his old friends would likely face the same difficulties he had once encountered.

No way to ascend, no place to land.

They could only drift in the cosmos.

With luck, they might find a great thousand world like the Xuan Realm to continue their cultivation.

With bad luck, they might end up in some small thousand worlds, barely surviving.

In the extreme case, they might even perish in the chaotic void currents!

Luo Chen had never dared to think deeply about this matter, because with his current strength, he couldn’t solve it.

Thinking about it only added to his worries.

Perhaps when he was stronger, such as at the Great Vehicle stage, he could use his overwhelming power to reopen the passage to the Mountains and Seas Realm and guide its cultivators into the Xuan Realm?

But that was too far away!

Even with his Five-Spirit Dao Body’s unparalleled cultivation speed, reaching the Great Vehicle stage would likely take over a thousand years.

By then, his old friends would either have ascended elsewhere with uncertain futures, or have already passed away.

In the end, there would only be sighs.

He Lansi thought highly of Luo Chen, so he took the initiative to greet him and introduce the Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace.

His intention was to inspire the other to cultivate diligently. But unexpectedly, as Luo Chen listened, a hint of gloom appeared on his face for some reason.

He stopped his introduction and patted Luo Chen’s shoulder warmly.

“The Void Refining stage may still be far from us, but the True Lords all cultivated up from our stage. Xuan Luo, you must not belittle yourself or fear the path.”

Luo Chen was momentarily speechless.

His grief for old friends was mistaken by Dean He as fear of the True Lord’s path?

But the gloom in his heart was somewhat dispelled by this encouragement.

He nodded firmly. “Thank you for the encouragement, Dean. This disciple will never retreat in the face of difficulty. It just so happens that I haven’t fully absorbed the insights I gained earlier. I’ll find a place to continue my seclusion!”

He Lansi was taken aback.

He had wanted to take this opportunity to bring Luo Chen together with his fellow disciples on this trip, to foster some camaraderie.

But he hadn’t expected the other to continue cultivating even during the journey.

Could his encouragement have backfired?
