# Chapter 1243: Friends and Enemies, Leaking Demonic Qi

People are different.

And worlds differ from one another as well.

If the Mountains and Seas Realm was a nascent minor thousand world, then the Xuan Realm was a major thousand world approaching its twilight years.

Not to mention anything else, just the sheer land area was countless times vaster.

The territory of a single Suling Sect was roughly the size of the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world back then. Only thus could it support so many high-level cultivators.

Naturally, the spatial power of the Xuan Realm also far exceeded that of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

This point could be seen from the details of the battle between Luo Chen and that unlucky Void Refining demon lord who happened to encounter him during his tribulation, shortly after he first entered the Xuan Realm.

That person was a genuine peak Void Refining existence!

If Luo Chen's battle with him had taken place in the Mountains and Seas Realm, it would likely have turned the sky dark and the earth dim, shattering space itself.

But in the end, there was no great commotion.

While part of the credit went to Luo Chen's perfect suppression of his opponent using the Primordial Unity Cauldron, the main reason was the stability of the Xuan Realm's space.

Yet at this moment, relying on the abnormality of the Vacuum Gu within his body, Luo Chen immediately detected something strange about this place.

Or perhaps, not just this place?

The entire ten-thousand-li radius of Guangling Commandery was in a state of unknown turmoil.

As if it could be torn apart by some force at any moment!

And that force was being forcibly suppressed by another power!

"The Demon-Suppressing Tower!"

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly as he gazed at the golden giant tower in the distance, faintly able to sense its extraordinary nature.

Both in the tower itself, and beyond it.

"Was this a method applied by a Great Vehicle Venerable?"

While Luo Chen was pondering, he suddenly felt gazes fall upon him.

He looked over without changing expression, and happened to see several Divine Transformation grand cultivators who had been chatting with Elder Gumu looking at him, their focus especially on the moss-like patterns on his face.

"Demon contamination?"

"How curious. The remnant demonic lineages in the Xuan Realm are basically all under the monitoring of Saint Sect or Source Court level powers. For ordinary cultivators, let alone demon contamination, it's hard even to come into contact with them."

"Could it be that this junior broke into some ruins left by a powerful demonic cultivator?"

In response to these questions, Elder Gumu laughed them off and changed the subject.

Clearly, regarding the matter of the Mo Yu space, the Suling Sect still didn't want outside forces to know about it for now.

Elder Gumu merely had those people familiarize themselves with Luo Chen and the others, to avoid their disciples and followers clashing with Suling Sect cultivators during the trial.

These Divine Transformation grand cultivators all smiled and responded, saying that in the coming days, the disciples from all sides should interact more and strengthen their relationships.

Of course, what would actually happen once they entered the Dark Demon Cave was another matter entirely.

Luo Chen also learned, through Fairy Yaoxi's voice transmission introduction, about the sect forces behind those people before him.

Divine Hand Valley, Illusion Spirit Fort, Azure Dragon Sect, and Waning Moon Merchant Alliance.

These were all forces that had relatively close relations with the Suling Sect. Among them were Divine Transformation level forces and Void Refining level forces.

The one requiring the most caution was the Azure Dragon Sect.

Because the founder of this sect was still alive, and as one of the Four Great Battle Generals of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, he was none other than that Azure Dragon True Lord!

It could be said that while others could be offended, the disciples of the Azure Dragon Sect should not be provoked.

Their status was even more sensitive than that of the Dao Sect's own disciples.

Because of the Azure Dragon True Lord's influence, the disciples of the Azure Dragon Sect were quite overbearing.

In comparison, the disciples of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect were themselves the masters of this territory, and had no need to act arrogantly and domineeringly toward these vassals.

Their pride and nobility were innate!

"Everyone, let's pause here. I still need to settle these disciples. We'll gather again later."

Elder Gumu took his leave.

The others didn't insist on keeping him; they had only come out for a brief meeting anyway.

Just as they were about to part, they suddenly raised their heads in unison, looking toward the distant high sky.

The prostrate heavenly prodigy Xiu Teng, who had disappeared earlier, reappeared and enthusiastically went to greet someone.

As the sea of clouds churned, five-colored radiance descended.

Then, a five-colored treasure ship broke through the clouds, its majestic momentum no less than that of the Suling Sect's Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace when it had appeared earlier.

"It's the Five Elements Divine Sect's people."

Elder Gumu said with a complicated expression, then without lingering, led the disciples into the palace complex behind him.

After they left, Elder Xu of the Blue Falling Hall and the others did not depart.

Instead, they watched the five-colored treasure ship land with great interest, chatting idly.

"No wonder Old Gu ran off so quickly—it's the Five Elements Divine Sect arriving!"

"Under Heaven Origin's rule, there are only a few Void Refining level forces in total. The Five Elements Divine Sect has the deepest foundation. Comparatively, the Suling Sect is merely a newly promoted Void Refining force, and one that only succeeded thanks to the help of that Alchemy Saint from the Dao Sect. Before other forces, the Suling Sect can still hold its head high, but before the Divine Sect, it doesn't measure up."

"Don't count my Azure Dragon Sect in that."

"Alright, alright. In this territory, Heaven Origin is number one, and you all are number two, is that fine? Speaking of which, just how long has that old ancestor of yours lived? It should far exceed the five-thousand-year lifespan limit of a Void Refining True Lord, shouldn't it?"

"Why are you asking about that? Looking for trouble?"

"Don't, fellow Daoist overthink it. I was just asking casually."

On the distant five-colored treasure ship.

A middle-aged man calmly swept his gaze downward, finally settling on the departing Suling Sect cultivators.

He beckoned.

A mature and steady-looking person stepped forward from behind.

"Did you see them?"

"Disciple saw them. Those are cultivators of the Suling Sect."

"Remember them. During the trial, if you encounter them, show no mercy. These people are the pillars of the Suling Sect for the next thousand years. Destroy them, and it won't be so easy for the Suling Sect to produce a large number of Divine Transformation cultivators. A thousand years from now, the Suling Sect might only have that one Supreme Tai Su True Lord left to support it. The entire sect will eventually decline. This territory doesn't need too many Void Refining forces!"

The middle-aged man spoke the most ruthless words—to destroy an entire sect's future—in the calmest tone.

But the person behind him showed no shock, merely nodding coolly.

"Disciple remembers!"

...

Thud!

The heavy door slowly closed.

Elder Gumu turned around, his expression serious as he looked at everyone.

"Did you all see that just now?"

He Lanzhi nodded solemnly. "Disciple saw them. They were people from the Five Elements Divine Sect."

Elder Gumu took a deep breath and slowly said, "On the Youxuan Continent, the two Source Court level forces, the Heavenly Phoenix Clan and the Nether Capital, occupy the east and west respectively. The central region is held by the Nine Great Saint Sects. In this way, human cultivators can maintain dominance over the continent while also serving as a buffer between the two Source Court forces. The Suling Sect's location is under the jurisdiction of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, bordering the Immortal Pill Tomb, both in the south. Setting aside the Immortal Pill Tomb for now, under the Dao Sect's command, there have never been fewer than five Void Refining level forces!"

"After the war to open up the Mountains and Seas ten thousand years ago, the Dao Sect grew even stronger, while the secondary Void Refining forces declined considerably. The old Great Soul Gate, for instance, split into three and fell to the Divine Transformation level. Currently, there are only five Void Refining level forces at our level."

"The Medicine Mother Palace, the Blue Falling Hall, the Shadow Teaching, and the Five Elements Divine Sect!"

"Don't think the numbers are small and the territory vast. For top-tier experts, resources are still insufficient. Especially under the Dao Sect's macroscopic control, the distribution of resources is fiercely contested down to the last detail. Under these circumstances, the fewer Void Refining forces, the better. When a Void Refining force declines, the Dao Sect takes the lion's share of its territory and population, while the rest of us nibble at the scraps. The Gray Triangle exclave, for example, was taken from the declining Great Soul Gate back then."

"In this situation, Void Refining forces are irreconcilably hostile to each other. We can coexist peacefully with Divine Transformation forces, and during the trial, if not working together, at least we can keep to ourselves. But if you encounter cultivators from the other four Void Refining forces, you must be extremely careful!"

Rarely, Elder Gumu spoke at length in one go.

And the meaning behind his words was singular.

Beware of the Divine Sect cultivators!

Or rather, not just the Divine Sect cultivators, but also those from the other three Void Refining forces.

During the trial, they were all potential enemies!

To prevent some from not taking his words seriously, Elder Gumu's tone grew even harsher.

"You were able to come here not only because of your own efforts, but also because of the sect's resource allocation!"

"The sect made you what you are. The future Suling Sect will also be yours."

"If you fall in the Demon-Suppressing Trial, it won't just be your loss—it will be a loss for our hard-won Suling Sect!"

"So remember this: during the trial, when you encounter disciples from the other four sects, either take a detour to avoid conflict, or strike without mercy. Do not hesitate in the slightest!"

Upon hearing this, everyone shuddered.

Then they all cupped their hands in acknowledgment.

Seeing this, Elder Gumu finally breathed a sigh of relief.

He had brought these people here. If too many died on the way back, although it wouldn't be blamed on him, it would still bring him shame.

He had made all the necessary preparations and given all the necessary warnings.

What happened next would depend on each person's fortune.

"Alright, go rest now! You will all be staying here for a year, to adapt as much as possible to the demonic qi environment. This will greatly help you when you enter the Dark Demon Cave later."

The Demon-Suppressing Trial was no small matter.

For the disciples of various sects, it might be an opportunity.

But for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and even the entire Youxuan Continent, it was a very important task.

They had to go deep into the demon cave, disperse the various demon lairs that had formed over a thousand years, to prevent the demonic disaster from expanding.

If the task was not completed properly and the demon lairs were allowed to form, the tragedy of the demon realm army invading the Xuan Realm ten thousand years ago might be repeated.

Therefore, every thousand-year demon suppression, the most outstanding disciples from each sect were sent to participate.

Before entering, they also had to live for a year in the demonic qi environment nearby, to familiarize themselves with the situation as much as possible.

These were all the precautions mentioned earlier.

No one had any questions. Each chose a room and settled in.

...

"So this is demonic qi?"

In the quiet room, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

Just now, he had tried circulating the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra* to absorb the surrounding demonic qi for cultivation.

There was no feeling of obstruction whatsoever!

For most cultivators in the Xuan Realm, this demonic qi was extremely difficult to adapt to and hard to convert into their own mana.

But he was different.

The *Chaos Indestructible Sutra* was a technique Luo Chen had painstakingly created himself in the Guixu.

If it could absorb even the most complex chaotic energy, mere demonic qi was nothing.

The only shortcoming was probably...

"The demonic qi here, though dense, is not enough to support my cultivation for breaking through to Divine Transformation."

Luo Chen frowned, but quickly relaxed.

This should be normal.

If the demonic qi in Guangling Commandery was dense enough to satisfy even his advancement to Divine Transformation, then Saint Sect or even Source Court level experts would have long come to investigate and deal with it.

The nearby demonic qi was more likely leaked from the Dark Demon Cave.

After the Demon-Suppressing Trial ended, even this thin demonic qi would probably vanish from this place.

If so, Luo Chen was quite looking forward to it.

If the leaked demonic qi had this concentration, then inside the Dark Demon Cave, it would likely be even denser.

If he added the spiritual opportunity generated after dispersing the demon lairs, recovering to the Divine Transformation stage would be an extremely easy matter.

"There's still a year. I'll endure for now, and also sort out my current cultivation progress."

Luo Chen glanced at his attribute panel.

Although his mana was profound, far exceeding even ordinary late-stage Yuan cultivators, his realm was still at early Nascent Soul.

It could be said that he had made no progress in twenty years, and that would be no problem.

This was Luo Chen's deliberate choice.

Within the Suling Sect, he still had to conceal things somewhat.

But as long as conditions were right, he could let loose his cultivation and rush straight to the Nascent Soul stage without any issue.

The Demon-Suppressing Trial was the best opportunity for him.

"If the spiritual opportunity is abundant, perhaps when I break through to Divine Transformation again, I might be able to comprehend a new law true intent by leveraging that state of heaven-earth resonance?"

Luo Chen had a sudden inspiration, a glint of light flashing in his eyes.

...

For high-level cultivators, a single seclusion could easily last a hundred or a thousand years.

A mere year passed in the blink of an eye.

A year later, following nine deep bell tolls, figures emerged one after another from the various palaces.

Nine was the ultimate number. Nine bell tolls, in any sect, were only sounded during celebrations or great calamities.

The stirring of demon lairs was a great calamity for the Youxuan Continent!

And what the disciples of various sects had to do was to cooperate with the Dao Sect's grand cultivators to eliminate this calamity in advance.

Luo Chen stepped out of the hall. His Suling Sect fellow disciples had already appeared.

Each was full of energy and in excellent condition.

It seemed they had adapted to the demonic qi environment over the past year.

He released a strand of "weak" divine sense, sweeping it around.

He found that a large group of people had also emerged from the neighboring palace.

Yes, a large group!

There were twelve people in total: three Nascent Soul grand cultivators and nine Yuan cultivators.

But they were not gathered together. Instead, they were divided into three groups, standing apart from each other, the atmosphere between them far from harmonious.

"Which sect is that?" Luo Chen asked curiously.

"Not one sect. They are the three sects—Great Soul Gate, Purple Flame Mountain, and Golden Bell Sect." Elder Gumu walked out from the hall, patiently explaining to Luo Chen and the others, and also looking around. "They were split from the former top-tier Void Refining force, the Great Soul Gate, inheriting its trial qualifications. So unlike ordinary Nascent Soul forces that only have one or two slots, these three sects have a total of nine slots, only one less than our Void Refining forces."

As he spoke, Elder Gumu exchanged glances and nods with the three Divine Transformation cultivators next door.

"Alright, it's time to enter the tower. Don't keep the True Lord and the Dao Sect's grand cultivators waiting."

Elder Gumu waved his hand, and everyone transformed into streaks of light, flying toward the distant golden giant tower.

The same happened in the other palaces. One streak of light after another rose and fell, shuttling across the sky.

Though few in number, at a glance, they seemed to cover the heavens and the earth.

These people, though not of the highest realms now, were all the Yuan elite of their respective sects, and the pillars of their sects for the next thousand years.

Perhaps countless years later, one or two among this group would give birth to a Body Integration Grand Venerable or even a Great Vehicle Venerable, dominating the winds and clouds of the Xuan Realm! (Chapter End)
