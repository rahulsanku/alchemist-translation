# Chapter 1253: Complete Soul Splitting, Primordial Spirit Dharma Aspect

Inside a valley.

Three figures surrounded a man in the middle.

The man appeared young, but his aura was exceptionally formidable, ranking among the best even among late-stage Sage grand cultivators. Yet at this moment, his face was filled with anger and grief as he glared at those encircling him.

"Ding Ling'er, Xiao Fufeng, we are from the same sect. Why would you collude with outsiders to harm me?"

Ding Ling'er, clad in a revealing golden short skirt, stood smiling at the valley's exit. Golden bells hung from her neck, wrists, and even her bare ankles. As she chuckled softly, those bells seemed connected, ringing out crisp sounds in unison.

"Senior Brother Yun, we've come to this point, yet you're still so naive. Everyone knows your Great Soul Gate is trying to absorb my Golden Bell Sect and Junior Brother Xiao's Purple Flame Mountain. If we let you achieve Ascension here and leave, our two sects would face disaster."

The bells chimed crisply, carrying a subtle enchanting tone.

The man called Senior Brother Yun was enveloped in a black halo, seemingly unaffected by the sound. Instead, he shouted angrily, "Our three sects were originally one. Reuniting would restore the glory of the Great Soul Gate. Why do you resist?"

Hearing this, Ding Ling'er frowned.

The purple-robed man beside her sneered, "Senior Sister Ding, why bother with these petty tricks to provoke him? Senior Brother Yun is a genius rarely seen in a millennium at the Great Soul Gate. He has already cultivated the *Soul-Splitting Art* to great perfection, barely a step away from condensing his primordial spirit. How could your mere Golden Bell Soul-Shaking Sound move his will?"

As he spoke, a purple true flame suddenly erupted from his body.

Senior Brother Yun swept his gaze over them. "The two of you alone are no match for me."

Ding Ling'er took a deep breath and admitted frankly, "Our two sects are merely weak branches split off from the Great Soul Gate. Naturally, we cannot compare to you, Senior Brother Yun, the sole true disciple of the Great Soul Gate, in terms of techniques, secret arts, or divine abilities. So, we invited Fairy Green Butterfly!"

As she spoke, the last of the three surrounding Senior Brother Yun threw back the hood covering her head.

Thousand strands of black hair cascaded down, revealing a face of breathtaking beauty.

She looked at Senior Brother Yun with curiosity. "The Great Soul Gate possesses a defensive soul technique said to be unshakable even by those a full realm higher. Even my father praised it back in the day. Fellow Daoist Yun, if you hand over that technique to me, I will leave immediately."

At these words, Ding Ling'er and Xiao Fufeng's expressions changed drastically.

"Fairy Green Butterfly!"

"You promised us!"

Fairy Green Butterfly was utterly unconcerned. She smoothed her disheveled hair and said with a chuckle, "After all, this is ultimately your family affair. If my Biluo Sect interferes too much, it wouldn't sound good. Fellow Daoist Yun, what do you think?"

When the words "Biluo Sect" appeared, Ding Ling'er and Xiao Fufeng, no matter how much resentment they felt, dared not speak out.

That was a Refining Void power. Its sect leader, Patriarch Green Robe, possessed the formidable strength of mid-Refining Void—definitely not someone they could provoke.

For a moment, they could only watch Senior Brother Yun nervously, afraid he might agree.

What had been a three-way encirclement now seemed to have a turning point because of Fairy Green Butterfly's words.

Senior Brother Yun stared silently at Fairy Green Butterfly, appearing somewhat tempted.

But when he caught the cunning gleam in the girl's eyes, his heart sank.

"Rumor has it that Fairy Green Butterfly takes after her father. I'm afraid that even if I hand over the Prison-Suppressing Soul Technique, Fairy will still make her move, just to enjoy the sight of me furious and desperate!"

Although Patriarch Green Robe was powerful, his reputation was far from good.

He was known for breaking promises and often betraying allies.

There was a particular incident where he had explored a secret realm with several friends. In the end, three died, two were severely injured, and only he emerged unscathed with a bountiful harvest. According to the two who escaped with severe injuries, Patriarch Green Robe's actions during the exploration were utterly contemptible.

Hearing Senior Brother Yun's words, Fairy Green Butterfly pouted.

"You're no fun at all. Fine then... let's fight!"

Before she finished speaking, the three had already struck.

Instantly, bells rang wildly, purple flames blazed across the sky, and the Yellow Springs of the Netherworld surged forth, flooding the entire valley.

Deep within, Senior Brother Yun was already on guard.

Ignoring the soul-seizing bell sounds, he spat out black flames to hold back the purple fire above. With both hands, he shook a large banner, summoning fierce winds that blew back the surging Yellow Spring water.

In that single moment, Senior Brother Yun's combat prowess was evident.

However, since the three dared to surround him, they had certainly prepared countermeasures.

Ding Ling'er slapped her smooth belly, and a sound like a frog's croak suddenly emanated from within her body, perfectly harmonizing with the bell sounds. Under their combined resonance, even Senior Brother Yun, protected by the Prison-Suppressing Soul Technique, momentarily slowed.

Seizing the opportunity, Xiao Fufeng formed hand seals, a pained expression on his face.

The overwhelming purple flames suddenly exploded.

A powerful wave of energy surged, pushing back the black flames layer by layer, even beginning to wrap around the large banner.

Senior Brother Yun was also experienced. He spat out a thunderous sound.

"Break!"

With the booming thunder, Ding Ling'er bore the brunt of it, her entire body shuddering.

Blood seeped from her smooth belly at the navel, as if a drum membrane had been ruptured.

The croaking sound vanished.

Then, the black banner suddenly contracted inward, wrapping up the layers of exploding purple flames.

Though they were fellow disciples of the same rank, there were still distinctions between superior and inferior.

Even with the two of them together, they were no match for him!

Senior Brother Yun's main focus remained on Fairy Green Butterfly.

After all, she was the daughter of a Refining Void True Lord and a prodigy of a great sect!

The next moment.

From within the rolling Yellow Springs, a green light suddenly flew out.

The green light fluttered its thin, delicate wings and drifted forward.

Upon closer inspection, it was actually a green butterfly.

Faced with this attack, Senior Brother Yun treated it with utmost seriousness. He opened his mouth and spat out a black light.

After emerging, the black light transformed into a three-inch flying dagger. It spun once and then slashed toward the green butterfly.

What happened next was unexpected.

Though the green butterfly was clearly struck, its speed was unaffected. Instead, it bypassed the dagger and flew straight toward Senior Brother Yun's face.

This sudden development caught Senior Brother Yun off guard.

A spell? A soul technique?

A divine ability?

He didn't know what kind of technique this was, but he dared not let the green butterfly touch him even slightly.

He retreated repeatedly.

But he was already surrounded by three people, with nowhere to retreat.

Golden rings flew in from all directions, attempting to bind his limbs and neck.

A purple seal crashed down from above, carrying the power to seal the void.

Senior Brother Yun had ways to counter each of these attacks individually, but when they came all at once, he had no perfect solution.

"My life is over!"

Just as the golden rings, purple seal, and green butterfly were about to reach him—

Suddenly!

The golden rings stopped, the purple seal halted, and the green butterfly inexplicably vanished.

Senior Brother Yun stared blankly at the scene, as if time itself had been frozen.

No!

It wasn't frozen—the origin qi of heaven and earth in this space had been seized by someone, suppressing all power within.

Such a method...

"An Ascendant!"

Senior Brother Yun blurted out.

The other three also realized this and dared not move recklessly.

In the oppressive atmosphere, a dignified, aged voice came from the air.

"The demon lair has undergone a strange change. Fortunately, Vermilion Bird True Lord has single-handedly suppressed it. You can enjoy the spiritual opportunities without any effort. Why are you fighting here?"

"Purple Flame, Golden Bell, Great Soul Gate—three sects originally one. Why internal strife?"

"The Demon-Suppressing Tower is about to open. Disperse and seize the time to comprehend the true meaning of the laws!"

The last sentence carried a veiled warning.

Xiao Fufeng and Ding Ling'er exchanged glances, filled with helplessness. They could only bow and retreat.

The ambush was thus abandoned.

Fairy Green Butterfly, however, looked curiously toward the sky. But she couldn't see the person's true face, only sensing that it might be an old man with a white beard.

"I wonder which senior from the Dao Sect this is. Green Butterfly sends greetings on behalf of my father, Patriarch Green Robe."

The Ascendant in the air spoke calmly, "If Green Robe were here, would he want to see you wasting such an opportunity?"

"Tch!"

Fairy Green Butterfly pouted but didn't argue further, obediently leaving.

After she left, Senior Brother Yun breathed a sigh of relief. He first retrieved his magic treasures, then bowed deeply toward the sky.

"This junior, Yun Dongjun of the Great Soul Gate, thanks the senior for saving my life. May I ask the senior's name, so that I may repay this debt of gratitude in the future."

"Forget the name. There's no need to wait for another day to repay the favor."

"Uh..."

Yun Dongjun was stunned. The next moment, he fell into a daze.

When he woke up, he found himself still standing perfectly fine in the valley, with no injuries on his body.

Only his storage treasure showed signs of having been opened.

He quickly checked but found nothing missing. Even the inherited technique manual he carried with him was still inside.

Surprised, he was even more horrified.

With his soul foundation, having cultivated the *Soul-Splitting Art* to great perfection, even though he hadn't yet condensed his primordial spirit, he was not inferior to a newly advanced Ascendant. Especially with the protection of the Prison-Suppressing Soul Technique, an Ascendant couldn't have made him fall for a trick in an instant.

But just now, he had indeed lost consciousness for a moment.

"Which old but young-at-heart senior from the Dao Sect is playing a joke on me?"

Yun Dongjun looked bewildered. He pondered for a moment on the spot, then hurriedly left.

This place was no longer safe to stay.

...

"Is this the complete *Soul-Splitting Art*?"

On a mountain peak, facing the breeze, Luo Chen finished the last stroke of his divine sense inscription on the jade slip.

This was the technique he had glimpsed from Yun Dongjun's storage treasure just now, now transcribed from memory.

Before him, Yun Dongjun's soul foundation was hardly impressive.

The Prison-Suppressing Soul Technique he relied on for defense was like a blank sheet of paper.

Luo Chen had long cultivated this technique to the great perfection level!

Back then, when Rosycloud had tried to harm him, she couldn't break through the Prison-Suppressing Soul Technique he had set up at the Sage realm, which gave him the chance to self-destruct his primordial spirit.

Even after falling into the Guixu and his primordial spirit Dao fruit collapsing, his main soul had remained intact.

Now, with Luo Chen's soul comparable to a Refining Void True Lord, if he were to set up the Prison-Suppressing Soul Technique again, even a Unity Grand Sovereign personally attacking might not be able to break through its defense.

Of course.

Luo Chen wasn't complacent about bullying a junior. His attention was on the complete *Soul-Splitting Art*.

All the text on the jade slip formed a continuous whole. Most of it he had seen before, but another part still caught his attention.

First was the method for strengthening split souls!

This was something he had desperately sought in the Mountains and Seas Realm but couldn't obtain, relying only on pills to gradually strengthen his split souls.

Now, it had come without effort.

Luo Chen carefully read through it and couldn't help nodding repeatedly.

If he used this method to strengthen his split souls, it might not be as fast as using pills, but it was gentler and more sustainable. Moreover, it could nourish the main soul in return without harming the soul's origin.

This explained why Han Zhan, whose realm was clearly lower than Luo Chen's in the later stages, could split off nine split souls at the early Initiate stage.

Meanwhile, Luo Chen, even with the help of pills and having later cultivated to the late Sage stage, had only barely gathered nine split souls.

What he had been missing was precisely this important content.

Main soul splits, split souls nourish in return, mutual balance, and joint growth!

"Although I've passed that stage, I can still practice this method a bit. It should also nourish my soul."

Luo Chen's soul was strong, but it had a hidden flaw.

Few knew of this flaw. Even Luo Chen himself had only discovered it after recently returning to the Ascendant realm.

That was that his soul wasn't condensed enough!

This sounded incredible—a soul comparable to a Refining Void True Lord, yet not condensed enough.

But that was the reality.

The culprit was when he fell into the Guixu. To prevent his overly powerful soul from attracting the Soul-Shaking Thunder Sound, he had endured immense pain and split his vast soul into hundreds of tiny fragments.

Although this had turned misfortune into fortune, allowing him to comprehend the method of nurturing aperture deities and raising over seven hundred martial gods, when he later re-condensed his main soul, the cracks from being split too many times still remained.

If he could resolve this hidden flaw by strengthening his split souls and nourishing the main soul in return, Luo Chen was willing to spend some time on it.

Apart from the method for strengthening split souls, what Luo Chen cared about most was another set of secret arts recorded in the complete *Soul-Splitting Art*.

The technique for condensing a dharma aspect!

A dharma aspect was the hallmark of a Refining Void True Lord!

Also known as a void aspect, after reaching that realm, a cultivator needed to condense a void aspect, then refine it from void to reality, achieving a solid dharma aspect.

This process was called "Refining Void."

And if the final refined dharma aspect could merge with the primordial spirit, one could break through to the Unity stage.

Using the cultivation theory of ancient Qi refiners to explain, these two steps were respectively "refining spirit into void" and "refining void into Dao."

As for how to condense a dharma aspect, each sect had its own methods.

During his time in the Guixu, Luo Chen had also collected some related cultivation insights.

For example, those left by True Lord Xiao Lan, the founder of the Lost Immortal City, in the city lord's mansion.

But most of those cultivation insights were naturally formed after normal cultivation to the Refining Void stage.

Nothing particularly exquisite.

However, what was recorded in the *Soul-Splitting Art* allowed one to attempt condensing a void aspect as early as the Ascendant stage, without affecting the primordial spirit.

Most importantly, the void aspect condensed using this method, after evolving into a solid dharma aspect, would greatly aid in breaking through to the Unity stage.

It could make the Unity process smoother.

In other words, it could increase the success rate of breaking through to the Unity stage!

Although not much—just ten to twenty percent—Luo Chen was overjoyed upon seeing it.

He wasn't too worried about his upcoming cultivation and breakthrough to the Refining Void stage.

But breaking through to the Unity stage was another matter.

After all, many powerful figures in the Xuan Realm were stuck at the Refining Void stage, unable to cross that seemingly insurmountable step. Even someone as brilliant as Devil Lord Heaven-Refining hadn't taken that step.

How could Luo Chen be absolutely certain?

Obtaining this secret art, which added ten to twenty percent chance of success, filled Luo Chen with great delight.

He even put aside the thought of hurrying to reunite with his fellow disciples from the Suli Sect and began to focus wholeheartedly on studying this set of secret arts.

Half a year later, Luo Chen suddenly woke from his focused state.

He looked up at the sky.

A vortex was slowly forming—a passage to the outside world.

"The Demon-Suppressing Trial is over!"
