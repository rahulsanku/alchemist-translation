# Chapter 1233: Returning to the Sect, the Method of Removing the Demon

A few days later.

A majestic flying boat returned to the Sùlíng Sect.

It did not attract much attention, yet it stirred no small waves among the sect's high-ranking members.

At that time, all eight Divine Transformation elders had gathered, and even the Sect Master, Tàisù Língjūn, had personally appeared.

Only four Nascent Soul cultivators were seated as attendees.

Yáoxī, Yōuhuáng.

Xuánluó, Jiānghuáng.

First came the report on the matter of the Grey Triangle Ink Jade Space.

The sect's high-ranking members were quite concerned about this matter.

After some discussion, they decided to make good use of that space.

After all, a place rich in demonic qi could grow many spirit plants and mineral materials not found in the Xuan Realm, and it could also be used for various experiments.

For this matter, one of the Divine Transformation elders would be personally dispatched to guard it for a period of time, to properly arrange things.

Then came the matter concerning the two juniors, Luó Chén and Jiānghuáng.

Demon moss attaching to the body, absorbing spiritual energy and transforming it into qi—this state was called "demon contamination" during the Xuan-Demon War ten thousand years ago.

Dealing with it was quite troublesome.

According to historical records, initially, the powerful figures of the Xuan Realm did not pay much attention to those contaminated by demons, mostly letting them be.

But later, they discovered that those contaminated by demons were easily controlled by powerful demonic beings, and so they were forced to begin dealing with it.

The so-called "dealing with it" was naturally bloody.

Either kill them or suppress them!

However, for those with prominent status and identity, this crude method was not suitable.

Thus, some effective methods were gradually researched.

Over ten thousand years had passed, the demon race had long been driven back, and those methods treasured by the top powers had leaked out.

At the very least, the Sùlíng Sect had two specific methods for resolving demon contamination.

One was to invite a powerful demonic being to forcibly devour the demonic qi on the contaminated person, and then use the method of cutting flesh to remove the demon moss.

The other was to use the method of one thing overcoming another to control the demon moss and reversely transform it into dharma power.

Both methods had their own advantages and disadvantages.

The former had a high cost, as hiring a powerful demonic being itself required significant resources, and even if successful, it caused great damage to the contaminated person.

Especially for Xuánluó and Jiānghuáng.

They were not ordinary demon contamination; they not only had demon moss attached to their bodies but had also undergone demonic qi initiation.

If the first method were used, their realms would inevitably regress greatly, even affecting their foundation and aptitude!

As for the second method, the conditions were also very harsh.

There were only a few things that could restrain demon moss, and most of them were in the Demon Realm.

The only thing that could be found in the Xuan Realm was demon marrow diamond.

And this thing was only produced in small quantities when a demon cave stirred.

In other words, to use demon marrow diamond to control demon moss, one had to go to a demon cave. And to go to a demon cave, one had to participate in the Demon Suppression Trial.

Moreover, it couldn't be a short visit where one went in and came out. One had to rely on the related demonic qi environment to achieve the best results.

The harsh conditions were secondary; this method also had its limitations and aftereffects.

Control, after all, was not complete eradication!

For the rest of their cultivation lives, the contaminated would inevitably carry the mark of the demon race.

When facing righteous experts who specialized in techniques that countered demonic arts, or top experts among the demon race, they would inevitably suffer greatly!

Therefore, when discussing matters related to Xuánluó and Jiānghuáng, there was considerable hesitation.

The high-ranking members of the sect, led by Elder Tiāngē, suggested inviting a powerful demonic being to implement the first method.

In this regard, the Sùlíng Sect did have some connections.

Because the Sùlíng Sect was close to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and within that sect, there were many remnants of the demon race.

Among those demon remnants, there was currently a Void Refining stage expert skilled in alchemy and formations, who had a good relationship with Alchemy Saint Chǔ Yán.

The Sect Master could step forward and entrust Chǔ Yán to help facilitate the matter.

On the other hand, the high-ranking members of the sect, led by Elder Gǔmù, suggested that Xuánluó and Jiānghuáng participate in the Demon Suppression Trial to find demon marrow diamond to control the Nine Nether Demon Moss.

Elder Gǔmù's thinking was simple.

With the first method, realm regression was secondary, but affecting foundation and aptitude would be greatly detrimental!

In his eyes, Xuánluó was an unparalleled alchemy genius of the Sùlíng Sect.

In the next few hundred or even thousand years, he would lead the alchemy lineage of the Sùlíng Sect and influence the future development of the entire sect.

If this affected Xuánluó's upper limit, it would be greatly regrettable.

As for the aftereffects of the second method, he didn't consider them significant.

What did it matter if one carried a bit of demonic qi?

What did it matter if one was restrained by righteous cultivators or even powerful demonic beings?

Would the vast Sùlíng Sect ever let Xuánluó go out to fight?

The two sides debated endlessly, with the focus mostly on Xuánluó.

As for Jiānghuáng?

In truth, not many cared about her future. After all, this woman had been declared missing two hundred years ago. It was already a blessing that she could return alive now.

Regardless of which method was used, as long as she could recover her sanity, that would be good enough.

Seeing that no conclusion could be reached for the time being, Tàisù Língjūn waved his hand in annoyance and told the two juniors to withdraw first.

The others also agreed.

After all, having gone through life and death, it would be good to let the two return early and rest.

Sage Yáoxī was kept behind.

After Luó Chén and the others left, Tàisù Língjūn interrupted the others' discussion and fixed his gaze on her.

"Yáoxī, these two are both your disciples. What do you think?"

Under everyone's gaze, Sage Yáoxī bit her lips tightly.

...

In fact, it wasn't just Luó Chén and the others who left the Sùwáng Hall; Sage Yōuhuáng also left.

She drove the flying boat to send the two back to Jade Spring Peak.

On the way, Sage Yōuhuáng looked at the two with a gentle, even somewhat kind, expression.

"Xuánluó, you heard everything just now. Do you have any thoughts?"

Luó Chén's gaze flickered. "Your disciple dares not think too much. I will leave everything to the sect's elders and my master to decide."

Sage Yōuhuáng sighed. "Yes, this matter will likely depend heavily on your master's opinion."

Luó Chén suddenly said, "However, in your disciple's opinion, if I truly had to choose, I would probably choose the second method."

"The method of using demon marrow diamond to control the Nine Nether Demon Moss?" Sage Yōuhuáng frowned, then reminded him, "Aren't you afraid of the aftereffects?"

Luó Chén said confidently, "The so-called aftereffects aren't that terrifying. Moreover, times are developing. In the future, I can always research the corresponding alchemical principles to solve these problems."

Sage Yōuhuáng was startled, then smiled slightly. "You are quite confident. That's true. With your talent in alchemy, you might indeed be able to research the relevant alchemical principles to resolve this matter in the future."

As she spoke, she fell silent.

After a long while, she said softly, "But to obtain demon marrow diamond, you must participate in the Demon Suppression Trial. And how dangerous is the Demon Suppression Trial? With the strength of you two, I'm afraid it will be very difficult."

Luó Chén blinked and said nothing more.

...

Sage Yōuhuáng's flying vehicle was not very fast, after all, they were within the sect. It wouldn't do to accidentally bump into any reckless disciples.

After all, the sect was vast, with too many disciples. From time to time, there would be juniors who, after breaking through a realm, would fly wildly through the sky, expressing their joy.

When they arrived at Jade Spring Peak, they saw over a hundred people waiting outside the mountain, having been there for some time.

Luó Chén glanced at Sage Yōuhuáng in surprise.

She nodded slightly. "I informed them. You and your fellow disciples should have a good reunion!"

Luó Chén understood, then led Jiānghuáng out of the flying boat.

In fact, this was only the second time he had fully seen his fellow disciples of Jade Spring Peak.

The first time was at his Adept-forming ceremony.

Sage Yáoxī's lineage had over a hundred direct disciples, and among them were many disciples taken by her own disciples—that is, the third generation.

Compared to the Sùlíng Seven Sages, who were her equals, this was hardly a thriving lineage.

But today was somewhat special.

Xuánluó looked in surprise at the person standing before the crowd.

Wàn Jiǔzhāng!

Early Nascent Soul stage.

His dharma power aura was balanced and harmonious, clearly indicating that his realm had stabilized.

If one added himself and Jiānghuáng, who had returned, then the once-weak Yáoxī lineage now had three Nascent Soul cultivators.

One peak, four Nascent Souls, three disciples all true cultivators.

Even within the Sùlíng Sect, this was considered outstanding.

"Senior Brother Wàn, you succeeded in forming your Nascent Soul?"

Wàn Jiǔzhāng stepped forward and said with emotion, "Somewhat fortunate, but at least I didn't disappoint Master's expectations."

"Congratulations, congratulations."

Wàn Jiǔzhāng waved his hand. "I am truly delighted that you two were able to return alive."

Then, with a complex expression, he looked at the woman standing beside Luó Chén, his eyes both sorrowful and gentle.

"Junior Sister Jiāng, you have finally returned."

Jiānghuáng looked up in confusion, murmuring, "Senior Brother..."

At those three words, Wàn Jiǔzhāng's eyes even grew moist.

Behind him, over a dozen Adept-stage cultivators surged forward, surrounding Jiānghuáng and showering her with concern and warmth.

This kind of intimate treatment was something Luó Chén, with his reputation as a genius, had never enjoyed before.

But he was not jealous.

After all, these people had been close friends with Jiānghuáng two hundred years ago.

Of course, he had no reason to be jealous.

Whether it was his own disposition or the equally warm treatment he received.

Liǔ Mùhán led a group of Initiate-stage disciples to welcome him.

"Senior Brother Xuánluó, you're back."

"Senior Brother Xuánluó, I knew you would be blessed by heaven!"

"How could those mere demon remnants harm Senior Brother Xuánluó in the slightest?"

...

This treatment was no less than what Jiānghuáng received.

After all, Jiānghuáng had been missing for two hundred years, and the title of Senior Sister was already unfamiliar to many.

Especially among the younger generation.

And Luó Chén, because of the Circulation Qi Pill, had no small reputation among his fellow disciples.

Liǔ Mùhán approached Luó Chén, looking at the eerie ink-green patterns on his face, biting her teeth, silent for a long time.

Luó Chén smiled and asked, "Senior Sister, I'm back. Won't you say a few words of greeting?"

"You're already at the Nascent Soul stage, and you still call me Senior Sister? And didn't we agree before?"

"Mùhán?"

"Mm, welcome back!"

Surrounded by the crowd, the two who had returned finally entered Jade Spring Peak.

Wàn Jiǔzhāng had long ordered a banquet to be prepared, to welcome them and cleanse them of bad luck.

The fellow disciples drank heartily until late at night before dispersing.

They were all curious about the Ink Jade Space, but Luó Chén did not say much.

He only said that after being captured, he lost some consciousness, and it was all thanks to Senior Sister's rescue that he woke up already at the Nascent Soul stage.

As for Jiānghuáng, her divine soul was damaged and her mind unclear, so she couldn't say much either.

Fortunately, the banquet atmosphere was very harmonious, with brothers and sisters showing mutual respect, a picture of harmony.

Xuánluó held the rear, protecting his fellow disciples. Jiānghuáng drew her sword, saving Xuánluó. These two matters were praiseworthy deeds regardless, and they also set an excellent example for Jade Spring Peak's conduct.

This could be seen from the gratified expression on Sage Yáoxī's face when she hurried back late at night.

"You have both done very well! It is I, your master, who has been too focused on my own cultivation and failed to fulfill my responsibilities as a teacher. I am truly sorry."

Jiānghuáng was dazed, Luó Chén silent.

Wàn Jiǔzhāng only said that the disciples were dull and did not blame their master.

Sage Yáoxī sighed, then spoke of the sect's high-ranking members' decision regarding Luó Chén and Jiānghuáng.

"The Demon Suppression Trial—you two will go with us!"

Hearing this, Wàn Jiǔzhāng hesitated, clearly aware of the dangers of the Demon Suppression Trial.

Luó Chén, on the other hand, was not too surprised.

Concerning the future of a genius like himself, the sect could not possibly choose the first method of removing the demon.

The Demon Suppression Trial was certainly dangerous, but as long as one was willing to pay a great price and make thorough preparations, there was always a chance.

And there was one thing that was completely different from before.

This time, the Suling Sect had a full ten slots, completely different from the two or three of the past.

Under the protection of the Sùlíng Seven Sages, and with the sect's high-ranking members giving more life-saving measures, it was not so dangerous.

Seeing that Luó Chén and Jiānghuáng did not object, Sage Yáoxī no longer spoke words of admonition or comfort, but directly began making arrangements.

"There is still about twenty years until the Demon Suppression Trial begins."

"Although it is a bit rushed, it is barely enough. We cannot waste time."

"First, Xiǎo Jiāng. For these twenty years, you will cultivate with me. I will do my best to help you recover your damaged divine soul, so that you can participate in the trial in the most comprehensive state."

"Then, Xuánluó. You must not slack off in the slightest. After all, your Nascent Soul realm was obtained through demonic qi initiation; it is too unstable. You must diligently cultivate the technique I gave you to stabilize your realm. At the same time, seize the time to refine a true artifact!"

As she spoke, she tossed out an object—a diamond-shaped shield.

"This treasure was obtained by me from Elder Tiāngē. It is very compatible with the *Tàiyǐ Gēngjīn Wànhuà Xuánzhāng*. Take it and bind it, use it for daily purposes. As for other life-saving measures, the sect's elders will bestow them when we set out."

Luó Chén took the palm-sized shield, somewhat moved.

This treasure was actually a precious defensive true artifact!

For early Nascent Soul cultivators, it was extremely valuable.

Clearly, this was an additional reward given in light of his short cultivation time and the embarrassing situation of his natal dharma treasure having been nurtured for too short a time.

After Luó Chén thanked her, Sage Yáoxī continued, "Also, I will open an additional fourth-grade cave dwelling on Jade Spring Peak for Luó Chén to cultivate in. The original Jade Ravine Cave Dwelling, after increasing its spiritual qi concentration, must be returned to your Senior Brother, as the familiar environment is also suitable for his recuperation."

To this, Luó Chén actually refused.

Under Sage Yáoxī's puzzled gaze, Luó Chén said frankly, "Your disciple already has a side hall in the Alchemy Court's Mountain-Sea Travel Palace, which also has a fourth-grade spirit vein, sufficient for cultivation needs."

Sage Yáoxī was somewhat surprised and subconsciously looked at Wàn Jiǔzhāng.

He nodded, corroborating Luó Chén's statement.

Sage Yáoxī was still hesitant.

Luó Chén added, "And as you said, my realm is currently unstable, and my dharma power occasionally leaks out. Your disciple, contaminated by the demon, has dharma power containing demonic qi, which can easily affect the fellow disciples of Jade Spring Peak. Just now at the banquet, there were signs of this. Some Initiate-stage junior brothers dared not even be in contact with me for too long. Changing places will neither affect cultivation nor others. Why not do it?"

Sage Yáoxī finally nodded.

This way, she also saved the trouble of opening a new cave dwelling on Jade Spring Peak.

"Finally, take this method and memorize it well!"

Two jade slips flew to Luó Chén and Jiānghuáng respectively.

What was recorded within was precisely the specific method of using demon marrow diamond to restrain the Nine Nether Demon Moss!

After doing all this, Sage Yáoxī rubbed her brow.

Looking at the three people in the hall, her mood was somewhat subtle.

Three disciples, all Nascent Soul.

Wàn Jiǔzhāng was gentle and honest, having accompanied her the longest and received the most guidance from her. His achievement of Nascent Soul was expected, but he would likely have no hope of reaching the Nascent Soul stage in the future.

Jiānghuáng was once the one she had the highest hopes for, even teaching her self-created Heavenly Waterfall Sword Art, but due to her own stubborn personality, she ended up missing for two hundred years. Now she has returned at the Nascent Soul stage, but her mind is unclear, a pity.

As for Xuánluó, she had hardly spent any effort on him. Yet, relying on himself alone, he had made a great name within the sect, and now, at the age of a hundred, he had formed his Nascent Soul. Even if it was through demonic qi initiation, it was still too astonishing.

This child's future, she could not predict.

After a long time, a faint murmur came from within the hall.

"Get through this trial, and the four of us, master and disciples, will have a bright future ahead, and our path to immortality will flourish!"
