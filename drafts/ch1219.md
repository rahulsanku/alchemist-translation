# Chapter 1219: Sect Grand Competition, Luo Chen Forms His Core

The closer the sect grand competition drew, the livelier things became within the sect.

Beams of light shuttled back and forth between every peak and vein.

Disciples moved about with increasing frequency.

Under the banner of the sect grand competition, disciples from various peaks who ordinarily had little interaction began to make contact with other fellow disciples.

Rather than calling it an internal sect competition, it seemed more like the sect had built a stage for fellow disciples to get to know each other and grow less distant.

In the mortal world, the phrase "meeting friends through martial arts" would be a very apt description.

However, for those disciples who aspired to achieve good rankings, they certainly would not let this competition pass them by. They were doing everything possible to increase their strength.

The transaction volume of pills, talismans, magical artifacts, and dharma treasures kept climbing higher.

As a result, the internal market of Suling Sect, as well as some shops in the surrounding immortal cities, had all been raking in profits hand over fist.

On Jade Spring Peak.

Wan Jiuzhang was still in charge. With his kind brows and gentle eyes, his aura had grown increasingly profound.

Clearly, he had not neglected his cultivation over these past few decades.

Looking at the junior apprentice-siblings who had gradually gathered, as well as the disciples he had taken on himself, he nodded with satisfaction.

Because of their master, Yao Xi Sage, Jade Spring Peak had a considerable reputation within Suling Sect. However, the number of disciples who had actually matured into talents was not that many.

Compared to another of the Seven Sages of Suling, Zhi Bei Sage, they were far behind.

After all, Zhi Bei Sage, also known as Dean He, personally oversaw the entire Alchemy Academy. Besides that, he had taken on disciples and also had a family backing him.

Within the entire Suling Sect, he was the foremost figure beneath the eight Divine Transformation elders.

But in recent years, thanks to Wan Jiuzhang's efforts, some commendable younger figures had gradually emerged on Jade Spring Peak.

For instance, Junior Apprentice-Sister Liu Muhan, who had finally broken through to form her core and emerged from seclusion ten years ago at just over a hundred years of age.

Another example was Ji Yue and Feng Zhiyan, who had been recruited in the last Spirit-Entry Assembly. They had also made gratifying progress at the Initiate realm.

And then there were the few disciples he had personally trained. Although they were still young, both their realms and their strength were presentable.

Taking advantage of this sect grand competition, he could let these young people show their faces in front of others.

"I won't say much about the competition rules. I'm sure you've all found out clearly enough, and those who have been selected all meet the age and realm restrictions. I only want to say one thing: during the matches, act according to your ability. This applies not only to yourselves but also to your fellow disciples. As for those highly lethal techniques, if you cannot fully control them, remember not to use them, lest you hurt yourselves or damage the harmony among fellow disciples!"

"Have you all heard me?"

At the end, his tone suddenly became much stricter, and his gaze swept over everyone with considerable pressure.

Some of those who harbored certain thoughts couldn't help but tremble inwardly under that gaze, abandoning certain notions.

After everyone responded, he nodded with satisfaction.

"Let's go. I'll personally escort you to Sword-Fighting Mountain."

Wan Jiuzhang raised his hand and tossed something out. A flying boat materialized before them.

Everyone boarded it one after another. Liu Muhan stood beside Wan Jiuzhang.

Now that they were both at the Adept realm, their conversation was no longer so constrained.

"Big Senior Brother, actually, I could have taken them there. I heard you're preparing for your Nascent Soul formation matters. I'm sorry to have delayed you," Liu Muhan said.

Wan Jiuzhang smiled. "The matter of forming a Nascent Soul is quite significant. Even though Master has arranged everything for me before going into seclusion, the chances of success are still slim. I don't have much confidence either. Moreover, breaking through to form a Nascent Soul is quite dangerous. There's even the possibility of failing and perishing, body and dao both destroyed. So before going into seclusion, I planned to go see some old friends first. Escorting you all is just on the way."

There was a hint of hesitation in his words.

Even Liu Muhan could see it, and she looked at him with concern.

As Yao Xi Sage's senior disciple, beneath Wan Jiuzhang's glorious exterior, he actually bore considerable pressure.

Other veteran Sage elders within the sect even had disciples or children who had successfully formed Nascent Souls. Although Yao Xi Sage's reputation was great, she hadn't achieved much in terms of nurturing disciples.

If Big Senior Brother could successfully form his Nascent Soul, then Jade Spring Peak would have two Sages on one peak.

Wan Jiuzhang seemed to notice her worry. He waved his hand and deliberately changed the subject with a nonchalant air.

"What about you? Do you have confidence in achieving a good ranking in this competition?"

Liu Muhan smiled bitterly. "Big Senior Brother is joking. Junior Sister has only just formed her core not long ago. I'm just going to broaden my horizons this time. If we're talking about achieving rankings, we'll have to look at Initiate disciples like Ji Yue."

Wan Jiuzhang didn't deny this. He turned his head to look at the lively crowd on the flying boat, feeling that something was missing.

That's right, someone was missing.

He sighed leisurely. "If Xuan Luo were also here, that would be wonderful."

At the mention of Xuan Luo, a smile bloomed on Liu Muhan's face, and her interest was piqued.

"I heard that during the thirty years I was in seclusion breaking through to the Adept realm, Junior Apprentice-Brother Xuan Luo made quite a splash. I only regret not being there at the time."

"That splash was indeed quite something." Wan Jiuzhang's gaze drifted into the distance, as if recalling that scene on Wonderful Pill Peak under the gaze of thousands. He had actually been there at the time.

He had witnessed with his own eyes Luo Chen's glorious moment, seen how the heads of the Three Halls and Five Academies had praised and rewarded Luo Chen, and had even seen Iron Horse Sage's attempt to take him as a disciple, only to be refused by Luo Chen.

Wan Jiuzhang hadn't disturbed Luo Chen at the time, but he had been quite moved inside.

If Luo Chen had really switched allegiances, then when Master emerged from seclusion, she would have been somewhat saddened!

Fortunately, that little junior apprentice-brother hadn't let him down.

It was precisely because of this that Wan Jiuzhang wanted even more to see Junior Apprentice-Brother Xuan Luo, in the name of Jade Spring Peak's true disciple, show his face at the sect grand competition and win some face for Jade Spring Peak.

Liu Muhan was somewhat curious. "But Junior Apprentice-Brother Xuan Luo has only achieved success in alchemy. The sect grand competition focuses on realm, cultivation, and combat techniques. Even if he were to participate, it should be difficult for him to achieve a good ranking, right?"

It wasn't strange for Liu Muhan to say this. After all, Luo Chen had technically been brought into the sect by her, and at that time, he had only been at the early Initiate realm.

However, contrary to her expectations, Big Senior Brother's lips curled upward.

"That's where you're wrong!"

"Oh?"

"Right when you had just gone into seclusion, Xuan Luo met with Master for the second time and had already broken through to the mid Initiate realm. His cultivation speed was far beyond imagination. Later, in between his alchemy work, I occasionally checked on his progress and found that he hadn't neglected his cultivation either. Especially..." Wan Jiuzhang thought of that batch of pills Luo Chen had refined at the Pill Tasting Assembly and said with great confidence, "Especially with the help of the Circulation Traveling Qi Pill, after so long, he has probably advanced even further!"

Liu Muhan hesitated. "Has he already reached the late Initiate realm?"

"More than that!" Wan Jiuzhang said meaningfully. "Before he announced his seclusion, he was already at the late Initiate realm."

Liu Muhan was stunned, unable to control the look of surprise that appeared on her beautiful face.

"Could it be that..."

"Alright, focus on the competition!"

Wan Jiuzhang waved his hand and manipulated the flying boat, leading everyone straight toward Sword-Fighting Mountain.

When they arrived at the scene, the lively spectacle nearly dazzled the lower-level disciples who had been cultivating on Jade Spring Peak and occasionally going out to do tasks.

Only then did they realize how vast Suling Sect truly was.

There were a great many young people whose cultivation levels were similar to their own.

And Sage elders would occasionally appear, making people envious.

Soon, the rewards for this grand competition were announced.

Ji Yue and Feng Zhiyan were most concerned about the Initiate realm. They quickly rushed into the crowd and looked at the list.

"A total of one hundred outstanding disciples will be selected! The last fifty will be rewarded with spirit stones, magical artifacts, and pills. In addition to those, the top fifty will also receive a rare mineral material to aid in refining their life-bound dharma treasure. The top ten..."

When they saw the rewards for the top ten, they were both stunned.

Because, aside from the usual pills, there was a reward called the Circulation Traveling Qi Pill.

This pill had gradually become known within Suling Sect in recent years. Its heaven-defying effects made it so that too many Initiate true cultivators sought after a single pill but couldn't obtain one.

Because of Luo Chen's connection, Jade Spring Peak could receive a batch regularly.

Although the quantity wasn't large, it had still greatly increased the cultivation speed of some Initiate true disciples.

It could be said that one of the reasons Wan Jiuzhang had the confidence to let Jade Spring Peak's disciples show their faces this time was precisely this.

And that was only the lower-grade Circulation Traveling Qi Pill.

But the reward announced on the list was clearly marked as the top-grade tier!

And it was personally refined by the alchemy master, Xuan Luozi!

The two of them looked at each other, exchanging bewildered glances.

Xuan Luozi?

Wasn't that their fellow disciple?

Wan Jiuzhang and Liu Muhan also noticed this and couldn't help but sigh with emotion.

Although Junior Apprentice-Brother Xuan Luo hadn't come in person, he had still participated in the sect grand competition in a certain way.

...

At the same time.

At the Alchemy Academy!

Because the sect grand competition was underway, the attention of most Suling Sect cultivators had been drawn to it.

The Alchemy Academy was no exception.

After all, these alchemists themselves had their own teachers from various peaks and veins. As long as their age and realm were suitable, they could also participate in the sect grand competition.

Therefore, after the competition began, the number of cultivators coming to the Alchemy Academy to seek pills and medicine, as well as the alchemists within the academy, had drastically decreased.

It wouldn't be an exaggeration to describe it as "sparrows can be caught at the door."

This was most evident on the two peaks of Wonderful Pill and Spirit Pill, which were mainly composed of Initiate and Adept realm alchemists.

On the other hand, Pill Emperor Mountain, which was mainly composed of Sage realm alchemists, wasn't as affected.

Those were all elderly Sage elders who had seen too many sect grand competitions and could no longer muster much interest.

Rather than going to watch the excitement, they preferred to focus their energy on alchemy.

Especially recently, they had been quite busy.

Not only had they had the entire Pill Emperor Mountain cleaned, but they were also building several new structures.

Among them, one building was quite large in scale, with very expensive materials. At a rough glance, it seemed to have the specifications of a traveling palace.

This posture didn't look like an expansion but rather like they were preparing to receive someone with great ceremony.

In fact, that was exactly the case!

In a great hall on Pill Emperor Mountain, over a dozen figures had gathered.

Each person wore a four-pattern token at their waist.

These were all the fourth-grade alchemists on Pill Emperor Mountain.

In the vast Suling Sect, with over a thousand Sage elders, aside from those "amateur enthusiasts" who refined pills in their spare time, this group on Pill Emperor Mountain represented the entire essence of the sect's alchemical dao.

Inside the hall, discussions were buzzing.

"According to the message from Elder Gu Mu, he will return to the sect in at most one or two decades."

"How long did he spend studying at the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect this time?"

"Almost sixty years!"

"Sixty years... tsk tsk. It seems that the Alchemy Saint who appeared five hundred years ago at the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is truly worthy of their reputation! For Elder Gu Mu, with his temperament, to endure studying by that person's side for so many years, that person must have extremely high attainments in the alchemical dao. Otherwise, they couldn't have convinced the only five-pattern alchemist in our Suling Sect."

"What you all may not know is that even among fifth-grade alchemists, there are differences."

"Oh? Dean, what do you mean by that?"

"In the common sense, whoever can refine a pill of a certain grade can be called an alchemist of that corresponding grade. But after the fifth grade, it's a bit different. Although refining a fifth-grade pill can also qualify one as a fifth-grade alchemist, most of them are limited by the true meaning of the laws they have comprehended. Many high-grade pills cannot be refined. Only one type of fifth-grade alchemist is not subject to this restriction!"

Amidst everyone's confusion, Dean He's gaze showed a look of longing.

"And that is an existence who has comprehended an exclusive alchemical dao law!"

The assembled Sages looked at each other, not understanding.

After all, they were not Ascendant-level great powers. Their understanding of the true meaning of laws was limited to what was recorded in scriptures.

Dean He shook his head, somewhat self-deprecating. "Unfortunately, that kind of person dedicates their entire life to the alchemical dao and rarely diverts their attention to other great daos. Only such an existence can comprehend an exclusive alchemical dao law among the three thousand great daos and countless laws. I'm not like that. In my early years, I mainly cultivated the sword dao. Although my alchemical dao later caught up, most of the credit goes to the Golden Emperor Devouring Flame I refined."

Someone asked tentatively, "Do you mean that person from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is such an existence?"

Dean He nodded affirmatively. "Yes. Otherwise, why would she be called the Alchemy Saint? And why, when she first entered the Dao Sect five hundred years ago, could she convince all the high-grade alchemists there? Even our sect master, Tai Su Lingjun, only managed to break through to the Void Refining stage with her help. Her alchemy skills have long reached the pinnacle, and few in the world can match her. Perhaps only that vanished forbidden existence could surpass her."

When the forbidden existence was mentioned, no one dared to respond.

The so-called forbidden existence was not just within Suling Sect but extended to the entire sphere of influence under the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. No one dared to discuss such an existence.

Dean He didn't want to dwell on it either and changed the subject.

"Elder Gu Mu will return, but not alone. Under the enthusiastic invitation of our sect master, the Alchemy Saint will visit Suling Sect for a short stay. We must seize this opportunity to have her offer guidance to the alchemists of the Alchemy Academy. Even if it's just a few words, we will benefit immensely! Therefore, the reception standards must not be lacking. We must ensure that the honored guest feels as comfortable and at ease as if she were at home."

What did "as if she were at home" mean?

It was difficult for everyone to grasp the exact measure. They could only act according to the instructions passed down from the sect master.

The elaborately constructed traveling palace on Pill Emperor Mountain was one of the tasks.

Just as everyone was discussing the details, Dean He's expression suddenly shifted, and his gaze looked outward.

The other Sage elders, because their realms were relatively lower, didn't react immediately, but they soon realized what was happening.

They sensed that a hundred li away, streams of pure spiritual qi were gathering from all directions, forming a huge funnel-shaped vortex in the sky, beginning to pour down into one location.

With such an unusual phenomenon, everyone naturally understood the situation.

"Dragon and tiger converge, spiritual qi pours down."

"Someone is forming their core?"

"How interesting. Forming their core during the sect grand competition. They must have deep foundations, yet they've missed out on the generous rewards of the competition."

"It's over at Spirit Pill Peak."

"Strange. The three-pattern alchemists on Spirit Pill Peak are mostly at the Adept realm. Although there are a few late Initiate three-pattern alchemists, they are all quite old, veteran disciples who no longer have the strength to break through. How could they trigger the phenomenon of core formation?"

"It's Xuan Luozi!"

Within the spiritual qi vortex, an extremely sharp dharma power aura began to manifest.

Dean He instantly recognized the owner of that dharma power aura.

When that name came out, the dozen or so Sages on Pill Emperor Mountain couldn't help but be greatly surprised.

That genius alchemist?

He was said to have already begun refining third-grade pills. His alchemical dao talent was terrifyingly exceptional.

Yet unexpectedly, at such a young age, he also possessed equally terrifying talent in cultivation!

"No wonder Iron Horse Sage was so determined to take him as a disciple!"

Someone who had been in the know at the time remarked with such emotion.

Xuan Luozi's age was not a secret. In fact, under the promotion of many, everyone knew he was less than a hundred years old, at most only fifty or sixty.

Ordinary cultivators at that age were still struggling with Initiate realm, while he was already beginning to condense his golden core.

The name Iron Horse Sage dispelled some people's inexplicable thoughts and also prompted admiration for Xuan Luozi's natural talent.

It was at this moment that Dean He suddenly spoke: "With such a genius, we cannot afford to neglect him. When the Alchemy Saint truly graces our sect, we must have her offer some guidance to Xuan Luozi, no matter the cost!"

(End of this chapter)
