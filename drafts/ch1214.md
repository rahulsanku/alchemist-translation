# Chapter 1214: The Demon Cave Stirs, Xuan Luozi

**Opening War!**

Luo Chen had heard this term countless times.

He had even experienced it himself.

But when he read in the ancient texts that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had taken the lead in entering the Mountains and Seas Realm, he finally understood—that too had been an opening war against a barbaric world!

However, the *Miscellaneous Notes on Spiritual Mechanisms* did not record much about this matter.

After skimming through it quickly, Luo Chen committed the gist to memory, then surged his divine sense and continued flipping through other books.

Before long, he found a detailed account in another book, the *Chronicles of Major Events in Youxuan*.

Approximately twelve thousand years ago, earthly qi shifted, the demon cave stirred, and the entire Xuan Realm trembled. At that time, another great realm—the Demon Realm—began to border the Xuan Realm. Spatial overlap occurred, and the Demon Realm started to invade the Xuan Realm. It took over a hundred years for the Xuan Realm cultivators to defeat the demon army that had breached their world and finally re-suppress the demon cave.

In the century that followed, Xuan Realm cultivators swept through various battlefields and discovered multiple spatial passages within the Xuan Realm. Some of these passages led to planets that harbored life.

The Mountains and Seas Realm was among them!

And that passage lay within the territory of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

At the time, the holy land of the Youxuan Continent, the Nether Origin Source Court, was busy suppressing the demon cave and had little interest in minor worlds, so they handed the matter over to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Thus, an opening war erupted!

The Heavenly Origin Ancestor of that era was nearing the end of his lifespan. Knowing he could not swallow such a fat morsel alone, he allied with two of the Nine Great Holy Sects—the Primordial Demon Sect and the Nether Origin Sect—along with many forces from the Youxuan Continent. He even brought in the Life and Death Gate, a holy sect from the Wànlíng Continent, to form a coalition army to open the Mountains and Seas Realm together.

However, because the spatial passage was narrow, cultivators of too high a realm could not pass through. Forcing entry would result in either a complete loss of cultivation or, worse, death and the end of the Dao.

Therefore, only low-ranking cultivators at the Ascendant stage and below could enter the Mountains and Seas Realm.

At that time, apart from the four great holy sects, other forces also invested heavily in manpower and resources.

Top-tier Void Refining powers of the era, such as the Hanging Temple, the Great Soul Gate, and the Five Elements Divine Sect, all participated.

But the coalition underestimated the native experts of the Mountains and Seas Realm!

Countless powerful desolate beasts lived there, including beings at the late Ascendant stage and even the Void Refining level.

Coupled with the advantage of terrain, the coalition's opening progress was not fast.

Some Void Refining powers, after suffering heavy losses, had no choice but to abandon the opening war.

Take the Great Soul Gate, for example!

Despite going all in, they not only failed to reap rich rewards but also suffered severe losses. Their Void Refining ancestor even passed away from the end of his lifespan. With no one to lead, the sect fell into internal strife and ultimately declined to an Ascendant-level power.

Even now, the Great Soul Gate is merely an ordinary Ascendant power, with only two Ascendant great cultivators struggling to hold it together.

Of course, the overall opening progress was still relatively smooth.

Several thousand years later, humanity had truly established a foothold in the Mountains and Seas Realm and set up five holy lands as Dao lineage inheritances.

Among them, the Western Desert—the most resource-poor and fiercely independent region—was claimed by the top Void Refining power, the Hanging Temple.

That war was recorded in the Mountains and Seas Realm as the ancient war between humans and demons.

In truth, the real protagonists were humans and desolate beasts. The demon race only gradually rose later, after some desolate beasts gained intelligence and turned to human cultivation methods.

After the ancient war ended, before people could reap greater rewards from the realm, bad news arrived.

The spatial passage connecting the Xuan Realm and the Mountains and Seas Realm began to shrink.

It was not man-made but the natural repair of the world's power.

The spatial anchors set by the five holy lands in the Mountains and Seas Realm also began to blur, making it impossible for cultivators from the other four sects to naturally descend to the main sects in the Xuan Realm.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was an exception!

They were already extremely close to the spatial passage—in fact, the passage lay within their territory.

Moreover, they had occupied the most essential land in the Mountains and Seas Realm and were the strongest.

Most importantly, someone had refined a spatial spirit treasure for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Relying on that treasure, they could maintain their control over the Mountains and Seas Realm.

"Star Gate!"

In an instant, Luo Chen realized what that spatial spirit treasure was. He even knew exactly who had refined it for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

He also understood why, in the records of the Mountains and Seas Realm after ancient times, all cultivators who ascended came from the Central Continent.

The shrinking spatial passage rendered the anchors of the other four sects useless. Only the Central Continent had a Star Gate that could open a path to the Xuan Realm, so naturally, ascension could only happen from there.

Perhaps this was also why many Sage and Ascendant cultivators later voluntarily submitted to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

After learning this secret history, many of Luo Chen's doubts were instantly resolved.

But new questions arose in his mind.

That was the "demon cave"!

Both ancient texts recorded the phrase "the demon cave stirred."

There was even the shocking history of the Demon Realm invading the Xuan Realm.

So what exactly was this demon cave?

Luo Chen wanted to investigate, but no matter how many books he flipped through on the seventh and eighth floors, even if he occasionally encountered related terms, there were no specific descriptions.

"It seems the Suling Sect either has no definitive records, or if they do, they are only on the ninth floor, which only Ascendant cultivators can access."

Luo Chen stopped regretfully.

By now, he had already stepped onto the sixth floor.

Outside, the sky had completely darkened.

A voice transmission echoed in his ear.

"Time is almost up, little one. Have you not decided yet?"

"Forgive my disturbance, senior."

Luo Chen apologized, then descended to the first floor. He took the list of books he had noted earlier and handed it to a gray-robed old man on duty there, who appeared to be only at the Adept stage.

The old man glanced at Luo Chen, then scanned the list. His brow immediately furrowed.

"Are you sure you want to exchange for this many spells?"

Luo Chen nodded.

The old man's tone suddenly turned stern. "The sect has few restrictions on techniques and spells below the Sage stage, but you must understand the principle of 'biting off more than you can chew.' This list of yours contains thirteen metal-attribute spells. Can you even practice them all? And while the Initiate-stage ones are acceptable, why are you also exchanging for Qi Refinement and Adept-stage spells?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Thank you for your advice, senior. Xuan Luo naturally understands the principle, but my situation is somewhat different from ordinary people."

"Oh?" The old man looked at Luo Chen with curiosity.

Luo Chen explained his background.

The old man nodded thoughtfully, sizing Luo Chen up. "So you were a rogue cultivator before, entering through the Admission Assembly."

Luo Chen nodded. "Yes. During my time as a rogue cultivator, I focused too much on raising my realm and lacked in Dao-protecting arts. So now I want to exchange for some Qi Refinement-stage metal-attribute spells to solidify my foundation. As for the higher-level Adept-stage spells, I just want to broaden my horizons."

The old man shook his head. "Without the corresponding realm, you cannot grasp the true meaning. Any high-level spell has cultivation thresholds—for example, mana or divine sense."

Luo Chen chuckled. "So the Adept-stage spell I selected is relatively simple, with a low threshold. And senior may not know, but I possess a Sharp Metal Body, giving me strong affinity for metal-attribute spells. A little practice should not be a problem."

This time, the old man was genuinely surprised.

He scrutinized Luo Chen for a long moment, then stroked his beard and nodded.

"Xuan Luo? You are a disciple of Sage Yaoxi?"

"Yes!"

"Very well. Hand over the contribution points, and I will engrave the spells for you."

Luo Chen took out his alchemist token, which surprised the old man again.

But this time, the other party did not ask further. After deducting the contribution points, he quickly engraved the spell copies for Luo Chen.

As Luo Chen left carrying a stack of spells, the gray-robed old man could not help but sigh.

At this age, with such talent and such a realm, he should have a promising future.

Yet he was dividing his attention among spells and even dabbling in the alchemy Dao. His future achievements would likely be limited.

He wondered how Junior Sister Yaoxi taught her disciples.

But ever since Junior Sister Yaoxi listened to the sect master's lecture that year, she had focused entirely on her own cultivation.

Two hundred years had passed on Jade Spring Peak, and no second Sage had emerged. It was clear that Yaoxi had not been very attentive to her disciples' training.

Judging by the mana aura on Xuan Luo just now, he was probably practicing the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Profound Chapter*. Perhaps he could ask a Sage from Elder Tiange's lineage to take Xuan Luo in for serious instruction, so as not to waste a good seedling.

"No rush. I'll wait and see. It might just be a young man's unsettled mind. He will change later."

Considering that transferring a disciple would reflect poorly on Junior Sister Yaoxi, the gray-robed old man temporarily suppressed his desire to nurture talent.

As the night deepened, the doors of the Scripture Pavilion slowly closed.

...

Inside the Kunping Cave Mansion.

Luo Chen looked at the thirteen spells he had exchanged for today and clicked his tongue twice.

One sect contribution point was equivalent to one low-grade spirit stone.

An ordinary first-order spell, however, started at a hundred contribution points.

Second-order spells were even more expensive, costing up to a thousand.

Today, he had exchanged for thirteen spells: four first-order, six second-order, costing a total of seven thousand contribution points.

And that simplest, barely third-order metal-attribute spell was worth three thousand contribution points.

In one go, he had exhausted the ten thousand contribution points that Gongsun Mao had given him.

Although the Suling Sect had relaxed conditions for spell exchanges, the prices were truly hard to bear.

But then Luo Chen thought about it and a faint smile curled at the corner of his mouth.

Clearly, this was a rule set by the Suling Sect to encourage disciples to do more for the sect.

"How strange. When did I start haggling over such trivial gains? Is it because I've been interacting with these low-ranking cultivators too much, reverting to the mindset of my Qi Refinement and Initiate days?"

Luo Chen chuckled at himself and stopped caring about such minutiae.

Ten thousand sect contribution points were worth at most a little over ten thousand spirit stones.

To him, that was nothing.

But if he could cultivate all thirteen spells to Great Perfection, he would gain sixty-five achievement points.

That was the real profit!

Without further hesitation, Luo Chen did not even look at the spell engravings. He directly began learning and casting the spells he had exchanged for today.

First, the first-order spell, Golden Light Thorn!

In an instant, a golden thorn as fine as an ox hair materialized before Luo Chen.

As he adjusted his spiritual power, the golden thorn alternately grew and shrank, flying back and forth silently.

At the same time.

On the attribute panel, the entry for Golden Light Thorn kept changing.

Familiar, Proficient, Perfect, Grandmaster, until Great Perfection!

In just a few breaths, a first-order metal-attribute spell that would take a Qi Refinement cultivator days or even months to barely master was cultivated to Great Perfection.

And five achievement points were easily obtained!

Luo Chen did not stop. He moved on to other spells.

On the attribute panel, the proficiency of the entries changed wildly.

By the time he had cultivated the top-tier second-order spell, Myriad Swords Art, to Great Perfection, sixty achievement points had been added to the end of the panel.

Combined with the points from practicing the *Metal Transformation Art* earlier, he now had a total of seventy achievement points.

As for that barely third-order metal-attribute spell, Luo Chen temporarily set it aside.

Its name was Golden Skin Art, a defensive spell.

It combined the physical body with mana, constantly protecting the body. Its defensive power was excellent, making it very suitable for Adept-stage cultivators who did not yet have a natal life-bound treasure.

However, after practicing it, a faint golden light would always appear, making it too conspicuous.

Especially since this spell involved the physical body, it was not easy to suppress.

Luo Chen had exchanged for it both to see if ordinary spells could benefit his current powerful Chaos Indestructible Body and to lay groundwork for the future.

"I'll wait a while longer!"

"When I have saved up more contribution points and can visit the Scripture Pavilion again, I'll learn it on the side."

"At that time, I can also use it to display my talent and deepen others' impression of me as a genius."

After finishing his spell practice, Luo Chen did not sleep.

Instead, he continued studying the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Profound Chapter*.

He planned to deduce a set of techniques suitable for simultaneous cultivation of the five elements based on this method, to facilitate his upcoming core formation and Sage condensation.

If he succeeded, it would greatly help him return to the Ascendant stage in the future.

Most importantly, it would wash away the traces left on him by the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture*.

Over five hundred years of cultivation in the Mountains and Seas Realm, the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* had been his lifebound technique since the early Adept stage. Its influence on him was too great.

Even though he had reorganized his physical body using the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra*, it still involved the Phoenix clan's Nine Tribulations Three Nirvanas technique.

If he wanted to re-cultivate the immortal path, if he wanted to be completely free from Rosycloud's influence in the future, he had to change!

...

In the days that followed.

Luo Chen's life became extremely routine.

At night, he cultivated and improved his deduced techniques.

During the day, he went out and mingled in the Alchemy Court.

Thanks to the theories he had proposed at the pill-testing meeting, He Jun's group received strong support from the Alchemy Court in terms of medicinal herbs.

Not forgetting the well-digger, they allocated a batch of resources to Luo Chen, allowing him to freely refine various first-order pills.

Wan Jiuzhang also kept his word, regularly sending Luo Chen large quantities of low-grade herbs to support his learning in the alchemy Dao.

Luo Chen did not waste these resources. He poured them all into refining low-grade pills.

The alchemy talent he displayed shocked the entire Alchemy Court once again.

Both the Wonderful Pill Peak and the Spirit Pill Peak came to know his name.

Especially three years later, when Luo Chen obtained the certification of a Two-Stripe Alchemist. Not only the Alchemy Court, but even the other three halls and five courts knew that a true alchemy genius had emerged from the Alchemy Court!

On the very day he became a Two-Stripe Alchemist, someone close to him jokingly added the character "zi" after his name.

Xuan Luozi, as a sign of respect!

Such behavior would normally invite ridicule.

A mere Initiate-stage cultivator, a mere Two-Stripe Alchemist, worthy of such an honorific?

But the three halls, five courts, and most other Suling Sect disciples raised no objection.

It was clear that many held high hopes for Luo Chen, believing he could one day reach the alchemy realm that matched this title.

In those three years, research on the Circulation Qi Pill had made rapid progress initially but then stalled.

Three years later, on the day Luo Chen became a Two-Stripe Alchemist, He Jun's group extended an invitation to him.

To this!

Luo Chen gladly agreed.

As a newly promoted Two-Stripe Alchemist, he officially joined the Circulation Qi Pill Mass Production Research Project.
