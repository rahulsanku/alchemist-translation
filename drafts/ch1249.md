# Chapter 1249: Demon Spirit Sweep, Vermilion Bird Descends

A demon spirit comparable to a Void Refining True Lord!

Luo Chen had imagined that within the Central Demon Abyss, there might exist extremely powerful beings—perhaps even surpassing the Ascendant level was not impossible.

But seeing one face to face, he still found it somewhat unbelievable.

The Demon Suppression Trial was held once every thousand years.

Over ten thousand years, the Nine Great Sacred Sects had sent Ascendant-level grandmasters frequently into this place, and each trial conducted a thorough purge.

It was a cleansing from the outside in, like sifting through a sieve.

In other words, the current batch of demon spirits had formed naturally within the last thousand years.

A thousand years—even among humans, renowned for their excellent comprehension and rapid cultivation speed, it was not guaranteed that one could cultivate to the Void Refining stage.

How could such a level of existence appear here?

Regarding demon spirits, Luo Chen had heard of them, seen them, and even observed them carefully.

These things were not purely flesh-and-blood creatures. They were mostly condensed from vast amounts of demonic qi, and their so-called intelligence was extremely low.

Most of the time, they didn't wander around but basically coiled up inside various demon lairs.

As for combat ability, it was not even worth mentioning.

That was why he could easily kill those fifth-rank demon spirits across ranks.

As for why other Nascent Soul cultivators couldn't achieve this, it was truly because the environmental restrictions here greatly suppressed the strength of Nascent Soul cultivators.

A thousand years—a Void Refining-level demon spirit appearing in the Dark Demon Cavern. Was there a problem with the Dark Demon Cavern itself, or had some strange change occurred in the Xuan Realm?

Luo Chen couldn't understand, and perhaps other Xuan Realm experts didn't know the reason either.

He merely watched coldly as that avian demon spirit flew into the distance, feeling relieved in his heart instead.

Such powerful existences, even within the Demon Abyss, there probably weren't many.

Now that one strange entity had departed, it was actually a good thing for him to sneak into the Demon Abyss and find a place to cultivate.

However, those human cultivators outside were likely in for a world of trouble.

Maintaining his previous state of concealment, Luo Chen stepped forward and directly vanished into the mist-shrouded Demon Abyss.

...

Several days later, the originally pitch-black lake had turned a verdant green.

The calm water surface reflected the dim sky. Though there was no sun or moon, the shimmering waves held a unique charm.

Suddenly!

A violent surge swept through, washing over everything.

A colossal figure leaped across the great lake. Though it didn't stop, it left behind crashing waves and surging tides.

At the bottom of the lake.

"Pfft!"

Nether Bamboo True Person, who had just found a water cave to begin cultivation, suddenly spat out a mouthful of blood.

It was far too sudden.

The formation she had set up earlier using the compass bestowed by her sect's elder had been broken.

And it was destroyed in the most brutal and violent manner.

With her mind and spirit connected to it, she had no time to prepare and was severely wounded instantly.

"What kind of existence is that?"

Nether Bamboo True Person sat on her meditation cushion, her body swaying unsteadily, her face deathly pale.

The tremendous commotion outside naturally couldn't be sensed by her alone.

Five figures simultaneously emerged from their respective lake-bottom cave dwellings and gathered at the Water Curtain Cave where Nether Bamboo True Person resided.

Amid everyone's worried expressions, they still looked toward Dean He.

The Seven True Ones of Plain Spirit regarded him as their leader, and he was the one issuing orders for this trial.

Under everyone's gaze, Dean He spoke solemnly: "No need to panic. If an enemy were attacking, they would have already broken in. They wouldn't have given us enough time to react. What just happened was more like a powerful existence passing by."

"Passing by?" Yao Xi True Person found it hard to believe. "What kind of existence could cause such a commotion just by passing by?"

Dean He thought for a moment and said, "Perhaps it was done deliberately to make its presence known?"

Make its presence known?

Everyone exchanged bewildered glances.

In such an environment, which cultivator wouldn't want to hide themselves well?

Making such a grand display was practically seeking death.

Or rather, was that thing just now not a cultivator, but a demon spirit?

But even a fifth-rank demon spirit shouldn't cause such a disturbance when flying, right?

Nether Bamboo True Person struggled to stand up. "Regardless, we still need to set up another concealment formation. Otherwise, none of us can cultivate in peace."

Yao Xi looked at her with concern. "Your condition?"

Nether Bamboo True Person rubbed her brow. "My body is fine, but my soul has suffered some damage. Once the formation is set up, I'll recuperate properly. But with this delay, I'm afraid it will be difficult to seize this opportunity."

Yao Xi sighed. "I'll help!"

The others didn't just stand by either. Though they weren't experts in formations, they all had some knowledge. With Nether Bamboo as the lead and them as support, they should be able to set up a decent concealment formation.

Amid the crowd, a voice suddenly rang out.

"Uncle-Master, take this. It might help."

Nether Bamboo True Person looked doubtfully at the pill Jiang Huang handed over. "Junior Disciple?"

But before her, Dean He had already reached out abruptly, taking the pill, his eyes gleaming.

"This is... such a rich pill fragrance. A soul-nourishing type pill? And it's as high as fourth-rank. Where did you get this?"

Jiang Huang hesitated for a moment but ultimately didn't mention Luo Chen's name.

She simply made up an excuse about spending a large sum to buy it at the market, brushing it off.

The others didn't seem inclined to dig deeper. After a brief examination, Dean He happily handed it to Nether Bamboo True Person.

With this pill, she might be able to recover her damaged soul in the short term.

Only Yao Xi True Person frowned slightly, looking at her disciple's profile.

In the twenty-plus years since her disciple had escaped from trouble and participated in the Demon Suppression Trial, she had been with her day and night. When did she have time to go to the market to buy pills?

And it was a fourth-rank soul-type pill that even Senior Brother He praised!

The only person Jiang Huang could have come into contact with regarding such matters was just one!

"Is it Xuan Luo?"

...

Not far from the great lake.

A figure flew low to the ground, hesitation and conflict flashing continuously in his eyes.

"I wonder how they're doing."

"Just take one look. If they're all dead, I won't have to worry about being held accountable when I return to the sect."

"If they're still alive... I..."

Hongzhan True Person's heart was filled with inner turmoil.

He had fled swiftly, abandoning his fellow disciples, driven by the instinct to survive.

But thinking back to what Sect Leader Tai Su Lingjun had said before their departure, he couldn't rest easy.

Moreover, without the mutual support of his fellow disciples, surviving alone in the current Dark Demon Cavern was practically a one-in-ten-thousand chance.

But if they were alive, the fate awaiting him after leaving wouldn't be much better.

For a moment, his thoughts were tangled in contradiction.

Just as he was hesitating whether to advance or retreat, a massive black shadow flew overhead.

Hongzhan True Person instinctively looked up and saw a stream of demonic qi piercing toward him.

Chi!

Before he could react.

In an instant, he fell silent and perished.

He didn't even have time for his Nascent Soul to escape.

In the sky above, the enormous avian didn't pause at all, as if it had merely crushed an ant.

And such actions would occur frequently from then on.

As the avian demon spirit left the Central Demon Abyss, wherever it passed, any exposed cultivator died horribly on the spot.

It was like patrolling its territory—anyone daring to invade the Dark Demon Cavern faced only one outcome.

Death!

And its target was very clear!

Those who shone like bright moons—the grandmasters of the Dao Sect!

In a basin that resembled ruins.

Dozens of figures bustled in and out.

An elder with an immortal aura sat inside a Dharma carriage placed at the center of the basin, rapidly recovering his mana.

In the previous battle, though he had relied on dozens of Nascent Soul disciples to trap and kill three Ascendant-level demon spirits one by one, he himself had suffered considerable damage.

Afterward, he had also dispersed a demon lair, which took some effort.

That was why he had to stop and recover a bit.

But thinking of the countless demon spirits now in the cavern, even he couldn't help but smile bitterly.

"Although clearing demon lairs was always a tough job within the sect, there was at least some gain. When did it become this dangerous?"

Just as he was muttering to himself, his heart suddenly stirred.

An inexplicable perception surged into his mind.

It was a premonition triggered by the trembling of his primordial spirit.

Without a second thought, he flew out of the Dharma carriage and roared.

"Enemy attack! Disciples, follow me to meet the enemy!"

As he roared, he drew a circle with one hand, and a bronze mirror emitting a milky white light appeared before him.

As he held the mirror and shone it, a powerful beam of milky white light shot into the distance.

"Watch my Haoran Mirror!"

The sacred and pure power of righteousness stirred the origin qi of heaven and earth, forming a majestic force, seeking to purify all evil in the world.

But the dim sky brought the breath of death.

A massive figure, facing the milky white light head-on, neither dodged nor avoided, breaking through heavily and descending into the basin.

Boom!

Qi force scattered in all directions, the aftershocks shaking ten thousand li around.

The hastily assembled Nascent Soul disciples of the Dao Sect had almost no power to resist before being crushed to dust by the powerful shockwaves.

The Dao Sect grandmaster's eyes nearly split with rage as the words burst from his mouth.

"A Void Refining demon spirit!"

...

Within the Demon Suppression Tower.

Two figures sat facing each other.

A chessboard lay between them.

"Junior Sister Vermilion Bird, your chess skills are regressing more and more!"

An old man in black robes with a jade belt around his waist smiled as he looked at the heroic woman across from him, who had just made a hasty move.

She wore a red shirt, her sword-like brows slanting into her temples, her nose high and straight, her bright, spirited large eyes giving an immediate impression of heroic vigor.

She snorted lightly. "I've never been interested in the game of Go, and you're good at defense. In the sect, aside from the Great Sovereign who can beat you, only that old fellow Azure Dragon can drag a game out with you for a hundred years. No more, no more."

Xuanwu True Lord smiled slightly. "How can one give up halfway? The trial isn't over yet. Consider it passing the time—finish this game with your elder brother!"

Vermilion Bird True Lord furrowed her sword-like brows. "Then hurry up and place your pieces. If you dawdle any longer, be careful—I might flip this board."

"Hehe." Xuanwu True Lord stroked his beard, about to say something, when his gaze fell outside.

Vermilion Bird True Lord also looked over and directly asked, "What is it?"

Outside the tower, a figure stood respectfully.

"Reporting to the two True Lords, an elder of the Dao Sect has inquired about how the trial is progressing."

Vermilion Bird True Lord said impatiently, "What's the rush? It's only been a year."

Xiu Teng bent over, standing at the entrance of the Demon Suppression Tower, his expression extremely respectful.

"The Dao Sect elder said that many of the soul plaques in his hands have dimmed significantly, indicating that the trial is probably not going smoothly. The other sects have also reported similar situations. That's why they asked me to come and inquire."

Vermilion Bird True Lord remained unconcerned. "A bunch of incompetent juniors—if they die, they die. Besides, which Demon Suppression Trial doesn't see dozens or even a hundred deaths? What's there to worry about? Go back. Once the overflowing demonic qi dissipates, it means the suppression mission is mostly complete. Then I'll release them."

"Yes, True Lord. I will relay this to the others and take my leave."

After Xiu Teng withdrew, Vermilion Bird True Lord curled her lips.

"So even the demon race, who are prone to great joy, great sorrow, and extremes, can be this docile?"

Across from her, Xuanwu True Lord held a chess piece, thinking as he spoke. "These are merely remnants of the demon race left behind in the Xuan Realm. After ten thousand years, they have long since diverged from the native demon race of the Demon Realm. Moreover, the former reliance of the Fudi lineage fell in that great battle. If they weren't docile, how could they survive?"

Mentioning that one, Vermilion Bird True Lord, who had been somewhat disdainful earlier, showed a slight change in expression.

Xuanwu True Lord looked up. "Speaking of which, that battle was fought by you and him together, wasn't it?"

Vermilion Bird True Lord nodded silently.

Xuanwu True Lord knew some inside information and could understand his fellow disciple's reaction.

That one was astonishingly talented, once hailed as the strongest Void Refining cultivator in history!

If he could one day break through to the Body Integration stage, he would undoubtedly become the pinnacle existence of the Xuan Realm.

If such an existence were human, it would be one thing—but he was a member of the demon race.

The Dark Myst Continent could not tolerate him!

The overlord of this land hinted, and Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign went along with the plan, orchestrating a battle while also eliminating another potential threat.

However, in a same-realm battle, the other party was also hailed as the most pure-blooded genius in the history of the Heavenly Phoenix Clan. Determining victory or defeat was easy, but life or death was extremely difficult.

Even with the assistance of Vermilion Bird True Lord, who was also skilled in fire-attribute laws, they only managed to knock Rosycloud down from the mortal world.

Fifteen hundred years later, not only had she been reborn through nirvana and returned to the Xuan Realm, but she had also used the insights gained between life and death to successfully break through to the Body Integration stage, surpassing them.

As for that one, he unfortunately fell in the small world.

Thinking of this, Xuanwu True Lord sighed with emotion but also harbored some doubt.

"Is he really dead?"

"He's dead. Isn't his grave left in the Mountains and Seas Realm?"

"But the Great Sovereign does not allow disciples of subordinate sects to explore the Demon-Fall Land. We only know what we've heard. And although Rosycloud is strong, she might not necessarily be able to kill him, right?"

"Why don't you go ask the Great Sovereign about this question?"

"You know the Great Sovereign is moody and unpredictable now. How would I dare to ask?"

"Then why are you asking me?"

"Just passing the time. How did we end up getting angry while chatting?"

"No more playing. You're clearly not as old as Azure Dragon, but you talk even more than he does."

Vermilion Bird True Lord reached out, about to sweep her hand across the chessboard.

Fortunately, Xuanwu True Lord knew this junior sister's temperament and had already reached out to stop her.

Just as he was about to say something with a smile, golden light suddenly blazed within the tower.

The two exchanged glances, both realizing what it meant.

"Mutiny in the demon cavern—an Ascendant is calling for help!"

A fierce look flashed across Vermilion Bird True Lord's face. "Open the restrictions. I'm going in!"
