# Chapter 1255: Settling the Spirit, Peering into the Void; Mountains and Seas, Flying Stars

“This person…”

“White Tiger, what about this person?”

White Tiger True Lord’s eyes revealed appreciation. “This person is merely at mid-Sage stage, yet he externally manifests pure Gold origin aura. Truly a genius!”

For such words to come from a man brimming with murderous aura and extreme brutality showed just how much White Tiger True Lord admired that young man.

Black Tortoise True Lord chuckled. “Has Senior Brother taken a liking to him? That makes sense. This child comes from the Plain Spirit Sect. His constitution is nothing special, merely a Sharp Gold Body. But his comprehension and cultivation talent are excellent—he advanced to Sage stage in just over a hundred years and even comprehended the origin of the Gold law. Such talent is perfectly suited for Senior Brother’s White Emperor Unbounded Severance!”

Upon hearing that the other party had only cultivated for a little over a hundred years, the light in White Tiger True Lord’s eyes grew even brighter.

“What is his name?”

“His name is Xuan Luo!” Black Tortoise True Lord, though not one to show his hand, had committed to memory the information of everyone participating in the Demon-Suppressing Trial, so he could speak it offhand. But he changed his tone. “A talent like this, I’m afraid the Plain Spirit Sect won’t let him go.”

White Tiger True Lord laughed heartily, confident even to the point of arrogance. “What’s there to let go or not? For a talent like this to enter my command is his honor, and the Plain Spirit Sect’s honor as well. If he cultivates successfully, won’t he be able to protect the Plain Spirit Sect in the future? Moreover…”

White Tiger True Lord pointed to another person in the treasure mirror. “Hasn’t the Plain Spirit Sect produced an Ascendant this time? Their harvest is already this great. What’s one mere Sage cultivator to give up to me?”

The other three just smiled and said nothing.

They all understood White Tiger True Lord. This man always acted without regard for rules, and within the sect, only the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign could suppress him.

Most importantly, the other party’s lifespan was nearing its end, and he still had no successor.

No wonder he had taken a liking to a junior at first sight and developed the desire to take him as a disciple.

Their attention shifted to other suspicious candidates—those whose demonic aura was heavier than others.

If any high-rank demon spirit had used a host body to escape the Dark Demon Cave, it would surely be among these people.

…

On the great ship.

The atmosphere was not one of relaxed joy after the trial’s conclusion, but rather heavy and low.

The losses from this trial far exceeded what any sect had imagined.

Even the Plain Spirit Sect, which had preserved its personnel relatively intact, had become sorrowful after the initial joy.

They too had lost three people.

“Qianxiu, Shenglin, Hongzhan… alas…”

When Elder Gumu mentioned those three, his tone was filled with regret.

The others felt the same, but when it came to Hongzhan, their expressions were all strange.

Elder Gumu naturally noticed this and found it somewhat odd.

“What’s wrong?”

The others found it hard to speak plainly, so they could only look at Dean He.

By now, Dean He had successfully advanced to Ascendant stage. Although his realm was not yet stable, he could be considered on equal footing with the Ascendant elder.

Unless there was a direct master-disciple relationship, it was always realm and strength that spoke.

Besides, he had always had a good relationship with Elder Gumu.

Now Dean He hesitated for a moment, then spoke frankly. “Nothing major. It’s just that during the trial, Junior Brother Hongzhan had some minor conflicts with us. Now that he’s dead, there’s no point in pursuing it further.”

Elder Gumu looked deeply at Dean He, then at the other juniors with their varied expressions. He more or less guessed what had happened.

But he didn’t expose it.

The dead are gone; pursuing blame was indeed meaningless.

He stood up, walked to the window, and looked outside.

On the great ship, not many disciples from other sects were moving about. Instead, Dao Sect cultivators, fully armed with weapons and armor, stood at various points along the deck and gunwales, as if guarding against external enemies, or more like monitoring everyone.

He silently watched this scene, and only after a long while did he speak softly. “Entering Heavenly Origin this time, I’m afraid it won’t be simply to admire the Dao Sect’s scenery. You all need to be careful.”

Everyone nodded one after another.

Such a major incident had occurred in the Dark Demon Cave. It was impossible for the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to have no reaction.

This was evident from the moment they left the Demon-Suppressing Tower and underwent the Demon-Examining Treasure Mirror inspection.

Presumably, after entering the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect’s interior, there would be other forms of scrutiny.

Seeing the oppressive atmosphere among the group, Elder Gumu suddenly smiled. “But you don’t need to worry. As long as your conscience is clear, that’s enough. Moreover, following past conventions, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, in order to win over the cultivators from various sects who successfully passed the Demon-Suppressing Trial, often brings out some good things as rewards. I imagine this time shouldn’t be any different.”

Good things?

At this, everyone actually became somewhat expectant.

Next, Elder Gumu asked in detail about each person’s gains, especially regarding their cultivation.

Learning that everyone had gained something, more or less, and that Sage Yaoxi had even had an enlightenment on law true intent, he was even more satisfied.

With that, the meeting came to an end.

After bidding farewell to his fellow sect members, Luo Chen returned to his private room.

The door was tightly shut, and the formation was activated.

Once everything had settled down, Luo Chen let out a slight sigh of relief.

He began to think about where to go from here.

His realm had already been restored, and he had even made progress, reaching mid-Ascendant stage.

Thus, the Plain Spirit Sect no longer held much benefit for him.

Especially since Grand Pure Spirit seemed to have grown suspicious of him. Cultivating under someone else’s roof always carried the risk of being discovered.

Luo Chen also didn’t want to continue suppressing and hiding his realm, only to pull off another miracle of reaching Ascendant in a hundred or two hundred years.

That would be detrimental to his cultivation.

And if he left the Plain Spirit Sect, where should he go?

“On the Youxuan Continent, there are the two great Source Court forces of Nether Capital and Heavenly Phoenix above, the nine great Saint Sects entrenched in the Central Lands, and countless large and small sects below. It can be said that every suitable cultivation territory has been developed to the extreme.”

“In this situation, whether I join any sect or become a rogue cultivator, neither is very suitable.”

“Perhaps leaving the Youxuan Continent for another Xuan Realm continent wouldn’t be a bad choice.”

Luo Chen thought of the *Xuan Realm Secret Record* that Nether Departure Divine Lord had given him long ago, which roughly recorded the situation of many Xuan Realm continents.

The situation on the Ten-Thousand Spirits Continent, where the Life and Death Gate was located, was very complex.

It wasn’t dominated by humans, nor by demons, but by a mix of ten thousand spirits.

All kinds of races, even sentient spirits and strange beings, and even Archaic beasts that had cultivated to the Great Vehicle stage—also known as Great Vehicle True Spirits—appeared from time to time.

That place would be very suitable for Luo Chen to travel and cultivate, but unfortunately, it was too far away.

He didn’t know how the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had contacted the Life and Death Gate when they first opened the Mountains and Seas Realm, or if some strong members among them had actively infiltrated the opening army.

Close to the Youxuan Continent was the Blood Rain Continent, separated only by the Boundless Sea.

According to records, the Blood Rain Continent had relatively weak sect forces, being dominated by families and rogue cultivators.

Because of the large number of rogue cultivators mixed in, coupled with the estrangement and conflicts between family forces, small-scale battles and even wars frequently occurred there.

One could say the wind and rain of blood never stopped, hence the continent’s name, Blood Rain.

Such a place was not unfamiliar to Luo Chen.

The Northern Sea cultivation world in the Mountains and Seas Realm was exactly like this.

The only difference was probably that the Northern Sea cultivation world, due to the separation of large sea areas, had a lower intensity of conflict than the Blood Rain Continent.

“The Blood Rain Continent… that’s also a good option.”

Luo Chen thought of one person.

Blood Qi Zi!

A famous artifact refiner on the Blood Rain Continent, even said to be able to directly refine spirit treasures.

Among the legacy of the Rending Feather Demon Lord in Luo Chen’s storage ring, the Eight Desolations Lightning-Absorbing Needle was made by Blood Qi Zi.

Its exquisite refinement and miraculous ability to assist in crossing tribulations had earned Luo Chen’s admiration.

Speaking of which, he had accumulated a large number of precious ores.

From the Chaos Stone brought out of the Return to Ruins long ago, to the large amount of Demon Marrow Diamonds obtained from the Dark Demon Cave recently.

It would be a waste to keep these things idle.

If he could commission someone to refine them, or even learn some higher-level artifact refinement techniques and use these materials to forge a magic treasure himself, that would be even better.

This thought wasn’t a sudden whim for Luo Chen.

He had always had this idea.

He had many magic treasures suitable for combat, but most were relatively low-grade and not suited for his current level of battle.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron was too precious. If he didn’t use it, fine, but once he did, he had to leave no survivors, lest someone covet it.

The only one suitable for combat was the Yuantu Sword.

But unfortunately, this treasure came from the hands of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining. If someone saw through its origins, it would cause trouble on the Youxuan Continent.

“It’s a pity that the Burial Bone Umbrella and that Ninefold Immortal Tower were destroyed in the process of escaping the Return to Ruins. Otherwise, why would I be in such a predicament?”

Luo Chen shook his head, gradually forming a direction in his mind.

But that was a matter for after he left the Plain Spirit Sect.

For now, he still had to go to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

The great ship wasn’t slow, but it wasn’t fast either. Crossing a single Guangling Commandery would take about a day or two.

Luo Chen didn’t plan to do nothing.

Cultivation was naturally out of the question.

But he could use the time for something else.

With a thought, two boxes appeared in Luo Chen’s hands.

He opened them one by one, revealing two pages.

Each page was densely wrapped in black threads.

All restrictions!

Luo Chen carefully touched one of them, then slowly released his divine sense.

As his divine sense contacted the page, a miraculous scene occurred.

The black threads, upon meeting Luo Chen’s divine sense, instantly melted like snow under the spring sun, and the content within revealed itself plainly.

“As expected, the pill recipes left by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining can only be opened with a soul of the corresponding realm!”

“Although I haven’t yet cultivated to the Body Integration stage, my soul strength is already no different from a Body Integration True Lord.”

“The soul restrictions I couldn’t budge before are now easily broken.”

Luo Chen eagerly looked at the first page.

**[Spirit-Settling Pill]**

As Luo Chen read carefully, the more he read, the more excited he became.

This was a pill recipe that assisted cultivators in their cultivation at the Body Integration stage.

Body Integration cultivators primarily cultivated their Void Phase!

And the Void Phase connected to the laws above and the primordial spirit and soul below. If one wanted the Void Phase to be stable, the primordial spirit naturally couldn’t be in chaos.

The function of the Spirit-Settling Pill was to stabilize the primordial spirit, preventing it from shaking the Void Phase.

Moreover, the Spirit-Settling Pill had another wonderful use. In extreme cases where the primordial spirit was about to collapse, taking this pill could stabilize it and allow for rapid reassembly after collapse.

Although the effect only worked once, for high-rank cultivators, it was no less than a free pass from death.

The first effect alone was enough to make Luo Chen treasure it like a precious gem, and the second effect made him even more covetous.

He already had the Prison Soul Art protecting his sea of consciousness. If he could also refine a Spirit-Settling Pill, then his soul defense would truly have no blind spots.

The only thing that gave Luo Chen pause was the complexity of the required ingredients. There were too many types.

To collect them all would likely require a great deal of effort.

The only thing Luo Chen had on hand that might be useful was the small amount of top-grade soul stones he had brought out of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Shaking his head, Luo Chen didn’t feel too discouraged.

He hadn’t reached the Body Integration stage yet. There was no rush.

He would have plenty of time later to collect those ingredients.

Then, following the same method, he dissolved the soul restriction on the other pill recipe.

**[Void-Peering Pill]**

This was also a sixth-rank pill recipe, used by Body Integration cultivators to cultivate their Void Phase.

However, this pill wasn’t taken by the cultivator themselves, but used on the Void Phase.

It transformed the void into reality, accelerating the condensation of the Void Phase into a Real Phase, achieving a Primordial Spirit Dharma Phase!

It could save a cultivator hundreds or even thousands of years of effort. Once the Primordial Spirit Dharma Phase was truly condensed, one would already be at the late Body Integration stage.

This time, Luo Chen was very happy!

Because he discovered that this pill had a single function and didn’t require too many ingredients.

Most importantly, he already had most of them on him!

This was quite unexpected, yet completely within reason.

The ingredients Luo Chen had on him came partly from his collection at the Plain Spirit Sect and partly from the legacy of the Rending Feather Demon Lord.

And Rending Feather Demon Lord himself was a peak Body Integration stage demon cultivator who also cultivated a Dharma Phase.

Thus, his storage treasure contained a large number of related ingredients.

Luo Chen compared them one by one and found that most were very suitable for refining the Void-Peering Pill.

The only thing missing was a type of insect egg called the Formless Void-Controlling Worm.

“Void-Controlling Worm eggs… I’ve never heard of them.”

After noting down this name, Luo Chen sealed the two pill recipes with his own techniques and carefully put them back into his storage ring.

After doing all this, time had unknowingly passed.

Outside, the sky had gone from bright to dark, and from dark to bright, emitting a faint light.

Luo Chen got up, pushed open the door, and walked out.

The joyful expression from before had long been restrained.

Because there was still a regret lingering in his heart.

The alchemy inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining back then had contained a large number of pill recipes.

For example, the fifth-rank divine pill, Law-Derivation Pill, had helped him comprehend the origin of Earth when he advanced to Ascendant.

Another example was the fifth-rank Soul-Scouring Pill refined from the Black Lotus Lotus Pod, which had helped him quickly stabilize his Ascendant realm.

Presumably, these Spirit-Settling and Void-Peering pills would also be of great use in Body Integration stage cultivation.

But!

Beyond Spirit-Settling and Void-Peering, he should have had one more high-rank pill recipe.

Its name was—Dao Union!

One could imagine what a pill recipe named “Dao Union” would involve.

But that pill recipe had been taken away by someone.

Rosycloud…

Just as Luo Chen silently recited this name in his heart, the great ship had already passed Guangling Commandery.

A flash of crimson light came into view amidst the faint light of dawn.

Cultivators from other sects had already come out of their rooms and onto the deck.

Seeing the crimson light, they all exclaimed.

“Cinnabar Vermilion Mountain has arrived!”

This was the gateway to Heavenly Origin. Once past Cinnabar Vermilion Mountain, one had entered the mountain gate of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Luo Chen silently watched this scene, not particularly surprised.

Even as the great ship passed by majestic or strange spirit mountains, he paid them little heed.

In the end, these immortal sights had nothing to do with him.

However, when the great ship flew over a ruined stone platform, his gaze stopped.

Was this the site of an ultra-large teleportation formation?

Just then, Elder Gumu walked up beside him.

Seeing Luo Chen’s expression, Elder Gumu smiled and introduced, “That is Flying Star Platform. It is said that the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has a branch in the Mountains and Seas Realm, and their disciples came to the Xuan Realm through Flying Star Platform. But this place has long been abandoned, no longer capable of ascension teleportation. The last person to ascend to the Xuan Realm through here was Alchemy Saint Chu Yan.”

Luo Chen pressed his lips together.

(End of chapter)
