# Chapter 1227: The Wild Goose Emperor Arrives, Gray Triangle

Plain King Immortal City.

As one of the immortal cities closest to the Plain Spirit Sect, it boasted the newest and best specialty products of the sect. Consequently, the number of cultivators passing through Plain King Immortal City far exceeded that of other locations.

Not only did a large number of cultivators from other sects or independent cultivators come here, but even disciples of the Plain Spirit Sect itself entered and exited this place.

On this day.

Outside Plain King Immortal City, a carriage flew through the high sky.

Two Qilin steeds pulled the carriage, a canopy covering the top. As flowing light drifted about, the true appearance of its master within could not be seen.

Upon seeing this carriage, the cultivators within the immortal city were quite curious.

A travel artifact of this caliber was not something an ordinary cultivator could possess, not even a typical Core Formation true disciple.

After all, those two third-rank Qilin steeds were themselves comparable to Core Formation cultivators.

Its specifications were likely second only to the Nascent Soul cultivators of the Plain Spirit Sect.

Was it the closed-door disciple of some Transformation God elder?

Aboard the Flying Steed Treasure Carriage.

Luo Chen was browsing a book of local customs and traditions he had borrowed earlier. It recorded the customs and practices of various continents across the Xuan Realm.

Not only the Nether Abyss Continent, but also the Blood Rain Continent he had heard of before, as well as the Ten Thousand Spirits Continent where the main sect of the Life and Death Gate of the Mountains and Seas Realm was located, were all described.

Additionally, some places Luo Chen had never even heard of were also roughly recorded, along with their iconic major powers and figures.

Compared to the *Xuan Realm Secret Records* that Nether Abyss Divine Lord had once gifted him, this *Pan-Realm Travelogue* was far more grand in scope. He wondered who had authored it?

But it must certainly have been someone of profound cultivation, otherwise their footprints could hardly have spanned all the continents of the Xuan Realm.

Since leaving the Plain Spirit Sect, he had been reading this book continuously.

Mainly to pass the time.

The Plain Spirit Sect's sphere of influence encompassed not only its vast mountain gate but also the surrounding hundreds of thousands of li, all under its jurisdiction.

He needed to leave traces; otherwise, it would be hard to explain how a Core Formation cultivator's flying speed could vanish so quickly from the Plain Spirit Sect's territory.

Thinking of this, Luo Chen couldn't help but put down the *Pan-Realm Travelogue*, a troubled expression on his face.

He had discovered a contradiction.

The Xuan Realm's teleportation formation technology far surpassed that of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Yet, teleportation formations were rarely seen!

Whether within the sect or in various immortal cities, teleportation formations were few and far between.

Even when they existed, they were only short-distance teleportation formations, rarely exceeding ten thousand li.

Luo Chen speculated that this might be related to the Xuan Realm's spatial barriers being too thick.

Because the spatial barriers were too thick, teleportation formations found it difficult to open space for long-distance teleportation, especially being unable to bear high-level cultivators who themselves contained immense power.

He had secretly experimented with this.

With his physical strength at the Destruction Realm, a single punch left space utterly unmoved; not even a spatial ripple appeared.

If this were the Mountains and Seas Realm, Luo Chen's physical strength would be enough to shatter a large expanse of space.

However, Luo Chen had glimpsed a corner of the Xuan Realm's teleportation formation technology at the Hundred Ingenious Academy.

Even if it was just basic theory, it was far more solid than that of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Luo Chen believed that without a large number of physical teleportation formations, yet with widespread foundational knowledge, it meant the source of this knowledge was not bottom-up, but top-down.

In other words, the most supreme teleportation formation technology, and the most powerful super teleportation formations, could only be controlled by the most top-tier major powers of the Xuan Realm.

For example, the Nether Abyss Source Court on the Nether Abyss Continent, and the Heavenly Phoenix Clan, which had a Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerable in residence.

However, there should be exceptions.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was that exception!

After other powers lost control of the Mountains and Seas Realm, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect still maintained contact with it, indicating they had some research in this area.

"Is it the Star Gate?" Luo Chen muttered to himself.

He looked outside, letting the Flying Steed Treasure Carriage pull him quickly away from the Plain Spirit Sect.

The lack of teleportation formations greatly hindered his long-distance travel, but once he left the general area of the Plain Spirit Sect, he could travel with all his might without restraint.

His goal was naturally to find a high-grade spirit vein with abundant spiritual qi and directly break through to the Nascent Soul stage.

If convenient, he could also complete the task he had accepted earlier.

That task was not particularly difficult. It was issued by a Nascent Soul cultivator from the Spirit Medicine Court who wanted to cultivate a hybrid spiritual plant and needed many experimental subjects.

One of the required materials was called Phoenix Eye Black Obsidian Moss.

Its grade was not high, but it was on the verge of extinction in the Xuan Realm, and the Plain Spirit Sect had no cultivation of it internally.

However, thirty years ago, it was said to have appeared at a certain independent cultivator trading fair, indicating that this item still existed on the Nether Abyss Continent.

This information was reported by a Core Formation disciple when he returned to the sect to report on his duties.

If Luo Chen wanted to complete this task, he could go to the place where that Core Formation disciple had once been stationed and seek assistance.

"Gray Triangle..."

The location marked on the task surfaced in Luo Chen's mind.

It was a buffer zone between three Transformation God sects, hence a large number of independent cultivators were always mixed up in the area.

Because of a certain special medicinal herb, the Plain Spirit Sect also had a piece of exclave near the Gray Triangle, specifically used to cultivate that herb. That was how the Core Formation disciple from back then had learned about the Phoenix Eye Black Obsidian Moss at the independent cultivator trading fair.

The Gray Triangle was a full several hundred thousand li away from the Plain Spirit Sect. Even for a Core Formation cultivator's flying speed, without affecting their mana, the journey would take several months.

According to records within the sect, the Gray Triangle was a very suitable place for independent cultivators to survive, with a vast number of spirit veins.

There was even a fourth-rank spirit vein running through the entire region.

"A fourth-rank spirit vein could serve as my place for forming my Nascent Soul."

Just as Luo Chen was lost in such thoughts, the hairs on his body suddenly stood on end.

He remained expressionless, continuing to control the Flying Steed Treasure Carriage forward.

After one breath, that extremely dangerous feeling disappeared.

Luo Chen breathed a sigh of relief. He did not spread his divine sense, nor did he even activate his martial arts divine intent. He only scanned his surroundings with his naked eye.

No discovery!

But he was certain that the feeling just now had not come for no reason.

"A powerhouse!"

"An absolute top-tier powerhouse!"

"One capable of threatening my life!"

It had been a long time since anyone had given Luo Chen this feeling.

The last time was in the Guixu, facing that Great Vehicle True Spirit, Golden Punishment Venerable.

At that time, Golden Punishment Venerable was suppressed by the Guixu environment, his strength reduced to less than one percent, roughly at the Body Integration stage.

In other words, the person who had just passed by was at least a Body Integration existence.

And perhaps not just early Body Integration.

Because the martial arts divine ability Luo Chen had mastered—Seven Hundred Divine Kills—was the most extreme killing move. He was confident he could fight even an early Body Integration cultivator.

For it to make his hair stand on end, that person's cultivation was likely at mid Body Integration, or even late Body Integration.

Luo Chen's gaze flickered. He decided not to casually reveal his strength, but only to fly slowly with the current speed of his treasure carriage.

Otherwise, if he happened to encounter a strange powerhouse, it could easily invite unnecessary trouble.

This Xuan Realm, this Nether Abyss Continent, was not yet a place where he could roam freely and unrestrained.

...

Above the mountain gate of the Plain Spirit Sect.

Suddenly, a gentle breeze swept through, and ripples resembling black feathered quills spread out.

A figure appeared above. His features were handsome, gentle and soft, but his eyes slanted upwards, giving an alternative sense of sharp authority.

The moment he appeared, his divine sense swept downwards without any restraint.

After a sweep, he found nothing.

He had not discovered any trace of a Body Integration powerhouse.

"It's been nearly sixty years. I've searched the entire Nether Abyss Continent and haven't found any independent cultivator at the Body Integration stage. It seems the one who killed that kid Lie Yu either has already left the Nether Abyss Continent, or is someone from the Nine Great Saint Sects."

The man in a black and red feathered robe muttered to himself, a fierce glint flashing in his eyes.

The Body Integration Great Venerables of the Nine Great Saint Sects were only a handful, and those who had enmity with the Heavenly Phoenix Clan and were likely to make a move were even fewer, narrowing the scope of suspicion.

"Heavenly Origin Great Venerable?"

After muttering to himself for a while, the man's figure gradually disappeared.

Moments later.

Within the Plain Spirit Sect, a graceful figure suddenly flew out.

Tai Su Lingjun looked around in shock and uncertainty, but found nothing.

However, the feeling that had made her heart pound just now was absolutely unmistakable.

"Who was it, so unscrupulously releasing their divine sense?"

"Why did they only sweep once and then leave?"

"Were they passing through, or searching for someone?"

Tai Su Lingjun ultimately found nothing and could only return to the sect with a grim expression.

Within the Tai Su Treasure Hall.

Eight figures looked at Tai Su Lingjun in confusion as she rushed out hastily and returned just as quickly.

"Sect Master, what happened?"

Tai Su Lingjun waved her hand in annoyance. "It's nothing. Continue discussing the matter of selecting candidates for the Demon Suppression Trial!"

Although the others were somewhat curious, they did not ask further and continued the previous discussion.

"This time, the Dao Sect has given us a full ten slots for the Demon Suppression Trial, which is quite ample."

"The Seven True Disciples of Plain Spirit will definitely go. They are the good seedlings we specifically selected to sprint for the Transformation God stage."

"Can that girl Yao Xi make it? She hasn't broken through to late Nascent Soul yet, has she?"

"Soon. This seclusion of hers has been exceptionally long. Once she comes out, she will definitely be able to break through to late Nascent Soul."

"There are three more slots. How should they be allocated?"

"Don't take the old, don't take the too young, don't take the weak, don't take the faint-hearted, don't take the untalented..."

"Tiange, with all these 'don'ts', our Plain Spirit Sect has roughly a thousand or so Nascent Soul cultivators. After eliminating them, how many will be left?"

"Even if we have to pick the best from a bunch of mediocrities, we can always find three. What's the rush?"

"I'm not in a rush, but the Demon Suppression Trial involves spiritual opportunity comprehension. A Nascent Soul cultivator resonates with heaven and earth and is easily assimilated. Only those at late Nascent Soul can hold their original mind. And most who can cultivate to that level are also quite old. As for what you said about faint-heartedness and talent, how do you compare that?"

Watching the noisy debate below, Tai Su Lingjun, who was already somewhat irritable, became even more agitated.

She tapped the table.

"What are you arguing about? It's not like none of you have participated in the Demon Suppression Trial before. Do you really think it's a sightseeing trip?"

Everyone fell silent for a moment.

The Demon Suppression Trial was indeed no easy matter.

Inside, demonic qi had seeped in over many years of accumulation, often generating a large number of demonic creatures.

Moreover, that environment was not suitable for Xuan Realm cultivators to fight in. A single misstep could lead to severe injury or even death.

And if the demonic cave expanded, forming a spatial channel, there might even be powerful beings from the demon realm crossing over.

If such a situation occurred, it would likely be a complete annihilation.

Such things had happened before.

Tai Su Lingjun swept her gaze over the crowd and made a decisive ruling: "For the remaining three slots, strength is paramount. Any Nascent Soul disciple who wants to go can compete. Tiange... no, let's have Ancient Wood handle this matter."

Compared to the hot-tempered Tiange, Tai Su Lingjun decided it was better to entrust this matter to Ancient Wood.

If any disciple under her command were to be injured, Ancient Wood's alchemy skills could quickly save them.

Elder Ancient Wood cupped his hands and accepted the task, then stated his plan.

"How about setting the time for forty years from now? First, send out the news so that disciples interested in participating in the Demon Suppression Trial can prepare. Then, hold the selection competition. Sect Master, what do you think?"

"Do as you see fit!"

...

Three months later.

At a mountain peak near the Gray Triangle, a treasure carriage pulled by Qilin spirit beasts broke through the air.

From within the carriage, a sound transmission talisman was sent out, and then it hovered quietly in the sky.

After a while, there was movement from within the mountain peak.

A figure flew up.

At the same time, a figure stepped out of the treasure carriage.

Green robe, white face, hair bound in a high crown—it was Luo Chen!

Seeing him, the leading figure cupped his hands and asked, "Are you Xuan Luozi in person?"

Luo Chen smiled and nodded. "Brother Fan Wen?"

Fan Wen smiled back, extended his hand, and said, "Indeed. This way, please!"

Luo Chen put away the Flying Steed Treasure Carriage and followed Fan Wen into Emerald Smoke Mountain.

Along the way, Fan Wen introduced the situation here.

Emerald Smoke Mountain was named after a type of medicinal herb called Emerald Smoke Flower, which emitted waves of green smoke when it bloomed.

It was not only planted here but also in several other places near the Gray Triangle.

For the Plain Spirit Sect, it was also a fairly valuable asset.

However, due to the great distance and the involvement of three nearby Transformation God sects, it was not suitable to station cultivators of too high a realm. Normally, only Core Formation cultivators were responsible for its care.

The three Transformation God sects were the Golden Bell Sect, the Purple Flame Mountain, and the Great Soul Gate.

"The sect forces here don't seem very strong on the surface, but the situation is quite complicated. And because there are many independent cultivators mixed in the Gray Triangle, robbery and plunder are common occurrences."

"The sect was quite active here two or three hundred years ago, but in recent years, they haven't cared much about this place anymore."

"Perhaps it's related to the sect having already researched how to transplant and cultivate the Emerald Smoke Flower."

Fan Wen said this, his expression somewhat bitter.

Clearly, being dispatched here, far from the sect's core, could be considered a form of exile for a Core Formation cultivator.

Then, his spirits lifted. "But this year, a batch of fellow disciples came here to carry out tasks."

Luo Chen asked curiously, "There are fellow disciples here too?"

"Yes, a full six people. Two Core Formation, four Foundation Establishment. They all came here after accepting tasks from the Spirit Medicine Court." Saying this, Fan Wen seemed to think of something and looked at Luo Chen. "Among them is a female cultivator who, like you, Xuan Luozi, comes from Jade Spring Peak."

"Oh? What's her name?" Luo Chen asked casually.

"Her name is Liu Muhan. She performed quite well in the sect's grand competition. This time, she's probably like you, doing tasks while also traveling a bit."

Luo Chen nodded, the image of that woman's face seeming to appear before him.

He didn't ask what task she had accepted, nor did he propose a meeting. Instead, he directly asked about matters related to the Phoenix Eye Black Obsidian Moss.

Fan Wen answered one by one.

The person who had discovered the Phoenix Eye Black Obsidian Moss was the Core Formation cultivator who had been stationed here in the previous term.

He had learned of it while attending an independent cultivator trading fair. After returning to the sect, he learned that a Nascent Soul cultivator needed it, so he reported the information.

Although the External Affairs Hall had instructed the Core Formation cultivator stationed at Emerald Smoke Mountain to keep an eye out for this matter, there had been no results.

Hence, Luo Chen had come in person.

"So, to get specific information, I need to go to the Gray Triangle myself, preferably to attend a relevant independent cultivator trading fair?"

"Something like that. But you'd better not get your hopes up too high. The independent cultivators here mostly like to hide their identities, making it very difficult to trace related information."

"It's fine. If I can find it, good. If not, the task fails, and no one will pursue it."

Luo Chen said this lightly, without any burden.

Fan Wen did not doubt his words. The name Xuan Luozi had already spread throughout the Plain Spirit Sect, and he had heard of it too.

Given the other's status within the sect, failing an unimportant task would not affect anything.

Next, Luo Chen asked for more detailed information.

For example, the location, time, and participation requirements of that year's trading fair; the specific circumstances of both parties involved in the Phoenix Eye Black Obsidian Moss transaction; and finally, the distribution of power within the Gray Triangle, and so on.

That evening, Fan Wen hosted a banquet, personally entertaining Luo Chen with great ceremony.

The next morning, Luo Chen personally stepped into the Gray Triangle.
