# Chapter 1205: The Name of Genius, Plain Spirit Immortal Sect

*Buzz…*

A tremendous buzzing sound echoed throughout Taisu Immortal City.

Countless people followed the sound with their eyes, watching as the formation covering the immortal city melted away like snow, and then a massive flying boat slowly rose from the center of the city.

That was the carriage of a Sage!

On the flying boat, the shadows of cultivators were numerous, no less than several hundred.

Everyone knew that this group of people was heading to join the Plain Spirit Sect, and most of them had considerable confidence in themselves.

Perhaps among those people on this journey, some might become true disciples of the Plain Spirit Sect in the future, making them people of a different world.

On the deck, a graceful and bright figure hurried past holding a registry.

The surrounding rogue cultivators made way one after another, addressing her as Senior Sister Liu.

This Senior Sister Liu mostly responded with a smile and a nod.

This greatly encouraged those rogue cultivators.

After all, they hadn't even joined the Plain Spirit Sect yet, and an authentic Plain Spirit true disciple was already showing such a good attitude, invisibly giving them some encouragement.

Senior Sister Liu's pace gradually quickened, and soon she arrived at a quiet area.

A small palace stood prominently on the flying boat.

At the entrance, two Initiate cultivators stood guard.

Seeing the woman in green, one of them smiled and said, "Senior Sister Liu, have you come to deliver the rogue cultivator registry to the Sage?"

Liu Muhan nodded, then asked curiously, "Is Sage Litin busy right now?"

Before the disciple could answer, a stern voice came from within the hall.

"Come in!"

The two Initiate disciples quickly opened the large doors.

Liu Muhan tidied her dress slightly, then carefully walked inside.

The hall was spacious and bright, with a gentle breeze passing through the windows.

Inside the hall, Liu Muhan kept her head lowered, holding the registry with both hands.

"Reporting to the Sage, all the registries of rogue cultivators in Taisu Immortal City who wish to participate in the Entry Assembly are here, including—"

"No need for detailed introductions. It's hard to say if even ten or twenty percent of these people will join our Plain Spirit Sect. Even if they do join, they'll only be outer disciples. Why bother introducing them?"

Litin Sage's expression was calm, but his words carried considerable impatience.

He said in a deep voice, "Just pick a few outstanding ones to introduce. I'll let the person in charge of the assembly know later... By the way, which fellow disciple is presiding over this Entry Assembly?"

Liu Muhan had long known that this Litin Sage was famously a cultivation maniac within the sect.

Aside from matters related to cultivation, he didn't care about anything else.

But she hadn't expected that he didn't even know which fellow disciple was presiding over this Entry Assembly.

She said helplessly, "It's precisely my master, Sage Yaoxi."

Litin Sage suddenly understood. "So it's Junior Sister Yaoxi! That makes sense. She hasn't been managing affairs since she became a Sage. Now the sect is starting to assign her tasks."

Speaking of this, a hint of a smile involuntarily appeared on his face.

He was probably imagining Junior Sister Yaoxi's troubled expression when faced with these tedious chores.

Shaking his head, Litin Sage motioned for Liu Muhan to continue.

Liu Muhan hummed in acknowledgment. Without opening the registry, she began to recount the people she had memorized.

"Tang Yuan, currently thirty-seven years old, early Initiate stage, from the Tang family of Taisu Immortal City. The Tang family ancestor is also a disciple of our Plain Spirit Sect..."

"Huang Yuanhua, early Initiate stage cultivator, somewhat advanced in age at fifty-four. Of rogue cultivator background, skilled in spirit planting techniques..."

"Xing Beichen, also of rogue cultivator background. He claims his origins are from Hao Province. However, this disciple sensed a strong baleful aura on him, suggesting he's not exactly a righteous rogue cultivator..."

At first, Litin Sage listened fairly attentively, but gradually he grew impatient.

"What righteousness or lack thereof among rogue cultivators? It's good enough if they can survive. These people are all too ordinary. Is there anyone who leaves a lasting impression?"

Liu Muhan took a deep breath, and a very young face flashed through her mind.

"Reporting to the Sage, there is one. He calls himself Xuan Luo, from Green Gorge Town bordering the Sunset Plains. His cultivation is around early Initiate stage, and his age... about twenty!"

Litin Sage indeed became interested. "Twenty years old at Initiate stage? Could he have a heavenly spirit root, or perhaps some special constitution?"

Liu Muhan shook her head. "This disciple doesn't know. After all, the real Entry Assembly hasn't started yet. The registry only has simple records. The information is mostly self-reported by them, without any verification."

"Alright, circle these people's names. I'll give them to your master later and let her pay attention. That way, I've completed my task."

Liu Muhan had no choice but to do as instructed.

She actually knew that when sect seniors went out to perform garrison duties, they often had to balance the task of discovering outstanding talents.

After all, there were many sects on Youxuan Continent. If you didn't take in good talents, other sects would take them.

If the Plain Spirit Sect wanted to flourish, they had to uphold this philosophy from top to bottom, not missing any cultivator talent in their domain.

But Litin Sage was simply too lazy.

Or rather, he only cared about his own cultivation and wasn't interested in these matters at all.

Now that the mission was over and they were returning to the sect, to save trouble, he was directly selecting candidates from the rogue cultivator registry.

What could she, Liu Muhan, an Initiate disciple, possibly criticize?

After Liu Muhan handed over the registry with the circled names, Litin Sage rarely showed a gentle expression.

"Junior Disciple Liu, I see that your cultivation has reached the ninth layer of Initiate stage. This return is to prepare for forming your core, isn't it?"

Speaking of cultivation matters, Liu Muhan became serious.

"As the Sage says, this disciple has indeed lingered at the ninth layer of Initiate stage for a long time. These years of going out on missions have also been to polish my spiritual power. Now I'm almost at full completion. After helping my master handle the Entry Assembly matters, I plan to go into seclusion to break through."

"Excellent. We cultivators should prioritize cultivation. All other trivial matters and worldly affairs are merely side branches." Litin Sage always appreciated those who worked hard at cultivation. He thought for a moment, then took out an item and placed it before Liu Muhan.

Liu Muhan was startled. "This is?"

"Your master and I both once listened to lectures under the Sect Master, so we share the same lineage. As your senior uncle, it's natural to guide the younger generation. What's written in this book are my insights from breaking through to the Adept stage. Take it and study it carefully. It might increase your chances of forming your core by ten or twenty percent." Litin Sage said.

Liu Muhan held the personally written breakthrough insights of the Sage in her hands, as if she had found a priceless treasure.

She quickly bowed in gratitude, feeling that all the hard work she had done before was worthwhile.

After taking her leave, Liu Muhan returned to the deck.

Her thoughts seemed to drift back to when she was young, participating in the Entry Assembly.

Back then, she had only wanted to find a sect as a backing.

She never imagined that she would have this day, with the Adept stage within reach.

Now, looking at these hopeful rogue cultivators, it was like seeing her former self.

All were travelers on the same path!

As she passed the railing, she could still hear the rogue cultivator named Huang Yuanhua excitedly telling the young man named Xuan Luo about the wonderful life after joining the Plain Spirit Sect.

That Xuan Luo seemed rather uninterested, even somewhat worried.

Liu Muhan smiled, reached out, and patted Xuan Luo on the shoulder.

"Strive on, all of you!"

After offering this encouragement, the woman drifted away.

Only Luo Chen stood there dumbfounded, as if flattered.

Huang Yuanhua was also stunned, then let out a loud, strange cry.

"Wow, Xuan Luo, that was Liu Muhan!"

"Is she famous?"

"Of course she's famous! Liu Muhan was originally an ordinary rogue cultivator who luckily passed the Entry Assembly and joined the Plain Spirit Sect. She went through the outer sect, inner sect, and finally became a true disciple, even taken in by a Sage. I heard she has hope of forming her core in the future and becoming a Superior."

"So, she's a role model for us rogue cultivators?"

"Definitely! Who wouldn't want to be like her—having both luck and hard work, eventually being noticed by a Sage? Who knows, she might even have a chance to touch the Sage realm in the future."

"The Sage realm... isn't so easy to achieve."

"Let's not talk about that for now. She actually took the initiative to encourage you just now. Is she hinting that you'll successfully join the Plain Spirit Sect?"

"Us!"

"But she patted *you*! And you were dumbstruck just now, standing there like a fool."

"Alright, stop!"

Huang Yuanhua wanted to say more, but Luo Chen stopped him.

Only then did he notice that the deck had fallen silent, and everyone was staring at them.

The complexity in those gazes was indescribable.

Huang Yuanhua swallowed his saliva and quickly turned around, pretending to admire the scenery beneath the flying clouds.

Luo Chen, however, remained unperturbed, still at ease.

His earlier daze hadn't been an act; it was a natural reaction.

After all, it was truly absurd for a Realm-Breaking martial artist and Ascendant-stage great power to be patted on the shoulder and encouraged to cultivate diligently by an Initiate-stage junior.

"But I really should get used to this."

"Since I plan to use an alias to blend into a sect and recover my cultivation, I'll inevitably encounter such situations. I need to show the natural reactions of my current realm."

"Liu Muhan, huh?"

A faint smile curved at the corner of Luo Chen's mouth.

With his divine sense, he could clearly see the other party's condition.

Dual spirit root aptitude—not bad, but not particularly good either.

Just over a hundred years old, with solid and pure spiritual power within her body, and a fairly substantial foundation of soul essence.

To have this level of cultivation at this age was decent.

But!

With this level of strength, she shouldn't have such a great reputation.

She was probably deliberately pushed forward by the Plain Spirit Sect as a living signboard to attract rogue cultivators to join the sect enthusiastically.

There should be more than one such living signboard.

Who the previous generation was, he didn't know.

But this generation...

"I wouldn't mind being this living signboard."

Luo Chen smiled, already having a plan in mind.

Even if his plan was to blend into a sect, he absolutely wouldn't blend in obscurity. Otherwise, why bother changing his name and appearance?

To recover his cultivation, he needed sufficient resources.

The best spiritual vein cave dwellings, the best cultivation resources. Although he didn't need guidance from a master, he wouldn't mind browsing through the scripture collections of a Refining Void sect.

Only by maximizing all conditions could he recover his immortal cultivation realm at an extremely fast speed.

Therefore, he had set his direction from the very beginning.

Change his name, disguise himself, and rise within the sect as a genius.

Not too old, not too low in cultivation, but with everything traceable.

That way, even if he encountered experts from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect in the future, they wouldn't suspect him of being Luo Chen from that small world.

As he pondered, some rogue cultivators participating in the Entry Assembly actively approached to get to know them.

Among these people, there might be some with hostile intentions.

But the assembly hadn't started yet, so they weren't enemies yet.

Even if the assembly ended and some failed the selection, if they could befriend a future Plain Spirit Sect disciple, it would be an excellent connection.

Towards these people, Luo Chen displayed a distant, aloof attitude.

Huang Yuanhua, however, cheerfully got to know everyone one by one, even making excuses for Luo Chen, saying that was just his personality and asking everyone not to mind.

Geniuses!

People could always tolerate all their flaws.

Therefore, as the journey progressed, Luo Chen's reputation didn't suffer because of his aloof and proud demeanor. On the contrary, it grew stronger.

Ten days later.

The flying boat arrived at a quiet place shrouded in clouds and mist.

Just as the rogue cultivators were puzzled, the Plain Spirit Sect disciples on the flying boat neatly lined up.

The young woman named Liu Muhan stood before everyone, also instructing the rogue cultivators to line up.

In the silence, Liu Muhan said respectfully, "Please, Sage, make your move!"

The next moment.

A golden stream of light as thick as a bowl shot out from the center of the flying boat, striking into the sea of clouds.

Immediately, the calm sea of clouds rolled layer upon layer, parting to reveal a path.

"Return to the sect!"

A majestic voice came faintly.

The Plain Spirit Sect disciples on the flying boat responded in unison, releasing their mana to control the flying boat as it advanced while descending.

As the altitude dropped, the previously blurry scenes became clear.

The quiet atmosphere also became lively and bustling.

Beneath the sea of clouds, thousands of mountains supported each other, layer upon layer of peaks.

In the mountain forests, lush greenery abounded, all emitting various colors and fragrances of medicinal herbs.

There were also rivers and lakes dotted among them.

Fish and dragons leaped from the water, splashing countless ripples that reflected seven-colored rainbows in the sunlight.

Figures of cultivators could be seen, either busy in mountain forests and medicinal gardens, or flying on swords, free and unrestrained.

Even though these rogue cultivators on the flying boat had already roamed Youxuan Continent, seeing this immortal scene still made them unable to help but let out exclamations of admiration.

Luo Chen was among them.

But what he admired wasn't the immortal scene, but the sea of clouds formation from earlier.

"Using the earth veins of a thousand mountains as a foundation, combined with celestial phenomena, to form a comprehensive concealment formation covering no less than ten thousand li. The calm sea of clouds also contains sharp killing intent. There must be a killing formation hidden within. If some petty villain tried to force their way in, they would probably be torn apart by this formation in an instant. Tsk tsk, the Plain Spirit Sect's protective sect formation is no ordinary item. I wonder which expert arranged it."

While Luo Chen was looking up to observe the formation, a faint fragrance suddenly approached.

"Xuan Luo, are you interested in formations?"

Luo Chen lowered his head, looking at the fair-skinned, beautiful young woman before him, and said calmly, "Somewhat."

Liu Muhan smiled. "Our Plain Spirit Sect mainly operates in industries related to medicinal herbs. The sect also has a large amount of medicinal herb cultivation. Involving this path, besides spirit planting techniques and alchemy, formations are also necessary. If you're interested, and if you successfully join the sect later, you could study this path a bit. If you perform well, you might catch the eye of a sect senior and be personally taken in as a true disciple."

"Then I'll take Senior Sister Liu's kind words." Luo Chen thanked her, then casually asked about matters related to the Entry Assembly. "So how exactly do we join the Plain Spirit Sect?"

"You've already said 'we,' so it seems you're determined."

Liu Muhan was infected by the strong confidence radiating from Luo Chen, so she simply began to introduce the specific procedures of the Entry Assembly.

"Unlike previous years, this Entry Assembly is divided into three trials. You must pass all of them to successfully join the sect."

"Three trials?"
