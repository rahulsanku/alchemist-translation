# Chapter 1237: Before the Trial, White Tiger True Lord

Luo Chen did not know where the Wandering Carefree Realm Master had obtained his Golden Erosion Script.

But after experimenting with the wondrous uses of the script the other had taught him on formation diagrams, he believed that this script indeed possessed earth-shattering power!

Not only could it activate the formation diagrams of the Ninefold Immortal Tower, but he had also seen small amounts of Golden Erosion Script on the old corpse demon's Whitebone Altar.

Therefore, before leaving the Return Ruins, when he had cultivated the Chaotic Indestructible Body, he had also used Golden Erosion Script to inscribe the words **[No Self-Image, No Other-Image, No Living-Being Image]** on the bone wheel atop his head.

This was one of the general principles of his martial dao cultivation, the *Chaotic Indestructible Scripture*.

Whether carving it with Golden Erosion Script would actually produce any effect, Luo Chen still did not know.

But at least so far, nothing had gone wrong.

Now, when it came to immortal dao law techniques, Luo Chen also had the intention to give it a try.

However, a difficulty stood before him.

That was, the number of Golden Erosion Script characters he knew was simply too few.

Back then, the Wandering Carefree Realm Master had only taught him a paltry hundred or so characters, and those were specifically used to activate the formation diagrams of the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

Using these hundred-odd characters to condense the vast number of techniques he had mastered would likely be insufficient.

"It seems I'll have to pay attention to gathering information on this matter in the future."

Luo Chen sighed and took the *Ten-Thousand-Word Illustrated Guide* to the exit to have a copy made.

This book had greatly inspired him. A single hasty browse was not enough; when he had free time, he would need to read it more thoroughly. Perhaps it might yield other insights.

The cultivator responsible for copying the book collection had already been replaced.

His cultivation was still at the Sage stage, and he was quite advanced in age.

Clearly, this was a way for the Plain Spirit Sect to arrange for their elderly cultivators.

It wasn't exactly neglect, more like letting them continue to contribute their remaining warmth. Especially for those Sage cultivators without disciples or blood descendants, being able to spend their later years peacefully in the Scripture Transmission Hall, and also feeling the youthful vigor of the young cultivators who came and went daily.

Luo Chen had some goodwill towards the previous Sage cultivator.

So, while waiting for the copy to be made, he casually asked about him.

The answer he received was somewhat sorrowful.

"Senior Brother Li has already passed away. Thirty years ago, one night. It was a natural death, a good ending to his lifespan."

Luo Chen sighed regretfully.

That old cultivator had repeatedly warned him, afraid that he would bite off more than he could chew and go astray.

He had truly been a kind elder.

But the path of cultivation was just like this. Let alone retreating when not advancing, even if one kept advancing, as long as one could not cross one great realm chasm after another, the final result was nothing but a handful of yellow earth.

"Alright, the copy of the *Ten-Thousand-Word Illustrated Guide* is ready. Take it and keep it safe."

As he handed the jade slip to Luo Chen, the cultivator suddenly smiled and said, "Junior Brother Xuan Luo was too absorbed in his reading. Someone has been waiting for you outside for quite a while."

Luo Chen showed an appropriate expression of surprise, thanked him, and walked out of the Scripture Pavilion.

Under an ancient tree that would take five people to encircle, a female cultivator with a resolute expression was leaning against the tree. When Luo Chen came out, she opened her eyes.

"Junior Brother Xuan Luo?"

"Senior Sister Jiang Huang."

"Let's go together!"

Luo Chen nodded and left the Scripture Transmission Hall with Jiang Huang.

Inside the Scripture Pavilion, the old cultivator watched the fading figures of the young man and woman, smacked his lips, and let out a sigh.

"Ah, to be young..."

---

After leaving the Scripture Transmission Hall.

Jiang Huang explained the reason for her visit.

Learning that the Demon-Suppressing Trial was about to begin and that a final training session was to be held at the Plain King Hall, Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

He then steered his flying light towards the Plain King Hall.

Jiang Huang did the same, following by Luo Chen's side.

The conversation between them was very calm, neither overly familiar nor distant.

It was just like a normal junior and senior sister relationship.

But the look Jiang Huang gave Luo Chen revealed a confusion that was very incongruous with her resolute face.

After a long while.

Just as they were about to arrive at the Plain King Hall, Jiang Huang suddenly spoke.

"Junior Brother Xuan Luo, was it really I who saved you from that Po Luo Demon?"

Without turning his head, Luo Chen let out a laugh. "Could it be that Senior Sister hasn't fully recovered her spirit yet? The Heavenly Waterfall Sword Qi on that Po Luo Demon's corpse—who else could have unleashed it besides you? It couldn't be that Master was also present in the Ink Jade Space at the time, could it?"

Jiang Huang was speechless.

The Heavenly Waterfall Sword Art was not easy to cultivate. It valued intent over form, was passed down to women but not men, and placed great importance on the cultivator's mana attributes and mental state.

Among the three generations of disciples on Jade Spring Peak, from top to bottom, even including Master Yao Xi, only three people had managed to cultivate this sword art.

She was one of them.

Liu Muhan, who came from a loose cultivator background and had a tenacious temperament, had only recently been taught.

Although Liu Muhan had also entered the Ink Jade Space at the time, given that little girl's cultivation level, it was naturally impossible.

So, was it really the last vestige of clarity in her own consciousness that, at the critical moment, drew her sword to save Junior Brother Xuan Luo?

But then why did her subconscious not think so?

And on Junior Brother Xuan Luo's body, there was a faint feeling that was both intimate and safe?

Luo Chen flew ahead, unaware of what Jiang Huang was thinking at that moment, but he could roughly guess the problem from her confused and doubtful tone.

He had been the one to control Jiang Huang to unleash the Heavenly Waterfall Sword Art, killing the Po Luo Demon.

Then, using his signature soul art, Mirror Flower Water Moon, he had constructed illusory memories for her.

This should have been foolproof.

But to prevent any strong cultivators at the Ascendant stage or above in the Plain Spirit Sect from discovering any clues, he had only done it superficially and had not set up a strong soul prohibition.

Those illusory memories, while they could influence Jiang Huang for a time, could not influence her forever.

Especially as her condition improved, she would increasingly feel that something was wrong.

But Luo Chen didn't mind.

He just needed to muddle through for now.

The Plain King Hall had arrived!

The two of them restrained their flying light and, without needing guidance from the disciples, entered the great hall.

At this moment, the ten core Sage disciples and seven Ascendant elders were all present inside.

The only Ascendant elder not present had likely been sent to guard the Ink Jade Space.

Luo Chen and Jiang Huang found a cattail mat in the back of the hall and sat down.

He looked around and had a good idea in his mind.

The candidates selected by the Plain Spirit Sect for this Demon-Suppressing Trial were, as expected, no surprise.

The famous Seven True Ones of Plain Spirit were definitely participating.

Among them, the weakest was Sage Yao Xi, his nominal master, who was at the early Sage stage.

Others, like Sage Zhibei, had long been renowned for his dual mastery of alchemy and swordsmanship. His individual reputation was no less than that of the assembled Seven True Ones of Plain Spirit.

Seeing Luo Chen enter, Sage Zhibei, who was also Dean He of the Alchemy Academy, turned his head and gave him a friendly smile.

Luo Chen nodded in acknowledgment, then sized up another person.

Apart from himself and Jiang Huang, the other slot had been taken by a Sage cultivator he was familiar with.

Lone Bamboo Peak—Sage Youhuang.

Mid-Sage stage, not exceptionally outstanding in strength, but his mastery of formations was unparalleled among the thousand-plus Sage cultivators in the sect.

Even Luo Chen had to acknowledge this. His earlier operation of using formations to control formations and constructing a small reverse spirit channel demonstrated his exquisite formation dao skill.

Looking at it this way, among the ten people, the weakest were Jiang Huang and himself, Luo Chen.

At least, that was how it appeared on the surface.

While he was sizing up the others, they were also using scrutinizing gazes to sweep over him and Jiang Huang.

There was nothing much to say about their mana cultivation levels; both were at the early Sage stage.

What truly attracted attention was the demonic contamination phenomenon on their bodies!

Luo Chen was relatively better off, with only some patterns on his face.

Jiang Huang's condition was more severe. The dark green battle armor on her body was not a magic treasure but a congealed demonic moss, already fused with her physical body and even her internal Purple Mansion.

The trouble of resolving demonic contamination of this level could be imagined.

Some people even began to worry whether they would hold them back.

In the quiet atmosphere of the hall, crisp footsteps came from behind the great hall.

Everyone perked up and stood up to bow.

"We greet the Sect Master!"

Supreme Plain Spirit waved her hand, smoothed her skirt, and sat down at the head.

Her dignified bearing was on full display.

She swept her gaze over the seated crowd below and slowly spoke:

"Everyone is here. Let us begin!"

"First, regarding the specific intelligence on the Demon-Suppressing Trial. Tian Ge, you will explain."

Elder Tian Ge immediately stood up and walked to the center of the great hall.

Mana surged from his body as he formed hand seals, and a bright light screen appeared in the hall.

Although the light screen was extremely bright, the scene inside was dim and difficult to discern direction.

"A thousand years ago, this old man participated in the previous Demon-Suppressing Trial with two fellow disciples. Combining the reports from the Sect Master and successive trial participants who returned, I have summarized the information and patterns of the Demon-Suppressing Trial."

"On the Youxuan Continent, the largest and most significant demonic cave is located within the territory of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. It was the same one that erupted over ten thousand years ago."

"After being sealed by the Nether Capital Source Court, it has been difficult for powerful demons to enter the Xuan Realm through the demonic cave."

"But the seal is not complete. The demonic cave still expands from time to time. Therefore, a Demon-Suppressing Tower was built above the demonic cave. With a cycle of a thousand years, the nine great sacred sects each dispatch powerful Void Refining True Lords to sit in guard. The Demon-Suppressing Tower cannot be moved lightly; it is the key to preventing the demonic cave from erupting at critical moments."

"The Demon-Suppressing Trial you are about to participate in involves entering the demonic cave, cooperating with the great powers of the Dao Sect, and clearing out the demon spirits that have been generated over the past thousand years and the spreading demonic moss."

"This process is very dangerous. Not only are the demon spirits powerful, but the demonic moss is also exceptionally stubborn. If you are severely injured during battle, it is easy to fall into a state of demonic contamination."

"But the danger does not end there!"

"Entering the demonic cave through the Demon-Suppressing Tower involves random teleportation, so it is difficult for familiar fellow disciples to gather quickly and fight together. Within the demonic cave, the suppression of divine sense is also extremely severe. Only the divine thoughts of Ascendant great powers are relatively better."

"The terrain of the demonic cave space also undergoes tremendous changes over a thousand years of evolution. The map I have constructed today can only be used as a reference. Do not follow it blindly!"

In Elder Tian Ge's resonant voice, the situation regarding the Demon-Suppressing Trial was explained in detail.

After the general overview, he introduced specific points in meticulous detail.

For example, the general types of demon spirits, their unique attack methods, how to deal with them, and so on.

Another example was that the more vigorous the Nine Nether Demonic Moss, the easier it was to condense small demonic caves. Often, such places would also have more demon spirits.

But risk accompanied great rewards. Once the demonic cave was dispersed with the cooperation of an Ascendant great power, when a spirit cave was generated, a large amount of spiritual energy would naturally dissipate.

Sage cultivators could use this to enter a state of enlightenment and comprehend the true meaning of the laws!

This process was very dangerous because, in the state of enlightenment, one's perception of the outside world would be reduced to a minimum.

Therefore, unless it was absolutely safe, it was best to maintain a sliver of clarity.

Additionally, because the Demon-Suppressing Tower was opened once every thousand years, due to the sinking of demonic qi within the cave, some special demonic realm resources would also be produced.

These were resources completely different from those of the Xuan Realm. If brought out, they often had great value.

Demon Marrow Diamonds were one such resource, hidden deep underground.

Because of these special resources, danger could also come from other places.

For example, from the human cultivators also participating in the Demon-Suppressing Trial!

In other words, apart from those of the same sect and clan, it was best not to trust anyone easily.

Even those of the same sect and clan should maintain a sliver of vigilance.

Of course, the Plain Spirit Sect did not advocate this.

After Elder Tian Ge had finished his introduction, Supreme Plain Spirit tapped the table, her expression turning serious.

"Second point, I will explain it."

"Our Plain Spirit Sect's foundation is neither deep nor shallow. Though we cannot compare to old Void Refining powers like the Five Elements Divine Sect, we far surpass ordinary Ascendant powers. Especially after advancing to a Void Refining power, the sect's development has been thriving. Therefore, I, as Sect Master, absolutely will not allow any internal scheming among you. You must support each other and cooperate sincerely!"

"If, after the trial ends, anyone disobeys my orders, do not say that I did not warn you beforehand!"

Everyone sat up straight, showing their determination.

Supreme Plain Spirit nodded in satisfaction and then continued, "To allow you to contact your fellow disciples as soon as possible after entering the demonic cave, I will plant a brand on you. This technique, which I learned from the Dao Sect, will still be effective even within the demonic cave."

As she spoke, she slowly raised her hands.

Ten white light dots flew out from her palms and drifted in front of the ten Sage cultivators.

"Do not resist. This is a Lotus Brand. It is harmless to you and merely serves to allow you to resonate with each other."

Everyone relaxed their minds and allowed the white light dots to land on their brows.

Luo Chen's expression was calm, but his heart felt a little strange.

He touched his brow.

He felt that the nine people around him seemed to have a kindred aura with him.

Especially after releasing his divine sense, that aura became even more apparent.

Was this a countermeasure against the random teleportation?

"Furthermore, He Lanshi, step forward!"

Dean He stood up and respectfully positioned himself below Supreme Plain Spirit.

The next moment.

A terrifying aura rose from Supreme Plain Spirit.

A long sword phantom materialized, finally condensing into a cyan-green physical entity, landing in Dean He's hand.

Dean He looked down and saw the cyan-green long sword emitting a vigorous vitality, with strands of vines entwining the sword hilt like the principles of the Great Dao.

He felt as if he were holding something that could destroy heaven and earth. Even his late Sage soul foundation trembled uncontrollably.

The others did not understand what this was.

But Luo Chen's gaze sharpened, and he murmured in his heart, "Spirit treasure splitting light, transforming into a spirit form!"

Sure enough, the Ascendant elders watching from the side also let out surprised exclamations upon seeing this.

"Sect Master, why go this far?"

"It's just a trial for the younger generation. How can it affect your cultivation?"

"Drawing upon the power of your natal spirit treasure to create such a spirit treasure split light is truly detrimental to the might of your Plain King Sword!"

Amidst the shocked words, Supreme Plain Spirit calmly tapped the table.

The great hall fell silent.

Only the woman's authoritative voice spread out.

"This Demon-Suppressing Trial is the first since our Plain Spirit Sect advanced to a Void Refining power. It cannot afford any mistakes. We must cultivate at least one Ascendant cultivator."

"He Lanshi, you will hold this sword to command your fellow disciples, to ward off external evils externally. But remember, unless you encounter an enemy you cannot defeat, never activate this sword."

Dean He nodded solemnly.

What was an enemy one could not defeat?

At the very least, it would have to be an Ascendant-stage demon spirit, or even stronger?

But according to the intelligence he knew, in previous Demon-Suppressing Trials, demon spirits of the Ascendant stage, or even the Void Refining stage, rarely appeared!

This could be seen from the fact that since the end of the Xuan-Demon War, the Demon-Suppressing Tower standing above the demonic cave had not been activated once in ten thousand years.

After all, the Demon-Suppressing Trial occurred once every thousand years. What kind of powerful demon spirit could be born in a mere thousand years?

At most, it would be at the Ascendant stage.

However, Supreme Plain Spirit did not think so.

"According to what I, this Sect Master, know, this Demon-Suppressing Trial might be different from any previous one. The specific reason is unclear, but it can be seen from the Dao Sect dispatching the strongest of the Four Great War Generals, White Tiger True Lord, and the most steady, Black Tortoise True Lord, to personally garrison the Demon-Suppressing Tower."

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was full of experts.

Below the Great Sovereign, the Four Great War Generals were the pillars that held up the sky.

The Four Great War Generals inherited the names of the Four Sacred Beasts from immortal and divine legends.

Azure Dragon was the oldest, White Tiger the strongest, Vermilion Bird the fiercest, and Black Tortoise the most steady.

Sending the strongest and the most steady of the two war generals clearly indicated that this would not be an ordinary Demon-Suppressing Trial.

Supreme Plain Spirit's caution was also inferred from the arrangements of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Among the crowd, Luo Chen's eyelids drooped, and a glint flickered in his eyes.

White Tiger True Lord?

In a sense, he was also an old acquaintance.

This Demon-Suppressing Trial... he wouldn't end up running into him, would he?
