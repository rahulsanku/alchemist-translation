# Chapter 1225: Identity Exposed, Demon-Suppression Trial

The woman's gaze met his, their silhouettes reflected against each other.

Luo Chen remained somewhat silent.

Because of this silence, Chu Yan's originally expectant expression gradually dimmed.

Suddenly!

The man's hands, which had been hidden beneath his wide sleeves, emerged. Ten slender fingers flexed at the joints.

As magical power circulated, a mysterious profundity spread through the air.

The Art of Refining Heaven's Mechanism was originally meant to target the spiritual mechanisms of heaven and earth, transforming them for one's own use.

But just as heaven and earth possessed spiritual mechanisms, living beings also had vital mechanisms, and spiritual medicines and rare flowers possessed life force even more so.

By reversing this art, one could precisely control the blending and even the separation of medicinal power during the refining process.

The man performed the alchemical art upon the void. Though there was no specific target, his technique was identical to what Chu Yan had used earlier, and even extended further as it progressed.

Clearly, he had already comprehended the unspoken meaning behind her actions.

When those hands once again disappeared into the wide sleeves, the woman beside him had already revealed a brilliant smile.

"Still learned quite a bit, didn't you!"

Having said that, she walked with light steps into the Mountain-Sea Palace.

Behind her, Luo Chen revealed a helpless expression.

His demonstration just now had essentially completely blown his cover.

But it no longer mattered.

From the moment she had detected that he also knew the Art of Refining Heaven's Mechanism in alchemy, she had already locked onto his identity. Excessive concealment would only have seemed insincere.

Still, Luo Chen hadn't expected that after three months of careful and cautious concealment, he would expose himself on the very path of alchemy where he excelled most.

Or rather, although this exposure was unexpected, it was also reasonable.

Because alchemy was precisely where Chu Yan excelled most, and she was even above Luo Chen in this regard.

She had deliberately waited until the last day to test him, and with the Five Elements Divine Pill involved, no matter how much Luo Chen tried to hide his skill, that momentary lapse had ultimately allowed her to see through him completely.

"Alas..."

Sighing, Luo Chen had no choice but to follow her back into the palace.

That night.

Neither party made further contact.

Chu Yan, now aware of Luo Chen's true identity, exercised admirable restraint.

Perhaps she also understood Luo Chen's current situation. Knowing in her heart was enough.

As for Luo Chen, he couldn't afford too much contact either. It wasn't that he feared Chu Yan would report him to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect—if she truly intended to do that, Luo Chen would have to accept his fate and prepare to flee. He believed she wouldn't. The reason he avoided excessive contact was that he still planned to remain in the Plain Spirit Sect. Getting too close to Chu Yan might attract the attention of Grandplain Spirit Lord.

The night passed without incident until dawn.

The next morning, the ceremonial procession that had stayed in the Plain Spirit Sect for several months gathered outside the palace.

Grandplain Spirit Lord had already arrived and entered the Alchemy Saint Hall to bid farewell to Chu Yan.

However, what surprised Grandplain Spirit Lord somewhat was that when it came time to part, there was no sadness on Chu Yan's face. Instead, she kept revealing heartfelt smiles.

"Perhaps she truly did enjoy herself these three months in the Plain Spirit Sect!"

Grandplain Spirit Lord thought to herself.

When the warm sun began to rise, the two women emerged.

Luo Chen, as he had done for the past three months, followed behind them without a word.

From time to time, he could still hear the two women conversing.

When they were about to board the magnificent cloud carriage of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, his steps were blocked by an old Ascendant cultivator.

"Let Xuan Luo see her off! You've spent three months together already; this short while more won't make a difference."

From within the cloud carriage, Chu Yan's clear, spring-like voice emerged.

The old Ascendant cultivator's gaze swept across Luo Chen's face, and then he tactfully stepped aside.

Luo Chen boarded the cloud carriage, urged the spirit beasts pulling it forward, and flew toward the mountain gate.

On the cloud carriage, Grandplain Spirit Lord withdrew her gaze from Luo Chen with some surprise.

"Sister, it seems you're quite satisfied with Xuan Luozi!"

Chu Yan smiled lightly. "The outstanding talents of your esteemed sect each have their own brilliance. Not just Xuan Luo, but also Dean He and the others. This way, in the Demon-Suppression Trial sixty years from now, the Plain Spirit Sect might have a chance to produce one or two more Ascendant great powers."

Grandplain Spirit Lord sighed. "If only it were that simple. Although the Demon-Suppression Trial holds great opportunities, it also carries great risks. This time, the Dao Sect gave the Plain Spirit Sect a few extra slots out of consideration for you, but who knows whether this will be a blessing or a curse for the disciples. After all, a demonic cave surge is a major event that even the Holy Sect and Source Court must handle with care."

Chu Yan patted the Spirit Lord's wrist reassuringly. "Don't worry. The classics have recorded countless demonic cave surges. Only the one ten thousand years ago caused some real trouble. Even if the sky falls, won't the Nether Capital Source Court be there to hold it up? Consider this an opportunity for your sect's Sages to gain some experience!"

Grandplain Spirit Lord hummed in agreement, having no choice but to comfort herself with this thought.

For a sect to develop and grow, relying on her alone was impossible.

The eight great Ascendant elders were mostly from the older generation, and most had little hope of breaking through to the Void Refining stage.

The future would have to rest on the younger generation.

If this batch of disciples could survive the Demon-Suppression Trial, even if they couldn't achieve Void Refining in the future, they would at least have a better chance of breaking through to the Ascendant stage.

Once they matured, the Plain Spirit Sect could truly secure its position as a Void Refining power, without suffering a gap in talent.

For this, even if there were some losses, it would be worth it!

A thousand-mile send-off must eventually end. Amidst their idle conversation, the cloud carriage had already traveled ten thousand li and arrived at the sect's mountain gate.

Inside and outside the cloud carriage, the two women bid each other farewell.

Chu Yan suddenly looked toward Luo Chen, who had remained silent all along.

"Xuan Luo, do not waste your talent in the alchemy path. I hope that one day I can exchange insights on the art of alchemy with you."

Before everyone, Luo Chen openly cupped his hands. "Thank you for your encouragement, Senior!"

Chu Yan said no more. She entered the cloud carriage and, under everyone's protection, departed from the Plain Spirit Sect.

After they left, a man and a woman still stood before the mountain gate.

After a long while.

Luo Chen cautiously asked, "Sect Master, are there any further instructions? If not, this disciple will return to cultivation."

"Oh, you may go back!" Grandplain Spirit Lord said, as if waking from a dream.

Just as Luo Chen was about to leave, she suddenly called him back.

"Wait."

Luo Chen turned around, looking at her with confusion.

Grandplain Spirit Lord frowned, then waved her hand. "Never mind. Go back. Just don't forget the encouragement and expectations the Alchemy Saint had for you just now."

"This disciple remembers."

Luo Chen bowed, then turned and retraced his steps.

Behind him, Grandplain Spirit Lord's gaze remained fixed on his retreating figure.

Although Chu Yan's words just now were merely an elder's encouragement to a junior, the expectations seemed too high.

After all, Xuan Luo was still only an Initiate cultivator at present. The highest grade of pill he could refine was only third-order.

Why would she say she wanted to exchange insights on alchemy with Xuan Luo in the future?

Was there something special about Xuan Luo that she hadn't discovered?

Grandplain Spirit Lord was deeply puzzled.

Perhaps only Luo Chen himself understood the true meaning behind Chu Yan's parting words.

An alchemist capable of exchanging insights with an Alchemy Saint could not be an ordinary alchemist.

Even someone who could refine fifth-order divine pills might not necessarily be worthy of her attention.

Only those who had comprehended the laws of alchemy could sit and discuss the Dao with her.

Through the Art of Refining Heaven's Mechanism in alchemy, she knew that he had obtained the inheritance she had left behind, but had not inherited the law insights she had left within it.

Although she hadn't said it explicitly, there was some regret.

Thus, the expectation in her words was a hope that Luo Chen would one day comprehend the laws of alchemy. Only then would the two of them be suited to sit and discuss the Dao together.

But for Luo Chen, the path of alchemy, and indeed the hundred arts of immortal cultivation, were ultimately merely miscellaneous arts that aided cultivation.

If he could comprehend them along the way, so be it.

If not, he wouldn't spend too much effort on this path.

Instead, he was quite interested in the conversation between Chu Yan and Grandplain Spirit Lord inside the cloud carriage.

One word had been mentioned again.

**[Demonic Cave]**

This term hadn't appeared very frequently in the information Luo Chen had encountered since entering the Xuan Realm.

But whenever he consulted the classics about major events, this term was often involved.

Whether it was the demon realm's invasion of the Xuan Realm ten thousand years ago, or the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's opening of the Mountains and Seas Realm, both were related to demonic cave surges.

And this time, the so-called Demon-Suppression Trial was also connected to demonic caves.

If he had the opportunity, he would like to personally observe what a demonic cave was, and what a demonic cave surge meant!

To this end, Luo Chen once again entered the sect's Scripture Pavilion.

His research targets were all shifted to classics related to demonic caves.

...

Inside the Scripture Pavilion.

Luo Chen separated his divine intent and divine sense, and the mode of acting purely on instinct finally became clear.

Previously, in order to conceal himself and avoid detection by Chu Yan and Grandplain Spirit Lord, he had merged his divine intent and divine sense, entering an instinctual state.

In that state, even a Void Refining True Lord would find it difficult to see through his disguise.

But the drawback was also obvious—some behaviors were purely instinctual.

That was why he had accidentally exposed himself when Chu Yan tested him with the Art of Refining Heaven's Mechanism in alchemy.

Luo Chen realized that this state might not be suitable for daily activities, but rather more appropriate for combat.

However, specific research could be conducted later.

For now, he needed to separate his divine sense to engage in large-scale reading of the classics.

With his divine sense fully activated, he swept through the many classics below the ninth floor.

Every book related to demonic caves, one by one, entered his divine soul.

**[A demonic cave is the same as a spiritual cave—a place where energy converges. However, one is formed by clear qi rising upward, while the other is formed by turbid qi sinking downward.]**

**[The demonic caves of the Xuan Realm are not naturally formed. They are created by top-tier Great Vehicle cultivators of the demon realm, who pierce through weak points in space, connecting the two realms. They are extremely difficult to eliminate.]**

**[A demonic cave surge indicates the formation of a spatial passage. The more intense the surge, the larger and more stable the spatial passage becomes, and the higher the cultivation level of the experts who can pass through. The most recent demonic cave surge occurred two thousand years ago, while the largest-scale one in recent times was over ten thousand years ago. During that surge, a Great Vehicle demon cultivator crossed over from the other realm, sparking the Xuan-Demon War.]**

**[The difficulty in eliminating demonic caves by human effort lies not only in spatial laws, but also in the power of the two realms merging. The demon side has various methods to try to expand the demonic caves. For example, planting demon moss, which absorbs spiritual qi and transforms it into a demonic qi environment suitable for demonic activity. This type of plant naturally contains a different kind of law essence and is a product of the Wood Demon race. It cannot be removed by anyone below the Ascendant stage.]**

**[Wherever demonic caves exist in the Xuan Realm, remnants of the demon race are often stationed to monitor them. Once the demonic qi grows too strong, a Demon-Suppression Trial must be conducted to disperse the smaller demonic caves.]**

**[The so-called demon race remnants are branches of the demon race left behind in the Xuan Realm after several large and small Xuan-Demon wars. After being subdued and assimilated, they were settled in various places across the Xuan Realm, forming their own races.]**

**[The Demon-Suppression Trial is extremely dangerous, but it also contains great opportunities. It allows Sage cultivators to absorb spiritual mechanisms, comprehend law essence, and advance to the Ascendant stage.]**

**[Spiritual mechanisms...]**

Several days later.

Luo Chen summarized all the information he had gathered and finally formed a complete concept of demonic caves.

He also understood what the Demon-Suppression Trial meant.

Various sects would dispatch Sage cultivators into the demonic caves, cooperating with Ascendant great powers to clear out demon moss and disperse the demonic caves.

During this process, they not only had to guard against naturally occurring spatial rifts, but also deal with demonic experts who might have secretly crossed over from the demon realm.

Thus, it was very dangerous.

But the opportunities were also great. When a demonic cave was dispersed and a spiritual cave re-formed, spiritual mechanisms were often generated.

The quantity wasn't large, but it was extremely beneficial for Sage cultivators to comprehend law essence and condense their primordial spirit.

Therefore, the Demon-Suppression Trial continued, and its scale grew larger and larger. Every sect wanted to participate.

In the past, the Plain Spirit Sect could only send a few people, barely getting any benefits.

But this time, because Grandplain Spirit Lord had advanced to the Void Refining stage, and also because of Chu Yan's influence, the Plain Spirit Sect had been allocated ten slots!

If managed properly, perhaps among those ten people, there was a chance that several future Ascendant great powers would emerge.

"With spiritual mechanisms and vast energy reserves, this Demon-Suppression Trial is an excellent opportunity for me."

Luo Chen thought to himself as he walked out of the Scripture Pavilion.

But to participate in the Demon-Suppression Trial, one needed at least Sage cultivation.

The next session was only about sixty years away.

Forming a core within sixty years had already been shocking enough.

If he were to form a Sage embryo within a hundred years, would that be too astonishing?

Luo Chen hesitated.

Even with reasons related to pills and talent, it would be hard to convince people.

He would need to fill in some additional opportunities to make the story self-consistent.

His gaze flickered.

For him, opportunities for forming a Sage embryo weren't really important, nor were they easy to find.

But if they didn't exist, he could create them himself!

As long as it happened outside the sect, the specific circumstances would be whatever he said they were.

But before that, he should first fill in the reasons related to pills.

Luo Chen smiled knowingly and left the Scripture Pavilion.

...

After the Alchemy Saint departed, the Plain Spirit Sect, which had been lively for a time, seemed to quiet down.

But this was only on the surface.

Privately, the sect's operations became increasingly frequent, especially in the Alchemy Academy. After receiving the Alchemy Saint's brief guidance, many difficult problems in alchemy were resolved.

From the three spiritual mountains, earth fire was continuously drawn out, and pill furnaces baked all day long.

One after another, new pills were successfully refined by the various alchemy masters.

The Mountain-Sea Palace, which had originally only served as a reception venue, had not been left idle except for the Alchemy Saint Hall. The remaining halls were distributed to a group of fourth-pattern alchemists.

These alchemists actually came from various peaks and branches and already had their own cave dwellings.

This distribution was more like a reward from the sect.

And Luo Chen was also among those rewarded.

The side hall where he had originally attended to the Alchemy Saint had its plaque changed and was directly bestowed upon him.

Its name was **[Xuanzhen Hall]** .

The intention was clear—they hoped that one day he would advance to become a true Sage.

Luo Chen naturally showed great delight at this.

In truth, he was also somewhat pleased.

At least after forming his Sage embryo, he wouldn't have to squeeze into Jade Spring Peak and live day and night with his cheap master.

Xuanzhen Hall had its own fourth-order spiritual vein.

But for now, he couldn't act too exceptionally.

All his energy was focused on refining the Five Elements Divine Pill.

Dean He and the others were naturally gratified by this. It showed that Luo Chen was indeed serious about the alchemy path, having received the Alchemy Saint's guidance and immediately wanting to turn it into practical results.

However, given the current busyness of the Alchemy Academy, no one was really supervising Luo Chen's pill-refining progress.

Thus, he was left to his own devices.

Luo Chen was happy with this arrangement. He frequently visited the Spiritual Medicine Court among the Three Halls and Five Courts, purchasing large quantities of raw materials for the Five Elements Divine Pill in his personal capacity.

Thanks to the promise made by the Spiritual Medicine Court's head during the pill-tasting gathering, his purchase price was not only lower than market rates, but even twenty percent cheaper than the sect's internal prices.

One day.

Luo Chen's figure appeared once again in the Spiritual Medicine Court.

For the reception disciples, seeing Luo Chen was no longer anything unusual.

They just smiled and greeted him.

"Senior Brother Xuan Luo is here again?"

"Mm."

"Still buying those specific medicinal ingredients? You don't have to come in person. Just send a message with a transmission talisman, and we'll have a disciple deliver them to your Xuanzhen Hall."

"It's better to choose them myself, since I'm spending my own contribution points."

"Senior Brother Xuan Luo knows the way well. Go ahead on your own. This junior brother has some troublesome matters to deal with."

"Oh? What kind of trouble could you be having?"

Luo Chen became interested and casually inquired.

The reception cultivator helplessly pointed at the account book.

"A few large-scale medicinal gardens have had reduced yields for over a hundred years. We never paid much attention before. During the Alchemy Saint's recent visit, the Spiritual Medicine Court discovered this situation. We're considering what level of task to issue to the External Affairs Hall, to have them recruit disciples to accompany the Spiritual Medicine Court for an inspection."

Luo Chen glanced at the account book and noted down those few place names.

No wonder this junior brother was so troubled. Those places were very far from the Plain Spirit Sect—tens of thousands of li away.

They were located in the buffer zones between several Ascendant-level sect territories. Strictly speaking, they were considered exclaves.

For such distant tasks, Qi Refinement and Initiate disciples generally wouldn't accept them.

And since it was merely an inspection task for reduced medicinal yields, the rewards wouldn't be very generous. Powerful Initiate cultivators would be even less inclined to take it.

Finding suitable candidates for the task was indeed quite troublesome.

Patting the junior brother's shoulder, Luo Chen smiled and offered a few words of comfort before heading toward the warehouse.

After selecting the medicinal ingredients he wanted, Luo Chen returned to Xuanzhen Hall.

A year later, as a rich pill fragrance wafted out, the alchemists of the Alchemy Academy knew that Luo Chen had successfully refined the Five Elements Divine Pill.

This speed was truly fast!

But with Luo Chen's talent beforehand and the Alchemy Saint's guidance afterward, it was only to be expected.

In truth, Luo Chen could have refined the Five Elements Divine Pill long ago.

After all, it was only a third-order pill.

He had been able to refine it back in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

All his previous pretenses were merely to make everything appear natural and logical.

In private, he had already refined a large batch of top-grade Five Elements Divine Pills to support his Initiate-stage cultivation.

Without drawing heavily on the Plain Spirit Sect's spiritual qi, top-grade Five Elements Divine Pills were the most suitable pills for increasing his magical power.

They were very balanced. Without needing deliberate refinement, they could meet the requirements of his Five-Colored Golden Core.

Of course!

On the surface, he still put on a show of arduous refinement for a few years before announcing that he had accumulated enough pills and needed to go into seclusion for cultivation.

By this time, his true cultivation level was already at the mid-Initiate stage.

He chose the Jade Ravine Cave Dwelling on Jade Spring Peak as his seclusion location.

He believed that by the time he emerged from seclusion, he would definitely be at the late-Initiate stage or even the Great Perfection stage.

Then he would casually find a task to go out, form his Sage embryo outside, and return to the sect to apply for participation in the Demon-Suppression Trial. He was confident the Plain Spirit Sect wouldn't refuse him.

Everything was ready. Let it begin!

The cave dwelling's door slowly closed. Luo Chen, carrying a large batch of Five Elements Divine Pills, began his seclusion. (End of chapter)
