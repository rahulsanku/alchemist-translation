# Chapter 1199: All Things Return to Void, Chaos Indestructible

A hundred years passed in the blink of an eye.

**Ninefold Immortal Tower!**

Ever since Luo Chen had released his restraint on chaotic energy, there was no longer any so-called Nine Rings of Destruction.

At the tower's summit, the once relatively calm Chaotic Sea had long since boiled like oil.

A figure sat in meditation within, emitting a dense golden light from his body.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's eyes snapped open. With a flick of his palm, an object appeared in his hand.

It was the Vacuum Gu.

No larger than a fingernail, yet the patterns on its surface were intricate and complex to the extreme.

Those folds that had once resembled ancient glaciers were now as fine as silk threads, utterly uncountable.

Its body, originally pure dark like glass, was now shrouded in a trace of five-colored starlight.

This starlight made it dreamlike and magnificent, while also appearing even more mysterious and unique.

"Has the final step of transformation finally been completed?"

As Luo Chen murmured to himself, the gu insect's body began to tremble gently.

Shua! Shua!

A pair of five-colored wings suddenly extended from its back.

As the wings vibrated, the surrounding void actually began to tremble, and even...

Luo Chen's scarlet-gold pupils abruptly opened wide as he stared fixedly at the surrounding void, a look of shock appearing on his face.

Because under the gaze of his spiritual eyes, there were actually faint void turbulence currents nearby!

What ordinary people understood as void turbulence was the surging flow of complex energies within the void, making it difficult for normal beings to fly or travel through it.

But for Luo Chen, who had some understanding of the dao of space, he knew what true void turbulence really was.

It wasn't just complex energy surging, but also countless tiny spatial nodes constantly being generated and destroyed within it!

It sounded simple, but the generation and destruction of spatial nodes created an extremely terrifying tearing force, even more so than ordinary spatial rifts.

A mature Vacuum Divine Gu could actually trigger void turbulence—how could Luo Chen not be shocked?

And the changes in the Vacuum Divine Gu seemed to be more than just this!

Its mouth, originally having no teeth, gradually protruded forward, finally forming a sharp, pointed proboscis, like a mosquito or a stink bug, or perhaps like certain tree insects that feed on sap.

This proboscis was extremely sharp, inspiring fear at a glance.

The moment the proboscis appeared, the Vacuum Divine Gu suddenly plunged it into the nothingness before it.

The next moment.

Boom!

The Ninefold Immortal Tower, which had been under Luo Chen's control, actually experienced a rare massive tremor.

Sensing something was wrong, Luo Chen quickly called for a stop.

This Vacuum Divine Gu had long been refined by him. Not only had he attached soul restrictions and magical power imprints, but he had also successively fed it his essence blood, making them spiritually connected.

After Luo Chen called for a stop, the little fellow reluctantly withdrew its proboscis, still sending out signals of hunger.

The tremor of the Ninefold Immortal Tower finally stopped.

Luo Chen realized that the little fellow, having just completed its transformation, was probably hungry.

Just like a snail that, after breaking out of its shell, would eat the shell that had incubated it, the purpose was to absorb nutrients.

And the most useful nutrient for the Vacuum Divine Gu was undoubtedly "space."

The Ninefold Immortal Tower itself was a broken immortal artifact containing a great spatial attribute.

"You can't eat this right now. Eat this instead!"

Luo Chen grabbed a handful of Void Bud Crystals, and the little fellow happily began to eat them.

Seeing this, Luo Chen couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

At least for now, the Vacuum Divine Gu hadn't grown tired of Void Bud Crystals. Otherwise, he really wouldn't have anything good to feed it.

Watching the little fellow insert its proboscis into the Void Bud Crystal and suck the food contentedly, Luo Chen fell into thought.

For now, the Vacuum Gu that had transformed into a divine gu had already revealed two innate abilities.

Triggering void turbulence and devouring space.

The rest was still unclear.

So how should he utilize the Vacuum Divine Gu?

Luo Chen recalled a late-stage Sage cultivator named Meng Shentong he had encountered during his journey to the Southern Frontier.

What he had used was a fourth-rank Vacuum Gu, which was quite helpful in combat and could even hide within the void.

But his own was a fifth-rank divine gu!

As he pondered, Luo Chen moved his mind and recalled the Vacuum Divine Gu.

Instantly, he sensed a difference from before.

His entire being seemed to have merged with this space. As long as he wished, he could integrate into it.

Not concealment, but integration!

Luo Chen was eager to try. He took a step forward.

When he reappeared, he had already crossed an extremely great distance and arrived elsewhere.

"Condensing earth into an inch, a thousand li in an instant... no, it's more like... Void-Shattering Divine Escape!"

A green-clad figure appeared in Luo Chen's mind.

He hadn't expected that he could also master such a mysterious escape technique, and without expending any effort.

With this escape technique at his side, his chances of successfully leaving the Guixu would be greatly increased.

And that wasn't all!

Luo Chen swept his sleeve, and the surrounding area suddenly became empty. The chaotic energy that had filled it seemed to vanish into thin air.

As he shook his sleeve again.

Rumble!

A large mass of chaotic energy suddenly poured out.

Luo Chen laughed heartily.

This Great Luo Heaven Sleeve, improved from Azure Frost's spatial divine ability Sleeve Within Sleeve, had increased in power by dozens of times!

The original improved Great Luo Heaven Sleeve was at most a top-tier spell.

But now, it already possessed the might of a divine ability!

Moving mountains and capturing seas was merely a matter of waving one's sleeve.

Amidst his joy, Luo Chen thought further.

Were there other wonderful uses?

Since he had already replicated two of Azure Frost's divine abilities, could he also replicate her killing move with the help of the Vacuum Divine Gu?

"That divine ability called Subspace Slash..."

Luo Chen murmured, making a hand blade.

The next moment, a wisp of pitch-black crack appeared with difficulty before him.

Just as it was about to take shape, Luo Chen's brow suddenly furrowed.

He withdrew his hand and dismissed this technique.

Looking at his palm, cracks like a tortoise shell had already appeared.

"Without understanding the details, without comprehending the laws, it's ultimately too forced."

Luo Chen stopped worrying.

The Vacuum Divine Gu should have more wonderful uses, but that would require more time to ponder and develop.

And for now, he didn't have that time.

Luo Chen returned to where the cultivation stone was located. Four figures were anxiously waiting there.

Fang Qingxue, Gu Xun, Xiao Lin, and Ice Heaven Son.

The Barbaric Martial Sect had ultimately failed.

Being too old, he hadn't managed to break through to Martial Immortal.

But that woman Ice Heaven Son had, fortunately, succeeded before the deadline, becoming one of the companions to brave the Destruction Gate together.

Seeing Luo Chen's return, the four all breathed sighs of relief.

Fang Qingxue greeted him, "You're finally back. Earlier, the Ninefold Immortal Tower made an abnormal tremor, and we thought it had started. If something had happened to you, we would have had even less hope."

"You worried for nothing. I was just testing something."

The tremor of the Ninefold Immortal Tower was related to the Vacuum Divine Gu trying to devour this space.

But...

Luo Chen put away his smile and looked up above.

That round Destruction Gate was frantically spewing chaotic energy, causing tremors a thousand times stronger than before!

"Soon, soon!"

Everyone could sense this, and they understood what Luo Chen meant by "soon."

Exchanging glances, Ice Heaven Son emitted a magnetic voice that was neither male nor female.

"Luo Chen, what should we do next?"

She had broken through the latest, had the fewest high-realm techniques, and was the least confident about leaving the Guixu.

Luo Chen said solemnly, "Although the Ninefold Immortal Tower is already badly damaged, since it has stood in the Guixu for so long, it naturally has its unique qualities. When the great limit arrives, you all hide in the tower's most core area and rely on the Ninefold Immortal Tower to withstand the first wave of impact. Then, we seize the opportunity to charge into the Destruction Gate and rely on the gaps in its spewing to leave the Guixu."

Ice Heaven Son was surprised, "Is it that simple?"

Luo Chen nodded, "Yes, that simple."

Gu Xun beside him said seriously, "The difficulty has never been how to enter the Destruction Gate, but how to survive the complex and terrifying impacts inside and hold on until reaching the outside world. The difficulty of this made even Carefree Abode Master hesitate. It's definitely not as simple as you think."

Ice Heaven Son was momentarily speechless, her emotions clearly somewhat low.

Her preparation time was too short. Even if she had barely broken through to the Immortal Realm, in that kind of situation...

The others had no time to pay attention to her. In fact, giving her this chance to leave together was already a grace.

Fang Qingxue was somewhat curious and looked at Luo Chen, "Brother Luo, you said earlier that we should hide in the tower's most core area to withstand the first wave of impact. What about you?"

"Me?"

Luo Chen smiled, looking confidently at the frantically writhing Destruction Gate.

"For me, the first wave of impact will be an opportunity."

The others were momentarily puzzled.

...

On the desolate, boundless earth.

At some point, a wisp of heavenly wind had arisen.

More and more thick fog, born from nowhere, slowly but inexorably began to shroud the entire world.

Roaring sounds filled the air, unceasing.

Wherever it passed, all living beings were extinguished.

Between the heavens, lightning flashed and thunder roared, shaking the soul.

Spatial rifts, tens of thousands of feet long, opened their deep, abyss-like mouths.

The once lively Seventy-Two Cities were now dead silent.

The Canopy Tower Forest was utterly lifeless.

The Weeping Abyss had its earth split open, buried by the endless fog.

A space only a foot in size sank deep into the ground, shrouded by a magnetic ore vein.

Within the space was a green figure, coiled with a snake's tail, as if in slumber.

When the fog arrived, the space began to shatter inch by inch. The green-robed figure blinked, but before it could react, it turned to ashes and merged into the Destruction Fog.

The once Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain was no longer visible with its bone seas and thousand mountains. Only shocking spatial rifts filled everything.

The Heavenly Wind Tribe, millions of beings, silently perished.

In the Sea of Stars.

The Star Sect Master, Danyang, sat cross-legged on the Purple Forbidden Enclosure. Beside him, the sundial gradually dimmed.

He looked up at the sky.

He saw three great suns, like fireballs, crashing down.

Boom! Boom! Boom!

The Guixu trembled, accelerating its destruction.

One by one, hidden spaces were stained by the fog, gradually destroyed along with the Guixu.

Even the space crack where the Ancient God's Dao Field was located had a huge gap exposed.

But when the Destruction Fog surged in, it was already empty inside.

That once magnificent Thirty-Three Layer Divine Mother Hall had vanished without a trace at some unknown time.

...

The entire world was dark, without light.

Only a spatial vortex was rapidly expanding.

The deep hole was like a door, or like a black hole that could devour everything.

That was what was called the Destruction Gate.

The Ninefold Immortal Tower, which had once barely covered it, had long since shrunk and stood on the ground below.

Without restraint, and with the empowerment of the Guixu's final destructive power, this spatial vortex had expanded to tens of millions of feet in size.

The entire world seemed about to be completely devoured as it expanded.

In the midst of repeated expansion and contraction.

Boom!

A shockwave of unimaginable vastness erupted from the hole!

Inside the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

Fang Qingxue and the other three had been watching the outside through the formation's central hub.

Especially that tiny figure sitting cross-legged below the Destruction Gate. Even though it had already unfolded a thousand-foot true body, compared to that enormous hole, it still appeared infinitely small.

When that terrifying shockwave erupted, the four cried out in unison.

But Luo Chen, at the center of the eruption, suddenly opened his eyes.

"Here it comes!"

He made no resistance, letting the vast force wash over him.

In just an instant.

Boom!

Everything was like a dream, a bubble, like dew, like lightning.

The thousand-foot true body, like a bubble, shattered with a bang.

Seeing this, the four hiding in the tower were struck by lightning, unable to speak.

And the terrifying shockwave continued to spread.

Even the Ninefold Immortal Tower, which had shrunk to its extreme and had all its defensive power activated, revealed inch-by-inch cracks under this impact.

Amidst their tension, they had to continue watching outside.

Since Luo Chen had said the first wave of impact was a great opportunity for him, he must have a backup plan and wouldn't be seeking death like this.

As if to verify their thoughts.

At the place where Luo Chen's body had disintegrated, a small gray cauldron slowly flew out.

Hum...

A peculiar humming sound rang out.

Then, a golden light emerged, until it was the size of a normal person.

It was a phantom shadow, reflecting Luo Chen's face.

But that wasn't Luo Chen's true body—it was his soul!

Not just the main soul, but also the divided souls recalled from the seven hundred and twenty apertures.

At this moment, they had all converged into one!

The moment the soul manifested, this collapsing Guixu world flashed with streaks of bright white lightning in the darkness, as if trying to destroy Luo Chen's soul.

But Luo Chen ignored it, still sitting cross-legged on the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

The soul-shaking thunder sounds crackled as they arrived.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron also flashed with lightning, emitting thunder sounds in resistance.

In the interval between their stalemate, there was a gap.

Luo Chen seized this gap and pointed at the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Instantly, the power accumulated over a hundred years poured out.

And Luo Chen's soul manipulated this power, circulating the *Chaos Indestructible Scripture*!

As if responding.

At the place where Luo Chen's true body had been destroyed, a blood-colored light gradually emerged.

This light grew brighter and brighter, until it reached the heavens and earth.

Within the blood light, a majestic figure quickly took shape.

Feet treading the void, legs like heavenly pillars.

Three heads and six arms, majestic and extraordinary.

Luo Chen's soul no longer lingered, turning into a stream of light that rushed into his main body.

The moment he opened his eyes!

Beneath his feet, a sea of blood surged, giving birth to a lotus platform with thirteen petals, as if signifying a breakthrough of the epoch's limit.

Above his head, golden light bloomed, solemn and dignified. Within the golden light was a terrifying skeletal halo. On the bone wheel were engraved many mysterious characters. Those familiar with Buddhist scriptures would recognize the words "No Self-Form, No Person-Form, No Sentient Being-Form."

But these words were not engraved in ordinary script but generated with Immortal Realm Gold-Eroded Script, appearing even more mysterious.

Luo Chen stepped on the thirteen-petal Blood Sea Lotus Platform, with the Formless Golden Light Bone Halo above his head, his eyes blazing with brilliant divine light. Like a god, a demon, and a buddha, he was truly terrifying.

The moment he regained clarity, he subconsciously uttered a name.

"Azure Frost?"

Then he collected his thoughts and examined himself.

Of course, in such a short time, his understanding was certainly not comprehensive. Luo Chen merely glanced at the attribute panel.

Sure enough, the entries on it had changed in an instant.

**[Chaos Indestructible Body]**

*Broken and then established, undying and indestructible.*

He had finally taken the last step, achieving the Martial Dao Destruction Realm, and forged the Chaos Indestructible Body he had dreamed of!

Not only that, the originally hidden **[Five Spirit Dao Body]** had also begun to emit a faint light, almost breaking through its seal!

"That's about enough!"

Luo Chen put away the attribute panel, his body rapidly shrinking back to normal size.

He reached out!

A stream of light flew before him, enveloping his entire being.

As soon as he entered the Ninefold Immortal Tower, four gazes simultaneously fell upon him.

Luo Chen let out a low shout.

"Let's go!"

The next moment, the Ninefold Immortal Tower turned into a stream of light, spinning like a moth diving into a flame as it charged into the Destruction Gate. (Chapter End)
