# Chapter: The End of the Road

The Mystic Realm!

The Mysterious Nether Continent.

Within the Tianfeng Clan's territory.

Inside a spirit mountain cave dwelling, a woman sat cross-legged.

Clad in simple green robes, her peerless beauty could not be concealed.

Spiritual energy swirled around her, and ripples frequently appeared in the void.

She sat there quietly, yet it was as if she was not there at all—as though in the next moment she would vanish into the void and disappear from this world.

As her cultivation art circulated, the aura emanating from the woman grew increasingly dense, until it could no longer be suppressed.

Nor did she wish to suppress it.

The next moment!

*Boom!*

Her aura burst forth in full force. Her massive primordial spirit flew out from her crown, manifesting in the sky above.

Rolling spiritual energy surged in like tidal waves.

A majestic phantom of a luan bird soared through the heavens, madly devouring the origin qi of heaven and earth.

Affected by this, the entire Tianfeng Clan was shaken.

One figure after another flew out from the spirit mountains and great rivers, gazing expectantly toward that place.

Some of these figures had taken human form, but most were bird-type demon beasts.

Seeing this scene, everyone knew that one of their own was about to break through to a new realm!

Not only these low-level demon beasts.

Within the Tianfeng Clan, the sole Great Vehicle stage Tianfeng also turned its gaze toward the place where the spiritual tide surged.

"As expected of a child specially brought up from the lower realm by Fenghuang'er. Her talent is truly outstanding. Even though she primarily cultivates the most difficult and powerful spatial laws, and also practices body refinement on the side, she still managed to successfully condense a phantom in just five hundred years. As long as she crosses this step, she will naturally enter the Law Manifestation stage. Her future achievements will surely be no less than Fenghuang'er or Lieyu's."

But the next moment.

The Tianfeng suddenly froze.

Where its divine sense reached, it saw the rolling spiritual tide abruptly dissipate, and the soaring luan bird phantom suddenly collapse.

Such a sudden change was truly unexpected!

For the sake of cultivation speed, their Tianfeng Clan had long abandoned the most ancient path of true spirits, instead pursuing the human method of qi-refining immortality.

Therefore, the process of breaking through realms was no different from that of humans.

Aside from the heavenly tribulations faced when breaking through to the Sage stage, Body Integration stage, and Great Vehicle ascension, normal breakthroughs to major realms did not attract thunder tribulations.

It was more about internal transformation!

As long as one's foundation was sufficient, breaking through was a matter of course.

The celestial phenomena brought about by a realm breakthrough were merely a gift from the world, a process that would typically last for some time, allowing one to absorb external energy to stabilize their current realm.

Thus, very few people would succeed in taking that step, only to fail afterward.

It was utterly unreasonable.

Could it be that the child had not yet adapted to the laws of heaven and earth in the Mystic Realm?

But that shouldn't be the case either!

She had already been in the Mystic Realm for five hundred years!

Everything that needed to be transformed had been transformed, everything that needed to be adapted to had been adapted to. There was no way such a mishap could occur.

"Could it be that her Dao heart has developed a problem, causing her phantom to collapse?"

The Tianfeng Ancestor, not knowing the truth, spread its divine sense toward her cave dwelling.

"Azure Frost, what happened? Why did you fail at the very threshold?"

Inside the cave dwelling.

The woman in green robes had a pale face, with faint traces of blood at the corner of her mouth.

She wiped away the blood and replied respectfully, "I have troubled the Ancestor with worry. It is because this child's cultivation is insufficient; I still need to polish it further."

"Cultivation insufficient?"

The Tianfeng Ancestor was half-convinced.

Nevertheless, it still comforted Azure Frost, telling her to recuperate well and prepare for the next breakthrough.

After the Ancestor's divine sense departed, Azure Frost fell into silence.

She blinked her eyes, as if the scene from before still lingered, refusing to dissipate.

She had seen it!

In a chaos.

A thousand-zhang giant was instantly destroyed by a terrifying storm, without any power to resist.

Along with it, the Life-and-Death Gu that had connected the two of them was completely destroyed.

At that moment, even the faintest connection, already weakened to the extreme, backlashed against her, causing the step that should have been easily crossed to become impossible.

"Luo Chen, have you finally died?"

The woman murmured softly. On her face, which had always been calm and cold as frost, a trace of guilt appeared.

That man was a true unparalleled genius!

Not only was his talent extraordinary, but his Dao heart was also unwaveringly firm. In terms of combat ability, he was no less than her.

Even if he could not successfully ascend, as long as he found a great world no less than the Mystic Realm, he could still achieve extraordinary accomplishments.

In five hundred years, his progress might even surpass hers.

But because of her sister Rosycloud, not only had his dream of ascension been shattered, but his subsequent path of cultivation had also been forcibly cut off.

After enduring bitterly for five hundred years, he had finally fallen.

The woman unconsciously pressed her hand to her heart, then slowly closed her eyes.

A faint murmur echoed from her lips.

"I'm sorry..."

...

*Crack!*

A sharp sound rang out in everyone's ears.

Fang Qingxue looked ahead and saw that the walls of the Ninefold Immortal Tower, which had been protecting them, were now covered with one hideous crack after another.

And this was inside the tower!

One could only imagine that the defensive layer outside the tower had likely been completely destroyed.

She finally understood why her ancestor, the Carefree Realm Master, had held the Ninefold Immortal Tower for thousands of years but had never attempted to use this treasure to cross the Gate of Destruction.

The pressure from both inside and outside was beyond what ordinary people could imagine.

Now, having only just entered the Gate of Destruction, the Ninefold Immortal Tower could no longer hold up. What about the journey ahead?

"Brother Luo, what do we do?"

Everyone's gazes turned to the white-robed man sitting at the center of the formation.

At that moment, Luo Chen suddenly opened his eyes.

He said gravely, "The Immortal Tower is about to be destroyed. Everyone, look after yourselves!"

As he spoke, the Golden Erosion Formation Diagram floating above his head also began to show cracks.

The entire Ninefold Immortal Tower let out a creaking sound, as if it could no longer bear the strain.

The four of them—Fang Qingxue, Gu Xun, Xiao Lin, and Ice Heavenly Son—did not dare to be careless. They took a deep breath and released their protective astral qi to shield themselves.

Not only that, but they also added various treasures to their defenses.

Fang Qingxue, in front of everyone, draped a light gauze robe over herself. Its treasure light flowed, clearly extraordinary. Then she released a flower basket that enveloped her entirely. She glanced at Luo Chen, hesitating slightly.

Gu Xun's expression was grim. He first swallowed a black pill, then put on a string of prayer beads. Not only that, but a damaged ancient bell hung high above his head. Finally, he scattered a handful of crystals that formed a tiny space, enclosing him within.

In comparison, Xiao Lin and Ice Heavenly Son were much more poorly equipped.

The former only had time to slap a yellowed talisman onto himself, from which powerful spiritual energy emanated without concealment.

Luo Chen glanced at it, somewhat surprised, but quickly understood.

That talisman should have been left behind by the former immortal Xiao Lan. Xiao Lin had simply activated it.

But since he did not practice the method of qi-refining immortality, no matter how powerful this talisman had once been, it could now probably only unleash half its effect at most.

As for Ice Heavenly Son, her face was bleak. She could only repeatedly urge her protective astral qi.

Gui Xu martial artists pursued the martial path and did not rely on external objects.

Moreover, she had advanced to the Human Immortal realm too late and had no time to find protective treasures.

Thus, she could only face the coming storm in this nearly "bare" state.

She instinctively turned her gaze to Luo Chen.

At this moment, Luo Chen had already walked to the front of the group. A blood-colored light flowed over his entire body, forming a blood-colored cloth robe.

With both hands extended forward, a divine astral light shield materialized.

Not only that, but starlight and thunderbolts burst forth from within his body, completely encircling him.

"Here it comes!"

No one knew when this voice had been uttered.

Then came a deafening explosion that rang in everyone's ears.

*Boom!*

The Ninefold Immortal Tower shattered.

A terrifying shockwave surged inward from the outside.

Luo Chen stood at the very front, letting out a muffled groan.

He had withstood the first wave!

But he could not help staggering backward.

Seeing this, Fang Qingxue's face finally hardened. She stepped forward to Luo Chen's side, and the flower basket above her head enveloped him as well.

The surrounding currents of air rushed past, beating against her gauze robe, making it rustle.

Gu Xun, Xiao Lin, and Ice Heavenly Son were relatively further behind, so they did not bear the brunt of the Ninefold Immortal Tower's destruction. But the subsequent aftershocks still rolled in.

The speed was too fast. The three had no time to react and could only defend passively.

*Rip...*

A tear appeared in Xiao Lin's talisman, and a trace of blood appeared on his face.

Gu Xun's entire body trembled. The Empty Sprout Crystal hut protecting him clattered noisily.

As for Ice Heavenly Son, her eyes widened, her pale lips slightly parted, but no sound came out.

*Boom!*

The aftershock passed, and Ice Heavenly Son's entire body was torn to pieces, vanishing from this strange space.

Sensing this, the four survivors all changed expression drastically.

"Don't stop! Keep moving forward!"

Luo Chen's voice was calm, even cold and harsh.

He took large strides, leaving the protection of the flower basket, and moved forward unwaveringly.

Fang Qingxue looked at Luo Chen's back, gritted her teeth, and followed.

The two behind also hurriedly followed.

...

Through his Red-Eyed Golden Pupils, he could see:

Black cracks, expanding and contracting transparent nodes, clusters of flowing gas impacts.

Occasionally, meteorites the size of stars would appear, but they would be ground to nothing in an instant.

A feeling of universal destruction, of all things returning to nothingness, arose in Luo Chen's heart.

For a moment, he was even dazed, as if some fleeting insight was about to reappear.

But soon, Luo Chen gathered his stray thoughts and calmed down.

The spatial vortex known as the Gate of Destruction had once been only a hundred zhang in size.

But by the time Gui Xu was about to be destroyed, it had expanded to who knows how large.

A thousand zhang, a hundred thousand zhang, a million zhang, or even ten million zhang?

Luo Chen did not know. He only knew that here, he was walking on thin ice. One wrong move and he would fall completely.

Even though he had cultivated the Chaos Indestructible Body, which in theory meant his bones would not be destroyed and a drop of blood could regenerate, as long as his martial will remained, he could recover again and again. But in this space where spatial storms could erupt at any moment, no matter how strong his physical body, he would only be reborn again and again, only to be torn apart again and again, until he had no more power to regenerate.

He could only rely on his instincts to move forward, forward, and forward again!

Time passed, and he did not know how long. A miserable scream came from behind.

Luo Chen did not look back. He knew that another of his companions had fallen.

It should be Xiao Lin!

That talisman could not last much longer.

Then who would be next?

Luo Chen did not want to think about it. The terrifying feeling of all things being destroyed always hung over his heart.

The Heavenly Star Primordial Magnetic Divine Thunder was almost exhausted.

The blood cloth robe formed by his martial divine ability was already tattered.

A fierce look flashed across Luo Chen's face. He raised his hand.

*Whoosh!*

A large umbrella unfurled like a flower.

The spirit treasure, Burial Bone Umbrella!

The astral qi in his body was rapidly converted through the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra*, forming long-unused mana, barely enough to drive the Burial Bone Umbrella.

The moment this umbrella appeared, its artifact spirit awakened.

It looked at this scene in horror, constantly sending out danger signals.

But Luo Chen ignored it all and continued forward.

"Be careful!"

A warning came from behind.

Luo Chen had already seen it. In his line of sight, a massive fireball like a great sun came roaring toward him.

It should be a fallen star that had been absorbed into this spatial vortex.

Luo Chen held the umbrella in one hand and made a grasping motion with the other. The Yuantu sword fell into his hand.

"Slash!"

A towering sword qi erupted violently.

It cut the terrifying falling star fireball in two.

The roaring sound of the meteor flying past rang in his ears. Before Luo Chen could breathe a sigh of relief, he heard the sound of the bone umbrella above his head cracking.

He knew that the mana he had hastily converted from his Blood-Refined Divine Astral, without undergoing primordial spirit tempering, could not fully drive this spirit treasure.

Especially—

Luo Chen looked around. The surging chaotic energy contained an even more terrifying power.

It was the cosmic force carried by the countless fragments of laws filling this place.

Even a spirit treasure with its own consciousness could not withstand the impact of such power.

"Luo Chen, where are we going?"

Gu Xun's hoarse voice came from behind.

The Empty Sprout Crystal hut had long been destroyed, and the broken ancient bell had disappeared at some point.

All that protected Gu Xun now were the prayer beads in his hand and the black light constantly emanating from his body.

With the blessing of that black light, Gu Xun's aura surged continuously, but his life force was rapidly draining away.

Where to go?

Luo Chen looked around. In this space, one could not distinguish day from night, there was no direction, and even up, down, left, and right seemed to have been reversed.

Only the intent of destruction filled it.

If before, he had only thought of taking it one step at a time.

But at the moment he reorganized his Chaos Indestructible Body, he had faintly sensed a place outside.

That place was like a coordinate, guiding him forward, preventing him from losing his current direction.

"Follow me closely!"

After saying this, Luo Chen never spoke again.

The three of them trudged forward with difficulty through the space of destruction.

Another period of time passed.

*Rip!*

The moment he heard this sound, Luo Chen knew that the Burial Bone Umbrella had been destroyed.

It must have been hit by a cluster of energy containing metal-attribute laws. There was no chance of saving it; it shattered on the spot.

Luo Chen felt some regret, but there was no time to mourn the loss of the spirit treasure.

He opened his mouth and spat.

A small cauldron flew out, spinning.

The small cauldron emitted a gray light, enveloping him completely.

With the blessing of this gray light, Luo Chen's steps became much lighter, and his forward speed nearly doubled.

Behind him, Fang Qingxue saw this and was momentarily stunned.

Her flower basket had long since disappeared, replaced by another treasure.

The Carefree Realm Master had ruled the Great Destruction Realm for three thousand years and had collected countless treasures.

Even though he had taken the top-tier ones with him, what he left for her were still rare and precious items, some of which were rumored to be heaven-reaching spirit treasures!

After being refined by special techniques, they could also be driven by her natal astral qi.

But the effect...

She instinctively looked back.

She only saw Gu Xun's prayer beads shatter, his black qi suddenly dissipate, and his entire body, like a withered tree, was swept away by the vast currents to a distant place.

Another companion had fallen.

Fang Qingxue felt her steps growing heavier and heavier. As she watched Luo Chen drift further and further away from her, the despair in her heart grew stronger and stronger.

She knew that here, she could only rely on herself. She should not demand anything from others.

Especially since Luo Chen had already been kind enough—controlling the Ninefold Immortal Tower to bring them in, bearing the brunt of the initial impact, and leading the way all along.

But the longer she stayed in this place, the more lost and confused she became inside.

"Can I really get out?"

*Hum...*

The green lamp in her hand trembled, and its flame suddenly went out.

Fang Qingxue felt her steps grow heavy, unable to take another step forward.

Another storm surged from ahead, and she was uncontrollably pushed backward.

Just then.

*Whoosh!*

Five blood dragons roared forward, transforming into a large hand that grabbed her and pulled her forward.

Chaotic gray light poured down, enveloping her. She felt an immense relief.

She instinctively looked up at Luo Chen. He did not say a word, still staring firmly ahead, taking step after step.

In silent soundlessness, the two continued forward.

Time passed, and they did not know how long. The gray light above their heads grew dimmer and dimmer, as if it would disappear at any moment.

When another spatial storm struck, Luo Chen was on high alert, frantically driving the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

A dull thumping sound beat against two uneasy hearts.

"Pfft!"

A mouthful of blood sprayed out, and Luo Chen's face turned pale.

They had managed to survive this wave.

But this time, it was not just one wave of spatial storms.

It seemed they were approaching the exit. The spatial storms outside were erupting wildly. The spatial cracks came like giant nets, making it impossible to dodge.

*Boom!!!*

Another round of spatial storms struck, and Fang Qingxue finally made her decision.

"Brother Luo, thank you."

She voluntarily walked out of the protection range of the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Luo Chen only had time to glance sideways before he saw the young woman swept away by the spatial storm, disappearing from his sight.

He opened his mouth, but in the end, said nothing.

For the sake of the Carefree Realm Master's face, for the sake of the favor Fang Qingxue had shown him when she used her flower basket to protect him upon entering this place, he had not stood by and watched her die. While he still had the strength, he had protected her.

But now, he could barely protect himself. In the end, he could only look after his own future.

What followed was wave after wave of spatial storms. Luo Chen was caught in the midst of them, like a fisherman in a tsunami.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron was pushed to its limit again and again, its gray light constantly blooming.

That was the power of complete laws, enough to resist the law fragments in the chaotic energy.

But human strength had its limits. The Primordial Unity Cauldron was, after all, not yet a legendary Dao artifact.

When the terrifying seven-wave storm had passed, the gray cauldron no longer emitted any light, and a crack even appeared on its surface.

Luo Chen endured the pain and withdrew it into his body.

For the road ahead, he would have to rely on his newly cultivated Chaos Indestructible Body to endure!

Not only that, but he also drove the dormant Vacuum Divine Gu within his body.

Although the Vacuum Divine Gu could now only trigger weak spatial turbulence, unable to directly contend with these spatial storms, it could at least have some effect.

But unexpectedly... the further he advanced, the smaller and fewer the spatial storms became!

It was as if the seven-wave storm before had been the last darkness before the dawn.

"What a pity."

The image of the young woman Fang Qingxue's melancholy face before she left flashed through Luo Chen's mind. He could only take steps forward alone.

And in his mind, that destination became clearer and clearer, as if it were just one step away.

The Vacuum Divine Gu seemed to sense something. It spread its dreamlike starlight wings and carried Luo Chen flying outward.
