# Chapter 1210: Alchemy Talent, Alchemist Xuan Luo

The Five Courts of the Three Halls and Five Courts—though called "courts"—were far from simple small courtyards.

In fact, judging by what Luo Chen had seen so far, the Alchemy Court alone was so vast in area and so broad in scope that it was no less than some ordinary Adept sects from the Mountains and Seas Realm of old.

Three spirit mountains stood arranged in a triangular formation, descending from high to low.

Thick spiritual qi and a complex mixture of various pill fragrances interwove and mingled together. For cultivators unaccustomed to alchemy, entering the Alchemy Court for the first time could even bring a sense of discomfort.

The two mountains on the left and right were respectively called the Spirit Pill Peak and the Marvelous Pill Peak.

Most of the cultivators coming and going within them were at the Initiate or Adept level.

As for the rearmost and tallest mountain, it was called the Pill Emperor Mountain.

From within it, Luo Chen sensed the auras of many Sage cultivators, and even the presence of late-stage Great Sages.

After a brief moment of thought, Luo Chen understood the purpose of this arrangement.

The Plain Spirit Sect was simply too vast.

Even if it was just a single Alchemy Court, the number of cultivators within it numbered in the thousands, and the range of pills involved was incredibly diverse.

Therefore, three pill mountains had been divided.

From the complex pill qi drifting through the air, one could tell that the three pill mountains corresponded to fourth-grade pills, third-grade pills, and second- and first-grade pills respectively.

In this way, for other disciples within the sect, they could know where to seek pills without being completely at a loss.

With Luo Chen's alchemy skill, he could naturally go directly to Pill Emperor Mountain.

But right now, he was "Xuan Luo."

A new disciple who had only been in the sect for ten years.

Everything had to start from the beginning.

However, going up to Pill Emperor Mountain was merely a matter of time for Luo Chen.

He smiled faintly and walked directly toward a courtyard at the entrance of the Alchemy Court.

Step Pill Court!

Inside, the figures of cultivators were bustling about, busy with activity.

As soon as he entered, Luo Chen was stopped by an Initiate cultivator.

"Register!"

"Jade Spring True Disciple—Xuan Luo."

"Realm?"

"Mid Initiate."

The cultivator looked at Luo Chen's face with surprise. Such a young mid-Initiate cultivator?

Even though most techniques of Jade Spring Peak had the effect of preserving youth and maintaining appearance, Luo Chen looked far too young, giving off a strong sense of youthfulness.

In fact, Luo Chen's facial features were deliberately crafted.

To avoid attracting the attention of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, he had abandoned his previous hard, steady, and weathered image, reshaping his facial muscles to appear exceptionally fair, making him look even more handsome.

If outsiders judged only by appearance, anyone would take Luo Chen for a young man who had just started out.

The registering cultivator asked curiously, "Senior Brother, you look unfamiliar?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "I've only been in the sect for ten years. It's normal that you don't recognize me."

"Ten years?"

The registering cultivator was startled, then quickly reacted.

Ten years ago—wasn't that the Entry Spirit Assembly?

Most of the rogue cultivators recruited in that year's Entry Spirit Assembly were still training in the outer sect.

Those who occasionally entered the inner sect were only ordinary inner disciples. No one had heard of any being taken as a true disciple by a peak's powerhouse.

But there was an exception!

At the Entry Spirit Assembly ten years ago, the presiding Sage Yaoyue had personally accepted three registered disciples, one of whom was at the Initiate realm.

The timing matched, and the realm matched.

The registering cultivator's expression turned solemn. He clasped his hands in greeting. "So you are under the esteemed Sage's tutelage. I am Lü Su. Greetings, Senior Brother."

Luo Chen returned the greeting. "Junior Brother Lü need not be so formal."

Lü Su relaxed his expression, revealing a smile. Pointing at the registration book, he asked, "Senior Brother Xuan Luo has come to the Alchemy Court. Are you seeking pills? Or inquiring about medicines?"

Luo Chen was puzzled. "Is there a difference?"

"Of course there is, and quite a big one at that."

Lü Su then carefully explained to Luo Chen.

The three peaks of the Alchemy Court produced large quantities of pills every year.

The first-grade pills produced by Marvelous Pill Peak were mostly sent to the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall to be sold.

The three peaks only sold the corresponding second- to fourth-grade pills.

As long as Plain Spirit Sect disciples had spirit stones or contribution points, they could come directly to the Alchemy Court to buy them.

There was one special point: contribution points had far greater purchasing power than spirit stones!

Luo Chen noted this information.

As for the difference between seeking pills and inquiring about medicines that Luo Chen had asked about, Lü Su also explained.

What was mentioned earlier all counted as seeking pills—just spend spirit stones or contribution points to buy them.

Inquiring about medicines was more troublesome.

This situation usually occurred when a cultivator couldn't find the pill they wanted within the sect or on the market, so they specially came to the Alchemy Court to ask the alchemists inside to refine it for them.

Not only did they have to provide the medicinal ingredients and pill formula themselves, but they also had to pay the alchemist an additional generous remuneration.

Note: a generous remuneration!

In many cases, the value of this remuneration alone was higher than the pill itself.

After all, refining an unfamiliar pill required the alchemist to spend a great deal of energy and time, and even endure many failures, along with the risk of a furnace explosion.

Hearing this, Luo Chen understood halfway through.

Wasn't this just custom pill refining!

He was already well-versed in this matter.

Back when he first returned to the Eastern Wilderness as a Sage, he had done this to make a name for himself.

Claiming that there was no pill he could not refine, he had specially crafted new pills for many Eastern Wilderness Sages, and indeed collected generous remuneration that ordinary people could hardly imagine.

For example, large amounts of Nine Firmament Qi.

For example, Ling Fengzi himself!

After understanding this, Luo Chen felt that he had indeed come to the right place.

With his mastery of many skills, he could gain something whether in the Spirit Medicine Court, the Formation Court, or even the Hundred Crafts Court. But where else could he find as many ways to earn contribution points as in the Alchemy Court?

Under Lü Su's curious gaze, Luo Chen smiled faintly.

"I neither seek pills nor inquire about medicines. Xuan has come to join the Alchemy Court and learn the art of alchemy."

"Ah?!"

Lü Su stood frozen in place, the brush in his hand falling onto the table without him noticing.

Then, he tentatively asked, "May I be so bold as to ask, Senior Brother Xuan Luo, how old are you now?"

Luo Chen answered frankly, "Thirty-three."

"A mid-Initiate at thirty-three!" Lü Su was shocked once again, then said earnestly, "Senior Brother has such talent, why waste your mind on these side paths? You should forge ahead vigorously, focus on cultivation, and strive to form your core as soon as possible!"

Perhaps thinking that Luo Chen was too young and inexperienced, and envied those respected alchemists, he spoke very seriously.

"The Dao of alchemy is vast and profound. Not only does it test one's talent, but it also involves other fields. It is by no means something that can be accomplished overnight. To achieve anything, one must count in terms of decades or centuries. Senior Brother, you absolutely must not scatter your energy, only to end up losing both your time and your resources."

Luo Chen looked at Lü Su with surprise, not expecting him to be so kind-hearted.

In fact, what he said was not wrong. Luo Chen himself had spent a great deal of energy and a long time on alchemy before finally achieving something.

Especially for those with good cultivation talent, there was no need to take this path at all.

Back then, his five-spirit root aptitude had been too much of a drag on his cultivation, forcing him to rely on side paths to assist his training.

Given the aptitude he was currently pretending to have, he really didn't need to learn alchemy.

In the world of immortal cultivation, cultivation realm was the foundation!

Seeing Luo Chen lost in thought, Lü Su breathed a sigh of relief. "Senior Brother, please think it over carefully."

However.

"Junior Brother Lü, rest assured. This decision was made after careful consideration. Taking the path of cultivation to an extreme is not good either. Especially, Master Yaoyue specifically reminded me that cultivating too quickly can easily lead to an unstable Dao heart. That's why I plan to find something else to do—as a diversion from cultivation, and also to have an extra skill to rely on."

"I see!"

Lü Su nodded. Since the other party had invoked a Sage, he naturally had no need to advise further.

After thinking for a moment, Lü Su said to Luo Chen, "Senior Brother, wait for me a moment."

Then he hurried away.

Soon, he came back with another Initiate cultivator.

"Let's go. Junior Brother Li will cover my shift for a while. I'll personally take Senior Brother Xuan Luo to Marvelous Pill Peak."

"Many thanks."

Luo Chen followed behind Lü Su, leaving Step Pill Court and heading toward Marvelous Pill Peak.

On the way, Lü Su introduced the situation of the Alchemy Court in detail.

Pill Emperor Mountain was exclusive to Sage cultivators, where most fourth-grade pills were refined. Occasionally, low-grade pills were also produced there, but they were new creations researched and innovated by the alchemy masters there.

Spirit Pill Peak was where Adept cultivators frequented, mostly producing third-grade pills.

Marvelous Pill Peak was where Initiate cultivators and even Qi Refinement cultivators could access. Unless they had special needs, Qi Refinement cultivators wouldn't come to Marvelous Pill Peak either, but would instead go to the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall to buy first-grade pills.

Step Pill Court at the entrance of the Alchemy Court was a place for handling miscellaneous affairs.

It was responsible for registering visitors, inquiring about specific needs, giving guidance and suggestions, and also providing logistical support for the three peaks.

If Luo Chen were to become an alchemist of the Alchemy Court in the future, he would inevitably have to deal with Step Pill Court.

But becoming a true alchemist was not so simple.

First, one had to undergo a talent test.

"On the way here, I already sent a transmission talisman. Marvelous Pill Peak will arrange for a second-grade alchemist to test you. If you pass, you can truly receive the Alchemy Court's Dao guidance."

As Lü Su spoke, he pointed to a corner of a roof hidden among the trees at the foot of the mountain in the distance.

"We're almost there. It's just ahead."

Luo Chen nodded, but his gaze was sweeping around.

The fire qi here was rich, suggesting that a large amount of earth fire was rising from the earth veins.

Indeed, it was an excellent place for alchemy and pill refining!

From a distance, the two spotted a green-robed cultivator standing at the palace at the foot of the mountain.

When they arrived, Lü Su took the initiative to greet him. "Master He, sorry for the trouble."

Master He's expression was indifferent. He simply extended his hand toward the two.

On the way here, Luo Chen had already learned some of the Alchemy Court's rules.

For instance, when testing alchemy talent, a second-grade alchemist would be responsible, and a certain remuneration had to be paid.

Luo Chen naturally handed over five mid-grade spirit stones.

Master He was stunned for a moment instead.

The remuneration required for the test was only one mid-grade spirit stone.

But seeing that Luo Chen acted as if this was only natural, he accepted it without hesitation.

However, his expression did become gentler.

"In a while, Master Gongsun from Spirit Pill Peak will be coming to Marvelous Pill Peak to refine a new pill for mass production. We second-grade alchemists have all been called over and are quite busy. I'm the only one who could spare the time today. Come in!"

Such a rapid change in attitude!

Lü Su sighed with emotion and transmitted to Luo Chen, "Alchemists are all like this. Most have eccentric tempers. But as long as the remuneration is sufficient, they are easy to get along with."

Luo Chen smiled without speaking.

The two entered the palace at the foot of the mountain.

Suddenly, a wave of scorching heat rushed toward them.

Inside the great hall, more than a dozen earth fire outlets had been opened, and quite a few cultivators were there trying to control the earth fire.

Lü Su explained to Luo Chen, "These people all failed the talent test in the past, but they refused to give up. So after paying a certain fee, they can also receive alchemy training. Actually, I also wanted to become an alchemist back then, but I failed at the first hurdle. And I didn't have enough spirit stones to afford self-funded study like these people with family resources. In the end, I could only take a guard duty post at Step Pill Court from the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall, at least being in the same court as the alchemists."

Luo Chen asked curiously, "So if one passes the test, they can receive the Alchemy Court's training?"

Lü Su hummed in affirmation. "Yes, but the training intensity is actually quite limited. You'll know the specifics once you pass the test."

The two stopped talking and followed Master He into a private room.

"Lü Su, stay by the side and don't disturb us."

"Alright!"

"Xuan Luo, is it? Come here. From these medicinal ingredients, pick out the ones I specify. Both the type and the medicinal potency must be correct."

Cabinets were filled with various medicinal ingredients.

Luo Chen received the list and then stood before those cabinets.

He glanced at the list and roughly knew what pill's ingredients the other party wanted him to pick.

It was Fasting Powder!

What was needed was angelica, ginseng, and some spirit beast meat.

But just under the ginseng category on the cabinet, there were many choices, each with different ages.

Without hesitation, Luo Chen quickly picked out the corresponding ingredients.

Master He nodded.

"It seems you've prepared beforehand. The ingredients you picked are all correct. But lest you go astray, I must say a few words. When we alchemists refine pills, we cannot rigidly follow the book. What grade of pill requires what age of medicinal potency is never fixed. You must learn to match them yourself. After all, the pill formula is dead, but the alchemist is alive."

This guidance was probably given because of the five mid-grade spirit stones.

Seeing that Luo Chen had taken it to heart, he asked again, "What is your spirit root?"

Luo Chen replied, "Gold and fire dual spirit root."

Master He frowned. "Mutually restraining spirit roots?"

But then, he waved his hand.

"Never mind. Although it's best for alchemists to have one of the water, fire, or wood spirit roots, or paired mutually generating spirit roots, having a fire spirit root is barely acceptable."

To prevent Luo Chen from not understanding, Master He specifically explained.

The Dao of alchemy also depended on spirit root attributes.

After all, different spirit root attributes produced very different dharma power attributes.

Water attribute was flexible and changeable, fire attribute made it easy to control pill fire, and wood spirit root had a clear and light nature—all very suitable for alchemy.

But the remaining gold and earth spirit roots were different.

One was too sharp, the other too sluggish and heavy. Neither was very compatible with alchemy, which required precise timing and heat control.

"Proceed to the second test. Let's see how well you control earth fire."

Luo Chen walked to the earth fire outlet, took a deep breath in a show of seriousness, and then began to perform the Flame Manipulation Art.

From the earth fire outlet, wisps of earth fire flew out as if guided.

The earth fire moved like spirit snakes, circling around Luo Chen.

Seeing this, Master He was startled.

"Do you primarily cultivate a fire-attribute technique?"

Luo Chen smiled without answering.

Since it involved the other party's cultivation, Master He did not ask further.

"You've passed the tests of eyesight and handiwork. Now, proceed directly to practical operation! Use the materials you just selected to refine a batch of Fasting Powder within the time of one incense stick."

Lü Su, watching from the side, had already grown nervous.

Alchemist talent tests usually used some ungraded pills.

He had gone through the same back then.

In fact, refining these pills was not difficult. The difficulty lay in the time limit.

Moreover, the eyesight and handiwork tests were not easy to pass either.

Xuan Luo had performed well earlier, but when it came to actual refining with a time limit...

However, what happened next shocked the two.

Luo Chen took only the time it takes to drink a cup of tea to refine a batch of Fasting Powder.

And the quality was excellent!

Master He couldn't help asking, "Have you refined before?"

Luo Chen answered truthfully, "During my time as a rogue cultivator, to save resources, I did refine some Fasting Powder myself."

This time, what he said was actually the truth!

Master He frowned. If that was the case, then today's talent test, Xuan Luo had taken advantage of it, happening to hit upon his test question.

But in the end, he had passed.

Moreover, the basic alchemy techniques he displayed were quite proficient.

This alchemy talent was already quite good!

In that case...

Master He took out a token carved with hazy cloud patterns and handed it to Luo Chen.

"Congratulations on successfully joining the Alchemy Court. Take this token to Step Pill Court to register, and from now on, you can freely enter and leave Marvelous Pill Peak. For now, you are still an unranked alchemist. Once you can truly refine a first-grade pill, one cloud pattern will light up on this token. One pattern means first-grade alchemist, two patterns means second-grade... and so on, up to fifth-grade. Our vast Plain Spirit Sect currently has only one fifth-pattern alchemist, and he is an Ascendant elder who rarely opens his furnace to refine pills."

"After registration, you can claim your first month's quota of ingredients and alchemy tools. Our Alchemy Court's quota is much more generous than other Void Refining sects, with one portion every month. This is thanks to our Plain Spirit Sect's medicinal ingredient business—we are never stingy in this regard."

"For now, you can only learn alchemy in the rooms of the mountain-foot pill hall. Once you become a first-pattern alchemist, you can have your own separate alchemy cave dwelling."

...

At the foot of Marvelous Pill Peak, two figures walked out.

Lü Su looked at Luo Chen with great envy. "Senior Brother Xuan Luo, congratulations."

Luo Chen smiled, tossing the cloud-pattern token in his hand.

With this identity, things would be much more convenient from now on.

Not only could he use it to earn a large number of contribution points, allowing him to browse the Plain Spirit Sect's collection of books and various low-level skills, rapidly accumulating achievement points.

He could also use the excuse of using pills to assist cultivation to explain why his realm would suddenly advance by leaps and bounds in the future.

When Luo Chen eventually returned to the Ascendant realm in an extremely short time, although it would be shocking, everything would have a traceable trail.
