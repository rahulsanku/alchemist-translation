# Chapter 1231: Five Elements Nascent Soul, Yao Xi Arrives

"Devil Lord Heaven-Refining? How much do you know about this person?"

Luo Chen did not continue with other topics but instead turned to Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

Maoqiu blinked. "That ancestor is far too old; I don't know much about him. But according to clan records, Ancestor Heaven-Refining was exceptionally talented. Even at the Ascendant stage, he comprehended over a dozen types of law true intent, astonishing heaven and earth. When he broke through to the Void Refining stage, he was the strongest True Lord! If he could have broken through to Body Integration, he would surely have led our Volcanic Earth lineage to flourish in the Profound Realm!"

"Flourish..."

Luo Chen let out a cold laugh.

How could the Profound Realm, dominated by humans and demons, tolerate the rise of the demon race?

With that thought, Luo Chen vaguely grasped why Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had met such an end.

He did not ask further but focused on what he wanted to know.

"The teleportation formation at the entrance—did you set it up?"

"Yes."

"That formation connects to the demon statue and has the effect of absorbing spiritual power. Quite exquisite. Do you have such formation attainments?"

"Within the Volcanic Earth lineage, there is a formation inheritance left by the ancestor, no less inferior to that of the humans. I, Maoqiu, am a rare genius among the Polo race. Learning a bit of this knowledge was effortless."

The formation inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining?

Li Canghai's figure flashed through Luo Chen's mind. From the fact that the other party had used formations to enter the Ascendant stage, one could see how formidable Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's formation attainments were.

He continued asking, "The soul restriction on this female cultivator—did you place it?"

Maoqiu nodded and asked in return, "Do you need me to remove it now?"

Luo Chen waved his hand, not in a hurry.

Instead, he began to stare thoughtfully at the ink-jade space.

After a long while, he asked softly, "I've heard that Netherworld Demon Moss only grows around demon caves. How did you cultivate such a large patch of demon moss?"

Maoqiu hesitated.

But under Luo Chen's sharp gaze, he couldn't help trembling and instinctively answered, "That rumor is wrong. It's not that demon moss can only grow around demon caves, but that demon caves must have demon moss. Moreover, as the demon moss absorbs spiritual power and converts it into demonic power, it can continuously expand the cave. This demon moss here was stolen from clan seeds and cultivated with secret techniques to facilitate my cultivation."

"Is that its only use?"

Luo Chen asked, a hint of a knowing smile in his eyes.

Maoqiu was startled and pretended not to understand. "Does master know of other uses?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "I never agreed to be your master, and besides..."

Luo Chen raised his right hand. Unbeknownst to him, a patch of ink-green patterns had appeared on it, like a growth of lichen.

It must have been when he used his astral qi great hand to capture Maoqiu that the other party had secretly contaminated him.

Through astral qi and even dharma power, tracing the flow back to affect the enemy's main body—this Netherworld Demon Moss was truly overbearing!

"You were waiting for this thing to take effect so you could turn the tables and kill me, weren't you?"

There was no answer.

Or rather, the moment Luo Chen raised his right hand, Maoqiu underwent a drastic change.

His snow-white fur suddenly expanded, his entire body swelling to hundreds of zhang in size, standing proudly on the earth.

Opening his mouth, he let out a roar so terrifying it seemed capable of fixing the soul and seizing the spirit.

Not only that, but the entire ink-green space trembled under his roar.

In an instant, the earth surged, and countless patches of Netherworld Demon Moss flew out from afar, crashing toward Luo Chen like wave after wave.

Faced with such an attack, Luo Chen stood there quietly, as if frightened into stupidity.

A smug smile appeared on Maoqiu's hideous, massive face.

"Arrogant human, how could you know the might of my demon clan's colonization domain? Through the Netherworld Demon Moss, I have long since fused this entire space with my Sage domain. Fighting me here is seeking your own death. But don't worry—this physical body of yours is so powerful, I wouldn't bear to kill you. I'll refine it properly and make it into the strongest demon king!"

As he spoke, layers of ink-green moss fell upon Luo Chen, beginning to frantically absorb his life force and blood qi.

His originally tall figure seemed to shrink somewhat.

This scene made Maoqiu even more arrogant and pleased, his former cute and endearing demeanor completely gone.

It was as if he could already see the scene of Luo Chen submitting to him not long after.

Suddenly!

The man standing quietly raised his hand, looking at his arm covered in ink-green moss, and spoke thoughtfully.

"So this is how you controlled the other cultivators."

The next moment, before Maoqiu could react.

A long sword suddenly flew up.

It was not Luo Chen's Liu Hong Sword, but the sword of the female cultivator Jiang Huang, who had previously been out of control.

Her sword was already hanging high in the air.

Her dharma power poured out uncontrollably, and a moment of clarity seemed to flash in her dazed eyes, yet she still appeared to be sinking.

But the Heavenly Waterfall Sword Art had already begun to circulate!

Moreover, its power was far more overbearing than when she herself executed it.

A blood-red sword light shot up from below, like a waterfall flowing backward.

Maoqiu stared at this scene in horror and blurted out, "You've already undone her soul control... no, that's not right—you're controlling her?"

Luo Chen did not answer.

He didn't even look at the other's fate. He simply turned around and fixed his gaze on Jiang Huang.

Behind him came the sound of a heavy fall.

In terms of soul attainment, Luo Chen was a master among masters.

A *Soul-Splitting Art* pieced together from Han Zhan's memories had been cultivated by him to the point of perfection.

During his trip to Guixu, in order to conceal his massive soul, he had split it into over seven hundred divided souls—an appalling degree.

After arriving in the Profound Realm, his main soul had returned to unity, his state of mind had undergone a transformation, and he had now reached the pinnacle.

Luo Chen did not know if the original *Soul-Splitting Art*, when cultivated to great completion, could truly break through to the Body Integration stage.

But he knew that his current soul foundation was already infinitely close to Ascendant perfection.

Undoing the soul restriction on Jiang Huang was as easy as flipping his hand.

However!

He had not yet undone her soul restriction.

Because Jiang Huang was still useful.

This demon named Maoqiu, although a Polo demon not skilled in killing, was ultimately a late-stage Sage existence.

His death here required an explanation.

Luo Chen's opportunity also required an explanation.

Thus, a story naturally formed.

Luo Chen's eyes fixed on the woman's eyes. Mirror Flower Water Moon began to circulate—not to impose a restriction, but to construct a lifelike illusion deep within her mind.

"Xuan Luo was captured by the demon, subjected to demonic qi initiation, and formed a Sage."

"You witnessed this with your own eyes. Stimulated by the sign of Jade Spring Peak, you regained a moment of clarity. At the critical moment, a single sword strike killed the unprepared demon."

"Then, your soul was wounded, and you fell into slumber."

Accompanied by the haunting demonic sound, scenes reflected in Jiang Huang's soul.

When Luo Chen lightly tapped her brow with a finger, she closed her eyes and fell into a deep sleep.

After doing all this, Luo Chen breathed a sigh of relief.

Originally, he had planned to find a place to form his Sage and fabricate an excuse about encountering an opportunity.

Now, it was like a pillow arriving just as he felt sleepy.

He leaped up, carrying Jiang Huang, and headed toward the tower from before.

A moment later.

Luo Chen arrived at the foot of the tower.

It was entirely black, with five edges.

If viewed from above, it looked like a black five-pointed star.

Based on the memories he had searched from Maoqiu's sea of consciousness, Luo Chen knew this tower was called the Five Turbidities Tower, a rare spatial true artifact.

It was divided into five levels inside, very suitable for cultivating spirit plants on the go.

The Netherworld Demon Moss here had originally been propagated using the Five Turbidities Tower.

"Quite similar to the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion I once obtained."

Luo Chen sighed with emotion and immediately walked inside.

What met his eyes were large quantities of wood-attribute spirit herbs, including medicinal ingredients used to break through to the Ascendant stage, all surrounded by patches of demon moss.

There were also some shelves scattered around, covered with magic tools and dharma treasures of cultivators, but most had been defiled by demonic qi.

Luo Chen paid them no attention. After settling Jiang Huang on the lower level, he went straight to the fifth floor.

A black five-pointed star formation sat in the center, where all the converted demonic qi gathered.

Its density was no less than the top-tier fourth-order spirit veins of immortal sect sects.

This was a treasure land capable of supporting the cultivation of a late-stage Sage!

Now, it had fallen into Luo Chen's hands.

Luo Chen smiled faintly and immediately sat down cross-legged.

He glanced at the Netherworld Demon Moss that still hadn't fallen off his body and pondered for a moment.

This thing was actually harmless to him.

With the Blood Cloth protecting his body, this demon moss couldn't absorb his dharma power or astral qi for the time being.

Removing it would be as easy as a thought.

But Luo Chen held back.

A thoughtful look appeared in his eyes.

"According to the sparse memories from Maoqiu's sea of consciousness, Netherworld Demon Moss is not without natural enemies. In the demon realm, there is a mineral called Demon Marrow Diamond that can restrain it."

"Demon Marrow Diamond is rare in the Profound Realm, but not absent. It is generated around demon caves."

"Perhaps I can keep the Netherworld Demon Moss on my body and use it as a reason to apply for the Demon-Suppressing Trial?"

With this thought, Luo Chen's train of thought became clear.

Not only did he not remove the demon moss attached to him, but he also diverted a wisp of weak blood qi to let it "feast."

Thus, at least in the eyes of others, Luo Chen would appear to be afflicted by the demon moss, with a close connection difficult to sever.

After doing this, Luo Chen first adjusted his state of mind.

Over the years, in order to allow his state of mind to naturally transform and reach the realm of no self and no sentient beings, he had rarely deliberately suppressed his emotions.

Even when facing Chu Yan, he had ultimately used the Heaven-Refining Technique to indirectly confess his identity.

But hearing about Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had caused some ripples in his state of mind.

Ordinarily, it wouldn't matter.

But since he was about to break through to the Sage stage, even though he had experienced it once before, Luo Chen dared not be careless. He needed to adjust his condition.

After all!

The Sage he was about to condense this time was not the single fire-attribute Sage from before, but a Five Elements Sage!

A thousand-mile journey begins with a single step.

For ordinary cultivators, the Initiate stage determined the general direction of cultivation.

But for Luo Chen now, the Adept stage was merely the foundation, and the Sage stage was about laying a solid groundwork, all for the sake of blooming the most brilliant flower when he returned to the Ascendant stage.

That flower was the Five-Spirit Dao Body!

Thus, Luo Chen was extremely cautious.

It wasn't just his state of mind; he had also been continuously adjusting his dharma power fluctuations.

It took a full three years before he achieved absolute balance in his five elements dharma power, with no deviation whatsoever.

Then, he activated the *Chaos Indestructible Scripture*, absorbing the vast wood-attribute spiritual power within the ink-jade space, converting it into five elements dharma power, and gradually expanding his golden core.

This process was somewhat tedious for ordinary Adept cultivators.

After all, few had to consider such comprehensive factors.

Even five-spirit-root cultivators only cared about whether their internal dharma power was balanced in attributes.

But Luo Chen spared no effort, striving for perfection in everything.

He was no longer the weak rogue cultivator who would stop at nothing to break through realms.

If he could pursue perfection, and now indeed had the chance to start over, then naturally he would do his best.

Gradually, the golden core within him bloomed with five colors.

The five-colored profound patterns radiated brilliantly, penetrating his body and floating around him like heavenly scriptures.

Even his natal dharma treasure, the Primordial Unity Cauldron, which he had sealed, began to stir restlessly.

Time flowed gently in this state of silent transformation.

Ten years later.

Within the ink-jade space.

Clouds suddenly gathered in the sky.

Thunder and lightning flashed within, as if a heavenly tribulation was about to descend.

But the lightning tribulation never fell.

Instead, vast amounts of heaven and earth spiritual power surged in from all directions.

Even outside the space, by the river, at Cuishan Mountain, and in the Gray Triangle region, there was unusual movement of spiritual power.

Some people noticed this phenomenon, but they didn't understand its cause and couldn't find the source.

Only two people's expressions changed slightly.

By the Emerald Stone River.

Two graceful figures stood proudly, one with a frosty expression.

Her usual lazy demeanor was completely gone, replaced by severity and coldness.

Sage Youhuang looked at her friend like this and could only offer kind advice. "In the end, he's just an Adept disciple. You've already done your duty by coming out of seclusion early for him before your realm has even stabilized. As for whether he lives or dies, that's up to fate."

Sage Yao Xi tightly pressed her red lips. "Two hundred years ago, it was like this. At that time, I had just broken through to the Sage stage, and my true disciple Jiang Huang went missing. Now it's like this again. I've just broken through to the late Sage stage, and another promising disciple has fallen into a demon cave. The Jade Spring Peak lineage is declining under my watch. How can you expect me not to be troubled?"

Sage Youhuang reminded her, "Yao Xi, the Demon-Suppressing Trial is ahead. Don't lose your composure."

Sage Yao Xi shook her head. "I haven't known that disciple for long, and our bond isn't that deep. But he is still my disciple, so I want to see him alive or his corpse dead!"

Sage Youhuang said with difficulty, "But the teleportation formation leading to that space has been destroyed. With our abilities, it's hard to force our way in."

"You and I can't, but an Ascendant great power can." Sage Yao Xi looked up at the sky. "Ascendant great powers can control heaven and earth origin qi and find the direction of its flow. They will surely be able to locate the true coordinates of that space."

"Are you really going to ask a great power from the sect?" Sage Youhuang hesitated.

Was this not overstepping for an Adept disciple?

Sage Yao Xi's expression was firm.

If it were anyone else, perhaps. But the achievements Xuan Luo had made over the years were enough to move the sect's Ascendant elders.

Especially according to Liu Muhan, when facing life-and-death moments, the other party had risked his life to protect his fellow disciples. With such character, how could she stand by and watch him die?

Just as she was about to speak, her divine sense suddenly stirred.

"Spiritual power is gathering, flowing to one place, covering such a wide area. Who is breaking through to the Sage stage?"

Sage Youhuang released her divine sense and was also startled, but then showed confusion.

"Judging by the coverage area of the spiritual power, it is indeed a breakthrough to the Sage stage. But why is there no heavenly tribulation descending?"

Sage Yao Xi had no time to answer this question. Her divine sense suddenly fell upon the ancient Daoist temple at the bottom of the river, and joy appeared in her eyes.

"The direction of the spiritual power flow is so obvious, and it leads to that space. Perhaps we don't need to ask an Ascendant great power. You and I can find the exact location!"

"Friend, come with me and lend me your strength!"
