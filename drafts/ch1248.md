# Chapter 1248: Starfire Burns the World, Refining a Void Demon Spirit

The jiaolong-shaped demon spirit screamed in agony as it plunged into the great lake.

Upon witnessing this scene, the seven Nascent Soul cultivators of the Sulling Sect could not help but breathe a sigh of relief.

Secluded Bamboo Sage withdrew the grand formation and looked worriedly at the old man at the center.

"Senior Brother He, are you alright?"

Dean He's aura was severely depleted, his mana nearly bottomed out.

He looked regretfully at the longsword phantom image lying across his knees, which was gradually fading until it dissipated entirely. Although the Plain King Sword was powerful, forcibly activating it with merely a Sage's cultivation was ultimately too much of a strain. What a pity to waste that spirit treasure's split radiance.

He sighed. "I'm fine, old man. What about you all?"

As he spoke, he looked around.

Plow Pavilion Sage and Iron Horse Sage both had bloodstained robes and were in poor condition. To deal with that high-rank demon spirit, they had suffered greatly.

Distant Twilight Sage's face was deathly pale, but fortunately she had no specific injuries.

Hongzhan gripped his magic treasure tightly, his body trembling faintly.

As for Jiang Huang, who had the lowest cultivation, she appeared completely unharmed, merely a hint of terror lingering in her eyes.

Everyone responded in unison.

"Fine!"

"Sorry to make you worry, Senior Brother."

"We are unharmed."

...

His weary gaze swept over the crowd, and Dean He breathed a sigh of relief.

But there was still a trace of grief in his eyes.

"Along the way, we have successively lost Junior Brothers Qiansiu and Shenglin. Plow Pavilion and Iron Horse even bore the brunt of a large number of high-rank demon spirits to cover for me, and now they have no strength left to fight."

"We cannot lose anyone else!"

"From now on, we must not separate. We have to stick together."

Distant Twilight Sage wanted to speak but hesitated. "But..."

Dean He shook his head heavily. "I know what you're worried about. I appreciate Xuan Luo more than any of you. But the situation in the Dark Nether Demon Cave is too dangerous and complicated now. Since he couldn't rendezvous with us from the start, from now on he can only rely on himself."

Distant Twilight Sage bit her lip, her already pale face growing even more somber.

Her good friend Secluded Bamboo Sage patted her shoulder. "Don't worry. Your disciple will be blessed by heaven."

"I hope so!"

Distant Twilight murmured, her gaze falling on the great lake ahead.

The others had already been staring fixedly at the lake even before her.

A demon lair capable of birthing a demon spirit at the Ascendant level was bound to be an excellent one.

If they could get a great cultivator from the Dao Sect to disperse it, during the process of condensing a spirit lair, the amount of spiritual opportunities generated would naturally not be small.

And this concerned their future Dao paths!

But if they hadn't looked, it would have been fine. One look, and they were shocked.

At some point, after the giant waves had fallen, the great lake had not calmed down. Instead, a whirlpool was rapidly forming.

A powerful aura, completely no weaker than that of the previous demon spirit, was madly spreading out.

Whoosh!

Suddenly, the sound of something breaking through the air was heard.

A tentacle, like a roof beam, pierced the water's surface and shot straight into the sky!

The tentacle was extremely thick, covered in dark red suckers, hideous beyond compare.

Seeing this, Dean He's eyes instantly widened in fury as he roared.

"Run!"

No one had expected that a single demon lair would contain two high-rank demon spirits.

Didn't these beings have any sense of territory at all?

Seven figures fled backward without a second thought.

But the aftereffects of the previous fierce battle still lingered, and they ended up splitting into three groups: front, middle, and back.

Dean He was at the front. Even though his mana was nearly bottomed out, his escape speed was still exceptional.

Hongzhan, Distant Twilight, and Secluded Bamboo were in the second tier, their condition relatively well-preserved.

As for the severely injured Plow Pavilion and Iron Horse, along with Jiang Huang, who had the lowest cultivation, they were at the very back.

The three at the back were terrified, even able to feel the fishy wind rushing toward them from the great lake.

Whoosh!

Whoosh!

Whoosh!

One tentacle after another shot out from the lake's surface.

Within the rolling whirlpool, the figure of a giant squid had already emerged at some point.

It stared at the seven fleeing figures, its eyes filled with rage.

Was it because of its companion's death, or because these humans had invaded its territory?

With a muffled roar, several tentacles broke through the air, like spears, aiming to pierce these fellows to death.

Jiang Huang was at the very back, despair rising in her heart.

Just as she was on the verge of death, a sudden gust of wind came from behind.

"Master?"

Jiang Huang looked in astonishment at Distant Twilight Sage, who had turned back.

"Focus on the enemy!"

With only this brief telepathic message, Distant Twilight Sage had already fully deployed her defensive measures, shielding herself in front of her disciple.

At two other locations, Dean He and Secluded Bamboo Sage also gritted their teeth and turned back, shielding Plow Pavilion and Iron Horse Sage in front of them.

Only Hongzhan Sage hesitated for but a moment, then continued to flee at top speed, leaving behind only a single sentence—"I'll go invite a great cultivator from the Dao Sect to come and save you!"

Dean He watched this scene, not knowing what to say, his expression incredibly complex.

Secluded Bamboo Sage stepped forward, and the compass given by the elder grew larger in the wind, transforming into a cylindrical pillar that enveloped everyone inside.

"Everyone, lend me your strength! Use the formation to block the demon spirit's attack!"

Without the slightest hesitation, everyone channeled their mana into the compass.

A thick pillar of light shot into the sky, colliding head-on with the tentacles that came stabbing in.

Bang!

A dull sound rang out, followed by the tearing of paper and silk, one after another.

The six watched helplessly as one tentacle after another slowly but inexorably pierced through the defensive light pillar.

Secluded Bamboo Sage saw this and grew anxious.

If they had been at their peak, jointly activating this compass might not have matched the power of the Plain King Sword to easily kill a fifth-rank demon spirit, but it would have been enough to block an Ascendant-level attack.

But they had just fought a fierce battle against a horde of demon spirits and a fifth-rank demon spirit.

Now...

"I can't hold on!"

The palace-robed beauty cried out in sorrow as the compass in her hand was on the verge of collapse.

Just then!

Crack!

An abrupt sound rang out, followed by a feeling of immense relief.

The crowd stared in shock at the tentacles before them that had pierced the light pillar. At some point, they had all drooped down powerlessly.

Following the direction from which the giant sword had come, they looked up.

In the sky, a young man had appeared at some point.

"Xuan Luo!"

Someone blurted out.

But they were quickly corrected.

"Jiang Huang, that's not Xuan Luo."

Jiang Huang was startled. Looking more carefully, she realized that in her daze, she had mistaken him for someone else.

Although the newcomer was also young, he was slightly shorter than Xuan Luo, and his facial features were completely different.

If Xuan Luo was a handsome, proud young man, then this person was like a weathered seeker of the Dao.

Especially his eyes—they seemed contemptuous, even dismissive, completely lacking Xuan Luo's gentle warmth.

He stood in the air, looking down at the crowd with indifference, as well as at the furious, roaring squid.

A squid emerging from a lake, even if enhanced by demonic qi, was nothing more than...

"An incongruous mess!"

The man spoke heavily, his single palm pressing down horizontally.

As he made his move, heaven and earth stirred.

In the pitch-black sky, a vast sea of fire appeared, with dazzling sparks falling like meteorites.

Within the great lake, the squid demon spirit roared as it extended tentacle after tentacle, trying to resist the heavenly might.

But the endless starfire fell, and upon contact, it clung like a stubborn parasite, impossible to shake off.

The squid demon spirit's massive body was burned in unbearable agony. It rolled and tumbled in the lake, trying to extinguish the terrifying starfire with the water, but no matter what, it couldn't be put out.

Strangely, the terrifying starfire did not spread to other places. It didn't even evaporate the lake water. It only burned the demon spirit's body.

This extreme level of flame control made Dean He of the Sulling Sect widen his eyes in disbelief.

He himself possessed a fifth-rank divine flame!

When it came to fire control techniques, he was confident he was no worse than some Ascendant great cultivators.

He had even been prepared, once the defense was broken, to forcibly activate his fifth-rank divine flame and stay behind to cover the retreat.

But compared to this person, his fire control technique was like the difference between a bright moon and a firefly.

"A great cultivator's methods are truly beyond the imagination of our kind!"

As he marveled, the battle was already drawing to a close.

The squid demon spirit simply could not resist this fire Dao technique that seemed capable of burning an entire world to ashes. Its furious cries of pain were nothing more than the lament of a cornered beast.

With the young man bending his finger and thrusting.

The blood-red longsword shot through the air, plunging straight into the demon spirit's body.

After a moment, there was no more sound.

Dean He took a deep breath, stepped forward, and cupped his hands in salute.

"Thank you, Senior, for saving our lives. We are disciples of the Sulling Sect. May I ask your esteemed title?"

The young man glanced at him indifferently. "The demon cave is dangerous. Do not wander recklessly. As for today's events, do not mention them to others."

With that, he raised his hand.

A flash of blood light shot into the sky, merging into his body. Then, with a single step, he vanished from everyone's sight.

From beginning to end, he never revealed his name, as if saving a few lives was a trivial matter not worth mentioning.

Of course!

Judging from the battle process, he had indeed done it casually, without any effort.

Secluded Bamboo Sage, holding the partially shattered compass, could not help but sigh. "The great cultivators of the Dao Sect have such noble character. I wonder which one he is. When we get out, we must find out his identity and repay him properly."

Distant Twilight pulled her friend back. "That senior clearly doesn't want any trouble. Let's not actively seek him out. If fate allows, we will meet him again in the future."

Dean He nodded, quite approving.

"Distant Twilight Junior Sister is right. Our top priority now is to find a safe place. The situation in this Dark Nether Demon Cave is far too dangerous. It's nothing like the previous Demon Suppression Trials. So-called opportunities are not worth as much as preserving our lives."

The words "opportunities" and "lives" weighed heavily on their hearts.

In just one short year of this journey, they had already lost two fellow disciples.

And there was Hongzhan Sage's shameful act of abandoning his fellow disciples, which made them feel deeply distressed.

Suddenly!

Plow Pavilion Sage's eyes widened, and he pointed excitedly at the great lake ahead.

"Look!"

At some point, the originally pitch-black lake water was turning from ink to green. A vibrant vitality was rising vigorously from the bottom of the lake.

Everyone looked at each other, a single guess rising in their minds.

The demon lair had been dispersed!

A new spirit lair was forming here, which meant... spiritual opportunities!

Dean He made a prompt decision. "Secluded Bamboo Junior Sister, is that compass still usable?"

Secluded Bamboo nodded like a chick pecking rice. "Although it's severely damaged, its basic ability to trap spirits and conceal qi is still no problem."

"Excellent. Let's all enter the lake bottom together and cultivate here. We are sure to achieve something!"

Without further hesitation, everyone flew toward the great lake.

Distant Twilight Sage was also overjoyed. Inside the dispersed demon lair, there would surely be a considerable amount of demon marrow diamonds. Perhaps this time, she could directly remove the demonic contamination from her disciple.

But just as she was about to move, she noticed that her disciple was still staring blankly at the distant sky.

"Jiang Huang, what's wrong?"

Jiang Huang withdrew her gaze. Although there was still some confusion in her heart, she ultimately said nothing.

"It's nothing."

"Don't delay. Hurry and follow me in. Before, we didn't have enough demon marrow diamonds. This time, we will definitely succeed in removing the demon from you!"

...

About a hundred li from the great lake.

Due to the suppression of divine sense in this place, Luo Chen could still sense the situation over there.

Seeing that they had all entered the lake bottom, Luo Chen sighed softly.

"Consider this repayment for the Sulling Sect's kindness to me over the past hundred years!"

A fifth-rank demon spirit was no match for him.

Even without using his martial Dao divine abilities, just a single move of Starfire Burns the World and the Yuantu Sword was enough to deal with it.

He did not take the spiritual opportunities generated after dispersing the demon lair, leaving them for Distant Twilight and the others.

Luo Chen had a clear sense of gratitude and grievance. The Sulling Sect had always valued him highly, providing him with all kinds of resources without stint.

Although it was also because of his outstanding performance, kindness was still kindness.

In the eyes of the Sulling Sect, Luo Chen was a future pillar of the sect, a seedling for Ascension.

But Luo Chen knew that he would eventually leave this small sect, and their cultivation of him would be like drawing water with a bamboo basket.

So he would give them back one, or perhaps more than one, Ascendant!

That demon lair had once been the shared dwelling of two fifth-rank demon spirits. After being dispersed and condensed into a spirit lair, it would inevitably generate a large amount of spiritual opportunities.

The six of them, including Distant Twilight, were not mediocre talents. They only lacked some opportunities for sudden enlightenment.

Among the six, perhaps two or three Ascendant great cultivators would emerge in the future.

"The rest is up to them!"

Sensing that they had already raised a formation to conceal their aura, Luo Chen left with peace of mind.

Next, his target was straight for the central demon abyss!

If a demon lair in the intermediate zone had such abundant spiritual opportunities, then the demon abyss would surely be a hundred or a thousand times better!

If this journey succeeded, perhaps he could not only restore his Ascendant cultivation but also take a step further!

Luo Chen's escape speed increased, drawing closer and closer to the central demon abyss.

...

A few days later.

At the edge of a cliff.

Luo Chen stood at the cliff's edge, having resumed his original appearance, his blood-red robe rustling in the wind.

His eyes emitted a golden light as he swept his gaze over the mist-shrouded world below.

It was like a bloody mouth, silently open, waiting for someone to enter, ready to devour.

Amidst the silence, there was no lack of low growls and roars.

It seemed that countless terrifying high-rank demon spirits were dwelling below.

Just as Luo Chen was quietly observing, preparing to choose a suitable direction to enter, he suddenly pointed a finger at himself.

In an instant, his aura converged to nothing, as if he were just another rock standing near the cliff.

At the same time.

In his eyes, a reflection appeared—a majestic, feathered, horned, and hideous flying beast shot up from the abyss.

Its black wings covered the earth, like the arrival of death itself.

Even though this terrifying flying demon spirit brushed past Luo Chen, it paid him no attention at all.

After emerging, it screeched and flew away from the demon abyss, speeding outward.

Watching the direction in which the flying demon spirit had departed, Luo Chen's pupils contracted slightly.

"A Void Refinement-level demon spirit!"
