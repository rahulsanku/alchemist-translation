# Chapter 1234: Refining the Two Modes Again, Divine Ability Seeds

Regarding Luo Chen and his companion's participation in the Demon-Suppressing Trial, it seemed that Yao Xi had shouldered considerable pressure.

She was their master, yet also one of the participants in the trial.

Having chosen this path when there were alternatives, she bore responsibility not only to the sect but also to the two individuals involved.

If they succeeded, all would be delighted.

If they failed, leaving aside how she would account to the sect, her prestige among the disciples of Jade Spring Peak would plummet significantly.

Luo Chen did not know what considerations she had made.

He only knew that his own objective had been achieved as if by natural progression.

As he walked out of Jade Spring Hall, his expression was somewhat subtle.

If everything went smoothly from here on, there was a high probability that he would break through to the Ascendant stage during the Demon-Suppressing Trial.

No!

It couldn't be called a breakthrough.

It should rather be called returning to the Ascendant realm.

By that time, perhaps the day of his departure from Sulling Sect would not be far off.

After all, Sulling Sect was merely a newly established Divine Transformation force. Its foundation was still substantial compared to ordinary powers, but for Luo Chen, the assistance it could provide would be much smaller.

Especially since the spiritual essence in the Xuan Realm was thin, and the benefits of high-grade spirit veins to Luo Chen were not as great as imagined.

Re-cultivating to his previous realm could still be satisfied, but if he wanted to continue climbing to higher peaks, Luo Chen would inevitably have to seek other opportunities.

In other words, the time he could stay in Sulling Sect was actually not that long.

Looking at the sparse lights on Jade Spring Peak, Luo Chen felt somewhat emotional.

Throughout his life, he had not stayed in many major powers—Luo Tian Sect, Green Parasol Mountain, Myriad Immortals Assembly, Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and even including the Nether Origin Sect where he had spent a very short time, that was only five.

Well, actually, that was quite a lot.

But the number of sect and clan powers he had seen with his own eyes was countless.

Within most of those powers, competition was fierce. If not brotherly harmony, then at least mutual deception and intrigue.

In comparison, Sulling Sect gave him a very good feeling.

There was little malicious competition within the sect; most helped each other, and the elders mostly had a benevolent, kind-hearted demeanor.

Of course, this might also be related to his identity as a genius.

But in any case, leaving such a place truly made him somewhat reluctant to part.

This reluctance showed on his expression, but Wan Jiuzhang, who had come out together with him, misinterpreted it.

"Junior Brother Xuan Luo, I truly apologize. I never expected that the senior sister would return, otherwise I wouldn't have rashly assigned her cave, which had been idle for two hundred years, to you. Having you move out now is indeed my fault."

Luo Chen laughed heartily and waved his hand.

"It's just a cave dwelling. Where has your mind wandered, Senior Brother? What I mean to say is, compared to the senior sister's return, compared to her saving my life, a mere cave dwelling is nothing at all. Once I resolve the matter of demonic contamination on my body, I will definitely study soul-type pills thoroughly and help the senior sister make up for the backlash damage to her soul."

When he said this, his words were sincere.

That Jiang Huang had saved his life was fabricated, but the damage to Jiang Huang's soul was indeed related to him.

At that time, he had used Jiang Huang's hand to kill the Poro Furball, which had led to the soul backlash injury.

Luo Chen didn't know if Sulling Sect had a way to heal this kind of injury, but at least when he had free time later, he would refine a few furnaces of pills to help Jiang Huang out.

Wan Jiuzhang patted Luo Chen on the shoulder after hearing this.

"The Demon-Suppressing Trial is certainly dangerous, but you are all people blessed with fortune. The fortunate have heaven's protection; you will surely return safely!"

"Haha, I didn't expect Senior Brother to believe in fortune and fate?"

"When fortune comes, heaven and earth lend their strength; when luck departs, even heroes are not free. Among us cultivators, who can escape the influence of fortune?"

"True enough. Time and fate—our Sulling Sect is indeed riding a wave of good fortune right now!"

Talking and laughing, the two descended Jade Spring Hall together.

Luo Chen returned to the Jade Stream Cave Dwelling and stayed for his last night.

Early the next morning, he packed up everything related to himself, including the Interstitial Grass.

Actually, changing locations was not a bad thing.

Although Jade Spring Peak had abundant spiritual energy, it was ultimately a place with many people and much gossip.

Over at Alchemy Emperor Mountain, there were usually only a dozen or so Fourth-Pattern Alchemists. Aside from discussing alchemy paths or gathering for specific projects, they mostly had their own cave dwellings and did not stay long on Alchemy Emperor Mountain.

Comparatively, it was much quieter.

Even without this incident, after forming his Nascent Soul, he would have stayed at Alchemy Emperor Mountain to cultivate.

As he exited the cave dwelling, Luo Chen didn't expect someone to be waiting outside early.

It was Liu Muhan.

And not just one person!

There was also Lu Zhenqiu, along with six Initiate-stage cultivators.

They were all fellow disciples who had carried out the mission together in the Ink Jade Space at Emerald Stone River.

They had come, first, to thank Luo Chen for covering their retreat and saving their lives back then, and second, to go together to the External Affairs Hall to submit the mission.

Luo Chen had casually mentioned this matter to Liu Muhan last night, but he hadn't expected her to gather the previous fellow disciples together.

Although somewhat surprised, Luo Chen smiled and dealt with it.

He appeared very calm and natural.

But when Lu Zhenqiu and the others saw the strange demonic moss patterns on his face, each of them felt a deep sense of regret.

Even forming a Nascent Soul at the age of a hundred could not dilute this regret in the slightest.

After all, a prodigy like Senior Brother Xuan Luo, whose future was destined to be bright, forming a Nascent Soul was an inevitable matter.

Although he had now quickly formed his Nascent Soul, the demonic contamination affecting his future ceiling was common knowledge.

How could they not feel sorrowful?

Next, everyone went together to the External Affairs Hall and submitted their respective missions.

The rewards were secondary.

The attention Luo Chen attracted by showing his face, however, caused quite a stir for a time.

Those afflicted with demonic contamination were rare to begin with, and now appearing within Sulling Sect—how could it not attract attention?

This also made Luo Chen alert; he would need to make some slight cover-ups.

Being too conspicuous, whether within the sect or when participating in the Demon-Suppressing Trial later, would not be conducive to his private actions.

...

At the Alchemy Academy.

Inside the Step Alchemy Courtyard, Lü Su was handing over his duties to a newly arrived junior brother.

He intended to resign from this position.

After all, the compensation for these simple daily tasks was not very high.

Previously, when he was at the early Initiate stage, it could barely satisfy his needs. Now that he had cultivated to the late Initiate stage, if he still wanted to achieve the Adept stage in the future, he would need more abundant resources.

That required him to work harder!

But serving in the Alchemy Academy for many years had not been without benefits.

He was now handing over the duties while giving advice to the newcomer.

"Although the benefits of this position are small, it allows you to make the acquaintance of many alchemists. Whether for future cultivation needs or expanding your network, it has great advantages. Therefore, you must never slack off in your daily work."

The disciple listened vaguely, half-believing and half-doubting.

Suddenly!

Outside the Step Alchemy Courtyard, a figure strode in.

Seeing this, Lü Su quickly greeted him and began chatting enthusiastically.

The new disciple stayed behind, watching the two talk happily. Occasionally, Lü Su would point toward the Step Alchemy Courtyard and Alchemy Emperor Mountain, saying something or other.

In the end, that handsome young man with an otherworldly, demonic aura patted Lü Su on the shoulder, handed him a storage bag, then rose into the air and disappeared above the Step Alchemy Courtyard.

Looking in the direction he had gone, the new disciple was stunned.

"Is that a senior from Alchemy Emperor Mountain?"

Lü Su walked back with a pleased expression. Hearing this, he laughed heartily: "Your eyesight isn't bad, kid! But this one is not just an ordinary senior from the sect!"

The disciple looked puzzled.

Lü Su didn't hold back and proudly explained the relevant situation.

In fact, as soon as the name "Xuan Luo" was spoken, the new disciple was already shocked.

He had never imagined that Senior Brother Lü Su could actually have connections with such an unparalleled prodigy.

It seemed that what Senior Brother Lü Su had said earlier was not just empty words.

If one did this Step Alchemy Courtyard position well, there was great future ahead!

"Senior Brother seems very happy. Did Master Xuan Luozi reward you with something?"

Lü Su chuckled slyly, "Not a reward, but better than a reward. Senior Brother Xuan Luo just gave me a task. If I complete it well, then my future resources for breaking through to the Adept stage, and even cultivation after that, won't be a worry."

"What task has such generous compensation?"

"Secret! Alright, I've explained enough. You work hard from now on. I'm off!"

With that, he swaggered away under the new disciple's envious gaze.

...

At the Mountain-Sea Travel Palace, a beam of light slowly descended.

Luo Chen released his divine sense and, aside from the ordinary disciples on daily duty, did not see a single Sage-level cultivator within the palace.

The vast palace was exceptionally quiet.

This was actually the normal state of Alchemy Emperor Mountain, so he was not surprised.

Welcomed by the disciples, he calmly stepped into the palace.

On the way, he had happened to run into a fellow disciple from when he first entered the Alchemy Academy, and he roughly remembered the other's name.

Hearing that the other party was now free, he had a sudden inspiration and gave him a task.

It wasn't actually a difficult task, just somewhat tedious.

The specific content was to help him collect over a hundred types of medicinal herbs.

The range covered was extremely broad, involving various attributes and types, and even included some minerals and demon beast materials.

To ordinary eyes, these medicinal herbs would seem chaotic and disorderly. Even an experienced alchemist would probably not see any pattern.

But Luo Chen had a great purpose for them.

Inside were actually the main ingredients for two types of pills!

One was the Soul-Nourishing Pill, used to treat Jiang Huang's soul damage.

The original Soul-Nourishing Pill was only of the third rank. After he improved and replaced the main ingredients, it was roughly at the fourth rank, effective for Sage-level cultivators.

The other, however, was the Two Modes Pill that Luo Chen had successfully refined once before!

This pill formula came from the inheritance of Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, and its first successful refinement was in the Alchemy Saint Hall of the Nether Origin Sect.

Its effect was special, greatly enhancing a cultivator's true flame, allowing it to grow stronger.

At that time, when Luo Chen underwent the Alchemy Saint inheritance trial, he had successfully refined one, which ultimately benefited the golden fire bird, allowing it to give birth to the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

Nirvana Sacred Flame...

Luo Chen's mouth twitched, and he stopped thinking about that disgusting thing.

In short!

He planned to refine some more Two Modes Pills, specifically to feed to the Withering-Bloom Fire Seed.

If successful, perhaps the Withering-Bloom Fire Seed could truly revive.

He had thought of this before, but at that time, he had just joined Sulling Sect and had little influence. Moreover, the medicinal herbs involved in the Two Modes Pill were too high-grade and too numerous, making it unsuitable for direct refinement.

Now, however, was the perfect time!

He had Lü Su collect them for him, allowing him to use the name of Xuan Luozi freely.

Even if there were some doubts within the sect, with the Demon-Suppressing Trial imminent, no one should come to question him.

At worst, he could bring out the Soul-Nourishing Pill to cover his tracks and brush things off.

Of course, these were all things done casually.

His current priority was still on the path of cultivation.

As his cheap master, Sage Yao Xi, had said, he had only recently formed his Nascent Soul, his realm was unstable, and his techniques were few. He needed to cultivate properly.

But when it came down to it, it wasn't what Sage Yao Xi was worried about.

What Luo Chen needed to do for cultivation was something he had been thinking about for a long time.

Entering his side hall.

As far as the eye could see, the windows were bright and clean, with no dust in sight.

Clearly, disciples regularly maintained and cleaned the place.

Luo Chen first activated the side hall's grand formation, sealing off the inside from the outside.

This way, even if fellow disciples came to visit, they wouldn't disturb him.

Then he found a newly opened medicinal garden, planted the Interstitial Grass, and placed the Vacuum Gu beside it.

Finally, he headed straight for the cultivation chamber.

Feeling the rich spiritual energy, Luo Chen nodded with satisfaction.

"This should barely be enough!"

Luo Chen sat down cross-legged, and his vast divine sense plunged directly into the Xuan Shu Purple Mansion.

In an instant, the five-colored Nascent Soul became incredibly lively.

His gaze fell upon his body, where lines of talisman patterns crisscrossed and interwove, as if forming a robe woven with the principles of heaven and earth.

This was the Xuan Zhang Bao Zhuan!

It had begun to be inscribed during the Initiate stage, was successfully condensed during the Adept stage, and by the Sage stage, it had truly attached itself to the Nascent Soul.

It could assist in cultivation and also be nurtured as a natal treasure.

And Luo Chen's Xuan Zhang Bao Zhuan was different from other cultivators who practiced the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Xuan Zhang*. He had a full five of them, covering the Five Elements!

At this moment, under his control, those talisman patterns slowly peeled off from the surface of the Nascent Soul, finally re-condensing into five Xuan Zhang Bao Zhuans within the vast Xuan Shu Purple Mansion.

The five Xuan Zhang Bao Zhuans floated in mid-air, surrounding the Nascent Soul's main body like heavenly texts.

The various spells inscribed on them numbered over a thousand, vast as an ocean.

With just these five Xuan Zhang Bao Zhuans, Luo Chen could be invincible among Sage-level cultivators.

After all, with this thing, as long as his mana and divine sense could keep up, he could seamlessly cast various high and low-level spells instantly.

But Luo Chen's expectations did not stop there.

Inscribing spells was only a preliminary experiment.

His true goal was to inscribe divine abilities!

Whenever a great divine ability was used, it inevitably caused a great commotion and involved a cumbersome process.

If he could inscribe a divine ability seed onto the Xuan Zhang Bao Zhuan, even if he couldn't cast it instantly, as long as he could save a little casting time, he would be in an invincible position in battles at the Ascendant stage and even higher realms in the future!

This concept was nothing short of earth-shattering.

Presumably, even the original creator of the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Xuan Zhang* would not have dared to have such extravagant hopes.

But Luo Chen, through inscribing a large number of ordinary spells, had already gained some insights.

Especially since he himself was proficient in both talisman and formation paths. Combining the two, it was not a delusion.

Most importantly, Luo Chen himself possessed a considerable number of divine abilities, far exceeding ordinary Ascendant-level great powers.

The first he had comprehended was his self-created Withering-Bloom Dao Finger.

Then there was the water-type divine ability Qi Sea Floating Heaven, which he had obtained from Zhou Yunshen's self-creation in his later years, and the Thousand Rivers Guide left in the three halls of the Nether Origin Sect.

Before ascending, during his journey through the desolate wilderness, he had observed a falling meteor and created a fire-type divine ability, Starfire Burning the World.

These were all divine abilities he was familiar with.

Not to mention others like the Great Luo Heaven Sleeve, the Thousand Skies Converging Stream, and so on.

Although the number was large, Luo Chen was now caught in a happy dilemma.

Which one should he choose as the first divine ability seed to inscribe?
