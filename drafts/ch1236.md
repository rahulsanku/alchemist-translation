# Chapter 1236: Anomalous Dao Body, Golden Erosion Bearing the Dao

Five Spirit Dao Body!

It originated from the Demon-Fall Land, where he had received the Devil Lord's inheritance to wash and transform his spirit springs.

Unfortunately, at that time, due to the conflict between the Nirvana Sacred Flame and the Nine-Refined Beacon Fire, he had been forced to undergo physical nirvana. In a desperate gamble, he had transformed into a Fire Spirit Body.

The Five Spirit Dao Body, which had originally been transformed from his desolate beast flesh and five spirit roots, vanished in an instant.

From beginning to end, Luo Chen had not even enjoyed the slightest convenience this special constitution could bring.

Now, after comprehending the true meanings of the five laws of metal, wood, water, fire, and earth, he had finally awakened it once more.

Counting it up.

This process of losing and regaining had lasted nearly a thousand years!

The hardships along the way, the complexity of the process—it would be no exaggeration to say that hearing this tale would bring tears to any listener's eyes.

But at this moment, Luo Chen naturally had no time for such sentiment. He felt only joy.

And curiosity about the changes in his own constitution.

"It hasn't manifested on the pure physical body, but rather settled on the spirit roots, the Purple Mansion, and even the Nascent Soul."

"The Five Spirit Dao Body and the Chaos Indestructible Body don't conflict. They coexist harmoniously, and the conversion between astral qi and dharma power is seamless."

"In fact, the Chaos Indestructible Body is more like a foundation capable of bearing everything, and upon it, the Five Spirit Dao Body has been derived!"

After careful observation, Luo Chen let out a relieved sigh.

He had genuinely been worried earlier that the Five Spirit Dao Body might conflict with the Chaos Indestructible Body he had cultivated in the Return Ruins Land.

Now, he had found no problems.

But this was also within reason.

After all, the first time the Five Spirit Dao Body had transformed, it had also been based on his desolate beast flesh, combined with his five spirit roots.

The Chaos Indestructible Body was an even more optimized version of the true spirit flesh, built upon the desolate beast flesh!

In terms of grade and inclusiveness, it was in no way inferior to any special constitution circulating in the cultivation world.

Perhaps only the Primal Embryo Dao Body, which in immortal and divine legends was called the Beginning of the One and the Embryo of Heaven and Earth, could barely suppress it.

Now, having regained the Five Spirit Dao Body, Luo Chen was overjoyed and began to experiment.

His understanding of the Five Spirit Dao Body was limited to some records in scriptures.

The most prominent point, described in all scriptures, was the [Cultivation of All Arts Simultaneously]!

The first experimental direction was this point.

Luo Chen first circulated the original *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Mystic Chapter*, and the five-element dharma power within his body naturally transformed.

Then came the wood-attribute *Evergreen Immortal Scripture*, with green light flowing over his body and vigorous life force surging.

Following that, the *Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture*, which he had primarily cultivated for a time, and the water and earth attribute techniques he had some exposure to, all circulated through his body in turn.

His aura constantly shifted, the attributes of his dharma power changing endlessly, without any sense of stagnation from beginning to end.

This alone was enough to confirm that the rumors were true!

The Five Spirit Dao Body could indeed cultivate all arts simultaneously without any loss.

With this alone, Luo Chen's mind was greatly at ease.

Then, he began to conduct further experiments on this basis.

His divine sense probed out and swept across the outside.

The group of Ascendant elders who had been watching earlier had retreated at some point.

Although their presence wouldn't have affected him, this made it more convenient for Luo Chen to act freely.

The second experimental direction began!

On the basis of cultivating all arts simultaneously, he converted the attributes of his dharma power, leveraged the origin qi of heaven and earth, and thereby executed the corresponding law divine abilities!

In an instant, wind and clouds gathered above Alchemy Emperor Mountain, with faint sparks of starlight flickering.

This phenomenon was retracted as soon as it appeared, quickly vanishing.

Inside the palace secret chamber.

Luo Chen's eyes shone as he looked at the starlight sparks in his palm, confirming without a doubt.

Using the dharma power freely converted by the Five Spirit Dao Body to drive the corresponding law divine abilities increased their power by a full three-tenths!

Moreover, it was much easier than the conversion through the *Chaos Indestructible Scripture*, as natural as breathing, requiring no effort at all.

"The experiment is successful. This characteristic can be named [Divine Ability Enhancement]!"

Luo Chen murmured to himself.

He had seen this direction mentioned in some scriptures before, but they were mostly vague.

After all, those who could execute divine abilities were mostly Ascendant great powers.

The books and scriptures he could access back then rarely reached this level.

Now, confirming that the rumors were not false, he felt at ease.

Finally, a rare trace of hesitation appeared on Luo Chen's face.

There was one more direction—should he experiment with it or not?

He had once browsed a fragmentary scroll in the Sutra Library of the Su Ling Sect.

It specifically recorded various special constitutions, their respective uses, and some speculations.

Regarding the Five Spirit Dao Body, the author of that book had a passage, offering a hypothesis.

[The Five Spirit Dao Body originates from the five-element spirit roots, yet it is not limited to five separate heavenly spirit roots. Those who bear five spirit roots have thick dharma power but it is mixed and impure; their strength is great but their cultivation is slow. An ordinary cultivator might only be able to absorb two portions of resources but can display three portions of power. A five-spirit-root cultivator can bear ten portions of resources entering the body, but the displayed intensity is only five or six portions. However, this predicament does not appear at all in those who possess the Five Spirit Dao Body. On the contrary, cultivators with the Five Spirit Dao Body are not only powerful but also cultivate extremely quickly. They are dozens, even hundreds of times faster than others with special constitutions!]

[On this basis, it can be reasonably suspected that the Five Spirit Dao Body produces some unique change, achieving one plus one greater than two. In fact, it might not be simple addition or subtraction, but multiplication. The specific situation is unknown; perhaps it is related to the mutual generation of the five elements?]

Now that Luo Chen had awakened his Dao body, he naturally intended to verify the predecessor's conjecture.

"Mutual generation of the five elements, mutual multiplication of dharma power?"

Luo Chen pursed his lips, and his entire aura suddenly changed.

Five-colored radiance flickered simultaneously.

In his Purple Mansion, the Nascent Soul's eyes were tightly shut, and strands of dharma power were being separated.

Metal qi was killing and desolate, wood qi was vigorous, water qi was pliant, fire qi was blazing, earth qi was heavy.

The five types of dharma power swirled around the Nascent Soul, appearing somewhat active.

But this activity lacked direction.

Luo Chen pondered briefly, then urged the *Chaos Indestructible Scripture*.

In an instant, the five types of dharma power began to circulate simultaneously.

At first, they collided with each other, occasionally causing the Nascent Soul to tremble and him to suffer internal injuries.

But gradually, this chaos moved toward unity, and along a certain direction, it continuously accelerated, even merging into the operation of another type of dharma power.

During this process, the dharma power became increasingly pure and refined, without needing deliberate polishing or tempering.

Meanwhile, in the outside world, a vast amount of origin qi of heaven and earth, without any fluctuation, silently entered Luo Chen's body, joining the operation of the five types of dharma power.

With this internal and external combination, Luo Chen's dharma power began to grow wildly at a visible rate.

Specifically manifested on the Nascent Soul, its infant-like body was continuously expanding!

Suddenly!

Luo Chen opened his eyes, his face full of shock.

He had never experienced such a cultivation speed!

It was like a river bursting its banks and pouring into the sea, or like spiritual energy being poured in reverse, causing his realm to explode upward.

Another metaphor!

This feeling was almost exactly like the heavenly bestowal after each breakthrough to a major realm!

"Is this possible?"

Luo Chen found it hard to believe.

Even refusing to accept it, he experimented again as before.

This experiment took a bit longer, and a strong feeling of distension came from his body, but it was still within an acceptable range.

When Luo Chen opened his eyes again, he was certain that this was the norm!

That is to say, Luo Chen's normal cultivation now was equivalent to the spiritual energy infusion of an ordinary cultivator after breaking through a major realm!

He was certain that this situation was caused by the Five Spirit Dao Body.

But he couldn't believe that cultivators in history who possessed the Five Spirit Dao Body could also achieve such a level?

Luo Chen recalled his previous experience, especially the uncontrollable feeling of distension, and gradually fell into thought.

"Perhaps they also had similar characteristics, which is why their cultivation speed exploded, exceeding ordinary people by dozens of times."

"But they absolutely could not maintain this state at all times; they could only use this characteristic intermittently."

"Because the cultivator's physical body could not withstand it!"

"But I am different. Beneath the Five Spirit Dao Body, I also have the Chaos Indestructible Body, which is sufficient to bear the power of chaos!"

After understanding this point, Luo Chen's mood was greatly relieved, and he couldn't help but burst into laughter.

The sound of laughter pierced through the formation and spread out of the palace.

Like a dragon's roar, it echoed within the Su Ling Sect.

Low-level disciples only thought it was a senior of the sect expressing their feelings, while the Ascendant great powers who knew the inside story believed it was Luo Chen's emotional release after comprehending the true meaning of the laws.

But only Luo Chen himself knew what complex emotions that laughter contained.

Five hundred years in the Return Ruins, like a cage.

It had once brought despair.

But he, who had walked out of the destruction of the world of all things returning to ruins, had forged a martial foundation that ordinary people could not imagine.

Now, this martial foundation was beginning to nourish his immortal cultivation, making his cultivation speed advance a thousand miles in a single day.

It could be imagined that ordinary Ascendant cultivators would need a thousand, even two thousand years to cultivate to the Void Refinement stage.

But he could shorten this time to one or two hundred years, at most three to five hundred years.

In fact, if this speed could be maintained, he had every hope of reaching the Body Integration stage within a thousand years!

This was the result of the Five Spirit Dao Body and the Chaos Indestructible Body adding to each other.

It was also the most beautiful flower that Luo Chen had bloomed after enduring countless hardships!

He deserved it!

The long roar gradually subsided. The Daoist was silent.

In the lonely secret chamber, the green-robed Daoist put away his wild emotions and once again entered a state of cultivation.

He needed to fully master the three major characteristics brought by the awakening of the Five Spirit Dao Body.

Whether it was commanding all arts, divine ability enhancement, or the mutual generation of the five elements and mutual multiplication of dharma power.

This would not only aid his future cultivation but also, for the present, allow him to maximize his gains in the Demon-Suppressing Trial.

...

The date of the Demon-Suppressing Trial was finally set.

It would be in three years.

Before that, the sect began the final "training" for the cultivators participating in the trial.

Sage Yao Xi received the order and then sent Jiang Huang to invite Luo Chen to meet at the Su King Hall.

But when Jiang Huang arrived at the Mountain-Sea Travel Palace, she did not see Luo Chen's trace.

This surprised her somewhat.

After some inquiries, she learned that he had gone down the mountain to the Step Dan Court.

She hurried to the Step Dan Court, only to learn that Luo Chen had met with a Foundation Establishment cultivator and then left in a hurry.

"It should be the Scripture Pavilion of the Transmission Hall. I heard Brother Xuan Luozi mention it casually."

Lü Suo said with a face full of joy.

He had just handed over the task that Xuan Luo had given him before.

In over ten years, although he had not fully completed the task of collecting medicinal herbs, he had gathered most of the items on the list.

Brother Xuan Luo was very satisfied and, according to the previous agreement, gave him generous compensation, which even included experience insights and auxiliary pills for breaking through to the Core Formation stage.

Next, he would have full confidence to sprint for the Adept stage!

Moreover, he would not have to worry about the resources needed for his future Adept stage cultivation.

This was the benefit of running errands for the sect's prodigies.

A little bit that leaked from between their fingernails was enough for ordinary disciples to eat their fill.

Jiang Huang had made another wasted trip, but she was not angry.

She turned and headed toward the Transmission Hall.

Meanwhile.

Inside the Scripture Pavilion, Luo Chen's figure wandered between the seventh and eighth floors, finally stopping before a bookshelf, lost in thought.

At this moment, if one set aside the strange ink-green patterns on his face, his entire aura was balanced and harmonious, without any sharpness.

Clearly, his realm was stable and reinforced.

But he was not concerned about the cultivation of the Sage stage.

His attention was focused on the book in front of him, which recorded "writing."

Yes, the content recorded in this ancient book was not some strange anecdote, nor any profound technique, but merely "writing."

It recorded tens of thousands of types of writing!

Human writing, demon writing, devil writing...

Under each race, it was further subdivided into dozens or hundreds of types.

This was not difficult to understand. After all, even within the human race, there were different factions, each with completely different scripts.

It was just that everyone usually spoke the common language of the Xuan Realm.

As for the demon and devil races, it was even more complicated.

After all, the demon and devil races were collective terms, with countless large and small races within them.

In fact, this book also recorded some special writings created by the living beings of small worlds discovered by the top forces of the Xuan Realm.

The reason Luo Chen was interested in this book was also related to his previous failed experience in engraving divine abilities.

He had sought many methods, all to no avail.

Finally, he attributed the root cause of the failure to the fact that ordinary talisman patterns could only bear ordinary spells, but could not bear divine abilities that could control the great power of heaven and earth.

This was a bit difficult to understand.

But if compared to writing, it was easy to understand.

Take a single character. Perhaps a character from a small demon race with shallow foundations could only bear a small amount of information.

But a character from the human race, with its deep foundations, could potentially contain rich information.

The difference came from historical accumulation and the evolution of humanities and sciences.

The talisman patterns of the formation and talisman paths were merely the ultimate expression of condensed human writing.

Luo Chen suspected that the reason he could not engrave divine abilities with talisman patterns lay precisely in this point.

After all, divine abilities were the Ascendant great powers' understanding of the laws and the Dao, limited by their own cognition. Naturally, they could not use the writing they had mastered to perfectly deconstruct, integrate, and even compress them into seeds.

Therefore, to solve this problem, he might need to switch to a more advanced type of writing that could accommodate more information.

At this moment, in this [Myriad Words Illustrated Guide], he had gained some inspiration.

Over dozens of epochs, the number of Ascendant cultivators who had ascended from the Xuan Realm had become increasingly scarce. There were even rumors that the Immortal Realm had collapsed and the path to immortality had been severed.

To seek a way to survive, the strong of the Xuan Realm had begun the journey of crossing the universe and opening up worlds.

Their main purpose was to find the ascension passage to the Immortal Realm.

In this process, Xuan Realm cultivators had successively discovered some fragmented [Immortal Domains], which further corroborated that rumor.

Of course, for the resources within the [Immortal Domains], the top powers of the major forces had naturally erupted in extreme conflicts time and again.

Over time, some things related to the Immortal Realm had circulated out from those fragmented Immortal Domains.

Among them was a type of writing called [Golden Erosion Script], which was sought after by the major forces.

It was said that this writing carried the ultimate principles of the Dao!

But unfortunately, few could fully interpret the Golden Erosion Script.

Even those who spent thousands or tens of thousands of years of their lifespan could only interpret a part of it, and most of it was kept as an untransmitted secret.

However, even this part had already changed many things.

Whether in cultivation, combat, or artifact refining and talisman making, they far surpassed ordinary people.

Of course, the interpretation of the Golden Erosion Script only circulated among the top forces of the Xuan Realm. For ordinary forces, let alone interpretation, even possessing a few Golden Erosion Script characters was considered a great blessing from heaven.

Luo Chen did not know if the Su Ling Sect had the Golden Erosion Script, but he was inspired.

If he used the Golden Erosion Script to condense divine ability seeds and engrave them on the Mystic Chapter Treasure Slips, would it be feasible?

If the Golden Erosion Script could bear law divine abilities, then he might have hope.

Because he actually knew some of this stuff.

Back in the Return Ruins, the Carefree Realm Lord, in order to let him control the Ninefold Immortal Tower, had personally taught him a portion of the Golden Erosion Script!
