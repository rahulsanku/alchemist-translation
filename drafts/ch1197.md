# Chapter 1197: Complete Origin, Golden Punishment Great Execution

The location of the third ring of the Nine Rings of Annihilation.

Lidust Island.

Surrounded by chaotic energy day and night, the arrangements left by Mu Bugui on the island had long since been corroded away, leaving only a desolate expanse.

On this day.

Two figures crossed the Chaotic Sea and arrived outside Lidust Island.

The two exchanged a tacit glance, then descended. Someone was already waiting on the island.

"Fellow Daoist Gu, Little Sister Fang, it's been many years."

"Actually, it hasn't been that long—just over seventy years."

"Brother Luo, you've been well... Eh!"

Fang Qingxue's words came to an abrupt halt. She stared at Luo Chen in shock. On his body, a faint golden light flickered, yet it was as conspicuous as a firefly in the dead of night.

Gu Xun also noticed it and could sense that this golden light was using a terrifying sharpness to continuously erode Luo Chen's body.

"What is this?" Fang Qingxue covered her mouth, her eyes filled with fear.

She could feel that sharpness—it was far too powerful.

If she were contaminated by it, she would probably be severed cleanly in no time!

Luo Chen gave a helpless, bitter smile. "While wandering the Guixu, I encountered a powerful enemy. This is the mark it left behind."

Gu Xun couldn't help but sigh deeply upon hearing this.

"Is the Guixu already so dangerous now? Someone as strong as you actually suffered such a severe injury."

Luo Chen waved his hand with a smile. "Though it was indeed dangerous, it wasn't quite to that extent. This enemy originated from the Ancient God Dao Arena."

Hearing the name Ancient God Dao Arena, the two immediately perked up and asked about the general situation.

Luo Chen concealed nothing and frankly recounted the whole story.

When they heard that over a hundred Martial Dao Human Immortals had gathered there, the two were filled with amazement.

The assembly of the four Annihilation Kings was awe-inspiring.

But the fact that a Great Vehicle True Spirit had crossed over, intending to destroy the evil god, only to be hunted down by the Guixu martial artists working together—this left them utterly dumbfounded.

The legendary feat had truly occurred.

And the man before them was one of the survivors of that battle!

The way the two looked at Luo Chen had completely changed.

It was no longer just the camaraderie of fellow cultivators, but also a trace of respect for a strong one.

They could also sense that although the golden light on Luo Chen's body was extremely dangerous, it also prevented him from concealing his own aura. The Blood Refining Divine Astral that constantly washed against the golden light appeared even more formidable, surpassing when he had left.

It seemed that during this journey, Luo Chen must have gained a great deal, advancing his realm even further.

Fang Qingxue clicked her tongue in admiration, then a look of worry appeared on her face.

"If what Brother Luo says is true, the outcome of that battle was extremely tragic. Many died, and the survivors were also injured. Does my ancestor still have a chance to regain control of the Divine Mother Hall?"

Luo Chen pondered for a moment, thinking of the nearly one-third of a True Spirit's flesh within the Primordial Unity Cauldron, and finally nodded heavily.

"There should still be a chance!"

Fang Qingxue sighed, unsure if Luo Chen was comforting her or if it was truly the case.

But for now, she could only pray for her ancestor, because even she herself had to face the grim reality a hundred years from now.

Luo Chen changed the subject and asked about the specific situation of the Nine Rings of Annihilation after he had left.

Although he had left behind a Martial Dao Deity before departing, that deity was mainly used to control the formation and maintain the Ninefold Immortal Tower. He knew very little about the specifics of the Nine Rings of Annihilation.

Fang Qingxue gave Luo Chen a detailed introduction.

Since his departure, the environment of the Nine Rings of Annihilation had become increasingly harsh.

An even greater amount of chaotic energy continuously poured down from the first ring, greatly affecting the cultivation environment of the original nine-ring martial artists.

Many martial artists who couldn't adapt were severely injured in the initial impact.

Now, over seventy years later, the number of Leakless Realm martial artists originally inside the tower, which had been several hundred, had dwindled to less than a hundred.

Hearing this, Luo Chen nodded solemnly.

In fact, before entering the tower, he had already glimpsed a clue from the outward overflow of chaotic energy.

If it was that dangerous outside, the situation inside the tower would only be worse.

"The cycle of the era is turning. The Great Annihilation Era is right before us."

Fang Qingxue sighed, then perked up. "But misfortune and blessing depend on each other. Those Leakless Realm martial artists who endured have benefited greatly. In particular, a few of them have made great strides in their realms, and are only one opportunity away from breaking through to the Human Immortal realm."

"Oh?"

Luo Chen became interested.

Without waiting for Fang Qingxue to name names, he actively released his Martial Dao Divine Intent, directly descending upon the middle three rings.

The sheer magnitude of this will startled Gu Xun and Fang Qingxue, who were right beside him.

Between each of the Nine Rings of Annihilation, due to the saturation of chaotic energy, there was a great separation, especially between the upper three rings and the lower six.

Descending upon the lower six rings with Martial Dao Divine Intent was something ordinary Martial Dao Human Immortals could not do.

Even though the two had successively broken through in their realms over the years, it was still difficult for them to achieve such a feat.

Only the Carefree Realm Master of the past could easily do so.

Luo Chen's current performance was already on par with the level of an Annihilation King.

Ignoring their surprise, Luo Chen quickly locked onto the few people Fang Qingxue had mentioned.

"All old acquaintances!"

Ice Heaven Son, Barbaric Wild Martial Sect, Martial Fanatic Xiao Lin!

These three were the closest to the Human Immortal realm. Their Martial Dao Divine Intent was growing increasingly dense, and they were only one opportunity away from blooming and bearing fruit.

As for the older generation of Martial Sects, who were more familiar, they had become increasingly desolate.

Luo Chen did not see Thousand Illusions, Extreme Yang, or Shepherd Cloud—these Martial Dao Grandmasters. They had likely already fallen due to the limits of their lifespans.

Luo Chen did not disturb those three and actively withdrew his divine intent.

Fang Qingxue spoke at the right time. "If these few can break through before the cycle of the era turns, they might become companions for us to forcefully charge through the Annihilation Gate."

"Let's hope so!"

Luo Chen nodded noncommittally.

After some further exchange, Gu Xun and Fang Qingxue took their leave, not wanting to disturb him further.

They could all see that Luo Chen's current condition was not optimistic and that he needed to recuperate. This meeting, which had allowed them to understand the situation outside the Great Annihilation Realm, was already a gain.

Watching their departing figures, Luo Chen let out a faint sigh.

Even if Xiao Lin, Ice Heaven Son, and the others could break through, so what?

In the end, they would only be newly ascended Martial Dao Human Immortals.

Even Gu Xun, a long-established Human Immortal, was at most on par with Yuan Qing in his normal state.

Within the Great Annihilation Realm, the only one he was relatively optimistic about was Fang Qingxue.

It wasn't just because she was young, but most importantly because of her strength.

This woman, having cultivated in the harsh Chaotic Sea, had now reached the late Human Immortal realm, comparable to the backbone martial artists of the Ancient God Dao Arena battle.

Moreover, she had previously received extensive guidance from her ancestor, the Carefree Realm Master, and surely had some trump cards up her sleeve.

The strength of the Carefree Realm Master, Luo Chen had witnessed firsthand.

The battle at the Ancient God Dao Arena!

Over a hundred Martial Dao Human Immortals charged fearlessly, the four Annihilation Kings struck together—it was truly tragic.

Among them, Corpse Old Demon and Heaven Cry Old Monster were quite troublesome, and Heavenblack Lie Su had the best record, severing the Golden Punishment Sovereign's physical body with a single halberd strike.

But the one with the greatest true strength was undoubtedly the Carefree Realm Master.

He was the first to drag the Golden Punishment Sovereign in, then held the line against him head-on, and finally used the strange treasure, the Ancient Mirror, to trap the Golden Punishment Sovereign's primordial spirit.

Such ability was truly unfathomable.

If measured by immortal cultivation standards, even a Void Refining True Lord might not be able to achieve such a feat.

This also forced Luo Chen to re-evaluate the comparison between martial arts realms and immortal cultivation realms.

The early and even middle stages of the Annihilation Realm should be roughly equivalent to a normal Void Refining True Lord.

But after entering the late Annihilation Realm, their strength was probably close to so-called Body Integration Great Sovereigns.

Especially old monsters like the Carefree Realm Master, who had guarded the Annihilation Gate for three thousand years—they were simply immeasurable.

"What a pity that time is not on my side. Otherwise, with the Primordial Unity Cauldron, I could cultivate before the Annihilation Gate for a few thousand years. Perhaps I could truly attempt to break through to the Nirvana Realm, which all martial artists dream of, and become the legendary Creator Lord!"

The seventh martial arts realm, Nirvana—opening a space within the body, containing martial arts creation within, also revered as the Creator Lord!

At that step, one would truly reach the pinnacle of martial arts.

Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath, waking from his unrealistic fantasies.

He seemed not to see the harsh environment around him, focusing all his energy on his own physical body.

The top priority was to heal his injuries!

Otherwise, with this injury on him, he wouldn't even qualify to enter the second ring for cultivation.

If his physical body was no longer leakless, chaotic energy would seep in through every pore. Even with the protection of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he would only end up with the regretful outcome of his body dying and his dao vanishing.

Everything else could be put aside for now.

Luo Chen casually chose a spot and sat down cross-legged.

He concentrated on the streak of golden light on his body.

Under the impact of the Blood Refining Divine Astral, the golden light's domineering posture remained undiminished.

One had to know that his Blood Refining Divine Astral was formed by fusing the Nine Tribulations Earthly Balefulness, the Unforgiving Heavenly Wind, and even Source Blood. After several hundred years of cultivation, it was extremely powerful.

This golden light almost completely ignored it.

"A complete law origin?"

Luo Chen murmured to himself.

He had already determined the attribute of the Golden Punishment Sovereign's law power—it was the metal system.

The metal origin within it was exceptionally sharp, continuously cutting and tearing apart the Blood Refining Divine Astral.

What truly blocked the golden light's continuous erosion was the Martial Dao Divine Intent contained within the astral energy!

He had been busy traveling before, but now that he was still, he had the energy to think.

Law origin was the goal of immortal cultivation.

Martial Dao Divine Intent, on the other hand, was the final product of martial arts cultivation.

The two should have been parallel products, as evidenced by the fact that Martial Dao Divine Intent could resist the soul attacks of cultivators.

This was also the main reason why Luo Chen's vast divine intent could resist the further erosion of the golden light.

But Luo Chen could only defend bitterly. This was even under the condition that the law power was greatly suppressed in the Guixu.

The reason for this situation was that the Golden Punishment Sovereign's origin power was not as weak as Luo Chen's initial mastery at the Ascendant realm. His metal origin had already reached great success—it could be called complete!

Therefore, Luo Chen could only defend, but could not eliminate it.

If that was the case...

Luo Chen's eyes narrowed slightly. He didn't know how the Carefree Realm Master and the others resolved such injuries, but he already had a rough plan.

"The Primordial Unity Cauldron!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath and, under the condition of no one watching, slowly spat out the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

With a pinch of his finger seal, he carefully activated the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

A faint gray halo spread out, attaching itself bit by bit to the golden light, then trying to contaminate, decompose, and devour it.

This process was extremely slow, but unwavering.

After a full three days and three nights, Luo Chen's spirits lifted.

"It's working!"

He could feel that the terrifying sharpness on his body had diminished by a fraction.

This also verified the feasibility of his method.

"The current Primordial Unity Cauldron is no longer an ordinary magic treasure or true artifact, nor even a spirit treasure. It already has the prototype of a dao artifact."

"Even something as complex as chaotic energy can be refined, and even law fragments can be absorbed bit by bit."

"A complete law origin, though troublesome, can be gradually removed as long as I persevere."

While relieved, Luo Chen did not completely relax.

As always, it came down to time!

At present, time was far too tight for him.

Healing his injuries alone was not enough; he also had to make sufficient preparations for leaving the Guixu.

"I need to speed up."

Muttering to himself, Luo Chen began to fully mobilize the Primordial Unity Cauldron, increasing the speed of devouring the golden light.

...

Time passed quickly.

The changes within the Guixu were different every day.

The erosion of the Great Annihilation Realm was becoming increasingly severe.

The situation inside the Ninefold Immortal Tower was also growing worse.

When another century's deadline arrived, the Martial Dao Deity that Luo Chen had left in the formation's core opened the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

The martial artists inside were eager to leave, while the Leakless Realm martial artists outside wanted to take refuge inside.

These two conflicting sentiments created a strange atmosphere in Carefree City.

Inside the tower, however, there was good news.

Another Martial Dao Human Immortal had been born.

It did not deviate from Gu Xun and Fang Qingxue's expectations. The one who ascended was not the deeply experienced Barbaric Wild Martial Sect, nor the talented Ice Heaven Son, but the Martial Fanatic Xiao Lin!

He began martial arts at seven, reached Vajra at twelve.

Entered the Furnace Realm before a hundred, and at two hundred, entered the Nine Rings of Annihilation for cultivation as a newly ascended Leakless Realm martial artist.

Four hundred years later, at the age of six hundred, he achieved the Martial Dao Human Immortal realm!

This cultivation speed was not considered fast, but there were almost no bottlenecks in the process.

If not for the lack of guidance from top experts like the Carefree Realm Master, his speed would likely have been even faster.

One could only say that he was worthy of being the descendant of the Immortal Remnant Xiao Lan!

When he ascended to the third ring, only Gu Xun and Fang Qingxue went to welcome him. Luo Chen did not attend.

Because at this time, Luo Chen was at a critical juncture.

He was only one step away from completely removing the golden light from his body.

On Lidust Island, Luo Chen was drenched in sweat. A huge wound on his chest and abdomen almost ran through half his body.

And the golden light within, like a stubborn bone-attached maggot, was being slowly pulled out.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron hovered above his head, spinning rapidly.

"Get out!"

A low roar, suppressed to the extreme, suddenly rang out. Luo Chen's astral energy shook with a bang.

That faint golden light finally floated out slowly.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron cast down gray light, grinding and absorbing it bit by bit.

Luo Chen pressed a hand to his body. Flesh and blood squirmed as it healed rapidly. His gaze fixed on the gradually dispersing golden light, and he felt an indescribable ease throughout his body.

Just then, before the golden light completely dissipated, a sharp yet chaotic sensation rushed toward his head.

"A soul attack?"

Luo Chen was startled.

But within his sea of consciousness, a reaction had already occurred.

His main soul, which had been feigning sleep for many years, suddenly opened its eyes and spat out a thunderous sound toward the void.

"Roar!"

The thunder roar was soundless, but it stirred up a thousand layers of golden waves in the sea of consciousness.

That soul attack finally dissipated.

At this point, the threat that the Golden Punishment Sovereign had brought to Luo Chen vanished completely.

Luo Chen hadn't expected that the Golden Punishment Sovereign's divine ability still contained a faint trace of a soul attack.

Fortunately, it wasn't a Great Vehicle cultivator, but a Great Vehicle True Spirit.

Otherwise, just this soul attack alone would have been enough to be unbearable.

While rejoicing, Luo Chen suddenly froze.

Within his sea of consciousness, his main soul, which should have continued to sleep, also revealed a thoughtful expression.

"Golden Punishment Great Execution?"
