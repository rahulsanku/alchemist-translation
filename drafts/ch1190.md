# Chapter 1190: Withered Bone Bodhi, Stars Fall Like Rain (Happy New Year!)

The setting sun cast its glow westward, and white bones paved the road.

Upon the earth, two figures trudged forward in silence.

Their pace was not fast; they had not even activated their astral qi, simply measuring the land with their own two feet.

The green-faced man muttered to himself, brows furrowed, expression tangled, as if troubled by some problem.

He wished to ask for guidance and instinctively looked toward the white-robed man beside him.

But what he saw was—

The white-robed man held a bone slip in his hands, his expression utterly absorbed, as if completely immersed in its contents.

"Forget it. Luo Chen has already told me the tricks; it wouldn't be right to pry further. I'll figure it out myself!"

The green-faced man sank back into contemplation, occasionally testing a move to verify his thoughts.

Beside him, Luo Chen was wholly immersed in a demonic cultivation technique called the *Pure World Withered Bone Bodhi Method*.

*[Use the bones of all beings as fuel, burn karma-fire to forge immortality;*
*Cross nine deaths to become withered bone, only then realize birthless Bodhi!]*

It was utterly different from the *Demon Aspect Art*, which also originated from the demon race.

This demonic technique had clearly been improved by someone, incorporating Buddhist concepts.

Even if those concepts were completely contrary to Buddhist philosophy.

This was evident from the many realms divided within the technique—there was not a trace of Buddhist compassion or kindness.

"Peel away the skin, let the white flesh fall, and draw the Nether Earth Fire to burn away all the flesh and blood of the body, leaving only the skeleton to bear the karma. As the skin and flesh peel away, chant the *Birth-Burying Curse*, using Buddhist power and karma power to temper the bone patterns."

"Devour the marrow, plant the golden lotus; bore open your own vertebrae, implant a resentful spirit Bodhi seed, draw resentful qi to irrigate it, and in the marrow sea, a pitch-black bone lotus will grow..."

"A thousand skeletons gather a hundred impurities; withered bone gives birth to Bodhi, until the pure world has no living beings!"

Luo Chen read for a long time, only then finishing this peculiar technique word by word.

After reading, Luo Chen's feelings were subtle.

This was not a qi-refining technique, nor was it a simple body-tempering technique.

It wasn't even particularly tied to the racial differences between humans and demons.

Every single realm's cultivation was extremely cruel.

Cruel to all beings, and cruel to oneself!

Yet only within such extreme pain could one transform karma and Buddhist power, empowering oneself with great strength.

And on this foundation, one could bear the faith of all beings and absorb the divine power of incense!

Thus!

This was a technique for cultivating the divine path of incense and faith!

Only upon reaching the realm of "withered bone gives birth to Bodhi" could one begin to absorb the power of incense and faith.

Then...

Luo Chen stroked his chest.

"Is this rib, which feels like jade, an immortal bone, a demon bone, or a Bodhi bone cultivated by Corpse himself?"

This thought flickered and vanished.

Then, he focused his attention on the *Pure World Withered Bone Bodhi Method*.

Clearly, aside from the content on the divine path of incense and faith, other parts of this book held great value for him.

As the split will of Corpse had said, the *Demon Aspect Art* that Luo Chen had previously drawn from should also originate from some great clan of the demon realm.

And based on Luo Chen's understanding of the Primordial Demon Sect's history, it was very likely the Blood Demon clan.

Because among the three lineages of the Primordial Demon Sect, the Primordial Demon lineage cultivated qi, the Soul-Refining lineage cultivated the soul, and only the Blood Sea lineage involved some body tempering.

Since both Blood Demons and Bone Demons belonged to the demon race, their corresponding body-tempering methods should naturally share commonalities.

"Peel skin, fall flesh; devour marrow, plant lotus; a thousand skeletons gather impurities; withered bone gives birth to Bodhi..."

Luo Chen silently recited these names, the specific cultivation methods constantly flowing through his mind.

Gradually, an overall concept emerged.

If viewed from the perspective of martial body tempering, this entire process was about destroying one's own physical body, leaving only the core parts. It seemed to run counter to the martial path of pursuing a complete and powerful body.

But what if all these processes were integrated into a single realm?

For instance, from the Human Immortal realm to the Destruction realm!

Wasn't this also a process of pursuing the unity of astral qi and the physical body?

A stage of breaking down to rebuild, of being undying and indestructible.

This concept was also present in the *Demon Aspect Art*.

What it described was transforming the body into a blood sea, incarnating into billions. As long as the blood sea was not destroyed, the human form would endure; a drop of blood could be reborn, a speck of dust could be immortal!

"Withered bone Bodhi, pure world indestructible."

"Blood sea eternal life, speck of dust immortal."

"If I could combine the two, supplemented by my True Spirit physical body, and merge the three into one..."

The more Luo Chen thought, the more excited he became, vaguely glimpsing a grand path of martial arts that led to the heavens.

If he succeeded, perhaps he could break through the bottleneck and directly enter the Destruction realm!

"But how exactly should I do it?"

After the excitement came a feeling of bewilderment, a thousand threads with no starting point.

He turned his head to look at his traveling companion, Yuan Qingzang, wanting to seek his advice.

At the same time, the other party also looked over with a troubled expression, clearly still vexed by the three-style fusion that Luo Chen had taught him.

Meeting each other's eyes, Luo Chen couldn't help but laugh.

When it came to martial arts knowledge, Yuan Qingzang had probably shared most of what he knew. Now, he was likely far inferior to Luo Chen.

Seeking his advice would be like playing the lute to a cow.

Or rather, in the current Great Destruction realm, the number of people who could guide Luo Chen in martial arts concepts was pitifully few.

Especially in the area of creating one's own techniques.

"Forget it. The path is long and arduous, but by walking, one arrives. Since I've found the direction, I can always polish it out. Besides, I still have the attribute panel in hand."

Luo Chen exhaled a breath of turbid qi and looked into the distance.

"How much further to the Sea of Stars?"

Hearing Luo Chen speak suddenly, Yuan Qingzang was startled for a moment, then naturally replied, "Almost there. We'll arrive soon."

"Is that so..."

Luo Chen murmured, a look of anticipation in his eyes.

The Sea of Stars, the place where stars fall.

He wanted to know what kind of place could deserve such a name.

---

It was soon!

Half a year after leaving the Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain, they reached their destination.

The Sea of Stars!

Though called a sea, there was no ocean.

Instead, the land was barren and desolate.

Everywhere they passed, huge craters dotted the wilderness, a shocking sight.

Those were meteorite craters!

Pits smashed out by falling stars.

Occasionally, there were remnants of stars, but they held no trace of life.

Perhaps the only beautiful scenery in this region was the brilliant starry sky after nightfall!

Under the night sky.

Luo Chen stood with his hands behind his back at the edge of a meteorite crater, looking up at the starry sky.

In his entire life, he had never seen such a beautiful sight.

Brilliant stars hung overhead, so close they seemed within reach.

So close, and so beautiful.

So beautiful that one dared not speak loudly, for fear of startling the immortals within the stars.

But in reality, everyone knew there were no immortals in those stars, not even normal living beings.

In fact, no one knew when these stars might fall.

"Will they really fall?" Luo Chen asked softly.

Yuan Qingzang nodded. "Yes. These stars are already on the verge of breaking apart, and they are so close to the Guixu. From time to time, they fall. It is said that when all things return to the Guixu, even the three suns will extinguish."

Luo Chen was silent.

Faced with such cosmic power, even someone as strong as him felt a trace of insignificance.

Yuan Qingzang looked around and sighed. "Though the falling stars are beautiful, they are still not as impressive as the powerful energy released when a star crashes to the ground and disintegrates. This energy is unique to this region, which is why someone established a Dao tradition here, passed down through generations."

"You mean the Myriad Manifestations Star Sect?"

"Yes."

The Myriad Manifestations Star Sect—a name that sounded ordinary.

If placed in the Mountains and Seas Realm, with its myriad of sects, it would be nothing special.

But in the Guixu, it was quite unique.

The beings here either huddled together in tribes for warmth, or used fortified cities as barriers for survival. The powerful ones, like Corpse, walked alone.

Only in the Sea of Stars was there a sect.

Luo Chen didn't know the specifics of their tradition, but since they were considered one of the three major human settlements, alongside the Great Destruction realm and the Heavenly Wind Tribe, they must have their unique qualities.

Seeing Luo Chen's eager expression, Yuan Qingzang had already guessed his intentions and couldn't help but show a helpless look.

"Are you going to take a look again?"

"Naturally!"

Luo Chen said it as a matter of course.

There was an unspoken implication in his words—he had been thinking about the follow-up technique for the *God-Devil Formless* all this time.

Thinking while traveling was too inefficient.

Having a stable place to stay would be more conducive to opening up his thoughts.

This time, Yuan Qingzang did not dissuade him.

He had witnessed Luo Chen's martial arts talent in fusing three techniques into one. He had seen Luo Chen's astonishing power in directly receiving Corpse Immortal's finger. With such an unrestrained and arrogant mindset, he naturally knew he couldn't dissuade him.

Besides.

Even if the Star Sect's master, Tianxuan Liesu, were present, he would probably treat Luo Chen with courtesy.

However, something that disappointed him, yet also brought relief, occurred.

Tianxuan Liesu was not in the sect!

Not only that, but in the entire Star Sect, there wasn't a single Martial Human Immortal.

Those who welcomed the two of them were a group of Leakless realm martial artists.

"The ancestors all left a hundred years ago and have not sent back any news since. Even my master, who had just reached the Human Immortal realm, followed in their footsteps."

The newly appointed Star Sect master said this.

Facing the Star Sect master, who handled himself with grace and propriety, Luo Chen did not make things difficult. He simply made a request.

"Where is the best place to observe the stars?"

The current Star Sect master hesitated for a moment, but facing the powerful Luo Chen and his companion, he ultimately issued a personal invitation.

"Senior, this way."

Passing through pavilions and towers, crossing layer upon layer of stairs.

For a moment, it felt as if he had returned to the Luo Tian Sect in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

It wasn't until all the buildings had disappeared beneath his feet and he stepped onto a high platform where the wind howled fiercely, facing the stars hanging directly overhead, that Luo Chen snapped back to reality.

"This place is called the Ziwei Enclosure, the beginning of the Myriad Manifestations, the head of all stars. It was where the ancestor used to observe the stars. Beside it are the Taiwei Enclosure and the Tianshi Enclosure. If Senior has any needs, you may command us from those two places."

Luo Chen stood on the high platform, surveying the view, quite satisfied.

"This will do!"

The Star Sect master breathed a sigh of relief and immediately took his leave.

After he left, Yuan Qingzang couldn't help but click his tongue in admiration.

"As expected of a martial artist trained by a sect. Though his realm is not high, he still faced us with grace and didn't immediately start shouting about fighting and killing."

He seemed to recall his own earlier behavior, as well as that of Lü Pao'er from the Sorrowful Weeping Abyss. When they first met Luo Chen, they had both thought of fighting first to establish their authority before talking.

That was the normal law of the jungle.

But in front of an extraordinary powerhouse like Luo Chen, it was an unwise move.

Luo Chen smiled, also very approving of the Myriad Manifestations Star Sect's achievements in teaching their disciples.

But this smile dimmed instantly when he thought of the Guixu.

If this were the outside world, with such disciples and such a sect culture, the Myriad Manifestations Star Sect would surely endure for a long time.

But this was the Guixu!

"So, whether it was the ancestor Tianxuan Liesu or the group of Martial Human Immortals, they all left the sect before the great calamity to seek their own way out."

With a sigh, Luo Chen took out a cushion, placed it on the high platform, and immediately sat down cross-legged.

"Alright, go on your way. I have some matters to attend to."

Yuan Qingzang didn't linger either; he still had to continue studying the martial divine ability of the three-style fusion.

After he left, Luo Chen adjusted his mindset slightly.

Then, he opened the attribute panel.

His gaze slid down until it reached the achievement points column.

**[200]**

A very neat number.

Previously, when he first deduced the *God-Devil Formless*, it had cost thirty-five points, causing the number to drop.

But during the two hundred years of cultivation in the Destruction Nine Rings, and the recent two years of travel, the proficiency gained from learning martial techniques had gradually brought it back up to two hundred points.

This number seemed ample.

But Luo Chen still felt a lack of confidence.

The rules for using achievement points were simple; Luo Chen had figured them out long ago.

Within the same realm, whether learning a technique or mastering alchemy or formation arts, it only cost ten points.

But cross-realm consumption increased tenfold.

If used for deducing techniques, the consumption would be even greater.

Even deducing within the same realm cost several times more.

Next, he wanted to deduce the follow-up technique for the *God-Devil Formless*. Its realm would be above the Human Immortal realm, directly leading to the Destruction realm, or even the Nirvana realm.

Two hundred points—he didn't know how far they would take him.

"Fortunately, my accumulation is sufficient. Whether it's the *Demon Aspect Art*, the *Pure World Withered Bone Bodhi Method*, or the techniques I borrowed from the Carefree Realm Master of the Fang family, they are all of extremely high grade. Now that I've found a general direction, I'm not completely without a starting point."

"Let these achievement points assist in the deduction. Later, even if the points run out, I can continue the deduction on my own."

The principle of the world was like this.

Reaching the peak might not be easy, but everything starts with the hardest part—the beginning.

For Luo Chen, the attribute panel allowed him to cultivate many techniques and divine abilities to their limits, even turning the ordinary into the miraculous. But these were all achievable through effort.

What was truly important was the word "beginner."

This meant talent!

Once you started, once you found the direction, you could race forward on the right path without straying onto the wrong one.

Luo Chen gathered all the techniques together, invested achievement points, and began the deduction.

His entire being sank into complete stillness.

Whenever he was in this state, Luo Chen missed the days when his soul was normal and he could freely activate his divine sense.

After all, there was nothing better than a cultivator's divine sense for deducing techniques.

Without the enhancement of divine sense, his efficiency in deducing techniques was greatly reduced.

Outside, time passed imperceptibly.

Sixty cycles of cold and heat, ten thousand days of waking and sleeping.

Luo Chen still sat cross-legged on the Ziwei Enclosure.

In the eyes of the Myriad Manifestations Star Sect's disciples, he had become a unique sight.

At first, the sect was quite resistant to Luo Chen's arrival.

Some were even dissatisfied with the current Star Sect master's cowardly retreat.

But over sixty years, Luo Chen had done nothing to harm the sect, nor had he demanded any resources.

The disciples gradually let it be.

The only point of dissatisfaction was probably that Luo Chen had occupied the best cultivation spot on the Ziwei Enclosure, which drew some criticism.

The Star Sect master, however, did not see it that way.

The Myriad Manifestations Star Sect was strong, but with the departure of the ancestor and all the Human Immortal elders, it had entered its weakest period in history.

Without a Martial Human Immortal to guard them, they were most afraid of external attacks.

Having Luo Chen there actually made them less worried.

That night, as yang exhausted and yin was born, and yin qi began to rise.

The Star Sect master, clad in a purple robe, led his disciples up to the Taiwei Enclosure to perform a sacrificial ceremony.

After the grand ceremony ended, the disciples withdrew one by one.

Before descending the high platform, the Star Sect master glanced up at the higher Ziwei Enclosure.

He wondered how much longer that senior would stay.

Just then!

Luo Chen suddenly opened his eyes, gazing at the night sky.

The Star Sect master felt the same and followed his gaze.

Under the brilliant night sky, it seemed as if a divine being had blinked. Then, they saw a streak of starlight falling from the highest heavens.

The Star Sect master blurted out:

"A falling star!"

But in the next moment, his expression turned extremely ugly.

Because it wasn't just one streak of starlight falling.

Under the night sky, a rain of stars poured down.
