# Chapter 1218: Refusing a Sage, Devouring a Fire Seed

The moment Iron Horse Sage spoke, the entire plaza fell silent.

It was both awe at the might of a late-stage Sage, and shock at the words he had spoken.

A late-stage Sage personally taking on a disciple, before so many fellow disciples, and when the other party already had a master—the firmness of his resolve was easy to imagine.

If Luo Chen truly agreed, the resources poured into his cultivation would be beyond what ordinary people could imagine.

Many put themselves in Luo Chen's position.

It was hard to even conceive of refusing.

Yet, beyond most people's expectations, Luo Chen merely cupped his hands.

"Thank you for your kindness, Uncle-Master. This disciple has no intention of switching allegiances."

He refused!

Murmurs of surprise rippled through the crowd.

Iron Horse Sage frowned. "If you're worried about your master, Yao Xi's thoughts on the matter, there's no need. I will personally go and explain it to her."

Luo Chen shook his head.

Iron Horse Sage looked at him with some pain. "You possess unparalleled cultivation talent. Junior Sister Yao Xi is currently at a critical juncture in her training and needs a long seclusion. For an Initiate cultivator, a single seclusion of a Sage might be half your lifetime. Do you truly intend to waste away like this? If you come under me, not to mention various resource support, I can give you the best guidance on the *Grand Mystery of Supreme Second Gold Myriad Transformations*. Xuan Luo, do not bring harm upon yourself!"

Yet Luo Chen refused again.

What the other party saw as a disadvantage was actually an advantage to him.

No one to guide him—he, who had once experienced cultivation at the Ascendant stage, didn't need guidance anyway.

On the contrary, Yao Xi Sage's long seclusion saved Luo Chen the trouble of carefully hiding his cultivation progress.

If someone were watching his training every day, *that* would be the real trouble.

"This disciple is quite comfortable on Jade Spring Peak. Thank you for your kindness, Uncle-Master."

Being rejected repeatedly by a junior, and in public, would stir anger in anyone.

Iron Horse Sage was no exception.

He stared hard at Luo Chen, his gaze shifting.

Dean He's expression changed slightly as he stepped forward. "Junior Brother Iron Horse, do not make things difficult for the younger generation."

"Heh."

Iron Horse Sage suddenly let out a light laugh.

As everyone watched in confusion, he raised his right hand, and a streak of light flew before Luo Chen.

"Cultivate well. Do not disappoint me!"

With those words, he ignored everyone else's gaze and flew away alone.

Luo Chen's gaze shifted from his retreating figure to the jade slip that the light had manifested into.

His spiritual sense probed it, and in an instant, he knew its contents.

It was Iron Horse Sage's cultivation insights on the *Grand Mystery of Supreme Second Gold Myriad Transformations*, complete from Qi Refinement to Sage stage.

And it even contained some Ascendant-stage insights, likely from the Ascendant elder, Heavenly Halberd!

Luo Chen stroked his chin, looking again at the figure that had already vanished, and couldn't help but smile lightly.

"Iron Horse... an interesting fellow!"

Knowing full well that he already had a master, he still dared to openly take him as a disciple.

And after being refused, instead of getting angry, he gifted a cultivation manual that ordinary people could never obtain.

He clearly admired his talent greatly.

This man had real bearing!

Or perhaps it wasn't just him.

Since joining the Su Ling Sect, most of the Su Ling disciples Luo Chen had met, despite their different personalities and cultivation levels, were generally kind.

Internal competition was usually kept within a certain scope.

Not like the sects Luo Chen had seen before, where internal struggles were life-and-death, with no holds barred.

The current Su Ling Sect was quite similar to the Luo Tian Sect he had founded in its early days.

Luo Chen roughly understood how this atmosphere had formed.

When a force was in a thriving, vigorous stage, its members would generally work hard and unite, focusing all their strength in one direction.

Now that the Su Ling Sect, with Supreme Su Ling's advancement to the Body Integration stage, had entered this phase.

On the contrary, large forces that had stagnated or even begun to decline would gradually fall into a vortex of vicious competition and endless internal strife.

These two stages were essentially a matter of resource distribution!

As Luo Chen smiled lightly, the much-talked-about Pill Tasting Conference drew to a close.

But Luo Chen couldn't relax just yet.

He and the seven members of his He Jun group were kept behind, waiting outside.

Dean He borrowed the Pill Hall of the Wonderful Pill Peak to hold a meeting with the heads of the other three halls and five courts. The only low-level cultivator allowed to attend was Gongsun Mao.

After a long while, Luo Chen and the others were summoned into the hall.

As soon as they entered.

Dean He's gaze fell on Luo Chen.

"We have discussed the mass production of the Circulation Qi Pill. The three halls and five courts will all cooperate fully."

"For the expanded production of various materials, the Spirit Medicine Court and External Affairs Hall will handle that. The Formation Court and Ingenuity Court will cooperate fully, and the Enforcement Court will provide enough manpower for protection."

"The Alchemy Court only needs to focus on large-scale refining."

"The Circulation Qi Pills produced will initially be supplied in large quantities to sect disciples, with only a small portion circulated outside as the sect's signature pill. This can also be used to establish relationships with other sects."

"As for the person to lead the mass production plan, Gongsun Mao recommended you."

"Xuan Luo, do you have confidence?"

At these words, several burning gazes fell on Luo Chen.

The full cooperation of the three halls and five courts, the handling of various resources, the interpersonal relationships involved... if he handled it all well and performed brilliantly, Luo Chen could hold a high position in the Su Ling Sect for the rest of his life, with nothing to worry about.

Yet!

Luo Chen's expression turned serious as he cupped his fists. "Uncle-Master, this disciple has confidence, but ultimately lacks experience. The successful refinement of the standard Circulation Qi Pill was not my achievement alone. Senior Brother He Jun, Senior Brother Zhu Xiao, Ning Fu... they all contributed greatly. Especially Senior Brother He Jun, who has been overseeing the overall situation and has far more experience with the entire process than I do. If anyone should lead the mass production plan, he is more suitable than I!"

As he finished speaking, the breathing behind him grew heavier.

Dean He looked at Luo Chen with surprise.

"Have you thought this through? Or is it because of what Iron Horse Sage said earlier that has affected you?"

Although Iron Horse Sage had spoken somewhat subtly in front of so many people, the word "harm" clearly referred to him possessing excellent cultivation talent but indulging in the alchemy path, going astray.

Luo Chen smiled and shook his head. "Dean, there's no need to worry. I can distinguish between cultivation and alchemy, and I won't go to extremes. I genuinely love the alchemy path, and even if I focus on cultivation, I won't stop delving into it. However, compared to leading a mass production plan, I would rather come into contact with more new pill formulas. So, let's leave this important task to someone more suitable!"

Dean He nodded in appreciation.

Not only was Luo Chen's speech appropriate, but he also knew how to control the balance.

And he was willing to yield benefits!

His gaze shifted as he continued to converse with the heads of the other three halls and five courts.

After a long while, everyone reached a consensus.

"He Jun!"

"Disciple is here!"

...

That night.

The Pill Hall on Wonderful Pill Peak was brightly lit for a long time, as the subsequent mass production of the Circulation Qi Pill was discussed down to every detail.

Mass-producing a new pill on a large scale wasn't as simple as relying solely on alchemists.

The complexity of the various stages involved was unimaginable to ordinary people.

Even the most basic raw material supply alone would require immense effort.

It was only because the Su Ling Sect was already skilled in medicinal herb cultivation and had extensive channels that the plan could be pushed forward without further delay.

But in any case, the matter was settled.

He Jun was put in charge, leading the entire plan—no, it should be called a project now.

Except for Ning Fu, all members of the original He Jun group joined in.

But Ning Fu wasn't completely detached either. Like Luo Chen, he remained in the project as an advisor, still contributing.

That night.

The seven of them drank heartily at the Jingxutian Villa until dawn!

For Luo Chen giving up the greatest benefits, He Jun and the others were extremely grateful, frequently toasting him at the celebration.

Luo Chen smiled and handled it all.

...

After everything was settled, Luo Chen's life seemed to return to normal.

But in reality, everything had changed.

His reputation, his status, and even his various permissions within the Su Ling Sect had reached a temporary peak.

This facilitated his subsequent actions.

He could freely enter and exit the three peaks of the Alchemy Court.

He could borrow various pill formulas and scriptures at will.

If there was something he didn't understand, as long as he asked, someone would always explain it to him without reservation.

He gradually understood the differences between the alchemy systems of the Xuan Realm and the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Overall, they were from the same lineage.

After all, the alchemy system of the Mountains and Seas Realm originated from the Xuan Realm.

But there were differences in the details.

That was in the alchemy materials.

The Mountains and Seas Realm had many demon beasts, so many pills used demon cores or materials from demon beasts.

Although the Xuan Realm also had many demon beasts, most were under the control of major clans and couldn't be killed casually. Therefore, the alchemy system relied more on natural materials like plants, metals, and stones.

Correspondingly, the alchemy techniques had different emphases.

Under the pretext of practicing pill formulas, Luo Chen exchanged for dozens of alchemy techniques.

Additionally, practicing pill formulas wasn't just an excuse.

He actually found several new pill formulas that delighted him in the Alchemy Court.

Especially a unique Su Ling Sect pill called *Heaven-Entwining Dew*, which had an extremely strong effect on accelerating the growth of medicinal herbs.

If not for its high refinement difficulty and the complexity of required ingredients, this pill would have been the Su Ling Sect's signature pill.

Its grade was as high as fourth-tier, enough to handle various grades of medicinal herbs.

When Luo Chen exchanged for this pill formula, a fourth-tier alchemist kindly reminded him not to aim too high and waste time and effort.

Luo Chen just smiled and brushed it off.

He wouldn't give up on refining *Heaven-Entwining Dew*.

He had a spiritual plant growing slowly that needed acceleration.

It was the Boundless Grass!

Under the pretext of refining various pill formulas, Luo Chen purchased a large amount of medicinal herbs from the Spirit Medicine Court at a 20% discount, spending nearly a million spirit stones in total!

Among them, he occasionally mixed in raw materials for *Heaven-Entwining Dew*.

After some preliminary research, he easily refined it.

And he put it into use for accelerating the growth of the Boundless Grass.

Finally, besides advancing in the alchemy path, Luo Chen also visited the Transmission Court several times.

From there, he exchanged for over a hundred spells!

They were of various grades, high and low.

Unlimited in type, covering all five elements!

This pained the Sage in the Scripture Pavilion greatly.

Initially, it was he who told Iron Horse Sage that a gold-element genius had appeared in the sect, which led Iron Horse Sage to observe Luo Chen and consider taking him as a disciple.

Luo Chen's refusal was another matter.

But Luo Chen's displayed alchemy talent was enough to be tolerated.

However, instead of restraining himself, Luo Chen exchanged for various spells, which made the Sage shake his head in pain. He once again stepped forward to remind Luo Chen not to spread himself too thin.

In response, Luo Chen merely revealed his further advancement to the fifth layer of Initiate stage, dispelling the other's concerns.

He also said that understanding spells of other attributes would help him in future battles.

This left the other party with no reason to obstruct.

After doing all this, Luo Chen announced that he would go into seclusion.

The location was set at a third-tier cave dwelling in the Alchemy Court.

It was Dean He's reward to him at the Pill Tasting Conference.

Inside the cave dwelling.

Luo Chen's gaze swept over the various alchemy techniques, pill formulas, spells, and killing techniques on the bookshelves, a satisfied look in his eyes.

The preparations were very thorough.

This seclusion, announced to the public, was nominally for cultivation, but in reality, it was to free up enough time to learn these things and rack up achievement points.

Of course, when he finally emerged, his cultivation level would inevitably be higher.

But before that, he had another matter to attend to.

Luo Chen took a deep breath and walked to the earthfire vent.

With a thought, as if summoned, a streak of cyan light flowed against the dense fire veins, rising upstream, and finally appeared before him.

Luo Chen looked at the cyan light, a hint of emotion in his eyes.

Four more years had passed since the Pill Tasting Conference.

If you added the thirteen years since joining the sect and the three years in the Supreme Su Immortal City, it had been a full twenty years.

Twenty years of continuous nurturing by the fire veins, and the Withering-Bloom Fire Seed still hadn't recovered its vitality. It was only in slightly better condition.

It showed how much the Withering-Bloom Fire Seed had sacrificed for Luo Chen.

"I hope this fire seed can awaken you."

Murmuring to himself, Luo Chen opened his mouth and spat out.

A flame like golden flowing liquid flew out.

It was the Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame that Dean He had bestowed upon him back then!

Its grade wasn't high, only third-tier.

But in essence, it had a potential as high as fifth-tier.

Similar to the peak of the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame.

If the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame could absorb it, it might have a chance to revive its intelligence.

Luo Chen's fingers moved slightly, forming layers of restrictions that fell upon the Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame, completely suppressing it.

This absorption would be total, leaving no room for anything else.

Luo Chen didn't want to see a repeat of what happened with the Nirvana Sacred Flame.

Back then, to covet the power of the Nirvana Sacred Flame, Luo Chen had chosen not to let the Withering-Bloom Divine Flame completely devour it, leaving a hidden danger for later.

Once bitten, twice shy. How could he repeat the same mistake!

He spat out a stream of spiritual power, wrapping around the Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame, and finally merged it into the cyan fire seed.

"Go!"

With a wave of his hand, the Withering-Bloom Fire Seed sank back into the depths of the fire veins.

After settling both the Withering-Bloom Fire Seed and the Boundless Grass, Luo Chen finally relaxed.

Now, it was time to rack up achievement points.

And also, using the cultivation insights gifted by Iron Horse Sage, to perfect his idea of covering the five elements with the *Grand Mystery of Supreme Second Gold Myriad Transformations*.

...

Luo Chen's seclusion, though somewhat surprising, didn't affect the vast Su Ling Sect.

The world wouldn't stop turning just because one person was missing.

All living beings had their own things to do.

He Jun led a group of alchemists, with the full support of the three halls and five courts, and began mass-producing the Circulation Qi Pill.

On Jade Spring Peak, time still flowed peacefully. Senior brothers and sisters trained hard and went out on missions.

But the atmosphere within the Su Ling Sect wasn't entirely quiet. Instead, it was gradually heating up.

Because the once-in-a-century sect grand competition was drawing near as time passed.

(End of chapter)
