# Chapter 1220: Five-Colored Mystic Chapters, Alchemy Saint Chu Yan

Dragon and tiger converged, spiritual energy poured in.

For an ordinary person, reaching this step would already count as successfully forming a core.

But on Spirit Pill Peak, things were different.

As time passed, the funnel-shaped spiritual energy vortex grew increasingly massive.

Yet it still refused to pour into the core-forming cultivator's body—a peculiarity that left countless onlookers baffled.

Luo Chen, the one at the center of it all, naturally sensed this, but still showed no urgency in drawing in external spiritual energy.

Because at this moment, he was at a critical juncture.

For normal cultivators, completing the core formation was sufficient.

But for cultivators practicing the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Mystic Chapter*, they had to inscribe the Mystic Chapter talismanic script in advance, reinforcing it with divine sense before absorbing external spiritual energy.

Otherwise, if the external spiritual energy directly scattered the Mystic Chapter talismanic script, the loss would outweigh the gain.

And Luo Chen's situation was even more special.

He had already inscribed the metal-attribute Mystic Chapter talismanic script earlier. Now, seizing the opportunity of breaking through realms, he was using his supreme divine sense to inscribe four other attribute Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts onto that golden core.

This was precisely the five-element simultaneous cultivation method he had researched!

If successful, it would not only achieve a perfect five-colored golden core, but also distribute his dharma power very evenly.

Even if, in the future, the external spiritual energy he absorbed had a heavier proportion of a certain type of spiritual energy, it could be evenly converted into other dharma powers through the five-element Mystic Chapters.

In this way, his foundation would be more solid, and breaking through to higher realms in the future would be easier.

Most importantly, Luo Chen wanted to activate the hidden Five-Spirit Dao Body in its most perfect state!

In silence, Luo Chen's eyes were tightly shut, his divine sense continuously circulating within his body.

His current body was completely different from that of ordinary cultivators.

The location of his qi sea was also more prominent.

This had much to do with his own special circumstances.

The broken Purple Mansion still existed, occupying the position of his former qi sea.

There was also the qi and blood furnace, like a majestic great sun residing in the center.

Therefore, he had used the method of shifting meridians and altering channels to transform his physical body, forcibly opening up a new dantian, and using this to evolve a qi sea.

Such self-modification—let alone cultivators—even martial arts powerhouses skilled in body refinement could not achieve.

Only Luo Chen, relying on the Chaos Indestructible Body, dared to let his imagination run wild—and succeeded.

Fortunately, the qi sea and meridians he had opened were indeed usable, having operated smoothly for so many years.

A massive strand of divine sense mapped itself within his body, straight into the qi sea.

Inside, a five-colored golden core slowly rotated like a star.

Upon it, there was already a golden Mystic Chapter.

Like a book page, resembling a talismanic script.

Mysterious characters, like silver hooks and iron strokes, were inscribed upon it.

What Luo Chen had to do was compose the Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts of the other four attributes.

This was not difficult for him, merely a matter of fine craftsmanship.

Under his careful execution, soon another Mystic Chapter talismanic script appeared on the golden core, colored azure blue—a water-attribute Mystic Chapter talismanic script.

Then came light cyan, crimson red, and finally mysterious yellow.

This order was also the result of Luo Chen's meticulous research.

Following the sequence of the five elements generating each other—metal generates water, water generates wood, wood generates fire, fire generates earth, and finally earth generates metal—completing a cycle.

If truly successful, then these five Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts, as the golden core rotated, would circulate on their own in a cycle, greatly enhancing his cultivation speed.

Perhaps even the original creator of the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Mystic Chapter* had never conceived such an astonishing idea!

But Luo Chen, relying on his vast divine sense, indestructible physical body, and rich cultivation experience, had forcibly adjusted and created the cultivation method most suitable for himself!

When all five Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts were successfully inscribed on the golden core, the external spiritual energy vortex, as if responding, instantly descended.

*Boom!*

The spiritual energy contained within the vortex, which had accumulated for so long, was far denser than any Adept cultivator could imagine.

But for Luo Chen, it was merely a matter of a single breath.

He opened his mouth and swallowed. The surging spiritual energy, like a tornado, was completely devoured into his body.

The five Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts began to operate on their own, instantly transforming into pure dharma power, strengthening the golden core by a third.

Luo Chen opened his mouth slightly, as if letting out a burp.

His divine sense swept over his entire body, and he nodded to himself.

The dharma power was pure, his aura was calm and peaceful—he didn't even need the step of stabilizing his realm that ordinary cultivators who had just broken through required.

But since he was cultivating right under others' noses, he still had to put on a show.

"Then I'll just go into seclusion for another ten years!"

Luo Chen smiled, without any complaint.

For him, ten or eight years were but a snap of the fingers.

It was also a good opportunity to refine the remaining spells and pill formulas, and brush up on the last wave of achievement points.

His gaze swept over the attribute panel, and Luo Chen's face revealed a smile even more unrestrained than when he had broken through.

On it, the number of achievement points had reached an unprecedented five hundred.

Such a massive amount was unimaginable in the past.

But it was indeed the result of Luo Chen using his authority over the years to exchange for a large number of low-level spells and various pill formulas, brushing them up day and night without ceasing.

And he knew deeply that this might be his only chance.

After all, he didn't want to go through the process of dropping realms and cultivating again, exploiting system loopholes.

So, the more he could brush up, the better.

After leaving seclusion, that million sect contribution points were useless anyway. He would exchange them all for relatively higher-level spells and pill formulas, and continue brushing up another wave.

No!

The scope couldn't be limited to spells and pill formulas.

Formation restrictions, artifact refinement, and various crafts—all could contribute achievement points.

It wasn't that Luo Chen was greedy, but he still lacked confidence in deducing the higher-level *Chaos Indestructible Sutra*.

The more achievement points, the better.

With a thought from Luo Chen, a large number of five-element spells flew out of his storage ring.

Looking at these spells, Luo Chen, who had originally planned to learn them as before, suddenly had a thought.

"According to the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Mystic Chapter*, the Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts condensed during the Initiate stage can be cultivated as a lifebound magic treasure after reaching the Adept stage."

"If I practice a large number of spells to the Great Perfection level, then condense them into dharma seals and inscribe them onto the Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts, what will happen?"

"Can spell dharma seals and Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts be considered another form of talismanic script?"

"This idea has a certain feasibility, after all, it's just changing the carrier from talisman paper to the Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts on the golden core."

"If successful, then some more cumbersome high-level spells can also be cast instantly."

"Even in the future, at the Sage or Ascendant realm, I can try to solidify divine abilities onto the Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts!"

At this thought, even Luo Chen couldn't help but breathe rapidly.

He had a bold personality, and the Chaos Indestructible Body gave him enough confidence.

He directly began to experiment.

...

After a month, the sect grand competition finally came to an end.

Many prodigies emerged, receiving competition rewards and catching the eyes of some high-level cultivators.

In particular, the scattered cultivators who had entered through the Spirit Gathering Assembly took this opportunity to perform well.

Those who performed outstandingly were able to enter the inner sect and become disciples of high-level cultivators.

For a time, many tales of excellence spread.

But after the competition ended, another news spread from the top down, from the Sage elders to the disciples of every peak and vein.

That was the alchemy genius Xuan Luozi, during the sect grand competition, had successfully formed his core on Spirit Pill Peak, stepping into the Adept realm!

This news truly shocked many people.

Not only was his alchemy talent terrifying, but Xuan Luozi could also become an Adept Superior at the age of sixty, showing his exceptional cultivation talent.

Many thought it was a rumor.

But with Iron Horse Sage accepting him as a disciple publicly, and the testimony of many alchemy masters from Alchemy Emperor Mountain, the rumor was disproven.

Later, even the details of Xuan Luozi's breakthrough and core formation were revealed.

According to reports, when Xuan Luozi formed his core, it triggered an extremely grand phenomenon.

The spiritual energy vortex formed lasted for most of a day, covering a range of a hundred li, encompassing the entire Spirit Pill Peak.

One could imagine how much help such a vast influx of spiritual energy would be to a cultivator.

It was likely that by the time that Xuan Luozi emerged from seclusion, his realm and dharma power would far surpass ordinary early-stage Adept cultivators!

...

Ten years passed in the blink of an eye.

Inside the cave dwelling, Luo Chen's expression was calm, his divine sense sinking into his qi sea.

On that five-colored golden core, five brilliant chapters were affixed, inscribed upon it.

On each brilliant chapter, there were a large number of dharma seals, like talismanic scripts, silently resting.

Luo Chen suddenly opened his eyes, forming a sword finger and pointing out instantly.

Without any hand seals, a magnificent scene of ten thousand swords flying in unison appeared behind him, and a sharp, clanging sound echoed through the cave dwelling.

Seeing this, Luo Chen nodded with satisfaction.

This technique was the highest among second-order spells—the Ten Thousand Swords Art.

Its power was no less than that of ordinary third-order spells.

But the price was that the hand seals for casting it were too cumbersome, and the requirements for dharma power and divine sense were extremely high.

Yet Luo Chen had released it without any hand seal movements, not even the step of mobilizing dharma power.

If used in actual combat, it would be enough to give the enemy a great surprise.

This was the wonder of the Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts!

At this moment, the five Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts on his golden core had inscribed not just one spell, but densely packed dharma seals numbering over a hundred.

In other words, as long as he wanted, he could cast over a hundred spells in an extremely short time.

This was even more terrifying than talisman cultivators who were skilled in using talismans for battle.

Throughout the ages, it was estimated that only Luo Chen could achieve such a level!

And Luo Chen's gains did not stop there.

In the past ten years, inscribing a large number of spells onto the Mystic Chapter talismanic scripts had also refined his use of divine sense to an extremely fine degree.

Combined with the subtle transformation of his state of mind earlier, his control over divine sense had now reached an unprecedented peak.

Under his deliberate concealment, his intense soul fluctuations could be as still as a calm lake, and his powerful divine sense could be hidden without a trace.

In Luo Chen's view, even some Void Refining lords might not be able to see through his current disguise.

This was good news.

As his realm grew, Luo Chen would inevitably come into contact with the high-level members of the Sùlíng Sect, and might even enter the eyes of that Grand Supreme Spirit Lord.

If his background were discovered, although Luo Chen was not afraid of Void Refining lords in terms of strength, it would always cause some trouble.

Now, things were much more convenient.

"It's time to leave seclusion."

Luo Chen muttered to himself, casually removing the cave dwelling's restrictions.

Instantly, streaks of light flew in.

They were transmission talismans.

Luo Chen was not surprised. When he broke through, he hadn't used any powerful concealment methods, so naturally, some people would notice.

Given his current status and position in the Sùlíng Sect, it was inevitable that some would be concerned.

"Oh!"

With a sweep of his divine sense, Luo Chen let out a surprised exclamation.

"Not only fellow disciples from Jade Spring Peak, but also He Jun and the others from the Alchemy Academy, and even transmission talismans from Alchemy Emperor Mountain, plus a large number of unfamiliar Sùlíng Sect fellow disciples."

Luo Chen selectively looked at some of the transmissions. Most were congratulating him.

And some from unfamiliar cultivators, besides congratulations, also introduced themselves, wanting to get to know Luo Chen.

None of these people were unknown; most were outstanding figures of the younger generation from various peaks and veins.

After seeing them, Luo Chen couldn't help but smile bitterly.

It seemed that even though he had laid enough groundwork, forming a core at such an age still attracted too much attention.

But it was all within expectations.

When he returned to Jade Spring Peak, he would set up a few tables, invite everyone from inside and outside to get to know each other, and just go through the motions.

But the transmission from Alchemy Emperor Mountain surprised Luo Chen a little.

"After leaving seclusion, there's a mission for me?"

Luo Chen stroked his chin, a sharp light flickering in his eyes.

"Let me go alchemy?"

Thinking behind closed doors yielded no answer, so Luo Chen simply stopped pondering.

He brushed the wrinkles on his green robe, drifted out of seclusion, and headed straight for Alchemy Emperor Mountain.

...

The person who received him was a Sage-level four-pattern alchemist, surnamed Ning, sharing the same surname as Ning Fu, whom Luo Chen had met at Wonderful Pill Peak.

In fact, the two were from the same family, with a close blood relationship.

This also made Luo Chen realize that even within the Sùlíng Sect, there was a considerable family-based faction.

He Jun and Dean He, Ning Fu and this Sage Ning, and many more.

Because of Luo Chen's relationship with Ning Fu, this person's attitude towards Luo Chen was quite gentle. He first praised Luo Chen's stable second-layer Adept realm, then said that the Sages on the mountain had been very busy recently and couldn't spare the time, so they had specially entrusted a matter to Luo Chen.

"Overseeing construction?" Luo Chen was very surprised, never expecting such a mission.

"Yes. You should have seen it when you came—the palace being built on the mountaintop. Its name is Mountains and Seas. It's already at the final stage of construction, so there's not much supervision needed. You just need to be responsible for the acceptance inspection. But you must be thoroughly familiar with every arrangement inside, so that when others ask, you can answer."

"The Mountains and Seas Palace?" Luo Chen's eyelids twitched after hearing this.

Sage Ning smiled and nodded. "This palace was specially built to receive a Void Refining lord. She comes from a small world called Mountains and Seas. To give her a feeling of being at home, our Sùlíng Sect temporarily constructed this palace based on various records of the customs and practices of the Mountains and Seas Realm. When that honored guest moves in, you will be responsible for daily reception. Don't be negligent."

Afraid that Luo Chen wouldn't take it seriously, Sage Ning's expression turned serious as he gave special instructions.

"This person has extremely high attainments in the Dao of Alchemy, and even the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect values her greatly. This time, it was the Sect Master and Elder Ancientwood who warmly invited her here. She is of great significance to our Sùlíng Sect's Alchemy Academy. Don't mess it up."

Across from him, Luo Chen's expression was calm, but his heart was already stirring.

The Mountains and Seas Realm, extremely high attainments in the Dao of Alchemy, a Void Refining lord, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

All the information combined, a name surfaced in his mind.

Luo Chen couldn't help but ask, "May I ask this person's name?"

Sage Ning slowly replied:

"Surnamed Chu, named Yan, titled Alchemy Saint!"
