# Chapter 1224: So It Was Her, Alchemy Art Reappears

"At that time, that person went by the alias Luo Hai and joined the Heavenly Origin Sect as a Wilderness Wanderer."

The story of the one known as "the Mountains and Seas Realm's Heaven-Destroyer" was slowly narrated by Chu Yan.

Compared to the hearsay of the Sulling Sect cultivators, the person in Chu Yan's account had a greater sense of reality.

Tai Su Lingjun teased, "Such a formidable figure actually went about hiding his identity?"

Chu Yan replied indifferently, "It was merely a matter of circumstance. The Mountains and Seas Realm was controlled by the Five Holy Lands, making it difficult for rogue cultivators to rise. It was quite common to adopt a false name and mingle within a sect."

For some reason, Luo Chen, who was driving the cloud carriage up ahead, faintly sensed a gaze sweep over him.

Was that a pointed remark?

Or just a casual glance?

"A rogue cultivator?" Tai Su Lingjun was taken aback. "How could a rogue cultivator achieve such a level? Was his talent exceptionally good?"

Chu Yan shook her head. "I wouldn't say his talent was that extraordinary. At least in my eyes, that person merely possessed a Fire Spirit Body."

Tai Su Lingjun frowned slightly.

"Although a Fire Spirit Body is good, it's not particularly rare. How did he manage to destroy Heavenly Origin and sever the passage?"

At this question, Chu Yan's tone suddenly became lighter.

She changed the subject and laughed, "Looking back now, the Dao Sect of the Mountains and Seas Realm meeting such an end is actually not unrelated to me, by about three parts."

"Oh?" Tai Su Lingjun became interested, showing an expression of wanting to get to the bottom of things.

Chu Yan chuckled softly. "When that person came to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, his body carried a faint, subtle pill fragrance—the qi of a fifth-grade divine pill that no spell could remove. Others couldn't see it, nor could they smell it, but I detected it. Although that person vehemently denied it, I had already determined that he was an unparalleled alchemy genius, and thus I felt a desire to draw close to him."

Another "unparalleled."

Tai Su Lingjun glanced at Xuan Luo's back, thinking that perhaps this was the reason Chu Yan had treated Xuan Luo differently earlier.

An alchemy genius always earned her favor.

"At that time, I allowed him to join Heavenly Origin, but I didn't actually have him formally enter the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. I only attached him as an external guest elder, thus allowing him to avoid the Dao Sect's method of extracting his life soul."

"When he was stuck in his cultivation, I manipulated the spiritual opportunity to help him achieve enlightenment."

"Finally, when I ascended and departed, I even gifted him a Heavenly Secret Pill, which would allow a Sage cultivator to comprehend the true meaning of the laws in advance."

"All these things probably laid the groundwork for him to eventually deal with the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect."

Tai Su Lingjun was extremely surprised. "There was such a secret history involved?"

Chu Yan waved her hand. "I merely did it casually. I never imagined things would develop to such an extent later on. Thinking back now, it's quite gratifying! Lingjun, although spreading this matter wouldn't affect me much, it's best not to mention it outside, lest it bring trouble to your sect."

Tai Su Lingjun hummed in acknowledgment and finally asked curiously, "What was his real name?"

Chu Yan's expression turned dazed. "I only found out later. I remember his name was Luo Chen."

"Luo Chen..." Tai Su Lingjun savored the name, her face thoughtful.

After that, the two changed the subject. They arrived at a new scenic spot, got off the carriage, and went to enjoy the view again.

Leaving Luo Chen alone, sitting on the carriage, staring blankly.

"So it was her!"

Luo Chen recalled a mysterious moment of enlightenment on Heaven-Earth Peak back then.

At that time, he was still worried about his main cultivation technique. He had planned to follow the example of his predecessors, compile the strengths of various schools, and create his own technique, but he had been unable to find the way in.

In his frustration, he had suddenly fallen into a state of enlightenment.

But at that time, there had been no sign of spiritual qi infusion.

This was completely different from his previous few enlightenments!

He had found it somewhat inexplicable back then but hadn't thought deeply about it.

Now it seemed, it had been done by Alchemy Saint Chu Yan.

If anyone else had said this, Luo Chen would certainly not have believed it. After all, how could an Ascendant cultivator casually manipulate such mysterious spiritual opportunity?

But the Alchemy Saint was different. She mastered a type of alchemy art called Refining Heavenly Opportunity!

Luo Chen also knew this art, understanding how to seek out spiritual opportunity, guide it, and refine it.

Back then, Chu Yan had done a good deed without leaving her name.

Although becoming an external guest elder hadn't allowed him to avoid the fate of having his life soul extracted, that had nothing to do with Chu Yan. It was because he himself had entered the Mirage Dragon Grotto-Heaven.

And the Heavenly Secret Pill she had gifted him upon her ascension had indeed been a great help.

Comprehending the true meaning of Withering-Bloom, creating his own divine ability, and later repeatedly displaying its power in the Eastern Wilderness great battle.

Alchemy Saint Chu Yan had shown him kindness.

Not to mention the Alchemy Saint's inheritance he had later obtained at the Nether Origin Sect, which had allowed him to learn many alchemy arts and refine the Initiation Pill.

Suddenly!

Luo Chen's expression shifted.

Alchemy arts!

His alchemy arts bore too obvious a trace of the Mountains and Seas Realm, including many techniques created by the Alchemy Saint herself.

Others might not have noticed, but that didn't mean Chu Yan wouldn't recognize them.

If it came to the alchemy refining stage later and Chu Yan discovered it, then no matter how exquisite his disguise, she would identify him as being from the Mountains and Seas Realm.

"This is something I need to be careful about."

Luo Chen's thoughts churned as he sifted through the alchemy arts he could openly display.

Next.

The two women continued to tour the scenic spots of the Sulling Sect.

They discussed cultivation matters very little.

As if this time, it was truly just to accompany Chu Yan on a relaxing outing.

Along the way, she would also ask him some basic questions about the alchemy path, and Luo Chen answered them one by one. Occasionally, he would feign ignorance, prompting her to offer guidance.

Over time, his performance not only earned Chu Yan's approval but also made Tai Su Lingjun look at him with new eyes.

Their sect had indeed produced an alchemy genius!

Three months passed in the blink of an eye.

In the final few days, Chu Yan no longer went out but stayed on Alchemy Emperor Mountain.

Those alchemists who had been waiting eagerly took out the difficult questions they had prepared.

Before the Alchemy Saint, the problems that had troubled the Sulling Sect's high-level alchemists for a long time were often solved in just a few words.

Not only that, but Chu Yan also organized the alchemy system for the Alchemy Emperor Mountain cultivators.

She wasn't limited to a specific pill or technique but explained and organized from a higher perspective and a broader scope.

The somewhat scattered Sulling Sect alchemy system gradually found its general direction after her organization.

To develop the Sulling Sect's advantage in medicinal herbs, avoid their shortcomings in alchemy techniques, and refine pills of higher quality within the same grade as much as possible.

This was very important!

To ordinary cultivators, an alchemist who could refine higher-grade pills was a skilled alchemist.

But to fellow alchemists, being able to consistently refine mid-grade or even high-grade pills of the same grade truly demonstrated one's ability!

In this area, the Sulling Sect had previously been quite weak.

Otherwise, why would the reward for the Foundation Establishment disciples in the sect's grand competition be the Circulation Qi Pills refined not by someone else, but by Luo Chen?

It was because, at that time, only Luo Chen could refine high-grade Circulation Qi Pills.

And Chu Yan had great insight into how to refine high-grade pills of the same grade, and she selflessly taught this to the Alchemy Emperor Mountain crowd.

This also greatly encouraged the Alchemy Emperor Mountain people.

As the date approached, Dean He could no longer sit still.

"Xuan Luo, weren't you researching the Five Elements Divine Pill before? Why don't you refine it now in front of the Alchemy Saint and let her offer some guidance?"

Luo Chen inwardly cried out in alarm.

Researching the Five Elements Divine Pill had been his plan before going into seclusion. At that time, he had no idea that Chu Yan would come to the Sulling Sect.

This move was originally intended to pave the way for his rapid advancement after the Adept stage, just like the Circulation Qi Pill.

Because of the Circulation Qi Pill's heaven-defying effect of accelerating cultivation, his forming a core in sixty years, though shocking, was still acceptable.

The Five Elements Divine Pill was the same!

Among third-grade pills, the Five Elements Divine Pill was the cream of the crop. Its refining difficulty was extremely high, and its effects were also extremely powerful.

Not only could it greatly increase a cultivator's mana, but it was also not limited by spiritual root aptitude.

If backed by this pill, even if he formed a Sage in over a hundred years later, he would have a reason.

However!

Refining the Five Elements Divine Pill in front of Chu Yan, of all things, would likely expose him easily.

Because the Five Elements Divine Pill existed not only in the Xuan Realm but also in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Sure enough, upon hearing this, Chu Yan also showed an interested expression.

"Isn't that the exclusive pill of the Five Elements Divine Sect? I didn't expect your sect to have this pill formula as well?"

Dean He explained, "This pill formula is incomplete. We derived it in reverse based on the various medicinal herbs that the Five Elements Divine Sect regularly purchases from us. Because Xuan Luo performed exceptionally well in improving the Circulation Qi Pill, we allowed him to consult the relevant classics and research the Five Elements Divine Pill. If he actually manages to refine it in the future, although we won't sell it to avoid angering the Five Elements Divine Sect, it will still be greatly beneficial for improving the cultivation of our sect's Adept disciples."

Chu Yan nodded. "Although the Five Elements Divine Sect is not as good as the Nine Great Holy Sects, it is still an old, established Void Refining sect with deep foundations. Back when the Mountains and Seas Realm was opened, some members of the Divine Sect participated and left a lineage there. I myself deduced the Five Elements Divine Pill formula in my youth. Xuan Luo, if you are interested in researching this pill, why not demonstrate a bit."

Luo Chen braced himself and put on a delighted expression.

"Please guide me, Alchemy Saint."

Soon, with the help of others, the required raw materials were prepared.

Luo Chen stood at the earth-fire vent, alone facing the massive pill furnace.

His mana surged, and a stream of Adept-grade pill flame shot out, mobilizing the surging earth fire to begin heating the furnace.

Dean He saw this and frowned.

"Why aren't you using the Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame I taught you?"

Luo Chen explained, "According to my research, the Five Elements Divine Pill emphasizes the balance of the five elements. The Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame has an extremely singular nature, which can easily affect the distribution of the medicinal power."

Dean He was taken aback and instinctively looked at the Alchemy Saint.

Chu Yan smiled slightly. "That makes sense. Xuan Luo does have some keen talent in theory."

But beneath that smile lay a hint of an indescribable meaning.

The fire used for alchemy did indeed affect the condensation and distribution of medicinal power, but compared to the improved efficiency and stable control, the gain or loss in this area wasn't that important.

This Xuan Luo wasn't using his origin true flame but instead consuming a great deal of mana to use his Adept-grade true flame.

Was it because he didn't know, or was he deliberately trying to hide something?

Luo Chen was indeed hiding something.

It wasn't just because the Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame had been devoured by the Withering-Bloom Fire Seed.

For him, even if it had been devoured, he could still imitate and display the Golden Emperor Devouring Spirit Flame.

What he was truly worried about was that once he revealed the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, Chu Yan would connect it to Luo Chen.

That would be the real trouble.

He suppressed the irritation in his heart and began to perform the unique alchemy arts of the Xuan Realm to process the various raw materials.

A large number of five-element attribute medicinal herbs gathered in the pill furnace. Under the calcination of his true flame, they gradually merged into one.

But just as they were about to truly coalesce into a pill, a sharp aura grew vigorously within, like an awl breaking through a bag.

At that moment.

Chu Yan suddenly made a move.

Her slender hand stretched out, and her delicate fingers tapped repeatedly. That sharp aura was immediately dispersed and merged into the complex medicinal liquid.

Luo Chen's pupils contracted. He recognized this technique.

Refining Heavenly Opportunity!

A self-created alchemy art of the Alchemy Saint!

He had learned it long ago at the Alchemy Saint Hall of the Nether Origin Sect.

He never expected that this art not only had the effect of refining spiritual opportunity but could also be used in reverse to suppress the conflict of pill qi during the pill-forming process.

If that were the case, then when refining high-grade pills in the future and on the verge of failure, he could completely use this art to turn the tide.

No!

Under his delight, Luo Chen thought further and more broadly.

Just like drawing inferences.

Since it could suppress the conflict of pill qi, it should also be able to separate the medicinal liquid and preserve most of the raw materials.

This would be extremely beneficial when refining high-grade pills that required extremely precious, rare, or even single-copy medicinal herbs as the main ingredient.

With this thought, a sincere smile appeared on Luo Chen's face.

At that moment, he saw the fleeting look of surprise on the woman's face across from him.

Damn it!

Luo Chen inwardly cried out in alarm.

An ordinary alchemist would have no idea how exquisite that move of the woman's just now was.

Even a high-level alchemist, if they didn't understand the principle of the Refining Heavenly Opportunity art, would only think that Chu Yan had used some powerful alchemy technique.

But he had just experienced the instinctive changes of three different emotions: shock, realization, and even ecstasy.

To others, this might not mean anything, but Chu Yan would surely know.

So, she had recognized him!

Putting aside what Chu Yan was thinking.

After her intervention, the medicinal power in the furnace stabilized, and a wisp of pill fragrance naturally emanated.

A group of alchemists exclaimed in wonder.

Just now, Xuan Luo was clearly about to fail, but the Alchemy Saint made a move and directly turned the tide, helping him succeed.

Truly worthy of her reputation!

Dean He waved his hand, and the furnace lid flew open. Five multicolored pill pellets flew out.

"The divine pill is finally complete, of lower grade."

Then, he looked at Luo Chen, also remembering the change in his expression just now, and his gaze softened involuntarily.

"Xuan Luo, the Alchemy Saint just lent you a hand. How much did you learn from it?"

Luo Chen opened his mouth and instinctively looked at Chu Yan.

Unexpectedly, the woman who had been gloomy before now had a hint of playfulness in her eyes.

He helplessly cupped his hands and said, "This disciple is dull-witted and didn't learn much."

Dean He was a little disappointed but quickly adjusted.

"Never mind. Once you have the first, the second will follow. Although this success wasn't solely your own effort, it shouldn't be too difficult to refine a true Five Elements Divine Pill in the future. Why don't you thank the Alchemy Saint?"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, his expression becoming extremely sincere.

He uttered a "Thank you!"

This thank you was not only for just now but also for the past.

Thank you for the past events in the Mountains and Seas!

As if she could feel Luo Chen's emotions, Chu Yan didn't decline and accepted it generously.

"That's enough for today. I will leave the Sulling Sect tomorrow. Fellow Daoists, you should practice diligently and walk higher and farther on the path of alchemy."

"Thank you for your guidance, Alchemy Saint!"

Amidst the crowd's farewells, Chu Yan smiled and left the hall.

As the steward of the Mountains and Seas Travel Palace, Luo Chen naturally followed closely behind.

The two walked in silence, the atmosphere quiet.

When they reached the entrance of the Travel Palace, looking at the bas-reliefs of desolate beasts on the walls, Chu Yan suddenly spoke.

"Did you really not learn much?"
