# Chapter 1203: Withering-Bloom Fire Seed, Demon Lord's Legacy

Tai Su Immortal City was rooted atop a thin fourth-order spirit vein.

To make effective use of the city's spiritual energy, numerous cave dwellings had been specially established beneath it, available for external lease at a rental fee.

These cave dwellings had a collective name: the Hundred Herbs Garden.

However, the rental prices for the Hundred Herbs Garden cave dwellings were extremely high, so most of them sat vacant on ordinary days.

Recently, one of these second-order cave dwellings, named the **Pugong Garden**, had attracted a new tenant.

This person was generous with their spending, leasing it for a full three years at once!

Yet such an action was not particularly unusual in recent days.

This was because all the cultivators in the vicinity knew that in three years, the Su Ling Sect would hold its Entry Spirit Conference. Those rogue cultivators, hoping to join the Void Refining sect, naturally had to prepare themselves thoroughly.

While there was still time, they had to do their utmost to raise their cultivation realms.

Even the most miserly rogue cultivators had recently been spending spirit stones on renting cave dwellings without any hesitation.

However, this one was different from the others.

The tenant of Pugong Garden, after moving in, did not devote himself to quiet cultivation and rushing to a higher realm. Instead, he frequently went out. It was only after spending several months that he truly settled down.

Inside the Pugong Garden cave dwelling.

A cluster of earth fire was burning vigorously at the fire vent. A young man in a white Daoist robe sat quietly before the earth fire, his brows tightly furrowed, his face full of worry.

The blazing earth fire beside him was something ordinary cultivators would usually avoid at all costs. But strangely, the white-robed man hadn't even activated a fire-avoidance formation, allowing his physical body to endure the scorching heat.

After a long time.

A low sigh echoed through the cave dwelling.

"Still no good?"

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his divine sense, a hint of exhaustion in his eyes.

Over the past few months, he had frequently gone out to gather large quantities of medicinal herbs, all for the purpose of refining several pills he was skilled at making.

Among them were pills like the Qionghai Pill, which were extremely rare even in the Mountains and Seas Realm!

But after consuming many pills, his shattered Purple Mansion showed no signs of recovery whatsoever.

Clearly, the Purple Mansion of an Ascendant cultivator, nourished by the origin qi of heaven and earth, had long since become different from the dantian qi sea of an ordinary cultivator. Such common pills were utterly incapable of repairing it.

Especially since Luo Chen's Purple Mansion barrier had been thicker and broader than others' from birth, making repair naturally even more difficult.

"Forget it, forget it!"

"If I want to restore my immortal cultivation realm, it's absolutely not something that can be accomplished in a day or two. I shouldn't be so impatient."

"These failed attempts have merely eliminated a few directions."

Luo Chen recited a Buddhist sutra, and his originally restless mind instantly calmed down.

This was the benefit of being able to freely move his soul!

No matter how troubling the matter, it could be quickly suppressed under the Clear Mind Mantra.

If it had been during his time in the Ruins, Luo Chen would probably have been plagued by distracting thoughts and would have needed a long time to calm his mood.

After tidying his mood, Luo Chen's divine sense swept over his physical body once more.

Unlike the "frail" bodies of cultivators, the strength of this physical body of his was simply inconceivable.

Every piece of flesh and blood within him, every cell, even deep within his bone marrow, there was an extremely lively sense of pulsation.

This was the Chaos Indestructible Body!

Having cultivated the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra*, and having endured the destructive impact of the turning of the epochs within the Ruins, he had thoroughly achieved the principle of breaking down and then establishing!

Under his repeated tests, even sharp spiritual treasures like the Yuantu Sword could not damage his physical body.

If he activated the Blood Refining Divine Astral to protect his body, he couldn't even leave a single scar!

At this level of strength, let alone Ascendant cultivators, even Void Refining True Lords probably couldn't harm him.

As for Unity Grand Sovereigns?

Luo Chen had never faced one head-on, so he still lacked confidence in his heart.

But with the Primordial Unity Cauldron in hand, preserving his life shouldn't be too difficult.

"The Primordial Unity Cauldron..."

Thinking of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, Luo Chen's mind stirred, and he summoned it.

*Dong!*

The small grey cauldron landed on the ground, emitting a dull, heavy sound.

Its round, plump belly looked somewhat endearingly naive, but the solemn aura it exuded was something no one could ignore.

Looking closely, the cauldron's body was covered in lines that emitted powerful auras.

They were like metal, like flowing water, possessing both a sense of vibrant life and the harshness of fire that burns all things.

Luo Chen knew that these lines should be complete laws condensed by the Primordial Unity Cauldron after absorbing enough law fragments.

Calling them "Dao marks" might be more appropriate.

Luo Chen stroked the warm Primordial Unity Cauldron, deeply moved in his heart.

This lifebound magic treasure, which he had spent great effort refining back then, had ultimately accompanied him step by step as he grew to his current level, containing power that was unfathomable and divine.

Although he didn't know if it had truly reached the level of a Dao artifact, just the brief exchange with the Splitfeather Demon Lord was enough to show its power.

Under the suppression of the Primordial Unity Cauldron, the peak Void Refining Demon Lord, who had already condensed a complete Law Manifestation, could be said to have been utterly powerless to resist.

It could be said that the Primordial Unity Cauldron deserved ninety-nine percent of the credit for being able to instantly kill the Splitfeather Demon Lord at that time!

Otherwise, if it had been a true head-on confrontation, even if Luo Chen wasn't afraid of the opponent, he would have had to use many more techniques.

Martial techniques like Raging Torrent Annihilation Realm or Heaven-Earth Inverse Reincarnation would probably have had to be forced out before a victor could be decided.

And that would have only decided victory or defeat. To completely kill the opponent would have required even more trouble.

"Such a supreme treasure must never be easily revealed before others. If seen by those with ill intentions, they must be killed!"

A fierce look flashed across Luo Chen's face.

Then, he reached out and beckoned.

A stream of light flew out from within.

Since he had decided not to easily reveal the Primordial Unity Cauldron in the future, he should also clean out the things stored inside.

The first thing to catch his eye was a three-foot-square piece of pitch-black stone.

This object had accompanied him for several hundred years.

It was the cultivation stone from the second ring of the Ninefold Immortal Tower!

Before leaving the Ruins, Luo Chen had collected it into the Primordial Unity Cauldron and brought it out of the Ruins together with him.

"This stone can withstand the scouring of the chaotic energy released by the Gate of Annihilation, and it stayed there for at least three thousand years. In terms of material, it surpasses all the cultivation stones of the Nine Rings of Annihilation."

"Not to mention its own strength, just that layer of stone membrane alone has defensive power that can be called unparalleled under heaven."

"If I can refine this stone into a magic treasure, especially a defensive type, then its upper limit will be incalculable."

Luo Chen's eyes showed a look of anticipation, but it quickly dimmed.

At least for now, he had no way to deal with this object.

Whether it was spiritual treasure cutting or divine ability attacks, none could harm it in the slightest.

This was probably also the reason why the Carefree Realm Lord had left this object behind when he left the Great Annihilation Realm—he too had no way to deal with this stone.

The Primordial Unity Cauldron did have the potential to refine it, but it would easily absorb it to strengthen itself.

Luo Chen was naturally willing for his lifebound treasure to become stronger, but if he could forge another trump card, that would be even better.

"If only the Divine Fire were still here."

With a fifth-order divine fire burning it day and night, spending enough time, he could always melt it bit by bit.

Luo Chen sighed, put this material, which he called "Chaos Stone," into his storage ring, and then pointed with two fingers.

*Chi...*

A cyan light flew out from the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

The cyan light was dim, its life force weak, and it exuded a sense of deathly stillness.

Although faint flames rose, it looked more like the last radiance before death.

Looking at this cyan flame, Luo Chen pursed his lips, a rare look of sadness appearing on his face.

The Withering-Bloom Divine Fire!

It had accompanied him since the Initiate stage, for nearly a thousand years.

The relationship between them could no longer be summarized by master and servant.

Over the past thousand years, whether in daily cultivation or battle, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire had helped him greatly.

Several times, when he was severely injured and on the verge of death, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire had automatically protected its master, exchanging its own life force for a thread of hope for Luo Chen.

This time was no different.

After being ambushed by Rosycloud, Luo Chen drifted through the universe.

If not for the protection of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, even with the guidance of the Calamity Gu's aura, he might not have successfully entered the Ruins.

But the price was its current state!

If it were someone else in Luo Chen's current situation, they would probably just abandon the fire seed of the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

After all, even at its peak, the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire was merely a fifth-order divine fire, utterly insignificant compared to Luo Chen's current strength, which could kill a peak Void Refining Demon Lord.

But Luo Chen couldn't do it.

"Even if it costs a great price and takes countless years, I will definitely make you revive and reignite the divine fire!"

Luo Chen took a deep breath, bit his fingertip, and squeezed out a drop of blood that was scarlet and ready to drip.

This was the Indestructible Blood condensed by his Chaos Indestructible Body. It not only contained exceptionally vigorous blood qi but also vast life force.

Even the physical body of the Splitfeather Demon Lord's true form couldn't compare.

With a flick of his finger.

This drop of Indestructible Blood fell into the cyan fire seed.

In the past, when the Withering-Bloom Fire Spirit encountered such a feast, it would devour it greedily and drink deeply.

But this time, facing the delivered Indestructible Blood, the cyan fire seed had almost no reaction.

Or rather, it couldn't!

This did not surprise Luo Chen.

Otherwise, back in the Ruins, he could have used the powerful life force from the bodies of many martial artists and even the True Spirit Golden Punishment Sovereign to repair the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire.

It was simply too weak.

Too weak to even take the initiative to feed.

"It will get better."

Luo Chen carefully stimulated that drop of blood, forming a red, glowing blood membrane that wrapped around the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, then pushed it into the earth fire before him.

And he kept pushing it downward, straight into the heart of the earth fire here!

Little by little!

Just like how his master Luo Chen planned to restore his immortal cultivation realm next—there was no rush.

Let the earth fire slowly nurture it. One day, it would recover its intelligence and once again display the might of the divine fire!

After temporarily settling the Withering-Bloom Divine Fire, Luo Chen looked back at the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

There were still things inside.

With a thought, an object that looked like a collar flew out.

"Using a collar as a storage magic treasure is quite rare."

Luo Chen chuckled, playing with the collar as his divine sense began to penetrate layer by layer.

This was precisely the legacy left by the Splitfeather Demon Lord!

After instantly annihilating Splitfeather back then, to prevent leaving any traces, Luo Chen had directly activated the Primordial Unity Cauldron to refine all the flesh and blood on his body.

There was no corpse left, and his primordial spirit was completely destroyed!

Only this collar, which contained spatial aura, remained.

"Let me see just how rich the family fortune of a generation of Void Refining Demon Lord is?"

With a light laugh, Luo Chen's divine sense surged in heavily.

The soul restrictions left on it were not complicated in Luo Chen's eyes.

But when he reached the final restriction, Luo Chen's unstoppable speed of cracking was halted.

It was the purest soul power!

There was no angle for clever breakthrough; he could only use even stronger soul power to break it.

"Although simple and crude, it has indeed stumped me."

Luo Chen's soul foundation was extremely powerful. When he was at the early Ascendant stage, his self-assessment was that he was not weaker than a late Ascendant powerhouse.

But compared to a peak Void Refining cultivator like the Splitfeather Demon Lord, he was ultimately still an entire level behind.

However!

"Hmph!"

Luo Chen snorted coldly, and a wisp of Blood Refining Divine Astral emerged from his body. The powerful martial divine intent on it immediately became extremely aggressive.

Since he couldn't break it with divine sense, why not try martial divine intent instead!

As the blood light quickly enveloped the golden collar, the martial divine intent penetrated layer by layer, eventually colliding head-on with the remaining soul power on it.

*Hum!*

An invisible wave spread out from the collar.

Even though Luo Chen had set up a shielding formation inside the Pugong Garden cave dwelling, he still couldn't suppress this wave.

In the span of a breath, this wave spread throughout the entire Tai Su Immortal City.

Those of lower realms felt nothing at all.

After all, this wave carried no malice.

But the only stationed Sage in the city was startled. He quickly flew out of his cave dwelling and frantically released his divine sense to scan the surroundings.

In the end, he found nothing.

The Sage looked confused and, after a long while, returned to his cave dwelling.

He could only assume it was some passing senior who casually released their divine sense to scan Tai Su Immortal City.

But it was somewhat rude!

Ever since their sect master, Tai Su Spiritual Lord, had advanced to the Void Refining stage, their Su Ling Sect's status among the surrounding major sects had risen like the tide. Even the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect treated them with courtesy.

Yet there was still such an ignorant existence, casually scanning low-level cultivators without even making any attempt to conceal it.

"Forget it, I'll just continue cultivating! If I can obtain a spot in the Demon-Suppressing Trial and advance to the Ascendant stage, I probably won't have to endure such humiliation again."

The stationed Sage of Tai Su Immortal City muttered to himself.

Meanwhile, in the Pugong Garden cave dwelling, Luo Chen had no choice but to stop his attempt to use martial divine intent to strike the remaining soul power of Splitfeather.

The collar in his hand had, at some point, developed cracks.

"Martial divine intent is too overbearing. Such a brute-force breakthrough will damage the magic treasure itself!"

Luo Chen muttered, having forgotten this point.

As an important means for martial artists to resist the soul attacks of cultivators, martial divine intent, regardless of its attribute, was inherently overbearing and unmatched.

It could be said that, apart from its own divine intent, it could tolerate no external existence with will.

If they didn't touch, it was fine. But once they clashed, it was a fight to the death, unless one side voluntarily retreated.

Clearly, the Splitfeather Demon Lord was already dead, and his remaining soul power didn't know how to yield.

If he continued to assault like this, Luo Chen could naturally break the final restriction, but this collar storage magic treasure would probably disintegrate as well.

Then, how much of the resources inside he could obtain would depend on luck.

After all, Luo Chen had experimented back then: after a storage magic treasure broke, most of the things in the space inside would mysteriously disappear, going to another space.

Perhaps to the Ruins, or perhaps somewhere else.

And Luo Chen's current wealth was not as impressive as it seemed on the surface.

When he ascended, he had left many resources for the disciples of the Luo Tian Sect.

What he carried with him, aside from spiritual treasures and pills, was only a small amount of spirit stones.

Over the past half year, he had spent most of it refining pills to repair his Purple Mansion.

Therefore, he valued this legacy of the Splitfeather Demon Lord quite a bit.

Just as Luo Chen was caught in a dilemma, his expression suddenly moved.

He opened his hand.

In his palm, flesh and blood squirmed, and then a dark-golden Gu insect, with a long, sharp mouthpart and fluttering dreamlike wings, slowly emerged.

"Kong Kong'er?"

It was the Vacuum Divine Gu.

After it advanced to the fifth order, Luo Chen had given it a name that was easy to call.

At this moment, the Vacuum Divine Gu appeared without any hesitation, flying directly onto the golden collar.

"Don't..."

Luo Chen reached out to stop it, but as the words left his mouth, he paused.

He remembered that the first thing Kong Kong'er wanted to do after its transformation was to eat the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

And that was a spatial immortal artifact!

Even if it was a severely damaged spatial immortal artifact.

Since Kong Kong'er could even eat the Ninefold Immortal Tower, then this golden collar...

Luo Chen looked at Kong Kong'er with a face full of anticipation.

He saw it extend its long mouthpart and stab it into the cracks of the collar.

As it sucked vigorously, the entire collar seemed to shrivel up, and its brilliant golden color gradually became dull and lusterless.

*Clang!*

The collar fell to the ground, instantly turning into powder.

Then Kong Kong'er opened its mouth and spat.

*Rumble!*

Pile after pile of things came out, accumulating in the Pugong Garden cave dwelling like a small mountain.

Luo Chen stared at this scene, dumbfounded.

"If I had known, I wouldn't have wasted all that effort. I would have just let you do it."

Luo Chen gave a wry smile.

Kong Kong'er fluttered its wings, circling around Luo Chen twice, looking quite pleased with itself.

Luo Chen shook his head and was about to put it away to sort through the spoils.

But Kong Kong'er flew into the small mountain, flapping its wings.

The miscellaneous items automatically moved aside, revealing a jade box.

Luo Chen raised an eyebrow, reached out, and the jade box fell into his hand.

There were no restrictions on the jade box, and his divine sense swept through it, finding nothing dangerous.

Luo Chen opened the jade box generously.

A small grass came into view.
