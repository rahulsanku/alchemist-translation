# Chapter 1245: Nether Capital Origin Court, Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable

The Youxuan Continent, far to the west.

Stretching for hundreds of millions of li, it occupied a full third of the entire continent's vast territory.

Within its borders, cultivators were as numerous as clouds, experts beyond count, and ancient families could be found everywhere.

Even the secular dynasties, which ruled over hundreds of millions of people, were not without traces of cultivators.

And this land had only one master.

That was—the Nether Capital!

A figure came from the far east, crossing the Nine Sects' Central Lands, finally arriving at the Nether Capital's territory.

Although this person was extremely powerful, he still restrained his aura along the way, not daring to let even a trace leak out.

Finally, his figure came to a stop before a majestic city.

This city was exceedingly grand, its city walls over a hundred zhang high, stretching beyond the limits of sight.

And its continuous expanse was like a winding giant dragon, its head and tail invisible, encompassing a territory of no less than tens of millions of li.

The strangest thing was that throughout the great city, there were no city gates!

A giant city without gates—such was the Nether Capital!

Legend had it that the Nether Capital had not originally been like this; the city had been built after the Xuan-Demon War ten thousand years ago.

The reason for not establishing city gates was also to guard against demon clan experts and to maximize the power of the formations.

"But legends are ultimately just legends. According to my clan's ancestor, this city is a giant heaven-reaching spirit treasure jointly refined by several Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerables. Once a critical moment arrives, activating this city and gathering the Origin Court's immense power would be enough to blast away any invading enemy!"

A man in a black and red imperial robe, through the beaded curtain of his crown, quietly surveyed the giant city before him.

There was both appreciation and shock in his eyes.

How could human refining techniques achieve such a level?

No wonder, with the ancestor's ancient Heavenly Phoenix body and terrifying strength ranking at the top of the Great Vehicle stage, the Heavenly Phoenix clan could only be relegated to a corner in the eastern part of the continent.

If it truly came to a fight, the ancestor might be able to roam freely, but the mere Heavenly Phoenix clan would likely be destroyed in an instant.

Taking a deep breath, the man descended from the clouds.

Ignoring the flying boats and fleets passing by all around, he slowly released his own aura.

In an instant, a majestic power connected heaven and earth.

All living beings nearby felt their hearts sink, as if facing the might of heaven itself.

If it was said that Ascendant cultivators, having comprehended the true meaning of laws, could borrow the origin qi of heaven and earth and wield a sliver of the Dao's authority.

Then this characteristic, which itself seemed like the Dao of heaven and earth, was beyond doubt.

Body Integration Grand Venerable!

Not borrowing, but fusing the laws and Dao with one's own primordial spirit, becoming a part of the laws and Dao themselves.

If one could achieve great success, they would become the supreme sovereign of heaven and earth above countless living beings!

That is, a Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerable!

The moment this power appeared, the Nether Capital seemed to react.

Streams of escape light shot out, standing atop the city walls, arrayed in full readiness.

An Ascendant cultivator stepped forward from the crowd, gazing at the man outside the city. Even though the gap in their realms was enormous, his gaze remained cold and sharp, without a trace of fear.

"What demon cultivator dares to act presumptuously before the Nether Capital?"

Faced with such insolent words, the man in the black and red imperial robe was displeased inside, but his face showed nothing.

He simply handed over a jade slip.

"I am Yan Emperor, under the command of the Heavenly Phoenix Ancestor. I seek an audience with the Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable of the Nether Capital!"

"Heavenly Phoenix Ancestor?"

The Nether Capital Ascendant was startled. Even with his noble status as an Origin Court cultivator, he still had to show respect when facing the strongest beings standing at the very top of this continent.

He thought for a moment, then clasped his hands and said, "Esteemed senior, please wait a moment. Allow me to report to the elders of the Illusory Dream Dao Field."

Yan Emperor nodded calmly.

But in his heart, ripples still surged uncontrollably.

A cultivator at his late Body Integration stage, wherever he went on any continent, even if he encountered an Origin Court-level force like the Nether Capital, would still receive courteous treatment.

But this was the Youxuan Continent!

Nominally, the Heavenly Phoenix clan and the Nether Capital Origin Court stood on equal footing, but everyone knew very well who the true overlord of this land was.

Only by invoking the ancestor's name could he be regarded with any esteem.

For a moment, a sense of competitiveness inevitably arose in his heart.

All of this was because the Heavenly Phoenix clan had only one Great Vehicle cultivator sitting in command, while the Nether Capital Origin Court had over a dozen.

If he himself could break through to that realm in the future, perhaps he could improve this embarrassing situation.

While inspiring himself inwardly, waiting was unavoidable.

And this wait lasted a full three days and three nights.

Three days later, the Ascendant cultivator returned, finally with a trace of respect on his face.

"Senior, the Illusory Dream Dao Field has approved your request for an audience. However, to avoid unnecessary trouble, please wear this token at your waist."

Yan Emperor frowned slightly but still accepted the token.

Then he entered the Nether Capital openly.

A few days later.

Within the Illusory Dream Dao Field, Yan Emperor, accompanied by one person, admired the unique scenery of this place.

Exquisite Illusory Dream Locust trees, one after another, were scattered throughout the Dao Field.

From time to time, some formless, bodiless strange existences were attracted here.

Those things were not powerful, but every time Yan Emperor sensed them, he still felt a thrilling sensation.

"Fellow Daoist Lin, what are those?"

The young man accompanying him said with a smile, "These are Ghost Nightmares. Brother Yan, have you heard of them?"

Yan Emperor raised an eyebrow. "Ghost Nightmares? Could they be the legendary Nightmares?"

Cultivator Lin smiled faintly. "Yes, Nightmare is a term used by mortals. However, Ghost Nightmares do enjoy feeding on the dreams of living beings to strengthen themselves."

Yan Emperor frowned. "These things should have long been extinct in the Xuan Realm. Why are there so many in the Illusory Dream Dao Field? Aren't you afraid they will affect the cultivation of your sect's disciples?"

"Affect?" Cultivator Lin did not explain why this place could attract so many Ghost Nightmares. Instead, he chuckled lightly. "The more of these things there are, the happier my ancestor is."

Yan Emperor was puzzled.

But before he could press further, his expression suddenly changed, and he instinctively bowed deeply.

The cultivator Lin beside him also put away his smile and respectfully bowed to the void.

"Greetings, Ancestor!"

"Junior Yan Xin, pays respects to Senior!"

A powerful divine sense descended from the void.

Wherever it passed, ripples stirred in the void itself.

The Ghost Nightmares perched on the Illusory Dream Locust trees, as if attracted, all rushed towards that divine sense in unison. Though there were only a few hundred of them, their momentum was like a mountain collapsing and the sea overturning.

Yet this momentum, upon reaching the divine sense, vanished like mud oxen entering the sea, disappearing in an instant.

Seeing this scene, Yan Emperor's heart trembled, and a guess had already formed in his mind.

This Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable actually fed on Ghost Nightmares. He must be cultivating some earth-shattering great technique!

After feasting on the Ghost Nightmares, the divine sense seemed to be in a very good mood.

"You are Yan Emperor, the most outstanding junior of the current Feather Clan?"

Yan Emperor bent his waist even lower. "The most outstanding—I dare not claim that. Before me, there was the Flying Peng King of Maitreya Continent who attained the Great Vehicle Dao. After me, there is my clan's Phoenix Child who broke through to Body Integration at less than eight thousand years of age. I, this junior, merely have a bit of undeserved fame."

"A Phoenix Clan member at eight thousand years old, a Body Integration cultivator? Converted to our human race's standards, that would be a peerless genius who broke through to the Body Integration stage at two thousand years old." Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable praised.

In terms of the lifespan of human cultivators at various realms, an Adept lived a thousand years, an Ascendant two thousand, and a Law Severing five thousand.

How many Law Severing cultivators, exhausting their five-thousand-year lifespans, could not break through to the Body Integration stage?

The Phoenix Child that Yan Emperor spoke of had used only the equivalent of two thousand human years to cultivate to the Body Integration stage, showing how terrifying her talent was!

Yan Emperor said modestly, "She also endured hardship before finding sweetness. Between life and death, she finally gained some insights and stepped into the Body Integration stage."

Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable laughed. "It's fine for you to be modest about yourself, but why are you being modest for others? Back during the Xuan-Demon War, I received a favor from your clan's ancestor. That favor, I naturally remember. Today, you have come to me in the name of the Heavenly Phoenix Ancestor, so there must be an important matter. Speak!"

Yan Emperor's expression turned serious, and he cupped his hands. "To be honest with you, Senior, this matter is related to that Phoenix Child of my clan."

"Oh? Could it be the matter of surviving a narrow escape from death that you mentioned?"

"Exactly!" Yan Emperor's face showed a timely expression of anger as he recounted the details one by one. "Two thousand years ago, my clan's Phoenix Child, Rosycloud, was at the peak of Law Severing. According to clan custom, she went out to travel, preparing for her breakthrough. Unexpectedly, she was framed and schemed against by villains, unfortunately falling into a small thousand world, trapped for many years. In that battle, only two Law Severing cultivators made a move, but the mastermind behind it was a Body Integration Grand Venerable. If it were only that, it would be one thing. But not long ago, another junior of my clan at the peak of Law Severing was ambushed and murdered, with no corpse left to be found. According to my secret investigation, this matter is inextricably linked to one of the Nine Great Sacred Sects—the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable clicked his tongue. "Old hatred, followed by new grievances. Are you planning to seek justice?"

Yan Emperor nodded. "Exactly. If we cannot punish the guilty and avenge our hatred through open and proper means, then wouldn't every genius of my clan live in fear? How can we talk about strengthening the clan in the future! I came to seek an audience this time precisely to inform the Nether Capital, so as to avoid any unnecessary misunderstandings."

"It is easy to cause misunderstandings! On the Youxuan Continent, the human and feather clans have coexisted peacefully for over ten thousand years. A major conflict could very well become the spark that ignites an all-out war." Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable said softly.

Yan Emperor was taken aback.

He hesitated. "Heavenly Venerable, do you forbid it?"

Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable said faintly, "If I forbade it, it would make me seem ungrateful for past favors. But as you said, to avoid unnecessary misunderstandings, it's better to keep the commotion small!"

"Small..." Yan Emperor pondered these words.

Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable suddenly asked another person.

"Xixiang, by my reckoning, it should be time for the Demon-Suppressing Trial, right?"

Lin Xixiang nodded. "Reporting to Master, it is indeed the Demon-Suppressing period."

"Which sect is in charge now?"

"It is the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

"Demon Suppression is a major matter; no one can delay it. Yan Xin, if you want to take revenge, do it after this. Do not harm the fortune of the Youxuan Continent."

Yan Emperor seemed to understand, then bowed deeply. "Thank you, Senior, for your approval!"

"Go now. Also, send my regards to your clan's ancestor. I fear the time for us to meet in the future is not many."

At these words, Yan Emperor's expression changed slightly.

But since the other party had already given him his leave, he could not stay long and could only take his leave and depart.

After he left.

Lin Xixiang remained where he was, a look of confusion on his face.

"Xixiang, what doubts do you have?" Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable was in a good mood. Having come out of seclusion for once, he took the opportunity to help his disciple with his questions.

Lin Xixiang asked doubtfully, "Master, all of this is just their side of the story. Why didn't you investigate further?"

"You mean evidence?" Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable chuckled lightly.

"Yes! Although nominally the Nine Great Sacred Sects are each independent, they are still part of the human race and all revere my Nether Capital Origin Court as the overlord of the continent!" Lin Xixiang had shed his gentle and refined demeanor from when he faced Yan Emperor, and his tone unconsciously revealed a lofty pride.

As if the demon clan targeting the Nine Great Sacred Sects was an affront to the Nether Capital's face.

If you want to hit the dog, you have to look at its owner!

Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable laughed. "You all, at least treat the Heavenly Phoenix clan as an Origin Court-level force. How can you look down on them so much?"

Lin Xixiang was stunned.

Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable explained, "Although the Heavenly Phoenix clan only has one Great Vehicle cultivator sitting in command, the Heavenly Phoenix Ancestor's strength is extraordinary. He is an old monster who has lived for tens of thousands of years and has endured as many as eight heavenly tribulations. He is not inferior to the Nine-Tribulation Heavenly Venerable who fell under the siege of over a dozen demon clan Saint Ancestors ten thousand years ago. Compared to him, my Nether Capital Origin Court merely wins by having enough Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerables in number. It can be said that as long as the Heavenly Phoenix Ancestor still exists for one day, the Heavenly Phoenix clan can still sit on an equal footing with us. For such an existence to make a move against a mere Body Integration force, why would they need solid evidence?"

Lin Xixiang hesitated. "If that's the case, then why did they come to seek an audience with Master?"

As soon as the words left his mouth, he immediately reacted.

"To avoid causing misunderstandings?"

"You haven't cultivated yourself into a fool." Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable teased him, then said seriously, "It's not just about avoiding misunderstandings with us, the Nether Capital. It's also about avoiding misunderstandings with all of humanity on the Youxuan Continent. Personal grudges don't matter; scenes of revenge and murder happen every day in the cultivation world. But if it involves a war between races, then my Nether Capital absolutely cannot sit idly by. I hope they can control the degree. If they go too far, even with the Heavenly Phoenix Ancestor's favor, it will be difficult for me to mediate."

Lin Xixiang suddenly understood.

The Heavenly Phoenix clan, because of their ancestor, naturally held a transcendent status.

Even the Nether Capital Origin Court gave them sufficient respect.

But if it came to a war between races, the Heavenly Phoenix clan, confined to the eastern part of the continent, would be completely outmatched.

As Lin Xixiang came to this realization, he suddenly recalled something.

He asked tentatively, "Master, you just said that the Heavenly Phoenix Ancestor has already endured eight Great Vehicle heavenly tribulations?"

This time, Illusory Dream Heavenly Venerable said nothing. His divine sense withdrew calmly, leaving only a soft laugh echoing.

And in that soft laugh, there was a hint of barely perceptible sigh and emotion.

Lin Xixiang remained where he was, his expression shifting uncertainly. Especially when he thought about the words his master had said before Yan Emperor left, he felt even more deeply.

"It seems the Heavenly Phoenix clan's plan to make a move against the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is not just about old and new hatreds!"

...

In a dim underground cavern.

A figure suddenly stopped his flight, his finger touching between his brows.

A lotus flower imprint appeared, flickering faintly.

"The Lotus Brand is indeed effective. It seems there is a fellow disciple of the Su Ling Sect nearby!"

Luo Chen's expression shifted slightly. As his gaze flickered, the hand seals at his fingertips changed, and the lotus imprint between his brows gradually dimmed.

From the very beginning of this trial, he had not planned to act together with anyone else.

And now...

He mobilized the spiritual power within his body, and the Blood-Refined Divine Astral also circulated along with it.

Both powers surged into his eyes.

In an instant, the complete Red Eyes Golden Pupils appeared once more!

In the dark demon lair, the suppression of divine sense had reached its extreme. Even his peak Ascendant-level divine sense could not spread beyond a hundred li.

In such a situation, it was very dangerous for ordinary cultivators who were always accustomed to relying on their divine sense.

But he had no major problems.

With these eyes, there was little that could deceive him.

As the golden light bloomed, Luo Chen suddenly raised an eyebrow.

"Found it!"

Eight hundred li away, layers of phantoms overlapped, and demonic qi blazed fiercely.

It was precisely a demon nest that had taken shape!
