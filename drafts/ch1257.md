# Chapter 1257: Revealing Identity, Unknown Ascendant

So beautiful!

Chu Yan had always been exceptionally beautiful. Ever since she came to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, she had earned the title of "Cold Beauty."

Even the renowned Supreme Spirit Lord of the Su Ling Sect, famous for her appearance and talent, was slightly inferior in comparison.

Yet when she smiled, Yu Jiajia suddenly realized that this was her most natural and beautiful state.

Shallow dimples, a charming smile.

Even as a fellow woman, she couldn't help but be captivated.

"I imagine even the Sword Lord has never seen Elder Chu like this, has he?"

Yu Jiajia respectfully lagged behind, but her gaze couldn't help sizing up the young man who had made Chu Yan smile.

Xuan Luo?

Just a mere Sage. How could he make someone like Chu Yan favor him so?

What identity did he have, or what outstanding qualities did he possess?

"Jiajia, thank you for bringing him here. You may go back now!"

Her voice was as cold as usual, but somehow, listening now, Yu Jiajia could always detect a hint of sweetness in it.

She bowed respectfully. "Yes, Elder!"

Luo Chen watched Yu Jiajia leave until her figure disappeared before turning to look at Chu Yan.

When their eyes met, they couldn't help but smile.

"Please!"

Luo Chen nodded and followed the woman as she stepped into Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak.

Wherever they passed, the clouds and mist automatically parted, then closed again.

In a daze, it seemed they had crossed thousands of miles.

But when they set foot, they were already within the mountain.

Luo Chen looked up at this peak, which seemed forgotten in a corner of the Dao Sect, and felt a sense of déjà vu.

"Sitting-in-Forgetfulness, Sitting-in-Forgetfulness... Senior, you still miss your homeland!"

With just this one sentence, he had essentially revealed his origins.

After all, few from other sects in the Xuan Realm knew about the Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak of the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Chu Yan seemed surprised by his directness, yet not unexpected.

"Luo Chen?"

Luo Chen nodded and turned around to face Chu Yan.

"How did you recognize me? If it's just the identity of a cultivator from the Mountains and Seas Realm, that wouldn't be hard to guess. But pointing to the name Luo Chen—there's no real clue there! I thought I had hidden myself quite well and shouldn't have revealed anything about my true background."

Chu Yan smoothed the hair by her cheek and nodded. "Indeed, even when you earlier displayed the Heaven-Refining Alchemy Art I left in the Mountains and Seas Realm, it only indicated you came from there."

"Yes, that's what puzzles me too."

"But hundreds of years ago, I knew most of the skilled alchemists in the Mountains and Seas Realm. The few unfamiliar ones were mostly experts from the demon race. That narrowed down the scope, didn't it?" Chu Yan smiled.

Luo Chen frowned. "When we met, it was only briefly on Heaven-Earth Peak. At that time, I hadn't displayed any particularly exceptional alchemy skills."

Chu Yan couldn't help but laugh. "Wasn't it impressive enough? At that time, you still had the residual aura of a fifth-grade divine pill on you!"

Luo Chen was stunned.

Was she talking about the Law-Derivation Pill?

But he had sealed that pill extremely tightly at the time.

If she meant he had once refined a fifth-grade divine pill, that was even more absurd.

He had always had the habit of using spells to remove foreign auras from his body, so unlike ordinary alchemists, he rarely had residual pill fragrance lingering on him.

Suddenly!

He remembered something.

When he joined the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect as a guest elder, Chu Yan had only spoken two sentences.

One was: "So you're an alchemist."

The other was: "Can you refine fifth-grade pills?"

Luo Chen hesitated. "So at that time, you suspected I had refined a fifth-grade divine pill?"

Chu Yan led Luo Chen into a simple palace within the mountain. The surrounding scenery was not as reminiscent of the Mountains and Seas Realm as the exterior of Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak had been.

However, the various pill fragrances wafting through the palace were still refreshing.

"Sit!"

Luo Chen sat down as instructed and watched the door close.

He could even sense that the entire peak was enveloped by restrictive formations, making it difficult for even cultivators above the Void Refining stage to spy here without alerting the master.

After Luo Chen sat down, he patiently waited for Chu Yan's answer.

After a long while!

Chu Yan spoke softly. "I've had a talent since childhood. It wasn't apparent at first, but after I began studying the alchemy dao and refining higher-grade pills, it became increasingly powerful. Its name is—Jade Pot Alchemy Heart."

A pure heart in a jade pot?

What kind of alchemy talent was this? Luo Chen had never heard of it, never seen it.

"Because of this talent, I can refine high-grade pills across stages, and I'm extremely sensitive to various types of pill qi. Even if you used some advanced spell to remove the pill qi from your body, it still existed. By intuition alone, I sensed something about you back then. That's why I asked that abrupt question."

Luo Chen smiled bitterly. So it was because of her constitution and talent?

But why did she sound so melancholy and gloomy?

Chu Yan continued on her own. "And locking down your identity wasn't actually difficult. High alchemy skill, remarkably fast cultivation speed, coming to the Xuan Realm but hiding your name as if afraid of being discovered. In the past thousand years, in the Mountains and Seas Realm, there was only one Luo Chen—after all, he destroyed the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's branch in the Mountains and Seas Realm. Most importantly..."

At this point, Chu Yan smiled slyly.

"You might not know this, but before I ascended, I was in a state of primordial spirit resonance with heaven and earth. At that time, the spiritual opportunities of the Central Continent were all flowing toward my primordial spirit, but a portion was absorbed by you while you were in an enlightenment state. I didn't disturb you then; instead, I used my alchemy art to refine the heavenly secrets and gifted you a share. I don't know what you ultimately comprehended, but at that time, I remembered your soul aura!"

Luo Chen's mouth fell open. He rarely lost his composure like this.

He never expected there to be such a connection.

But he had indeed had that experience back then.

On Heaven-Earth Peak, moved by inspiration, he had used the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* as his foundation, integrating numerous high-grade fire-attribute techniques to create his own *Withering-Bloom Dao Scripture*.

At that time, he had fallen into an enlightenment state.

When he woke up, not only had he created the technique, but his realm and mana had inexplicably increased by a large margin.

He had been puzzled at the time but hadn't delved into it.

Now it seemed it was Chu Yan who had secretly helped him.

If it was under those circumstances...

"I was in Sage enlightenment, you were in primordial spirit resonance, and I borrowed your heavenly spiritual opportunity. No wonder you could recognize me."

He didn't directly thank her.

Back then, Chu Yan had done him a favor without mentioning it, which meant she didn't seek repayment.

And there was also the gift of the pill.

These kindnesses couldn't be repaid with a simple "thank you."

Luo Chen simply kept it in his heart, intending to find an opportunity to repay her someday.

After Luo Chen fully admitted his identity, the atmosphere between them became increasingly familiar.

After all, they were both fellow townspeople from the same small world.

Chu Yan blinked her big eyes. "Luo Chen, now can you tell me what fifth-grade divine pill you refined back when you were at the Sage stage?"

Luo Chen was amused. "Senior, you are now a distinguished Void Refining True Lord. Ordinary fifth-grade pills must be child's play for you. Why are you still so fixated on things from back then?"

Chu Yan shook her head and said very naturally, "Although pills have grade distinctions, they have no inherent nobility or baseness. No matter what kind of pill it is, if I haven't seen it, I'm curious. Oh, and don't call me senior anymore. With your talent, advancing to the Void Refining stage is only a matter of time."

Luo Chen readily complied and addressed her as "Daoist friend."

Then he began to speak of the past.

"Actually, that pill wasn't refined during my Sage stage, but during my Adept stage, in a secret realm."

"Adept stage?!"

"Yes! Its name is the Law-Derivation Pill. Its effect is..."

"Wait, the Law-Derivation Pill? Could it be the divine pill created by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining? Did you refine it in the Demon-Fall Land?"

"Daoist Chu, you know of this pill too?"

"Have you forgotten the Heavenly Secret Pill I gave you? I created it by studying the incomplete pill formula left by the Devil Lord, combined with the incomplete formula of the ancient Initiation Pill. Its effect is not as good as the Law-Derivation Pill, but it achieves three to five tenths of the effect."

"I see. No wonder the principles behind them are very similar. Oh, by the way, regarding the ancient Initiation Pill formula you mentioned, I later completed it and successfully refined it."

"Really? Tell me about it quickly!"

One could only say, this was worthy of an Alchemy Saint who had achieved Ascension through the alchemy dao.

Even though she was now at the Void Refining stage, as long as it involved matters related to pills, Chu Yan was still full of enthusiasm.

Luo Chen had also immersed himself in this dao his entire life. When discussing the alchemy dao, although he wasn't as eloquent as Chu Yan, he still spoke with authority.

Their conversation grew increasingly cheerful.

Time passed imperceptibly, and both were completely absorbed.

Later, talking alone wasn't enough; they directly began live demonstrations, taking out medicinal herbs and furnaces to refine pills.

If at first they were fellow townspeople meeting each other, by the end it had completely turned into an alchemy enthusiast exchange meeting.

...

On Juan Ke Mountain.

Youhuang True Person walked out of a room, looking somewhat dazed.

When she saw Yao Xi coming out of the neighboring room, the two couldn't help but exchange bitter smiles.

"How many times have we been questioned in the past half month?"

"The fifth time. Once every two or three days, endlessly tiresome."

"What exactly are they trying to find out? And I notice that people from other sects aren't being questioned as frequently as we are."

"Not sure, but toward the end, they were focusing on asking about the Ascendant who saved us. Could it be that person's identity is problematic?"

"But isn't he a Dao Sect great power?"

"It seems not now. And it's not just our Su Ling Sect. Isn't Yun Dongjun of the Great Soul Gate being called in for questioning frequently too?"

"It seems he also encountered an Ascendant of unknown identity?"

The two walked out while conversing.

Combining the questions they had been asked, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect seemed to have locked onto an Ascendant of unknown identity.

They just didn't know if this person had infiltrated them, or if, like Dean He, they had broken through to the Ascendant stage during the Demon-Suppressing Trial.

But this person was hiding their identity and hadn't revealed themselves yet. The Dao Sect was determined to dig them out.

"Regardless, this person saved us after all. Brother He's achievement of the Ascendant stage also owes something to him. We originally promised to keep it secret and not tell outsiders, but unfortunately, under the influence of the Dao Sect's formations, his existence was ultimately exposed. If it doesn't involve anything major, we should also plead for this person when the time comes," Yao Xi said.

Youhuang True Person sighed instead. "Judging from how frequently the Dao Sect cultivators are questioning us, this person's involvement is probably no small matter. Our words carry little weight; it will be hard to make a difference."

Yao Xi thought about it and realized this was true. Before a behemoth like the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, they indeed had little say.

"Let's not worry about that for now. Elder Gumu said he has something to gather us for. Let's hurry over and meet up!"

The two quickly arrived at Elder Gumu's residence. Everyone else was there too.

But one person was missing.

"Where's Xuan Luo?" Yao Xi asked curiously.

Elder Gumu chuckled. "You might not know this, but Xuan Luo hadn't been at the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect for two days before he was invited by the Alchemy Saint to be a guest."

Everyone was stunned.

Elder Gumu rubbed his hands together, somewhat excited. "It seems that back in the Su Ling Sect, the Alchemy Saint was very satisfied with Xuan Luo. Now that she's on her home turf and has called him over, she'll probably give him some guidance. If Xuan Luo can learn something, our Su Ling Sect will be blessed in the future."

Yao Xi breathed a sigh of relief. "That's good. At least he won't be like us, being questioned day in and day out. Sometimes the same question is asked over and over, like interrogating a criminal. The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect is really going too far."

At this mention, everyone present showed indignation.

Facing the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, although their realms were low, outside, which of them wasn't a highly respected Sage?

Being treated like this, it wasn't just them; cultivators from other sects also harbored some resentment.

But they only dared to be angry, not to speak out.

Elder Gumu coughed. "Alright, keep these words among ourselves. Don't let them spread."

Seeing that everyone's anger hadn't subsided.

Elder Gumu changed the subject. "Don't dwell on it. The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect will give us an explanation. It's said that the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign will soon emerge from seclusion, and you may be able to meet a Body Integration Great Sovereign. Moreover, the Dao Sect will distribute rewards."

Everyone exchanged glances. Was this a form of compensation?

Meanwhile, on the other side.

Several Dao Sect disciples, carrying thick interrogation records, arrived outside a sword pavilion.

"Reporting to the Sword Lord, all the interrogation records of cultivators from various sects are here."

A vigorous voice, tinged with sharpness, came from within the pavilion.

"Any results?"

"According to our investigation, there are no hidden demon spirits among those people."

"It's impossible for demon spirits to escape the Demon-Suppressing Tower. Those Xuanwu fellows are making a mountain out of a molehill."

"However, during the investigation, we discovered a very suspicious matter."

"Oh? Tell me about it?"

The disciples outside dared not be negligent and recounted the interrogation process and the intelligence obtained in detail.

"At first, the Su Ling Sect disciples tried to conceal something, but the formation detected it."

"We pressed them repeatedly, especially seizing on the flaws in their statements, and finally learned of an Ascendant who saved them from the demon spirit attack."

After comparing it with the movement trajectory of the ten Ascendants from our sect who participated in the Demon-Suppressing Trial, we confirmed that this person is not one of our sect's Ascendants.

"Additionally, Yun Dongjun of the Great Soul Gate, Xiao Fufeng of the Purple Flame Mountain, Ding Ling'er of the Golden Bell Sect, and Green Butterfly of the Azure Fall Sect all encountered an Ascendant of unknown identity."

"Most importantly, according to intelligence sent back by True Lord Vermilion, someone broke through to the Ascendant stage in the Central Demon Abyss. Their current status is unknown, and their whereabouts are uncertain."

"We suspect these may all be the same person!"

Inside the sword pavilion.

The voice sounded again, still impatient.

"Willing to save people, probably not a bad person. Although hiding one's identity is somewhat suspicious, as long as they don't hinder our sect, it's fine. Describe the techniques this person used. If I encounter them in the future, I'll be able to distinguish them myself."

"Yes!"

The disciples outside briefly outlined the information based on the answers from the Su Ling Sect and Great Soul Gate cultivators.

"A sword?"

The sword pavilion's gate burst open, and a figure appeared before the several disciples.

He casually took the records, his sword-browed, bright-eyed face showing a hint of interest.

"An Ascendant who uses a sharp sword? I wonder if they achieved Ascension through the sword dao?"

But this hint of interest vanished in an instant.

After all, it was just an Ascendant hiding their identity.

"Alright, you may go back now. The interrogation of disciples from various sects is temporarily suspended. The Sect Master is about to emerge from seclusion, and I need to make some preparations. I must go see Junior Sister Chu and ask her to refine a batch of pills to appease the complaints from the various sects."

When mentioning the words "Junior Sister Chu," a look of anticipation appeared on Nine Yang Sword Lord's face.

Ignoring the disciples' greetings below, his figure transformed into a rainbow light and flew toward Sitting-in-Forgetfulness Peak. (End of chapter)
