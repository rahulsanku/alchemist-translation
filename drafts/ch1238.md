# Chapter 1238: Realm of Weapons Torrent, Divine Ability Primer

While Luo Chen was silently grumbling, the agenda within the hall had already reached its final item.

It was also the main reason the seven Divine Transformation elders were present.

All the Nascent Soul cultivators of the Sulling Sect would personally bestow protective techniques to safeguard the ten Nascent Soul cultivators of the Sulling Sect as safely as possible through the Demon-Suppressing Trial.

This was something only sects with sufficient foundation could accomplish.

And it also required boldness!

After all, in Luo Chen's view, arranging those protective techniques to suit Nascent Soul cultivators was a very mentally taxing task for the Nascent Soul elders.

For instance, the fragment of the Plain Sword spirit treasure that Dean He had received earlier would also harm Tai Su Lingjun's cultivation.

So who would provide protection for Luo Chen?

The answer was Elder Tiange!

An old man in a golden robe with a stern expression walked up to Luo Chen.

On that stern face, however, there was now a hint of appreciation.

"Come with me!"

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

Because it seemed the others didn't need to leave the Plain King Hall; they could receive their gifts from their elders right inside the hall.

Yet he was the one called out.

While he was puzzled, he caught the gaze of his cheap master, Yao Xi Zhenren, which was full of encouragement.

Luo Chen understood in his heart—perhaps this would be a good thing.

He calmly rose and followed Elder Tiange out of the main hall.

As the residence of the sect master, Tai Su Lingjun, the Plain King Hall stood atop the highest peak of the Sulling Sect, surrounded by thick clouds and mist.

The surging clouds and mist, like waves, formed a vast sea of clouds.

Two figures walked one after another, parting the sea of clouds as they went, until they reached the edge of a cliff.

Standing before the towering, precipitous cliff, Luo Chen quietly awaited what was to come.

After a long while, Elder Tiange finally spoke in a resonant, forceful voice, getting straight to the point.

"Do you know why you are different from the others? They are all being rewarded with protective magical treasures, yet you alone were called out by me?"

Luo Chen shook his head. "Disciple does not know."

Elder Tiange turned around, his brow slightly furrowed. "This doesn't quite match the rumors of the exceptionally intelligent and talented Xuan Luozi!"

Recalling the cultivation technique practiced by Elder Tiange's lineage, a thought surfaced in Luo Chen's mind, and he tentatively said, "Could it be related to the disciple's recent comprehension of the true meaning of the law?"

"Now that's more like it!" Elder Tiange rarely showed a smile, even stroking his beard, which resembled a sword tassel, taking on the appearance of a kindly elder.

However, his temperament was indeed not well-suited to the kindly elder style.

Thus, Elder Tiange quickly withdrew his smile, his expression turning serious.

"Next, I will execute a divine ability technique. Watch and learn. How much you comprehend is up to you."

With that, without waiting for Luo Chen's reaction.

His aura suddenly exploded. He brought his hands together, fingers forming sword gestures, and began to trace in the void like a silver hook carving iron paintings.

Instantly, a sharp, overbearing aura shot into the sky.

It was as if a sharp sword had torn through the sky!

The heavens and earth trembled, and then vast, boundless origin qi of heaven and earth came rolling in.

Luo Chen watched this scene, his eyes unconsciously narrowing.

This starting posture was clearly the gold-attribute divine ability described in the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Profound Chapter*—the **Realm of Weapons Torrent**!

He also finally understood why Elder Tiange was personally responsible for him.

In others' eyes, Luo Chen also cultivated the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Profound Chapter*, sharing the same origin as Elder Tiange's lineage.

Now he had also comprehended the Gold Origin.

He already possessed the qualifications to learn law-based divine abilities!

Therefore, while the other Nascent Soul cultivators were rewarded with protective magical treasures, he, Luo Chen, was rewarded with a divine ability for protection.

And it wasn't just a reward; there was also an intention to teach.

"It seems the Sulling Sect has high expectations of me! But that's fine too; I was also lacking a gold-attribute divine ability to have on hand."

Luo Chen murmured to himself. Unbeknownst to him, a faint golden light had already appeared in his eyes.

With the wonder of his Crimson Eye Golden Pupil, he could better see every detail of Elder Tiange's technique execution.

In his eyes, Elder Tiange's originally extremely fast movements suddenly became incomparably slow.

From the surging of mana, to the interweaving of divine sense, to resonating with heaven and earth, and controlling the flow of origin qi—many details were laid bare before him, like a woman's naked body, without any secrets.

When all kinds of strange and varied weapons and blades emerged in the sky,

this technique had already taken shape!

Countless weapons hung in the sky, filled with a sharp and fierce aura.

It was as if, in the next moment, they would descend like a torrential flood, destroying everything in their path.

"This is the Realm of Weapons Torrent. Watch carefully, Xuan Luozi!"

Elder Tiange's voice was still in his ears.

Then, he pointed with his sword fingers, and the torrential flood tilted down, heading straight for Luo Chen.

For a moment, Luo Chen even felt a hint of threat.

Clearly, the killing power of this technique was extremely strong; otherwise, it wouldn't have made Luo Chen, who possessed the Chaos Indestructible Body, feel this way.

"Compared to the many divine abilities I possess, this technique's power surpasses my Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, Qi Sea Floating Heaven, and Thousand Rivers Guide. It's roughly on par with my self-created Starfire Scorches the World, only weaker than that one technique requiring a massive amount of Nine Firmament Qi and a hundred years of accumulation—the Thousand Firmaments Converging Flow."

"But most importantly, the conception of this divine ability is very clever. It's not just about simply controlling the origin qi of heaven and earth; it also materializes the prototypes of weapons, simulating the charm of a realm's power in attack."

"Speaking of which, it's not that I lack gold-attribute divine abilities, but that one technique requires the use of flesh and blood power to execute, which is a bit too primitive."

"This technique suits me better."

As thoughts surged in Luo Chen's mind, the Realm of Weapons Torrent was already upon him.

But he was not panicked.

Because he knew the other party wouldn't truly want to kill him.

Sure enough!

As it drew closer, Elder Tiange's sword fingers interchanged, beginning an extremely complex operation.

Under his control, the torrential flood rapidly shrank, eventually transforming into a single golden halberd, suspended before Luo Chen.

At that moment, the fierce wind gusted, and the young man's robes rustled.

Elder Tiange looked at this scene with great satisfaction. "Facing the might of an entire realm's attack without changing expression—excellent mental fortitude!"

Then he flicked his finger, and the golden halberd landed in Luo Chen's palm.

After doing all this, Elder Tiange's face showed signs of fatigue. Clearly, this kind of exertion was also very draining on his origin qi.

Luo Chen raised his hand, looking at the golden halberd mark in his palm, and asked doubtfully, "A divine ability seed?"

Elder Tiange waved his hand. "This doesn't count as a seed. At best, it's just a primer."

"A primer?" Luo Chen showed confusion, but in his heart, he suddenly understood.

He had thought as much. If he, who possessed many divine abilities and had studied them for so many years, couldn't successfully construct a divine ability seed, how could an ordinary Divine Transformation great power easily do so?

The word "primer" was more appropriate.

Seeing Luo Chen's confusion, Elder Tiange briefly explained.

"This is the starting posture of the Realm of Weapons Torrent, condensed from my mana combined with the origin qi of heaven and earth. You can use the law origin power you have comprehended to drive it, executing the Realm of Weapons Torrent once."

"But remember, there is only one chance. Unless you encounter someone you absolutely cannot defeat, do not use it lightly."

"Moreover, this primer does not last long. As time passes, the origin qi of heaven and earth within it cannot be sealed, and it will eventually dissipate into the void."

Luo Chen nodded in understanding.

Even if it was just a primer, it benefited him greatly.

The technique involved was not too complicated. Perhaps he could learn from it and use it later in his research on constructing divine ability seeds.

At the same time, it made him realize that even ordinary Divine Transformation great powers had their unique insights in cultivation and combat.

At least, he himself wouldn't have thought of leaving a divine ability primer on a cultivator's body.

Because the power of divine abilities was too strong, and cultivators' bodies were weak. One misstep could backfire and harm the host.

The two divine abilities he had left in the Luo Tian Sect back then were specifically arranged using formations and treasures.

Actually, that made sense.

Since they could cultivate to the Divine Transformation level, how could they be called "ordinary"?

Although his own strength was formidable, his cultivation experience was still too shallow compared to the billions of years of history in the Mystic Realm. Perhaps he should communicate more with high-level cultivators in the future.

While Luo Chen was lost in thought, Elder Tiange finally couldn't help but ask, "When you watched me execute the technique just now, did you learn anything?"

Luo Chen thought for a moment and replied, "Not much. I only understood how to use my own mana to leverage the origin qi of heaven and earth."

"Is that all?"

Elder Tiange was somewhat disappointed.

But in an instant, he smiled self-deprecatingly.

"I suppose I expected too much of you. To comprehend the true meaning of the law at your age, and to understand how to leverage the origin qi of heaven and earth after seeing me execute the technique just once, is already commendable. Why should I force it?"

"It's just a pity that time is too short for me to teach you much."

"After the Demon-Suppressing Trial, when you return, I will teach you some things!"

Luo Chen bowed deeply, his tone sincere.

"Thank you, Elder, for bestowing the technique!"

This gratitude was genuine, without any pretense.

Even just the method of constructing a short-term divine ability primer was very useful to him.

Elder Tiange accepted Luo Chen's thanks calmly.

Then he led Luo Chen back to the Plain King Hall.

Before entering the hall, he suddenly reminded him, "Although divine abilities are powerful, they are ultimately too close to heaven and earth. You are only at the Nascent Soul stage now. Do not learn and attempt them too frequently, or you may easily be assimilated by heaven and earth, leading to a loss of control over your Nascent Soul!"

Luo Chen replied, "Disciple understands."

He had known these principles for a long time and had even experienced them.

For Nascent Soul cultivators to execute great divine abilities, there was a risk of assimilation, and it was far greater than the risks of daily cultivation!

Elder Tiange glanced at Luo Chen and, seeing that he seemed to have taken it to heart, said no more.

The two of them ultimately had no master-disciple relationship.

However, Elder Tiange had already made up his mind. After the Demon-Suppressing Trial ended, he would definitely apply to the sect master to transfer Luo Chen from Jade Spring Peak to Tiange Peak.

Even if he didn't take him as a disciple, he still wanted to guide him well.

Such an excellent seedling—how could it be wasted!

When Luo Chen returned to the hall, he noticed it was very quiet.

The group of Nascent Soul cultivators who were to participate in the Demon-Suppressing Trial were quietly refining their treasures.

Some were true artifacts, some were talismans, and there were also formation plates, spirit robes, and so on.

And each one was perfectly suited to their individual circumstances.

Luo Chen glanced over. He didn't use his divine sense, but he could still roughly discern the situation.

Most of these items were personally crafted by the Nascent Soul elders, specifically arranged as protective techniques for the Nascent Soul cultivators.

They might not be as powerful as divine abilities, but they far exceeded the capabilities of Nascent Soul cultivators.

Actually, this was the norm!

The higher a cultivator's realm, the more difficult it was to leave behind defensive measures for lower-level cultivators.

The difficulty wasn't about having nothing to give, but about achieving the original owner's own level of power, which required very harsh conditions.

For example, a Divine Transformation great power's strength far exceeded that of a Nascent Soul true person.

But the essential changes were the primordial spirit derived from divine sense, the divine abilities that could control the origin qi of heaven and earth, and the spirit treasures that gave birth to spirituality.

How could these three points be imparted to a Nascent Soul true person?

They couldn't just split their soul, or bestow a treasure that sealed the power of a spirit treasure, could they?

Both of these would affect the Nascent Soul cultivator's own cultivation.

For Tai Su Lingjun to cut off part of her spirit treasure's spirituality and give away a fragment of the spirit treasure's light was a great loss to the foundation of her Plain Sword.

If not for the long-term interests of the sect, she certainly wouldn't have been willing.

Perhaps she hoped that during this Demon-Suppressing Trial, the wisp of the Plain Sword's spirit treasure light wouldn't be used, and she could retrieve it later using a secret technique.

As for bestowing divine abilities, that was even more of a fantasy.

Elder Tiange could only bestow a divine ability primer, and that was based on Luo Chen having comprehended the corresponding law origin himself.

"It's a good thing, though. At least during the Demon-Suppressing Trial, cultivators from other sects won't be able to produce Divine Transformation-level or higher methods, so I won't have to expose my martial arts cultivation at the Destruction Realm."

Luo Chen smiled to himself, then naturally found a cushion in the corner where he had been sitting earlier and sat down.

The other Divine Transformation elders were somewhat surprised by how quickly they had returned.

After communicating with Elder Tiange through divine sense and learning that Luo Chen had understood how to use mana to leverage the origin qi of heaven and earth, they all cast appreciative glances at him.

Clearly, they also believed Luo Chen had exceptional talent.

As long as he could remove the demonic contamination, he would surely be a future Divine Transformation seed.

Some even had another thought: perhaps they shouldn't let Luo Chen participate in the dangerous Demon-Suppressing Trial. The sect master could ask experts from the demon clan's remnant lineage to use the first method to remove the demonic contamination for him.

After all, he had already comprehended the law origin and didn't need the sudden enlightenment power of spiritual guidance.

If Luo Chen knew that comprehending the Gold Origin would lead to such complications, he would probably be at a loss for words.

However, these thoughts were all rejected by the sect master.

This surprised the Divine Transformation elders.

To this, Tai Su Lingjun only replied lightly, "Since a decision has been made, how can it be easily changed? Let him go and gain some experience. It's also a good thing."

Time flew by.

By the time night fell, the process of refining the treasures in the hall had finally come to an end.

When Jiang Huang, who had the lowest cultivation level, finally established a connection with that spirit robe, the atmosphere in the hall relaxed.

Tai Su Lingjun looked at everyone with satisfaction and cleared her throat.

"You are all the most outstanding disciples of my Sulling Sect. This Demon-Suppressing Trial is not only about personal cultivation but also about the rise and fall of the sect. Therefore, you must do your utmost, strive to gain something in the trial, and absolutely not disgrace the sect's reputation!"

The ten Nascent Soul cultivators stood up and said in unison, "We obey the sect master's decree!"

Tai Su Lingjun waved her hand. "Alright, that's all for today! Elder Gumu, I leave the rest to you."

With that, she left the hall.

The other Nascent Soul elders also departed one after another.

Only Elder Gumu remained.

He looked at everyone and showed a kind smile.

"One month from now, gather at the mountain gate. You will accompany me to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!"

(End of Chapter)
