# Chapter 1196: Dividing the Flesh, Returning to the Immortal Tower

The golden scales transformed into blades, descending from the heavens.

It was the purest form of power!

Ignoring everything, crushing all!

Anything that dared stand in its way would be torn to shreds!

Screams of agony rang out one after another.

The Primordial Magnetic Divine Power swept back toward the Bitter Wind Old Monster.

The blood-colored thunderclaps produced no explosions, only mournful cries.

The River of Forgetfulness was severed midstream, leaving only the old man's expression of despair.

And those beings known as epoch monsters, beneath this descending golden blade, seemed to have been sentenced to death.

In a tragically magnificent manner, they bloomed with the final brilliant radiance of their lives within the Ancient God Dao Field.

But Golden Punishment Venerable's move, capable of slaughtering existences below the Destruction Realm, could not ignore the peak strikes of the four Destruction Kings.

A hazy green light diffused from the ancient mirror brimming with immortal intent, silently passing through the golden blade and enveloping its ten-thousand-zhang body. As the green light trembled, it seemed to breathe, actually pulling a phantom soul-shadow from within its body bit by bit!

That was Golden Punishment Venerable's primordial spirit!

It naturally refused to sit idly and await death, struggling madly.

Not only did its primordial spirit struggle, but its ten-thousand-zhang main body also thrashed about, slamming into Carefree Realm Master.

The immense force repeatedly cracked the ancient mirror.

Carefree Realm Master's face no longer bore its leisurely expression; his eyes blazed with fervent flames as he poured all his power into the strange treasure in his hands, willing to let it shatter if necessary.

The other three attacks, while he and Golden Punishment Venerable were locked in stalemate, descended simultaneously.

Corpse Old Demon pointed a finger at himself.

Then his black robe exploded, transforming into surging black qi that rolled over and plunged into Golden Punishment Venerable's massive body.

Immediately after, the sharp sound of bone spurs piercing skin rang out.

Chi! Chi! Chi!

One after another, hideous bones, mixed with flesh and blood, burst forth from Golden Punishment Venerable's physical body.

Those bones and flesh seemed to possess a will of their own, constantly extending and retracting, as if trying to shrink back.

But then Heaven Cry Old Monster raised his head and wailed at the sky, waving his great banner.

The condensed-to-extreme Unforgivable Heavenly Wind, carrying the divine will and astral qi of dozens of Martial Dao Human Immortals, swept back across the horizon.

As it fell upon Golden Punishment Venerable's body, those protruding, ferocious bones were visibly ground away inch by inch.

This trend not only failed to weaken but instead expanded, rapidly spreading to the exposed flesh and blood.

The Unforgivable Heavenly Wind dissolved bones and corroded flesh!

Even though Golden Punishment Venerable desperately bloomed with golden light to block the wind's corrosion, it was like trying to put out a blazing cartload of firewood with a cup of water.

Then came the Star Giant, wielding a primitive war halberd as it charged forward, striking at the seven-inch vital point.

Clang! Clang! Clang! Clang! Clang!

Five layers of golden shackles abruptly emerged, blocking the war halberd's path.

Yet as they clashed, the primitive war halberd pressed forward undaunted, shattering again and again, only to reform again and again.

It was determined to cleave it in two with a single strike!

Amidst this deadlock.

Suddenly, a fist came through the air!

This fist appeared ordinary at first glance, yet it seemed to contain the true essence of power.

In an upright and dignified manner, it heavily slammed into Golden Punishment Venerable's body.

Dong!

A dull sound suddenly rang out.

The next moment, Golden Punishment Venerable began to tremble violently at an uncontrollable frequency, like a sieve.

All resistance paused for an instant.

The Star Giant seized the opportunity, swinging its primitive war halberd, gathering boundless starlight as if cleaving open a river of stars, and struck down from the sky.

Kacha!

That ten-thousand-zhang body was severed in two!

Then, the Unforgivable Heavenly Wind and Black Demon Astral Qi each followed the cuts, rushing into Golden Punishment Venerable's body.

An indescribable agony surged forth.

"ROAR!!!"

A sound like a cornered beast's final cry echoed through the Ancient God Dao Field.

Carefree Realm Master's expression changed drastically. "Not good, he's trying to escape!"

In the midst of this terrifying struggle, he could no longer control Golden Punishment Venerable's primordial spirit.

That ancient mirror brimming with immortal intent shattered into pieces.

A massive primordial spirit emerged from within Golden Punishment Venerable's body. It cast a furious and terrified glance at everyone, then transformed into golden light and dove into the spatial rift from which it had come.

The interception measures Corpse Old Demon had arranged along the way had no effect whatsoever; it passed straight through.

The moment it escaped.

Within the Dao Field, five streams of light shot into the sky.

Carefree Realm Master grabbed Golden Punishment Venerable's head with a claw, twisted and pulled, tearing it off.

Starlight and heavenly wind surged together, falling upon the upper half of the body as a tug-of-war began.

On the lower half of the body, a surge of black demonic qi and a stream of blood-colored astral qi refused to yield to each other, colliding fiercely.

"Mine!"

"Get lost!"

Amidst the cold shouts, a piercing clang rang out.

Zheng!

A dazzling sword light shot into the sky, wrapped in blood-colored astral qi, and heavily struck the half-section of the body.

Rip...

The massive body was severed in two.

Two figures abruptly separated.

Luo Chen swept his gaze over the crowd, especially meeting Carefree Realm Master's eyes, then without hesitation, he dragged the three-thousand-zhang tail and rapidly retreated along the path he had come.

Corpse Old Demon revealed his form, looking at the Golden Punishment Venerable flesh beside him that was less than a thousand zhang long, his face full of fury.

"Don't think you can escape!"

He was about to give chase when a flash of green light appeared before him, revealing an elegant figure.

Corpse Old Demon saw the one blocking his path and a murderous aura flickered across his face.

"Carefree Realm Master, don't stop me!"

Carefree Realm Master sighed. "It's enough."

"Not enough!" Corpse Old Demon said coldly. "That bastard snatched nearly a third of the True Spirit's flesh, and on top of that, he destroyed the clone I left outside. How can I let him escape?"

Carefree Realm Master did not yield an inch. "If you insist on pursuing and killing him, then your next opponent will be me!"

Corpse Old Demon's anger flared. "Are you really going to be my enemy for that bastard's sake?"

Carefree Realm Master shook his head. "It's not about being your enemy, but about considering the overall situation."

As he spoke, he gestured for Corpse Old Demon to look around.

On the battlefield, corpses lay everywhere, and wails filled the air.

Of the original hundred-plus Martial Dao Human Immortals, less than ten or twenty percent were still alive.

The battle had been truly tragic.

Especially that thirty-three-layer Divine Mother Hall, which was also unstable, emitting streams of milky-white qi.

Finally!

Carefree Realm Master pointed at his own body and Corpse Old Demon's.

Golden light still clung to them like stubborn parasites, lingering without fading.

That was Golden Punishment Venerable's strongest move—the Golden Punishment Great Execution!

Even though this move had been suppressed to the extreme by the Guixu World, even though its power had been divided into a hundred portions, it had still inflicted injuries on them, the Destruction Kings.

"Under the current circumstances, we're already short-handed, and our own consumption is great. If you insist on fighting, Luo Chen is no pushover either. You saw that punch, didn't you?"

Corpse Old Demon's expression was somewhat unnatural, and there was even a hint of fear in his eyes.

Carefree Realm Master continued, "If you fight him, it's hard to say who will win, but it's very likely a mutual defeat. If that situation arises, how will we control the Divine Mother Hall? How will we leave Guixu?"

Before Corpse Old Demon could object, he raised his hand in the void.

Golden Punishment Venerable's terrifying head floated before them.

"Although we failed to successfully hunt the Great Vehicle True Spirit, we've at least obtained most of its flesh. With the residual laws on it, plus the Divine Mother Hall, we have a good chance of leaving. Are you really going to ignore the current situation?"

Corpse Old Demon fell silent.

Just then, the struggle at another location also came to an end.

Heavenly Mystery Array of Stars and Heaven Cry Old Monster each flew over carrying a portion of Golden Punishment Venerable's flesh.

Although their auras were weak, their moods were extremely good.

They hadn't fought to the death; after a brief exchange, they had decided through verbal communication to divide the flesh equally.

Faced with Corpse Old Demon's failure to snatch the flesh from Luo Chen, although somewhat surprised, they didn't press the matter. Instead, they began discussing their next course of action.

The general trend was difficult to resist, and Corpse Old Demon ultimately held back.

Carefree Realm Master looked in the direction Luo Chen had left, letting out a relieved sigh.

The favor he had once bestowed had been repaid by Luo Chen's crucial blow that broke the deadlock. The debt of gratitude was now settled.

From now on, each must fend for themselves.

"Fellow Daoist Luo Chen, I hope we can meet again outside one day!"

---

At the exit of the Ancient God Dao Field.

A piece of flesh over three thousand zhang long crashed heavily to the ground.

Its scales were gone, leaving only torn skin and exposed bones and blood.

Starlight flowed across it, growing dimmer as it moved further from its owner.

Such a massive object could not be stored in a storage ring alone.

Luo Chen held the Yuantu Sword, opened his mouth, and spat out a small gray cauldron.

The small cauldron spun in the air, scattering a hazy gray light that enveloped the dragon-like, python-like flesh, pulling it in zhang by zhang.

The entire process required no effort.

Seeing this, Luo Chen was not surprised at all.

Before his ascension, he had refined three fifth-rank divine mountains into the Primordial Unity Cauldron.

Among them was the Southern Frontier's Mi Jie Mountain!

As a divine mountain containing countless spatial nodes, Mi Jie Mountain was itself one of the strongest spatial treasures in the world.

After absorbing it, the Primordial Unity Cauldron had gained similar effects.

Basic storage functions were naturally no problem.

"Hoo..."

Opening his mouth, he swallowed the small cauldron, which turned into a tiny black dot and returned to his body.

Luo Chen looked back at the Ancient God Dao Field.

It was too far away to see the battlefield.

But in the sky, spatial cracks like centipedes were shockingly visible.

"Even the most stable spatial interstices, under such intense battle, are ultimately heading toward destruction."

"Time is running out for them."

"And time for me..."

Luo Chen touched his waist.

There, a golden light flickered faintly, almost cutting his entire body in two. Layer after layer of Blood-Refining Divine Astral surged over, but were all blocked by the golden light.

Golden Punishment Venerable's devastating strike was not easy to withstand.

Even though Luo Chen and the others had severely wounded it, they hadn't come out unscathed.

If Golden Punishment Venerable had chosen not to abandon its physical body and flee, but to stay and fight to the death, the final outcome would have been uncertain.

One could only say that a Great Vehicle True Spirit suppressed by the Guixu World was still a Great Vehicle True Spirit—not so easily dealt with.

That they had managed to gain something was due, aside from their strength, mostly to that relentless, death-defying madness of the Guixu martial artists.

For Luo Chen, the greatest gain from this trip might not be part of Golden Punishment Venerable's flesh, but rather...

"Complete laws can indeed break free from Guixu's shackles!"

Golden Punishment Venerable's free movement was proof!

And Luo Chen also possessed complete laws!

That was the Primordial Unity Cauldron!

Taking one last look at the Ancient God Dao Field, Luo Chen put away the Yuantu Sword and, without hesitation, stepped onto the stone platform and turned to leave.

---

Mofeng City!

As one of the border cities of the Great Destruction Realm, although it was a bitterly cold place, it had once been bustling with activity due to several unique mineral deposits.

But many years had passed, and it had long become dilapidated.

On the long streets, there was not a single figure.

The originally towering and sturdy city walls had also collapsed in large sections at some unknown time.

Suddenly, a heavenly wind swept by, followed by thin mist slowly rolling in.

Within the mist, faint, terrifying low growls and roars could be heard.

Suddenly!

A few streaks of blood light appeared, and those low growls and roars instantly vanished.

Then, a figure emerged from the thin mist.

Dressed in white, with sword-like brows and bright eyes, his handsome face bore traces of wind and frost.

Looking back at the path he had come from, already obscured by the thin mist, Luo Chen's expression was grim.

"The epoch is approaching; the Destruction Mist is already visible everywhere. Ordinary people simply cannot survive."

Ten years!

It had taken him a full ten years to traverse the vast Guixu from the Ancient God Dao Field back to the Great Destruction Realm.

This speed was many times slower than when he had gone.

This was by no means due to the injuries Luo Chen bore.

Golden Punishment Venerable's move had indeed caused him immense suffering, and he had not yet managed to eliminate it.

But it wasn't enough to affect his normal travel.

What truly affected him was the increasingly harsh environment of Guixu.

The heavenly winds that blew all day, the frequent spatial cracks, the earth-fire baleful qi erupting everywhere, the soul-shaking thunder sounds, the icy-cold ghost rain, and even the Destruction Mist that had grown into a force of its own.

All of this was now the norm in Guixu.

Even someone as strong as Luo Chen could not ignore it.

When he heard the low growls and roars coming from the mist again, Luo Chen frowned and cursed.

"Troublesome!"

Those mist beasts, though seemingly weak to him, were also extremely troublesome.

Because as long as the mist did not disperse, the mist beasts would continuously resurrect and revive, simply inexhaustible.

One could imagine that when the Destruction Mist swept across all of Guixu, all living beings within it would be besieged and killed by mist beasts.

How were these things even generated?

Luo Chen no longer had the mood to study them. The priority was to rush back to Carefree City.

The deeper he went into the Great Destruction Realm, the more evident the situation in the seventy-two cities became.

The outer cities, like Mofeng City, had been gradually abandoned by humans.

The inner cities, though overcrowded, were filled with panic.

Everyone was barely surviving, hiding beneath sturdy city walls.

When he arrived at Carefree City, what he saw was the most common and ugliest side of human nature.

Arguments, infighting, wanton burning, killing, and looting.

Only the Fang family's ancestral land could provide a moment of peace.

"This is probably the reason Fang Qingxue stayed behind!"

The influence of a Martial Dao Human Immortal, after all, inspired fear, buying the Fang clan's members a final period of peace.

Luo Chen did not intervene in the various chaotic scenes, choosing to turn a blind eye.

He went straight to outside the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

Layer after layer of chaotic energy erupted from within the Immortal Tower.

Even the Destruction Mist could not approach.

This situation was far more dangerous than before.

If the external chaotic energy was leaking so much, one could imagine how turbulent it was inside.

He wondered how many martial artists were still alive and cultivating within.

Luo Chen immediately sat cross-legged and dispersed his Martial Dao divine will.

At the same time, at the formation hub of the Ninefold Immortal Tower, another "Luo Chen" holding the Golden Erosion Formation Diagram opened his eyes.

He pointed a finger at the formation diagram and softly commanded.

"Open!"

In an instant, the earth shook and the mountains trembled, and brilliant light burst forth.

The external Luo Chen rose to his feet and, under the assault of layers of chaotic energy, forcibly entered the Ninefold Immortal Tower.

At that very moment.

All the martial artists still alive within the Destruction Nine Rings felt a vast and familiar will emerge.

"He's back!"
