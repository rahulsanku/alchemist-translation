# Chapter 1260: A Display of Force, Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign

“The primordial spirit is strong enough to carry mountains—how can it be stabilized?”

“Nine-Peak Rock specifically counters divine thought; it is sufficient to stabilize and capture it.”

“In great destruction there is new life—how should that be interpreted?”

“Interpret it as nirvana!”

“Nirvana?”

“Placing oneself in a desperate situation to seek survival, or distancing oneself from inverted dreams, or even abiding in wondrous accord, rooted in profound oneness—where things are not different from me, and I am not different from things, where things and self mysteriously converge, returning to the limitless… all such things are nirvana.”

“You seem to have considerable insight into this path?”

“I dabbled in it during my cultivation. Applied to alchemy, it’s merely different paths converging to the same end.”

“Mm. And this part—how did you think about it?”

…

In a side hall of the Pill Saint Hall, two figures sat cross-legged facing each other, before them a large number of papers recording various theoretical details.

The two were none other than Luo Chen and Chu Yan.

What they were discussing was the Spirit-Stabilizing Pill.

A sixth-grade pill. Even Chu Yan could not rush into refining it hastily; every preparation had to be made.

First, studying the pharmacology, the required alchemical arts, and even the habits of the corresponding medicinal ingredients—all were necessary matters.

In this regard, Luo Chen had previously studied the Spirit-Stabilizing Pill formula, and he himself had considerable expertise in the soul aspect. Thus, he could often offer Chu Yan some unique insights.

And so, with the two discussing day and night without pause, combined with that complete pill formula, in just a few days they had worked out seven or eight parts of it.

“Next comes the refining.”

Chu Yan suddenly reached out and turned all the papers recording the research details to ashes.

Luo Chen nodded, then asked with slight hesitation, “With the wealth of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, they should be able to collect all the required medicinal ingredients, right?”

Chu Yan was somewhat surprised. “Why ask only now?”

Luo Chen said matter-of-factly, “Because I didn’t expect you to be in such a hurry, planning to refine it directly right now.”

“With such a wonderful formula before me, how could I hold back? Besides…” Chu Yan gave Luo Chen a deep, meaningful look. “Based on the theoretical direction of this pill, the general effects of the finished pill can be deduced. How could I waste your painstaking efforts?”

From beginning to end, she never asked where Luo Chen had obtained the pill formula, nor did she ask about its specific effects.

But from the details of their discussion, she had already understood Luo Chen’s intentions.

Since that was the case, why delay?

“Then let’s begin!” Luo Chen said with a carefree smile.

“This pill involves many medicinal ingredients. I’ll need to have the cultivators of the Pill Saint Hall gather some for me. The basic materials are available in the hall. You take charge of the preliminary refining work first. Later, when all the ingredients are fully prepared, I’ll take over.”

Luo Chen nodded without the slightest hesitation, showing no fear.

Although he had always been the one presiding over alchemy, with others assisting him, assisting Chu Yan was hardly a grievance for him. Moreover, he was confident that his profoundly deep alchemical foundation was sufficient to complete all the preliminary refining work.

“After all, it’s a sixth-grade pill. Ordinary alchemy tools cannot bear it. Use my Sun-Moon Pot!”

Chu Yan vaguely sensed that Luo Chen’s reluctance to use his own customary alchemy tools might involve some concerns.

So she generously summoned her life-bound treasure, the Sun-Moon Pot.

She even specially employed techniques to allow Luo Chen to temporarily control this spirit treasure—a clear display of complete trust.

And before truly beginning, she reminded Luo Chen once more.

“This alchemy may take a long time and could delay your participation in the Heavenly Origin Grand Ceremony.”

“Then please trouble you, in the name of the Pill Saint, to notify my fellow sect members.”

“Alright!”

With everything ready, Luo Chen directly began refining the Spirit-Stabilizing Pill.

To be honest, this was remarkably fast.

In his entire life, Luo Chen had refined countless pills. The higher the grade, the more time the preliminary preparation and study took. Never had it been this swift.

But neither had he ever been this confident.

Only because he had Chu Yan, a master of the alchemical path, by his side—like a stabilizing sea anchor. Even without borrowing the attribute panel to get started, he was full of confidence.

As for the so-called Heavenly Origin Grand Ceremony, missing it was no great loss.

Once the Spirit-Stabilizing Pill was refined, he could also leave the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect with peace of mind, leave the Su Ling Sect, leave this Youxuan Continent that had bound him hand and foot.

…

Time flew. In the blink of an eye, a full month had passed since the cultivators of the Demon-Suppressing Trial had entered the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Over this past month, all the cultivators had experienced the most anxious and stifling period of their lives.

Being interrogated—no matter what realm a cultivator was in, it was unpleasant. The higher the realm, the more unbearable.

But this situation had finally been endured.

In the end, nothing was found. There was no case of a high-rank demon spirit possessing someone and escaping the Dark Demon Cave.

It could be considered a false alarm.

And to “calm the nerves” of everyone, the Heavenly Origin Grand Ceremony was quickly prepared and was about to be held.

On the eve of the ceremony, the cultivators of the Su Ling Sect gathered once more.

Through inquiries by Elder Gumu, they roughly learned the procedure of the next day’s ceremony.

The overall focus was on congratulating the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign on emerging from seclusion, possibly accompanied by some internal lectures from the Great Sovereign of the Integration stage.

The only part that involved them was one segment.

That was the reward!

And Elder Gumu had also learned the content of the rewards in advance.

“Congratulations, Master He. Although that Demon-Transforming Phase Pill is not famous, it is a miraculous medicine that us Divine Transformation cultivators dream of. Within the entire Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, only the Pill Saint can refine it. With this pill’s assistance, your future breakthrough to the Void Refining stage will have a few more chances of success.”

Dean He was filled with excitement. “All of this depends on the sect’s cultivation.”

Elder Gumu waved his hand and looked at the others. “You also have rewards. I won’t reveal the specifics, but they will greatly benefit your cultivation. This time, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has truly spared no expense to appease us.”

Yao Xi tentatively asked, “And what about my disciple, Xuan Luo?”

Elder Gumu laughed heartily. “Being able to follow by the Pill Saint’s side for a period is already a tremendous reward. Why worry? Besides, given the Pill Saint’s favor toward Xuan Luozi, what is due will naturally not be lacking.”

Yao Xi breathed a sigh of relief.

The others also relaxed, looking forward to the next day’s ceremony.

They had heard that not only would they see the esteemed Integration Great Sovereign, but also some Dao Sect experts who had become famous in earlier years would appear at this ceremony.

Those people were all powerful beings at the Nascent Soul and Void Refining stages.

For ordinary Core Formation cultivators, this might be a spectacle they would never see in a lifetime.

Being able to witness it in person, even without those rewards, would be enough material for future conversation.

The night passed without incident.

The next day.

As the six bells tolled and the warm sunlight spilled down, the Heavenly Origin Grand Ceremony slowly began.

…

“It’s starting!”

The sound of the six bells was long and far-reaching, as if it could pierce through all restrictions—at least within the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

Any cultivator within the mountain gate could hear this sound.

Including the Pill Saint Hall!

Although Luo Chen did not care much about this grand event, he still felt some regret. He had seen Void Refining Lords and even fought against Great Vehicle True Spirits, but he had never witnessed an Integration Great Sovereign.

This time, he would miss it.

Beside him, Chu Yan seemed oblivious, only suddenly reminding him, “Next, we must reverse yin and yang, overturn sun and moon. Remember the method I taught you?”

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment, and the hand seals in his hands immediately began to change.

Not only that, but from his mouth, he spat out a brilliant, deep red flame that began to scorch the Sun-Moon Pot.

This was the final step before the pill was formed!

The true flame required was extremely vast, and it could not be interrupted.

Chu Yan glanced at him, a flicker of surprise flashing in her eyes.

“Vermilion Bird Divine Flame?”

Although this step required the strongest flame possible, she had not expected Luo Chen to be able to spit out Vermilion Bird Divine Flame.

And judging by its aura, although it was not as powerful as the Vermilion Bird Lord of the Dao Sect, in terms of purity, there seemed to be no difference between the two.

The surprise passed in a flash. Chu Yan held nothing back. Her small mouth opened slightly, and a wisp of flame like white frost slowly drifted out, enveloping the other half of the Sun-Moon Pot.

Even though Luo Chen had seen Chu Yan use this flame several times during the earlier refining of the Demon-Transforming Phase Pill, he could not help but admire it now.

Precious Frost Flame!

Beautiful and magnificent, as clear and pure as frost and snow.

Yet within that clarity, it contained a terrifying power that could freeze all things and burn everything to powder.

Luo Chen could imagine how lethal this flame would be in the hands of a cultivator skilled in combat with ice attributes.

Although Chu Yan did not waste it by using it for alchemy—on the contrary, it was like a tiger gaining wings—in his eyes, as someone skilled in combat, its development was still insufficient.

Each had their own thoughts, but their cooperation was seamless.

Under the superimposed burning of the Vermilion Bird Divine Flame and the Precious Frost Flame, the Sun-Moon Pot began to tremble faintly.

A wisp of wondrous pill fragrance gradually wafted out from within.

When cultivators smelled it, they felt both stirred and yet quite at ease.

The Spirit-Stabilizing Pill was about to be completed!

…

Yao Mountain!

The forbidden land of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, and also its sacred land.

This was the place where the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign went into seclusion.

On ordinary days, let alone cultivators from other sects, even the sect’s own disciples could not easily set foot here.

But today, the mountain gate was wide open, filled with a peaceful and celebratory atmosphere.

Figures strolled through the mountain, like pilgrims heading toward the summit.

Cultivators came and went in an unbroken stream, each exuding a powerful aura.

Those Core Formation cultivators who were still considered figures in the outside world now trembled like ants.

The Nascent Soul elders of each sect stood at the front of their disciples, occasionally introducing the Dao Sect experts who passed by.

“That is Daoist Huawu, at the Nascent Soul stage, long renowned.”

“That female great one who just passed by is named Xianghua. Don’t be fooled by her youthful appearance; she actually has the powerful cultivation of late-stage Nascent Soul and is very likely the next Dao Sect expert to break through to the Void Refining stage.”

“You know Cave Master Honghai, right? I didn’t expect him to come either. He’s been cultivating outside all this time.”

“Xingzhi and Zhaohui are arriving together. It is said that these two studied Buddhist scriptures while traveling in other continents and have now returned to the sect.”

One Divine Transformation great one after another walked on Yao Mountain like ordinary mortals.

Although their expressions varied, each exuded a powerful aura.

The disciples of the various sects who had participated in the Demon-Suppressing Trial were almost dazzled.

Divine Transformation great ones, who were immensely powerful in the outside world, were now before them like cabbages in a market, visible everywhere.

After the shock came numbness.

The strength of the Dao Sect had only existed in words before, but now it was deeply ingrained in their hearts.

Even Dean He, who had already broken through to the Divine Transformation stage, could not help but feel stirred.

Elder Gumu, while introducing them, observed the situation of his disciples. Seeing this scene, he could not help but sigh inwardly.

This so-called Heavenly Origin Grand Ceremony appeared to be a celebration of the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign’s emergence from seclusion and a way to appease the cultivators of the various sects, but was it not also the most blatant display of force?

One could imagine!

The disciples of the various sects attending the ceremony were naturally talented and had survived the Demon-Suppressing Trial. They would surely become the pillars of their respective sects in the future.

Having witnessed the glory and majesty of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect today, they would never harbor any thoughts of resistance in the future.

Even if someone in the future could break through to the Void Refining stage like their sect master, Tai Su Lingjun, or even cultivate to the Integration stage, they would surely remember this scene deeply and treat the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect with utmost respect.

It was an invincible might rooted in everyone’s hearts from childhood!

“Ah well, we’ve reached the summit.”

Sighing inwardly, Elder Gumu led his disciples and the cultivators of other sects to a corner of the summit square of Yao Mountain, where they sat down obediently.

Looking around now, the vast square was like a chessboard.

Dozens or even hundreds of Divine Transformation cultivators were scattered across it like chess pieces.

And in the center, ten chairs were still empty.

The topmost one went without saying—it was for the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign.

The remaining nine were clearly a tier above everyone else, presumably prepared for the Void Refining Lords.

Sure enough!

As a beam of sword light whistled in, everyone rose to their feet.

“Greetings, Nine Yang Sword Lord!”

Nine Yang Sword Lord waved his hand. “Today is the Sect Master’s joyous day—how can order be disrupted? Everyone, sit down!”

He sat down on an outer chair without a care, then looked expectantly toward the entrance of the square.

The disciples of the various sects, without needing their elders to introduce them, knew who had arrived.

Nine Yang Sword Lord!

In the past thousand years, one of only two geniuses of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect to break through to the Void Refining stage, once as famous as the Pill Saint who had ascended from the lower realm.

Moreover, it was said that he too had ascended from the lower realm.

Specializing in the sword path, his strength was not to be underestimated, even as a newly advanced Void Refining Lord.

And the next figure to enter—an aged figure shrouded in a black robe—made everyone’s hearts tighten.

Hundred Poison Lord!

Cultivating poison arts, he had reached the Void Refining stage through this niche path, making people talk about him with fear.

According to legend, this person had also ascended from the lower realm.

Approximately two thousand years ago.

The Divine Transformation elders of the various sects exchanged glances, their hearts filled with indescribable feelings.

Especially the Divine Transformation cultivators of the Five Elements Divine Sect and the Great Soul Sect, whose faces were taut.

The more they learned about these top experts of the Dao Sect, the more they envied the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect for once possessing a complete small world.

That world not only had abundant cultivation resources but was also the best cradle for talent.

Not counting the barbaric era of the opening period, in just the three to four thousand years after it stabilized, it had sent countless Divine Transformation great ones to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

And among those Divine Transformation great ones, several Void Refining Lords had emerged.

Hundred Poison Lord, Nine Yang Sword Lord, Pill Saint Chu Yan…

These were the more recent ones. Of the remaining six Void Refining Lords, aside from the four long-famous War Generals, one of the other two was also suspected to have come from that small world.

And once upon a time, the Five Elements Divine Sect and the Great Soul Sect had also participated in the war to open up the Mountains and Seas Realm.

But in the end, one had come to nothing, while the other had declined to its current miserable state.

Fortunately, it seemed they had lost control of that small world?

As their thoughts churned, the last four people entered simultaneously.

Two old men, a middle-aged man, and a woman in red with a weary expression.

They were the Four Great War Generals of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!

Named after the four sacred beasts, passed down through generations, they guarded the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

As soon as these four appeared, they did not sit down. Instead, they stood respectfully at the front, leading everyone in a chorus of shouts.

“Greetings, Great Sovereign!”

“Greetings, Great Sovereign!”

“Greetings, Great Sovereign!”

Three shouts in succession, spreading far and wide, echoing atop Yao Mountain.

A figure descended from the sky and sat upon the lofty throne.

Wearing a black-and-white Daoist robe, with a long wispy beard hanging beneath his chin, his eyes divine and bright—the image of an immortal with a transcendent bearing instantly imprinted itself in everyone’s eyes.

It was the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign!
