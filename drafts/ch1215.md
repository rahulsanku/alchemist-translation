# Chapter 1215: The Multitude of Living Beings, Ripples in the Dao Heart

On Wonderful Pill Peak.

Within a standalone courtyard, wisps of smoke curled upward and the fragrance of pills drifted through the air.

Six alchemists, each bearing a two-stripe token at their waists, stood inside the courtyard's pill chamber. All gazes converged on the young figure at the center of the room.

Compared to the youthful, rosy-lipped, white-toothed boy of years past, this one had now matured considerably. A hint of steadiness had settled between his brows.

But the six paid no attention to such details. They focused solely on watching him execute one alchemical technique after another, methodically processing various medicinal ingredients.

As time passed, the admiration in their eyes grew increasingly intense.

When they exchanged glances with one another, the appreciation they displayed was utterly undisguised.

"Xuan Luozi's Burning Crane technique is utterly devoid of worldly smoke and fire. It shows his foundation is solid, not merely superficial."

"If you ask me, it was the Cloud Transformation Hand from earlier that was truly astonishing. Like an antelope hanging its horns—utterly untraceable. His proficiency even surpasses ours!"

"Now I finally understand what an alchemy genius looks like!"

"This is genius. It doesn't rely purely on quantity accumulation. He can pick up a technique and use it immediately, and his comprehension speed is astonishing."

"Everyone, does Xuan Luozi have the qualifications to join us?"

"If he doesn't, then no one on the entire Wonderful Pill Peak does, right?"

...

When the young man lifted the furnace lid and a rich pill fragrance wafted out, the private transmissions among the six abruptly ceased.

What followed were sounds of applause and praise.

"Excellent!"

"As expected of a once-in-a-century genius from the Suling Sect!"

"Once a century? Senior Brother Zhu is being too restrained. In this younger sister's opinion, Xuan Luozi's alchemy talent might not appear even once in a millennium for our sect!"

Amidst the chorus of praise, Luo Chen modestly waved his hand. "You all flatter me too much. My skills are still somewhat unrefined. I've troubled everyone today by making you set aside your own work to observe my pill refinement."

He Jun snapped out of his examination of the pill quality. Facing the expectant looks of the others, he nodded heavily.

"The medicinal power is evenly distributed, and the pill's nature is balanced without being too harsh... Second-grade, lower tier!"

Even though everyone had anticipated this result, they still couldn't help revealing expressions of shock.

It wasn't just that Luo Chen had successfully refined a batch of second-grade pills right before their eyes—the success rate was terrifyingly high.

Among those dozen or so pills, only a few were charred. The vast majority had intact coloring and excellent appearance.

What was a genius?

*This* was a genius!

The group clasped their fists in unison toward Luo Chen. "Welcome to our team!"

Luo Chen returned the gesture. "Senior Brothers and Sisters, let us encourage each other in the days ahead!"

He Jun stepped before Luo Chen and said seriously, "Xuan Luozi, it's not that we wanted to make things difficult for you today. We simply needed to fully understand your alchemy level so we can assign suitable tasks for you."

Luo Chen smiled and nodded. "No need to worry. I understand."

Seeing Luo Chen so reasonable, He Jun breathed a sigh of relief.

"Come, let the disciples clean up the pill chamber. We'll go to the main hall for a detailed discussion."

The group left the fragrance-laden pill chamber, chatting and laughing, and headed to the spacious hall next door.

After they took their seats, He Jun began explaining the general situation to Luo Chen.

To refine the Circulation Qi Pill, under the leadership of Grandmaster Gongsun, Wonderful Pill Peak had mobilized all its strength.

Leaving aside the other teams, He Jun's group alone had been allocated the exclusive [Xujing Heaven] courtyard. Several earth-fire veins had been specially channeled there, ranging from first to third grade.

Medicinal resources, manpower, and materials had not been spared either.

Aside from Luo Chen, the six members were all outstanding alchemists who had immersed themselves in the Dao of alchemy for many years, each with their own specialization.

The only woman among the six, Ning Fu, was proficient in pharmacology. She was not only an alchemist in the Alchemy Court but also a pharmacist in the Spirit Medicine Court.

The other five included Zhu Xiao, who was skilled in seventeen different alchemical techniques. His knowledge was comprehensive, allowing him to handle experimental refinement of various pills during the research process.

The others either had superb fire control, possessed powerful divine sense that made them exceptionally good at sensing the optimal moment to open the furnace, or—in one case—had accidentally ingested a strange medicine as a child, granting him a body immune to all poisons. He relied on this to consume various pills and reverse-engineer their compositions, thus embarking on the path of alchemy.

He Jun's alchemy skills were also considerable, but within the team, he functioned more like a major steward.

He was responsible for coordinating all aspects.

Upward, he connected with the Alchemy Court and Grandmaster Gongsun. Downward, he assigned tasks to each member. On a daily basis, he also consolidated information from all sides, formulated plans, and determined the direction of their efforts.

It could be said that even among the hundred or so groups on Wonderful Pill Peak, this team belonged to the most elite batch.

Even if they were given a third-grade pill formula, they would likely have a strong chance of refining it together.

Now, with Luo Chen joining them, it was like adding wings to a tiger.

However, when it came to assigning Luo Chen specific tasks, some disagreements arose.

The truth was, he had performed too well earlier.

"Come with me to study pharmacology. After all, the theoretical direction of our team was originally proposed by Junior Brother Xuan Luozi. He also pointed out the distinction between primary and supplementary ingredients."

"That's not appropriate. Although Xuan Luozi is a genius, understanding pharmacology is a matter of water-grinding effort—it takes time to accumulate. This wouldn't make use of his strengths."

"I'm under a lot of pressure. You all come up with new ideas every few days, and relying on me alone to refine the pills is quite taxing. You've all seen Xuan Luozi's alchemy skills just now. Let him follow me! In the meantime, I can also teach him some new techniques."

"Senior Brother Zhu is too modest. In three years, you've only blown up five furnaces. I wouldn't call that taxing."

"I noticed that Xuan Luozi's control of the earth-fire was quite skilled just now. He could develop further in that area. Mastering ten things is not as good as mastering one..."

Amidst the heated discussion in the hall, He Jun found himself momentarily indecisive.

He looked at Luo Chen.

"Xuan Luozi, what do you think?"

Luo Chen smiled faintly. "I'm fine with anything. I'll follow Senior Brother's arrangements."

He Jun fell into deep thought.

He seemed oblivious to the burning gazes around him.

After pondering for a long while, he finally spoke.

"Everyone, I understand your thoughts somewhat. But let me make one thing clear: if we truly achieve results, everyone will share in the credit. It won't be limited to any single person. But if it all comes to nothing, then it will merely be a vain hope for all of us."

At these words, everyone's expressions shifted slightly.

Clearly, He Jun had hit the nail on the head.

Why were they so eager to recruit Luo Chen? Wasn't it so that this alchemy genius could make breakthroughs in their respective areas of responsibility?

When it came time to distribute rewards, they could claim a larger share.

But as He Jun said, that was all *if* they actually achieved results!

He Jun changed his tone. "As for Xuan Luozi, my idea is that he has just arrived and is still unfamiliar with our current progress. Therefore, I plan to have him study under each of you for a period of time before making a final decision."

Ning Fu couldn't help asking, "Does that include my area too?"

He Jun nodded lightly. "Yes. As you said, the broad theoretical direction was proposed by Xuan Luozi. Although his pharmacological knowledge is insufficient, with you to supplement it, he can learn while working."

Ning Fu turned to Luo Chen, revealing a brilliant smile.

"Junior Brother Xuan Luozi, I look forward to working with you from now on."

The others also voiced their agreement.

Clearly, they had accepted He Jun's arrangement.

A rotating position!

Luo Chen responded modestly while casting an appreciative glance at He Jun.

This nominal senior brother actually had some real skill.

He could both pacify internal tensions and stir people's enthusiasm, and his arrangement for Luo Chen was quite thoughtful.

On the surface, it didn't focus on any single area. But if Luo Chen truly was an alchemy genius, then going through a full cycle of learning would yield gains comparable to decades of effort.

In one go, He Jun had found him five experienced second-grade alchemists as teachers!

For Luo Chen, it might not be particularly useful, but for a true beginner, it would be an enormous gift!

And so, Luo Chen officially joined He Jun's team, beginning his research on the Circulation Qi Pill.

In truth, improving a third-grade pill formula wasn't particularly difficult for Luo Chen.

But the Circulation Qi Pill was special. It was an ancient remnant formula from the Xuan Realm, with many primary and supplementary ingredients already lost.

Merely restoring it had cost Gongsun Mao over a hundred years of effort, let alone improving it for mass production.

Luo Chen was also quite interested in this pill.

If he could successfully refine it, it would not only cement his position as an alchemy genius in the Suling Sect but also provide an explanation for his upcoming explosive breakthrough in cultivation.

However, during this process, he would not use his attribute panel or waste achievement points.

The higher his cultivation level, the less help the attribute panel provided. Many things could no longer be achieved through mere practice making perfect.

Instead, the achievement points—which had seemed less important at first—had become his greatest aid.

Deduction of techniques, practice of divine abilities, grasping the true direction of the Great Dao.

He had to use his accumulated achievement points where they would be most effective!

With this in mind, he became increasingly dedicated to his work.

Aside from returning to his cave dwelling for daily cultivation, he spent the vast majority of his time in the Alchemy Court.

...

Among a pile of waste pills.

Little Poison Lord smacked his lips, as if savoring something.

Beside him, Luo Chen asked curiously, "How is it?"

Little Poison Lord spat out a mouthful of saliva. "A bit spicy!"

"Spicy?" Luo Chen frowned. "Too intense?"

Little Poison Lord nodded. "Pretty much. Not in the body, but in the soul. It gives me a feeling of intense agitation."

Luo Chen recorded this on a bamboo slip, thoughtfully saying, "This could be caused either by a mistake in the fire control or by the supplementary ingredient we chose—Flame Spirit Flower."

Little Poison Lord looked at Luo Chen with surprise. "You deduced the source of the problem that quickly? Couldn't it be something else?"

Luo Chen lightly chuckled and closed the bamboo slip. "Of course, other things could be wrong. But these two are the most likely. Let's eliminate them first."

"Fair enough." Little Poison Lord didn't object. "You can go ask Senior Brother Xiao or Senior Sister Ning."

Luo Chen took his leave, but before departing, he couldn't help asking, "Senior Brother Tang, I understand why they call you Poison Lord. But why add the word 'Little' in front?"

Little Poison Lord laughed heartily and pointed at himself. "This body of mine, immune to all poisons, came from accidentally ingesting a strange medicine—pure luck. But within the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, there is a true Hundred Poisons True Lord who reached the Void Refining stage by relying on poison arts. Naturally, everyone calls me Little Poison Lord. Hah, it's just flattery."

Luo Chen was momentarily stunned. He hadn't expected that.

But reaching the Void Refining stage through poison arts?

Or was there some kind of poison that could deal with Ascendant and Void Refining cultivators?

...

Inside the pill chamber, Senior Brother Xiao was patting his chest, swearing to Zhu Xiao and Luo Chen that his fire control was absolutely fine.

Even if there were problems, they were only minor ones.

Under their intense gazes, he could only mutter quietly that he would try a few more times.

Under constantly changing fire conditions, he ended up covered in dust and ashes, with none of the dignity befitting an Initiate cultivator.

Even Luo Chen couldn't help bursting into laughter at the sight.

...

In the medicine library.

Candlelight flickered like a bean, and a faint fragrance lingered in the air.

Ning Fu floated down lightly from a bookshelf, approached Luo Chen, and pointed to an open page.

"Your deduction was correct. According to another ancient record, the Flame Spirit Flower indeed has an additional property: intense heat that stirs lust and harms the soul. We overlooked this earlier. Fortunately, Junior Brother Tang is called Little Poison Lord and has a body immune to all poisons. If someone else had consumed that pill, even an Initiate cultivator would have made a fool of themselves."

"Stirs lust and harms the soul"—from the wording alone, one could guess what it meant.

Ning Fu smoothed her hair. "But this property doesn't usually manifest. Perhaps the abundant spiritual energy of the main ingredient activated it?"

Luo Chen nodded. "That's possible. But it also means Flame Spirit Flower is unsuitable as a supplementary ingredient for stimulating the soul. We'll need to replace it with other soul-related medicinal herbs. Do you have any recommendations?"

Ning Fu cast a flirtatious glance at Luo Chen. "What's with all the 'you, you, you'? Call me Senior Sister! Or how about 'Sister Ning'? Junior Brother Xuan Luozi."

Junior Brother Xuan Luozi!

Either the respectful title "Xuan Luozi" or, based on seniority and order of entry, "Junior Brother Xuan Luo."

But mixing respect with familiarity—what was that?

Flirting?

Luo Chen felt helpless.

Sometimes, he regarded all beings below the Ascendant stage as mere ants.

But when he truly interacted with them, he realized that the multitude of living beings were all real, living people.

It had nothing to do with cultivation level or strength. It was only about temperament and personality.

Here in the Alchemy Court of the Suling Sect, on this tiny Wonderful Pill Peak, these nominal senior brothers and sisters—whom he should have paid no attention to—always managed to stir ripples in his heart.

With his Dao heart, this should not be happening!

At this thought, Luo Chen's helpless expression suddenly froze.

He recalled that not long ago, he had been haggling over a few contribution points, as if he had returned to the Dahe Market days.

Was his Dao heart unstable?

But even with his cultivation level having fallen, his Dao heart—tempered through countless trials—should not waver in the slightest.

While Luo Chen was lost in thought, Ning Fu pouted and patted his shoulder.

"Alright, can't take a joke."

Then she bent over the desk, writing as she spoke.

"I have a few alternatives in mind: Stardust Grass, Bodhi Insect, Mirage Fruit... Which do you think would be more suitable?"

Luo Chen's gaze, empty and unfocused, swept over her voluptuous figure and across the names of the medicinal ingredients.

His mouth moved almost unconsciously. "Bodhi Insect!"

After saying this, he staggered to his feet and took his leave.

Behind him, Ning Fu furrowed her delicate brows.

Had she overstepped her boundaries?

Or was Junior Brother Xuan Luo too strait-laced?

But from their earlier interactions, he didn't seem like someone who couldn't take a joke!

Where she could not see, Luo Chen exited the Xujing Heaven courtyard and walked down Wonderful Pill Peak under the night sky.

He did not fly on his artifact. He simply walked along the mountain path, treading beneath the moonlight.

On his usually resolute face, an expression of confusion had inexplicably appeared.

Moreover, a mysterious and profound aura lingered around him, refusing to dissipate.
