# Chapter 1235: Completing the Gold Source, Dao Body Revives!

The first to be eliminated was naturally Thousand Rivers Guidance.

This water-attribute divine ability, obtained from the Nether Origin Sect, he had already learned before ascending, but he was not proficient in it.

Although divine abilities do not have a concept of proficiency, only depending on one's level of comprehension of the true meaning of laws, there is still the matter of aptitude.

Whether before or after becoming an Ascendant, he had never been particularly skilled in water-attribute laws.

Next were Qi Sea Floating Heaven, Great Luo Heaven Sleeve, and Thousand Skies Converging Flow—these three divine abilities.

The reason for eliminating these three was not complicated.

Because these three divine abilities were not traditional spell-type divine abilities, but rather heaven-reaching methods that required external objects to cultivate.

Qi Sea Floating Heaven was created by Zhou Yunshen and passed down to Luo Chen's clone, Zhou Fuyou. Its cultivation required absorbing the qi of the vast world to achieve boundless grandeur. Things like miasma, baleful qi, poisonous qi, and even extreme yin and extreme cold—many formidable powers. The more one absorbed, the greater the divine ability's power.

Great Luo Heaven Sleeve was obtained from Azure Frost before her ascension. It was a weakened version of her Sleeve Universe technique. Previously, Luo Chen needed a specific dharma robe sleeve and a pre-engraved formation to execute it. Although he now had the aid of the Vacuum God Gu, allowing its power to rival the original Sleeve Universe when manifested, it still relied on external objects.

Thousand Skies Converging Flow was the same. This was a sword dao divine ability that required refining a large amount of Nine Firmament Qi before it could be executed.

Luo Chen had already cultivated the *Thousand Skies Myriad Transformations Sword Canon* to the Great Perfection level, even condensing a sword seed within his body. As long as the sword seed remained, his Crimson Firmament Sword Qi would be inexhaustible.

Comparatively speaking, it no longer mattered whether it was engraved on the Mysterious Chapter Treasure Slip.

After eliminating these, only two divine abilities remained for selection.

Withering-Bloom Dao Finger and Starfire Burning Worlds!

Both of these divine abilities were self-created, and he understood them the most deeply.

Their respective strengths and weaknesses were also clear at a glance.

One was a single-point attack, the other was a large-area attack.

Both required a relatively long time to gather power when executed, making them easy to interrupt.

Engraving them on the Mysterious Chapter Treasure Slip could effectively avoid this shortcoming.

For a moment, Luo Chen was somewhat hesitant.

The first engraving on the treasure slip, forming the divine ability seed, should ideally succeed, as it would set a good precedent for what followed.

If it were before, he would have unhesitatingly chosen to engrave Withering-Bloom Dao Finger.

After all, this was the first divine ability he had comprehended, and it was also the most frequently used.

But now, he had a different idea.

He decided to choose to engrave Starfire Burning Worlds!

The reason was actually not complicated.

He had been cultivating for over a thousand years now.

Excluding the five hundred years of Return to Ruins body refinement, his true cultivation time was only about five hundred years in the Mountains and Seas Realm and eighty years in the Xuan Realm, totaling roughly six hundred years.

Within these six hundred years, the main dao direction he had truly cultivated was the fire attribute.

No matter how much he hated Rosycloud, the *Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture* had left a deep imprint on him.

Fire-attribute techniques, fire-type spells, and even the fire origin he had comprehended—all had long been integrated into his bones.

Therefore, he could easily be inspired by the sight of a falling meteor and create the fire-attribute divine ability—Starfire Burning Worlds.

For him, the dao of fire was as simple as breathing.

To condense a divine ability seed and engrave the Mysterious Chapter Treasure Slip, he naturally had to start with the simple.

"Then let's begin!"

Having made his decision, Luo Chen no longer hesitated.

He recalled his previous experiences engraving various types of spells and began attempting the engraving.

Using his divine sense as a brush, his dharma power as ink, and the Mysterious Chapter Treasure Slip as paper.

With extreme caution, yet as if the brush moved like a dragon and snake.

Strands of talisman marks were imprinted onto the five-colored mysterious chapter.

Initially, it went smoothly, just like when he had previously engraved high and low-level spells, encountering no major problems.

But gradually, Luo Chen felt somewhat inadequate.

Or rather, it wasn't that he lacked the strength, nor that the Mysterious Chapter Treasure Slip couldn't withstand that power—after all, Sage cultivators could also roughly execute Ascendant-level divine abilities.

The real problem appeared in the talisman marks outlined by his divine sense.

Those talismans were derived from his attainments in formation dao and talisman dao, and their power was sufficient to carry various high and low-level spells.

But when it came to divine abilities containing the great power of heaven and earth, there was often a feeling of being unable to sustain the brushstroke.

Luo Chen fell into confusion for a time.

Engraving divine abilities was an idea he had long harbored, and he had even made preparations in advance.

But when it came to actually implementing it, the difficulty still exceeded his expectations.

Several years later.

Luo Chen's eyes were bloodshot, and the Five Elements Sage Infant he had only recently condensed was even somewhat wilted and exhausted.

The experimental process was still stuck at the final hurdle.

The divine ability seed simply could not be formed!

Although it was called the final hurdle, for Luo Chen, it was actually the first threshold to entry.

"Where exactly is the problem?"

"I have optimized a large number of talismans, but I still can't do it. Is it that my attainments in formation dao and talisman dao are insufficient?"

For a moment, Luo Chen was even somewhat lost.

It was as if there was a treasure vault before him. He clearly knew the direction, but lacking the key, he could never find the way in.

If he could truly enter the treasure vault and develop the treasures within...

He might be able to become the most powerful Ascendant in history!

Regardless of body refinement realm, ignoring spirit treasures and external objects, purely relying on divine abilities to dominate as an Ascendant great power!

But he was just missing that little bit.

Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath, and his state of mind gradually settled.

Haste makes waste!

The Xuan Realm's history spanned who knew how many epochs. Throughout the ages, there had certainly been cultivators with fanciful ideas, trying to operate as he did.

Yet there was still no precedent, no one had succeeded.

This naturally illustrated the difficulty.

There was no point in rushing.

First, restore his condition. Don't let the newly condensed Five Elements Sage Infant collapse.

If he had just recovered his realm only to fall again, that would be a bitter joke.

Luo Chen circulated the *Chaos Indestructible Scripture*, beginning to absorb external spiritual qi and convert it into his own dharma power.

The wilted and exhausted Sage Infant, nourished by the dharma power, gradually improved over time.

During this process, Luo Chen suddenly felt a powerful force from the outside world, faintly resonating with some power within his body.

This surprised him.

To be honest.

Since condensing his Sage Infant in the Ink Jade Space, this was the first time he had seriously engaged in Sage-level cultivation.

Using the Sage Infant to resonate with heaven and earth, divine sense to control spiritual qi, absorbing it into the body, chaotic conversion...

"What is it that is resonating with me?"

Luo Chen became interested and instinctively began to explore.

He even opened the thick barrier of his profound Purple Mansion, allowing his Sage Infant to manifest between heaven and earth in an almost "exposed" manner.

In this state of absorption, time passed quickly in units of years.

In the blink of an eye, ten years had passed.

On this day.

Within the Su Ling Sect, a flash of spiritual light suddenly appeared!

Liu Muhan was cultivating under a majestic waterfall on Jade Spring Peak.

Across her knees lay a long sword, like autumn water, with dharma power accumulating on it, forming a thick layer of water vapor.

What she was cultivating was the Heavenly Waterfall Sword Art passed down by her master, Yao Xi.

This sword technique was immensely powerful, emphasizing intent over form. When wielded, it was like a heavenly waterfall scouring the earth, making it difficult for same-realm Adepts to withstand its vast might.

Even within the Su Ling Sect, with its vast ocean of techniques, the Heavenly Waterfall Sword Art was a top-tier high-level sword technique, usable all the way to the Ascendant stage.

This could be seen from the fact that her senior sister, Jiang Huang, had once relied on this sword technique to dominate the Adept stage and even used it to slay Poro demons in reverse.

Suddenly!

Liu Muhan grunted, opening her eyes in shock.

Unbeknownst to her, the long sword across her knees was trembling slightly.

A feeling of losing control arose spontaneously.

How was this possible?

This sword was her natal dharma treasure, only recently refined. Even if the refinement time was short, it should not have fallen out of her control!

"What is going on?"

As Liu Muhan wondered in astonishment, her expression suddenly changed drastically.

Her connection with her natal dharma treasure had inexplicably been severed!

The next moment.

The long sword on her knees shot straight into the sky.

Liu Muhan's eyes widened as she stood up, staring at the sky in shock.

At the same time, a large number of dharma treasures within the Su Ling Sect shot into the sky.

Most were swords and sabers, along with other weapons, including many dharma treasures mixed with metal-attribute mineral materials.

Countless dharma treasures hung upside down in the sky, forming a terrifying dark cloud.

A fierce, killing aura of all things withering permeated the surroundings.

On the ground, earth veins trembled.

Strands of metal-attribute spiritual qi, as if summoned, broke through the ground and traversed the void.

Such an anomaly alarmed nearly half of the Su Ling Sect.

Countless people cried out in alarm.

The blowing wind even injured many low-level cultivators.

The first Su Ling Ascendant to notice this anomaly was Elder Tian Ge.

He was just giving final instructions to his personal disciple, Iron Horse Sage, who was about to participate in the Demon-Suppressing Trial, when his expression suddenly froze, and he walked out of the main hall.

At that time, the terrifying phenomenon of ten thousand swords hanging upside down and a hundred treasures forming clouds had not yet appeared.

But through the boiling and killing spiritual qi anomalies in heaven and earth, he had already detected the commotion.

"This is... the power of laws!"

Iron Horse Sage, with a stern face, also followed behind, looking at the dharma treasures flying from all directions in the sky, and even feeling the restlessness of the metal-attribute dharma power within his body. He asked in surprise, "Which elder is manipulating the heaven and earth origin qi?"

Elder Tian Ge shook his head solemnly, "If it were an Ascendant elder, it wouldn't be so unrestrained. This is more like a cultivator comprehending the power of laws for the first time, hence the lack of control, even affecting the fish here."

As he spoke, he suddenly detected some clues from within.

Especially when countless dharma treasures gathered in the sky, forming a trend, his expression changed drastically, even showing ecstasy.

"Ten thousand swords hanging upside down, a hundred treasures forming clouds, all things withering, the torrent of the weapon realm!"

"Although this divine ability has not truly formed, it already has a prototype."

"This is clearly someone cultivating the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Mysterious Chapter*, having an epiphany and comprehending the Gold Origin!"

As he spoke, he had already shot into the sky.

Then, he released his own vast dharma power. The countless dharma treasures accumulated in the sky immediately lost the control of the power of heaven and earth, falling back into the hands of their respective owners.

This action would not affect the cultivator in epiphany.

A large amount of spiritual qi in heaven and earth was still gathering towards a certain place.

Hearing the restless shouts within the sect, Elder Tian Ge shouted sternly.

"Disciples, do not panic! Return to your caves, attend to your own affairs!"

Finally, he waved at Iron Horse Sage.

"Follow me!"

With a flash of escaping light, the two flew towards the direction where the spiritual qi was gathering.

In a short while, the two appeared before a spiritual mountain.

This place was very familiar to Iron Horse Sage.

It was one of the three peaks of the Alchemy Courtyard—Danhuang Mountain!

At this moment, outside the Mountains and Seas Travel Palace, a large number of alchemists in cloud-patterned dharma robes were curiously wandering about.

Iron Horse Sage released his divine sense and immediately locked onto the location of the spiritual qi anomaly.

In an instant, his mouth fell open.

"It's him?"

Elder Tian Ge had discovered it earlier but did not explain.

As a fellow cultivator of the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Mysterious Chapter* from his lineage, Iron Horse was one of the best.

But even at the late Sage stage, he had not yet comprehended the true meaning of laws.

Now, a rising star had seen through the Gold Origin, surpassing him from behind.

This would undoubtedly be a great blow to Iron Horse.

But for Elder Tian Ge, it was an unexpected joy.

Or rather, not just him.

When the eight Ascendant elders of the sect gathered one after another outside the Mountains and Seas Travel Palace, there was a hint of surprise in each other's eyes.

The spiritual opportunities of the Xuan Realm were scarce, as high-level cultivators well knew.

Except for advancing to Ascendant, it was very difficult for Sage cultivators to comprehend the true meaning of laws in advance.

Even if they did, it would only appear in the Holy Sect Source Courts, the exclusive domain of those truly unparalleled geniuses.

Unexpectedly, their Su Ling Sect had also produced such an unparalleled genius.

"The Sharp Gold Body actually has such fortune?"

For a moment, amidst their surprise, they even doubted their own understanding.

The person who had comprehended the Gold Origin here was someone they all knew, and their intelligence was already well understood.

Although bearing the name of a genius, what the sect valued more was his talent in the alchemy dao.

Although there were also signs in cultivation, in the end, it was only a Sharp Gold Body of mid-upper aptitude.

In the Xuan Realm, the evaluation of talent was vastly different from the Mountains and Seas Realm.

The number of spiritual roots only applied to low realms.

Even a Heavenly Spirit Root was only considered mid-grade talent.

If one had wind, thunder, ice, darkness, yin-yang, light, and other special spiritual roots, it only added a bit of color.

Above that, possessing one of the spiritual bodies or dharma bodies could be considered mid-upper aptitude.

True top-grade talent was often those with a Dao Body, or those with past-life wisdom and excellent comprehension.

Xuan Luozi, who had a considerable reputation within the Su Ling Sect, was only considered excellent when placed in a Holy Sect Source Court.

But now, he had done something that only those unparalleled geniuses with top-grade talent could possibly achieve.

Comprehending the true meaning of laws in advance at the Sage stage!

As everyone was discussing in low voices, a ethereal voice transmission arrived.

Everyone was startled, then dispersed one after another.

Outside the Su Wang Hall.

Tai Su Lingjun, in a green palace dress, stared at the direction of Danhuang Mountain with great interest.

At this moment, the image of her good friend Chu Yan's stay as a guest in the Su Ling Sect flashed through her mind, especially the expectations the other party had for someone before leaving.

Unbeknownst to her, a curve had already risen at the corner of her mouth.

"Xuan Luo, is it..."

...

Within the Mountains and Seas Travel Palace.

Golden wind was fierce, and the killing aura had risen to its extreme.

Luo Chen sat cross-legged in the quiet room, his green robe rustling.

Spiritual light transformed into essence, and the flesh and blood all over his body squirmed. A power that had been dormant for a long time moved from the inside out, finally affecting heaven and earth.

Yes, from the inside out!

Unlike ordinary cultivators who comprehend the true meaning of laws from the outside in, sensing it from heaven and earth.

And completely different from any of his previous experiences in comprehending the true meaning of laws.

This time, it was from the inside out, awakened from his own flesh and blood, from within his own body.

"Gold Punishment Sovereign, Great Vehicle True Spirit, one-third of the physical body, Chaos Indestructible Body, Gold Origin!"

With all these elements gathered, during his first active resonance of his Sage Infant with heaven and earth, he finally awakened the power sleeping within his physical body.

Like water flowing into a channel, it was so natural.

Without any need for spiritual opportunity assistance, he had simply and easily comprehended the Gold Origin.

Compared to the twists and turns of the past few years, where he had tried to comprehend and engrave divine abilities but could not find the way in, comprehending the Gold Origin was a matter of convenience.

It could be said to be a loss in one place, a gain in another.

After a long time.

When Luo Chen woke up and slowly opened his eyes.

The golden light on his body suddenly retracted, and then five-colored radiance, accompanied by dharma power, swirled around his entire body, filling the entire cultivation room.

An indescribable feeling arose spontaneously.

That feeling was called regaining what was lost!

Without even needing to open the attribute panel, Luo Chen knew what had happened.

The Five-Spirit Dao Body had fully revived!
