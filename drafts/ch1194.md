# Chapter 1194: Source of Compassionate Light, Reverse Divine Passage

On the pristine white square, everyone stood ready, poised for action.

Luo Chen slowly raised his head, gazing at the faintly visible portal in the sky.

The Ancient God's Cave Dwelling—the entrance to the Dao Field!

Only by opening this door could one enter the Ancient God's Dao Field.

Vaguely, the golden splendor behind the door had already revealed a corner of its majesty, dignified and filled with sanctity.

Before his arrival, Tianxuan Liexiu and the others had attempted it several times, but each attempt had ended in failure.

The crux of the matter was not that everyone lacked sufficient strength.

Strictly speaking, this gathering of martial artists, whether in quality or quantity, surpassed any known gathering in the past ten thousand years.

What truly hindered them was timing!

The opening of the Ancient God's Dao Field gate was not a fixed occurrence but followed certain specific patterns.

For instance, when the small-scale Destruction Mist spread, or when the cycle of epochs drew near.

"I won't waste my last remaining hundred years here."

"If it doesn't open after two or three attempts... I'll leave."

Luo Chen thought to himself.

This idea had formed in his mind ever since he perfected his cultivation technique in the Sea of Stars.

The reason he had still come to the Ancient God's Dao Field was merely to witness it.

Compared to this, the cultivation environment of the Nine Rings of Destruction was more conducive to his next breakthrough, and he already had some vague ideas about crossing the Gate of Destruction.

Of course!

If the Ancient God's Dao Field truly had a passage leading to the outside world, he wouldn't mind taking that route out.

As he pondered, Luo Chen had already made his move.

Like the others, a stream of astral qi shot from his mouth, soaring straight toward the empty gate in the sky.

Not only that, but within the astral qi, his martial will was exceptionally active, continuously assaulting the Dao Field gate.

The Carefree Realm Master cast an unexpected glance at Luo Chen.

Because the martial will Luo Chen was now releasing was not the one he had displayed when breaking through to Martial Immortal in the Fourth Ring of Destruction. Instead, it was a Thunderbolt Will similar to that of Yuan Qingcang beside him, and even the attribute of his astral qi had transformed.

"No wonder he has cultivated to such a level in just two hundred years. His refinement of his own martial will is already greatly accomplished. He must have truly completed the condensation of his inner martial deity."

As the Carefree Realm Master thought this, he released his own martial will.

It was an existence that was ethereal, traceless, and difficult to grasp.

Seemingly ordinary, yet it overpowered all others.

At the same time, the other three Destruction Kings also made their moves.

Tianxuan Liexiu let out a soft shout, and behind him, stars suddenly appeared, neatly arranged, guarding him at the center.

The Heaven-Weeping Old Monster laughed strangely from within his belly, strands of heavenly wind swirling around him before all surging into the Dao Field gate.

The Corpse remained silent, but around him, a sea of bones and a thousand mountains had already manifested, resembling a scene from hell.

Luo Chen had originally taken the opportunity to observe the others' movements, wanting to understand the external differences between the Destruction Realm and the Martial Immortal Realm.

And indeed, he had discerned some clues.

For instance, the unity of martial will, astral qi, and physical body in the Carefree Realm Master and the others was already indistinguishable.

Moreover, they began to influence the external world with themselves as the center.

Without any need for preparation, following their heart's movement, the ground beneath their feet became their home field.

One could imagine that under such circumstances, even a Void Refinement Lord who had comprehended the true meaning of laws, or even a Body Integration Grand Venerable, would find it difficult to influence them through the grand momentum of heaven and earth.

From this, one could also clearly distinguish the essential difference between body refinement and qi refinement.

One influences the external world with one's own will as the center; the other imposes the principles of heaven and earth upon oneself to control the external world.

In this, the difference lies in the single-minded distinction between divine will and divine thought!

Luo Chen even thought further.

If one day he could leave the Guixu Land, he would inevitably pick up qi refinement again, and it would certainly not be difficult to rebuild to the Divine Transformation stage.

By then, how would martial will and primordial spirit thought coexist?

Just as Luo Chen was thinking this, something in his bosom suddenly trembled.

"It's that rib!"

Luo Chen subconsciously looked at the Corpse Old Demon.

Vaguely, from the details of the other's martial will manifestation, he could detect a trace of his original Bone Demon clan aura.

Could it be him...

"No!"

Luo Chen suddenly raised his head, staring fixedly at the Dao Field gate.

At that moment.

Tianxuan Liexiu's face showed ecstasy.

"Everyone, the time has come. Increase your strength!"

*Hum!*

A peculiar hum, silent yet spreading to everyone.

It was as if they had merged together, and the faintly visible gate in the sky was slowly opening to them.

No one dared to be careless, outputting double the astral qi within their bodies.

Luo Chen was among them.

Immersed completely, strands of milky white light seeped out from behind the gate, scattering over everyone.

The originally anxious, urgent, joyful, and shocked faces gradually became calm.

And some of the martial artists' accumulated hidden injuries, under the envelopment of this light, healed at an unimaginable speed.

The feeling was like a baby returning to its mother's womb.

Serene and tranquil.

...

A remnant body lights a lamp, illuminating the path of eternal glory.

Grief serves as fuel, burning away the cold nights of all worlds.

On the ten-thousand-year glacier, the Divine Mother walks barefoot, and wherever she passes, flowers bloom.

On the cracked earth, the Divine Mother sheds tears, and rain falls.

In the interminable hell, as the world's people suffer, the Divine Mother descends, and hell is instantly emptied.

Luo Chen curled up his body, revealing unease like a child.

Then compassionate light sprinkled down, and his expression involuntarily relaxed.

The physical body, which had recently been forcibly unified internally with external force, was also greatly soothed within the compassionate light.

As if sensing something, he opened his eyes and looked into the distance.

There was an illusory female figure, kind and peaceful, looking at him like a mother.

"Compassionate Light Divine Mother!"

A name naturally surfaced in his heart.

Correspondingly, an impulse to prostrate in worship arose spontaneously.

Suddenly!

A wisp of chaotic gray light emanated from within his body, cutting off the milky white compassionate light.

This chaotic gray light came quickly and left just as quickly.

Luo Chen, as if struck by a sudden awakening, instantly regained clarity.

When he looked around again, where was any divine spirit or holy mother? There were only the chanting sounds of all beings prostrating in worship.

And these chanting sounds came from the fellow martial artists.

"Compassionate light washes away the dust of calamity, divine tears heal the vast desolation.
Lowering the gaze sees the sea of suffering, raising the hand is the compassionate ferry..."

Luo Chen's heart tightened, and he immediately understood the source of the earlier strangeness.

He murmured, "Compassionate Light Divine Mother!"

At that moment, four gazes fell sharply upon Luo Chen.

It was the four Destruction Kings.

The Carefree Realm Master cast an appreciative glance at Luo Chen.

Tianxuan Liexiu was quite surprised.

Even the Heaven-Weeping Old Monster was somewhat shocked, seemingly not expecting Luo Chen to awaken so quickly from the faith baptism.

Only the Corpse Old Demon's face grew even more gloomy.

Luo Chen wanted to speak, but seeing the four of them remain silent, he held back his impulse.

Instead, he turned to observe the others.

The milky white light still enveloped everyone, and from within it, chanting sounds occasionally emerged.

Watching this scene, Luo Chen showed a thoughtful expression.

"It's incense and fire divine power!"

"And the situation just now, where the divine power was added to the body, clearly belongs to the process of faith baptism."

"Only after passing through this can one obtain the identity of a believer of the Dao Field's master, and thus enter His Dao Field."

The weaker one was, the deeper the baptism.

The stronger one was, the faster one could break free from the influence and constraints.

Then next...

About the time it takes to burn an incense stick.

The chanting on the square completely ended.

Those who had awakened, more or less, had a bit more piety on their faces.

Even Tianxuan Liexiu and the others had become more reverent.

"Hmph, the gate is open. What are you waiting for?"

The Corpse Old Demon snorted coldly, ignoring everyone, and flew directly into the gate.

The others, unwilling to fall behind, turned into streaks of light and rushed in.

Luo Chen lagged behind, still able to feel the Corpse Old Demon's spying on him before he left.

"Troublesome fellow."

Luo Chen muttered.

Yuan Qingcang beside him was full of energy, his face devout. "Let's go, let's go pay homage to the Source of Compassionate Light!"

Source of Compassionate Light!

That was the revered name of that Divine Mother.

In the baptism just now, everyone had already learned all of this.

Luo Chen was no exception, but his pace of entry was relatively slower.

No matter how slow, however, there was still the moment of entry.

The moment he passed through that incense-and-fire gate, a wondrous scene burst into his eyes.

A colossal building hung high in the void.

It was divided into thirty-three levels.

Each level had pavilions, towers, flying eaves, and painted corners.

Each level had chanting, scripture recitation, and devout prayers.

And figures sat cross-legged within, silently facing away from all beings.

Above the thirty-third level, streams of milky white qi could be seen surging from the void, converging into the thirty-three golden halls.

Seeing this, Luo Chen finally understood what was meant by the Ancient God's Dao Field.

This was the Ancient God's Dao Field!

Suddenly!

*Whoosh!*

Someone shot out a stream of astral qi, targeting a seated figure on the first golden hall.

The moment the astral qi touched it, that figure instantly disintegrated, turning into a pile of bone ash.

"This..."

The martial artists were all horrified.

"Cough, cough."

A cough drew everyone's attention.

The Carefree Realm Master sighed. "No need to test. Those people are fellow Daoists who ventured into the Ancient God's Dao Field in earlier years, just like us."

Everyone's expressions changed drastically.

"What does this mean?"

"Are you saying that we might end up like this too?"

"Clearly dead for a long time, yet their decayed bodies remain, still emitting chanting sounds?"

"What kind of existence is the Compassionate Light Divine Mother?"

Questions arose one after another.

The Carefree Realm Master calmly looked at the Corpse. "Fellow Daoist, some here know, but some are newcomers. You'll have to explain!"

The Corpse snorted coldly. "Those trapped here are nothing but a bunch of failures."

After sneering, he explained the situation to everyone.

"Just like the information you received during the baptism earlier, the master of this place is an ancient god named the Source of Compassionate Light."

"He walks the world in a maternal image, facing all beings with compassionate thoughts."

"His origins and background are unknown, but he has existed in Guixu for tens of thousands of years, enduring calamities without decay, truly immortal."

Red Lotus Master spoke up. "We came here to find a way out. What does this have to do with these matters?"

The Corpse gave her a cold glance, still speaking in the same unhurried tone.

"The reason the Source of Compassionate Light is immortal and undecaying lies in his unique cultivation method—the Incense and Fire Divine Path!"

"This method does not seek from oneself but instead relies on believers."

"As long as believers still exist and faith is not extinguished, divinity endures forever."

The Incense and Fire Divine Path!

Everyone was curious upon hearing this cultivation method.

Luo Chen stroked his chin, connecting the dots, vaguely understanding some things.

Strictly speaking, this method was not particularly brilliant.

Not to mention the cultivation world, even in the mortal realm, it was widely circulated.

It was nothing more than establishing a golden body, spreading doctrine, and accepting believers.

What was truly brilliant was the secret technique for converting incense and fire divine power.

And this secret technique, he had already obtained from the *Pure World Withered Bone Bodhi Method*.

If one day he could contact his old friends in the Mountains and Seas Realm, and if his disciples and descendants there were sufficiently devout and faithful to him, he could also convert his own incense and fire divine power.

Observing the occasional carved texts on the golden halls around him, Luo Chen judged that the Corpse's *Pure World Withered Bone Bodhi Method* might also originate from this place.

Perhaps that rib was not his but left behind by the ancient god of this place.

The Corpse Old Demon's words continued.

"After tens of thousands of years, the Ancient God's Dao Field still remains, and the incense fire is still unextinguished. Clearly, outside Guixu, there are still people who believe in the Source of Compassionate Light."

"The bond connecting both sides is the power of faith, that is, the incense and fire divine power!"

"You have all seen those clearly visible milky white forces in the void. That is the concrete manifestation of incense and fire divine power under the influence of these thirty-three Divine Mother Halls."

"If—"

At this point, the Corpse Old Demon's tone grew heavier, and the other three Destruction Kings also became serious.

The Corpse Old Demon swept his gaze over everyone and said word by word:

"If we can master the thirty-three Divine Mother Halls, using the strongest faith anchor point from the outside world, we have a chance to open the Reverse Divine Passage and leave Guixu!"

As soon as these words were spoken, the crowd erupted.

Amidst the uproar, there was immense excitement.

What the Corpse Old Demon said did have a certain feasibility.

But!

There were still obvious flaws within.

Luo Chen spoke up. "If what you say is true, then why didn't the master of this place use this method to leave Guixu?"

The Corpse Old Demon raised his eyes and coldly replied, "The Incense and Fire Divine Path—success lies in believers, failure also lies in believers. If the cultivator of this path cannot hold onto their nature, they will lose themselves in the vast sea of incense and fire divine power, becoming an existence without intelligence or order."

Luo Chen frowned. "Are you saying that the Source of Compassionate Light, that is, the Compassionate Light Divine Mother, has already lost her self and has no autonomous consciousness?"

"Exactly. Strictly speaking, she has long since fallen. The arrangements here continue to operate only through the instinctive drive of her residual divine power."

The Corpse Old Demon did not want to answer Luo Chen, but he had to clear up everyone's confusion.

Finally, he emphasized, "The thirty-three Divine Mother Halls have endured calamities without damage and are too powerful to be controlled by a single person. That is why, each time, many powerful martial artists need to enter together. And each time one attempts to control a single Divine Mother Hall, they must face a massive surge of faith. Those whose martial will is not firm enough will lose themselves and ultimately fall. Moreover, they cannot be mastered one by one slowly; all thirty-three Divine Mother Halls must be controlled at roughly the same time."

So this was the truth behind the martial artists who had fallen in the Ancient God's Dao Field over the years?

Luo Chen's gaze fell upon that golden, sacred building.

The Carefree Realm Master stepped forward.

"Everyone, at this point, we have no retreat. The thirty-third Divine Mother Hall will be mine."

As soon as these words were spoken, everyone was shocked.

And the Carefree Realm Master no longer concealed himself. All his apertures opened wide, and an unfathomably powerful astral qi surged around him.

The vastness of his power even made Luo Chen turn his head in surprise.

This was the foundation of three thousand years of guarding the Gate of Destruction!

Even the Corpse Old Demon, who had survived the longest and was most familiar with the Ancient God's Dao Field, did not contend with the Carefree Realm Master for the highest Divine Mother Hall.

He only said coldly, "The thirty-second is mine!"

Tianxuan Liexiu, already aware, said solemnly, "The thirty-first is mine!"

The Heaven-Weeping Old Monster, with an expression that was neither a smile nor a cry, said, "It seems this old man has no choice. Then the thirtieth Divine Hall will be mine. As for the others..."

The four Destruction Kings turned their gaze to everyone, pausing the longest on Luo Chen.

Everyone hesitated.

They knew that the Ancient God's Dao Field held the possibility of escape, which was why countless martial artists had come one after another over the countless years.

But they had never expected it to be like this.

Gathering everyone's strength to control the thirty-three Divine Mother Halls, opening the Reverse Divine Passage, and using the faith anchor point to return to the real world.

This process was by no means as simple as it sounded.

After all, even the ancient god Compassionate Light Divine Mother had lost her self-awareness. How could they underestimate the impact of the vast sea of incense and fire divine power?

If they accidentally lost themselves, their fate might be the decayed bones and broken bodies they now saw in those pavilions and towers.

Just as everyone hesitated.

*Rip!*

A crack suddenly appeared in the sky, as if torn open by someone.

A golden claw, shocking to the eye, appeared in everyone's sight. (End of chapter)
