# Chapter 1247: Late Nascent Soul, Suwang Sword Appears

The demon lair was dark, with no sight of the passage of sun and moon.

The Daoist cultivated bitterly, barely sensing the flow of time.

A year passed in the blink of an eye.

At the outermost edge of the Dark Demon Lair, the earth was scorched yellow, stretching for over ten thousand li.

Any cultivator passing through would not find it strange, merely assuming that some trial participant had arrived early, scattering a large amount of demon-eliminating liquid to complete the task of clearing the Nine Nether Demon Moss.

Thus, no one lingered.

But in truth, the withering of the demon moss here was completely different from other places.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's demon-eliminating liquid, in essence, only suppressed the growth of the demon moss, cooperating with the dispersal of demon nests to prevent their expansion. Otherwise, the Demon-Suppressing Trial would not be held once every millennium, continuously.

But the Nine Nether Demon Moss here had been completely eradicated at the root!

It was as if someone had fully absorbed and refined the demonic qi they relied on to survive, stripping them of the nutrients needed for growth and survival.

To achieve such a feat in the current Xuan Realm was extremely rare.

Even the Primordial Demon Sect, one of the Nine Great Saint Sects, which claimed to transform demonic power and return to origin, cultivating demonic methods with a human body, would never dare to directly absorb demonic qi.

Some Great Vehicle Heavenly Sovereigns might be able to do it, but refining demonic qi was ultimately an unrewarding task.

Hence the current situation in the Xuan Realm, where demon lairs everywhere were like a centipede that dies but never stiffens.

Luo Chen had not anticipated that loosening his restrictions would have such a significant impact.

It could only be said that the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra*, created in that wretched place called Guixu to adapt to the environment, was truly without scruples.

Absorbing and transforming demonic qi had become as instinctive to him as breathing.

The only difference was whether to convert the demonic qi into Blood-Refined Divine Astral Power or immortal dao mana.

Now that he was eager to recover his Ascendant realm, naturally, the more mana, the better.

In one year, the Nascent Soul within his Purple Mansion had visibly grown much larger.

Those snow-white arms, like sections of lotus root, the plump round face, the chubby body—everything radiated abundant spirit and vigorous vitality.

And indeed, that was the reality!

"Late Nascent Soul!"

In the rough cave dwelling, Luo Chen slowly opened his eyes.

He was very satisfied with this cultivation progress.

This was the cumulative effect of the Chaos Indestructible Body and the Five-Spirit Dao Body.

The speed of cultivation could no longer be described as a thousand li in a single day.

However, this place could no longer support him in taking another step forward.

Luo Chen could sense that the surrounding demonic qi had become extremely thin, with only a small amount occasionally emerging from the deep earth.

If he wanted to break through to the Ascendant realm, he would need to find a place with denser demonic qi.

"One level higher than here... no, perhaps that's still not enough!" Luo Chen pondered silently.

If it were the him of the past, no matter how deep his foundation, a mere fifth-rank spirit land would have sufficed for his breakthrough to the Ascendant stage.

But now, having re-cultivated the immortal dao, his foundation was a hundred times deeper than before!

In other words, the demonic qi and spiritual opportunity required to break through a major realm would also be far greater.

Luo Chen's gaze pierced through the cave, looking into the distance.

He skipped directly over the middle zone recorded on the map, as if landing on the center of the Dark Demon Lair—the Central Demon Abyss!

According to legend, that was the true birthplace of the Dark Demon Lair.

In past years, it was also where high-rank demon spirits comparable to late Nascent Soul or even Ascendant level often lurked.

Cultivators from various sects dared not enter easily; they had to wait for a great power from the Saint Sects to lead them in for cultivation.

But Luo Chen had no such concerns.

However, before leaving, Luo Chen still had one matter to investigate.

He withdrew his gaze and looked at the ground.

Without any visible action, the Yuantu Sword flew out, drilling straight into the earth like a knife cutting through tofu.

Luo Chen rose calmly and followed behind.

After descending who knew how many zhang, he finally arrived at a dim, lightless place.

He casually picked up a black stone. It had no deep luster, instead revealing a gray, dusty appearance.

This was a demon crystal!

It could be regarded as the equivalent of a spirit stone in the immortal cultivation world, normally containing a large amount of demonic qi within.

The reason it had become like this was that Luo Chen's cultivation had been too unrestrained. Not only had he sucked dry the demonic qi for ten thousand li around, but he had also devoured the demon crystals of the demon nest here clean.

Yes, this place was already a demon nest!

Only within a demon nest could large quantities of demon crystals be born.

Luo Chen's purpose in coming here was naturally not to find these useless discarded demon crystals.

He released his divine sense, and soon!

Luo Chen's expression shifted. He reached out and beckoned.

Instantly, the earth rumbled. Hard rock split apart, and irregular crystals flew out from the deep ground.

These crystals were pitch black all over, their irregular edges sharp and dangerous, easily cutting through a cultivator's flesh and blood.

Within them flowed some kind of unknown liquid, like mercury, or perhaps like human bone marrow.

It was demon marrow diamond!

This material was a high-grade metal-type material, extremely effective against wood-type magical artifacts and techniques.

Earlier, when he had feigned the demonic contamination state, to resolve it, he would have needed a secret art to refine this substance, using it to eliminate the erosion of the Nine Nether Demon Moss on his body.

Although he no longer needed it now, demon marrow diamond was still a good thing.

Whether added to magical artifacts to increase sharpness, or used to refine new offensive treasures, it had great uses.

Even from Luo Chen's perspective, when his Yuantu Sword was refined, some demon marrow diamond had been added; otherwise, it would not have reached such a terrifying degree of sharpness.

In his early years, Luo Chen had even used this sword to wound Azure Frost, who possessed the Azure Luan Divine Body.

"Not many, just over a dozen or so. I'll keep them for now!"

After storing the demon marrow diamonds, Luo Chen looked at this demon nest.

Even though he had drained it dry, causing the Nine Nether Demon Moss to wither over a large area, faint demonic qi was still emerging from within.

This situation could not be solved by simply destroying the terrain with ordinary means.

To disperse a demon nest, one must use the great divine ability of a mighty power, connecting with the origin qi of heaven and earth, to thoroughly resolve it.

Although Luo Chen did not have the great ambition of saving the world, he truly did not want to see scenes of war between foreign races and humans again.

He had seen too much of that in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Every time, it was nothing but mountains of corpses and seas of blood, with millions upon millions of people dying in endless cycles of misery.

Therefore, before leaving, he intended to completely eradicate this demon nest, and also wanted to witness the generation of a spirit nest after the demon nest was dispersed.

With a thought, Luo Chen split off part of his divine sense and infused it into his Nascent Soul.

In a sense, the Nascent Soul at this point was no different from the primordial spirit of a normal Ascendant cultivator.

The only difference was probably that Luo Chen's current Nascent Soul strength, even if a hundred times that of his former Nascent Soul stage, could not fully bear his peak Ascendant divine soul power.

When Luo Chen's "primordial spirit" reappeared, that familiar feeling of controlling the origin qi of heaven and earth surfaced once more.

"Long time no see."

Luo Chen sighed with emotion, then formed hand seals, slowly pointing a finger.

With his slow movements, the origin qi of heaven and earth in the vicinity converged and flowed, finally gathering entirely on that one finger.

Wood Dao Divine Ability—Withering-Bloom Dao Finger!

One finger pointed out, and everything around turned pale.

The origin of demonic qi entrenched here visibly collapsed and disintegrated.

Furthermore, the earth's qi became unbalanced, erupting chaotically.

Suddenly, a loud noise was heard.

Boom!

It was like an explosion.

Luo Chen was at the center of the explosion. The Blood Cloth Robe naturally appeared, completely ignoring the tremendous force.

But he did not resist either. Following the power released after the demon nest was dispersed, he was actively pushed out.

In his line of sight, the originally towering mountain peak was collapsing inch by inch.

Yet within the destruction, new life was being nurtured.

It was like the Withering-Bloom Dao Finger, which had both the effect of imparting the dao meaning of withering death and the ability to evolve life.

Luo Chen could feel a large amount of origin qi of heaven and earth gathering, and a tiny spirit nest was slowly forming.

And in this process of formation...

"Spiritual opportunity!"

Luo Chen finally captured that trace of spiritual opportunity.

But he did not act hastily to collect it. Instead, he continued to observe this state.

Gradually, Luo Chen had a feeling.

If the Xuan Realm were compared to a human body, then these Nine Nether Demon Mosses were the source of disease, like clinging bone maggots, and the demon nests generated from them had become lesions like tumors.

If this continued for a long time, the Xuan Realm might not perish, but the entire world would gradually be shrouded in demonic qi, eventually becoming a place similar to the Demon Realm.

The Saint Ancestor of the Wood Demon Race, who came up with the method of using Nine Nether Demon Moss to invade the Xuan Realm, was truly a peerless genius!

Instead of attacking the Xuan Realm with a violent invasion, they used the gentle method of demon moss converting spiritual qi into demonic qi to devour the spirit realm.

If they truly took root, they could indeed fight a protracted war with the powerful beings of the Xuan Realm.

However, for the Xuan Realm, cultivators were like living cells and organs, naturally moving to resolve these lesions that affected them.

In this process, the Xuan Realm would also provide assistance.

The appearance of spiritual opportunity was clear proof.

"The world has spirit; opportunities should naturally belong to those who contribute!"

Having observed enough, Luo Chen did not want to delay here any longer.

With a slight smile, his hands moved swiftly, forming a spirit seal he had not used for a long time.

Instantly!

Within the spirit nest, the faint spiritual opportunity seemed to be attracted, all surging into Luo Chen's body.

In a trance, Luo Chen's primordial spirit trembled slightly, and countless brilliant ideas emerged.

This was a sign of sudden enlightenment!

But Luo Chen forcibly suppressed this feeling, still operating the previous hand seal, mobilizing strands of origin qi of heaven and earth to seal those spiritual opportunities, trapping them within his Purple Mansion.

This method did not resemble any spell or divine ability, nor any prohibition or formation.

It was more like alchemy!

Using alchemy techniques to channel energy, accumulating it within a pill.

Indeed!

What he had just performed was indeed an alchemy technique, called Refining Heavenly Secrets.

Obtained from Chu Yan's inheritance, it could draw upon spiritual opportunity and refine it into a pill.

He used himself as the pill, temporarily sealing this faint spiritual opportunity.

It was not that he did not want to have sudden enlightenment, but the time was not right, and the quantity was insufficient!

"All of this must be saved for when I break through to the Ascendant realm."

Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath, ignoring the discomfort of that origin qi of heaven and earth lingering in his Purple Mansion, and without looking back, he left this place, flying deeper into the Dark Demon Lair.

...

In the past, the duration of the Demon-Suppressing Trial was not fixed, ranging from a few months to many years.

Everything depended on the growth of the Nine Nether Demon Moss in the Dark Demon Lair and the number of demon nests.

But the longest had never exceeded three years!

Because leaving the suppression of the Demon-Suppressing Tower open for too long would not only allow demonic qi to leak out but also let more spiritual qi of heaven and earth enter.

Luo Chen was well aware of this.

Therefore, his travel speed increased continuously from then on.

Even if he occasionally encountered Nascent Soul cultivators from other sects along the way, he ignored them.

During the journey, he once sensed a great power from the Dao Sect at a distance, working on dispersing a demon nest. He did not approach but took a detour.

With his powerful divine sense, these Dao Sect great powers, who were generally only at the early Ascendant stage, found it difficult to detect him.

However, during his travels, Luo Chen gradually realized that the situation in the Dark Demon Lair was very different from what those from the Su Ling Sect had said.

The number of high-rank demon spirits was simply too many!

Although cultivators from various sects had spent a year outside adapting to the demonic qi environment, once inside, they had to face not only the demon lair shrouded in demonic qi but also their most relied-upon divine sense being greatly suppressed.

Their overall strength could only exert fifty to sixty percent.

Facing high-rank demon spirits born and raised here, they were almost always at a disadvantage.

Along the way, he sensed that the number of fallen cultivators was no less than double digits.

And this was just in one direction of his travel. How many had fallen elsewhere was estimated even greater.

Luo Chen was not purely traveling either.

Occasionally, when he encountered rare treasures, he would actively stop to collect them.

Things like special medicinal herbs that could only grow in a demonic qi environment, or strange ores.

Overall, these stops did not affect his travel speed.

But after truly entering the middle zone, he realized something was wrong.

Fifth-rank demon spirits, comparable to Ascendant cultivators, had appeared!

And in no small number, entrenched in various demon nests.

This was not something that should be in the middle zone.

In the past, these things were all lurking in the Central Demon Abyss.

In a thousand years, how could things have deteriorated to this point?

Luo Chen did not understand, but his heart sank slightly.

If fifth-rank demon spirits had appeared in the middle zone, then in the Central Demon Abyss, would there be demon spirits comparable to late Ascendant, or even Void Refining stage?

Luo Chen could only do his best to restrain his aura, to avoid attracting the attention of powerful demon spirits.

His main task on this trip was still to break through to the Ascendant realm, not unnecessary battles.

Just like one day, when he saw a great power from the Dao Sect leading dozens of Dao Sect Nascent Souls in a desperate struggle against three fifth-rank demon spirits, he did not intervene. He merely glanced and took a detour.

"One person fighting three fifth-rank demon spirits alone, even with the help of many disciples forming a formation, it still shows the might of a Dao Sect Ascendant."

"In comparison, low-rank cultivators from the Mountains and Seas Realm might have rich combat experience, but the top Ascendant great powers lack experience fighting peers of the same rank. Compared to these native Xuan Realm Ascendants, they are slightly inferior."

"But this is only a temporary illusion. Once integrated into the Xuan Realm and after a few more battles, the Ascendant cultivators who came up from the lower realm will naturally show their superiority."

"Especially since they almost all grasp the essence of a realm, their potential is even greater than that of Xuan Realm great powers."

In that brief glance, Luo Chen made some personal judgments.

But his travel speed ultimately came to a halt.

By the shore of an underground great lake.

Suddenly, a startling sword light shot up into the sky.

Where the sword light passed, it swept the surroundings clean. Sharp screams nearly shattered eardrums.

A demon spirit, like a flood dragon, fell powerlessly into the great lake, splashing up city-wall-like giant waves.

Beneath the waves were seven figures gathered together.

Six were on the outside, forming a great formation.

At the formation's eye, an old man sat cross-legged, holding an extremely illusory sword shadow, his face haggard.

"Suwang Sword!"

"So it's them!"

Luo Chen murmured, his gaze involuntarily falling into the great lake, his brow furrowing slightly.

After the city-wall-like waves fell, a whirlpool was slowly forming. (Chapter End)
