# Chapter 1212: An Extraordinary Invitation, First Display of Talent

He Jun!

He was merely a second-grade alchemist, not particularly outstanding within the vast Suling Sect. The reason many people called him Master He was largely due to his background.

His great-uncle was none other than Zhibei Sage, one of the Seven Sages of Suling, renowned as a dual master of alchemy and swordsmanship.

Alchemy came before the sword!

Neither his late-stage Sage cultivation nor the strength displayed by his identity as a sword cultivator could compare to the immense influence brought by Zhibei Sage's status as a grandmaster of the alchemical dao.

According to Luo Chen's divine sense probing, the late-stage Sage grand cultivator permanently stationed at Danhuang Peak was precisely Zhibei Sage.

Interestingly enough.

In the past, only the sect master, Tai Su Lingjun, and the eight Nascent Soul elders had been famous outside the sect.

But after Tai Su Lingjun broke through to the Unity stage, within a mere two hundred years, the title of the Seven Sages of Suling had spread far and wide.

Aside from Luo Chen's nominal master, Yaoxi Sage, who was renowned for her talent, the other six Sages were all at the late-stage Sage realm with extremely formidable combat power, far surpassing the thousand-plus Sages of the Suling Sect.

Luo Chen couldn't help but suspect that the Suling Sect wanted to expand its external influence, so they specially selected seven Sage cultivators with extraordinary combat power to serve as the sect's representatives.

Otherwise, it would be hard to understand why they would include a long-renowned alchemical grandmaster among the Seven Sages of Suling.

But that was beside the point.

Regarding He Jun's personal invitation for Luo Chen to attend a pill-testing conference hosted by an alchemy master, Luo Chen was initially somewhat "flattered."

But he quickly understood the intention behind such an extraordinary invitation.

There were probably two reasons!

First, they were astonished by Luo Chen's terrifying talent in refining three types of first-grade pills within just three months, showing a desire to appreciate and promote him.

Second, it was likely related to his master.

Yaoxi Sage and Zhibei Sage were both among the Seven Sages of Suling, both hailed as the most promising candidates to become the ninth Nascent Soul elder.

While their relationship wasn't close, it was at least harmonious.

He Jun's proactive goodwill gesture also served to bring the two families closer.

Naturally, Luo Chen gladly accepted.

He wasn't particularly interested in testing whatever pills, but he was somewhat interested in that Master Gongsun.

After all, he was the author of the Suling Sect's *Elementary Introduction to the Alchemical Dao*.

He had previously thought about getting to know him when he had the chance, and now that the opportunity had come, how could he refuse?

Early the next morning.

Luo Chen hurried out.

When he arrived at the Alchemy Courtyard, he unexpectedly met an "old acquaintance."

Wan Jiuzhang stood outside the Step Pill Pavilion, his face full of worry.

He had come today seeking pills but was refused entry.

According to the duty cultivator there, the second-grade alchemists on Miaodan Peak were all busy today and not receiving visitors.

He heard there was a pill-testing conference being held in the courtyard?

It seemed he would have to come back tomorrow.

Just as Wan Jiuzhang was about to leave after being shut out, a voice of "surprise" came from behind him.

"Senior Brother, why are you here?"

Wan Jiuzhang turned around and saw a young man in green robes with rosy lips, white teeth, and a face like jade standing before him.

He exclaimed in surprise, "Xuan Luo, why are you here?"

Luo Chen smiled slightly and pointed to the cloud-patterned token hanging at his waist.

"Senior Brother, don't you know that your junior brother is now a first-grade alchemist in the Alchemy Courtyard?"

Wan Jiuzhang looked closely and saw a cloud pattern shining brightly.

He knew that was the symbol of a first-grade alchemist in the sect.

He was initially somewhat delighted, but then his brow furrowed involuntarily.

"Junior Brother, you should be diligently cultivating now. How can you waste your energy on these trivial arts?"

Well!

For those who sought immortality, everything other than cultivation was a trivial art.

Luo Chen smiled helplessly and brought out the same explanation he had used before.

"Before going into seclusion, Master advised me. She said my cultivation speed was too fast, which could easily lead to an unstable Dao heart and demonic possession. So I've been distracting myself with miscellaneous arts to temper myself. As it happens, I have some talent in the alchemical dao, so I joined the Alchemy Courtyard."

"Master's advice?" Wan Jiuzhang suddenly understood and didn't press further.

Luo Chen proactively changed the subject, or rather, returned to the original topic.

"Senior Brother, why have you come here? Are you seeking pills and medicine?"

"Yes!"

"That's strange. With your cultivation level, Senior Brother, shouldn't you be seeking pills at Lingdan Peak or Danhuang Mountain? Why come to Miaodan Peak?"

Hearing this, Wan Jiuzhang sighed, a look of shame on his face.

"Before going into seclusion, Master instructed me to take good care of the junior brothers and sisters. Last month, when I checked some people's progress, I found they had been slacking off and advancing slowly. It was my dereliction of duty. So I thought I'd come to the Alchemy Courtyard to order a batch of custom pills to help them cultivate better."

Speaking thus, he sighed again.

"But it turns out the Alchemy Courtyard has internal business today, and the alchemists I wanted to see are all busy."

Luo Chen suddenly understood and couldn't help but chuckle.

Those devoted to bitter cultivation regarded alchemy and talisman-making as trivial arts, but when they truly encountered a bottleneck in their cultivation, they would unhesitatingly seek the assistance of these same trivial arts.

"Senior Brother, come up the mountain with me! If the pill-testing conference ends early, you might still be able to see a few alchemists."

Wan Jiuzhang's face lit up. "Is that possible?"

Luo Chen shook the token at his waist. "I have at least this much authority."

"Haha, I never thought little junior brother had such talent, becoming a first-grade alchemist so quickly."

"You flatter me. Such talent is not worth mentioning. I merely dabbled in it during my time as a rogue cultivator."

The two chatted and laughed as they headed toward Miaodan Peak.

Thanks to Luo Chen's identity as a first-grade alchemist, they encountered no obstacles.

Upon reaching the pill hall at the peak, Luo Chen gave a few instructions before they entered together.

Wan Jiuzhang said nothing, merely waiting quietly on the side.

But as he entered the hall and watched Luo Chen, guided by He Jun, making acquaintances with one alchemist after another, he began to notice something strange.

To make sure he wasn't mistaken, he carefully released his divine sense to scan the entire hall.

Second-grade!

Second-grade!

Still second-grade!

In the vast pill hall today, over a thousand alchemists had gathered, all of them second-grade.

The only exception was his little junior brother—Xuan Luo!

This scene left Wan Jiuzhang speechless.

Perhaps Xuan Luo's alchemical talent was not merely "some" after all.

...

"This is Ning Fu, who specializes in water-based alchemy."

"This is Zhu Xiao, proficient in seventeen types of pill arts and skilled in the refining methods of five types of second-grade pills."

"Junior Brother Xuan Luo, a true disciple of Yuquan Peak under Yaoxi Sage. Although he is a first-grade alchemist, he has already learned to refine three types of first-grade pills, and in just three months!"

Accompanied personally by He Jun, Luo Chen continuously made new friends.

These were all alchemists who had good relations with He Jun, so they showed great goodwill toward Luo Chen.

Upon hearing He Jun's praise, they couldn't help but regard Luo Chen more highly.

Such was the way of the world—those with talent could do whatever they pleased!

Although Luo Chen was only a first-grade alchemist now, who knew how high his ceiling would reach in the future?

As He Jun put it, his extraordinary invitation to Luo Chen today was precisely because of his terrifying alchemical talent.

"Junior Brother Xuan Luo need not worry. Today, just listen and don't feel compelled to offer any insights. If you can pick up a few words of wisdom, it will greatly benefit your future study of alchemy. Don't be fooled by Master Gongsun being only a third-grade alchemist; in theoretical matters, he is by no means inferior to a fourth-grade alchemist. For instance, he can freely come and go from Danhuang Mountain."

He Jun said this.

Luo Chen nodded, putting on an appearance of being eager to learn.

The atmosphere in the hall was quite lively.

When a white-haired old man entered the hall, the lively atmosphere reached its peak.

Everyone bowed in unison. "Greetings, Master Gongsun!"

The old man's face was covered in wrinkles, like the bark of a thousand-year-old tree, but his eyes were exceptionally bright, like the bright moon, unforgettable at first sight.

He smiled kindly and pressed down with his hand.

"Fellow junior brothers need not stand on ceremony. Please, be seated!"

The alchemists all sat cross-legged on their meditation cushions.

Luo Chen was among them, seated at the very end, curiously watching the old man above.

As his divine sense swept over him, he was startled to discover that the old man was only at the early-stage Adept realm, and there was a thick aura of death about him. He likely had only about ten years left to live.

A grandmaster at the end of his road?

Facing everyone, Gongsun Mao spoke in a very calm and natural tone.

"I am now over five hundred and thirty years old. My lifespan will likely come to an end soon. All of you are the future hope of our Suling Sect's alchemical lineage. To be able to cooperate with you all before my end is truly my fortune."

Everyone was stunned. They had never expected such an opening.

Yet there was no trace of sorrow or despair on Gongsun Mao's face. Instead, he seemed quite invigorated.

"I have had three wishes in my life. First, to independently refine a fourth-grade pill. Second, to write a book and pass it down to posterity. Third, to spread the fame of the Suling Sect's alchemical dao."

"Of these three wishes, the first two have already been fulfilled. Only the third has yet to show results. Although the Suling Sect has now established an independent Alchemy Courtyard, relying on its abundant medicinal herb resources to delve deeply into alchemy, the outside world's impression of us is still that we are proficient in the medicinal herb industry. When it comes to alchemical reputation, we are not even comparable to the Immortal Pill Tomb of the Nine Great Saint Sects, let alone powers like the Medicine Mother Palace and the Divine Hand Valley, which have Unity and Divine Transformation cultivators."

"The reason is that these powers all have unique pills renowned far and wide!"

"As for the pills refined by our Suling Sect, they are merely the most common varieties found on the Youxuan Continent. Other powers also sell them, so naturally, others will not think highly of us."

"Therefore, I have always wanted to refine a unique pill with our sect's characteristics, to spread the fame of the Suling Sect's alchemical dao."

"Fortunately, while organizing the library and writing my alchemy book, I discovered an ancient remnant formula and managed to complete and refine it."

"It is the Circulation Qi Pill!"

Luo Chen sat quietly below, listening to these words, and couldn't help but feel moved.

This Gongsun Mao, in terms of alchemical skill, might not be as accomplished as some he had met before.

But his vision was grand, truly moving.

Personal pursuits, writing books to pass down to posterity, and still wanting to contribute to the sect in his old age.

Perhaps this was also why his cultivation level was not high—because he had devoted all his energy to the alchemical dao.

Although Luo Chen did not fully agree with this approach, he still respected it.

Moreover, he was very interested in the Circulation Qi Pill that Gongsun Mao had mentioned.

Yesterday, He Jun had briefly introduced this ancient pill.

Its miraculous effects were such that even a master alchemist like Luo Chen couldn't help but admire it.

It was a third-grade pill!

After taking it, without actively circulating their techniques, a cultivator's mana would automatically follow their familiar cultivation routes, greatly enhancing their cultivation efficiency.

Moreover, the burden on the soul and body was extremely, extremely small!

This was practically a cultivation cheat!

Others could only cultivate once or twice a day, or at most for a few days at a time, but with the aid of this pill, they could greatly increase the number of cultivation sessions and even extend the maximum cultivation time.

If such a pill could truly be refined.

Not only would it greatly enhance the strength of the Suling Sect's disciples, but if sold outside, it would surely cause a sensation in the cultivation world, with countless cultivators flocking to obtain just one pill!

But such a miraculous pill naturally had its stringent requirements.

The simplest point was the scarcity of its main ingredients!

The most important main ingredient involved had long been extinct on the Youxuan Continent.

Even with the Suling Sect's expertise in medicinal herb cultivation and vast resources, they had only managed to cultivate a few plants, barely allowing Gongsun Mao to refine a single third-grade Circulation Qi Pill.

Therefore!

Gongsun Mao had settled for the next best thing, intending to reduce the medicinal power and refine a weakened second-grade Circulation Qi Pill, and achieve mass production!

If this could truly succeed, its benefit to the Suling Sect would be no less than gaining an additional Divine Transformation great power!

Today's pill-testing conference was for this purpose.

Gongsun Mao had already perfected the theoretical assumptions on Lingdan Peak with a group of third-grade alchemists, deducing enough possibilities.

Then he came to Miaodan Peak to enlist the help of over a thousand second-grade alchemists to test those possibilities.

At the main seat of the pill hall.

The old man spoke eloquently, explaining the various theories related to the Circulation Qi Pill one by one.

This was to lay the groundwork for the subsequent group experiments in pill refining.

During this time, any second-grade alchemist on Miaodan Peak who could not understand could ask him questions.

Of course, if anyone had different understandings, they could boldly raise them. Gongsun Mao would seriously consider them before deciding whether to adopt them.

Moreover, he had distributed the completed third-grade pill formula to everyone as the most fundamental theoretical basis.

Luo Chen did not receive one, as he was not qualified for this meeting.

But after He Jun reviewed it, he gave his own copy to Luo Chen.

Upon first obtaining the original pill formula, Luo Chen began to read it carefully.

As he read, listening to the old man above expounding on various theories, he gradually formed the most intuitive feeling.

"The problem shouldn't lie with the main ingredients!"

To take care of Luo Chen, He Jun had sat beside him.

Hearing this low murmur, he asked curiously, "What?"

Luo Chen smiled, then stood up.

He Jun was startled, instinctively looking up.

Because of Luo Chen's sudden movement, the alchemists in the back rows noticed and looked over in confusion.

The originally lively atmosphere turned strange.

This strange atmosphere spread forward, drawing one gaze after another turning back.

Until Gongsun Mao looked over in confusion.

Luo Chen's expression was calm. "Master Gongsun, might I be permitted to say a few words?"

Gongsun Mao was already somewhat surprised to see Luo Chen's youthful face, and when he noticed the first-grade token at his waist, he was even more astonished.

"You are?"

He Jun quickly stood up to introduce him to the old man, and to the many alchemists who didn't know him.

"This is Xuan Luo, a true disciple of Yuquan Peak under Yaoxi Sage. He joined the Alchemy Courtyard three months ago and has refined three types of first-grade pills from scratch, showing excellent talent. That's why I specially invited him to observe the pill-testing conference. This junior brother is still young. If he says anything inappropriate, I ask all senior brothers to be lenient."

After learning Luo Chen's background, the hall buzzed with discussion.

But Gongsun Mao's eyes lit up. "Three types of first-grade pills in three months. It seems Junior Brother Xuan Luo indeed has outstanding alchemical talent. If you have any thoughts, speak frankly. Don't worry too much."

Facing everyone's gaze, the young Xuan Luo showed no sign of stage fright.

He flicked the pill formula in his hand and said loudly, "Master, you are mistaken. The key should not lie with the main ingredients!"

As soon as these words were spoken, the entire hall was shocked!
