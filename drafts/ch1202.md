# Chapter 1202: Continental Overlord, Taisu Immortal City

Within the Heavenly Phoenix Clan.

There stood a parasol tree rare in the current age, towering ten thousand zhang high, piercing straight into the clouds.

Even the branches spreading out from its main trunk were far thicker and sturdier than ordinary ancient trees.

Birds carrying powerful auras circled and shuttled back and forth among the lower branches, while the very top remained utterly pure and undisturbed, with none daring to trespass.

For there resided the cave dwelling of the Heavenly Phoenix Clan's sole Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerable, Ancestor Heavenly Phoenix.

Each branch, like a stone slab, had formed a magnificent palace resembling a bird's nest, where Ancestor Heavenly Phoenix made her abode.

Suddenly!

She opened her wrinkled eyes and gazed into the depths of the palace.

In a secret chamber, rows of soul lamps were neatly arranged, each burning with vigorous flames.

The method of making soul lamps had been learned by the Heavenly Phoenix Clan from the human race.

However, the Heavenly Phoenix Clan's soul lamps only took a tiny bit of soul power from their clansmen, without causing any loss of soul or affecting normal cultivation.

Setting up soul lamps was merely a convenient way to monitor the survival of the clan's prodigies.

This was by no means a useless measure!

Years ago, there was a clansman named Rosycloud, who possessed the most orthodox phoenix bloodline. From birth, she became the most watched prodigy within the clan. Ancestor Heavenly Phoenix personally taught her, imparting peerless techniques.

Two thousand years ago, Rosycloud mysteriously disappeared, and the outside world spread word that she had already fallen.

But Ancestor Heavenly Phoenix had never panicked, precisely because of the soul lamp.

Even though Rosycloud's soul lamp flame flickered weakly, it still burned tenaciously.

Later, relying on the soul lamp's guidance, they managed to pull Rosycloud back.

The establishment of soul lamps was an important means of ensuring the survival of the Heavenly Phoenix Clan's prodigies.

Whether as a warning reminder, a distress signal in times of peril, or providing coordinates for subsequent rescue operations, as long as one was still within the Xuan Realm, this lamp could guide the way to life.

And yet!

*Pfft!*

As if blown by the wind, among the neatly arranged soul lamps, one trembled violently, and the flame atop it suddenly extinguished.

There was no time to react; it went out so abruptly, catching everyone off guard.

"Lie Yu... has fallen."

Ancestor Heavenly Phoenix uttered a voice filled with complex emotions, containing shock, anger, and regret.

On the Youxuan Continent, the human race's power was the greatest!

The reason the Heavenly Phoenix Clan could hold a place was entirely due to her, an ancient Heavenly Venerable with a long lifespan, standing alone in support.

But the Heavenly Phoenix Clan's own strength was actually not that strong.

The number of Unity cultivators within the clan was scarce. Not to mention comparing with the superpower that ruled the Youxuan Continent, the Nether Capital Source Court, even compared to its nine great sacred sects, they were only on par.

Over so many years, Ancestor Heavenly Phoenix had poured her heart and soul into nurturing descendants, finally bearing fruit.

The phoenix child Rosycloud Sovereign needed no further mention. After returning from her tribulation, she smoothly broke through to Unity, and was the one with the greatest hope of advancing to Great Vehicle in the future, the one to take charge of the Heavenly Phoenix Clan.

Below the phoenix child, there had successively emerged stunning collateral clansmen like Lie Yu and Azure Frost.

Everything seemed to be thriving, moving in a better direction.

However, recent events seemed to show bad omens.

Azure Frost, who was originally certain to break through, had strangely failed in her breakthrough to Nascent Shattering.

Lie Yu, who had gone out to travel, had fallen in an instant.

This made Ancestor Heavenly Phoenix's heart inexplicably heavy.

She always felt a sense of impending storm!

But for now, she still had to send someone to handle Lie Yu's matter.

Who to send?

"Yan Emperor! With his late Unity strength, whether facing the nine great sacred sects or the Nether Capital Source Court, he can advance and retreat freely."

With her mind made up, her divine sense went out.

On a spiritual mountain a hundred thousand li away from the Phoenix Nest.

A man dressed in a black and red imperial robe was conversing with a friend when his expression suddenly froze.

He closed his eyes, listening intently.

The friend beside him sensed something and dared not disturb.

After a long while, the dignified man slowly opened his eyes.

The friend asked cautiously, "Imperial Lord, what has happened, to trouble the Ancestor to personally send a message?"

Yan Emperor's gaze fell upon his friend. "Lie Ying, this matter is also related to your Lie Feng lineage."

Lie Ying Unity was stunned.

When Yan Emperor narrated the matter in detail, Lie Ying Unity's expression had already changed drastically.

The Heavenly Phoenix Clan revered the phoenix, but its composition was actually quite complex.

Because the divine beast phoenix reproduced with difficulty, the core clansmen were actually only a few.

What truly formed the Heavenly Phoenix Clan was a large number of other feather tribes containing thin phoenix bloodlines.

For instance, the Lie Feng Beast was a rather important branch within the Heavenly Phoenix Clan. Especially with the strong rise of the junior Lie Yu Demon Lord, this lineage had the potential to simultaneously possess two Unity cultivators.

By then, their status within the clan would surely rise to another level.

But now...

Lie Ying Unity's expression turned extremely ugly, with even a hint of disbelief on his face:

"How could this be? Lie Yu's strength was not weak, and he had cultivated the *Nine Transformations of Heaven-Splitting*. Not to mention being invincible at the same level, even if he couldn't defeat an opponent, he could always escape!"

Yan Emperor pondered for a moment, then slowly said, "I naturally believe in the life-saving methods of your lineage's *Nine Transformations of Heaven-Splitting*. But the Ancestor's transmission said that Lie Yu fell in an instant. There was no time for any reaction, not even a chance to send a warning or plea for help through the soul lamp. I suspect the enemy was not a Nascent Shattering cultivator."

Lie Ying Unity's vertical pupils turned cold. "You mean, a Unity cultivator personally made a move?"

"That's probably it. Otherwise, I can't think of any existence that could instantly kill a Lie Feng Beast known for its unparalleled escape speed." Yan Emperor shook his head, then continued, "Besides, the Ancestor entrusted this matter to me, which also means she thinks the same way."

After confirming this idea, Lie Ying Unity's expression grew even uglier.

Within the Heavenly Phoenix Clan, Yan Emperor was one of the few late Unity powerhouses, only a step away from the Great Vehicle stage.

If it were just an ordinary enemy, there would be no need to send him.

In other words, it was very likely that a Unity cultivator had bullied the weak, instantly killing his clansman.

Yan Emperor drained the wine in his cup, slowly rose, and cast his gaze westward.

"The Youxuan Continent is led by the human race. This matter should be investigated starting from the human side."

"Coincidentally, regarding the Rosycloud matter back then, although on the surface it was the work of demon race remnants, according to what Rosycloud said after her return, it was also inseparable from human forces."

"This time, I will investigate everything thoroughly!"

...

West of the Sunset Plains.

As they left the wilderness, traces of human habitation gradually appeared.

Mountain villages, mortal kingdoms, cultivation markets, sect immortal cities...

Luo Chen traveled along, releasing his divine sense, taking in everything.

A long-lost feeling of ease finally surged into his heart.

Being able to release his divine sense so freely—how could that not bring joy?

It was just a pity that his primordial spirit had collapsed and his Purple Mansion was withered. Even with powerful divine sense, it was like rootless duckweed.

This left a constant sense of unease lingering in his heart.

Suddenly, a mixed and rich spiritual energy came through his divine sense.

Luo Chen looked down and saw a magnificent immortal city sitting upon the earth.

"Taisu Immortal City?"

Luo Chen's eyes rolled, and he made a decision. He put away his flying speed and descended.

Outside the moat, Luo Chen stood still.

Surrounding him, streaks of light came and went.

One by one, cultivators with an immortal air gathered from all directions, then entered the city named Taisu.

In the sky, many dharma boats circled.

Such a scene was so familiar!

It actually gave Luo Chen a feeling of returning to the Eastern Wilderness cultivation world.

This feeling grew even stronger when he paid spirit stones to enter the city.

"Is this also a custom in the Xuan Realm?"

Just as this thought arose, Luo Chen couldn't help but smile.

If his judgment back then was correct, the five sacred lands of the Mountains and Seas Realm originated from the Xuan Realm. Even if their rules and regulations were adapted to local conditions, they were generally similar.

Familiarity was only natural.

Luo Chen strolled along the wide streets, spreading his divine sense to continue observing his surroundings.

The cultivation levels of the passing cultivators were generally not weak.

Most were at the Adept stage, and Sage cultivators were also common.

In contrast, Initiate cultivators were relatively rare, and Qi Refinement cultivators were even fewer, hard to spot even after half a day.

That made sense!

Just the single top-grade spirit stone required to enter the city was enough to filter out most Qi Refinement cultivators.

From this point, Luo Chen roughly guessed the economic situation of the cultivation world around the Xuan Realm... no, it should be said, around Taisu Immortal City.

The bottom-level cultivators were still as poor as ever!

As for another matter Luo Chen was concerned about, he also got a rough picture under his careful perception.

"The spiritual energy concentration in the immortal city is not bad, generally at the second-order spirit vein level."

"The buildings in the core area of the city emit a spiritual energy concentration only found in third-order spirit veins."

"Fourth-order spirit vein... there is one place, but although the concentration is there, the purity is insufficient. Clear but not excellent, containing impurities within. Sage cultivators can stay here, but long-term cultivation is quite unsuitable."

These situations could all be observed through divine sense.

But for more detailed information about this place, he would need to put in some effort.

Luo Chen's divine sense swept, and he found a target.

He walked straight towards the city gate. A young man in a yellow earth dharma robe was squatting on the ground, chewing on a blade of grass, looking boredly at the passing crowd.

When Luo Chen's figure appeared before him, a look of anticipation appeared on his face, and he quickly stood up to greet him.

...

"You mean, immortal cities like Taisu City are countless on the Youxuan Continent?"

"Yes! After all, the Youxuan Continent has abundant spiritual energy and rich resources. Major sects always need bases to collect resources, and immortal cities are the product of such a system. However, the establishment of immortal cities also has strict standards. An immortal city of Taisu City's scale can only be built by existences at the Ascendant level and above. Sects below Ascendant can only build smaller immortal cities, or even markets."

Huang Yuanhua said eagerly, his words often expanding several times based on the other's questions.

He was putting in so much effort for a reason.

Ten top-grade spirit stones was his reward.

Such a price was the biggest deal he had encountered in recent years.

Not to mention Initiate cultivators, even Adept cultivators weren't so generous.

If it weren't for the fact that the man before him, who looked even younger than him, showed no trace of dharma power and couldn't be identified, he would even suspect that the other party was an old Sage.

But true old Sages, which one wasn't widely read and deeply knowledgeable? How could they be completely ignorant of the local situation?

Huang Yuanhua suspected that the other party was probably a genius carefully cultivated by some small sect, who had been secluded in the sect for decades, diligently cultivating, hence so wealthy yet ignorant.

After listening to the introduction, Luo Chen pondered for a moment, then slowly asked, "On the Youxuan Continent, are there many sects at the Ascendant level and above?"

Huang Yuanhua nodded heavily, "Many, very many. Those with names, those without names, count in the tens of thousands. The reason for this situation is not complicated, because the overlord of this continent, the Nether Capital Source Court, doesn't really manage things. Apart from limiting the number of sacred sects, they don't care about the rest of the small sects."

"Nether Capital Source Court?"

"Yes, that is the only overlord-level force on the Youxuan Continent. Within it, there are countless experts. Even the number of Great Vehicle cultivators, known as Heavenly Venerables, probably exceeds double digits! In comparison, although the Heavenly Phoenix Demon Clan barely survives, they only hold a corner."

When Luo Chen heard that the Nether Capital had over ten Great Vehicle cultivators, his expression was extremely vivid.

Even though the *Xuan Realm Secret Record* had repeatedly mentioned that the Nether Capital was a top force in the Xuan Realm, he only thought they had one or two Great Vehicle Celestial Venerables within the sect. He never expected their foundation to be so deep!

Or rather, had he underestimated the foundation of the Xuan Realm?

Taking a deep breath, Luo Chen first set aside the Nether Capital Source Court and asked about the rest.

Huang Yuanhua thought while quickly speaking.

Below the Nether Capital Source Court, there were nine great sects, known as sacred lands, called sacred sects, each with Unity cultivators sitting in charge!

Below the nine great sacred sects were the countless large and small sects Huang Yuanhua had mentioned earlier.

The outstanding ones included the Ten Thousand Flowers Sect, the Five Elements Divine Sect, the Cold River Sect, and so on.

Hearing the name "Five Elements Divine Sect" again, a strange look flashed across Luo Chen's face.

"The Taisu Immortal City we are in now was built by the Su Ling Sect. The Su Ling Sect is not very strong. I heard that its sect master only broke through to Nascent Shattering a few hundred years ago. However, because they deal in medicinal herb business, the Su Ling Sect has a great reputation. Even in front of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, one of the nine great sacred sects, they can speak."

Huang Yuanhua enthusiastically shared gossip, a look of pride on his face.

Luo Chen had gradually sorted out the general situation.

On the Youxuan Continent, the Heavenly Phoenix Clan held a corner, while the Nether Capital was the true overlord, known as the Nether Capital Source Court.

But the Nether Capital did not manage things, allowing the forces below to grow wildly.

Thus, the nine great sacred sects were active on the surface.

Moreover, because the spiritual energy concentration in the Xuan Realm was extremely high, many independent cultivators attained the Dao. To pass on their traditions, they often established sects and families to inherit their own techniques and philosophies.

The current area was under the jurisdiction of the Su Ling Sect.

And the nearest of the nine great sacred sects was the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!

"Speaking of which, the Su Ling Sect will be holding an Initiation Conference in three years. That will be a great opportunity for us independent cultivators."

"Initiation Conference?"

"That is, selecting disciples!"

Luo Chen was curious. "How so? You all have your own skills, and your cultivation levels are somewhat accomplished. How could a mere Nascent Shattering force choose disciples from among you?"

Huang Yuanhua explained, "The Su Ling Sect is different from other sects with strict inheritance. They mainly deal in medicinal herb business and have a huge medicinal herb cultivation system within the sect. Therefore, they are in urgent need of manpower. Every so often, they widely recruit disciples. Especially for independent cultivators who have achievements in the dao of medicinal herbs, they will open the door wide. Of course, having a clean background is still a requirement."

Speaking of this, Huang Yuanhua showed a rather proud smile.

"I, Huang, am not boasting. I am proficient in many spirit planting techniques, and I have a great chance of being selected!"

After hearing this, Luo Chen showed a thoughtful expression.

The dao of medicinal herbs?

Spirit planting techniques?
