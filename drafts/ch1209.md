# Chapter 1209: Myriad Transformations Mystic Chapter, Re-entering the Dao of Alchemy

Deep within the earth vein, spiritual light flickered.

It reflected upon the green-robed man's face, light and shadow alternating, dreamlike and illusory.

If judged by the quantity of spiritual qi alone, this place could be considered high-quality. However, the convergence of earth veins caused a large amount of filthy, turbid, baleful qi to settle, merging into the clear spiritual qi, making it unsuitable for human absorption instead.

This was the main reason the spiritual qi concentration in this basin cave dwelling was not high.

However, those filthy, turbid, baleful qi did not seem to affect the green-robed man at all.

A layer of blood-colored cloth robe emerged spontaneously.

Stream after stream of turbid qi surged into the man's body. Not only did it fail to pollute him, but it was all absorbed by that layer of blood-colored cloth robe, making it even deeper in hue.

Luo Chen was not at all surprised by this scene.

The Blood Cloth Robe, as a martial dao divine ability he had created himself, was not particularly strong initially.

But it possessed growth potential!

Regardless of whether it was the resentment qi and death qi of enemies he had killed, or the filthy turbid balefulness harmful to ordinary people, it could all be devoured, gradually forming an extremely powerful defense.

At this moment, most of his attention was on the jade slip *Golden Transformation Art* in his hand.

Sage Yaoxi hadn't even asked Luo Chen and the others what techniques they had previously cultivated before directly bestowing this technique. She presumably believed that the *Golden Transformation Art* was certainly superior to the common, mass-produced techniques that rogue cultivators practiced.

Hence, she had them directly switch their main cultivation techniques.

Luo Chen carefully read through it and couldn't help but nod to himself.

It truly was so!

Based on his experience, he could discern that even with only the Qi Refinement and Initiate sections, the *Golden Transformation Art* already showed signs of its wondrous subtlety.

Far surpassing the many low-level techniques he had collected back in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

It could be imagined that the subsequent chapters of the *Golden Transformation Art* must be even more miraculous!

"Originally, I only planned to practice it casually, just to go through the motions for Yaoxi's inspection."

"Now it seems I can practice it properly, and also conveniently increase my proficiency, supplementing my achievement points."

Back when he was deducing the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra*, Luo Chen's achievement points had truly been completely exhausted.

Reaching an all-time low since he started.

To prepare for unforeseen future circumstances, it was necessary to replenish some.

Luo Chen mobilized his divine sense and carefully perused the *Golden Transformation Art* once more.

After thoroughly understanding and digesting it, he assumed a cross-legged sitting posture with his five hearts facing the sky, his hands forming the shape of a sword seal.

"Golden essence transforms into qi, spirit illuminates the myriad meridians. Apertures store the soldier's pivot, melt gold into the sea..."

As he murmured the incantation, the surrounding spiritual qi, as if guided, surged toward his body.

...

One month later.

Luo Chen's gaze swept over the attribute panel, revealing a satisfied expression.

**[Golden Transformation Art: Grand Completion]**

It had taken him only a single month to successfully switch his main cultivation technique and also cultivate both the Qi Refinement and Initiate sections of the *Golden Transformation Art* to the Grand Completion level.

Ten achievement points, pocketed.

"Is this considered exploiting a loophole in the system?"

Luo Chen couldn't help but chuckle softly.

The attribute panel seemed to grant achievement points simply for learning something successfully.

But it wasn't that simple.

Otherwise, Luo Chen would have spammed low-level spells back in the Mountains and Seas Realm long ago, stockpiling thousands or tens of thousands of achievement points.

In reality, as his realm improved, cultivating low-level skills almost no longer yielded many achievement points.

This wasn't just true for the immortal dao; the martial dao was the same.

Unless he had no achievements whatsoever on that particular path.

But back in the Guixu Realm, when he switched to orthodox martial dao, even low-level martial techniques could bring him a considerable number of achievement points.

Now that he had arrived in the Xuan Realm, due to the complete loss of his immortal dao realm, re-cultivating low-level spells could also yield achievement points.

It seemed that when the attribute panel tallied, it used Luo Chen's own current state as a reference.

"This is not bad. I might be able to spend some time on low-level skills, grind some more achievement points, and prepare for the future deduction of the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra*."

The *Chaos Indestructible Sutra* was deduced from the *God-Devil Formless*. It was brought to Great Accomplishment while watching stars fall in the Sea of Stars.

But in terms of the technique's upper limit alone, it was only at the Great Accomplishment stage, not yet Perfection.

At the very least, Luo Chen was still unclear on how to cultivate from the Destruction Realm to the Martial Dao Nirvana Realm.

And he held unimaginably high hopes for this technique!

In his view, the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra* was not merely a martial dao technique. Because it could absorb various types of energy, it could completely cover Luo Chen's immortal dao technique, allowing him to maintain the unity of immortal and martial arts, cultivating both body and dharma!

After all, it was just the conversion and utilization of energy.

As for the law cultivation required for the higher realms of the immortal dao, Luo Chen felt it had little to do with the main cultivation technique anymore.

The key lay in the word "comprehension."

Therefore, the two could be completely separated.

If he could truly advance the deduction of the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra* further, he could not only progress further in the martial dao but also convert his dharma power more easily and freely.

Thus, achievement points related to technique deduction were very necessary.

"Aside from my cultivation talent, perhaps I can display more abilities to gain higher authority within the Sushu Sect, allowing me to access more fields."

Luo Chen pondered silently, already having a plan in mind.

Of course, that was for later.

For now, the priority was still to increase his cultivation and first pass Yaoxi's inspection.

Luo Chen closed his eyes and once again activated the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra*. The surrounding spiritual qi surged violently, swallowed by him like a whale.

Most of this spiritual qi was converted into Blood Refining Divine Astral Gang, with only a small portion transforming into Five Elements spiritual power.

Time passed bit by bit in this monotonous cultivation.

...

Ten years later.

On Jade Spring Peak, within the Jade Spring Palace.

Three figures stood silently, as if waiting for something.

Suddenly!

The lamps and candles within the hall flickered, and a fragrant breeze arrived.

The three bowed respectfully, calling out in unison: "Master!"

Yaoxi's gaze swept over the three, a look of satisfaction appearing in her eyes.

With her divine sense inspection, she naturally knew that these three registered disciples had all completed the conversion of their main cultivation techniques, and their spiritual power had become much purer.

It showed that they were not only perceptive but also very hardworking.

With such aptitude and temperament, they were worthy of becoming her true disciples.

But just as her gaze was about to turn away, she suddenly let out a surprised exclamation.

"Xuan Luo, you've reached the mid-Initiate stage?"

At these words, both Ji Yue and Feng Zhiyan subconsciously turned their gazes to Luo Chen, quite shocked.

The techniques of the Sushu Sect were so profound and unfathomable. Being able to convert within ten years was already no small feat. Yet Xuan Luo had actually managed to break through a minor realm as well.

Luo Chen said respectfully, "Master's eyes are like torches. This disciple has indeed cultivated to the fourth layer of Initiate."

Yaoxi clicked her tongue in admiration. "I never expected your Sharp Gold Body to be so compatible with your spiritual root. It seems being a rogue cultivator outside really held you back!"

Luo Chen smiled. "It's all thanks to the miraculous technique bestowed by Master and the assistance of the sect's cave dwelling!"

Yaoxi didn't think much of it. With Luo Chen's aptitude, even without the sect's help or high-level techniques, he could have achieved something in the future as a rogue cultivator.

It seemed the Sushu Sect had really found a treasure.

Her gaze swept over the three, lost in thought.

Ji Yue and Feng Zhiyan were both a bit anxious, worried about what she might say next.

Luo Chen, on the other hand, was perfectly calm.

In reality, his true realm was naturally not just the mid-Initiate stage.

In ten years, he had not only cultivated the *Golden Transformation Art* to Grand Completion but also absorbed a large amount of spiritual qi through the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra*, pushing his realm to the late Initiate stage—specifically, the ninth layer of Initiate!

If he wanted, even forming a golden core could happen in the next moment.

The reason he hadn't shown it was merely to slightly conceal his talent, not wanting to be too shocking.

Forming a core at a hundred years old was already considered a peerless genius.

If he formed a golden core in his thirties, he would practically be an immortal reincarnated.

It would be hard to avoid attracting the attention of Tai Su Lingjun, the master of the Sushu Sect.

His divine sense couldn't yet fool a Void Refining True Lord!

While Sage Yaoxi was assessing the trio's progress, Luo Chen was also secretly observing this convenient master of his.

From the moment she entered the palace and caused the lamps to flicker, he had sensed something unusual.

Her dharma power was abundant, even slightly leaking out.

Such an obvious anomaly was a sign of an impending breakthrough.

It seemed the sect members' talk of Sage Yaoxi about to advance to the late Sage stage was not just a rumor!

As the three waited, Yaoxi finally gave her answer.

"Very good. You have all passed my assessment. You can remove the 'registered' prefix and become my true disciples."

The three all sighed in relief, bowed, and once again addressed her as Master.

Sage Yaoxi smiled slightly, then sighed. "Unfortunately, I am currently at a critical juncture in my cultivation and cannot personally guide your practice."

Ignoring the disappointed expressions on their faces, she continued, "You have already converted your main cultivation techniques, which are sufficient for you to cultivate to the next realm. If you have any questions, you may ask your senior brother, Wan Jiuzhang. If he is unavailable, your other senior brothers and sisters can also help."

Xuan Luo, your situation is somewhat special. Your Sharp Gold Body gives you unique convenience in cultivating metal-attributed techniques. I'm afraid you might break through to the Adept stage during a single deep seclusion of mine.

"To prevent you from lacking subsequent techniques, your master will now pass on the complete technique to you."

Under the envious gazes of the other two, a jade slip flew out from Sage Yaoxi's bracelet and floated before Luo Chen.

Sage Yaoxi looked at the jade slip quite solemnly and said, "This technique is called the *Taiyi Geng Metal Myriad Transformations Mystic Chapter*. It is a top-tier technique that can lead directly to the Ascendant stage. Even within the Sushu Sect, it is one of the most preeminent inheritances. Only a few disciples from the lineage of the Ascendant elder, Heaven Halberd, practice it. Your master spent considerable effort to obtain the complete version. If you have free time in the future, you can go to Heaven Halberd Mountain to exchange with the senior brothers there."

"Additionally, I have set seals on each stage. You must break through the current realm before you can view and cultivate the subsequent technique."

"Remember, although cultivation can be fast, you must not covet rapid progress. Otherwise, your Dao heart will be unstable, and you will inevitably fall into deviation!"

These words were very detailed, and the latter part was a stern warning.

But the earnest teaching intention contained within was palpable to everyone.

Luo Chen held the jade slip, quite moved, and promised Yaoxi that he would steadily and solidly cultivate well.

"Alright, you may withdraw! If you wish to change your cave dwelling, you can go to the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall with your true disciple token."

Sage Yaoxi waved her hand, and the three took their leave.

As he exited the hall, Luo Chen looked back.

A troubled expression appeared on Sage Yaoxi's face, and she kept rubbing her brow with her fingers.

The late Sage stage was not so easy to break through!

How many brilliant and talented individuals had been trapped at this minor realm.

But if she could succeed, she would not only become a Grand Cultivator revered by the world but also have the hope of advancing to the Ascendant stage.

Ascendant!

That was a realm where one could previously ascend to the immortal realm and be recorded in the immortal registry!

It could be imagined that this seclusion of Sage Yaoxi's would take a considerable amount of time.

By the time she emerged, he might have logically advanced to the Sage stage.

But before that, he should first grind some achievement points!

...

Leaving the Jade Spring Palace, the three felt relieved and were full of joy.

Feng Zhiyan even playfully cupped her hands and smiled at the two, "Senior Brother Xuan Luo! Senior Brother Ji Yue!"

Ji Yue didn't let the atmosphere fall flat and also changed his address for the two.

When it came to Luo Chen, he hesitated for a moment before calling out "Senior Brother Xuan Luo."

Luo Chen smiled. "Junior Sister Feng, Junior Brother Ji Yue, what are your plans next?"

Feng Zhiyan said spiritedly, "The technique I've switched to is very compatible, and I've also felt the bottleneck. I plan to go into seclusion again and strive to break through to the Initiate stage."

"Striking while the iron is hot and going all out—that's a good idea." Luo Chen expressed his approval, then looked at Ji Yue.

The latter thought for a moment, a look of longing appearing on his face.

"Ten years of seclusion has made me a bit weary. I want to go to the Three Halls and Five Courts to look around before deciding on my next plan."

Luo Chen waved his hand. "Let's go together then. I also want to take a look."

And so they parted ways.

Feng Zhiyan stayed on Jade Spring Peak, while Luo Chen and Ji Yue flew toward the Three Halls and Five Courts.

...

There was no need to go to the Miscellaneous Affairs Hall; neither of them planned to change their cave dwelling for now.

The two were most interested in the Transmission Hall. Coincidentally, a Golden Core Superior was giving a lecture that day, so they went to listen.

Luo Chen naturally wasn't interested in the lectures of a Golden Core cultivator. What interested him was the Scripture Pavilion within the Transmission Hall. Apart from the sect master and the eight Ascendant elders, it was the place with the richest and most complete collection of books in the Sushu Sect.

It contained not only technique classics but also spell secret records, travelogues, and so on.

Unfortunately, with a true disciple token, one could only access the books corresponding to their realm.

To see more, one needed sect contribution points!

And sect contribution points came from the External Affairs Hall and the Five Courts.

The External Affairs Hall was easy to understand. Completing tasks issued by the sect naturally earned contribution points.

As for the Five Courts...

Whether it's researching spirit herb cultivation, alchemy or formation arrangement, or being proficient in a hundred arts while on law enforcement patrol, as long as there is innovation or breakthrough, you can earn a large number of contribution points. Moreover, mastering these skills gives you more convenience when accepting tasks from the External Affairs Hall.

The duty disciple of the Transmission Hall explained thus to Luo Chen.

Upon hearing that Luo Chen was proficient in metal-attributed techniques, that disciple enthusiastically recommended the Law Enforcement Court.

Because metal-system cultivators were often good at combat!

But after some thought, Luo Chen still arrived at the gates of the Alchemy Court.

Not only because he was proficient in alchemy—this was the most familiar path.

But also because...

"I wonder how the alchemy system of the Spirit Realm differs from that of the Mountains and Seas Realm. I need to familiarize myself with it to avoid revealing any flaws later."

"Moreover, among the large number of alchemy inheritances I obtained from Devil Lord Heaven-Refining back then, there are still three pill recipes that haven't had their seals unlocked."

"The Spirit-Stabilizing Pill and the Void-Seeing Pill both correspond to the Void Refining stage. And that Dao Union Pill, as high as eighth-grade, clearly relates to the Body Integration stage from its name."

To make long-term plans, Luo Chen needed to start preparing now.

Looking at the bustling Alchemy Court, with beams of escape light coming and going, Luo Chen narrowed his eyes slightly, hesitated no longer, and strode forward with large steps.

The surrounding disciples who had come to seek pills were completely oblivious, only treating Luo Chen as an ordinary cultivator.

But they had no idea that the Alchemy Court of the Sushu Sect had just welcomed an Alchemy Saint!
