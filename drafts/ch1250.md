# Chapter 1250: Ten-Thousand-Year Demon Milk, Recondensing the Primordial Spirit

Within the Central Demon Abyss.

Mist filled the air, so thick that one could not see one's hand before one's face.

A figure drifted like a wisp of willow catkin within, rising and sinking, his whereabouts uncertain.

By his ears, low growls like those of wild beasts came from time to time.

Luo Chen, bold with confidence in his own abilities, felt no fear whatsoever. He focused intently on searching for a suitable place to go into seclusion.

In the course of this search, he gained a much deeper understanding of the Central Demon Abyss—a place that trial disciples would avoid at all costs.

The density of demonic qi far exceeded that of the outside world!

The drifting mist was all materialized demonic qi. Every single breath he took caused his *Chaos Indestructible Sutra* to circulate a little faster.

Even if it wasn't as good as the energy concentration of the shattered upper three rings in the Ruins, it was still comparable to the lower three rings.

Had it always been like this, or had it only become this way recently?

Luo Chen had no way of knowing.

As his footsteps carried him deeper, his expression gradually turned grave.

At this point, he was no longer using his divine sense or his Crimson-Golden Pupils to probe the abyss. Instead, he actively released his martial divine will, which he had restrained for a hundred years.

If a peak Ascendant's divine sense was still suppressed here, only able to probe a radius of about a hundred li, then his Selfless Divine Will, under these bizarre circumstances, was not suppressed at all. It could spread out normally.

A hundred li, a thousand li... all the way to ten thousand li.

The Central Demon Abyss was larger than Luo Chen had imagined.

It was as if someone had forcibly carved out this vast, magnificent fissure deep beneath the ground of the Youxuan Continent with knives and axes.

"One to the east, two to the west, two to the south, and two to the north."

"All of them are at the Void Refining level, just like that flying avian demon spirit!"

"Is this possible?"

Not to mention the Ascendant-level demon spirits scattered everywhere, the sheer number of Void Refining demon spirits he had detected was enough to make him draw in a sharp breath of cold air.

This was definitely not normal!

"It seems that the Youxuan Continent... no, perhaps the entire Xuan Realm, is about to encounter a major problem."

What could cause a major problem for the Xuan Realm was naturally not these demon spirits, which appeared immensely powerful to outsiders but were actually outwardly strong and inwardly weak.

By observing a small part, Luo Chen could already see much further.

The Nine Great Sacred Sects conducted the Demon-Suppressing Trial once every thousand years to sweep away the demonic aura. Yet, even under these circumstances, such an inconceivable change had occurred.

It was easy to imagine that other demon lairs would inevitably experience similar phenomena.

If such a large-scale mutation truly occurred, then it was possible that experts from the Demon Realm might seize the opportunity to invade the Xuan Realm through the demon lairs all at once.

In Luo Chen's view, these demon lairs, stubbornly rooted in the Xuan Realm, were themselves the weak points in the spatial fabric connecting the two realms!

"But all of that is too far away. It has nothing to do with me right now."

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his martial divine will, his gaze settling on an inward-curving cliff wall within the abyss.

This was the place!

In his perception, an Ascendant-level demon spirit was coiled here. Its aura was extremely powerful, far surpassing the ordinary demon spirits outside.

It was only slightly inferior to the Void Refining demon spirits in other directions.

It should be on the verge of advancement.

This was also evident from the demonic qi converging nearby, its density far exceeding that of other locations.

It was a stroke of luck for Luo Chen.

Whoosh...

A gentle breeze blew, parting the mist.

Inside the concave cave, everything was crystalline and translucent.

Stalactites, like sharp swords, hung upside down from the cave ceiling. From time to time, plump, round droplets of black liquid dripped down from them, eventually flowing along small channels on the ground and gathering into a small pool about a zhang in size at the center of the cavern.

Beside the small pool, a demon spirit that resembled a dragon and a python was curled up, fast asleep.

Its entire body was covered in iron armor condensed from pitch-black demonic qi. Especially at the tip of its occasionally swishing tail, there was a huge iron ball.

One could imagine that if a cultivator were struck by that tail and iron ball, their physical body would instantly disintegrate.

Just then, the Iron-Armored Dragon Python slowly opened its eyes, looking suspiciously at the entrance.

At some point, a figure had silently walked in, his gaze sweeping across the cave environment with strange curiosity.

From the crystalline ores, to the cliff walls embedded with various gems, and finally following the direction of the hanging stalactites, his eyes landed on the small pool.

"Spirit Milk?"

"No!"

"Demon Milk!"

Luo Chen had never expected such an unexpected harvest.

Although he had chosen this place because of the powerful demon spirit residing here, knowing that the quality of this demon lair was not bad, the more important reason was its relative remoteness.

Even if breaking through to the Ascendant realm caused a huge commotion, it would give him some buffer time.

He never imagined that such a rare treasure would be found in such a remote place!

Although Demon Milk and Spirit Milk were fundamentally different in nature, the principle behind them was exactly the same.

Both were formed when a certain type of energy reached a certain density and accumulated over countless years, eventually materializing into a tangible form.

The You'an Demon Lair had been formed over ten thousand years ago, and this place was a low-lying area where energy would settle, which met the conditions for the formation of Demon Milk.

This was a treasure as good as Ten-Thousand-Year Spirit Milk.

Whether used to refine certain specific pills or to cultivate special medicinal herbs, it would be of great benefit.

Even if taken orally in the simplest and most direct way, it could instantly restore a large amount of a demon clan expert's mana!

"I've struck gold!"

Luo Chen's face was full of joy. As for the Iron-Armored Dragon Python demon spirit, it seemed he had never even considered it a threat.

Such a blatantly dismissive attitude naturally angered the other party.

A dull sound rang out as the Dragon Python slowly stood up. Only then did one notice that it actually had two thick, powerful legs beneath its abdomen.

Huff!

It exhaled a mouthful of hot air, then roared as it charged at Luo Chen.

But it had only made it halfway when Luo Chen casually extended his right hand.

A burst of blood-colored light surged out, instantly transforming into five giant dragons that, in the nick of time, firmly grasped its massive body.

This was no magical technique or divine ability, but the most profound martial combat technique.

One that Luo Chen had learned and improved upon in the Ruins:

The Great Luo Five Dragon Hand!

Terrifying force was applied to the Iron-Armored Dragon Python's body like shackles, rendering it completely unable to move.

The blood-colored astral qi possessed the property of corroding flesh and dissolving bones. As Luo Chen exerted a little force, it seeped into the Iron-Armored Dragon Python's body.

"You can't be wasted either."

"Go!"

Luo Chen muttered, and the Yuantu Sword responded with a ringing sound.

It circled once in the cave, then, under the terrified gaze of the Iron-Armored Dragon Python, drilled straight into its head.

"Roar..."

The intense pain and the premonition of imminent death caused the Iron-Armored Dragon Python to struggle desperately, but the outcome was already sealed.

A moment later.

Luo Chen flicked his sleeve, dispersing the nauseating stench of blood within the cave.

He did not retrieve the Yuantu Sword. Instead, he surveyed the surroundings.

After a long while, he nodded slightly.

"It's a suitable place to set up a formation."

Breaking through a realm was no child's play.

Even with prior experience, one could not afford to be careless in the slightest.

Especially since, when Luo Chen had broken through to the Ascendant realm back then, there had been an example of an enemy trying to ambush him.

Therefore, Luo Chen naturally would not come unprepared.

He pressed his fingers together like a sword, his sword seals flying, as streaks of sword qi burst forth from his body.

The sword qi was sharp and fierce, colored in five hues.

These sword qi, powerful enough to shatter mountains and split rocks, did not explode directly. Instead, under the suppression of some force, they slowly sank into the ground outside the cave.

Connecting with each other, a terrifying aura gradually coalesced.

Seeing this, Luo Chen was very satisfied.

It had been many years since he last used the Grand Five Elements Sword Formation. Now, casting it out of thin air, it was not only unfamiliar but had actually doubled in power.

This was probably related to his comprehension of the Five Elements origins.

The Grand Five Elements Sword Formation itself was divided into the power of the Five Elements. In the past, he could only set it up using sword pellets. How could that compare to his current origin divine power?

However, it was not enough!

Without physical sword pellets, these sword qi would not remain stable for long.

Although they would not disappear, they would inevitably dissipate.

Therefore, a formation eye was still needed.

Luo Chen's gaze fell upon the Yuantu Sword.

The sword spirit, understanding his intention, let out a ringing sound and directly drilled into the formation.

In an instant, the five-colored radiance gradually dimmed beneath the blood-colored sword light.

But the terrifying sword qi was accumulating within.

One could imagine that if an enemy tried to interrupt his breakthrough, they would suffer the most ferocious blow!

"It's barely enough. I suppose this breakthrough won't take too long."

Luo Chen smiled slightly and walked over to the black pool.

The Iron-Armored Dragon Python had chosen to cultivate here precisely because of the dense demonic qi.

He naturally would not waste it.

Letting out a turbid breath, Luo Chen calmed his mind and began to adjust his state.

Even if he was doing it a second time, he had to ensure no accidents occurred.

One month later!

In the dimly lit cave dwelling, at some point, a small Nascent Soul figure had left his Purple Mansion and was hovering above Luo Chen's head.

Like his main body, it was forming seals with its eyes closed, emitting a mysterious aura from its body.

At this moment, Luo Chen's vast soul also escaped his sea of consciousness and began to gradually merge with the Nascent Soul.

He was going to recondense his primordial spirit!

Even though the Nascent Soul could not yet fully bear his vast soul, this step had to be taken.

To prevent the Nascent Soul from being torn apart, Luo Chen had another move.

As if by divine inspiration, five streams of light rose up, enveloping the "fragile" Nascent Soul.

The power of origin, encompassing all Five Elements!

It perfectly matched Luo Chen's Five-Spirit Dao Body!

With the assistance of the Five Elements origins, Luo Chen's vast soul slowly and forcibly merged with the Nascent Soul.

This process was very slow, and Luo Chen could not even feel the passage of time.

He was completely focused on it, afraid of making the slightest mistake.

In fact, this was also the reason why many top experts, after a severe drop in their cultivation realm, found it very difficult to cultivate back to their original level.

A severe drop in realm often meant that their Dao foundation, or even their Dao fruit, was broken.

Even if their soul remained undying, the newly cultivated Dao foundation or Dao fruit would still be unable to accommodate or bear it.

The reason Luo Chen had been able to smoothly recultivate all the way back to the Sage realm was actually due to his powerful physical body, which possessed infinite inclusiveness.

But when it came to the Ascendant barrier, the true transformation point for a cultivator, it had nothing to do with the physical body.

Because this was the final step of the soul's transformation!

The yin soul crossing into yang, the Nascent Soul becoming an Ascendant.

Fortunately, Luo Chen had the Five Elements origins he had comprehended, which could serve as a medium to balance the powerful soul and the weak Nascent Soul.

The fusion process was relatively smooth.

As long as the Nascent Soul could fully bear the vast soul, Luo Chen could rely on his past experience to directly condense his primordial spirit.

At that point, the primordial spirit would resonate with heaven and earth, and breaking through to the Ascendant realm would be an instantaneous matter.

However, as the last portion of his soul entered the Nascent Soul, Luo Chen faintly sensed something was wrong.

It was as if there was a gap, causing the power of his Nascent Soul to continuously leak out.

"Is the Five Elements origin incomplete?"

In an instant, Luo Chen realized where the problem lay.

But this problem caught him off guard.

He had indeed comprehended the five great origins of Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, and Earth, and had even fully awakened his Five-Spirit Dao Body. How could there be a defect?

"No!"

"The problem lies with the Withering-Bloom True Intent!"

Luo Chen continuously mobilized his origin mana, searching for the crux of the problem, and finally discovered the anomaly.

The other four great origins were the most fundamental origin powers.

As for the Withering-Bloom True Intent, this law true intent he had comprehended earliest had accompanied him through many difficulties and obstacles. Its grade was also far above that of ordinary law true intents.

But it was not the fundamental Wood Origin!

Ordinarily, this was not prominent, and the Five-Spirit Dao Body could also accommodate it.

But here, in the most meticulous process of fusing the soul with the Nascent Soul, this difference became a defect.

Luo Chen did not fall into a panic.

In his thousand years of cultivation, he had encountered countless unexpected events. Panic could not solve any problem.

Moreover, the impact of this problem was not too great. As long as he was careful, he could slowly endure it.

Luo Chen strained to adjust the Withering-Bloom True Intent, making sure its two major characteristics of withering and blooming were not too prominent. Instead, he continuously suppressed it, making it gentle and peaceful.

If there truly was a Wood Origin, it should probably be like this.

As he continuously adjusted, Luo Chen's agitated state of mind grew calmer and calmer. It seemed he was truly about to grasp the essence of the Wood Origin.

Just then!

Luo Chen's mind suddenly stirred. Even his divine sense, which was about to completely fuse into the Nascent Soul, paused for a moment.

An inexplicable resonance emanated from his body, gradually connecting to the outside world, and even to the entire Nine Nether Demon Lair.

"This resonance?"

In his confusion, Luo Chen traced it back to its source and was surprised to find that it was the Nine Nether Moss he had absorbed into his body earlier to disguise his demonic contamination.

And the resonance was the connection between the moss in his body and the moss that covered the ground throughout the demon lair.

A warm but tenacious force spread across this land.

Even if cultivators used various methods to wither the moss, it was only temporarily dormant.

After a thousand years, the Nine Nether Moss would once again cover the entire demon lair!

The original grass withers and blooms, the spring wind brings it back to life.

Beneath the appearance of withering and blooming lay the most fundamental Wood Origin!

According to legend, the Nine Nether Moss was a method developed by a Great Vehicle Saint Ancestor of the Wood Demon Clan in the Demon Realm, specifically used to encroach upon the Xuan Realm.

Now it seemed, it was clearly a perfected Wood Origin!

Borrowing the resonance of the Nine Nether Moss within his body, guided by the Withering-Bloom True Intent, Luo Chen formed a temporary connection with the Nine Nether Moss throughout the You'an Demon Lair.

Seeing through the phenomenon to grasp the essence, a borrowed insight arose spontaneously.

His Five-Spirit Dao Body seemed to be shaken, withdrawing that green-white light and transforming it into a pure green origin, completely enveloping the Nascent Soul.

Luo Chen hesitated no longer. His vast soul completely merged into the Nascent Soul.

At this moment, the primordial spirit was restored!

Luo Chen pressed on in one go. The primordial spirit transformed into a giant that stood tall between heaven and earth, breaking through the confines of the cave and leaping into the abyss.

In an instant, surging demonic qi rolled in, rushing towards the giant primordial spirit.

He was going to break through to the Ascendant realm in one fell swoop!
