# Chapter 1192: Human Immortal Perfection, Entering the Dao Field

"Why did you save them?"

"It was just a trivial effort."

"You know full well that when the Guixu ends, all these people will die. Why bother with such a futile gesture?"

"After all, there are still one or two hundred years left. Perhaps that is someone's entire lifetime."

"Since when did I know you, Luo Chen, to possess such a compassionate heart?"

"Heh."

Luo Chen chuckled softly, offering no further explanation.

To him, saving the disciples of the Myriad Manifestations Star Sect was indeed merely a trivial effort. Perhaps, as he had told the Star Sect's master, Danyang, it was simply repaying the favor of borrowing the Ziwei Courtyard's sixty-year cultivation grounds.

But Luo Chen had not come away empty-handed either.

During that period, the Primordial Unity Cauldron had absorbed a vast amount of starlight, devoured countless star remnants, and even absorbed a considerable amount of star crystals. If he hadn't done this, how could he have obtained such gains?

Every bite and every sip is determined by heaven.

Yuan Qingzang did not dwell on this matter either. Instead, he was more curious about something else.

He probed, "Were you cultivating at that time?"

Luo Chen did not avoid the question and frankly nodded.

Yuan Qingzang's spirits lifted, and his gaze incessantly swept over Luo Chen.

"Have you broken through now?"

The reason he asked was that he could no longer see through Luo Chen's current realm. From his perspective, not to mention the martial artist's aura, even Luo Chen's very existence had become ethereal and elusive. It was as if Luo Chen stood right there, yet he was like a chaotic blur, impossible to grasp.

This feeling was something even the Lord of Carefree Realm had never given him.

Previously, Luo Chen's realm was roughly equivalent to his own, around the late stage of the Human Immortal realm. If he had broken through, then Luo Chen's current realm would be the Destruction realm! He would be a newly ascended Destruction King, aside from those four.

However, this time, Luo Chen shook his head.

Yuan Qingzang was stunned. Under those circumstances, Luo Chen still hadn't broken through?

To this, Luo Chen could only sigh softly.

"After all, they were only dying stars!"

Yes, only dying stars.

The Sea of Stars hung with countless stars. From a mortal's perspective, they looked no different from real stars. But for people like Luo Chen, they understood their essence. Those stars were all on the verge of extinction, drawn in by the Guixu. To be honest, they were essentially no different from beings like Luo Chen who had drifted into the Guixu.

These dying stars could not compare to the vibrant, true stars of the greater cosmos. The power contained within them was less than one ten-thousandth. Even if they exploded instantly, they could hardly harm Luo Chen in the slightest.

This kind of external pressure provided very little stimulation to Luo Chen. Even the final explosion of hundreds of dying stars was insufficient to support him in taking another step forward.

Of course!

Under the extreme conditions of last night, Luo Chen had not gained nothing.

By relying on the unceasing external pressure, he had initially achieved the unification of his physical body, astral qi, and martial dao divine intent. Now, his entire body was indistinguishable, tightly connected. To outsiders, he appeared as a chaotic blur, his divine intent introverted, his aura unmanifested, making it very difficult to see through his true realm.

If one were to judge by realm, he could perhaps be considered a Human Immortal at the Great Perfection!

Aside from this, the greatest gain was not an improvement in strength or realm.

It was the deduction of his lifebound technique!

All his achievement points had been exhausted.

*Divine Demon Formless* had been forcibly pushed to its limit by Luo Chen. From now on, the path of cultivation would be unimpeded all the way to the Nirvana realm.

The details of the deduction process were difficult to articulate, but the most important point was that Luo Chen had found a method of balance.

Whether it was demonic body refinement or human martial arts, their focuses were too extreme. Truly integrating them, even with the help of the attribute panel, was incredibly difficult. Under the internal and external pressure of last night, Luo Chen finally found a spark of inspiration.

He combined his past cultivation experiences and tried to integrate the Buddhist techniques he had mastered.

*Bodhi Dharma*

*Three Heads and Six Arms*

*Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Mantra*

Among these were the method of molding a golden body from the Incense Fire Divine Path, a Buddhist body-refining divine ability, and a Bodhisattva's spirit-condensing technique. By integrating them, he finally achieved the ultimate balance.

The attribute panel also showed that it could be cultivated.

It was foreseeable that this would be a mixed yet comprehensive path of cultivation, encompassing five body-refining paths: divine, human, demon, devil, and Buddhist. The difficulty of cultivation could be imagined, but it was also the path most suited to Luo Chen's own situation. Whether it was his selfless divine intent or the chaotic energy that had achieved his current realm, they could all accommodate these body-refining paths.

Only one thing stood before Luo Chen.

He needed an external pressure far greater than the falling of stars to truly take the first step in chaotic body refinement!

And while such external pressure was hard to find in the world, it was not without trace.

"The Great Destruction Era..."

Luo Chen squinted, murmuring to himself.

"What did you say?"

Yuan Qingzang heard Luo Chen's muttering but didn't understand its meaning. However, he explained it to himself.

"Indeed, as the Great Destruction Era approaches, the changes in the Guixu are growing more and more severe. A terrifying scene like last night's simultaneous fall of stars has never occurred in ten thousand years. The Corpse Remnant Immortal Domain we passed earlier is also much harsher than it was a few hundred years ago. It can be imagined that other places in the Guixu are also constantly deteriorating. Everything is heading toward its end. I'm afraid my Three Thousand Pagoda Forest is also..."

When he said this, Yuan Qingzang's face couldn't help but show a look of gloom.

Unlike outsiders like Luo Chen, the Lord of Carefree Realm, and Mu Bugui, he was a native-born creature of the Guixu. He had grown up in the Three Thousand Pagoda Forest and cultivated step by step to his current realm. His deep feelings for it were no less than Mu Bugui's longing for his homeland.

Now, leaving his homeland, abandoning his clan, and seeking a way out, the personal emotional conflict was truly beyond the understanding of outsiders.

In the oppressive atmosphere, Yuan Qingzang slowly adjusted his mood.

"Next, are we going to the Ancient God Dao Field?"

Luo Chen pondered for a moment, then nodded gently.

In truth!

After witnessing the might of the Primordial Unity Cauldron resisting the simultaneous fall of stars last night, coupled with the requirements of his newly deduced technique, returning to the Destruction Gate was his best choice. But since he had come this far, it would be a pity not to see the Ancient God Dao Field and witness the final frenzy of the Guixu martial artists.

On the remaining journey, the two deviated no more.

Even if there were some small settlements nearby, they held no interest for them. Their figures sped straight toward the location of the Ancient God Dao Field on the map.

However, despite the map's guidance, finding the entrance to the Ancient God Dao Field was not smooth sailing.

As Yuan Qingzang had said earlier, the closer the Great Destruction Era, the more frequent the anomalies in the Guixu. Especially since the Ancient God Dao Field was located in a spatial crevice. Affected by the changes in the Guixu, the surrounding spatial rifts were also increasing.

These spatial rifts were generated internally and did not interact with the outside world, making them extremely dangerous.

To give an analogy.

Consider the Guixu world and other ordinary planetary worlds as a person. Spatial rifts can be seen as wounds on the body. In an ordinary planetary world, spatial rifts are often nodes that interact with the greater cosmos. As long as one withstands the pressure, they might be expelled at worst.

But in the Guixu, spatial rifts are the collapse and fragmentation of the internal body. Once caught in one, it would only accelerate the entire world's digestion of the person.

That was a power no martial artist could withstand!

Even Luo Chen could not!

Because he had not yet reached the point of breaking through the Guixu's restrictions.

Thus, finding the crevice of the Ancient God Dao Field amidst the countless spatial rifts became quite dangerous and difficult.

This also explained why the Lord of Carefree Realm and the others had set out one or two hundred years in advance.

After a full six months of single-minded searching, Luo Chen and Yuan Qingzang finally groped their way to the entrance.

Before a stone platform.

Two figures emerged, looking somewhat disheveled.

Looking back at the path they had come, the blue-faced man still had a lingering fear.

"I didn't expect that right at the doorstep, there would be such a hidden spatial rift lying across the entrance. If it weren't for your eyes catching the anomaly at the last moment, Yuan would have fallen into a ditch."

As he spoke, he subconsciously looked at Luo Chen's eyes.

The bloodshot veins were gradually fading, and the golden light was also dimming.

The more he interacted with Luo Chen, the more he discovered the unfathomable depth of his terrifying foundation. A pair of eyes could even cultivate such a divine ability to see through the void. Was this the capability of a cultivator?

No wonder he had heard that the outside world was the domain of cultivators!

Luo Chen rubbed the corners of his eyes, chuckling softly without a word.

His Crimson Eyes Golden Pupils possessed the divine ability to break through illusions, illusions, and even the void. He could identify and avoid the sudden appearance and disappearance of spatial rifts in advance. It was precisely with these eyes that their journey to find the way had been relatively smooth, finally arriving at their destination safely.

"Is this the entrance to the Ancient God Dao Field?"

Luo Chen's gaze fell on the stone platform, carefully observing it, especially the peculiar patterns.

A teleportation formation?

An altar?

A vague sense of déjà vu washed over him.

"Yes, entering is also very simple. The relevant methods have long been circulated in the Guixu."

Yuan Qingzang reached out, bit his fingertip, and squeezed out a drop of blood.

The blood fell onto the stone platform with a *pat*, instantly activating the patterns, which flowed like liquid mercury.

Luo Chen frowned. "Must it be activated with blood?"

Yuan Qingzang smiled. "Don't worry, I know what you're concerned about. But nothing has ever happened at the Ancient God Dao Field. Martial artists who fall only fall inside the Dao Field itself, and people can still come and go freely from within. This shows that blood is merely a condition to open the entrance and does not restrict your or my freedom."

Luo Chen hummed in acknowledgment, followed suit, and squeezed out a drop of blood.

A brilliant light burst forth, and the two stepped onto the platform, then vanished.

...

In a square, over a hundred martial artists sat cross-legged.

Their eyes were tightly shut, and their auras were fully released.

Astral qi flowed distinctly, without interfering with each other. Martial dao divine intent blazed, guiding the astral qi toward a milky-white gate in the sky.

Suddenly!

One person at the center of the square abruptly opened his eyes, staring intently at a certain spot.

Not only that, but he also silently pointed a finger.

*Whoosh!*

A pitch-black finger pierced through the air, emitting a terrifying screech.

Everyone was startled, waking from their previous actions, and subconsciously looked at the place he was attacking.

Two figures slowly materialized.

Facing the sudden attack, the leading man showed a hint of surprise but no panic.

He simply flicked his sleeve.

The blood-colored cloth robe trembled, deflecting the pitch-black finger away, sending it crashing into the void.

*Chi...*

The pitch-black finger instantly dissipated.

The white-robed man raised his head, coldly staring at the one who had attacked him without reason, and uttered a single word.

"Corpse!"

The gaunt old man shrouded in a black robe let out a hoarse roar. "It was you who destroyed the doppelgänger I left outside to endure the tribulation!"

Luo Chen showed no fear, only a cold smile. "So what if it was me? If you want revenge, I'm ready!"

"You're asking for death!" Corpse was furious, his eyes filled with hatred.

Everyone present was an elite of the Guixu martial dao. From the brief exchange between the two, they understood the origin of their grudge.

Doppelgänger?

They exchanged glances, understanding Corpse's plan.

Unlike the others who had staked everything on the Ancient God Dao Field, he had left a doppelgänger outside. If he failed here, he still harbored the delusion of using that doppelgänger to survive the great calamity of the Guixu's destruction.

However, this attempt had been thwarted by the young man before them.

Hence, the moment they met, their eyes were red with enmity, and they directly called for a fight.

Understanding this, they were too lazy to speculate on Corpse's plans, instead becoming quite curious about the white-robed man.

This man was unknown, and his appearance had never been seen before. He was presumably not a long-established old-timer. Observing his aura and realm, they were indistinct and difficult to judge. If he were truly such a newly ascended martial artist, he probably wouldn't be very formidable.

But he had destroyed Corpse's doppelgänger left outside!

As Corpse's contingency for the tribulation, it would certainly not have been left without any protection. For this man to have successfully overcome it and killed Corpse's doppelgänger, his own ability was definitely not weak.

Moreover, Yuan Qingzang was following behind him, acting as if he were taking his lead.

Could this man also be a Destruction King of the same realm as Corpse?

To verify this conjecture was actually very simple.

All the martial dao Human Immortals turned their gazes to the other three among them.

The dignified and majestic Tianxuan Lie Su scrutinized Luo Chen, his eyes full of curiosity.

The gray-robed old man, Tianku Laoguai, had a calm expression, but a sinister chuckle came from within his abdomen, its meaning unclear.

If the reactions of these two weren't enough to explain everything, then the response of the Lord of Carefree Realm could declare something.

"Luo Chen, you've finally made it!"

Luo Chen cupped his hands in salute. "Lord of the Realm, I've kept you all waiting."

The Lord of Carefree Realm laughed heartily. "We've waited this long, what's a little more time? On the contrary, your talent has not disappointed. At the final juncture, you've cultivated to such a level, just in time to join us in exploring the Ancient God Dao Field."

Behind him, Mu Bugui, who had been away for many years, also showed a friendly smile to Luo Chen.

However, someone couldn't stand to see this harmonious scene.

Corpse snorted coldly. "The Dao Field gate is about to open. It's not lacking one more person. Whether he's allowed in or not, you'll have to ask me!"

The Lord of Carefree Realm's smile didn't fade. He said lightly, "We have all used every effort to enter the Ancient God Dao Field, but you, Daoist friend, have secretly left a contingency outside. That hardly seems fair. Now that we have an additional helper, why refuse him at the door?"

"Helper?" Corpse stared darkly at Luo Chen, his eyes full of chill. "Fang Wuyou, are you really going to make things difficult for me over this junior?"

Before the Lord of Carefree Realm could answer, Luo Chen spoke up with a hearty laugh.

"Old thing, living long doesn't mean you're strong. If you and I were to fight, the outcome would be uncertain. Besides, do you really think you can hide what you did outside from everyone?"

As he spoke, he extended his hand.

A red lotus mark slowly flew out.

A red-dressed beauty sitting cross-legged in the square saw the red lotus mark, and her expression changed.

Without needing guidance, the red lotus flew to her like a fledgling returning to its nest.

In an instant, information poured in.

The red-dressed beauty's aura exploded, and she stared at Corpse with hatred in her eyes.

"Old thing, give my Red Lotus Tribe an explanation!"

Before Corpse could answer, Luo Chen spoke again with a smile from afar.

"Fellow Daoist Red Lotus, it's not just your Red Lotus Tribe that has suffered his poison. There's also Chonghai Tower, Xueyan Gorge, Icefire Valley... Are the leaders of these tribes present?"

The moment his words fell.

Several auras shot up into the sky from the square, forming a tremendous pressure that bore down on the black-robed old man.

In an instant, the atmosphere was tense, swords drawn, and a storm was about to break!
