# Chapter 1241: Reversing the Formula, Initial Success of a Divine Ability

*Boom!*

With a tremendous sound, the Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace spewed forth vast spiritual power, gradually beginning to activate.

Jiang Huang was the last to board, her mind still somewhat dazed.

Yet it was not because of her own soul, but because she had been shocked upon first seeing the True Lord's personal vehicle.

In truth, any Sage who witnessed a treasure of this level would inevitably be shaken.

Yao Xi Sage walked ahead, beginning to introduce the heavenly palace to Jiang Huang just as Helan Shi had done earlier, helping her calm down.

There was also the intention of using the younger generation's admiration for the True Lord's treasure to encourage them to cultivate diligently.

While listening to Yao Xi Sage's introduction, Jiang Huang looked around.

After a moment, she suddenly asked, "Master, where is Junior Brother Xuan Luo?"

Yao Xi Sage paused mid-sentence, showing no sign of annoyance. She only said calmly, "The heavenly palace is setting sail; everyone should be here. You need not worry."

That made sense.

Jiang Huang realized she had been worrying unnecessarily.

Yao Xi Sage, however, was somewhat curious. "You seem to have a good relationship with Xuan Luo?"

Jiang Huang subconsciously nodded, but quickly shook her head. "I haven't interacted much with him, but... perhaps because we've been through hardships together, I feel a sense of closeness."

Just closeness?

Yao Xi Sage carefully examined her disciple, who had been missing for many years, trying to discern something.

In truth, her earlier question had carried a hint of ulterior motive.

Jiang Huang was the descendant of an old friend!

She had raised her since childhood, doted on her considerably, which had shaped her into someone both resolute and stubborn.

Back then, preoccupied with cultivation, and because her disciple had achieved some success in her training, she had reduced her care.

Even if Jiang Huang being controlled by the demons was not directly her fault, Yao Xi Sage still felt a measure of guilt.

After her disciple returned, she had spent a great deal to make amends.

Yet Yao Xi Sage knew well that no matter how much she compensated, the two hundred lost years could never be recovered.

Moreover, the depth of Jiang Huang's demonic contamination was such that even after successfully expelling the demon, there was no hope for further advancement on her future path.

Under such circumstances, for an ordinary male cultivator, there would indeed be no way out. They would be trapped at the Sage realm for life, at most contributing through other skills or sect management.

But for female cultivators, especially beautiful ones, there was still a glimmer of hope!

That was to find a suitable powerful man for dual cultivation, mutually benefiting each other.

The stronger the other party, the better the effect!

It was like a great tree growing strong; when it reached the heavens, the vines clinging to its branches could also see the most extreme heights.

Of course, this strength was not that of crossing a major realm, otherwise it would be pure cauldron cultivation.

Comparatively, Xuan Luo was a very good candidate!

His talent was outstanding, possessing a Sharp Metal Body.

Among the Five Elements, Metal generates Water, which could precisely benefit Jiang Huang, while Jiang Huang could also nourish him in return.

If their shared hardship in the Ink Jade Space could be used to bring them together, perhaps Jiang Huang might have a glimmer of hope for the future.

Yao Xi Sage did not point this out on the spot.

Not to mention whether Jiang Huang had such intentions, the current Xuan Luo was no longer someone to be easily manipulated.

As their master, she preferred to let fate naturally bring about a marriage between them.

"Come, let's go to Qingxiao Hall first and meet with the fellow disciples. Your Junior Brother Xuan Luo should be there too."

Yao Xi Sage led the way toward a palace.

Yet upon arriving inside, she was startled to find no trace of Xuan Luo.

Helan Shi smiled and explained that Xuan Luo had seized the time to cultivate.

Yao Xi Sage breathed a sigh of relief but also felt a slight complaint.

High-level cultivators often measured their training in centuries; why rush for a moment?

It was one thing back in the sect, but now they were traveling.

If he didn't take this opportunity to build good relationships with his fellow disciples, he would find himself in the awkward position of fighting alone once they entered the Dark Abyss Demon Cave.

Her disciple didn't seem like someone ignorant of worldly affairs, did he?

As she wondered, the expected voices arose in the hall.

"Junior Sister Yao Xi, your genius disciple is truly diligent!"

Yao Xi Sage's expression turned cold as she fixed her gaze on one person.

This person had never gotten along with her.

Whether in their youth or after reaching the Sage realm, they often clashed verbally.

In the end, no matter how good the atmosphere of the Sulling Sect was, with millions of disciples and over a hundred million subjects under its jurisdiction, it was no longer a small society.

It was only natural for people to have various personalities and thoughts.

She remembered that the last time they left the Suwang Hall, this fellow had also been the first to gossip about Xuan Luo.

This was taking his grudge against her out on the younger generation!

"Hongzhan, I wasn't aware you cared so much about the younger generation's affairs?"

Hongzhan Sage shrugged and curled his lips. "It's not that I care, but as an elder, there are some things I can't help but say. Cultivation immortality has never been just about fighting and killing; it's also about human relationships and social connections. Didn't the Sect Master also tell us to unite and resist external enemies? As an elder, I think this simple truth should be reminded to that child."

Having said this, he gave Yao Xi a meaningful smile, picked up his teacup, and blew on it leisurely.

"I'm just afraid some people don't know how to teach disciples, leading others astray like they did with Jiang Huang back then. Hoo... worthy of being Senior Brother Helan's treasured tea!"

Hearing these words, as if a sore spot had been touched, Yao Xi Sage trembled all over.

Seeing her at a loss, Youhuang Sage, her close friend of many years, came to her defense. "Senior Brother Hongzhan, why must you be like this? Although Yao Xi focuses on cultivation, she has never neglected teaching her disciples. Otherwise, how could Jade Spring Peak have produced three Sages in one go? That is all thanks to Yao Xi's guidance!"

Hongzhan Sage's expression darkened.

This was what annoyed him most.

Yao Xi's luck was simply too good!

First, she succeeded in forming her Nascent Soul and gained the Sect Master's favor, receiving his former personal vehicle.

Advancing to late-stage Sage was also smooth sailing.

That was one thing, but the disciples she took on, without her putting much effort into them, cultivated themselves to the Sage realm on their own.

Now, within the sect, Yao Xi Sage's reputation was so good that countless low-level disciples wanted to become her students.

How could Hongzhan Sage accept this?

He said sarcastically, "Teaching well? Then I'd like to know, with this diligence of using travel time to cultivate, what exactly can he cultivate?"

The atmosphere in the hall became somewhat awkward due to their confrontation.

Those accustomed to it were fine, but juniors like Jiang Huang couldn't interject and could only stand quietly with eyes downcast.

Helan Shi naturally wouldn't let them continue their nonsense. He quickly interrupted their argument and steered the conversation toward the upcoming Demon-Suppressing Trial.

"Within the demon cave, spiritual energy is thin, mostly transformed by the Nine Nether Demon Moss into fierce demonic qi, which is very disadvantageous for our battles. So..."

...

In a quiet room, Luo Chen sat cross-legged on a cushion, slowly withdrawing his divine sense.

He had heard the argument in Qingxiao Hall clearly, but felt no emotional fluctuation.

The reason he didn't attend the gathering of fellow disciples was simply that he didn't want to waste energy on useless social interactions.

Especially since, due to his disguise, he was seen as a junior before everyone else and couldn't interject in many matters.

It was better to simply ignore it and cut off contact entirely.

Once he truly entered the so-called Dark Abyss Demon Cave, he wouldn't need the help of these Sages anyway.

Speaking of which, he did notice something about his cheap master Yao Xi Sage's condition.

"Unstable state of mind!"

Although it was because of those close to her, Yao Xi's tendency to anger easily was clearly not the state a Sage should be in.

It was probably due to her breakthrough to late-stage Sage after nearly a hundred years of bitter cultivation!

As soon as she broke through, without stabilizing her realm, she went out to rescue Luo Chen.

After returning, she devoted herself wholeheartedly to helping her disciple Jiang Huang recover.

She lacked that most important period of settling.

If she participated in the Demon-Suppressing Trial in this state, with bad luck, problems might arise.

"Considering her care for me, I might leave some marks for Yao Xi before entering and lend a hand when convenient."

Luo Chen muttered to himself, then focused his attention on his hand.

A Golden Halberd Mark slowly emerged amidst the surging of his mana.

It was precisely the divine ability primer left by Elder Heavenly Halberd!

When he said he wanted to cultivate, it wasn't just empty words.

Taking advantage of his free time, he might as well study this thing.

He naturally knew how to activate it.

Simply infuse mana and divine sense into it, and under the guidance of the law essence of Gold Origin, this mark would attract heaven and earth origin qi and release the complete divine ability.

But Luo Chen wasn't concerned with that.

He was more interested in how Elder Heavenly Halberd had created this divine ability primer.

"The state isn't very stable; mana leaks out from time to time. It will completely dissipate within a few years."

"It seems Elder Heavenly Halberd isn't very skilled at this technique either."

"The divine sense imprint inside isn't very heavy, but the mana contains a trace of powerful force—it's the Gold Origin!"

"Additionally, there are several formulas I've never seen before."

"It seems the key lies in these formulas. If I can master them, I can create similar divine ability primers, and might even make further progress in researching divine ability seeds!"

Luo Chen showed a thoughtful expression as his mind gradually sank into the Golden Halberd Mark.

With each precisely timed stimulation, the various reactions of the Golden Halberd Mark entered his perception.

The corresponding formula reactions were also recorded by Luo Chen one by one.

In this process, he even gained a preliminary understanding of the divine ability *Soldier Realm Torrent*.

...

Time slowly passed, and a year flew by in an instant.

Although the Sulling Sect was within the jurisdiction of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, their mountain gates were actually extremely far apart.

Even with the Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace, a True Lord's vehicle, it took a full year to enter the territory without full activation.

The Xuan Realm was simply too vast.

Not to mention the Youxuan Continent, even the territories under each of the Nine Great Sacred Sects spanned millions of li.

As Hongzhan Sage had said earlier, the short time of travel wasn't actually short.

So after gathering a few times, everyone also found quiet places for final cultivation.

On this day.

Inside a secret chamber.

Iron Horse Sage was cultivating when he suddenly sensed an abnormality in the surrounding spiritual energy.

He also cultivated the *Supreme Unity Metal Transformation Mystic Scripture*, requiring the absorption of large amounts of metal-attributed spiritual energy.

At this moment, he suddenly noticed that the external spiritual energy had become restless, with metal-attributed spiritual energy especially active.

Puzzled, he left the quiet room and rose into the air.

He found that Helan Shi and Li Ting Sage had also come out at some point, looking seriously at the sky.

Seeing him emerge, the two didn't speak, only nodding in greeting.

Iron Horse Sage immediately looked at the sky, where a figure stood in midair, silently observing the surroundings.

"It's Elder Ancient Wood. Is the current anomaly caused by him?"

"No!"

"That direction is where Xuan Luo resides?"

Following Elder Ancient Wood's gaze, Iron Horse Sage was shocked to discover that the source of the heaven and earth spiritual energy disturbance was actually Xuan Luo!

Moreover, as weapons materialized in the void and the momentum of heaven and earth surged, he already knew this was not a Sage's method of manipulating the same-attributed heaven and earth spiritual energy, but a technique to drive the movement of one side's heaven and earth origin qi.

A divine ability!

"Could it be that Xuan Luo accidentally triggered the divine ability left by his master? If so, how will he protect himself in the Demon-Suppressing Trial?"

Iron Horse Sage looked worried.

But seeing Elder Ancient Wood's expression of both surprise and delight, it clearly wasn't that.

In other words...

"Could it be that Xuan Luo has truly learned the divine ability *Soldier Realm Torrent*?"

Just as Iron Horse Sage's thoughts surged like a tide, a torrent began to appear in the void!

Heaven and earth origin qi surged, and countless weapons materialized according to some will, forming a mighty torrent, seemingly ready to roll forward and destroy everything.

This momentum accumulated for a long time but had nowhere to vent.

Elder Ancient Wood made his move.

With a casual wave, a token floated up.

As he struck out various hand seals, the Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace shook with a rumble, and the formations in various places generated tremendous suction, absorbing and dissolving the heaven and earth origin qi in the void one by one.

After doing this, Elder Ancient Wood's lips moved without sound, as if transmitting a message to someone.

Then he flew down.

Iron Horse Sage bowed in greeting, as did Helan Shi and the others.

Hongzhan Sage had also come out early, his expression uncertain.

Elder Ancient Wood ignored him, bypassing him to arrive before Yao Xi.

"You truly have taken in a good disciple!"

This single statement was an endorsement.

Yao Xi Sage's expression was complex, unsure of what to say.

Meanwhile.

Inside the secret chamber.

Luo Chen slowly withdrew his divine sense, looking at his empty palm, lost in thought.

The *Soldier Realm Torrent* just now had not been triggered by the divine ability primer left by Elder Heavenly Halberd.

In order to study the formulas within, he had reversed the state, deducing it backward, gradually dissolving it.

The phenomenon just now was simply the divine ability he had cast himself.

He had already learned this technique!

And regarding the creation formulas for divine ability primers, he had also groped toward some key points.

With a thought, Luo Chen formed a sword finger with his left hand and slowly but firmly began to carve on his right palm.

After a moment, a Golden Halberd Mark slowly emerged.

Although it wasn't very stable, with large amounts of mana constantly leaking out, it was essentially no different from what Elder Heavenly Halberd had left behind.

Seeing this, Luo Chen clenched his fist and chuckled softly.

"Truly, I found it without any effort after searching high and low."

"I've found the direction!"

"As long as I find the suitable Gold Erosion Script and use this formula, I can carve out a divine ability seed!"

Amidst his soft laughter, Luo Chen rose to his feet, walked out of the secret chamber, and opened the doors of the palace where he resided.

Outside.

Figures stood one after another, facing Luo Chen, who was full of spirit and smiling at the corners of his mouth. Each person's expression was complex.

Yao Xi stepped forward, all the complex emotions finally transforming into a face full of relief.

"Xuan Luo, congratulations!"
