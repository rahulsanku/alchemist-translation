# Chapter 1204: Five-Spirit Dao Body, the Wanderer Xuan Luo

The small blade of grass stood straight, crystalline and translucent as if colorless, with faint green veins visible within, like blood vessels.

If it hadn't been placed so carefully and deliberately inside a box, anyone would have overlooked this transparent blade of grass.

But the moment Luo Chen saw it, three words tumbled from his lips.

"Boundless Grass!"

After the shock came a face full of delighted surprise.

Luo Chen had some understanding of this item.

Back when he was in the Mountains and Seas Realm, Nine Spirits had fought a great battle with the demon race over a Void-Collecting Flower, and Luo Chen had been dragged into that affair as well.

In the end, that Void-Collecting Flower had been seized by Azure Frost, who after careful refinement had broken through to the Ascendant stage.

Because of that incident, Luo Chen had later investigated Void-Collecting Flowers specifically, and that was how he learned about Boundless Grass, which was equally renowned.

Boundless Grass!

From birth, it contained a thread of spatial law power within!

If cultivated to maturity, even without special refinement, it would be comparable to a powerful offensive spatial spirit treasure!

If carefully refined, whether for attack, defense, or other special uses, it would possess all sorts of miraculous effects.

He had never imagined that within the Storage Necklace of Split Feather Demon Lord, there would actually be such a spiritual plant.

But after careful inspection, he felt some regret.

"Too young. It's unknown how long until it reaches maturity. Even if I refined it now, it would only add a trace of spatial attribute to a magic treasure."

"And cultivating it would be far too troublesome."

"For Boundless Grass to reach maturity through natural growth, it would need at least a thousand years just to be barely sufficient. If I wanted to accelerate it, I would have to place it in a place rich in spatial power..."

As he thought, Luo Chen's gaze froze.

Kong Kong'er was dancing around the Boundless Grass, acting intimately toward it.

Kong Kong'er itself was a gu insect possessing vast spatial power. If it accompanied the Boundless Grass as it grew, wouldn't that perfectly satisfy the conditions for accelerating its maturation?

If he could accelerate its maturation several times over...

No!

Even if it only halved the time, Luo Chen could harvest a spatial spiritual grass comparable to a spirit treasure in five or six hundred years.

If he refined it into a magic treasure and fought alongside Kong Kong'er...

"Huff!"

Luo Chen's breathing grew somewhat rapid as he already envisioned that scene.

"Split Feather Demon Lord, though I don't know if you were the 'human tribulation' I faced beyond the wind and thunder calamities after entering the Xuan Realm, the legacy you left behind is truly a great gift!"

And the gifts didn't stop there!

Luo Chen's divine sense began to sweep over the pile of things stacked like a small mountain.

Spirit stones!

Top-grade spirit stones, rarely seen even in the Mountains and Seas Realm!

Before Luo Chen now were fully ten thousand of them, with all five elements present.

Just these top-grade spirit stones alone, if exchanged for suitable resources, would be enough to allow a cultivator with decent talent to cultivate all the way to the Ascendant stage.

Additionally, there were large quantities of medicinal herbs!

Herbs that could catch the eye of a generation Demon Lord would certainly not be low-grade.

With just a glance, Luo Chen discovered many sealed medicinal herbs brimming with spirituality.

Even if used to refine fifth-grade or even sixth-grade pills, they would be more than sufficient.

Beyond these, there were also some peculiar treasures.

Eight thick, long, blackened iron needles, at the spirit treasure level, forming a complete set that required a formation disk to use.

However, their material seemed somewhat fragile?

It seemed they could only be used once or twice.

With Luo Chen's experience, he could naturally tell that these eight iron needles had no offensive or defensive function. Their characteristic was to guide and divide.

When his divine sense touched that brand-new formation disk, a somewhat sinister residual divine thought actively greeted him.

[Lightning-Drawing Needles, Eight Wastelands Formation Disk. When used together, they can deploy the Eight Wastelands Lightning-Drawing Formation. Before activation, the owner's essence blood must be used to soak them, lest the heavenly tribulation be dispersed. Specially made for demon cultivators undergoing tribulation, they can divide the heavenly punishment lightning tribulation, greatly reducing its power. Note: This treasure is a one-time-use spirit treasure. If arranged in reverse, it can easily attract a Body Integration heavenly tribulation. Use with caution—Blood Qi Zi, Blood Rain Continent.]

Thinking of Split Feather Demon Lord's cultivation level, Luo Chen easily understood that this was a tribulation-crossing treasure specially commissioned by him.

But the concept of a "one-time-use spirit treasure" was somewhat rare.

And the master who refined this item also piqued Luo Chen's curiosity.

Generally speaking, all spirit treasures were formed by cultivators nurturing true artifacts, gradually giving birth to intelligence.

At least, that was the common knowledge in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

The only person he knew who could refine spirit treasures was Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, master of the Demon-Fall Land.

And the master of this sinister divine thought, who could refine the Lightning-Drawing Needles and Eight Wastelands Formation Disk, seemed no less accomplished in the art of artifact refinement than Devil Lord Heaven-Refining.

"Blood Qi Zi of Blood Rain Continent. If I have the chance, I'd like to make his acquaintance."

After muttering to himself, Luo Chen put away this set of formation spirit treasures.

He himself had no need for such things.

If he had to cross a lightning tribulation, what could be simpler and easier than the Primordial Unity Cauldron?

That wasn't crossing tribulation—it was a great supplement!

Luo Chen continued to inventory the spoils.

He successively discovered several more treasures used for crossing tribulation.

It seemed Split Feather Demon Lord had prepared extremely thoroughly for crossing the Body Integration heavenly tribulation.

But most weren't as impressive as the Eight Wastelands Lightning-Drawing Formation, so they didn't catch Luo Chen's eye.

There was one item, however, that Luo Chen held and played with for a long time, feeling a sense of affinity toward it.

It was a string of dark yellow prayer beads, carved with images of various Buddhas and Bodhisattvas.

Just holding it filled one with a sense of purity.

After slightly converting some mana to activate it, Luo Chen appeared even more solemn and dignified, like a Bodhisattva or Arhat.

"Brahman Treasure Beads, a Buddhist secret treasure. Calms the mind, concentrates the spirit, resists heart demons."

After understanding the use of this treasure, Luo Chen couldn't help but smile.

This item was truly fated for him!

Whether it was the Three Heads Six Arms he cultivated, or the Moonlight Heart-Cleansing Mantra he chanted in his early years, both were orthodox Buddhist techniques.

Even the Chaos Indestructible Scripture he now primarily cultivated had incorporated a large amount of Buddhist philosophy. Even his Formless Bone Wheel was engraved with the Buddhist secret saying, "No self, no person, no sentient being."

The Brahman Treasure Beads were perfectly suited for him!

Luo Chen continued to inventory the remaining items. With his divine sense speed, he quickly finished.

Perhaps because he was preparing for the Body Integration heavenly tribulation, apart from the medicinal herbs needed for his own cultivation, Split Feather Demon Lord had exchanged most of his resources for tribulation-crossing treasures.

The hard currency was that batch of ten thousand top-grade spirit stones.

The rest wasn't particularly remarkable.

Finally, there was a book woven from cyan feathers.

Luo Chen glanced at it, and his mind gradually sank into it.

A long time later, he slowly withdrew his mind.

Caressing the feather book, a surge of anger faintly rose in Luo Chen's heart.

Ordinarily, he would quickly cut off such anger, but this time he let it spread without any attempt to stop it.

This book itself wasn't special. Its name was Split Heaven Nine Transformations, the inherited technique of the Split Wind Beast clan, allowing one to cultivate to the Body Integration stage.

What truly angered Luo Chen was the richness of its content and the detail of its annotations. It pointed out every pitfall one might encounter during cultivation.

Moreover, it even included three divine abilities.

This was worthy of the title "clan-defining technique"!

Compared to it, the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture that Luo Chen had cultivated back then was simply crude to the extreme.

Aside from the basic content of breathing, circulation, refining spirit into qi, there was only one secret technique for Nine Tribulations Three Nirvana Transformations.

As for other supporting divine abilities, techniques, or cultivation notes—there were none at all!

And that was supposedly a top-tier technique of the Xuan Realm, capable of allowing one to cultivate to the Great Vehicle stage!

If it were before, Luo Chen would have accepted Rosycloud's explanation—after all, it was something modified from a demon race technique, and there was no precedent for human cultivation. But after experiencing Rosycloud's scheme against him, Luo Chen was more inclined to believe that she had done it deliberately, to prevent Luo Chen from discovering the hidden backdoor vulnerabilities in the Heavenly Phoenix Nirvana Scripture through its supporting techniques.

"Back then, I had weathered countless storms in the cultivation world and considered myself shrewd and calculating. But in the end, I had only cultivated for a few hundred years. Compared to these great demons who had lived for millennia, I was still too naive."

"But Rosycloud, the me of today is no longer the me of yesterday. Don't let me catch you."

"Otherwise, I will repay you a hundredfold and torment you day and night!"

As his anger rose, Luo Chen put away all the miscellaneous items and swallowed the Primordial Unity Cauldron back into his body.

Thus, he was completely unburdened.

The Brahman Treasure Beads on his wrist emitted a cooling sensation, gradually suppressing that surge of anger.

After calming down, Luo Chen began to face reality once more.

Leaving aside where to find Rosycloud, even at her peak, she had been a top-tier Refining Void Sage, extremely difficult to kill.

If she had advanced further in these five hundred years, then even if Luo Chen encountered her, he might not be able to take revenge.

No matter how angry he was, it was just impotent rage.

Therefore, the top priority was still to increase his strength.

Only two paths lay before Luo Chen!

Body refinement and Qi refinement!

In the path of body refinement, he had just stepped onto a new level, reaching the Destruction stage. Even without using the Primordial Unity Cauldron, he was enough to match most Refining Void Sages.

But the path of cultivation ahead left Luo Chen somewhat confused and uncertain.

The path of body refinement required even more resources than the Qi Refinement stage.

Especially from the Destruction stage to the subsequent Nirvana stage—there was simply no path to follow.

To quickly increase his strength, he would have to start from the other path.

Although his primordial spirit had collapsed and his Purple Mansion was damaged, it wasn't a dead end.

At worst, he would walk the path he had taken before!

Build the Dao foundation, form the golden core, condense the Nascent Soul, and enter the Ascendant stage again!

With his rich cultivation experience from before, plus the assistance of a divine soul comparable to the late Ascendant stage, this time would certainly not take five hundred years like before.

Moreover, in retreading the immortal path, Luo Chen had another advantage.

He called up his attribute panel and stared at the physique column for a long time.

[Physique: Chaos Indestructible Body, Five-Spirit Dao Body (Hidden)]

The once-dim Five-Spirit Dao Body was now occasionally flickering with faint light, as if it could revive at any moment.

Luo Chen already understood the reason for this situation.

As long as he could comprehend the power of the five elemental laws, the Five-Spirit Dao Body would fully awaken.

Before ascending, he already had the four great laws of Wood, Water, Fire, and Earth by his side, only one step away from the complete awakening of the Five-Spirit Dao Body.

And that step, after experiencing the Return to Nothingness, had been shortened to the extreme.

He had devoured nearly one-third of the fleshly body of Metal Punishment Lord!!!

That was a Great Vehicle-level true spirit who had fully mastered the Metal origin!

Even if most of that power had been transformed into the Chaos Indestructible Body, the Metal origin contained within still remained on Luo Chen, only he hadn't had the time to comprehend it yet.

If Luo Chen retread the immortal path and comprehended the Metal origin, he would truly cultivate all five elements simultaneously.

At that time, he would possess both the Five-Spirit Dao Body and the Chaos Indestructible Body, raising his upper limit even further, certainly no weaker than any divine beast like the phoenix.

With simultaneous immortal and martial cultivation, he would have more confidence when encountering Rosycloud again.

And after taking revenge, he would have greater foundation to face the Great Vehicle Heavenly Phoenix behind Rosycloud.

Of course, a thousand-mile journey begins with a single step.

What Luo Chen had to consider at this stage was another matter.

Spiritual qi!

The spiritual qi of Taisu Immortal City was too ordinary, and the quantity was pitifully scarce, insufficient to support his rapid recovery of cultivation.

The Xuan Realm had many sects, and all the good spiritual veins were occupied by various major sects and families.

Given his identity, he naturally couldn't join a cultivation family that relied on bloodline inheritance.

And if he casually made a move to seize the territory of some Ascendant-level sect to borrow their spiritual vein for cultivation, it would easily attract the attention of the Nine Great Sacred Sects.

According to what Huang Yuanhua had said, all the major and minor sects on Youxuan Continent had to revere the Nine Great Sacred Sects; there was no such thing as absolute independence.

Before he grew strong, Luo Chen didn't want to face Body Integration Great Sovereigns.

Therefore, he needed to keep a low profile for a while and develop quietly.

"Let it be the Plain Spirit Sect!"

"This sect primarily deals in medicinal herb business. During my retraining process, I will inevitably need the assistance of pills, so I can borrow their strength. Moreover, this sect has a newly advanced Refining Void Sage who can provide shelter, sparing me from the troubles of small sects fighting among themselves."

"There's about two more years. I can prepare."

Luo Chen's gaze flickered as a plan formed in his mind.

...

In the following time, Luo Chen stayed completely indoors, beginning to cultivate in seclusion like everyone else.

But his method of cultivation was completely different from others.

Because his Purple Mansion was damaged, his Qi-Blood Furnace hung high, and his fleshly body was already completely different from ordinary cultivators.

He activated the Chaos Indestructible Scripture and began to transform his physical body.

At the microscopic level, he reopened his dantian and accumulated spiritual qi.

This process no longer attempted to convert Blood-Refining Divine Astral into mana, merely absorbing spiritual qi from the outside.

Starting from the lowest level of spiritual power, he began the conversion.

His cultivation level also began to rise rapidly.

Between breaths, he stepped into the Qi Refinement stage.

Then, like eating and drinking, he rapidly advanced his cultivation.

Qi Refinement first layer, Qi Refinement second layer, Qi Refinement mid-stage, Qi Refinement late stage...

The spiritual power in his dantian was no longer limited to one or two types but encompassed all five elements, evenly distributed.

Since he planned to cultivate all five elements simultaneously and revive the Five-Spirit Dao Body, he had to start from the very basics.

The cultivation of spiritual power and mana was the foundation of all foundations.

Two years later.

A transmission talisman flew into Luo Chen's cave dwelling.

After reading it, Luo Chen smiled slightly, withdrew his Withering-Bloom Fire Seed from the earth fire vein, and calmly left the Pu Gong Garden.

Outside the garden, Huang Yuanhua was idly waiting, chewing on a blade of grass.

Seeing Luo Chen, his eyes lit up.

He felt that the young man before him was ethereal and unrestrained, with an even stronger aura of lightness and agility.

"Senior, after three years of seclusion, your cultivation must have greatly improved!"

Luo Chen smiled and shook his head. "There's no need to call me senior. In truth, Fellow Daoist Xuan and you are at the same level."

As he spoke, he released his mana aura, which was at the early Initiate stage.

Huang Yuanhua was stunned, caught off guard.

Although over these three years, Luo Chen had actively explained that he wasn't a disciple trained by some power, just a wandering cultivator.

He had still thought Luo Chen was a senior of high cultivation.

He never expected that he was actually at the same level as himself, merely an early Initiate cultivator.

Before Huang Yuanhua could press further, Luo Chen proactively asked about the upcoming journey.

"Is it time to go to the Plain Spirit Sect?"

Huang Yuanhua nodded blankly. "Yes."

But soon he waved his hand. "We don't need to waste effort flying there. We can take Taisu Immortal City's flying ship to the main sect. Consider it a benefit of renting the herbal garden cave dwelling all these years."

Luo Chen let out an "oh" and smiled, gladly agreeing.

The two chatted as they walked toward the center of the immortal city. Along the way, the number of cultivators increased, each wearing expressions of anticipation and excitement.

It seemed they shared the same purpose.

Huang Yuanhua took the opportunity to introduce them to Luo Chen.

It was nothing more than who was a formidable figure among the wandering cultivators, who was the scion of some fallen family, and how they were going to the Spirit-Entering Assembly to join the Plain Spirit Sect and revive their family's prestige.

In Huang Yuanhua's mouth, these were all their competitors for joining the Plain Spirit Sect.

Luo Chen listened with a smile, watching with a smile.

Acting as if he was interested in everything.

But the calmness deep in his eyes showed that he wasn't interested in any of it.

When they turned a street corner, a massive flying ship burst into their view.

A long line had already formed before the flying ship.

Most of those in line were Initiate cultivators, with occasional Adept masters and Qi Refinement cultivators.

Luo Chen was no exception. Under Huang Yuanhua's greeting, he joined the queue.

Soon, it was their turn.

A young woman in green stood before the flying ship, recording names.

"Name, cultivation level, origin."

"Xuan Luo, early Initiate, from Green Gorge Town, Sunset Plains."

The young woman raised her head and met a pair of smiling eyes.

She felt as if she had fallen into a vortex, unable to control her mind, and could only murmur, "Xuan Luo..."

Luo Chen smiled and nodded.

Xuan Luo.

Taking the realm as his surname, and his surname as his name.

This would be his alias from now on.
