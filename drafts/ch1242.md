# Chapter 1242: Demon-Suppressing Land, Spatial Anomaly

The Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace, with its winding corridors and suspended walkways.

Two figures walked side by side. The woman, whose entire body was wrapped in ink-jade spirit armor, spoke methodically to the man beside her.

"The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect has a long and storied history. Even among the Nine Great Sacred Sects, its foundations are deep, perhaps second only to the Nether Origin Court, the overlord of the Nether Profound Continent."

"Within the Dao Sect, there are countless experts, with the strongest being the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign, who takes the sect's name as his Dao title. It is said his talent is terrifying beyond measure. Two thousand years ago, he was merely at the early Body Integration stage, yet now he has cultivated to the late Body Integration stage, only a single step away from the Great Vehicle Heavenly Sovereign realm."

"Many wish to enter the Dao Sect's gates, or at least behold the splendor of its mountain gate."

"For this trial, we will not enter the true mountain gate of the Dao Sect. Instead, we will be at Guangling Commandery, not far from Danzhu Mountain, which serves as their portal. The Demon-Suppressing Tower is established there, and beneath the tower lies the Dark Nether Demon Cave, where demonic rifts are born. Additionally, Guangling Commandery differs from ordinary human regions. A branch of the demon race, known as the Prostrate Earth Remnant Demons, has been stationed there for a long time."

"However, according to past convention, after the trial concludes, those who successfully pass will be invited into the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect's mountain gate to behold the sacred sect's scenery!"

"At that time, Junior Brother, you must be careful with your words and actions. Do not provoke any Dao Sect experts."

Jiang Huang spoke each word deliberately, conveying the information she had gathered from the fellow disciples' gatherings that Luo Chen had missed while in seclusion.

This was also the instruction of their master, Yao Xi Sage.

Jiang Huang followed it to the letter, afraid of omitting any key information.

Luo Chen nodded lightly, his expression calm yet carrying a hint of thoughtfulness.

This information was largely the same as what he knew from the classics.

Yet there were also some details unknown to ordinary people.

For instance, the cultivation speed of the current Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign was somewhat heaven-defying.

Although the Profound Realm and the Mountains and Seas Realm were worlds apart, the lifespans of high-level cultivators were generally not much different.

A Divine Transformation great power could live two thousand years, a Void Refiner five thousand, and the Body Integration stage, in total, only ten thousand years.

For ordinary people to cultivate from early to late Body Integration, it often took thousands of years!

Even for geniuses who appeared once in a thousand or ten thousand years, it was the same.

After all, those who could cultivate all the way from Qi Refinement—which one of them wasn't a peerless genius who had fought their way through billions of people?

But the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign had done it in just over two thousand years. That was truly shocking.

Even Luo Chen, calculating with the terrifying cultivation speed of his Five-Spirit Dao Body, found it hard to achieve such a feat without some extraordinary encounter.

Could it be that the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign possessed some physique even more heaven-defying than the Five-Spirit Dao Body?

That wasn't right either!

According to the classics and what predecessors had said, cultivation after the Body Integration stage was the true practice of the great Dao of laws. While powerful physiques were helpful, they couldn't make that much of a difference.

Or perhaps...

"Two thousand years..."

Luo Chen murmured this time in his heart, a flash of inspiration seeming to appear in his mind, but it passed in an instant.

Then, upon hearing that after the Demon-Suppressing Trial, they would be invited to visit the Dao Sect's scenery, he no longer concerned himself with the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign's situation. His attention shifted to this matter.

The Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was completely different from the Su Ling Sect!

In the Su Ling Sect, the Void Refiner realm Tai Su Ling Lord was exalted high above, rarely seen in person.

But within the Dao Sect, while Void Refiner True Lords were not everywhere, there was still a group of them!

If he were careless and exposed himself, it would be no different from falling into a tiger's den!

Luo Chen subconsciously touched his face.

The appearance and physique he had changed using the Flesh and Blood Derivation Art were completely different from Luo Chen of the Mountains and Seas Realm. He believed that even a Body Integration Great Sovereign would not be able to see through it face to face.

After all, with his physical cultivation having reached this level, the transformation of appearance and physique was no longer an illusory technique, but a real face.

The only thing that might expose him was his powerful soul, which was different from his disguised cultivation level.

"If I'm not exposed, so be it. But if I am..."

Luo Chen slowly clenched his fist.

Before leaving the Su Ling Sect, he had not only recalled the Chaos Myriad Spirit Flame.

He had also brought the Vacuum Gu and the Interval Grass with him.

The Interval Grass had been transplanted into the Five-Pointed Black Star Pagoda of the Polo Demon race within the Ink Jade Space.

That pagoda was essentially similar to the Penglai Octagonal Pavilion he had obtained before—a spatial treasure that could nurture spiritual plants.

As for the Vacuum Gu, Luo Chen carried it on his person.

Once things went wrong, aside from flying away and the Five-Phase Escape Arts he was already familiar with, he still had the Void-Shattering Divine Escape, which relied on the Vacuum Gu, to save his life.

This kind of spatial escape technique, combined with his Destruction Realm martial arts strength, would make it difficult for even a Void Refiner True Lord, let alone a Body Integration Great Sovereign, to keep him here.

Of course!

That was only as a last resort. Luo Chen had never seen a Body Integration cultivator's full-speed flying escape, so he didn't know which was superior.

"Master asked me to give this to you."

A jade bottle was handed to him.

Luo Chen glanced at Jiang Huang, took the jade bottle, and opened the stopper. His brow lifted slightly.

"A fourth-tier pill, and top-grade at that?"

Jiang Huang nodded. "Yes, this is the Return Immortal Pill personally refined by Elder Gumu. It is made with ten-thousand-year spiritual essence and Six-Return True Yang Liquid as the main ingredients. Don't be fooled by there being only three pills. Each one can instantly restore the mana of a Sage cultivator. Three pills mean three chances to save your life. Don't waste them unless absolutely necessary."

Luo Chen was somewhat surprised.

Whether it was ten-thousand-year spirit milk or Six-Return True Yang Liquid, both were supreme-grade spirit materials, difficult even for ordinary Divine Transformation great powers to possess.

In all his years of alchemy, he had never found such materials.

It was only in the legacy of that unlucky Void Refiner demon lord he had killed that he had fortuitously obtained a portion of Six-Return True Yang Liquid.

Such good treasures were usually used to refine fifth-tier divine pills.

For Elder Gumu to use them to refine fourth-tier pills was truly a shameful waste!

But he could also understand. For this Demon-Suppressing Trial, the Su Ling Sect had paid a great price, determined to successfully produce a few Divine Transformation great powers.

Luo Chen carefully put away the jade bottle and asked, "And you, Senior Sister Jiang?"

Jiang Huang forced a smile. "Don't worry. I have mine too. All ten Nascent Soul cultivators have them."

"Oh."

Luo Chen nodded, then with a flick of his hand, another jade bottle appeared.

Jiang Huang looked at it and asked doubtfully, "This is...?"

Luo Chen said calmly, "This is a fourth-tier pill I refined in my spare time after forming my Nascent Soul and returning to the sect. It is beneficial for soul damage. Senior Sister, take it. When you are expelling demons, use it in conjunction. It may help restore your soul foundation."

Jiang Huang stared blankly at the jade bottle, momentarily unable to react.

A fourth-tier pill, and a soul-type one at that?

Even within the Su Ling Sect, alchemists capable of refining such pills were few and far between.

Not only could Luo Chen refine it, but he was also generously giving it to her?

Did Luo Chen not know how precious this pill was?

She opened her mouth, intending to decline, but the words stuck in her throat.

Two hundred years of being controlled by demonic creatures had caused great damage to her soul foundation. Even though Master Yao Xi had spent twenty years healing her, she still had not reached the level expected of a Sage cultivator.

This item was indeed of great use to her!

Just as the woman was hesitating, Luo Chen suddenly looked up into the distance.

"The elder is summoning us. Time to gather."

With that, he shoved the jade bottle into the woman's hand and flew up himself.

Leaving Jiang Huang standing there, staring blankly at the jade bottle in her hand.

...

"Xuan Luozi has arrived!"

"The dean has been waiting for a long time."

"Junior Disciple Xuan Luo has adjusted his condition well. This trip will surely yield results!"

"I accept your auspicious words, Senior Uncle."

"I never thought that after receiving you into the sect just over a hundred years ago, you would be joining us in the Demon-Suppressing Trial so soon. It's quite poignant. I wonder if it's us old fellows who have stopped progressing, or if you younger generation are truly the waves that push the waves ahead."

"Senior Uncle Li Ting is joking. This disciple still has much to strive for."

Luo Chen smiled and greeted an Sage cultivator.

This was none other than Li Ting Sage, who had once been stationed at Tai Su Immortal City and had guided local rogue cultivators into the Su Ling Sect for the Entry Assembly. He was one of the Seven Sages of Su Ling, like Yao Xi.

In fact, it wasn't just him. The other Sage cultivators also gave Luo Chen simple nods of acknowledgment.

Including Hong Zhan Sage, who was at odds with Yao Xi!

From these details, it was clear that Luo Chen's status had changed compared to before.

And all of this stemmed from Luo Chen having recently learned a law divine ability.

Luo Chen responded calmly to these interpersonal exchanges, but his steps did not stop. He soon arrived beside his nominal master, Yao Xi Sage.

Before long, Jiang Huang also caught up.

"Did you give it to him?" Yao Xi asked.

"I did." Jiang Huang replied, her expression somewhat complicated.

"Did you pass on the message?" Yao Xi asked again, her gaze falling on Luo Chen.

Luo Chen smiled slightly. "Senior Sister conveyed all the words you instructed."

Yao Xi Sage nodded, then her expression turned serious. "Then let's not say more. From now on, follow the elder's arrangements."

As the three chatted, a figure had already appeared before the group.

Elder Gumu manipulated the Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace, slowly descending from the clouds.

Without the cloud cover, they could see a red mountain range stretching and undulating in the distance, a magnificent sight.

That was Danzhu Mountain, the portal of the Dao Sect.

The Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace did not head straight for Danzhu Mountain. Instead, it landed nearby in a sparsely populated area.

Although sparsely populated, it was not desolate.

On the ground, palaces stood in rows, giant statues overlooked the world, and a golden, brilliant tower stood tall on the earth, suppressing all evil!

This was Guangling Commandery!

As soon as the Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace appeared, a powerful divine sense swept over unceremoniously.

Elder Gumu smiled. "Xiu Teng, don't you recognize me anymore?"

"So it's you, old fellow. So the Su Ling Sect sent you to lead this time. The Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace, tsk, quite the display!"

As the voice spread, a figure shot up from below and arrived outside the palace.

Everyone looked and was startled.

The newcomer had a handsome face, appearing as a young man.

But on his head were two curved horns, greatly marring his otherwise perfect image.

Elder Gumu asked curiously, "Are you in charge here? Where are the fellow Daoists of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect?"

The young man called Xiu Teng waved his hand. "They've all entered the Demon-Suppressing Tower ahead of time to familiarize themselves with the situation. Come with me first!"

Before setting off, he deliberately turned back to look at the massive palace.

"Put it away before you land. Don't crush the nearby arrangements."

Elder Gumu acknowledged and then manipulated the Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace to descend slowly.

Luo Chen stood at the back, looking at that person, somewhat astonished. "A demon?"

Yao Xi Sage nodded, but then shook her head. "He is a demon, but not a pure one. This person is a human-demon hybrid, having absorbed the strengths of both races. His talent is exceptional. At only eight hundred years old, he has already cultivated to the Divine Transformation realm. He is the most outstanding genius of the Prostrate Earth lineage at present."

A human-demon hybrid. This truly surprised Luo Chen.

But in an instant, he thought of someone.

Devil Lord Heaven-Refining!

Tai Sui had once told him that Devil Lord Heaven-Refining was not a pure demon, but also possessed human and demon beast bloodlines.

Especially the demon beast bloodline, which was said to be a Heavenly Demon bloodline!

The so-called Heavenly Demon was actually an honorific title.

It often referred to those Divine Transformation great demon beasts from the Mountains and Seas Realm who had successfully ascended.

If placed in the Profound Realm or the Demon Realm, it might correspond to Great Vehicle stage demon beasts.

Luo Chen hadn't expected that the most outstanding descendant left behind by Devil Lord Heaven-Refining's Prostrate Earth lineage in the Profound Realm would also be a hybrid demon.

Yao Xi Sage continued, "Speaking of which, he is somewhat similar to you. Not only does he have excellent cultivation talent, but he is also quite accomplished in the Dao of alchemy. Elder Gumu has had much contact with him, and the two are friends."

Luo Chen understood. "So that's why the sect didn't send the other, more powerful Divine Transformation elders, but specifically sent Elder Gumu to lead this time?"

"That is one reason, but not the only one." Yao Xi Sage explained carefully to Luo Chen. "Elder Gumu, as our sect's most powerful fifth-tier alchemist, has a wide circle of friends and extensive connections. He knows people on the demon remnant side, and also has many friends in other sects, including the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect. Having him lead allows us to build good relations with the other sects participating in the Demon-Suppressing Trial. At the very least, we won't enter and find everyone as our enemy."

So that was it.

A weak sect naturally had to send its strongest expert to lead, so as not to diminish the sect's reputation.

A powerful sect had no need for such formalities.

The Su Ling Sect was a newly established Void Refiner power, neither too strong nor too weak, so they had to compromise. Sending someone like Elder Gumu was just right.

As the two chatted, the Myriad Manifestations Heavenly Palace had descended to a certain height.

The scene on the ground was visible to the naked eye.

Due to the massive commotion caused by the True Lord's carriage, figures had appeared from the various palaces, all looking up at the behemoth in the sky with curiosity and shock.

Some Divine Transformation cultivators leading their respective sects had also come out.

With a wave of Elder Gumu's hand, the group left the palace.

Elder Gumu cast a few hand seals at the palace, and the behemoth rapidly shrank, finally stopping at about a hundred zhang, steadily resting in an open space.

Being able to shrink such a behemoth to this extent was even more shocking.

Elder Gumu laughed heartily and walked towards a few Divine Transformation cultivators gathered together.

"Fellow Daoist Xu, you all have arrived quite early! Come, come, let me introduce my sect's disciples."

"You old fellow, what are you plotting now? Let me say first, I have no meeting gifts for the juniors."

"No matter. Just getting familiar with each other's faces is fine. If you run into them later, just look out for them."

The group of Su Ling Sect Sages followed behind. Usually imposing Sage cultivators, they were now all respectful.

Luo Chen mingled among them, his gaze only briefly sweeping over those few Nascent Soul cultivators before looking into the distance.

That tower, golden and brilliant, connected heaven and earth!

Its name was not particularly outstanding—the common "Demon-Suppressing" found throughout the cultivation world.

But what was suppressed beneath it was what Luo Chen was most concerned about.

The Dark Nether Demon Cave, capable of generating demonic rifts!

And most importantly, the moment Luo Chen arrived at this place, the Vacuum Divine Gu attached to his body began to stir slightly.

Luo Chen was extremely surprised in his heart.

"The space in the Profound Realm is supposed to be extremely stable. Why is it somewhat thin here?"
