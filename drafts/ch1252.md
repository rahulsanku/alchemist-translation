# Chapter 1252: Mid Ascension, Devouring the Vermilion Bird

“Black Tortoise, quickly activate the Demon-Suppressing Tower and destroy the Dark Demon Cave!”

Within the golden giant tower, a figure staggered out from ripples in the void.

The moment she appeared, she spoke urgently.

No sooner had the words left her mouth than she spat out a mouthful of blood.

Black Tortoise True Lord was startled. Seeing his fellow disciple in such a state, he was greatly shocked. “What happened? And why has your dharma manifestation been scattered?”

As he spoke, he suddenly made his move, slapping out over a hundred hand seals in succession, heavily imprinting them upon the red-robed woman.

Those hand seals stirred spiritual light, transforming into the shape of a tortoise shell that wrapped around her.

Vermilion Bird True Lord’s aura was withered and weak. The mana throughout her body leaked out uncontrollably like a burst dam—this was a sign of her Great Dao dharma manifestation being shattered.

Fortunately, Black Tortoise True Lord’s efforts forcibly stabilized her, at least preventing the situation from becoming irredeemable.

With external help, Vermilion Bird True Lord finally recovered.

She spoke quickly. “The Ninefold Seal has been broken. A Sacred Ancestor from the Demon Realm has crossed over. We must use the ten-thousand-year accumulated power of the Demon-Suppressing Tower to destroy that Sacred Demon’s left hand before he can establish a foothold!”

“But…” Black Tortoise True Lord hesitated.

Vermilion Bird True Lord’s eyes sharpened. “What ‘buts’? Are you worried about those ants in the demon cave? Don’t forget—what is our mission in guarding the Demon-Suppressing Tower? If the seal is completely broken and the demon army charges through here, the casualties won’t be just a few hundred people.”

The Demon-Suppressing Tower had stood in Guangling Commandery for over ten thousand years. The power it had accumulated was terrifying, and its connection to this demon cave was extremely tight.

Once that power was released, although it might not be enough to kill a Sacred Ancestor of the Demon Realm, if it was only a left hand that had crossed over, it would be sufficient to repel it.

Of course!

Under such a devastating strike, no living being within the Dark Demon Cave would have any chance of survival.

Even disregarding the disciples of the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect, the other Sage cultivators were all the future hopes and even the pillars of their respective sects.

If the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect truly did this, although it wouldn’t directly shake their foundation, it would certainly lead to estrangement and loss of loyalty in the future.

If this continued for long, whether the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect could maintain absolute dominance in this region was questionable.

Vermilion Bird True Lord thought Black Tortoise True Lord was worried about this, so her tone was quite severe.

Decisive action was needed at this moment!

However…

Black Tortoise True Lord pointed nearby and said in a gentle tone, “Junior Sister, look at the Demon-Suppressing Tower.”

Vermilion Bird True Lord looked around in confusion.

Golden light shone brightly, peaceful and upright, just as before.

The Demon-Suppressing Tower was closely connected to the Dark Demon Cave, sharing honor and disgrace with the Ninefold Seal.

If the seal had truly been broken, the Demon-Suppressing Tower should not have been so calm.

“How can this be?”

Black Tortoise True Lord didn’t know the specifics either, and it wasn’t convenient for him to personally enter the demon cave to investigate, especially since sending Vermilion Bird True Lord down earlier had already cost a great price.

But he didn’t doubt what Vermilion Bird True Lord had said earlier either.

“It seems the Heavenly Sovereigns of the Nether Capital Source Court left some contingency within the seal, thus preventing a potential demon calamity!”

“Contingency?” Vermilion Bird True Lord shook her head. She hadn’t seen any contingency earlier.

Black Tortoise True Lord continued, “Since the Demon-Suppressing Tower shows no unusual activity now, it means the situation is still under control. The trial cultivators inside the demon cave are the future of various sects. It wouldn’t be good to casually kill them—the repercussions would be too severe. Of course, Junior Sister, your concerns are not without reason. To prevent any troublemakers from being mixed in, we cannot easily open the Demon-Suppressing Tower now. Let them stay inside for a while. I will notify the sect. Everything will be decided by the Sect Master.”

In the end, he looked worriedly at the red-robed woman.

“Junior Sister, you should meditate and regulate your breathing as soon as possible. Restrain your mana and don’t let your dharma manifestation truly collapse, otherwise…”

He didn’t finish his words, but the unspoken meaning was clear to both of them.

Vermilion Bird True Lord was silent for a moment, then finally nodded lightly.

There was no other choice.

…

Within the Dark Demon Cave, at the Central Demon Abyss.

On a plain stretching thousands of li, a great storm was raging!

At the eye of the storm, Luo Chen sat cross-legged, a look of surprise on his face.

After a cultivator breaks through a major realm, they often receive a heavenly backlash, granting them a massive amount of energy to help stabilize their current realm.

Especially since he had previously interrupted the process of absorbing heaven and earth origin qi in order to dive deep into the ocean of origin qi.

Now, with origin qi pouring into his body, it was to be expected.

But this amount… seemed a bit too much?

Luo Chen thoughtfully looked at the sealing formation not far away that was automatically recovering, then shook his head.

“No, it’s not attracted by this sealing formation.”

“It seems more like…”

“A gift from the Xuan Realm to me?”

The more Luo Chen thought about it, the more he felt this was the case.

With his martial arts strength, even if he wasn’t afraid of Body Integration cultivators, it was impossible for him to repel a Sacred Ancestor of the Demon Realm with a single sword strike.

Especially since that sword strike hadn’t used his Blood-Refined Divine Astral or martial arts divine intent.

It was the purest sword strike driven by mana.

A strike that manipulated heaven and earth origin qi.

But at that time, he had acted naturally, following the will of heaven.

In that state, he seemed to have become this very heaven and earth. Using the power of the Destruction Great Dao he had just comprehended, he drew upon the ocean of origin qi and unleashed a sword of destruction that surpassed his current realm.

It could be said that he had struck out and repelled the Sacred Ancestor of the Demon Realm, but it could also be said that this heaven and earth had automatically resisted.

After all, heaven and earth had spirit. The ancient Xuan Realm also possessed a mature world will.

If the Demon Realm truly succeeded in invading, not to mention anything else, the mere act of the Nine Nether Demon Moss transforming heaven and earth and converting spiritual qi could potentially make the Xuan Realm no longer the Xuan Realm.

As a meritorious official acting on behalf of heaven, and just in a state after a successful breakthrough, this heaven and earth had given Luo Chen the greatest “reward.”

This reward was indeed substantial, arriving at just the right time, enough to allow Luo Chen to stabilize his current realm and even go a step further.

Luo Chen naturally didn’t hesitate and began to devour greedily.

Energy clusters like a storm transformed into a tornado that he swallowed mouthful after mouthful into his body.

For an ordinary Ascendant cultivator, no matter how talented, they wouldn’t dare to absorb so wantonly.

But Luo Chen wasn’t afraid at all!

With his two powerful physiques—the Chaos Indestructible Body and the Five-Spirit Dao Body—no matter how much energy came, he could withstand it.

Besides, this was a heavenly blessing.

As the massive energy entered his body, the *Chaos Indestructible Sutra* activated, quickly refining and absorbing it.

Strands of mana stamped with Luo Chen’s imprint were naturally generated, the entire process going smoothly.

And his realm, during this process, rapidly stabilized and continuously rose.

Half a year later.

A figure slowly opened his eyes.

The aura on his body had undergone earth-shaking changes.

“Mid Ascension!”

“Hoo…”

Luo Chen exhaled a turbid breath, a look of satisfaction in his eyes.

After completely absorbing that massive energy cluster, his realm had directly crossed the need to stabilize at Early Ascension and arrived at Mid Ascension.

This realm, considering only a hundred years of cultivation in the Xuan Realm, was enough to shock the world.

But if measured against Luo Chen’s current lifespan of nearly twelve hundred years, it was merely unremarkable.

What he cared about more now was not these minor realm improvements.

But the various laws and great daos he had comprehended.

After comprehending the Wood Origin, the Five-Spirit Dao Body had become more complete.

This was not an illusion!

During these six months of absorbing and refining that storm-like massive energy cluster, the Five-Spirit Dao Body had played an extremely important role.

Originally estimated to take much longer, it had only taken half a year in the end.

From this, one could see how terrifyingly efficient a truly complete Five-Spirit Dao Body was at refining various types of spiritual qi.

Additionally, there was the Destruction Law he had painstakingly comprehended.

“No! This is no longer just a law’s true intent, but a self-contained Great Dao!”

Luo Chen gazed into the void.

The attribute panel had long been unfolded. In the law column, it clearly marked [Destruction Great Dao].

This showed its uniqueness.

Unfortunately, now was not the time or place to study this dao.

Luo Chen frowned as he looked at the sky. Golden light still filled the air, illuminating the entire Dark Demon Cave in every detail.

It had been a full half year since the Sacred Demon’s left hand had crossed over earlier.

Why hadn’t the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect made any move?

No one had been sent in, nor had anyone been allowed out. Everything seemed as if nothing had happened.

“No matter what, this place is ultimately not somewhere to stay long. I should have left long ago.”

Luo Chen stood up, ready to leave.

But just as he was about to depart, his heart stirred, and his eyes looked in several directions.

“Oh, there are still such good things here. I was focused on cultivation earlier and didn’t notice, but you found them.”

Luo Chen smiled slightly, opened his mouth, and gently spat.

In an instant, a streak of mixed-colored cyan light flew out, transforming into a wisp of flame that eagerly danced before Luo Chen.

“Go, don’t waste it!”

As he spoke, the Chaos Myriad Spirit Flame couldn’t wait to fly out, as if it had found some kind of food.

And indeed, it was food!

At this moment, on this plain that had become the Central Demon Abyss, seven clusters of crimson flames were scattered in isolation across various places.

Each cluster contained extremely powerful energy, and within them was even a fierce, baleful will.

Vermilion Bird Divine Fire!

Luo Chen picked one cluster and examined it closely.

This was clearly the origin divine fire that Vermilion Bird True Lord had split off, specifically used to incinerate those Void Refining demon spirits and prevent them from regenerating.

Now, it had become Luo Chen’s gain.

But strangely, upon closer inspection, Luo Chen felt a sense of familiarity.

Suddenly!

“Demon-Fall Land, Blazing Refinement Prison, Vermilion Bird Mountain!”

Luo Chen finally found where that sense of familiarity came from.

In his youth, he had explored the Demon-Fall Land with others, seeking opportunities to form a Sage.

He had once, in a fire-attributed spiritual land, accidentally discovered a powerful source-less fire that had even given birth to weak spiritual intelligence.

At that time, Taisui had called it Vermilion Bird Sacred Flame. But now, comparing it to Vermilion Bird True Lord’s origin divine fire, the two seemed to be of the same origin?

Luo Chen’s thoughts drifted far away, memories constantly emerging.

According to Taisui, when the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had first arranged the Five Elements Heaven, there was no Vermilion Bird Sacred Flame.

That fire spirit had instead appeared after the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining had fallen.

In other words, that fire spirit was more likely obtained by the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining before his fall, sealed within the Blazing Refinement Prison. After he fell a thousand years later, when Luo Chen and the others entered, they witnessed the birth of the fire spirit.

Then, was that Vermilion Bird Sacred Flame a cluster of origin divine fire that Vermilion Bird True Lord had split off, just like now?

Luo Chen had some guesses in his heart, but his face showed nothing.

“Quickly devour it, then return to the Purple Mansion. Don’t let a single trace of its aura leak out!”

Luo Chen instructed the Chaos Myriad Spirit Flame, then spread his divine sense. His mana transformed into large hands, grabbing in all directions.

This demon abyss had existed for a long time, being the essence of the Dark Demon Cave.

Good things weren’t just the scraps left behind by Vermilion Bird True Lord.

Soon, streaks of light flew back with the mana hands, floating before Luo Chen.

Countless demon marrow diamonds!

Extremely pure and massive demon crystals containing pure demon qi!

And various ores with unknown names, as well as some demon realm medicinal herbs that Luo Chen could barely name.

These had all survived from the earlier great battle.

For Luo Chen now, this was also an additional harvest.

Their specific uses weren’t for consideration now; he would decide later.

After the Chaos Myriad Spirit Flame flew back, full and satisfied, Luo Chen no longer hesitated and immediately changed his appearance.

His face became younger, like a youth.

His stature also grew a few inches.

Even his temperament and bearing became much more spirited and confident.

It was exactly the appearance of Xuan Luo from before!

After doing all this, Luo Chen revealed the mana fluctuations of a Mid Sage and directly left this battlefield.

…

A figure flew unhurriedly through the sky above the Dark Demon Cave.

It was Luo Chen.

As he flew, his eyes occasionally swept downward.

Unlike the dark, ink-green earth before, although traces of battle could still be seen, the ground had already grown various other common plants of the Xuan Realm.

Flowers adorned the brocade, trees stood in forests.

Clear springs flowed, spiritual qi flourished.

What a scene of all things competing and thriving with vitality.

Through the remaining traces of battle, Luo Chen could roughly infer what had happened in these places.

It should have been Vermilion Bird True Lord’s handiwork.

Before entering the Central Demon Abyss, she had swept through the entire Dark Demon Cave alone, not only killing a large number of demon spirits but also dispersing various demon caves.

Then new spiritual caves were generated, resulting in the current transformed appearance.

“This is a benefit for those Sage cultivators participating in the demon-suppressing trial.”

Luo Chen was well aware of how beneficial such an environment was for Sage cultivators, especially those with outstanding talent who had cultivated to the Late Sage stage.

As long as they weren’t too foolish, finding a newborn spiritual cave to cultivate in peace would easily put them into a state of heaven-earth sympathetic enlightenment.

Taking advantage of this to comprehend law true intent would be perfectly natural.

Even attempting to break through to the Ascension realm wasn’t impossible.

Thinking this way, his risky act of entering the Central Demon Abyss to break through seemed somewhat unnecessary.

Staying outside, he wouldn’t have been unable to break through either.

Of course, without that risk, without the massive spiritual opportunity of the Central Demon Abyss, he wouldn’t have comprehended the Destruction Great Dao.

“First, I’ll reunite with my fellow disciples and quietly leave the Dark Demon Cave as a member of the Plain Spirit Sect!”

This was Luo Chen’s current goal.

And finding them wasn’t difficult.

Not to mention that Luo Chen knew where they were cultivating in seclusion, the lotus brand, which had already had its restrictions lifted, could point him in the right direction.

Just as Luo Chen was about to approach the great lake where the Plain Spirit Sect cultivators were cultivating in seclusion, his steps suddenly paused, and his gaze fell not far below.

The corners of his mouth unconsciously lifted.

“Indeed, bad things either don’t come, or they come one after another. Good things are the same. Now, this is truly multiple joys arriving at the door!”
