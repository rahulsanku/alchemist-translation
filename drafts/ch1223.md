# Chapter 1223: Have We Met? I Have Seen Him!

Because Dean He had singled him out, and the Alchemy Mountain elders had supported him, Luo Chen was able to take his place here today.

But he was still ranked last.

After all, he was ultimately still only a low-grade alchemist, not even touching the hem of an Alchemy Saint's robe.

Being able to follow along, listen to a few words, receive a hint of guidance—that alone could benefit him for a lifetime.

For this reason, Alchemy Mountain had even entrusted Luo Chen with the task of later introducing the Mountains and Seas Travel Palace, giving him a chance to show his face before the Alchemy Saint.

But no one had expected something abrupt to happen.

"Who is that?"

Everyone followed the sound and looked over, momentarily stunned.

Tai Su Lingjun's gaze also fell upon Luo Chen. Based on the cyan robe he wore, she could tell he was a disciple of her own sect.

But on this occasion, Adept-stage cultivators were only allowed to observe from afar, not approach.

How had this person managed to sneak in?

Instantly, Tai Su Lingjun's brow furrowed, and her questioning eyes swept over Elder Gumu.

Elder Gumu gave a wry smile, then looked toward Dean He.

"Dean He, explain!"

Dean He took a deep breath and replied with utmost respect: "Reporting to the True Lord, this person is named Xuan Luo, an unprecedented alchemy genius of our Su Ling Sect. Today, this junior boldly brought him along, only so he could witness the True Lord's bearing, take you as his role model, and be spurred to work even harder in the future."

The words flowed smoothly, proper and dignified, yet not lacking in respect.

Tai Su Lingjun was very satisfied. No wonder he was one of the deans of the three halls and five courts.

Then she looked at Chu Yan.

Unexpectedly, the latter still had her slender brows slightly furrowed, a look of half-belief in her eyes.

"Unprecedented? How unprecedented?"

Dean He took a deep breath and said with considerable pride: "Disciple Xuan Luo entered the alchemy path, refining a finished pill on his very first day. In three years, he reached the second rank. Now, he can independently refine third-rank pills. Not only that, but his theoretical knowledge is also unique. He has helped our sect perfect and improve ancient remnant formulas and has even achieved mass production. Most importantly, his cultivation has not fallen behind. If given a few hundred years, he might become a fifth-rank alchemist!"

Chu Yan let out an "Oh," and said, "Not bad."

Then, her gaze passed over the crowd and lingered on the young man who was still bowing in salute.

"Raise your head!"

Upon hearing this, the young man straightened up gracefully, revealing a face of peerless handsomeness.

The moment she saw that face, Tai Su Lingjun's eyes lit up instead.

Talent was one thing, but his appearance was certainly proper.

Chu Yan stared at this face. It did not overlap with any impression in her memory, which made her somewhat disappointed.

But she still asked one last question.

"Have we met?"

Her voice was incomparably beautiful, like a heavenly sound.

The others hadn't noticed, but Tai Su Lingjun was startled.

The Su Ling Sect disciple named Xuan Luo, upon hearing this question, blurted out: "Today is the first time we meet."

As soon as this answer came out, the disappointment in Chu Yan's eyes became thick.

She shook her head and said to Tai Su Lingjun: "The journey was too long, I'm a bit tired. I'd like to rest for a day and tour the Su Ling scenery tomorrow."

Tai Su Lingjun nodded. "Very well. Please!"

The two moved with lotus steps, walking side by side.

Behind them, Elder Gumu, Dean He, and the others followed one by one.

When they reached the end of the procession, as the head and tail crossed paths, Tai Su Lingjun glanced at the youngest cyan-robed man at the very back.

Interesting!

Just now, Chu Yan had actually used her soul-bewitching method to induce him to tell the truth.

Did this disciple of hers look very much like an old acquaintance of Chu Yan's?

The head and tail of the procession crossed, and Luo Chen, at the very end, entered the Mountains and Seas Travel Palace.

Because the Alchemy Saint had said she was tired, there was no arrangement today for Luo Chen to explain the layout of this travel palace to her.

He was happy to have it that way.

But beneath his respectful expression, there was a hint of strangeness.

Chu Yan still remembered him?

She had even almost recognized him!

Was it because of the pill he had given her on Heaven-Earth Peak back then, or because he had once received a legacy in the Alchemy Saint Hall of the Nether Origin Sect?

Luo Chen didn't know. Perhaps it was just a woman's intuition.

What he was grateful for was that his research into the unity of mind and will over the past few years had made some progress. Just now, he had restrained his soul to the utmost, fooling her.

And most importantly, what he had said was technically the truth.

He himself had indeed never met Sage Chu Yan in person.

Whether in the Star Gate back then, or at the Ascension Ceremony, he had only seen her illusions and externalized primordial spirit, never meeting her face to face.

Even when he had just raised his head and seen Chu Yan's true appearance, he hadn't immediately matched it with the idealized image of the Alchemy Saint in his memory.

Five hundred years of great changes. Even for cultivators, there were indelible transformations.

The Chu Yan of today, even as a Void Refining True Lord, had lost the high-spirited vigor of her ascension days, replaced by a lonely, gloomy air.

And he, Luo Chen, was no longer the Wilderness Wanderer who had just entered the Sage stage.

The procession gradually entered the depths of the travel palace.

Sect Master Tai Su Lingjun and Alchemy Saint Chu Yan entered the bedchamber, while the group of alchemists from Alchemy Mountain waited behind Elder Gumu.

After a while, Tai Su Lingjun came out.

"Sect Master!"

"We are within the sect, no need for formalities."

Tai Su Lingjun waved her hand, her gaze sweeping over the crowd.

"Act as you normally would from now on. Do not disturb the Alchemy Saint."

"As for your alchemy questions, the Alchemy Saint will naturally find time to guide you."

"Who is currently in charge of the Mountains and Seas Travel Palace?"

Luo Chen stepped forward. "Reporting to the Sect Master, this disciple is in charge."

Tai Su Lingjun was somewhat surprised. She looked at the others, and each of them wore an expression of natural acceptance.

Clearly, they all thought highly of this disciple to entrust him with such an important task.

While she hadn't been paying attention, an extraordinary genius had indeed emerged within the sect!

Tai Su Lingjun thought for a moment and instructed: "Handle the trivial matters of the travel palace meticulously. Do not be the slightest bit negligent towards our honored guest. If you lack people or things, just ask. Gumu, notify the three halls and five courts to cooperate with Xuan Luo. Also..."

She paused, looking at Luo Chen with interest.

"The Alchemy Saint will be staying in our sect for a few months. When she tours the scenic spots within the sect, she will need a disciple to attend to her. You will do it!"

Luo Chen's heart tightened, but he still braced himself and replied: "As you command!"

Tai Su Lingjun gave a faint, knowing smile. "Perform well. Don't disappoint your elders!"

With that, she drifted away.

Elder Gumu and the others called out in unison: "Farewell, Sect Master."

After she left, Elder Gumu led the group out of the travel palace.

Once outside, he looked helplessly at Dean He and the others.

"You all... what can I say? You almost caused an accident."

Dean He said solemnly: "Our actions were for the sake of the sect. If a genius like Xuan Luo can receive guidance from a stronger alchemist, it would be a great boon for the sect."

"Alright, alright, I understand." Elder Gumu waved his hand, then turned to Luo Chen with a serious expression. "The Alchemy Saint lives up to her reputation. During your attendance in the coming days, be sure to mind your words and actions. Even if you cannot win her favor, do not offend her. Otherwise, even with the Sect Master's protection, your path will be difficult. Do you understand?"

Luo Chen bowed respectfully. "Thank you for the advice, Elder."

After giving these instructions, Elder Gumu also left.

But Luo Chen did not get any peace, as the alchemists from Alchemy Mountain gathered around him again, giving him all sorts of advice.

Though troublesome, Luo Chen could feel that these people genuinely meant well for him.

Respecting their elders, planning for the sect, and seeking the best for their disciples.

Luo Chen couldn't help but feel a surge of emotion. With people like this, as long as no accidents happened, the Su Ling Sect was bound to prosper.

After everyone had left, only Luo Chen remained at the entrance of the travel palace.

Of course, the vast travel palace had disciples to look after it, at least Adept-stage cultivators. But they were not allowed to speak with the Alchemy Saint.

Luo Chen was the sole person in charge, which could be seen as the position of travel palace steward.

He stood at the entrance for a while, not knowing what he was thinking.

After a long time, he turned and went back inside the travel palace.

Crossing the Little Nether Abyss, passing by waterside pavilions and kiosks, many disciples greeted him along the way.

He responded to each with a smile.

But as he walked, his smile became somewhat strained.

Unbeknownst to him, a gaze had fallen from a high place, intermittently sweeping over his back.

On a distant stargazing tower that resembled Heaven-Earth Peak, the Alchemy Saint Chu Yan, who should have been resting, stood alone upon it.

Luo Chen did not dare to look back. He maintained his original pace and frequency, as if he had noticed nothing.

But in his heart, he couldn't help but complain.

"This task is really going to be the death of me. I have to go all out with my disguise for the next few months and not let a single flaw slip."

...

Early the next morning.

Tai Su Lingjun came to the Mountains and Seas Travel Palace and invited Chu Yan to go sightseeing.

Chu Yan gladly accepted.

Luo Chen was responsible for driving the cloud carriage, acting as the charioteer.

The Su Ling Sect was vast—vast in a geographical sense.

Even back when it was still a Divine Transformation-level sect, its mountain gate area was not inferior to some Void Refining-level powers.

Such a vast area was naturally not all conquered by the Su Ling Sect itself.

It was related to a special permit from the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect!

After all, most of the medicinal herbs produced by the Su Ling Sect were supplied to the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect.

To facilitate the Su Ling Sect's herb cultivation, they were naturally granted a vast area for their mountain gate.

From Luo Chen's perspective, the mountain gate area of the Su Ling Sect alone was no smaller than the entire Eastern Wilderness he had once been in, and perhaps even larger!

In all his years since joining the Su Ling Sect, his footsteps had only been confined to a few peaks.

This time, attending to the Alchemy Saint allowed him to see many beautiful sights within the sect that he had only heard of but never seen.

Strange mountains and extraordinary peaks, seas of clouds and rainbow bridges.

Flying waterfalls and hidden ravines, ancient trees and giant forests.

There were countless such beautiful scenes.

While admiring the scenery, he would stand far away, not disturbing the two women.

But while driving the carriage, he occasionally caught snippets of their idle chatter.

It wasn't just private women's talk; there were also comments and critiques on the cultivation world, and occasionally discussions about rising geniuses from various families and sects.

The geniuses who could catch the eyes of these two women were at least at the Sage or Nascent Soul level, and some were even at the Body Integration level, like the Great Sovereigns.

For example, the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign!

"That one, I cannot see through. In the eight hundred years since I joined the sect, I have only met him twice."

"Aside from the Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerables, how many people in the Youxuan Continent can see through him? Even though the Nine Great Saint Sects have countless experts, he is still one of the foremost powerhouses. Otherwise, how could he have blocked the Nether Origin Sect's request for his younger sister back then? But I am curious, have you really only seen him twice?"

"Yes. Once was when I ascended, and he received me. The other was five hundred years ago, when he summoned me and gave me a scroll of pill formulas, asking me to try to refine them."

"Pill formulas?"

"Yes."

"What kind of pill formula was so important that he treated it with such gravity?"

"This is a secret, not easy to disclose. But that pill is extremely difficult. After five hundred years of painstaking research, I have only just managed to get started. However, the Taiyin Pill that was simplified from that pill formula along the way—you have experienced its effects, Lingjun."

"The Taiyin Pill that helped me break through to the Void Refining stage back then was merely a simplified version of that pill formula?"

"Yes. That's why I say that pill formula is too difficult. In my humble opinion, that pill formula might not even be an object of the Xuan Realm."

"Not from the Xuan Realm? Could it be from some lower realm... no, a demon realm pill formula? I heard that in his early years, the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign had a close relationship with the Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, a remnant of the demon race. And that Devil Lord Heaven-Refining, known as the strongest Void Refining cultivator, in the dao of alchemy..."

"Lingjun, be careful with your words!"

Luo Chen finally understood why he had been chosen to drive the carriage.

When the Alchemy Saint had come to the Su Ling Sect, she had brought over a hundred attendants. Any one of them could have done a better job than him.

What the two women were discussing now involved a Great Sovereign of the Dao Sect. If someone else had overheard, who knew what trouble it would cause.

With Chu Yan's reminder, Tai Su Lingjun did not press further on this matter.

She merely sighed about how powerful the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect was. Although it only had one Body Integration cultivator, the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign, its prestige rivaled that of the foremost of the Nine Great Saint Sects.

His four great war generals were all outstanding.

As for the other Void Refining True Lords, they were also far beyond the reach of others.

If the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign could break through to the Great Vehicle stage, he might even lead the Dao Sect to become a Source Court, standing shoulder to shoulder with forces like the Youdu Source Court and the Heavenly Phoenix Clan.

Luo Chen was already well aware of the hierarchy of forces in the Xuan Realm.

The distinctions between great sects and superior sects in the Mountains and Seas Realm were not popular here.

Often, a force was classified based on the cultivation level of its strongest member.

Only the Body Integration stage and the Great Vehicle stage were different.

A force with a Body Integration cultivator was called a Saint Sect.

And a force with a Great Vehicle cultivator was called a Source Court!

The factors involved were roughly related to the laws of origin.

What surprised Luo Chen, however, was that the Heavenly Origin Great Sovereign had grown so powerful, dominating the Body Integration stage and approaching the Great Vehicle stage!

If that was the case, the pill formula he treated with such gravity might involve a pill for breaking through to the Great Vehicle stage?

Luo Chen did not dare to dwell on this line of thought.

The two women behind him did not discuss it further either.

Instead, under Tai Su Lingjun's deliberate change of subject, the conversation turned to Chu Yan's journey here.

"You said the Mountains and Seas Realm?"

"Yes. I noticed that when you looked at the Mountains and Seas Travel Palace, you didn't show any sign of nostalgia. Could it be that it was built too poorly and doesn't match your impression of the Mountains and Seas Realm?"

"It's not that it's poorly built. It's just that the records in the classics are somewhat different from the actual customs and scenery. Moreover, my memories of my homeland are already very blurry. It's hard for mere scenery to move me. If I could meet some old acquaintances from the Mountains and Seas Realm, that might touch me more deeply."

"Old acquaintances? I heard that the Mountains and Seas Realm once produced a peerless genius who, at the Sage stage, defied the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect and even severed the passage between the two realms. I wonder if you know that person, sister?"

Faced with this question, Chu Yan was stunned.

Through the beaded curtain, she subconsciously glanced at Luo Chen's back, then withdrew her gaze.

She said softly, "I have seen him."
