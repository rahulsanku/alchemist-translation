# Chapter 1206: Three Gates of Entry, Sharp Metal Physique

The entry tests of the Plain Spirit Sect consisted of three gates.

The first gate was the most complex and general.

They respectively tested spirit root constitution, cultivation realm, and true bone age.

Among these tests, aside from true bone age, none of the other items had specific requirements stated.

It seemed that as long as one possessed the aptitude for cultivation, they could join the Plain Spirit Sect.

As for bone age, it was linked to cultivation realm.

Those below Initiate stage had to be under sixty years old.

Those at Initiate stage or above had to be under one hundred and eighty years old.

If one exceeded these limits, joining the Plain Spirit Sect would be quite difficult.

Luo Chen could understand this point.

Although the Plain Spirit Sect had a policy of opening its mountain gates wide to select disciples based solely on will and not aptitude, practical considerations still had to be taken into account.

If someone hadn't reached Initiate stage by sixty, or hadn't formed a core by nearly two hundred, then basically they had no future in the path of immortality.

The Plain Spirit Sect couldn't possibly invest resources in such people.

As for the second gate, it was relatively much simpler.

The first gate could be summarized as the testing gate, while the second gate was the demonstration gate.

As an entry assembly open to rogue cultivators, most participants had struggled in the cultivation world for a long time and possessed at least one skill.

If one failed the previous tests but could impress the Plain Spirit Sect with their talents demonstrated in the second gate, they might still be accepted through special dispensation.

This point matched what Huang Yuanhua had once told Luo Chen.

The Plain Spirit Sect's main business was managing medicinal herb resources. If a rogue cultivator could master some spirit plant techniques in advance, their chances of being accepted would greatly increase.

As for the third gate…

Liu Muhan had been relatively relaxed before, but when she introduced the third gate, her expression couldn't help but turn serious.

"Previously, there was no third gate. But ever since our sect's ancestor, Grandmaster Taisu, broke through to the Void Refining stage, the sect's status has risen accordingly. As a result, it's inevitable that spies and agents from other sects will try to infiltrate our sect, especially in occasions like the Entry Assembly where dragons and fish mingle together. Therefore, this time—or rather, every time from now on—an additional gate of assessment will be added. Its name is the Heart-Questioning Gate, presided over exclusively by Sages, to screen out those with ill intentions."

Luo Chen nodded, indicating his understanding.

Small sects and minor factions were ignored and left alone.

But major powers always attracted attention, and having spies planted by other forces was a common occurrence.

Grandmaster Taisu had broken through to the Void Refining stage.

In the cultivation world, this could be described as "when one person attains the Dao, even their chickens and dogs ascend to heaven."

Her subordinate sect had accordingly risen from a Nascent Soul-level force to a Void Refining-level force, drawing a clear boundary with the countless ordinary sects.

But this period was quite sensitive.

If one wasn't careful, the sect might fall into internal chaos before it could even take advantage of the opportunity to develop.

To prevent problems before they occurred, the Plain Spirit Sect must have implemented many internal reforms. Establishing a third gate for the Entry Assembly was probably just the tip of the iceberg among those reforms!

Secretly catching a glimpse of Luo Chen's understanding expression, Liu Muhan inexplicably felt relieved in her heart.

"Before the assembly begins, special personnel will announce the details. If anyone is unwilling, they can inform us in advance and not participate in the Entry Assembly. After the assembly ends, our sect will send people to escort them away along with the failures."

Luo Chen nodded understandingly. "That's quite benevolent."

"Benevolent?" Liu Muhan asked rhetorically, then answered herself: "It's just trying to make fewer enemies."

Luo Chen glanced at Liu Muhan and found that this girl was quite clear-headed.

She didn't say what the consequences would be for those with ill intentions who were detected in the third gate, but it was certainly nothing good.

Giving advance warning to make some people back off also avoided offending the forces behind those people.

However, the effectiveness of such warnings probably wouldn't be great.

"When does the assembly begin?" Luo Chen asked.

Liu Muhan looked down at the square below, which was extremely wide and held over a thousand people.

She made a rough estimate and gave an answer.

"You might have to wait a while longer!"

As the two spoke, the flying boat also descended to that square.

Under the guidance of Plain Spirit Sect disciples, the rogue cultivators on the flying boat began to enter the square in an endless stream.

Liu Muhan cupped her hands toward Luo Chen. "I still have some matters to attend to. I'll see you later."

"Farewell, Senior Sister… Liu." Luo Chen returned the gesture.

Liu Muhan smiled and departed with a fragrant breeze.

Luo Chen followed her retreating figure to see where she was going.

From the square upward, there was a staircase paved with green jade, rising all the way into the depths of the clouds and mist.

Vaguely, golden light seemed to flicker at the end of the staircase.

There, Luo Chen sensed an extraordinary divine sense.

It seemed Liu Muhan was going to meet a Sage.

"Stop staring, Fellow Daoist Xuan Luo. No need to be so reluctant to part. With your age and aptitude, joining the Plain Spirit Sect is a sure thing. You'll have plenty of opportunities to see this Senior Sister Liu then!"

Beside him came Huang Yuanhua's teasing voice.

Luo Chen smiled but didn't explain much.

Following the flow of people, he also stepped onto the square. With a casual sweep, he roughly knew that there were already over a thousand people here.

"Are there so many rogue cultivators participating in the Entry Assembly?"

Huang Yuanhua waved his hand. "This is nothing! These people are probably like us—rogue cultivators who have rented caves in the immortal cities under the Plain Spirit Sect's jurisdiction in recent years. That's why they got the convenience of taking the immortal city's flying boat to arrive at the Plain Spirit Sect early. The real bottom-tier rogue cultivators are both poor and weak, and can't afford to rent caves in immortal cities. Therefore, they have to rush to the Plain Spirit Sect on their own, wait outside the mountain gate first, and then come in together. Wait a few more days; the Entry Assembly won't start for at least two or three days."

Luo Chen suddenly understood.

Without any particular urgency, he found a corner, crossed his arms over his chest, and closed his eyes to rest.

As he waited, flying boats entered and left the Plain Spirit Sect one after another, dropping off groups of rogue cultivators.

Streams of flying light were like threads all around, and the atmosphere of the upcoming Entry Assembly gradually grew lively.

...

Three days later.

On the white stone square, there was a sea of people.

Dense figures stretched as far as the eye could see, probably tens of thousands of rogue cultivators!

Even though no one was shouting loudly, the occasional murmurs gathered together were like a pot of boiling water, extremely noisy.

"Xuan Luo, do you think we can successfully join the Plain Spirit Sect?"

Huang Yuanhua's voice came again from beside him, his tone growing more worried.

Luo Chen opened his eyes, looked at him, and his brow furrowed almost imperceptibly.

The other party's mind was so unsettled; his Dao heart was really somewhat lacking.

He had only reached Initiate stage in his fifties, and his spirit root aptitude wasn't particularly good either.

This wasn't a good sign!

"Xuan Luo..."

"Stop nagging. Conserve your energy and face the upcoming tests in your best condition."

Luo Chen only gave this piece of advice, then closed his eyes to rest again.

Considering that within the Plain Spirit Sect, there were not only Divine Transformation great cultivators but also a Void Refining True Lord, Luo Chen didn't actively spread his divine sense. Instead, he tried his best to hide himself.

His specific understanding of this sect was also quite limited.

But there was one thing that had been bothering him these past three days.

"Why is the spiritual opportunity so thin?"

As a first-rate force capable of supporting Divine Transformation great cultivators and even a Void Refining True Lord, the sect naturally had a fifth-order spirit vein, and it must be a large-scale fifth-order vein.

Such a level of spirit vein would naturally produce spiritual opportunity, facilitating cultivators in comprehending Dao methods, sensing laws, and even achieving sudden enlightenment in an instant!

That was the purpose of spiritual opportunity.

But in Luo Chen's perception, he had hardly detected any spiritual opportunity.

Occasionally, when he briefly opened his Crimson-Golden Pupils for a quick scan, he gained little.

This made Luo Chen especially puzzled.

He had to suspect that either the Plain Spirit Sect had special arrangements to constrain the spiritual opportunity extremely well, or the situation in the Xuan Realm was vastly different from the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Regardless of which it was, it would greatly affect his future return to the Ascendant stage.

Just as Luo Chen was pondering, he suddenly sensed something and slowly opened his eyes.

Several dozen streaks of escaping light flew down from the jade staircase above, finally stopping in front of the square.

A middle-aged man with a plump figure and a gentle face stepped forward, his gaze warmly sweeping over the crowd.

Without him speaking, the noisy atmosphere within the venue gradually quieted down.

When there was complete silence, with only the sound of mountain wind rustling robes echoing, the middle-aged man nodded with satisfaction.

"I am Wan Jiuzhang, the senior disciple under Sage Yaoxi of Jade Spring Peak. I am presiding over this Entry Assembly on behalf of my master. Unlike previous times, this Entry Assembly has added one more gate, making a total of three gates of assessment. The specific details are as follows..."

From the mouth of this late-stage Adept great cultivator, the assessment rules of this Entry Assembly were clearly transmitted into everyone's ears, word by word.

Especially when it came to the third gate, the Heart-Questioning Gate, everyone's expression underwent subtle changes.

Heart-questioning?

Questioning what heart?

The Dao heart, the original heart, or an unclear ulterior motive?

For rogue cultivators, this was the most elusive test.

Even if one perfectly passed the first two gates, if they failed to gain the Plain Spirit Sect's approval in the third gate, did that mean all previous efforts were in vain?

Seeming to sense the anxiety of the rogue cultivators, Wan Jiuzhang spoke loudly: "The third gate also has its standards. If one can smoothly ascend the nine hundred and ninety-nine steps of green jade behind me, then they will be considered to have passed the Heart-Questioning Gate. This gate is personally presided over by a Sage, and the pressure varies from person to person, not limited by cultivation realm. Everyone need not fear being disadvantaged by different cultivation levels. Of course... if anyone has matters in their heart they don't wish to be pried into, they may also choose not to participate in this Entry Assembly."

At these words, everyone looked at each other.

Then came enthusiastic shouts.

"Then what are we waiting for?"

"Let's begin!"

"I consider my aptitude not weak, and my Dao heart is even more steadfast. I've only lacked guidance from experts and resource assistance. Since it's not limited by cultivation realm, I'm sure I can ascend the nine hundred and ninety-nine steps of green jade."

"Let's pass the first gate first!"

"Hurry up!"

Faced with this lively scene, Wan Jiuzhang couldn't help but stroke his beard and smile.

He cupped his hands toward the group of Plain Spirit Sect cultivators beside him. "I'll trouble all you junior brothers and sisters next."

"Understood!"

The crowd responded, then descended to a row of platforms left open on the square. Around them, many Initiate-stage cultivators' flying lights also landed among them.

Immediately, the group of cultivators activated their techniques together, and flowing light flickered on the platforms.

Among them were stone gates, jade plates, and even a precious mirror hanging high.

These were the treasures needed for the first gate's tests.

Next, the Plain Spirit Sect cultivators began calling names based on the rogue cultivator registers collected from various places earlier.

Those called would go up to the platform for the first gate's tests.

"Ji Yue of Tanzhou!"

"Present!"

"Jiang Wenda of Danshui!"

"Present!"

"Xuan Luo of Taisu!"

Luo Chen was startled for a moment, then smiled and responded, calmly walking up.

Behind him, a group of rogue cultivators who had come together from Taisu Immortal City had already realized something.

These people called up in the first batch were clearly the better seedlings from various regions that the Plain Spirit Sect valued more. That's why they received priority testing, avoiding the long waiting time.

However, the first one called from the Taisu Immortal City region was Xuan Luo, not Tang Yuan, who had considerable connections with the Plain Spirit Sect, nor Xing Beichen, who had a great reputation among rogue cultivators. Instead, it was the obscure Xuan Luo, who had only recently gained some fame. This was somewhat unexpected.

"Perhaps it's because of his age!"

Everyone could only think this way.

After all, Luo Chen's age was truly too young, and his external appearance was indeed that of a young man, even carrying a bit of "youthfulness."

When Luo Chen arrived at the platform, under the guidance of the Plain Spirit Sect cultivators, he first stepped through that stone gate.

Instantly, rippling light shimmered, and spiritual energy surged.

Although it was fleeting, the stone gate had already left marks.

"Initiate stage, early phase, stable realm." Several Initiate-stage cultivators responsible for the testing exchanged glances and nodded in approval.

"Next is testing your age. To prevent inaccuracy, we will mainly test bone age."

One of the Initiate-stage cultivators briefly explained to Luo Chen, then held up a precious mirror aimed at him.

"Alright!"

Luo Chen nodded and let the mirror light shine on his body.

But the mirror light lingered outside, unable to penetrate.

The Initiate-stage cultivator frowned and reminded him: "Don't be nervous. Don't use magical power to resist. Relax your mind."

Luo Chen was already relaxed enough. He knew the mirror light couldn't penetrate his body for another reason.

Then, with a thought, he dispersed his magical power and even retracted all his astral qi within.

Not only that, he also released a slight divine sense to sweep over the crowd.

In a daze, the several cultivators saw the mirror light sweep over Luo Chen, penetrating flesh and blood, landing on the bones within.

"Bone age: twenty-three!"

The several cultivators exchanged glances, clearly showing expressions of pleasant surprise.

It was indeed consistent with the content submitted in the register.

This rogue cultivator had actually cultivated to the Initiate stage at around twenty years of age.

Even within immortal sects, this was a talent found once in ten thousand!

Then, his spirit root aptitude...

"Finally, test the spirit root constitution!"

With anticipation, the group indicated for Luo Chen to place his hand on that jade plate.

Instantly, golden light blazed brightly!

Not only that, but within it also vaguely permeated a fiery red glow.

Seeing the rich golden light, the crowd was first delighted.

"Sure enough, it's a heavenly spirit root, metal attribute. No wonder he broke through to Initiate stage at twenty!"

"No?"

After that fiery red glow flickered out, the crowd was stunned again.

"Why is there also a fire spirit root?"

The exclamations on this platform had already attracted the attention of many rogue cultivators in the square who were craning their necks to watch.

Over twenty years old, Initiate stage early phase, dual spirit root aptitude—any two of these three points combined would be an enviable genius, but all three together were too incongruous.

Because dual spirit root aptitude couldn't possibly allow one to break through to Initiate stage at such a young age!

A streak of escaping light landed on Luo Chen's platform.

It was the earlier late-stage Adept great cultivator.

Wan Jiuzhang nodded to Luo Chen, then unceremoniously released his divine sense, sweeping it over Luo Chen's entire body.

Gradually, surprise appeared in his eyes.

"Sharp Metal Physique!"
