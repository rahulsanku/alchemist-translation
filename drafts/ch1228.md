# Chapter 1228: Meeting the Split Soul Again, Riverbed Daoist Temple

Gray stone pillars stood tall upon the earth.

Scattered buildings were distributed in all directions.

This place was the Gray Triangle!

Gazing at those ancient stone pillars that seemed utterly out of place among the surrounding structures, a wealth of information about this location surfaced in Luo Chen's mind.

Strictly speaking, the Gray Triangle had not originally been a gathering place for loose cultivators, nor a buffer zone between the three great Ascendant sects.

This place had once been the central territory of a Void Refining power. But due to an internal conflict, that power had split into three. This area, damaged by the battles, had gradually become a buffer zone between the three sects.

The Great Soul Sect!

Yes, the three surrounding powers—whether the Golden Bell Sect or the Purple Flame Mountain—were all once parts of the former Great Soul Sect.

The reason for the split could be traced back ten thousand years.

At that time, the Heavenly Origin Dao Sect had opened up the Mountains and Seas Realm. Not only had several great holy sects participated, but they had also summoned a large number of other powers.

Among them was the Great Soul Sect!

However, during the process of opening the Mountains and Seas Realm, the Great Soul Sect had suffered heavy losses, leading to a gap in talent.

After the older generation passed away, the Great Soul Sect began its internal strife, eventually splitting into three.

The Gray Triangle became the place that divided the three sects.

This could be seen from those ancient stone pillars.

Although these stone pillars no longer contained much power, Luo Chen could tell from the broken formation patterns around them and their distribution that they had once been incredibly powerful.

They could not have been set up by loose cultivators.

It was more likely that they were remnants left behind after the Great Soul Sect split into three sects.

Luo Chen felt a pang of melancholy at this.

Was there truly any power in this world that could last forever?

Strong as the Great Soul Sect had been—at its peak, comparable to the Five Elements Divine Sect, a top-tier existence only a step away from the Nine Great Holy Sects—it had ended up in its current fallen state.

In comparison, the legacy he had left behind in the Mountains and Seas Realm had even shallower foundations. He wondered how it was faring after several hundred years.

Amidst his reflections, Luo Chen entered the Gray Triangle. Following the map Fan Wen had given him, he arrived at a shop in a secluded alleyway.

"Customer, take a look at whatever you want to buy," said an old man with graying hair, lying on a rocking chair with his eyes half-open.

Luo Chen swept his gaze over the place, then, without a word, took out a token.

The token was pure white, with wisps of green smoke rising from it.

The old man's eyes snapped open. "An Emerald Smoke Token. Fellow Daoist, are you from the Verdant Spirit Sect?"

Luo Chen nodded and asked directly, "When will the Gray Trace Trading Fair be held?"

The so-called Gray Trace Trading Fair was the loose cultivator trading fair mentioned earlier.

It was a large-scale, underground trading event involving cultivators of various realms.

Unable to discern Luo Chen's cultivation level, the old man cautiously replied, "Fellow Daoist has arrived at just the right time. The Gray Trace Trading Fair is held once every ten years, and it will be held in a few days. Do you wish to participate?"

Luo Chen grunted in affirmation.

The old man then took out an invitation and handed it to Luo Chen.

"When the trading fair begins, you can enter with this invitation."

Luo Chen took the invitation, put it away without even glancing at it, and said to the old man, "Keep an eye out for a loose cultivator with a black mole at the corner of his mouth and a gloomy look in his eyes. If you get any news, inform me directly."

The old man nodded first, then smiled bitterly. "I can certainly keep an eye out, but loose cultivators often disguise themselves. It will be difficult to find any leads."

"It doesn't matter. Just do as I say."

With those words, Luo Chen turned and left the small shop.

The old man watched his retreating figure and clicked his tongue in admiration.

"Hasn't the Verdant Spirit Sect lost interest in the Gray Triangle? Why have they sent a disciple here again?"

After leaving the shop, Luo Chen began to stroll around the Gray Triangle.

His powerful divine sense, in a concealed manner, surveyed the surroundings.

Under his inspection, the situation here was clear at a glance.

The overall area was not large, only a few dozen li in radius.

However, the number of powerful loose cultivators lurking within was considerable.

There were over a dozen Adept cultivators alone, and even a Sage hidden in the shadows.

They all relied on a fourth-order spirit vein hidden deep underground for their cultivation.

And upon detecting that fourth-order spirit vein, Luo Chen couldn't help but frown.

"A severed vein?"

Yes, under his divine sense's probing, that fourth-order spirit vein was clearly a severed earth vein.

Both the northern and southern ends, as well as the extending branch veins, had been artificially cut off.

As a result, it was only a matter of time before its grade dropped.

Luo Chen extended his divine sense towards the source and roughly found the clue.

It was most likely the three Ascendant sects that had actively severed it, to prevent the sect's spiritual energy from leaking into the Gray Triangle.

If this continued, within a hundred years at most, the Gray Triangle, this sacred ground for loose cultivators, would decline.

Luo Chen didn't care about the future, but for now, this wasn't good news for him either.

"With this level of spiritual energy concentration, it's not suitable for me to break through to the Sage stage quickly. After I perfunctorily deal with this mission, I'll find another high-grade spirit vein."

...

Three days later.

Relying on that invitation, Luo Chen entered an underground cavern.

Inside, figures were numerous, numbering over a thousand.

This showed how large the cavern was.

And it was said that there were many such caverns around the Gray Triangle.

Luo Chen wasn't very interested in this trading fair; it was just going through the motions.

But unexpectedly, he did make an unexpected gain.

Looking at the bone fragment he had exchanged for a high price in spirit stones, he was somewhat surprised.

"The Soul-Splitting Art!"

Yes, the technique recorded on this bone fragment was precisely the soul-refining technique he had primarily cultivated in the Mountains and Seas Realm.

Although it was only the introductory chapter, explaining how to cultivate a single divided soul.

Only one.

But its content was actually able to connect with the authentic version of the *Soul-Splitting Art*!

Luo Chen's *Soul-Splitting Art* originated from Han Zhan, which was part of the Falling Cloud Sect's Three Arts and Two Techniques.

However, he had obtained it from the fragmented memories of Han Zhan's divine soul, so it was incomplete. It only contained how to cultivate the divided soul and deepen the connection between the main soul and the divided soul, but it lacked the most basic method of splitting the main soul and the later method of accelerating the growth of the divided soul.

For this reason, Luo Chen had later spent a lot of effort to deduce the splitting method on his own and used pills to assist in strengthening the divided soul.

But that was ultimately not the original content.

And this Foundation Establishment chapter clearly recorded the most basic splitting method!

For Luo Chen now, this kind of technique was naturally useless, but it could clearly deduce one thing.

The *Soul-Splitting Art* originated from the Xuan Realm, and it was more likely to have come from the Great Soul Sect near the Gray Triangle!

Because this bone fragment was put up for sale by a female cultivator who had disguised her identity but was actually from the Golden Bell Sect.

The Golden Bell Sect was once a part of the Great Soul Sect.

If he pushed this deduction further, another conjecture surfaced.

That was, within the Great Soul Sect, there must be a complete version of the *Soul-Splitting Art*, especially the true method for accelerating the growth of the divided soul!

This conjecture had traces to follow.

Many years ago, Luo Chen had believed that the *Soul-Splitting Art* was not a self-created technique of the Falling Cloud Sect. The evidence was that the attribute panel showed this technique was once as high as seventh-order.

Seventh-order corresponded to the Body Integration stage!

And according to rumors, the cultivator who founded the Falling Cloud Sect had obtained the legacy of an ancient cultivator's cave dwelling, which allowed him to cultivate successfully and establish his own sect.

According to Xuan Realm records, the Great Soul Sect had participated in opening the Mountains and Seas Realm.

That is to say, the ancient cultivator who left behind the technique was very likely a cultivator sent down by the Great Soul Sect.

At this thought, Luo Chen couldn't help but feel a stirring in his heart.

The complete *Soul-Splitting Art* not only had the method for strengthening the divided soul, but its grade was also as high as seventh-order. It might even be helpful for his future breakthrough to the Body Integration stage.

"Should I go to the Great Soul Sect to secretly investigate?"

Luo Chen couldn't help but ponder the feasibility of this plan.

The Great Soul Sect was now long in decline. Not only had it split into three, but the number of Ascendants within the sect was pitifully small.

With his strength, he shouldn't be discovered.

Moreover, it was possible that the splintered Golden Bell Sect and Purple Flame Mountain also possessed the complete *Soul-Splitting Art*.

Just as Luo Chen was pondering, the corner of his eye suddenly caught something.

A figure who had just exchanged for some resources was warily withdrawing from the underground cavern.

"What a coincidence!"

Luo Chen smiled slightly, rose unhurriedly, and also left the underground cavern where the trading fair was being held.

...

Under the night sky, the moonlight was strong.

A figure emerged from the ground and warily looked behind him.

Seeing no one following, he couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief.

This kind of loose cultivator trading fair with no guarantees was the most dangerous, especially for those who had traded for good things.

Fortunately, he was cautious enough. Apart from a mistake the first time, he hadn't had any problems in all the subsequent times, and he could always buy the resources he wanted.

Thinking of the medicinal herbs in his storage pouch, the man in black couldn't help but reveal a smile, even the black mole at the corner of his mouth trembling slightly.

"With this thing, I have a bit more confidence in breaking through to the Adept stage... Who's there?"

Suddenly, his gaze froze.

On the street ahead, a figure was standing quietly, as if waiting for someone.

He instinctively wanted to take out his magical artifact, but when his eyes met those of the figure, which were like whirlpools, he was completely stunned.

Luo Chen smiled. The Mirror Flower Water Moon was still so easy to use!

"Name?"

"Huang Wei."

"Background and origins?"

"A discarded disciple of the Purple Flame Mountain, now a loose cultivator."

"Twenty-seven years ago, did you sell a Phoenix Eye Black Obsidian Moss?"

"Yes."

"Where did you get it?"

"One hundred and twenty li west of Emerald Smoke Mountain, at the place where the great river surges. Below it is an underground cavern. Inside is an ancient Daoist temple, surrounded by Phoenix Eye Black Obsidian Moss..."

...

In a daze, Huang Wei seemed to have experienced a long time.

But when he opened his eyes, the shadow cast by the moonlight hadn't changed at all, indicating that only an instant had passed.

He looked around in terror, but found nothing.

Suddenly, he felt something heavy in his hand.

He looked down and saw a jade bottle.

Hesitantly opening the jade bottle, Huang Wei was stunned.

"Nine Seas Qi-Transforming Pill!"

Immediately, a surge of ecstasy washed over his face.

This was precisely the famous pill for forming a core in the Youxuan Continent. It could forcibly fuse the spiritual liquid in the nine qi seas of an Initiate cultivator in an instant, helping them break through to the Adept stage.

He had done nothing, and yet he had obtained such a precious pill?

No!

Perhaps he hadn't done nothing.

Thinking of the dazed state he had been in just now, Huang Wei couldn't help but swallow his saliva.

That was clearly a state where his divine soul had been intimidated, even drawn into an illusion, and he had poured out his heart without any reservation.

To be able to do this to an Initiate True Cultivator like him, the other party was at least a mid-to-late stage Adept, or even a Sage.

But what exactly had he said?

Huang Wei didn't dare to think too much. He tightly gripped the jade bottle and hastily fled.

...

West of Emerald Smoke Mountain, a great river surged past. Its name was the Emerald Stone River.

By the riverbank, chaotic rocks stood like forests.

Under the night sky, they resembled ghosts and monsters, brandishing their claws and teeth.

A figure drifted over and quietly gazed at this stretch of river.

It was Luo Chen, who had just obtained the specific information from the loose cultivator Huang Wei.

That loose cultivator was the very person who had sold the Phoenix Eye Black Obsidian Moss at the trading fair back then. Because of his excessive caution, the Verdant Spirit Sect cultivator who had been tasked with the mission had never been able to find him.

Luo Chen, not paying attention, had almost missed him.

Fortunately, his spiritual eyes had seen through the other party's disguise.

In fact, the other party's disguise was not very sophisticated. He didn't even have the methods to conceal his qi or hide his form, let alone the technique to restrain his spiritual sense.

Yet, he had used the simplest disguise technique—covering his characteristic black mole with a full beard and adjusting the corners of his eyes and the bridge of his nose with powder—making it very easy to overlook.

Sometimes, for high-level cultivators, the simpler the concealment method, the more effective it was.

Luo Chen sighed at his caution. And because the other party had once been a loose cultivator like himself, after obtaining the information, he not only spared his life but also gifted him a Nine Seas Qi-Transforming Pill that he had refined after coming to the Xuan Realm.

Consider it a repayment to that cautious version of himself from back then.

Now, standing by the Emerald Stone River, Luo Chen spread out his divine sense and carefully explored.

A moment later, his expression suddenly moved.

Luo Chen controlled his escape light and flew upstream.

Soon, he saw a group of people on the river.

He couldn't help but stroke his chin. "They're here too?"

As he appeared, the six figures on the river also reacted, warily looking at him.

Before Luo Chen could speak, one of them suddenly let out a surprised cry.

"Is that Xuan Luo?"

Luo Chen smiled and descended to stand opposite the six, treading on the clear waves. "Senior Sister Mu Hanyan, long time no see."

Liu Muhan was overjoyed. "It has been a long time. I didn't expect to meet you here..."

As she spoke, she suddenly froze.

"Xuan Luo, your cultivation level?"

She didn't mention it, but the others were also shocked.

Especially another female cultivator traveling with her, whose eyes widened as she blurted out, "Mid-stage Adept?"

All the Verdant Spirit Sect disciples knew that the sect had produced a peerless genius. Not only was he talented in the Dao of alchemy, but he had also formed his core at the age of sixty.

But it had only been a little over ten years since Luo Chen had formed his core.

And now, the cultivation level he was revealing was already mid-stage Adept.

Such progress was truly unimaginable and astonishing!

Luo Chen nodded, then quickly changed the subject.

"What are you all doing here?"

Liu Muhan suppressed the shock in her heart and explained, "We accepted a mission from the Spiritual Medicine Hall to investigate the reduction in medicinal herb production on Emerald Smoke Mountain. We only recently found a clue."

A mission from the Spiritual Medicine Hall? A reduction in herb production?

Luo Chen was taken aback, as if remembering something.

He did recall this matter. He had stumbled upon it once when he went to the Spiritual Medicine Hall to buy a Five Elements Divine Pill.

He hadn't expected that his fellow disciple, Liu Muhan, had taken this mission and that he would run into her.

Liu Muhan pointed down at the great river.

"For some reason, the spiritual energy in the Emerald Smoke Mountain and even the Gray Triangle region is distributed unevenly. In particular, the concentration of wood-attributed spiritual energy is extremely low. Based on past clues, we found that this situation has persisted for over two hundred years, steadily drawing away wood-attributed spiritual energy. Therefore, the reduction in herb production has been changing at a subtle rate, and no one noticed. After many days of investigation, we discovered that the direction of the spiritual energy loss is precisely this area. Now, we are searching for the true source."

As she spoke, Luo Chen had already secretly extended his divine sense.

Sure enough!

The wood-attributed spiritual energy of this heaven and earth was flowing towards their area with an undeniable trend.

But strangely, after so many years of wood-attributed spiritual energy accumulation, there was no place below with a particularly rich concentration of spiritual energy.

"How peculiar!"

Thinking this, a flying sword had already appeared in Luo Chen's hand.

"No need to search. The source should be below."

As he spoke, the flying sword suddenly grew larger and thicker, becoming a hundred-zhang giant sword.

With a casual slash, the surging great river was cut in two. Amidst the towering waves, an ancient Daoist temple at the bottom of the river leaped into view.
