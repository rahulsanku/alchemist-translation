# Chapter 1216: Heart-Mind Transformation, Circulation Pill Completed

At the foot of the mountain, Luo Chen stood in the Step Pill Courtyard, silently watching the bustling activity within.

After a long while, things finally quieted down, and figures began to emerge one after another.

As they passed by Luo Chen, none seemed to notice him.

Until he spoke. "Lü Su, what are you all doing?"

Lü Su, who was locking the door, paused for a moment, then naturally broke into a smile.

"So it's Xuán Luózi!"

He pointed at the several warehouses inside the courtyard and explained, "It's just that recently, the masters up on the mountain have needed more and more medicinal herbs for alchemy. Our Step Pill Courtyard has to handle the logistics. Everything inside was just received from the Spirit Herb Courtyard."

"Oh."

Luo Chen gave a perfunctory reply and turned to leave, just as before.

Lü Su watched his retreating figure, somewhat puzzled. Why would the other ask such an obvious question?

Leaving Wonderful Pill Peak, Luo Chen didn't stop.

He followed the forest path, crossing by the lakeshore and stream bank.

Under the night sky, the Spirit Vegetation Sect had its own unique charm.

The streaks of light from cultivators flying past were like shooting stars—brilliant and eye-catching.

Suddenly, one streak of light stopped. A figure descended, put away a handkerchief-shaped flying treasure, and enthusiastically greeted him.

"Senior Brother Xuán Luózi!"

Luo Chen looked at the newcomer. It was Feng Zhiyan, who had entered the sect alongside him.

Several years had passed, and her cultivation had changed.

"Junior Sister Feng, you've reached Initiation!"

Feng Zhiyan showed a genuine smile. "I owe it all to the help of the senior brothers and sisters on Jade Spring Peak. I barely managed to form my foundation. And you, Senior Brother? What are you doing out here so late?"

Luo Chen calmly pointed toward his cave dwelling. "Going home."

"Why not fly? Walking like this will take until dawn." Feng Zhiyan was very curious, then seemed to guess something and said with a grin, "Senior Brother, are you savoring the life of a mortal, measuring the earth with your feet, engaging in a higher level of cultivation?"

Luo Chen was speechless but didn't refute her, because he himself didn't know what was going on with him.

He just looked at her with some confusion. The trace of exhaustion on her face was impossible to hide.

"Where are you coming back from?"

Feng Zhiyan perked up. "Cultivation has gotten harder after Initiation. I can't rely on myself alone. This morning, I went to the Scripture Transmission Hall to attend a lecture, and this afternoon, I took a task from the External Affairs Hall—going to the East Brilliance Peak Herb Garden to do some pest removal."

Luo Chen nodded. "You're keeping yourself quite busy."

"It's nothing." Feng Zhiyan waved her hand, her eyes practically shining. "In another thirty years, it'll be time for the sect's grand competition. I want to take on as many tasks as I can before then, train myself hard, and when the time comes, I'll make a name for myself and bring glory to our Jade Spring Peak!"

Luo Chen gave a vague "oh." The sect grand competition—he thought he'd heard someone mention it.

But the Alchemy Institute had been busy researching new pills in recent years, so he hadn't paid much attention.

"Senior Brother, are you going to participate?" Feng Zhiyan asked expectantly.

"Me?" Luo Chen smiled but didn't give an answer.

They chatted a little longer, then parted ways.

After she left, Luo Chen stood still for a long time. The confusion in his eyes lingered, but gradually, a hint of clarity began to emerge.

He finally understood why his Dao heart—which should have been as still as an ancient well—would occasionally stir and ripple.

This time, it wasn't the pressure of a powerful enemy, nor some earth-shattering event.

It was simply that, while living incognito in the Spirit Vegetation Sect, he had come to know a group of "living, breathing" people.

It had been a long time since he'd felt this way.

Was it after reaching the Sage stage? Or after the Ascendant stage?

Luo Chen didn't know. He only knew that when he first entered the world of immortal cultivation, everyone and everything around him was vivid, full of life.

But the higher his cultivation level rose, the fewer people and things could move his heart.

The so-called "ants" were born in that stage.

Toward those whose cultivation was inferior to his, Luo Chen often regarded them with an indifferent mindset.

The journey to the Guixu had amplified this mentality to the extreme.

Whether it was Jing Lan, who had saved his life, or Xiang Fei, who had fought him before becoming friends and spent a hundred years together, or anyone else—

In Luo Chen's eyes, they were all just symbols.

He had never truly opened his heart to these people. He had always interacted with them as an observer.

This state had reached its peak when the entire Guixu was destroyed!

The annihilation of countless living beings had left him unmoved.

Even after arriving in the Xuan Realm, this state persisted.

So even though he had met many people in the Spirit Vegetation Sect, Luo Chen remained indifferent, still regarding them as "ants."

In Buddhist terms, this could be explained as a mind of discrimination.

In Daoist terms, it might be like a single leaf blocking the view.

It wasn't until today—the idle chatter with Little Poison Lord, Senior Brother Xiao's stubbornness, Ning Fu's teasing—that they inadvertently stirred his long-sealed Dao heart.

Only then did he suddenly realize that he might have taken a wrong path, or perhaps he was at some important bottleneck in his state of mind.

He regarded others as ants, but in the eyes of those at higher realms, wasn't he also an ant?

But in truth, although people may differ in cultivation, status, wealth, gender, or personality, at their core, they are all still human.

Expanding this view, all things in the world should be seen the same way.

Mountains are still mountains, water is still water. The essence of all living beings has no distinction.

The absence of a self-image should not mean only the self-image, but also the absence of the image of others, and the absence of the image of all beings!

After realizing this, the mysterious aura around Luo Chen grew even denser.

And deep within his buried sea of consciousness, the enormous divine soul seemed to have been bathed in spring rain, becoming more rounded and flawless.

Luo Chen did not stop this change.

He vaguely sensed that if he continued living with this state of mind, his heart-mind would reach a higher level, and even his divine soul would benefit.

"Although I haven't detected much spiritual opportunity in the Spirit Vegetation Sect, which is disappointing, just this change in heart-mind alone might make this journey worthwhile."

Luo Chen closed his eyes and lay down by the lakeshore.

When he opened them again, it was dawn, and the sun was just rising.

...

A few days later.

In the Xu Jing Tian Courtyard of Wonderful Pill Peak, a cry of surprise erupted.

"It moved, it moved, it's moving!"

Five or six figures rushed over upon hearing the sound.

Then they saw the faint qi whirlpools on Little Poison Lord's body.

It was the scene of the outside world's spiritual qi being absorbed into his body!

And throughout this process, Little Poison Lord had not actively circulated his cultivation technique, nor did he show any signs of pain. He wasn't even in a state of cultivation.

But the spiritual power within his body had already begun cycling through the meridians according to his original technique's path, one cycle after another.

This was exactly the description of the Circulation Qi-Transporting Pill taking effect!

Seeing this, everyone cheered in unison.

They had succeeded!

About half an incense stick later, the whirlpools suddenly disappeared.

Little Poison Lord touched his body, somewhat at a loss.

"That's it?"

The others were also caught off guard.

"Did it fail after all?"

But He Jun stepped forward.

"No, what just happened was exactly how the third-grade Circulation Qi-Transporting Pill is described in the formula. It was just a bit short in duration. That doesn't mean failure—it just means the medicinal power wasn't enough."

With that, he instinctively looked at Zhu Xiao, who was in charge of alchemy, and Ning Fu, who was in charge of studying the pharmacology.

"What was different about this batch compared to before?"

Zhu Xiao first shook his head. "Nothing different!"

But then he frowned. "It seems Ning Fu replaced one of the auxiliary ingredients in the formula?"

Ning Fu bit her lip and turned her gaze to the silent Luo Chen.

"One auxiliary ingredient was replaced. I took Junior Brother Xuán Luózi's suggestion and swapped the Flame Spirit Flower for Bodhi Worm."

Bodhi Worm?

Everyone knew this herb.

It looked like a silkworm but was actually a grass root, buried deep underground until maturity.

Its nature was gentle, and it was one of the main ingredients used to refine Soul-Calming Incense.

In the Spirit Vegetation Sect, several herb gardens were cultivating large quantities of it.

They all followed Ning Fu's gaze to Luo Chen.

Luo Chen sighed inwardly. He hadn't expected that a casual remark, based purely on intuition, would lead them to the best auxiliary ingredient for the Circulation Qi-Transporting Pill.

Facing everyone's eyes, he shrugged.

"What are you waiting for? Since we've found the right direction, let's conduct detailed research on everything—from the ratio of ingredients, to fire control, to the timing of adding and removing the pill. Maybe soon, we'll have a finished product and can start mass-producing this pill."

No one hesitated any longer. They all clenched their fists and threw themselves into the subsequent experiments with full passion.

The entire Xu Jing Tian Courtyard became a hive of activity.

Even in the dead of night, the lights were still blazing.

Everyone realized what this progress meant.

If they truly succeeded, even if they couldn't become founders of a lineage or advance to Ascendant in the future, they would still leave a brilliant mark in the history of the Spirit Vegetation Sect.

...

On Spirit Pill Peak, separated from Wonderful Pill Peak by just one mountain.

Gongsun Mao's residence was here.

He had long abandoned his cave dwelling within the sect and had lived permanently in the Alchemy Institute for many years.

But compared to the once-spirited Master Gongsun, he was now aged beyond measure, his decrepit appearance impossible to hide.

In recent days, his qi and blood had been failing, and he had become quite depressed.

Especially after reading the trial pill report sent over from Wonderful Pill Peak, his mood had sunk to the lowest point.

"Is it still not working?"

"Before my great limit arrives, will I never see the hope of mass-producing the Circulation Qi-Transporting Pill?"

Just as he closed the last page of the report, hurried footsteps came from outside the door.

Soon, his attendant pill boy led a middle-aged man inside.

"So it's He Jun. Do you have any progress over there?"

Facing Gongsun Mao's hopeless inquiry, He Jun took a deep breath, held out a jade box with both hands, and presented it.

"Master, it's done!"

Gongsun Mao's eyes flew wide open. The usually murky gaze now held a glint of sharp light.

His trembling hands took the jade box.

Half an hour later, invitations began to be sent out from Spirit Pill Peak.

...

Inside the Xu Jing Tian Courtyard.

The members of He Jun's group, after resting for a few days, gathered together once more.

The topic of discussion was only one.

"Who will go?"

Master Gongsun was about to hold a "Pill Tasting Gathering" on Spirit Pill Peak, inviting many important figures.

Not only alchemists from the two peaks of Spirit and Wonderful, but even fourth-grade alchemists from Alchemy Emperor Mountain would come.

Furthermore, the hall masters and courtyard masters of the Three Halls and Five Courtyards were also on the invitation list.

Some Sage-level powerhouses within the sect who were interested in the Dao of alchemy would also attend.

For the humble Wonderful Pill Peak, this might be a first in history!

But no one would question whether Wonderful Pill Peak deserved such an honor.

If the Circulation Qi-Transporting Pill could truly be mass-produced, it would not only mean that the Spirit Vegetation Sect would gain a signature pill, but also that the cultivation levels of the sect's lower-level disciples would receive a massive boost.

In the future, more high-level powerhouses would be born!

At this pill-tasting gathering, Master Gongsun had specifically instructed that He Jun's group must send one alchemist to perform a complete pill refinement from start to finish in front of everyone.

This would be a demonstration of whether the Circulation Qi-Transporting Pill could be mass-produced.

For the members of He Jun's group, this was also a golden opportunity to make a name for themselves.

But there was only one slot!

"Naturally, it should be Senior Brother Zhu Xiao. After all, he's been in charge of the experimental refinement work all these years," Luo Chen said.

The others had initially had their own ideas, but when Luo Chen said this, they gave up their thoughts.

However, this time, Zhu Xiao was silent for a long time.

Finally, he smiled and spoke. "Forget it. I'll pass. What if I fail on the spot? That would be embarrassing."

He Jun frowned. "Are you afraid?"

Zhu Xiao shrugged. "You could say that. This pill-tasting gathering is too important. I don't want to mess it up. And you all know my success rate isn't that high. I've wasted a lot of herbs. I bet Senior Brother He Jun, every time you go to get the herbs, you get complaints too."

He Jun opened his mouth but didn't deny it.

Zhu Xiao suddenly turned his gaze to Luo Chen.

"You go, Xuán Luózi!"

"Me?"

Luo Chen was slightly surprised. It wasn't that he lacked confidence, but he hadn't expected the other to voluntarily give up such a chance to shine.

"Yes, you." Zhu Xiao said with a calm expression. "The theoretical direction of our group was proposed by you. The most important auxiliary ingredient was found by you. Even the first Circulation Qi-Transporting Pill that met the standard was refined by you. If you don't go, who will?"

This time, even the others were convinced.

Just as Zhu Xiao had said, although Luo Chen had been in the group for the shortest time—barely a year in total—

His contributions had been the greatest.

Moreover, he was the youngest and had the highest talent. If he could successfully refine a pill that might change the course of the Spirit Vegetation Sect's history in a highly publicized setting, it would be the perfect rise of a genius alchemist!

Years later, the disciples of the Spirit Vegetation Sect would surely remember this pill-tasting gathering!

"Yes, let Xuán Luózi go!"

"My master's master is coming. I definitely don't dare to go on stage."

"Besides, the pills Xuán Luózi refines taste better. It should be you."

"Maybe if you perform well, the senior elders will give you lots of rewards. Don't hog them all, okay?"

"Junior Brother Xuán Luózi, you're not scared, are you? Your senior sister supports you the most."

Amid the enthusiastic voices, He Jun coughed heavily.

He looked at Luo Chen with a gentle gaze.

He didn't ask if he had confidence, nor did he worry about his alchemy skills.

He simply spoke two words.

"You go!"

At that moment, Luo Chen naturally felt a surge of emotion.

In a thousand years of cultivation, he had seen too much treachery, betrayal, and enmity—people fighting to the death over the slightest benefit.

Now, with such a great benefit before them, these "senior brothers and sisters" whom he had only known for a short time could set aside their own interests and jointly lift him onto a stage that others would consider immensely important.

This feeling was like the early days of the Luo Tian Sect's founding, when a group of friends had struggled together with him.

He took a deep breath and nodded firmly.

"I'll go. I won't let everyone down!"
