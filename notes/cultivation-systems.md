# Cultivation Systems Reference

---

## Mountains and Seas Realm — Qi Cultivation

Stage names are shared with the Profound Realm. The Mountains and Seas Realm's ceiling is **Ascendant** — above that, cultivators must ascend to a higher realm.

| Stage | Alt. Name | Chinese | Sub-stages | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Initiate | Foundation Establishment | 筑基 | — | Entry-level cultivation |
| Adept | Golden Core | 金丹 | Stages 1–9 (early/mid/late) | "Core Formation" in some contexts |
| Sage | Nascent Soul | 元婴 | Early / mid / late; up to 9th layer | Title: True Person (真人) at higher layers |
| **Ascendant** | Divine Transformation | **化神** | Early / mid / late | Peak of the Mountains and Seas Realm. Grants qualifications to ascend to the Profound Realm. Lifespan: ~2,000 years |

> **Translation note:** The drafts use both "Ascendant" and "Divine Transformation" for 化神. Standardise to **Divine Transformation** to match the Profound Realm usage (same Chinese term).

## Mountains and Seas Realm — Body Cultivation

Parallel path. The endpoint (Human Immortal) is explicitly stated to be equivalent in prestige and power to Ascendant on the qi path.

| Tier | Stage Name | Chinese | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | (unnamed) | 炼体一阶 | — |
| 2nd | (unnamed) | 炼体二阶 | — |
| 3rd | Furnace Realm | 炉炼境 | Open and polish all acupoints with astral qi |
| 4th | Leakless Realm | 无漏境 | Also called Leakless Golden Body (无漏金身) |
| 5th | **Human Immortal** | **人仙境** | Early / mid / late. "Indestructible divine form, breaks the void" (见神不坏打破虚空). The endpoint of body cultivation in this realm. |
| Beyond | Destruction Realm | 破灭境 | "Martial deity perfected — the will survives even if the body is destroyed." Extremely rare. |

> **Key equivalence (stated explicitly in novel):** "An Ascendant grandmaster possesses the qualifications to ascend and become an immortal, no different from the endpoint of our body refinement — the Human Immortal realm."

> **Martial Human Immortal** (武道人仙) — the novel's specific term for martial-path cultivators who reached the Human Immortal stage.

## Profound Realm (Xuan Realm) — Qi Cultivation

The Profound Realm has the same lower stages as the Mountains and Seas Realm, but continues further above Divine Transformation. Cultivators who ascend from the Mountains and Seas Realm enter at the Divine Transformation tier.

| Stage | Alt. Name | Chinese | Title | Lifespan | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Initiate | Foundation Establishment | 筑基 | — | — | |
| Adept | Golden Core | 金丹 | — | — | Stages 1–9 |
| Sage | Nascent Soul | 元婴 | Sage (真人 at higher layers) | ~1,000 years | Early / mid / late, up to 9th layer |
| Divine Transformation | Ascendant | 化神 | Great Power (大能) | ~2,000 years | Equivalent ceiling to the Mountains and Seas Realm. Immortal cities require Ascendant-level+ to establish. |
| **Void Refining** | — | 炼虚 | **True Lord** (真人 / 真君) | ~5,000 years | First stage unique to the Profound Realm |
| **Body Integration** | — | 合体 | **Great Sovereign** (大尊) | ~10,000 years total | Lightning tribulation stage |
| **Great Vehicle** | — | 大乘 | **Heavenly Venerable** (天尊) | — | Highest mortal stage; allows ascension to further realms |
| ↓ Ascension | 飞升 | — | — | — | Leaves the Profound Realm entirely |

### Sect / Force Ranking by Cultivation Level
- **Divine Transformation-level force** — highest cultivator is at Divine Transformation
- **Void Refining-level force** — highest cultivator is a True Lord
- **Sacred Sect** (圣宗) — top-tier Void Refining forces
- **Origin Court** (源庭) — forces with Great Vehicle Heavenly Venerables; supreme tier

## Luo Chen's Cultivation Status (as of Profound Realm arc)

| Path | Level | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Body cultivation | Human Immortal (5th tier) | Intact but fully concealed |
| Qi cultivation | Divine Transformation (化神) | Rebuilt in the Profound Realm after primordial spirit collapsed on ascension. Milestone: ch1231 "Five Elements Divine Transformation" |
| Physique | Chaos Indestructible Body + Five-Spirit Dao Body (hidden) | Five-Spirit Dao Body awakening as he completes the five elemental laws |

**Disguise:** Masquerading as a young dual-spirit-root (metal + fire) cultivator at Initiate stage in his 20s within the Su Ling Sect under the alias Xuan Luo.

**Why his qi cultivation collapsed:** When ascending from the Mountains and Seas Realm, his primordial spirit collapsed and his Purple Mansion was damaged. He had to rebuild qi cultivation entirely from Initiate, while his body cultivation remained as a hidden trump card.
